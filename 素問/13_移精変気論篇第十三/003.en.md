# 素問 移精変気論篇第十三 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. I wish, when attending a patient, to see whether he will die or live and to resolve doubts; I wish to know the key to this as clearly as the light of the sun and moon. May I hear it?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks for the key to judging a patient's death or life. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- Daizhige (*Huangdi neijing suwen*) writes 病患 for 病人.
