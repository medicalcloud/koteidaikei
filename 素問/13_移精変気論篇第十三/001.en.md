# 素問 移精変気論篇第十三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I have heard that in ancient times, in treating illness, they only shifted the essence and changed the qi, and could end it by incantation. Today, in treating illness, toxic drugs treat the interior and needles and stones the exterior, yet sometimes there is a cure and sometimes not. Why is this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] 移 means to shift and change; 変 means to alter and amend. Both mean preventing evil from harming the correct, so that the essence and spirit become strong again and guard within. The *Shengqi tongtian lun* (chapter 3) says, "The sages transmit their essence and spirit and take in the qi of heaven," and the *Shanggu tianzhen lun* (chapter 1) says, "When the essence and spirit keep to the interior, from where could illness come?"

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: in ancient times illness was cured merely by shifting the essence and changing the qi through incantation (*zhuyou*), whereas now even toxic drugs and needles sometimes fail; why? The chapter title, "Shifting the Essence and Changing the Qi", comes from this.
- *Zhuyou* is generally understood as treatment by announcing the origin of the illness in prayer; Quan Yuanqi, cited by the Song editors, says *Zhuyou* is a spirit of the south (unit 002).
