# 素問 移精変気論篇第十三 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The utmost key of treatment is not to lose sight of complexion and pulse; to use them without confusion is the great rule of treatment. If counterflow and compliance are inverted and the tip and the root are not obtained, the spirit is lost and the state is lost. Leave the old and go to the new, and then one obtains the true person."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The utmost key of treatment ... the great rule of treatment"] 惑 means confusion; 則 means rule. It says that the correspondences of complexion and pulse are clear and do not deceive; if one simply uses them in compliance without disordering their order, this is the great rule for treating illness precisely and correctly.

[On "If counterflow and compliance are inverted ... the state is lost"] "Inverting counterflow and compliance" means turning compliance into counterflow; "the tip and the root are not obtained" means physician and patient fail to suit each other. Acting inversely against principle, what one does is not in compliance; it is not only that in treating people their spirit qi is harmed — if such a person were made to assist a ruler, the fortune of the state would also not be kept safe and peaceful.

[On "Leave the old and go to the new, and then one obtains the true person"] When the tip and root are not obtained and physician and patient fail to suit each other, one should leave the person of old, perverse principles and go to the newly enlightened scholar; only then does one obtain a person of utmost truth and subtle understanding and preserve oneself.

## Notes

The key of treatment is not to lose sight of complexion and pulse; if counterflow and compliance are confused and physician and patient do not match, both person (spirit) and state are lost. Leaving the old and mistaken for the new and enlightened, one obtains the true person.
- 到 in 逆従到行 is the same as 倒 "inverted"; Daizhige writes 倒.
- "Leave the old and go to the new": Wang Bing takes it as leaving a mistaken physician for an enlightened one.
