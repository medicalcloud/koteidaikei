# 素問 移精変気論篇第十三 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Close the doors and block the windows, attach [your mind] to the patient, repeatedly ask about his condition, and follow his intentions. Those who have the spirit flourish; those who lose the spirit perish."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Close the doors ... follow his intentions"] One asks what he desires and examines what is right and wrong.

## Notes

Closing the doors and concentrating on the patient, one repeatedly asks about his condition and feelings and follows his intentions. Those who have the spirit (vitality) flourish; those who lose it perish.
- "Those who have the spirit flourish; those who lose the spirit perish" is a well-known phrase on prognosis by the presence of spirit.
- Daizhige writes 系 (simplified form) for 繋.
