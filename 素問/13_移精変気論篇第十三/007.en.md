# 素問 移精変気論篇第十三 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I have heard the key from you, Master. You say not to depart from complexion and pulse; this I already know."

## Notes

Huangdi says he already knows not to depart from complexion and pulse, and seeks a deeper key. There is no note by Wang Bing.
