# 素問 移精変気論篇第十三 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The One is obtained by means of [asking]."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] It is obtained by means of asking.

## Notes

"The One" is obtained by asking (the patient).
- Wang Bing takes 因 as "by means of asking", to be read with "repeatedly ask about his condition" in the next unit.
