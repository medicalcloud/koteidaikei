# 素問 刺志論篇第五十三 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Repletion is qi entering; deficiency is qi leaving. Replete qi is heat; deficient qi is cold. In needling excess, open the needle hole with the left hand; in needling deficiency, close the needle hole with the left hand."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Repletion is qi entering; deficiency is qi leaving"] Entering is yang; leaving is yin. Yin arises within, so it leaves; yang arises outside, so it enters.

[On "Replete qi is heat; deficient qi is cold"] Yang is abundant and yin resists within; therefore there is heat. Yin is abundant and yang is feeble outside; therefore there is cold.

[On "In needling excess, open the needle hole ... close the needle hole with the left hand"] This concerns supplementing and draining with the needle. The right hand holds the needle and the left hand presses the point; therefore in excess the left hand opens the needle hole to drain it, and in deficiency the left hand closes the needle hole to supplement it.

## Notes

The nature of deficiency and excess and the technique of supplementing and draining with the needle (opening or closing the needle hole with the left hand); this ends the chapter. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 004, ends here.
