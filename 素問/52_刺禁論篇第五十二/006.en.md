# 素問 刺禁論篇第五十二 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If needling the inner thigh strikes the great vessel and bleeding does not stop, one dies. If needling Kezhuren sinks in and strikes the vessel, there are internal leakage and deafness. If needling the kneecap lets out fluid, there is lameness. If needling the arm greater yin vessel lets out much blood, one dies at once. If needling the foot lesser yin vessel doubly depletes it and lets out blood, the tongue has difficulty speaking. If needling the breast sinks in and strikes the lung, there are panting, counterflow and breathing with the head thrown back. If needling the elbow sinks in, qi returns there and one cannot bend or stretch. If needling three *cun* below the inner thigh sinks in, it causes incontinence of urine. If needling between the flanks below the armpit sinks in, it causes coughing. If needling the lower abdomen strikes the bladder and urine comes out, it makes the lower abdomen full. If needling the calf sinks in, there is swelling. If needling above the eye socket sinks into the bone and strikes the vessel, there are leakage and blindness. If needling a joint lets fluid out from within, one cannot bend or stretch."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If needling the inner thigh strikes the great vessel ... one dies"] Within the inner thigh is the spleen vessel. The spleen is the central earth, the solitary zang that irrigates the four sides; now if bleeding does not stop, the spleen qi is about to be exhausted; therefore one dies.
Song editors: Huangfu Shi'an moved the item on "needling the inner thigh strikes the great vessel" to follow "needling the instep strikes the great vessel" above; from here to the end of the chapter the items alternate with the earlier ones.

[On "If needling Kezhuren sinks in ... internal leakage and deafness"] Kezhuren is the name of a point, now called Shangguan, at the raised bone on the upper edge in front of the ear, where there is a space when the mouth is open; the hand lesser yang and foot *yangming* vessels meet in it. "Sinking into the vessel" means needling too deeply; needling too deeply ruptures the crossing vessels; therefore there is leakage within the ear. When the vessels leak within, the qi does not nourish; therefore there is deafness.
Song editors: On examination, the Kezhuren point here is the same as in the note to the *Qixue lun*. The *Jiayi jing* and the notes to the *Qixue lun* and *Qifu lun* say "the meeting of the three vessels of the hand and foot lesser yang and foot *yangming*"; here the foot lesser yang is suspected to have dropped out.

[On "If needling the kneecap lets out fluid, there is lameness"] The knee is the house of the sinews, where the sinews meet. When fluid comes out, the sinews dry; therefore there is lameness.

[On "If needling the arm greater yin vessel lets out much blood, one dies at once"] The arm greater yin is the lung vessel. The lung governs the movement of the nutritive and defensive, and the regulation of yin and yang proceeds from it. If much blood is let, the nutritive and defensive are cut off; therefore one dies at once.

[On "If needling the foot lesser yin vessel ... difficulty speaking"] The foot lesser yin is the kidney vessel; it passes through the kidney, connects with the lung and is tied to the root of the tongue; therefore doubly depleting it and letting blood makes speech difficult.

[On "If needling the breast sinks in and strikes the lung ... head thrown back"] It is caused by lung qi leaking upward and counterflowing.

[On "If needling the elbow sinks in ... cannot bend or stretch"] "The elbow" means the crease of the elbow, at the Chize point. If needling passes the sunken vessel, bad qi returns there and the qi stiffens the joint; therefore one cannot bend or stretch.

[On "If needling three *cun* below the inner thigh sinks in ... incontinence of urine"] Three *cun* below the thigh is the kidney network vessel. The thoroughfare vessel and the lesser yin network vessel both arise below the kidney, emerge at Qijie and follow the inner thigh together; the ascending part emerges in the uterus. Therefore needling the sunken vessel causes incontinence of urine.

[On "If needling between the flanks below the armpit sinks in, it causes coughing"] Below the armpit is the lung vessel; the lung vessel emerges transversely from the lung connector below the armpit; the straight course of the vessel of the true heart zang goes from the heart connector back up below the armpit. Needling the sunken vessel stirs both heart and lung; therefore there is coughing.

[On "If needling the lower abdomen strikes the bladder ... the lower abdomen full"] The qi of the bladder leaks out and the grain qi returns there; therefore the lower abdomen is full. The lower abdomen means below the navel.

[On "If needling the calf sinks in, there is swelling"] Within the calf is the foot greater yang vessel; the greater yang qi leaks; therefore there is swelling.

[On "If needling above the eye socket ... leakage and blindness"] 匡 means the eye socket; "in the bone" means in the bone of the eye socket. The vessel in the socket bone is the eye connector, the liver vessel. If needling sinks in, the eye connector is cut off; therefore there are leakage of the eye and blindness.

[On "If needling a joint lets fluid out from within, one cannot bend or stretch"] All sinews belong to the joints, moistened by the fluids; when the fluid comes out, the sinew membranes dry; therefore one cannot bend or stretch.
