# 素問 刺禁論篇第五十二 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "The zang have vital places that must be examined. The liver generates on the left, the lung stores on the right, the heart is stationed at the exterior, the kidney governs the interior, the spleen serves as their envoy, and the stomach as their market. Above the diaphragm and *huang* there are the father and mother within; beside the seventh vertebra there is the small heart within. Following them brings fortune; going against them brings calamity.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The zang have vital places ... The liver generates on the left"] The liver resembles wood and dominates in spring; in spring yang arises; therefore it generates on the left.

[On "the lung stores on the right"] The lung resembles metal and dominates in autumn; in autumn yin gathers and kills; therefore it stores on the right.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says, "The liver is lesser yang, the beginning of the growth of yang; hence 'generates'. The lung is lesser yin, the beginning of the storage of yin; hence 'stores'."

[On "the heart is stationed at the exterior"] Yang qi governs the outside; the heart resembles fire.

[On "the kidney governs the interior"] Yin qi governs the inside; the kidney resembles water.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says, "The heart is the master of the stations of the five zang; hence it can be called 'stationed'. The moving qi between the kidneys governs the five zang within; hence 'governs'."

[On "the spleen serves as their envoy"] It moves without ceasing, making water and grain into dregs; hence it is the envoy.

[On "and the stomach as their market"] It is where water and grain go, and the five flavours all enter, mixed as in a market; hence it is the market.

[On "Above the diaphragm and *huang* there are the father and mother within"] Above the diaphragm and *huang* the sea of qi lies in the centre. Qi is the source of life, and life is the master of fate; therefore the sea of qi is a person's father and mother.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says, "Below the heart and above the diaphragm is the *huang*. The heart is yang, the father; the lung is yin, the mother. The lung governs qi and the heart blood, together nourishing and defending the body; hence they are father and mother."

[On "beside the seventh vertebra there is the small heart within"] The small heart means the true heart, the palace of the spirit.
Song editors: The *Taisu* writes 志心 "will-heart" for 小心. Yang Shangshan says, "The spine has three times seven, twenty-one vertebrae; the kidney is beside the seventh vertebra from the bottom. The spirit of the kidney is called the will. The spiritual faculties of the five zang are all called spirit; the reason the spirit in charge is called the will is that it is the spirit of the heart."

[On "Following them brings fortune; going against them brings calamity"] 従 means follow and comply. These eight are what make a person live and the form complete; therefore following them prolongs fortune and going against them brings calamity.

## Notes

As vital places that must not be needled, the positions and functions of the five zang and the stomach, the "father and mother" and the "small heart" are given. Qibo's speech continues to unit 006.
- 肝生於左，肺蔵於右 is often understood as referring not to the anatomical position of the organs but to the direction of their qi; Wang Bing also explains it by the yang of spring and the yin of autumn.
- 小心: Wang Bing takes it as the true heart; Yang Shangshan as the kidney (will).
