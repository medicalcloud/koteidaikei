# 素問 刺禁論篇第五十二 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Do not needle those who are very drunk; it disorders their qi. Do not needle those who are very angry; it makes their qi counterflow. Do not needle those who are greatly exhausted, those who have just eaten their fill, those who are very hungry, those who are very thirsty, or those who are greatly frightened.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Do not needle those who are very drunk; it disorders their qi"] The pulse rate exceeds the norm; therefore needling disorders it.
Song editors: According to the *Lingshu jing*, 気乱 should be 脈乱 "disorders the pulse".

[On "Do not needle those who are very angry; it makes their qi counterflow"] In anger the qi counterflows; therefore needling makes it worse.

[On "Do not needle those who are greatly exhausted"] The channel qi has overflowed.

[On "those who have just eaten their fill"] The qi is abundantly full.

[On "those who are very hungry"] The qi is insufficient.

[On "those who are very thirsty"] The blood vessels are dry.

[On "or those who are greatly frightened"] The spirit is agitated and scattered and the qi is not in order.
Song editors: On examination, these seven items from "Do not needle those who are very drunk" to here roughly correspond to the *Lingshu jing*, which says, "Do not needle just after sexual intercourse; after needling do not have intercourse. Do not needle in great anger; after needling do not become angry. Do not needle in great exhaustion; after needling do not exhaust oneself. Do not needle when very drunk; after needling do not get drunk. Do not needle when very full; after needling do not overeat. Do not needle when very hungry; after needling do not go hungry. Do not needle when very thirsty; after needling do not go thirsty. In great fright or fear, one must first settle the qi and then needle."

## Notes

Bodily states in which needling is prohibited (great drunkenness, anger, exhaustion, just after eating, great hunger, thirst, fright).
- The *Lingshu jing* passage quoted by the Song editors presumably corresponds to the *Zhongshi* chapter of the *Lingshu*. 己 in the Song editors' 己刺 means 已.
