# 素問 刺禁論篇第五十二 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If needling strikes the heart, one dies in one day; its movement is belching. If it strikes the liver, one dies in five days; its movement is talking. If it strikes the kidney, one dies in six days; its movement is sneezing. If it strikes the lung, one dies in three days; its movement is coughing. If it strikes the spleen, one dies in ten days; its movement is swallowing. If it strikes the gallbladder, one dies in a day and a half; its movement is vomiting.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If needling strikes the heart ... belching"] In terms of qi, the heart produces belching.

[On "If it strikes the liver ... talking"] In terms of qi, the liver produces talking.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Jiayi jing* write 欠 "yawning" for 語. Yuanqi says, "When the kidney is harmed there is yawning; mother and child affect each other." Mr Wang changed 欠 to 語.

[On "If it strikes the kidney ... sneezing"] In terms of qi, the kidney produces sneezing.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Jiayi jing* write three days for six days.

[On "If it strikes the lung ... coughing"] In terms of qi, the lung produces coughing.

[On "If it strikes the spleen ... swallowing"] In terms of qi, the spleen produces swallowing.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Jiayi jing* write fifteen days for ten days. Striking the five zang by needling repeats the *Zhenyao jingzhong lun* and the *Sishi ci nicong lun*. The order of the five zang here follows the generating sequence; the *Jiayi jing* orders them heart, lung, liver, spleen, kidney, following the controlling sequence; the old text of Quan Yuanqi's edition is disordered without sequence.

[On "If it strikes the gallbladder ... vomiting"] The gallbladder qi is brave; therefore there is vomiting.
Song editors: In the *Zhenyao jingzhong lun*, after "striking the gallbladder" it also says, "Striking the diaphragm is called harming the centre; though the illness may heal, one dies within a year."

## Notes

The time of death and symptoms when the five zang or gallbladder are struck by needling in error.
- According to the Song editors, Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Jiayi jing* differ in the number of days and the symptoms.
