# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In heat malaria, the lung has always had heat, the qi is abundant in the body, counterflowing and surging upward, and the qi within is replete and does not leak outward. Then, when one exerts oneself and the interstices open, wind and cold reside inside the skin between the divisions of the flesh and break out. When they break out, yang qi becomes abundant; when yang qi is abundant and does not decline, there is illness. Its qi does not reach yin; therefore there is only heat and no cold. The qi is stored within in the heart and resides outside between the divisions of the flesh, making the person waste away and lose flesh; therefore it is named heat malaria."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Its qi does not reach yin"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu* read 不反之陰, and Chao Yuanfang reads 不及之陰.

## Notes

Heat malaria is an illness in which a person with long-standing lung heat receives wind and cold, shows only heat, and wastes away. There is no note by Wang Bing.
