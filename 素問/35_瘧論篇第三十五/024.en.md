# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Those that skip days are when the evil qi and the defensive qi lodge in the six fu and at times miss each other and cannot meet; therefore it rests for several days and then breaks out. Malaria is yin and yang prevailing in turn, sometimes severely and sometimes not; therefore sometimes there is thirst and sometimes not."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Those that skip days ... then breaks out"] The qi do not meet; therefore it cannot break out for several days.

[On "Malaria is yin and yang prevailing in turn ... sometimes not"] When yang prevails and [the decline of] yin is severe, there is thirst; when yang prevails and [the decline of] yin is not severe, there is no thirst. 勝 means being stronger than the other qi.

## Notes

The reasons for attacks every several days and for the presence or absence of thirst.
