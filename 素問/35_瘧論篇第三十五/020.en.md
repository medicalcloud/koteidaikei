# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When malaria is about to break out and yin and yang are about to shift, it surely begins from the four extremities. Once yang has been harmed, yin follows it. Therefore, before the time, bind the place tightly so that the evil qi cannot enter and the yin qi cannot come out; observe carefully to see it, and take all minute network vessels that are full, hard and bloody. This is when the true [qi] has gone but has not yet been able to combine."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "observe carefully to see it ... has not yet been able to combine"] It says that if one binds the four limbs firmly so that the qi stays in place, where the evil resides will surely show itself; once it is seen, needle and let out its blood. 往 means go.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 其往 for 真往, and the *Taisu* writes 直往.

## Notes

The method of binding the limbs before an attack to reveal where the evil is, and taking the blood of the minute network vessels.
- The base text writes 巳 for the 已 of 陽已傷; the translation takes it as 已.
- 此真往而未得并者也 is obscure and read in various ways. According to the Song editors, the *Jiayi jing* writes 其往 and the *Taisu* 直往.
