# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Malarial qi surely becomes abundant and depleted in turn, according to where the qi is. When the illness is in yang, there is heat and the pulse is agitated; when in yin, there is cold and the pulse is calm. At the extreme, yin and yang both decline and separate from the defensive qi; therefore the illness can rest. When the defensive qi gathers, the illness returns."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the illness is in yang ... the pulse is calm"] Yin is calm and yang agitated; therefore the pulse follows them.

[On "At the extreme, yin and yang both decline ... the illness returns"] They press on each other to the extreme; things that reach their extreme reverse; therefore at the extreme yin and yang both decline.

## Notes

The mechanism of attack and remission in malaria.
