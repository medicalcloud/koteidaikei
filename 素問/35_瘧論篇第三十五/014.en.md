# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When in summer one is harmed by great heat, sweats profusely, and the interstices are open, and then meets the chilly water-cold of summer, [the cold] is stored in the interstices and skin; when in autumn one is harmed by wind, the illness is complete. Cold is yin qi and wind is yang qi; one is harmed first by cold and afterwards by wind; therefore there is first cold and then heat. The illness breaks out at regular times and is called cold malaria."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "and then meets the chilly water-cold of summer"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu* write 小寒迫之 "a slight cold presses on it" for 水寒.

[On "when in autumn one is harmed by wind, the illness is complete"] Summer heat is yang qi; wind striking is received by yang qi; therefore when one is harmed by wind in autumn, the illness is complete.

[On "Cold is yin qi ... called cold malaria"] When the body is exposed, wind and cold harm it.

## Notes

Cold malaria, which arises when one sweats in summer and receives cold (such as cold water), and is then harmed by wind in autumn.
