# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Its qi resides deep and presses inward on yin; the yang qi breaks out alone while the yin evil clings within; yin contends with yang and cannot come out; therefore it occurs every other day."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "yin contends with yang and cannot come out; therefore it occurs every other day"] It does not meet the defensive qi; therefore it breaks out every other day.

## Notes

When the evil enters deeply, the interval at which it meets the defensive qi lengthens, giving attacks every other day.
