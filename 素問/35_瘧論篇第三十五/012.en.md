# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Wind qi stays in its place; therefore it is always present. Malarial qi follows the channels and network vessels, sinking and pressing inward; therefore it breaks out only when the defensive qi responds."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Malarial qi follows the channels and network vessels, sinking and pressing inward"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* reads 次以内伝 "in order transmitted inward".

[On "therefore it breaks out only when the defensive qi responds"] 留 means stay; 随 means follow.

## Notes

Wind stays in one place and so is always present; malarial qi follows the channels inward and so breaks out only when it meets the defensive qi.
