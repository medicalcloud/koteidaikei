# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "You say that each time the defensive qi reaches Fengfu, the interstices open, and when they open the evil qi enters, and when it enters the illness breaks out. Now if the defensive qi descends one vertebra a day, the place where its qi breaks out is not at Fengfu. How then does it break out daily?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks about an apparent inconsistency in the previous explanation. There is no note by Wang Bing.
