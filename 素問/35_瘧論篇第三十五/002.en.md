# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "When a malarial attack first breaks out, it begins in the fine hairs; one stretches and yawns, and then it comes on: shivering with cold and chattering jaw, with pain in both lumbus and spine. When the cold goes, inside and outside are all hot, the head aches as if splitting, and one is thirsty and wants cold drinks."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a malarial attack first breaks out ... chattering jaw"] 慄 means shivering; 鼓 means shaking.

## Notes

The typical course of a malarial attack (shaking chills followed by high fever).
