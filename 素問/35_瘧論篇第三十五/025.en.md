# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "The discourse says, 'If harmed by summer heat in summer, one will surely have malaria in autumn.' Now malaria does not necessarily correspond to this. Why?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The discourse says, 'If harmed by summer heat in summer, one will surely have malaria in autumn'"] Song editors: The *Shengqi tongtian lun* and *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* both say, "If harmed by summer heat in summer, one will surely have intermittent malaria in autumn."

[On "Now malaria does not necessarily correspond to this. Why?"] It says that it is not necessarily always so.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about exceptions to the link between summer heat and autumn malaria.
