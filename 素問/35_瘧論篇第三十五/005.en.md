# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why do some attacks occur every other day?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why do some attacks occur every other day?"] 間日 means every other day.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why some malarial attacks occur every other day.
