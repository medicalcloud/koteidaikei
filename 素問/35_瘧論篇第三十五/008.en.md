# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The evil qi lodges at Fengfu and descends along the spine. The defensive qi makes a great meeting at Fengfu once in a day and a night; the next day [the evil] descends one vertebra a day; therefore the attack comes later. This is when it first lodges in the spine and back. Each time [the defensive qi] arrives at Fengfu the interstices open; when the interstices open the evil qi enters; when the evil qi enters the illness breaks out; therefore the daily attacks come gradually later. After it leaves Fengfu, it descends one vertebra a day; in twenty-five days it reaches the sacrum; on the twenty-sixth day it enters inside the spine and pours into the hidden spinal vessel. Its qi then rises, and in nine days emerges in the supraclavicular fossa. Its qi rises higher each day; therefore the attacks come earlier each day. Those that break out every other day are due to the evil qi pressing inward on the five zang and connecting crosswise with the membrane source. Its path is long, its qi deep and its movement slow; it cannot move together with the defensive qi and cannot come out all at once; therefore it occurs every other day."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The evil qi lodges at Fengfu and descends along the spine"] Fengfu is the name of a point, on top of the head, two body-*cun* inside the hairline, in the hollow inside the great sinew. 膂 means both sides of the spine.

[On "The defensive qi makes a great meeting ... the daily attacks come gradually later"] 節 means the joints of the spinal bones. When the evil qi is distant, the meeting is delayed; therefore the attack comes late.

[On "After it leaves Fengfu ... pours into the hidden spinal vessel"] From the nape down to the coccyx there are twenty-four joints in all; therefore descending one joint a day, in twenty-five days it reaches the sacrum, and on the twenty-sixth day it enters inside the spine and pours into the hidden spinal vessel. The hidden spinal vessel means the kidney vessel running hidden between the spinal muscles. The kidney vessel follows the posterior edge of the inner thigh, passes through the spine and belongs to the kidney; its straight course rises from the kidney through the liver and diaphragm and enters the lung. Because it passes through the spine and does not run in correspondence with points but runs hidden along the spinal muscles, it is called the hidden spinal vessel.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition writes twenty-one days for twenty-five and twenty-two days for twenty-six; the *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu* are the same. For "hidden spinal vessel", the *Jiayi jing* writes "*taichong* vessel" and Chao Yuanfang "hidden thoroughfare".

[On "Its qi then rises ... the attacks come earlier each day"] The kidney vessel passes through the spine, belongs to the kidney and rises into the lung; the lung has the supraclavicular fossa as its passage. Yin qi moves fast; therefore its qi rises and in nine days emerges in the supraclavicular fossa.

[On "Those that break out every other day ... it occurs every other day"] The membrane source means the source connection of the diaphragm membrane.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition writes 膜 for 募; the *Taisu* and Chao Yuanfang are the same. The *Jutong lun* also writes 膜原.

## Notes

Because the evil qi descends one vertebra a day along the spine, the attacks come later each day; when it enters the hidden spinal vessel and rises, they come earlier each day; when the evil enters deeply into the five zang and the membrane source, attacks occur every other day.
- According to the Song editors, Quan Yuanqi's edition, the *Jiayi jing* and the *Taisu* read twenty-one and twenty-two days for twenty-five and twenty-six.
- 募原: according to the Song editors, other editions write 膜原.
- Wang Bing's location of Fengfu here ("two body-*cun* inside the hairline") does not agree with his note to the *Relun* (chapter 31), which says one *cun*.
