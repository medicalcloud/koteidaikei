# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. Wind and malaria are alike and of the same kind; yet wind is always present, while malaria has times when it rests. Why is this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good. Wind and malaria ... Why is this?"] Wind and malaria both have rise and decline; hence "alike and of the same kind".

## Notes

Huangdi asks the difference between wind illness and malaria.
