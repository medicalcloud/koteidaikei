# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "The classic says that what is in surplus is drained and what is insufficient is supplemented. Now heat is surplus and cold is insufficiency. The cold of malaria cannot be warmed by hot water or fire, and its heat cannot be cooled by ice or water; these are all of the category of surplus and insufficiency. At such times even a good practitioner cannot stop it and must wait for it to decline by itself before needling. Why is this? I wish to hear the explanation."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "At such times ... I wish to hear the explanation"] It asks why, though there is time, one does not [treat] early, but waits for it to reach its peak and stop by itself.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why one does not needle during an attack but waits for it to decline.
