# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "In warm malaria and cold malaria, where does [the evil] reside? In which zang does it reside?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In warm malaria and cold malaria ... In which zang does it reside?"] 安 means where. 舎 means reside and stay. 蔵 means the five spirit-storing zang.

## Notes

Huangdi asks in which zang the evil of warm and cold malaria resides.
