# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. What qi makes the attacks come later each day or earlier each day?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good. What qi makes the attacks come later each day ...?"] 晏 means late in the day.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why the time of attack becomes later or earlier each day.
