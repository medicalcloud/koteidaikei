# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "This is the case of evil qi lodging in the head and nape and descending along the spine. Thus when depletion and repletion differ and the evil strikes different places, it need not be at Fengfu. Thus when evil strikes the head and nape, illness arises when the qi reaches the head and nape; when it strikes the back, illness arises when the qi reaches the back; when it strikes the lumbus and spine, illness arises when the qi reaches the lumbus and spine; when it strikes the hands and feet, illness arises when the qi reaches the hands and feet. Where the defensive qi is and meets the evil qi, the illness breaks out. Therefore wind has no fixed residence: where the defensive qi breaks out, the interstices surely open, and where the evil qi meets it, that is its residence."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Qibo said"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition, the *Jiayi jing* and the *Taisu* all lack the eighty-eight characters from "This is the case of evil qi lodging in the head and nape" to "the illness breaks out" below.

[On "when it strikes the hands and feet, illness arises when the qi reaches the hands and feet"] Therefore the following chapter needles each according to where the evil resides.

[On "Where the defensive qi is ... that is its residence"] Although depletion and repletion differ and the evil strikes different places, when the defensive qi and the evil meet, the illness breaks out; it need not always break out at Fengfu.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* and Chao Yuanfang write 其病作 "the illness breaks out" for 則其府也.

## Notes

The evil strikes various places, and an attack occurs wherever the defensive qi meets it there; it need not be at Fengfu.
- According to the Song editors, the first part of this unit (eighty-eight characters) is absent from Quan Yuanqi's edition, the *Jiayi jing* and the *Taisu*.
- The "following chapter" in Wang Bing's note presumably refers to the *Cinüe pian* (chapter 36).
