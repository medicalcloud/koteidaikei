# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Here one is harmed first by wind and afterwards by cold; therefore there is first heat and then cold. It too breaks out at regular times and is called warm malaria. When there is only heat and no cold, the yin qi is first cut off and the yang qi breaks out alone; then there are shortness of breath, vexation and distress, hot hands and feet, and the urge to vomit. It is called heat malaria."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Here one is harmed first by wind ... called warm malaria"] Because it begins with heat, it is called warm.

[On "When there is only heat and no cold ... called heat malaria"] 癉 means heat; extreme heat produces it.

## Notes

Warm malaria (heat first, then cold) and heat malaria (heat only, no cold).
