# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "Intermittent fevers all arise from wind. Why do they lie dormant and break out at regular times?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Intermittent fevers all arise from wind ... at regular times?"] 痎 means old, and also emaciated.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* reads "Malarial illnesses all arise from wind; why do they occur by the day and break out by the hour?", which differs from this text. The *Taisu* agrees with the present text. Yang Shangshan says, "Regarding 痎, some say that what breaks out once every two days is called 痎瘧. This classic simply calls the illness that arises in autumn after harm from summer heat either 痎瘧 or just 瘧; 痎 is not necessarily determined by breaking out daily or every other day. It is called 痎 only because its form differs according to the four seasons."

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: why do the attacks of 瘧 (a malaria-like illness with periodic fever and chills) have a periodicity?
- 痎瘧: Wang Bing takes 痎 as "old" or "emaciated". Yang Shangshan, quoted by the Song editors, rejects the view that 痎瘧 is what breaks out every other day.
