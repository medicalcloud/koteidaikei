# 素問 瘧論篇第三十五 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "That is what corresponds to the four seasons. Those whose illness takes a different form go against the four seasons. Those who fall ill in autumn have severe cold; in winter, cold that is not severe; in spring, aversion to wind; in summer, much sweating."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Those who fall ill in autumn have severe cold"] The qi of autumn is clear and cool, yang qi descends, and heat is stored in the flesh; therefore the cold is severe.

[On "in winter, cold that is not severe"] The qi of winter is severe and bitter; yang qi lies hidden and does not contend with cold; therefore the cold is not severe.

[On "in spring, aversion to wind"] The qi of spring is warm and mild; yang qi leaks outward and the interstices open; therefore one dislikes wind.

[On "in summer, much sweating"] The qi of summer is hot; the fluids are full and leak out through the skin; therefore there is much sweating.

## Notes

Differences in symptoms when malaria occurs in seasons other than autumn.
