# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong said, "Liver depletion, kidney depletion and spleen depletion all make the body heavy with vexation and distress. One should apply toxic medicines, needling and moxibustion, stone needles and decoctions; sometimes they cure and sometimes not. I wish to hear the explanation."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "One should apply toxic medicines ... I wish to hear the explanation"] Since the Emperor's question asked him to speak of the errors of the five viscera, toxic medicines, decoctions and flavors, Leigong asks about this illness.

## Notes

Leigong asks why treatments of liver, kidney and spleen depletion sometimes work and sometimes not.
