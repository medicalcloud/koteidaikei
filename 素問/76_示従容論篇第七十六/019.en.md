# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

This failure is my fault: thinking you knew it, I did not tell you. Clearly drawing on comparison of categories and unhurried [examination] is therefore called 'diagnosis 軽 [classic]'; this is called the utmost Way."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "This failure is my fault: thinking you knew it, I did not tell you"] 是 means "this". He says your rough view of this illness is because I did not tell you the way of comparing categories; hence he calls it his own fault.

[On "Clearly drawing on comparison of categories and unhurried [examination] is therefore called 'diagnosis 軽'"] Song editors: The *Taisu* has 経 for 軽.

[On "is therefore called 'diagnosis 軽'; this is called the utmost Way"] Clearly drawing on form and pattern and comparing and weighing categories, following now the meaning of *Congrong*, even light and slight [illnesses] will not be missed. Why so? Because of the utmost subtlety of the Way. *Congrong* is the name of a chapter of an ancient classic. How do we know? In the *Yinyang lei lun* Leigong says, "目 [I] have fully devoted my mind to receiving the transmission, recited the channels, obtained the way of *Congrong* and matched it with *Congrong*." That the ancient texts had a *Congrong* is clear.

## Notes

The Emperor says not teaching comparison of categories was his own fault, and concludes that comparison of categories and unhurried examination are the utmost Way. His speech ends here.
- 目 in 目悉尽意 in Wang Bing's note is read as 臣 ("I") from the text of the *Yinyang lei lun*, but this has not been verified.
