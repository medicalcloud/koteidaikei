# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong said, "Here is a person with limp limbs, panting, coughing and bloody discharge. I diagnosed it as injury to the lungs; on feeling the pulse it was floating, large and tight, and I did not dare treat it. A crude practitioner applied stone needles, and the illness was cured; much blood came out, and when the bleeding stopped the body felt light. What is this?"

## Notes

Leigong cites a patient he diagnosed as lung injury and did not treat, whom a crude practitioner cured with stone needles. There is no note by Wang Bing.
