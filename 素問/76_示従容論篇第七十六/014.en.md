# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Now a pulse that is floating, large and empty means the spleen qi is cut off outwardly, leaving the stomach and returning outward to *yangming*.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "means the spleen qi is cut off outwardly, leaving the stomach and returning outward to *yangming*"] A branch of the foot greater yin network vessel enters to network the intestines and stomach. Hence spleen qi is cut off outwardly and, not reaching the stomach, returns outward to *yangming*.

## Notes

The pulse in Leigong's case indicates illness of the spleen qi.
