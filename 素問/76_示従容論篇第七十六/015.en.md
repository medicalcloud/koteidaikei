# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Two fires cannot overcome three waters; hence the pulse is disordered and without constancy.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Two fires cannot overcome three waters; hence the pulse is disordered and without constancy"] The two fires are the two yang viscera; the three waters are the three yin viscera. The two yang viscera are the heart and lungs, because they lie above the diaphragm; the three yin viscera are the liver, spleen and kidneys, because they lie below it. The qi of the three yin rise and overcome the two yang; yang cannot overcome yin, hence the pulse is disordered and without constancy.

## Notes

The Emperor says the pulse is disordered because yin overcomes yang.
