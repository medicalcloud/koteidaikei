# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "What you can treat and know is also much, but with this illness you went wrong. It is like a wild goose flying that happens to soar into the sky.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What you can treat and know is also much, but with this illness you went wrong"] Thinking it lung injury and not daring to treat it was a wild view, an error against the rules.

[On "It is like a wild goose flying that happens to soar into the sky"] A wild goose flying up into the sky does so by chance; how could it be the ability of its wings? The crude practitioner applying stone needles was likewise.

## Notes

The Emperor states that Leigong's diagnosis was wrong and that the crude practitioner's cure was by chance. His speech continues through unit 19.
