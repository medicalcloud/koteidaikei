# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong said, "Here is a person with headache, cramped sinews, heavy bones, timidity and shortage of qi, hiccup and belching, abdominal fullness, occasional fright, and no liking for lying down. From which viscus does this arise? The pulse is floating and string-like, and on pressing hard as stone. I do not know the explanation. I asked again about the three viscera in order to know how to compare their categories."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I do not know the explanation. I asked again about the three viscera in order to know how to compare their categories"] Because the pulse is floating, string-like, stony and hard, he says "I asked again about the three viscera in order to know how to compare their categories".

## Notes

Leigong gives a concrete case and asks which viscus is ill.
