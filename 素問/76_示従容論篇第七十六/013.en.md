# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When sages treat illness, they follow the rules and keep the measures, draw on things and compare categories, transform in the obscure, follow the upper and reach the lower; why must they keep to the channels?

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "transform in the obscure, follow the upper and reach the lower; why must they keep to the channels"] 経 means the channels, not the classic rules.

## Notes

The Emperor says the sages' treatment is flexible, based on comparing categories.
