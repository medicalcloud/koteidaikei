# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor sat at leisure, summoned Leigong and asked him, "You have received the art and recited the writings. If you can survey miscellaneous learning, extend it to comparing categories, and connect and fit it with principle, tell me what you are good at. The five viscera and six bowels, the gallbladder, stomach, large and small intestines, spleen, womb and bladder, brain and marrow, snivel and spittle, wailing, weeping, sorrow and grief, and the paths along which water moves: all these are what humans live by, and in treating them there are errors. If you strive to clarify them, you can achieve complete success; if you cannot know them, you will be resented by the world."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the paths along which water moves: all these are what humans live by, and in treating them there are errors"] In the *Wuzang bie lun* the Yellow Emperor asks, "I have heard that some practitioners take the brain and marrow as viscera, some take the intestines and stomach as viscera, and some as bowels. I venture to ask: they contradict each other, yet each says he is right. I do not know the way; I wish to hear the explanation." Qibo says, "The brain, marrow, bones, vessels, gallbladder and womb: these six are produced by earth's qi; all store in yin and resemble earth; hence they store and do not drain, and are called the extraordinary bowels. The stomach, large intestine, small intestine, triple burner and bladder: these five are produced by heaven's qi; their qi resembles heaven; they drain and do not store; they receive the turbid qi of the five viscera, and are called the bowels of conveyance and transformation." Hence those of old who treated illness regarded [confusing these] as an error.

[On "If you strive to clarify them, you can achieve complete success; if you cannot know them, you will be resented by the world"] Not knowing these, one harms life whenever one acts; hence when people hear discussion, many feel resentment and blame.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter: the Yellow Emperor asks Leigong to state what he knows about the viscera, bowels, fluids and other bases of human life.
