# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

It is like heaven being formless and earth patternless: white and black are far apart.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is like heaven being formless and earth patternless: white and black are far apart"] It says that lung injury and spleen injury differ widely in form and pattern, likening it to the distance between heaven and earth and the different appearances of black and white.

## Notes

The Emperor illustrates the difference between the two illnesses with a simile.
