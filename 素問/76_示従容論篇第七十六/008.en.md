# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

That the three viscera, earth, wood and water, dwell together is something a child knows. Why ask about it?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "That the three viscera ... dwell together is something a child knows. Why ask about it"] The spleen matches earth, the liver wood, the kidneys water; the three viscera all lie below the diaphragm and dwell close together.

## Notes

The Emperor says anyone knows the three viscera lie close together. His speech ends here.
