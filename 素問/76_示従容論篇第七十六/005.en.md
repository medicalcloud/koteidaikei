# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "How old you are, sir, yet how childish your question! Truly in asking I have invited error upon myself.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How old you are, sir, yet how childish your question! Truly in asking I have invited error upon myself"] It says the question does not correspond. Because it does not correspond, he says that by truly asking he has invited an erroneous answer upon himself.

## Notes

The Emperor points out that Leigong's question does not answer his own. The Emperor's speech continues through unit 8.
