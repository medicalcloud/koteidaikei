# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Try separately to master the errors of the five viscera, the disharmonies of the six bowels, the failures of needles and stones, what toxic medicines suit, and decoctions and nourishing flavors; describe their conditions fully and answer completely. Ask about what you do not know."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "describe their conditions fully and answer completely. Ask about what you do not know"] 過 means errors, that is, illness arising from not following the constant signs. Toxic medicines 政 [attack] pathogens; nourishing flavors fill and nourish. He tests what Leigong knows and does not know.
Song editors: The *Taisu* has 誠別而已 for 別試.

## Notes

The Emperor asks Leigong to describe illnesses of the viscera and bowels and their treatments.
- 政 in 毒薬政邪 in Wang Bing's note is read as 攻 ("attack").
