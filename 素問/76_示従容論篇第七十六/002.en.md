# 素問 示従容論篇第七十六 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong said, "The upper and lower chapters of the *Classic of Pulses* that I have recited are very many, yet in distinguishing differences and comparing categories I still cannot achieve complete success. How could I be fit to clarify these?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "yet in distinguishing differences and comparing categories I still cannot achieve complete success. How could I be fit to clarify these"] It says: the two chapters of the *Classic of Pulses* I have recited are many, but in distinguishing differences and comparing categories I still cannot grasp their meaning and achieve complete success; how could I be fit to understand the ultimate principle in my mind? 安 means "how".

## Notes

Leigong replies that he cannot yet achieve complete success.
