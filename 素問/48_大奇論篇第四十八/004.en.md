# 素問 大奇論篇第四十八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the spleen pulse drums outward and is sunken, there is intestinal flux, which ends by itself in time. When the liver pulse is small and relaxed, there is intestinal flux, easy to treat. When the kidney pulse is small, beating and sunken, there is intestinal flux with blood; if the blood is warm and the body hot, one dies. Heart and liver flux also discharge blood; when both zang are ill together, it can be treated. When their pulses are small, sunken and rough, there is intestinal flux; if the body is hot, one dies; once heat appears, death comes in seven days.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the spleen pulse drums outward and is sunken ... ends by itself in time"] "Drums outward" means beating on the outer side of the forearm.

[On "When the liver pulse is small and relaxed ... easy to treat"] A small, relaxed liver pulse means the spleen encroaches on the liver; therefore it is easy to treat.

[On "When the kidney pulse is small, beating and sunken, there is intestinal flux with blood"] Small means yin qi is insufficient; beating means yang qi encroaches on it; heat is in the lower burner; therefore blood is discharged.

[On "if the blood is warm and the body hot, one dies"] Warm blood and a hot body mean the yin qi is lost and ruined; therefore one dies.

[On "Heart and liver flux also discharge blood"] The liver stores blood and the heart nourishes blood; therefore their flux both discharge blood.

[On "when both zang are ill together, it can be treated"] The heart is fire and the liver wood; wood and fire generate each other; therefore it can be treated.

[On "When their pulses are small, sunken and rough, there is intestinal flux"] When the pulses of the heart and liver are small, sunken and rough, it is flux.

[On "if the body is hot, one dies; once heat appears, death comes in seven days"] Intestinal flux with blood and a hot body means the fire qi is cut off within, leaving the heart and returning outward; therefore one dies. The completing number of fire is seven; therefore death in seven days.

## Notes

The prognosis of intestinal flux (diarrhoea and bleeding) by the pulse.
- The base text writes 己 for the 已 of 久自已; the translation takes it as 已.
