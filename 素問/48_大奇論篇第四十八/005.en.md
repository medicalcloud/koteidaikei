# 素問 大奇論篇第四十八 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the stomach pulse is sunken, drumming and rough, the stomach [pulse] drums outward and large, or the heart pulse is small, hard and tense, all indicate blockage and hemiplegia. In men it breaks out on the left, in women on the right. If there is no loss of voice and the tongue moves, it can be treated and one rises in thirty days. In compliant cases with loss of voice, one rises in three years. Those under twenty die in three years.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the stomach pulse is sunken ... blockage and hemiplegia"] "Drums outward" means it does not strike at the *chi* and *cun* positions but beats on the outer side of the forearm.

[On "In men it breaks out on the left, in women on the right"] Yang governs the left and yin the right; hence this. The *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* says, "Left and right are the pathways of yin and yang" — this is the meaning.

[On "If there is no loss of voice ... one rises in thirty days"] Loss of voice in hemiplegia means the vessels of the kidney and uterus are cut off within. The uterine vessel is tied to the kidney; the kidney vessel rises from the kidney through the liver and diaphragm, enters the lung, follows the throat and runs alongside the root of the tongue; therefore when the qi is cut off within, there is loss of voice.

[On "In compliant cases with loss of voice, one rises in three years"] "Compliant" means breaking out on the left in men and on the right in women. Even if the illness follows the proper side, if there is loss of voice, one can rise only after three years of treatment.

[On "Those under twenty die in three years"] Because their five zang have only just settled and their blood and qi are just vigorous; zang just settled are easily harmed, and vigorous qi is spent heavily; being easily harmed and spending heavily, they die in three years.

## Notes

The diagnosis and prognosis of hemiplegia by the pulse.
