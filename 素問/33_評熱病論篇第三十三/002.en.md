# 素問 評熱病論篇第三十三 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "The illness is called the crossing of yin and yang; those in whom they cross die."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The illness is called the crossing of yin and yang ... die"] 交 means mingling, so that the qi of yin and yang are not separated.

## Notes

The illness in which fever does not fall and the pulse does not calm after sweating is named "crossing of yin and yang" and is a sign of death.
