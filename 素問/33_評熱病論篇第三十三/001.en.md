# 素問 評熱病論篇第三十三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "There are those with warm illness who, after sweating, at once become hot again, whose pulse is agitated and rapid and does not abate with sweating, who talk wildly and cannot eat. What is the name of the illness?"

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter. There is no note by Wang Bing.
