# 素問 評熱病論篇第三十三 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There are those with kidney wind whose face and insteps are swollen and puffy, with obstruction that hampers speech. May they be needled?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "There are those with kidney wind ... May they be needled?"] 痝然 describes swelling. 壅 means the area below the eyes is puffed up like a lying silkworm. The kidney vessel rises from the kidney through the liver and diaphragm, enters the lung, follows the throat and runs alongside the root of the tongue; therefore it hampers speech.

## Notes

Huangdi asks whether the oedema of kidney wind may be needled.
- 胕 is taken by some as the instep (跗) and by others as swelling; the translation takes it as the instep.
- For 蚕 in Wang Bing's note, Kanripo writes 𧖟.
