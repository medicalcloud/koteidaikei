# 素問 評熱病論篇第三十三 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Where pathogen gathers, the qi must be deficient. When yin is deficient, yang will surely gather there; therefore there are shortness of breath, intermittent heat and sweating. Yellow urine means there is heat in the lower abdomen. Inability to lie flat on the back means the stomach is not in harmony. Lying flat brings severe coughing because [the qi] rises and presses on the lung. In all cases with water qi, slight swelling first appears below the eyes."

## Notes

Explains several symptoms of wind edema. 邪之所湊，其気必虚 is a frequently quoted phrase. There is no note by Wang Bing.
