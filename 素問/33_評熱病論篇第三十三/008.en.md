# 素問 評熱病論篇第三十三 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The greater yang governs the qi; therefore it receives the evil first. The lesser yin is its exterior-interior partner; on receiving heat it follows it upward, and following it there is reversal."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "on receiving heat it follows it upward, and following it there is reversal"] "Follows it upward" means the lesser yin follows the greater yang and rises.

## Notes

The greater yang receives wind evil, and the qi of the lesser yin, its exterior-interior partner, follows the heat and counterflows upward; this is wind reversal.
