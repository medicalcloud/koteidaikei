# 素問 評熱病論篇第三十三 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It is deficiency and should not be needled. If needled when it should not be, after five days its qi will surely arrive."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is deficiency and should not be needled ... its qi will surely arrive"] "Arrive" means the qi of the illness comes. Assigning one day to each zang, it reaches the kidney in five days. The kidney is already insufficient and wind presses on it within; if one takes the swelling as excess and drains greatly with the needle, one instead harms the qi of the zang; the genuine qi is insufficient and cannot recover; therefore after needling, in five days its qi will surely arrive.

## Notes

Kidney wind is a deficiency pattern and must not be needled; if wrongly needled, the qi of the illness arrives after five days.
