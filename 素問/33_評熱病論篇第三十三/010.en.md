# 素問 評熱病論篇第三十三 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Needle the exterior and interior, and give a decoction to drink."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Needle the exterior and interior, and give a decoction to drink"] It means draining the greater yang and supplementing the lesser yin. Giving a decoction to drink means stopping the counterflowing kidney qi.

## Notes

The treatment of wind reversal.
