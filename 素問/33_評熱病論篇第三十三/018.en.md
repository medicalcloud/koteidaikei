# 素問 評熱病論篇第三十三 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When it arrives, there will surely be shortness of breath and intermittent heat; the heat rises from the chest and back to the head; there are sweating, hot hands, dry mouth with bitterness and thirst, yellow urine, swelling below the eyes, rumbling in the abdomen, heaviness of the body making walking difficult, absence of menses, vexation with inability to eat, and inability to lie flat on the back; lying flat on the back brings coughing. The illness is called wind water. It is discussed in *Cifa*."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The illness is called wind water. It is discussed in *Cifa*"] *Cifa* is the name of a chapter, now lost from the classic.

## Notes

The symptoms of wind water that appear after wrongful needling.
- According to Wang Bing, *Cifa* is a lost chapter.
