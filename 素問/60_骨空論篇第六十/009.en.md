# 素問 骨空論篇第六十 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The method of moxibustion for cold and heat: first apply moxibustion to Dazhui on the nape, with as many cones as the years of age. Next apply moxibustion to the coccyx, with as many cones as the years of age. Look at the back *shu* points and apply moxibustion where they are sunken. Raise the arm and apply moxibustion to the depression above the shoulder. Apply moxibustion between the two lower ribs. Apply moxibustion at the end of Juegu above the outer ankle. Apply moxibustion between the little toe and the next toe. Apply moxibustion to the sunken vessel below the calf. Apply moxibustion behind the outer ankle. Above the bone of Quepen, palpate, and where it is hard, painful and like a sinew, apply moxibustion. Apply moxibustion between the sunken bones in the middle of the chest. Apply moxibustion below the bundled bones of the palm. Apply moxibustion at Guanyuan, three [*cun*] below the navel. Apply moxibustion at the pulsing vessel at the pubic hairline. Apply moxibustion in the division three *cun* below the knee. Apply moxibustion at the pulsing vessel on the instep of the foot *yangming*. Apply moxibustion once on top of the vertex. Apply three cones at the place bitten by a dog, then apply moxibustion according to the method for dog-bite illness. In all, twenty-nine places should be treated with moxibustion. Injury by food is also treated with moxibustion. If it does not stop, one must look at where the channel passes over into the yang, needle its *shu* points repeatedly, and give medicines."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "first apply moxibustion to Dazhui on the nape, with as many cones as the years of age"] As many as the patient's years.

[On "Next apply moxibustion to the coccyx, with as many cones as the years of age"] The tail end is called the 橛骨 (coccyx).

[On "Look at the back *shu* points and apply moxibustion where they are sunken"] There are sunken places at the edge of the shoulder-blade bones of the back.

[On "Raise the arm and apply moxibustion to the depression above the shoulder"] The Jianyu point, between the two bones at the tip of the shoulder, the meeting of the hand *yangming* and the *qiao* vessel; one may needle six body-*fen* and retain six breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "Apply moxibustion between the two lower ribs"] The Jingmen point, the alarm point of the kidney, in the lumbus at the iliac bone, at the root of the lower ribs, flanking the spine; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "Apply moxibustion at the end of Juegu above the outer ankle"] The Yangfu point, above the outer ankle, in front of the auxiliary bone, about three body-*fen* in front of the end of Juegu, seven *cun* from Qiuxu, where the foot lesser yang vessel travels; one may needle five body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says "four *cun* above the outer ankle".

[On "Apply moxibustion between the little toe and the next toe"] The Xiaxi point, between the forked bones of the little toe and the next toe, in the depression in front of the base joint, where the foot lesser yang vessel flows; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain three breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: According to the *Jiayi jing*, 流 "flows" should be 留 "lodges".

[On "Apply moxibustion to the sunken vessel below the calf"] The Chengjin point, in the depression at the center of the calf, where the vessel qi of the foot greater yang issues; needling is prohibited; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: The note to the *Ci yaotong pian* says "at the center of the calf, in what seems an outer depression".

[On "Apply moxibustion behind the outer ankle"] The Kunlun point, behind the outer ankle, in the depression above the heel bone, where a fine pulse is felt under the hand, where the foot greater yang vessel travels; one may needle five body-*fen* and retain ten breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "Above the bone of Quepen ... apply moxibustion"] The classic lacks its name; apply moxibustion wherever [the hardness and pain] is found.

[On "Apply moxibustion between the sunken bones in the middle of the chest"] The Tiantu point; location, moxibustion, needling, *fen* and cones are as for "the one in the middle of Quepen" above.

[On "Apply moxibustion below the bundled bones of the palm"] The Yangchi point, in the depression on the back of the wrist, where the hand lesser yang vessel passes; one may needle two body-*fen* and retain six breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "Apply moxibustion at Guanyuan, three [*cun*] below the navel"] It is exactly three body-*cun* below the navel, the meeting of the three foot yin and the *ren* vessel; one may needle two body-*cun* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, seven cones.
Song editors: The note to the *Qifu lun* saying "one may needle one *cun* two *fen*" is wrong.

[On "Apply moxibustion at the pulsing vessel at the pubic hairline"] The place is where the pulse is felt under the hand; this is the Qijie point.

[On "Apply moxibustion in the division three *cun* below the knee"] The Sanli point, three body-*cun* below the knee, in the division between the two muscles at the outer edge of the shin bone, where the foot *yangming* vessel enters; one may needle one body-*cun* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "Apply moxibustion at the pulsing vessel on the instep of the foot *yangming*"] The Chongyang point, at the pulsing vessel between the bones five body-*cun* up the instep, where the foot *yangming* vessel passes; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain ten breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* and Quan Yuanqi's edition have 灸之 after 足陽明; together with 跗上動脈 these are two points. Now Wang removed the two characters 灸之, yet it is apparent that there are two points. We now keep the two characters 灸之 in the note, to open the question.

[On "Apply moxibustion once on top of the vertex"] The Baihui point, at the center of the crown in the hair whorl, in a depression that admits a finger, the crossing of the *du* vessel and the foot greater yang vessel; one may needle three body-*fen*; for moxibustion, five cones.

[On "Apply three cones at the place bitten by a dog ... dog-bite illness"] For those who develop cold and heat from a dog bite, apply three cones by the dog-bite method.

[On "In all, twenty-nine places ... Injury by food is also treated with moxibustion"] Illness from injury by food also produces cold and heat, so moxibustion is applied.
Song editors: On examination, if the foot *yangming* is not treated separately there are twenty-eight places; we suspect Wang was wrong to remove the two characters 灸之 above.

## Notes

The chapter ends by listing the twenty-nine places for moxibustion in cold and heat. Qibo's speech ends here.
- According to the Song editors, the *Jiayi jing* and Quan Yuanqi's edition separate "foot *yangming*, moxibustion" and "pulsing vessel on the instep, moxibustion" into two places; because Wang Bing removed 灸之 there are only twenty-eight. How the list in the main text is counted to reach twenty-nine has not been checked.
- The Song editors' 則見二穴 is hard to construe; the translation stays close to the characters.
- 殆知閣 (黄帝内经素问) places a 巻第十六 break at the end of this unit; this is that source's division into juan.
