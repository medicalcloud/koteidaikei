# 素問 骨空論篇第六十 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The fifty-seven water points: above the sacrum, five rows of five each; above Futu, two rows of five each; on the left and right, one row each of five; above the ankles, one row each of six points.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The fifty-seven water points ... six points"] Location, needling, moxibustion, *fen* and cones are given in full in the *Shuire xue lun*. These are all bone hollows, so they are repeated here as in the *Qixue pian*.

## Notes

The arrangement of the fifty-seven water points, overlapping with the *Shuire xue lun* and *Qixue lun*.
