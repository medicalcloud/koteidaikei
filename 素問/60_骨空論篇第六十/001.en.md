# 素問 骨空論篇第六十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I have heard that wind is the beginning of the hundred illnesses. How is it treated with the needle?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I have heard that wind is the beginning of the hundred illnesses. How is it treated with the needle?"] 始 means beginning.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: needling for wind illness. The chapter further describes the courses of the extraordinary vessels and the bone hollows (points).
