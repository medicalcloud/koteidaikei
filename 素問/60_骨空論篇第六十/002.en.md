# 素問 骨空論篇第六十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "When wind enters from outside, it makes one shiver with cold, sweat, have headache, a heavy body and aversion to cold. Treatment is at Fengfu. Regulate yin and yang: supplement what is insufficient and drain what is in surplus.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When wind enters from outside ... aversion to cold"] When wind strikes the body, the interstices close tightly, yang qi resists within, and cold prevails outside; prevailing and resisting press on each other and the nutritive and defensive lose their places; hence this.

[On "Treatment is at Fengfu"] The Fengfu point, in the hollow on the nape one body-*cun* inside the hairline, the meeting of the *du* vessel and foot greater yang; one may needle four body-*fen*; for moxibustion, five cones.
Song editors: Fengfu has already been annotated in the *Qixue lun* and *Qifu lun*, in agreement with the *Jiayi jing*. This note's "meeting of the *du* vessel and foot greater yang, five cones of moxibustion" describes the Fengmen Refu point. It should say "the meeting of the *du* vessel and *yangwei*, retain three breaths, no moxibustion".

[On "Regulate yin and yang: supplement what is insufficient and drain what is in surplus"] The way of the needle must follow the constancy of heaven: drain the abundant and supplement the depleted; this is the norm.

## Notes

The symptoms of illness from wind entering from outside, and treatment by needling Fengfu. Qibo's speech continues to unit 009.
