# 素問 骨空論篇第六十 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Above the auxiliary bone and below the transverse bone is the *jian*. Flanking the hip bone is the *ji*. The knee joint is the *haiguan*. The bones flanking the knee are the *lianhai*. Below the *hai* is the *fu*. Above the *fu* is the popliteal fossa. Above the popliteal fossa is the *guan*. The transverse bone of the head is the *zhen*.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Above the auxiliary bone ... The transverse bone of the head is the *zhen*"] By this, above the auxiliary bone of the knee and below the hip bone of the lumbus is the *jian*; above the *jian* is the *ji*; the outside of the knee is the *haiguan*; behind the *jian* is the *guan*; below the *guan* is the popliteal fossa; below the popliteal fossa is the auxiliary bone; above the auxiliary bone is the *lianhai*; the *lianhai* is where the bones of the leg join. The transverse bone on top of the head is the occipital bone.

## Notes

Definitions of the place names in the previous unit.
- Wang Bing's ordering ("below the popliteal fossa is the auxiliary bone", "above the auxiliary bone is the *lianhai*") does not match the main text's "below the *hai* is the *fu*; above the *fu* is the popliteal fossa". The translation follows each text's characters.
