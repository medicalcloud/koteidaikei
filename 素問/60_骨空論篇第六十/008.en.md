# 素問 骨空論篇第六十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The marrow hollow is three *fen* behind the brain, below the pointed bone at the edge of the skull. One is below the gum base. One is below the 復骨 in the middle of the back of the nape. One is the hollow above the spine, above Fengfu. The lower hollow of the spine is in the hollow below the sacrum. Several marrow hollows are in the face flanking the nose. Some bone hollows are below the mouth, corresponding to the two shoulders. The bone hollows of the two shoulder blades are on the yang side in the middle of the shoulder blades. The bone hollow of the arm is on the yang side of the arm, four *cun* from the wrist, between the hollows of the two bones. The upper hollow of the femur is on the yang side of the thigh, four *cun* above the knee. The bone hollow of the shin is at the upper end of the auxiliary bone. The bone hollow at the edge of the thigh is below the pulse in the pubic hair. The bone hollow of the sacrum is behind the hip bone, four *cun* apart. Flat bones have seeping patterns converging on them but no marrow orifice; likewise, the marrow has no hollow.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The marrow hollow is three *fen* behind the brain"] This means Fengfu, which communicates with the brain.

[On "below the pointed bone at the edge of the skull. One is below the gum base"] In the depression of the bone below the 頣 [頤, chin]; there is a point that holds a bean. The *Zhonggao* names it Xiayi.

[On "One is below the 復骨 in the middle of the back of the nape"] This means the Yinmen point, in the hollow at the hairline of the nape, entering to connect to the root of the tongue, the meeting of the *du* vessel and *yangwei*; take it with the head raised; one may needle four body-*fen*; moxibustion is prohibited.

[On "One is the hollow above the spine, above Fengfu"] "Above" means the Naohu point, in the hollow one *cun* five *fen* behind Dayu above the occipital bone, the meeting of the *du* vessel and foot greater yang. It is distinct from the door of the brain. Do not apply moxibustion rashly; if it goes badly it makes one mute. One may needle three body-*fen* and retain three breaths.
Song editors: According to the *Jiayi jing*, Dayu is another name for Qiangjian. The note to the *Qifu lun* says "for moxibustion, five cones".

[On "The lower hollow of the spine is in the hollow below the sacrum"] It does not serve for treatment; the classic lacks its name.
Song editors: According to the *Jiayi jing*, Changqiang is at the end of the coccyx, precisely below the sacrum. Is not 主氏 [Wang]'s statement that "it does not serve for treatment; the classic 関 [lacks] its name" mistaken?

[On "Several marrow hollows are in the face flanking the nose"] This means the Quanliao and other points; the classic does not point out each place because they are small.

[On "Some bone hollows are below the mouth, corresponding to the two shoulders"] This means the Daying point; location, needling, moxibustion, *fen* and cones are as for "flanking the chin" above.

[On "The bone hollows of the two shoulder blades are on the yang side in the middle of the shoulder blades"] Near the Jianyu point; the classic gives no name.

[On "The bone hollow of the arm ... between the hollows of the two bones"] One body-*cun* above Zhigou; this is called Tongjian.
Song editors: According to the *Jiayi jing*, one *cun* above Zhigou is named Sanyangluo. Might Tongjian be another name for it?

[On "The upper hollow of the femur is on the yang side of the thigh, four *cun* above the knee"] Above Yinshi and below the Futu point, at Chengjian.

[On "The bone hollow of the shin is at the upper end of the auxiliary bone"] This means the Dubi point, below the kneecap and above the shin bone, in the large sinew flanking the joint, where the vessel qi of the foot *yangming* issues; one may needle six body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "The bone hollow at the edge of the thigh is below the pulse in the pubic hair"] The classic lacks its name.

[On "The bone hollow of the sacrum is behind the hip bone, four *cun* apart"] This means the eight *liao* points of the sacrum.

[On "Flat bones have seeping patterns ... the marrow has no hollow"] Flat bones means the flat, twisted bones between the buttocks. On these bones there are seeping patterns converging, with no separate marrow orifice. 易 means "also". If the bone has an orifice, the marrow has an orifice; if the bone has none, the marrow also has none.

## Notes

A list of the positions of the bone (marrow) hollows of the whole body.
- In Wang Bing's note 頣 is presumably an error for 頤; in the Song editors' note 主氏 for 王氏 and 経関 for 経闕.
- Wang Bing reads 易 as 亦 "also"; the translation follows him.
- 或骨空在口下当両肩 is identified by Wang Bing as Daying, but the sense is obscure.
