# 素問 熱論篇第三十一 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In those not dually affected by cold, on the seventh day the illness of the greater yang declines and the headache eases a little; on the eighth day the illness of the *yangming* declines and the body's heat eases a little; on the ninth day the illness of the lesser yang declines and the deafness gives way to slight hearing; on the tenth day the illness of the greater yin declines, the abdomen shrinks back to normal and one thinks of food and drink; on the eleventh day the illness of the lesser yin declines, the thirst stops, there is no fullness, the dryness of the tongue ends and one sneezes; on the twelfth day the illness of the *jueyin* declines, the scrotum relaxes, the lower abdomen eases down slightly, the great qi all departs, and the illness ends day by day."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In those not dually affected by cold ... the headache eases a little"] The evil qi gradually withdraws and the channel qi gradually harmonizes; therefore it eases a little.

[On "on the twelfth day ... the illness ends day by day"] "Great qi" means the qi of the great evil. This is why those who recover all do so after ten days or more of illness.

## Notes

In cold damage without dual affection, the illness of each channel declines in turn from the seventh day and is cured by the twelfth. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 004, ends here.
