# 素問 熱論篇第三十一 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In treating it, open the vessels of each zang, and the illness declines day by day and ends. Before three days are complete, sweating will end it; after three days are complete, draining will end it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Before three days are complete ... draining will end it"] This states the general principle of exterior and interior. The *Zhengli shanghan lun* says, "When the pulse is large, floating and rapid, the illness is in the exterior and one should promote sweating; when the pulse is fine, sunken and rapid, the illness is in the interior and one should purge." Accordingly, even if many days have passed, if there are exterior signs and the pulse is large, floating and rapid, one should still promote sweating; even if few days have passed, if there are interior signs and the pulse is sunken, fine and rapid, one should still purge. One should sweat or purge precisely according to the pulse and signs.

## Notes

As a principle of treating cold damage, sweating is used before three days and draining (purging) after three days. Wang Bing notes that one should follow the pulse and signs rather than the number of days.
- The *Zhengli shanghan lun* passage quoted by Wang Bing has not been checked.
