# 素問 熱論篇第三十一 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I wish to hear about its manifestations."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I wish to hear about its manifestations"] It means the signs of those without dual affection.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about the course of cold damage without dual affection.
