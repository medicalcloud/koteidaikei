# 素問 熱論篇第三十一 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When febrile illness has eased a little, eating meat brings relapse, and eating much leaves residue. These are its prohibitions."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When febrile illness has eased a little ... These are its prohibitions"] This is what is called warning against relapse from food. Although the fever has eased a little, it has not been fully removed; the qi of the spleen and stomach is depleted and cannot yet digest. Meat is hard and the food stays; therefore heat arises again. 復 means relapse into the former illness.

## Notes

In convalescence from febrile illness, eating meat and overeating are prohibited.
