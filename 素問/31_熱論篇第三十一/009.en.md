# 素問 熱論篇第三十一 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Whatever remains is because one was forced to eat while the fever was severe; therefore something remains. In such cases the illness has already declined, but heat is stored somewhere; it presses together with the grain qi, and the two heats combine; therefore something remains."

## Notes

If one is made to eat while the fever is still severe, the residual heat and the heat of the grain qi combine and fever remains. There is no note by Wang Bing.
