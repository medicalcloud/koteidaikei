# 素問 熱論篇第三十一 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Look at its depletion and repletion and regulate its adversity and compliance, and it can surely be ended."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Look at its depletion and repletion ... it can surely be ended"] Examine its depletion and repletion and supplement or drain accordingly, and it will surely end.

## Notes

Residual fever is treated by supplementing or draining according to depletion and repletion.
