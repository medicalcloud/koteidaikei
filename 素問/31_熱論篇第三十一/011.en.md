# 素問 熱論篇第三十一 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Look at its deficiency and excess and regulate its adversity and compliance, and it can surely be ended."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Look at its deficiency and excess ... it can surely be ended"] Examine its deficiency and excess and supplement or drain accordingly, and it will surely end.

## Notes

Residual fever is treated by supplementing or draining according to deficiency and excess.
