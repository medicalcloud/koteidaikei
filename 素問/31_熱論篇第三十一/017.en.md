# 素問 熱論篇第三十一 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The *yangming* is the chief of the twelve channel vessels, and its blood and qi are abundant; therefore after one no longer recognizes people, it takes three days for its qi to be exhausted, and then one dies. In general, when cold damage turns into warmth, if it is before the day of the summer solstice it is warm illness; if after the day of the summer solstice, it is summer-heat illness. Summer heat should all come out with the sweat; do not stop it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The *yangming* is the chief ... and then one dies"] Above, it receives the sea of qi; therefore only when the qi is exhausted after three days does one die.

[On "In general, when cold damage turns into warmth ... do not stop it"] This takes its meaning from the amount and intensity of the heat. When yang heat is not yet abundant and is held in check by cold, the illness is called warm; when yang heat is greatly abundant and cold cannot hold it in check, the illness is called summer heat. Summer-heat illness should be cured by sweating; one should not instead stop it and make it worse.
Song editors: From "In general, when cold damage" on is in the *Qibing lun* in Quan Yuanqi's edition; Mr Wang moved it here. Yang Shangshan says, "When the cold damage in winter is light, it breaks out before the summer solstice as warm illness; when the cold damage in winter is severe, it breaks out after the summer solstice as summer-heat illness."

## Notes

Because the *yangming* is abundant in blood and qi, life lasts three days after loss of consciousness; latent cold breaks out as warm illness or summer-heat illness before or after the summer solstice. This ends the chapter.
- According to the Song editors, the passage from 凡病傷寒 on was in the *Qibing lun* in Quan Yuanqi's edition and was moved here by Wang Bing.
- 暑当与汗皆出，勿止: Wang Bing takes it to mean that summer-heat illness should be cured by sweating and the sweat should not be stopped.
