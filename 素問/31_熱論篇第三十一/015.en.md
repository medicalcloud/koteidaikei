# 素問 熱論篇第三十一 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In those dually affected by cold, on the first day of illness the greater yang and lesser yin are both ill: the head aches, the mouth is dry, and there is vexation and fullness. On the second day the *yangming* and greater yin are both ill: the abdomen is full, the body hot, one does not want to eat, and there is delirious speech. On the third day the lesser yang and *jueyin* are both ill: one is deaf, the scrotum retracts and there is reversal; water and broth do not go down, one does not recognize people, and on the sixth day one dies."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In those dually affected by cold, on the first day ... vexation and fullness"] Song editors: The *Shanghan lun* says "vexation, fullness and thirst".

[On "On the second day ... delirious speech"] "Delirious speech" means talk that is wild, mistaken and disordered.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says, "It is excessive talk."

[On "On the third day ... on the sixth day one dies"] The greater yang and lesser yin are exterior and interior; the *yangming* and greater yin are exterior and interior; the lesser yang and *jueyin* are exterior and interior. Therefore in dual affection the cold qi strikes both at once.

## Notes

In cold damage with dual affection, the paired exterior and interior channels fall ill at once, all six channels are reached in three days, and death comes on the sixth day.
