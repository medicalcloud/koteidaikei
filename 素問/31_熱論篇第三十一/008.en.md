# 素問 熱論篇第三十一 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why is it that after febrile illness has healed, something sometimes remains?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why is it that after febrile illness has healed, something sometimes remains?"] The evil qi has declined and left but not completely, as if something were left behind in the person.

## Notes

Huangdi asks the cause of residual fever after febrile illness.
