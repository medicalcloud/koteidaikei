# 素問 蔵気法時論篇第二十二 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In liver illness, there is pain below both flanks drawing to the lower abdomen, making one prone to anger. When depleted, the eyes are blurred and see nothing, the ears hear nothing, and one is prone to fear, as if someone were about to seize one. Take its channels, the *jueyin* and lesser yang. When the qi counterflows, there are headache, deafness with poor hearing and swollen cheeks; take [the vessels] with blood.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In liver illness, there is pain below both flanks drawing to the lower abdomen, making one prone to anger"] The liver *jueyin* vessel rises from the foot, encircles the genitals, reaches the lower abdomen, rises again through the liver and diaphragm and spreads over the flanks and ribs; therefore there is pain below both flanks drawing to the lower abdomen. When its qi is replete, one is prone to anger. The *Lingshu jing* says, "When the liver qi is replete, there is anger."

[On "When depleted, the eyes are blurred ... as if someone were about to seize one"] The liver *jueyin* vessel runs from the flanks and ribs along the throat, enters the nasopharynx and connects with the eye connector. A branch of the gallbladder lesser yang vessel enters the ear from behind it, emerges in front of the ear and reaches behind the outer canthus. Hence such illness. "Fear" means dread, with the *hun* soul unsettled.

[On "Take its channels, the *jueyin* and lesser yang"] "Channels" means the channel vessels; the illness is not in the network vessels, so take the channels. Take the *jueyin* to treat the liver qi and the lesser yang to regulate the counterflow of qi. Hence the text below says:

[On "When the qi counterflows, there are headache, deafness with poor hearing and swollen cheeks"] The liver *jueyin* vessel rises from the eye connector, emerges at the forehead and meets the *du* vessel at the vertex; hence headache. A branch of the gallbladder lesser yang vessel emerges from the ear and runs in front of it, and another branch passes over Jiache; a branch of the *jueyin* vessel descends from the eye connector inside the cheek. Hence deafness and swollen cheeks. This is why the text above also takes the lesser yang.

[On "take [the vessels] with blood"] When a vessel is full of blood and unlike the usual, this is a sign of counterflowing qi; according to left or right, where it is found, needle it.

## Notes

Symptoms of repletion and depletion in liver illness, and the needling (take the *jueyin* and lesser yang channels and needle vessels full of blood).
- 䀮䀮 is understood as a state of blurred vision.
