# 素問 蔵気法時論篇第二十二 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In heart illness, there is pain in the chest, propping fullness of the flanks, pain below the flanks, pain in the breast, back and between the shoulder blades, and pain on the inside of both arms. When depleted, the chest and abdomen are enlarged, and below the flanks and the lumbus pull on each other and ache. Take its channels, the lesser yin and greater yang, and [the vessels] under the tongue with blood. When the illness changes, needle [the vessels] at the cleft with blood.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In heart illness, there is pain in the chest ... pain on the inside of both arms"] A branch of the lesser yin vessel runs along the chest and emerges at the flanks. The hand heart-master *jueyin* vessel arises in the chest; a branch also runs along the chest, emerges at the flanks three *cun* below the armpit, rises to below the armpit, descends along the inside of the upper arm between the greater yin and lesser yin, enters the elbow, and descends along the forearm between the two tendons. The straight course of the heart lesser yin vessel goes from the heart connector back up to the lung, emerges below the armpit, descends along the posterior edge of the inner upper arm behind the greater yin and heart master, descends to the inside of the elbow, follows the posterior edge of the inner forearm and reaches the end of the pisiform bone behind the palm. And the small intestine greater yang vessel rises from the forearm and upper arm, winds around the shoulder blade and crosses above the shoulder. Hence such illness.

[On "When depleted, the chest and abdomen are enlarged, and below the flanks and the lumbus pull on each other and ache"] The hand heart-master *jueyin* vessel emerges from the chest, belongs to the pericardium, descends through the diaphragm and connects in turn with the triple burner; a branch runs along the chest and emerges at the flanks. The heart lesser yin vessel descends from the heart connector through the diaphragm and connects with the small intestine. Hence such illness.

[On "Take its channels, the lesser yin and greater yang, and [the vessels] under the tongue with blood"] The lesser yin vessel rises from the heart connector alongside the throat; therefore take the blood below the root of the tongue and in the channel vessels.

[On "When the illness changes, needle [the vessels] at the cleft with blood"] If it changes, for instance with vomiting, needle the cleft of the lesser yin where the blood is full. The cleft of the hand lesser yin is in the vessel behind the palm, half a *cun* from the wrist, behind the little finger.

## Notes

Symptoms of repletion and depletion in heart illness, and the needling.
- "When the illness changes": Wang Bing takes it as a change such as vomiting.
- "Cleft": Wang Bing takes it as the cleft point of the hand lesser yin (the position of the later Yinxi point).
