# 素問 蔵気法時論篇第二十二 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When illness is in the lung, it heals in winter; if it does not heal in winter, it worsens in summer; if one does not die in summer, it holds in late summer and one rises in autumn. Avoid cold drink and food and cold clothing. Lung illness heals on *ren* and *gui*; if it does not heal on *ren* and *gui*, it intensifies on *bing* and *ding*; if one does not die on *bing* and *ding*, it holds on *wu* and *ji*, and one rises on *geng* and *xin*. Lung illness is clear in the late afternoon, worse at midday and calm at midnight. The lung desires contraction; quickly eat sour things to contract it. Use sourness to supplement it and acridity to drain it.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "if one does not die in summer, it holds in late summer and one rises in autumn"] As in the case of the liver.

[On "Avoid cold drink and food and cold clothing"] The lung dislikes cold qi; therefore clothing and food are restricted. The *Lingshu jing* says, "When the body is cold and one drinks cold things, the lung is harmed." If even drink harms the lung, food does so all the more. The lung not only dislikes cold but also fears heat.

[On "Lung illness heals on *ren* and *gui*"] They correspond to winter-water.

[On "if it does not heal on *ren* and *gui*, it intensifies on *bing* and *ding*"] They correspond to summer-fire.

[On "if one does not die on *bing* and *ding*, it holds on *wu* and *ji*"] They are late-summer earth.

[On "and one rises on *geng* and *xin*"] They correspond to autumn-metal.

[On "Lung illness is clear in the late afternoon, worse at midday and calm at midnight"] When metal dominates it is clear, when water dominates it is calm, when fire dominates it is worse.

[On "The lung desires contraction; quickly eat sour things to contract it"] Because sourness is by nature astringent.

[On "Use sourness to supplement it and acridity to drain it"] Sourness is astringent, so it supplements; acridity disperses, so it drains.

## Notes

The course of lung illness and the flavours that supplement and drain it.
