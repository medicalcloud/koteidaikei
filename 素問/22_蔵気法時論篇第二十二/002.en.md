# 素問 蔵気法時論篇第二十二 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "The five phases are metal, wood, water, fire and earth. They are noble and base in turn; by them one knows death and life, decides success and failure, and determines the qi of the five zang, the times when [illness] eases or worsens, and the dates of death and life."

## Notes

The rise and fall of the five phases determine the severity of illness and the times of death and life. There is no note by Wang Bing.
