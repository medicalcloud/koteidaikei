# 素問 蔵気法時論篇第二十二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "In treating by matching the human form [to heaven and earth] and modelling it on the four seasons and the five phases, how does one comply and how does one go against? I wish to hear about the meaning of gain and loss."

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: compliance and opposition, gain and loss, in treating the body according to the four seasons and five phases. There is no note by Wang Bing.
