# 素問 蔵気法時論篇第二十二 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When illness is in the kidney, it heals in spring; if it does not heal in spring, it worsens in late summer; if one does not die in late summer, it holds in autumn and one rises in winter. Avoid scorched and roasted hot food and warmed, heated clothing. Kidney illness heals on *jia* and *yi*; if it does not heal on *jia* and *yi*, it worsens on *wu* and *ji*; if one does not die on *wu* and *ji*, it holds on *geng* and *xin*, and one rises on *ren* and *gui*. Kidney illness is clear at midnight, worse in the four seasonal [hours] and calm in the late afternoon. The kidney desires firmness; quickly eat bitter things to firm it. Use bitterness to supplement it and saltiness to drain it.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "if one does not die in late summer, it holds in autumn and one rises in winter"] As in the case of the liver.

[On "Avoid scorched and roasted hot food and warmed, heated clothing"] The kidney by nature dislikes dryness; therefore these are prohibited.
Song editors: Another edition writes 焠 for 焠.

[On "Kidney illness heals on *jia* and *yi*"] They correspond to spring-wood.

[On "if it does not heal on *jia* and *yi*, it worsens on *wu* and *ji*"] They are late-summer earth.

[On "if one does not die on *wu* and *ji*, it holds on *geng* and *xin*"] They correspond to autumn-metal.

[On "and one rises on *ren* and *gui*"] They correspond to winter-water.

[On "Kidney illness is clear at midnight, worse in the four seasonal [hours] and calm in the late afternoon"] When water dominates it is clear, when earth dominates it is worse, when metal dominates it is calm.

[On "The kidney desires firmness; quickly eat bitter things to firm it"] Because bitterness is by nature firming and drying.

[On "Use bitterness to supplement it and saltiness to drain it"] Bitterness supplements by its firming; saltiness drains by its softening. Softness is what damp earth controls; therefore it is used to drain.

## Notes

The course of kidney illness and the flavours that supplement and drain it.
- 焠㶼 is commonly understood as food scorched or roasted over fire.
- In the transmitted text of the Song editors' note "another edition writes 焠 for 焠" the two characters are the same, so it is unclear which variant is meant.
- "The four seasonal [hours]" is commonly understood as the hours *chen*, *xu*, *chou* and *wei*, when earth dominates; Wang Bing says only "when earth dominates".
- The base text's 戊巳 means 戊己 (see the note to unit 006).
