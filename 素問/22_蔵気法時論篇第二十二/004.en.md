# 素問 蔵気法時論篇第二十二 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The liver governs spring; the foot *jueyin* and lesser yang govern its treatment; its days are *jia* and *yi*. The liver suffers from tension; quickly eat sweet things to relax it. The heart governs summer; the hand lesser yin and greater yang govern its treatment; its days are *bing* and *ding*. The heart suffers from slackness; quickly eat sour things to contract it. The spleen governs late summer; the foot greater yin and *yangming* govern its treatment; its days are *wu* and *ji*. The spleen suffers from damp; quickly eat bitter things to dry it. The lung governs autumn; the hand greater yin and *yangming* govern its treatment; its days are *geng* and *xin*. The lung suffers from upward counterflow of qi; quickly eat bitter things to discharge it. The kidney governs winter; the foot lesser yin and greater yang govern its treatment; its days are *ren* and *gui*. The kidney suffers from dryness; quickly eat acrid things to moisten it; [acrid things] open the interstices, bring the fluids and make the qi communicate.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The liver governs spring"] It corresponds to wood.

[On "the foot *jueyin* and lesser yang govern its treatment"] The *jueyin* is the liver vessel and the lesser yang the gallbladder vessel; the liver and gallbladder are paired, so their treatment is the same.

[On "its days are *jia* and *yi*"] *Jia* and *yi* are wood, the stems of the east.

[On "The liver suffers from tension; quickly eat sweet things to relax it"] Sweetness is by nature mild and relaxing.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi says, "That the liver suffers from tension means its qi is in surplus."

[On "The heart governs summer"] It corresponds to fire.

[On "the hand lesser yin and greater yang govern its treatment"] The lesser yin is the heart vessel and the greater yang the small intestine vessel; the heart and small intestine are paired, so their treatment is the same.

[On "its days are *bing* and *ding*"] *Bing* and *ding* are fire, the stems of the south.

[On "The heart suffers from slackness; quickly eat sour things to contract it"] Sourness is by nature astringent.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition says, "That the heart suffers from slackness means the heart qi is depleted."

[On "The spleen governs late summer"] Late summer means the sixth month. Summer is the mother of earth; earth grows in the middle [of the year] and governs by growing; hence it is called "long summer".
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi says, "The spleen dominates in the four [last months of the] seasons." The sixth month is where fire dominates. The spleen governs the centre, and the sixth month is the middle of the twelve months, half of the year; therefore the spleen governs the sixth month.

[On "the foot greater yin and *yangming* govern its treatment"] The greater yin is the spleen vessel and the *yangming* the stomach vessel; the spleen and stomach are paired, so their treatment is the same.

[On "its days are *wu* and *ji*"] *Wu* and *ji* are earth, the stems of the centre.

[On "The spleen suffers from damp; quickly eat bitter things to dry it"] Bitterness is by nature drying.

[On "The lung governs autumn"] It corresponds to metal.

[On "the hand greater yin and *yangming* govern its treatment"] The greater yin is the lung vessel and the *yangming* the large intestine vessel; the lung and large intestine are paired, so their treatment is the same.

[On "its days are *geng* and *xin*"] *Geng* and *xin* are metal, the stems of the west.

[On "The lung suffers from upward counterflow of qi; quickly eat bitter things to discharge it"] Bitterness is by nature dispersing and discharging; therefore the lung uses it.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi says, "Upward counterflow of lung qi means its qi is in surplus."

[On "The kidney governs winter"] It corresponds to water.

[On "the foot lesser yin and greater yang govern its treatment"] The lesser yin is the kidney vessel and the greater yang the bladder vessel; the kidney and bladder are paired, so their treatment is the same.

[On "its days are *ren* and *gui*"] *Ren* and *gui* are water, the stems of the north.

[On "The kidney suffers from dryness ... make the qi communicate"] Acridity is by nature moistening. When the interstices open and the fluids reach, the lung qi flows downward; the kidney and lung communicate; hence "make the qi communicate".

## Notes

The season, channels and days (heavenly stems) governed by each of the five zang, what each zang suffers from, and the flavour that treats it. Qibo's speech continues to the end of the chapter (unit 016).
