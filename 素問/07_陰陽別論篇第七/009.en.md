# 素問 陰陽別論篇第七 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the three yin all beat, death comes at midnight on the twentieth day; when the two yin all beat, death comes at dusk on the thirteenth day; when the one yin all beat, death in ten days; when the three yang all beat and also drum, death in three days; when the three yin and three yang all beat, with fullness of the heart and abdomen that breaks out fully and inability in private matters, death in five days; when the two yang all beat and the illness is warm, the patient dies and cannot be treated, dying within ten days."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the three yin all beat, death comes at midnight on the twentieth day"] This is the remainder of the completing numbers of the spleen and lung. "Beat" means hidden drumming that differs from the usual signs. Because the yin qi reaches its extreme abundance, death comes at midnight.

[On "when the two yin all beat, death comes at dusk on the thirteenth day"] These are the completing numbers of the heart and kidney. Because the yin qi has not yet reached its extreme, death comes at dusk.

[On "when the one yin all beat, death in ten days"] These are the generating and completing numbers of the liver and heart.

[On "when the three yang all beat and also drum, death in three days"] Because the yang qi is swift and urgent.

[On "when the three yin and three yang all beat ... death in five days"] It includes the yin qi. "Private matters" here means evacuation.

[On "when the two yang all beat and the illness is warm ... dying within ten days"] This is the dominant number of the intestines and stomach.
Song editors: "The one yang beating" is missing here.

## Notes

The times of death when the three yin, two yin, one yin, three yang, three yin and three yang together, or two yang all beat; this ends the chapter. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 002, ends here.
- Wang Bing explains the numbers of days by the generating and completing numbers of the zang.
- Here Wang Bing glosses "private matters" (隠曲) as evacuation, differing from his reading in unit 004.
- The Song editors note that "the one yang beating" is missing.
