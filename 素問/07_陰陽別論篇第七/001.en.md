# 素問 陰陽別論篇第七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "People have four regular [pulses] and twelve conformities. What does this mean?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] 経 means the channel vessels; 従 means conformity.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: what are the four regular pulses (of the four seasons) and the twelve conformities (pulses corresponding to the twelve months)?
