# 素問 金匱真言論篇第四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "Heaven has eight winds, and the meridians have five winds. What does this mean?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] "Meridians" means the meridian vessels, which make the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi, blood and qi flow and communicate.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on the relation between the eight winds of heaven (from the eight directions) and the five winds in the meridians (of the five zang).
- "Meridians": Wang Bing takes them as the meridian vessels.
