# 素問 金匱真言論篇第四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "Heaven has eight winds, and the channels have five winds. What does this mean?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] "Channels" means the channel vessels, which make the nutritive and defensive qi, blood and qi flow and communicate.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on the relation between the eight winds of heaven (from the eight directions) and the five winds in the channels (of the five zang).
- "Channels": Wang Bing takes them as the channel vessels.
