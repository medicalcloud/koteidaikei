# 素問 金匱真言論篇第四 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "They do. The east, green in colour, enters and communicates with the liver; it opens its orifice in the eyes and stores its essence in the liver. Its illness issues as fright and alarm. Its flavour is sour; its kind is grass and trees; its domestic animal is the chicken; its grain is wheat. Corresponding to the four seasons, it is above the Year Star (Jupiter); therefore the qi of spring is in the head. Its note is *jue*; its number is eight; therefore one knows that the illness is in the sinews. Its odour is rank. The south, red in colour, enters and communicates with the heart; it opens its orifice in the ears and stores its essence in the heart; therefore the illness is in the five zang. Its flavour is bitter; its kind is fire; its domestic animal is the sheep; its grain is glutinous millet. Corresponding to the four seasons, it is above the Sparkling Deluder (Mars); therefore one knows that the illness is in the vessels. Its note is *zhi*; its number is seven; its odour is scorched. The centre, yellow in colour, enters and communicates with the spleen; it opens its orifice in the mouth and stores its essence in the spleen; therefore the illness is at the root of the tongue. Its flavour is sweet; its kind is earth; its domestic animal is the ox; its grain is panicled millet. Corresponding to the four seasons, it is above the Quelling Star (Saturn); therefore one knows that the illness is in the flesh. Its note is *gong*; its number is five; its odour is fragrant. The west, white in colour, enters and communicates with the lung; it opens its orifice in the nose and stores its essence in the lung; therefore the illness is in the back. Its flavour is acrid; its kind is metal; its domestic animal is the horse; its grain is rice. Corresponding to the four seasons, it is above the Great White (Venus); therefore one knows that the illness is in the skin and body hair. Its note is *shang*; its number is nine; its odour is fishy. The north, black in colour, enters and communicates with the kidney; it opens its orifice in the two yin and stores its essence in the kidney; therefore the illness is in the ravines. Its flavour is salty; its kind is water; its domestic animal is the pig; its grain is beans. Corresponding to the four seasons, it is above the Chronograph Star (Mercury); therefore one knows that the illness is in the bones. Its note is *yu*; its number is six; its odour is putrid. Therefore those who are good at the pulse carefully examine the five zang and six fu, going against and following, and the order of yin and yang, exterior and interior, female and male; they store this in their hearts and minds and join their hearts to the essence. If not the right person, do not teach; if not the true [one], do not transmit. This is called attaining the Way."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The east ... stores its essence in the liver"] "Essence" means essential qi. It is the qi of the essence of wood, whose spirit is the *hun*. It is the direction where yang rises and uses the eyes; therefore it opens its orifice in the eyes.

[On "Its illness issues as fright and alarm"] This resembles the bending, stretching and swaying of wood.
Song editors: For the east it says "its illness issues as fright and alarm", but this is missing for each of the other directions. Since the *Wuchang zhengda lun* (chapter 70) says of the year of Bent Harmony, "what it issues is fright and alarm", we suspect this text is an interpolation.

[On "its kind is grass and trees"] Its nature is soft and brittle, bending and straight.

[On "its domestic animal is the chicken"] The chicken is taken as its animal by reference to *Xun*; the *Yi* says, "*Xun* is the chicken."

[On "its grain is wheat"] Wheat is the chief of the five grains; therefore the east uses it. The *Bencao* says: "Wheat is the chief of the five grains."
Song editors: The *Wuchang zhengda lun* (chapter 70) says: "Its animal is the dog, its grain is hemp."

[On "Corresponding to the four seasons, it is above the Year Star"] The essential qi of wood rises to become the Year Star, which circles heaven once in twelve years.

[On "therefore the qi of spring is in the head"] The myriad things issue and flourish above; therefore the qi of spring is in the head.
Song editors: For the east it says "the qi of spring is in the head" and not "therefore the illness is in the head"; for the other directions it says "therefore the illness is in such-and-such" and not "such qi is in such place". These are complementary expressions.

[On "Its note is *jue*"] *Jue* is the sound of wood. In the first month of spring the pitch-pipe is *taicu*, generated from *linzhong* by adding a third; the pipe is eight *cun* long. In the second month of spring the pitch-pipe is *jiazhong*, generated from *yize* by adding a third; the pipe is seven *cun* five *fen* long [Song editors: Zheng Kangcheng says "seven *cun* and 1,075/2,187 of a *cun*"]. In the third month of spring the pitch-pipe is *guxian*, generated from *nanlü* by adding a third; the pipe is seven and 1/20 *cun* long [Song editors: Zheng Kangcheng says "1/9 of a *cun*"]. All these three pipes correspond to the qi of wood.

[On "its number is eight"] The generating number of wood is three and its completing number eight. The "Hongfan" of the *Shangshu* says: "The third is wood."

[On "therefore one knows that the illness is in the sinews"] Because the firmness and suppleness of wood resemble the qi of the sinews.

[On "Its odour is rank"] Generally, qi altered by wood becomes rank.
Song editors: For 臊 the "Yueling" writes 羶.

[On "The south ... stores its essence in the heart"] It is the qi of the essence of fire, whose spirit is the spirit. The tongue is the official of the heart, so one should say "tongue"; but the tongue functions without being an orifice, so it says "ears". The *Miuci lun* (chapter 63) says, "The network vessel of the hand lesser yin meets in the ear"; the meaning is taken from this.

[On "therefore the illness is in the five zang"] Because the qi of summer is in the zang.

[On "its kind is fire"] Its nature is to blaze upward and burn.

[On "its domestic animal is the sheep"] The sheep is taken as its animal by reference to *wei* (the sheep branch); since it flourishes together with earth, they are spoken of together.
Song editors: The *Wuchang zhengda lun* (chapter 70) says: "Its animal is the horse."

[On "its grain is glutinous millet"] Glutinous millet is red in colour.

[On "it is above the Sparkling Deluder"] The essential qi of fire rises to become the Sparkling Deluder, which circles heaven once in 740 days.

[On "therefore one knows that the illness is in the vessels"] The restless movement of fire resembles the qi of the vessels.

[On "Its note is *zhi*"] *Zhi* is the sound of fire. In the first month of summer the pitch-pipe is *zhonglü*, generated from *wuyi* by adding a third; the pipe is six *cun* seven *fen* long [Song editors: Zheng Kangcheng says "six *cun* and 12,974/19,683 of a *cun*"]. In the second month of summer the pitch-pipe is *ruibin*, generated from *yingzhong* by adding a third; the pipe is six *cun* three *fen* long [Song editors: Zheng Kangcheng says "six *cun* and 26/81 of a *cun*"]. In the third month of summer the pitch-pipe is *linzhong*, generated from *huangzhong* by subtracting a third; the pipe is six *cun* long. All these three pipes correspond to the qi of fire.

[On "its number is seven"] The generating number of fire is two and its completing number seven. The "Hongfan" of the *Shangshu* says: "The second is fire."

[On "its odour is scorched"] Generally, qi altered by fire becomes scorched.

[On "The centre ... stores its essence in the spleen"] It is the qi of the essence of earth, whose spirit is intention. The spleen transforms grain and the mouth governs receiving food; therefore it opens its orifice in the mouth.

[On "therefore the illness is at the root of the tongue"] The spleen vessel connects upward to the root of the tongue; therefore the qi of illness resides there.

[On "its kind is earth"] Its nature is calm and still, transforming and creating.

[On "its domestic animal is the ox"] Earth flourishes in the four seasons; therefore the animal taken is the ox of *chou*, and also because the ox is yellow.

[On "its grain is panicled millet"] It is yellow in colour and sweet in flavour.

[On "it is above the Quelling Star"] The essential qi of earth rises to become the Quelling Star, which circles heaven once in twenty-eight years.

[On "therefore one knows that the illness is in the flesh"] Because the softness and thickness of earth resemble the qi of the flesh.

[On "Its note is *gong*"] *Gong* is the sound of earth. The treatise on the pitch-pipes takes *huangzhong* as the low *gong* and *linzhong* as the high *gong*, probably because *linzhong* corresponds to the pipe of the sixth month. Of the five notes, *gong* is the chief, and the pitch-pipes begin with *huangzhong*; [therefore] *huangzhong* is the low *gong* and *linzhong* the high *gong*.

[On "its number is five"] The number of earth is five. The "Hongfan" of the *Shangshu* says: "The fifth is earth."

[On "its odour is fragrant"] Generally, qi altered by earth becomes fragrant.

[On "The west ... stores its essence in the lung"] It is the qi of the essence of metal, whose spirit is the *po*. The lung stores qi and the nose lets breath through; therefore it opens its orifice in the nose.

[On "therefore the illness is in the back"] Because the lung is in the chest, and the back is the storehouse of the chest.

[On "its kind is metal"] Its nature is resonant, firm and strong.

[On "its domestic animal is the horse"] The horse is taken as its animal by reference to *Qian*; the *Yi* says, "*Qian* is the horse."
Song editors: The *Wuchang zhengda lun* (chapter 70) says: "Its animal is the chicken."

[On "its grain is rice"] Rice is hard and white.

[On "it is above the Great White"] The essential qi of metal rises to become the Great White, which circles heaven once in 365 days.

[On "therefore one knows that the illness is in the skin and body hair"] The firmness and density of metal resemble the skin and body hair.

[On "Its note is *shang*"] *Shang* is the sound of metal. In the first month of autumn the pitch-pipe is *yize*, generated from *dalü* by subtracting a third; the pipe is five *cun* seven *fen* long. In the second month of autumn the pitch-pipe is *nanlü*, generated from *taicu* by subtracting a third; the pipe is five *cun* three *fen* long. In the third month of autumn the pitch-pipe is *wuyi*, generated from *jiazhong* by subtracting a third; the pipe is five *cun* long. All these three pipes correspond to the qi of metal.

[On "its number is nine"] The generating number of metal is four and its completing number nine. The "Hongfan" of the *Shangshu* says: "The fourth is metal."

[On "its odour is fishy"] Generally, qi altered by metal becomes a fishy, rank qi.

[On "The north ... stores its essence in the kidney"] It is the qi of the essence of water, whose spirit is the will. The kidney stores essence and the yin [orifices] discharge; therefore it opens its orifice in the two yin.

[On "therefore the illness is in the ravines"] "Ravines" are the small meeting places of the flesh. The *Qixue lun* (chapter 58) says: "The large meeting places of the flesh are valleys; the small meeting places of the flesh are ravines."

[On "its kind is water"] Its nature is to moisten and descend, to seep and irrigate.

[On "its domestic animal is the pig"] 彘 is the pig.

[On "its grain is beans"] Beans are black.

[On "it is above the Chronograph Star"] The essential qi of water rises to become the Chronograph Star, which circles heaven once in 365 days.

[On "therefore one knows that the illness is in the bones"] The kidney governs the dark and hidden, and the body of the bones is stored within; being of the same kind, the illness resides in the bones.

[On "Its note is *yu*"] *Yu* is the sound of water. In the first month of winter the pitch-pipe is *yingzhong*, generated from *guxian* by subtracting a third; the pipe is four *cun* seven and a half *fen* long. In the second month of winter the pitch-pipe is *huangzhong*, generated from *zhonglü* by adding a third; the pipe is nine *cun* long. In the third month of winter the pitch-pipe is *dalü*, generated from *ruibin* by adding a third; the pipe is eight *cun* four *fen* long. All these three pipes correspond to the qi of water.

[On "its number is six"] The generating number of water is one and its completing number six. The "Hongfan" of the *Shangshu* says: "The first is water."

[On "its odour is putrid"] Generally, qi altered by water becomes a putrid, rotting qi.

[On "Therefore those who are good at the pulse ... join their hearts to the essence"] When the heart joins with the subtle essence, one deeply knows change and adaptation.

[On "If not the right person, do not teach ... This is called attaining the Way"] Give to each according to his ability; this is called attaining the way of teaching between master and disciple. The *Lingshu jing* says: "Those with clear eyes may be made to examine colours; those with keen ears may be made to listen to sounds; those quick and skilled in speech may be made to discourse; those who are unhurried and calm, skilful of hand and careful of mind, may be made to apply needles and moxa, regulate blood and qi, adjust counterflow and compliance, examine yin and yang and also handle the prescriptions. Those with relaxed joints, supple sinews and a harmonious heart may be made to practise *daoyin* and circulate qi. Those whose words are venomous and who belittle others may be made to spit on abscesses and incant over illness. Those with bitter nails and harmful hands, who tend to injure in what they do, may be made to press on accumulations and suppress impediments." By this, only when each obtains his proper ability can the method be practised and his name become known. Therefore it says: "If not the right person, do not teach; if not the true [one], do not transmit."

## Notes

The correspondences of the five zang and the four seasons, set out for each of the five directions (east, south, centre, west, north) in the order: colour, organ, orifice, site of illness, flavour, kind (phase), domestic animal, grain, planet, note, number and odour. It concludes that one good at the pulse should store the order of yin and yang of the zang and fu in the heart and teach only suitable people. The chapter ends here.
- "Its illness issues as fright and alarm" (east): the Song editors suspect it is an interpolation, since the other directions lack it.
- The Song editors regard "the qi of spring is in the head" (east) and "therefore the illness is in ..." (other directions) as complementary expressions.
- "Opens its orifice in the ears" (south): Wang Bing explains that the tongue is the heart's official but not an orifice, so the ears are named.
- The Song editors note that the *Wuchang zhengda lun* (chapter 70) assigns different animals and grains (east: dog and hemp; south: horse; west: chicken).
- Wang Bing assigns to each note the lengths of the pitch-pipes of the twelve tones. In the base text his note writes 元射 for 無射, 沽洗 for 姑洗, 太呂 for 大呂, and 啇 for 商; the translation uses the usual names.
- The *Lingshu jing* passage cited by Wang Bing corresponds to the "Guanneng" chapter.
