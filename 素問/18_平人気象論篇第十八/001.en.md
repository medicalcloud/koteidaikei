# 素問 平人気象論篇第十八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "What is a normal person like?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] A normal person is one whose qi and signs are balanced and regulated.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on the pulse of a normal (healthy) person.
