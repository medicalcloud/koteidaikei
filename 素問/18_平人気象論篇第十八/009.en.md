# 素問 平人気象論篇第十八 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In winter, with stomach and slightly stony, it is called normal; more stony and less stomach, it is called kidney illness; only stony without stomach, it is called death; stony with hooked, it is called summer illness; markedly hooked, it is called present illness. The true qi of the zang goes down to the kidney; the kidney stores the qi of the bones and marrow. The great network vessel of the stomach is called Xuli; it pierces the diaphragm, connects with the lung and emerges below the left breast; its beat can be felt through the clothes; it is the ancestral qi of the vessels. If it is abundant, panting and frequently interrupted, the illness is in the centre; if it is knotted and lies crosswise, there is an accumulation; if it is cut off and does not come, it is called death. If below the breast its beat shakes the clothes, the ancestral qi is leaking.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "more stony and less stomach ... only stony without stomach, it is called death"] It means like a rope being snatched, snapping like flicking a stone.

[On "stony with hooked, it is called summer illness"] Hooked is the summer pulse, fire combined with earth qi. By the order of encroachment, hooked should be called weak. Earth dominates in long summer but does not show its proper form; therefore stony with hooked combines earth.

[On "markedly hooked, it is called present illness"] Water receives the evil of fire and earth; therefore the illness is present.

[On "The true qi of the zang goes down to the kidney ..."] The kidney is in the lower burner; hence "the true qi goes down". The kidney transforms the bones and marrow; therefore it stores the qi of the bones and marrow.

[On "The great network vessel of the stomach ... the ancestral qi of the vessels"] 宗 means honoured, master; it means the honoured master of the twelve channels. "Pierces the diaphragm, connects with the lung and emerges below the left breast" means it comes out from the diaphragm to below the breast and there connects with the lung.

[On "If it is abundant, panting and frequently interrupted, the illness is in the centre"] 絶 means momentarily interrupted.

[On "if it is knotted ... it is called death"] All of these are forms of the beat below the left breast. "Centre" means the abdomen.

[On "If below the breast its beat shakes the clothes, the ancestral qi is leaking"] 泄 means discharge.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition lacks these eleven characters, as does the *Jiayi jing*. Considering the meaning of the text before and after, these eleven characters are superfluous and should be removed.

## Notes

The winter pulse: with stomach qi and slightly stony is normal; if hooked appears, illness comes in summer. Then follows the diagnosis by Xuli, the great network vessel of the stomach (the beat below the left breast, corresponding to the apex beat of the heart).
- Diagnosis by the beat of Xuli is a well-known doctrine of this chapter.
- The Song editors regard the last eleven characters, "If below the breast its beat shakes the clothes, the ancestral qi is leaking", as superfluous.
