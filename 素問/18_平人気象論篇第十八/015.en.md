# 素問 平人気象論篇第十八 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the neck vessels throb, with panting, rapid breathing and coughing, it is water. When the lower eyelids are slightly swollen, like a sleeping silkworm rising, it is water. Yellow-red urine with a liking for lying quietly is yellow *ju*. Being hungry even after eating is stomach jaundice. Swelling of the face is wind; swelling of the feet and shins is water; yellow eyes are jaundice.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the neck vessels throb ... it is water"] When water qi overflows upward, the lung is fumigated by heat and the yang qi counterflows upward; therefore the neck vessels drum abundantly and there is coughing and panting. The neck vessels are the Renying pulses below the ears and beside the Adam's apple.

[On "When the lower eyelids are slightly swollen ... it is water"] The *Ping rebing lun* (chapter 33) says: "Water is yin; below the eyes is also yin; the abdomen is where the utmost yin dwells; therefore when there is water in the abdomen, it must make the area below the eyes swell."

[On "Yellow-red urine with a liking for lying quietly is yellow *ju*"] 疽 means exhaustion. The kidney is exhausted and the womb hot; therefore the urine is yellow-red. The *Zhengli lun* says: "It is called exhaustion jaundice; it is contracted through sexual exhaustion."
Song editors: Wang Bing's taking 疸 as exhaustion is wrong. To say that jaundice is contracted through sexual exhaustion is acceptable, but taking 疸 as exhaustion is wrong.

[On "Being hungry even after eating is stomach jaundice"] This is heat in the stomach; heat consumes grain; therefore one is hungry even after eating.

[On "Swelling of the face is wind"] When in addition the face is swollen, it is the diagnosis of stomach wind. Why? The stomach's *yangming* vessel starts at the nose, crosses at the root of the nose and descends along the outside of the nose.

[On "swelling of the feet and shins is water"] This means there is water in the lower burner. The kidney's lesser yin vessel emerges at the sole, rises along the shin, passes the inner thigh, and from the kidney rises through the liver and diaphragm; therefore when there is water in the lower burner, the feet and shins swell.

[On "yellow eyes are jaundice"] Yang is pent up above, heat accumulates in the chest, and the yang qi burns upward; therefore the eyes are yellow. The *Lingshu jing* says: "Yellow eyes mean the illness is in the chest."

## Notes

From outward signs — the neck pulse, swelling below the eyes, colour of urine, appetite, swelling of face or feet, yellow eyes — one recognizes water, jaundice, stomach jaundice and wind.
- "Yellow *ju*" (黄疽): all other sources write 黄疸 "jaundice". Wang Bing glosses 疽 as "exhaustion", which the Song editors reject.
- "Lower eyelids" (目裏): Wikisource (the plain text) and Daizhige write 目裹.
- Kanripo writes a variant form of 蚕 "silkworm".
