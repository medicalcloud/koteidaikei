# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "Illness has branch and root, and needling has contrary and compliant [methods]. What does this mean?"

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on branch and root, contrary and compliant. There is no note by Wang Bing.
