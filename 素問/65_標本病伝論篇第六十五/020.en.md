# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In bladder illness urine is blocked; in five days the lower abdomen is distended, the lumbus and spine hurt and the shins ache; in one day the abdomen is distended; in one day the body aches; if it does not end in two days, death follows—in winter at cockcrow, in summer in the late afternoon.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In bladder illness urine is blocked"] Because it is the bowel of fluids.

[On "in five days the lower abdomen is distended, the lumbus and spine hurt and the shins ache"] It returns to its own depot.

[On "in one day the abdomen is distended"] The kidneys in turn transmit to the small intestine.

[On "in one day the body aches"] The small intestine transmits to the spleen.
Song editors: The *Lingshu jing* says "in one day it goes up to the heart"; this is a bowel transmitting to a depot. The *Jiayi jing* writes "goes to the spleen", agreeing with Wang's note.

[On "if it does not end in two days ... in summer in the late afternoon"] Cockcrow means early cockcrow, the middle of *chou*. Late afternoon means the sun descending at the *bu* hour, five *ke* after *shen*.

## Notes

The transmission and time of death of bladder illness.
- 巳 in 不巳 is read as 已.
