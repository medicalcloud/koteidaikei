# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Carefully examine whether it is mild or severe and regulate it by judgment. For the mild, treat [branch and root] together; for the severe, treat one alone. When urine and stool first fail to pass and illness arises after, treat the root.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Carefully examine whether it is mild or severe and regulate it by judgment"] 間 means many; 甚 means few. "Many" means many outward signs but light and easy; "few" means few outward signs but heavy and difficult. "Regulate it by judgment" means carefully weighing branch and root, insufficiency and surplus; it does not mean abandoning method and acting recklessly by whim.

[On "For the mild, treat together; for the severe, treat one alone ... treat the root"] 并 means that other vessels together receive the pathogenic qi and are ill together; 独 means that one channel receives the illness and no other qi is involved. When combined and severe, it is transmitted; when transmission is rapid, death also follows.

## Notes

Treatment according to the severity of illness, and the case where obstruction of urine and stool comes first.
- Wang Bing explains 間/甚 and 并/独 in terms of whether many channels or one are ill. The translation follows the characters of the main text.
