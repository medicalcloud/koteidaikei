# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In stomach illness there is distension and fullness; in five days the lower abdomen, lumbus and spine hurt and the shins ache; in three days the sinews of the back and spine hurt and urine is blocked; in five days the body is heavy; if it does not end in six days, death follows—in winter after midnight, in summer in the early afternoon.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In stomach illness there is distension and fullness"] Since its vessel follows the abdomen, this results.

[On "in five days the lower abdomen, lumbus and spine hurt and the shins ache"] The stomach transmits to the kidneys.

[On "in three days the sinews of the back and spine hurt and urine is blocked"] It transmits to its own bowel and goes on to the spine.

[On "in five days the body is heavy"] The bladder, the water bowel, transmits to the 胛 [spleen].
Song editors: The *Lingshu jing* and *Jiayi jing* each say "in five days it goes up to the heart". This is the bladder transmitting to the heart, overcoming it, so that the body becomes heavy. Wang's saying it transmits to the spleen is wrong.

[On "if it does not end in six days ... in summer in the early afternoon"] After midnight means eight *ke* after *zi*, the middle of *chou*. Early afternoon means eight *ke* after *wu*, the middle of *wei*.

## Notes

The transmission and time of death of stomach illness.
- 巳 in 不巳 is read as 已. 胛 in Wang Bing's note is presumably an error for 脾 (the Song editors also take it as "transmits to the spleen").
