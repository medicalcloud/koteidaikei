# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When illness comes first and fullness in the center arises after, treat the branch. When fullness in the center comes first and vexation of the heart after, treat the root.

## Notes

There is no note by Wang Bing.
- For 先 in 後先中満, Wikisource and two 殆知閣 texts have 生 (see the variants). The translation reads it as 生 "arise".
