# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In lung illness there is panting and coughing; in three days the flanks are propped, full and painful; in one day the body is heavy and aches; in five days there is distension; if it does not end in ten days, death follows—in winter at sunset, in summer at sunrise.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In lung illness there is panting and coughing"] The true [qi] of the depots is high in the lungs, and [the lungs] govern breathing; hence panting and coughing.

[On "in three days the flanks are propped, full and painful"] The lungs transmit to the liver.

[On "in one day the body is heavy and aches"] The liver transmits to the spleen.

[On "in five days there is distension"] It transmits to its own bowel.

[On "if it does not end in ten days ... in summer at sunrise"] In the middle of the first month of winter, the sun sets at the eighth *ke* three *fen* of *shen*; in the middle of the second month of winter, at the seventh *ke* three *fen* of *shen*; in the third month of winter, the sun sets in *shen* as in the first month. In the middle of the first month of summer, the sun rises at the eighth *ke* one *fen* of *yin*; in the middle of the second month of summer, at the tenth *ke* three *fen* of *yin*; in the third month of summer, the sun rises in *yin* as in the first month.

## Notes

The transmission and time of death of lung illness.
- 巳 in 不巳 is read as 已.
