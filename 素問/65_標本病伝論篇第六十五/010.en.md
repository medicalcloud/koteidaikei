# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

People have guest qi and have like qi.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "People have guest qi and have like qi"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition writes 固 for 同.

## Notes

A distinction between qi that comes from outside (guest qi) and qi that is native (like qi, or 固 "inherent" qi). There is no note by Wang Bing.
