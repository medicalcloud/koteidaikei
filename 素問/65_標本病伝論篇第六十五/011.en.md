# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When urine and stool do not pass freely, treat the branch; when urine and stool pass freely, treat the root.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When urine and stool do not pass freely ... treat the root"] The root is the earlier illness, the branch the later illness; one must examine them carefully.

## Notes

The order of branch and root is decided by whether urine and stool pass.
