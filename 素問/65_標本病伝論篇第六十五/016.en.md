# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In liver illness the head and eyes are dizzy and the flanks propped and full; in three days the body is heavy and aches; in five days there is distension; in three days the lumbus, spine and lower abdomen hurt and the shins ache; if it does not end in three days, death follows—in winter at sunset, in summer at early mealtime.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In liver illness the head and eyes are dizzy and the flanks propped and full"] The true [qi] of the depots disperses in the liver, and [its] vessel connects inwardly to the eyes and flanks; hence this.

[On "in three days the body is heavy and aches"] The liver transmits to the lungs [presumably the spleen].

[On "in five days there is distension"] It transmits to its own bowel.

[On "in three days the lumbus, spine and lower abdomen hurt and the shins ache"] This means the stomach transmits to the kidneys. Its vessel starts at the foot, follows the inside of the calf, emerges at the inner edge of the popliteal fossa, goes up the posterior edge of the inner thigh, pierces the spine, connects to the kidneys and networks the bladder; hence this. The lumbus is the residence of the kidneys; hence lumbar pain.

[On "if it does not end in three days, death follows—in winter at sunset"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes "midday".

[On "in winter at sunset, in summer at early mealtime"] The earliness or lateness of sunset is as in the winter method. Early mealtime means earlier than the mealtime, that is, the middle of *mao*.

## Notes

The transmission and time of death of liver illness.
- 巳 in 不巳 is read as 已.
- Wang Bing's "the liver transmits to the lungs" is presumably an error for "to the spleen", since a heavy, aching body is a spleen symptom and the following notes continue "transmits to its own bowel" and "the stomach transmits to the kidneys".
- The Song editors' "the *Jiayi jing* writes midday" presumably means 日中 for 日入 in 冬日入.
