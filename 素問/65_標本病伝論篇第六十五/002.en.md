# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "In all methods of needling, one must distinguish yin and yang, make front and back correspond, be able to apply contrary and compliant [methods], and shift between branch and root. Hence it is said: there are cases where it is in the branch and is sought in the branch, cases where it is in the root and is sought in the root, cases where it is in the root and is sought in the branch, and cases where it is in the branch and is sought in the root. So in treatment there are those obtained by taking the branch, those obtained by taking the root, those obtained by contrary taking, and those obtained by compliant taking. So one who knows contrary and compliant acts correctly without asking; one who knows branch and root succeeds ten thousand times in ten thousand attempts; not knowing branch and root is called acting recklessly.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "So in treatment there are those obtained by taking the branch ... those obtained by compliant taking"] When one grasps the nature of the illness and knows the main outline of treatment, both contrary and compliant can be applied and will surely hit the mark.

[On "So one who knows contrary and compliant acts correctly without asking ... ten thousand attempts"] When there is no doubt about the way and understanding is already deep and clear, one need not ask others; acting correctly, everything is on target.

[On "not knowing branch and root is called acting recklessly"] When understanding is still 𥚹 [narrow] and shallow and the way not yet lofty and deep, errors appear as soon as one acts; hence actions are often reckless.

## Notes

The importance of knowing branch and root, contrary and compliant. Qibo's speech continues to the end of the chapter.
- 𥚹 in Wang Bing's 𥚹浅 is translated as a variant of 褊 "narrow", but this has not been checked.
