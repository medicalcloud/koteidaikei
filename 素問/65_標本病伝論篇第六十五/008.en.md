# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When illness comes first and diarrhea after, treat the root. When diarrhea comes first and other illness arises after, treat the root: one must first regulate it, and then treat the other illness.

## Notes

There is no note by Wang Bing.
- For 且 in 必且調之, 殆知閣 (白文) has 先 (see the variants). The translation reads "first".
