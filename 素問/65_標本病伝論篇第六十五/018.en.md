# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In kidney illness the lower abdomen, lumbus and spine hurt and the shins ache; in three days the sinews of the back and spine hurt and urine is blocked; in three days the abdomen is distended; in three days both flanks are propped and painful; if it does not end in three days, death follows—in winter at full dawn, in summer at late afternoon.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In kidney illness the lower abdomen, lumbus and spine hurt and the shins ache"] The true [qi] of the depots descends to the kidneys; hence this.

[On "in three days the sinews of the back and spine hurt and urine is blocked"] It transmits to its own bowel.
Song editors: The *Lingshu jing* says "it goes to the spine and bladder". This is transmission to its own bowel and going on to the spine.

[On "in three days the abdomen is distended"] The bladder transmits to the small intestine.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says "in three days it goes up to the heart, and the heart is distended".

[On "in three days both flanks are propped and painful"] The bowel transmits to the depot.
Song editors: The *Lingshu jing* says "in three days it goes to the small intestine; in three days it goes up to the heart". Here "both flanks are propped and painful" means the small-intestine bowel transmits to the heart depot and produces pain.

[On "if it does not end in three days ... in summer at late afternoon"] Full dawn means nine *ke* after *yin*, when it is fully light; late afternoon means nine *ke* after *shen*, toward dusk.

## Notes

The transmission and time of death of kidney illness.
- 巳 in 不巳 is read as 已. 𦙼𦛗 is written 背 or 背月呂 etc. in other sources (see the variants) and is read as "back and spine".
