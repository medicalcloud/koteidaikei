# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When heat comes first and illness arises after, treat the root. When heat comes first and fullness in the center arises after, treat the branch.

## Notes

Fullness in the center is treated first even though it is the branch. There is no note by Wang Bing.
