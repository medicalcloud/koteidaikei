# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When illness is transmitted: in heart illness, first the heart hurts; in one day there is coughing; in three days the flanks are propped and painful; in five days there is blockage with no passage, the body aches and is heavy; if it does not end in three days, death follows—in winter at midnight, in summer at midday.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When illness is transmitted: in heart illness, first the heart hurts"] The true [qi] of the depots communicates with the heart; hence the heart hurts first.

[On "in one day there is coughing"] Heart fire overcomes metal and is transmitted to the lungs. The lungs' change and movement is coughing; hence this.

[On "in three days the flanks are propped and painful"] Lung metal overcomes wood and is transmitted to the liver. Since its vessel follows the flanks and ribs, this results.

[On "in five days there is blockage with no passage, the body aches and is heavy"] Liver wood overcomes earth and is transmitted to the spleen. The spleen is by nature calm and settled, and wood qi takes advantage of it; hence blockage with no passage, and the body aches and is heavy.

[On "if it does not end in three days, death follows"] They attack one another by overcoming, always following the weak. When four of the five depots are harmed, how can one last long? Hence death follows at once.

[On "in winter at midnight, in summer at midday"] This means precisely the hours of *zi* and *wu*. Some say winter and summer differ, but this is wrong; that they are the middle of night and of day is perfectly clear.
Song editors: The *Lingshu jing* says: "When qi enters the depots, the illness first arises in the heart; in one day it goes to the lungs, in three days to the liver, in five days to the spleen; if it does not end in three days, death follows—in winter at midnight, in summer at midday." The *Jiayi jing* says: "When illness first arises in the heart, the heart hurts; in one day it goes to the lungs with coughing; in five days to the liver with propped pain of the ribs; in five days to the spleen with blockage and no passage, the body ill and heavy; if it does not end in three days, death follows—in winter at midnight, in summer at midday." On examination, the *Suwen* speaks of the illnesses, the *Lingshu* of the depots, and the *Jiayi jing* combines the texts of the two classics, naming both illnesses and depots.

## Notes

The course by which heart illness is transmitted in the order of overcoming until death, and the time of death. The transmission of illness for each depot and bowel follows.
