# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In spleen illness the body aches and is heavy; in one day there is distension; in two days the lower abdomen, lumbus and spine hurt and the shins ache; in three days the sinews of the back and spine hurt and urine is blocked; if it does not end in ten days, death follows—in winter at bedtime, in summer at late mealtime.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In spleen illness the body aches and is heavy"] The true [qi] of the depots moistens the spleen, and [the spleen] governs the flesh; hence this.

[On "in one day there is distension"] It transmits to its own bowel.

[On "in two days the lower abdomen, lumbus and spine hurt and the shins ache"] The stomach transmits to the kidneys.

[On "in three days the sinews of the back and spine hurt and urine is blocked"] It transmits to its own bowel and goes on to the spine.

[On "if it does not end in ten days ... in summer at late mealtime"] Bedtime means twenty-five *ke* after *shen*; late mealtime means twenty-five *ke* after *yin*.

## Notes

The transmission and time of death of spleen illness.
- 巳 in 不巳 is read as 已. 𦛗 is read as a variant of 膂 "spine".
