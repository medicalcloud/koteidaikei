# 素問 標本病伝論篇第六十五 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the various illnesses are transmitted in this order, all have a fixed time of death and must not be needled. When [transmission] skips one depot and stops, or reaches the third or fourth depot, then one may needle."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the various illnesses are transmitted in this order ... must not be needled"] The shifting among the five depots is always like this. Some are transmitted slowly, some quickly. In slow cases death may come in one, two or three years; next, in three or six months. In quick cases death comes in one, two, three or four days, or five or six days; this is the class meant here. Looking into this method of transmission, it is all the qi of the five phases, but when one considers the numbers of days, the reasoning does not correspond. If the five phases are the framework and transmission to what is overcome follows the number of the one that is overcome, then fire to metal should be one day, metal to wood two days, wood to earth four days, earth to water three days, and water to fire five days. If transmission to what is not overcome follows the number of what 巳 [oneself] overcomes, then wood transmits to earth in three days, earth to water in five, water to fire in one, fire to metal in two, and metal to water [presumably wood] in four. The days of transmission in the classic seem to follow the qi of the three yin and three yang. The *Yuji zhenzang lun* says: "The shifting among the five depots always has an order. If not treated, death will come by transmission in three or six months, or three or six days." This is the same. Even so, one must still examine the number of days carefully at the bedside to know fully what is right and wrong.

[On "skips one depot and stops"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* lacks the character 止.

[On "When [transmission] skips one depot and stops ... then one may needle"] "Skips one depot and stops" means passing over the next depot and transmitting no further: wood to earth, earth to water, water to fire, fire to metal, metal to wood, and stopping—each skips one depot. "Reaches the third or fourth depot" means reaching the third or fourth depot ahead. Reaching the third depot is always the qi that oneself does not overcome; reaching the fourth depot is always reaching the parent that generates oneself. When it is not overcome, it cannot harm the other; when it is the generating relation, parent and child have no time of conflict and the qi moves in order. Hence needling is permissible.

## Notes

The chapter ends: illness transmitted in the order of overcoming must not be needled, while illness that skips a depot or reaches the third or fourth depot may be. Qibo's speech ends here.
- 巳 in Wang Bing's 巳勝 and 至巳所生 is presumably an error for 己 "oneself". 水 in 金四日伝於水 is presumably an error for 木, given the order of the phases (wood → earth → water → fire → metal → wood).
- 殆知閣 (重广补注黄帝内经素问) places a 巻十九 break at the end of this unit; this is that source's division into juan.
