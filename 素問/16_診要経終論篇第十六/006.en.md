# 素問 診要経終論篇第十六 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If in autumn one needles the spring division, the illness does not end, and the person becomes apprehensive, wanting to do something but forgetting it on rising. If in autumn one needles the summer division, the illness does not end, and the person becomes ever more fond of lying down and also dreams much. If in autumn one needles the winter division, the illness does not end, and the person shivers and is at times cold.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If in autumn one needles the spring division ... forgetting it on rising"] Because the liver is depleted; the needling was inappropriate.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* (chapter 64) says: "If in autumn one needles the channels, the blood and qi counterflow upward and the person is forgetful."

[On "If in autumn one needles the summer division ... dreams much"] When the heart qi is scant, the spleen qi is isolated; therefore one is fond of lying down. The heart governs dreams, which the spirit makes; therefore one dreams much.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* says: "If in autumn one needles the network vessels, the qi does not move outward and the person lies down unable to move."

[On "If in autumn one needles the winter division ... at times cold"] Yin qi invades upward; therefore one is at times cold. 洒洒 describes shivering.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* says: "If in autumn one needles the sinews and bones, the blood and qi [scatter] within and the person is cold and shivers."

## Notes

The harm of mistakenly needling other seasons' parts in autumn.
- 㝱 is a variant of 夢 "dream"; Wikisource (the plain text) and Daizhige write 夢.
