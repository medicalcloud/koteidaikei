# 素問 診要経終論篇第十六 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I would like to hear about the termination of the twelve channels."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] 終 means exhaustion.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about the signs when the qi of the twelve channels is exhausted (at the approach of death). The "Jingzhong" of the chapter title comes from this.
