# 素問 診要経終論篇第十六 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If in winter one needles the spring division, the illness does not end, and the person wants to lie down but cannot sleep, and on sleeping sees things. If in winter one needles the summer division, the illness is not cured, the qi rises and breaks out as various impediments. If in winter one needles the autumn division, the illness does not end, and the person is often thirsty.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If in winter one needles the spring division ... on sleeping sees things"] The liver qi is scant; therefore one wants to lie down but cannot sleep. The liver governs the eyes; therefore on sleeping one seems to see the shapes of things.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* (chapter 64) says: "If in winter one needles the channels, the blood and qi all escape and the person's eyes are not clear."

[On "If in winter one needles the summer division ... various impediments"] Because the pulse qi is drained.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* says: "If in winter one needles the network vessels, the blood and qi leak outward and remain as great impediment."

[On "If in winter one needles the autumn division ... often thirsty"] The lung qi is insufficient; therefore thirst arises.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* says: "If in winter one needles the flesh, the yang qi is exhausted and cut off and the person is often thirsty."

## Notes

The harm of mistakenly needling other seasons' parts in winter.
