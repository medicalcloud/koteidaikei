# 素問 診要経終論篇第十六 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "In the first and second months, the qi of heaven begins to be correct, the qi of earth begins to issue, and the human qi is in the liver. In the third and fourth months, the qi of heaven is fully correct, the qi of earth issues steadily, and the human qi is in the spleen. In the fifth and sixth months, the qi of heaven is abundant, the qi of earth is high, and the human qi is in the head. In the seventh and eighth months, the yin qi begins to kill, and the human qi is in the lung. In the ninth and tenth months, the yin qi begins to freeze, the qi of earth begins to close, and the human qi is in the heart. In the eleventh and twelfth months, ice lies thick, the qi of earth is sealed, and the human qi is in the kidney.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the first and second months ... the human qi is in the liver"] 方 means correct. It says that the qi of heaven and earth are correct and bring forth the myriad things. Wood governs the east and dominates for seventy-two days, until twelve days after the node of the third month; this is wood in charge. Taken by months, in the first and second months the human qi is in the liver.

[On "In the third and fourth months ... the human qi is in the spleen"] The qi of heaven is fully correct because the yang qi is bright and abundant; the qi of earth issues steadily because the myriad things flower and are about to fruit. At the end of the season earth lodges and dominates, and earth is also generated at *bing*; therefore the human qi is in the spleen.

[On "In the fifth and sixth months ... the human qi is in the head"] The yang of heaven is blazing and the flames of earth rise high; hence "the qi of heaven is abundant, the qi of earth high". The nature of fire is to blaze upward; therefore the human qi is in the head.

[On "In the seventh and eighth months ... the human qi is in the lung"] In the seventh month the three yin branch forth, and in the eighth month yin begins to be austere and killing; hence "the yin qi begins to kill". The austere killing of the yin qi is of the kind of metal, and the lung qi resembles metal; therefore the human qi is in the lung.

[On "In the ninth and tenth months ... the human qi is in the heart"] The yin qi begins to congeal and the qi of earth begins to close; [the human qi] follows the yang inward; therefore the human qi is in the heart.

[On "In the eleventh and twelfth months ... the human qi is in the kidney"] The yang qi lies deep; therefore the qi is in the kidney. The changes of qi: it issues and grows in wood, grows and flourishes in earth, is abundant and rises high, is austere and killing in metal, avoids the cold in fire, and lies hidden in water. All this follows the rising and sinking of the qi of yin and yang. The *Wuzang shengcheng* (chapter 10) says, "The images of the five zang can be inferred by analogy" — this refers to such categories of qi.

## Notes

The year is divided into six two-month periods, with the state of the qi of heaven and earth and the organ in which the human qi lies (liver, spleen, head, lung, heart, kidney). Qibo's speech continues to unit 008.
- Unlike the usual five-phase scheme (spring liver, summer heart, etc.), the ninth and tenth months are assigned to the heart and the fifth and sixth to the head; Wang Bing explains that the qi follows the yang inward to the heart.
