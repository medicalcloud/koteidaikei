# 素問 診要経終論篇第十六 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If in spring one needles the summer division, the pulse is disordered and the qi faint, [evil] enters and floods the bones and marrow, the illness cannot be cured, and the person loses appetite and is short of qi. If in spring one needles the autumn division, the sinews cramp, counterflowing qi circulates and causes coughing, the illness is not cured, and the person is at times frightened and also weeps. If in spring one needles the winter division, evil qi adheres to the zang, the person becomes distended, the illness is not cured, and he also wants to talk.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If in spring one needles the summer division ... short of qi"] The heart governs the vessels; therefore the pulse is disordered and the qi faint. Water receives qi in summer and the kidney governs the bones; therefore [evil] enters and floods the bones and marrow. When heart fire is faint, stomach earth is insufficient; therefore there is loss of appetite and shortness of qi.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* (chapter 64) says: "If in spring one needles the network vessels, the blood and qi overflow outward and the person becomes short of qi."

[On "If in spring one needles the autumn division ... also weeps"] Wood receives qi in autumn and the liver governs the sinews; therefore needling the autumn division makes the sinews cramp. If the qi counterflows and circulates, there is coughing. The liver governs fright; therefore one is at times frightened. The lung governs qi; therefore the qi counterflows and one also weeps.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* says: "If in spring one needles the flesh, the blood and qi circulate in counterflow and the person has rising qi."

[On "If in spring one needles the winter division ... wants to talk"] Winter governs the hidden storage of the yang qi; therefore evil qi adheres to the zang. When the kidney is replete there is distension; therefore needling the winter division makes the person distended. Fire receives qi in winter and the heart governs speech; therefore one wants to talk.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* says: "If in spring one needles the sinews and bones, the blood and qi adhere within and the person's abdomen is distended."

## Notes

The harm of mistakenly needling the parts belonging to other seasons in spring. Units 004 to 007 set out the harm of such errors for each season.
- The Song editors cite the corresponding passages of the *Sishi ci nicong lun* (chapter 64), which describe the needled parts differently.
