# 素問 診要経終論篇第十六 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If in summer one needles the spring division, the illness is not cured and the person becomes languid and lazy. If in summer one needles the autumn division, the illness is not cured, and the person has no wish to speak in his heart and is apprehensive as if someone were about to seize him. If in summer one needles the winter division, the illness is not cured, and the person becomes short of qi and is at times inclined to anger.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If in summer one needles the spring division ... languid and lazy"] The liver nourishes the sinews; the liver qi is insufficient; therefore the strength of the sinews slackens.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* (chapter 64) says: "If in summer one needles the channels, the blood and qi are exhausted and the person becomes languid and lazy."

[On "If in summer one needles the autumn division ... as if someone were about to seize him"] Liver-wood makes speech. If the autumn division is harmed, liver-wood is depleted; therefore one fears as if someone were about to seize him. Because the liver is insufficient, one does not wish to speak and is also afraid.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* says: "If in summer one needles the flesh, the blood and qi withdraw inward and the person is prone to fear." The *Jiayi jing* has 悶 "oppression" [for "has no wish to speak in his heart"].

[On "If in summer one needles the winter division ... inclined to anger"] In summer the kidney is harmed, and the emotions of the liver and lung [㪍] are insufficient within; therefore the person becomes short of qi and is at times inclined to anger.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* says: "If in summer one needles the sinews and bones, the blood and qi counterflow upward and the person is prone to anger."

## Notes

The harm of mistakenly needling other seasons' parts in summer.
- 㪍 in Wang Bing's 肝肺㪍之志 is the base text's character, whose meaning is unclear; it is shown as it stands.
