# 素問 診要経終論篇第十六 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the greater yang vessel terminates, the eyes turn upward, the body arches backward, there are convulsions, the colour is white, and terminal sweat comes out; when it comes out, one dies. When the lesser yang terminates, there is deafness, all the joints slacken, the eyes stare and the eye connector is cut off; when the connector is cut off, death comes in a day and a half, and at death the colour first turns green-white, then one dies. When *yangming* terminates, the mouth and eyes twitch, one is easily startled and talks wildly, the colour is yellow; when the channels above and below are abundant and there is numbness, it terminates. When the lesser yin terminates, the face is black, the teeth lengthen and are coated, the abdomen is distended and blocked, above and below do not communicate, and it terminates. When the greater yin terminates, the abdomen is distended and blocked, one cannot breathe, belches and vomits often; vomiting brings counterflow, and counterflow makes the face red; without counterflow, above and below do not communicate; not communicating, the face is black, the skin and body hair scorched, and it terminates. When *jueyin* terminates, there is heat within, the throat is dry, urination is frequent, the heart is vexed; in severe cases the tongue curls and the testicles retract upward, and it terminates. These are the ruinations of the twelve channels."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the greater yang vessel terminates ... when it comes out, one dies"] "The eyes turn upward" means the pupils do not move and look up. The foot greater yang vessel starts at the inner canthus, rises to the forehead, crosses at the vertex, enters from the vertex to connect with the brain, comes out again, branches down the nape, follows the inside of the shoulder blade, and runs alongside the spine to the lower back; its branch descends along the foot to the outer side of the little toe. The hand greater yang vessel starts at the tip of the little finger, follows the arm, rises to the shoulder and enters the supraclavicular fossa; its branch rises to the cheek, reaches the inner canthus and meets the foot greater yang [Song editors: the *Jiayi jing* reads "connects obliquely with the cheekbone"]; another branch goes from the supraclavicular fossa along the neck, up the cheek, to the outer canthus [Song editors: the *Jiayi jing* writes 兌 for 外]. Therefore the eyes turn upward, the body arches, there are convulsions, the colour is white and terminal sweat comes out. Terminal sweat means sweat that suddenly comes out like beads, does not flow, and quickly dries again. When the greater yang reaches its extreme, sweat comes out; therefore when it comes out one dies.

[On "When the lesser yang terminates ... then one dies"] The foot lesser yang vessel starts at the outer canthus, rises to the corner of the head and descends behind the ear; its branch enters the ear from behind and emerges in front of the ear. A branch of the hand lesser yang vessel also enters the ear from behind and emerges in front of it. Therefore at termination there is deafness, the eyes stare and the connector is cut off. The lesser yang governs the bones; therefore when its qi terminates, all the joints slacken. A green-white colour means metal and wood press on each other; therefore death appears. 睘 means staring straight as if startled.

[On "When *yangming* terminates ... it terminates"] The foot *yangming* vessel starts at the nose, crosses at the root of the nose, descends along the outside of the nose, enters the upper gums, comes out again to skirt the mouth and circle the lips, meets below at Chengjiang, runs back along the lower edge behind the chin, emerges at Daying, follows Jiache, rises in front of the ear, passes Kezhuren, and follows the hairline to the forehead; its branch descends from in front of Daying to Renying, follows the throat, enters the supraclavicular fossa and descends through the diaphragm. The hand *yangming* vessel starts at the hand, follows the arm to the shoulder, rises to emerge at the meeting of the pillar bone, then descends into the supraclavicular fossa and connects with the lung; its branch rises from the supraclavicular fossa to the neck, pierces the cheek, enters the lower teeth, comes out again to skirt the mouth, crosses at Renzhong — the left going right and the right going left — rises alongside the nostrils and meets the foot *yangming* [Song editors: the *Jiayi jing* writes 孔 for 鼽 and lacks the four characters 抵足陽明]. Therefore at termination the mouth and eyes twitch. "Twitching of the mouth and eyes" means the eyes flicker and the jaw clacks. When the stomach is ill one dislikes people and fire, is alarmed on hearing the sound of wood, and curses, cursing without distinguishing close and distant; therefore one is easily startled and talks wildly. Yellow is the colour of earth. "Above" means the hand vessels and "below" the foot vessels. "The channels are abundant" means the face, eyes, neck, jaw, instep, wrists and shins all throb restlessly. "Numbness" means not knowing good from bad. All of these are signs of exhausted qi; therefore it terminates.

[On "When the lesser yin terminates ... it terminates"] When the qi of the hand lesser yin is cut off the blood does not flow; when the qi of the foot lesser yin is cut off the bones are not soft. When the bones are hard, the gums recede upward; therefore the teeth lengthen and accumulate coating. When sweat and blood are ruined, the colour of the skin dies; therefore the face is like lacquer and not red. The foot lesser yin vessel rises from the kidney through the liver and diaphragm into the lung; the hand lesser yin vessel starts in the heart, emerges to belong to the heart connector, descends through the diaphragm and connects with the lower abdomen. Therefore at termination the abdomen is distended and blocked, and above and below do not communicate.
Song editors: Wang Bing's note says "the bones are not soft, the bones are hard", but since the *Nanjing* and *Jiayi jing* say "if the bones are not moistened, the flesh cannot adhere", it should be "the bones are not moistened". For "the hand lesser yin vessel connects with the lower abdomen", the *Jiayi jing* has "connects with the small intestine".

[On "When the greater yin terminates ... belches and vomits often"] The foot greater yin vessel enters the abdomen from the front inner edge of the thigh, belongs to the spleen, connects with the stomach and rises through the diaphragm. The hand greater yin vessel starts in the middle burner, descends to connect with the large intestine, returns along the stomach opening, rises through the diaphragm and belongs to the lung. Therefore at termination it is so. The *Lingshu jing* says: "When the foot greater yin vessel is stirred, the illness is vomiting on eating, abdominal distension and frequent belching."

[On "vomiting brings counterflow, and counterflow makes the face red"] Vomiting makes the qi counterflow; therefore the face is red.
Song editors: The *Lingshu jing* reads: "frequent belching; belching brings vomiting; vomiting brings counterflow".

[On "without counterflow ... and it terminates"] With vomiting, the upper part communicates; therefore the face is merely red. Without vomiting, the lower part is already closed and the upper part also does not communicate, and the heart qi burns outward; therefore the skin and body hair are scorched and it terminates. Why? A branch of the foot greater yin vessel also separates from the stomach, rises through the diaphragm and pours into the heart; by this, the scorching of skin and hair arises from the heart qi burning outward.

[On "When *jueyin* terminates ... the testicles retract upward, and it terminates"] The network vessel of the foot *jueyin* follows the shin, rises to the testicles and knots at the penis; its main channel enters the pubic hair, passes the genitals, rises to the lower abdomen, runs alongside the stomach, rises behind the throat and enters the nasopharynx. The hand *jueyin* vessel starts in the chest and emerges to belong to the pericardium. Therefore at termination there is heat within, a dry throat, frequent urination and vexation of the heart. The *Lingshu jing* says: "The liver is joined with the sinews; the sinews gather at the genitals and the vessel connects with the root of the tongue." Therefore in severe cases the tongue curls and the testicles retract upward; also because the *jueyin* vessel passes the genitals.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 睪 for 臯 and 環 "circles" for 過 "passes".

[On "These are the ruinations of the twelve channels"] The three yin and three yang of the hand and of the foot are the twelve channels. 敗 means the qi terminating and being exhausted and ruined.
Song editors: [The terminations of] the twelve channels also appear in the *Lingshu jing* and [other chapters of] the *Suwen*.

## Notes

The signs when the qi of each of the six channels (greater yang, lesser yang, *yangming*, lesser yin, greater yin, *jueyin*) is exhausted and death approaches; this ends the chapter, and Qibo's speech ends here.
- Wang Bing explains the signs by the courses of the channels as described in the "Jingmai" chapter of the *Lingshu jing*.
- "Terminal sweat": Wang Bing explains it as sweat that suddenly comes out like beads, does not flow and quickly dries.
- "The eyes stare" (目睘): Wikisource (the plain text) and Daizhige (the plain text) write 目寰.
- Kanripo has different characters in parts of the Song editors' notes (the translation follows the base text).
