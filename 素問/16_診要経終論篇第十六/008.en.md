# 素問 診要経終論篇第十六 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In all needling of the chest and abdomen, one must avoid the five zang. Striking the heart, death after one circuit [of the qi]; striking the spleen, death in five days; striking the kidney, death in seven days; striking the lung, death in five days; striking the diaphragm, in all cases the centre is harmed, and though the illness may be cured, death will surely come within a year. Avoiding the five zang in needling is knowing counterflow and compliance. What is called compliance is [knowing] the locations of the diaphragm, spleen and kidney; one who does not know goes against it. In needling the chest and abdomen one must cover them with cloth and then needle through a single layer of cloth. If needling does not cure, needle again. In needling one must be quiet. In needling a swelling, shake the needle; in needling a channel, do not shake it. This is the way of needling."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In all needling of the chest and abdomen, one must avoid the five zang"] The heart and lung are above the diaphragm, the kidney and liver below it, and the spleen, resembling earth, lies in the centre; therefore in needling the chest and abdomen one must avoid them. The five zang store the essence, spirit, *hun*, *po*, intention and will; if they are damaged, the five spirits depart, and when the spirits depart, death comes. Therefore one cannot but be careful.

[On "Striking the heart, death after one circuit"] When the qi has gone once round the circuit, one dies; this means precisely going round the twelve double-hours.
Song editors: The *Ci jin lun* (chapter 52) says "death in one day; its movement is belching", and the *Sishi ci nicong lun* (chapter 64) is the same. This classic lacks the time of death for striking the liver; the *Ci jin lun* says "striking the liver, death in five days; its movement is talking", and the *Sishi ci nicong lun* is the same.

[On "striking the spleen, death in five days"] The number of earth is five.
Song editors: The *Ci jin lun* says "striking the spleen, death in ten days; its movement is swallowing", and the *Sishi ci nicong lun* is the same.

[On "striking the kidney, death in seven days"] The completing number of water is six; when the number of water is finished, death should come on the seventh day. One version has "death in ten days", which is a scribal error.
Song editors: The *Ci jin lun* says "striking the kidney, death in six days; its movement is sneezing", and the *Sishi ci nicong lun* says "striking the kidney, death in six days; its movement is sneezing and yawning".

[On "striking the lung, death in five days"] The generating number of metal is [ ] four; when the number of metal is finished, death should come on the fifth day. One version has "death in three days", also a scribal error.
Song editors: The *Ci jin lun* says "striking the lung, death in three days; its movement is coughing", and the *Sishi ci nicong lun* is the same. Wang Bing's note to the *Sishi ci nicong lun* says: "These three treatises are all Qibo's words; that they differ is due to errors in transmission."

[On "striking the diaphragm ... death will surely come within a year"] The qi of the five zang together govern a year; when the diaphragm is harmed, the qi of the five zang control and attack one another; therefore death will surely come within a year.

[On "What is called compliance ... one who does not know goes against it"] The kidney adheres to the spine, the spleen lies in the centre, and the diaphragm connects at the edge of the flanks. One who knows complies; one who does not know harms the zang.

[On "one must cover them with cloth and then needle through a single layer of cloth"] When the form is fixed, one does not mistakenly strike the five zang.
Song editors: Another edition writes 撽 for 憿.

[On "If needling does not cure, needle again"] The essential is to take the arrival of the qi as the effect. The *Zhenjing* says, "If on needling the qi does not arrive, [needle] without counting the number of times; if on needling the qi arrives, remove the needle and do not needle again" — this is what is meant.

[On "In needling one must be quiet"] 粛 means quiet and solemn, in order to observe whether the qi is present or not.

[On "In needling a swelling, shake the needle"] In order to release much pus and blood.

[On "in needling a channel, do not shake it"] Because one does not want the channel qi to leak.

## Notes

In needling the chest and abdomen one must avoid the five zang; the times of death for striking the heart, spleen, kidney, lung and diaphragm are given, along with covering the area with cloth and other cautions (needling quietly, shaking the needle for swellings but not for channels). Qibo's speech, begun in unit 002, ends here.
- The Song editors point out that the times of death differ from those in the *Ci jin lun* (chapter 52) and *Sishi ci nicong lun* (chapter 64) (e.g. heart one day, spleen ten, kidney six, lung three), and that the liver is missing here.
- "Cover them with cloth": taken as covering and fixing with cloth. According to the Song editors, another edition writes 撽 for 憿.
- In Wang Bing's 金生数　四 the base text has a blank between characters.
