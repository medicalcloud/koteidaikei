# 素問 診要経終論篇第十六 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore in spring needle the scattered transport points and the divisions of the flesh, stopping when blood comes out; in severe cases the qi is passed on, in mild cases it circulates. In summer needle the network transport points, stopping when blood is seen; when the qi is exhausted and [the point] closed, [the channels] circulate, and painful illness will surely go down. In autumn needle the skin, following the divisions, the same method above and below, stopping when the spirit changes. In winter needle the transport openings in the divisions of the flesh; in severe cases go straight down, in mild cases go down scattering. Spring, summer, autumn and winter each have their places to needle, following where [the qi] is.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Therefore in spring needle the scattered transport points and the divisions of the flesh, stopping when blood comes out"] The scattered transport points are points in between; the divisions are the divisions of the flesh.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* (chapter 64) says, "The qi of spring is in the channels"; these scattered transport points are the transport points of the channels. Further, the *Shuire xue lun* (chapter 61) says, "In spring take the network vessels and the divisions of the flesh."

[On "in severe cases the qi is passed on, in mild cases it circulates"] This distinguishes mild and severe illness qi. 伝 means passing on to each other; 環 means circulating. Passing on, it goes to what cannot overcome; circulating, it goes round the five qi.
Song editors: The *Taisu* writes 環已 for 環也.

[On "In summer needle the network transport points ... painful illness will surely go down"] "Exhausting the qi" means letting blood to exhaust [the evil] and drawing off with the needle the abundant evil qi of the ill vessel. When the evil qi is exhausted and the point is closed tightly, the channels circulate and the qi of painful illness will surely go down and depart. Because the yang qi is greatly abundant, one needles by this method.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* says, "The qi of summer is in the minute network vessels"; these network transport points are the points of the minute network vessels. Further, the *Shuire xue lun* says, "In summer take the abundant channels and the divisions and interstices."

[On "In autumn needle the skin ... stopping when the spirit changes"] "Following the divisions" means following the divisions of the flesh. "Above" means the hand vessels and "below" the foot vessels. "The spirit changes" means the pulse qi changes and differs from before needling. The pulse is the function of the spirit; hence it is said so.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* says, "The qi of autumn is in the skin," which agrees with this. Further, the *Shuire xue lun* says, "Take the stream points to drain yin evil and the sea points to deplete yang evil." Huangfu Shi'an says: "This is the variation of treatment at the beginning of autumn."

[On "In winter needle the transport openings ... in mild cases go down scattering"] "Going straight down" means going down directly; "going down scattering" means going down spreading out.
Song editors: The *Sishi ci nicong lun* says, "The qi of winter is in the bones and marrow"; these transport openings are those of the bones and marrow. Further, the *Shuire xue lun* says, "In winter take the well and spring points." Huangfu Shi'an says: "This is the variation of treatment at the end of winter."

## Notes

Needling in each of the four seasons: in spring the scattered transport points and divisions of the flesh, in summer the network points, in autumn the skin, in winter the transport openings, following where the seasonal qi lies.
- The Song editors compare this with the seasonal needling in the *Sishi ci nicong lun* (chapter 64) and *Shuire xue lun* (chapter 61).
- 尽気閉環 is difficult to read; the translation follows Wang Bing. Daizhige (the plain text) writes 死循 for 閉.
- Wikisource (the plain text) writes 宜下 for 直下.
