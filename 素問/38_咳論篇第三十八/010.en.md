# 素問 咳論篇第三十八 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "To treat the zang, treat their stream points; to treat the fu, treat their uniting points; for puffiness, treat their river points."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "To treat the zang ... for puffiness, treat their river points"] The stream points of the zang are all the third point from where the vessel begins; the uniting points of the fu are all the sixth point from where the vessel begins. The river point is the fourth point from the beginning of a zang vessel and the fifth from the beginning of a fu vessel. The *Lingshu jing* says, "Where the vessel pours is the stream point, where it moves is the river point, where it enters is the uniting point" — this is what is meant.

## Notes

As a principle for needling cough, the stream points are used for the zang, the uniting points for the fu, and the river points for puffiness.
