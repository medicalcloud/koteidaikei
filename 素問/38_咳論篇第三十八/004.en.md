# 素問 咳論篇第三十八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The skin and hair correspond to the lung. The skin and hair first receive the evil qi, and the evil qi follows to what they correspond with. When cold food and drink enter the stomach and rise along the lung vessel to the lung, the lung becomes cold; when the lung is cold, the outer and inner evils combine and lodge there, producing lung cough. The five zang each receive illness in their own season; when it is not their season, each transmits it [to the lung]. Humans correspond with heaven and earth; therefore each of the five zang, when affected by cold in the season it governs, receives illness: when slight, there is cough; when severe, there is diarrhoea or pain. In autumn the lung receives the evil first; in spring the liver receives it first; in summer the heart receives it first; in the utmost yin the spleen receives it first; in winter the kidney receives it first."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The skin and hair correspond to the lung ... follows to what they correspond with"] The evil means cold qi.

[On "When cold food and drink enter the stomach ... producing lung cough"] The lung vessel arises in the middle burner, descends to connect with the large intestine, returns along the mouth of the stomach, rises through the diaphragm and belongs to the lung; hence "rise along the lung vessel to the lung".

[On "The five zang each receive illness in their own season ... each transmits it"] "Season" means the month in which it dominates. Outside its dominant month it does not receive the evil; therefore each transmits it.

[On "Humans correspond with heaven and earth ... diarrhoea or pain"] When the cold qi is slight, it corresponds outwardly to the skin and hair and communicates inwardly with the lung; therefore there is cough. When the cold qi is severe, it enters the interior; when the interior is torn there is pain; when it enters the intestines and stomach, there is diarrhoea.

[On "In autumn the lung receives the evil first ... the kidney receives it first"] Because it is the time when it is in charge, it receives the evil qi first.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu* lack the three characters 乗秋則; it is suspected that this text has many errors.

## Notes

How lung cough arises (external cold and cold food and drink), and how each of the five zang receives cold in its dominant season and transmits it to the lung, causing cough.
- 傅 means 伝 "transmit" (other sources write 伝); Wang Bing's note also writes 伝.
