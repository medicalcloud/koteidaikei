# 素問 咳論篇第三十八 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Long-standing cough of the five zang is transferred to the six fu. If spleen cough does not end, the stomach receives it; in stomach cough one coughs and vomits, and when the vomiting is severe, long worms come out. If liver cough does not end, the gallbladder receives it; in gallbladder cough one coughs and vomits bile. If lung cough does not end, the large intestine receives it; in large intestine cough one coughs and loses control of stool. If heart cough does not end, the small intestine receives it; in small intestine cough one coughs and passes wind, the wind escaping with the cough. If kidney cough does not end, the bladder receives it; in bladder cough one coughs and leaks urine. If long-standing cough does not end, the triple burner receives it; in triple burner cough one coughs with abdominal fullness and no desire for food or drink. All these gather in the stomach and concern the lung, making one produce much mucus and spittle, with facial puffiness and counterflowing qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If spleen cough does not end ... long worms come out"] The spleen and stomach are paired; also, the stomach vessel follows the throat, enters the supraclavicular fossa, descends through the diaphragm, belongs to the stomach and connects with the spleen; therefore if spleen cough does not end, the stomach receives it. When the stomach is cold there is vomiting; when the vomiting is severe, the intestinal qi counterflows upward; therefore roundworms come out.

[On "If liver cough does not end ... vomits bile"] The liver and gallbladder are paired; also, the gallbladder vessel descends from the supraclavicular fossa into the chest, passes through the diaphragm and connects with the liver; therefore if liver cough does not end, the gallbladder receives it. Gallbladder qi tends to counterflow; therefore one vomits warm, bitter fluid.

[On "If lung cough does not end ... loses control of stool"] The lung and large intestine are paired; also, the large intestine vessel enters the supraclavicular fossa and connects with the lung; therefore if lung cough does not end, the large intestine receives it. The large intestine is the fu of conveyance; therefore when cold enters, the qi is not held.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 遺矢 "leak stool" for 遺失.

[On "If heart cough does not end ... the wind escaping with the cough"] The heart and small intestine are paired; also, the small intestine vessel enters the supraclavicular fossa and connects with the heart; therefore if heart cough does not end, the small intestine receives it. The cold of the small intestine is abundant and its qi enters the large intestine; when one coughs, the qi of the small intestine rushes down; therefore one passes wind.

[On "If kidney cough does not end ... leaks urine"] The kidney and bladder are paired; also, the bladder vessel runs from inside the shoulder blade alongside the spine to the lumbus, enters along the spinal muscles, connects with the kidney and belongs to the bladder; therefore if kidney cough does not end, the bladder receives it. The bladder is the fu of fluids; therefore urine leaks.

[On "If long-standing cough does not end ... counterflowing qi"] The triple burner here does not mean the hand lesser yang, but precisely the upper and middle burners. Why? The upper burner emerges at the upper mouth of the stomach, rises alongside the gullet, passes through the diaphragm, spreads in the chest and goes to the armpit. The middle burner also reaches the mouth of the stomach and emerges behind the upper burner; the qi it receives separates the dregs, steams the fluids, transforms their subtle essence and pours it up into the lung vessel, where it is transformed into blood. Hence "all these gather in the stomach and concern the lung". When the two burners receive illness, the evil qi fumigates the lung and the lung qi is full; therefore one produces much mucus and spittle, with facial puffiness and counterflowing qi. Abdominal fullness and no desire for food are because the stomach is cold. The stomach vessel descends from the supraclavicular fossa along the inner edge of the breast and down the abdomen to Qijie; a branch goes again from the lower mouth of the stomach along the inside of the abdomen to join at Qijie. Now the stomach receives the evil; hence such illness. How is it clear that the lower burner is not meant? The lower burner separates at the ileum and pours into the bladder; water and grain always stay together in the stomach, the dregs go down together into the large intestine, and the fluid is separated and seeps along the lower burner into the bladder. Considering this process, it is far from the mouth of the stomach; therefore it is not meant.
Song editors: For the stomach vessel's 下循腹 "down along the abdomen", the *Jiayi jing* writes 下侠臍 "down alongside the navel".

## Notes

When the cough of the five zang lasts long, it is transferred to the paired fu with their respective symptoms; all coughs gather in the stomach and concern the lung.
- Wang Bing takes the "triple burner" here not as the fu of the hand lesser yang but as the upper and middle burners related to the mouth of the stomach.
- 扺 in Wang Bing's note is a variant of 抵.
