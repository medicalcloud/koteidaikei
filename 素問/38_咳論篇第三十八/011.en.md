# 素問 咳論篇第三十八 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good."

## Notes

This ends the chapter. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- Daizhige (重广补注黄帝内经素问) has 巻十一 at the end of the chapter; this marks the juan division in that source.
