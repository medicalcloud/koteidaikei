# 素問 咳論篇第三十八 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "The five zang and six fu all make people cough, not the lung alone."

## Notes

A frequently quoted statement that cough can be caused not only by the lung but by all five zang and six fu. There is no note by Wang Bing.
