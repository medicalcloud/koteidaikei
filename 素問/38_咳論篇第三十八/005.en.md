# 素問 咳論篇第三十八 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How are they distinguished?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How are they distinguished?"] He wishes to clarify their signs.

## Notes

Huangdi asks how the coughs of the five zang are distinguished.
