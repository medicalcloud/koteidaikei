# 素問 著至教論篇第七十五 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong said, "My treatments rarely cure; I only ask you to explain the meaning."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "My treatments rarely cure; I only ask you to explain the meaning"] Leigong says that what he treats seldom recovers, and asks to be told the deep meaning, after which [his doubts] will end. The doubting mind will stop; that is, once he obtains an explanation, his doubts will stop.

## Notes

Leigong asks for further explanation.
