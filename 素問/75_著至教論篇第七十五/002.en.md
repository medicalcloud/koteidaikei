# 素問 著至教論篇第七十五 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong replied, "I recite and can understand fairly well, but understanding, I cannot yet distinguish; distinguishing, I cannot yet clarify; clarifying, I cannot yet make manifest. It is enough to treat the officials but not enough for lords and kings. I wish to receive [the teaching of] setting up the measures of heaven, combining the four seasons and yin and yang with them, and distinguishing the lights of the stars, the sun and the moon, so as to make manifest the art of the classics, make it ever clearer for later generations, reach up to Shennong, and set out the utmost teaching, so that it may be 疑 [compared] to that of the two sovereigns."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "understanding, I cannot yet distinguish; distinguishing, I cannot yet clarify; clarifying, I cannot yet make manifest"] It says that what he knows and understands is only obtaining the methods and keeping to the numbers; he cannot yet fully grasp the wondrous use of the subtle.
Song editors: According to Yang Shangshan, "There are five stages in learning the Way: first reciting, second understanding, third distinguishing, fourth clarifying, fifth making manifest."

[On "It is enough to treat the officials but not enough for lords and kings"] Leigong does not presume to exalt his way. So the treatments for commoners and for those who receive sacrifices differ.

[On "I wish to receive [the teaching of] setting up the measures of heaven ... make it ever clearer for later generations"] "Setting up the measures of heaven" means high and far without limit. "Combining the four seasons and yin and yang with them" means following the order of the qi. "Distinguishing the lights of the stars, the sun and the moon" means distinguishing learners so that the size of the two lights differs.
Song editors: The *Taisu* has 列 for 別.

[On "reach up to Shennong, and set out the utmost teaching, so that it may be compared to that of the two sovereigns"] Leigong wishes his classic methods to be clearly set out and to reach Shennong, so that later generations seeing them would suppose they were teachings practiced by the two sovereigns together.
Song editors: The Quan Yuanqi edition and the *Taisu* have 擬 for 疑.

## Notes

Leigong describes his stage of learning and asks to receive the teaching on the measures of heaven and related matters.
