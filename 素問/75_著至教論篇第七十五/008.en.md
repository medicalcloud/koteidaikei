# 素問 著至教論篇第七十五 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong said, "The three yang cannot be withstood; may I hear the explanation?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The three yang cannot be withstood; may I hear the explanation"] "Cannot be withstood" means the qi combine and arrive and cannot be withstood.

## Notes

Leigong asks for an explanation of the three yang.
