# 素問 著至教論篇第七十五 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. Do not lose it. All these are the mutual conveyance and response of yin and yang, exterior and interior, above and below, female and male. The Way, knowing astronomy above, geography below, and human affairs in between, can last long; teaching it to the multitudes, there will be no doubt or danger. Treatises discussing the way of medicine can be transmitted to later generations and treasured."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Treatises discussing the way of medicine can be transmitted to later generations and treasured"] Because they are clearly set out.

## Notes

The Emperor says that the way of medicine is knowing astronomy, geography and human affairs.
