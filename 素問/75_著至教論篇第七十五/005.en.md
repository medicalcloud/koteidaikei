# 素問 著至教論篇第七十五 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Have you not heard of the *Yinyang zhuan*?"

## Notes

The Emperor asks whether he knows the *Yinyang zhuan*. There is no note by Wang Bing.
