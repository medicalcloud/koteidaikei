# 素問 著至教論篇第七十五 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong said, "Your words on yang I cannot distinguish; your words on yin I cannot put in order. I ask to rise and receive the explanation, to take it as the utmost Way."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Leigong said"] Song editors: According to [our examination], the Quan Yuanqi edition makes the text from here to the end of the chapter a separate chapter named *Fang shengshuai*.

[On "your words on yin I cannot put in order. I ask to rise and receive the explanation, to take it as the utmost Way"] The Emperor has not yet granted that he knows deeply, so he asks again.

## Notes

Leigong again asks for an explanation.
