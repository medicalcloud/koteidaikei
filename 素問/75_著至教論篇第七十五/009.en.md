# 素問 著至教論篇第七十五 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "When the three yang arrive alone, it is the three yang arriving together. Arriving together, they are like wind and rain: above they cause disease of the vertex, below leaking disease. Outside there is nothing to expect, inside nothing proper; they do not fit the regular network, and diagnosis has no above and below. Distinguish them by the writings."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Arriving together, they are like wind and rain: above they cause disease of the vertex, below leaking disease"] Arriving together means the qi of the three yang of hand and of foot combine and arrive. The foot greater yang vessel starts at the inner canthus, rises across the forehead, and crosses at the vertex; its branch goes from the vertex to the upper corner of the ear; its straight course enters from the vertex to network the brain, comes back out and descends the nape separately, follows the inside of the shoulder blade, flanks the spine to the lower back, enters along the paravertebral muscles, networks the kidneys and belongs to the bladder. The hand greater yang vessel starts at the hand, follows the arm upward, crosses on the shoulder, enters the supraclavicular fossa, networks the heart, follows the throat down through the diaphragm to the stomach, and belongs to the small intestine. Hence disease of the vertex above and leaking disease below. Leaking means blood and pus coming out. "Like wind and rain" means there is no constant standard; hence the text below says [what follows].
Song editors: According to Yang Shangshan, "Leaking disease means the bladder leaks, with frequent stool and urine that cannot be held."

[On "Outside there is nothing to expect ... Distinguish them by the writings"] When the three yang arrive together, above and below have no constancy; outside there is no color or qi to expect, inside no proper channel constancy; at the time of arrival none fits the regular network of the channels, and the patterns of illness again have no constancy above and below. One should record and weigh them in writing in order to distinguish them.

## Notes

The Emperor describes illness when the three yang arrive together.
- 𦛗 in 循𦛗 in Wang Bing's note is read as 膂 ("paravertebral muscles").
