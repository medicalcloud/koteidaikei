# 素問 著至教論篇第七十五 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong said, "I ask to receive the Way, to recite it and understand it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I ask to receive the Way, to recite it and understand it"] 誦 also means instructing; 諷諭 is a way of making comparisons close and pertinent so that one understands.

## Notes

Leigong asks to be taught and given explanations.
