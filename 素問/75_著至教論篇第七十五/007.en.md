# 素問 著至教論篇第七十五 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

[The Emperor] said, "The three yang take heaven as their work; above and below they have no constancy; when they combine, illness arrives, harming yin and yang one-sidedly."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The three yang take heaven as their work"] "Take heaven as their work" means the three yang qi move and reside in the upper part of the human body. The *Yinyang zhuan* is the name of a book of high antiquity.
Song editors: The *Taisu* has 太 for 天.

[On "above and below they have no constancy; when they combine, illness arrives, harming yin and yang one-sidedly"] "No constancy above and below" means the qi go astray and flow without being fixed above or below. "When they combine, illness arrives" means the three yang qi of hand and foot combine and arrive as illness. When yang combines and arrives, the essential qi becomes faint, so the functions of yin and yang are one-sidedly damaged.

## Notes

The Emperor says that the three yang qi combine to cause illness.
