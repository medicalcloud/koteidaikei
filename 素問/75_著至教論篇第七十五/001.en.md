# 素問 著至教論篇第七十五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor sat in the Bright Hall, summoned Leigong and asked him, "Do you know the way of medicine?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "summoned Leigong and asked him, 'Do you know the way of medicine?'"] The Bright Hall is the palace from which government is issued. It has eight windows and four gates, is round above and square below, and stands south of the capital; hence it is called the Bright Hall. To seek out the people's ailments and pity their hidden suffering is how the great sage uses his mind; hence he summoned Leigong and asked about the way of rescuing living beings.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter: the Yellow Emperor asks Leigong whether he knows the way of medicine.
