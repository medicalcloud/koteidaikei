# 素問 著至教論篇第七十五 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "If you receive the transmission but do not know how to fit it to the utmost Way, you will confuse your teacher's teaching. Let me tell you the essentials of the utmost Way. When illness harms the five viscera and the sinews and bones waste away, if what you say is unclear and undistinguished, the learning of the world's rulers is at an end. When the kidneys are about to be cut off, there is sorrowful [decline] as at dusk; [the qi] lingers and does not come out, and human affairs are not vigorous."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If you receive the transmission ... Let me tell you the essentials of the utmost Way"] Not knowing the essentials, one scatters without end. When later generations learn from one another, far removed from the sages, and learners each hold their own methods to be right, they confuse and disorder the meaning of their teachers' teaching.

[On "When illness harms the five viscera ... the learning of the world's rulers is at an end"] If even deep and grave illnesses cannot be clearly distinguished, how could light and slight ones be opened and cured? Could one now know them all? Thus, through this unclarity, the way of the learning and teaching of the world's rulers ends here.

[On "When the kidneys are about to be cut off ... human affairs are not vigorous"] He cites the viscus easiest to know. When the kidney pulse is about to be cut off, the heart spirit is consumed within, and the sinews, bones, vessels and flesh ache and feel empty at evening. 暮 means evening. If, of this kind, the qi of all the viscera are scant and do not come out, human affairs wither and weaken and are no longer vigorous and abundant. The reason is insufficiency of the kidneys, not injury.
Song editors: The *Taisu* has "when the kidneys are about to be cut off, death; death at dusk".

## Notes

As the essentials of the utmost Way, the Emperor speaks of illness of the five viscera and signs of the kidneys being cut off. The chapter ends here.
