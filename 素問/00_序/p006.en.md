# 素問 序 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Gao Baoheng: Gentleman for Court Service, Acting Erudite of the Directorate of Education, Co-collator of Medical Books, Supreme Commandant of Cavalry, granted the scarlet fish pouch.

## Notes

The titles and name of a collator. There are no notes.
