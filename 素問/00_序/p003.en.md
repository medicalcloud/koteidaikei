# 素問 序 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Composed by Wang Bing, Master Qixuan.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Composed by Wang Bing, Master Qixuan"] Song editors: According to the *Tang renwu zhi*, Bing served the Tang as Director of the Imperial Stud and died of old age at over eighty.

## Notes

The signature of Wang Bing's preface.
