# 素問 序 §7 (English translation)

Sun Qi: Gentleman for Court Service, Acting Director of the State Farms Bureau of the Department of State Affairs, Co-collator of Medical Books, Commandant of Cavalry, granted the scarlet fish pouch.

The titles and name of a collator. There are no notes.
