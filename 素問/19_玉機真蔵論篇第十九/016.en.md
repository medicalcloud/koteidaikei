# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When its qi comes hair-like, hard in the centre and empty on both sides, this is called excess, and the illness is outside; when its qi comes hair-like and faint, this is called insufficiency, and the illness is in the centre."

## Notes

Excess and insufficiency of the autumn pulse. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- Daizhige (*Huangdi neijing suwen*) writes 旁 for 傍.
