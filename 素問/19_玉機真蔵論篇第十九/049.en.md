# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §49 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When thin gruel enters the stomach and the outpouring diarrhoea stops, those with depletion survive; when the body sweats and there is free evacuation, those with repletion survive. These are the signs."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole passage] Quan [Yuanqi]'s note: If drink and gruel can enter the stomach, the stomach qi is harmonized, the diarrhoea gradually stops, the stomach qi becomes replete, and those with depletion can survive. Those with repletion, obtaining sweat, open outwardly, and then obtaining free evacuation, they become regulated and balanced of themselves.

## Notes

Even with the five depletions one survives if gruel enters and diarrhoea stops; even with the five repletions one survives if there is sweating and free evacuation. This ends the chapter.
- Wang Bing cites Quan Yuanqi's note.
