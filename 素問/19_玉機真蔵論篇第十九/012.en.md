# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Excess makes a person's body hot and skin painful, with spreading sores; insufficiency makes a person vexed at heart, with coughing and spitting above and leaking of qi below."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole passage] The heart's lesser yin vessel starts in the heart, emerges to belong to the heart connector, descends through the diaphragm to connect with the small intestine, and also goes back from the heart connector up to the lung. Therefore when the heart is excessive, the body is hot and the skin painful, with sores spreading over the body; when insufficient, there is vexation of the heart, coughing and spitting above, and leaking of qi below.

## Notes

Excess of the summer pulse brings fever, painful skin and spreading sores; insufficiency brings vexation, coughing and spitting, and leaking of qi.
