# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. The summer pulse is like a hook; what makes it hook-like?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks what it means for the summer pulse to be hooked. There is no note by Wang Bing.
