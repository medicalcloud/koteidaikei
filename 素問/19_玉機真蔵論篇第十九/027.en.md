# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The spleen pulse is earth; it is the solitary organ that irrigates the four sides."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] It takes in water and grain, transforms them into fluids, and irrigates the liver, heart, lung and kidney. Because it does not properly govern one of the four seasons, it is called the solitary organ.

## Notes

The spleen is earth, the solitary organ that governs no particular season but nourishes the organs on all four sides.
- Daizhige (*Huangdi neijing suwen*) writes 旁 for 傍.
