# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi said: "Why is it said that when the true zang appears there is death?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks why the appearance of the true-zang pulse means death. There is no note by Wang Bing.
