# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the great bones are withered, the great flesh sunken, the chest full of qi, the breathing laboured and difficult, and the qi moves the body, death is expected in six months; when the true-zang pulse appears, then give the day. When the great bones are withered, the great flesh sunken, the chest full of qi, the breathing laboured and difficult, with internal pain pulling to the shoulders and nape, death is expected in one month; when the true zang appears, then give the day. When the great bones are withered, the great flesh sunken, the chest full of qi, the breathing laboured and difficult, with internal pain pulling to the shoulders and nape, the body hot, the flesh wasted and the muscle masses broken, and the true zang appears, death comes within ten months. When the great bones are withered, the great flesh sunken, the marrow of the shoulders wasted within, activity ever weaker, even if the true zang has not yet appeared [base: "comes and appears"; following the Song editors], death is expected in one year; when its true zang appears, then give the day. When the great bones are withered, the great flesh sunken, the chest full of qi, pain in the abdomen, discomfort in the heart, the shoulders, nape and body hot, the muscle masses broken and flesh wasted, the eye sockets sunken, and the true zang appears, if the eyes do not see people, death is immediate; if they still see people, death comes at the time of what it cannot overcome. When sudden depletion comes abruptly upon the body, the five zang are cut off and closed, the paths of the vessels do not open and the qi does not come and go; it is like falling or drowning, and no term can be set. When the pulse is cut off and does not come, or when it comes five or six times per breath, even if the body and flesh are not wasted and the true zang does not appear, death still follows. When the true liver pulse arrives, it is tense inside and out, like running the hand along a knife blade, sharp and taut, like pressing the string of a zither; the colour is green-white without lustre, the hair breaks, and then one dies. When the true heart pulse arrives, it is hard and beating, like running the hand over Job's-tears seeds, string upon string; the colour is red-black without lustre, the hair breaks, and then one dies. When the true lung pulse arrives, it is large and empty, like feathers touching a person's skin; the colour is white-red without lustre, the hair breaks, and then one dies. When the true kidney pulse arrives, it beats and is cut off, like flicking a stone with the finger, snapping; the colour is black-yellow without lustre, the hair breaks, and then one dies. When the true spleen pulse arrives, it is weak, now rapid and now sparse; the colour is yellow-green without lustre, the hair breaks, and then one dies. All in whom the true-zang pulses appear die and cannot be treated."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the great bones are withered ... when the true-zang pulse appears, then give the day"] The skin is dry and clings to the bones and the flesh between the bones is sunken; this is "the great bones withered, the great flesh sunken". The places attached to the bones and hollow places are of the same kind. Fullness of qi in the chest with laboured breathing means the lung has lost its mastery; the lung governs regulation and breathing depends on it. "The qi moves the body" means that because the qi does not connect, one raises the shoulders and back to seek qi from afar. In such cases the storehouses of form are already ruined and the storehouses of spirit are also harmed. Those with these signs die within 180 days. One waits for the true-zang pulse to appear and then gives the day of death. The diagnosis of the true-zang pulses is set out in the text below. This is [the death of] the lung.

[On "with internal pain pulling to the shoulders and nape, death is expected in one month ..."] The essence of fire goes out, the yang qi burns upward, and metal suffers the calamity of fire; therefore there is internal pain [pulling to] the shoulders and nape. Such patients die within thirty days. This is the heart.

[On "the body hot, the flesh wasted and the muscle masses broken ... death comes within ten months"] The yin qi is faint and weak and the yang qi burns within; therefore the body is hot. The muscle masses are the tips of the flesh, and the spleen governs the flesh; therefore the flesh seems wasted away and the muscle masses broken. Those with these signs die within 300 days. 䐃 means the lumpy flesh behind the elbows and knees. This is the spleen.

[On "the marrow of the shoulders wasted within ... when its true zang appears, then give the day"] "The marrow of the shoulders wasted within" means the supraclavicular fossae become deep; "activity weaker" means sexual capacity gradually diminishes. Because the other zang are still intact, death comes within 365 days. This is the kidney.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Jiayi jing* read "the true zang has not yet appeared"; 来 should be 未, a scribal error.

[On "pain in the abdomen, discomfort in the heart ... death comes at the time of what it cannot overcome"] Wood generates its fire and the liver qi communicates with the heart; [the liver] vessel reaches the lower abdomen, rises to spread over the flanks and ribs, follows behind the throat and enters the nasopharynx; therefore there is abdominal pain, discomfort in the heart, heat in the shoulders, nape and body, broken muscle masses and wasted flesh. The liver governs the eyes; therefore the eye sockets are sunken, and if one does not see people, death is immediate. "The time of what it cannot overcome" means the months of *geng* and *xin*. This is the liver.

[On "When sudden depletion comes abruptly ... no term can be set"] When the five zang shift and pass to what they cannot overcome, one can wait for the true-zang pulse to appear and then give the day of death. When depleting evil suddenly strikes within the body, the five zang are cut off and closed, the vessels do not open, and the qi does not come and go — like falling or drowning — and no day of death can be given.

[On "When the pulse is cut off ... death still follows"] This is the pulse of sudden depletion arriving abruptly.
Song editors: How could a pulse of five or six beats per breath mean death? The character 息 "breath" must be wrong; it should be 呼 "exhalation", and then it is right.

[On "All in whom the true-zang pulses appear die and cannot be treated"] Song editors: Yang Shangshan says: "It is called 'true' because nothing else is mixed with it. The qi of the five zang are all harmonized by the stomach qi and cannot function alone. What is utterly hard cannot function alone; functioning alone it breaks; harmonized and softened it is firm. When the qi of the five zang is harmonized with the stomach qi, one obtains long life; if the true [zang] appears alone, one must die. If you wish to know that the appearance of the true zang means death and harmony with the stomach means life, diagnose at the *cunkou*. If what appears is like a string, it is the liver pulse; slightly wiry is balanced. Slightly wiry means two parts stomach qi and one part wiry qi moving together; when all three parts are wiry without stomach qi, it is called the appearance of the true zang. The other four zang follow this."

## Notes

The times of death when signs of severe wasting (great bones withered, great flesh sunken, etc.) and the true-zang pulse appear are given in the order lung, heart, spleen, kidney, liver; with sudden depletion no term can be set. Finally the forms of the true-zang pulse of each organ and the signs of death are listed. Huangdi's speech, begun in unit 036, ends here.
- Wang Bing takes each section as corresponding to the death of the lung, heart, spleen, kidney and liver in turn.
- The Song editors take 来 in 真蔵来見 as an error for 未 "not yet", and 息 in 一息五六至 as an error for 呼.
- Yang Shangshan, cited by the Song editors, explains the relation of stomach qi and the true-zang pulse (slightly wiry = two parts stomach qi, one part wiry).
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 目眶 for 目匡 and 胭 for 䐃.
