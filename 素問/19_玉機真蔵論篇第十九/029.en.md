# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The good cannot be seen; the bad can be seen."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] It does not properly govern a season but lodges and dominates in the four seasonal ends; therefore the good cannot be seen and the bad can.

## Notes

Because the spleen lodges in the four seasons, its normal pulse does not appear on its own, but its morbid pulse does.
