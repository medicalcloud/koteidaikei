# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §45 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The five repletions mean death; the five depletions mean death."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] The five repletions are excess of the five zang; the five depletions are deficiency of the five zang.

## Notes

When all five zang are in excess (five repletions) or all deficient (five depletions), there is death.
