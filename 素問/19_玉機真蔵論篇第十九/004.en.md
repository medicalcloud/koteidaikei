# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When its qi comes excess and strong, this is called excess, and the illness is outside; when its qi comes not excess and feeble, this is called insufficiency, and the illness is in the center."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] When the qi is in surplus, the illness takes form outside; when the qi is scant, the illness is in the center.
Song editors: Lü Guang says: "Excess and strong means the yang qi is abundant. The lesser yang should be feeble and weak; now it is instead excess and strong, which is called excess. Yang lies in the exterior; therefore the illness is outside. The qi of the *Jueyin* nourishes the sinews and its pulse is wiry; now it is instead deficient and feeble, hence called insufficiency. Yin lies in the center; therefore the illness is within."

## Notes

If the spring pulse is in excess and strong, it is excess with illness outside; if not excess and feeble, insufficiency with illness in the center.
