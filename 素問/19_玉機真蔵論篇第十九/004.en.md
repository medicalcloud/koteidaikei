# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When its qi comes replete and strong, this is called excess, and the illness is outside; when its qi comes not replete and faint, this is called insufficiency, and the illness is in the centre."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] When the qi is in surplus, the illness takes form outside; when the qi is scant, the illness is in the centre.
Song editors: Lü Guang says: "Replete and strong means the yang qi is abundant. The lesser yang should be faint and weak; now it is instead replete and strong, which is called excess. Yang lies in the exterior; therefore the illness is outside. The qi of the *jueyin* nourishes the sinews and its pulse is wiry; now it is instead depleted and faint, hence called insufficiency. Yin lies in the centre; therefore the illness is within."

## Notes

If the spring pulse is replete and strong, it is excess with illness outside; if not replete and faint, insufficiency with illness in the centre.
