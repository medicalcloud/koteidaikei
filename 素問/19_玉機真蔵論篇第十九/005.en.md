# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What are the illnesses of excess and insufficiency of the spring pulse?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks about the illnesses of excess and insufficiency of the spring pulse. There is no note by Wang Bing.
