# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The Emperor said: Good"] Song editors: The passage from "Huangdi asked" to here is in the *Taiyin yangming biaoli* chapter of scroll 4 in Quan Yuanqi's edition; Wang Bing moved it here. We mention this to make clear how much Mr Wang contributed to the *Suwen*.

## Notes

The close of the exchange.
