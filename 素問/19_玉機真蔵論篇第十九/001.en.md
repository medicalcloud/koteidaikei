# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "The spring pulse is like a string; what makes it string-like?"

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on what it means for the spring pulse to be wiry. The following units discuss in turn the normal, excessive and insufficient pulses of the four seasons. There is no note by Wang Bing.
