# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §47 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "An abundant pulse, hot skin, abdominal distension, blockage front and back, and oppressed confusion — these are called the five repletions. A fine pulse, cold skin, scant qi, diarrhoea front and back, and inability to take food and drink — these are called the five depletions."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "An abundant pulse ... these are called the five repletions"] Repletion means the evil qi is abundant and replete. The abundant pulse is the heart, hot skin the lung, abdominal distension the spleen, blockage front and back the kidney, oppressed confusion the liver.

[On "A fine pulse ... these are called the five depletions"] Depletion means the true qi is insufficient. The fine pulse is the heart, cold skin the lung, scant qi the liver, diarrhoea front and back the kidney, inability to take food and drink the spleen.

## Notes

The signs of the five repletions and five depletions; Wang Bing assigns each to one of the five zang.
