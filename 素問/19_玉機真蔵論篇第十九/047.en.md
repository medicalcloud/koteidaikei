# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §47 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "An abundant pulse, hot skin, abdominal distension, blockage front and back, and oppressed confusion — these are called the five repletions. A thready pulse, cold skin, scant qi, diarrhea front and back, and inability to take food and drink — these are called the five depletions."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "An abundant pulse ... these are called the five repletions"] Excess means the pathogenic qi is abundant and excess. The abundant pulse is the heart, hot skin the lung, abdominal distension the spleen, blockage front and back the kidney, oppressed confusion the liver.

[On "A thready pulse ... these are called the five depletions"] Deficiency means the genuine qi is insufficient. The thready pulse is the heart, cold skin the lung, scant qi the liver, diarrhea front and back the kidney, inability to take food and drink the spleen.

## Notes

The signs of the five repletions and five depletions; Wang Bing assigns each to one of the five zang.
