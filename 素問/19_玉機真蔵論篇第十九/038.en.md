# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

But when an illness breaks out suddenly, it need not be treated according to the transmission; or its transmission and transformation may not follow the order. Those that enter out of order are due to worry, fear, grief, joy and anger, which prevent it from following its order; therefore they cause great illness. Thus, with joy and great depletion the kidney qi encroaches; with anger the liver qi encroaches; with grief the lung qi encroaches; with fear the spleen qi encroaches; with worry the heart qi encroaches. This is the way of it. Therefore illness has five [forms], and five times five makes twenty-five changes, extending to their transmissions and transformations. "Transmission" is another name for encroachment.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "But when an illness breaks out suddenly, it need not be treated according to the transmission"] It does not follow the order of transmission; therefore it need not be treated by the transmission.

[On "Those that enter out of order ... they cause great illness"] Worry, fear, grief, joy and anger break out without a constant portion; when touched off they break out; therefore the qi of illness also arises out of order.

[On "with joy and great depletion the kidney qi encroaches"] With joy, the heart qi moves to the lung and the heart qi does not keep guard; therefore the kidney qi encroaches. The *Xuanming wuqi* (chapter 23) says: "When the essential qi combines in the heart, there is joy."

[On "with anger the liver qi encroaches"] With anger the qi counterflows; therefore the liver qi encroaches on the spleen.

[On "with grief the lung qi encroaches"] With grief, the lung qi moves to the liver and the liver qi receives the evil; therefore the lung qi encroaches. The *Xuanming wuqi* says: "When the essential qi combines in the lung, there is grief."

[On "with fear the spleen qi encroaches"] With fear, the kidney qi moves to the heart and the kidney qi does not keep guard; therefore the spleen qi encroaches. The *Xuanming wuqi* says: "When the essential qi combines in the kidney, there is fear."

[On "with worry the heart qi encroaches"] With worry, the liver qi moves to the spleen and the liver qi does not keep guard; therefore the heart qi encroaches. The *Xuanming wuqi* says: "When the essential qi combines in the liver, there is worry."

[On "This is the way of it"] This is the regular way of [illness] out of order.

[On "Therefore illness has five ... extending to their transmissions and transformations"] The five zang combine with one another, each with five; multiplying five by five gives twenty-five changes. Their changes are transmitted according to what overcomes, transmitted out of order, and the changes are manifold.
Song editors: The *Yinyang bielun* (chapter 7) says, "In all, yang has five; five times five makes twenty-five yang"; the meaning is consistent with this.

[On "'Transmission' is another name for encroachment"] What is transmission? It is another name for mutual encroachment.

## Notes

Illness that breaks out suddenly may not follow the controlling order; through the emotions (worry, fear, grief, joy, anger) the qi of another organ encroaches out of order. With five changes for each of the five zang, there are twenty-five changes.
- One of Wang Bing's citations of the *Xuanming wuqi* is written 宜明五気篇 in the base text (an error for 宣).
