# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor rose in alarm, bowed twice, knocked his head to the ground and said: "Good. I have obtained the great key of the pulse. The utmost numbers of the world — by the changes of the five colours and the pulse one measures the extraordinary and the constant; the Way lies in the One. The spirit turns and does not go back; if it goes back it does not turn, and then it loses its mechanism. The key of the utmost numbers is close at hand yet subtle. I shall inscribe it on jade tablets, store it in the treasury, read it every morning, and call it the Jade Mechanism."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The utmost numbers of the world ... the Way lies in the One"] 瞿然 describes haste. It says that if one connects them all by excess and insufficiency, measuring the extraordinary and the constant are all comprehended.

[On "The spirit turns and does not go back ... it loses its mechanism"] The five qi circulating without missing the seasonal order is the spirit qi flowing and turning without going back. If it moves backward, so that decline and dominance go against the constant qi of heaven, it goes back and does not turn; going back and not turning, it loses the mechanism of vital qi.

[On "The key of the utmost numbers is close at hand yet subtle"] If one has the essential way of the utmost numbers, its application is close at hand and subtle. 迫 means close.

[On "I shall inscribe it on jade tablets ... call it the Jade Mechanism"] It is inscribed on jade tablets, hence the name; it says this is the mechanism of vital qi of the jade tablets.
Song editors: From "the utmost numbers" to "call it the Jade Mechanism" overlaps with the text of the *Yuban lunyao* above; the note there is quite detailed.

## Notes

Having heard Qibo, Huangdi says he has obtained the key of the pulse, inscribes it on jade tablets to read every morning and names it the Jade Mechanism. The chapter title comes from this.
- According to the Song editors, this passage overlaps with the *Yuban lunyao* (chapter 15).
