# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When it comes like flowing water, this is called excess, and the illness is outside; when it is like a bird's beak, this is called insufficiency, and the illness is in the centre."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "when it is like a bird's beak ... in the centre"] Song editors: The *Pingren qixiang lun* (chapter 18) has "like a bird's beak"; another edition writes 啄 "pecking" for 喙.

## Notes

Excess and insufficiency of the spleen pulse.
- Daizhige (*Chongguang buzhu Huangdi neijing suwen*) writes 烏 "crow" for 鳥.
