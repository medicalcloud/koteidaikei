# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Excess makes a person languid, with pain in the vessels of the spine, short of breath and unwilling to speak; insufficiency makes a person's heart feel suspended as if suffering hunger, with cold in the soft flanks, pain in the spine, fullness of the lower abdomen and changes in the urine."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Excess makes a person languid"] Song editors: The meaning of 解㑊 is given in the note in scroll 5.

[On "insufficiency makes a person's heart feel suspended ... changes in the urine"] The kidney's lesser yin vessel pierces the spine from the back inner edge of the thigh, belongs to the kidney and connects with the bladder; its straight course rises from the kidney through the liver and diaphragm into the lung, follows the throat and runs alongside the root of the tongue; its branch emerges from the lung, connects with the heart and pours into the chest. Therefore the illness is like this. 䏚 is the empty, soft place below the lower ribs on either side of the spine; the outside of the kidney corresponds to it; therefore it is cold.

## Notes

Excess of the winter pulse brings languor, spinal pain and shortness of breath; insufficiency brings a hungry, suspended feeling in the heart, cold in the lumbar region, spinal pain, fullness of the lower abdomen and changes in urination.
- Wikisource (the plain text) writes 眇 for 䏚.
