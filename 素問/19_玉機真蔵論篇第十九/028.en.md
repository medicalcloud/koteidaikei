# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Then can the good and bad of the spleen be seen?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks whether the good and bad states of the spleen pulse can be seen. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 然而 for 然則.
