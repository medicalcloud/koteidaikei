# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi said: "The five zang communicate with one another, and their shifts all have an order. When the five zang are ill, each transmits it to what it overcomes. If untreated, as a rule in three months or six months, or in three days or six days, it is transmitted through the five zang and death should come; this is the order of transmission in compliance to what is overcome. Therefore it is said: one who distinguishes the yang knows where the illness comes from; one who distinguishes the yin knows the time of death or life. This means knowing that one dies on reaching what afflicts one.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The five zang communicate with one another ... each transmits it to what it overcomes"] Since the text above concerns death by transmission in counterflow, it says this is the order of transmission in counterflow to what is overcome.
Song editors: "Counterflow" in "the order of transmission in counterflow to what is overcome" should be "compliance". The text above already spoke of transmission in counterflow; what the text below speaks of is the order of transmission in compliance.

[On "If untreated ... this is the order of transmission in compliance to what is overcome"] Three months means the shift of the qi of one zang; six months means reaching the position of what it overcomes. Three days is the number of the three yang matched to days; six days counts the three yin as well. The *Relun* (chapter 31) says, "In cold damage, on the first day the greater yang receives it, on the second the *yangming*, on the third the lesser yang, on the fourth the greater yin, on the fifth the lesser yin, on the sixth the *jueyin*" — this is the meaning.
Song editors: The seven characters 是順伝所勝之次 in the text above belong after the preceding note and have mistakenly been placed below this classic text; not only do they make no sense, but on comparison neither Quan Yuanqi's *Suwen* nor the *Jiayi jing* has them. We wished to remove them directly but, fearing to cause doubt among those who have not understood, we now preserve them in the note.

[On "Therefore it is said: one who distinguishes the yang ... the time of death or life"] If one concentrates on distinguishing the signs of the three yin and three yang, one knows what the qi of wind evil cannot overcome. Hence it says below:
Song editors: In the old edition the note of this passage had been copied as classic text; we have restored it as note. Further, the *Yinyang bielun* (chapter 7) says, "One who distinguishes the yang knows where the illness is; one who distinguishes the yin knows the time of death or life," and also, "One who distinguishes the yang knows the times the illness must avoid; one who distinguishes the yin knows the time of death or life"; the meaning is the same as here.

[On "This means knowing that one dies on reaching what afflicts one"] "Afflicts" means reaching what it cannot overcome. The text above says, "It dies at what it cannot overcome."

## Notes

Huangdi says illness of the five zang is transmitted in the controlling order, and if untreated death comes within set periods. Huangdi's speech continues to unit 039.
- The Song editors say "counterflow" in Wang Bing's note should be "compliance", and that the seven characters 是順伝所勝之次 in the classic are a misplaced note absent from Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Jiayi jing*.
- Wang Bing cites the six-day progression of cold damage in the *Relun* (chapter 31).
