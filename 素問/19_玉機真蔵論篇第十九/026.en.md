# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "In the order of the four seasons there are variations of counterflow and compliance; but what alone does the spleen pulse govern?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the order of the four seasons there are variations of counterflow and compliance"] The pulse is wiry in spring, hooked in summer, floating in autumn and camp-like in winter; these show different forms as variations of counterflow and compliance.

[On "but what alone does the spleen pulse govern?"] "Govern" means governing seasons and months.

## Notes

The seasonal pulses have been assigned to the liver, heart, lung and kidney; Huangdi asks which season the spleen pulse governs.
