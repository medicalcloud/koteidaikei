# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The summer pulse is the heart, the south, fire, by which the myriad things grow abundantly. Therefore its qi comes abundant and goes declining; hence it is called hook-like. What goes against this is illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Therefore its qi comes abundant and goes declining; hence it is called hook-like"] It means the pulse comes abundant and goes declining, like the curve of a hook.
Song editors: Yueren says: "The summer pulse is hooked; it is the south, fire. The myriad things are abundant, hanging their branches and spreading their leaves, all bent down like hooks; therefore its pulse comes fast and goes slow." Lü Guang says: "Because yang is abundant it comes fast; because yin is depleted it goes slow. The pulse rises from below to the *cunkou* fast and returns to the *chi* slowly."

## Notes

The summer pulse corresponds to the heart, the south and fire; it comes abundant and goes declining, hence called hooked.
