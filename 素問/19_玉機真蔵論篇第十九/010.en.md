# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When its qi comes abundant and goes also abundant, this is called excess, and the illness is outside; when its qi comes not abundant and goes instead abundant, this is called insufficiency, and the illness is in the centre."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When its qi comes abundant and goes also abundant ... outside"] The pulse coming and going abundant is abundance of yang; the heart qi is in surplus; this is excess.

[On "when its qi comes not abundant ... in the centre"] Song editors: For the pulses of the four zang liver, heart, lung and kidney, Yueren always takes strong and replete as excess and depleted and faint as insufficiency, which differs from the *Suwen*.

## Notes

Excess and insufficiency of the summer pulse.
- The Song editors point out that the *Nanjing* (Yueren) uses different criteria for excess and insufficiency.
