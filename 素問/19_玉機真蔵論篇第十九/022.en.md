# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When its qi comes like flicking a stone, this is called excess, and the illness is outside; when it goes as if rapid, this is called insufficiency, and the illness is in the centre."

## Notes

Excess and insufficiency of the winter pulse. There is no note by Wang Bing.
