# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Excess makes a person's four limbs unable to be raised; insufficiency makes a person's nine orifices not open; this is called double stiffness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Excess makes a person's four limbs unable to be raised"] Because [the spleen] governs the four limbs, when it is ill they cannot be raised.

[On "insufficiency makes a person's nine orifices not open; this is called double stiffness"] The solitary organ of the spleen irrigates the four sides; now when it is ill the five zang are not in harmony; therefore the nine orifices do not open. The *Eighty-one Difficulties* says: "When the five zang are not in harmony, the nine orifices do not open." 重 means the qi of the zang piled up; 強 means the qi not harmonious and compliant.

## Notes

Excess of the spleen makes the limbs unable to be raised; insufficiency closes the nine orifices ("double stiffness").
