# 素問 玉機真蔵論篇第十九 §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi said: "I have heard that life and death are decided by depletion and repletion. I would like to hear the facts of it."

## Notes

Huangdi asks how life and death are decided by depletion and repletion. There is no note by Wang Bing.
