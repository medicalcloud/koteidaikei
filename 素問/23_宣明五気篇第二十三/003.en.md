# 素問 宣明五気篇第二十三 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where the five essences combine: when essential qi combines in the heart there is joy; when it combines in the lung, sorrow; in the liver, worry; in the spleen, dread; in the kidney, fear. These are called the five combinations; they are combinations that occur through depletion.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "when essential qi combines in the heart there is joy"] Essential qi means the essential qi of fire. When the lung is depleted and the essence of the heart combines in it, there is joy. The *Lingshu jing* says, "Joy and pleasure without limit harm the *po*." The *po* is the spirit of the lung. This shows heart-fire combining in lung-metal.

[On "when it combines in the lung, sorrow"] When the liver is depleted and the lung qi combines in it, there is sorrow. The *Lingshu jing* says, "Sorrow and grief that move the interior harm the *hun*." The *hun* is the spirit of the liver. This shows lung-metal combining in liver-wood.

[On "in the liver, worry"] When the spleen is depleted and the liver qi combines in it, there is worry. The *Lingshu jing* says, "Anxiety and worry that are not resolved harm the intention." The intention is the spirit of the spleen. This shows liver-wood combining in spleen-earth.

[On "in the spleen, dread"] One classic says "hunger". When the kidney is depleted and the spleen qi combines in it, there is dread. 畏 means dread. The *Lingshu jing* says, "Fear and dread that are not resolved harm the essence." The essence is the spirit of the kidney. This shows spleen-earth combining in kidney-water.

[On "in the kidney, fear"] When the heart is depleted and the kidney qi combines in it, there is fear. The *Lingshu jing* says, "Apprehension and anxious thought harm the spirit." The spirit is the master of the heart. This shows kidney-water combining in heart-fire. 怵惕 means fright and dread. ## Notes

When a zang is depleted, the essential qi of the zang that overcomes it combines in it, and emotional changes appear. Wang Bing takes the combining essential qi to be not that of the zang named but that of the zang which overcomes the depleted one (e.g., "combines in the heart" means the heart's essence combining in the depleted lung).
- Phrases such as 明心火并於肺金也 in Wang Bing's note are read as "this shows heart-fire combining in lung-metal"; reading 神明 together is also possible.
- The *Lingshu jing* passages quoted by Wang Bing correspond to the *Benshen* chapter of the *Lingshu*.
- In the note on 怵惕, 神為心主明 may be an error for 神為心神明 judging from the pattern of the other phrases, but this has not been checked.
