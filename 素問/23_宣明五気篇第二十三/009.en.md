# 素問 宣明五気篇第二十三 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where the five evils appear: in spring one obtains an autumn pulse, in summer a winter pulse, in late summer a spring pulse, in autumn a summer pulse, in winter a late-summer pulse. This is called "yin coming out and going to yang"; the illness is prone to anger and is incurable. These are called the five evils; all share the same fate: death, incurable.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Where the five evils appear ... death, incurable"] Song editors: "Yin coming out and going to yang, the illness is prone to anger" has already appeared in the previous item. Saying it again here does not fit the sense of the text; it must be a displacement of slips in the ancient text.

## Notes

When the pulse of the season that overcomes the current season appears, this is called the five evils and is a sign of death.
- The Song editors regard "This is called 'yin coming out and going to yang'; the illness is prone to anger" as displaced from the previous item (unit 008).
- There is no note by Wang Bing.
