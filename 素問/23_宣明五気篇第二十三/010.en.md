# 素問 宣明五気篇第二十三 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What the five zang store: the heart stores the spirit, the lung stores the *po*, the liver stores the *hun*, the spleen stores the intention, the kidney stores the will. These are called what the five zang store.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the heart stores the spirit"] It is formed by the transformation of essential qi. The *Lingshu jing* says, "When two essences press together, it is called spirit."

[On "the lung stores the *po*"] It is the aide of essential qi. The *Lingshu jing* says, "What goes in and out alongside the essence is called the *po*."

[On "the liver stores the *hun*"] It is the assistant of the spirit qi. The *Lingshu jing* says, "What comes and goes following the spirit is called the *hun*."

[On "the spleen stores the intention"] It is what records and does not forget. The *Lingshu jing* says, "When the heart has something it recalls, it is called intention."

[On "the kidney stores the will"] It is what concentrates the intention without shifting. The *Lingshu jing* says, "Where the intention abides is called the will." The kidney receives the essence of the five zang and six fu; it is the root of the original qi, the basis of generation and completion, and the gate of the stomach. Therefore when the will is capable, life flows.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says, "There are two kidneys: the left is the kidney, which stores the will; the right is the gate of life, which stores the essence."

## Notes

The mental faculties (the five spirits) stored by each of the five zang. The *Lingshu jing* passages quoted by Wang Bing correspond to the *Benshen* chapter of the *Lingshu*.
