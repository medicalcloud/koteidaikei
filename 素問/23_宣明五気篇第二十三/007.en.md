# 素問 宣明五気篇第二十三 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where the five illnesses arise: yin illness arises in the bones, yang illness arises in the blood, yin illness arises in the flesh, yang illness arises in winter, yin illness arises in summer. These are called the five arisings.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "yin illness arises in the bones, yang illness arises in the blood, yin illness arises in the flesh"] The bones and flesh are yin and still; hence yang qi follows them. The blood vessels are yang and moving; hence yin qi rides on them.

[On "yang illness arises in winter, yin illness arises in summer"] In summer yang qi is abundant; hence yin illness arises in summer. In winter yin qi is abundant; hence yang illness arises in winter. Each follows where it is scant.

## Notes

The places and seasons in which yin and yang illnesses arise.
