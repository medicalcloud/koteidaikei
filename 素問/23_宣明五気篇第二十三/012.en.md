# 素問 宣明五気篇第二十三 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What the five strains harm: prolonged looking harms the blood, prolonged lying harms the qi, prolonged sitting harms the flesh, prolonged standing harms the bones, prolonged walking harms the sinews. These are called what the five strains harm.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "prolonged looking harms the blood"] It strains the heart.

[On "prolonged lying harms the qi"] It strains the lung.

[On "prolonged sitting harms the flesh"] It strains the spleen.

[On "prolonged standing harms the bones"] It strains the kidney.

[On "prolonged walking harms the sinews"] It strains the liver.

## Notes

Five kinds of damage from prolonged postures or activities.
