# 素問 宣明五気篇第二十三 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What the five zang dislike: the heart dislikes heat, the lung dislikes cold, the liver dislikes wind, the spleen dislikes damp, the kidney dislikes dryness. These are called the five dislikes.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the heart dislikes heat"] With heat the vessels break down and become turbid.

[On "the lung dislikes cold"] With cold the qi lingers and stagnates.

[On "the liver dislikes wind"] With wind the sinews become dry and tense.

[On "the spleen dislikes damp"] With damp the flesh wastes and swells.

[On "the kidney dislikes dryness"] With dryness the essence is exhausted and dried up.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says, "Elsewhere it says 'the lung dislikes dryness'. Here it says the lung dislikes cold and the kidney dislikes dryness because dryness, in autumn, is the beginning of cold, and cold, in winter, is the end of dryness. The lung belongs to autumn and dislikes cold intensely, so its end is mentioned; the kidney belongs to winter and its dislike is not intense, so its beginning is mentioned."

## Notes

The climatic influences each of the five zang dislikes.
- 筯 in Wang Bing's note is a variant of 筋.
- The view of Yang Shangshan quoted by the Song editors corresponds to his commentary on the five aversions in the *Taisu* (燥在於秋，寒之始也；寒在於冬，燥之終也, etc.).
