# 素問 宣明五気篇第二十三 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The five pulses corresponding to images: the liver pulse is stringlike, the heart pulse hooked, the spleen pulse intermittent, the lung pulse hairlike, the kidney pulse stonelike. These are called the pulses of the five zang.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the liver pulse is stringlike"] Soft, empty and slippery, straight and long.

[On "the heart pulse hooked"] Like a hook laid down: abundant when coming, weakening when going.

[On "the spleen pulse intermittent"] Soft and weak.

[On "the lung pulse hairlike"] Light, floating and empty, like feathers.

[On "the kidney pulse stonelike"] Sunken, firm and beating, like a thrown stone.

## Notes

The images of the pulses of the five zang; this ends the chapter.
- Daizhige (黄帝内经素问) has 巻第七 at the end of the chapter; this marks the juan division in that source.
