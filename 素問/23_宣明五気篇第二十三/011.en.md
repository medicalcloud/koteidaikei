# 素問 宣明五気篇第二十三 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What the five zang govern: the heart governs the vessels, the lung governs the skin, the liver governs the sinews, the spleen governs the flesh, the kidney governs the bones. These are called the five governances.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the heart governs the vessels"] They contain the nutritive qi and move in accordance with the breath.

[On "the lung governs the skin"] It wraps the sinews and flesh and repels all evils.

[On "the liver governs the sinews"] They bind the joints and move following the spirit.

[On "the spleen governs the flesh"] It covers and conceals the sinews and bones and lets the defensive qi pass.

[On "the kidney governs the bones"] They stretch the sinews, transform the marrow and form the trunk that keeps the body upright.

## Notes

The bodily tissues governed by each of the five zang.
