# 素問 宣明五気篇第二十三 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where the five evils cause disorder: when evil enters yang there is mania; when evil enters yin there is impediment. When it strikes yang there is illness of the vertex; when it strikes yin there is loss of voice. When yang enters and goes to yin there is quiet; when yin comes out and goes to yang there is anger. These are called the five disorders.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "when evil enters yang there is mania; when evil enters yin there is impediment"] When evil resides in the yang vessels, the four limbs become hot and abundant; hence mania. When evil enters the yin vessels, the six channels congeal and do not flow; hence impediment.

[On "When it strikes yang there is illness of the vertex"] When evil strikes yang within, the flow of the vessels becomes pressing and fast; hence illness above at the vertex.

[On "when it strikes yin there is loss of voice"] When evil strikes yin within, the vessels do not flow; hence loss of voice and inability to speak.
Song editors: The *Nanjing* says, "Double yang is mania, double yin is epilepsy." Chao Yuanfang says, "When evil enters yin there is epilepsy." The *Maijing* says, "When yin attaches to yang there is mania; when yang attaches to yin there is epilepsy." Sun Simiao says, "When evil enters yang there is mania; when evil enters yin there is blood impediment. When evil enters yang and is transmitted, there is epilepsy and convulsions; when evil enters yin and is transmitted, there is pain and loss of voice." Quan Yuanqi says, "When evil has entered yin and is transmitted again to yang and the evil qi is abundant, the fu and zang receive the evil, their qi does not pay homage, and the nutritive qi no longer circulates through the body; the evil and upright qi strike each other and break out as epilepsy. When evil has entered yang and the yang is now transmitted again to yin, the zang and fu receive the evil; hence one cannot speak. This is [the evil] overcoming the upright." The views of the various authorities differ; all are recorded here.

[On "When yang enters and goes to yin there is quiet; when yin comes out and goes to yang there is anger"] Illness arises according to where it goes. 之 means go.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi says, "When yang enters yin there is quiet; when it comes out there is fear." The *Qianjin fang* says, "When yang enters yin the illness is quiet; when yin comes out to yang the illness is anger."

## Notes

The pathology when evil enters yin or yang.
- "Illness of the vertex" (巔疾): Wang Bing takes it as illness of the top of the head; as in the works cited by the Song editors, it is also read as 癲 (epilepsy-like illness).
