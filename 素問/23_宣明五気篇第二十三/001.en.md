# 素問 宣明五気篇第二十三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where the five flavours enter: sour enters the liver, acrid enters the lung, bitter enters the heart, salty enters the kidney, sweet enters the spleen. These are called the five entries.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "sour enters the liver"] The liver corresponds to wood, and its flavour is sour.

[On "acrid enters the lung"] The lung corresponds to metal, and its flavour is acrid.

[On "bitter enters the heart"] The heart corresponds to fire, and its flavour is bitter.

[On "salty enters the kidney"] The kidney corresponds to water, and its flavour is salty.

[On "sweet enters the spleen"] The spleen corresponds to earth, and its flavour is sweet.
Song editors: The *Taisu* further says, "Bland enters the stomach."

[On "These are called the five entries"] Song editors: The *Zhizhenyao dalun* (chapter 74) says, "When the five flavours enter the stomach, each goes to what it favours: thus sour first enters the liver, bitter first enters the heart, sweet first enters the spleen, acrid first enters the lung, salty first enters the kidney."

## Notes

This chapter lists matters relating to the five zang in the form "the five ...". This section gives the zang that each of the five flavours enters. The chapter contains no dialogue.
