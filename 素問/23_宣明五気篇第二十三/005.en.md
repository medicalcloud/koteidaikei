# 素問 宣明五気篇第二十三 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The fluids transformed by the five zang: the heart produces sweat, the lung nasal mucus, the liver tears, the spleen drool, the kidney spittle. These are called the five fluids.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the heart produces sweat"] It leaks through the skin and interstices.

[On "the lung nasal mucus"] It moistens the nasal openings.

[On "the liver tears"] It pours into the eyes.

[On "the spleen drool"] It overflows at the lips and mouth.

[On "the kidney spittle"] It arises at the teeth.

## Notes

The fluids that each of the five zang transforms and produces.
