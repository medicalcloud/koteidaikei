# 素問 陽明脈解篇第三十 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The *Yangming* governs the flesh, and its vessel is abundant in blood and qi. When pathogen lodges in it there is heat; when the heat is severe one dislikes fire."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The *Yangming* governs the flesh, and its vessel"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 肌 "muscle" for 脈 "vessel".

## Notes

Since the *Yangming* is abundant in blood and qi, when pathogen enters the heat becomes severe and one dislikes fire. There is no note by Wang Bing.
