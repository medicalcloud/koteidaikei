# 素問 陽明脈解篇第三十 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The four limbs are the root of all yang. When yang is abundant the four limbs are replete, and when replete one can climb high places."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The four limbs are the root of all yang ... one can climb high places"] Yang receives qi in the four limbs; therefore the four limbs are the root of all yang.
Song editors: The *Maijie* says, "Yin and yang contend and combine outwardly in yang."

## Notes

When yang becomes abundant the limbs are replete and one can climb high places one normally could not.
