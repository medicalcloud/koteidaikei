# 素問 陽明脈解篇第三十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the reversal and counterflow reach the zang, one dies; when they reach only the channels, one lives."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the reversal and counterflow reach the zang ... one lives"] "Channels" means the channel vessels; "zang" means the five spirit-storing zang. One dies when it reaches the zang because the spirit departs.

## Notes

The prognosis depends on whether the reversal reaches the zang or stays in the channels.
