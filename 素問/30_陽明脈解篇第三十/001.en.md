# 素問 陽明脈解篇第三十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "When the foot *yangming* vessel is ill, one dislikes people and fire, and on hearing the sound of wood one is startled and frightened, while bells and drums do not disturb one. Why is one frightened on hearing the sound of wood? I wish to hear the reason."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the foot *yangming* vessel is ill ... I wish to hear the reason"] The previous chapter says, "When it enters the six fu, the body is hot, one cannot lie down at times, and above there is panting and shouting." The *yangming* is the stomach vessel. Now the illness is not as the previous chapter describes; instead one is frightened on hearing the sound of wood. Hence he asks about the difference.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: why, in illness of the *yangming*, does one dislike people and fire and become frightened at the sound of wood?
- 𭥍 in Wang Bing's note is a variant of 旨 (Kanripo writes 旨).
