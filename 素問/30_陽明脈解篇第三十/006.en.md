# 素問 陽明脈解篇第三十 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the *yangming* is in reversal, there is panting and oppression; with oppression one dislikes people."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the *yangming* is in reversal ... one dislikes people"] With oppression the heat stagnates within; therefore one dislikes people.
Song editors: The *Maijie* says, "Why does one wish to close the doors and windows and stay alone? Yin and yang strike each other, yang is exhausted and yin abundant; therefore one closes the doors and windows and stays alone."

## Notes

When the *yangming* qi counterflows, the chest is oppressed and one dislikes people.
- The *Maijie* quoted by the Song editors is the *Maijie pian* (chapter 49).
