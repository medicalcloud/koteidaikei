# 素問 陽明脈解篇第三十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "The *yangming* is the stomach vessel, and the stomach is earth. Therefore being frightened on hearing the sound of wood is because earth dislikes wood."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The *yangming* is the stomach vessel ... earth dislikes wood"] The books of yin and yang say, "Wood controls earth"; therefore earth dislikes wood.

## Notes

Explained by the controlling cycle of the five phases (wood controls earth).
