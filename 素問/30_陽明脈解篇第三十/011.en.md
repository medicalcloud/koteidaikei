# 素問 陽明脈解篇第三十 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why does one throw off one's clothes and run?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why does one throw off one's clothes and run?"] 棄 means not using.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why one throws off one's clothes and runs.
