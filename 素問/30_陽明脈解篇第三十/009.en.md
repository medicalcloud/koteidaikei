# 素問 陽明脈解篇第三十 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. When the illness is severe, one throws off one's clothes and runs, climbs high places and sings, or goes for days without eating, leaps over walls and climbs onto roofs — places one could never normally climb. Why can one do so when ill?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good. When the illness is severe ... Why can one do so when ill?"] 素 means ordinarily. 踰垣 means leaping over a wall. He wonders at its being somewhat different from the usual.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about the manic symptoms when *yangming* illness is severe.
