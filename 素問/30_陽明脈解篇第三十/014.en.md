# 素問 陽明脈解篇第三十 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When yang is abundant, it makes one talk wildly and curse, without regard to intimates or strangers, and not want to eat; not wanting to eat, one runs about wildly."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When yang is abundant ... one runs about wildly"] The foot *yangming* stomach vessel descends through the diaphragm, belongs to the stomach and connects with the spleen. The foot greater yin spleen vessel enters the abdomen, belongs to the spleen and connects with the stomach, rises through the diaphragm alongside the throat, joins the root of the tongue and spreads under the tongue. Hence such illness.

## Notes

Abundant yang causes manic speech and behaviour and loss of appetite; this ends the chapter.
- Daizhige (重广补注黄帝内经素问) has 巻九 at the end of the chapter; this marks the juan division in that source.
