# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot lesser yang, it causes pain lodged in the pivot so that the thigh cannot be raised. Needle in the pivot with the fine needle; if there is cold, retain the needle long. Use the waxing and waning of the moon for the number, and it ends at once.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot lesser yang ... the thigh cannot be raised"] Its channel emerges at Qijie, wraps around the hairline and enters crosswise into the hip socket. Hence the pain lodges behind the hip pivot and the joint cannot be raised. 枢 means the hip pivot.

[On "Needle in the pivot with the fine needle ... it ends at once"] Behind the hip pivot is the Huantiao (環銚) point. Since it is exactly behind the hip pivot, it says "needle behind the hip pivot". Huantiao is where the foot lesser yang vessel qi issues; one may needle one body-*cun* and retain twenty breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. The fine needle is the seventh needle.
Song editors: In the *Jiayi jing* Huantiao is in the hip pivot, and the *Qixue lun* says "in the divisions of the two hip sockets". This classic says "needle in the pivot", so Wang's "behind the hip pivot" is wrong.

## Notes

Needling for hip-joint pain of the foot lesser yang network vessel.
- 巳 in 立巳 is read as 已.
- 環銚 in Wang Bing's note is the Huantiao (環跳) point. Wang Bing restates the main text's "in the pivot" as "behind the hip pivot", which the Song editors call an error.
