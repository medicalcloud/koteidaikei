# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Now if a pathogen lodges in the skin and hair, enters and resides in the minute network vessels, stays and does not leave, and is blocked so that it cannot enter the channels, it flows over into the great network vessels and produces unusual illness. When a pathogen lodges in the great network vessels, the left pours into the right and the right into the left; above and below, left and right, it interferes with the channels and spreads to the four extremities. Its qi has no fixed place and does not enter the channel points. [Needling it] is called crosswise needling."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "it flows over into the great network vessels and produces unusual illness"] The illness is in the blood network vessels; this is called an unusual pathogen.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi says "the great network vessels are the fifteen network vessels".

[On "Its qi has no fixed place ... is called crosswise needling"] The four extremities means the four limbs.

## Notes

A pathogen that cannot enter the channels overflows into the great network vessels and produces unusual illness; needling it is crosswise needling.
