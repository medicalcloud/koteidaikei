# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Now if a pathogen lodges in the skin and hair, enters and resides in the minute collaterals, stays and does not leave, and is blocked so that it cannot enter the meridians, it flows over into the great collaterals and produces unusual illness. When a pathogen lodges in the great collaterals, the left pours into the right and the right into the left; above and below, left and right, it interferes with the meridians and spreads to the four extremities. Its qi has no fixed place and does not enter the meridian points. [Needling it] is called crosswise needling."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "it flows over into the great collaterals and produces unusual illness"] The illness is in the blood collaterals; this is called an unusual pathogen.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi says "the great collaterals are the fifteen collaterals".

[On "Its qi has no fixed place ... is called crosswise needling"] The four extremities means the four limbs.

## Notes

A pathogen that cannot enter the meridians overflows into the great collaterals and produces unusual illness; needling it is crosswise needling.
