# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot lesser yin, it causes sudden heart pain, acute distension, and propping fullness of the chest and flanks. If there is no accumulation, needle in front of the Ran bone to let blood; it ends in about the time of a meal. If it does not end, take the right for the left and the left for the right. If the illness has newly arisen, take it and it ends in five days.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot lesser yin ... propping fullness of the chest and flanks"] Its branching network vessel runs with the main channel up from the kidneys through the liver and diaphragm to the pericardium; so when a pathogen lodges in it, this illness results.

[On "If there is no accumulation, needle in front of the Ran bone ... the time of a meal"] In front of the Ran bone is the Rangu point, in the depression below the large raised bone in front of the inner ankle, the *ying* point of the foot lesser yin; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain three breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Needling it with much blood shown makes the person immediately hungry and wanting to eat.

[On "If it does not end, take the right for the left and the left for the right"] It means that for pain on the left, take the right, and for pain on the right, take the left. The rest follows this example.

[On "If the illness has newly arisen, take it and it ends in five days"] When someone has long had this illness and it newly flares up, needle it first and it ends completely in five days.

## Notes

Crosswise needling of the foot lesser yin network vessel. The methods for each network vessel follow. Qibo's speech continues to the end of the chapter.
- 巳 in 不巳 and 五日巳 is read as 已.
