# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I wish to hear how crosswise needling is done. How does one take [the points]?"

## Notes

A question on the concrete method of crosswise needling. There is no note by Wang Bing.
