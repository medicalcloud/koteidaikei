# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the hand lesser yang, it causes throat obstruction, curled tongue, dry mouth, vexation of the heart, pain on the outer edge of the forearm, and inability to raise the hand to the head. Needle above the nail of the finger next to the middle finger, a leek-leaf's breadth from the tip, one puncture each. In the robust it ends at once; in the old it ends after a while. Take the right for the left and the left for the right. If this is a new illness, it ends in a few days.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "it causes throat obstruction ... inability to raise the hand to the head"] Its vessel follows the back of the hand, emerges on the outside of the forearm, goes up the shoulder, enters Quepen, spreads in the chest center, and disperses to network the pericardium. Its branch goes up from the chest center, emerges at Quepen and ascends the nape. Also the heart governs the tongue. Hence this illness.

[On "Needle above the nail of the finger next to the middle finger ... one puncture each"] This means the Guanchong point, the well point of the lesser yang; one may needle one body-*fen* and retain three breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Both hands are needled; hence "one puncture each". 痏 means a sore (puncture wound).
Song editors: According to the *Jiayi jing*, the Guanchong point emerges at the tip of the finger next to the little finger; saying "middle finger" here is an error.

## Notes
