# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges in the collateral of the foot *Jueyin*, it causes sudden *shan* with acute pain. Needle above the nail of the big toe where it meets the flesh, one puncture each. In men it ends at once; in women it ends after a while. Take the right for the left and the left for the right.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a pathogen lodges in the collateral of the foot *Jueyin*, it causes sudden *shan* with acute pain"] Its collateral leaves [the meridian] five body-*cun* above the inner ankle and branches off to the lesser yang. Its branch follows the shin up to the testicles and binds at the penis. Hence it 今 [causes] sudden *shan* with acute pain. 睾 means the testicles.

[On "Needle above the nail of the big toe where it meets the flesh, one puncture each"] This means the Dadun point, at the tip of the big toe, a leek-leaf's breadth from the corner of the nail, the well point of the *Jueyin*; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain ten breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.

## Notes

Crosswise needling of the foot *Jueyin* collateral.
- 今 in Wang Bing's 故今人 is presumably an error for 令.
- In the base, 2 instance(s) of 巳 meaning 已 "already/stop" to 已 are emended (graphic confusion; the base often does not distinguish 巳, 已 and 己, while Wikisource's plain text and other sources mostly have the correct character).
