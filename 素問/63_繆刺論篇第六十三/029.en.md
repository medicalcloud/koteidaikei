# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, the method of needling is first to look at the channels, palpate and follow them, determine their depletion and repletion, and regulate them. Those that do not become regulated are needled at the channel. Where there is pain but the channel is not ill, use crosswise needling. Then look at the skin regions, and where there are blood network vessels, take them all. This is the method of crosswise needling."

## Notes

The chapter ends by summarizing when to use channel needling and crosswise needling. Qibo's speech ends here. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 殆知閣 (黄帝内经素问) places a 巻第十八 break at the end of this unit; this is that source's division into juan.
