# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When a pathogen lodges in a channel, if the left is abundant the right is ill, and if the right is abundant the left is ill. There are also cases of shifting, where before the pain on the left has ended, the right pulse first becomes ill. Such cases must be treated with great crosswise needling, which must hit the channel, not the network vessels. Thus when the network vessels are ill, the pain is at a place askew from the channel; hence it is called crosswise needling."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "if the left is abundant the right is ill ... There are also cases of shifting"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 病易且移.

[On "before the pain on the left has ended, the right pulse first becomes ill ... not the network vessels"] "First becomes ill" means that while the pain there has not yet stopped, this side becomes ill first and receives it.

[On "Thus when the network vessels are ill ... it is called crosswise needling"] The network vessels are the side branches of the main channels, not the main divergences; they also include the separate network vessels such as Gongsun and Feiyang.
Song editors: Wang says "not the main divergences". But in this chapter, the note to "when a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot greater yin, it causes lumbar pain" quotes "[entering] lengthwise into the thigh, joining the *yangming*, going up to network the throat and piercing the middle of the tongue", which is the main divergence of the greater yin. This too includes the main [divergence] of the vessel. How can one deny calling it a main divergence?

## Notes

The distinction between great crosswise needling, for a pathogen in the channels, and crosswise needling, for a pathogen in the network vessels.
- 巳 in 左痛未巳 is read as 已.
- The Song editors' closing 安得謂之作正別也 is hard to construe; it is translated as criticizing Wang Bing's "not the main divergences".
