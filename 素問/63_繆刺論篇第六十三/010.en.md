# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot greater yang, it causes pain in the head, nape and shoulders. Needle above the nail of the little toe where it meets the flesh, one puncture each, and it ends at once. If it does not end, needle below the outer ankle three punctures, taking the right for the left and the left for the right; it ends in about the time of a meal.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot greater yang, it causes pain in the head, nape and shoulders"] The main part of its channel emerges from the brain and branches down the nape; its branch divides left and right from inside the shoulder blade and descends. Also its network vessel ascends from the foot, follows the back and goes up to the head. Hence pain in the nape, head and shoulders.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says "its branch enters from the vertex to network the brain, comes back out and branches down the nape". 正 in Wang's "the main part of its channel" should be 攴 [支, branch].

[On "Needle above the nail of the little toe ... it ends at once"] This means the Zhiyin point, the well point of the greater yang; one may needle one body-*fen* and retain five breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says "on the outer side of the little toe, a leek-leaf's breadth from the corner of the nail".

[On "needle below the outer ankle three punctures ... the time of a meal"] This means the Jinmen point, the cleft point of the foot greater yang, below the outer ankle; one may needle three body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones.

## Notes

Crosswise needling of the foot greater yang network vessel.
- 巳 in 立巳, 不巳 and 食頃巳 is read as 已.
