# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot greater yang, it causes spasms and tightness of the back, drawing to the flanks with pain. To needle it, count the vertebrae starting from the nape, press quickly alongside the spine, and where it responds to the hand as if painful, needle beside it three punctures, and it ends at once.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot greater yang ... drawing to the flanks with pain"] Its channel divides left and right from inside the 踝 [presumably shoulder blade] and descends, pierces the paraspinal muscles and joins in the popliteal fossa. Hence the illness causes spasms, tightness of the back, and pain drawing to the flanks.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Jiayi jing* add, after "drawing to the flanks with pain", "drawing inward to the heart with pain".

[On "To needle it, count the vertebrae starting from the nape ... it ends at once"] "Count the vertebrae starting from the nape" means counting from Dazhui to the second vertebra, and pressing along both sides of the spine within one body-*cun* five *fen* on each side; where there is pain responding to the hand, that is where the pathogen lodges. Needle it at once, according to the depth at which the pain responds to the hand. Since the pathogen lodges on both sides of the spine, it says "needle beside it".

## Notes

Crosswise needling of the back for the foot greater yang network vessel.
- 踝 in Wang Bing's 従踝内 is the same in the Siku quanshu edition (Daizhige *Huangdi neijing suwen*), but is taken as an error for 髆, since the foot greater yang vessel 循肩髆內，挾脊 in the *Lingshu*, "Jingmai" chapter, and Wang Bing's note in unit 10 has 従髆内左右別下.
