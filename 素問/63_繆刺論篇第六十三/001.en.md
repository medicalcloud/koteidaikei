# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I have heard of crosswise needling but have not grasped its meaning. What is crosswise needling?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I have heard of crosswise needling ... What is crosswise needling?"] Crosswise (繆) needling means that the use of the points needled is as if askew from the main lines.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on the meaning of crosswise needling.
