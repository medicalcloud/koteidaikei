# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I wish to hear how crosswise needling takes the right for the left and the left for the right. How is it distinguished from great crosswise needling?"

## Notes

A question on the distinction between crosswise needling and great crosswise needling. There is no note by Wang Bing.
