# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot lesser yang, it causes flank pain with inability to breathe, and coughing with sweating. Needle above the nail of the toe next to the little toe where it meets the flesh, one puncture each; the inability to breathe ends at once and the sweating stops at once. For coughing, warm the clothes and give food and drink, and it ends in a day. Needle the right for the left and the left for the right, and the illness ends at once. If it does not end, needle again by the method.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot lesser yang ... coughing with sweating"] The branch of its vessel goes down from the outer canthus to Daying, joins the hand lesser yang below the cheekbone, overlies Jiache, goes down the neck to join at Quepen, descends into the chest, pierces the diaphragm, networks the liver, [connects to] the gallbladder and follows the flank. Hence flank pain, coughing and sweating.

[On "Needle above the nail of the toe next to the little toe where it meets the flesh, one puncture each"] This means the Qiaoyin point, the well point of the lesser yang; one may needle one body-*fen* and retain one breath; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says "Qiaoyin is at the tip of the toe next to the little toe, a leek-leaf's breadth from the corner of the nail".

## Notes

Crosswise needling of the foot lesser yang network vessel.
