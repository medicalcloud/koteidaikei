# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot greater yin, it causes lumbar pain drawing to the lower abdomen and pulling the soft flank, with inability to breathe with the head back. Needle at the joint of lumbus and sacrum, above the two paraspinal muscles; this is Yaoshu. Use the waxing and waning of the moon for the number of punctures; on withdrawing the needle it ends at once. Needle the right for the left and the left for the right.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "it causes lumbar pain drawing to the lower abdomen and pulling the soft flank, with inability to breathe with the head back"] The network vessel of the foot greater yin goes from the thigh to join the *yangming*, goes up through the sacral bone, binds with the *jueyin* and lesser yang at Xialiao, follows the inside of the sacrum into the abdomen, and goes up to network the throat and pierce the middle of the tongue. So lumbar pain draws to the lower abdomen and pulls into the soft flank. 䏚 means the empty soft place below the lower ribs. When it receives pathogenic qi, the network vessel becomes tense; hence one cannot lean back and stretch to pant. In the *Ci yaotong pian* there is no character 息.
Song editors: On examination, Wang's note says "network vessel of the foot greater yin", but according to the *Jiayi jing* this is the main divergence of the greater yin, not a network vessel. Why Wang calls it a network vessel is unclear.

[On "Needle at the joint of lumbus and sacrum ... the left for the right"] The space between the lumbar and sacral bones is called the joint; in its middle is Yaoshu. One may needle two body-*cun* [Song editors: the note to the *Qifu lun* writes "two *fen*", the note to the *Cire lun* "two *fen*", the note to the *Shuixue pian* "two *fen*", the note to the *Rexue pian* "two *cun*", and the *Jiayi jing* "two *cun*".] and retain seven breaths. Its indications are as in the classic. The *Zhonggao kongxue jing* says "take the right for the left and the left for the right", but since the point is on the midline, this cannot be so. Next, below the lumbus flanking the sacrum there are four bone hollows on each side, all governing lumbar pain. Xialiao has indications as in the classic and is where the foot greater yin, *jueyin* and lesser yang bind; one may needle two body-*cun* and retain ten breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. 胂 means the paraspinal muscles of the two hip bones. Yaoshu and the 髁伸 [胂] should all be taken.
Song editors: This item, "when a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the foot greater yin" with its needling method, already appears in the *Ci yaotong pian*, where the note is very detailed; here there are only the three extra characters 是腰兪. Further, Quan Yuanqi's edition originally lacked these three characters. Wang knew well that there is no reason to take Yaoshu crosswise from left and right and annotated accordingly, but did not know that Quan Yuanqi's edition originally lacked these three characters.

## Notes

Crosswise needling for lumbar pain of the foot greater yin network vessel. The Song editors hold that, since the midline Yaoshu cannot be needled crosswise, the three characters 是腰兪, absent from Quan Yuanqi's edition, are a later addition.
- 伸 in Wang Bing's 髁伸 is presumably an error for 胂.
- 䏚 is written 眇 in Wikisource and 殆知閣 (白文) (see the variants).
