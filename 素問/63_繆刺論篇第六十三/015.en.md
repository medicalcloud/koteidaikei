# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the hand *yangming*, it causes deafness, with times of not hearing sounds. Needle above the nail of the finger next to the thumb, a leek-leaf's breadth from the tip, one puncture each, and hearing returns at once. If it does not end, needle above the nail of the middle finger where it meets the flesh, and hearing returns at once. Those who cannot hear, not just at times, must not be needled. Wind arising in the ear is also needled by this number. Needle the right for the left and the left for the right.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the hand *yangming*, it causes deafness, with times of not hearing sounds"] The branch of its channel goes up the neck from Quepen and pierces the cheek. Also the branch of its network vessel enters the ear and meets the ancestral vessels. Hence the illness causes deafness with times of not hearing sounds.

[On "Needle above the nail of the finger next to the thumb ... hearing returns at once"] This too is the Shangyang point, as above.

[On "If it does not end, needle above the nail of the middle finger where it meets the flesh, and hearing returns at once"] This means the Zhongchong point, the well point of the hand heart governor, in the depression at the tip of the middle finger, a leek-leaf's breadth from the nail; one may needle one body-*fen* and retain three breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. The old classic has lost bamboo strips and there is no network vessel to trace here. Probably it means needling above the nail of the little finger where it meets the flesh. Why? Because the text below says "the network vessel of the hand lesser yin meets in the ear". The tip of the little finger is called Shaochong, the well point of the hand lesser yin; one may needle one body-*fen* and retain one breath; for moxibustion, one cone.
Song editors: Wang says "probably the Shaochong point above the nail of the little finger". But according to the *Jiayi jing*, the main divergence of the hand heart governor goes up along the throat, emerges behind the ear and joins the lesser yang below Wangu. In that case, why not needle Zhongchong instead of suspecting Shaochong?

[On "Those who cannot hear, not just at times, must not be needled"] Those who cannot hear, not just at times, have exhausted network-vessel qi; hence they must not be needled.

## Notes

Crosswise needling for deafness of the hand *yangming* network vessel.
- 巳 in 不巳 is read as 已.
- 其不時聞者 is read with Wang Bing as "those who cannot hear, not just at times [i.e. constantly]".
