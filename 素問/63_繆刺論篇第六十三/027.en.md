# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When [the illness] is transmitted crosswise and draws to the upper teeth, with cold pain of the teeth and lips, look at the vessels on the back of the hand with blood and remove it; [needle] above the nail of the middle toe of the foot *Yangming*, one puncture, and above the nail of the finger next to the thumb, one puncture each, and it ends at once. Take the right for the left and the left for the right.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When [the illness] is transmitted crosswise ... remove it"] If the illness is transmitted crosswise, draws to the upper teeth, and the teeth and lips are cold and painful, needle the hand *Yangming* collateral on the back of the hand.

[On "above the nail of the middle toe of the foot *Yangming* ... the left for the right"] This means the Lidui point on the second toe. The finger next to the thumb means the Shangyang point, the well point of the hand *Yangming*. The *Zhenjing* says: "For toothache without aversion to cold drinks, take the foot *Yangming*; with aversion to cold drinks, take the hand *Yangming*."
Song editors: On examination, in the earlier text "when a pathogen lodges in the foot *Yangming*, needle above the nail of the toe next to the middle toe", the two characters 次指 are a superfluous error; it should simply say "above the nail of the middle toe", as here.

## Notes

Crosswise needling for toothache transmitted crosswise.
- In the base, 1 instance(s) of 巳 meaning 已 "already/stop" to 已 are emended (graphic confusion; the base often does not distinguish 巳, 已 and 己, while Wikisource's plain text and other sources mostly have the correct character).
