# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For deafness, needle the hand *yangming*. If it does not end, needle its connecting vessel that emerges in front of the ear.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "For deafness, needle the hand *yangming* ... in front of the ear"] The hand *yangming* means the one mentioned above, "the finger next to the thumb, a leek-leaf's breadth from the tip"; this is called Shangyang. According to the *Zhonggao kongxue tujing*, among the points of the hand *yangming* vessel, Shangyang, Hegu, Yangxi and 徧 [Pian]li all govern deafness. What the classic points to here is Shangyang above, not Hegu and the like. The connecting vessel in front of the ear is the hand *yangming* vessel, exactly at the division of Tinghui; needle four body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones.

## Notes

Needling for deafness.
- Wang Bing places the connecting vessel in front of the ear at Tinghui, but Tinghui is often given as a foot lesser yang point. This has not been checked.
