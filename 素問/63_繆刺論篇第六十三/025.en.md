# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For dental caries, needle the hand *yangming*. If it does not end, needle its vessel that enters the teeth, and it ends at once.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "For dental caries, needle the hand *yangming* ... it ends at once"] According to the *Jiayi liuzhu tujing*, among the points of the hand *yangming* vessel, the seven points Shangyang, Erjian, Sanjian, Hegu, Yangxi, 徧 [Pian]li and Wenliu all govern toothache. The hand *yangming* vessel pierces the cheek and enters the lower teeth; the foot *yangming* vessel follows the outside of the nose and enters the upper teeth.

## Notes

Needling for dental caries.
- In the base, 1 instance(s) of 巳 meaning 已 "already/stop" to 已 are emended (graphic confusion; the base often does not distinguish 巳, 已 and 己, while Wikisource's plain text and other sources mostly have the correct character).
