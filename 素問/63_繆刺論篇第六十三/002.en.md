# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "When a pathogen lodges in the body, it must first take up residence in the skin and hair. If it stays and does not leave, it enters and resides in the minute vessels. If it stays and does not leave, it enters and resides in the network vessels. If it stays and does not leave, it enters and resides in the channels, connects inwardly to the five depots and spreads to the intestines and stomach. When yin and yang are both affected, the five depots are harmed. This is the sequence by which a pathogen enters from the skin and hair and reaches the five depots. In such a case, treat the channel.

## Notes

The sequence by which a pathogen enters from the skin to the five depots; a pathogen in the channels is treated at the channel. Qibo's speech continues to unit 003. There is no note by Wang Bing.
