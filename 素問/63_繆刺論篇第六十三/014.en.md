# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a person has fallen and bad blood remains inside, the abdomen is full and distended, and there is no passing of stool or urine, first give a purgative to drink. This is injury above to the *jueyin* vessel and below to the lesser yin network vessel. Needle the blood vessel below the inner ankle in front of the Ran bone to let blood, and needle the pulsing vessel on the instep. If it does not end, needle above the three hairs, one puncture each; when blood is seen it ends at once. Needle the right for the left and the left for the right. For proneness to sorrow and fright without joy, needle by the method given above.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Needle the blood vessel below the inner ankle in front of the Ran bone to let blood"] This is the network vessel of the lesser yin.
Song editors: On examination, 脈 in 血脈出血 is suspected to be 絡.

[On "and needle the pulsing vessel on the instep"] This means the Chongyang point, the source point of the stomach; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain ten breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. It governs a large abdomen with no desire for food; since the abdomen is distended and full, it is taken.

[On "If it does not end, needle above the three hairs ... the left for the right"] This means the Dadun point, the well point of the *jueyin*.

[On "For proneness to sorrow and fright without joy, needle by the method given above"] Proneness to sorrow and fright without joy is also needled by the method above.

## Notes

Needling for static blood from a fall.
- 巳 in 不巳 and 立巳 is read as 已.
- 右方 is read as "the method given above" (literally "to the right", in vertical writing).
