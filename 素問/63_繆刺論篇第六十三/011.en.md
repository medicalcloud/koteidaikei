# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges in the network vessel of the hand *yangming*, it causes qi to fill the chest, panting with propping of the flanks, and heat in the chest. Needle above the nail of the finger next to the thumb, a leek-leaf's breadth from the tip, one puncture each, taking the right for the left and the left for the right; it ends in about the time of a meal.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "it causes qi to fill the chest, panting with propping of the flanks, and heat in the chest"] Its channel enters Quepen from the tip of the shoulder and networks the 脈 [lungs]; its branch goes straight up the neck from within Quepen. Hence this illness.

[On "Needle above the nail of the finger next to the thumb ... the time of a meal"] This means the Shangyang point, the well point of the hand *yangming*; one may needle one body-*fen* and retain one breath; for moxibustion, one cone.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says "Shangyang is on the inner side of the finger next to the thumb, a leek-leaf's breadth from the corner of the nail".

## Notes

Crosswise needling of the hand *yangming* network vessel.
- 巳 in 食頃巳 is read as 已. 脈 in Wang Bing's 絡脈 is presumably an error for 肺 given the course of the channel, but this has not been checked.
