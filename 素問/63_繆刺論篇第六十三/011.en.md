# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges in the collateral of the hand *Yangming*, it causes qi to fill the chest, dyspnea with propping of the flanks, and heat in the chest. Needle above the nail of the finger next to the thumb, a leek-leaf's breadth from the tip, one puncture each, taking the right for the left and the left for the right; it ends in about the time of a meal.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "it causes qi to fill the chest, dyspnea with propping of the flanks, and heat in the chest"] Its meridian enters Quepen from the tip of the shoulder and networks the 脈 [lungs]; its branch goes straight up the neck from within Quepen. Hence this illness.

[On "Needle above the nail of the finger next to the thumb ... the time of a meal"] This means the Shangyang point, the well point of the hand *Yangming*; one may needle one body-*fen* and retain one breath; for moxibustion, one cone.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says "Shangyang is on the inner side of the finger next to the thumb, a leek-leaf's breadth from the corner of the nail".

## Notes

Crosswise needling of the hand *Yangming* collateral.
- 脈 in Wang Bing's 絡脈 is taken as an error for 肺, since in the *Lingshu*, "Jingmai" chapter, the hand *Yangming* vessel 下入缺盆，絡肺.
- In the base, 1 instance of 巳 meaning 已 "already/stop" is emended to 已 (graphic confusion; the base often does not distinguish 巳, 已 and 己, while Wikisource's plain text and other sources mostly have the correct character).
