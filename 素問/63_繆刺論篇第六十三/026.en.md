# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges between the five depots, the illness is pain drawing along the vessels, coming at times and stopping at times. Look at the illness and needle crosswise above the nails of the hands and feet; look at the vessels and let blood. Needle once every other day; if one needling does not end it, five needlings will.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Look at the illness and needle crosswise above the nails of the hands and feet"] Needle each well point, taking the right for the left and the left for the right.

[On "look at the vessels and let blood ... five needlings will"] Where there are blood vessels, needle them, according to this number.

## Notes

Crosswise needling for a pathogen between the five depots.
