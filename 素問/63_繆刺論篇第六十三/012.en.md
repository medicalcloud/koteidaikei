# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges between the forearm and palm so that it cannot bend, needle behind the wrist bone. First press with the finger, and where it hurts, needle it. Use the waxing and waning of the moon for the number: as the moon grows, one puncture on the first day, two on the second, fifteen on the fifteenth, and fourteen on the sixteenth.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a pathogen lodges between the forearm and palm ... needle behind the wrist bone"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi says "this is the knob at the base joint of the hand".

[On "fifteen on the fifteenth, and fourteen on the sixteenth"] Follow the number of days. Before mid-month is called growing; after mid-month is called dying; they differ by waning and waxing.

## Notes

Crosswise needling for a pathogen between forearm and palm, with the number of punctures by the phase of the moon.
- 乃 in 痛乃刺之 is written 刀 in the base text and was corrected from the other sources (see the emendation).
