# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In treating the various channels by needling, if the places they pass are not ill, use crosswise needling.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In treating the various channels by needling ... use crosswise needling"] 王言也. If the channel is not ill, the pathogen is in the network vessel; hence crosswise needling. If there is illness where the channel passes, it is a channel illness and crosswise needling is not appropriate.

## Notes

The principle that crosswise needling is used when the course of the channel is not ill.
- 王言也 at the start of Wang Bing's note is obscure and may be corrupt; not checked.
