# 素問 繆刺論篇第六十三 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen lodges in the network vessels of the hand and foot lesser yin and greater yin and the foot *yangming*—these five network vessels all meet in the ear and go up to network the left corner [of the head]—and the five network vessels are all exhausted, the vessels throughout the body all pulse, yet the body is without awareness; the condition is like a corpse, and it is also called corpse-like reversal. Needle above the nail on the inner side of the big toe, a leek-leaf's breadth from the tip; then needle the sole of the foot; then needle above the nail of the middle toe, one puncture each; then needle the inner side of the thumb, a leek-leaf's breadth from the tip; then needle the hand heart governor and the tip of the sharp bone of the lesser yin, one puncture each, and it ends at once. If it does not end, blow into both ears with a bamboo tube, shave a square *cun* of hair from the left corner [of the head], burn and powder it, and give it to drink in a cup of fine wine; for one who cannot drink, pour it in, and it ends at once.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a pathogen lodges in the network vessels ... go up to network the left corner"] The hand lesser yin true heart vessel, the foot lesser yin kidney vessel, the hand greater yin lung vessel, the foot greater yin spleen vessel, and the foot *yangming* stomach vessel: these five network vessels all meet in the ear and emerge to network the left corner of the forehead.

[On "and the five network vessels are all exhausted ... it is also called corpse-like reversal"] It means suddenly falling into a stupor like a dead body, while the pulses of the body still beat as in a normal person. When yin qi is abundant above, the qi below fumigates upward and the pathogenic qi counterflows; when the pathogenic qi counterflows, yang qi is disordered; when yang qi is disordered, the five network vessels close and bind and do not pass. Hence the condition is like a corpse. Since it arises from reversal, it is also called corpse-like reversal.

[On "Needle above the nail on the inner side of the big toe, a leek-leaf's breadth from the tip"] This means the Yinbai point, the well point of the foot greater yin; one may needle one body-*fen* and retain three breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "then needle the sole of the foot"] This means the Yongquan point, the well point of the foot lesser yin; needle as in the earlier method for Yongquan.

[On "then needle above the nail of the middle toe, one puncture each"] This means the second toe, the well point of the foot *yangming*; needle as in the earlier method for Lidui.

[On "then needle the inner side of the thumb, a leek-leaf's breadth from the tip"] This means the Shaoshang point, the well point of the hand greater yin; one may needle one body-*fen* and retain three breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "then needle the hand heart governor"] This means the Zhongchong point, the well point of the hand heart governor; one may needle one body-*fen* and retain three breaths; for moxibustion, one cone.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* does not needle the hand heart governor. On examination, the hand heart governor is not among these five network vessels either, so needling it here makes six network vessels, which does not agree. Wang Bing merely annotated it following [the text] without making the point clear.

[On "and the tip of the sharp bone of the lesser yin, one puncture each, and it ends at once"] This means the Shenmen point, in the depression at the tip of the sharp bone behind the palm, the *shu* point of the hand lesser yin; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain three breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "If it does not end, blow into both ears with a bamboo tube"] It means making qi enter the ears to assist the five network vessels within so that the qi passes again. Insert the tube into the ear, seal it tightly with the hand so that no air escapes, and blow hard; the qi then suddenly passes through the network vessels.
Song editors: Tao Yinju says: "Blow hard into the left ear three times, then into the right ear three times."

[On "shave a square *cun* of hair ... and it ends at once"] The hair at the left corner is the surplus of the blood of the five network vessels; so it is shaved, burned, powdered and given in fine wine. Wine carries the force of medicine, and it also flames upward and goes inward to the heart; the heart governs the vessels; hence it is taken in fine wine.

## Notes

Needling and treatment for corpse-like reversal arising when the five network vessels are exhausted.
- 巳 in 立巳 is read as 已.
- 治 in 燔治 is read as "powder".
