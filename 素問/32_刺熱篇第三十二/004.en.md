# 素問 刺熱篇第三十二 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In lung febrile illness, first there is shivering reversal, the fine hairs stand up, one dislikes wind and cold, the tongue is yellow on top, and the body is hot. When the heat contends, there are panting and coughing, pain running to the chest, breast and back, inability to take a deep breath, unbearable headache, and sweating with chills. It worsens on *bing* and *ding* and there is great sweating on *geng* and *xin*; if the qi counterflows, one dies on *bing* and *ding*. Needle the hand greater yin and *yangming*, letting out blood the size of a soybean, and it ends at once.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In lung febrile illness, first there is shivering reversal ... the body is hot"] The lung governs the skin and outwardly nourishes the hair; therefore when heat strikes it, first one shivers, dislikes wind and cold, and the fine hairs stand up. The lung vessel arises in the middle burner, descends to connect with the large intestine, and returns along the mouth of the stomach. Now the lung heat enters the stomach and the stomach heat rises; therefore the tongue is yellow on top and the body is hot.

[On "When the heat contends ... sweating with chills"] The lung lies above the diaphragm and its qi governs the chest and breast; among its changes is coughing; it stores qi and governs respiration; the back is the house of the chest. Therefore there are panting and coughing, pain running to the chest, breast and back, and inability to take a deep breath. The network vessel of the lung rises to meet in the ear; now hot qi rises and fumigates; therefore there are unbearable headache and sweating with chills.

[On "It worsens on *bing* and *ding* ... one dies on *bing* and *ding*"] The lung governs metal; *bing* and *ding* are fire, and fire melts metal; therefore it worsens and one dies on *bing* and *ding*. *Geng* and *xin* are metal; therefore there is great sweating on *geng* and *xin*. The signs of counterflowing qi are missing from the classic and not yet written.

[On "Needle the hand greater yin and *yangming* ... it ends at once"] The greater yin is the lung vessel and the *yangming* the large intestine vessel. Look for where the network vessels are abundant and needle there to let out blood.

## Notes

The symptoms, prognosis and needling of lung febrile illness.
- 大 in 大爍金 in Wang Bing's note is taken from the context as an error for 火 "fire".
