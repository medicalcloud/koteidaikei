# 素問 刺熱篇第三十二 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In liver febrile illness the left cheek first turns red; in heart febrile illness the forehead first turns red; in spleen febrile illness the nose first turns red; in lung febrile illness the right cheek first turns red; in kidney febrile illness the chin first turns red. Even if the illness has not yet broken out, needle where red colour is seen; this is called treating illness before it arises. When febrile illness arises from its own region, it ends when its date comes; if the needling is reversed, it ends after three circuits; if reversed twice, one dies. All who should sweat sweat profusely on the day of what they overcome. In treating febrile illness, give cold water to drink and then needle; one must dress the patient in cool clothing and keep him in a cool place, and stop when the body becomes cool.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In liver febrile illness the left cheek first turns red"] Liver qi corresponds to wood, and wood qi corresponds to spring; when one faces south and arranges it properly, it is the left cheek.

[On "in heart febrile illness the forehead first turns red"] Heart qi corresponds to fire, and the qi of fire blazes upward; pointing to the image to clarify the sign, it is observed at the 顔, which is the forehead.

[On "in spleen febrile illness the nose first turns red"] Spleen qi corresponds to earth, and earth dominates in the centre; the nose lies in the centre of the face; therefore it is read at the nose.

[On "in lung febrile illness the right cheek first turns red"] Lung qi corresponds to metal, and the qi of metal corresponds to autumn; when one faces south and arranges it properly, it is the right cheek.

[On "in kidney febrile illness the chin first turns red"] Kidney qi corresponds to water, and water only moistens downward; pointing to the image to clarify the sign, it is observed at the chin.

[On "Even if the illness has not yet broken out ... treating illness before it arises"] "The sage does not treat illness that has arisen but treats it before it arises; does not put in order disorder that has arisen but before it arises" — this is what is meant.

[On "When febrile illness arises from its own region, it ends when its date comes"] The date is the day of great sweating, such as *jia* and *yi* for the liver, *bing* and *ding* for the heart, *wu* and *ji* for the spleen, *geng* and *xin* for the lung, and *ren* and *gui* for the kidney; these are the dates.

[On "if the needling is reversed, it ends after three circuits"] "Reversed" means taking its qi in reverse: needling the spleen for liver illness, the kidney for spleen illness, the heart for kidney illness, the lung for heart illness, the liver for lung illness — all these needle the qi of the five zang in reverse. "Three circuits" means going around the forms of the vessels of the three yin and three yang three times. Also, needling and draining the *yangming* for greater yang illness, the lesser yang for *yangming* illness, the greater yin for lesser yang illness, the lesser yin for greater yin illness, the *jueyin* for lesser yin illness — this is taking the vessel qi of the three yin and three yang in reverse.

[On "if reversed twice, one dies"] The first needling was already reversed and the qi of the illness was transmitted; needling it in reverse again is double reversal. Even with a single reversal it ends only after three circuits; how could one survive a double reversal?

[On "All who should sweat ... on the day of what they overcome"] When it dominates, it overcomes the evil; therefore each sweats on its dominant day.
Song editors: The text of this item and its note, twenty-four characters, duplicate the preceding text and should be deleted. The *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu* also do not repeat it.

[On "In treating febrile illness ... stop when the body becomes cool"] When cold water is in the stomach, the yang qi becomes abundant outside; therefore one gives cold to drink and then needles. When the heat recedes, coolness arises; therefore one stops needling when the body becomes cool.

## Notes

Where on the face each zang's febrile illness first appears, needling at the stage before illness arises, compliance and reversal in needling, and precautions in treating febrile illness.
