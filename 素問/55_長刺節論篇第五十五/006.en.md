# 素問 長刺節論篇第五十五 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the illness is in the sinews, with contracted sinews and painful joints so that one cannot walk, it is called sinew impediment. Take needling on the sinews as the criterion; needle between the divisions of the flesh, without striking the bone. When the sinews where the illness arises become hot and the illness ends, stop.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Take needling on the sinews as the criterion ... without striking the bone"] 分 means the space between the divisions of flesh where the sinews tie and connect. In needling the sinews do not injure the bone; therefore do not strike the bone.

[On "When the sinews where the illness arises become hot and the illness ends, stop"] Sinew impediment arises from cold in the sinews; therefore when the sinews become hot and the illness ends, stop.

## Notes

The needling of sinew impediment.
