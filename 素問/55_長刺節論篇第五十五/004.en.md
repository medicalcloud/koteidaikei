# 素問 長刺節論篇第五十五 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the illness is in the lower abdomen with accumulation, needle from the *pikui* downward and stop at the lower abdomen; needle between the fourth vertebrae on both sides of the spine; needle the two iliac *liao* points and between the ribs at the lower ribs, guiding the hot qi in the abdomen downward, and it ends.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the illness is in the lower abdomen with accumulation ... and it ends"] Accumulation in the lower abdomen means the knotting and accumulation of the qi of cold and heat. *Pikui* means the transverse crease five body-*cun* below the navel. Needle carefully and not too deeply. The *Cijin lun* says, "If needling the lower abdomen strikes the bladder and urine comes out, it makes the lower abdomen full"; therefore it must not be deep. Between the fourth vertebrae alongside the spine there is no transport point according to the classic; it should probably say between the fifth vertebrae. Below the fifth vertebra on both sides is precisely the heart transport point, and the heart corresponds to the lower abdomen; therefore it should say between the vertebrae. 髂 is the lumbar bone. 髎 is written 髀 in one version, an error due to similar shapes. 髎 means Juliao, the point on the side of the lumbus. Between the ribs at the lower ribs presumably means needling the Jingmen point between the lower ribs.
Song editors: The *Shiyin* writes 皮䯏 for 皮𩩻, pronounced *kui*; thus 䯏 was miswritten as 𩩻. Searching the dictionaries thoroughly, there is no character 𩩻, only 䯏, meaning the end of a bone. 皮䯏 presumably means the end of the transverse bone below the navel. Quan Yuanqi's edition writes 皮髄, and Yuanqi's note says "a raised mound beside the navel", which is also not right.

## Notes

The needling of accumulation in the lower abdomen.
- 𩩻 was supplied where it was lost in the sources (see the 【補】 notes). The Song editors take it as an error for 䯏 "end of a bone".
- Wang Bing suggests that "between the fourth vertebrae" should be "between the fifth vertebrae".
