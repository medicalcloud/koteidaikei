# 素問 長刺節論篇第五十五 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In wind illness, now cold and now hot, with heat and sweating several times a day, first needle the divisions and the network vessels. If sweating with alternating cold and heat continues, needle once every three days, and in a hundred days it ends.

## Notes

The needling of wind illness. There is no note by Wang Bing.
