# 素問 長刺節論篇第五十五 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the illness first breaks out once a year, and if untreated once a month, and if untreated four or five times a month, it is called epilepsy. Needle the divisions and vessels; for those without cold, regulate with the needle, and the illness stops.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Needle the divisions and vessels ... the illness stops"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says, "Needle the divisions; if the vessels are especially cold, supplement with the needle."

## Notes

The course and needling of epilepsy. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 已 is supplied in the base's 病止, giving 病已止 ("the illness ceases"). The *Taisu*, the *Leijing*, Zhang Zhicong's *Jizhu*, Wikisource (the plain text) and the Daizhige *Chongguang buzhu* text all read 病已止, as does the *Suwen* quoted in the notes of the *Jiayi jing*; 已 is taken to have dropped out. The sense is unchanged.
