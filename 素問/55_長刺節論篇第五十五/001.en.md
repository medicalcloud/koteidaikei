# 素問 長刺節論篇第五十五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The needling expert, without taking the pulse, listens to what the patient says. When [the illness] is in the head with sharp headache, needle deeply; needle to the bone, and when the illness ends, withdraw. Do not injure the bone, flesh or skin; the skin is the path [of the needle].

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The needling expert ... needle deeply"] 蔵 means deep: it says to needle deeply. Hence the text below says:
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition says 為鍼之 without the character 蔵.

[On "needle to the bone ... the skin is the path"] The skin is the path of the needle; therefore in needling the bone do not injure the bone, flesh or skin.

## Notes

This chapter lists needling methods according to the location and type of illness; it contains no dialogue. This unit concerns needling for headache.
- 刺家不診，聴病者言 is understood to mean that one versed in needling can needle by listening to the patient's complaints even without pulse diagnosis.
- 病已上 is taken as "when the illness ends, withdraw [the needle]".
