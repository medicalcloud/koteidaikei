# 素問 長刺節論篇第五十五 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the illness is in the yang vessels, now cold and now hot, and the divisions now cold and now hot, it is called mania. Needle it to deplete the vessels; watch the divisions, and when all are hot and the illness ends, stop.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the illness is in the yang vessels ... it is called mania"] The qi is mad and disordered.

## Notes

The needling of mania.
