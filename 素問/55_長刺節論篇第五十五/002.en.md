# 素問 長刺節論篇第五十五 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yin needling: insert one [in the centre] and four around it; it treats cold and heat. When it is deep and concentrated, needle the great zang; when it presses on the zang, needle the back, that is, the back transport points. Needling these presses toward the zang because they are the meeting places of the zang; stop when the cold and heat in the abdomen are gone. The essential of this needling is to release the needle and let out blood shallowly.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Yin needling: insert one and four around it; it treats cold and heat"] When there is cold and heat in the head, treat it with the method of yin needling. Yin needling means needling suddenly in this number.
Song editors: Another edition writes 平刺 "level needling" for 卒刺. The *Jiayi jing* says, "Yang needling: insert one directly and four beside it. Yin needling: needle suddenly on left and right." This "yin needling" is suspected to be yang needling.

[On "When it is deep and concentrated, needle the great zang"] When the qi of cold-heat illness is deep and concentrated in attacking the interior, one should needle the five zang to resist it.

[On "when it presses on the zang, needle the back, that is, the back transport points"] 迫 means near. As it gradually nears the zang, needle the back transport points of the five zang.

[On "Needling these presses toward the zang because they are the meeting places of the zang"] It explains why the needling is close to the zang: because these are where the qi of the zang meets and issues.

[On "stop when the cold and heat in the abdomen are gone"] It says that in needling the back transport points, regardless of the number, the essential is to stop the needle only when the cold and heat have gone.

[On "The essential of this needling is to release the needle and let out blood shallowly"] If needling them together with the other transport points, do it this way.

## Notes

The needling method for cold and heat (yin needling), and needling the back transport points for deep illness.
- In light of the definition in the *Jiayi jing*, the Song editors suspect that "yin needling" here is yang needling.
- The punctuation and interpretation of this unit vary; the translation follows Wang Bing.
