# 素問 長刺節論篇第五十五 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the illness is in the flesh and skin, with pain all over the flesh and skin, it is called flesh impediment, due to harm by cold and damp. Needle the large and small divisions, releasing the needle many times and deeply, taking heat as the criterion. Do not injure the sinews and bones; if the sinews and bones are injured, abscesses break out or [the illness] changes. When all the divisions are hot and the illness ends, stop.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Needle the large and small divisions ... taking heat as the criterion"] Large divisions are the divisions of large muscles; small divisions are those of small muscles.

[On "Do not injure the sinews and bones ... abscesses break out or [the illness] changes"] The *Zhenjing* says, "If the illness is shallow and the needle deep, good flesh is harmed within and the skin develops abscesses," and also, "If the needle is too deep, the evil qi instead sinks and the illness worsens." Injuring the sinews and bones means the needle is too deep; therefore abscesses break out or it changes.

[On "When all the divisions are hot and the illness ends, stop"] Heat can dispel cold; therefore stop when the illness ends.

## Notes

The needling of flesh impediment.
