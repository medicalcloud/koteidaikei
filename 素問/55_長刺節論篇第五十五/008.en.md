# 素問 長刺節論篇第五十五 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the illness is in the bones, the bones are heavy and cannot be lifted, the marrow aches and cold qi arrives, it is called bone impediment. Needle deeply, taking not injuring the vessels and flesh as the criterion; its path is through the large and small divisions. When the bones are hot and the illness ends, stop.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Needle deeply ... stop"] Why must the vessels and flesh not be injured in needling bone impediment? Because one needles its qi, passing through the large and small divisions of the flesh.

## Notes

The needling of bone impediment.
