# 素問 長刺節論篇第五十五 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

To treat putrid swellings, needle over the putrefaction; looking at the size of the abscess, needle deep or shallow. When needling large ones, let much blood; small ones, needle deep. Always insert the needle straight as the criterion, and stop.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "To treat putrid swellings ... needle deep or shallow"] Putrid swelling means a swelling in which the flesh has rotted into pus and blood. Small abscesses are needled shallowly, large ones deeply.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Jiayi jing* write 癰 "abscess" for 腐.

[On "When needling large ones, let much blood ... and stop"] For large abscesses let out much blood; for small ones simply needle them straight.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says, "When needling large ones, [let] much and needle deep; always insert the needle straight as the criterion and correct it." This text's "small ones, needle deep" is suspected to be an error.

## Notes

The needling of abscesses and swellings.
- 小者深之 "small ones, needle deep" does not agree with Wang Bing's note that small abscesses are needled shallowly; the Song editors also suspect an error. The translation keeps the characters.
