# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Head wind: much sweating on the head and face, aversion to wind; one day before the wind, the illness is worse, and the headache prevents one from going out of the room; on the day of the wind, the illness eases somewhat.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Head wind ... the illness eases somewhat"] The head is the meeting place of all the yang; when wind lodges there, the skin and interstices are loose; therefore there is much sweating on the head and face. A person's yang qi corresponds outwardly with wind; therefore one day before the wind the illness is worse; because it worsens first, it also declines first; therefore on the day of the wind the illness eases somewhat. 内 means inside the house. One cannot go out of the house because the headache is severe and one does not like the outside wind.
Song editors: Sun Simiao says, "Catching wind just after bathing is head wind."

## Notes

Head wind.
