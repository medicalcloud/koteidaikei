# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When wind strikes the transport points of the five zang and six fu, it also becomes wind of the zang and fu; each enters by its gate, and where it strikes there is one-sided wind.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When wind strikes the transport points ... one-sided wind"] When it strikes one-sidedly according to the left or right transport points, there is one-sided wind.

## Notes

Wind of the zang and fu entering through the back transport points, and one-sided wind.
