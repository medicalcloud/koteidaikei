# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How do the forms of the winds of the five zang differ? I wish to hear their diagnosis and manifestations."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How do the forms of the winds of the five zang differ? ... manifestations"] 診 means the signs that can be described; 能 means the form of the illness arising within.

## Notes

Huangdi asks the diagnosis and manifestations of the winds of the five zang.
