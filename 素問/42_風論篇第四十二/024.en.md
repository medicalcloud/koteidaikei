# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Draining wind: much sweating, the sweat leaking onto the clothes, a dry mouth, the surface soaked; this wind [illness] prevents toil, and when the whole body aches there is cold."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Draining wind ... there is cold"] 上漬 means the surface of the skin is damp as if soaked in water, because much sweat comes out. When sweating is profuse, the fluids dry up; therefore the mouth is dry. When the body toils, sweating is severe; therefore one cannot undertake toil. The whole body aches because the sweating is profuse; profuse sweating loses yang; therefore there is cold.
Song editors: Sun Simiao says, "Catching wind just after sexual intercourse is inner wind; its signs are aversion to wind and sweat flowing and soaking the clothes." It is suspected that this draining wind is inner wind. The earlier text of this discourse first speaks of leaking wind, inner wind and head wind, then of entering the centre as intestinal wind and being outside as draining wind. Now there is draining wind but no inner wind, and the inner wind recorded by Sun Simiao has the signs of this draining wind; hence 泄 is suspected to be an error for 内.

## Notes

Draining wind. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 016, ends here.
- The Song editors suggest that this "draining wind" is an error for "inner wind" in the earlier text.
- 上漬其風 is read in various ways; the translation draws on Wang Bing.
