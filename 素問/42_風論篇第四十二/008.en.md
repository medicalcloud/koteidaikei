# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When wind enters and is tied to the head, there is eye wind with cold eyes.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When wind enters and is tied to the head ... cold eyes"] Fengfu is the name of a point, exactly one *cun* inside the hairline of the nape, in the hollow inside the great sinew, the meeting of the *du* and *yangwei* vessels. Above Fengfu is Naohu, the meeting of the *du* vessel and foot greater yang. Therefore rising along Fengfu there is brain wind. The foot greater yang vessel arises at the inner canthus, rises over the forehead, crosses at the vertex, enters to connect with the brain and comes out again; therefore when wind enters and is tied to the head, there is eye wind with cold eyes.

## Notes

Eye wind. Wang Bing's note also explains brain wind from the previous unit.
