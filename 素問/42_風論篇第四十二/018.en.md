# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Liver wind: much sweating, aversion to wind, prone to sorrow, a slightly green-blue complexion, dry throat, prone to anger, and at times aversion to women; the diagnostic sign is below the eyes, where the colour is green-blue.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Liver wind ... where the colour is green-blue"] When the liver is ill the heart zang is not nourished and the heart qi is depleted; therefore one is prone to sorrow. The liver corresponds to wood, whose colour is green-blue; therefore the complexion is slightly green-blue. The liver vessel follows the inner thigh, enters the pubic hair, encircles the genitals, reaches the lower abdomen, runs alongside the stomach, belongs to the liver and connects with the gallbladder, rises through the diaphragm, spreads over the flanks, follows the back of the throat, enters the nasopharynx, rises to emerge at the forehead and meets the *du* vessel at the vertex; a branch descends from the eye connector. Therefore the throat is dry, one is prone to anger, at times dislikes women, and the diagnosis is below the eyes. Green-blue is the liver's colour.
