# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Lung wind: much sweating, aversion to wind, a pale white complexion, occasional cough and shortness of breath, better by day and worse in the evening; the diagnostic sign is above the eyebrows, where the colour is white.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Lung wind ... where the colour is white"] When there is much wind qi within, heat is in surplus; with heat the interstices open; therefore there is much sweating. Wind presses within; therefore one dislikes wind. 皏 means pale white. The lung's colour is white; among its changes is coughing; it governs the storage of qi; wind presses on it within; therefore the complexion is pale white, with occasional cough and shortness of breath. By day the yang qi is in the exterior; therefore it is better. In the evening the yang qi enters the interior and the wind responds within; therefore it is worse. "Above the eyebrows" means above the space between the eyebrows, the region of the *queting*, which outwardly governs the signs of the lung; hence the diagnosis is there. White is the lung's colour.

## Notes

From here to unit 024 the symptoms and diagnostic signs of the winds of the five zang, stomach wind, head wind, leaking wind and draining wind are described; Qibo's speech continues to unit 024. This unit concerns lung wind.
