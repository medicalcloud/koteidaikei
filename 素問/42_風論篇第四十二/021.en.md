# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Stomach wind: much sweating on the neck, aversion to wind, food and drink do not go down, the diaphragm is blocked, the abdomen is often full, without enough clothing there is distension, and eating cold things brings diarrhoea; the diagnostic sign is an emaciated form with a large abdomen.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Stomach wind ... an emaciated form with a large abdomen"] A branch of the stomach vessel goes from the lower edge behind the chin past Renying, follows the throat, enters the supraclavicular fossa, descends through the diaphragm, belongs to the stomach and connects with the spleen; its straight course descends from the supraclavicular fossa along the inner edge of the breast and down alongside the navel into Qijie; another branch arises at the lower mouth of the stomach and follows the inside of the abdomen to join at Qijie. Therefore there are much sweating on the neck, food and drink not going down, blockage of the diaphragm, and frequent abdominal fullness. Without enough clothing, the outside is cold and the inside hot; therefore the abdomen is distended. When one eats cold things, the cold presses on the stomach and the yang does not digest them within; therefore there is diarrhoea. The stomach is paired with the spleen and governs the flesh; when the stomach qi is insufficient, the flesh does not grow; therefore one is thin. Wind qi accumulates in the stomach; therefore the abdomen is large.
Song editors: Sun Simiao says, "Catching wind just after eating is stomach wind."

Stomach wind.
