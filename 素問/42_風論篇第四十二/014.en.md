# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore wind is the chief of the hundred illnesses. When it transforms, it becomes other illnesses, without fixed pattern; yet they are brought about by wind qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Therefore wind is the chief of the hundred illnesses ... brought about by wind qi"] 長 means first: it comes before the hundred illnesses.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Jiayi jing* write 故攻 for 致.

## Notes

Wind is the chief of the hundred illnesses; this concludes Qibo's speech begun in unit 002. 風者百病之長也 is a frequently quoted phrase.
