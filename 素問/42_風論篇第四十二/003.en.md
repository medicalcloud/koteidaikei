# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Wind qi enters the stomach with the *yangming*, follows the vessel up to the inner canthus. If the person is fat, the wind qi cannot leak outward, and there is heat in the centre with yellow eyes; if the person is thin, it leaks outward and there is cold, producing cold in the centre with tearing.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Wind qi enters the stomach with the *yangming* ... cold in the centre with tearing"] The *yangming* is the stomach vessel. The stomach vessel arises at the nose, crosses at the bridge, descends along the outside of the nose, enters the upper teeth, comes out alongside the mouth and around the lips, crosses below at Chengjiang, turns back along the lower edge behind the chin, follows the throat, enters the supraclavicular fossa, descends through the diaphragm and belongs to the stomach; therefore it enters the stomach with the *yangming* and follows the vessel up to the inner canthus. When a person is fat, the interstices are dense and tight; therefore [the wind] cannot leak outward, and there is heat in the centre with yellow eyes. When a person is thin, the interstices are open and loose, and the wind can leak outward; therefore there is cold in the centre with tearing.

## Notes

Heat in the centre and cold in the centre are explained by constitution (fat or thin).
