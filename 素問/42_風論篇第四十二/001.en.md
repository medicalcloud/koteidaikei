# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "When wind harms people, it may produce cold and heat, heat in the centre, cold in the centre, pestilential wind, hemiplegia, or wind [illness]. The illnesses differ and their names are not the same; sometimes it reaches the five zang and six fu within. I do not know the explanation and wish to hear it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When wind harms people ... wish to hear it"] "Harms" means a person is struck by it.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: why do illnesses caused by wind differ so much?
