# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When it is outside in the interstices, there is draining wind.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When it is outside in the interstices, there is draining wind"] When wind resides in the interstices, the sweat pores open, the wind presses and sweat leaks; hence it is called draining wind.

## Notes

Draining wind (泄風).
