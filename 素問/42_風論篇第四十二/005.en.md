# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Those harmed by wind on *jia* and *yi* days in spring have liver wind; those harmed by wind on *bing* and *ding* days in summer have heart wind; those harmed by evil on *wu* and *ji* days in late summer have spleen wind; those struck by evil on *geng* and *xin* days in autumn have lung wind; those struck by evil on *ren* and *gui* days in winter have kidney wind.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Those harmed by wind on *jia* and *yi* days in spring ... kidney wind"] *Jia* and *yi* of spring are wood, governed by the liver; *bing* and *ding* of summer are fire, governed by the heart; *wu* and *ji* of late summer are earth, governed by the spleen; *geng* and *xin* of autumn are metal, governed by the lung; *ren* and *gui* of winter are water, governed by the kidney.

## Notes

The wind of the five zang is distinguished by season and day.
