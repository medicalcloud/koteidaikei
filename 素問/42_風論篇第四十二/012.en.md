# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When long-standing wind enters the centre, there are intestinal wind and diarrhoea with undigested food.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When long-standing wind enters the centre ... undigested food"] Wind in the intestines rises and fumigates the stomach; therefore food is not transformed and comes out below. 飱泄 means food coming out undigested.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi says, "飱泄 is diarrhoea in which water and grain are not separated."

## Notes

Intestinal wind and diarrhoea with undigested food.
