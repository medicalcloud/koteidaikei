# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When one is struck by wind after washing the hair, there is head wind.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When one is struck by wind after washing the hair, there is head wind"] One washes the hair, is struck by wind, and it lodges in the head; hence it is called head wind.

## Notes

Head wind.
