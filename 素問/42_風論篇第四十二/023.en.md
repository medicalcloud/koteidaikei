# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leaking wind: there may be much sweating; one can never wear just a single layer; on eating, sweat comes out; when severe, the body sweats, with panting and aversion to wind; the clothes are always wet; the mouth is dry with frequent thirst; and one cannot undertake toil.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Leaking wind ... cannot undertake toil"] There is wind heat in the spleen and stomach; therefore one cannot wear a single layer. The interstices are open and loose; therefore sweat comes out on eating. When severe, wind presses on the lung; therefore the body sweats, there is panting and aversion to wind, the clothes are wet, the mouth dry with frequent thirst. When the body toils there is panting; therefore one cannot undertake toil.
Song editors: Sun Simiao says, "Catching wind while drunk is leaking wind. Its signs are aversion to wind, much sweating, shortness of breath, dry mouth and frequent thirst; near clothing the body is hot as fire; on facing food sweat flows like rain; the joints are languid and one does not wish to exert oneself."

## Notes

Leaking wind.
- 常不可単衣 can be read either as being unable to wear only a single layer (feeling cold) or being unable to wear even a single layer (feeling hot); the translation follows Wang Bing.
