# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Heart wind: much sweating, aversion to wind, scorched and cracked [lips], prone to anger and intimidation, a red complexion; when the illness is severe, speech is not easy; the diagnostic sign is at the mouth, where the colour is red.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Heart wind ... where the colour is red"] 焦絶 means the lips are scorched and their lines broken. Why? Because heat makes the skin peel. When wind presses on the heart, the spirit is disordered; therefore one is prone to anger and intimidates people. A branch of the heart vessel rises from the heart connector alongside the throat and governs the tongue; therefore when the illness is severe, speech is not easy. The mouth and lips are red; hence the diagnosis is there. Red is the heart's colour.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* lacks the character 嚇.
