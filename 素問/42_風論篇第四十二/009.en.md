# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When one is struck by wind after drinking alcohol, there is leaking wind.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When one is struck by wind after drinking alcohol, there is leaking wind"] Heat stagnates and the interstices loosen; struck by wind, one sweats much, as if fluid were leaking; hence it is called leaking wind. The classic fully names it alcohol wind.

## Notes

Leaking wind. Wang Bing's "the classic fully names it alcohol wind" presumably refers to "alcohol wind" in the *Bingneng lun* (chapter 46).
