# 素問 風論篇第四十二 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When one sweats after entering the bedchamber and is struck by wind, there is inner wind.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When one sweats after entering the bedchamber ... inner wind"] Within, one consumes one's essence; outside, the interstices open; wind attacks through the [bedchamber] within; hence it is called inner wind. The classic fully names it taxation wind.

## Notes

Inner wind. Wang Bing's "taxation wind" presumably refers to taxation wind in the *Ping rebing lun* (chapter 33).
