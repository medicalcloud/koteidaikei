# 素問 逆調論篇第三十四 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "When a person's body is not cold from clothing and there is no cold qi within, yet cold arises from within, why is this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a person's body is not cold from clothing ... why is this?"] It says he does not know what the original cause is.

## Notes

Huangdi asks the cause of cold without an external cause.
