# 素問 逆調論篇第三十四 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Such a person has always had prevailing kidney qi and made water his business; the greater yang qi declines, the kidney fat withers and does not grow, and one water cannot overcome two fires. The kidney is water and generates in the bones; when the kidney does not generate, the marrow cannot be full; therefore the cold is severe and reaches the bones. The reason one does not shiver is that the liver is one yang, the heart is two yang, and the kidney is a solitary zang; one water cannot overcome two fires; therefore one cannot shiver. The illness is called bone impediment. Such a person will suffer contraction of the joints."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Such a person ... the cold is severe and reaches the bones"] "Made water his business" means indulging desire [sexual activity].

[On "The reason one does not shiver ... contraction of the joints"] When the kidney does not generate, the marrow is not full; when the marrow is not full, the sinews dry and shrink; therefore the joints contract and are cramped.

## Notes

Bone impediment, in which the kidney essence is damaged, the marrow is not full and cold reaches the bones.
- 以水為事: Wang Bing takes it as indulging in sexual activity; others take it as working with or in water.
- 肝一陽也，心二陽也: the yang (fire) of the liver and heart makes two, which the one water of the kidney cannot overcome. Why the liver is called "one yang" is not clear from the text.
