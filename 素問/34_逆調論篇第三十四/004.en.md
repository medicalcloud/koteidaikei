# 素問 逆調論篇第三十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Such a person has much impediment qi; yang qi is scant and yin qi abundant; therefore the body is cold as if just out of water."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Such a person has much impediment qi ... as if just out of water"] It says this arises naturally from the yin and yang of form and qi, not from cold clothing or cold within.

## Notes

When by constitution yang qi is scant and yin qi abundant, the body is cold.
