# 素問 逆調論篇第三十四 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Such a person has depleted yin qi and abundant yang qi. The four limbs are yang; the two yang reinforce each other while the yin qi is depleted and scant. A little water cannot extinguish an abundant fire, and yang governs alone. What governs alone cannot generate and grow; it merely prevails alone and stops there. Those who, on meeting wind, feel as if roasted, as if on fire, will suffer melting of the flesh."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Such a person ... merely prevails alone and stops there"] Water is yin and fire yang. Now yang qi is in surplus and yin qi insufficient; hence "a little water cannot extinguish an abundant fire". 治 means dominate; 勝 means be abundant; hence "prevails alone and stops there".

[On "Those who, on meeting wind ... melting of the flesh"] 爍 means wasting. It says that in time the person's flesh will waste away.
Song editors: On examination, 如炙如火 should be 如炙於火 following the *Taisu*.

## Notes

In a constitution of yin depletion and yang abundance, the yang of the limbs and the external yang reinforce each other, the heat becomes severe, and in time the flesh wastes.
