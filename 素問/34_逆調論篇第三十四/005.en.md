# 素問 逆調論篇第三十四 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There are people whose four limbs are hot and who, on meeting wind and cold, feel as if roasted, as if on fire. Why is this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "There are people whose four limbs are hot ... Why is this?"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition lacks the two characters 如火. The *Taisu* reads 如炙於火 "as if roasted over fire"; the *Taisu* text should be followed.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why the limbs are hot and become burning hot on exposure to wind and cold. There is no note by Wang Bing.
