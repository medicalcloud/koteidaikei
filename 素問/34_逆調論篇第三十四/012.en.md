# 素問 逆調論篇第三十四 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Being unable to lie down with noisy breathing is counterflow of the *Yangming*. The three yang of the foot run downward; now they counterflow and run upward; therefore the breathing is noisy. The *Yangming* is the stomach vessel; the stomach is the sea of the six fu, and its qi also runs downward. When the *Yangming* counterflows and cannot follow its course, one cannot lie down. The *Xiajing* says, 'When the stomach is not in harmony, one cannot lie down in peace' — this is what is meant. Having daily life as usual but noisy breathing is counterflow of the collaterals of the lung. The collaterals cannot rise and fall with the meridians; therefore [the qi] lingers in the meridians and does not move. The illness the collaterals cause is slight; therefore daily life is as usual but the breathing is noisy. Being unable to lie down and dyspnea when lying is the lodging of water qi. Water flows following the fluids; the kidney is the water zang, governs the fluids, and governs lying down and dyspnea."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The *Yangming* is the stomach vessel; the stomach is the sea of the six fu"] The sea of water and grain.

[On "The *Xiajing* says ... this is what is meant"] The *Xiajing* is a classic of high antiquity.

## Notes

Gives the causes of three of the six kinds (counterflow of the *Yangming*, counterflow of the lung collaterals, water qi).
- 胃不和則臥不安 is a frequently quoted phrase.
