# 素問 逆調論篇第三十四 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good"] Examining the purport of what the classic explains, the three cases — unable to lie down with silent breathing, able to lie down but panting when walking, and unable either to lie down or walk and panting — are all missing and not discussed; this too is an ancient loss of slips.

## Notes

This ends the chapter. Wang Bing notes that the explanations for three of the six kinds asked about are missing.
- Daizhige (重广补注黄帝内经素问) has 巻十 at the end of the chapter; this marks the juan division in that source.
