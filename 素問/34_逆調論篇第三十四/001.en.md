# 素問 逆調論篇第三十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "When a person's body is unusually warm and unusually hot, and on account of this there is heat with vexation and fullness, why is this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a person's body is unusually warm ... why is this?"] It differs from the usual signs; hence "unusually".
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* lacks the three characters 為之熱.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: the cause of abnormal heat with vexation and fullness.
- 非常温也，非常熱也 is also read as "it is not due to ordinary warmth [of clothing etc.] or ordinary heat"; the translation follows Wang Bing.
