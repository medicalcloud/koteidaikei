# 素問 逆調論篇第三十四 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "When a person's flesh is numb, even if covered with clothing and padding, it is still numb. What disease is this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a person's flesh is numb ... What disease is this?"] 苛 means 𤸷重 (numbness and heaviness).

## Notes

Huangdi asks about the symptom of dull, heavy sensation in the flesh.
- 𤸷重 in Wang Bing's note is understood as numbness and heaviness, but the meaning of the character 𤸷 has not been checked.
