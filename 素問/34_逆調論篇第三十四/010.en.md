# 素問 逆調論篇第三十四 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The ying-nutrient qi is deficient and the wei-defensive qi excess. When the ying-nutrient qi is deficient there is numbness; when the wei-defensive qi is deficient there is loss of function; when both ying-nutrient and wei-defense are deficient, there is numbness and loss of function, while the flesh remains as before. When the person's body and will do not possess each other, it is called death."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the person's body and will do not possess each other, it is called death"] When the body acts, the will does not respond; when the will acts, the body does not cooperate; the two seem not to possess each other.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 三十日死 "death in thirty days" for 曰死.

## Notes

Disorders of sensation and movement due to deficiency of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi.
- "The ying-nutrient qi is deficient and the wei-defensive qi excess" seems inconsistent with "when the wei-defensive qi is deficient there is loss of function", and the passage is read in various ways; the translation keeps the text as it is.
- According to the Song editors, the *Jiayi jing* reads "death in thirty days".
