# 素問 逆調論篇第三十四 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Among people with counterflowing qi, there are those who cannot lie down and whose breathing is noisy; those who cannot lie down and whose breathing is silent; those whose daily life is as usual but whose breathing is noisy; those who can lie down but pant when walking; those who can neither lie down nor walk and who pant; and those who cannot lie down and pant when lying. Which zang causes each? I wish to hear the reasons."

## Notes

Huangdi asks the causes of six kinds of breathing disorder due to counterflowing qi. There is no note by Wang Bing.
