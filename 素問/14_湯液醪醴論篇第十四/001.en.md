# 素問 湯液醪醴論篇第十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "How are decoctions and wines of the five grains made?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] 液 means a clear liquid; 醪醴 means wines.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: how to make decoctions (clear liquids) and wines from the five grains. The chapter title comes from this.
