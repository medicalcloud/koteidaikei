# 素問 湯液醪醴論篇第十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It obtains the harmony of heaven and earth and what suits high and low, so it can be perfectly complete; it is cut and taken at the right time, so it can be perfectly firm."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] Rice grows from the essence of yin water and bears on its head the qi of heavenly yang; only when the two unite does it transform and mature. Hence "it obtains the harmony of heaven and earth and can be perfectly complete". The qi of autumn is strong and sharp, and frost and dew congeal; rice is harvested in winter. Hence "it is cut and taken at the right time and can be perfectly firm".

## Notes

Rice obtains the harmony of heaven and earth (the yin of water and the yang of heaven) and so is complete; harvested in autumn-winter, its straw is firm.
