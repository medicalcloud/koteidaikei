# 素問 湯液醪醴論篇第十四 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why is it so?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] It asks how they can be complete and firm.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why rice is complete and its straw firm.
