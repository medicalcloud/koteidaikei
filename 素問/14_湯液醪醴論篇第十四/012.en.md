# 素問 湯液醪醴論篇第十四 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Needles and stones are a means. If the essence and spirit do not advance and the will and intention are not ordered, the illness cannot be cured. Now the essence is ruined and the spirit gone, and the nutritive and defensive qi cannot be recovered. Why? Desires are endless and worries never cease, so the essential qi slackens and is ruined, the nutritive qi becomes rough and the defensive qi is removed; therefore the spirit departs and the illness is not cured."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Needles and stones are a means"] It says that the spirit cannot make use of the wondrous function of needles and stones. Why? Because the will and intention go against what the teacher shows.

[On "If the essence and spirit do not advance ... the illness cannot be cured"] Because in their actions they depart from the Way and squander the heavenly truth.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition reads, "If the essence and spirit advance and the will and intention are settled, the illness can be cured"; the *Taisu* reads, "If the essence and spirit overflow and the will and intention scatter, the illness cannot be cured."

[On "Now the essence is ruined ... the illness is not cured"] The essence and spirit are the source of life; the nutritive and defensive qi are the masters of qi. If the masters of qi do not assist, the source of life also wastes away; if the spirit does not dwell within, how can the illness be cured?

## Notes

Needles and stones are only a means; unless the patient's essence and spirit advance and will and intention are ordered, illness cannot be cured. Desire and worry ruin the essential qi, the nutritive and defensive qi fail, and the spirit departs, so the illness is not cured.
- According to the Song editors, Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu* word the sentence differently.
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 営 for 栄.
