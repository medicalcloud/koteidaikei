# 素問 湯液醪醴論篇第十四 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "In the present age they do not necessarily cure. Why?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] It asks why it is not necessarily as in the age of middle antiquity.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why decoctions and wines do not necessarily cure in the present age.
