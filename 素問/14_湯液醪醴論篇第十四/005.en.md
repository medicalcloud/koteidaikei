# 素問 湯液醪醴論篇第十四 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "The sages of high antiquity made decoctions and wines, but made them without using them. Why?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks why the sages of high antiquity made decoctions and wines but did not use them. There is no note by Wang Bing.
