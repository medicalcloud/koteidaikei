# 素問 湯液醪醴論篇第十四 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "One must use rice and cook it with rice straw: rice is complete and rice straw is firm."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] "Firm" means relying on its firm strength; "complete" means taking its completeness. If it is complete, the wine is clear and cool; if firm and strong, the qi is swift and the effect fast.

## Notes

Decoctions and wines must be made from rice and cooked with rice straw, because rice is complete and the straw is firm.
