# 素問 湯液醪醴論篇第十四 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In the present age one must compound toxic drugs to attack the interior and use sharp stones, needles and moxa to treat the exterior."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] It says the methods differ from those of antiquity.

## Notes

In the present age decoctions and wines alone are not enough; one must attack the interior with toxic drugs and treat the exterior with sharp stones, needles and moxa.
- 斉 is taken in the sense of 剤 "to compound".
