# 素問 湯液醪醴論篇第十四 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Treat for balance by the steelyard and beam; remove the long-accumulated stale chaff; gently move the four extremities; warm the clothing; needle the place contralaterally to restore the form. Open the ghost gates and cleanse the clean storehouse; the essence will then submit in season; the five yang will be spread; dredge and wash the five zang. Thus the essence arises of itself, the form becomes abundant of itself, bones and flesh preserve each other, and the great qi becomes balanced."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Treat for balance by the steelyard and beam; remove the long-accumulated stale chaff"] Song editors: The *Taisu* writes 茎 for 莝.

[On the whole passage] "Treating for balance by the steelyard and beam" means examining whether the pulse is floating or sinking. A floating pulse means [the illness] is in the exterior; a sinking pulse means it is in the interior. What is in the interior is drained; what is outside is sweated out; hence it continues below with "open the ghost gates and cleanse the clean storehouse". "Remove the long-accumulated stale chaff" means removing water that has accumulated for a long time, just as grass stalks must not remain long in the body. The Quan edition writes 草莝. "Gently move the four extremities" means slightly moving the four limbs so that the yang qi gradually spreads and moves; hence it also says "warm the clothing". When the channels are full, the network vessels overflow; when the network vessels overflow, one needles them contralaterally to regulate them, so that the form returns as before and is not swollen; hence "needle the place contralaterally to restore the form". "Opening the ghost gates" is opening the dark mansions to send off the qi. The five yang are the yang qi of the five zang. "Cleansing the clean storehouse" means draining the bladder so that water departs. When the pulse is harmonious, the qi of the five essences submit in season to the kidney. Then the yang of the five zang gradually spreads, and the foul qi outside the five zang is also removed. Thus the essence and marrow arise of themselves and the flesh of the body becomes abundant of itself. When the zang and fu are in harmony, the qi of bones and flesh again preserve and embrace each other, and the qi of the great channels then return to balance.

## Notes

The treatment for the oedema of the previous unit: judging exterior or interior by the floating or sinking of the pulse, removing the accumulated water, gently moving the limbs and warming the clothing, needling contralaterally to restore the form, opening the ghost gates (sweat pores) to sweat and cleansing the clean storehouse (bladder) to promote urination. Then the essence arises, the form becomes abundant and the qi becomes balanced.
- "Open the ghost gates and cleanse the clean storehouse" became a well-known phrase for treating oedema by sweating and diuresis.
- "Remove the long-accumulated stale chaff": Wang Bing takes it as removing long-accumulated water. According to the Song editors, the *Taisu* writes 茎 for 莝.
