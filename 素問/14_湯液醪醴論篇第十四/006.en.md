# 素問 湯液醪醴論篇第十四 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "From of old, the sages made decoctions and wines as a provision. In high antiquity they made decoctions, but made them without taking them. In the age of middle antiquity, the Way and virtue declined somewhat and evil qi arrived at times; taking them was entirely effective."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "From of old, the sages made decoctions and wines as a provision"] The sages took pity on living beings, guarded against illness before it sprouted and set out their methods to provide for the unforeseen.

[On "In high antiquity ... made them without taking them"] The sages do not treat what is already ill but treat what is not yet ill; therefore they only prepared them as a provision and did not take them.

[On "In the age of middle antiquity ... taking them was entirely effective"] Although the Way and virtue declined somewhat and evil qi arrived at times, since their hearts were still close to the Way, taking them was entirely effective.

## Notes

In high antiquity decoctions and wines were made only as a provision and not taken; in middle antiquity, when the Way declined somewhat and evil qi arrived, they were taken and were entirely effective.
