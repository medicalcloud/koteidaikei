# 素問 湯液醪醴論篇第十四 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "When the body is worn out and the blood exhausted [by treatment], yet no effect is achieved — why?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks why treatment pursued to exhaustion has no effect. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 応 for 立.
