# 素問 湯液醪醴論篇第十四 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "When an illness first arises, it is extremely slight and extremely subtle, and must first enter and bind in the skin. Now good physicians all declare the illness formed and call it adverse, so that needles and stones cannot treat it and good drugs cannot reach it. Now good physicians all have the methods and keep to the rules; relatives and brothers, near and far, [gather]; [the patient's] voice is heard daily by their ears and the five colours seen daily by their eyes; yet the illness is not cured. Why did they not act earlier?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why did they not act earlier?"] Song editors: In another edition 暇 is written 謂.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why, though illness begins slight in the skin, and good physicians have the methods and see and hear the patient daily, the illness becomes established and incurable — why was it not treated earlier?
- 亦何暇不早乎 is difficult; according to the Song editors another edition writes 謂 for 暇.
