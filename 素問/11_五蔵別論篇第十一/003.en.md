# 素問 五蔵別論篇第十一 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why is it that the qi opening alone is the master of the five zang?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] The qi opening is the *cunkou*, also called the pulse opening. Because the abundance and decline of the qi can be observed at the *cunkou*, it is called the qi opening; because the movement and stillness of the pulse can be felt there, it is called the pulse opening. All are taken at the same place, one body-*cun* behind the thenar eminence of the hand; this is the *cunkou*.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why the state of the five zang can be known from the qi-opening (radial) pulse alone.
- Wang Bing explains that qi opening, *cunkou* and pulse opening are names for the same site.
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 五臓之主 for 五蔵主.
