# 素問 鍼解篇第五十四 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "One is heaven, two earth, three human, four the seasons, five the notes, six the pitch-pipes, seven the stars, eight the winds, nine the fields; the body also corresponds to them. Each needle has its suitable use; hence they are called the nine needles. The human skin corresponds to heaven, the flesh to earth, the vessels to human, the sinews to the seasons, the voice to the notes, the combining of yin and yang qi to the pitch-pipes, the teeth, face and eyes to the stars, the outgoing and incoming breath to the winds, and the nine orifices and three hundred sixty-five network vessels to the fields. Therefore the first needle is for the skin, the second for the flesh, the third for the vessels, the fourth for the sinews, the fifth for the bones, the sixth regulates yin and yang, the seventh benefits the essence, the eighth removes wind, the ninth opens the nine orifices and removes the qi of the three hundred sixty-five joints. This is what is called each having what it governs.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the body also corresponds to them. Each needle has its suitable use; hence they are called the nine needles"] Song editors: On examination, this text roughly corresponds to the *Lingshu jing*.

[On "The human skin corresponds to heaven"] It covers things; it is the image of heaven.

[On "the flesh to earth"] Soft, thick, calm and still; it is the image of earth.

[On "the vessels to human"] Rising, declining and changing; it is the image of the human.

[On "the sinews to the seasons"] Firm, solid and truly settled; it is the image of the seasons.

[On "the voice to the notes"] Because it contains the five notes.

[On "the combining of yin and yang qi to the pitch-pipes"] Crossing and meeting, the qi communicates, generating each other without interruption; this is the image of the pitch-pipes.
Song editors: Another edition writes 度 for 気.

[On "the teeth, face and eyes to the stars"] The human face corresponds to the seven stars in that the face has seven openings corresponding to them.
Song editors: On examination, this note is the words of Quan Yuanqi.

[On "the outgoing and incoming breath to the winds"] Moving out and coming and going; it is the image of the winds.

[On "the nine orifices and three hundred sixty-five network vessels to the fields"] The outside of the body; it is the image of the fields.

[On "Therefore the first needle ... each having what it governs"] One is the arrowhead needle, two the round needle, three the spoon needle, four the sharp-edged needle, five the 䤵 (sword) needle, six the round-sharp needle, seven the filiform needle, eight the long needle, nine the large needle.
Song editors: Another edition writes 鈹 for 䤵.

## Notes

The nine needles are matched with the nine of heaven, earth, human, seasons, notes, pitch-pipes, stars, winds and fields, and what each governs is stated. Qibo's speech continues to unit 007.
- According to the Song editors, Wang Bing's note on "the teeth, face and eyes to the stars" is the words of Quan Yuanqi.
