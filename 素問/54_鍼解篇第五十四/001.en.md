# 素問 鍼解篇第五十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I wish to hear the explanation of the nine needles and the way of depletion and repletion."

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, which explains passages of the needling classics (mainly from the *Jiuzhen shi'er yuan* and *Xiaozhen jie* chapters of the *Lingshu* and the *Baoming quanxing lun*). There is no note by Wang Bing.
