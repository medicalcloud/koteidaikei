# 素問 鍼解篇第五十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What is called Sanli is three *cun* below the knee. What is called *fuzhi* means the division is easy to see when the knee is raised. Juxu is where the shin alone sinks when the foot is lifted. Xialian is where it sinks down."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What is called Sanli is three *cun* below the knee. What is called *fuzhi*"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition writes 低胻 for 跗之, and the *Taisu* writes 付之. In view of the *Gukong lun*, 跗之 is suspected to be 跗上 "on the instep".

[On "What is called *fuzhi* means the division is easy to see when the knee is raised"] Sanli is the name of a point, exactly three *cun* below the knee between the divisions of flesh of the two sinews outside the shin. Pressing it very hard stops the pulse on the instep; hence "the division is easy to see when the knee is raised".

[On "Juxu is where the shin alone sinks when the foot is lifted"] Juxu is the name of a point. 蹻 means lift. To take the lower Juxu, one should lift the foot; then the space between the two sinews outside the shin sinks.

[On "Xialian is where it sinks down"] To find the Xialian point, the place between the two sinews outside the shin that alone sinks is the spot.

## Notes

The method of locating Sanli, Juxu and Xialian. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 002, ends here.
- 跗之 is obscure; the Song editors suspect an error for 跗上. The translation keeps the characters.
