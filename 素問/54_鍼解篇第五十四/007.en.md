# 素問 鍼解篇第五十四 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The human heart and intention correspond to the eight winds; human qi corresponds to heaven; human hair, teeth, ears, eyes and the five voices correspond to the five notes and six pitch-pipes; the human yin and yang vessels, blood and qi correspond to earth; the human liver and eyes correspond to it as nine. Nine orifices, three hundred sixty-five. With the human one, observe movement and stillness; with heaven two, observe the five colours; the seven stars correspond to it, to observe hair lacking lustre; with the five notes one, observe *gong*, *shang*, *jue*, *zhi* and *yu*; the six pitch-pipes' surplus and insufficiency correspond to it; with earth two, one, observe high and low and surplus; the nine fields, one, the joint transport points correspond to it, to observe closed joints; with human three, change, one part, observe a person's teeth leaking, much or little blood; ten parts, the change of *jue*; five parts, to observe slackness and tension; six parts, insufficiency; three parts, cold and joints; the ninth part, the four seasons, a person's cold, warmth, dryness and damp; the four seasons, one, correspond to it, to observe what is contrary; one, the four directions, each making an explanation."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The human heart and intention correspond to the eight winds"] Moving and still without taking form; it is the image of wind.

[On "human qi corresponds to heaven"] Circulating without rest; it is the image of heaven.

[On "human hair, teeth, ears, eyes and the five voices correspond to the five notes and six pitch-pipes"] Hair and teeth grow, ears and eyes are clear and open, and the five voices correspond alike; therefore they correspond to the five notes and six pitch-pipes.

[On "the human yin and yang vessels, blood and qi correspond to earth"] Human yin and yang cross and meet to generate and complete; the vessels, blood and qi have emptiness and fullness, rise and decline; therefore they correspond to earth.

[On "the human liver and eyes correspond to it as nine"] Liver qi communicates with the eyes. The generating number of wood is three; three times three corresponds to nine.

[On "Nine orifices, three hundred sixty-five"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition lacks these seven characters.

[On "With the human one, observe movement and stillness ... each making an explanation"] These one hundred twenty-four characters come from worm-eaten slips with rotted text; the meaning is broken and incomplete and cannot be traced. But since it is a book of high antiquity, it is recorded here for the time being, awaiting a complete edition later.
Song editors: On examination, Mr Wang says one hundred twenty-four characters, but there are now one hundred twenty-three; another character has been lost.

## Notes

According to Wang Bing, the latter part of this unit is corrupted text with worm-eaten gaps, whose meaning cannot be determined, and is recorded as it stands. The translation is a provisional rendering close to the characters, and the punctuation is also provisional. This ends the chapter. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 006, ends here.
