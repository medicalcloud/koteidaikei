# 素問 鍼解篇第五十四 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I have heard that the nine needles correspond above to heaven and earth, the four seasons, and yin and yang. I wish to hear the method so that it can be handed down to later generations as a norm."

## Notes

Huangdi asks about the correspondence of the nine needles with heaven and earth, the four seasons, and yin and yang. There is no note by Wang Bing.
