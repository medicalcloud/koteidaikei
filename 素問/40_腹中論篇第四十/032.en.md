# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Febrile illness is of the yang vessels, due to the movement of the three yang. When Renying is once abundant it is the lesser yang; twice abundant, the greater yang; three times abundant, the *yangming*, which enters yin. Yang enters yin; therefore the illness is in the head and abdomen, with distension and headache."

## Notes

Pain in febrile illness occurs because abundance of the yang vessels appears at the Renying pulse and yang enters yin. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- According to the Song editors (unit 033), a similar passage occurs in the *Liujie zangxiang lun*, and the *Jiayi jing* lacks the three characters 入陰也.
