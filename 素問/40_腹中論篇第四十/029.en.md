# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. How does one know that a woman is pregnant and the child is developing?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks how pregnancy is recognized. There is no note by Wang Bing.
