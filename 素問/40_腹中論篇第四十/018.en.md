# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The illness is called hidden beam; its root is wind. Its qi overflows into the large intestine and clings to the *huang*; the source of the *huang* is below the navel; therefore there is pain around the navel. It must not be moved; moving it produces an illness of rough urination."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The illness is called hidden beam"] These twenty-six characters are a displacement and are in the *Qibing lun*. Without these twenty-six characters, the text below would have no basis.
Song editors: On examination, there is no commentary here at all; it is all in the *Qibing lun* in the following juan.

[On "its root is wind"] These four characters were originally in this chapter and also occur in the *Qibing lun*.

[On "Its qi overflows into the large intestine ... rough urination"] This too is the thoroughfare vessel. "Below the navel" means Boyang, two and a half body-*cun* below the navel. The *Lingshu jing* says, "The source of the *huang* is called Boyang."

## Notes

Another hidden beam, with pain around the navel.
- According to Wang Bing, the text of this unit (twenty-six characters) is a displacement that also occurs in the *Qibing lun* (chapter 47); according to the Song editors, the commentary is all on the *Qibing lun* side. Which characters Wang Bing's "twenty-six characters" and "four characters" refer to is not clear from the notes alone.
