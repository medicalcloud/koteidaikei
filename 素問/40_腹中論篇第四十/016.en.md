# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Below, it follows the genitals and surely discharges pus and blood; above, it presses on the stomach duct, emerges at the diaphragm, and forms an internal abscess alongside the stomach duct. This is a long-standing illness and hard to treat. If it lies above the navel it is adverse; if below the navel, compliant. Do not move it, but remove it repeatedly. It is discussed in *Cifa*."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Below, it follows the genitals ... an internal abscess alongside the stomach duct"] It lies exactly in the region of the thoroughfare and belt vessels. The belt vessel arises at the lower ribs, circles the body once and connects transversely below the navel. The thoroughfare vessel, together with the network vessel of the foot lesser yin, arises below the kidney, emerges at Qijie and follows the inner thigh; its ascending branch emerges three body-*cun* below the navel in the region of Guanyuan, runs straight up alongside the navel, follows the abdomen and meets at the throat. Therefore when the illness is in this region, the lower abdomen is swollen with roots above, below, left and right. Its upper and lower parts are hard and full as if a beam were hidden; hence the illness is called hidden beam and cannot be treated. Because it wraps a large amount of pus and blood outside the intestines and stomach, pressing it causes unbearable pain and oppression; therefore each hard press leads to death. This is because the descending part of the thoroughfare vessel connects with the genitals and the ascending part follows the abdomen: above it presses close to the stomach duct, below it presses on the genitals. If it presses on the genitals, pus and blood are discharged in the stool; if it presses close to the stomach, the qi of the illness rises and emerges at the diaphragm and grows its abscess alongside the stomach duct within. Why? Because there is originally a large amount of pus and blood outside the intestines and stomach. 生 should be 出, a copying error.
Song editors: The *Taisu* writes 使胃 for 侠胃.

[On "This is a long-standing illness ... remove it repeatedly"] If the wrapped pus and blood lies above the navel, it gradually harms the heart zang; therefore it is adverse. If it lies below the navel, it is somewhat farther from the heart and can still be attacked gradually; therefore it is compliant. 従 means compliant; 亟 means repeatedly; 奪 means remove. It says that one must not move it but simply remove it repeatedly.

[On "It is discussed in *Cifa*"] Now lost from the classic.

## Notes

The pathology, prognosis and principle of treatment of hidden beam.
- Wang Bing takes 生 in 生鬲 as an error for 出.
- *Cifa* is a lost chapter (see also Wang Bing's note in chapter 33).
