# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good"] Song editors: The *Liujie zangxiang lun* says, "When Renying is once abundant the illness is in the lesser yang; twice abundant, in the greater yang; three times abundant, in the *yangming*," which agrees with this discourse. Moreover, in the *Jiayi jing* the three characters 入陰也 after 三盛陽明 are absent.

## Notes

This ends the chapter. There is no note by Wang Bing.
