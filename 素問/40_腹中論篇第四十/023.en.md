# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. There is an illness with swelling of the breast, pain in the neck, fullness of the chest and distension of the abdomen. What illness is this, and how is it contracted?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good. There is an illness with swelling of the breast"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 㿈腫.

[On "pain in the neck ... how is it contracted?"] 膺 is the side of the chest; 頸 is the front of the nape; 胸 is between the breasts.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about an illness with breast swelling, neck pain, chest fullness and abdominal distension.
