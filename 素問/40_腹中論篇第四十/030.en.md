# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The body has illness but there is no evil pulse."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The body has illness but there is no evil pulse"] "Illness" means amenorrhoea. The *Maifa* says, "When the pulse at the *chi* comes and breaks off, it is amenorrhoea; when the menses do not flow and the pulse at the *chi* is cut off, it is amenorrhoea." Now if there is amenorrhoea but the pulse is as usual, it is a sign of pregnancy; hence "the body has illness but there is no evil pulse".

## Notes

When the menses have stopped (seeming like illness) but the pulse is normal, it is pregnancy.
- The "pulse method" quoted in Wang Bing's note is close to 尺脈來而斷絕者，月水不利 in the *Maijing* (as quoted in the *Nüke jinglun*).
