# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why does it sometimes recur?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why does it sometimes recur?"] 復 means recurring as before.

## Notes

Huangdi asks the reason for recurrence.
