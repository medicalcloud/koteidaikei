# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There is an illness with propping fullness of the chest and flanks that hinders eating. When the illness comes, one first smells a rank, fishy odour, discharges clear fluid, first spits blood, the four limbs are cold, the eyes are dizzy, and from time to time there is bleeding from front and back. What is the illness called, and how is it contracted?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "There is an illness with propping fullness ... how is it contracted?"] Clear fluid is clear water, also called clear mucus; clear mucus means fluid overflowing and running down from the leaking orifices, the water coming out clear and cold. 眩 means vision spinning. "Bleeding from front and back" means bleeding from the genital and anal orifices.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about an illness with propping fullness of the chest and flanks, a fishy odour, spitting of blood, and bleeding from front and back.
