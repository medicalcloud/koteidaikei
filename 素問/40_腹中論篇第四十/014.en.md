# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It wraps a large amount of pus and blood and lies outside the intestines and stomach. It cannot be treated; each time one presses it hard in treating it, it leads to death."

## Notes

Hidden beam wraps pus and blood outside the intestines and stomach, and pressing it hard leads to death. Wang Bing's note on this is given in unit 016.
