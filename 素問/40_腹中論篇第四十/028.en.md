# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The yang qi is doubled above and in surplus above. Moxibustion makes the yang qi enter yin, and when it enters there is loss of voice. The stone needle depletes the yang qi, and when it is depleted there is mania. Wait until the qi combines and then treat it, and one can be made whole."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Moxibustion makes the yang qi enter yin ... there is mania"] Moxibustion adds the qi of fire to yang, and yang becomes abundant; therefore it enters yin. The stone needle lets the yang qi out; when the yang qi goes out, the interior is insufficient; therefore there is mania.

[On "Wait until the qi combines ... one can be made whole"] 并 means combine. If one waits for them to combine by themselves, both qi are whole; therefore it can be treated. Otherwise, applying moxibustion or the stone needle leads to one-sided victory and defeat; therefore one cannot be whole and there is loss of voice or mania.

## Notes

Why moxibustion and the stone needle cause loss of voice and mania in reversal counterflow.
