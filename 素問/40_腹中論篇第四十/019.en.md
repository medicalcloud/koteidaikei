# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "You often say that in heat in the centre and wasting in the centre one must not take rich food, aromatic herbs or mineral drugs; that mineral drugs cause epilepsy and aromatic herbs cause mania. Those with heat in the centre and wasting in the centre are all wealthy and noble people. Now if rich food is prohibited, it goes against their hearts; if aromatic herbs and mineral drugs are prohibited, the illness is not cured. I wish to hear the explanation."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "You often say ... aromatic herbs cause mania"] Drinking much and urinating often is called heat in the centre; eating much and urinating often is called wasting in the centre. Excessive joy is called epilepsy; excessive anger is called mania. 芳 means delicious.

[On "Those with heat in the centre ... I wish to hear the explanation"] Heat in the centre and wasting in the centre are caused by spleen qi overflowing upward due to sweet and fatty food; therefore eating rich food and fragrant, delicious herbs is prohibited. The *Tongping xushi lun* says, "In treating wasting-thirst, in fat noble persons fond of sweets, these are illnesses of rich food." The *Qibing lun* also says, "When the five flavours enter the mouth, they are stored in the stomach, and the spleen moves their essential qi for it; the fluids are in the spleen; therefore the mouth tastes sweet. This arises from rich, fine food. Such a person surely eats sweet and fine food often and is fat. Fat makes one hot within, sweet makes one full in the centre; therefore its qi overflows upward and turns into wasting-thirst" — this is what is meant. Wealthy and noble people are arrogant and self-indulgent, look down on others, and no one can restrain them; restraining them goes against their will, while complying adds to their illness. The Emperor considered this a hard problem and therefore asked. 高 means fat; 梁 means grain. Mineral drugs are quartz and stalactite. Aromatic herbs are rich and fine things. These five are what the wealthy and noble always take and are hard to prohibit.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why rich food, aromatic herbs and mineral drugs are prohibited for patients with heat in the centre and wasting in the centre (wasting-thirst).
- According to Wang Bing, mineral drugs means mineral medicines such as quartz and stalactite.
- The *Qibing lun* passage quoted by Wang Bing is in chapter 47.
