# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The illness is called blood desiccation. It is contracted when, in youth, one has had a great loss of blood, or has entered the bedchamber drunk, so that the qi within is exhausted and the liver harmed. Therefore the menses diminish and do not come."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The illness is called blood desiccation ... do not come"] Heavy bleeding is called loss of blood; uterine bleeding, nosebleed and bleeding with vomiting are all the same. When drunk, the blood vessels are abundant; when they are abundant, there is internal heat; if one then enters the bedchamber, the marrow fluid all descends; therefore the qi in the kidney is exhausted. The liver stores blood; losing a great deal of blood when young harms the liver. In men the semen declines and is deficient; in women the menses diminish and do not come.

## Notes

Blood desiccation due to great loss of blood in youth or sexual activity while drunk.
- 酔入房中，気竭肝傷 is read as "entered the bedchamber drunk, so that the qi within is exhausted"; Wang Bing says "the qi in the kidney is exhausted".
