# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Treat it with chicken-dung wine; with one dose there is effect, with two doses it ends."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Treat it with chicken-dung wine ... with two doses it ends"] According to the ancient *Bencao*, chicken dung does not treat drum distension; it only greatly promotes urination and is slightly cold. In present prescription methods, when it is used, it is steeped in hot water and taken.

## Notes

Drum distension is treated with chicken-dung wine, one of the few drug prescriptions in the *Suwen*.
- Wang Bing notes that in the ancient *Bencao* chicken dung does not treat drum distension but promotes urination.
