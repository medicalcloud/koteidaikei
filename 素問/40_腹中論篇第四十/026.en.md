# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Moxibustion makes one lose the voice; the stone needle makes one manic. Wait until the qi combines, and then it can be treated."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the stone needle makes one manic ... then it can be treated"] 石 means opening and breaking with a stone needle.

## Notes

For reversal counterflow both moxibustion and the stone needle are harmful; one should wait for the qi to combine before treating.
