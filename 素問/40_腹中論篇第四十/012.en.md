# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The illness is called hidden beam."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The illness is called hidden beam"] Hidden beam is the accumulation of the heart.
Song editors: On examination, this hidden beam differs greatly from the hidden beam that is the accumulation of the heart. There are more than one illness with the same name but different substance; this is such a case.

## Notes

The accumulation in the lower abdomen is called hidden beam.
- The Song editors reject Wang Bing's note, holding that this hidden beam is a different illness from the heart accumulation of the same name (as in the *Nanjing*).
