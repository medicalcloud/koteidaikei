# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The qi of aromatic herbs is rich and the qi of mineral drugs is fierce; the qi of both is urgent, swift, hard and strong. Therefore only those with relaxed hearts and harmonious natures may take these two."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The qi of aromatic herbs is rich ... may take these two"] Illness arises from overflowing spleen qi. When the qi is rich it doubly fills the spleen, and the qi of wasting heat becomes agitated and swift; when the qi is fierce it further feeds the heat. If a person's nature is harmonious and the heart relaxed, the qi even and gentle, not contending with things, serene and at ease, the spirit is not agitated or pressed and there is no fear of inner harm. Therefore only those with relaxed hearts and harmonious natures may take these two. 悍 means sharp; 堅 means fixed, firm; 勁 means strong. It says that the qi of aromatic herbs and mineral drugs is fixed, firm and lasting, strong and fierce, and does not quickly cease; these two are such.

## Notes

The qi of aromatic herbs and mineral drugs is violent, so only people of calm temperament may take them.
