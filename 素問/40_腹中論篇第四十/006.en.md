# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "This is because eating and drinking are unregulated; therefore at times one falls ill. Even though the illness is about to end, at times one falls ill again as before, because qi gathers in the abdomen."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Even though the illness is about to end ... qi gathers in the abdomen"] Unregulated eating and drinking harm the stomach. The stomach vessel runs down along the inside of the abdomen; therefore in those who fall ill at times from unregulated eating and drinking, the qi of the illness again gathers in the abdomen.

## Notes

Unregulated eating and drinking cause the recurrence.
- 雖然其病且已，時故当病 is read in various ways; the translation takes 故 as "as before".
