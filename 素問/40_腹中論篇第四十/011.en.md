# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There is an illness in which the lower abdomen is swollen, with roots above, below, left and right. What illness is this? Can it be treated?"

## Notes

There is no note by Wang Bing.
