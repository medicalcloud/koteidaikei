# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "There is an illness with fullness of the heart and abdomen, in which if one eats in the morning one cannot eat in the evening. What illness is this?"

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, which discusses illnesses within the abdomen and others in dialogue. There is no note by Wang Bing.
