# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "It is called drum distension."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is called drum distension"] The heart and abdomen are distended and full, one cannot eat a second time, and the shape is distended like a drum; hence it is called drum distension.
Song editors: The *Taisu* writes 穀 "grain" for 鼓 "drum".

## Notes

The illness in which the abdomen swells like a drum is called drum distension.
