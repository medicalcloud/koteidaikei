# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It is called cold limbs."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is called cold limbs"] It arises from counterflow of qi; hence it is called cold limbs.

## Notes

This illness is called cold limbs.
