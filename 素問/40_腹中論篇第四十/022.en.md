# 素問 腹中論篇第四十 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Hot qi is swift and fierce, and so is the qi of these drugs. When the two meet, there is fear of harming the spleen within. The spleen is earth and dislikes wood. For those who take these drugs, reassess on the *jia* and *yi* days."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Hot qi is swift and fierce ... harming the spleen within"] 慓 means swift.

[On "The spleen is earth and dislikes wood ... reassess on the *jia* and *yi* days"] When hot qi is swift and abundant, wood qi is in surplus within; therefore unless the heart is harmonious and relaxed, agitated anger arises often; when it does, hot qi harms the spleen through wood. *Jia* and *yi* are wood; therefore on the *jia* and *yi* days one reassesses the increase or decrease of the spleen illness.

## Notes

Hot qi and drug qi combine to harm the spleen.
- Wang Bing's note writes 慄 as the headword, but it is presumably a note on 慓 in the text.
