# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *dingmao* year, the first qi, by the heavenly number, begins at seventy-six *ke* and ends at sixty-two and a half *ke*; the second qi begins at sixty-two *ke* six *fen* and ends at fifty *ke*; the third qi begins at fifty-one *ke* and ends at thirty-seven and a half *ke*; the fourth qi begins at thirty-seven *ke* six *fen* and ends at twenty-five *ke*; the fifth qi begins at twenty-six *ke* and ends at twelve and a half *ke*; the sixth qi begins at twelve *ke* six *fen* and ends at the hundredth *ke* of the water clock. This is the so-called six-four, the number of heaven.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the *dingmao* year, the first qi, by the heavenly number, begins at seventy-six *ke*"] The first *ke* of the beginning of *hai*.
Song editors: The years *xinwei*, *yihai*, 巳 [*ji*]*mao*, *guiwei*, *dinghai*, *xinmao*, *yiwei*, 巳 [*ji*]*hai*, *guimao*, *dingwei*, *xinhai*, *yimao*, *jiwei* and *guihai* are the same as this—the so-called *mao*, *wei* and *hai* years, whose qi meet and agree.

[On "and ends at sixty-two and a half *ke*"] The middle of *you*.

[On "the second qi begins at sixty-two *ke* six *fen*"] North of the middle of *you*.

[On "and ends at fifty *ke*"] Four *ke* after *wei*.

[On "the third qi begins at fifty-one *ke*"] The second *ke* of the beginning of *shen*.

[On "and ends at thirty-seven and a half *ke*"] The middle of *wu*.

[On "the fourth qi begins at thirty-seven *ke* six *fen*"] West of the middle of *wu*.

[On "and ends at twenty-five *ke*"] Four *ke* after *chen*.

[On "the fifth qi begins at twenty-six *ke*"] The first *ke* of the beginning of *si*.

[On "and ends at twelve and a half *ke*"] The middle of *mao*.

[On "the sixth qi begins at twelve *ke* six *fen*"] South of the middle of *mao*.

[On "and ends at the hundredth *ke* of the water clock"] Four *ke* after *chou*.

## Notes

The times of beginning and ending of the six qi in the *dingmao* year.
- 巳卯 and 巳亥 in the Song editors' note are read as 己卯 and 己亥.
- Wang Bing's "the second *ke* of the beginning of *shen*" does not agree with "the first *ke* of the beginning of *shen*" given for the same fifty-one *ke* elsewhere (units 038 and 040). It may be an error, but this has not been checked.
