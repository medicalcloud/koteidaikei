# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §57 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The rising and descending of qi is heaven and earth acting in alternation."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The rising and descending of qi is heaven and earth acting in alternation"] Rising means going up; descending means going down. When rising reaches its extreme, there is descent; when descent reaches its extreme, there is rising. Since rising and descending never cease, the alternating action of heaven and earth is manifest.

## Notes

The rising and descending of qi is the alternating action of heaven and earth.
