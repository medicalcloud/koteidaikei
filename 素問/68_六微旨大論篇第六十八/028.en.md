# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Those struck in [a year of] the law enforcer fall ill quickly and dangerously; those struck in [a year of] the decree executor fall ill slowly and lingeringly; those struck in [a year of] the noble person fall ill violently and die."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Those struck in [a year of] the law enforcer fall ill quickly and dangerously"] The law enforcer is the measuring line of officials; when it itself acts perversely, the illness is quick and dangerous.

[On "those struck in [a year of] the decree executor fall ill slowly and lingeringly"] A regional lord has no power of law enforcement; so there is no quick harm, and the illness merely lingers.

[On "those struck in [a year of] the noble person fall ill violently and die"] By right there should be no transgression [against it]; hence the illness is violent and fatal.

## Notes

The differences in illness when struck by a pathogen in years of heavenly tally, year-meeting and supreme-one heavenly tally.
- For 特 in 徐而特, the other sources have 持 (see the variants), and Wang Bing also explains it as "lingering". The translation reads it as 持.
