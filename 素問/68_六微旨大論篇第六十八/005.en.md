# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There are cases where [the qi] arrives at its time, cases where the time arrives but it does not, and cases where it arrives in excess. Why?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "There are cases where [the qi] arrives at its time ... Why?"] All refer to the six qi of heaven. The first qi rises some fifteen days before the Beginning of Spring; the second, third, fourth, fifth and final qi arrive in order, each governing a division of sixty days plus eighty-seven and a half *ke*.

## Notes

A question on the different ways the six qi arrive.
