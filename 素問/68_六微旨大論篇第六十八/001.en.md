# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "Alas, how far it is! The way of heaven is like meeting floating clouds, like looking into a deep abyss. Looking into a deep abyss, one can still measure it; meeting floating clouds, one cannot know their limit. You, Master, have often spoken of respectfully serving the way of heaven. I have heard and stored it, but privately find it strange and do not know what you mean. I wish you, Master, to give full rein to your thoughts and speak of it completely, so that it will never perish but last without break. May I hear of the way of heaven?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "like meeting floating clouds ... one cannot know their limit"] A deep abyss is still and clear, so looking into it one can measure its depth; floating clouds drift, gather and scatter, so meeting them one cannot reach their edge. It means that the image of the blue heaven can be seen like an abyss, but the way of the movements and transformations of scaled and shelled creatures, like clouds, is unfathomable in its coming and going. The six qi are deep and subtle; for their movements and transformations, one should understand this metaphor.
Song editors: On examination, this text duplicates the text of the *Shu wuguo lun*.

[On "so that it will never perish ... May I hear of the way of heaven?"] It is the way of movement, transformation, generation and completion.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on the way of heaven (the movement of the six qi).
- 䟽五過論 in the Song editors' note is the *Shu wuguo lun* (疏五過論).
