# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §60 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. Cold and damp meet, dryness and heat face each other, wind and fire encounter each other. Is there anything to be heard on this?"

## Notes

There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 殆知閣 (重广补注) has 間 for 聞 in 其有聞乎 (see the variants).
