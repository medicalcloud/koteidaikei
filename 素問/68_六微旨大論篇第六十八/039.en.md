# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *yichou* year, the first qi, by the heavenly number, begins at twenty-six *ke* and ends at twelve and a half *ke*; the second qi begins at twelve *ke* six *fen* and ends at the hundredth *ke* of the water clock; the third qi begins at one *ke* and ends at eighty-seven and a half *ke*; the fourth qi begins at eighty-seven *ke* six *fen* and ends at seventy-five *ke*; the fifth qi begins at seventy-six *ke* and ends at sixty-two and a half *ke*; the sixth qi begins at sixty-two *ke* six *fen* and ends at fifty *ke*. This is the so-called six-two, the number of heaven.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the *yichou* year, the first qi, by the heavenly number, begins at twenty-six *ke*"] The first *ke* of the beginning of 已 [*si*].
Song editors: The years *jisi*, *guiyou*, *dingchou*, *xinsi*, *yiyou*, *jichou*, *guisi*, *dingyou*, *xinchou*, *yisi*, *jiyou*, *guichou*, *dingsi* and *xinyou* are the same as this—the so-called *si*, *you* and *chou* years, whose qi meet and agree.

[On "and ends at twelve and a half *ke*"] The middle of *mao*.

[On "the second qi begins at twelve *ke* six *fen*"] South of the middle of *mao*.

[On "and ends at the hundredth *ke* of the water clock"] Four *ke* after *chou*.

[On "the third qi begins at one *ke*"] Again the first *ke* of the beginning of *yin*.

[On "and ends at eighty-seven and a half *ke*"] The middle of *zi*.

[On "the fourth qi begins at eighty-seven *ke* six *fen*"] Due east of the middle of *zi*.

[On "and ends at seventy-five *ke*"] Four *ke* after *xu*.

[On "the fifth qi begins at seventy-six *ke*"] The first *ke* of the beginning of *hai*.

[On "and ends at sixty-two and a half *ke*"] The middle of *you*.

[On "the sixth qi begins at sixty-two *ke* six *fen*"] North of the middle of *you*.

[On "and ends at fifty *ke*"] Four *ke* after *wei*.

[On "This is the so-called six-two, the number of heaven"] The first six is called first-six, the second six is called six-two; it is the order of naming.

## Notes

The times of beginning and ending of the six qi in the *yichou* year.
- 已初 in Wang Bing's note is read as 巳初.
