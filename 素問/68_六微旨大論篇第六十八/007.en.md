# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What of not arriving when the time comes, and arriving before the time?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of not arriving when the time comes, and arriving before the time?"] It refers to the response of arriving late or early relative to the due time in years of excess or deficiency.

## Notes

A question on the consequences of deficiency and surplus.
