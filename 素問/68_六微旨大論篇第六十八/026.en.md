# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Heavenly tally is the law enforcer; year-position is the decree executor; supreme-one heavenly tally is the noble

## Notes

Heavenly tally, year-position (year-meeting) and supreme-one heavenly tally are likened to official ranks. The unit breaks after 貴, and 人 is in the next unit, as is Wang Bing's note.
- 殆知閣 (重广补注) has 歳会 for 歳位 (see the variants).
