# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Positions have end and beginning; qi have an initial and a middle part. Above and below differ, so seeking them also differs."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Positions have end and beginning ... seeking them also differs"] Position means the position of earth; qi means the qi of heaven. Qi and position shift relative to each other. So in the initial part of a qi heaven acts, and in the middle part earth governs; when earth governs, qi flows into the earth; when heaven acts, qi rises to heaven. Initial and middle both divide heaven's step and apportion the *ke*; each is thirty days plus forty-three and three-quarter *ke*.

## Notes

Each step's qi divides into an initial and a middle part.
