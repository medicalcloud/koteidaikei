# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Wood movement meeting *mao*, fire movement meeting *wu*, earth movement meeting the four seasonal branches, metal movement meeting *you*, and water movement meeting *zi* are the so-called year-meetings, in which the qi is balanced."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Wood movement meeting *mao* ... the qi is balanced"] Neither excessive nor deficient; this is called a balanced movement governing the year. In the qi of a balanced year, the growth of things and the response of the pulse all match their periods, neither early nor late.
Song editors: On examination, wood movement meeting *mao* is the *dingmao* year; fire movement meeting *wu*, the *wuwu* year; earth movement meeting the four seasonal branches, the *jiachen*, *jiaxu*, *jichou* and *jiwei* years; metal movement meeting *you*, the *yiyou* year; water movement meeting *zi*, the *bingzi* year. Among them *wuwu*, *jichou*, *jiwei* and *yiyou* are also supreme-one heavenly tallies.

## Notes

The definition of year-meeting.
