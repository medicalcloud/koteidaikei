# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §70 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. Is there anything that neither generates nor transforms?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Is there anything that neither generates nor transforms?"] It asks whether there are people who escape yin and yang and avoid generation and transformation, neither generating nor transforming, without beginning or end, the same as the naturalness of the great void.

## Notes

A question on whether any being transcends generation and transformation.
