# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "One who speaks of heaven seeks it in the root; one who speaks of earth seeks it in the positions; one who speaks of humans seeks it in the intersection of qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "One who speaks of heaven seeks it in the root ... in the intersection of qi"] The root means the six qi of heaven: cold, summer heat, dryness, damp, wind and fire. The three yin and three yang are generated and transformed from them; hence they are called the root, the so-called six origins. The positions mean metal, wood, fire, earth, water and sovereign fire. [The intersection of qi] is where the qi of heaven and earth meet above and below, and where humans dwell.

## Notes

Heaven, earth and humans are sought respectively in the root, the positions and the intersection of qi.
