# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §59 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "What, having risen, descends—the descending is called heaven; what, having descended, rises—the rising is called earth. Heaven's qi descends and the qi flows into the earth; earth's qi rises and the qi soars to heaven. So high and low summon each other, rising and descending depend on each other, and change arises."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What, having risen, descends ... the rising is called earth"] In the initial part of a qi, earth's qi rises; in the middle part, heaven's qi descends. "Having risen, descends" shows heaven's qi flowing downward; "having descended, rises" shows earth's qi responding upward. Heaven's qi descending and earth's qi rising, heaven and earth meeting, is the image of Tai (Peace). The *Changes* says "Heaven and earth intermingle: Peace." Thus the rising and descending of the qi of heaven and earth always alternate down and up every thirty and a half days without ceasing; so the generation and transformation of the myriad things never rest, and each obtains its place.

[On "So high and low summon each other ... and change arises"] Because qi has prevalence and retaliation, change arises.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says: "What of the fullness and emptiness of the qi of heaven and earth?" The answer: "When heaven's qi is insufficient, earth's qi follows it; when earth's qi is insufficient, heaven's qi follows it. The movement resides between them and always takes precedence. It hates what it does not overcome, returns to what is harmonious and alike, and following the movement, returning and following, produces illness. So when the upper prevails, heaven's qi descends downward; when the lower prevails, earth's qi shifts upward. The divisions differ according to how much: slight cases differ slightly, severe cases greatly. When severe, the positions change; when the intersection of qi changes, great change arises and illness occurs."

## Notes

The descent of heaven's qi and the rise of earth's qi, and change arising from their interaction.
