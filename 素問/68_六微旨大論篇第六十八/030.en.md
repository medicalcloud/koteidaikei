# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The sovereign in the minister's position is compliance; the minister in the sovereign's position is contravention. In contravention the illness is near and its harm swift; in compliance the illness is far and its harm slight. This refers to the two fires."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The sovereign in the minister's position is compliance ... This refers to the two fires"] Ministerial fire residing in sovereign fire is a minister in the sovereign's position; hence contravention. Sovereign fire residing in ministerial fire is the sovereign in the minister's position, the sovereign presiding over the minister's place; hence compliance. Far means the distance is long; near means the distance is short.

## Notes

Compliance and contravention when the positions of sovereign and ministerial fire are exchanged.
