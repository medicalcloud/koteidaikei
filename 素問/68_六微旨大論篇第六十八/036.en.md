# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The qi of heaven begins at *jia*; the qi of earth begins at *zi*. The union of *zi* and *jia* is called the establishment of the year. Carefully observe its times, and the qi can be anticipated."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The union of *zi* and *jia* ... the qi can be anticipated"] The union of *zi* and *jia*, called the establishment of the year, is the *jiazi* year. Carefully observing the earliness or lateness by the water clock, all six qi can be anticipated.

## Notes

Observing the times of the qi taking *jiazi* as the starting point.
