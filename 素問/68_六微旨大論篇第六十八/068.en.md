# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §68 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Can there be no generation and transformation?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Can there be no generation and transformation?"] It asks whether there is also something that neither generates nor transforms.

## Notes

A question on whether anything can be without generation and transformation.
