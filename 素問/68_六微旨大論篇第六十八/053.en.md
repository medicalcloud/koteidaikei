# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §53 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "They divide heaven and earth."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "They divide heaven and earth"] By this one knows that the height and depth of the qi produce and govern people's illnesses.

## Notes

Wang Bing's note is hard to construe; the translation stays close to the characters.
