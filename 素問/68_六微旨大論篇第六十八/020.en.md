# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It is when the year does not meet it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is when the year does not meet it"] It does not meet its own branch.

## Notes

Being out of position means not being a year-meeting.
