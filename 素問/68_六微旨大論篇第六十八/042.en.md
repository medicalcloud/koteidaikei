# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the next year, *wuchen*, the first qi again begins at one *ke*. It is always so without end, circling round and beginning again."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the next year, *wuchen* ... circling round and beginning again"] Beginning with the *jiazi* year and ending with the *guihai* year, four years always make a small cycle and fifteen cycles a great cycle. Naming the years by their branches, the qi can be anticipated.

## Notes

After four years the cycle comes round and begins again in the *wuchen* year. Qibo's speech ends here.
- In the base, 1 instance of 巳 meaning 已 "already/stop" is emended to 已 (graphic confusion; the base often does not distinguish 巳, 已 and 己, while Wikisource's plain text and other sources mostly have the correct character).
