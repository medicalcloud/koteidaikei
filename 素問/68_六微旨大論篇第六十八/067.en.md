# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §67 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Not generating and not transforming is the time of stillness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Not generating and not transforming is the time of stillness"] Of the times of humans, two can be seen; the times of heaven and earth cannot be seen. The two that can be seen are, first, the end of life, and second, change into the same body as what is above. Thereafter one abandons small generation and transformation and returns to the great transformation; after death, transformation and change have not yet ceased. Hence there are two that can be seen. The end of heaven and earth and the lifespan of humans have their measures, and long and short do not reach each other; hence few people see it.

## Notes

The cessation of generation and transformation is the time of stillness. From 与上同体 on, Wang Bing's note is hard to construe; the translation stays close to the characters.
