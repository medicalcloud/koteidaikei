# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §71 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "How thorough the question! Only the true person is united and one with the way."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Only the true person is united and one with the way"] The body of the true person appears and disappears unfathomably, goes out and comes in within and beyond heaven and earth, and lives by following the way and the supreme truth. At its smallest it enters where there is no gap; at its greatest it passes beyond the realm of empty space. Who could be so unless one with the way?

## Notes

Only the true person, one with the way, transcends generation and transformation.
