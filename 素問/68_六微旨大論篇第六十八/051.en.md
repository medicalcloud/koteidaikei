# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §51 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The initial part is about thirty degrees and a fraction; the middle qi follows the same rule."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The initial part is about thirty degrees and a fraction; the middle qi follows the same rule"] The fraction means thirty days plus forty-three *ke* and thirty-fortieths of a *ke*. Initial and middle together make sixty days plus eighty-seven and a half *ke*. Since each has a remainder of thirty-fortieths of a *ke*, it says "the middle qi follows the same rule".

## Notes

The lengths of the initial and middle parts.
