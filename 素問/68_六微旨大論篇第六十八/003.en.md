# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I wish to hear what is meant by the abundance and decline of the six-six stages of the way of heaven."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I wish to hear what is meant by the abundance and decline of the six-six stages"] The six-six stages have already been answered in the classic, but the Heavenly Teacher 夫 [had not yet] set out their meaning; hence the question is asked again.

## Notes

A question on the abundance and decline of the six-six stages.
- 夫 in Wang Bing's 天師夫敷其旨 is translated as an error for 未 "not yet", but this has not been checked.
