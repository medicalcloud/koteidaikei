# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence the *Tianyuan ce* calls it heavenly tally." [The Emperor said:] "What of heavenly tally that is also a year-meeting?"

## Notes

There is no note by Wang Bing.
- There is no speaker marker before 天符歳会何如, but from the context it is read as the Emperor's question, and "[The Emperor said:]" is supplied.
