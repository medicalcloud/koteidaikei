# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "This is heaven meeting it.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "This is heaven meeting it"] The qi of heaven meets the qi of the movement.
Song editors: On examination, earth movement with greater yin appearing above is *jichou* and *jiwei*; fire movement with lesser yang above is *wuyin* and *wushen*, with lesser yin above *wuzi* and *wuwu*; metal movement with *yangming* above is *yimao* and *yiyou*; wood movement with *jueyin* above is *dingsi* and *dinghai*; water movement with greater yang above is *bingchen* and *bingxu*. Among them *jichou*, *jiwei*, 戌 [*wu*]*wu* and *yiyou* are also supreme-one heavenly tallies. The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says: "Those excessive and the same as heaven's transformation are three, and those deficient and the same as heaven's transformation are also three. *Wuzi* and *wuwu*, great *zhi* with lesser yin presiding above; *wuyin* and *wushen*, great *zhi* with lesser yang presiding above; *bingchen* and *bingxu*, great *yu* with greater yang presiding above: such are three. *Dingsi* and *dinghai*, lesser *jue* with *jueyin* presiding above; *yimao* and *yiyou*, lesser *shang* with *yangming* presiding above; *jichou* and *jiwei*, lesser *gong* with greater yin presiding above: such are three." Whether excessive or deficient, those presided over are all called heavenly tallies.
