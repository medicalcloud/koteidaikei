# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §48 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Within the intersection of qi, between the positions above and below, is where humans dwell.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Within the intersection of qi ... is where humans dwell"] Below heaven and above earth is the division where the two qi meet and combine. Humans dwell on the earth; hence within the meeting of qi is where humans dwell. Thus generation, transformation, change and alteration all take place within the intersection of qi.

## Notes
