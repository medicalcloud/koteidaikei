# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "How thorough the question! When the sun completes one circuit, heaven's qi begins at one *ke*; at the second circuit, at twenty-six *ke*; at the third circuit, at fifty-one *ke*; at the fourth circuit, at seventy-six *ke*; at the fifth circuit, heaven's qi again begins at one *ke*. This is called one period. Therefore the *yin*, *wu* and *xu* years meet and agree in qi; the *mao*, *wei* and *hai* years meet and agree in qi; the *chen*, *shen* and *zi* years meet and agree in qi; the *si*, *you* and *chou* years meet and agree in qi; ending and beginning again."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the sun completes one circuit, heaven's qi begins at one *ke*"] The *jiazi* year.

[On "at the second circuit, at twenty-six *ke*"] The *yichou* year.

[On "at the third circuit, at fifty-one *ke*"] The *bingyin* year.

[On "at the fourth circuit, at seventy-six *ke*"] The *dingmao* year.

[On "at the fifth circuit, heaven's qi again begins at one *ke*"] The *wuchen* year. The remaining fifty-five years also cycle round and begin again.

[On "This is called one period"] The rule takes four years as one period, cycling without end. Beyond that, every three years they meet and agree once; hence there are the three combinations.

[On "Therefore the *yin*, *wu* and *xu* years ... ending and beginning again"] The method of yin and yang takes these as the three combinations because their qi meet and agree. Otherwise they would each be in one direction, with no reason to combine.

## Notes

Four years make one period, and the years whose qi meet and agree form the three combinations.
- 巳 in Wang Bing's 循環不巳 is read as 已.
