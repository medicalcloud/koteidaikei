# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "To the right of the manifest brightness is the position of the sovereign fire. Stepping back one step to the right of the sovereign fire, ministerial fire governs; another step, earth qi governs; another step, metal qi governs; another step, water qi governs; another step, wood qi governs; another step, sovereign fire governs. Below ministerial fire, water qi supports it; below the position of water, earth qi supports it; below the position of earth, wind qi supports it; below the position of wind, metal qi supports it; below the position of metal, fire qi supports it; below sovereign fire, yin essence supports it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "To the right of the manifest brightness ... ministerial fire governs"] Sunrise is called manifest brightness; it is the position of *mao*, where the qi divides spring. From the Spring Equinox for sixty-odd days, from the Dipper pointing at the middle of *mao* to the middle of *si*, is the position of the sovereign fire. From the Dipper at the middle of *si* to the middle of *wei* is the division of the third qi, governed by ministerial fire, the so-called lesser yang. The position of the sovereign fire is the so-called division of lesser yin heat. When the heavenly degrees reach here, warmth and mildness prevail; though residing in the division of heat, it does not bring scorching heat; this is the virtue of the sovereign. If lesser yang resides here, it is usurpation: great heat comes early and epidemics arise. If *yangming* resides here, warmth and coolness are untimely. If greater yang resides here, cold rain interrupts the heat. If *jueyin* resides here, there is wind and damp, and rain produces feathered creatures. If lesser yin resides here, there are blemishes and epidemics throughout the realm, because it has its own position and the sovereign's decrees are proclaimed. If greater yin resides here, there is timely rain. Fire has two positions; hence the sovereign fire is the beginning of the six qi. The ministerial fire covers thirty days before and after the summer solstice. The division of lesser yang is the position of fire. When the heavenly degrees reach here, scorching heat prevails. If lesser yang resides here, heat comes suddenly, grasses wilt, rivers dry, the blaze is excessive, and the transformation of damp spreads late. If *yangming* resides here, cool qi breaks out at intervals. If greater yang resides here, cold qi comes at intervals and contends with the heat, producing ice and hail. If *jueyin* resides here, wind and heat prevail, and rain produces feathered creatures. If lesser yin resides here, there is great summer heat and excessive blaze. If greater yin resides here, there are clouds, rain, thunder and lightning. "Stepping back" means being to the right of the position as seen facing south. One step is about sixty days and eighty-seven and a half *ke*; the other qi follow the same rule.

[On "another step, earth qi governs"] The division of rain: the sixty-odd days before the Autumn Equinox, from the Dipper at the middle of *wei* to the middle of *you*, the fourth qi. When the heavenly degrees reach here, clouds and rain prevail and damp steaming arises. If lesser yang resides here, there is boiling heat, clouds, rain, thunder and hail. If *yangming* resides here, clear rain, fog and dew. If greater yang resides here, cold rain harms things. If *jueyin* resides here, violent wind and rain smash and snap, and rain produces naked creatures. If lesser yin resides here, cold and heat act contrary, clouds float over mountains and marshes, with violent rain and sultry steaming. If greater yin resides here, heavy, incessant rain.

[On "another step, metal qi governs"] The division of dryness: the sixty-odd days after the Autumn Equinox, from the Dipper at the middle of *you* to the middle of *hai*, the fifth qi. When the heavenly degrees reach here, all things are dry. If lesser yang resides here, warmth and clarity alternate properly and all things flourish. If *yangming* resides here, great coolness and swift dryness. If greater yang resides here, early cold. If *jueyin* resides here, cool wind prevails and rain produces shelled creatures. If lesser yin resides here, autumn is damp and heat illness is epidemic. If greater yin resides here, timely rain and deep overcast.

[On "another step, water qi governs"] The division of cold: thirty days before and after the winter solstice, from the Dipper at *hai* to the middle of *chou*, the sixth qi. When the heavenly degrees reach here, cold qi prevails. If lesser yang resides here, winter is warm, hibernating creatures do not hide, flowing water does not freeze. If *yangming* resides here, dry cold is sharp and cutting. If greater yang resides here, great cold and freezing. If *jueyin* resides here, cold wind gusts and rain produces scaled creatures. If lesser yin resides here, hibernating creatures come out and flowing water does not freeze. If greater yin resides here, congealed yin and cold snow, and earth qi is damp.

[On "another step, wood qi governs"] The division of wind: the sixty-odd days before the Spring Equinox, from the Dipper at the middle of *chou* to the middle of *mao*, the first qi. When the heavenly degrees reach here, wind qi moves; it is the beginning of the 今 [commands] of the spirits of heaven and earth, the messenger of heaven. If lesser yang resides here, warm epidemics arrive. If *yangming* resides here, clear wind, fog and dew, and murkiness. If greater yang resides here, cold wind is cutting and bitter, with frost, snow and ice. If *jueyin* resides here, great wind brings out flourishing, and rain produces furred creatures. If lesser yin resides here, hot wind harms people and seasonal qi spreads. If greater yin resides here, wind, rain and congealed yin do not disperse.

[On "another step, sovereign fire governs"] The division of heat, again beginning from the Spring Equinox, from the Dipper at the middle of *mao* to the middle of *si*, the second qi. These six positions complete the record of a year: six times sixty is three hundred and sixty days; six times eighty is four hundred and eighty *ke*; six times seven is forty-two *ke*; the remaining half *ke* accumulate to three, roughly completing three hundred and sixty-five degrees. The remaining fraction may be apportioned finely.

[On "Below ministerial fire, water qi supports it"] When heat is abundant, water supports it: stems spread soft and weak, moisture gathers and overflows; the image of water can be seen.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "where lesser yang arrives, fire arises, and at the end there is sultry steaming", and 刖 [thus] the meaning of water supporting it can be seen. It also says "where lesser yang arrives, there are whirlwinds, burning and congealed frost"; this too is the water qi supporting from below.

[On "below the position of water, earth qi supports it"] When cold is extreme, things are hard, water freezes and streams dry up; the image of earth appears here. That it supports from below is clear.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "where greater yang arrives, there are cold snow, ice, hail and white dust"; this is the meaning of earth qi supporting it.

[On "below the position of earth, wind qi supports it"] After a gale, timely rain falls; this is damp blown by wind and transformed into rain.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "where greater yin arrives, damp arises, and at the end there is pouring rain"; this is wind qi supporting below the position of earth and making rain. It also says "where greater yin arrives, there are thunder, sudden downpours and fierce wind"; this is the meaning of wind supporting it.

[On "below the position of wind, metal qi supports it"] When wind moves, the qi is clear and all things dry; that metal supports below wood is plainly visible.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "where *jueyin* arrives, wind arises, and at the end there is austerity"; the meaning of metal supporting it can be seen. It also says "where *jueyin* arrives, there are furious gusts and great coolness"; this too is metal supporting it.

[On "below the position of metal, fire qi supports it"] Forging metal produces heat; that is, fire melts metal. [Fire] riding over metal is not without reason.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "where *yangming* arrives, there are scattering, falling and warmth"; this is the meaning of fire riding over it.

[On "below sovereign fire, yin essence supports it"] That great heat does not prevail in the position of the sovereign fire is presumably because yin essence controls and supports it from below. Whenever a prevailing qi rides below, it breaks the excessive abundance. This is the general principle of the creation of heaven and earth.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "where lesser yin arrives, heat arises, and the middle is cold"; the meaning of yin supporting it can be known. It also says "where lesser yin arrives, there is great warmth and cold"; this too is the meaning. Further, the *Liuyuan zhengji* says: "When water erupts, there are hail and snow; when earth erupts, gusts and downpours; when wood erupts, destruction and breaking; when metal erupts, clear brightness; when fire erupts, murky dimness. What qi makes it so? The qi have more and less, and eruptions are slight or severe. The slight correspond to their own qi; the severe include what is below them; by verifying the qi below them, it can be known." The so-called "verifying what is below them" refers to these six supporting qi.

## Notes

The order of the six positions of earth's qi (host qi), and how the overcoming qi below each supports and restrains it (excess is harmful, support restrains). Wang Bing describes the weather when each guest qi is added to each position.
- 今 in Wang Bing's 号今之始 is presumably an error for 令, and 刖 in the Song editors' 刖水承之義 for 則.
