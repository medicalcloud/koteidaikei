# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Excess is harmful; what supports it restrains it. Restraint brings generation and transformation, with abundance and decline arrayed outwardly. Harm brings ruin and disorder, and generation and transformation fall gravely ill."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Excess is harmful ... fall gravely ill"] 亢 means going beyond to the extreme. Things abhor their extreme.

## Notes

The principle of "excess is harmful, support restrains".
