# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §61 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Qi has prevalence and retaliation. In the arising of prevalence and retaliation, there are virtue, transformation, function and change. With change, pathogenic qi takes up residence."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "there are virtue, transformation, function and change. With change, pathogenic qi takes up residence"] Clapping the hands makes a sound; pouring onto fire produces boiling. When things meet, images arise between them; all kinds meeting is likewise. When heaven and earth meet, the eight winds drum and split, and the six qi race across between them. So qi that cannot remain upright becomes pathogenic qi.

## Notes

The arising of prevalence and retaliation has virtue, transformation, function and change; pathogenic qi resides in change.
