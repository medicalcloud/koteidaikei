# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "In a year of earth movement greater yin appears above; in a year of fire movement lesser yang or lesser yin appears above; in a year of metal movement *yangming* appears above; in a year of wood movement *jueyin* appears above; in a year of water movement greater yang appears above. What of these?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "in a year of fire movement lesser yang or lesser yin appears above"] Lesser yin and lesser yang are both fire qi.

## Notes

A question on cases where the movement and the controller of heaven are the same qi.
