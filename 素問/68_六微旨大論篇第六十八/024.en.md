# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It is the meeting of the supreme-one heavenly tally."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is the meeting of the supreme-one heavenly tally"] This is called the three combining: first the meeting with heaven, second the meeting with the year, third the meeting with the movement. The *Tianyuan ji dalun*'s "the three combining is governance" refers to this.
Song editors: The details of the supreme-one heavenly tally are in the note to the *Tianyuan ji dalun*.

## Notes

What is both heavenly tally and year-meeting is the supreme-one heavenly tally.
