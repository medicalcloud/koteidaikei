# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §62 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What is meant by pathogenic?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What is meant by pathogenic?"] Pathogenic is the name for what is not upright. When heaven and earth prevail and retaliate, the six qi—cold, summer heat, dryness, damp, wind and fire—become pathogenic to each other.

## Notes

A question on the meaning of pathogenic.
