# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Responding is compliance; not responding is contravention. Contravention produces change, and change brings illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Responding is compliance ... change brings illness"] Matching the due time is responding; missing the time is not. The generation and transformation of the qi of heaven and earth never cease and are never obstructed. Being present when it should not be, or absent when it should be, is the qi of creation losing its constancy. When constancy is lost, qi changes; when constancy changes, qi and blood are disordered and illness results. When heaven and earth change and lose their constancy, all things fall ill.

## Notes

Compliance, contravention, change and illness depend on whether qi responds to its time.
