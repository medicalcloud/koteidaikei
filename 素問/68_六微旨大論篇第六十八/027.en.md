## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Heavenly tally is the law enforcer ... supreme-one heavenly tally is the noble person"] The law enforcer is like a chief minister, the decree executor like a regional lord, the noble person like the sovereign.
