# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "A step is sixty degrees and a fraction; so twenty-four steps accumulate to fill a hundred *ke* and make a day."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "A step is sixty degrees and a fraction"] The fraction means eighty-seven and five-tenths *ke*.

[On "so twenty-four steps accumulate to fill a hundred *ke* and make a day"] This speaks of the remainder of the heavenly degrees. The degrees of the heavenly circuit are three hundred sixty-five and a quarter. Twenty-four steps are exactly four years. A quarter degree is twenty-five *ke*. In four years the qi accumulates and fills a hundred *ke*; hence it makes one day, and the degree is one day.

## Notes

The length of a step, and how its fraction makes up one day in four years.
