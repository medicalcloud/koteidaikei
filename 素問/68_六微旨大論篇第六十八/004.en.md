# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Above and below have their positions; left and right have their order. So to the right of lesser yang, *yangming* governs; to the right of *yangming*, greater yang governs; to the right of greater yang, *jueyin* governs; to the right of *jueyin*, lesser yin governs; to the right of lesser yin, greater yin governs; to the right of greater yin, lesser yang governs. This is what is called the branch of the qi; one faces south and awaits them. So the saying 'Following the order of heaven and the times of abundance and decline, shift the light and fix the position, stand upright and await it' means this. Above lesser yang, fire qi governs, and in the middle *jueyin* appears; above *yangming*, dry qi governs, and in the middle greater yin appears; above greater yang, cold qi governs, and in the middle lesser yin appears; above *jueyin*, wind qi governs, and in the middle lesser yang appears; above lesser yin, heat qi governs, and in the middle greater yang appears; above greater yin, damp qi governs, and in the middle *yangming* appears. These are the so-called roots. Below the root is what appears in the middle; below what appears is the branch of the qi. Root and branch differ, and the qi respond with different images."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Above and below have their positions; left and right have their order"] Above and below are the two qi controlling heaven and earth; the remaining four qi, left and right, are to the left and right of the year.

[On "This is what is called the branch of the qi; one faces south and awaits them"] 標 means the tip. The sage stands facing south to watch the arrival of the qi.

[On "shift the light and fix the position, stand upright and await it ... means this"] "Shift the light" means the sun shifting its light; "fix the position" means facing south to observe the qi. Standing upright and observing the number of the year, one can await the arrival of the qi.

[On "Above lesser yang, fire qi governs, and in the middle *jueyin* appears"] Lesser yang is the fire of the south; hence fire qi appears above and governs. It is paired with *jueyin*; hence *jueyin* appears in the middle.

[On "above *yangming*, dry qi governs, and in the middle greater yin appears"] *Yangming* is the metal of the west; hence dry qi governs above. It is paired with greater yin; hence below the dry qi, 大 [greater] yin appears in the middle.

[On "above greater yang, cold qi governs, and in the middle lesser yin appears"] Greater yang is the water of the north; hence cold qi governs above. It is paired with lesser yin; hence below the cold qi, lesser yin appears in the middle.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "where greater yang arrives, cold arises, and the middle is warm"; the sense is the same.

[On "above *jueyin*, wind qi governs, and in the middle lesser yang appears"] *Jueyin* is the wood of the east; hence wind qi governs above. It is paired with lesser yang; hence below the wind qi, lesser yang appears in the middle.

[On "above lesser yin, heat qi governs, and in the middle greater yang appears"] Lesser yin is the sovereign fire of the southeast; hence heat qi governs above. It is paired with greater yang; hence below the heat qi, greater yang appears in the middle.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "where lesser yin arrives, heat arises, and the middle is cold"; the sense is the same.

[On "above greater yin, damp qi governs, and in the middle *yangming* appears"] Greater yin is the earth of the southwest; hence damp qi governs above. It is paired with *yangming*; hence below the damp qi, *yangming* appears in the middle.

[On "These are the so-called roots. Below the root is what appears in the middle; below what appears is the branch of the qi"] The root means the original qi. When qi is the governor, what the text says is evident.
Song editors: On examination, the note's 文言著矣 is suspected to be in error.

[On "Root and branch differ, and the qi respond with different images"] The root is the origin of the response; the branch is the beginning of the illness. When illness arises in the form, seek its working in the branch; in applying treatment, seek it in the root. When root and branch differ, seek it in what appears in the middle. This is the method that never fails.
Song editors: The *Zhizhen yao dalun* says: "The root and branch of the six qi differ. Some qi follow the root, some [follow both] root and branch, and some follow neither root nor branch. Lesser yang and greater yin follow the root; lesser yin and greater yang follow the root and follow the branch; *yangming* and *jueyin* follow neither root nor branch but follow the middle. So those that follow the root are transformed from the root; those that follow root and branch have the transformations of both; those that follow the middle take the middle qi as their transformation."

## Notes

The order of the positions of the six qi (the branch), and the relation of root, middle appearance and branch between the six qi and the three yin and three yang.
