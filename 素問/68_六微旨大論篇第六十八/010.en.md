# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The growth of things is its response; the qi and the pulse are its response."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The growth of things is its response; the qi and the pulse are its response"] The growth and flourishing of things has its constant season; the arrival of the pulse has its constant period. In years of surplus it is early, in years of deficiency late; all arrive according to their period.

## Notes

The response of qi shows in the growth of things and in the pulse.
