# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §55 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The initial part is earth's qi; the middle part is heaven's qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The initial part is earth's qi; the middle part is heaven's qi"] In the initial part of a qi, heaven acts; when heaven acts, earth's qi rises within the great void. In the middle part of a qi, earth's qi governs; when earth's qi governs, heaven's qi descends into what has substance.

## Notes

The initial part is earth's qi and the middle part heaven's qi. Wang Bing's note is worded differently from his note to unit 034 (initial heaven, middle earth), but explains it through the rising and descending of qi.
