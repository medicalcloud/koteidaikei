# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §49 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence the saying: 'Above the heavenly pivot, heaven's qi governs; below the heavenly pivot, earth's qi governs; in the division of the intersection of qi, human qi follows it and the myriad things depend on it.' This is what is meant."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Hence the saying ... This is what is meant"] The heavenly pivot is on both sides of the navel, the so-called middle of the body. When one stretches the arms to point to the sky, the heavenly pivot is exactly at the middle of the body. Dividing it into three, the upper part corresponds to heaven, the lower to earth, and the middle to the intersection of qi. At the juncture where the qi of heaven and earth meet, there are the changes and transformations of prevalence and retaliation of cold, summer heat, dryness, damp, wind and fire; hence human qi follows them, and the generation and transformation of all things combine and disperse through them.

## Notes

Heaven's qi, earth's qi and the intersection of qi are divided at the heavenly pivot (beside the navel).
