# 素問 六微旨大論篇第六十八 §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *bingyin* year, the first qi, by the heavenly number, begins at fifty-one *ke* and ends at thirty-seven and a half *ke*; the second qi begins at thirty-seven *ke* six *fen* and ends at twenty-five *ke*; the third qi begins at twenty-six *ke* and ends at twelve and a half *ke*; the fourth qi begins at twelve *ke* six *fen* and ends at the hundredth *ke* of the water clock; the fifth qi begins at one *ke* and ends at eighty-seven and a half *ke*; the sixth qi begins at eighty-seven *ke* six *fen* and ends at seventy-five *ke*. This is the so-called six-three, the number of heaven.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the *bingyin* year, the first qi, by the heavenly number, begins at fifty-one *ke*"] The first *ke* of the beginning of *shen*.
Song editors: The years *gengwu*, *jiaxu*, *wuyin*, *renwu*, *bingxu*, *gengyin*, *jiawu*, *wuxu*, *renyin*, *bingwu*, *gengxu*, *jiayin*, 戌 [*wu*]*wu* and *renxu* are the same as this—the so-called *yin*, *wu* and *xu* years, whose qi meet and agree.

[On "and ends at thirty-seven and a half *ke*"] The middle of *wu*.

[On "the second qi begins at thirty-seven *ke* six *fen*"] West of the middle of *wu*.

[On "and ends at twenty-five *ke*"] Four *ke* after *chen*.

[On "the third qi begins at twenty-six *ke*"] The first *ke* of the beginning of *si*.

[On "and ends at twelve and a half *ke*"] The middle of *mao*.

[On "the fourth qi begins at twelve *ke* six *fen*"] South of the middle of *mao*.

[On "and ends at the hundredth *ke* of the water clock"] Four *ke* after *chou*.

[On "the fifth qi begins at one *ke*"] The first *ke* of the beginning of *yin*.

[On "and ends at eighty-seven and a half *ke*"] The middle of *zi*.

[On "the sixth qi begins at eighty-seven *ke* six *fen*"] Left of the middle of *zi*.

[On "and ends at seventy-five *ke*"] Four *ke* after *xu*.

## Notes

The times of beginning and ending of the six qi in the *bingyin* year.
- 戌午 in the Song editors' note is an error for 戊午.
