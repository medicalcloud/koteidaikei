# 素問 経絡論篇第五十七 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "The channels have constant colours, but the network vessels are inconstant and change."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The channels have constant colours, but the network vessels are inconstant and change"] The channels move qi; therefore their colour always corresponds to the season. The network vessels govern blood; therefore when they receive evil they change and are not uniform.

## Notes

The colour of the channels is constant, but that of the network vessels changes.
