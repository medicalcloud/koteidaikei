# 素問 経絡論篇第五十七 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The color of the yin collaterals corresponds to their meridians; the color of the yang collaterals changes without constancy, following the four seasons. When cold predominates they congeal and become rough; when they congeal, they are green-blue and black. When heat predominates they are moist and glossy; when moist and glossy, they are yellow and red. All these are normal colors and are called no illness. When all five colors appear together, it is called cold and heat."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the color of the yang collaterals changes without constancy, following the four seasons"] It follows the movement and rest of the transformations of qi in the four seasons.

[On "When cold predominates ... it is called cold and heat"] 淖 means damp; 沢 means moistening fluid: slightly damp and moist.

## Notes

The color of the yin collaterals corresponds to the meridians, while that of the yang collaterals changes with the cold and heat of the seasons; these are normal colors, not illness. When all five colors appear together, it is the illness of cold and heat.
