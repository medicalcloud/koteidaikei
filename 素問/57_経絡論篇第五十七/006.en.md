# 素問 経絡論篇第五十七 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The colour of the yin network vessels corresponds to their channels; the colour of the yang network vessels changes without constancy, following the four seasons. When cold predominates they congeal and become rough; when they congeal, they are green-blue and black. When heat predominates they are moist and glossy; when moist and glossy, they are yellow and red. All these are normal colours and are called no illness. When all five colours appear together, it is called cold and heat."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the colour of the yang network vessels changes without constancy, following the four seasons"] It follows the movement and rest of the transformations of qi in the four seasons.

[On "When cold predominates ... it is called cold and heat"] 淖 means damp; 沢 means moistening fluid: slightly damp and moist.

## Notes

The colour of the yin network vessels corresponds to the channels, while that of the yang network vessels changes with the cold and heat of the seasons; these are normal colours, not illness. When all five colours appear together, it is the illness of cold and heat.
