# 素問 経絡論篇第五十七 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The heart is red, the lung white, the liver green-blue, the spleen yellow and the kidney black; all these also correspond to the colors of their meridian vessels."

## Notes

The colors of the five zang are the constant colors of their meridians. There is no note by Wang Bing.
