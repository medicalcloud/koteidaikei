# 素問 経絡論篇第五十七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "When the network vessels appear, their five colours differ — green-blue, yellow, red, white and black are not the same. Why is this?"

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: why the colours of the network vessels differ. There is no note by Wang Bing.
