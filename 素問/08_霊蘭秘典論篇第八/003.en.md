# 素問 霊蘭秘典論篇第八 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi said: "Excellent! I have heard the Way of essential radiance and the work of the great sages, and the great Way has been proclaimed and made clear. Without fasting and purification and choosing an auspicious day, I dare not receive it." Huangdi then chose an auspicious day and a good omen, and stored it in the Chamber of the Numinous Orchid, so that it would be transmitted and preserved.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Without fasting and purification and choosing an auspicious day, I dare not receive it"] Because of deep reverence. Han Kangbo says: "Cleansing the heart is called fasting; guarding against calamity is called purification."

[On "Huangdi then chose ... so that it would be transmitted and preserved"] This is the utmost of secrecy.

## Notes

Revering Qibo's teaching, Huangdi fasted, chose an auspicious day, and stored it in the Chamber of the Numinous Orchid for transmission and preservation; this ends the chapter. The chapter title, "Secret Canon of the Numinous Orchid", comes from this.
- Daizhige (*Huangdi neijing suwen*) has "Juan 3" in place of Wang Bing's note "This is the utmost of secrecy" (a marker of the division into scrolls).
