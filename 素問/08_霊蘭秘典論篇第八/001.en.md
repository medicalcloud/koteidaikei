# 素問 霊蘭秘典論篇第八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I would like to hear how the twelve organs employ one another, and what their ranks are."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] 蔵 means "to store". It refers to what is stored within the abdomen; it does not mean that there are twelve storehouses of form and spirit.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: how the twelve organs employ one another and how they are ranked.
- Wang Bing takes 蔵 here not as the five zang but as what is stored in the abdomen (all the organs).
