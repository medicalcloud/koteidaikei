# 素問 陰陽離合論篇第六 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "Yin and yang can be counted to ten and extended to a hundred, counted to a thousand and extended to ten thousand; the greatness of ten thousand cannot be counted to the end, yet their essential point is one. Heaven covers and earth carries, and the myriad things are about to be born. What has not yet come out of the earth is termed 'dwelling in yin' and named 'the yin within yin'; what has come out of the earth is termed 'the yang within yin'. Yang gives them what is correct; yin acts as their master. Therefore generation depends on spring, growth on summer, gathering on autumn and storage on winter; if the norm is lost, heaven and earth are blocked on all four sides. As for the changes of yin and yang, those in people can likewise be counted."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the greatness of ten thousand cannot be counted to the end, yet their essential point is one"] "One" means separation and union. Although they cannot be counted to the end, their essential subtlety can all be known by reckoning through separation and union.

[On "What has not yet come out of the earth is termed 'dwelling in yin' and named 'the yin within yin'"] Because it dwells in the midst of yin, it is called "dwelling in yin". Form that has not yet moved out is also yin; being yin dwelling in yin, it is called "the yin within yin".

[On "what has come out of the earth is termed 'the yang within yin'"] Form that has moved out is yang; being yang dwelling in yin, it is called "the yang within yin".

[On "Yang gives them what is correct; yin acts as their master"] When yang bestows correct qi, the myriad things are born; when yin acts as master and sustains, all forms are established.

[On "Therefore generation depends on spring ... heaven and earth are blocked on all four sides"] Spring and summer are yang, so there are generation and growth; autumn and winter are yin, so there are gathering and storage. If their constant way is lost, there is no generation in spring, no growth in summer, no gathering in autumn and no storage in winter. Then the qi of the four seasons is closed and blocked, and the qi of yin and yang has nowhere to move.

[On "As for the changes of yin and yang, those in people can likewise be counted"] Although the yin and yang of heaven and earth cannot be counted to the end, their number in the functioning of the human body can be known.

## Notes

Yin and yang can be divided endlessly, but their essential point is one (which Wang Bing takes as "separation and union"). Things in the earth are the yin within yin, and once out of the earth the yang within yin; yang gives what is correct and yin acts as master, generating, growing, gathering and storing with the four seasons. The changes of yin and yang in people can be counted.
- "Their essential point is one": Wang Bing takes "one" as separation and union (the chapter title).
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 夫 for 失 in 失常.
