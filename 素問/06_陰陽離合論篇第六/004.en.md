# 素問 陰陽離合論篇第六 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The sage stands facing south. The front is called Broad Brightness and the back is called Great Thoroughfare. The ground of the Great Thoroughfare is named lesser yin; above the lesser yin is named greater yang. The greater yang has its root at Zhiyin and its knot at the Gate of Life; it is named the yang within yin. From the middle of the body upward is named Broad Brightness; below Broad Brightness is named greater yin; in front of the greater yin is named *yangming*. *Yangming* has its root at Lidui; it is named the yang within yin. The exterior of *jueyin* is named lesser yang. Lesser yang has its root at Qiaoyin; it is named the lesser yang within yin. Thus, in the separation and union of the three yang, greater yang is the opening, *yangming* the closing and lesser yang the pivot. The three channels must not lose one another; when [the pulse] beats without floating, it is termed one yang."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The sage stands facing south. The front is called Broad Brightness and the back is called Great Thoroughfare"] 広 means great. The south is ruled by the position of fire, *bing* and *ding*; the yang qi is abundantly bright, hence "Great Brightness". One governs things facing the brightness; therefore the sage stands facing south. The *Yi* says, "They see each other in *Li*" — this is surely what is meant. Within the human body, the heart is in the south, so the front is called Broad Brightness; the *chong* vessel is in the north, so the back is called Great Thoroughfare. The Great Thoroughfare is so called because the kidney vessel and the *chong* vessel combine and are abundant and great. Hence the text below says:

[On "The ground of the Great Thoroughfare is named lesser yin"] This makes clear precisely that the two vessels combine to form exterior and interior.

[On "above the lesser yin is named greater yang"] The kidney zang is yin and the bladder fu is yang; the yin qi is below and the yang qi above. This is the channel qi of one union. The *Lingshu jing* says, "The foot lesser yin vessel is the kidney vessel; it starts below the little toe and runs obliquely to the sole," and also, "The foot greater yang vessel is the bladder vessel; it follows Jinggu to the outer side of the little toe." For this reason, above the lesser yin is named greater yang. Hence the text below says:

[On "The greater yang has its root at Zhiyin and its knot at the Gate of Life; it is named the yang within yin"] Zhiyin is the name of a point on the outer side of the little toe. The Gate of Life is where the essence of the zang shines forth, that is, the two eyes. The greater yang vessel starts at the eye and descends to the foot; therefore it is rooted at the tip of the toe and knotted at the eye. The *Lingshu jing* says, "The Gate of Life is the eyes"; this agrees with the meaning of the *Lingshu*. Because the greater yang dwells on the ground of the lesser yin, it is called the yang within yin.
Song editors: In the *Suwen*, root and knot are mentioned for the greater yang but the knots are not mentioned for the other channels; the *Jiayi jing* now has them all.

[On "From the middle of the body upward is named Broad Brightness; below Broad Brightness is named greater yin"] The *Lingshu jing* says: "Heaven is yang, earth is yin; above the waist is heaven, below the waist is earth." The point of dividing the body is that above the middle of the body belongs to Broad Brightness and below Broad Brightness belongs to the greater yin. Also, the heart is Broad Brightness, and below that zang is the spleen zang of the greater yin.

[On "in front of the greater yin is named *yangming*"] In the human body, the stomach is *yangming*, and its vessel runs in front of the spleen vessel; the spleen is greater yin, and its vessel runs behind the stomach vessel. The *Lingshu jing* says: "The foot greater yin vessel is the spleen vessel; it starts at the tip of the big toe, follows the inner side of the toe at the border of the white flesh, passes behind the protruding bone, rises along the front edge of the inner ankle, ascends inside the calf and follows behind the shin bone." "The foot *yangming* vessel is the stomach vessel; three *cun* below the knee it branches off and descends into the outer side of the middle toe." For this reason, in front of the greater yin is named *yangming*. Hence the text below says:

[On "*Yangming* has its root at Lidui; it is named the yang within yin"] Lidui is the name of a point at the tip of the second toe. Because *yangming* dwells in front of the greater yin, it is called the yang within yin.

[On "The exterior of *jueyin* is named lesser yang"] In the human body, the lesser yang vessel of the gallbladder runs outside the portion of the liver vessel, and the *jueyin* vessel of the liver runs inside the position of the gallbladder vessel. The *Lingshu jing* says: "The foot *jueyin* vessel is the liver vessel; it starts at the hairy region of the big toe and rises along the upper edge of the instep." "The foot lesser yang vessel is the gallbladder vessel; it follows the top of the instep and emerges at the tip of the fourth toe." For this reason, the exterior of *jueyin* is named lesser yang. Hence the text below says:

[On "Lesser yang has its root at Qiaoyin; it is named the lesser yang within yin"] Qiaoyin is the name of a point at the tip of the fourth toe. Because the lesser yang dwells on the exterior of *jueyin*, it is called the lesser yang within yin.

[On "Thus, in the separation and union of the three yang, greater yang is the opening, *yangming* the closing and lesser yang the pivot"] "Separation" means separating to function in response; "union" means being paired and united with yin. When separated, they are rightly placed as the three yang; when paired and united, they form exterior and interior and become zang and fu. "Opening, closing and pivot" means that the qi of the three yang are unequal in amount and differ in movement and function. The opening governs the basis of movement and stillness; the closing holds the power of restraint and securing; the pivot governs the subtlety of movement and turning. Because the functions of the qi differ in this way, they change into these three.
Song editors: The *Jiuxu* says: "Greater yang is the barrier, *yangming* the closing, lesser yang the pivot. Therefore when the barrier breaks, the flesh and joints break down and slacken and sudden illness arises; so those who observe sudden illness take [points on] the greater yang. When the closing breaks, the qi has nowhere to stop and rest, and palpitations arise; so those with palpitations all take the *yangming*. When the pivot breaks, the bones shake and one cannot stand firmly on the ground; so those with shaking bones take the lesser yang." The *Jiayi jing* is the same.

[On "The three channels must not lose one another; when [the pulse] beats without floating, it is termed one yang"] When the three channels arrive and beat against the hand without difference of light or heavy, it can rightly be called the qi of one yang, and there is no longer the function of the three yang descending in gradation.

## Notes

The separation and union of the three yang. Taking the sage standing facing south as reference, the front is Broad Brightness and the back the Great Thoroughfare; the positions of greater yang, *yangming* and lesser yang and their roots (the well points Zhiyin, Lidui and Qiaoyin) are given. Among the three yang, greater yang is the opening, *yangming* the closing and lesser yang the pivot; when the three channels are in harmony and the pulse beats without floating, it is called one yang.
- "Opening, closing, pivot": according to the Song editors, the *Jiuxu* and the *Jiayi jing* write 関 "barrier" for 開 "opening".
- "Gate of Life": Wang Bing, following the *Lingshu jing*, takes it as the eyes (different from the later "Gate of Life" between the kidneys).
- 荖降 in Wang Bing's note is the base text's form, translated as 差降.
