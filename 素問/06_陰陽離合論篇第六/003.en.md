# 素問 陰陽離合論篇第六 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I would like to hear about the separation and union of the three yin and three yang."

## Notes

Huangdi asks about the separation and union of the three yin and three yang. There is no note by Wang Bing.
