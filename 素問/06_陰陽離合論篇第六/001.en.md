# 素問 陰陽離合論篇第六 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I have heard that heaven is yang and earth yin, the sun yang and the moon yin, and that the long and short months, three hundred and sixty days, make one year, and that people also correspond to this. Now the three yin and three yang do not correspond to [this] yin and yang. Why is this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the long and short months ... people also correspond to this"] Because the four seasons and five phases operate within, people also correspond to them.
Song editors: From "heaven is yang" to "make one year" overlaps with the *Liujie zangxiang lun* (chapter 9).

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: heaven and earth, sun and moon form a pair of yin and yang, yet the human channels are divided into three yin and three yang; why do they not match?
- According to the Song editors, the opening sentence overlaps with the *Liujie zangxiang lun* (chapter 9).
