# 素問 陰陽離合論篇第六 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Yin and yang go back and forth unceasingly, accumulating and passing on to make one circuit. Qi is within and form without, and they complete each other."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] 𩅞𩅞 means the coming and going of qi. "Accumulating" means accumulating the movements of the pulse; "passing on" means the flowing on of the qi of yin and yang. The pulse qi comes and goes, moving without stopping. Accumulating its movements, qi and blood circulate, making one circuit of the body in correspondence with two marks of the water clock. Hence "accumulating and passing on to make one circuit". The nutritive and defensive qi spread through breathing, flow around the surface of the body, resist and ward off depleting evils, and govern inside and outside, establishing one another. Hence "qi is within and form without, and they complete each other".
Song editors: Another edition writes 衝衝 for 𩅞𩅞.

## Notes

The qi of yin and yang come and go unceasingly and accumulate to make a circuit of the body; qi is within and form without, completing each other. This ends the chapter, and Qibo's speech begun in unit 006.
- 𩅞𩅞: Wang Bing explains it as the coming and going of qi. The characters were lost in the transcription of the base text and have been supplied (see the 【補】 note). According to the Song editors another edition writes 衝衝, and Daizhige has 沖沖 and other forms.
- 住来 in Wang Bing's note is the base text's form, translated as 往来.
