# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The essence brightness [eyes] and the five colours are the flowering of qi. Red should be like vermilion wrapped in white [silk], not like ochre; white should be like goose feathers, not like salt; green should be like the lustre of a blue jade disc, not like indigo; yellow should be like realgar wrapped in gauze, not like yellow earth; black should be like the colour of layered lacquer, not like the grey of the earth. When the signs of [the ruin of] the subtle essence of the five colours appear, the life span will not be long. The essence brightness is what sees the myriad things, distinguishes white and black, and judges long and short. If one takes long for short and white for black, the essence has declined.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The essence brightness and the five colours are the flowering of qi"] The essential flowering of the five qi rises and appears as the five colours, changing around the essence brightness. The *Liujie zangxiang lun* (chapter 9) says: "Heaven feeds people with the five qi; the five qi enter through the nose and are stored in the heart and lung; above they make the five colours refined and bright." This is examining the five colours clearly.

[On "white should be like goose feathers, not like salt"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* reads "white should be like the lustre of a white jade disc, not like chalk"; the *Taisu* gives both.

[On "black should be like the colour of layered lacquer, not like the grey of the earth"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes "the colour of charcoal".

[On "When the signs of [the ruin of] the subtle essence ... not be long"] The appearance of an ochre, salt, indigo, yellow-earth or earth-grey colour is in each case a sign of the ruin of the subtle essence; therefore the life span is not long.

[On "If one takes long for short ... the essence has declined"] This warns against such errors; all of these are errors due to the decline of the essence brightness.

## Notes

The eyes and complexion are the flowering of qi; for each of the five colours a desirable (lustrous) and an undesirable (lustreless) shade are contrasted. Misperceiving things with the eyes is a sign of declining essence.
- According to the Song editors, the *Jiayi jing* uses different images for white and black.
