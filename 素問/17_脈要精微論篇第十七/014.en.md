# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The illness is called heart hernia; there should be a form in the lower abdomen."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] The heart is a male zang, and its qi corresponds to yang; now the pulse is instead cold; therefore there is hernia. Whenever a pulse is strong and tense, there is cold. "Form" means the form of the illness.

## Notes

A tense heart pulse means heart hernia, with a mass in the lower abdomen.
