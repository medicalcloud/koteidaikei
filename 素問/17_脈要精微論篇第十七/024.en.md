# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "These are illnesses of the four seasons; treat them with what overcomes them, and they are cured."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] "Overcome" means to conquer and control: metal overcomes wood, wood earth, earth water, water fire, and fire metal. This is mutual overcoming.

## Notes

Illnesses of the four seasons are treated with what overcomes them in the controlling cycle of the five phases.
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 比 for 此.
