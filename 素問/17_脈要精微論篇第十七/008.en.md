# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How does the pulse move with the four seasons? How does one know where the illness is? How does one know how the illness changes? How does one know when the illness is suddenly within? How does one know when the illness is suddenly without? I ask about these five; may I hear them?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I ask about these five; may I hear them?"] It says he wishes [to know] the forms and signs of following the four seasons and the correspondence of yin and yang.

## Notes

Huangdi asks five things: how the pulse relates to the four seasons, and how to know the location, changes, and interior or exterior position of an illness.
