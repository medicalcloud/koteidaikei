# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The five zang are the strength of the body. The head is the storehouse of the essence brightness; when the head droops and the gaze is sunken, the essence and spirit are about to be seized. The back is the storehouse of the chest; when the back is bent and the shoulders sag, the storehouse is about to collapse. The lower back is the storehouse of the kidney; when one cannot turn or sway, the kidney is about to be exhausted. The knees are the storehouse of the sinews; when one cannot bend or stretch, and walks stooped and bent, the sinews are about to be exhausted. The bones are the storehouse of the marrow; when one cannot stand for long and trembles when walking, the bones are about to be exhausted. Those who keep their strength live; those who lose it die."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The five zang are the strength of the body"] When the zang are at peace the spirit guards; when the spirit guards the body is strong; hence "the strength of the body".

[On "walks stooped and bent"] Song editors: Another edition writes 俯 for 附, and the *Taisu* writes 跗.

[On "The bones are the storehouse of the marrow ... the bones are about to be exhausted"] In each case, where it resides or what it passes through is taken as its storehouse.

[On "Those who keep their strength live; those who lose it die"] "Strength" means the central qi being strong and firm, garrisoning and guarding.

## Notes

The five zang are the strength of the body; the head, back, lower back, knees and bones are the storehouses of the essence brightness, chest, kidney, sinews and marrow respectively, and the signs of their decline are described. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 002, ends here.
- This is well known as the doctrine of the "five storehouses" (head, back, lower back, knees, bones).
- According to the Song editors, 附 in 僂附 is written 俯 in another edition and 跗 in the *Taisu*.
