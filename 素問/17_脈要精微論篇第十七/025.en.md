# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "When there is a long-standing illness, the five zang are stirred, and the pulse and colour are harmed as a result, how does one know in each case whether it is a chronic illness or one that has come suddenly?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] It again uses colour and qi to clarify that the hard, long pulses of the five zang described earlier include signs of one's own illness, of long-standing illness, and of illness due to injury.

## Notes

Huangdi asks how to tell from the pulse and complexion whether an illness is chronic or new.
