# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "If the stomach pulse is replete, there is distension; if depleted, diarrhoea."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] A replete pulse means surplus qi, hence distension and fullness; a depleted pulse means insufficient qi, hence diarrhoea.
Song editors: This answers the Emperor's earlier question, "How does one know where the illness is?"

## Notes

A replete stomach pulse means distension, a depleted one diarrhoea.
- The Song editors point out that this answers "How does one know where the illness is?" in unit 008.
