# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In what goes against the four seasons, surplus is essence and insufficiency is wasting. Where there should be excess, insufficiency is essence; where there should be insufficiency, surplus is wasting. When yin and yang do not correspond, the illness is called closure and rejection."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Qibo said"] Song editors: There is no question before this "Qibo said".

[On the whole passage] It broadly sets out the correspondences of the pulse. In what goes against the four seasons, all insufficiency means blood and qi wasting and damaged, and all surplus means evil qi overcoming the essence. When the qi of yin and yang do not correspond and cannot nourish each other, it is called closure and rejection.

## Notes

The surplus and insufficiency of pulses that go against the four seasons, and the closure and rejection that occur when yin and yang do not correspond.
- The Song editors point out that there is no question from Huangdi before this "Qibo said".
- The passage is hard to follow and read in various ways; the translation follows the characters with reference to Wang Bing.
