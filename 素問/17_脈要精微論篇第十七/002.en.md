# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "Diagnosis should always be done at dawn, when the yin qi has not yet stirred, the yang qi has not yet dispersed, food and drink have not yet been taken, the channels are not yet abundant, the network vessels are evenly regulated, and qi and blood are not yet disordered; therefore one can diagnose a faulty pulse. Feel the movement and stillness of the pulse, look at the essence brightness, examine the five colours, observe the surplus and insufficiency of the five zang, the strength and weakness of the six fu, and the abundance and decline of the body; compare them together to decide between death and life.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Diagnosis should always be done at dawn ... one can diagnose a faulty pulse"] 動 means stirring and descending low; 散 means spreading out. 過 means differing from the usual signs.
Song editors: The *Maijing* and the *Qianjin fang* write 過此非也 for 有過之脈. Wang Bing's note takes "the yin qi has not yet stirred" as stirring and descending low; but since the *Jingui zhenyan lun* says "from dawn to midday is the yang of heaven, the yang within yang", dawn is the time of pure yang in the day, when the yin qi has simply not yet stirred. What meaning of descending low is there?

[On "Feel the movement and stillness ... to decide between death and life"] 切 means bringing the fingers close to the pulse. Jingming ("essence brightness") is the name of a point at the inner canthus of each eye, to the left and right of the *mingtang*; because it is near the eyes it is called essence brightness. It says that by the abundance and decline of form and qi and the amount of pulse, one looks at the qi and colour around the essence brightness, observes the insufficiency and surplus of the zang and fu, and compares them by category to decide between death and life.

## Notes

Diagnosis is best done at dawn, when neither the qi of yin and yang nor qi and blood are disturbed, so that an abnormal pulse can be detected. Pulse, eyes (essence brightness), the five colours, the organs and the body are compared to decide between death and life. Qibo's speech continues to unit 006.
- "Essence brightness" (精明): Wang Bing takes it as the name of a point at the inner canthi; in unit 004 it refers to the eyes themselves.
- "The yin qi has not yet stirred": the Song editors criticize Wang Bing's interpretation.
