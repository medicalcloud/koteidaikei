# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "If on diagnosis one finds the stomach pulse, what is the form of the illness?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks about illnesses shown by the stomach pulse. There is no note by Wang Bing.
