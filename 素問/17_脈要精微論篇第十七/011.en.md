# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore there is a way of holding the pulse: emptiness and stillness are its treasure. On spring days [the pulse] floats, like a fish swimming in the waves; on summer days it is at the skin, brimming as if the myriad things were in surplus; on autumn days it is below the skin, like hibernating insects about to depart; on winter days it is at the bones, like hibernating insects closely sealed and the gentleman staying in his rooms. Therefore it is said: one who knows the interior presses and orders it; one who knows the exterior follows it from end to beginning. These six are the great method of holding the pulse.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Therefore there is a way of holding the pulse: emptiness and stillness are its treasure"] Before, the correspondences of the pulse were clarified; here the means of holding the pulse are set out. The way of holding the pulse requires emptying the mind and stilling the will; only then can one fix fullness and emptiness without error.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 宝 "treasure" for 保.

[On "On spring days it floats, like a fish swimming in the waves"] Though it has come out, it is not yet fully floating.

[On "on summer days it is at the skin, brimming as if the myriad things were in surplus"] 泛泛 describes evenness. The yang qi is greatly abundant, and the pulse qi also resembles the surplus of the myriad things, easy to grasp and surging.

[On "on autumn days it is below the skin, like hibernating insects about to depart"] It follows the gradual descent of the yang qi; hence "below the skin". How is the gradual descent of the yang qi shown? The hibernating insects are about to hide away.

[On "on winter days it is at the bones ... the gentleman staying in his rooms"] "At the bones" means the pulse is deep and sunken; "hibernating insects closely sealed" means the yang qi lies hidden; "the gentleman staying in his rooms" is a human matter.

[On "one who knows the interior presses and orders it"] Knowing the interior means knowing the pulse qi; therefore one presses it and takes it as the guide.

[On "one who knows the exterior follows it from end to beginning"] Knowing the exterior means knowing the images of colour; therefore by the five colours one goes from end back to beginning.

[On "These six are the great method of holding the pulse"] Only after seeing these six can one know the shifts and changes of the pulse.
Song editors: This answers the Emperor's earlier question, "How does the pulse move with the four seasons?"

## Notes

In holding the pulse it is essential to keep the mind empty and still; the depth of the pulse in the four seasons is described by images (floating in spring, at the skin in summer, below the skin in autumn, at the bones in winter). Knowing the interior (pulse) and exterior (colour) together make the six great methods of holding the pulse.
- The Song editors point out that this unit answers the question in unit 008, "How does the pulse move with the four seasons?"
