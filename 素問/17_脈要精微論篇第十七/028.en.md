# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When [the pulse] is coarse and large, yin is insufficient and yang in surplus, causing heat in the center. When it comes fast and goes slowly, excess above and deficient below, there is reversal with illness of the vertex; when it comes slowly and goes fast, deficient above and excess below, there is malign wind.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When it is coarse and large ... heat in the center"] Coarse and large means the pulse is surging and large. A surging pulse is heat; hence "heat in the center".

[On "when it comes slowly and goes fast ... malign wind"] This too is a form of the pulse.

## Notes

Illnesses (heat in the center, reversal with illness of the vertex, malign wind) indicated by a coarse, large pulse, by the speed of its coming and going, and by deficiency or excess above and below.
