# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "If on diagnosis one finds the heart pulse tense, what illness is it? What is its form?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The Emperor said"] Song editors: From "The Emperor said" to "it is cured by treating it with what overcomes it", Quan Yuanqi's edition places in the *Tangye* chapter.

## Notes

Huangdi asks what illness is indicated by a tense heart pulse.
- According to the Song editors, Quan Yuanqi's edition places this unit through unit 024 in the *Tangye* [*laoli lun*] chapter.
