# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Abscesses and swellings, cramped sinews and aching bones — where do these all arise from?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "where do these all arise from?"] 安 means "what"; it asks how they arise.

## Notes

Huangdi asks the cause of abscesses, cramped sinews and aching bones.
