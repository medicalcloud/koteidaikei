# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "These are swellings from cold qi, changes from the eight winds."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] The eight winds are the winds of the eight directions. Abscesses and swellings are changes from being harmed by south-east and south-west winds; cramped sinews and aching bones are changes from being harmed by east and north winds. The *Lingshu jing* says: "Wind from the east is called the infant wind; when it harms a person, it is outside in the sinews. Wind from the south-east is called the weak wind; it is outside in the flesh. Wind from the south-west is called the scheming wind; it is outside in the flesh. Wind from the north is called the great hard wind; it is outside in the bones." From the changes of these four winds the three illnesses arise; hence the question and answer below.

## Notes

Abscesses, cramped sinews and aching bones arise from cold qi and the changes of the eight winds.
- Wang Bing relates the directional winds to the parts affected, following the "Jiugong bafeng" chapter of the *Lingshu jing*.
