# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore the voice accords with the five notes, the complexion with the five phases, and the pulse with yin and yang. Thus one knows that when yin is abundant one dreams of wading through great waters in fear; when yang is abundant, of great fires burning; when yin and yang are both abundant, of killing and injuring one another; when the upper part is abundant, of flying; when the lower part is abundant, of falling; when very full, of giving; when very hungry, of taking; when the liver qi is abundant, of anger; when the lung qi is abundant, of weeping; when short worms are many, of crowds gathering; when long worms are many, of fighting and injuring one another.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Therefore the voice accords with the five notes ... the pulse with yin and yang"] The voice expresses *gong*, *shang*, *jue*, *zhi* and *yu*, so it accords with the five notes; the complexion shows green, yellow, red, white and black, so it accords with the five phases; the pulse displays the rest and dominance of cold and heat, so it accords with the qi of yin and yang.

[On "when yin is abundant one dreams of wading through great waters in fear"] Yin is water; therefore one dreams of wading through water in fear. The *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* (chapter 5) says: "Water is yin."

[On "when yang is abundant, of great fires burning"] Yang is fire; therefore one dreams of great fires burning. The *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* says: "Fire is yang."

[On "when yin and yang are both abundant, of killing and injuring one another"] This too is the image of qi of contending kinds.

[On "when the upper part is abundant, of flying; when the lower part is abundant, of falling"] When qi rises one dreams of going up, hence flying; when qi descends one dreams of going down, hence falling.

[On "when very full, of giving"] Because there is surplus within.

[On "when very hungry, of taking"] Because there is insufficiency within.

[On "when the liver qi is abundant, of anger"] The emotion of the liver is anger.

[On "when the lung qi is abundant, of weeping"] The voice of the lung is sorrowful; therefore weeping.
Song editors: From "Thus one knows that when yin is abundant one dreams of wading through great waters" to here is text from the *Lingshu* mistakenly placed here. It also lacks the dreams when the qi of the heart, spleen and kidney is abundant, which are now found in the *Jiayi jing*.

[On "when short worms are many, of crowds gathering"] When there are many short worms in the body, one dreams of crowds gathering.

[On "when long worms are many, of fighting and injuring one another"] When long worms move, the interior is not at peace; when the interior is not at peace, the spirit is agitated; therefore one has such dreams.
Song editors: These two phrases also should not appear here; they are probably a displaced slip from another classic.

## Notes

The voice, complexion and pulse accord with the five notes, the five phases and yin and yang; the dreams produced by abundance of yin or yang, fullness or hunger, abundant organ qi, and intestinal worms are described.
- The Song editors regard the passage on dreams as text from the *Lingshu* ("Yinxie fameng") mistakenly placed here, and the last two phrases on worms as a displaced slip from another classic.
