# 素問 脈要精微論篇第十七 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The heart is a male zang, and the small intestine is its envoy; therefore it is said there should be a form in the lower abdomen."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] The lower abdomen means the small intestine. The *Linglan midian lun* (chapter 8) says, "The small intestine is the office of receiving." Because it receives, the form lies within.

## Notes

Because the small intestine, paired with the heart, lies in the lower abdomen, the form of heart hernia appears there.
