# 素問 生気通天論篇第三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi said: "From of old, communication with heaven has been the basis of life, and [this basis] rests on yin and yang. Between heaven and earth, within the six directions, its qi — the nine provinces, the nine orifices, the five zang and the twelve joints — all communicate with the qi of heaven. What it generates is five; its qi is three. If one repeatedly offends against this, evil qi harms the person. This is the basis of the life span.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Between heaven and earth ... all communicate with the qi of heaven"] The six directions are the four quarters, above and below. The nine provinces are Ji, Yan, Qing, Xu, Jing, Yang, Yu, Liang and Yong. Outwardly the nine provinces are laid out, and inwardly the nine orifices correspond to them; hence "the nine provinces, the nine orifices". The five zang are the five spirit storehouses: the liver stores the *hun*, the heart stores the spirit, the spleen stores intention, the lung stores the *po*, and the kidney stores the will; by these the body is formed. The twelve joints are the twelve qi: the twelve nodal qi of heaven, to which the twelve channels of the person correspond outwardly, all sharing the order of heaven; hence it says they "all communicate with the qi of heaven". The twelve channels are the three yin and three yang of the hand and the three yin and three yang of the foot.
Song editors: On "communication with heaven is the basis of life", the note to the *Liujie zangxiang lun* (chapter 9) is very detailed. Further, Zheng Kangcheng says: "The nine orifices are the seven yang orifices and the two yin orifices."

[On "What it generates is five; its qi is three ... This is the basis of the life span"] What human life performs rests inwardly on the five qi; the qi that fill and stabilize [the body] within heaven and earth correspond to the three origins. "Three" refers to the qi of heaven, the qi of earth and the qi of circulation. "Offend" means evil qi touching and offending the vital qi. When evil qi offends repeatedly, the vital qi is endangered; therefore treasuring and nourishing the heavenly truth is the basis of the life span. Gengsang Chu says: "The sage controls the myriad things in order to keep his heaven whole; when heaven is whole, the spirit is whole." The *Lingshu jing* says: "Blood and qi are a person's spirit; they must be carefully nourished." This is what is meant.

## Notes

The opening of the chapter. Human life rests on communication with heaven, whose basis is yin and yang. Every part of the body communicates with the qi of heaven, and repeated offence against it lets evil qi harm the person.
- "Nine provinces": Wang Bing lists the nine provinces of the earth, to which the nine orifices of the person correspond.
- "Twelve joints": Wang Bing takes these as the twelve nodal qi, to which the twelve channels correspond.
- "What it generates is five; its qi is three": Wang Bing takes five as the five qi (five phases) and three as the qi of heaven, earth and circulation.
- Daizhige (the plain text) has 島 before 九州.
- In this chapter Huangdi speaks through unit 009, and "Qibo said" appears from unit 010 (see the Song editors' note there).
