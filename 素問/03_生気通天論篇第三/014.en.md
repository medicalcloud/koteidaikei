# 素問 生気通天論篇第三 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, the key to yin and yang is that when yang is dense, [both] are firm. If the two are not in harmony, it is like spring without autumn or winter without summer. To harmonize them accordingly is called the measure of the sage. Therefore if yang is strong but cannot be dense, the yin qi is cut off. When yin is even and yang is sealed, the essence and spirit are ordered. When yin and yang separate and break apart, the essential qi is cut off.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In general, the key to yin and yang is that when yang is dense, [both] are firm"] The key to the meeting of yin and yang lies precisely in the yang qi being closed and dense and not leaking recklessly. If it is dense and does not leak recklessly, the vital qi is strong and firm and can last long. This is the way of the sage.

[On "If the two are not in harmony, it is like spring without autumn or winter without summer"] "The two" means yin and yang; "harmony" means harmonious union, that is, intercourse. 若 means "like". It says that one who cuts off the way of harmonious union of yin and yang is like the four seasons of heaven having spring without autumn or winter without summer. The reason is that it cuts off and abandons generation and completion. Therefore the sage does not cut off the way of harmonious union, but values being closed and dense as the method of guarding and securing the heavenly truth.

[On "To harmonize them accordingly is called the measure of the sage"] To unite only when the yang qi is abundantly issuing, inside and outside respond to each other, and there is surplus vigour, is the sage's rule for intercourse.

[On "Therefore if yang is strong but cannot be dense, the yin qi is cut off"] If yang is strong of itself but cannot be closed and dense, the yin leaks and drains and the essential qi is exhausted and cut off.

[On "When yin is even and yang is sealed, the essence and spirit are ordered"] When the yin qi is harmonious and even and the yang qi is closed and dense, the function of the essence and spirit becomes better ordered day by day.

[On "When yin and yang separate and break apart, the essential qi is cut off"] If yin is not harmonious and even, yang is not closed and dense, and one forcibly emits and drains, damaging and wasting the heavenly truth, the two qi separate, the channels and network vessels break and weaken, the essential qi does not transform, and its flow is cut off.

## Notes

The key to yin and yang lies in yang being dense; disharmony between them is like a year lacking one of its seasons. When yin is even and yang sealed, essence and spirit are ordered; when yin and yang separate, the essential qi is cut off.
- Following the previous unit's "forcing one's strength" (forced sexual activity), Wang Bing reads this whole unit as concerning the measure of sexual union; he takes "to harmonize them accordingly is the measure of the sage" as uniting only when the yang qi is abundant.
- "When yin is even and yang is sealed, the essence and spirit are ordered" is a well-known phrase on the harmony of yin and yang.
