# 素問 生気通天論篇第三 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore the sages set yin and yang in order; their sinews and vessels are harmonious and alike, their bones and marrow are firm, and their qi and blood all comply. In this way inside and outside are in harmony, evil cannot harm, the ears and eyes are keen, and the qi stands as before.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Therefore the sages set yin and yang in order ... their qi and blood all comply"] 従 means comply. It says that if one follows the laws of yin and yang and draws near to the way of nourishing life, the sinews, vessels, bones and marrow each obtain what is fitting; therefore the qi and blood can all comply with the seasons and harmonize the qi.
