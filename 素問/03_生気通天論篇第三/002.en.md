# 素問 生気通天論篇第三 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the qi of the blue heaven is pure and still, the will and intention are ordered; when one complies with it, the yang qi is firm. Even if there are thieving evils, they cannot do harm. This is due to the order of the seasons. Therefore the sages transmit their essence and spirit, take in the qi of heaven, and communicate with the spirit illumination. If one loses it, the nine orifices close within, the flesh is congested without, and the defensive qi disperses and comes apart. This is called harming oneself; it is the paring away of qi.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the qi of the blue heaven is pure and still ... the yang qi is firm"] Spring is the blue heaven, the master of issuing forth and generation. The yang qi is the qi of heaven. The *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* (chapter 5) says, "Clear yang becomes heaven" — this is the meaning. It is based on the principle that when heaven is whole the spirit is whole; when [the spirit] is whole, the body too is whole.

[On "Even if there are thieving evils ... This is due to the order of the seasons"] Because one follows the sequence of the qi of the four seasons of heaven, the qi of thieving evils cannot harm.

[On "Therefore the sages transmit their essence and spirit ... communicate with the spirit illumination"] Essence and spirit can be transmitted, but only sages who have attained the Way can do so. If one takes in the qi of heavenly truth for a long time, its wondrous function naturally communicates with the spirit illumination.

[On "If one loses it ... the defensive qi disperses and comes apart"] "Losing" means going against the principle of the purity and stillness of the blue heaven. The defensive qi accords with the yang qi of heaven. The previous chapter says, "The yang qi closes and blocks," meaning that when the yang qi makes a person ill, the passages of the orifices close and block. The *Lingshu jing* says: "The defensive qi warms the parted flesh, fills the skin, fattens the interstices and governs opening and closing." Therefore if it loses its measure, the nine orifices close within and the flesh is congested without; since the defence does not circulate, it says it disperses and comes apart.

[On "This is called harming oneself; it is the paring away of qi"] To go against the qi of the blue heaven and violate the principle of purity and stillness, so that the correct true qi is pared away, is not something heaven sends down; people do it to themselves.

## Notes

When the qi of heaven is pure and still the will is ordered; complying with it, the yang qi is firm and evils cannot harm. Losing it, the orifices close and the defensive qi disperses — and this is not heaven's doing but self-inflicted harm.
- "Blue heaven": Wang Bing takes it as the heaven of spring.
- "Transmit their essence and spirit": Daizhige (*Chongguang buzhu Huangdi neijing suwen*) writes 摶 "concentrate" for 伝.
- "The previous chapter" refers to the *Siqi tiaoshen dalun* (chapter 2).
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 清静 for 清浄 and has an extra 解 after 散解.
