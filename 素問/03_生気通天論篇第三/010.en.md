# 素問 生気通天論篇第三 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Yin stores the essence and repeatedly rises [to respond]; yang defends the exterior and makes it firm.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Qibo said"] Song editors: The beginning of the chapter says "the Emperor said", and here it says "Qibo said", but they are not question and answer to each other.

[On "Yin stores the essence ... yang defends the exterior and makes it firm"] This speaks of their functions in a person. 亟 means repeatedly.

## Notes

The basic functions of yin and yang: yin stores essence, yang defends the exterior.
- The Song editors point out that although the chapter begins with "the Emperor said" and here we have "Qibo said", the two are not in the form of question and answer.
- *Qi qi* (起亟): Wang Bing glosses 亟 as "repeatedly"; it is taken here as yin storing essence and repeatedly rising to respond to yang.
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 極 for 亟.
- Qibo's speech continues to the end of the chapter (unit 018).
