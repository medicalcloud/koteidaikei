# 素問 生気通天論篇第三 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore, if one carefully harmonizes the five flavours, the bones are upright and the sinews supple, the qi and blood flow thereby, and the interstices become dense thereby. In this way the qi of the bones becomes strong. If one carefully follows the Way according to its laws, one will long enjoy one's heaven-given life span."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In this way the qi of the bones becomes strong ... one will long enjoy one's heaven-given life span"] This is what is called the utmost Way of cultivating the heavenly truth.

## Notes

If the five flavours are harmonized, bones, sinews, qi and blood, and interstices are all regulated, and if one keeps to the Way according to its laws, one can live out one's heaven-given span. This concludes the chapter, and Qibo's speech begun in unit 010 ends here.
- 湊理 is the same as 腠理 "interstices"; the three Daizhige sources write 腠.
