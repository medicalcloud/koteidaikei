# 素問 生気通天論篇第三 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore the yang qi governs the exterior during the day. At dawn the human qi arises, at midday the yang qi is at its height, and when the sun is in the west the yang qi is already depleted and the qi gates close. Therefore at dusk one should gather in and resist [evil], not disturb the sinews and bones, and not be exposed to fog and dew. If one goes against these three times, the body will be distressed and oppressed."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Therefore the yang qi governs the exterior during the day"] By day the yang qi is in the exterior and circulates through the body twenty-five times. The *Lingshu jing* says: "When the eyes open, the qi moves up to the head, and the defensive qi moves through the yang twenty-five times."

[On "At dawn the human qi arises ... the qi gates close"] 隆 is like "high" or "abundant". All that has qi goes from small to strong; warmth accumulates to become blaze, and when the blaze reaches its peak it turns cool again — this is the principle of things. Therefore the yang qi arises at dawn, is abundant at midday, and when the sun is in the west it has already diminished and become depleted. The qi gates are the dark mansions [sweat pores]; because they are where the qi of the channels and of the nutritive and defensive qi is discharged, they are called qi gates.

[On "Therefore at dusk one should gather in ... the body will be distressed and oppressed"] All this is to follow the yang qi: when yang comes out, one comes out; when yang is stored, one stores. At dusk the yang qi declines and moves within in the yin portion; therefore it is fitting to gather in and resist depleting evils. Disturbing the sinews and bones goes against the yang and wastes the essence; exposure to fog and dew lets cold and dampness invade together. Therefore if one follows these three times, the heavenly truth lasts long.

## Notes

Within a day the yang qi arises at dawn, peaks at midday and is depleted by evening, when the qi gates close. Therefore at dusk one should gather in the qi, not overwork the sinews and bones, and avoid fog and dew. Huangdi's speech, begun in unit 001, ends here.
- "Qi gates": Wang Bing takes them as the dark mansions (sweat pores).
- "The three times": dawn, midday and the setting sun.
- In Wang Bing's note the base text writes 巳 in 日西而巳減虚; it is translated as 已.
