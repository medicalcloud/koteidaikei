# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diagnosis has ten measures: measure the person's pulse, measure the viscera, measure the flesh, measure the sinews, measure the transport points. When the qi of yin and yang are fully measured, the person's illness appears of itself. When the pulse moves without constancy, yin scattered and leaning to yang, the pulse is lost and incomplete, and diagnosis has no constant practice; diagnosis must measure above and below, commoners, lords and ministers. Not completing what one receives from one's teacher, leaving the art unclear and not examining counterflow and compliance is reckless practice; holding the female and losing the male, abandoning yin and attaching to yang, not knowing how to combine them, the diagnosis is therefore unclear; passed on to later generations, the contradictions show themselves.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "measure the person's pulse, measure the viscera, measure the flesh, measure the sinews, measure the transport points"] Each measure has two aspects; hence two times five make ten measures.

[On "When the qi of yin and yang are fully measured, the person's illness appears of itself"] When diagnosis is complete and the principles of depletion and abundance of yin and yang are exhausted, the person's illness appears of itself and is known.

[On "When the pulse moves without constancy ... measure above and below, commoners, lords and ministers"] When the pulse moves without constant rate, yin is scattered and yang rather regulated. If the pulse diagnosis is omitted and incomplete, there is no constant diagnosis to practice. To examine it, one should measure the differences in care among the three, commoners and lords and ministers. Why? Because their shares of worry and joy, suffering [and ease] differ, and their ranks differ.

[On "holding the female and losing the male ... the diagnosis is therefore unclear"] All refer to learning that is not comprehensive.

[On "passed on to later generations, the contradictions show themselves"] 章 means exposed. If what is unclear is handed on to others, the traces contrary to antiquity naturally become exposed.

## Notes

The ten measures of diagnosis, measuring commoners, lords and ministers, and the errors of those whose learning is incomplete.
