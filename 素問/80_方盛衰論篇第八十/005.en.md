# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence reversal from scant qi makes people dream wildly, and at its extreme leads to confusion. When the three yang are cut off and the three yin faint, this is scant qi.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Hence reversal from scant qi makes people dream wildly, and at its extreme leads to confusion"] When qi is scant and there is reversal counterflow, people dream wildly; when the reversal is abundant and extreme, their dreams lead to confusion and disorder.

[On "When the three yang are cut off and the three yin faint, this is scant qi"] When the three yang pulses are suspended and cut off and the three yin are thin and faint on diagnosis, these are signs of scant qi.
Song editors: The *Taisu* has "reaching yang cut off from yin; this is scant qi".

## Notes

Reversal from scant qi causes wild dreams; the dreams of depletion of each of the five viscera follow.
