# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

[The Yellow Emperor] answered, "When one [channel's qi] rises and does not descend, cold reversal reaches the knees; the young die in autumn and winter, the old live in autumn and winter. When qi rises and does not descend, there is headache and disease of the vertex; seeking yang one cannot find it, seeking yin one cannot be sure; the five regions are remote and without signs. It is as if dwelling in a vast wilderness, as if lying in an empty room; faint and thready, [life] hangs on for less than a day.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When one rises and does not descend, cold reversal reaches the knees; the young die in autumn and winter, the old live in autumn and winter"] How is one channel's qi reversing upward with yang qi not descending distinguished? By cold reversal reaching the knees. The four limbs are the root of all yang and should be warm, yet they are cold upward; hence "cold reversal". Autumn and winter mean returning to yin; returning to yin, the illness arises following the right. The young, whose yang qi is in charge, die in autumn and winter; the old, whose yin qi is in charge, live in autumn and winter.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says, "The depleted have reversal. When yang qi rises once to the head and does not descend to the feet, the feet and shins are depleted, hence cold reversal reaches the knees."

[On "When qi rises and does not descend, there is headache and disease of the vertex"] 巔 means the top of the body; disease of the vertex is disease of the head.

[On "seeking yang one cannot find it ... [life] hangs on for less than a day"] Calling it yang, the pulse seems yin-abundant; calling it yin, the pulse again seems yang-abundant; hence "seeking yang one cannot find it, seeking yin one cannot be sure". The five regions are the regions of the five viscera; 隔 means remote; 無徴 means nothing reliable to verify. Seeking yang one does not find its heat; seeking yin one cannot be sure it is cold; the regions of the five viscera are remote and offer nothing reliable; hence the phrase. Such cases arise from long-standing counterflow of qi, not from the qi of yin, yang, cold or heat. "As if dwelling in a vast wilderness" means the heart-spirit is scattered; "as if lying in an empty room" means the intent is sunk and hidden. Scattered because the qi counterflows and the pain is severe and unrelenting; sunk because even when the pain settles one fears it will come again. "Faint and thready" means movement and breathing are faint. Though the body lingers faintly, what the mind clings to and hopes for cannot last out the day; hence "faint and thready, [life] hangs on for less than a day".
Song editors: The *Taisu* has [the five characters] 為陰陽之有 after 若伏空室; perhaps these five characters have dropped out here.
