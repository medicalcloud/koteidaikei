# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus the sage's way of holding diagnosis is to hold it by the order of yin and yang. The force of the extraordinary and constant is the sixty headings; diagnose by matching subtle matters, follow the changes of yin and yang, make clear the state of the five centers, and by the theory within them grasp the essentials of depletion and repletion and settle the matters of the five measures. Only knowing these is one fit to diagnose.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "grasp the essentials of depletion and repletion and settle the matters of the five measures. Only knowing these is one fit to diagnose"] The *Qiheng shi* and *Liushi shou* are not transmitted in the present age.

## Notes

The sage's way of diagnosis.
