# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the extreme yin is depleted, heaven's qi is cut off; when the extreme yang is abundant, earth's qi is insufficient. Making yin and yang intersect together is what the perfected person practices. When yin and yang intersect together, yang qi arrives first and yin qi later.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the extreme yin is depleted, heaven's qi is cut off; when the extreme yang is abundant, earth's qi is insufficient"] When the extreme yin is depleted, heaven's qi is cut off and does not descend; when the extreme yang is abundant, earth's qi is faint and does not rise. This is what is called failing to intersect. 至 means most abundant.

[On "Making yin and yang intersect together is what the perfected person practices"] 交 means intersecting and communicating. Only the perfected person can regulate it and make it work.

[On "When yin and yang intersect together, yang qi arrives first and yin qi later"] When the qi of yin and yang move together and intersect at one place, yang qi should arrive first and yin qi later. Why? Yang is fast and yin slow. The *Lingshu jing* says, "What is called intersecting is moving together as one count"; thus the two qi too meet at one place.

## Notes

The intersection of the qi of heaven and earth, and the order of arrival when yin and yang intersect.
