# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus if feeling the yin one does not get the yang, the diagnosis perishes; if getting the yang one does not get the yin, one's learning is not deep. Knowing the left but not the right, knowing the right but not the left, knowing the upper but not the lower, knowing the first but not the after, treatment does not last. Knowing the bad and the good, knowing illness and non-illness, knowing high and low, knowing sitting and rising, knowing going and stopping, and applying them with order, the way of diagnosis is complete and will not fail for ten thousand generations.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "and applying them with order, the way of diagnosis is complete and will not fail for ten thousand generations"] This is the sage's clear admonition on holding diagnosis.

## Notes

The importance of knowing yin and yang, left and right, upper and lower together.
