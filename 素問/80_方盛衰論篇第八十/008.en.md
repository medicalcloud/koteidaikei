# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When liver qi is depleted, people dream of fragrant fungi and growing plants; in its season, they dream of lying under a tree, not daring to rise.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When liver qi is depleted, people dream of fragrant fungi and growing plants"] Fragrant fungi and growing plants are of the kind of plants and trees; the liver matches plants and trees, so one dreams of them.
Song editors: The Quan Yuanqi edition says, "菌香 is cinnamon."

[On "in its season, they dream of lying under a tree, not daring to rise"] The three spring months.

## Notes

Dreams when liver qi is depleted.
