# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When spleen qi is depleted, people dream of insufficient food and drink; in its season, they dream of building walls and roofing houses.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When spleen qi is depleted, people dream of insufficient food and drink"] The spleen takes in water and grain, so one dreams of insufficient food and drink.

[On "in its season, they dream of building walls and roofing houses"] Its season means the months *chen*, *xu*, *chou* and *wei*, each flourishing for eighteen days. Building walls and roofing houses are functions of earth.

## Notes

Dreams when spleen qi is depleted.
