# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

[Leigong] asked, "Do those in surplus have reversal?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "[Leigong] asked, 'Do those in surplus have reversal'"] It means: if non-compliance of the scant is counterflow, does surplus become an illness of reversal and counterflow?

## Notes

A question whether surplus also produces reversal.
