# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When kidney qi is depleted, people dream of boats and drowning people; in its season, they dream of lying in water, as if afraid.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When kidney qi is depleted, people dream of boats and drowning people"] Boats and drowning are both functions of water; the kidneys resemble water, so dreams take this form.

[On "in its season, they dream of lying in water, as if afraid"] The three winter months.

## Notes

Dreams when kidney qi is depleted.
