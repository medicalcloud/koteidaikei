# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All these are depletion of the qi of the five viscera, with yang qi in surplus and yin qi insufficient. Matching them with the five diagnoses and regulating yin and yang are in the *Jingmai*.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "All these are depletion of the qi of the five viscera, with yang qi in surplus and yin qi insufficient"] The bowels are yang qi; the viscera are yin qi.

[On "Matching them with the five diagnoses and regulating yin and yang are in the *Jingmai*"] The *Lingshu jing* has full accounts of regulating yin and yang and matching the five diagnoses; hence it cites it as "in the *Jingmai*". *Jingmai* is a chapter title of the *Lingshu*.

## Notes

A summary of the dreams of the five viscera.
