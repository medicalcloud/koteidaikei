# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence when lung qi is depleted, people dream of white things and of people being slashed with blood everywhere; in its season, they dream of battles.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "when lung qi is depleted, people dream of white things and of people being slashed with blood everywhere"] White things resemble the color of metal; slashing is the function of metal; 藉藉 means dreaming of the appearance of death.

[On "in its season, they dream of battles"] Its season means the three months of autumn. Metal is weapons and armor, hence dreams of battles.

## Notes

Dreams when lung qi is depleted.
