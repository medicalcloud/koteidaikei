# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor answered, "Yang follows the left and yin the right; the old follow the upper and the young the lower. Hence in spring and summer, returning to yang is life and returning to autumn and winter is death; in the reverse [season], returning to autumn and winter is life. Hence when the amounts of qi run counter, all is reversal."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The Yellow Emperor answered, 'Yang follows the left and yin the right'"] The amounts of yang qi all follow the left, those of yin qi all follow the right; following is compliance, reversing is counterflow. The *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* says, "Left and right are the roads of yin and yang."

[On "the old follow the upper and the young the lower"] The old have declining grain [qi], so following the upper is compliant; the young have intense desires, so following the lower is compliant.

[On "Hence in spring and summer, returning to yang is life and returning to autumn and winter is death"] Returning to autumn and winter means returning instead to yin; returning to yin is complying with the qi of killing.

[On "in the reverse [season], returning to autumn and winter is life"] The reverse means autumn and winter; in autumn and winter, returning to yin is life.

[On "Hence when the amounts of qi run counter, all is reversal"] When the amounts of yang qi follow the right instead, and those of yin qi follow the left instead, they are not compliant; hence "the amounts of qi run counter". All such non-compliance of following left or right is reversal; reversal means qi counterflow; hence "all is reversal".

## Notes

The answer on how qi follows left, right, upper and lower, life and death by season, and how counterflow of qi becomes reversal.
