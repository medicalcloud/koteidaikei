# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Raising what is in surplus [in oneself], know what is insufficient [in the patient]; measure matters above and below, and the matter of the pulse is thereby reached. Thus when the form is weak and the qi depleted, death; when the form qi is in surplus and the pulse qi insufficient, death; when the pulse qi is in surplus and the form qi insufficient, life.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Raising what is in surplus [in oneself], know what is insufficient [in the patient]"] The *Baoming quanxing lun* says, "Inner and outer match, without putting the form first." Speaking of raising the surplus of one's own body, one should know the insufficiency of the patient.

[On "measure matters above and below, and the matter of the pulse is thereby reached"] Measuring what suits above and below, the matter of the pulse is thereby brought to subtlety. 格 means reach.

[On "Thus when the form is weak and the qi depleted, death"] Both inner and outer are insufficient.

[On "when the form qi is in surplus and the pulse qi insufficient, death"] The viscera are declining, so the pulse is insufficient.

[On "when the pulse qi is in surplus and the form qi insufficient, life"] The viscera are abundant, so the pulse qi is in surplus.

## Notes

Knowing the patient's insufficiency through one's own surplus, and life and death by the qi of form and pulse.
