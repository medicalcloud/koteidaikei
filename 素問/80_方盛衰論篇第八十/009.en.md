# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When heart qi is depleted, people dream of putting out fires and of yang things; in its season, they dream of burning and scorching.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When heart qi is depleted, people dream of putting out fires and of yang things"] The heart matches fire, so one dreams of it; yang things are also of the kind of fire.

[On "in its season, they dream of burning and scorching"] The three summer months.

## Notes

Dreams when heart qi is depleted.
