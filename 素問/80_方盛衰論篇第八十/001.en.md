# 素問 方盛衰論篇第八十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong asked, "Regarding more and less qi, which is counterflow and which is compliance?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter: Leigong asks which amounts of qi are counterflow and which compliance. There is no note by Wang Bing.
