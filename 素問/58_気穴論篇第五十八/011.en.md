# 素問 気穴論篇第五十八 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The great meetings of the flesh are valleys; the small meetings of the flesh are ravines. In the spaces between the divisions of flesh, the meetings of the valleys and ravines move the nutritive and defensive and gather the great qi. When evil overflows and qi is blocked, the vessels become hot and the flesh rots, the nutritive and defensive do not move, and pus will surely form, wasting the bones and marrow within and breaking the large muscles without. If it lingers where the joints converge, ruin will surely follow. When accumulated cold lingers and lodges and the nutritive and defensive do not stay, the flesh curls and the sinews shrink, the ribs and elbows cannot stretch; within there is bone impediment and without numbness. This is called insufficiency: great cold lingering in the valleys and ravines. The valleys and ravines meet at three hundred sixty-five points, also corresponding to the year. Small impediments that overflow and come and go along the vessels are within reach of the fine needle, following the same method."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the meetings of the valleys and ravines move the nutritive and defensive and gather the great qi"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* reads 以舎大気 "lodge the great qi".

[On "and pus will surely form, wasting the bones and marrow within and breaking the large muscles without"] Excessive heat causes this.

[On "If it lingers where the joints converge, ruin will surely follow"] If it lingers between the bones and joints, where the fluids converge, the marrow fluid between the joints all ulcerates into pus; therefore it surely ruins the sinews and bones and one cannot bend or stretch.

[On "When accumulated cold lingers and lodges ... the sinews shrink"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition reads 寒肉縮筋.

[On "within there is bone impediment ... great cold lingering in the valleys and ravines"] The evil qi is very abundant, the true qi does not nourish, and the marrow overflows and wastes within; hence this. "Insufficiency" means insufficiency of yang qi. Cold evil presses outside, accumulates and lingers long; yang does not prevail outside and the sinews and marrow waste within; hence "this is called insufficiency: great cold lingering in the valleys and ravines".

[On "Small impediments that overflow ... following the same method"] When a slight cold qi flows and overflows, coming and going with the vessels as impediment illness, regulating it with the needle is the same as the ordinary method.

## Notes

The function of the valleys and ravines (meetings of the flesh) and the illnesses arising when hot or cold evil lingers there.
