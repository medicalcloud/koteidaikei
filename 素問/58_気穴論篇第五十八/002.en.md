# 素問 気穴論篇第五十八 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo bowed his head to the ground twice and replied: "What a difficult question! Who but a sage emperor could exhaust its way? I therefore ask to pour out my thoughts and tell all the places."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Who but a sage emperor ... tell all the places"] 孰 means who.

## Notes

Qibo offers to answer.
