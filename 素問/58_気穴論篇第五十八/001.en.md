# 素問 気穴論篇第五十八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I have heard that there are three hundred sixty-five qi points corresponding to the year, but I do not yet know where they are. I wish to hear all of it."

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: the location of the three hundred sixty-five qi points (acupuncture points). There is no note by Wang Bing.
