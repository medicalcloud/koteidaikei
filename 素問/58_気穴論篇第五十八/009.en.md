# 素問 気穴論篇第五十八 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The minute collaterals meet at three hundred sixty-five points, also corresponding to the year; through them unusual pathogens overflow and the ying-nutrient and wei-defense pass. When the ying-nutrient and wei-defense are retained, the wei-defense scatters and the ying-nutrient overflows, the qi is exhausted and the blood clings, outwardly producing fever and inwardly shortness of breath. Drain quickly without delay to open the ying-nutrient and wei-defense; when [such vessels] are seen, drain them without regard to where they meet."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Drain quickly without delay ... without regard to where they meet"] When the ying-nutrient accumulates and the wei-defense is retained and inside and outside press on each other, one should drain at once on seeing the blood collaterals, without regard to the transport or meeting points of the vessel.

## Notes

The minute collaterals also have three hundred sixty-five meeting points and are pathways for the overflow of pathogen; when blood collaterals are seen, they should be drained regardless of points.
- 滎 in 以通滎衛 means 栄 "ying-nutrient" (Wikisource writes 栄).
