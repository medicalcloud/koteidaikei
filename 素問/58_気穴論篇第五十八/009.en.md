# 素問 気穴論篇第五十八 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The minute network vessels meet at three hundred sixty-five points, also corresponding to the year; through them unusual evils overflow and the nutritive and defensive pass. When the nutritive and defensive are retained, the defensive scatters and the nutritive overflows, the qi is exhausted and the blood clings, outwardly producing fever and inwardly shortness of breath. Drain quickly without delay to open the nutritive and defensive; when [such vessels] are seen, drain them without regard to where they meet."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Drain quickly without delay ... without regard to where they meet"] When the nutritive accumulates and the defensive is retained and inside and outside press on each other, one should drain at once on seeing the blood network vessels, without regard to the transport or meeting points of the vessel.

## Notes

The minute network vessels also have three hundred sixty-five meeting points and are pathways for the overflow of evil; when blood network vessels are seen, they should be drained regardless of points.
- 滎 in 以通滎衛 means 栄 "nutritive" (Wikisource writes 栄).
