# 素問 気穴論篇第五十八 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor then dismissed his attendants, rose, bowed twice and said: "Today my ignorance has been opened and my confusion dispelled. I will store this in the golden casket and not dare bring it out again." He then stored it in the Golden Orchid Chamber, labelled "The Locations of the Qi Points".

## Notes

Huangdi stores the teaching in the Golden Orchid Chamber. There is no note by Wang Bing.
