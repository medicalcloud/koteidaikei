# 素問 気穴論篇第五十八 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I now know the places of the qi points, the lodgings where the needle roams. I wish to hear whether the minute network vessels and the valleys and ravines also have correspondences."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I now know the places of the qi points ... also have correspondences"] The minute network vessels are small network vessels, branches of the network vessels.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about the correspondences of the minute network vessels and the valleys and ravines.
- The base text writes 己 for the 已 of 已知; the translation takes it as 已.
