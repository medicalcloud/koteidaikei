# 素問 気穴論篇第五十八 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The minute collaterals that branch from the meridians, whose blood is abundant and should be drained, are also three hundred sixty-five vessels. Together they pour into the collaterals and are transmitted into the twelve collaterals, not only the fourteen collaterals. Those that drain inward into the interior through the clefts are ten vessels."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "and are transmitted into the twelve collaterals, not only the fourteen collaterals"] The fourteen collaterals are the collaterals of the twelve meridians together with those of the *ren* and *du* vessels. The great collaterals of the spleen arises from the spleen itself; therefore it is not included.

[On "Those that drain inward into the interior through the clefts are ten vessels"] 解 means the meridians and collaterals in the clefts of the bones. Although they run separately, the pathogen they receive also pours and drains along them into the vessels of the five zang, five on each side; hence ten vessels.

## Notes

The number of minute collaterals and their pouring into the twelve collaterals and the vessels of the five zang; this ends the chapter.
