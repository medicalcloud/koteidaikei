# 素問 気穴論篇第五十八 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The minute network vessels that branch from the channels, whose blood is abundant and should be drained, are also three hundred sixty-five vessels. Together they pour into the network vessels and are transmitted into the twelve network vessels, not only the fourteen network vessels. Those that drain inward into the interior through the clefts are ten vessels."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "and are transmitted into the twelve network vessels, not only the fourteen network vessels"] The fourteen network vessels are the network vessels of the twelve channels together with those of the *ren* and *du* vessels. The great network vessel of the spleen arises from the spleen itself; therefore it is not included.

[On "Those that drain inward into the interior through the clefts are ten vessels"] 解 means the channels and network vessels in the clefts of the bones. Although they run separately, the evil they receive also pours and drains along them into the vessels of the five zang, five on each side; hence ten vessels.

## Notes

The number of minute network vessels and their pouring into the twelve network vessels and the vessels of the five zang; this ends the chapter.
