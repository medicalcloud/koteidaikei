# 素問 気穴論篇第五十八 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor, raising his hands in respect, drew back and said: "As you open the way for me, though my eyes have not yet seen the places and my ears not yet heard the numbers, my eyes have become bright and my ears keen."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "As you open the way for me ... my ears keen"] "My eyes have become bright and my ears keen" means the heart and will have become clear and penetrating, far beyond what was hoped.

## Notes

Huangdi humbly praises Qibo's teaching.
