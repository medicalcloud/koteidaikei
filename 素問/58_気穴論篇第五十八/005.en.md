# 素問 気穴論篇第五十八 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I am not a sage easy to talk to. People say the true numbers open the mind; what I now ask about are the true numbers, to open ignorance and dispel confusion, not yet enough for discussing [deep matters]. But I wish to hear you pour out your thoughts and tell all the places, so that I may understand their meaning; I will store it in the golden casket and not dare bring it out again."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "what I now ask about are the true numbers ... not yet enough for discussing"] Opening up the true numbers of the qi points may dispel the confusion of ignorance, but is not yet enough to expound the deep and subtle meaning.

[On "But I wish to hear you pour out your thoughts ... not dare bring it out again"] "Tell the places" means the locations of the points.

## Notes

Huangdi again requests to be taught the locations of the qi points.
