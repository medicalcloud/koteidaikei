# 素問 五蔵生成篇第十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What the heart is joined with is the vessels; its splendor is the complexion; its master is the kidney. What the lung is joined with is the skin; its splendor is the body hair; its master is the heart. What the liver is joined with is the sinews; its splendor is the nails; its master is the lung. What the spleen is joined with is the flesh; its splendor is the lips; its master is the liver. What the kidney is joined with is the bones; its splendor is the hair of the head; its master is the spleen.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What the heart is joined with is the vessels"] The qi of fire is active and restless, and the vessels are of the same kind; the heart corresponds to fire; therefore it is joined with the vessels.

[On "its splendor is the complexion"] Fire blazes upward and its color is red; therefore it shows its splendor in the face in a red color.
Song editors: Wang Bing's taking the red color as the splendor of the face is not convincing. In general, all colors that appear in the face are the splendor of the heart; why only red?

[On "its master is the kidney"] "Master" means mainly that it stands in awe of the kidney. Fire fears water, and water acts as its official; therefore it fears the kidney.

[On "What the lung is joined with is the skin"] The qi of metal is firm and settled, and so is the image of the skin; the lung corresponds to metal; therefore it is joined with the skin.

[On "its splendor is the body hair"] The body hair is attached to the skin; therefore it is the outward splendor.

[On "its master is the heart"] Metal fears fire, and fire acts as its official; therefore it mainly fears the heart.

[On "What the liver is joined with is the sinews"] The nature of wood is bending and straight, and so is the body of the sinews; the liver corresponds to wood; therefore it is joined with the sinews.

[On "its splendor is the nails"] The nails are the surplus of the sinews; therefore they are the outward splendor.

[On "its master is the lung"] Wood fears metal, and metal acts as its official; therefore it mainly fears the lung.

[On "What the spleen is joined with is the flesh"] The nature of earth is soft and thick, and so is the body of the flesh; the spleen corresponds to earth; therefore it is joined with the flesh.

[On "its splendor is the lips"] The mouth is the official of the spleen; therefore its splendor is in the lips. "Lips" means the white-colored area around the edges, not the red part.

[On "its master is the liver"] Earth fears wood, and wood acts as its official; therefore it mainly fears the liver.

[On "What the kidney is joined with is the bones"] The nature of water is to flow and moisten, and so is the essential qi; the bones communicate with the essence and marrow; therefore it is joined with the bones.

[On "its splendor is the hair of the head"] The brain is the sea of marrow and the kidney qi governs it; therefore its outward splendor is the hair.

[On "its master is the spleen"] Water fears earth, and earth acts as its official; therefore it mainly fears the spleen.

## Notes

The opening of the chapter: for each of the five zang, the part of the body it is joined with (vessels, skin, sinews, flesh, bones), its outward splendor (complexion, body hair, nails, lips, hair), and its master (the zang that controls it).
- "Its master": Wang Bing explains it as the zang that controls it in the controlling cycle, which it fears.
- "Its splendor is the complexion": Wang Bing takes it as the red color of the face; the Song editors take it as all colors appearing in the face.
- "Its splendor is the lips": Wang Bing takes it as the white area around the edges of the lips.
