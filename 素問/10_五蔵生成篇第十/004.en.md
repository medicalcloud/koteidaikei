# 素問 五蔵生成篇第十 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

[The colour] arising from the heart is like vermilion wrapped in white silk; from the lung, like red wrapped in white silk; from the liver, like dark blue wrapped in white silk; from the spleen, like a trichosanthes fruit wrapped in white silk; from the kidney, like purple wrapped in white silk. These are the outward splendour arising from the five zang.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "arising from the heart ... like purple wrapped in white silk"] This is the true appearance of the colour of life. 縞 means white; 紺 means pale blue-green.

[On "These are the outward splendour arising from the five zang"] 栄 means beautiful colour.

## Notes

The colours of healthy zang appear faintly from within, as if wrapped in white silk.
- In the previous unit Wang Bing says colours are best when they appear faintly, and he takes this unit as its explanation.
- Daizhige writes 括蔞 or 栝蔞 for 栝楼.
