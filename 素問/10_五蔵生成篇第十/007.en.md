# 素問 五蔵生成篇第十 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A person has twelve divisions of great valleys and three hundred and fifty-four named small ravines, less the twelve transport points. These are all where the defensive qi lingers and stops and where evil qi lodges. Needles and stones make [the evil] follow along and depart.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "A person has twelve divisions of great valleys"] Where the great channels meet are called great valleys; the twelve divisions are the regions of the twelve channels.

[On "three hundred and fifty-four named small ravines, less the twelve transport points"] Where the small network vessels meet are called small ravines. Speaking of the 365 small network vessels, excluding the twelve transport points there should be 353 names. That the classic says 354 is because in transmission and copying in running script, three was mistakenly written as four.
Song editors: Another edition, Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu* write 関 "gates" for 兪.

[On "These are all where the defensive qi lingers and stops and where evil qi lodges"] When the defensive qi is full and circulates, evil qi cannot reside; when the defensive qi is deficient and lingers, the evil qi lodges there; hence "where evil qi lodges".

[On "Needles and stones make [the evil] follow along and depart"] 縁 describes following along and departing. It says that where evil qi lodges and the defensive qi lingers, if one needles those ravines and valleys, the evil qi follows along the vessels and departs.

## Notes

The numbers of the great valleys (the regions of the twelve channels) and small ravines (the meeting places of the small network vessels) are given; these are where the defensive qi lingers and evil qi lodges, and the evil is removed by needles and stones.
- "354": Wang Bing says it should be 353 (365 less the twelve transport points), the "four" being a copying error.
- According to the Song editors, another edition, Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu* write "twelve gates" for "twelve transport points".
