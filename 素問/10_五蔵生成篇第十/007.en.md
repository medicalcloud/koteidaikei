# 素問 五蔵生成篇第十 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

A person has twelve divisions of great valleys and three hundred and fifty-four named small ravines, less the twelve transport points. These are all where the wei-defensive qi lingers and stops and where pathogenic qi lodges. Needles and stones make [the pathogen] follow along and depart.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "A person has twelve divisions of great valleys"] Where the great meridians meet are called great valleys; the twelve divisions are the regions of the twelve meridians.

[On "three hundred and fifty-four named small ravines, less the twelve transport points"] Where the small collaterals meet are called small ravines. Speaking of the 365 small collaterals, excluding the twelve transport points there should be 353 names. That the classic says 354 is because in transmission and copying in running script, three was mistakenly written as four.
Song editors: Another edition, Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu* write 関 "gates" for 兪.

[On "These are all where the wei-defensive qi lingers and stops and where pathogenic qi lodges"] When the wei-defensive qi is full and circulates, pathogenic qi cannot reside; when the wei-defensive qi is deficient and lingers, the pathogenic qi lodges there; hence "where pathogenic qi lodges".

[On "Needles and stones make [the pathogen] follow along and depart"] 縁 describes following along and departing. It says that where pathogenic qi lodges and the wei-defensive qi lingers, if one needles those ravines and valleys, the pathogenic qi follows along the vessels and departs.

## Notes

The numbers of the great valleys (the regions of the twelve meridians) and small ravines (the meeting places of the small collaterals) are given; these are where the wei-defensive qi lingers and pathogenic qi lodges, and the pathogen is removed by needles and stones.
- "354": Wang Bing says it should be 353 (365 less the twelve transport points), the "four" being a copying error.
- According to the Song editors, another edition, Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu* write "twelve gates" for "twelve transport points".
