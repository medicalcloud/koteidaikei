# 素問 五蔵生成篇第十 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In general, in examining the five colours with irregular pulses: those with a yellow face and green eyes, a yellow face and red eyes, a yellow face and white eyes, or a yellow face and black eyes all do not die. Those with a green face and red eyes, a red face and white eyes, a green face and black eyes, a black face and white eyes, or a red face and green eyes all die.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In general, in examining the five colours with irregular pulses ... all do not die"] "Irregular pulse" means the pulse does not match the colour. Whenever yellow appears in the colour, there is stomach qi; therefore they do not die.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* lacks the three characters 之奇脈.

[On "Those with a green face and red eyes ... all die"] That all those without yellow die is because they have no stomach qi. The five zang take the stomach qi as their root; therefore all without yellow are said to die.

## Notes

Judging life and death by combinations of facial and eye colour: with yellow in the face there is stomach qi and one does not die; without yellow one dies. This ends the chapter.
- "Irregular pulses": Wang Bing explains it as a pulse not matching the colour. According to the Song editors, the *Jiayi jing* lacks the three characters 之奇脈.
