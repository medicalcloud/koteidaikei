# 素問 五蔵生成篇第十 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Colours and flavours correspond to the five zang: white corresponds to the lung and acrid; red to the heart and bitter; green to the liver and sour; yellow to the spleen and sweet; black to the kidney and salty. Therefore white corresponds to the skin, red to the vessels, green to the sinews, yellow to the flesh, and black to the bones.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Colours and flavours correspond to the five zang ... black to the kidney and salty"] Each corresponds to what it responds to and becomes colour and flavour.

[On "Therefore white corresponds to the skin ... black to the bones"] Each returns to the qi of the zang that nourishes it.

## Notes

A summary of the correspondences of the five colours and five flavours with the five zang and the five tissues (skin, vessels, sinews, flesh, bones).
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 赤当辛 for 赤当心 and 鹸 for 鹹.
