# 素問 五蔵生成篇第十 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The qi of the five zang [show in the complexion]. Therefore if the colour appearing is green like fresh grass, death; yellow like a bitter-orange fruit, death; black like soot, death; red like clotted blood, death; white like a dry bone, death. These are the five colours that show death. Green like kingfisher feathers, life; red like a cockscomb, life; yellow like a crab's belly, life; white like pig fat, life; black like crow feathers, life. These are the five colours that show life.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The qi of the five zang"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition reads "This is how the five flavours correspond to the qi of the five zang", joining it to the text above; the *Taisu* is the same.

[On "Therefore if the colour appearing is green like fresh grass, death"] 茲 means 滋 (fresh growth); it means like the green colour of grass just sprouted.

[On "yellow like a bitter-orange fruit, death"] The colour is green-yellow.

[On "black like soot, death"] 炲 means soot.

[On "red like clotted blood, death"] Clotted blood is spoiled, bad, congealed blood; its colour is red-black.

[On "white like a dry bone, death"] White and withered, like the whiteness of a dried bone.

[On "These are the five colours that show death"] Because the zang are ruined, the colour of death appears. The *Sanbu jiuhou lun* (chapter 20) says, "When the five zang are already ruined, the colour must be dull; if dull, one must die" — this is what is meant.

[On "Green like kingfisher feathers, life ... These are the five colours that show life"] This means lustrous and moist. Even if a colour is pleasing, it is best when it appears faintly. Hence the text below says:

## Notes

Judging life and death by the complexion: for each of the five colours, a lustreless shade (fresh grass, bitter orange, soot, clotted blood, dry bone) means death and a lustrous shade (kingfisher feathers, cockscomb, crab's belly, pig fat, crow feathers) means life.
