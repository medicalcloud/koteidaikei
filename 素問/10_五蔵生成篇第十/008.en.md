# 素問 五蔵生成篇第十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

At the beginning of diagnosing illness, the five decisions are the guiding principle. To know its beginning, first establish its mother. The so-called five decisions are the five pulses. Thus, headache and illness of the vertex, with depletion below and repletion above: the fault lies in the foot lesser yin and greater yang; if severe, it enters the kidney. Dizzy dimness and shaking, with dim eyes and deaf ears, repletion below and depletion above: the fault lies in the foot lesser yang and *jueyin*; if severe, it enters the liver. Abdominal fullness and distension, propping fullness of the diaphragm and flanks, reversal below and clouding above: the fault lies in the foot greater yin and *yangming*. Coughing and rising qi, with reversal in the chest: the fault lies in the hand *yangming* and greater yin. Vexation of the heart and headache, with the illness in the diaphragm: the fault lies in the hand greater yang and lesser yin.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "At the beginning of diagnosing illness, the five decisions are the guiding principle"] The five decisions means taking the pulses of the five zang as the guiding principle for deciding life and death.

[On "To know its beginning, first establish its mother"] 建 means establish. "Mother" means the dominant qi corresponding to the season. One first establishes the dominant qi corresponding to the season, and then looks for the evil and correct qi.

[On "The so-called five decisions are the five pulses"] This means the pulses of the five zang.

[On "Thus, headache and illness of the vertex ... if severe, it enters the kidney"] The foot lesser yin is the kidney vessel and the greater yang the bladder vessel. The bladder vessel starts at the inner canthus, rises to the forehead and crosses at the vertex; its branch goes from the vertex to the upper corner of the ear; its straight course enters from the vertex to connect with the brain, comes out again, branches down the nape, follows the inside of the shoulder blade, runs alongside the spine to the lower back, enters along the paravertebral muscles, connects with the kidney and belongs to the bladder. When the kidney is depleted and cannot draw on the qi of the greater yang, there is headache and illness of the vertex above. When the channel illness becomes severe, it enters the zang.

[On "Dizzy dimness and shaking ... if severe, it enters the liver"] 徇 means sudden; 蒙 means unclear: it means the eyes suddenly become ill and unclear. 招 means shaking, swaying without settling; 尤 means severe. Eyes ill and unclear and the head shaking severely refers to sudden illness; dim eyes and deaf ears refer to gradual illness. The foot lesser yang is the gallbladder vessel and *jueyin* the liver vessel. The *jueyin* vessel rises from the lower abdomen, runs alongside the stomach, belongs to the liver and connects with the gallbladder, pierces the diaphragm, spreads over the flanks and ribs, follows behind the throat, enters the nasopharynx, rises to emerge at the forehead and meets the *du* vessel at the vertex; its branch descends from the eye connector inside the cheek. The foot lesser yang vessel starts at the outer canthus, rises to the corner of the head, descends behind the ear, follows the neck and enters the supraclavicular fossa; its branch enters the ear from behind; another branch separates from the outer canthus, descends past the cheekbone, joins at Jiache, descends the neck to meet at the supraclavicular fossa, and from there descends into the chest, pierces the diaphragm, connects with the liver and belongs to the gallbladder. Now the qi is insufficient; therefore there is this illness.
Song editors: Wang Bing's note takes 徇蒙 as the eyes suddenly becoming ill and unclear, but the meaning is not very clear. 徇蒙 probably means the eyelids twitching rapidly with dim vision. Also, "descends past the cheekbone" in the lesser yang vessel is written 下䪼 in the *Jiayi jing*.

[On "Abdominal fullness ... the fault lies in the foot greater yin and *yangming*"] 胠 means above the flanks. "Reversal below and clouding above" means the qi counterflows from below upward and clouds the eyes. The foot greater yin is the spleen vessel and *yangming* the stomach vessel. The foot greater yin vessel enters the abdomen from the front inner edge of the thigh, belongs to the spleen, connects with the stomach and rises to the diaphragm. The foot *yangming* vessel starts at the nose, crosses at the root of the nose, descends along the outside of the nose, connects below with the chin and jaw, enters the supraclavicular fossa from the throat, belongs to the stomach and connects with the spleen; its straight course descends from the supraclavicular fossa along the inner edge of the breast, runs down alongside the navel and enters Qijie; its branch starts at the lower opening of the stomach, follows inside the abdomen to Qijie, where it joins, and from there descends the thigh. Therefore there is this illness.

[On "Coughing and rising qi ... the fault lies in the hand *yangming* and greater yin"] The hand *yangming* is the large-intestine vessel and the greater yin the lung vessel. The hand *yangming* vessel rises from the front edge of Jianyu to emerge at the meeting of the pillar bone, then descends into the supraclavicular fossa, connects with the lung, descends through the diaphragm and belongs to the large intestine. The hand greater yin vessel starts in the middle burner, descends to connect with the large intestine, returns along the opening of the stomach, rises through the diaphragm, belongs to the lung, and from the lung connector emerges crosswise below the armpit. Therefore there is coughing and rising qi with reversal in the chest.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 病 "illness" for 厥 "reversal".

[On "Vexation of the heart and headache ... the fault lies in the hand greater yang and lesser yin"] The hand greater yang is the small-intestine vessel and the lesser yin the heart vessel. The greater yang vessel enters the supraclavicular fossa from above the shoulder, connects with the heart, follows the throat, descends through the diaphragm, reaches the stomach and belongs to the small intestine; its branch follows the neck from the supraclavicular fossa, rises to the cheek and reaches the outer canthus. The hand lesser yin vessel starts in the heart, emerges to belong to the heart connector, descends through the diaphragm and connects with the small intestine. Therefore there is vexation of the heart and headache with the illness in the diaphragm.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* reads: "Pain in the chest, propping fullness, and pain in the lower back and back pulling on each other: the fault lies in the hand lesser yin and greater yang."

## Notes

In diagnosis, the pulses of the five zang (the five decisions) are the guiding principle, and one first establishes the dominant qi of the season (the mother). Then, according to the symptoms, the illness is located in the foot lesser yin and greater yang, the foot lesser yang and *jueyin*, the foot greater yin and *yangming*, the hand *yangming* and greater yin, or the hand greater yang and lesser yin.
- "First establish its mother": Wang Bing takes "mother" as the dominant qi corresponding to the season.
- 徇蒙招尤 is an obscure phrase. Wang Bing takes it as the eyes suddenly becoming unclear and the head shaking; the Song editors suggest twitching eyelids and dim vision.
- Wang Bing describes the course of each channel in detail following the "Jingmai" chapter of the *Lingshu jing*.
- 𠻳 in Wang Bing's 咳𠻳 is the base text's form, translated as 嗽.
