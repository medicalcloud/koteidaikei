# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §43 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When lesser yin controls heaven, hot qi presides below and lung qi follows above; white [things] rise [in price] and metal is put to use; plants suffer calamity; there are panting, vomiting, cold and heat, sneezing, nasal congestion and nosebleed, blocked nose; great summer heat prevails, and in severe cases there are sores and ulcers and burning, metal melts and stones flow. The earth is dry and clear, chill and cold arrive often, there are flank pain and frequent sighing, austere killing proceeds, and plants change.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When lesser yin controls heaven ... great summer heat prevails"] The signs of *zi* and *wu* years. Heat controls heaven's qi, so these illnesses arise, brought about by heaven's qi.

[On "in severe cases there are sores and ulcers and burning, metal melts and stones flow"] The intersection of heaven.

[On "The earth is dry and clear ... plants change"] 変 means altering the guest's substance. Flank pain and sighing arise from earth's qi.

## Notes

The weather and illnesses in a year when lesser yin controls heaven and *yangming* is in the spring.
