# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What of long life and early death?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of long life and early death?"] It refers to the longevity or early death of people living in each land.

## Notes

A question on the relation between terrain and lifespan.
