# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Heaven is insufficient in the northwest, cold on the left and cool on the right; earth is not full in the southeast, hot on the right and warm on the left. What is the reason?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Heaven is insufficient in the northwest ... What is the reason?"] It is said facing Xun (southeast).

## Notes

A question on the relation between terrain and cold and heat.
