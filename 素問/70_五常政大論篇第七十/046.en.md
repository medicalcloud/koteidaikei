# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Among the six qi and the five kinds [of creatures], there is overcoming and restraint: what is the same is made abundant, what is different is made to decline. This is the way of heaven and earth and the norm of generation and transformation. So when *jueyin* controls heaven, furred creatures are still, feathered creatures grow, and shelled creatures do not mature; when it is in the spring, furred creatures grow, naked creatures diminish, and feathered creatures do not grow.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "So when *jueyin* controls heaven, furred creatures are still, feathered creatures grow, and shelled creatures do not mature"] This refers to the years *yisi*, *dingsi*, *jisi*, *xinsi*, *guisi*, *yihai*, *dinghai*, *jihai*, *xinhai* and *guihai*. 静 means without sound; it also means quietly withdrawing without taking the lead. The feathered are creatures of fire, whose qi is the same as that of earth [in the spring]. Fire restrains the transformation of metal, so shelled creatures do not mature; it means that 曰 [white] armored creatures seldom breed.

[On "when it is in the spring, furred creatures grow, naked creatures diminish, and feathered creatures do not grow"] Earth's qi restrains earth, and yellow naked [creatures] diminish; when the year rides on a wood movement, it is worse. Feathered creatures do not grow because lesser yang [controlling heaven] suppresses them itself; these are the five *yin* and five *shen* years. Whenever it says "do not grow" or "do not mature", it means few, not none at all.

## Notes

The overcoming and restraint between the six qi and the five kinds of creatures (furred, feathered, naked, scaled, shelled), and the flourishing and decline of creatures when *jueyin* controls heaven or is in the spring. Qibo's speech continues to unit 052.
- The base text writes 岐伯 in this unit.
- 曰 in Wang Bing's 謂曰色有甲之虫 is presumably an error for 白.
