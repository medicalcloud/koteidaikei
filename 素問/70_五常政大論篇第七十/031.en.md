# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence it is said: where the qi is cold and cool, treat with cold and cool, and soak in running water; where the qi is warm and hot, treat with warm and hot, and strengthen inner guarding. One must make it the same as the qi; then it can be balanced. The exceptions are treated the opposite way."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "One must make it the same as the qi; then it can be balanced. The exceptions are treated the opposite way"] For cold regions use cold, for hot regions heat, for warm regions warmth, for cool regions coolness; this is the proper method, making it the same as the qi. "Soak in running water" means soaking in a bath. 平 means balancing and regulating. If in the west and north there is a cold illness, one borrows hot and warm prescriptions to remove it; if in the east and south there is a heat illness that must be treated with cool or cold prescriptions, one takes it contrary to the proper method above.

## Notes

Treating with medicines of the same nature as the region's qi is the proper method; exceptions go the opposite way. Qibo's speech ends here.
- Following Wang Bing's note, 気寒気涼，治以寒涼 is read as using cold and cool prescriptions in cold and cool regions as the proper method. The bracketed supplements in the Japanese translation are not a checked interpretation.
