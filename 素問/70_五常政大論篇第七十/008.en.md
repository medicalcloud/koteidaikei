# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "How thorough the question! In the period of spreading harmony, the virtue of wood circulates everywhere, yang unfolds and yin spreads, and the five transformations are proclaimed and balanced. Its qi is upright, its nature compliant, its function bending and straightening, its transformation generating and flourishing, its kind plants and trees, its government dispersing, its indication warmth and mildness, its decree wind, its depot the liver—the liver fears clarity—its governed organ the eyes, its grain hemp, its fruit the plum, its fruit-type the pit, its correspondence spring, its creatures the furred, its domestic animal the dog, its color green, what it nourishes the sinews, its illness internal tension and propping fullness, its flavor sour, its note *jue*, its things hard in the center, its number eight.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How thorough the question!"] Song editors: This treatise corresponds to the *Wuyunxing dalun*, *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* and *Jingui zhenyan lun*.

[On "the virtue of wood circulates everywhere ... balanced"] It occupies its own position and does not contend with things; hence the transformations of the five qi each spread their government and decrees in the four directions without trespassing on one another.
Song editors: Wang's notes record the years for excess and deficiency, but this note on balanced wood movement does not, because the years of balanced qi cannot be fixed. Some wish to supplement the note with "the *dingsi*, *dinghai*, *renyin* and *renshen* years", but this is not understanding it.

[On "Its qi is upright"] 端 means straight and fair.

[On "its nature compliant"] It complies with the transformation of things.

[On "its function bending and straightening"] Crooked and straight timbers and trunks all serve their uses.

[On "its transformation generating and flourishing"] When the transformation of wood spreads, things grow and flourish beautifully.

[On "its kind plants and trees"] The body of trees is hard and tall, the form of grasses low; but each has hard and brittle, firm and soft, creeping, knotted and bending kinds.

[On "its government dispersing"] Spring qi disperses and things receive it to grow; this is the transformation of wood.

[On "its indication warmth and mildness"] The qi of mild spring.

[On "its decree wind"] The decree of wood proceeds by mild wind.

[On "its depot the liver"] Among the qi of the five depots, it is the same as the liver.

[On "the liver fears clarity"] Clarity is the decree of metal. The nature of wood is warm, so it fears clarity. The *Wuyunxing dalun* says "wood, its nature is warmth" and "dryness overcomes wind".

[On "its governed organ the eyes"] Yang rises and things are clearly seen; the eyes are the same.

[On "its grain hemp"] Its color is green.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* says "its grain is wheat", which differs.

[On "its fruit the plum"] Its taste is sour.

[On "its fruit-type the pit"] Those with a hard pit inside.

[On "its correspondence spring"] Among the four seasons, it is the same as the transformation of spring.

[On "its creatures the furred"] When the transformation of wood spreads, furred creatures are born.

[On "its domestic animal the dog"] Like the growth of plants, it avoids nothing.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* says "its domestic animal is the chicken".

[On "its color green"] When the transformation of wood spreads, things are tinged with green.

[On "what it nourishes the sinews"] Sourness enters the sinews.

[On "its illness internal tension and propping fullness"] Produced by wood qi.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* says "thus one knows that the illness is in the sinews".

[On "its flavor sour"] When the transformation of wood spreads harmony, things are rich in sour flavor.

[On "its note *jue*"] Harmonious and straight.

[On "its things hard in the center"] It images wood within earth.

[On "its number eight"] The completion number.
