# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When *jueyin* controls heaven, wind qi presides below and spleen qi follows above; earth also rises; yellow [things] rise [in price] and water suffers calamity; the use of earth changes; there are heaviness of the body, wasting of the flesh, reduced eating and loss of taste; wind moves through the great void, clouds and things sway, the eyes spin and the ears ring. Fire gives free rein to its violence, the earth is hot, great heat melts and scorches, there is red discharge below, hibernating creatures often appear, and flowing water does not freeze. Its outbreak is as swift as a trigger.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When *jueyin* controls heaven ... the eyes spin and the ears ring"] The signs of 己 [*si*] and *hai* years. 土隆 and 土用革 mean that earth qi is used and changes its body; it also means earthworks. Clouds and things swaying means high wind. These illnesses arise from heaven's qi.

[On "Fire gives free rein to its violence ... flowing water does not freeze"] Lesser yang is in the spring and fire supervises the earth; hence this. The origin and signs of illness arise from earth's qi.

[On "Its outbreak is as swift as a trigger"] The qi of lesser yang and *jueyin* change suddenly and urgently; the illnesses they produce come as swiftly as a released trigger; hence "its outbreak is as swift as a trigger".

## Notes

The weather and illnesses in a year when *jueyin* controls heaven and lesser yang is in the spring.
- 己亥之歳 in Wang Bing's note is read as 巳亥之歳. 口爽 is read as loss of taste.
