# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §64 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Illnesses are long-standing or new, prescriptions large or small, with or without poison; there should of course be constant rules. When treating illness with greatly poisonous [medicines], remove six parts in ten; with ordinarily poisonous, seven in ten; with slightly poisonous, eight in ten; with non-poisonous, nine in ten. [The rest] is finished by nourishing with grains, meats, fruits and vegetables; do not overdo it and harm the upright. If it is not finished, proceed again as before. One must first [know] the year's qi and not violate heaven's harmony; do not make the abundant more abundant or the depleted more depleted, leaving people heaven's calamity; do not invite the pathogen or lose the upright, cutting off people's long lives."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When treating illness with greatly poisonous [medicines], remove six parts in ten"] The poison of lower-grade medicines, the greatest poison.

[On "with ordinarily poisonous, seven in ten"] The poison of middle-grade medicines, next to the lower grade.

[On "with slightly poisonous, eight in ten"] The poison of upper-grade medicines, the smallest poison.

[On "with non-poisonous, nine in ten"] Non-poisonous medicines of the upper, middle and lower grades are all called balanced.

[On "[The rest] is finished by nourishing ... and harm the upright"] The nature of great poison is fierce and it harms much; the nature of 少 [slight] poison is mild and it harms little. The nature of ordinary poison is one degree less than great poison and one degree more than slight poison; its harm can be inferred. So when the limit is reached, one must stop and wait for the next signs. Even non-poisonous medicines, though mild in nature, when used long and much, give one-sided prevalence and one-sided exhaustion of qi; attacking long with them makes the depot qi one-sidedly weak; weakened and distressed, how can one not fear? Hence stop taking them after removing nine parts in ten. After reaching the limit, give the five grains, five meats, five fruits and five vegetables according to what suits the five depots, to finish the remaining illness. Using medicine and food together is also acceptable.
Song editors: The *Zangqi fashi lun* says "poisonous medicines attack pathogens, the five grains nourish, the five fruits 肋 [assist], the five domestic animals benefit, the five vegetables fill".

[On "If it is not finished, proceed again as before"] The rule means the four limits above. If the remaining illness is not finished, do it again, stopping at the limit according to the strength of the poison, never overdoing it.

[On "One must first [know] the year's qi and not violate heaven's harmony"] The year has six qi, which govern separately, with governments facing south and north. If one first knows where these six qi are, the pulse responds at the *chi* and *cun*: where greater yin is, the pulse is sunken; where lesser yin is, hooked; where *jueyin* is, stringlike; where greater yang is, large and long; where *yangming* is, short and rough; where lesser yang is, large and floating. These six pulses are called heaven's harmony. Not recognizing them and calling them cold or heat, if one attacks cold to make heat, the pulse does not change and a heat illness has already arisen; if one restrains heat to make cold, the pulse is as before and a cold illness arises again. How can one find the right balance? Early deaths mostly come from this.

[On "do not make the abundant more abundant ... leaving people heaven's calamity"] If one does not examine depletion and repletion but thinks only of attacking, the abundant become more abundant and the depleted more depleted. Myriad illnesses worsen from this; the true qi wanes daily and the illness encroaches daily. Calamity comes as if sent by heaven and is hard to escape. Alas!

[On "do not invite the pathogen or lose the upright, cutting off people's long lives"] This is the so-called violation of heaven's harmony. Attacking depletion and calling it repletion invites the pathogen; not recognizing the depletion of the depots is losing the upright. Once the qi is lost, it becomes the cause of death.

## Notes

The limits of taking medicines according to the strength of their poison, finishing with food, and knowing the year's qi so as not to violate heaven's harmony.
- The blank in Wang Bing's 其為傷也　…少 is as in the base text. 肋 in 五果為肋 is presumably an error for 助.
