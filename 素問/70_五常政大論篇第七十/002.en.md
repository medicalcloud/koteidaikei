# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "How illuminating the question! [The balanced] wood is called spreading harmony, fire rising brightness, earth complete transformation, metal discerning balance, and water quiet compliance."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "wood is called spreading harmony"] It spreads harmonious qi, by which things grow and flourish.

[On "fire rising brightness"] The qi of fire is high and bright.

[On "earth complete transformation"] It covers all broadly with the qi of transformation and 損 [provides for] all kinds.

[On "metal discerning balance"] The qi of metal is clear and discerning, balanced and settled.

[On "water quiet compliance"] The body of water is clear and quiet and complies with things.

## Notes

The names of the balanced qi of the five movements.
- 損 in Wang Bing's 損於群品 may be an error for something like 備 from the sense, but this has not been checked.
