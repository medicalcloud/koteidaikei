# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When greater yin controls heaven, damp qi presides below and kidney qi follows above; black [things] rise [in price] and water changes; dust covers and there are clouds and rain; the chest is not at ease; there is impotence with greatly declined qi, unable to rise and to be used; in its season there is on the contrary pain in the lumbus and buttocks, difficulty in moving and turning, and reversal. The earth stores yin, great cold is about to arrive, hibernating creatures cling early, there is obstruction and pain below the heart, the ground cracks and ice is hard, there is lower abdominal pain, and at times eating is impaired. When it rides on metal, still water increases and its taste becomes salty, and flowing water decreases."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When greater yin controls heaven, damp qi presides below, kidney qi follows above, black [things] rise [in price] and water changes"] Song editors: On examination, by the texts before and after, the three characters 火廼眚 ("fire suffers calamity") are missing here.

[On "the chest is not at ease; there is impotence with greatly declined qi, unable to rise and to be used"] Song editors: On examination, the two characters 不用 should be 水用 ("water is used").

[On "in its season there is on the contrary pain in the lumbus and buttocks, difficulty in moving and turning"] The signs of *chou* and *wei* years. 水変 means sweet springs turning salty. 埃 means earthen fog; 冒 means the distance cannot be made out; clouds and rain are the transformation of earth. 脽 means the flesh of the buttocks. These illnesses arise from heaven's qi.

[On "and reversal"] Song editors: On examination, the two characters 厥逆 are suspected to belong with the text above.

[On "The earth stores yin ... flowing water decreases"] Still water means wells and springs; flowing water means rivers and canals. Though still water increases, its normal sweetness changes to a salty taste. These illnesses arise from earth's qi.
Song editors: On examination, for the transformation of greater yin controlling heaven, it does not say "in severe cases such-and-such illness" but "in its season", and also "when it rides on metal, etc.", mutually illuminating the earlier items.

## Notes

The weather and illnesses in a year when greater yin controls heaven and greater yang is in the spring. The discussion of the six qi controlling heaven and in the spring, and Qibo's speech, end here.
- Following the Song editors, 厥逆 is read as belonging with the preceding text.
