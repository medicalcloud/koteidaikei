# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §47 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When lesser yin controls heaven, feathered creatures are still, shelled creatures grow, and furred creatures do not mature; when it is in the spring, feathered creatures grow, and shelled creatures diminish and do not grow.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When lesser yin controls heaven ... furred creatures do not mature"] This refers to the years *jiazi*, *bingzi*, *wuzi*, *gengzi*, *renzi*, *jiawu*, *bingwu*, *wuwu*, *gengwu* and *renwu*. "Still" refers to the kinds such as the swallows of Hu and Yue and the shrike. In these years few black furred creatures breed and mature.

[On "when it is in the spring, feathered creatures grow, and shelled creatures diminish and do not grow"] Earth's qi restrains metal, so white shelled creatures do not grow; when the year rides on a fire movement, this is worse. These are the five *mao* and five *you* years.
Song editors: On examination, shelled creatures diminish because lesser yin is in the spring and fire conquers metal; they do not grow because *yangming* is in heaven and suppresses them itself.

## Notes

The flourishing and decline of creatures when lesser yin controls heaven or is in the spring.
