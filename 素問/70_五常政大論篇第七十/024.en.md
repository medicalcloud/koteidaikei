# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence the saying: 'If it does not keep its virtue constant, what it overcomes comes to retaliate; if its government keeps its principle constant, what it overcomes transforms together with it.' This is what is meant."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Hence the saying ... This is what is meant"] Not keeping constant means relying on 已 [one's own] surplus to override and offend what it does not overcome. Keeping constant means transforming according to the norm without giving free rein to punishment. In this case even the qi that conquers 已 [oneself] shares governing transformation in that year.
Song editors: On examination, the account of the excess of the five movements is given in full in the *Qijiaobian dalun*.

## Notes

An old saying is cited to conclude the discussion of the periods of the three kinds of qi. Qibo's speech ends here.
- 已 in Wang Bing's note is read as 己.
