# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §56 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It is restrained by earth's qi. It is not that heaven does not generate or that earth does not grow."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is restrained by earth's qi. It is not that heaven does not generate or that earth does not grow"] Though heaven and earth have no intention in generation and transformation, the qi of generation and transformation naturally have sameness and difference. Why? Because six [qi] enter the body of earth. Since the qi have sameness and difference, there are things that generate, that transform, that do not generate, that do not transform, that generate and transform little, and that generate and transform broadly. So between heaven and earth there is nothing that must generate and must transform, must not generate and must not transform, must generate little and transform little, or must generate broadly and transform broadly; each follows what its qi likes, dislikes, differs from and agrees with.

## Notes

The differences in the generation and transformation of the five flavors are due to the restraint of earth's qi.
