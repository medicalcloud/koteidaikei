# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "For the qi of the northwest, disperse and cool it; for the qi of the southeast, gather and warm it. This is what is called treating the same illness differently.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "For the qi of the northwest ... treating the same illness differently"] People of the west and north have dense skin and interstices and all eat hot food; so dispersing and cooling are appropriate. People of the east and south have loose skin and open interstices and all eat cold food; so gathering and warming are appropriate. Dispersing means warm bathing to make inner and outer flow freely; gathering means warming the center without releasing the exterior. Now local customs all do the opposite; treating by them makes it worse.
Song editors: On examination, treatment by region is also given in the *Yifa fangyi lun*.

## Notes

The same illness is treated differently by region. Qibo's speech continues into unit 031.
