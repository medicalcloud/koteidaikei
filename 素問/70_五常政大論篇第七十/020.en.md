# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The period of blazing brilliance is called flourishing luxuriance. Yin qi transforms within, yang qi flourishes without, scorching heat spreads its transformation, and things prosper by it. Its transformation is growth, its qi loftiness, its government movement, its decree sounding and manifest, its movement blazing, scorching, wild and disturbed, its virtue warmth, summer heat and sultry steaming, its change fierce blaze and boiling, its grains wheat and beans, its domestic animals the sheep and pig, its fruits the apricot and chestnut, its colors red, white and black, its flavors bitter, acrid and salty, its image summer, its channels hand lesser yin and greater yang, hand *jueyin* and lesser yang, its depots the heart and lungs, its creatures the feathered and scaled, its things the veined and juicy, its illness laughing, malaria, sores and ulcers, bleeding, mania and red eyes. When *yu* is above, it is the same as proper *zhi*; its gathering is equal; its illness is tetany. When *zhi* is above, the gathering qi comes late. If its government is violent and fierce, the storing qi retaliates, congealing bleakness appears at times, and in severe cases there are rain, ice, frost, hail and biting cold; the pathogen harms the heart.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The period of blazing brilliance is called flourishing luxuriance"] When things meet the great yang, they flourish luxuriantly. This refers to the years *wuchen*, *wuyin*, *wuzi*, *wuxu*, *wushen* and *wuwu*.
Song editors: Some say 太陽 in the note should be 太徴 (great *zhi*). On examination, the notes on excessive wood, earth, metal and water do not mention the *jue*, *gong*, *shang* and *yu* movements, and the note on excessive water says "yin qi prevails greatly". For this excessive fire it is "things meeting the great yang"; how could it be called great *zhi*?

[On "Yin qi transforms within, yang qi flourishes without"] The qi of yin and yang obtain their order.

[On "scorching heat spreads its transformation, and things prosper by it"] Because the growing qi is plentiful.

[On "Its transformation is growth, its qi loftiness"] When the transformation of growth proceeds, things are large in form; when lofty qi reaches out, things are bright in color.

[On "its government movement"] It changes its image and is not constant.

[On "its decree sounding and manifest"] When fire is used there is sound; when fire burns there are flames. That its image has nowhere to hide is the proof. 顕 means exposed.

[On "its movement blazing, scorching, wild and disturbed"] 妄 means erroneous; 擾 means disturbing.

[On "its virtue warmth, summer heat and sultry steaming"] Produced by the transformation of heat, it grows things.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "its transformation is warm clamor and sultry heat", also written "warm brilliance".

[On "its change fierce blaze and boiling"] Prevalence and retaliation reach their extreme here.

[On "its grains wheat and beans"] Fire equals the transformation of water.

[On "its domestic animals the sheep and pig"] They equally breed.
Song editors: In the text above in this treatise, the horse is the animal of fire. That it now says sheep is suspected to be 馬 miswritten as 羊. Although the *Jingui zhenyan lun* and *Zangqi fashi lun* both write 羊, this treatise writes 馬, and its text should be followed.

[On "its fruits the apricot and chestnut"] They equally fruit.

[On "its colors red, white and black"] Red is added to white, and black is itself proper.

[On "its flavors bitter, acrid and salty"] Acrid things include bitterness, and the transformation of saltiness completes equally.

[On "its image summer"] Like the heat of summer qi.

[On "its channels hand lesser yin and greater yang"] Lesser yin is the heart vessel, greater yang the small intestine vessel.

[On "hand *jueyin* and lesser yang"] *Jueyin* is the pericardium vessel, lesser yang the triple burner vessel.

[On "its depots the heart and lungs"] The heart overcomes the lungs.

[On "its creatures the feathered and scaled"] Fire being in surplus, the scaled and feathered transform equally.

[On "its things the veined and juicy"] The veined are things of fire, the juicy things of water; water and fire are equal.
Song editors: On examination, 脈 is the same as 絡; the words differ but the sense is the same.

[On "its illness laughing, malaria ... red eyes"] Because fire is abundant.

[On "When *yu* is above, it is the same as proper *zhi*; its gathering is equal; its illness is tetany"] When greater yang appears above, heaven's qi restrains it, so excessive fire is on the contrary the same as the generation and transformation of balanced fire movement; it appears above in the *wuchen* and *wuxu* years. If it is the same as balanced fire movement, the qi of the five constants do not override one another; hence the generation and transformation of metal's gathering qi is equal.

[On "When *zhi* is above, the gathering qi comes late"] When lesser yin or lesser yang appears above, its generation and transformation govern on their own, and metal qi cannot transform equally with it. In *wuzi* and *wuwu* years lesser yin appears above; in *wuyin* and *wushen* years lesser yang appears above. Fire is abundant, so the gathering qi transforms late.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "when the year's fire is excessive and lesser yin or lesser yang presides above, fire burns, springs dry up and things are scorched and withered".

[On "If its government is violent and fierce ... the pathogen harms the heart"] It does not attend to its virtue and brings this on by slighting and insulting.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "rain, ice, frost and cold"; the two passages complement each other.
