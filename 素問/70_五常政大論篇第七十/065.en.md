# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §65 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Among those long ill, some have compliant qi yet are not well, or the illness has gone yet they are emaciated. What then?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "some have compliant qi yet are not well ... What then?"] 従 means compliant.

## Notes

A question on how to handle convalescence from long illness.
