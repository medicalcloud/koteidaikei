# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The period of issuing forth is called opening the old. The soil is loose and drains, green qi reaches out, yang harmony spreads and transforms, yin qi follows, the generating qi transforms richly, and the myriad things flourish. Its transformation is generation, its qi beauty, its government dispersal, its decree straight unfolding, its movement shaking, dizziness and illness of the vertex, its virtue sounding, bending, opening and splitting, its change shaking, snapping, smashing and uprooting, its grains hemp and rice, its domestic animals the chicken and dog, its fruits the plum and peach, its colors green, yellow and white, its flavors sour, sweet and acrid, its image spring, its channels foot *jueyin* and lesser yang, its depots the liver and spleen, its creatures the furred and shelled, its things hard in the center and hard outside, its illness anger. Great *jue* is the same as upper *shang*. When *zhi* is above, its qi counterflows, and the illness is vomiting and diarrhea. If it does not attend to its virtue, the gathering qi retaliates; autumn qi is sharp and cutting, in severe cases austere killing; clear qi comes greatly, plants wither and fall, and the pathogen harms the liver.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The period of issuing forth is called opening the old"] Things ride on wood qi to issue forth and open out their forms and substance. This refers to the transformation of the six years *renshen*, *renwu*, *renchen*, *renyin*, *renzi* and *renxu*. 𢽬 is an old form of 陳.

[On "The soil is loose and drains, green qi reaches out"] The generating qi issues upward, so the body of the soil is loose and drains; wood monopolizes government, so green qi reaches upward. 達 means passing through, emerging, moving.

[On "yang harmony spreads and transforms, yin qi follows"] Lesser yang first issues on the surface of the myriad things; *jueyin* follows next, circulating within all phenomena.

[On "the generating qi transforms richly, and the myriad things flourish"] The year's wood is in surplus and metal does not come to prevail, so the decree of generation spreads and transforms and things unfold and flourish.

[On "Its transformation is generation, its qi beauty"] When the transformation of wood spreads, the forms of things are fine and beautiful.

[On "its government dispersal"] It spreads generation and flourishing everywhere.

[On "its decree straight unfolding"] 条 means straight and orderly; 舒 means opening. Straight and unfolding, the myriad things follow it; nothing in the transformation of issuing forth fails to follow order.

[On "its movement shaking, dizziness and illness of the vertex"] 掉 means shaking; 眩 means spinning; 巔 means the top of the head; 疾 means the qi of illness.
Song editors: On examination, Wang does not explain the meaning of "its movement". Later, for "its movement is damp accumulation and gathering" in the period of thick mound, Wang's note says "movement means change"; and for "its movement is violent breaking and sores" in the period of firm completion, Wang's note says "illness arises through movement". It presumably means that once qi has changed, illness arises through movement; the meaning of "movement" is the same for wood, fire, earth, metal and water. Also Wang's note to the *Maiyao jingwei lun* says "巔疾 is illness of the top of the head", and his note to the *Qibing lun* says "巔 means the top, that is, the head". In this note, "巔 means the top of the head; 疾 means the qi of illness", the character 気 is superfluous.

[On "its virtue sounding, bending, opening and splitting"] Produced by wind qi.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "its transformation is sounding, stirring, opening and splitting".

[On "its change shaking, snapping, smashing and uprooting"] 振 means shaking in fury; 拉 means breaking in the middle; 摧 means toppling and falling; 抜 means coming out from the root.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* is the same.

[On "its grains hemp and rice"] The transformation of wood is equal to that of metal.

[On "its domestic animals the chicken and dog"] Chickens equally breed.

[On "its fruits the plum and peach"] Plums and equally peaches fruit.

[On "its colors green, yellow and white"] Green is added to yellow, and white is itself proper.

[On "its flavors sour, sweet and acrid"] Sour enters sweet, and acrid transforms equally.

[On "its image spring"] Like spring qi spreading yang harmony.

[On "its channels foot *jueyin* and lesser yang"] *Jueyin* is the liver vessel, lesser yang the gallbladder vessel.

[On "its depots the liver and spleen"] The liver overcomes the spleen.

[On "its creatures the furred and shelled"] Wood is in surplus, so the furred and equally the shelled are nurtured.

[On "its things hard in the center and hard outside"] Things hard in the center with a pit are equal to the kinds with skins and shells.

[On "its illness anger"] Because wood is in surplus.

[On "Great *jue* is the same as upper *shang*"] Excessive wood qi is equal to the transformation of metal.
Song editors: Among the five excessive movements, only great *jue* is said to be "the same as upper *shang*"; the other four movements say nothing of the kind, so this text is suspected to be superfluous.

[On "When *zhi* is above, its qi counterflows, and the illness is vomiting and diarrhea"] When lesser yin or lesser yang appears above, the qi moves counter. In *renzi* and *renwu* years lesser yin appears above; in *renyin* and *renshen* years lesser yang appears above. Wood in surplus meets fire, so the qi does not comply.
Song editors: The *Wuyunxing dalun* says "illness when the qi suit each other is because what is below presides over what is above, not in its proper position". Upper *yu* is not mentioned because water presiding over wood is suiting each other.

[On "If it does not attend to its virtue ... the pathogen harms the liver"] Relying on 已 [its own] excess, it overrides and offends earth; when earth qi is pressed to the limit, metal avenges it; metal carries out the decree of killing, so the pathogen harms liver wood.

## Notes

The record of excessive wood (issuing forth).
- 𢽬 in 啓𢽬 had been lost in the base text's input and was supplied from the Daozang edition (see the note in the variants). Wang Bing's note says "𢽬 is an old form of 陳"; the translation reads 啓陳.
- Wang Bing's 斉鶏孕也 and 李斉桃実也 are hard to construe; the translation stays close to the characters.
