# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the period of complete transformation, its qi accords with heaven's blessing, its virtue flows into the four governments, and the five transformations are equally ordered. Its qi is balanced, its nature compliant, its function high and low, its transformation abundance and fullness, its kind earth, its government calm stillness, its indication sultry steaming, its decree damp, its depot the spleen—the spleen fears wind—its governed organ the mouth, its grain foxtail millet, its fruit the jujube, its fruit-type the fleshy, its correspondence late summer, its creatures the naked, its domestic animal the ox, its color yellow, what it nourishes the flesh, its illness obstruction, its flavor sweet, its note *gong*, its things the skinned, its number five.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "its qi accords with heaven's blessing ... equally ordered"] The virtue of earth is still; it divides to assist the four directions and helps complete the governments of metal, wood, water and fire. The qi of earth is thick and corresponds to heaven's qi of blessing and harmony, generating, growing, gathering and storing, ending and beginning again; hence the five transformations are equally ordered.

[On "Its qi is balanced"] What earth generates is level and upright.

[On "its nature compliant"] It responds to and complies with all kinds, completing their transformation.

[On "its function high and low"] Fields high and low all serve their uses.

[On "its transformation abundance and fullness"] Nothing but the transformation of earth can make the myriad things abundant and full.

[On "its kind earth"] Among the transformations of the five phases, it is of the same kind as earth.

[On "its government calm stillness"] The body of earth is thick and its virtue still; hence its government and transformation are likewise.

[On "its indication sultry steaming"] 溽 means damp; 蒸 means heat.

[On "its decree damp"] When the transformation of damp does not run out, the decree of earth is prolonged.

[On "its depot the spleen"] The same as the spleen qi.

[On "the spleen fears wind"] Wind is the decree of wood. Although the nature of the spleen combines the four qi, what it governs still fears wood. The *Wuyunxing dalun* says "the spleen, its nature is stillness and inclusion" and "wind overcomes damp".

[On "its governed organ the mouth"] The body of 上 [earth] contains and holds; the mouth governs receiving.

[On "its grain foxtail millet"] Its color is yellow.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* writes "foxtail millet", the *Zangqi fashi lun* "nonglutinous rice".

[On "its fruit the jujube"] Its taste is sweet.

[On "its fruit-type the fleshy"] Those with flesh inside.

[On "its correspondence late summer"] Late (長) summer means the summer of growing and nurturing.
Song editors: Wang's note to the *Zangqi fashi lun* says "summer is the mother of earth; earth grows in the middle and governs by growth; hence it is called 長夏". His note to the *Liujie zangxiang lun* says "the so-called 長夏 is the sixth month; earth is born of fire and grows within summer; having grown it flourishes; hence 長夏".

[On "its creatures the naked"] Without fur, feathers, scales or shells, they are like the form of earth.

[On "its domestic animal the ox"] Completing the sowing and reaping is the function of earth; the ox serves in this, slow and gentle.

[On "its color yellow"] The same as earth.

[On "what it nourishes the flesh"] What it nourishes is thick and still.

[On "its illness obstruction"] The nature of earth is to block and hinder.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* says "the illness is at the root of the tongue; thus one knows that the illness is in the flesh".

[On "its flavor sweet"] When the qi of complete transformation is abundant, things are sweet and rich in flavor.

[On "its note *gong*"] Great and heavy.

[On "its things the skinned"] Things that receive the qi of complete transformation have much flesh.

[On "its number five"] The generation number, because upright earth adds nothing in vain.

## Notes

The record of balanced earth (complete transformation).
- 上 in Wang Bing's 上体包容 is presumably an error for 土.
