# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §49 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When lesser yang controls heaven, feathered creatures are still, furred creatures grow, and naked creatures do not mature; when it is in the spring, feathered creatures grow, shelled creatures diminish, and furred creatures do not grow.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When lesser yang controls heaven ... naked creatures do not mature"] This refers to the years *jiayin*, *bingyin*, *wuyin*, *gengyin*, *renyin*, *jiashen*, *bingshen*, *wushen*, *gengshen* and *renshen*. Naked creatures means green ones. Feathered creatures means black things with wings, such as the swallows of Yue and the shrike.

[On "when it is in the spring, feathered creatures grow, shelled creatures diminish, and furred creatures do not grow"] Earth's qi restrains metal, so white shelled [creatures] diminish; when the year rides on a fire movement, it is worse. Furred creatures do not grow because heaven's qi restrains them. These are the five *si* and five *hai* years.

## Notes

The flourishing and decline of creatures when lesser yang controls heaven or is in the spring.
- Wang Bing's "naked creatures means green ones" does not fit his explanations of naked creatures elsewhere and may contain an error; not checked.
