# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "[Deficient] wood is called subdued harmony, fire hidden brightness, earth humble supervision, metal compliant change, and water dried-up flow."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "wood is called subdued harmony"] The qi of yang harmony is bent and little used.

[On "fire hidden brightness"] The qi of brilliance is bent down and hidden and does not extend.

[On "earth humble supervision"] Though earth is humble and scant, it still supervises the generation and transformation of the myriad things.

[On "metal compliant change"] Complying and changing, it makes the myriad things firm and complete.

[On "water dried-up flow"] Water is scant, so its flow dries up.

## Notes

The names of the deficient five movements.
