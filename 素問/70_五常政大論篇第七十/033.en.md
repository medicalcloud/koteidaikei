# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It is the principle of high and low, caused by the terrain. Where it is lofty, yin qi governs; where it is low-lying, yang qi governs. Where yang prevails, things precede heaven; where yin prevails, they follow heaven. This is the norm of the pattern of the land and the way of generation and transformation."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Where yang prevails, things precede heaven; where yin prevails, they follow heaven"] Preceding heaven means preceding heaven's seasons; following heaven means following heaven's seasons. All speak of the earliness or lateness of growth, flourishing, withering and falling on the land. As with things, so with people.

## Notes

Even within one region, the timing of generation and transformation differs by elevation.
