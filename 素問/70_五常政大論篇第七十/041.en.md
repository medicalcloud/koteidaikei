# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When greater yang controls heaven, cold qi presides below and heart qi follows above; fire is also bright [and put to use]; red [things] rise [in price] and metal suffers calamity; cold and clarity arise at times; when it prevails, water freezes, fire qi is high and bright, the heart is hot and vexed, the throat dry with much thirst, there is nasal congestion and sneezing, frequent sorrow and frequent yawning; hot qi moves recklessly, cold retaliates, frost falls out of season, there is forgetfulness, and in severe cases heart pain. The earth is moist, water abounds and overflows, cold comes as a guest, deep overcast transforms, damp qi alters things, fluids accumulate within, there is fullness in the center with inability to eat, numbness of skin and flesh, the sinews and vessels do not move freely, and in severe cases puffy swelling and abscesses on the back of the body.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When greater yang controls heaven, cold qi presides below, heart qi follows above, and fire is also bright"] Song editors: On examination, the three characters 火且明 should be the two characters 火用 ("fire is used").

[On "red [things] rise [in price] and metal suffers calamity ... in severe cases heart pain"] The signs of *chen* and *xu* years. Cold and clarity arising at times is the decree of greater yang. Fire qi high and bright means burning things. Out of season means too early, also harming one-sidedly, not following the seasonal decree and not reaching all things. These illnesses arise from heaven's qi.

[On "The earth is moist ... abscesses on the back of the body"] Greater yin is in the spring and damp supervises the earth; hence this. The source and beginning of these illnesses arise from earth's qi.
Song editors: On examination, 身後癰 should be 身後難 ("difficulty with the back of the body").

## Notes

The weather and illnesses in a year when greater yang controls heaven and greater yin is in the spring.
- According to the Song editors, 火且明 should be 火用; the translation supplies [and put to use].
