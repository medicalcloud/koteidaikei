# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §59 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence it is said: to supplement above and below, follow them; to treat above and below, go against them; regulate according to the cold and heat, abundance and decline where they are.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Hence it is said: to supplement above and below ... where they are"] Above means the controller of heaven; below means the spring in earth. When the qi controlling heaven or earth is excessive, treat it by going against its flavor; when the qi controlling heaven or earth is deficient, harmonize it by following its flavor. 従 means following.

## Notes

The direction of supplementing and draining is decided by the abundance or decline of the qi of the controller of heaven and the spring in earth.
