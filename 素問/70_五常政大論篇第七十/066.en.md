# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §66 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "How illuminating, the sage's question! Transformation cannot be substituted for, and the seasons cannot be defied. When the channels and network vessels are open and blood and qi compliant, restore what is insufficient so that the person becomes like everyone else; nourish and harmonize, wait quietly for the right time, carefully guard the qi and do not let it tilt or shift; then the body becomes manifest and the living qi grows. This is called the sage king. Hence the *Great Essentials* says: 'Do not substitute for transformation, do not defy the seasons; always nourish and harmonize, and wait for recovery.' This is what is meant."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Transformation cannot be substituted for, and the seasons cannot be defied"] Transformation means creation. One who carves in place of the master carpenter still cuts his hand; how much less can a person substitute his strength for the qi of creation? Generation, growth, gathering and storing each correspond to the transformation of the four seasons; even the most skillful cannot bring them about ahead of time; clearly this is beyond human power. Seen thus, the generation, growth, gathering and storing of things must await their time, and the success, failure, order and disorder of things also await their time. As with things, so it should be with people. Those who say that force can surely achieve it and can substitute for creation and defy the four seasons are deluded.

## Notes

Recovery requires waiting on creation and the seasons, nourishing and harmonizing.
