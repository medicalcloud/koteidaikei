# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §62 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "How thorough the question! Where there is no accumulation, seek the depot; if depleted, supplement it; expel it with medicine, follow it with food, soak it in running water, harmonize the inner and outer, and it can be completely cured."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Where there is no accumulation, seek the depot; if depleted, supplement it"] According to where the illness is, name its depot and supplement it.

[On "expel it with medicine, follow it with food"] Give non-poisonous medicines as food, following decoctions and pills to drive it out completely.

[On "soak it in running water ... it can be completely cured"] When inner and outer communicate harmoniously and qi flows without obstruction, it dissolves away and the true qi becomes balanced of itself.

## Notes

The treatment of illness in the middle without accumulation.
