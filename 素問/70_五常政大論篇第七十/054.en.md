# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §54 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "What is rooted within is called spirit mechanism: when the spirit leaves, the mechanism stops. What is rooted without is called qi-established: when the qi stops, transformation ends. So each has restraint, each has overcoming, each has generation, each has completion. Hence the saying 'Without knowing what is added to the year and the sameness and difference of qi, one is not fit to speak of generation and transformation' means this."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What is rooted within ... when the qi stops, transformation ends"] Among all things with form, those rooted within have the source of life tied to heaven, and in all their movement and stillness spirit qi is the master that triggers the mechanism; hence what they do, no thing knows. So when the spirit departs, the way of triggering, moving and functioning stops. Those rooted without have the source of life tied to earth, and their generation, growth, transformation, completion, gathering and storing are all established by the qi of creation; hence where they come from, too, no thing knows. So when the qi stops, the way of generation, transformation and forming is extinguished. Wood, fire, earth, metal and water, dry, damp, liquid, hard and soft, though their constant natures do not change, when external things are removed, the living qi leaves its root and transformation ends, their constant body, nature and color all change somewhat from what they were.
Song editors: The 六元 [*Liu*]*wei zhi dalun* says: "When going out and coming in cease, the transformation of the spirit mechanism is extinguished; when rising and descending stop, the qi-established are isolated and endangered. So without going out and coming in, there can be no birth, growth, maturity, aging and ending; without rising and descending, there can be no generation, growth, transformation, gathering and storing."

[On "So each has restraint, each has overcoming, each has generation, each has completion"] Inner and outer rooting are all like this.

[On "Hence the saying ... means this"] Song editors: The *Liujie zangxiang lun* says: "If one does not know what is added to the year, the abundance and decline of qi, and where depletion and repletion arise, one cannot be a practitioner."

## Notes

Definitions of spirit mechanism (inner rooting) and qi-established (outer rooting).
- 六元微旨大論 in the Song editors' note is an error for 六微旨大論.
