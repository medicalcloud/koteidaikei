# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The period of overflowing stream is called sealing and storing. Cold controls the transformation of things, heaven and earth are severely frozen, the government of storing spreads, and the decree of growth is not raised. Its transformation is chill, its qi hardness, its government tranquility, its decree flowing and pouring, its movement drifting, draining, soaking and welling, its virtue congealing bleakness and cold mist, its change ice, snow, frost and hail, its grains beans and foxtail millet, its domestic animals the pig and ox, its fruits the chestnut and jujube, its colors black, red and yellow, its flavors salty, bitter and sweet, its image winter, its channels foot lesser yin and greater yang, its depots the kidneys and heart, its creatures the scaled and naked, its things the juicy and full, its illness distension. When *yu* is above, the growing qi does not transform. When its government exceeds, the transforming qi rises greatly, dust and gloom intersect, heavy rain falls at times, and the pathogen harms the kidneys.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The period of overflowing stream is called sealing and storing"] When yin qi prevails greatly, it is the transformation of heaven and earth sealing and storing. This refers to the years *bingyin*, *bingzi*, *bingxu*, *bingshen*, *bingwu* and *bingchen*.

[On "Cold controls the transformation of things, heaven and earth are severely frozen"] The qi of yin.

[On "the government of storing spreads, and the decree of growth is not raised"] When the storing qi is used, the transformation of growth stops; hence its decree is not raised.

[On "Its transformation is chill, its qi hardness"] When cold qi reaches things, they become hard and fixed.

[On "its government tranquility"] 謐 means still.

[On "its decree flowing and pouring"] The image of water.

[On "its movement drifting, draining, soaking and welling"] 沃 means froth; 涌 means overflowing.

[On "its virtue congealing bleakness and cold mist"] The transformation of cold.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* writes "its transformation is congealing bleakness and fierce cold".

[On "its change ice, snow, frost and hail"] Occurring out of season.

[On "its grains beans and foxtail millet"] Water equals the transformation of earth.

[On "its domestic animals the pig and ox"] They equally breed.

[On "its fruits the chestnut and jujube"] Water and earth fruit equally.

[On "its colors black, red and yellow"] Black is added to red, and yellow is itself proper.

[On "its flavors salty, bitter and sweet"] Salty enters bitter, and the transformation of sweet is equal.

[On "its image winter"] The order of the qi congealing and austere resembles the transformation of winter.

[On "its channels foot lesser yin and greater yang"] Lesser yin is the kidney vessel, greater yang the bladder vessel.

[On "its depots the kidneys and heart"] The kidneys overcome the heart.

[On "its creatures the scaled and naked"] Water is in surplus, so the scaled and naked are equally nurtured.

[On "its things the juicy and full"] Juicy is the transformation of water, full of earth.
Song editors: For deficient earth it is written "fleshy", for excessive earth "flesh", and here "full"; they complement each other.

[On "its illness distension"] Water in surplus.

[On "When *yu* is above, the growing qi does not transform"] When greater yang appears above, fire cannot spread its transformation for growth and nurturing. In the *bingchen* and *bingxu* years [greater yang] appears above; it is a heavenly-tally water movement.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "when greater yang presides above, rain, ice, snow and frost fall out of season and damp qi alters things". Upper *zhi* is not mentioned because [fire] is what the movement overcomes.

[On "When its government exceeds ... the pathogen harms the kidneys"] Violent cold arising often is called government exceeding. Fire is overridden by water, and earth comes to retaliate as an enemy; hence heaven and earth are dark and shaded, the qi of earth and water intersect, heavy rain falls, and the pathogen harms the kidneys.

## Notes

The record of excessive water (overflowing stream).
