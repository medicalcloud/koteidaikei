# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Heaven's qi restrains it, and the qi has something it follows."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Heaven's qi restrains it, and the qi has something it follows"] 従 means serving the other, having no time to attend to its own affairs, and being used in response to it.

## Notes

The qi of the controller of heaven restrains the qi of the depots.
