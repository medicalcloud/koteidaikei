# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "[Excessive] wood is called issuing forth, fire blazing brilliance, earth thick mound, metal firm completion, and water overflowing stream."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "wood is called issuing forth"] It spreads and issues the qi of generation, by which the myriad things flourish.

[On "fire blazing brilliance"] Abundant and bright.

[On "earth thick mound"] 敦 means thick; 阜 means high. Earth is in surplus, so it is high and thick.

[On "metal firm completion"] The air is crisp and the wind strong, making all things firm and complete.

[On "water overflowing stream"] 衍 means spreading and overflowing.

## Notes

The names of the excessive five movements.
