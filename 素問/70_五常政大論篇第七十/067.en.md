# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §67 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good"] The *Great Essentials* is the classic law of high antiquity. The essential teaching of antiquity is cited to make clear that the seasons and transformation cannot be defied nor substituted for by force.

## Notes

The close of the chapter. Wang Bing's note concerns the *Great Essentials* in the previous unit.
- 殆知閣 (重广补注黄帝内经素问) places a 巻二十一 break in this unit; this is that source's division into juan.
