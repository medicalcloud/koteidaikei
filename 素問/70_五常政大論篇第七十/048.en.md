# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §48 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When greater yin controls heaven, naked creatures are still, scaled creatures grow, and feathered creatures do not mature; when it is in the spring, naked creatures grow and scaled creatures do not mature.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When greater yin controls heaven ... feathered creatures do not mature"] This refers to the years *yichou*, *dingchou*, *jichou*, *xinchou*, *guichou*, *yiwei*, *dingwei*, *jiwei*, *xinwei* and *guiwei*. Naked creatures means humans and the kinds of toads. Feathered creatures means the green ones, such as parrots, woodpeckers and kingfishers, all green feathered things. When the year rides on a metal movement, it is worse.

[On "when it is in the spring, naked creatures grow, scaled creatures"] Song editors: On examination, a character 耗 ("diminish") is missing here.

[On "when it is in the spring, naked creatures grow and scaled creatures do not mature"] Earth's qi restrains water, so black scaled [creatures] do not grow; when the year rides on an earth movement, it is worse still. These are the five *chen* and five *xu* years.

## Notes

The flourishing and decline of creatures when greater yin controls heaven or is in the spring.
