# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus it generates without killing, grows without punishing, transforms without restraining, gathers without harming, stores without suppressing; this is called balanced qi.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Thus it generates without killing ... this is called balanced qi"] When the generating qi governs the year, the gathering qi cannot give free rein to its killing; when the growing qi governs the year, the storing qi cannot give free rein to its punishment; when the transforming qi governs the year, the generating qi cannot give free rein to its restraint; when the gathering qi governs the year, the growing qi cannot give free rein to its harm; when the storing qi governs the year, the transforming qi cannot give free rein to its suppression. In all such cases heaven's qi is balanced and earth's qi upright, and the qi of the five transformations do not use overcoming and conquest; hence it is called balanced, harmonious qi.

## Notes

A summary of the meaning of balanced qi. The discussion of balanced qi ends here; Qibo's speech continues with the periods of deficiency and excess (to unit 024).
