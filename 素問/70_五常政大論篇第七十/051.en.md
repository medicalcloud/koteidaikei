# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §51 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When greater yang controls heaven, scaled creatures are still and naked creatures grow; when it is in the spring, scaled creatures diminish and naked creatures do not grow. Whenever it rides on the movement of what does not mature, it is worse.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When greater yang controls heaven, scaled creatures are still and naked creatures grow"] This refers to the years *jiachen*, *bingchen*, *wuchen*, *gengchen*, *renchen*, *jiaxu*, *bingxu*, *wuxu*, *gengxu* and *renxu*. Naked creatures grow because they are the same as earth's qi. "Scaled creatures are still" means yellow scaled [creatures] are not used. In these years thunder rarely arises, because heaven's qi suppresses it.
Song editors: On examination, this should say "scaled creatures do not mature".

[On "when it is in the spring, scaled creatures diminish and naked creatures do not grow"] Heaven's qi restrains what prevails, so yellow and black scaled [creatures] diminish. These are the five *chou* and five *wei* years.
Song editors: On examination, this should be "scaled creatures grow, feathered creatures diminish, naked creatures do not grow". The character 鱗 in the note should also be 羽.

[On "Whenever it rides on the movement of what does not mature, it is worse"] Riding on a water [wood] movement, naked creatures do not mature; riding on a fire movement, shelled creatures do not mature; riding on an earth movement, scaled creatures do not mature; riding on a metal movement, furred creatures do not mature; riding on a water movement, feathered creatures do not mature. Those that fall in such a year are as in the text above: few can breed. These are all cases where the movement and the qi are the same. When the movement rides on its prevalence and retaliation and meets a heavenly tally or year-meeting, not one or two in ten conceptions are completed.

## Notes

The flourishing and decline of creatures when greater yang controls heaven or is in the spring, and aggravation by the movement.
- According to the variants, Kanripo includes part of the Song editors' note in the main text here.
- In Wang Bing's 乗水之運倮虫不成, 水 is taken as an error for 木, since Wang Bing's note as quoted in the medical section of the *Gujin tushu jicheng* has 乘木之運，則倮蟲不成.
