# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The period of dried-up flow is called turning to yang. The storing decree is not raised, the transforming qi prospers, the growing qi spreads, hibernating creatures do not hide, the earth is moist, springs diminish, plants grow luxuriant branches, and flourish and flower fully and abundantly. Its qi is stagnant, its function seeping and draining, its movement hardening and stopping, its outbreak drying and withering, its depot the kidneys, its fruits the jujube and apricot, its fruit-types the juicy and fleshy, its grains broomcorn millet and foxtail millet, its flavors sweet and salty, its colors yellow and black, its domestic animals the pig and ox, its creatures the scaled and naked, what it governs gathering dust and murky shade, its notes *yu* and *gong*, its illness flaccidity, reversal and hardness below; it follows the transformation of earth. Lesser *yu* is the same as lesser *gong*; upper *gong* is the same as proper *gong*. Its illness is urinary blockage and constipation; the pathogen harms the kidneys. When there are dust, gloom and sudden rain, there follow shaking, snapping, smashing and uprooting, with calamity in the first [direction, the north]. What it governs is the appearance of furred beasts, foxes and badgers, uncanny transformations, and nothing kept stored. Thus [the prevailing qi] takes advantage of danger to act, comes without being invited, is violent and without virtue, and disaster turns back upon it. Slight [prevalence] brings slight retaliation, severe brings severe; this is the constancy of qi.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The period of dried-up flow is called turning to yang"] Yin qi is deficient and on the contrary yang qi takes its place. This refers to the years *xinwei*, *xinsi*, *xinmao*, *xinyou*, *xinhai* and *xinchou*.

[On "The storing decree is not raised, the transforming qi prospers"] Water is scant and earth abundant.

[On "the growing qi spreads, hibernating creatures do not hide"] When greater yang is in the spring, this goes against the text of the classic; when *jueyin* or *yangming* controls heaven, it is as the classic says.

[On "the earth is moist ... fully and abundantly"] The qi of growth and transformation are rich and thick.

[On "Its qi is stagnant"] It follows earth.

[On "its function seeping and draining"] It cannot flow.

[On "its movement hardening and stopping"] This refers to bowel movements. When water is scant and does not moisten, they become dry, hard and stopped; when the storing qi cannot hold, they pour down and rush quickly.

[On "its outbreak drying and withering"] Because yin is scant and yang abundant.

[On "its depot the kidneys"] The governed depot falls ill.

[On "its fruits the jujube and apricot"] The jujube is the fruit of earth, the apricot of fire.

[On "its fruit-types the juicy and fleshy"] Juicy is the transformation of water, fleshy of earth.

[On "its grains broomcorn millet and foxtail millet"] Broomcorn millet is the grain of fire, foxtail millet of earth.
Song editors: In the text above in this treatise, wheat is the grain of fire. That it now says broomcorn millet is suspected to be 麦 miswritten as 黍. Although the *Jingui zhenyan lun* writes 黍, this treatise writes 麦, and the text of this chapter should be followed.

[On "its flavors sweet and salty"] Sweetness enters saltiness, and the taste is sweet and fine.

[On "its colors yellow and black"] Yellow is added to black.

[On "its domestic animals the pig and ox"] Water follows the animal of earth.

[On "its creatures the scaled and naked"] The scaled follow the naked.

[On "what it governs gathering dust and murky shade"] Earth prevails.

[On "its notes *yu* and *gong*"] *Yu* follows *gong*.

[On "its illness flaccidity, reversal and hardness below"] Water and earth are mixed together; hence this.

[On "it follows the transformation of earth"] Not prevailing over earth, it follows another's transformation.

[On "Lesser *yu* is the same as lesser *gong*"] Water and earth each transform by half.
Song editors: On examination, of the six years of the lesser *yu* movement, apart from *xinchou* and *xinwei*, the same as proper *gong*, the remaining four years *xinmao*, *xinyou*, *xinsi* and *xinhai* are the same as lesser *gong*. Hence it does not say "half *gong*".

[On "upper *gong* is the same as proper *gong*"] When greater yin appears above, it is the same as the generation and transformation of the balanced earth movement; it appears above in the *xinchou* and *xin*末 [*wei*] years.
Song editors: On examination, upper *jue* and upper *shang* are not mentioned here, presumably because water does not conquer or punish metal or wood.

[On "Its illness is urinary blockage and constipation"] 癃 means urine not passing; 閟 means dry, hard stool not passing freely.

[On "the pathogen harms the kidneys"] When a pathogen prevails, it comes back to the kidneys.

[On "When there are dust, gloom and sudden rain, there follow shaking, snapping, smashing and uprooting"] Dust, gloom and sudden rain are earth's cruelty; shaking, snapping, smashing and uprooting are wood's retaliation.

[On "with calamity in the first [direction]"] One is the north. Whenever "direction" is said, it means the direction of the borders of states, commanderies, provinces and counties.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "the disaster is in the first palace".

[On "What it governs is the appearance of furred beasts, foxes and badgers, uncanny transformations, and nothing kept stored"] The appearance of furred beasts means furred creatures—elk, deer, river deer, muntjacs, hog-badgers, hares, tigers and wolves—appearing and injuring yellow fruits and also harming the chief of the naked creatures. Uncanny transformations means becoming goblins, as foxes and raccoon dogs do. Nothing kept stored means harming the sacrificial grain, as rats, hog-badgers, hares, raccoon dogs and badgers do. This is what is meant by the appearance of furred beasts and nothing kept stored.

[On "Thus [the prevailing qi] takes advantage of danger to act ... this is the constancy of qi"] This speaks generally of prevalence and retaliation when the qi of the five phases are scant. Taking advantage of the other's isolation and danger, relying on one's own strength and abundance, going uninvited and giving free rein to punishment, one invites resentment and misfortune on oneself; whom else is there to blame? Suppose wood is weak and metal qi comes to ride over it, violent and abrupt; this is lacking virtue. When wood is harmed by metal, fire will surely avenge it; when metal is burned by fire, disaster reaches it. In such cases, severe punishment brings severe retaliation and slight punishment slight retaliation. This is the constancy of the movement of qi and is as it should be. The principle of the five phases is so in every case.
Song editors: The details of the deficiency of the five movements are given in full in the *Qijiaobian dalun*.

## Notes

The record of deficient water (dried-up flow), and the general rule of prevalence and retaliation for deficient movements.
- 辛末 in Wang Bing's note is an error for 辛未.
