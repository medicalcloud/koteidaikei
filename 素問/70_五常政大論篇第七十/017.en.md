# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The period of compliant change is called breaking of gathering. The gathering qi comes late, the generating qi rises, growth and transformation unite their virtue, the government of fire spreads, and all kinds flourish. Its qi is rising, its function agitated and urgent, its movement hacking cough, retention, oppression and reversal, its outbreak cough and panting, its depot the lungs, its fruits the plum and apricot, its fruit-types the shelled and veined, its grains hemp and wheat, its flavors bitter and acrid, its colors white and red, its domestic animals the chicken and sheep, its creatures the shelled and feathered, what it governs brightness, shining, blazing and scorching, its notes *shang* and *zhi*, its illness sneezing, cough, nasal congestion and nosebleed; it follows the transformation of fire. Lesser *shang* is the same as lesser *zhi*; upper *shang* is the same as proper *shang*; upper *jue* is the same as proper *jue*; the pathogen harms the lungs. When there are blazing light and fierce brilliance, there follow ice, snow, frost and hail, with calamity in the seventh [direction, the west]. What it governs is the scaled, the hidden, pigs and rats; the year's qi arrives early, producing great cold.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The period of compliant change is called breaking of gathering"] Fire breaks the gathering qi of metal. This refers to the years *yichou*, *yihai*, *yiyou*, *yiwei*, *yisi* and *yimao*.

[On "The gathering qi comes late, the generating qi rises"] 後 means not in time. When the gathering qi cannot act in its season, the generating qi naturally responds, spreads and rises, and is used.

[On "growth and transformation unite their virtue, the government of fire spreads, and all kinds flourish"] The qi of fire and earth share generation and transformation. 宣 means acting.

[On "Its qi is rising"] It follows fire.

[On "its function agitated and urgent"] Though scant and used late, when used it is urgent, agitated as it follows fire.

[On "its movement hacking cough, retention, oppression and reversal"] 鏗 means the sound of coughing; 禁 means retention of the two yin [orifices]; 瞀 means oppression; 厥 means qi counterflowing upward.

[On "its outbreak cough and panting"] Cough is metal having a sound; panting because the lungs store qi.

[On "its depot the lungs"] The governed depot falls ill.

[On "its fruits the plum and apricot"] The plum is the fruit of wood, the apricot of fire.

[On "its fruit-types the shelled and veined"] Fruits with a shell outside and branching veins inside.

[On "its grains hemp and wheat"] Hemp is the grain of wood, wheat of fire; wheat is red in color.

[On "its flavors bitter and acrid"] Bitter overcomes acrid; acrid includes bitter.

[On "its colors white and red"] Red is added to white.

[On "its domestic animals the chicken and sheep"] Metal follows the combined transformation of fire and earth.
Song editors: On examination, the animal of fire is the horse and that of earth the ox; since it now says sheep, Wang's note says "following the combined transformation of fire and earth makes the sheep". Some say the character 土 should be removed from the note, which is quite wrong.

[On "its creatures the shelled and feathered"] The shelled follow the feathered.

[On "what it governs brightness, shining, blazing and scorching"] Fire prevails.

[On "its notes *shang* and *zhi*"] *Shang* follows *zhi*.

[On "its illness sneezing, cough, nasal congestion and nosebleed"] Illnesses of metal.

[On "it follows the transformation of fire"] Fire qi comes to prevail, so it bends 已 [itself] to follow it.

[On "Lesser *shang* is the same as lesser *zhi*"] Metal being scant, it is half the same as the transformation of fire.
Song editors: On examination, of the six years of the lesser *shang* movement, apart from *yimao* and *yiyou*, the same as proper *shang*, and *yisi* and *yihai*, the same as proper *jue*, the remaining two years *yiwei* and *yichou* are lesser *shang* the same as lesser *zhi*. Hence it does not say "half *zhi*".

[On "upper *shang* is the same as proper *shang*"] When *yangming* appears above, it is the same as the generation and transformation of the balanced metal movement; it appears 止 [above] in the *yimao* and *yiyou* years.

[On "upper *jue* is the same as proper *jue*"] When *jueyin* appears above, it is the same as the generation and transformation of the balanced wood movement; it appears above in the *yisi* and *yihai* years.
Song editors: On examination, metal and earth do not conquer each other, so the classic does not say "upper *gong* is the same as proper *gong*".

[On "the pathogen harms the lungs"] When a pathogen prevails, it comes back to the lungs.

[On "When there are blazing light and fierce brilliance, there follow ice, snow, frost and hail"] Blazing light and fierce brilliance are fire lacking virtue; ice, snow, frost and hail are water's retaliation. In water's retaliation, hail is shaped like half a pearl.
Song editors: On examination, in the note's "hail is shaped like half a pearl", the character 半 is suspected to be an error.

[On "with calamity in the seventh [direction]"] Seven is the west.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "the disaster is in the seventh palace".

[On "What it governs is the scaled, the hidden, pigs and rats"] Creatures that dart, twist and lurk; the governor of the year lets them loose to injure red fruits and feathered creatures.

[On "the year's qi arrives early, producing great cold"] The transformation of water.

## Notes

The record of deficient metal (compliant change).
- 𭣣 in 折𭣣 is written 収 in other sources (see the variants); the translation reads 折収.
- 已 in Wang Bing's 屈已 is presumably an error for 己, and 止 in 其歳止見 for 上.
