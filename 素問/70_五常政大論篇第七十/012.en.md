# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the period of quiet compliance, it stores without harming, governs and readily goes low, and the five transformations are all orderly. Its qi is bright, its nature downward, its function soaking and overflowing, its transformation congealing and hardening, its kind water, its government flowing and spreading, its indication congealing austerity, its decree cold, its depot the kidneys—the kidneys fear damp—its governed organs the two yin [orifices], its grain beans, its fruit the chestnut, its fruit-type the juicy, its correspondence winter, its creatures the scaled, its domestic animal the pig, its color black, what it nourishes the bones and marrow, its illness reversal, its flavor salty, its note *yu*, its things the moist, its number six.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "it stores without harming, governs and readily goes low, and the five transformations are all orderly"] 治 means transformation. The nature of water is to go down, so its virtue is complete. Rivers and seas can be lords of the hundred valleys because they readily go low.

[On "Its qi is bright"] Clear, pure and luminous: what the qi of water governs.

[On "its nature downward"] It flows back downward.

[On "its function soaking and overflowing"] Its function is not a pure matter, so froth arises and it flows over. 沃 means froth; 衍 means overflowing.

[On "its transformation congealing and hardening"] When the storing qi spreads and transforms, watery things congeal and harden.

[On "its kind water"] The transformation of 浄 [quiet] compliance is of the same kind as water.

[On "its government flowing and spreading"] Wells and springs not running dry and rivers not ceasing to flow: this is the meaning of flowing and spreading.

[On "its indication congealing austerity"] 凝 means cold; 粛 means still; the indication of cold arriving.

[On "its decree cold"] When the decree of water spreads, cold controls the transformation of things.

[On "its depot the kidneys"] The function of the kidney depot is the same as the transformation of water.

[On "the kidneys fear damp"] Damp is the qi of earth. The nature of the kidneys is chill, so they fear the damp of earth. The *Wuyunxing dalun* says "the kidneys, their nature is chill".

[On "its governed organs the two yin [orifices]"] Their flowing corresponds.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* says "the black color of the north enters and communicates with the kidneys, which open their orifices at the two yin".

[On "its grain beans"] Its color is black.
Song editors: This agrees with the *Jingui zhenyan lun* and the *Zangqi fashi lun*.

[On "its fruit the chestnut"] Its taste is salty.

[On "its fruit-type the juicy"] They have fluid inside.

[On "its correspondence winter"] Among the transformations of the four seasons, it is the same as winter qi.

[On "its creatures the scaled"] Scaled creatures are born of the transformation of water.

[On "its domestic animal the pig"] It readily goes low. 彘 means pig.

[On "its color black"] The same color.

[On "what it nourishes the bones and marrow"] The qi enters there.

[On "its illness reversal"] 厥 means qi counterflowing to override the upper, moving backward and not complying.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* says "the illness is in the gorges [of the flesh]; thus one knows that the illness is in the bones".

[On "its flavor salty"] The same flavor.

[On "its note *yu*"] Deep and harmonious.

[On "its things the moist"] When the transformation of water is abundant and pervasive, all things are moist.

[On "its number six"] The completion number.

## Notes

The record of balanced water (quiet compliance).
- 浄順 in Wang Bing's note is read as 静順.
