# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The period of thick mound is called broad transformation. Its thick virtue is clear and still; following growth it fills; the utmost yin is solid within, the transformation of things is full and complete; smoke and dust gather darkly and appear on the thick earth [mountains]; heavy rain falls at times, damp qi is exercised, and the government of dryness is pushed aside. Its transformation is roundness, its qi abundance, its government stillness, its decree completeness everywhere, its movement damp accumulation and gathering, its virtue softness, moisture, heaviness and wetness, its change quaking, alarm, gusts, downpours, landslides and collapse, its grains foxtail millet and hemp, its domestic animals the ox and dog, its fruits the jujube and plum, its colors yellow, black and green, its flavors sweet, salty and sour, its image late summer, its channels foot greater yin and *yangming*, its depots the spleen and kidneys, its creatures the naked and furred, its things the fleshy and the pit, its illness abdominal fullness and inability to raise the four limbs. When a great wind comes swiftly, the pathogen harms the spleen.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The period of thick mound is called broad transformation"] Earth is in surplus, so the transforming qi covers things broadly. This refers to the years *jiazi*, *jiaxu*, *jiashen*, *jiawu*, *jiachen* and *jiayin*.

[On "Its thick virtue is clear and still; following growth it fills"] The nature of earth is to serve compliantly without contending with things; hence its virtue is thick and not agitated, and following fire's nurturing growth it fills the transforming qi of all things.

[On "the utmost yin is solid within, the transformation of things is full and complete"] The utmost yin is the essential qi of earth. The myriad things are transformed and completed because the numinous qi of the utmost yin generates and transforms within them.

[On "smoke and dust gather darkly and appear on the thick earth"] Thick earth means mountains; smoke and dust are earth qi.

[On "heavy rain falls at times ... pushed aside"] When damp qi is exercised, the government of dryness is pushed aside; this is a natural principle.

[On "Its transformation is roundness, its qi abundance"] The transforming qi is abundant and round because it is clear and still.

[On "its government stillness"] Being still it can last, so its government is always present.

[On "its decree completeness everywhere"] The qi is relaxed, so it is complete everywhere.

[On "its movement damp accumulation and gathering"] 動 means change.

[On "its virtue softness, moisture, heaviness and wetness"] Still, soft and moist, so its thick virtue is always present.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zheng*経 [*ji*] *dalun* says "its transformation is softness, moisture, heaviness and luster".

[On "its change quaking, alarm, gusts, downpours, landslides and collapse"] Quaking and alarm are the arising of thunder; gusts and downpours are violent wind and rain arriving. When heavy rain pours violently, mountains slide and soil collapses, flowing away with the water.

[On "its grains foxtail millet and hemp"] Earth and wood transform equally.

[On "its domestic animals the ox and dog"] They equally breed.

[On "its fruits the jujube and plum"] Earth equals the transformation of wood.

[On "its colors yellow, black and green"] Yellow is added to black, and green is itself proper.

[On "its flavors sweet, salty and sour"] Sweet enters salty, and sour transforms equally.

[On "its image late summer"] Its generation and transformation are the same as the qi of the sixth month.

[On "its channels foot greater yin and *yangming*"] Greater yin is the spleen vessel, *yangming* the stomach vessel.

[On "its depots the spleen and kidneys"] The spleen overcomes the kidneys.

[On "its creatures the naked and furred"] Earth is in surplus, so the furred and naked transform equally.

[On "its things the fleshy and the pit"] The fleshy is the transformation of earth, the pit of wood.

[On "its illness abdominal fullness and inability to raise the four limbs"] The nature of earth is stillness, so the illness is like this.
Song editors: On examination, upper *yu* and upper *zhi* are not mentioned here because *zhi* and *yu* cannot diminish or fill earth, so there are no other signs.

[On "When a great wind comes swiftly, the pathogen harms the spleen"] Wood rages abundantly, so the spleen of earth is harmed.
