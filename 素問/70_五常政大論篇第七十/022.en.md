# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The period of firm completion is called gathering and drawing in. Heaven's qi is pure, earth's qi bright, yang qi follows yin to govern transformation, dryness carries out its government, things are thereby completed, the gathering qi spreads abundantly, and the pervasion of transformation does not end. Its transformation is completion, its qi paring, its government austerity, its decree sharp and cutting, its movement sudden breaking, sores and infections, its virtue fog, dew and bleak wind, its change austere killing and withering, its grains rice and broomcorn millet, its domestic animals the chicken and horse, its fruits the peach and apricot, its colors white, green and red, its flavors acrid, sour and bitter, its image autumn, its channels hand greater yin and *yangming*, its depots the lungs and liver, its creatures the shelled and feathered, its things the shelled and veined, its illness panting and wheezing, fullness of the chest, and breathing with the head thrown back. When *zhi* is above, it is the same as proper *shang*; its generation is equal; its illness is cough. When its government changes violently, famous trees do not flourish, soft and brittle things are scorched at the top, the growing qi comes to rescue, great fire spreads, blazing and scorching arrive, creepers are about to wither, and the pathogen harms the lungs.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The period of firm completion is called gathering and drawing in"] 引 means contracting. Yang qi is gathered and yin qi used, so the myriad things contract. This refers to the years *gengwu*, *gengchen*, *gengyin*, *gengzi*, *gengxu* and *gengshen*.

[On "Heaven's qi is pure, earth's qi bright"] The high, pure qi of autumn is the same as metal qi.

[On "yang qi follows yin to govern transformation"] Yang complies with yin to generate and transform.

[On "dryness carries out its government, things are thereby completed"] Dry qi has transformation, and the myriad things are wholly overseen in their ripening without omission.

[On "the gathering qi spreads abundantly, and the pervasion of transformation does not end"] The gathering and killing qi comes early, so the transformation of earth cannot complete its function.
Song editors: On examination, the character 繁 is suspected to be an error.

[On "Its transformation is completion, its qi paring"] 削 means reducing.

[On "its government austerity"] 粛 means clear and still.

[On "its decree sharp and cutting"] The function of its qi does not yield; it is strong and urgent.

[On "its movement sudden breaking, sores and infections"] Illness arises through movement.

[On "its virtue fog, dew and bleak wind"] The transformation of dryness. 蕭飋 is the sound of wind. When still, it is fog and dew; when active, wind arises.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* writes 化 for 徳.

[On "its change austere killing and withering"] Falling to the 欲 [ground].

[On "its grains rice and broomcorn millet"] Metal and fire transform equally.
Song editors: In the text above in this treatise, wheat is the grain of fire; it should say "its grains rice and wheat".

[On "its domestic animals the chicken and horse"] They equally breed.

[On "its fruits the peach and apricot"] Metal and fire fruit equally.

[On "its colors white, green and red"] White is added to green, and red is itself proper.

[On "its flavors acrid, sour and bitter"] Acrid enters sour, and bitter transforms equally.

[On "its image autumn"] The air crisp, clear and pure, like the transformation of autumn.

[On "its channels hand greater yin and *yangming*"] Greater yin is the lung vessel, *yangming* the large intestine vessel.

[On "its depots the lungs and liver"] The lungs overcome the liver.

[On "its creatures the shelled and feathered"] Metal is in surplus, so the shelled and feathered are equally nurtured.

[On "its things the shelled and veined"] The shell is the transformation of metal, the veins of fire.

[On "its illness panting and wheezing ... breathing with the head thrown back"] Because metal qi is in surplus.

[On "When *zhi* is above, it is the same as proper *shang*; its generation is equal; its illness is cough"] When lesser yin or lesser yang appears above, heaven's qi is suppressed; hence its generation and transformation are the same as a balanced-metal year. In *gengzi* and *gengwu* years lesser yin appears above; in *gengyin* and *gengshen* years lesser yang appears above. The fire above restrains metal, so the generating qi transforms equally with it. Fire rides over metal and the lungs, so there is cough.
Song editors: On examination, upper *yu* is not mentioned here because water and metal do not overcome or conquer each other.

[On "When its government changes violently ... the pathogen harms the lungs"] 変 means too extreme. When the government is too extreme, the generating qi is suppressed; so trees do not flourish and grasses are scorched at the top and die. If the government is violent without stopping, fire qi breaks out in fury; so fire spreads, blazing and scorching arrive, and soft twigs, creeping plants and brittle things all dry up and die. Fire rides over metal qi, so the lungs are harmed.

## Notes

The record of excessive metal (firm completion).
- 陽気随陰治化 is read as one phrase, following Wang Bing's note.
- 欲 in Wang Bing's 隕墜於欲 may be an error for something like 地, but this has not been checked. 慿 in 胸慿 is written 憑 in other sources (see the variants).
