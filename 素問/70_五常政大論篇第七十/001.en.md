# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "The great void is vast and empty; the five movements revolve and press on one another; their decline and abundance differ, and loss and gain follow one another. I wish to hear how the balanced qi are named and how they are recorded."

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on the names and records of the balanced, deficient and excessive five movements. There is no note by Wang Bing.
