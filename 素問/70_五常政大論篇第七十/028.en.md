# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "People where the essence of yin is offered live long; people where the essence of yang descends die early."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "People where the essence of yin is offered live long ... die early"] Where the essence of yin is offered is high land; where the essence of yang descends is low land. In lands of the yin direction, yang does not leak recklessly, cold qi holds the exterior, pathogens seldom strike, and the upright qi guards firmly; hence life is prolonged. In lands of the yang direction, yang qi is consumed and dispersed, discharging without measure; wind and damp often strike and the true qi is depleted; hence early death. Verifying by fact, in the central plain today, people in the northwest live long and people in the southeast die early; and within each there are degrees. This is the great difference of longevity and early death. Let practitioners examine it.

## Notes

People on high land live long and those on low land die early.
