# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Where it is high, the qi is long-lived; where it is low, the qi is short-lived. It is the difference of the land's small and large scale: small scale, small differences; large scale, large differences. So one who treats illness must understand the way of heaven and the pattern of earth, the alternating prevalence of yin and yang, the precedence and lateness of qi, people's longevity and early death, and the periods of generation and transformation; only then can one know people's form and qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is the difference of the land's small and large scale ... large differences"] Large means east, west, south and north some ten thousand *li* apart; small means places of residence differing in height only twenty or thirty *li* or about a hundred *li* apart. Where the terrain's heights differ so widely that they cannot be compared, taking the near as small, it is ten or twenty *li*; where heights are level and gradual and the qi connects, taking the far as small, it is three hundred or two hundred *li*, and the land's qi differs.

[On "So one who treats illness must understand ... know people's form and qi"] If one does not understand the qi of heaven and earth and is ignorant of the signs of yin and yang, one takes long life for early death and early death for long life. Even if one exhausts the sages' way of saving life and masters the wonders of channels, medicines and stones, one cannot escape the slander of the world.

## Notes

Differences in longevity by the scale of terrain, and the physician's need to know the way of heaven and the pattern of earth.
