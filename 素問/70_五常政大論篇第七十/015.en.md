# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The period of hidden brightness is called overcoming of growth. The growing qi does not spread, the storing qi on the contrary spreads, the gathering qi governs on its own, the transforming decree is level, cold and clarity arise often, the decree of summer heat is weak; things born receiving transformation are born but do not grow, form fruit while still immature, and are already old when they meet transformation; yang qi is bent and hidden, and hibernating creatures hide early. Its qi is pent-up, its function violent, its movement manifest and hidden, changing and alternating, its outbreak pain, its depot the heart, its fruits the chestnut and peach, its fruit-types the veined and juicy, its grains beans and rice, its flavors bitter and salty, its colors black and red, its domestic animals the horse and pig, its creatures the feathered and scaled, what it governs ice, snow, frost and cold, its notes *zhi* and *yu*, its illness confusion, sorrow and forgetfulness; it follows the transformation of water. Lesser *zhi* is the same as lesser *yu*; upper *shang* is the same as proper *shang*; the pathogen harms the heart. When there are congealing bleakness and biting cold, there follow violent rain and incessant downpours, with calamity in the ninth [direction, the south]. What it governs is sudden downpours, thunder, quaking and alarm, deep overcast and soaking rain.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The period of hidden brightness is called overcoming of growth"] The storing qi overcomes growth. This refers to the years *guiyou*, *guiwei*, *guisi*, *guimao*, *guichou* and *guihai*.

[On "The growing qi does not spread, the storing qi on the contrary spreads"] The growing qi of fire cannot act and transform, so the storing qi of water on the contrary spreads in its season.

[On "the gathering qi governs on its own, the transforming decree is level"] The relation of metal and earth to the year qi involves no offense; so metal carries out its own government and earth levels its own qi.

[On "cold and clarity arise often, the decree of summer heat is weak"] Because fire qi is not exercised.

[On "things born receiving transformation are born but do not grow"] The decree of fire is not vigorous, so things born receiving transformation do not grow.

[On "form fruit while still immature, and are already old when they meet transformation"] The fruit of things ripens while the shoots are still young and short; when they meet the transforming qi, before growth reaches its peak, the qi is already old.

[On "yang qi is bent and hidden, and hibernating creatures hide early"] Yang is not exercised and yin prevails. In the *guimao* and *guiyou* years, presided over from above, hibernating creatures on the contrary do not hide.
Song editors: On examination, in the *guisi* and *guihai* years too, hibernating creatures do not hide.

[On "Its qi is pent-up"] Sultry and not freely flowing.

[On "its function violent"] Swift.

[On "its movement manifest and hidden, changing and alternating"] 彰 means manifest; 伏 means hidden; 変易 means its image appearing irregularly.

[On "its outbreak pain"] Pain arises from the heart.

[On "its depot the heart"] The qi of the year's movement communicates with the heart.

[On "its fruits the chestnut and peach"] The chestnut is the fruit of water, the peach of metal.

[On "its fruit-types the veined and juicy"] 絡 means branching veins; 濡 means having juice.

[On "its grains beans and rice"] Beans are the grain of water, rice of metal.

[On "its flavors bitter and salty"] Bitterness includes saltiness.

[On "its colors black and red"] Red things when ripe also have black.

[On "its domestic animals the horse and pig"] Fire follows the animal of water.

[On "its creatures the feathered and scaled"] The feathered follow the scaled.

[On "what it governs ice, snow, frost and cold"] The qi of water.

[On "its notes *zhi* and *yu*"] *Zhi* follows *yu*.

[On "its illness confusion, sorrow and forgetfulness"] The agitated movement of 大 [fire] does not keep to its regular measure. Yin covers yang fire, so there is confusion and disorder; heart qi is insufficient, so there are frequent sorrow and forgetfulness.

[On "it follows the transformation of water"] Fire is weak and water strong, so the period of hidden brightness half follows the government and transformation of water.

[On "Lesser *zhi* is the same as lesser *yu*"] Fire being scant, it is half the same as the transformation of water.
Song editors: On examination, of the six years of the lesser *zhi* movement, *guimao* and *guiyou* are the same as proper *shang*, and *guisi* and *guihai* the same as year-meetings; the remaining two years, *guiwei* and *guichou*, are lesser *zhi* the same as lesser *yu*. Hence it does not say "half *yu*".

[On "upper *shang* is the same as proper *shang*"] When *yangming* appears above the year, it is the same as the transformation of a balanced-metal year; in the *guimao* and *guiyou* years *yangming* appears above.
Song editors: On examination, upper *gong* and upper *jue* are not mentioned here, presumably because *gong* and *jue* inflict no great conquest or punishment on fire, so the classic does not mention them.

[On "the pathogen harms the heart"] What receives the illness is the heart.

[On "When there are congealing bleakness and biting cold, there follow violent rain and incessant downpours"] Congealing bleakness and biting cold are water lacking virtue; violent rain and incessant downpours are earth's retaliation.

[On "with calamity in the ninth [direction]"] Nine is the south.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "the disaster is in the ninth 官 [palace]".

[On "What it governs is sudden downpours, thunder, quaking and alarm"] Changes arising from the contention of the qi of heaven and earth; within the intersection of qi, the harm reaches the sacrificial grain and injures scaled creatures.

[On "deep overcast and soaking rain"] Deep overcast and soaking rain are produced by the change of damp. 𩃬 is pronounced *yin*.

## Notes

The record of deficient fire (hidden brightness).
- 霖𩆍 is written 霖霪 in other sources (see the variants). 沈𩃬 is read as 沈陰 following Wang Bing's note.
- 大 in Wang Bing's 大之躁動 is presumably an error for 火, and 官 in the Song editors' 九官 for 宮.
