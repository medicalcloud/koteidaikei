# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The period of subdued harmony is called overcoming of generation. The generating qi does not govern, the transforming qi rises, the growing qi is naturally balanced, the gathering decree comes early, cool rain falls at times, wind and clouds arise together, plants flourish late, dry and fall while green, things flower and fruit, and their skin and flesh are full within. Its qi is contracting, its function gathering, its movement shrinking, twisting, tense and slack, its outbreak fright and alarm, its depot the liver, its fruits the jujube and plum, its fruit-types the pit and the shelled, its grains foxtail millet and rice, its flavors sour and acrid, its colors white and green, its domestic animals the dog and chicken, its creatures the furred and shelled, what it governs fog, dew and chill, its notes *jue* and *shang*, its illness shaking and fear; it follows the transformation of metal. Lesser *jue* is the same as half *shang*; upper *jue* is the same as proper *jue*; upper *shang* is the same as proper *shang*. Its illnesses are crippled limbs, carbuncles, swellings, sores and ulcers; its sweet things have insects; the pathogen harms the liver. Upper *gong* is the same as proper *gong*. When there are bleak wind and austere killing, there follow blazing and boiling, with calamity in the third [direction, the east]; this is what is called retaliation. What it governs is flying insects, grubs, maggots and pheasants; then there is thunder.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The period of subdued harmony is called overcoming of generation"] The years *dingmao*, *dingchou*, *dinghai*, *dingyou*, *dingwei* and *dingsi*.

[On "The generating qi does not govern, the transforming qi rises"] Wood is scant, so the generating qi does not govern; earth is relaxed, so the transforming qi rises.

[On "the growing qi is naturally balanced, the gathering decree comes early"] Fire is not offended, so the growing qi is naturally balanced; wood qi being scant, the gathering decree comes early.

[On "cool rain falls at times, wind and clouds arise together"] Coolness is the transformation of metal; rain is damp qi; wind is the transformation of wood; clouds are damp qi.

[On "plants flourish late, dry and fall while green"] Because metal qi is in surplus and wood cannot overcome it.
Song editors: On examination, in the period of subdued harmony, wood is deficient and metal qi rides over it; so they dry and fall while green. It is not that metal qi is in surplus and wood cannot overcome it; rather wood is insufficient and metal overcomes it.

[On "things flower and fruit, and their skin and flesh are full within"] Though the year's growth is late, what ripens is full and solid, because the transforming qi of earth is quick.

[On "Its qi is contracting"] Because gathering and contracting include metal qi.

[On "its function gathering"] It does not spread out.

[On "its movement shrinking, twisting, tense and slack"] 緛 means shrinking short; 戻 means twisting; 拘 means tense; 緩 means unable to contract.

[On "its outbreak fright and alarm"] Great bending and sudden stretching is the image of fright.

[On "its depot the liver"] It corresponds inwardly to the liver.

[On "its fruits the jujube and plum"] The jujube is the fruit of earth, the plum of wood.
Song editors: On examination, the plum is the fruit of wood. According to the fruits of deficient fire, earth, metal and water, 李 should be 桃 (peach), and Wang's note is also wrong.

[On "its fruit-types the pit and the shelled"] The pit is governed by wood, the shell by metal.

[On "its grains foxtail millet and rice"] The grains of metal and earth.

[On "its flavors sour and acrid"] Sour things, when 孰 [ripe], also have acridity.

[On "its colors white and green"] Green things, when 孰 [ripe], also have white.

[On "its domestic animals the dog and chicken"] Wood follows the animal of metal.

[On "its creatures the furred and shelled"] The furred follow the shelled.

[On "what it governs fog, dew and chill"] The transformation of metal.

[On "its notes *jue* and *shang*"] *Jue* follows *shang*.

[On "its illness shaking and fear"] Wood receives the pathogen.

[On "it follows the transformation of metal"] Wood does not attack on its own, so its transformation follows metal.

[On "Lesser *jue* is the same as half *shang*"] Lesser *jue* is deficient wood, so it is half the same as the transformation of *shang* (metal). 判 means half.
Song editors: In the texts for fire, earth, metal and water, 判 is written 少; so this should say "lesser *jue* is the same as lesser *shang*". It does not say lesser *shang* presumably because the lesser *jue* movement has six years: *dingsi* and *dinghai* are upper *jue*, the same as proper *jue*; *dingmao* and *dingyou* are upper *shang*, the same as proper *shang*; *dingwei* and *dingchou* are upper *gong*, the same as proper *gong*. These six years each have something they are the same as, unlike the lesser movements of fire, earth, metal and water. Hence it does not say "the same as lesser *shang*", but speaks only roughly of following half the transformation of *shang*.

[On "upper *jue* is the same as proper *jue*"] When *jueyin* appears above, it is the same as the transformation of the year of spreading harmony; this refers to what appears above in the *dinghai* and *dingsi* years.

[On "upper *shang* is the same as proper *shang*"] When *yangming* appears above, it is the same as the transformation of a balanced-metal year; in the *dingmao* and *dingyou* years *yangming* appears above.

[On "Its illnesses are crippled limbs, carbuncles, swellings, sores and ulcers"] Metal punishes wood.

[On "its sweet things have insects"] The child is within the mother.

[On "the pathogen harms the liver"] Though its transformations are all the same as metal, what it harms comes back to liver wood.

[On "Upper *gong* is the same as proper *gong*"] When earth covers its wood, it is the same as not yet having emerged; wood not yet emerged from earth is the same as having no wood, and earth acts on its own; hence it is the same as the transformation of a proper earth-movement year. Greater yin appearing above is called upper *gong*; in the *dingchou* and *dingwei* years greater yin appears above, transformed by the controller of heaven.

[On "When there are bleak wind and austere killing, there follow blazing and boiling"] Bleak wind and austere killing are metal lacking virtue; blazing and boiling are fire's retaliation.

[On "with calamity in the third [direction]"] Fire retaliates on behalf of wood, so its calamity is in the east; three is the east. This speaks of the things of metal prevailing.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "the disaster is in the third palace".

[On "this is what is called retaliation"] 復 means repayment.

[On "What it governs is flying insects, grubs, maggots and pheasants"] Flying means feathered creatures; 蠧 means insects born inside things; 蛆 means what flies breed—these transform by themselves inside things; 雉 means birds diminishing.

[On "then there is thunder"] 雷 means a great sound arising in the dark clouds of the great void; 霆 means swift thunder bursting suddenly like fire exploding, that is, a thunderclap.

## Notes

The record of deficient wood (subdued harmony).
- 孰 in Wang Bing's 孰兼 is read as 熟 "ripe". 雉鳥耗也 is hard to construe.
