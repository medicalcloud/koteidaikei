# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §60 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence the saying 'Take from above and below, take from within and without, to seek the fault; for those who can bear poisons use strong medicines, for those who cannot, mild medicines' means this. When the qi is contrary: if the illness is above, take it from below; if below, take it from above; if in the middle, take it from the side. Treat heat with cold, administered warm; treat cold with heat, administered cool; treat warmth with clarity, administered cold; treat coolness with warmth, administered hot. So in dissolving and paring, inducing vomiting and purging, supplementing and draining, long-standing and new illnesses follow the same method."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Hence the saying 'Take from above and below ... mild medicines' means this"] Taking from above means restraining the faulty qi with medicine; if restrained but not compliant, induce vomiting. Taking from below means removing illness below with swift medicine; if attacked but not gone, purge. Taking from within means taking food or medicine internally, examining its cold and heat to regulate it. Taking from without means making the ill qi regulated and suited by medicinal hot compresses. When it should be cold but is on the contrary hot, regulate with cold; when it should be hot but is on the contrary cold, harmonize with warmth. If abundance above does not stop, induce vomiting to remove it; if abundance below does not stop, purge to take it away. This is called the way of finding the fault of the qi. Strong and mild medicines refer to the strength and mildness of their qi and flavor.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says: "Those with thick stomachs, dark complexion, big bones and fat flesh can all bear poisons; the thin with thin stomachs cannot." Also the *Yifa fangyi lun* says: "The people of the west live on hills with much wind; water and soil are hard; they wear coarse cloth instead of fine clothes, eat rich food and are fat; so pathogens cannot harm their bodies, and their illnesses arise within; poisonous medicines suit their treatment."

[On "When the qi is contrary: if the illness is above, take it from below ... take it from the side"] Taking from below means that when cold counterflows below and heat attacks above, the lower part is not free and qi fills the upper part, then warm the lower part to regulate it. Taking from above means that when cold has accumulated below and warming does not remove it, and the yang depots are insufficient, supplement their yang. Taking from the side means that if qi gathers on the left, apply medicinal compresses on the right, and if it gathers on the right, on the left, to harmonize it. One must suit it to cold and heat. In all these seven, the illness has nowhere to escape, and any action must hit the mark; this is wondrous application.

[On "Treat heat with cold, administered warm ... treat coolness with warmth, administered hot"] The nature of qi has hardness and softness, the signs have lightness and heaviness, prescriptions have large and small, and regulation has cold and warm. When abundant and great, take it by following the nature of the qi; when small and soft, attack it by going against the nature of the qi. When the qi differ, the governing one will not tolerate it; when the force is double, attack it and surely win. This is called the rule of regulating qi with decoctions.
Song editors: The *Zhizhen yao dalun* says: "Use heat through cold and cold through heat; one must substitute for what governs it and put first what it relies on; the beginning is the same and the end different; it can break accumulations, dissolve hardness, harmonize qi, and surely bring the illness to an end."

[On "So in dissolving and paring ... follow the same method"] Measure the abundance or depletion of qi and apply the method; there is no different way for new or long-standing illnesses.

## Notes

The principles of treatment: taking from above, below, within and without, and treating heat with cold administered warm, and so on. Qibo's speech ends here.
