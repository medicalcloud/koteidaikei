# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When *yangming* controls heaven, dry qi presides below and liver qi follows above; green [things] rise [in price] and wood is put to use and stands; earth suffers calamity; chill and cold arrive often; trees are felled and grasses wither; there are flank pain, red eyes, shaking, trembling and shivering, and flaccid sinews so that one cannot stand long. Sudden heat arrives, the earth is hot, yang qi is pent up and bursts out, urine changes, cold and heat are like malaria, and in severe cases there is heart pain; fire moves over dry things, flowing water does not freeze, and hibernating creatures appear.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When *yangming* controls heaven ... cannot stand long"] The signs of *mao* and *you* years. 木用 also means woodwork. 淒滄 means great coolness. These illnesses arise from heaven's qi.

[On "Sudden heat arrives ... hibernating creatures appear"] Lesser yin is in the spring and heat supervises the earth; hence this. These illnesses arise from earth's qi.

## Notes

The weather and illnesses in a year when *yangming* controls heaven and lesser yin is in the spring.
