# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the period of rising brightness, it governs by upright yang, its virtue is bestowed everywhere, and the five transformations are evenly balanced. Its qi is high, its nature swift, its function burning and scorching, its transformation flourishing luxuriance, its kind fire, its government bright shining, its indication scorching heat, its decree heat, its depot the heart—the heart fears cold—its governed organ the tongue, its grain wheat, its fruit the apricot, its fruit-type the veined, its correspondence summer, its creatures the feathered, its domestic animal the horse, its color red, what it nourishes the blood, its illness twitching and spasms, its flavor bitter, its note *zhi*, its things the veined, its number seven.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "it governs by upright yang ... evenly balanced"] 均 means equal; 衡 means balanced.

[On "Its qi is high"] Fire blazes upward.

[On "its nature swift"] The nature of fire is agitated and swift.

[On "its function burning and scorching"] 灼 means burning; both 燔 and 灼 are functions of fire.

[On "its transformation flourishing luxuriance"] The growing qi is abundant, so things 火 [flourish].

[On "its kind fire"] Among the qi of the five phases, it is of the same kind as fire.

[On "its government bright shining"] Virtue matching loftiness and brightness is the government of fire.

[On "its indication scorching heat"] The arrival of the qi is observed by this.

[On "its decree heat"] When heat arrives, its decree proceeds.

[On "its depot the heart"] The heart qi corresponds to it.

[On "the heart fears cold"] Cold is the decree of water. The nature of the heart is summer heat, so it fears cold. The *Wuyunxing dalun* says "the heart, its nature is summer heat" and "cold overcomes heat".

[On "its governed organ the tongue"] Fire illuminates what is dark; the tongue expresses and clarifies.

[On "its grain wheat"] Its color is red.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* says "its grain is broomcorn millet", while the *Zangqi fashi lun* says "wheat".

[On "its fruit the apricot"] Its taste is bitter.

[On "its fruit-type the veined"] Those with branching veins inside.

[On "its correspondence summer"] Among the qi of the four seasons, it is the same as summer qi.

[On "its creatures the feathered"] Feathers are the image of fire; when the transformation of fire spreads, feathered creatures are born.

[On "its domestic animal the horse"] Robust, decisive, restless and swift, of the same kind as fire.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* says "its domestic animal is the sheep".

[On "its color red"] The same color, and also bright.

[On "its illness twitching and spasms"] The nature of fire is movement.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* says "thus one knows that the illness is in the vessels".

[On "its flavor bitter"] When qi transforms brightly outside, the bitter flavor of things is pure.

[On "its note *zhi*"] Harmonious and beautiful.

[On "its things the veined"] Many branching veins inside is the transformation of fire.

[On "its number seven"] The completion number.

## Notes

The record of balanced fire (rising brightness).
- 火 in Wang Bing's 故物火 may be an error for something like 茂 from the context, but this has not been checked.
