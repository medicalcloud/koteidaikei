# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The period of humble supervision is called reduced transformation. The transforming qi does not issue decrees, the government of generation alone is manifest, the growing qi is orderly, rain misses its time, the gathering qi is balanced, wind and cold arise together, plants flourish beautifully but flower without fruit, and what forms is blighted. Its qi is dispersing, its function still and settled, its movement ulcers, vomiting, splitting and rotting, carbuncles and swellings, its outbreak dampness and stagnation, its depot the spleen, its fruits the plum and chestnut, its fruit-types the juicy and the pit, [its] grains beans and hemp, its flavors sour and sweet, its colors green and yellow, its domestic animals the ox and dog, its creatures the naked and furred, what it governs gusting fury, shaking and issuing, its notes *gong* and *jue*, its illness retention, fullness, obstruction and blockage; it follows the transformation of wood. Lesser *gong* is the same as lesser *jue*; upper *gong* is the same as proper *gong*; upper *jue* is the same as proper *jue*. Its illness is diarrhea of undigested food; the pathogen harms the spleen. When there are shaking, snapping, gusting and sweeping, there follow drying while green and scattering down, with calamity at the four corners. What it governs is destruction and breaking, tigers and wolves; the clear qi is exercised and the government of generation is humiliated.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The period of humble supervision is called reduced transformation"] It means the transforming qi is reduced and scant. The years *jisi*, *jimao*, *jichou*, *jihai*, *jiyou* and *jiwei*.

[On "The transforming qi does not issue decrees, the government of generation alone is manifest"] Earth is scant and wood monopolizes its function.

[On "the growing qi is orderly, rain misses its time, the gathering qi is balanced"] Not offending each other, they are balanced and orderly. The transforming qi is reduced, so rain misses its time.

[On "wind and cold arise together, plants flourish beautifully"] Wind is wood; cold is water. Earth being scant, cold qi can move. The generating qi alone is manifest, so plants spread, flourish and are fine and beautiful.

[On "but flower without fruit, and what forms is blighted"] Flourishing, flowering and beauty come because the qi arises from wood; the transforming qi is not full, so the fruit of things is empty inside and hence blighted.

[On "Its qi is dispersing"] The qi is not calm; water also rides over it and follows the wind of wood; hence it disperses.

[On "its function still and settled"] Though it cannot monopolize government over the things of the season, when it is used, it ultimately returns to the virtue of earth and is still and settled.

[On "its movement ulcers, vomiting, splitting and rotting, carbuncles and swellings"] 瘍 means sores; 涌 means vomiting; 分 means splitting; 潰 means rotting; 癰腫 means pus sores.

[On "its outbreak dampness and stagnation"] The nature of earth; 濡 means damp.

[On "its depot the spleen"] The governed depot falls ill.

[On "its fruits the plum and chestnut"] The plum is the fruit of wood, the chestnut of water.

[On "its fruit-types the juicy and the pit"] Juicy means having juice inside; pit means hard inside.
Song editors: On examination, before and after, juicy fruit belongs to water; here 濡 should be 肉 (fleshy), and Wang's note is also wrong.

[On "[its] grains beans and hemp"] Beans are the grain of water, hemp of wood.

[On "its flavors sour and sweet"] Sweet things when ripe also have sourness.

[On "its colors green and yellow"] Yellow things also have green outside.

[On "its domestic animals the ox and dog"] Earth follows the animal of wood.

[On "its creatures the naked and furred"] The naked follow the furred.

[On "what it governs gusting fury, shaking and issuing"] The function of wood qi.

[On "its notes *gong* and *jue*"] *Gong* follows *jue*.

[On "its illness retention, fullness, obstruction and blockage"] Because earth qi blocks and hinders.

[On "it follows the transformation of wood"] Not prevailing, it follows another's transformation.

[On "Lesser *gong* is the same as lesser *jue*"] Earth being scant, it half follows the transformation of wood.
Song editors: On examination, of the six years of the lesser *gong* movement, apart from *jichou* and *jiwei*, the same as proper *gong*, and *jisi* and *jihai*, the same as proper *jue*, there remain two years, *jimao* and *jiyou*, lesser *gong* the same as lesser *jue*. Hence it does not say "half *jue*".

[On "upper *gong* is the same as proper *gong*"] When greater yin appears above, it is the same as the generation and transformation of the balanced earth movement; it appears in the *jichou* and *jiwei* years.

[On "upper *jue* is the same as proper *jue*"] When *jueyin* appears above, it is entirely the period of spreading harmony; it appears in the *jihai* and *jisi* years.

[On "Its illness is diarrhea of undigested food"] Wind prevails.

[On "the pathogen harms the spleen"] Giving free rein to the various qi, when metal falls ill, it harms the spleen itself.
Song editors: On examination, upper *shang* is not mentioned here because earth and metal do not conquer or punish each other, so the classic does not record it. Also, in the note's "giving free rein to the various qi, when metal falls ill, it harms the spleen itself", the character 金 is suspected to be an error.

[On "When there are shaking, snapping, gusting and sweeping, there follow drying while green and scattering down"] Shaking, snapping, gusting and sweeping are wood lacking virtue; drying while green and scattering down are metal's retaliation.

[On "with calamity at the four corners"] Southeast, southwest, northeast and northwest are the positions of earth.
Song editors: The *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "the disaster is in the fifth palace".

[On "What it governs is destruction and breaking, tigers and wolves"] Tigers, wolves, monkeys, jackals, leopards, deer, horses, river deer, muntjacs and other four-footed beasts harm the sacrificial grain and lives.

[On "the clear qi is exercised and the government of generation is humiliated"] When metal qi moves, wood qi yields.
