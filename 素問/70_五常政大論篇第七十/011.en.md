# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the period of discerning balance, it gathers without contending and kills without trespass, and the five transformations are proclaimed and clear. Its qi is pure, its nature hard, its function scattering and falling, its transformation firm contraction, its kind metal, its government sharp austerity, its indication clear and urgent, its decree dryness, its depot the lungs—the lungs fear heat—its governed organ the nose, its grain rice, its fruit the peach, its fruit-type the shelled, its correspondence autumn, its creatures the shelled, its domestic animal the chicken, its color white, what it nourishes the skin and hair, its illness cough, its flavor acrid, its note *shang*, its things hard outside, its number nine.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "it gathers without contending ... proclaimed and clear"] 犯 means punishing and violating things. To gather without contending and kill without trespass—how could this be done except by the virtue of discerning balance?

[On "Its qi is pure"] The qi of metal is devoted to purity, whiteness and clarity.

[On "its nature hard"] Its nature is hard, so it smashes and breaks things.

[On "its function scattering and falling"] When metal acts, the myriad things scatter and fall.

[On "its transformation firm contraction"] Gathering in, contracting, firm and strong: the transformation of metal.

[On "its kind metal"] The transformation of discerning balance is of the same kind as metal.

[On "its government sharp austerity"] Its transformation is swift, orderly and austere. 勁 means sharp.

[On "its indication clear and urgent"] 清 means great coolness; 切 means urgent, the sound of wind.

[On "its decree dryness"] 燥 means dry.

[On "its depot the lungs"] The function of lung qi is the same as the transformation of metal.

[On "the lungs fear heat"] Heat is the decree of fire. The nature of the lungs is cool, so they fear fiery heat. The *Wuyunxing dalun* says "the lungs, their nature is coolness".

[On "its governed organ the nose"] The lungs store qi and the nose conducts breath.

[On "its grain rice"] Its color is white.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* writes "rice", the *Zangqi fashi lun* "yellow broomcorn millet".

[On "its fruit the peach"] Its taste is acrid.

[On "its fruit-type the shelled"] Those with a hard shell outside.

[On "its correspondence autumn"] Among the transformations of the four seasons, it is the same as autumn qi.

[On "its creatures the shelled"] Those covered outside with hard armor.

[On "its domestic animal the chicken"] Its nature, fond of fighting and wounding, images the function of metal.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* says "its domestic animal is the horse".

[On "its color white"] The same color.

[On "what it nourishes the skin and hair"] The same firmness.

[On "its illness cough"] An illness with sound is the response of metal.
Song editors: The *Jingui zhenyan lun* says "the illness is in the back; thus one knows that the illness is in the skin and hair".

[On "its flavor acrid"] When the transformation of discerning balance is in order, the acrid flavor of things is proper.

[On "its note *shang*"] Harmonious, keen and rising.

[On "its things hard outside"] When the transformation of metal spreads, the bodies of things are hard outside.

[On "its number nine"] The completion number.

## Notes

The record of balanced metal (discerning balance).
