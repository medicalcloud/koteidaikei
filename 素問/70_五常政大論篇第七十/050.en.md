# 素問 五常政大論篇第七十 §50 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When *yangming* controls heaven, shelled creatures are still, feathered creatures grow, and shelled creatures do not mature; when it is in the spring, shelled creatures grow, furred creatures diminish, and feathered creatures do not mature.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When *yangming* controls heaven ... shelled creatures do not mature"] This refers to the years *yimao*, *dingmao*, 巳 [*ji*]*mao*, *xinmao*, *guimao*, *yiyou*, *dingyou*, 巳 [*ji*]*you*, *xinyou* and *guiyou*. The feathered are creatures of fire, so they flourish. The shelled creatures [that do not mature] are those with red shells; red shelled [creatures] do not grow because heaven's qi restrains them.

[On "when it is in the spring, shelled creatures grow, furred creatures diminish, and feathered creatures do not mature"] Earth's qi restrains wood, so black furred creatures diminish; when the year rides on a metal movement, the damage is worse. These are the five *zi* and five *wu* years. Feathered creatures do not mature because lesser yin appears above.

## Notes

The flourishing and decline of creatures when *yangming* controls heaven or is in the spring.
- Shelled creatures appear twice in the first clause; Wang Bing takes the second as red shelled creatures.
- 黒 in Wang Bing's 黒毛虫 may be an error in view of the color correspondences elsewhere; not checked.
