# 素問 気厥論篇第三十七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "What happens when the five zang and six fu transfer cold and heat to one another?"

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: illnesses arising when cold or heat is transferred between the zang and fu. There is no note by Wang Bing.
