# 素問 四気調神大論篇第二 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore the sages do not treat what is already ill but treat what is not yet ill; they do not put in order what is already in disorder but put in order what is not yet in disorder. This is what is meant.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] This is the utmost of knowledge.

## Notes

The famous phrase "treating what is not yet ill": the way of the sage is not to treat illness once it has arisen but to prevent it beforehand by nourishing life.
- In the base, 2 instances of 巳 meaning 已 "already/stop" are emended to 已 (graphic confusion; the base often does not distinguish 巳, 已 and 己, while Wikisource's plain text and other sources mostly have the correct character).
