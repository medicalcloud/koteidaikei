# 素問 四気調神大論篇第二 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

To give medicine only after an illness has already formed, or to restore order only after disorder has already formed, is like digging a well when thirsty or casting weapons when the fighting has begun. Is it not too late?

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "like digging a well when thirsty or casting weapons when the fighting has begun. Is it not too late?"] This is knowledge that does not come in time. Guarding against depleting evils is like seizing a tiger: if one gives medicine only after being bitten, regret is of no use.

## Notes

Following the previous unit, it compares dealing with illness or disorder only after it has arisen to digging a well when already thirsty. The chapter ends here.
- 錐 *zhui* is generally taken to mean a weapon (a spearhead or the like).
- The three Daizhige sources write 斗 for 鬬 (from the simplified-character conversion).
