# 素問 気府論篇第五十九 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The points where the vessel qi of the hand *yangming* issues are twenty-two: two each at the outer edge of the nostril and on the nape; one each in the bone hollow of Daying; one each at the meeting of the pillar bone; one each at the meeting of the shoulder bone; from the elbow down to the base of the index finger, six transport points each.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "twenty-two: two each at the outer edge of the nostril and on the nape"] This means Yingxiang and Futu, two points each. Yingxiang is beside the lower opening of the nose, the meeting of the hand and foot *yangming*; one may needle three body-*fen*. Futu is one body-*cun* below the angle of the jaw, behind Renying, where the vessel qi of the hand *yangming* issues; take it face up; one may needle four body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "one each in the bone hollow of Daying"] Daying is the name of a point, at the artery in the bone depression one body-*cun* three *fen* in front of the angle of the jaw, where the vessel qi of the foot *yangming* issues; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: On examination, Daying has already appeared under the foot *yangming* and appears here again; Mr Wang does not comment, but it should be understood like Quanliao appearing twice.

[On "one each at the meeting of the pillar bone"] This means the two Tianding points, on the neck above the supraclavicular fossa, directly below Futu, half a body-*cun* behind Qishe, where the vessel qi of the hand *yangming* issues; one may needle four body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes one and a half *cun*.

[On "one each at the meeting of the shoulder bone"] This means the two Jianyu points; location and needling as in the *Qixue lun*.
Song editors: The shoulder bone (Yugu) is not in the note to the *Qixue lun* but in the notes to *Cire*, *Shuire xue* and *Gukong lun*.

[On "from the elbow down to the base of the index finger, six transport points each"] This means the six points Sanli, Yangxi, Hegu, Sanjian, Erjian and Shangyang; counting left and right, twelve. Location and needling as in the *Qixue lun*.
Song editors: The note to the *Qixue lun* has Quchi and not Sanli; Quchi is the uniting point of the hand *yangming*. Here Sanli is wrongly given and Quchi omitted.

## Notes

The points where the vessel qi of the hand *yangming* issues.
- The Song editors take Sanli among the six transport points in Wang Bing's note as an error for Quchi.
