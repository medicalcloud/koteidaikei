# 素問 気府論篇第五十九 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The points where the vessel qi of the hand lesser yang issues are thirty-two: one each below the cheekbone; one each behind the eyebrow; one each above the [temporal] corner; one each below and behind Wangu; one each in the nape in front of the foot greater yang; one each beside Futu; one each at Jianzhen; one each in the division three *cun* below Jianzhen; from the elbow down to the base of the fourth finger, six transport points each.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "thirty-two: one each below the cheekbone"] This means the two Quanliao points; location and needling as for the hand greater yang vessel. In this point the vessel qi of the hand lesser yang and greater yang meet equally, neither predominating; therefore it is described again here. The same applies to those below.

[On "one each behind the eyebrow"] This means the two Sizhukong points, in the depression behind the eyebrow, where the vessel qi of the hand lesser yang issues; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain six breaths; moxibustion is not allowed; moxibustion may unfortunately make the eyes small or blind.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes "foot lesser yang" for "hand lesser yang" and three breaths for six.

[On "one each above the corner"] This means the two Xuanli points; the same as under the foot lesser yang vessel, since they are the meeting of these two vessels.
Song editors: Under the foot lesser yang it says "below the corner", here "above the corner"; this is suspected to be an error.

[On "one each below and behind Wangu"] This means the two Tianyou points; location and needling as in the *Qixue lun*.

[On "one each in the nape in front of the foot greater yang"] This means the two Fengchi points, in the depression behind the ear, which draws into the ear when pressed, the meeting of the hand and foot lesser yang; one may needle four body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says "behind the temple at the hairline, the meeting of the foot lesser yang and *yangwei*, needled three *fen*".

[On "one each beside Futu"] This means the two Tianchuang points, in the depression below the angle of the jaw behind Futu where the pulse is felt, where the vessel qi of the hand greater yang issues; one may needle six body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "one each at Jianzhen"] Jianzhen is the name of a point, below the curve of the shoulder blade, between the two bones, in the depression behind Jianyu, where the vessel qi of the hand greater yang issues; one may needle eight body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "one each in the division three *cun* below Jianzhen"] This means Jianliao, Naohui and Xiaoluo, two points each, each in the divisions of the flesh. Jianliao is at the tip of the shoulder above the upper arm, taken with the arm raised obliquely, where the vessel qi of the hand lesser yang issues; one may needle seven body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones. Naohui is on the anterior edge of the arm, three body-*cun* from the tip of the shoulder, the meeting of the qi of the hand *yangming* and lesser yang network vessels; one may needle five body-*fen*; for moxibustion, five cones. Xiaoluo is below the shoulder on the outer arm, related to the armpit, in the division running obliquely down to the elbow, a meeting of the hand lesser yang vessel; one may needle five body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "from the elbow down to the base of the fourth finger, six transport points each"] This means the six points Tianjing, Zhigou, Yangchi, Zhongzhu, Yemen and Guanchong; counting left and right, twelve. Location and needling as in the *Qixue lun*.

## Notes

The points where the vessel qi of the hand lesser yang issues.
- 齀骨 is 鼽骨 "cheekbone" (other sources write 鼽).
