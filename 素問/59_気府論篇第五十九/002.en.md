# 素問 気府論篇第五十九 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

[The final 也 of the previous unit.] The points where the vessel qi of the foot lesser yang issues are sixty-two: two each above both [temporal] corners; five each inside the hairline directly above the eyes; one each above the corner in front of the ear; one each below the corner in front of the ear; one each below the sideburns; one each at Kezhuren; one each in the depression behind the ear; one each at Xiaguan; one each behind the jaw below the ear; one each at Quepen; three *cun* below the armpit and from below the flanks to the sides, one each in eight spaces; one each beside the centre of the hip joint; from the knee down to the fourth toe, six transport points each.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The points ... sixty-two: two each above both corners"] This means Tianchong and Qubin, two each left and right. Tianchong is three body-*fen* forward above the ear, the meeting of the foot greater yang and lesser yang; one may needle three body-*fen*; for moxibustion, five cones. Qubin is in the depression at the curved corner inside the hairline above the ear, where there is a space when the jaw is clenched, the meeting of the foot greater yang and lesser yang; needling and moxibustion as for Tianchong.

[On "five each inside the hairline directly above the eyes"] These are Linqi, Muchuang, Zhengying, Chengling and Naokong, left and right. Linqi is directly above the eye, five body-*fen* inside the hairline, the meeting of the foot greater yang, lesser yang and *yangwei*; retain seven breaths. Muchuang is one body-*cun* behind Linqi, Zhengying one body-*cun* behind Muchuang, Chengling one and a half body-*cun* behind Zhengying, and Naokong one and a half body-*cun* behind Chengling, beside the back of the occipital bone and above it; all are meetings of the foot lesser yang and *yangwei*. [Naokong] may be needled four body-*fen*, the others three body-*fen*; for moxibustion, five cones each.
Song editors: For Naokong "behind and above the occipital bone", the *Jiayi jing* writes "below the jade-pillow bone".

[On "one each above the corner in front of the ear"] This means the two Hanyan points, below the curved corner, on the upper edge above the temple, the meeting of the hand and foot lesser yang and foot *yangming*; one may needle seven body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Deep needling makes one unable to hear.

[On "one each below the corner in front of the ear"] This means the two Xuanli points, above the curved corner, on the lower edge of the temple, the meeting of the four vessels of the hand and foot lesser yang and *yangming*; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: Below, under the hand lesser yang, it says "above the corner", while here it says "below the corner"; one must be wrong.

[On "one each below the sideburns"] This means the two Heliao points, at the transverse artery below the sideburns in front of the ear, the meeting of the hand and foot lesser yang; one may needle three body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says "the meeting of the hand and foot lesser yang and hand greater yang".

[On "one each at Kezhuren"] Kezhuren is the name of a point, at the raised bone on the upper edge in front of the ear, where there is a space when the mouth is open, the meeting of the hand and foot lesser yang and foot *yangming*; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* and the notes to the *Qixue lun* and *Cijin lun* all say "the meeting of the hand lesser yang and foot *yangming*", which differs from this.

[On "one each in the depression behind the ear"] This means the two Yifeng points, in the depression behind the ear; pressing it draws into the ear; the meeting of the hand and foot lesser yang; one may needle three body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones.

[On "one each at Xiaguan"] Xiaguan is the name of a point; location and needling as in the *Qixue lun*.

[On "one each behind the jaw below the ear"] This means the Jiache point; needling as in the *Qixue lun*.

[On "one each at Quepen"] Quepen is the name of a point, in the depression of the transverse bone above the shoulder, where the vessel qi of the foot *yangming* issues; one may needle two body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Too deep, it makes the breath counterflow.
Song editors: The note to the *Gukong lun* writes "hand *yangming*".

[On "three *cun* below the armpit and from below the flanks to the sides, one each in eight spaces"] "Three *cun* below the armpit" is in body-*cun*. Below the armpit means Yuanye, Zhejin and Tianchi; from below the flanks to the sides means Riyue, Zhangmen, Daimai, Wushu, Weidao and Juliao: nine points, eighteen left and right. Yuanye is three body-*cun* below the armpit, where the vessel qi of the foot lesser yang issues; found with the arm raised; one may needle three body-*fen*; moxibustion is prohibited. Zhejin is three body-*cun* below the armpit and one body-*cun* further forward, where it rubs against the flank [Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 著 for 搓, likewise below], where the vessel qi of the foot lesser yang issues; one may needle six body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones. Tianchi is two body-*cun* [Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes one *cun*] behind the nipple, three *cun* below the armpit, against the flank, between the ribs directly below the armpit, the meeting of the hand heart master and foot lesser yang; one may needle three body-*fen* [Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes seven *fen*]; for moxibustion, three cones. Riyue is the alarm point of the gallbladder, at the end of the third rib, two and a half body-*cun* to each side of the line of the xiphoid process, directly below the nipples [Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says Riyue is five *fen* below Qimen], the meeting of the foot greater yin and lesser yang; one may needle seven body-*fen*; for moxibustion, five cones. Zhangmen is the alarm point of the spleen, at the end of the floating rib, the meeting of the foot *jueyin* and lesser yang; take it lying on the side with the upper leg bent, the lower leg stretched and the arm raised; one may needle eight body-*fen* and retain six breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Daimai is one body-*cun* eight *fen* below the floating rib, the meeting of the foot lesser yang and belt vessel; one may needle six body-*fen*; for moxibustion, five cones. Wushu is three body-*cun* below Daimai, the meeting of the foot lesser yang and belt vessel; one may needle one body-*cun*; for moxibustion, five cones. Weidao is five body-*cun* three *fen* below Zhangmen, the meeting of the foot lesser yang and belt vessel; needling and moxibustion as for Zhangmen. Juliao is four body-*cun* three *fen* below Zhangmen, in the depression above the iliac bone [Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 監骨], the meeting of the yang *qiao* and foot lesser yang; needling and moxibustion as for Weidao. They are called "eight spaces" because from three *cun* below the armpit to the floating rib there are eight ribs.

[On "one each beside the centre of the hip joint"] This means the two Huantiao points; needling as in the *Qixue lun*.
Song editors: The *Qixue lun* has "in the divisions of both hip sockets", which Wang's note takes as the Huantiao point; and the note in the *Jiayi jing* places Huantiao in the hip joint. "One each beside the centre of the hip joint" here presumably means this point is in the hip joint, and "one each beside" means one point each on left and right; it does not mean Huantiao is beside the hip joint.

[On "from the knee down to the fourth toe, six transport points each"] This means the six points Yanglingquan, Yangfu, Qiuxu, Linqi, Xiaxi and Qiaoyin; counting left and right, twelve transport points. Their location and needling follow the *Qixue lun*.

## Notes

The points where the vessel qi of the foot lesser yang issues.
- The initial 也 is the final character of the previous unit (foot greater yang), placed here by the division into units.
- 自窓 and 曲陽 in Wang Bing's notes are presumably errors for 目窓 (Muchuang) and 曲隅 "curved corner"; the translation follows these readings.
