# 素問 気府論篇第五十九 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The points where the qi of the thoroughfare vessel issues are twenty-two: half a *cun* to each side outside Jiuwei, one each *cun* down to the navel; five *fen* to each side below the navel, one each *cun* down to the pubic bone — this is the rule of the abdominal vessel.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "twenty-two: half a *cun* to each side outside Jiuwei, one each *cun* down to the navel"] This means the six points Youmen, Tonggu, Yindu, Shiguan, Shangqu and Huangshu, twelve points left and right. Youmen is in the depressions half a body-*cun* to each side of Juque; the five points below are each one body-*cun* apart; all are meetings of the thoroughfare vessel and foot lesser yin; each may be needled one body-*cun*; for moxibustion, five cones.
Song editors: Here "each needled one *cun*", but the *Jiayi jing* says "Youmen and Tonggu are needled five *fen*".

[On "five *fen* to each side below the navel ... the rule of the abdominal vessel"] This means the five points Zhongzhu, Suifu, Baomen, Yinguan and Xiaji, ten points left and right. Zhongzhu is five body-*fen* below Huangshu, directly below Youmen; the four points below are each one body-*cun* apart; all are meetings of the thoroughfare vessel and foot lesser yin; each may be needled one body-*cun*; for moxibustion, five cones.

## Notes

The points where the qi of the thoroughfare vessel issues.
- 腎兪 in Wang Bing's note is presumably an error for 肓兪 (Huangshu) from the context; the translation gives Huangshu. 巨関 is presumably an error for 巨闕 (Juque).
- Suifu, Baomen, Yinguan and Xiaji in Wang Bing's note are said to correspond to the later point names Siman, Qixue, Dahe and Henggu, but this has not been checked.
