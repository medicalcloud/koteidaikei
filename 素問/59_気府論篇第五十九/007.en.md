# 素問 気府論篇第五十九 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The points where the qi of the *du* vessel issues are twenty-eight: two in the centre of the nape; eight in the middle behind the hairline; three in the middle of the face; from Dazhui down to the tailbone and beside it, fifteen points; down to the sacrum, twenty-one vertebrae in all — this is the rule of the spine.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The points where the qi of the *du* vessel issues are twenty-eight"] Now one point is missing.
Song editors: Counting the two Huiyang points, there are twenty-nine points, one in excess, not one missing; 少 should be 剰 "excess".

[On "two in the centre of the nape"] These are the two points Fengfu and Yinmen, both in the nape; the remaining one is now lost. Fengfu is on the nape one body-*cun* inside the hairline, in the hollow inside the great sinew, the meeting of the *du* vessel and *yangwei*; one may needle four body-*fen* and retain three breaths; moxibustion must not be applied rashly, as it may unfortunately cause loss of voice. Yinmen is in the hollow at the hairline of the nape, one body-*cun* from Fengfu, the meeting of the *du* and *yangwei* channels; take it with the head tilted back; one may needle four body-*fen*; moxibustion is prohibited, as it causes loss of voice.
Song editors: Mr Wang's "Fengfu and Yinmen are both in the nape; the remaining one is now lost" does not mean one of these twenty-eight points is lost. Mr Wang presumably saw that "one each on both sides above Dazhui" in the *Qixue lun* is also a point on the nape and is now lost, and so said "the remaining one is now lost".

[On "eight in the middle behind the hairline"] This means the eight points Shenting, Shangxing, Xinhui, Qianding, Baihui, Houding, Qiangjian and Naohu, exactly in the middle of the hairline. Shenting is at the hairline directly above the nose, the meeting of the *du* vessel and the foot greater yang and *yangming*; needling is prohibited — needling it causes vertex illness and loss of sight; for moxibustion, three cones. Shangxing is on the skull directly above the centre of the nose, in the depression one body-*cun* inside the hairline admitting a bean. Xinhui is in the depression one body-*cun* behind Shangxing. Qianding is in the depression between the bones one body-*cun* five *fen* behind Xinhui. Baihui is one body-*cun* five *fen* behind Qianding, in the depression admitting a finger in the whorl at the centre of the vertex, the meeting of the *du* vessel and foot greater yang. Houding is one body-*cun* five *fen* behind Baihui. Qiangjian is one body-*cun* five *fen* behind Houding. Naohu is one body-*cun* five *fen* behind Qiangjian, the meeting of the *du* vessel and foot greater yang; moxibustion is not allowed. These eight are all where the qi of the *du* vessel issues. Shangxing, Baihui, Qiangjian and Naohu may each be needled three body-*fen*; retain Shangxing six breaths and Naohu three breaths; the others may be needled four body-*fen*; for moxibustion, five cones.
Song editors: In the *Jiayi jing* Naohu must not receive moxibustion; the note to the *Gukong lun* says "do not apply moxibustion rashly".

[On "three in the middle of the face"] This means the three points Suliao, Shuigou and Yinjiao. Suliao is at the upper end of the nasal column, where the qi of the *du* vessel issues; one may needle three body-*fen*. Shuigou is in the philtrum below the nasal column, taken directly above the lip, the meeting of the *du* vessel and hand *yangming*; one may needle two body-*fen* and retain six breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Yinjiao is inside the lip, at the seam of the gum above the teeth, the meeting of the *du* and *ren* channels; it may be needled upward three body-*fen*; for moxibustion, three cones. These three lie exactly in the centre of the face between left and right.

[On "from Dazhui down to the tailbone and beside it, fifteen points"] Between the vertebrae there are fifteen transport points: Dazhui, Taodao, Shenzhu, Shendao, Lingtai, Zhiyang, Jinsuo, Zhongshu, Jizhong, Xuanshu, Mingmen, Yangguan, Yaoshu, Changqiang and Huiyang. Dazhui is in the depression above the first vertebra, the meeting of the three yang and the *du* vessel. Taodao is in the space below the Dazhui joint of the nape, the meeting of the *du* vessel and foot greater yang; take it bending forward. Shenzhu is in the space below the third vertebra; take it bending forward. Shendao is below the fifth; Lingtai below the sixth; Zhiyang below the seventh; Jinsuo below the ninth; Zhongshu below the tenth; all taken bending forward. Jizhong is below the eleventh, taken bending forward; moxibustion is prohibited, as it makes one hunchbacked. Xuanshu is below the thirteenth, taken lying prone. Mingmen is below the fourteenth, taken lying prone. Yangguan is below the sixteenth, taken sitting. Yaoshu is in the space below the twenty-first vertebra. Changqiang is at the tip of the coccyx, where the branch of the *du* vessel and the lesser yin knot. Huiyang is on both sides of the coccyx. All fifteen are where the qi of the *du* vessel issues. Yaoshu and Changqiang may each be needled two body-*fen* [Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes two *cun*, and the note to the *Shuixue lun* two *fen*; for Yaoshu, the notes to the *Miuci lun* and the heat points write two *cun*, and the note to *Cire* two *fen*. The notes differ. Though the *Jiayi jing* writes two *cun*, this is suspected to be too deep; better to err on the shallow side than the deep; the view of two *fen* should be followed], retaining seven breaths. Xuanshu may be needled three body-*fen*; Huiyang eight body-*fen*; the others five body-*fen*. Retain Taodao and Shendao five breaths each. Taodao, Shenzhu, Shendao and Jinsuo may receive five cones; Dazhui nine; the others three.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* lacks the three points Lingtai, Zhongshu and Yangguan.

[On "down to the sacrum, twenty-one vertebrae in all — this is the rule of the spine"] Including the three joints of the neck bones, there are twenty-four.

## Notes

The points where the qi of the *du* vessel issues.
- Wang Bing says one point is missing, but the Song editors note that counting Huiyang there are twenty-nine, one in excess.
- 谷豆 and 頂大椎 in Wang Bing's notes are presumably errors for 容豆 "admitting a bean" and 項大椎 "Dazhui of the nape"; the translation follows these readings.
