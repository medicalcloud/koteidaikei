# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §124 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The pulse arrives compliantly, but on pressing it does not drum. All yang [illnesses] are so."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The pulse arrives compliantly, but on pressing it does not drum. All yang [illnesses] are so"] It means that when one is ill with heat and the pulse is rapid but does not move on pressing, it is caused by abundant cold blocking out yang; it is not heat.

## Notes

The answer that what looks like yang illness, with a pulse that does not drum on pressing, is really cold.
