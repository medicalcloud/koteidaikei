# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "This is what the Way governs, and what practitioners are unsure of."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "This is what the Way governs, and what practitioners are unsure of"] Not knowing its essentials, one scatters without end.

## Notes

The answer that this is the essential of the Way, on which practitioners are confused.
