# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §60 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When wind transforms in heaven and coolness prevails over it instead, treat with sour and warm, assist with sweet and bitter. When heat transforms in heaven and cold prevails over it instead, treat with sweet and warm, assist with bitter, sour and acrid. When damp transforms in heaven and heat prevails over it instead, treat with bitter and cold, assist with bitter and sour. When fire transforms in heaven and cold prevails over it instead, treat with sweet and hot, assist with bitter and acrid. When dryness transforms in heaven and heat prevails over it instead, treat with acrid and cold, assist with bitter and sweet. When cold transforms in heaven and heat prevails over it instead, treat with salty and cold, assist with bitter and acrid."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When wind transforms in heaven ... assist with sweet and bitter"] *Hai* and *si* years.

[On "When heat transforms in heaven ... assist with bitter, sour and acrid"] *Zi* and *wu* years.

[On "When damp transforms in heaven ... assist with bitter and sour"] *Chou* and *wei* years.

[On "When fire transforms in heaven ... assist with bitter and acrid"] *Yin* and *shen* years.

[On "When dryness transforms in heaven ... assist with bitter and sweet"] *Mao* and *you* years.

[On "When cold transforms in heaven ... assist with bitter and acrid"] *Chen* and *xu* years.

## Notes

The answer giving treatments when the qi controlling heaven is overcome.
