# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §103 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. I wish to hear what is meant by the threefold division of yin and yang."

## Notes

A question about the meaning of the three yin and three yang. There is no note by Wang Bing.
