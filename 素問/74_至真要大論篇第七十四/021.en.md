# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "They are the concentrated essence of heaven and earth."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "They are the concentrated essence of heaven and earth"] Medicines of concentrated essential qi are rich and thick, and in use they hit their proper qi and flavor.
Song editors: On examination, 先歳 is perhaps to be read 司歳.

## Notes

The answer that the year's things concentrate the essence of heaven and earth.
