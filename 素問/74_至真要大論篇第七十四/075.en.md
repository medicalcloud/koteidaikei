# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §75 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the retaliation of *yangming*, clear qi rises greatly, forest trees dry while still green, and hairy creatures suffer pestilence. Illness arises in the flanks and rib-sides, qi gathers on the left, there is frequent deep sighing, and in severe cases heart pain with glomus and fullness, abdominal distension with diarrhea, bitter vomiting, coughing and hiccup, vexed heart; the illness is in the diaphragm, with headache, and in severe cases it enters the liver, with fright and cramping of the sinews. If Taichong is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the illness is in the diaphragm, with headache, and in severe cases it enters the liver, with fright and cramping of the sinews"] Killing qi rises greatly and wood cannot overcome it, so blue-green leaves dry up before they can yellow. 厲 means dying of pestilence and epidemic. It is because coolness is severe within and heat pent up outside.

[On "If Taichong is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated"] Taichong is the pulse qi of the liver.

## Notes

The retaliation of *yangming*.
