# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §99 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Repress what is high, raise what is low, break what is in excess, supplement what is insufficient; assist with what benefits, harmonize with what suits; be sure to settle host and guest and adjust their cold and warmth; go against those that are alike and follow those that differ."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "be sure to settle host and guest ... follow those that differ"] Repressing the high restrains its prevalence; raising the low aids its weakness; breaking the excess bends its sharpness; supplementing the insufficient makes its qi whole. Though one restrains the prevailing and supports the weak, host and guest must be settled. If one qi loses its place, contradictions arise one after another, thorns spring up on both sides, each watches for its advantage, their wills do not agree, they run rampant within and combine without, and the causes of danger and ruin arise. "Alike" means the qi of cold, heat, warmth and coolness that are akin and harmonious; "differ" means water, fire, metal, wood and earth that are not akin and harmonious. When the qi suit each other, treat by going against the prevailing qi; when they do not suit, treat by following the qi that cannot prevail. In treating the prevalence and defeat of fire, what is to be increased is done by its flavor, and what is to be drained also by its flavor; prevailing or not, its qi is broken in both cases. Why? Because its nature is agitated and moving. Treating heat is the same.
