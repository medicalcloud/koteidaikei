# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. What of balanced qi?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of balanced qi"] 平 means diagnosing balanced, harmonious qi.

## Notes

A question about diagnosing balanced qi.
