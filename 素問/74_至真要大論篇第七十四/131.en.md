# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §131 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence the *Great Essentials* says, 'The warmth of spring becomes the heat of summer; the resentment of autumn becomes the rage of winter. Carefully examine the four corners and observe them, and all return [to order]; their end can be seen and their beginning known.' This is what it means."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "their end can be seen and their beginning known.' This is what it means"] It speaks of the youth and maturity of the qi: yang in youth is warmth, in maturity heat; yin in youth is resentment, in maturity rage. All this means that the different qi of youth and maturity attest to the abundance and decline of their functioning. Just set abundance and decline at the positions of the four corners, and the beginning and end of yin and yang and their responses and uses can all be known.

## Notes

Concluding with the quotation from the *Great Essentials*. Qibo's speech ends here.
