# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §84 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "From the first qi to the end of the third qi, heaven's qi governs; this is the constant [time] of prevalence. From the fourth qi to the end of the final qi, earth's qi governs; this is the constant [time] of retaliation. Where there is prevalence there is retaliation; where there is no prevalence there is none."

## Notes

The answer that the first half of the year is the time of prevalence and the second half of retaliation, and that without prevalence there is no retaliation. There is no note by Wang Bing.
