# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §65 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the prevalence of lesser yang, heat lodges in the stomach, with vexed heart and heart pain, red eyes and nausea, vomiting of sour fluid and frequent hunger, ear pain and red urine, frequent fright and delirium. Violent heat consumes and melts; grasses wither, water dries up, and shelled creatures are subdued. There is lower abdominal pain and discharge red and white.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "grasses wither, water dries up, and shelled creatures are subdued. There is lower abdominal pain and discharge red and white"] The five *yin* and five *shen* years. Heat is violently severe, so grasses wither, water dries up, and yin qi is consumed. Shelled creatures are the transformation of metal; fire qi greatly prevails, so they are subdued and hide. Sour [vomit] is vinegary water.

## Notes

The prevalence of lesser yang.
