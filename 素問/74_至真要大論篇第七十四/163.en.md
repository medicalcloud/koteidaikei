# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §163 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the qi of prevalence and retaliation meet, there is more or less. When yin qi is more and yang qi less, the outbreaks are far apart; when yang qi is more and yin qi less, the outbreaks are close together. These are the rhythms of abundance and decline as prevalence and retaliation press on each other; malaria follows the same rule."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "These are the rhythms of abundance and decline as prevalence and retaliation press on each other; malaria follows the same rule"] When yin and yang are equal, cold and heat are half and half within one day. When yang is more and yin less, it breaks out once a day, with heat only and no cold. When yang is less and yin more, it breaks out every other day, first cold then hot. Even with retaliating and prevailing qi, if the qi is slight, after one outbreak it breaks out again only after six or seven days; people of the time say it was cured and recurred. Some break out three days running and stop for six or seven days; some break out after ten days' interval and stop for four or five days; all depend on the amount of qi and whether they meet or not. Popular views do not see far: they call it a sudden disease from ghosts and spirits, and pray and hide, and even when the force of the illness has passed, it comes round to their death. When the sick die, they say it was their lot. Thus wronged souls block the dark road and untimely deaths fill the wilds. Could anyone of humane love, seeing this, not grieve? The custom is long established and hard to change quickly; it cannot be changed. What can be done? Alas, alas!

## Notes

The answer that the interval of attacks depends on the proportions of yin and yang qi.
