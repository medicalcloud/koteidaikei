# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §54 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When greater yang controls heaven and cold runs rampant and prevails, cold qi arrives instead, water freezes, blood changes within and breaks out as abscesses and sores. People suffer reversal heart pain, vomiting blood, bloody discharge, stuffy nose and nosebleed, frequent sorrow, and occasional dizziness and falling. If the fire of the movement blazes fiercely, rain falls violently and turns to hail. Fullness of the chest and abdomen, hot hands and cramped elbows, surging at the armpit, violent throbbing of the heart, unease of the chest, rib-sides and stomach duct, red face and yellow eyes, frequent belching and dry throat, and in severe cases a sooty complexion with thirst and desire to drink. The illness is rooted in the heart. If Shenmen is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated. This is what is meant by knowing the viscera through the moving qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "unease of the chest, rib-sides and stomach duct ... The illness is rooted in the heart"] This refers to the *jiachen*, *bingchen*, *wuchen*, *gengchen*, *renchen*, *jiaxu*, *bingxu*, *wuxu*, *gengxu* and *renxu* years. Greater yang controls heaven and cold qi spreads its transformation, so water freezes, blood congeals in the skin, and defensive qi knots and gathers, forming abscesses. If it rides on a fire movement, fire heat blazes fiercely and battles with water, so violent rain [mixes with] hail half the shape of pearls. Heart qi becomes belching, hence frequent belching. In these years people's illnesses gather in the heart and rib-sides. Yang qi is pent up within and damp qi steams from below, so there is reversal heart pain, vomiting blood, bloody discharge, stuffy nose and nosebleed, red face and yellow eyes, frequent belching, hot hands, cramped elbows, swollen armpits and dry throat. In severe cases cold qi overcomes yang, water advances and overrides fire, fire qi is pent up within, hence thirst and desire to drink. The illness begins in the heart and is overridden by yin, hence "rooted 手 [in] the heart".
Song editors: According to the *Jiayi jing*, "hot hands, cramped elbows, swollen armpits, in severe cases propping fullness of the chest and rib-sides, violent throbbing of the heart, red face and yellow eyes" is illness of the hand heart master; also "when the pathogen is in the heart, one suffers heart pain, frequent sorrow, and occasional dizziness and falling". Presumably in years when greater yang controls heaven, water overcomes fire, hence such illnesses.

[On "The illness is rooted in the heart. If Shenmen is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated"] Shenmen is at the end of the sharp bone behind the palm, where the pulsing vessel meets the hand; it is the qi of the true heart. Water advances and rides on fire, heart qi knots within, and the spirit qi is already gone; what is there but death? One should know this diagnosis well; hence it cannot be treated.

[On "This is what is meant by knowing the viscera through the moving qi"] How does one know death by examining? In every case, through the moving qi of the channels of the viscera one knows whether the spirit stores survive or perish.

## Notes

The case of greater yang controlling heaven (*chen* and *xu* years). Finally it says that the survival of the viscera is known from the moving qi of the pulses. Qibo's speech ends here.
