# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §53 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When *yangming* controls heaven and dryness runs rampant and prevails, trees flourish late, grasses grow late, and the sinews and bones change within. People suffer pain in the left flank and rib-side, and cold coolness in the center which they respond to with malaria. Great coolness alters the season; there is coughing, rumbling in the abdomen, pouring diarrhea and duck-like sloppy stool. Famous trees draw in their life, which is pent up below; grasses are scorched at their tips. Sudden pain of the heart and rib-sides so that one cannot turn over, dry throat, a dusty face, lumbar pain, in men bulging hernia, in women lower abdominal pain, dim eyes and sores at the canthi, sores, pimples and abscesses. Hibernating creatures come out. The illness is rooted in the liver. If Taichong is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "dim eyes and sores at the canthi, sores, pimples and abscesses. Hibernating creatures come out. The illness is rooted in the liver"] This refers to the *yimao*, *dingmao*, *jimao*, *xinmao*, *guimao*, *yiyou*, *dingyou*, *jiyou*, *xinyou* and *guiyou* years. Metal prevails, so plants grow and flourish late. Applied to the human body, the sinews and bones respond within and are useless. When the qi of great coolness alters the season, people have cold coolness arising within, and responding internally to cold qi they get malaria. The large intestine is on the right and lung qi connects with it; now lung qi runs rampant within, and the liver is on the left, so the left flank and rib-side hurt as if stabbed and cut. If in that year people have pouring diarrhea of themselves, there is no disease of rampant prevalence. Great coolness is next to cold. When great coolness is even more severe, yang qi does not move, so trees draw in their form and the flourishing of grasses is all late. The generative qi has risen, but yang does not spread its decree, so the generative qi is shut in and accumulated below. In humans, the corresponding thing is painful qi residing in the lower abdomen, which breaks out as disease in midsummer. Sores persist even into mid-autumn. Sores and pimples arise above, abscesses and swellings below. Though the sores are red in color, their centers are white; this is the constancy of the qi of things.
Song editors: According to the *Jiayi jing*, "lumbar pain with inability to bend forward and back, in men bulging hernia, in women swelling of the lower abdomen, in severe cases dry throat and dusty face" is illness of the liver; also "chest fullness and throughflux diarrhea" is illness of the liver; also "pain of the heart and rib-sides with inability to turn over, pain at the outer canthus, swelling and pain in the supraclavicular fossa, swelling below the armpit, saber and pearl-string lumps, sweating and shivering malaria" is illness of the gallbladder. Presumably in years when *yangming* controls heaven, metal overcomes wood, hence such illnesses. Also, according to the *Maijie*: "What is called bulging hernia and women's lower abdominal swelling in *jueyin*: *jueyin* is *chen*; the third month is yin within yang, and the pathogen is in the center; hence bulging hernia and lower abdominal swelling."

[On "The illness is rooted in the liver. If Taichong is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated"] Taichong is two inches behind the base joint of the big toe, where the pulse moves against the hand; it is the qi of the liver. Metal comes to cut down wood, liver qi is cut off within, and the true does not overcome the pathogen; death is only to be expected.

## Notes

The case of *yangming* controlling heaven (*mao* and *you* years).
