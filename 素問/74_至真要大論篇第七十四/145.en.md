# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §145 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "'Examine the mechanisms of illness carefully and do not miss what suits the qi': this is what it means."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Examine the mechanisms of illness carefully and do not miss what suits the qi': this is what it means"] If one grasps the essentials of the mechanism, small action has great effect and shallow use has deep effect.

## Notes

The answer that the key is to examine the mechanisms of illness and not miss what suits the qi.
