# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "*Jueyin* controlling heaven is the transformation of wind; in the spring, the transformation of sour; controlling qi [the movement], the transformation of blue-green; as intervening qi, the transformation of movement. Lesser yin controlling heaven is the transformation of heat; in the spring, the transformation of bitter; it does not control the transformation of qi [the movement]; as dwelling qi, the transformation of scorching. Greater yin controlling heaven is the transformation of damp; in the spring, the transformation of sweet; controlling qi, the transformation of yellow; as intervening qi, the transformation of softness. Lesser yang controlling heaven is the transformation of fire; in the spring, the transformation of bitter; controlling qi, the transformation of crimson; as intervening qi, the transformation of brightness. *Yangming* controlling heaven is the transformation of dryness; in the spring, the transformation of acrid; controlling qi, the transformation of white; as intervening qi, the transformation of clarity. Greater yang controlling heaven is the transformation of cold; in the spring, the transformation of salty; controlling qi, the transformation of black; as intervening qi, the transformation of storing. Hence one who treats illness must understand the divided governance of the six transformations, what the five flavors and five colors produce, and what suits the five viscera; only then can one speak of the threads of fullness, emptiness and the arising of illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*Jueyin* controlling heaven is the transformation of wind"] *Si* and *hai* years. Wind is high and qi distant; clouds fly and things are lifted. The transformation of wind.

[On "in the spring, the transformation of sour"] In *yin* and *shen* years wood controls the earth's qi, so the transformation of things follows sourness.

[On "controlling qi, the transformation of blue-green"] The qi of the wood movement, *ding* and *ren* years. The transformation is blue-green.

[On "as intervening qi, the transformation of movement"] Each governs sixty days plus eighty-seven and a half marks.
Song editors: On examination, in *chou* and *wei* years *jueyin* is the first qi; in *zi* and *wu* years the second; in *chen* and *xu* years the fourth; in *mao* and *you* years the fifth.

[On "Lesser yin controlling heaven is the transformation of heat"] *Zi* and *wu* years. The sunlight blazes and warmth and heat circulate. The transformation of heat.

[On "in the spring, the transformation of bitter"] In *mao* and *you* years fire controls the earth's qi, so things are born with bitterness.

[On "it does not control the transformation of qi"] The sovereign does not govern a movement.
Song editors: According to the *Tianyuan ji dalun*, "sovereign fire [functions] by name, ministerial fire by position"; this means sovereign fire does not govern a movement.

[On "as dwelling qi, the transformation of scorching"] Sixty days plus eighty-seven and a half marks. Being in its own position is "dwelling"; it should not be called intervening.
Song editors: On examination, lesser yin is not said to be "intervening qi" but "dwelling qi" presumably out of reverence for sovereign fire: there is nowhere it does not dwell, and it should not be treated as intervening. Wang Bing's note "being in its own position is dwelling; it should not be called intervening" would imply that being in another position is not dwelling and may be called intervening. In *yin* and *shen* years it is the first qi; in *chou* and *wei* years the second; in *si* and *hai* years the fourth; in *chen* and *xu* years the fifth.

[On "Greater yin controlling heaven is the transformation of damp"] *Chou* and *wei* years. Dust is oppressive and dim; clouds and rain moisten. The transformation [of damp].

[On "in the spring, the transformation of sweet"] *Chen* and *xu* years. Earth controls the earth's qi, so the transformation of sweet comes first.

[On "controlling qi, the transformation of yellow"] The qi of the earth movement, *jia* and 巳 [*ji*] years. 黅 is yellow.

[On "as intervening qi, the transformation of softness"] When the transformation of damp proceeds, all things become soft.
Song editors: On examination, greater yin is the first qi in *mao* and *you* years, the second in *yin* and *shen* years, the fourth in *zi* and *wu* years, the fifth in 己 [*si*] and *hai* years.

[On "Lesser yang controlling heaven is the transformation of fire"] *Yin* and *shen* years. Flames are brilliant and fierce, burning and scorching. The transformation of fire.

[On "in the spring, the transformation of bitter"] *Si* and *hai* years. Fire controls the earth's qi, so the transformation of bitter comes first.

[On "controlling qi, the transformation of crimson"] The qi of the fire movement, *wu* and *gui* years.

[On "as intervening qi, the transformation of brightness"] 明 means shining bright; it also refers to glowing rosy clouds.
Song editors: On examination, lesser yang is the first qi in *chen* and *xu* years, the second in *mao* and *you* years, the fourth in *yin* and *shen* years, the fifth in *chou* and *wei* years.

[On "*Yangming* controlling heaven is the transformation of dryness"] *Mao* and *you* years. Clear, sharp, high and bright, with mist and dew and soughing wind. The transformation of dryness.

[On "in the spring, the transformation of acrid"] *Zi* and *wu* years. Metal controls the earth's qi, so the transformation of acrid comes first.

[On "controlling qi, the transformation of white"] The qi of the metal movement, *yi* and *geng* years.

[On "as intervening qi, the transformation of clarity"] Wind arises high and sharp; plants are clear and cold. The transformation of clarity.
Song editors: On examination, *yangming* is the first qi in *si* and *hai* years, the second in *chen* and *xu* years, the fourth in *yin* and *shen* years, the fifth in *chou* and *wei* years.

[On "Greater yang controlling heaven is the transformation of cold"] *Chen* and *xu* years. Severe, austere, stern and orderly, bleak, shivering, congealing and hard. The transformation of cold.

[On "in the spring, the transformation of salty"] In *chou* and *wei* years water controls the earth's qi, so transformation follows saltiness.

[On "controlling qi, the transformation of black"] The qi of the water movement, *bing* and *xin* years.

[On "as intervening qi, the transformation of storing"] Yin congeals and is cold, and all things draw in their forms. The transformation of 歳 [storing].
Song editors: On examination, greater yang is the first qi in *zi* and *wu* years, the second in 己 [*si*] and *hai* years, the fourth in *mao* and *you* years, the fifth in *yin* and *shen* years.

[On "what suits the five viscera; only then can one speak of the threads of fullness, emptiness and the arising of illness"] In learning, one should not tire of thorough practice.

## Notes

The answer giving the transformations of each of the six qi when controlling heaven, in the spring, controlling qi (the movement), and as intervening qi. Lesser yin (sovereign fire) does not govern a movement and is called "dwelling qi" rather than "intervening qi".
- In the Song editors' note on lesser yang as intervening qi, "fourth qi in *yin* and *shen* years, fifth in *chou* and *wei* years" should, by the reckoning used in the other notes, be "fourth in *chou* and *wei* years, fifth in *zi* and *wu* years"; that it repeats the years of the *yangming* note is presumably an error.
- 歳 in 歳之化 in Wang Bing's note on greater yang as intervening qi is read as 蔵.
