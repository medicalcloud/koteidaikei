# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Those controlling qi are the same as those governing the year, but there is surplus and insufficiency."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Those controlling qi are the same as those governing the year, but there is surplus and insufficiency"] The five movements governing the year have surplus and insufficiency. Compared with the year's things, one fears some may be thin; medicines of a surplus year are concentrated in essence.

## Notes

The answer that the movement qi are similar but subject to surplus and insufficiency.
