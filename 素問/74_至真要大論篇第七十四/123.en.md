# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §123 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "When the pulse complies but the illness is contrary, how is it diagnosed?"

## Notes

A question about diagnosis when pulse and illness diverge. There is no note by Wang Bing.
