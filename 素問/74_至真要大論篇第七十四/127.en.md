# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §127 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "What of the earliness or lateness of the changes of prevalence and retaliation?"

## Notes

A question about the timing of prevalence and retaliation. There is no note by Wang Bing.
