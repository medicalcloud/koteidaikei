# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §114 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the illness is contrary to its root, one has illness of the tip; when the treatment is contrary to the root, one has the formula for the tip."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the illness is contrary to its root ... the formula for the tip"] This refers to the two qi of lesser yin and greater yang; for the other four qi, tip and root are the same.

## Notes

The answer that illness of the tip is treated contrary to the root.
