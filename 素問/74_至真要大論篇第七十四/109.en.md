# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §109 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Qi may be more or less, illness abundant or declining, treatment moderate or urgent, formulas large or small. I wish to hear the summary of these. What is it?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Qi may be more or less, illness abundant or declining"] Song editors: According to the *Tianyuan ji dalun*, "the body has abundance and decline".

## Notes

A question asking for a summary on the size of formulas and related matters.
