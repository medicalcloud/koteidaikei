# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §102 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For the guest *jueyin*, supplement with acrid, drain with sour, moderate with sweet. For the guest lesser yin, supplement with salty, drain with sweet, contract with salty. For the guest greater yin, supplement with sweet, drain with bitter, moderate with sweet. For the guest lesser yang, supplement with salty, drain with sweet, soften with salty. For the guest *yangming*, supplement with sour, drain with acrid, percolate with bitter. For the guest greater yang, supplement with bitter, drain with salty, harden with bitter, moisten with acrid. [This] opens the interstices, brings fluids, and makes qi flow."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "For the guest lesser yin, supplement with salty, drain with sweet, contract with salty"] Song editors: According to the *Zangqi fa shi lun*, "The heart suffers from slackness; quickly feed it sour to contract it. The heart desires softness; quickly feed it salty to soften it." Here "contract with salty" is an error.

[On "moisten with acrid. [This] opens the interstices, brings fluids, and makes qi flow"] Each guest's section governs sixty-one days; it has no fixed dwelling and shifts with the year. When the guest prevails, drain the guest and supplement the host; when the host prevails, drain the host and supplement the guest. Treat according to whether moderation or urgency is called for.

## Notes

The answer giving the flavors for supplementing and draining the guest qi. Qibo's speech ends here.
