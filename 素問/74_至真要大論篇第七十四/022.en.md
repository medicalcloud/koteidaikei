# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "What of those controlling qi?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of those controlling qi"] Those controlling the qi of the movements.

## Notes

A question about the qi controlling the movements.
