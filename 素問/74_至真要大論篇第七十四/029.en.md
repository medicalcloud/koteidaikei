# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the upper runs rampant over the lower, calm it with what overcomes it; when the outer runs rampant within, treat it with what overcomes it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the upper runs rampant over the lower ... treat it with what overcomes it"] Running rampant means going to what cannot overcome oneself. The upper running rampant over the lower is heaven's qi; the outer running rampant within is earth's qi. Following what restrains and overcomes, calm and treat it. Restraining and overcoming means using the five flavors and cold, heat, warmth and coolness according to what overcomes; this is detailed in the text below.
Song editors: On examination, when heaven's qi gives birth to the year, though there is rampant prevalence, it should only be calmed and regulated; hence it says not "treat" but "calm".

## Notes

The answer: rampancy of the qi controlling heaven is calmed, that of the spring in earth is treated, with what overcomes it.
