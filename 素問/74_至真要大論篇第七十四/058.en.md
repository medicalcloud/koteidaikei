# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §58 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When wind controls the earth and coolness prevails over it instead, treat with sour and warm, assist with bitter and sweet, calm it with acrid. When heat controls the earth and cold prevails over it instead, treat with sweet and hot, assist with bitter and acrid, calm it with salty. When damp controls the earth and heat prevails over it instead, treat with bitter and cold, assist with salty and sweet, calm it with bitter. When fire controls the earth and cold prevails over it instead, treat with sweet and hot, assist with bitter and acrid, calm it with salty. When dryness controls the earth and heat prevails over it instead, treat with balanced cold, assist with bitter and sweet, calm it with sour, taking harmony as the benefit. When cold controls the earth and heat prevails over it instead, treat with salty and cold, assist with sweet and acrid, calm it with bitter."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When wind controls the earth and coolness prevails over it instead ... calm it with acrid"] When *jueyin* is in the spring, wind controls the earth: the five *yin* years and five *shen* years. The pathogenic qi prevails abundantly, so first drain it with sour, assisted by bitter and sweet; when the pathogenic qi recedes, the proper qi is depleted, so supplement and nourish it with acrid to calm it.

[On "When heat controls the earth and cold prevails over it instead ... calm it with salty"] When lesser yin is in the spring, heat controls the earth: the five *mao* and five *you* years. First drain the pathogen, then calm the proper qi.

[On "When damp controls the earth and heat prevails over it instead ... calm it with bitter"] When greater yin is in the spring, damp controls the earth: the five *chen* and five *xu* years. The meaning of supplementing and draining is the same for the other qi.

[On "When fire controls the earth and cold prevails over it instead ... calm it with salty"] When lesser yang is in the spring, fire controls the earth: the five *si* and five *hai* years.

[On "When dryness controls the earth and heat prevails over it instead ... taking harmony as the benefit"] When *yangming* is in the spring, dryness controls the earth: the five *zi* and five *wu* years. The nature of dryness dislikes heat and also fears cold; hence the harmonious balance of cold and heat is made the rule of the formula.

[On "When cold controls the earth and heat prevails over it instead ... calm it with bitter"] When greater yang is in the spring, cold controls the earth: the five *chou* and five *wei* years. These formula treatments for the six qi follow a different thread from the earlier method for rampant prevalence: "treat" means draining the prevailing qi of the guest pathogen; "assist" means what benefits and suits; "calm" means supplementing the already weakened proper qi.

## Notes

The answer giving treatments when the qi in the spring is overcome (pathogenic qi prevailing instead).
