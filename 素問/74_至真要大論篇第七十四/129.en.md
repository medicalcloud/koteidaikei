# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §129 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "When prevalence and retaliation arise, they move without matching their positions, or arrive later than their time. Why is this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When prevalence and retaliation arise ... Why is this"] Yang flourishing in summer, yin in winter, coolness in autumn and warmth in spring are the constant signs of heaven; yet the functioning of the qi of prevalence and retaliation differs from the four seasons. Why? That is the question.

## Notes

A question about why prevalence and retaliation lag behind their proper times.
