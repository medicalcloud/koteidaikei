# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §171 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What governs the illness is called the sovereign; what assists the sovereign is called the minister; what responds to the minister is called the envoy. It does not refer to the three grades, upper and lower."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What governs the illness is called the sovereign ... It does not refer to the three grades, upper and lower"] Making upper-grade medicines sovereigns, middle-grade ministers and lower-grade assistants and envoys distinguishes their names and ranks by good and bad, and the way of taking [medicinal] food should follow this as the rule. The way of treating illness need not be so: what governs the illness is the sovereign, what assists the sovereign the minister, what responds to the minister's function the envoy; all serve to help complete the formula's use.

## Notes

The answer that sovereign, minister and envoy in formulas differ from the three grades of medicines.
