# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §175 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In formulas for regulating the qi, one must distinguish yin and yang, determine inner and outer, and keep each to its place. What is inside is treated from inside; what is outside is treated from outside. The slight is regulated; the next is calmed; the abundant is seized. Sweat [者] and purge; cold, heat, warmth and coolness are reduced according to their categories, following what benefits. Carefully follow the way according to the method, and one succeeds ten thousand times in ten thousand; qi and blood are properly balanced, and one long keeps heaven's mandate."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Sweat and purge; cold, heat, warmth and coolness are reduced according to their categories, following what benefits"] Illnesses are inner or outer, and treatments exterior or interior. What is inside is harmonized by methods of inner treatment; what is outside by methods of outer treatment. When the qi is slightly unharmonious, regulate it by the method of regulating qi; the next, larger, calm it by the method of calming qi; when abundant and severe without stopping, seize its qi to reduce it greatly. For example, slight cold qi is harmonized with warmth; great cold qi is taken with heat; extreme cold qi is seized by purging; if seizing does not end it, break it by going against; if breaking does not exhaust it, seek its category to reduce it. Slight heat qi is harmonized with coolness; great heat qi is taken with cold; extreme heat qi is released by sweating; if releasing does not exhaust it, restrain it by going against; if restraining does not exhaust it, seek its category to reduce it. Hence "sweat and purge; cold, heat, warmth and coolness are reduced according to their categories, following what benefits". 攸 means "what".

[On "Carefully follow the way according to the method ... one long keeps heaven's mandate"] Keeping to the way in action, nothing one undertakes misses. Hence one can drive herbs and minerals, summon spirits, regulate and govern yin and yang, and remove all diseases; blood and qi keep the signs of balance and harmony, and the heavenly true has no cause of depletion. This is because extending and contracting lie in the mind, and departing and staying follow one's intent; hence the essence and spirit are guarded within and life is long.

## Notes

The answer summarizing formulas for regulating qi: distinguish yin, yang, inner and outer, and treat according to slightness or severity.
- 汗者下之 in the main text is read following 汗之下之 as quoted in Wang Bing's note.
