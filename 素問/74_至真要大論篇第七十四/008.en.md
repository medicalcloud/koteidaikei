# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The signs are the same as for the controller of heaven, and so it is with the intervening qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The signs are the same as for the controller of heaven, and so it is with the intervening qi"] The root of each of the six qi has its own constant nature; hence even when the position changes, their transformation and governance are the same.

## Notes

The answer that the transformations of the spring in earth and the intervening qi are the same as those of the controller of heaven.
