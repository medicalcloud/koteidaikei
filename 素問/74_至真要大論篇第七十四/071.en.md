# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §71 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What a thorough question! In the retaliation of *jueyin*, the lower abdomen is hard and full, with internal tension and sudden pain. Trees are flattened and sand flies; naked creatures do not flourish. There is reversal heart pain, sweating and vomiting, food and drink do not go in, or go in and come back out; sinews and bones tremble with dizziness, there is cold reversal, and in severe cases it enters the spleen, with food impediment and vomiting. If Chongyang is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "sinews and bones tremble with dizziness, there is cold reversal, and in severe cases it enters the spleen, with food impediment and vomiting"] 裏 is the inside of the abdomen and rib-sides. Trees flattened and sand flying indicate great wind. Wind is wood's prevalence, so [the creatures of] earth do not flourish. Qi reversal means qi strikes the chest and rib-sides and encroaches on the heart. The stomach receives counterflow qi which attacks upward, hence heart pain; when the pain is severe, sweat breaks out. 掉 means movement within the flesh. Cold reversal means cold hands and feet. Food impediment means that after eating there is pain below the heart, dull and indescribable, unbearable, which stops only when vomited; this is stomach qi counterflowing and not flowing down. Food and drink not going in, or going in and coming back out, is because the liver rides on the spleen and stomach.

[On "If Chongyang is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated"] Chongyang is the pulse qi of the stomach.

Among the retaliations of the six qi, the retaliation of *jueyin*. Qibo's speech continues through unit 76.
