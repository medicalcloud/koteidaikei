# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §133 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Again, about thirty degrees [days]."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Again, about thirty degrees"] 度 means days.
Song editors: According to the *Liuyuan zhengji dalun*: "Does the deviation have a number?" "In every case it lags by thirty-odd degrees." Here "thirty degrees" is an abbreviated text.

## Notes

The answer that the deviation is about thirty days (the same exchange as chapter 71, units 98–99).
