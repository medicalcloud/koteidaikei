# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §73 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the retaliation of greater yin, changes of damp arise; the body is heavy and the center full, food and drink are not digested, yin qi reverses upward, the chest is uncomfortable, fluid breaks out within, and there is coughing and panting with sound. Heavy rain falls at times and scaled creatures appear on land. The vertex of the head is painful and heavy, trembling and spasms are especially severe; one retches and is silent, spitting clear fluid, and in severe cases it enters the kidneys, with uncontrolled discharge from the orifices. If Taixi is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "one retches and is silent, spitting clear fluid, and in severe cases it enters the kidneys, with uncontrolled discharge from the orifices"] Damp qi counterflows within, cold qi does not move, and greater yang flows upward, hence these illnesses. When the vertex is painful and heavy, the trembling and spasms in the brain are especially severe. The intestines and stomach are cold and damp, heat has nowhere to go and again burns the chest, so the chest is uncomfortable and food and drink are not digested. Retching and silence means wishing to be still and settled. The throat dislikes cold, so one spits cold water. When cold qi changes position and enters the lungs and throat, the airways are obstructed, so there is coughing and panting with sound in the throat. When water lies on the flat marshes, fish swim in the markets. The vertex and fontanel hurt; women also have pain between the eyebrows.
Song editors: According to the text above, greater yin in the spring has "headache, vertex [nape] as if pulled out", and greater yin controlling heaven has "pain of the head and nape"; here it says "pain of the head and vertex", so 頂 ("vertex") should perhaps be 項 ("nape").

[On "If Taixi is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated"] Taixi is the pulse qi of the kidneys.

## Notes

The retaliation of greater yin.
