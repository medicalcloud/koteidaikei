# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §91 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. What of prevalence and retaliation between guest and host?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of prevalence and retaliation between guest and host"] Guest means the six qi of heaven; host means the positions of the five phases. The qi may suit or not, hence each has prevalence and retaliation.

## Notes

A question about prevalence and retaliation between guest qi and host qi.
