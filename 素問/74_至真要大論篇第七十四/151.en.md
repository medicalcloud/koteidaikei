# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §151 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Whether toxic or non-toxic, what is to be treated is primary; the rule is set to fit the size [of the illness]."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Whether toxic or non-toxic, what is to be treated is primary; the rule is set to fit the size"] It means that whatever can break accumulations, cure disease, relieve urgency and rescue from death is a good formula. It does not necessarily say that toxic first is right and toxic after wrong, or non-toxic wrong and toxic right; one must weigh the lightness, severity and size of the illness to set the rule.

## Notes

The answer that toxicity is chosen by the illness treated, and the formula sized to fit.
