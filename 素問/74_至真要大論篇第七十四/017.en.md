# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence the saying 'Carefully watch for what suits the qi and do not miss the mechanism of illness' means this."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Carefully watch for what suits the qi and do not miss the mechanism of illness ... means this"] The mechanisms of illness are given in detail in the text below.

## Notes

Concluding with a saying about what suits the qi and the mechanism of illness. Qibo's speech ends here.
