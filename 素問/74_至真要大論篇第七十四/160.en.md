# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §160 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. What of the inner and outer aspects of illness?"

## Notes

A question about the inner and outer aspects of illness and the order of treatment. There is no note by Wang Bing.
