# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §87 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Why does [the qi] fall ill instead after retaliating?"

## Notes

A question about why the retaliating qi itself falls ill. There is no note by Wang Bing.
