# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §63 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the prevalence of lesser yin, there is heat below the heart, frequent hunger, movement instead below the navel, and qi wandering in the triple burner. Blazing heat arrives, trees ooze sap, and grasses wither. There is retching counterflow, agitation and vexation, abdominal fullness and pain, sloppy diarrhea, which passes on to become red discharge.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "retching counterflow ... passes on to become red discharge"] The five *zi* and five *wu* years. 沃 means 洙.

## Notes

The prevalence of lesser yin.
- 洙 in Wang Bing's note 沃洙也 may be an error for 沫 ("froth"), but this has not been verified.
