# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §51 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When greater yin controls heaven and damp runs rampant and prevails, deep yin spreads, and rain changes what is withered. [People suffer] puffy swelling, bone pain, yin impediment (yin impediment is when pressing does not find [the pain]), pain of the lower back, spine, head and nape, occasional dizziness, difficult defecation, loss of function of yin qi, hunger without desire to eat, blood when coughing and spitting, and a heart as if suspended. The illness is rooted in the kidneys. If Taixi is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "hunger without desire to eat ... The illness is rooted in the kidneys"] This refers to the *yichou*, *dingchou*, *jichou*, *xinchou*, *guichou*, *yiwei*, *dingwei*, 巳 [*ji*] *wei*, *xinwei* and *guiwei* years. 沈 means long-lasting. Kidney qi receives the pathogen, water cannot moisten, and the lower burner withers and dries, hence difficult defecation.
Song editors: According to the *Jiayi jing*, "hunger without using food, blood when coughing and spitting, heart suspended as if hungry" is illness of the kidneys; also "when the pathogen is in the kidneys, there is bone pain and yin impediment (yin impediment is when pressing does not find it), abdominal distension, lumbar pain, difficult defecation, stiffness and pain of the shoulders, back, neck and nape, occasional dizziness". Presumably in years when greater yin controls heaven, earth overcomes water, hence such illnesses.

[On "The illness is rooted in the kidneys. If Taixi is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated"] Taixi is behind the inner ankle, above the heel bone, where the pulsing vessel meets the hand; it is the qi of the kidneys. The earth pathogen prevails over water, kidney qi is cut off within, the pathogen is severe and the proper qi faint, so no formula can be used.

## Notes

The case of greater yin controlling heaven (*chou* and *wei* years).
