# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §161 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When it goes from inside to outside, regulate the inside; when it goes from outside to inside, treat the outside. When it goes from inside to outside and is abundant outside, first regulate the inside and then treat the outside; when it goes from outside to inside and is abundant inside, first treat the outside and then regulate the inside. When inside and outside do not reach each other, treat the main illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When it goes from inside to outside, regulate the inside; when it goes from outside to inside, treat the outside"] Each cuts off its source.

[On "when it goes from outside to inside and is abundant inside, first treat the outside and then regulate the inside"] In each case, first remove what belongs to the root, then pare away the branches.

[On "When inside and outside do not reach each other, treat the main illness"] When inside and outside do not reach each other, each is a separate illness.

## Notes

The answer that the order of treatment depends on where the illness began.
