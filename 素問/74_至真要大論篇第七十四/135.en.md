# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §135 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The deviation follows the same rule as the proper [time]; [the pulse] waits for its time and then departs.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The deviation follows the same rule as the proper [time]; [the pulse] waits for its time and then departs"] The pulse too deviates; it responds according to the qi. Waiting for the deviation is called sufficient; it departs when the flourishing qi arrives.

## Notes

The answer that the pulses deviate like the qi and depart at their time. Qibo's speech continues through unit 137.
- The latter part of Wang Bing's note from 待差曰足 is hard to construe; the translation is tentative (not verified).
