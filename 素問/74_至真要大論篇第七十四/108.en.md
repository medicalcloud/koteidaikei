# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §108 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "It is the two yin meeting and coming to an end."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is the two yin meeting and coming to an end"] The *Lingshu*, *Xi riyue lun*, says: "*Xu* is the ninth month and governs the *jueyin* of the right foot; *hai* is the tenth month and governs the *jueyin* of the left foot. The two yin meet and come to an end, hence *jueyin*."

## Notes

The answer that *jueyin* is the two yin meeting and coming to an end.
