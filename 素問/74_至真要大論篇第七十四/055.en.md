# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §55 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. How are they treated?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How are they treated"] This refers to those that can be attacked and treated.

## Notes

A question about treating illness from rampancy of the qi controlling heaven.
