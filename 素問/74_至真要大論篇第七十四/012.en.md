# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Those governing the year mark the year; the intervening qi mark the steps."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Those governing the year mark the year; the intervening qi mark the steps"] A year is three hundred sixty-five and a quarter days; a step is sixty days plus eighty-seven and a half marks. The days of the steps accumulate to make the year.

## Notes

The answer that the year's governors govern the year, the intervening qi a step (sixty-odd days).
