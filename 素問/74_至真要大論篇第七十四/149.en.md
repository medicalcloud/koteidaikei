# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §149 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Acrid and sweet release and disperse and are yang; sour and bitter cause welling up and draining and are yin; the salty flavor causes welling up and draining and is yin; the bland flavor percolates and drains and is yang. These six contract or disperse, moderate or tense, dry or moisten, soften or harden. Use them according to what benefits, regulate the qi, and make it balanced."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Use them according to what benefits, regulate the qi, and make it balanced"] 涌 means vomiting; 泄 means diarrhea; percolating drainage means urine. It means that fluids seep out separately from the winding intestine, the juice percolates into the bladder, is transformed by qi from the bladder into urine, and drains out.
Song editors: According to the *Zangqi fa shi lun*, "Acrid disperses, sour contracts, sweet moderates, bitter hardens, salty softens"; and also, "Acrid, sour, sweet, bitter and salty each have what they benefit: dispersing, contracting, moderating, tensing, hardening or softening. Illnesses of the four seasons and five viscera follow what suits the five flavors."

## Notes

The answer giving the yin-yang nature and actions of the five flavors (and bland).
