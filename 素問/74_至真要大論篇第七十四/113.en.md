# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §113 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. Illness arising from the root I know. How is illness arising from the tip treated?"

## Notes

A question about treating illness arising from the tip. There is no note by Wang Bing.
