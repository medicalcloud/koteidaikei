# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §153 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "One sovereign and two ministers is the small rule; one sovereign, three ministers and five assistants the medium rule; one sovereign, three ministers and nine assistants the large rule. Heat what is cold, cool what is hot, go against what is slight, follow what is severe; pare what is hard, remove what lodges, warm what is exhausted, disperse what is knotted, attack what lingers, moisten what is dry, moderate what is tense, contract what is scattered, warm what is damaged, move what is stagnant, calm what is frightened; raise it, lower it, rub it, bathe it, press it, force it, open it, release it; take fitting the case as the aim."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Heat what is cold, cool what is hot, go against what is slight, follow what is severe"] Slight illness is like 水 [human] fire: meeting grass it burns, getting 水 [wood] it blazes, but it can be subdued by damp and extinguished by water; hence one goes against its nature and qi to break and attack it. Very severe illness is like dragon fire: getting damp it flames, meeting water it blazes. If, not knowing its nature, one tries to break it with water or damp, it only makes the flames reach heaven and stops when things are exhausted. One who knows its nature, by the principle of going against the usual, drives it out with fire; then the burning disappears of itself and the flames are beaten out. Thus going against means attacking heat with cold and cold with heat; following means attacking with cold or heat that, though following its nature, need not be entirely the same in what is used. Hence the text below says, "Going against is regular treatment; following is contrary treatment; whether to follow little or much depends on the case"; is this not what it means?
Song editors: According to the *Shennong*, "Medicines have sovereign, minister, assistant and envoy, which spread and regulate one another. In combining them, one sovereign, two ministers, three assistants and five envoys is suitable; or one sovereign, two ministers and nine assistants and envoys."

[On "raise it, lower it, rub it, bathe it, press it, force it, open it, release it; take fitting the case as the aim"] Weigh the signs and patterns of the illness and apply them to fit the case.

## Notes

The answer giving the numbers of sovereign, minister and assistant drugs in small, medium and large formulas, and various treatments according to the condition.
- The first part of Wang Bing's first note is hard to construe; it is translated supplying 人火 ("human fire") for 水火 and 木 ("wood") for 水 in 得水而燔, but this has not been verified.
