# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §100 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Treating cold with heat and heat with cold, going against qi that suit each other and following those that do not, I already know. What of their proper flavors?"

## Notes

A question about the proper flavors for host and guest qi. There is no note by Wang Bing.
