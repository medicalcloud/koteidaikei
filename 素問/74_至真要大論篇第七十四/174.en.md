# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §174 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. What of the inner and outer aspects of illness?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of the inner and outer aspects of illness"] The earlier question about the inner and outer aspects of illness concerned the method of regulating qi; here it was not yet complete, so he asks again. The answer below should follow "seeking what it belongs to" above; it is an ancient misplacement of slips.

## Notes

A second question about the inner and outer aspects of illness. Wang Bing says the following answer should come after unit 165 ("seeking what it belongs to"), and is an ancient misplacement.
