# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Carefully examine where yin and yang are and regulate them, aiming at balance. Treat the regular by regular treatment and the contrary by contrary treatment."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Carefully examine where yin and yang are ... the contrary by contrary treatment"] Knowing where yin and yang are, one knows whether the 天 [cubit?] and inch [pulses] respond or not. Not knowing where yin and yang are, one takes gain for loss and counterflow for compliance; hence one examines carefully. When yin is ill and yang not, or yang ill and yin not, this is regular illness, and it is treated regularly: treating heat with cold and cold with heat. When a yang pulse appears in the yin position, or a yin pulse in the yang position, this is contrary illness, and it is treated contrarily: treating cold with cold and heat with heat. All the formulas' rules are not like this; hence "the contrary by contrary treatment".

## Notes

The answer: examine where yin and yang are, aim at balance, and treat regular illness regularly and contrary illness contrarily.
- 天寸 in Wang Bing's note may be an error for 尺寸 ("cubit and inch"), but this has not been verified.
