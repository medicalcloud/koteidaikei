# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §110 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Qi may be high or low, illness near or far, patterns internal or external, treatment light or heavy; the aim is to fit [the medicine] to reach its place.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "patterns internal or external, treatment light or heavy; the aim is to fit [the medicine] to reach its place"] The positions of the viscera are high or low, the qi of the bowels near or far, the patterns of illness exterior or interior, the use of medicines light or heavy. Adjust their amount and harmonize their tightness and slackness so that the qi of the medicine reaches the place of illness; this is the aim, with neither excess nor insufficiency.

## Notes

The beginning of the answer: a formula is set so that the qi of the medicine reaches the site of illness. Qibo's speech continues through unit 112.
