# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §169 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What a thorough question! It is because the categories of the five flavors are not treated. When the five flavors enter the stomach, each goes to what it likes. 攻 [Hence] sour enters the liver first, bitter the heart first, sweet the spleen first, acrid the lungs first, salty the kidneys first. That over time they increase the qi is the constancy of the transformation of things; that the qi is increased for a long time is the cause of early death."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "sweet the spleen first, acrid the lungs first, salty the kidneys first"] Song editors: According to the *Xuanming wuqi pian*, "Where the five flavors enter: sour enters the liver, acrid the lungs, bitter the heart, salty the kidneys, sweet the spleen. These are called the five entries."

[On "That over time they increase the qi ... is the cause of early death"] Entering the liver it becomes warm, the heart hot, the lungs cool, the kidneys cold; entering the spleen it becomes extreme yin, combining the four qi. In every case they increase the flavor and augment the qi; hence each follows the function of the qi of its own viscus. So taking Coptis and Sophora root for a long time and becoming hot instead is of this kind. The other flavors are all likewise, but people are careless and cannot observe precisely. Hence "that over time they increase the qi is the constancy of the transformation of things". When the qi increases without end over years, the qi of a viscus prevails one-sidedly; where there is one-sided prevalence, there is one-sided exhaustion; where a viscus is one-sidedly exhausted, there is sudden early death. Hence "that the qi is increased for a long time is the cause of early death". Thus the *Zhengli guanhua yaoji shangjiao fu'er* says, "If medicines lacking the five flavors and the four qi are taken for a long time, though one gains advantage for a while, over time one will surely die suddenly young"; this is what it means. Why do those who abstain from grain and take [medicinal] food not die suddenly? Because they lack the support of the flavors of the five grains. If they are made to eat grain again, they too die young.

## Notes

The answer that long increasing the qi of the viscera that the five flavors enter causes reversed effects and early death.
- 攻 in 攻酸先入肝 in the main text is presumably an error for 故 ("hence"); the translation reads 故.
- 正理観化薬集商較服餌 in Wang Bing's note seems to be a book title, but its exact extent has not been verified.
