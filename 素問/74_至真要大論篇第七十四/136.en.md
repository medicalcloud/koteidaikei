# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §136 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The *Essentials of the Pulse* says, 'Spring not sunken, summer not string-like, winter not rough, autumn not rapid: this is called the four blockages. Excessively sunken is called illness; excessively string-like, illness; excessively rough, illness; excessively rapid, illness; appearing mixed, illness; appearing again, illness; departing before it should, illness; not departing when it should, illness; contrary, death.'

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Spring not sunken ... this is called the four blockages"] The qi of heaven, earth and the four seasons are shut and blocked with nowhere to circulate.

[On "excessively string-like, illness; excessively rough, illness; excessively rapid, illness"] Qi that simply accords with heaven's harmony is balanced; when its form appears excessive, it is produced by force; how can what is produced by force last? Hence all excess is illness.

[On "departing before it should, illness; not departing when it should, illness"] 参 means appearing mixed with other qi; appearing again means qi that has already declined or died appearing a second time. Departing means departing when its flourishing is over. If the sun's course has not yet passed the deviation [but the pulse departs], heaven's qi has not yet left; if the sun's course has passed the deviation, heaven's qi has gone, and if the pulse is still there it is not a proper response; hence illness.

[On "not departing when it should, illness; contrary, death"] Sunken in summer, rapid in autumn, moderate in winter, rough in spring: this is called contrary. Offending against heaven's mandate, how can life last?
Song editors: On examination, the text above says "autumn not rapid: this is called the four blockages", while this note says "rapid in autumn is contrary". Presumably, since the deviation of the pulse is only within the middle month, if the deviation period is over and the rapid pulse does not leave, i.e., if the pulse is still rapid in the last month of autumn, it is contrary.

## Notes

Quoting the *Essentials of the Pulse* on balanced, ill and fatal pulses according to seasonal deviation.
