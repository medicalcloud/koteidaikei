# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §64 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the prevalence of greater yin, fire qi is pent up within, sores arise within and flow out and spread outside, the illness is in the flanks and rib-sides, and in severe cases there is heart pain, blocking heat, headache, throat impediment and stiff neck. When it prevails alone, damp qi is pent up within, cold presses on the lower burner, pain lingers at the vertex and pulls on the space between the eyebrows, and the stomach is full. Rain comes frequently and the transformation of dryness appears. The lower abdomen is full, the lower back and buttocks heavy and stiff, inner function impaired, with frequent pouring diarrhea, warmth under the feet, heavy head, puffy swelling of the feet and shins, fluid breaking out in the center, and puffy swelling above.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "heavy head, puffy swelling of the feet and shins, fluid breaking out in the center, and puffy swelling above"] The five *chou* and five *wei* years. When damp prevails above, fire qi is pent up within; when it prevails in the center, cold presses on the lower burner. When water overflows the rivers and canals, scaled creatures leave the water. 脽 means the flesh of the buttocks. Impaired function means the lower back is heavy and stiff within, with difficulty bending and stretching. Prevailing alone means not combined with pent-up fire. Puffy swelling above means the head and face. Swelling of the feet and shins arises from pent-up fire.
Song editors: On examination, the note says "when water overflows the rivers and canals, scaled creatures leave the water", but this note of Wang Bing explains nothing in the classic. Also, under the retaliation of greater yin it says "heavy rain falls at times and scaled creatures appear on land"; so in this text the four characters 鱗見於陸 ("scaled creatures appear on land") have dropped out after "rain comes frequently". Otherwise Wang Bing's note would have no basis.
