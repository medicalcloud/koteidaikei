# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §76 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the retaliation of greater yang, reversal qi travels upward, water congeals and rain turns to ice, and feathered creatures die. Cold arises in the heart and stomach, the chest and diaphragm are obstructed, there is heart pain with glomus and fullness, headache, frequent sorrow, occasional dizziness and falling, reduced eating, pain instead in the lower back and buttocks, and difficulty bending and stretching. The ground cracks, ice is hard, and the sunlight does not govern. The lower abdomen pulls on the testicles and draws on the lower back and spine, surging up to the heart; one spits clear water and has hiccup and belching; in severe cases it enters the heart, with forgetfulness and frequent sorrow. If Shenmen is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "one spits clear water and has hiccup and belching; in severe cases it enters the heart, with forgetfulness and frequent sorrow"] Rain ice means hail. That [feathered creatures] die when hail comes in the cold is only to be expected. Cold transforms in the earth and earth again [covers] it above, so the body of the earth splits and cracks, water accumulates, ice is hard and does not melt for long; this is the qi of sunlight failing to govern things congealed by cold. If the retaliation of greater yang and 不 [earth] do not hold each other off, there is damp above and cold below, fire has nowhere to go, heart qi is pent up within, heat arises from this, and fire heat burns within, hence these illnesses.
Song editors: On examination, 不 in 与不相持 in the note should perhaps be 土 ("earth").

[On "If Shenmen is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated"] Shenmen is the pulse qi of the true heart.

## Notes

The retaliation of greater yang. Qibo's speech ends here.
