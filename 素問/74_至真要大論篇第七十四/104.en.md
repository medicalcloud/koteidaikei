# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §104 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The qi differ in amount and thus in function."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The qi differ in amount and thus in function"] Greater yin is full yin, greater yang full yang; the next lesser is lesser yin, the next lesser is lesser yang; next again is *yangming*, and next again *jueyin*. *Jueyin* means exhaustion. The meaning is detailed in the *Lingshu*, *Xi riyue lun*.
Song editors: According to the *Tianyuan ji dalun*: "What is meant by qi having more and less?" Guiyuqu said, "The qi of yin and yang each have more and less; hence there are three yin and three yang."

## Notes

The answer that they are divided by the amount of qi.
