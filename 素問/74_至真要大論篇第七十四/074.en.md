# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §74 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the retaliation of lesser yang, great heat is about to arrive, there is withering, dryness and burning, and shelled creatures dwindle. There is fright and spasms, coughing and nosebleed, heat in the heart with vexation and agitation, frequent stool and urine, aversion to wind; reversal qi travels upward, the face looks as if covered with floating dust, and the eyes twitch; fire qi breaks out within, above causing mouth ulcers and retching counterflow, overflowing and discharge of blood; it breaks out as malaria with aversion to cold and drum-like shivering, and when the cold reaches its extreme there is heat instead; the throat's network vessels are scorched and withered, thirst draws for water and drinks, the complexion changes to yellow-red, there is shortage of qi and wilting of the vessels; it transforms into water and passes on to puffy swelling, and in severe cases enters the lungs, with coughing and bloody discharge. If Chize is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "it transforms into water and passes on to puffy swelling ... with coughing and bloody discharge"] Fire qi is exclusive and violent, withering and drying plants; burning, flames arise of themselves; hence "burning". 爇 is pronounced like 焫. Fire blazes within, hence fright and spasms, coughing and nosebleed, heat in the heart with vexation and agitation, frequent stool and urine, and aversion to wind. When fire flames above, all things lose their color, so the face looks as if covered with dust and the eyes twitch. When fire melts within, the mouth and tongue ulcerate, there is retching counterflow, and overflowing and discharge of blood. When wind and fire press on each other, there is warm malaria. When qi steams and heat transforms, there is water illness, which passes on to puffy swelling. 胕 means skin and flesh both swollen, sinking like mud when pressed and not springing back. Such patterns all arise from fire qi.

[On "If Chize is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated"] Chize is the pulse qi of the lungs.
