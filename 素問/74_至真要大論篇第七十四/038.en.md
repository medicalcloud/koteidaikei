# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence the saying 'One who knows the essentials ends in a single word; one who does not know the essentials scatters without end' means this."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "One who knows the essentials ... scatters without end' means this"] The essentials means knowing where yin and yang are. Knowing it, one uses it without confusion. Not knowing it, since the sinking, floating, smallness and size of the qi at the cubit and inch always shift once every three years, seeking their meaning is like circling a tree asking about its branches: though one toils until one's hair is white, one still does not know where one is going. How much less could one know it in ten days or a month?

## Notes

Concluding with the saying on the importance of knowing the essentials. Qibo's speech ends here.
