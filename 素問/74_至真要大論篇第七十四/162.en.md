# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §162 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. When fire heat retaliates, with aversion to cold and fever like malaria, sometimes breaking out daily and sometimes at intervals of several days, why is this?"

## Notes

A question about why malaria-like attacks occur at different intervals. There is no note by Wang Bing.
