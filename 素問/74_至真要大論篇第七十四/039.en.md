# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. What of illness when the qi of heaven and earth run rampant within?"

## Notes

A question about illness from rampancy of the qi in the spring. There is no note by Wang Bing.
