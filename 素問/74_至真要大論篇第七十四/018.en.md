# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "What of [the medicines] that govern illness?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of [the medicines] that govern illness"] This refers to the years for gathering medicines.

## Notes

A question about medicines that treat illness.
