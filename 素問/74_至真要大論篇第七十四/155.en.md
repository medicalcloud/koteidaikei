# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §155 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Going against is regular treatment; following is contrary treatment. Whether to follow little or much depends on the case."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Going against is regular treatment; following is contrary treatment. Whether to follow little or much depends on the case"] It says that going against is regular treatment and following is contrary treatment. Going against the illness qi with regular treatment means attacking heat with cold and cold with heat. Complying with the illness qi is the method of contrary treatment. Following little means one alike and two different; following much means two alike and three different. Saying all are alike is the odd rule.

## Notes

The answer that going against is regular treatment and following is contrary treatment.
- The last sentence of Wang Bing's note (言尽同者是奇制也) is hard to construe; the translation is tentative (not verified).
