# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Name it by what [the viscus] cannot overcome; that is the essential."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Name it by what [the viscus] cannot overcome; that is the essential"] Such as wood not overcoming metal, metal not overcoming fire.

## Notes

The answer that harm is named by the qi the viscus cannot overcome.
