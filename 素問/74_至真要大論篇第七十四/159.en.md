# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §159 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Go against it, follow it, go against it and then follow, follow it and then go against; free the qi and make it regulated. That is the way."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Go against it, follow it ... free the qi and make it regulated. That is the way"] Going against means treating regularly against the illness qi; following means treating contrarily in accord with the illness qi. Going against its qi with regular treatment makes it compliant; following its illness and taking it contrarily makes it harmonious. Hence "going against and following". If one does not free the qi and open its roads, the qi responds to cold and heat and becomes change, and transformations begin to multiply.

## Notes

The answer: free the qi by going against, following, and combinations of these.
