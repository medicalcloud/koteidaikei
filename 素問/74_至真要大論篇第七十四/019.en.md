# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Prepare things according to [the qi] controlling the year, and nothing is left out of what governs [illness]."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Prepare things according to [the qi] controlling the year, and nothing is left out"] If one carefully watches what is generated and transformed by the controllers of heaven and earth, their flavors flourish in that very year. Hence if the pharmacist gathers medicines solely according to the qi controlling the year, then for one or two years nothing is omitted from what they govern and are used for. Now on examination, the character 前 should be 則.

## Notes

The answer: prepare medicines according to the qi controlling the year.
- 今詳前字当作則 at the end of Wang Bing's note resembles the Song editors' phrasing, but in both the base text and the Siku quanshu edition (Daizhige *Huangdi neijing suwen*) it stands within Wang Bing's note. It presumably means that some edition has 前 for 則 in the main text, but this has not been verified. The Siku quanshu edition has 其味正當其歲 for 其味王當其歲 in the note.
