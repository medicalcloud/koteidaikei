# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §139 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The two yin meeting and coming to an end is called dark; the two yang combining their brightness is called bright. The arrangement of dark and bright differs from that of cold and heat."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The two yin meeting and coming to an end is called dark ... differs from that of cold and heat"] The two yin meet and come to an end at *xu* and *hai*; the two yang combine their brightness at *chen* and *si*. The *Lingshu*, *Xi riyue lun*, says: "*Hai* is the tenth month, *jueyin* of the left foot; *xu* is the ninth month, *jueyin* of the right foot; here the two yin meet and come to an end, hence *jueyin*. *Chen* is the third month, *yangming* of the left foot; 已 [*si*] is the fourth month, *yangming* of the right foot; here the two yang combine in front, hence *yangming*." When yin meets there is darkness; when yang combines there is brightness; the images of dark and bright should come from this. The positions of cold and heat are southwest and northeast; the positions of dark and bright are northwest and southeast. The arrangement of dark and bright and the positions of cold and heat truly differ thus.
Song editors: According to the text of the *Taishi tianyuan ce*, "When dark and bright are in position, cold and heat slacken and tighten."

## Notes

The answer that dark corresponds to *jueyin* and bright to *yangming*, and that their directions differ from those of cold and heat.
