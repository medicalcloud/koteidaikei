# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §167 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "It is because one treats the flourishing qi; hence the contrary result."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is because one treats the flourishing qi; hence the contrary result"] Things have cold and heat in their substance, and qi has yin and yang in its nature. Touching the flourishing qi strengthens its function. Liver qi is warm and mild, heart qi hot, lung qi clear and cool, kidney qi cold and severe; spleen qi combines them. Treating the liver with coolness in spring and getting warmth instead, the heart with cold in summer and getting heat instead, the lungs with warmth in autumn and getting coolness instead, the kidneys with heat in winter and getting cold instead, is presumably because the flourishing qi is supplemented too much. When the flourishing is supplemented too much, the cold or heat qi of the viscus naturally increases.

## Notes

The answer that treating the flourishing qi of a viscus reverses the effect.
