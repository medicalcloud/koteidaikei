# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §111 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The *Great Essentials* says, 'One sovereign and two ministers is the odd rule; two sovereigns and four ministers is the even rule; two sovereigns and three ministers is the odd rule; two sovereigns and six ministers is the even rule.'

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "two sovereigns and three ministers is the odd rule; two sovereigns and six ministers is the even rule"] Odd means the single formulas of old; even means the compound formulas of old. Each rule, single or compound, has small and large; hence for odd formulas it says one sovereign and two ministers, two sovereigns and three ministers; for even formulas, two sovereigns and four ministers, two sovereigns and six ministers. Illnesses are small or large, qi near or far, treatment light or heavy, each with what suits it; hence "rule".

## Notes

A quotation on the numbers of sovereign and minister drugs in odd and even formulas.
