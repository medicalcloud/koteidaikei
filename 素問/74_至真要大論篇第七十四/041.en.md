# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In years when lesser yin is in the spring and heat runs rampant and prevails, flames float over rivers and marshes, and shady places become bright instead. People suffer constant rumbling in the abdomen, qi rising to strike the chest, panting so that they cannot stand long, cold and heat with skin pain, dim vision, toothache, swelling of the cheekbones, aversion to cold and fever like malaria, pain in the lower abdomen, and enlarged abdomen. Hibernating creatures do not hide.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "aversion to cold and fever like malaria, pain in the lower abdomen, and enlarged abdomen. Hibernating creatures do not hide"] This refers to the *yimao*, *dingmao*, *jimao*, *xinmao*, *guimao*, *yiyou*, *dingyou*, *jiyou*, *xinyou* and *guiyou* years. Shady places are the north. Being unable to stand long means the feet lack strength. Enlarged abdomen means insufficiency of heart qi. Metal and fire 簿 [press] against each other, causing this.
Song editors: According to the *Jiayi jing*, "toothache, swelling of the cheekbones" is illness of the large intestine; "thunderous rumbling in the abdomen, qi constantly striking the chest, panting so that they cannot stand long" is pathogen in the large intestine. Presumably in years when lesser yin is in the spring, fire overcomes metal, so the large intestine falls ill.

## Notes

The case of lesser yin in the spring (*mao* and *you* years).
