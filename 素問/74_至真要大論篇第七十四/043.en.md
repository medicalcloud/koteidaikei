# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §43 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In years when lesser yang is in the spring and fire runs rampant and prevails, flames brighten the countryside, and cold and heat arrive in turn. People suffer pouring diarrhea, red and white, lower abdominal pain, red urine, and in severe cases bloody stool. The signs are the same as for lesser yin.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "lower abdominal pain, red urine, and in severe cases bloody stool. The signs are the same as for lesser yin"] This refers to the *yisi*, *dingsi*, *jisi*, *xinsi*, *guisi*, *yihai*, *dinghai*, *jihai*, *xinhai* and *guihai* years. In the cold season heat replaces its qi; when the heat qi has gone, the cold qi comes after; hence "arrive in turn". The other signs are exactly the same as for lesser yin in the spring.

## Notes

The case of lesser yang in the spring (*si* and *hai* years).
