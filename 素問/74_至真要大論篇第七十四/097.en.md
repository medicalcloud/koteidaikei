# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §97 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When *jueyin* is in the spring, if the guest prevails the large joints are stiff, internally there is tetany, rigidity and spasms, externally impaired movement; if the host prevails, the sinews and bones shake together and the lower back and abdomen hurt at times. When lesser yin is in the spring, if the guest prevails there is lumbar pain, illness of the buttocks, thighs, knees, upper legs, calves, shins and feet, oppressive heat with aching, puffy swelling with inability to stand long, and change in urine and stool; if the host prevails, reversal qi travels upward, with heart pain and fever, all kinds of impediments arising in the diaphragm and breaking out in the flanks and rib-sides, spontaneous sweating that will not stop, and cold limbs arising. When greater yin is in the spring, if the guest prevails the feet are limp and the lower body heavy, stool and urine are irregular, damp lodges in the lower burner and breaks out as soggy diarrhea, or as swelling and diseases of the private parts; if the host prevails, cold qi counterflows with fullness, food and drink do not go down, and in severe cases there is hernia. When lesser yang is in the spring, if the guest prevails there is pain of the lower back and abdomen with aversion to cold instead, and in severe cases white discharge and white urine; if the host prevails, heat instead travels upward and lodges in the heart, with heart pain, fever, and blockage in the center with retching. Lesser yin has the same signs. When *yangming* is in the spring, if the guest prevails, cool qi stirs below, the lower abdomen is hard and full with frequent diarrhea; if the host prevails, the lower back is heavy and the abdomen hurts, cold arises in the lower abdomen, below there is duck-like sloppy stool, cold reverses in the intestines and surges up into the chest, and in severe cases panting with inability to stand long. When greater yang is in the spring, if cold returns and is in excess within, there is pain of the lower back and buttocks, difficulty bending and stretching, and pain in the thighs, shins, feet and knees."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "if the host prevails, the sinews and bones shake together and the lower back and abdomen hurt at times"] The five *yin* and five *shen* years. The large joints are the lower back and knees.

[On "breaking out in the flanks and rib-sides, spontaneous sweating that will not stop, and cold limbs arising"] The five *mao* and five *you* years.

[On "if the host prevails, cold qi counterflows with fullness, food and drink do not go down, and in severe cases there is hernia"] The five *chen* and five *xu* years. Diseases of the private parts means illness of the hidden, concealed and winding places.

[On "with heart pain, fever, and blockage in the center with retching. Lesser yin has the same signs"] The five *si* and five *hai* years.

[On "cold reverses in the intestines and surges up into the chest, and in severe cases panting with inability to stand long"] The five *zi* and five *wu* years. 鶩 means duck; it means like a duck's droppings.

[On "pain of the lower back and buttocks, difficulty bending and stretching, and pain in the thighs, shins, feet and knees"] The five *chou* and five *wei* years.
Song editors: On examination, it does not speak of guest or host prevailing here presumably because greater yang, as water, dwells in the position of water.

## Notes

The answer giving the illnesses when the guest or host qi prevails in the spring; only for greater yang in the spring are guest and host prevalence not distinguished. Qibo's speech ends here.
