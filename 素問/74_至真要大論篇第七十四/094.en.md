# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §94 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The host prevailing is contrary; the guest prevailing is compliant. This is the way of heaven."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The host prevailing is contrary; the guest prevailing is compliant. This is the way of heaven"] The guest receives heaven's mandate and governs its quarter section by section; the host is below it and should reverently serve heaven's mandate. If it prevails without complying, heaven's mandate is not carried out, hence contrary. The guest prevailing over the host is the principle of acting by heaven, hence compliant.

## Notes

The answer that the host's prevalence is contrary and the guest's compliant.
