# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In years when greater yin is in the spring, grasses flourish early. When damp runs rampant and prevails, dust darkens the cliffs and valleys, yellow appears instead in the black [quarter], and the extreme yin meets. People suffer accumulated fluid with heart pain, deafness with muddled confusion, swollen throat and throat impediment, yin illness with bleeding, pain and swelling of the lower abdomen with inability to urinate, and surging headache; the eyes feel as if they would fall out, the nape as if pulled out, the lower back as if broken; the thighs cannot turn, the backs of the knees feel tied, and the calves as if split.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In years when greater yin is in the spring, grasses flourish early"] Song editors: On examination, these four characters [grasses flourish early] are perhaps an interpolation.

[On "the thighs cannot turn, the backs of the knees feel tied, and the calves as if split"] This refers to the *jiachen*, *bingchen*, *wuchen*, *gengchen*, *renchen*, *jiaxu*, *bingxu*, *wuxu*, *gengxu* and *renxu* years. Greater yin is earth; its color should appear in correspondence with the yellow of heaven's center, but instead appears in the black place of the north. Water and earth appear together, hence "the extreme yin meets", combining their colors. Surging headache means pain behind the brain and between the eyebrows. 膕 is the middle of the bend behind the knee. 腨 is the soft flesh behind the shin.
Song editors: According to the *Jiayi jing*, "deafness with muddled confusion, swollen throat and throat impediment" is illness of the triple burner; "surging headache; the eyes as if they would fall out, the nape as if pulled out, the lower back as if broken; the thighs cannot turn, the backs of the knees feel tied, and the calves as if 列 [split]" is illness of the bladder, foot greater yang; also "pain and swelling of the lower abdomen with inability to urinate" is pathogen in the triple burner. Presumably in years when greater yin is in the spring, earth directly overcomes greater yang, hence such illnesses.

## Notes

The case of greater yin in the spring (*chen* and *xu* years).
