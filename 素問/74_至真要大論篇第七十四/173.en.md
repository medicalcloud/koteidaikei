# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §173 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "They serve to distinguish the different threads of good and bad."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "They serve to distinguish the different threads of good and bad"] The three grades are upper, middle and lower grade; this clarifies that medicines differ in nature and function according to their goodness and badness.
Song editors: According to the *Shennong*, "Upper-grade medicines are sovereigns, govern the nourishing of life, and correspond to heaven; middle-grade medicines are ministers, nourish the nature, and correspond to humans; lower-grade medicines are assistants and envoys, govern the treatment of illness, and correspond to earth."

## Notes

The answer that the three grades distinguish good and bad medicines.
