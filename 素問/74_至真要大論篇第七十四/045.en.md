# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §45 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In years when greater yang is in the spring and cold runs rampant and prevails, there is congealing austerity and bleak shivering. People suffer the lower abdomen pulling on the testicles and drawing on the lower back and spine, surging up to the heart with pain, bleeding, sore throat and swollen jaw."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "surging up to the heart with pain, bleeding, sore throat and swollen jaw"] This refers to the *yichou*, *dingchou*, *jichou*, *xinchou*, *guichou*, *yiwei*, *dingwei*, *jiwei*, *xinwei* and *guiwei* years. Congealing austerity means cold qi hangs in the air, congealed and unmoving, and all things are still and austere in form. Bleak shivering means severe cold. 控 means pull. 睪 is the testicle. 頷 is in front of the jaw-hinge, below the molars.
Song editors: According to the *Jiayi jing*, "sore throat and swollen jaw" is illness of the small intestine; also "the lower abdomen pulling on the testicles and drawing on the lower back and spine, surging up to the heart and lungs" is pathogen in the small intestine. Presumably in years when greater yang is in the spring, water overcomes fire, hence such illnesses.

## Notes

The case of greater yang in the spring (*chou* and *wei* years). Qibo's speech ends here.
