# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §125 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "When the yin [illnesses] are contrary, what is the pulse like?"

## Notes

A question about the pulse when yin illness is contrary. There is no note by Wang Bing.
