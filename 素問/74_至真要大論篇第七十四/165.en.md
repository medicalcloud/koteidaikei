# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §165 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In all cases where cooling leaves heat, take it from yin; where heating leaves cold, take it from yang. This is what is called seeking what it belongs to."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In all cases where cooling leaves heat, take it from yin ... seeking what it belongs to"] It means increasing the source of fire to dispel the shade of yin, and strengthening the 生 [master] of water to restrain the light of yang; hence "seeking what it belongs to". Crude practitioners have shallow learning, not yet refined or deep. They attack cold with heat and treat heat with cold. Before the heat is cured, cold disease has already arisen; the deeper they attack the cold each day, the more heat illness arises again. Heat arises while internal cold remains; cold arises while external heat is not removed. Wanting to attack the cold, they fear the heat and do not advance; wanting to treat the heat, they think of the cold and stop again. Advancing and retreating battle each other, and danger is upon them. How would they know that the source of the viscera and bowels has a master of cold, heat, warmth and coolness? Taking from the heart need not be regulated with heat; taking from the kidneys need not be regulated with cold. Simply increase the yang of the heart and cold too will flow and move; strengthen the yin of the kidneys and even heating it will show results. Seeing the reason for this, one may treat heat with heat and cold with cold, succeeding ten thousand times in ten thousand. Who knows this meaning? Thinking of formulas exhausts wisdom; principle is exhausted and words fail. Alas, when people die, why call it fate rather than say that foolish practitioners killed them?

## Notes

The answer: where cooling leaves heat, supplement yin (kidney water); where heating leaves cold, supplement yang (heart fire).
- 壮水之生 in Wang Bing's note is often quoted in later times as 壮水之主 (生 may be an error for 主), but this has not been verified. The reading of 𥚹 in 粗工𥚹浅 is uncertain.
