# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked, "That the five qi combine and fullness and emptiness arise in turn, I know. When the six qi divide the governance, how do those controlling heaven and earth arrive?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "That the five qi combine ... how do those controlling heaven and earth arrive"] The five phases govern the years, and years have less or more; hence "fullness and emptiness arise in turn". The *Tianyuan ji dalun* says, "At the start, when surplus goes, insufficiency follows it; when insufficiency goes, surplus follows it"; this is the meaning. Heaven divides the six qi, which are scattered and born in the great void; the third qi controls heaven and the final qi oversees the earth. The generation and transformation of heaven and earth is the great record. Hence it speaks of "those controlling heaven and earth"; the remaining four can be known [from them].

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter: a question about the arrival of the six qi controlling heaven and earth.
