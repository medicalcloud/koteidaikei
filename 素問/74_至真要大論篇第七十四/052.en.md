# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §52 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When lesser yang controls heaven and fire runs rampant and prevails, warm qi circulates and metal's governance is not balanced. People suffer headache, fever and aversion to cold turning into malaria, heat rising with skin pain, color changing to yellow-red, transmission into water [swelling] with puffy swelling of the body and face, abdominal fullness with breathing face-up, pouring diarrhea red and white, sores, coughing and spitting blood, vexed heart, heat in the chest, and in severe cases stuffy nose and nosebleed. The illness is rooted in the lungs. If Tianfu is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "vexed heart, heat in the chest, and in severe cases stuffy nose and nosebleed. The illness is rooted in the lungs"] This refers to the *jiayin*, *bingyin*, *wuyin*, *gengyin*, *renyin*, *jiashen*, *bingshen*, *wushen*, *gengshen* and *renshen* years. When fire comes into play, metal qi receives the pathogen; hence "metal's governance is not balanced". Fire blazes above, the metal lungs receive the pathogen, guest heat burns within, and water cannot rescue it, hence the various illnesses arise. Restrain the fire guest and it 巳 [ends].
Song editors: According to the *Jiayi jing*, "when the pathogen is in the lungs, the skin is painful and there is cold and heat". Presumably in years when lesser yang controls heaven, fire overcomes metal, hence such illnesses.

[On "The illness is rooted in the lungs. If Tianfu is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated"] Tianfu is behind the elbow, above on the side of the 彼 [arm], three inches below the armpit, where the pulsing vessel meets the hand; it is the qi of the lungs. Fire prevails and the metal pulse is cut off, hence death.

## Notes

The case of lesser yang controlling heaven (*yin* and *shen* years).
