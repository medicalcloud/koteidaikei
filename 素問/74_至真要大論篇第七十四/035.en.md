# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In years of northern governance, when lesser yin is in the spring, the inch opening does not respond; when *jueyin* is in the spring, the right does not respond; when greater yin is in the spring, the left does not respond. In years of southern governance, when lesser yin controls heaven, the inch opening does not respond; when *jueyin* controls heaven, the right does not respond; when greater yin controls heaven, the left does not respond. In all cases of non-response, reverse the diagnosis and it will be seen."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In years of northern governance, when lesser yin is in the spring, the inch opening does not respond"] The wood, fire, metal and water movements face north to receive qi. Whatever qi is in the spring, its pulse is not seen at all; only the pulses of the qi to its left and right can be seen. When the qi in the spring is good, it is not seen; when bad, it is seen. Illness is named by the qi and the rampant prevalence of guest and host. The qi in heaven is likewise.

[On "when *jueyin* is in the spring, the right does not respond"] Because lesser yin is on the right.

[On "when greater yin is in the spring, the left does not respond"] Because lesser yin is on the left.

[On "In years of southern governance, when lesser yin controls heaven, the inch opening does not respond"] In earth-movement years [the movement] faces south to issue decrees; hence when lesser yin controls heaven, the inch openings of both hands do not respond.

[On "when *jueyin* controls heaven, the right does not respond; when greater yin controls heaven, the left does not respond"] Again the meaning of left and right.

[On "In all cases of non-response, reverse the diagnosis and it will be seen"] Non-response always means the pulse is sunken. When the pulse is sunken and low, with the hand palm-up it is sunken; turn the hand over, and sunken becomes floating, thin becomes large.

## Notes

The answer that the location of the non-responding inch pulse depends on southern governance (earth-movement years) or northern governance (other years) and the position of lesser yin.
