# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §66 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the prevalence of *yangming*, coolness arises within, the left flank and rib-side hurt, there is sloppy diarrhea, internally the throat is blocked, externally bulging hernia breaks out. Great coolness brings austere killing, flowers change their appearance, and hairy creatures suffer calamity. The chest is uncomfortable, the throat blocked, and there is coughing.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "hairy creatures suffer calamity. The chest is uncomfortable, the throat blocked, and there is coughing"] The five *mao* and five *you* years. Great coolness brings austere killing and metal qi overcomes wood, so the flowers of plants are damaged and pared by killing qi, change their form, and are scorched at their tips. Hairy creatures are the transformation of wood and their qi does not suit metal, so when metal's governance prevails hairy creatures die and dwindle. The qi of liver wood governs the genital region below, so when great coolness prevails, bulging hernia breaks out. The chest being uncomfortable means that in breathing and turning there is pain or tension and it is not easy. The qi is too abundant, hence the throat is blocked and there is coughing. 嗌 is below the throat, connecting with the chest, between the two lobes of the lungs.

## Notes

The prevalence of *yangming*.
