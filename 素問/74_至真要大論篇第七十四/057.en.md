# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §57 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. When pathogenic qi prevails instead, how is it treated?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When pathogenic qi prevails instead, how is it treated"] [The year's qi] cannot run rampant over other qi; instead, the qi it cannot overcome becomes the pathogen and prevails over it.

## Notes

A question about treatment when the qi in the spring is itself overcome.
