# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §141 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The arrival of qi is called the solstice; the division of qi is called the equinox. At the solstices the qi are the same; at the equinoxes the qi differ. This is what is called the proper record of heaven and earth."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "At the solstices the qi are the same; at the equinoxes the qi differ. This is what is called the proper record of heaven and earth"] Prompted by the question about dark and bright, he gives form to this meaning. The two solstices of winter and summer mean that the qi of heaven and earth governing the year arrive where they reside. The two equinoxes of spring and autumn are where the four intervening qi, the first, second, fourth and fifth, each divide their governance to the left and right of the year's governors. Hence "at the solstices the qi are the same; at the equinoxes the qi differ". Speaking of the arrangement of the qi of the solstices and equinoxes is because this is the so-called proper record of the qi of heaven and earth.

## Notes

The answer that the solstices are the arrival of the qi controlling heaven and earth, and the equinoxes the division of governance among the intervening qi.
