# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §128 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "For the prevailing: when prevalence arrives there is already 巳 illness; when the illness is already 巳 smoldering, retaliation is already 巳 sprouting. For the retaliating: it rises when the prevalence is exhausted and becomes severe when it gains its position. Prevalence may be slight or severe, retaliation less or more; if prevalence is mild, [retaliation] is mild; if prevalence is depleted, [retaliation] is depleted. This is the constancy of heaven."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "when prevalence arrives there is already illness; when the illness is already smoldering, retaliation is already sprouting"] The resentment of the retaliating heart is never far away.

## Notes

The answer that when prevalence arrives illness results and retaliation already sprouts, and that retaliation matches the degree of prevalence.
- 巳 in the main text is read as 已 ("already") in all three places.
