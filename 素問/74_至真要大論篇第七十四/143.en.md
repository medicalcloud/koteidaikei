# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §143 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "For what the upper and lower govern, follow what benefits them and set their flavors right; this is the essential. Left and right follow the same rule. The *Great Essentials* says, 'When lesser yang governs, first sweet, then salty; when *yangming* governs, first acrid, then sour; when greater yang governs, first salty, then bitter; when *jueyin* governs, first sour, then acrid; when lesser yin governs, first sweet, then salty; when greater yin governs, first bitter, then sweet. Assist with what benefits, support with what generates; this is called obtaining the qi.'"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Assist with what benefits, support with what generates; this is called obtaining the qi"] 主 means governing the year; 得 means obtaining its nature and function. When one obtains its nature and function, extending or contracting is up to the person; if one does not, any action produces opposition. How then can one hope to expel pathogens? It would only serve to assault the wondrous qi of heavenly truth. These flavors, first and after, all mean that when there is illness one first drains and then supplements.

## Notes

The answer giving, for each of the six governing qi, the flavor to drain first and the flavor to supplement after.
