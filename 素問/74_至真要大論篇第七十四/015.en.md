# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "That when *jueyin* is in the spring the transformation of sour comes first, I know. How does the transformation of wind proceed?"

## Notes

A question about how wind's transformation proceeds when *jueyin* is in the spring. There is no note by Wang Bing.
