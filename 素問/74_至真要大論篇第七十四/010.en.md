# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Those controlling left and right are called the intervening qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Those controlling left and right are called the intervening qi"] In the division of transformation among the six qi, two qi always control heaven and earth as upper and lower; matters of good and ill fortune, prevalence and retaliation, guest and host, and the regrets and troubles within the year are clarified by them. The other four qi are scattered left and right. Hence the *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* says, "Heaven and earth are the above and below of the myriad things; left and right are the roads of yin and yang"; this is what it means.

## Notes

The answer that the intervening qi are those controlling left and right.
