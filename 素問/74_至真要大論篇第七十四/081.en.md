# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §81 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Do the movements of prevalence and retaliation have constant times? Is the qi certain?"

## Notes

A question about the timing and certainty of prevalence and retaliation. There is no note by Wang Bing.
