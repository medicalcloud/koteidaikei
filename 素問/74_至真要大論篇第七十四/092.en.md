# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §92 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The qi of guest and host prevail but do not retaliate."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The qi of guest and host prevail but do not retaliate"] Guest and host have their own more and less, and their prevalence differs from ordinary prevalence.

## Notes

The answer that between guest and host there is prevalence but no retaliation.
