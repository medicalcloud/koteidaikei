# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §166 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. Why does taking cold [medicine] produce heat instead, and taking hot [medicine] produce cold instead?"

## Notes

A question about why medicines produce effects opposite to their cold or heat. There is no note by Wang Bing.
