# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Look at whether the year is southern or northern, and it can be known."

## Notes

The answer that it is known from whether the year is of southern or northern governance. There is no note by Wang Bing.
