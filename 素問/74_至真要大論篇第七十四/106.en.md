# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §106 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "It is the two yang combining their brightness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is the two yang combining their brightness"] The *Lingshu*, *Xi riyue lun*, says: "*Chen* is the third month and governs the *yangming* of the left foot; 已 [*si*] is the fourth month and governs the *yangming* of the right foot. The two yang combine in front, hence *yangming*."

## Notes

The answer that *yangming* is the combining of two yang.
