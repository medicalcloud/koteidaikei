# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In years of northern governance, when the three yin are below, the inch does not respond; when the three yin are above, the cubit does not respond. In years of southern governance, when the three yin are in heaven, the inch does not respond; when the three yin are in the spring, the cubit does not respond. Left and right are the same.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In years of northern governance, when the three yin are below, the inch does not respond; when the three yin are above, the cubit does not respond"] Controlling heaven is called above; in the spring is called below.

[On "when the three yin are in the spring, the cubit does not respond. Left and right are the same"] The 天 [cubit] not responding, and left and right, all have the same meaning as the inch not responding.

## Notes

The answer on when the cubit pulse does not respond. Qibo's speech continues into the next unit.
- 天 in 天不応 in Wang Bing's note may be an error for 尺 ("cubit"), judging from the main text, but this has not been verified.
