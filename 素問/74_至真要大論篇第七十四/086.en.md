# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §86 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When prevalence arrives there is retaliation; there is no fixed number; it stops only when [the qi] declines. When retaliation ends and there is prevalence again, if there is no retaliation, there is harm; this injures life."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When prevalence arrives there is retaliation; there is no fixed number; it stops only when [the qi] declines"] When prevalence is slight, retaliation is slight; hence after retaliation ends there is prevalence again. When prevalence is severe, retaliation is severe; hence after retaliation ends, prevalence seldom recurs. Even if there is prevalence, it is retaliated against according to its slightness or severity. Though the way of prevalence and retaliation has no fixed number, when they decline and fade, both stop of themselves.

[On "When retaliation ends and there is prevalence again, if there is no retaliation, there is harm; this injures life"] Prevalence without retaliation means the retaliating qi has declined; being too weak to retaliate means the heavenly true qi has been severely injured and the will to live is exhausted.

## Notes

The answer that prevalence and retaliation have no fixed number, and failure to retaliate injures life.
