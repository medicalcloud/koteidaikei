# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §157 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Use heat [medicine] by way of cold; use cold [medicine] by way of heat; use blocking for blockage; use freeing for free flow. One must conceal what is primary and put first what [the illness] relies on. At the start they are alike; in the end they differ. Thus one can break accumulations, burst hardness, harmonize the qi, and surely end [the illness]."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Thus one can break accumulations, burst hardness, harmonize the qi, and surely end [the illness]"] When great cold knots within and accumulates into hernial conglomerations, it is attacked and removed with heat. In trying to remove the cold, [the cold] blocks out the heat and instead runs unchecked, and when it does, the attacks of pain are especially severe. If one attacks it, the heat [medicine] [the base text has a blank here] cannot advance. So the formula uses aconite decocted in honey, assisted with hot honey, increasing the medicine; once taken, [the illness] disperses. This is how Master Zhang, following this, used heat by way of cold. When fire qi stirs and, after taking cold things past the heat, it is blocked by cold, the body is cold, there is retching and hiccup, a dry throat and bitter mouth, aversion to heat and liking for cold. Everyone agrees in calling it heat; treating with cold makes it worse. What then? Going against its liking makes it reject treatment; following its wishes adds to the illness. If one regulates cold and heat, goes against coolness and insists on heat, one gives hot things cold. Once past the throat, when the cold body has dispersed, the hot nature takes effect; thereby the illness qi is cured and the retching and hiccup are removed. Without going against the patient's feelings, great benefit results. Drinking pure wine cold is of this kind. This is using heat by way of cold. What is called aversion to heat: all the residual qi of foods governing life [meaning unclear].
Song editors: On examination, 王 is perhaps an error; what appears above is already vomiting [meaning unclear].
Again, when a patient with heat illness is attacked with cold and it does not go in, it is because he dislikes the cold. If it overcame the heat, [the heat] would be removed; but following its qi increases the heat, and attacking with cold does not go in. Steep fermented soybeans, beans and other cold medicines in wine, or warm them, and have him take them; the heat of the wine is the same as his qi, so there is no opposition. When the heat of the wine is spent, the cold medicine acts, and following the taking, the heat at once disperses. This is using cold by way of heat. Or mixing various cold things with hot preparations to be taken and eaten, so that the heat 囲 [thereby] resolves, is also using cold by way of heat. Also seasoning hot foods such as pork, starch, mallow and milk with hot preparations of pepper, ginger and tangerine peel is of this kind. When heat is in the lower burner the treatment is the same. For example, when the lower qi is depleted and the middle-burner qi congested, the flanks and rib-sides very full and worse after eating, a crude practitioner cannot decide. If he would disperse the fullness, he fears depleting the lower; if he supplements the lower, the fullness in the middle worsens. Dispersing qi further depletes the lower burner; supplementing the depletion further increases the central fullness. Doctor and patient confer and agree in word and intent; not rescuing the depletion, they first attack the fullness. When the medicine goes in, it lessens; when the medicine passes, it is as before. So the illness of central fullness and lower depletion persists. They do not know to open up the middle and strongly supplement the lower. Taken in small amounts it aids the congestion; taken in large amounts it opens and frees. Treating thus, the central fullness clears of itself and the lower depletion becomes replete. This is using blocking for blockage. Again, when great heat knots within with incessant pouring diarrhea, heat should be treated with cold and the knot too must be removed; purging it with cold, the knot disperses and the diarrhea stops. This is using freeing for free flow. Again, when great heat congeals within and long-standing diarrhea and sloppy stool cure and recur, dragging on for years, purging with heat removes the cold and stops the diarrhea; this too is of this kind. Giving cold [medicine] with heat [cooled] so that it goes; giving heat [medicine] with cold [warmed] so that it goes; "alike at the start, different in the end" means this. Such cases are truly numerous; I have roughly given the main signs; this is the way of contrary treatment, and these are its kinds.
Song editors: According to the *Wuchang zheng dalun*, "Treat heat with cold [medicine] taken warm; treat cold with heat [medicine] taken cool"; this too is the meaning of using heat by way of cold and cold by way of heat.

## Notes

The answer explaining contrary treatment (heat by way of cold, cold by way of heat, blocking for blockage, freeing for free flow).
- This note of Wang Bing contains a blank (characters lost in the base text's transcription) and a Song editors' note inserted mid-way, and parts are hard to construe; the translation is tentative (not verified). 熱 in 大熱凝内 should perhaps be 寒 ("cold") from the context, but this has not been verified.
