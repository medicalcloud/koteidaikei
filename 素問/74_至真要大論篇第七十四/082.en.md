# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §82 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The times have constant positions, but the qi is not certain."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The times have constant positions, but the qi is not certain"] Though the positions are constant, whether [the qi] breaks out is not necessarily fixed.

## Notes

The answer that the positions of the times are fixed, but whether prevalence or retaliation occurs is not. The base text writes 岐伯 only in units 82–84.
