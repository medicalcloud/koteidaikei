# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §70 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "What of the retaliations of the six qi?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of the retaliations of the six qi"] Retaliation means requiting, requiting the prevalence. Whenever there is first prevalence, there must later be retaliation.
Song editors: According to the *Xuanzhu*: "The six qi are divided into proper transformation and opposite transformation. *Jueyin* properly controls at *hai* and transforms oppositely at *si*; lesser yin properly controls at *wu* and oppositely at *zi*; greater yin properly at *wei* and oppositely at *chou*; lesser yang properly at *yin* and oppositely at *shen*; *yangming* properly at *you* and oppositely at *mao*; greater yang properly at *xu* and oppositely at *chen*. In proper control the transforming decree is replete; in opposite control it is depleted. When opposite transformation prevails there is retaliation; when proper transformation prevails there is none." So this note's "whenever there is first prevalence, there must later be retaliation" seems not to be so.

## Notes

A question about the retaliations of the six qi.
