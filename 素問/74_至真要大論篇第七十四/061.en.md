# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §61 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "What of the six qi prevailing over one another?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of the six qi prevailing over one another"] First their functioning is raised as prevalence.

## Notes

A question about the prevalence of the six qi.
