# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §101 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "For the host in the position of wood, drain with sour and supplement with acrid. For the host in the position of fire, drain with sweet and supplement with salty. For the host in the position of earth, drain with bitter and supplement with sweet. For the host in the position of metal, drain with acrid and supplement with sour. For the host in the position of water, drain with salty and supplement with bitter.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "For the host in the position of wood, drain with sour and supplement with acrid"] The position of wood is the sixty-one days before the spring equinox, the first qi.

[On "For the host in the position of fire, drain with sweet and supplement with salty"] The position of sovereign fire is the sixty-one days after the spring equinox, the second qi; the position of ministerial fire is thirty days on either side of the summer solstice, the third qi. The qi of the two fires differ, but their functioning is one.

[On "For the host in the position of earth, drain with bitter and supplement with sweet"] The position of earth is the sixty-one days before the autumn equinox, the fourth qi.

[On "For the host in the position of metal, drain with acrid and supplement with sour"] The position of metal is the sixty-one days after the autumn equinox, the fifth qi.

[On "For the host in the position of water, drain with salty and supplement with bitter"] The position of water is thirty days on either side of the winter solstice, the final qi.

## Notes

The answer giving the flavors for draining and supplementing the host qi (the positions of the five phases). Qibo's speech continues into the next unit.
