# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §116 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "By taking advantage of their arrival. When clear qi comes greatly, dryness prevails; wind-wood receives the pathogen and liver illness arises. When heat qi comes greatly, fire prevails; metal-dryness receives the pathogen and lung illness arises. When cold qi comes greatly, water prevails; fire-heat receives the pathogen and heart illness arises. When damp qi comes greatly, earth prevails; cold-water receives the pathogen and kidney illness arises. When wind qi comes greatly, wood prevails; earth-damp receives the pathogen and spleen illness arises. This is what is called contracting pathogens and producing illness. Taking advantage of the year's depletion, the pathogen is severe; losing the harmony of the season, the pathogen is also severe; meeting the emptiness of the moon, the pathogen is also severe. Contracting pathogens doubly, the illness is dangerous. Where there is prevailing qi, retaliation must come."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When clear qi comes greatly ... liver illness arises"] It flows to the 瞻 [gallbladder].

[On "When heat qi comes greatly ... lung illness arises"] It flows to the winding intestine and large intestine.
Song editors: On examination, the note says "winding intestine and large intestine", but according to the *Jiayi jing* the winding intestine is the large intestine.

[On "When cold qi comes greatly ... heart illness arises"] It flows to the triple burner and small intestine.

[On "When damp qi comes greatly ... kidney illness arises"] It flows to the bladder.

[On "When wind qi comes greatly ... spleen illness arises"] It flows to the stomach.

[On "This is what is called contracting pathogens and producing illness"] There is such qi outside and the inside dislikes it; neither inside nor outside welcomes it, and thereby illness follows. This is called contracting.

[On "Taking advantage of the year's depletion, the pathogen is severe"] When the year's wood is insufficient, there is a cool pathogen outside; when fire is insufficient, a cold pathogen; when earth is insufficient, a wind pathogen; when metal is insufficient, a heat pathogen; when water is insufficient, a damp pathogen. This is the year's depletion. When the year's qi is insufficient, outside pathogens converge severely.

[On "losing the harmony of the season, the pathogen is also severe"] When the six qi preside and govern and overcome the qi of the position, and one contracts it and falls ill, it also corresponds with the internal viscus according to what cannot overcome, and the pathogen is again severe.

[On "meeting the emptiness of the moon, the pathogen is also severe"] This means before the first quarter and after the last quarter, when the middle of the moon's disk is empty.

[On "Contracting pathogens doubly, the illness is dangerous"] The year is already insufficient and pathogenic qi arrives greatly: this is one contraction. The year is already insufficient and heaven's qi overcomes it; contracting a pathogen at this time is double contraction. When the inner qi invites the pathogen and heaven's qi does not help, how can the illness not be dangerous?

[On "Where there is prevailing qi, retaliation must come"] The qi of heaven and earth cannot do without each other; hence where there is prevailing qi, retaliation must come.

## Notes

The answer giving the visceral illnesses from the prevalences of the six qi, and how the year's depletion, losing the season's harmony, the moon's emptiness and double contraction make pathogens severe.
- 瞻 in Wang Bing's note is read as 胆 ("gallbladder").
