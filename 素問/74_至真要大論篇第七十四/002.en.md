# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo bowed twice and replied, "A clear question! It is the great record of heaven and earth, where humans and spirits communicate and respond."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is the great record of heaven and earth, where humans and spirits communicate and respond"] The changes of heaven and earth and the workings of humans and spirits differ as inner and outer, but their communication and response are one.

## Notes

Qibo says the question concerns the great record of heaven and earth.
