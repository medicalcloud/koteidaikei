# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §77 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. How are they treated?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How are they treated"] The retaliating qi is double the prevailing, so he first asks how to treat it.

## Notes

A question about treating the retaliations of the six qi.
