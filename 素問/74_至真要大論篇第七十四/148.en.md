# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §148 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. What are the yin and yang functions of the five flavors?"

## Notes

A question about the yin-yang nature and actions of the five flavors. There is no note by Wang Bing.
