# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Dispersed. Hence the substance is the same but the grade differs. Qi and flavor may be thin or thick, nature and function agitated or calm, treating and preserving more or less, strength and transformation shallow or deep; this is what it means."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Dispersed"] Without concentrated essence there is dispersed qi; with dispersed qi, things are not pure.

[On "Hence the substance is the same but the grade differs"] Though form and substance are the same, strength and function differ; hence they are not esteemed.

[On "treating and preserving more or less, strength and transformation shallow or deep; this is what it means"] This is why things differ from [those of] the year.

## Notes

The answer that things not matching the year's qi have dispersed qi and are less potent.
