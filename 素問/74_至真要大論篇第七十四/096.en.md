# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §96 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When *jueyin* controls heaven, if the guest prevails there is ringing in the ears, trembling and dizziness, and in severe cases coughing; if the host prevails, pain of the chest and rib-sides and difficulty speaking. When lesser yin controls heaven, if the guest prevails there is stuffy nose and sneezing, stiffness of the neck and nape, oppressive heat in the shoulders and back, headache, shortage of qi, fever, deafness and dim vision, and in severe cases puffy swelling, overflowing of blood, sores, coughing and panting; if the host prevails, heat in the heart with vexation and agitation, and in severe cases rib-side pain with propping fullness. When greater yin controls heaven, if the guest prevails there is puffy swelling of the head and face and panting; if the host prevails, fullness of the chest and abdomen and clouding after eating. When lesser yang controls heaven, if the guest prevails, red rashes break out on the surface, along with erysipelas and sores, retching counterflow, throat impediment, headache, swollen throat, deafness and overflowing of blood, and internally tugging and slackening; if the host prevails, fullness of the chest, coughing and breathing face-up, in severe cases with blood, and hot hands. When *yangming* controls heaven, if coolness returns and is in excess within, there is coughing, nosebleed, blocked throat, and heat in the heart and diaphragm; if coughing does not stop and white blood comes out, death. When greater yang controls heaven, if the guest prevails the chest is obstructed and clear snivel runs, and on contact with cold there is coughing; if the host prevails, there is a sound in the throat.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "if the host prevails, pain of the chest and rib-sides and difficulty speaking"] The five *si* and five *hai* years.

[On "if the host prevails, heat in the heart with vexation and agitation, and in severe cases rib-side pain with propping fullness"] The five *zi* and five *wu* years.

[On "if the host prevails, fullness of the chest and abdomen and clouding after eating"] The five *chou* and five *wei* years.

[On "if the host prevails, fullness of the chest, coughing and breathing face-up, in severe cases with blood, and hot hands"] The five *yin* and five 甲 [*shen*] years.

[On "heat in the heart and diaphragm; if coughing does not stop and white blood comes out, death"] Returning means returning to its old dwelling. White blood means pale red blood coughed up, like flesh or like lung. The five *mao* and five *you* years.
Song editors: On examination, it does not speak of guest or host prevailing here because metal dwells in the position of fire, so there is no principle by which the guest could prevail.

[On "clear snivel runs, and on contact with cold there is coughing; if the host prevails, there is a sound in the throat"] The five *chen* and five *xu* years.

## Notes

The answer giving the illnesses when the guest or host qi prevails while controlling heaven; only for *yangming* controlling heaven are guest and host prevalence not distinguished. Qibo's speech continues into the next unit.
- 甲 in 五甲 in Wang Bing's note is read as 申.
