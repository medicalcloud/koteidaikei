# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §130 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The birth of qi differs from the decline and abundance of its transformation. The functioning of the abundance and decline of cold, heat, warmth and coolness lies at the four corners. Hence the movement of yang begins in warmth and flourishes in heat; the movement of yin begins in coolness and flourishes in cold. Spring, summer, autumn and winter each deviate from their divisions.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Hence the movement of yang begins in warmth ... each deviate from their divisions"] It means the four proper qi of spring, summer, autumn and winter lie in the divisions of the four corners. Testing it by events: the warmth of spring is properly in the months *chen* and *si*, the heat of summer in *wu* and *wei*, the coolness of autumn in *xu* and *hai*, the cold of winter in *yin* and *chou*. Spring begins in mid-spring, summer in mid-summer, autumn in mid-autumn, winter in mid-winter. Hence in the *chou* month yin forms layered ice in the thick earth; in the *wei* month yang flames and lightning flash at the edge of the sky; in the *xu* month frost is clear and killing and all things harden; in the *chen* month the wind fans mildly and old branches flourish and flower. This deviation of the qi from their divisions is clear and cannot be hidden. Yet the birth, growth, gathering and storing of the qi of yin and yang accord with the constant rule; examining their transformations and responses in humans, each season deviates by some days and departs from the constant rule; following the rule of deviation, they fit exactly.

## Notes

The beginning of the answer that the peaks of the seasonal qi deviate into the four corners (the last month of each season). Qibo's speech continues into the next unit.
