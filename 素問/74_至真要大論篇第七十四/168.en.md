# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §168 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Why is it so even when the flourishing is not treated?"

## Notes

A question about reversed effects even when the flourishing qi is not treated. There is no note by Wang Bing.
