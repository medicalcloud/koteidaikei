# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "What are the 先 [controlling] year's things?"

## Notes

A question about the year's things. There is no note by Wang Bing (the Song editors in the next unit suggest 先歳 is an error for 司歳).
