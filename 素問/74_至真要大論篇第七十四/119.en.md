# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §119 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "The tips and roots of the six qi differ in what they follow. How so?"

## Notes

A question about what the tips and roots of the six qi follow. There is no note by Wang Bing.
