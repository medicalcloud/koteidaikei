# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §90 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In the prevalence of qi, follow the slight and restrain the severe. In the retaliation of qi, calm the mild and seize the violent. In all cases follow the prevailing qi, settle what is bent and hidden, regardless of the number, aiming at balance. This is the way."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In all cases follow the prevailing qi ... This is the way"] 随 means following it; 安 means complying with the prevailing qi to harmonize it. 制 means restraining and stopping; 平 means balancing and regulating; 奪 means seizing its abundant qi. In treating this, one does not go by the number but takes the balance and harmony of the qi as the standard.

## Notes

The answer giving treatment according to the slightness or severity of prevalence and retaliation.
