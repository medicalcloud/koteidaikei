# 素問 至真要大論篇第七十四 §72 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the retaliation of lesser yin, warm heat arises within, with vexation and agitation, stuffy nose and sneezing, and wrenching pain in the lower abdomen. Fire appears and burns; the throat is dry; [stool and urine] pour separately and stop at times; qi moves on the left and rises to the right; there is coughing, skin pain, sudden loss of voice, heart pain, oppression and clouding so that one does not recognize people, then shivering aversion to cold, trembling and delirium; when the cold ends there is heat, thirst with desire to drink, shortage of qi, bone wilting, intestines blocked and not passing, puffy swelling outside, hiccup and belching. Red qi transforms late, flowing water does not freeze, heat qi prevails greatly, and shelled creatures do not recover. People suffer prickly heat, rashes, sores, abscesses, ulcers, pimples and hemorrhoids, and in severe cases it enters the lungs, with coughing and nasal deep-source discharge. If Tianfu is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "People suffer prickly heat ... coughing and nasal deep-source discharge"] The qi of fire heat goes from the small intestine along the left below the navel into the large intestine, rises to the left rib-side, and in severe cases rises to the right and enters the lungs; hence "moves on the left and rises to the right", with skin pain. Pouring separately means both stool and urine come down. Bone wilting means the bones are weak and lack strength. Blocked intestines means the intestines are as if cut off and do not pass; 瀉也 [meaning unclear]. It is so when cold and heat are severe. *Yangming* prevailed first, so red qi transforms late. Flowing water does not freeze because the root of lesser yin controls the earth; in humans, correspondingly, the winter pulse does not congeal. In the highest places such as high mountains and deep valleys water should still freeze; flat, low-lying rivers are as the classic says. Fire qi steams within and metal qi resists outside; yang heat is pent up within, hence prickly heat, rashes and sores; severe rashes also become sores. With little heat, prickly heat and rashes arise outside; with much heat, abscesses and pimples knot within. When there is heat in the small intestine, hemorrhoids form inside and out. The changes of retaliating heat all cause illness on the back and outer sides of the body. Sores, prickly heat and rashes arise above; abscesses, ulcers, pimples and hemorrhoids below; those that reverse these places are counterflow.

[On "If Tianfu is cut off, it is death and cannot be treated"] Tianfu is the pulse qi of the lungs.
Song editors: According to the text above, for lesser yin controlling heaven with heat running rampant it says "if Chize is cut off, death"; for lesser yang controlling heaven with fire running rampant, "if Tianfu is cut off, death"; here for the retaliation of lesser yin it says "if Tianfu is cut off", and below for the retaliation of lesser yang "if Chize is cut off". The texts seem contradictory; presumably, since Chize and Tianfu are both where the hand greater yin vessel pulses, the terms are used interchangeably here.
