# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Yin and yang both have their points and meeting places. Yang pours into yin, and yin, when full, [pours] outward; yin and yang are evenly balanced and fill the form, and the nine indicators are as one; this is called a normal person. Pathogens arise either in the yin or in the yang. Those arising in the yang are contracted from wind, rain, cold and summer heat; those arising in the yin are contracted from food and drink, dwelling, yin and yang [sexual activity], joy and anger."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "yin and yang are evenly balanced ... this is called a normal person"] A normal person means a person in balance and harmony.

## Notes

The normal person, with yin and yang in balance, and pathogens arising in the yang (external causes) and the yin (internal causes).
- 陰陽 (after 飲食居処) is read as sexual activity.
