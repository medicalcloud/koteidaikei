# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "There are five kinds of surplus and also five of insufficiency. What does the Emperor wish to ask?"

## Notes

There are five kinds each of surplus and insufficiency. There is no note by Wang Bing.
