# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Take the spaces between the divisions of the flesh, without hitting the channel or harming the network vessel; the defensive qi is restored and the pathogenic qi is exhausted."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Take the spaces between the divisions of the flesh ... the pathogenic qi is exhausted"] The defensive qi warms the divisions of the flesh, fills the skin, fattens the interstices and controls opening and closing. So when the flesh squirms, take the spaces between the divisions of the flesh, merely opening the divisions of the flesh to let out the pathogen; therefore do not hit the channel or harm the network vessel. The defensive qi returns to its former state and the pathogenic qi is entirely exhausted. 索 means scattered and used up.
