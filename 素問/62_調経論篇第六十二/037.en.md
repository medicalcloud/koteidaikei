# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. I have already heard the forms of depletion and repletion, but I do not know how they arise."

## Notes

A question on how depletion and repletion arise. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 己 in 余己聞 is read as 已 "already".
