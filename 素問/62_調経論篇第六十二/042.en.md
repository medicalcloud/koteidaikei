# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "What is present is repletion; what is absent is depletion. So when qi merges there is no blood, and when blood merges there is no qi. Now blood and qi lose each other; hence there is depletion. When the network vessels and minute vessels together transport into the channels and blood and qi merge, there is repletion. When blood and qi merge and rush upward, there is great reversal; with reversal there is sudden death. If the qi returns, one lives; if it does not, one dies."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What is present is repletion; what is absent is depletion"] When qi merges with blood, blood is absent; when blood merges with qi, qi is absent.

[On "So when qi merges there is no blood ... hence there is depletion"] When qi merges with blood, blood loses its qi; when blood merges with qi, qi loses its blood; hence "blood and qi lose each other".

## Notes

The definitions of depletion and repletion, and great reversal when blood and qi merge and rush upward.
