# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "What is present is excess; what is absent is deficiency. So when qi merges there is no blood, and when blood merges there is no qi. Now blood and qi lose each other; hence there is deficiency. When the collaterals and minute vessels together transport into the meridians and blood and qi merge, there is excess. When blood and qi merge and rush upward, there is great reversal; with reversal there is sudden death. If the qi returns, one lives; if it does not, one dies."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What is present is excess; what is absent is deficiency"] When qi merges with blood, blood is absent; when blood merges with qi, qi is absent.

[On "So when qi merges there is no blood ... hence there is deficiency"] When qi merges with blood, blood loses its qi; when blood merges with qi, qi loses its blood; hence "blood and qi lose each other".

## Notes

The definitions of deficiency and excess, and great reversal when blood and qi merge and rush upward.
