# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §60 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Reversal qi counterflows upward, cold qi accumulates in the chest and is not drained; when it is not drained, warm qi departs, cold alone remains, and the blood congeals; when it congeals, the vessels do not open, and the pulse is abundant, large and rough; hence there is cold in the center."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "cold alone remains, and the blood congeals; when it congeals, the vessels do not open"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes "the interstices do not open".

[On "the pulse is abundant, large and rough; hence there is cold in the center"] Warm qi means yang qi. When yin counterflows and fills the interior, yang qi departs outside the skin.

## Notes

Why yin abundance produces cold inside.
