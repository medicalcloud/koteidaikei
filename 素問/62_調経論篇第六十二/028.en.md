# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When form is in surplus, drain its yang channel; when insufficient, supplement its yang network vessel."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When form is in surplus ... supplement its yang network vessel"] Both are the channel and network vessel of the stomach.

## Notes

Supplementing and draining for surplus and insufficiency of form.
