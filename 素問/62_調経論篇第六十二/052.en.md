# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §52 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "With joy, qi descends; with sorrow, qi dissipates; when it dissipates, the vessels become depleted and empty. When, through cold food and drink, cold qi fumigates and fills, blood congeals and qi departs; hence it is called depletion."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "when it dissipates, the vessels become depleted and empty. When, through cold food and drink, cold qi fumigates and fills"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 動蔵.

## Notes

How depletion from internal causes arises.
- The Song editors' 甲乙經作動藏 is taken to mean that the *Jiayi jing* has 動藏 for 熏滿, since in the Siku quanshu edition (Daizhige *Huangdi neijing suwen*) this note stands right after 寒氣熏滿 in the main text.
