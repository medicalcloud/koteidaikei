# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §51 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How does depletion arise in the yin?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How does depletion arise in the yin?"] Depletion means that essential qi is taken away.

## Notes

A question on depletion from internal causes.
