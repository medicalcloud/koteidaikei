# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When qi is in surplus, drain its meridian tunnels, without harming the meridian, letting out blood or leaking qi. When insufficient, supplement its meridian tunnels without letting out qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When qi is in surplus ... without letting out qi"] Qi means ying-nutrient qi. If in draining with the needle one harms the meridian, blood comes out and the ying-nutrient qi leaks away; so one does not want to let out blood or leak qi, but only drains the wei-defensive qi. When supplementing with the needle, one should carefully close the point; and one does not want to leak the wei-defensive qi either.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says: "The meridian tunnel is the branch of the hand greater yin that runs from the hand greater yin to the hand *Yangming*, that is, the road by which the hand greater yin goes toward the hand *Yangming*. Since one wishes to guide the yin and yang of the zang organs and bowels, supplementing and draining are both done through the collaterals that branch off from the main meridians. Drain the branching road of the yin meridian and do not harm the main meridian."

## Notes

Supplementing and draining for surplus and insufficiency of qi.
