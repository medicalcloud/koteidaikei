# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Look at its blood collaterals, needle and let out the blood, so that bad blood cannot enter the meridians and produce illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Look at its blood collaterals ... produce illness"] When the blood collaterals are full, needle and press to let it out; then the bad-colored blood cannot enter the meridians.

## Notes

Needling lingering blood.
