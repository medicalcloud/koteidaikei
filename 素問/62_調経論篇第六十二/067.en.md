# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §67 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "You, Master, say that depletion and repletion are of ten kinds and arise from the five depots, but the five depots are only five vessels. The twelve channels all produce their own illnesses, yet you speak only of the five depots. The twelve channels all network the three hundred and sixty-five joints; when a joint is ill, it must affect the channels, and the illnesses of the channels all have depletion and repletion. How are these brought together?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The twelve channels all produce their own illnesses"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says 皆生百病 ("all produce the hundred illnesses"), and the *Taisu* agrees.

## Notes

A question on the relation between depletion and repletion of the five depots and the illnesses of the twelve channels.
