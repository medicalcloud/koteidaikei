# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §67 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "You, Master, say that deficiency and excess are of ten kinds and arise from the five zang organs, but the five zang organs are only five vessels. The twelve meridians all produce their own illnesses, yet you speak only of the five zang organs. The twelve meridians all network the three hundred and sixty-five joints; when a joint is ill, it must affect the meridians, and the illnesses of the meridians all have deficiency and excess. How are these brought together?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The twelve meridians all produce their own illnesses"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says 皆生百病 ("all produce the hundred illnesses"), and the *Taisu* agrees.

## Notes

A question on the relation between deficiency and excess of the five zang organs and the illnesses of the twelve meridians.
