# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When blood is in surplus, drain its abundant channel and let out its blood. When insufficient, look at its depleted channel, insert the needle into the vessel, retain it long and watch; when the vessel becomes large, withdraw the needle quickly and do not let blood leak."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "retain it long and watch"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says 久㽞之血至, and the *Taisu* agrees.

[On "when the vessel becomes large ... do not let blood leak"] When the vessel is abundant and full, blood is in surplus, so it is let out. When the channel qi is depleted, blood is insufficient, so it is not allowed to leak. Retaining long and withdrawing quickly is supplementing. The *Zhenjie lun*'s "slowly and then quickly brings repletion" has the same sense.

## Notes

Supplementing and draining for surplus and insufficiency of blood.
- 久㽞之血至 quoted from the *Jiayi jing* by the Song editors presumably means "retain long until the blood arrives", but the sense is unclear.
