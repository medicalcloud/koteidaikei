# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I have heard that the needling method says: 'Drain what is in surplus; supplement what is insufficient.' What is meant by surplus, and what by insufficiency?"

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on the meaning of surplus and insufficiency. There is no note by Wang Bing.
