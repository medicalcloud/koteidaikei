# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. What are surplus and insufficiency of qi like?"

## Notes

A question on surplus and insufficiency of qi. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 気 is supplied in the base's 有余不足奈何. The *Taisu*, the *Leijing*, Zhang Zhicong's *Jizhu* and two Daizhige texts all read 気有余不足奈何; this matches the questions on spirit, blood, form and will, so 気 is taken to have dropped out.
