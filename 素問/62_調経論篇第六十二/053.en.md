# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §53 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "The classic says: 'When yang is deficient, the exterior is cold; when yin is deficient, the interior is hot; when yang is abundant, the exterior is hot; when yin is abundant, the interior is cold.' I have already heard this, but I do not know why it is so."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I have already heard this, but I do not know why it is so"] "The classic says" means the words of the classic of high antiquity.

## Notes

A question on the relation between deficiency and abundance of yin and yang and cold and heat inside and outside.
- In the base, 1 instance(s) of 巳 meaning 已 "already/stop" to 已 are emended (graphic confusion; the base often does not distinguish 巳, 已 and 己, while Wikisource's plain text and other sources mostly have the correct character).
