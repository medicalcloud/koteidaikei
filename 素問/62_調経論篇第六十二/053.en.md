# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §53 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "The classic says: 'When yang is depleted, the exterior is cold; when yin is depleted, the interior is hot; when yang is abundant, the exterior is hot; when yin is abundant, the interior is cold.' I have already heard this, but I do not know why it is so."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I have already heard this, but I do not know why it is so"] "The classic says" means the words of the classic of high antiquity.

## Notes

A question on the relation between depletion and abundance of yin and yang and cold and heat inside and outside.
- 巳 in 余巳聞 is read as 已 "already".
