# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Spirit may be in surplus or insufficient; qi may be in surplus or insufficient; blood may be in surplus or insufficient; form may be in surplus or insufficient; will may be in surplus or insufficient. These ten are not equal in their qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Spirit may be in surplus or insufficient ... These ten are not equal in their qi"] Spirit belongs to the heart, qi to the lungs, blood to the liver, form to the spleen, will to the kidneys. Since each has its own source, they are not equal.

## Notes

Spirit, qi, blood, form and will each have surplus and insufficiency.
