# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "They all arise from the five depots. The heart stores the spirit, the lungs store qi, the liver stores blood, the spleen stores flesh, and the kidneys store the will, and by these the form is made. When will and intent communicate, connecting inwardly to bone and marrow, the bodily form and the five depots are made. The ways of the five depots all go out through the channel tunnels to circulate blood and qi. When blood and qi are not in harmony, the hundred illnesses arise through transformation. Therefore one guards the channel tunnels."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "They all arise from the five depots"] This means the five spirit depots.

[On "The heart stores the spirit ... and by these the form is made"] Why do illnesses all arise from the five depots? Because they store the five spirits within and make the form.

[On "When will and intent communicate ... the bodily form and the five depots are made"] Will and intent is a general term for the five spirits as a whole; bone and marrow is a general term for the formation of exterior and interior. It means that when the five spirits communicate freely, bone and marrow are formed, and the bodily form is established, the five depots come to support each other.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* lacks the two characters 五蔵.

[On "The ways of the five depots ... one guards the channel tunnels"] 隧 means a hidden road. The channels travel hidden and unseen, hence they are called channel tunnels. Blood and qi are a person's spirit. When a pathogen invades them, blood and qi become irregular; hence transformation occurs and the hundred illnesses arise. The channels are what decide life and death, deal with the hundred illnesses and regulate depletion and repletion; therefore one guards the channel tunnels.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 経渠 for 経隧; each sense works.

## Notes

All illness arises from the five depots, and guarding their roads, the channel tunnels, is the key to treatment.
- According to the Song editors the *Jiayi jing* lacks 五蔵 in 而成身形五蔵; then the reading would be 而成身形。五蔵之道…….
- The 二字 in Wang Bing's note in Kanripo mentioned in the variants presumably relates to the gap in the Song editors' 無五蔵　二字, but this has not been checked.
