# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §54 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Yang receives qi from the upper burner to warm the skin and the spaces between the divisions of the flesh. Now if cold qi is outside, the upper burner does not open; when the upper burner does not open, cold qi alone remains outside; hence there is shivering with cold."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "when the upper burner does not open, cold qi alone remains outside; hence there is shivering with cold"] 慄 means shaking and shivering.

## Notes

Why yang depletion produces cold outside.
- For 令 in 令寒気在外, two 殆知閣 texts have 今 (see the variants). The translation reads it as 今 "now".
