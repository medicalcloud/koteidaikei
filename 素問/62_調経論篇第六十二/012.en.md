# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Massage without letting go, apply the needle without pushing, and move qi to where it is insufficient; then the spirit qi can be restored."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Massage without letting go ... the spirit qi can be restored"] Massage the affected place without releasing the hand, and apply the needle to the affected place also without pushing it in. Make the person's spirit qi gather inwardly to the needle, and move the person's spirit qi 今 [so that it] becomes full of itself; then the slight illness departs of itself and the spirit qi returns to normal.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* and the *Taisu* say 移気於足 without the character 不. Yang Shangshan says: "Massage makes the qi reach the heel."

## Notes

Needling the slightness of spirit: apply the needle shallowly and move the spirit qi to fill [the insufficiency].
- 今 in Wang Bing's 今自充足 is presumably an error for 令.
