# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Take it on the spot, without hitting the channel; the place of the pathogen can then be emptied at once."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Take it on the spot ... emptied at once"] Without seeking the points, take directly the place where the pathogen resides; hence "take it on the spot".
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 以去其邪 for 邪所.

## Notes

Needling the slight illness of will (not yet merged). The discussion of surplus and insufficiency of the five—spirit, qi, blood, form and will—ends here.
