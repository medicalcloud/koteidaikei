# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When wind and rain injure a person, they first lodge in the skin and pass into the minute vessels; when the minute vessels are full, they pass into the network vessels; when the network vessels are full, they transport into the great channels. Blood, qi and the pathogen merge and lodge in the divisions and interstices, and the pulse is hard and large; hence it is called repletion. In repletion the exterior is hard and full; it cannot be pressed, and pressing it is painful."

## Notes

How repletion from wind and rain is transmitted, and its symptoms. There is no note by Wang Bing.
