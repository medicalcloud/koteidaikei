# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §62 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "To needle this, take it at the channel tunnels; take blood at the nutritive and qi at the defensive; use the form; and follow the four seasons for how much and how high or low."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "take blood at the nutritive ... how much and how high or low"] The nutritive governs blood and is yin qi; the defensive governs qi and is yang qi. The way of applying the needle requires first knowing the length of the form and the breadth of the bones, following the method of the three preparations, and reckoning the whole body to apply the *fen* and *cun*; hence "use the form". How much and how high or low according to the four seasons is set out in a later chapter.

## Notes

The principles of needling once qi and blood have merged and the illness is formed.
- 三備 in Wang Bing's note matches the name of the Daoist book *Beidi sanbei jing* recorded in the bibliographical section of the *Tongzhi*, and may refer to it, but its content has not been verified.
