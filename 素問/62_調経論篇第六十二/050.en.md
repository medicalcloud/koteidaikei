# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §50 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When joy and anger are immoderate, yin qi counterflows upward; when it counterflows upward, below becomes depleted; when below is depleted, yang qi rushes there; hence it is called repletion."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When joy and anger are immoderate ... hence it is called repletion"] Song editors: In the classic's "when joy and anger are immoderate, yin qi counterflows upward", we suspect 喜 is superfluous.

## Notes

How repletion from internal causes arises. The Song editors suspect 喜 here is superfluous, since joy makes qi descend (next unit).
