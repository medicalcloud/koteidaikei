# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §48 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When cold and damp strike a person, the skin does not contract, the flesh is hard and tight, the nutritive blood congeals and the defensive qi departs; hence it is called depletion. In depletion [the skin] is wrinkled and folded and the qi is insufficient; pressing it makes the qi sufficient to warm it, so it feels pleasant and there is no pain."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold and damp strike a person, the skin does not contract"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi says "not contracting means numbness". The *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu* say "the skin contracts", without 不.

[On "In depletion [the skin] is wrinkled and folded ... there is no pain"] 聶 means wrinkled; 辟 means folded over.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 摂辟, and the *Taisu* also writes 摂辟.

## Notes

The symptoms of depletion from cold and damp.
