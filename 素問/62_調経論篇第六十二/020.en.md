# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When blood is in surplus, there is anger; when insufficient, fear. When blood and qi have not yet merged and the five depots are stable, if the minute network vessels overflow outward, the channels have lingering blood."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When blood is in surplus, there is anger; when insufficient, fear"] This is the depot of the liver. The *Zhenjing* says: "The liver stores blood. When liver qi is depleted there is fear; when replete, anger."
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition writes 悲 for 恐, and the *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu* agree.

[On "When blood and qi have not yet merged ... the channels have lingering blood"] When the network vessels have a pathogen and are abundant, it enters the channels; hence "if the minute network vessels overflow like water, the channels have 㽞 [lingering] blood".

## Notes

The symptoms of surplus and insufficiency of blood (liver), and its slight illness.
- The base's 孫絡水溢 is emended to 孫絡外溢 "the minute network vessels overflow outward". The *Taisu*, the *Jiayi jing*, the *Leijing* and the Daizhige *Huangdi neijing suwen* read 外溢 (the *Taisu*'s editorial note says the *Suwen* has 水溢), and the *Leijing* explains that when one sees the minute network vessels overflowing outward, one knows there is lingering blood in the great channels. Wang Bing's note, Zhang Zhicong's *Jizhu* and Wikisource (the plain text) read 水溢, and the translation of Wang Bing's note follows that reading; but since the *Taisu* and the *Jiayi jing*, older than Wang Bing, agree on 外溢, the main text is emended accordingly.
