# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When blood is in surplus, there is anger; when insufficient, fear. When blood and qi have not yet merged and the five depots are stable, if the minute network vessels overflow like water, the channels have lingering blood."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When blood is in surplus, there is anger; when insufficient, fear"] This is the depot of the liver. The *Zhenjing* says: "The liver stores blood. When liver qi is depleted there is fear; when replete, anger."
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition writes 悲 for 恐, and the *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu* agree.

[On "When blood and qi have not yet merged ... the channels have lingering blood"] When the network vessels have a pathogen and are abundant, it enters the channels; hence "if the minute network vessels overflow like water, the channels have 㽞 [lingering] blood".

## Notes

The symptoms of surplus and insufficiency of blood (liver), and its slight illness.
- For 孫絡水溢, 殆知閣 (黄帝内经素问) reads 孫絡外溢 (see the variants). The translation follows the base text.
