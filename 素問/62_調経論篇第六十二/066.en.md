# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §66 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Hold the needle without putting it down, settle the intention, wait for exhalation and insert the needle; as the qi goes out the needle goes in, the needle hole is blocked on all sides, and essence has no way to escape. When it has just become replete, withdraw the needle quickly; as the qi comes in the needle comes out, and the heat cannot return. Close and block the gate; the pathogenic qi spreads and scatters and the essential qi can be preserved. To move the qi, wait for the right time; if the near qi is not lost, the distant qi comes. This is called pursuing it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the pathogenic qi spreads and scatters and the essential qi can be preserved. To move the qi, wait for the right time"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 動無後時.

[On "if the near qi is not lost ... This is called pursuing it"] It means just closing the point tightly so that its qi does not scatter and leak. The near qi is qi that has already arrived; the distant qi is qi that has not yet arrived. When one wishes to move the channel qi to supplement it, one must always determine by the water clock where the qi is and needle it; this is called obtaining the time and regulating it. 追 means supplementing. The *Zhenjing* says "pursue and assist it, and how could there be no repletion?"; this is what is meant.

## Notes

The needling method for supplementing depletion.
