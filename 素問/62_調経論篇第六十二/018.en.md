# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Massage without letting go; take out the needle and show it, saying, 'I am going to needle this deeply.' On applying it to the person, keep it to the hide. The essential qi then hides of itself, the pathogenic qi scatters in disorder with nowhere to rest, the qi leaks out through the interstices, and the true qi then attains [its place]."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Massage without letting go ... the true qi then attains [its place]"] This too means massaging the affected place. 革 means skin. "I am going to needle this deeply. On applying it to the person, keep it to the hide" means saying one will needle deeply but needling shallowly. When thus threatened, the person shows fear; hence the essential qi hides. Because the needle is applied to the skin, the essential qi hides and the pathogen has nothing to hold on to; so it scatters in disorder with nowhere to rest and leaks out through the interstices. Once the pathogenic qi has leaked out, the true qi attains its place with the skin and interstices.
Song editors: 揚 [Yang] Shangshan says: "革 means change. When people hear that pleasure is coming, body and mind are delighted; when they hear that pain will reach the body, their feelings inevitably change. When delighted, all the body relaxes; when changed, the feelings resist; when they resist, the pathogenic qi dissipates and hides."

## Notes

Needling the slight illness of qi: announce a deep insertion but needle shallowly in the skin, so that the essential qi hides and the pathogenic qi scatters.
- Wang Bing reads 革 as "skin", Yang Shangshan as "change". The translation follows Wang Bing.
- 揚上善 in the Song editors' note is an error for 楊上善.
