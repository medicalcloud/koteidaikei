# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How does one needle the slight [condition]?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How does one needle the slight [condition]?"] This returns to the earlier "arising in the fine hairs and not yet entered the channels and network vessels".

## Notes

A question on needling the slightness of spirit.
