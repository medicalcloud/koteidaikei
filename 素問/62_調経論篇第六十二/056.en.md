# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §56 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When there is exhaustion from labor, form and qi decline and diminish, grain qi is not abundant, the upper burner does not move, the lower stomach duct does not open, stomach qi becomes hot, and the hot qi fumigates the chest; hence there is heat inside."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "grain qi is not abundant, the upper burner does not move, the lower stomach duct does not open"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes "the lower burner does not open".

[On "stomach qi becomes hot ... hence there is heat inside"] Using one's strength excessively leads to exhaustion. Being absorbed in work without eating, grain qi is not abundant.

## Notes

Why yin depletion produces heat inside.
