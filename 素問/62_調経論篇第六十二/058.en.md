# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §58 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the upper burner does not open and flow freely, the skin becomes dense, the interstices close up, the dark mansions do not open, and the defensive qi cannot leak out and escape; hence there is heat outside."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the skin becomes dense, the interstices close up, the dark mansions do not open"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu* lack the two characters 玄府.

[On "the defensive qi cannot leak out and escape; hence there is heat outside"] Injured outside by cold toxin, it presses inward on the various yang. When cold is abundant outside, the skin contracts; when the skin contracts, the interstices become dense; so the defensive qi accumulates with nowhere to flow. Cold qi presses from outside, yang qi contends within, and accumulated fire burns inside; hence heat is produced outside.

## Notes

Why yang abundance produces heat outside.
