# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When qi is in surplus, there is panting, coughing and rising qi; when insufficient, breathing is free but qi is scant. When blood and qi have not yet merged and the five depots are stable, a slight illness of the skin is called slight leakage of white qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When qi is in surplus ... qi is scant"] This is the depot of the lungs. The lungs store qi; when breath does not flow freely, there is panting. The *Zhenjing* says: "When lung qi is depleted, nasal breathing is free but qi is scant; when replete, there is panting and wheezing, the chest is 慿 [full], and one breathes with the head thrown back."

[On "When blood and qi have not yet merged ... slight leakage of white qi"] The lungs correspond to the 脾 [skin], and their color is white; hence a slight illness of the skin is called slight leakage of white qi.

## Notes

The symptoms of surplus and insufficiency of qi (lungs), and its slight illness (slight leakage of white qi).
- In Wang Bing's note 慿 in 胸慿 is presumably an error for 盈, and 脾 in 肺合脾 for 皮.
