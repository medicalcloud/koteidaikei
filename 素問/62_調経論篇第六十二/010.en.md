# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When spirit is in surplus, drain the blood of its small network vessels; in letting blood, do not push deep and do not hit the great channels; then the spirit qi becomes level. When spirit is insufficient, look at the depleted network vessels, press to bring [the qi], needle to free it, do not let out blood and do not leak qi, so as to open the channel; then the spirit qi becomes level."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When spirit is in surplus, drain the blood ... the spirit qi becomes level"] Since the pathogen has entered the small network vessels, one may drain those vessels and let out their blood. Do not push the needle deep; a deep needle harms the flesh. Since the pathogen resides in the small network vessels, one does not want the needle to hit the great channels. Once the blood of the network vessels has come out, the spirit qi levels of itself. 斥 means push. Small network vessels are the minute network vessels. The *Zhenjing* says: "The channels are the 裹 [interior]; their branches running across are the network vessels; the branches of the network vessels are the minute network vessels." 平 means 平謂 [presumably "level and regulated"].
Song editors: On examination, the *Zhenjing* quoted in this note is also quoted twice in the note to the *Sanbu jiuhou lun*, where it is called the *Lingshu*, while here it is called the *Zhenjing*; thus Wang means the *Lingshu* by *Zhenjing*. In the present notes to the *Suwen*, quotations from the *Zhenjing* are mostly *Lingshu* text, but since the *Lingshu* is not complete today, we cannot know them all.

[On "When spirit is insufficient ... the spirit qi becomes level"] Just open the channels so that they are harmonious and free, and press the depleted network vessels to bring their qi. Because spirit is insufficient, one does not want to let out blood or leak qi.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 切 for 按 and 和 for 利.

## Notes

Supplementing and draining for surplus and insufficiency of spirit.
- In Wang Bing's note 裹 is presumably an error for 裏, and 平謂平謂也 for something like 平謂平調也, but this has not been checked.
