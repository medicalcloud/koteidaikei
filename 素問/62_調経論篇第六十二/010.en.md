# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When spirit is in surplus, drain the blood of its small collaterals; in letting blood, do not push deep and do not hit the great meridians; then the spirit qi becomes level. When spirit is insufficient, look at the deficient collaterals, press to bring [the qi], needle to free it, do not let out blood and do not leak qi, so as to open the meridian; then the spirit qi becomes level."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When spirit is in surplus, drain the blood ... the spirit qi becomes level"] Since the pathogen has entered the small collaterals, one may drain those vessels and let out their blood. Do not push the needle deep; a deep needle harms the flesh. Since the pathogen resides in the small collaterals, one does not want the needle to hit the great meridians. Once the blood of the collaterals has come out, the spirit qi levels of itself. 斥 means push. Small collaterals are the minute collaterals. The *Zhenjing* says: "The meridians are the 裹 [interior]; their branches running across are the collaterals; the branches of the collaterals are the minute collaterals." 平 means 平謂 [presumably "level and regulated"].
Song editors: On examination, the *Zhenjing* quoted in this note is also quoted twice in the note to the *Sanbu jiuhou lun*, where it is called the *Lingshu*, while here it is called the *Zhenjing*; thus Wang means the *Lingshu* by *Zhenjing*. In the present notes to the *Suwen*, quotations from the *Zhenjing* are mostly *Lingshu* text, but since the *Lingshu* is not complete today, we cannot know them all.

[On "When spirit is insufficient ... the spirit qi becomes level"] Just open the meridians so that they are harmonious and free, and press the deficient collaterals to bring their qi. Because spirit is insufficient, one does not want to let out blood or leak qi.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 切 for 按 and 和 for 利.

## Notes

Supplementing and draining for surplus and insufficiency of spirit.
- 平謂平謂也 in Wang Bing's note is read as 平謂平調也, as in the Siku quanshu edition (Daizhige *Huangdi neijing suwen*). 裹 in 経脈為裹 is taken as an error for 裏, since the *Lingshu*, "Maidu" chapter, has 經脈為裏，支而橫者為絡，絡之別者為孫 (the Siku quanshu edition also has 裹).
