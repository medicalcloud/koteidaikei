# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Blood and qi like warmth and hate cold. When cold, they congeal and cannot flow; when warm, they melt and depart. Therefore where qi merges, blood is deficient; where blood merges, qi is deficient."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold, they congeal and cannot flow; when warm, they melt and depart"] 泣 means congealing and stopping without moving on, like snow in water.

[On "Therefore where qi merges, blood is deficient; where blood merges, qi is deficient"] When qi merges with blood, blood becomes scant; hence blood is deficient. When blood merges with qi, qi becomes scant; hence qi is deficient.

## Notes

Where qi and blood merge, the other is deficient.
