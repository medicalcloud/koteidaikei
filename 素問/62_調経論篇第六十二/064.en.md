# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §64 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "To drain repletion, insert the needle when the qi is abundant [on inhalation], so that needle and qi go in together, opening the gate as if freeing the door; needle and qi come out together, so that essential qi is not harmed and pathogenic qi descends. Do not close the outer gate, so as to let out the illness; shake and widen its road as if freeing the path. This is called great draining. One must withdraw urgently; then the great qi yields."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "To drain repletion ... then the great qi yields"] It means wishing to open the hole and let the qi leak out. 切 means urgent, i.e. withdrawing the needle urgently. The *Zhenjie lun* says "quickly and then slowly brings depletion", meaning withdraw the needle quickly and press slowly. The great qi means a great pathogenic qi. 屈 means retreat and yield.

## Notes

The needling method for draining repletion.
- [on inhalation] in 気盛乃内鍼 is supplied on the view that it pairs with 候呼内鍼 in supplementing (unit 066). Wang Bing does not comment on this point.
