# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §49 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. How does excess arise in the yin?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How does excess arise in the yin?"] Excess means that pathogenic qi is abundant.

## Notes

A question on excess from internal causes.
