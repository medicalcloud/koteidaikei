# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When will is in surplus, drain where Ranjin has blood; when insufficient, supplement Fuliu."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When will is in surplus, drain where Ranjin has blood"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* and the *Taisu* say "drain where Ranjin has blood and let out the blood". Yang Shangshan says "Ranjin should be the sinew below Rangu". Further, on examining the places that cite Rangu, most say "where there is blood in front of the Ran bone". We suspect that the two characters 骨之 are missing and that 前 has been miswritten as 筋.

[On "drain where Ranjin has blood; when insufficient, supplement Fuliu"] 然 means Rangu, the *ying* point of the foot lesser yin, in the depression below the large bone in front of the inner ankle. When the blood network vessels are abundant, drain them; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain three breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Fuliu is the *jing* (river) point of the foot lesser yin, in the depression two body-*cun* above the inner ankle; one may needle three body-*fen* and retain three breaths; for moxibustion, five cones.

## Notes

Supplementing and draining for surplus and insufficiency of will.
- The character 溜 in 復溜 is an image in the base text's input and was set as 溜 from the Daozang edition (see the note in the variants).
