# 素問 調経論篇第六十二 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How does one needle the slight [condition]?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How does one needle the slight [condition]?"] This returns to the earlier "slight leakage of white qi".

## Notes

A question on needling the slight leakage of white qi.
