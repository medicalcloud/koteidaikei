# 素問 異法方宜論篇第十二 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The north is the region where heaven and earth close and store. Its land is high; [the people] live on hills; the wind is cold and the ice freezing. Its people enjoy living in the open and eat milk products; the zang become cold and illnesses of fullness arise. Their treatment is best with moxibustion and cauterization. Thus moxibustion and cauterization too came from the north.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The north ... the wind is cold and the ice freezing"] It follows the qi of winter.

[On "Its people enjoy living in the open ... illnesses of fullness arise"] The water is cold and the ice freezing; therefore the zang become cold and illness arises.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* lacks the character 満 "fullness".

[On "Their treatment is best with moxibustion and cauterization"] Burning with fire and mugwort is called moxibustion and cauterization.

[On "Thus moxibustion and cauterization too came from the north"] The people of the north still properly practise this method.

## Notes

The north is a land of closing and storing, high and cold; its people live in the open and eat milk products, and their zang become cold with illnesses of fullness. Moxibustion, suited to treating them, came from the north.
- According to the Song editors, the *Jiayi jing* lacks the character "fullness".
