# 素問 異法方宜論篇第十二 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The south is where heaven and earth grow and nourish, the place where yang is abundant. Its land is low, the water and soil weak, where fog and dew gather. Its people like sour things and eat fermented food. Therefore its people all have fine skin texture and a red complexion, and their illnesses are cramps and impediment. Their treatment is best with fine needles. Thus the nine needles too came from the south.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The south ... where fog and dew gather"] It follows the qi of summer. When the land is low, water flows down and collects there; with much water the soil is weak, and fog and dew gather.

[On "Its people like sour things and eat fermented food"] This means what they eat is not fragrant.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi says: "It means they eat fish."

[On "Therefore its people all have fine skin texture ... cramps and impediment"] Sour flavour contracts; therefore all have dense, fine flesh texture. It is a place of abundant yang; therefore the complexion is red. Damp qi fills within and hot qi presses within; therefore the sinews cramp and the vessels are impeded.

[On "Their treatment is best with fine needles"] 微 means fine and small. Fine, small needles regulate the decline and abundance of the vessels.

[On "Thus the nine needles too came from the south"] The people of the south greatly revere them.

## Notes

The south is a land of growth and nourishment where yang is abundant; it is low and damp with much fog and dew. Its people like sour things and eat fermented food, have fine red skin, and suffer from cramps and impediment. Fine needles (the nine needles), suited to treating them, came from the south.
- "Eat fermented food" (食胕): Wang Bing takes it as eating food that is not fragrant (spoiled); Quan Yuanqi, cited by the Song editors, takes it as eating fish.
