# 素問 異法方宜論篇第十二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "When physicians treat illness, for one and the same illness the treatments differ, yet all cure it. Why is this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] "Differ" refers to needles and stones, moxibustion and cauterization, toxic drugs, *daoyin*, and massage.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: why do different treatments for the same illness all cure it?
- Wang Bing lists the different treatments: needles and stones, moxibustion, toxic drugs, *daoyin* and massage, which are assigned to the five directions in the following units.
