# 素問 異法方宜論篇第十二 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore the sage combines them in treatment, and each obtains what suits it. That the treatments differ yet the illnesses are all cured is because he grasps the nature of the illness and knows the general principles of treatment."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Therefore the sage combines them in treatment, and each obtains what suits it"] Using them according to the direction, each obtains what is fitting; only the method of the sage can do this.

[On "because he grasps the nature of the illness and knows the general principles of treatment"] It is so because he penetrates natures and keeps the reasons in mind.

## Notes

The sage combines the treatments of the five directions and chooses what suits each case, so that although treatments differ all are cured; this ends the chapter. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 002, ends here.
- Wang Bing's short note 達性懐故然 is unclear; it is translated literally. Daizhige (*Huangdi neijing suwen*) has "Juan 4" in place of this note.
