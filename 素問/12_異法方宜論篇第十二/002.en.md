# 素問 異法方宜論篇第十二 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "The lie of the land makes it so.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The lie of the land makes it so"] This means following the generation, growth, gathering and storage of heaven and earth and the configuration of high and low, dry and damp.

## Notes

Qibo answers that differences in treatment are due to the lie of the land. The following units describe, for each of the five directions, the land, the people's way of life, their illnesses and the treatment suited to them. Qibo's speech continues to unit 008.
