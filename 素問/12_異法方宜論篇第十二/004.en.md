# 素問 異法方宜論篇第十二 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The west is a region of metal and jade, a place of sand and stone, where heaven and earth gather and draw in. Its people live on high ground with much wind; the water and soil are hard and strong. Its people do not wear [silk or cotton] clothes but use coarse wool and grass mats; its people eat rich food and are fat. Therefore evil cannot harm their bodies, and their illnesses arise from within. Their treatment is best with toxic drugs. Thus toxic drugs too came from the west.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The west is a region of metal and jade ... where heaven and earth gather and draw in"] It follows the qi of autumn. "Draw in" means pulling so as to contract.

[On "Its people live on high ground with much wind; the water and soil are hard and strong"] Their dwellings are like hills, hence "live on high ground"; the qi of metal is austere and killing, hence the water and soil are hard and strong.
Song editors: In general the west is high land and the people live on high hills, hence much wind; it is not necessarily that their dwellings are like hills.

[On "Its people do not wear clothes ... eat rich food and are fat"] They do not wear silk or cotton, hence "do not wear clothes". 褐 means woollen cloth; 薦 means fine grass. 華 means fresh and fine foods such as butter, curds, bones and meat. Because they eat fresh and fine food, their bodies are fat.

[On "Therefore evil cannot harm their bodies, and their illnesses arise from within"] The water and soil are hard and strong, and their food is rich and fatty; their skin and interstices are closed and sealed and their blood and qi full; therefore evil cannot harm them. "Within" means excess of joy, anger, grief, worry and fear, and of eating and drinking and sexual activity.
Song editors: 悲 "grief" is written 思 "thought" in one version, and should be 思; this is already set out in the note to the *Yinyang yingxiang dalun*.

[On "Their treatment is best with toxic drugs"] What can attack illness is called a toxic drug. Because their blood and qi are abundant, their flesh firm, their food rich and their water and soil strong, their illnesses are best treated with toxic drugs. The drugs prescribed to control illness are herbs, trees, insects, fish, birds and beasts — all that can remove illness.

[On "Thus toxic drugs too came from the west"] The methods of the people of the west are still followed today.

## Notes

The west is a land of metal and jade, sand and stone; its people live on high ground with much wind, use wool and grass, and eat rich food and are fat. They are hard for external evils to harm, and their illnesses arise from within. Toxic drugs (drug therapy), suited to treating them, came from the west.
- "Toxic drugs": Wang Bing says that whatever can attack illness is a toxic drug, including all herbs, trees, insects, fish, birds and beasts that remove illness.
