# 素問 異法方宜論篇第十二 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus the region of the east is where heaven and earth first give birth. It is a land of fish and salt, on the seashore beside the water. Its people eat fish and like salty food; all are content in their place and find their food delicious. Fish makes a person hot within, and salt overcomes the blood. Therefore its people are all dark in complexion with coarse skin texture, and their illnesses are all abscesses and ulcers. Their treatment is best with stone needles. Thus stone needles too came from the east.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Thus the region of the east is where heaven and earth first give birth"] It follows the qi of spring.

[On "It is a land of fish and salt, on the seashore beside the water"] The land of fish and salt is the profit of the sea. 浜 is the water's edge; they live close to it according to their occupation.

[On "Its people eat fish ... find their food delicious"] Because their profits are rich, they dwell in peace; because they indulge in its flavours, they find their food delicious.

[On "Fish makes a person hot within, and salt overcomes the blood"] That fish brings out sores is proof that it makes one hot within; that salt brings on thirst is a sign that it overcomes the blood.

[On "Therefore its people are all dark ... abscesses and ulcers"] The blood is weak and hot; therefore they are prone to abscesses and ulcers.

[On "Their treatment is best with stone needles"] Stone needles are needles made of stone. The *Shanhai jing* says, "On the mountain of the Gao clan there is a jade-like stone that can be made into needles" — these are stone needles.
Song editors: 氏 is written 伐 in one version.

[On "Thus stone needles too came from the east"] People of the east still use them today.

## Notes

The east is a land of fish and salt; its people like fish and salty food, are dark with coarse skin and suffer from abscesses and ulcers. The stone needles suited to treating them came from the east.
- "Stone needles": Wang Bing takes them as needles made of stone, citing the *Shanhai jing*.
