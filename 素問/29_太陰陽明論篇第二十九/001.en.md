# 素問 太陰陽明論篇第二十九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "The greater yin and *yangming* are exterior and interior, the vessels of the spleen and stomach. Why are the illnesses they produce different?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The greater yin and *yangming* ... Why are the illnesses they produce different?"] The spleen and stomach, zang and fu, both correspond to earth, yet the illnesses that arise differ; hence he asks about the difference.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: why do the illnesses of the greater yin (spleen) and *yangming* (stomach), which are exterior and interior, differ?
