# 素問 太陰陽明論篇第二十九 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The foot greater yin is the three yin; its vessel passes through the stomach, belongs to the spleen and connects with the throat; therefore the greater yin moves qi to the three yin for [the stomach]. The *yangming* is the exterior, the sea of the five zang and six fu; it too moves qi to the three yang for [the spleen]. The zang and fu each receive qi from the *yangming* through their channels; therefore [the spleen] moves the fluids for the stomach. If the four limbs cannot receive the qi of water and grain, they decline more each day, the yin pathways are not free, and the sinews, bones and flesh have no qi by which to live; therefore they cannot be used."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The *yangming* is the exterior"] The stomach is the exterior of the spleen.

[On "The zang and fu each receive qi ... therefore they cannot be used"] This again clarifies the principle that the spleen governs the four limbs.

## Notes

The spleen and stomach move qi to the three yin and three yang through the channels; the spleen's governance of the four limbs is explained again. This ends the chapter.
