# 素問 太陰陽明論篇第二十九 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why does the spleen not govern a season?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why does the spleen not govern a season?"] The liver governs spring, the heart summer, the lung autumn, the kidney winter; the four zang all have proper correspondences, but the spleen has nothing proper to govern.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why only the spleen has no corresponding season.
