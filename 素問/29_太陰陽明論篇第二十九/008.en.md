# 素問 太陰陽明論篇第二十九 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The spleen is earth and governs the centre. It constantly nurtures the four zang through the four seasons, lodging and governing for eighteen days in each; it does not alone govern a season. The spleen zang constantly clings to the essence of stomach-earth. Earth generates the myriad things and models on heaven and earth; therefore it reaches above and below, to head and feet, and does not govern a season."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The spleen is earth ... does not govern a season"] 治 means govern. 著 means constantly bound and attached to the stomach. The qi of earth dominates for eighteen days lodged at the end of each of the four seasons; thus each of the five phases dominates seventy-two days, completing the days of a year. Outwardly it governs the four [last months of the] seasons; in the person it corresponds inwardly to the hands and feet.

## Notes

The spleen (earth) does not govern a particular season but dominates during the last eighteen days of each season (the idea of *doyō*).
- Daizhige (重广补注) writes 上下至紛 for 上下至頭足.
