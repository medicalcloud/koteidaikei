# 素問 太陰陽明論篇第二十九 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The four limbs all receive qi from the stomach, but [the stomach qi] cannot reach the channels; only through the spleen can they receive it. Now if the spleen is ill and cannot move the fluids for the stomach, the four limbs cannot receive the qi of water and grain, the qi declines day by day, the pathways of the vessels are not free, and the sinews, bones and flesh have no qi by which to live; therefore they cannot be used."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The four limbs all receive qi from the stomach, but [the stomach qi] cannot reach the channels"] Song editors: The *Taisu* writes 径至 "reach directly" for 至経. Yang Shangshan says, "The stomach supplies the four limbs with water and grain but cannot reach the four limbs directly; it must obtain the fluids of water and grain through the spleen to nourish and defend the four limbs."

[On "only through the spleen can they receive it"] The spleen qi spreads and transforms the essential fluids of water and grain, and only then can the four limbs receive them.

## Notes

The four limbs receive the stomach qi through the spleen; therefore when the spleen is ill the limbs do not work.
- According to the Song editors, the *Taisu* writes 径至 "reach directly" for 至経.
- 津腋 in the Song editors' note is presumably an error for 津液; the translation gives "fluids".
