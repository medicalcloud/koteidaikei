# 素問 太陰陽明論篇第二十九 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "The spleen and stomach are merely joined by a membrane; how can [the spleen] move the fluids for it?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The spleen and stomach are merely joined by a membrane"] Song editors: The *Taisu* reads "are opposed through the alarm points". Yang Shangshan says, "The spleen is yin and the stomach yang; the spleen is inner and the stomach outer; their positions differ; therefore they are opposed."

## Notes

Huangdi asks how the spleen moves fluids for the stomach. There is no note by Wang Bing.
