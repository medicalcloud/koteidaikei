# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Ascent and descent above and below, shifting to the proper position and withdrawing from position, each have their regular principles; when above or below fails to advance, this is called losing hold. Hence when the meeting of qi loses and changes position, the meeting of qi changes, the changes are abnormal, the four [seasons] lose their order, the myriad transformations are unsettled, and it changes into people's illness."

## Notes

The answer that failure of the qi above and below to advance in ascent, descent, shifting and withdrawing is called losing hold. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 四失序 in the main text is read in the sense of 四時失序 ("the four seasons lose their order").
