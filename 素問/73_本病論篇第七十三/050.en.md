# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §50 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In all the five losses of hold above, heaven is depleted and the person is depleted. When the spirit wanders and loses hold of its position, the five corpse-ghosts invade people and cause sudden death; this is called corpse reversal. When a person offends so that the five spirits change their positions, the light of the spirit is not complete. Not only corpse-ghosts: whenever any pathogen offends, it is always because the spirit has lost hold of its position. This is what is meant by 'Those who keep hold live, those who lose hold die; those who obtain the spirit flourish, those who lose the spirit perish.'"

## Notes

A summary of the five losses of hold, closing the chapter. Only four viscera are actually given in units 46–49. Qibo's speech ends here. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
