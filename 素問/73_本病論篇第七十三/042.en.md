# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suppose that in the yang year *gengchen*, which should be excessive, the heavenly numbers of *jimao* are in surplus: although the year has changed to *gengchen*, *Yangming* still governs heaven, while the earth has already shifted to its proper position with greater yin controlling the earth, and last year's lesser yin has become the right intervening [qi]. So heaven is *Yangming* and earth greater yin, and earth does not serve heaven. *Yi* and *ji* meet; the metal movement is greatly deficient and instead suffers fire's prevalence, so it is not excessive. In the pipe of Guxian, great *shang* does not respond; when fire prevails heat transforms, and when water retaliates cold punishes. Here *yi* and *geng* lose their hold, and in the following three years it transforms into metal epidemic; quickly, it arrives in *renwu*; slowly, in *guiwei*. For its scale and good or bad, infer from the heavenly numbers of the year and the Taiyi. Again, in a *gengchen* year, even if *geng* reaches *chen* and governs heaven in accord with the exchange of control, when the lower *yiwei* has not yet shifted to its proper position and the earth's *jiawu* lesser yin has not yet withdrawn, *yi* and *geng* do not unite in virtue. The soft stem of the lower *yiwei* loses its hard, and the metal movement too is slightly deficient, slightly overcome, perhaps without retaliation; in the following three years it transforms into pestilence, called metal pestilence, whose form is like metal epidemic. The treatment is as before.

## Notes

The case of a *gengchen* year (metal movement). The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- In the base (Wikisource plain text), 乙良 is emended to 乙庚 and 且三年 to 後三年 ("the following three years"), following the Daizhige texts (*Chongguang buzhu Huangdi neijing suwen*, *Huangdi neijing suwen*, *Huangdi neijing suwen yipian*). 巳 in 乙巳相会 is also emended to 己 (the texts also have 巳, but it is understood, as in unit 40, as the previous year's stem meeting the lower stem; not verified).
