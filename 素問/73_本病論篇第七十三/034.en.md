# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When *yangming* does not withdraw from its position, clear cold arises in spring, plants flourish late, cold and heat alternate, and people suffer vomiting, sudden diarrhea, food and drink not going down, dry stool, inability to raise the four limbs, and dim vision with tremors and dizziness.

## Notes

The case of *yangming* not withdrawing from its position. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
