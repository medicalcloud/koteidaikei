# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When *yangming* does not shift to its proper position, summer heat transforms first and austere killing comes later, plants flourish instead, and people suffer cold and heat, stuffy nose and sneezing, broken skin hair, withered and scorched nails, and in severe cases panting and coughing with high breathing, sorrow and joylessness. When the transformation of heat spreads and the transformation of dryness has not yet decreed, clear sharpness has not yet prevailed, and lung metal falls ill again.

## Notes

The case of *yangming* not shifting to its proper position. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
