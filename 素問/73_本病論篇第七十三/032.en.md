# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When greater yin does not withdraw from its position, 而取 cold and heat are out of season, dust spreads darkly, the decree of damp does not leave, and people suffer lack of strength in the four limbs, food and drink not going down, pouring diarrhea, dribbling and fullness, cold feet and shins, impotence, blockage, incontinence and frequent urination.

## Notes

The case of greater yin not withdrawing from its position. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 而取 in the main text corresponds to 即 in the other units, but its meaning is unclear; it is left untranslated.
