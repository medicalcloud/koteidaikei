# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "On ascent and descent failing to advance I have clearly understood the principle. I wish to hear about shifting to the proper position. Can you make it clear?"

## Notes

A question about shifting to the proper position. There are no notes.
