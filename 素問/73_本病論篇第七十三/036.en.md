# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "The early and late of heaven's years I already know. I wish to hear about the numbers of the earth. May I hear them?"

## Notes

A question about the earth (the spring in earth). There are no notes.
