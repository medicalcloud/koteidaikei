# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When lesser yin does not shift to its proper position, cold qi does not withdraw, spring is cold and cold persists afterward, warmth is out of season, and people suffer cold and heat, vexing pain of the four limbs, and rigidity of the lower back and spine. Although wood qi is in surplus, its position does not go beyond sovereign fire.

## Notes

The case of lesser yin not shifting to its proper position. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
