# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus in *si* and *hai* years, when sovereign fire is about to ascend to heaven and its governor is blocked by Tianpeng, [Tianpeng] prevails and it does not advance. Again, if *jueyin* has not yet shifted to its proper position, lesser yin cannot yet ascend to heaven. When the water movement arrives in the middle, sovereign fire seeks to ascend but the middle water movement represses it, and it does not advance: clear cold arises again and chill appears morning and evening. People suffer hidden yang with vexing heat arising within, alarm and palpitations of the heart-spirit, and alternating cold and heat. When over time it becomes constraint, violent heat arrives, with red wind and 瞳 [swollen?] haze; it transforms into epidemics, warm pestilence arises with the warmth, red qi becomes evident and transforms into fire epidemics. All have vexation with dry thirst; when the thirst is severe, treat by draining and it can be stopped.

## Notes

The case of sovereign fire unable to ascend in *si* and *hai* years. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 瞳 in 赤風瞳翳 in the main text may be an error for 腫, judging from 赤風気腫翳 in unit 9 and 太虚腫翳 in chapter 71, but this has not been verified.
