# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus in *zi* and *wu* years, when greater yin is about to ascend to heaven and its governor is blocked by Tianchong, [Tianchong] prevails and it does not advance. Again, when it meets *renzi* and the wood movement arrives before heaven['s time], the middle wood movement represses it; ascending to heaven, it does not advance: wind and dust rise on all sides, dust rises from time to time and darkens, and rain and damp do not transform. People suffer wind reversal with surging drool, hemiplegia with loss of use, and distension and fullness. When over time it becomes hidden constraint, yellow dust transforms into epidemics; people die young, with jaundice of the face, limbs and bowels, and fullness and blockage; the decree of damp does not spread and the transformation of rain is slight.

## Notes

The case of greater yin unable to ascend in *zi* and *wu* years. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
