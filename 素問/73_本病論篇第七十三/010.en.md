# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus in *yin* and *shen* years, when *yangming* is about to ascend to heaven and its governor is blocked by Tianying, [Tianying] prevails and it does not advance. Again, when it meets *wushen* or *wuyin* and the fire movement arrives before heaven['s time], metal seeks to ascend to heaven but the fire movement represses it, and it does not advance: timely rain does not fall, west winds rise often, and salty crust and dryness arise. People suffer heat above, panting and coughing, and overflowing of blood. When over time it transforms into constraint, white dust clouds like fog, clarity gives rise to killing qi, and people suffer rib-side fullness, sorrow and grief, stuffy nose and sneezing from cold, dry throat, cracked hands and dry skin.

## Notes

The case of *yangming* unable to ascend in *yin* and *shen* years. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
