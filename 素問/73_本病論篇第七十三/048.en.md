# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §48 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a person sits long on damp ground or forces himself to enter water, the kidneys are harmed. The kidneys are the office of vigor; skill and ingenuity come from them. When there is thus triple depletion, the kidney spirit loses its hold, the spirit and will lose their position, and the light of the spirit does not gather; if it then meets a year of insufficient water, or *xin* not meeting its tally, or a *bing* year that has lost hold, or depletion of greater yang controlling heaven, the yellow corpse-ghost arrives and appears to him and causes sudden death.

## Notes

The case of triple depletion of the kidneys, violated by the yellow corpse-ghost. Unlike the other viscera, the condition of heaven's depletion is abbreviated. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
