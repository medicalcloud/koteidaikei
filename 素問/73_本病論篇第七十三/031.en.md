# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When lesser yin does not withdraw from its position, warmth arises in spring and winter, hibernating creatures appear early, plants sprout and grow, and people suffer diaphragm heat, dry throat, overflowing of blood, fright, red and rough urination, erysipelas, sores and lingering toxin.

## Notes

The case of lesser yin not withdrawing from its position. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
