# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "It is caused by prevailing [qi] meeting and repressing. Thus in *chen* and *xu* years, when wood qi is about to ascend and its governor meets Tianzhu, [Tianzhu] prevails and it does not advance. When it also meets *gengxu*, the metal movement comes before heaven['s time], the middle movement prevails over it, and suddenly it does not advance. When the wood movement is about to ascend to heaven and metal represses it, so that it ascends but does not advance, coolness arises and wind is scant, there is austere killing in spring, dew and frost fall again, and plants wither. People suffer early outbreaks of warm epidemics, dryness of the throat, fullness of the four limbs, and pain in all the joints. When over time it transforms into constraint, great winds smash and break, [trees] snap and fall with a chaotic roar, and people suffer sudden stroke, hemiplegia, and numbness of the hands and feet.

## Notes

The weather and illnesses when wood cannot ascend in *chen* and *xu* years. Units 6–11 describe the six years in which [the qi] cannot ascend. Qibo's speech continues through unit 11. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
