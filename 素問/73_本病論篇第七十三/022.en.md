# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When *jueyin* does not shift to its proper position, wind and warmth are out of season, flowers and plants wither, and people suffer dribbling urination, turning of the eye connectors, cramps, irascibility and red urine. Wind seeks to decree but cold still does not leave; warmth is not proper and the propriety of spring misses its time.

## Notes

The case of *jueyin* not shifting to its proper position. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
