# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus in *mao* and *you* years, when greater yin is about to descend to the earth and its governor is blocked by Dicang, [Dicang] prevails and it does not enter. Again, if lesser yang has not yet withdrawn from its position, greater yin cannot yet descend. Or when the wood movement arrives, the wood movement receives it, and it descends but does not go down: yellow clouds appear and blue-green rosy clouds become evident, stifling steam arises with great wind, fog clouds and dust prevail, and [trees] snap and fall. When it does not descend for long and, hidden, transforms into constraint, in heaven there is dust and yellow qi, on earth damp and steam spread, and people suffer inability to raise the four limbs, clouding dizziness, joint pain, and abdominal fullness with chest congestion.

## Notes

The case of greater yin unable to descend in *mao* and *you* years. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
