# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suppose that in the yang year *jiazi* the earth movement is severely blocked, as when the heavenly numbers of *guihai* are in surplus: although the year has changed to *jiazi*, *jueyin* still governs heaven, while the earth has already shifted to its proper position with *yangming* in the spring, and last year's lesser yang has become the right intervening [qi]. So [heaven is] *jueyin* and earth *yangming*, and they do not harmonize and serve each other. *Gui* and 巳 [*ji*] meet; the earth movement [is nominally] excessive, but depleted, it instead suffers wood's prevalence, so it is not excessive. How can one speak of the earth movement as excessive? Moreover Huangzhong [pitch] does not respond and it is severely blocked; when wood prevails metal retaliates in turn; when metal has retaliated and lesser yin arrives, wood's prevalence is like fire and metal's retaliation is slight. In such a case *jia* 已 [*ji*] lose their hold, and in the following three years it transforms into earth epidemic; late, it arrives in *dingmao*; early, in *bingyin*. For its scale and its good or bad, infer from heaven and earth and examine it by the Taiyi. Again, in a *jiazi* year, even if *jia* reaches *zi* and matches, and governs heaven in accord with the exchange of control, when the lower *jimao* has not yet shifted to its proper position and *wuyin* lesser yang has not yet withdrawn, then too *jia* and 已 [*ji*] below 有合 [do not match]. The earth movement is not excessive; wood takes advantage of the depletion to prevail over earth, and metal next comes to retaliate and prevail over it; this is instead a pathogenic transformation. Only yin and yang, heaven and earth, differ; their scale and good or bad all follow the same principle as heaven and earth.

## Notes

The case in which a *jiazi* year (earth movement) becomes insufficient because the previous controller of heaven has not withdrawn, leading to earth epidemic or pestilence. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 甲已 in the main text is read as 甲己, and 巳 in 癸巳相会 as 己 (understood, like 乙辛相会, 丁辛相合会 and 丁癸相会 in units 41–44, as the previous year's stem meeting the lower stem).
- 亦甲已下有合也 seems, from 亦丙辛不合徳也 in unit 41, to mean "do not match", but the Daizhige texts (*Chongguang buzhu Huangdi neijing suwen*, *Huangdi neijing suwen*, *Huangdi neijing suwen yipian*) have the same characters, and this has not been verified.
