# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus in *yin* and *shen* years, when lesser yin is about to descend to the earth and its governor is blocked by Dixuan, [Dixuan] prevails and it does not enter. Again, when it meets *bingshen* or *bingyin* and the water movement is excessive and arrives before heaven['s time], sovereign fire seeks to descend but the water movement receives it, and it descends but does not go down: red clouds barely appear when black qi arises instead; warmth seems about to unfold, but cold constantly spreads snow, chill arises again, and the clouds of heaven are dismal and bleak. When it does not descend for long and, hidden, transforms into constraint, after cold prevails there is heat again, red wind transforms into epidemics, and people suffer red face, vexed heart, headache and dizziness. Red qi becomes evident and warm disease is about to arise.

## Notes

The case of lesser yin unable to descend in *yin* and *shen* years. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
