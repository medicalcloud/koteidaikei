# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §45 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When human qi is insufficient and heaven's qi is depleted, the human spirit loses its hold, the light of the spirit does not gather, and evil ghosts invade people, causing premature death. May I hear about this?"

## Notes

A question about ghosts invading when both humans and heaven are depleted. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
