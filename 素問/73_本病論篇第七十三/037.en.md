# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The rule for shifting to the proper position below the earth, ascending to heaven, and withdrawing from position failing to advance is that, in what the earth's soil produces and transforms, the transformations of the myriad things lose their time."

## Notes

The answer that when the earth's qi fails to shift, ascend or withdraw, the transformation of the earth's products loses its time. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
