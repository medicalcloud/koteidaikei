# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What a thorough question! This is called the subtle meaning of heaven and earth; let me set out this way in full. What has ascended must descend. After reaching heaven for three years, in the next year it must descend; descending, it enters the earth and first becomes the left intervening [qi]. Such ascent and descent, going and coming, are named the six records.

## Notes

What has ascended descends after three years; the six years in which [the qi] cannot descend are then described. Qibo's speech continues through unit 19. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
