# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus in *chou* and *wei* years, when lesser yang is about to ascend to heaven and its governor is blocked by Tianpeng, [Tianpeng] prevails and it does not advance. Again, if greater yin has not yet shifted to its proper position, lesser yin [yang] cannot yet ascend to heaven. When the water movement arrives, ascending to heaven it does not advance: cold ice spreads instead, chill as in winter; water dries up again, ice forms again; warmth arises suddenly, but cold spreads in summer, and cold and warmth are out of season. People suffer hidden yang within, vexing heat arising in the center, alarm and fright of the heart-spirit, and alternating struggles of cold and heat. When over time it becomes constraint, violent heat arises, with red wind and swollen, hazy qi; it transforms into epidemic pestilence, and further into hidden heat with internal vexation, numbness giving rise to reversal, and in severe cases overflowing of blood.

## Notes

The case of lesser yang unable to ascend in *chou* and *wei* years. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 少陰 in 即少陰未升天也 in the main text is read as 少陽 from the context.
