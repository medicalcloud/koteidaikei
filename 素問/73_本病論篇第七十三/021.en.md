# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Properly controlling the central position is called shifting to the proper position. When the controller of heaven cannot shift to its proper position, it is because, after the day of exchange of control has passed, the previous controller of heaven is excessive and has surplus days; the old one continues to govern heaven's numbers, and the new controller of heaven cannot yet shift to its proper position.

## Notes

The answer explaining what shifting to the proper position is and why it may fail; each of the six qi failing to shift is then described. Qibo's speech continues through unit 27. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
