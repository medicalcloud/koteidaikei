# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When lesser yang does not withdraw from its position, heat arises in spring and summer heat transforms later, winter is warm without freezing, flowing water does not ice over, hibernating creatures come out, and people suffer shortage of qi, alternating cold and heat, blood in the stool, heat above, hardness and fullness of the lower abdomen, and red, turbid urine; in severe cases blood overflows.

## Notes

The case of lesser yang not withdrawing from its position. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
