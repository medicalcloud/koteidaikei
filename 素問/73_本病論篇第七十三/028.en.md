# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "On early and late shifting to the proper position you have told me the meaning. I wish to hear about withdrawing from position. Can you make it clear?"

## Notes

A question about withdrawing from position. There are no notes.
