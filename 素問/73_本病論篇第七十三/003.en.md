# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "I wish to hear why ascent and descent fail to advance. How can changes in the meeting of qi be clearly known?"

## Notes

A question about why ascent and descent fail to advance and how changes in the meeting of qi are known. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
