# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus in *zi* and *wu* years, when greater yang is about to descend to the earth and its governor is blocked by Difu, [Difu] prevails over it, and it descends but does not enter. Again, when it meets an excessive earth movement arriving before heaven['s time], the earth movement receives it, and it descends but does not enter: black qi becomes evident in the sky, dark, gloomy, bleak and dismal; yellow dust is barely spread when damp spreads; the transformation of cold decrees the qi, and steaming damp decrees again. When it does not descend for long and, hidden, transforms into constraint, people suffer great reversal, heaviness and fatigue of the four limbs, impotence and lack of strength; heaven spreads deep yin, and steaming damp arises in alternation."

## Notes

The case of greater yang unable to descend in *zi* and *wu* years. Qibo's speech ends here. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
