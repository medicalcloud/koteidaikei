# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suppose that in the yang year *wushen*, which should be excessive, the heavenly numbers of *dingwei* are excessive: although the year has changed to *wushen*, greater yin still controls heaven, while the earth has already shifted to its proper position with *jueyin* in the spring, and last year's *renxu* greater yang has withdrawn to become the right intervening [qi]. So heaven is *dingwei* and earth *guihai*, and earth does not serve heaven's transformation. *Ding* and *gui* meet; the fire movement is greatly depleted and instead suffers water's prevalence, so it is not excessive. In the pipe of Yize, the upper great *zhi* does not respond. Here *wu* and *gui* lose their meeting, and in the following three years it transforms into epidemic; quickly, it arrives in *gengxu*. For its scale and good or bad, infer from the heavenly numbers of the year it arrives and the Taiyi. Again, in a *wushen* year, even if *wu* reaches *shen* and governs heaven in accord with the exchange of control, when the lower *guihai* has not yet shifted to its proper position and the earth's lower *renxu* greater yang has not yet withdrawn, *wu* and *gui* [亥] are seen not yet to unite in virtue. The soft stem of the lower *gui* loses its hard, and the fire movement is seen to be slightly depleted, slightly overcome, perhaps without retaliation; in the following three years it transforms into pestilence, called fire pestilence. The treatment is as before; the method of treating it is to cool and drain."

## Notes

The case of a *wushen* year (fire movement). Unlike the other years, the year of late arrival is not given. Qibo's speech ends here. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 亥 in 戊癸亥未合徳 in the main text is taken as an interpolation, since the Daizhige texts (*Chongguang buzhu Huangdi neijing suwen*, *Huangdi neijing suwen*, *Huangdi neijing suwen yipian*) have 戊癸未合徳.
