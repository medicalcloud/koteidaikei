# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "I have heard that the two *jiazi* [cycles] of heaven and earth, the ten stems and twelve branches, form the warp and weft of heaven and earth above and below, and that their numbers may shift in alternation and lose hold of their positions. Can you make this clear?"

## Notes

A question about the two *jiazi* cycles of heaven and earth losing their positions. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
