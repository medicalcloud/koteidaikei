# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "I wish to hear the reasons why, in the meeting of qi, [qi] meet, prevail and repress, and how severe the resulting illnesses of the people are."

## Notes

A question about the reasons for prevailing and repressing and the severity of resulting illness. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
