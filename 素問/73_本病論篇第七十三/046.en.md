# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When one of a person's five viscera is insufficient and moreover meets a depletion of heaven, it responds to the arrival of pathogens. When a person worries, grieves and ponders, the heart is harmed. When moreover lesser yin controls heaven with insufficient heavenly numbers and greater yin arrives in succession as intervening [qi], this is called depletion of heaven. Here human qi and heaven's qi are both depleted. When moreover one is startled and the essence is snatched away, and sweat comes from the heart, there is thus triple depletion, and the spirit illumination loses its hold. The heart is the office of the 群 [sovereign] ruler; spirit illumination comes from it. When the spirit loses hold of its position, it wanders to the upper cinnabar field, which lies below the Niwan palace of the Emperor Taiyi, the Emperor-群 [Lord]. Once the spirit has lost its hold, the light of the spirit does not gather; if it then meets a year of insufficient fire, the black corpse-ghost appears to him and causes sudden death.

## Notes

The case of triple depletion of the heart, violated by the black corpse-ghost. The spleen, kidneys and liver follow (no section on the lungs appears). Qibo's speech continues through unit 50. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 群主 in the main text is read as 君主. 在帝太一帝群泥丸宮一下 is read as 在帝太一帝君泥丸宮下, as in the Daizhige texts (*Chongguang buzhu Huangdi neijing suwen*, *Huangdi neijing suwen*, *Huangdi neijing suwen yipian*).
