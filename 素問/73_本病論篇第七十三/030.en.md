# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When *jueyin* does not withdraw from its position, great winds rise early, timely rain does not fall, the decree of damp does not transform, and people suffer warm epidemics, crippling, and wind arising; all have joint pain, pain of the head and eyes, hidden heat with inner vexation, and a dry throat with desire to drink.

## Notes

The case of *jueyin* not withdrawing from its position. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
