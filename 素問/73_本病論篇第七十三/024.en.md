# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When greater yin does not shift to its proper position, clouds and rain lose their decree, the myriad things wither and scorch, what should be born does not come forth, and people suffer swelling and fullness of the hands, feet and joints, water swelling of the large abdomen, chest congestion with inability to eat, swill diarrhea, rib-side fullness, and inability to raise the four limbs. The transformation of rain seeks to decree, but heat still governs, warming the qi; it is excessive and gives no moisture.

## Notes

The case of greater yin not shifting to its proper position. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
