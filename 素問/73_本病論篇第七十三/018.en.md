# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus in *si* and *hai* years, when *yangming* is about to descend to the earth and its governor is blocked by Ditong, [Ditong] 用 [prevails] and it does not enter. Again, when greater yang has not yet withdrawn from its position, *yangming* cannot descend. When the fire movement arrives, the fire movement receives it and it does not go down: the sky is clear and austere, yet red qi becomes evident and warm heat arises instead; people are all confused and weary, sleep restlessly at night, have a dry throat and want to drink, with anguished heat and inner vexation. Though the sky is clear morning and evening, warmth arises again. When it does not descend for long and, hidden, transforms into constraint, the sky is clear and thinly cold, white qi arises in the distance, and people suffer tremors and dizziness, stiff and numb hands and feet, pain in both rib-sides, and 満目然 [blurred vision?].

## Notes

The case of *yangming* unable to descend in *si* and *hai* years. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 用 in 用而不入 in the main text is read in the sense of 勝 ("prevail"), judging from 勝之不入 in the other units, but this has not been verified.
- 満目然 at the end of the main text is hard to construe; characters may be missing, but this has not been verified.
