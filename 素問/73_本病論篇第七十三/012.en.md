# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "On ascent failing to advance I have fully learned the meaning. I wish to hear about descent failing to go down. Can you make it clear?"

## Notes

A question about descent failing to go down. There are no notes.
