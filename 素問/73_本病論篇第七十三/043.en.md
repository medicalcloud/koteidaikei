# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §43 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suppose that in the yang year *renwu*, which should be excessive, the heavenly numbers of *xinsi* are in surplus: although the year has changed to *renwu*, *jueyin* still governs heaven, while the earth has already shifted to its proper position with *yangming* in the spring, and last year's *bingshen* lesser yang has become the right intervening [qi]. So heaven is *jueyin* and earth *yangming*, and earth does not serve heaven. *Ding* and *xin* meet; the wood movement is greatly depleted and instead suffers metal's prevalence, so it is not excessive. In the pipe of Ruibin, great *jue* does not respond; metal prevails with dryness, and fire transforms with heat in retaliation. If severe, quickly; if slight, slowly. For the scale and good or bad of the arriving epidemic, infer from the heavenly numbers of the year it arrives and the Taiyi. Again, even if *ren* reaches *wu* and governs in accord with the exchange of control, when the lower *dingyou* has not yet shifted to its proper position and the earth's lower *bingshen* lesser yang has not yet withdrawn, *ding* and *ren* are seen not to unite in virtue. The soft stem *ding* loses its 賜 [hard], and the wood movement too is slightly depleted, slightly overcome and slightly retaliated against; in the following three years it transforms into pestilence, called wood pestilence, whose form is like wind epidemic. The treatment is as before.

## Notes

The case of a *renwu* year (wood movement). The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 賜 in 失賜 in the main text is read as 剛 ("hard"), from 失剛 in the other units.
