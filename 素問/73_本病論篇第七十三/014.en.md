# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus in *chou* and *wei* years, when *jueyin* is about to descend to the earth and its governor is blocked by Dijing, [Dijing] prevails and it does not advance. Again, when lesser yin has not yet withdrawn from its position, *jueyin* has not yet descended. When the metal movement arrives in the middle, the metal movement receives it, it descends but does not go down, and repressed, it changes into constraint. When wood seeks to descend and the metal movement receives it, so that it descends but does not go down, blue-green dust is seen in the distance and white qi receives it, wind rises and dust darkens, clarity and dryness work killing, frost and dew fall again, and austere killing spreads its decree. When it does not descend for long and, repressed, transforms into constraint, wind and dryness hide each other; it is warm yet instead cool; when plants sprout, killing frost falls; hibernating creatures do not appear; one fears that coolness will harm the viscera.

## Notes

The case of *jueyin* unable to descend in *chou* and *wei* years. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
