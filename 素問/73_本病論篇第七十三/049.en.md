# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §49 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a person is resentful and angry and the qi counterflows upward and does not descend, the liver is harmed. When moreover *jueyin* controls heaven with insufficient heavenly numbers and lesser yin arrives in succession as intervening [qi], this is called depletion of heaven; this is called depletion of heaven and depletion of the person. When moreover one runs fast or is afraid and sweat comes from the liver [there is triple depletion]. The liver is the office of the general; planning comes from it. The spirit's position loses its hold and the light of the spirit does not gather; if it then meets a year of insufficient wood, or a *ding* year not matching its tally, or a *ren* year that has lost hold, or depletion of *jueyin* controlling heaven, the white corpse-ghost appears to him and causes sudden death.

## Notes

The case of triple depletion of the liver, violated by the white corpse-ghost. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
