# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §47 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a person [is damaged by] food and drink or fatigue, the spleen is harmed. When moreover greater yin controls heaven with insufficient heavenly numbers and lesser yang arrives in succession as intervening [qi], this is called depletion. Here human qi is depleted and heaven's qi is depleted. When moreover one eats to excessive fullness and sweat comes from the stomach, or has intercourse when drunk and full and sweat comes from the spleen, there is thus triple depletion, and the spleen spirit loses its hold. The spleen is the office of the remonstrating counselor; comprehensive wisdom comes from it. Once the spirit has lost its hold, the light of the spirit loses its position and does not gather; if it then meets a year of insufficient earth, or a 已 [*ji*] year or a *jia* year that has lost hold, or depletion of greater yin heaven, the blue-green corpse-ghost appears to him and causes sudden death.

## Notes

The case of triple depletion of the spleen, violated by the blue-green corpse-ghost. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 已年 in the main text is read as 己年.
