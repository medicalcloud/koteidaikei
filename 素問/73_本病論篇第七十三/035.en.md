# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When greater yang does not withdraw from its position, spring cold arises in summer, cold hail falls, deep yin darkens and clouds, the cold still does not leave in the second qi, and people suffer numbness and reversal, impotence, incontinence, pain in the lower back and knees; warm pestilence breaks out late."

## Notes

The case of greater yang not withdrawing from its position. Qibo's speech ends here. There are no notes.
