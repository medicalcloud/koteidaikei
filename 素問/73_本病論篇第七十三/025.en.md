# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When lesser yang does not shift to its proper position, blazing heat does not decree, seedlings and ears do not flourish, there is fierce heat in autumn, austere killing arrives late, frost and dew are out of season, and people suffer malaria, bone heat, palpitations and fright, and in severe cases overflowing of blood.

## Notes

The case of lesser yang not shifting to its proper position. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
