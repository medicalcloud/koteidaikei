# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What is called not withdrawing is when heaven's numbers are not yet finished, when heaven's numbers are in surplus; it is called spreading governance again, and so it is called governing heaven a second time. Heaven's decree remains as before and it does not withdraw from its position.

## Notes

The answer explaining what failure to withdraw is; each of the six qi failing to withdraw is then described. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
