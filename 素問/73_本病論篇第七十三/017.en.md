# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus in *chen* and *xu* years, when lesser yang is about to descend to the earth and its governor is blocked by Dixuan, [Dixuan] prevails and it does not enter. Again, when it meets an excessive water movement arriving before heaven['s time], the water movement receives it, and it descends but does not go down: red clouds barely appear when black qi arises instead; warmth is about to arise when cold qi suddenly arrives, and in severe cases there is ice and hail. When it does not descend for long and, hidden, transforms into constraint, after icy qi there is heat again, red wind transforms into epidemics, and people suffer red face, vexed heart, headache and dizziness. Red qi becomes evident and heat disease is about to arise.

## Notes

The case of lesser yang unable to descend in *chen* and *xu* years. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
