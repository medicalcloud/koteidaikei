# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus in *mao* and *you* years, when greater yang is about to ascend to heaven and its governor is blocked by Tianrui, [Tianrui] prevails and it does not advance. Again, if *yangming* has not yet shifted to its proper position, greater yang has not yet ascended to heaven. When the earth movement arrives, water seeks to ascend to heaven but the earth movement represses it, and it does not advance: there is damp with steaming heat, and cold arises between [heaven and earth]. People suffer pouring diarrhea and food not being digested. When over time it becomes constraint, cold comes and lodges in the heat, ice and hail suddenly arrive, and people suffer reversal counterflow with hiccup, heat arising within, qi numbness outside, aching pain of the feet and shins, and instead palpitations and anguished heat, sudden vexation and again reversal."

## Notes

The case of greater yang unable to ascend in *mao* and *you* years. Qibo's speech ends here. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
