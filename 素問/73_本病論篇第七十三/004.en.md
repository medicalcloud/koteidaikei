# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "A clear question! You are clear about the Way. Changes in the meeting of qi are called the mechanism of heaven and earth. When [qi] seeks to descend but cannot, it is because the earth's blockage punishes it. Again, when one of the five movements is excessive and arrives before heaven['s time], the meeting does not advance: when [qi] seeks to ascend but cannot, the middle movement represses it; when it seeks to descend but cannot, the middle movement represses it. Thus there are cases where ascent does not advance and descent does not go down, cases where descent does not go down but ascent reaches heaven, and cases where both ascent and descent fail to advance. Making such distinctions is the change in the meeting of qi; the changes differ, are never the same, and the disasters are slight or severe."

## Notes

The answer that the earth's blockages and an excessive middle movement repress ascent and descent. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
