# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When [*yangming*: interpolated] greater yang does not shift to its proper position, winter is clear and cold instead, its decree is shifted to spring, killing frost comes first and cold ice later, the sunlight governs again, chill does not arise, and people suffer the arrival of warm pestilence, blocked and dry throat, vexation and agitation with thirst, and panting with audible breath. The transformation of cold waits for dryness [to withdraw], but [dryness] still governs heaven's qi; [the qi] goes beyond and loses its order, and brings disaster to the people."

## Notes

The case of greater yang not shifting to its proper position. Qibo's speech ends here. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 陽明 in 陽明太陽不遷正 in the main text is taken as an interpolation, since the Daizhige *Huangdi neijing suwen yipian* has 太陽不遷正.
