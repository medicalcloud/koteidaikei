# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Losing position in alternation means that although the year is properly obtained, control of the proper position has not yet been obtained. Then the four seasons lose their regularity and great epidemics arise. [Note:] The *Xuanzhu miyu* says: 'Of the thirty yang years, excluding six years of heavenly punishment, there are twenty-four years of excess; apart from these six years, all function as excess.' [Here] the meaning is that it is not so. Now speaking of branches and positions alternating means that all can become insufficient.

## Notes

The beginning of the answer: losing position means obtaining the year's stem and branch without obtaining control of the proper position, so that even yang years can be insufficient. The part from 注 seems to be a note that has entered the main text (in the Daizhige *Huangdi neijing suwen yipian* the same words stand within the notes; translated with "[Note:]" supplied). Qibo's speech continues through unit 44. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
