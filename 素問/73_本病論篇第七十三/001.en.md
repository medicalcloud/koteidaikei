# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked, "The nine blockages of the heavenly origin I already know. I wish to hear about the meeting of qi: what is called losing hold?"

## Notes

A question about what "losing hold" means in the meeting of qi. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
