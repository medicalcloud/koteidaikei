# 素問 本病論篇第七十三 §41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suppose that in the yang year *bingyin*, which should be excessive, the heavenly numbers of *yichou* are in surplus: although the year has changed to *bingyin*, greater yin still governs heaven, while the earth has already shifted to its proper position with *jueyin* controlling the earth, and last year's greater yang has become the right intervening [qi]. So heaven is greater yin and earth *jueyin*, and earth does not serve heaven's transformation. *Yi* and *xin* meet; the water movement is greatly depleted and instead suffers earth's prevalence, so it is not excessive. In the pipe of Taicou, great *yu* does not respond; when earth prevails rain transforms, and when wood retaliates there is wind. Here *bing* and *xin* lose their meeting, and in the following three years it transforms into water epidemic; late, it arrives in *jisi*; early, in *wuchen*; if severe, quickly; if slight, slowly. For its scale and good or bad, infer from the numbers of heaven and earth and the Taiyi's travels through the palaces. Again, in a *bingyin* year, even if *bing* reaches *yin* and matches, and governs heaven in accord with the exchange of control, when *xinsi* has not yet shifted to its proper position and *gengchen* greater yang has not yet withdrawn, *bing* and *xin* also do not unite in virtue. The water movement is slightly depleted and slightly overcome, perhaps with retaliation, and in the following three years it transforms into pestilence, called water pestilence, whose form is like water epidemic. The treatment is as before.

## Notes

The case of a *bingyin* year (water movement). The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
