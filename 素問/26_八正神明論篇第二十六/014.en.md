# 素問 八正神明論篇第二十六 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Let me speak of spirit. Spirit, oh spirit! The ear does not hear it, but the eye is bright, the heart opens and the will goes ahead. Clearly one alone understands, yet the mouth cannot express it. Looking together [with others], one alone sees; what seemed dim becomes brilliantly clear to one alone, like wind blowing away clouds. Therefore it is called spirit. The three regions and nine indicators are its source; the discourse on the nine needles need not be kept."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Let me speak of spirit ... Therefore it is called spirit"] "The ear does not hear it" refers to the subtlety and intimacy of the spirit's function. "The eye is bright, the heart opens and the will goes ahead" means the heart's understanding is like opening a scroll in the dark, the eye's seeing is like mist and haze clearing to brightness; although the spirit is fused within, the will has already gone ahead. 慧然 means clear and refreshed. 悟 means fully understanding. "Clearly one alone understands, yet the mouth cannot express it" means that within the heart all is clear and understood, but the mouth cannot utter it to relieve the heart. "Looking together, one alone sees; what seemed dim" exclaims at the uniqueness and swiftness of the seeing: looking together with the crowd, I suddenly see alone, while [to them] it is still as if dim. Once one has seen alone and the heart understands, heart and eye are brilliant and alone can discern clearly, like clouds rolled away by the wind and the sun shining in the sky. How perfect is the spirit! Its marvellous function is such that it cannot be put into words.

[On "The three regions and nine indicators are its source; the discourse on the nine needles need not be kept"] If one takes the channel vessels of the three regions and nine indicators as the root and source, one can penetrate the marvellous function of spiritual understanding. If one merely debates by the discourse on the nine needles, its purport is only transmitted and its knowledge grows ever more distant. Hence it says "The three regions and nine indicators are its source; the discourse on the nine needles need not be kept."

## Notes

"Spirit" means seeing clearly and understanding alone, even when looking at the same things as others; the diagnosis of the three regions and nine indicators is its foundation. This ends the chapter.
- 倶視独見，適若昏 can also be read, following Wang Bing, as "when one sees alone while looking together [with others], [to them] it is as if dim". The translation follows the word order.
