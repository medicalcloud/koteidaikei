# 素問 八正神明論篇第二十六 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Modelling on antiquity means first knowing the *Zhenjing* (Needle Classic). Verifying in the present means first knowing the cold and warmth of the sun and the emptiness and fullness of the moon, observing thereby the floating and sinking of the qi and regulating it in the body, and seeing that it has immediate effect. Observing the obscure means that form and qi, nutritive and defensive, do not show outwardly, yet the practitioner alone knows them; he regulates them by combining the cold and warmth of the sun, the emptiness and fullness of the moon, and the floating and sinking of the qi of the four seasons. The practitioner always sees it first, yet it does not show outwardly; hence it is called observing the obscure. What penetrates the inexhaustible can be handed down to later generations; this is why the practitioner is different. Yet it does not show outwardly, so [others] cannot see it. Looking at it there is no form; tasting it there is no flavour; therefore it is called obscure, dim like the spirit. Depleting evil is the depleting evil qi of the eight nodal points. Upright evil is when the body exerts strength, sweat comes out, the interstices open, and it meets a depleting wind; its striking a person is subtle, so no one knows its condition and no one sees its form. The superior practitioner rescues it at the sprouting; he always first sees the qi of the three regions and nine indicators and regulates them all, rescuing before ruin; hence he is called a superior practitioner. The inferior practitioner rescues what has already formed and what is already ruined. Rescuing what has already formed means not knowing that the three regions and nine indicators have lost their mutual balance, and ruining it through the illness. Knowing where it is means knowing to diagnose where the diseased pulse is among the three regions and nine indicators and treating it; hence it is called guarding the gates and doors: though no one knows its condition, one sees the form of the evil."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Verifying in the present ... has immediate effect"] The observation of qi is not mistaken; therefore there is immediate effect.

[On "Observing the obscure means ... yet the practitioner alone knows them"] This clarifies the previous chapter's "Calm the mind and look to the principle; observe the changes of adjustment. This is called the obscure; no one knows its form." Although form and qi, nutritive and defensive, do not show outwardly, the practitioner understands clearly with his heart-spirit and alone can know their decline and abundance; good and bad can all be made clear.
Song editors: The previous chapter is the *Baoming quanxing lun*.

[On "he regulates them by combining ... hence it is called observing the obscure"] Why can the practitioner always see it first? Because he keeps to the method and his spirit is penetrating and clear.

[On "What penetrates the inexhaustible ... this is why the practitioner is different"] The method is manifest, so it can be handed down to later generations; if it is not cut off in later generations, its application extends without end. He sees and knows alone; this is why the practitioner differs from others.

[On "Yet it does not show outwardly, so [others] cannot see it"] The practitioner differs from the crude because the crude all cannot see it.

[On "Looking at it there is no form ... dim like the spirit"] It says that form and qi, nutritive and defensive, do not show outwardly and cannot be seen; hence looking there is no form and tasting there is no flavour. Lying low like a crossbow laid flat, rising like a released trigger, profound and obscure, no one knows the original master; it is dim like the movement of the spirit. 若 means like.

[On "Depleting evil is the depleting evil qi of the eight nodal points"] The depleting evil of the eight nodal points means the depleting evil of the eight nodal days. It comes from the region of depletion, attacks depletion and enters to cause illness; hence it is called the depleting evil of the eight nodal points.

[On "Upright evil is ... no one sees its form"] Upright evil does not come from the region of depletion. Its striking a person is subtle; therefore no one knows its condition or sees its form.

[On "The superior practitioner rescues it at the sprouting ... ruining it through the illness"] The meaning is fully given in the *Lihe zhenxie lun* (chapter 27).

[On "Knowing where it is ... one sees the form of the evil"] The three regions and nine indicators are the gates and doors for observing evil. By guarding the gates and doors one sees the form of the evil. Its striking a person is subtle; therefore no one knows its condition.

## Notes

Explains terms such as "the obscure" from the *Baoming quanxing lun* (chapter 25) and describes the difference between superior and inferior practitioners.
- Depleting evil and upright evil: depleting evil is wind coming from a direction unsuited to the season (depleting wind); upright evil is wind suited to the season that strikes a person when exertion has caused sweating and opened the interstices.
- 牙 in 萌牙 means 芽 "sprout" (Daizhige writes 芽).
- 莫知其情而見邪形也 is translated "though no one knows its condition, one sees the form of the evil"; it can also be read "[others] do not know its condition, [while the practitioner] sees the form of the evil".
