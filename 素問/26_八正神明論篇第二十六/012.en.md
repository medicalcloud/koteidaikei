# 素問 八正神明論篇第二十六 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Let me speak of form. Form, oh form! To the eye it is obscure. Ask where the illness is and seek it in the channels; it is clearly before one, yet pressing it one cannot get it, and one does not know its condition. Therefore it is called form."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Let me speak of form ... Ask where the illness is"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* reads "feel where it hurts"; that sense also works.

[On "seek it in the channels ... Therefore it is called form"] Outwardly it conceals what has no form; therefore to the eye it is obscure and unseen. Inwardly it contains what has an image; therefore by diagnosis it can be sought in the channels. "Clearly before one, yet pressing it one cannot get it" means encountering it suddenly within the three regions and nine indicators, without being able to fix its timing or standard. The *Lihe zhenxie lun* (chapter 27) says, "Whether in yin or yang, it cannot be measured; follow and examine it, and when suddenly encountering it in the three regions and nine indicators, block its path early" — this is the meaning.

## Notes

"Form" means that, though invisible to the eye, the location of illness can be known by seeking it in the channels through questioning and pulse diagnosis.
- 形乎形 and 慧然在前，按之不得 are obscure and read in various ways; the translation draws on Wang Bing.
