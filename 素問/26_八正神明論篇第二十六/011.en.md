# 素問 八正神明論篇第二十六 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Marvellous is this discourse! Matching the human form to yin and yang and the four seasons, the responses of depletion and repletion, the timing of the obscure — if not you, Master, who could penetrate them? But you often speak of form and spirit. What is called form? What is called spirit? I wish to hear all of it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "But you often speak of form and spirit ... I wish to hear all of it"] Spirit means the penetrating understanding of the spiritual intelligence; form means what can be observed by examining the form.

## Notes

Huangdi asks the meaning of form and spirit.
