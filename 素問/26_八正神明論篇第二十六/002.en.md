# 素問 八正神明論篇第二十六 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "Model on heaven, follow earth, and accord with the lights of heaven."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Model on heaven, follow earth, and accord with the lights of heaven"] It means according with the courses of the sun, moon and stars.

## Notes

The needle is to be used modelling on heaven and earth and according with the movements of the sun, moon and stars.
