# 素問 八正神明論篇第二十六 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "In the practice of using the needle there must be models and rules. Now, what is the model and what is the rule?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the practice of using the needle ... what is the rule?"] 服 means practice. 法 means model; 則 means standard, guideline.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: what are the norms for using the needle?
