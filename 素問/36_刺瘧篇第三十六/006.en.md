# 素問 刺瘧篇第三十六 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Malaria of the foot *jueyin* makes one's lumbus ache and lower abdomen full; urination is not free, as if with retention, but it is not retention; one urinates frequently, the mind is fearful, the qi insufficient, and the abdomen feels oppressed. Needle the foot *jueyin*.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Malaria of the foot *jueyin* ... the abdomen feels oppressed"] The foot *jueyin* vessel follows the inner thigh, enters the pubic hair, encircles the genitals and reaches the lower abdomen; hence such illness. 癃 means inability to urinate. 悒悒 describes oppression.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 数噫 "frequent belching" for the three characters 数便意.

[On "Needle the foot *jueyin*"] Taichong governs it, in the depression two body-*cun* behind the base joint of the big toe; it is the stream point of the *jueyin*. One may needle three body-*fen* and retain ten breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.
Song editors: The note to the *Ci yaotong pian* says "behind the base joint, on the inner side, where the artery is felt".

## Notes

The symptoms and needling of malaria of the foot *jueyin*.
- 数便，意恐懼: according to the Song editors, the *Jiayi jing* writes 数噫 for 数便意. The translation divides 数便，意恐懼; it can also be divided 数便意，恐懼 "frequent urge to urinate, fear".
- 髦 in Wang Bing's note means 毛 "hair".
