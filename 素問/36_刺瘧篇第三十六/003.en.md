# 素問 刺瘧篇第三十六 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Malaria of the foot *Yangming* makes one cold first, shivering and shivering; the cold is severe and only after a long time is there heat. When the heat goes and sweat comes out, one likes to see the light of sun and moon and the qi of fire, and only then feels at ease. Needle the foot *Yangming* on the instep.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Malaria of the foot *Yangming* ... only then feels at ease"] When yang is deficient, the outside is cold first; when the deficiency of yang reaches its extreme, it becomes abundant again; therefore the cold is severe and only after a long time is there heat. When the heat goes and the sweating ends, yin is again strong within and yang does not prevail over yin; therefore one likes to see the light of sun and moon and the qi of fire, and only then feels at ease.

[On "Needle the foot *Yangming* on the instep"] This is the Chongyang point, five body-*cun* up on the instep at the artery between the bones, three body-*cun* above Xiangu; it is the source point of the *Yangming*. One may needle three body-*fen* and retain ten breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.

## Notes

The symptoms and needling of malaria of the foot *Yangming*.
