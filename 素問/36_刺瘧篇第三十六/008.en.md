# 素問 刺瘧篇第三十六 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When malaria breaks out and the body is just becoming hot, needle the artery on the instep, open its hole and let out its blood, and it cools at once. When malaria is just about to turn cold, needle the hand *Yangming* and greater yin and the foot *Yangming* and greater yin.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When malaria breaks out and the body is just becoming hot, needle the artery on the instep"] That is the *Yangming* vessel.

[On "open its hole and let out its blood, and it cools at once"] The *Yangming* vessel has much blood and much qi; the heat is abundant and the qi vigorous; therefore letting out its blood cools it at once.

[On "When malaria is just about to turn cold ... the foot *Yangming* and greater yin"] This too means opening the point and letting out its blood; one should needle according to the well and stream points.

## Notes

Needling for the hot phase and the cold phase of malaria.
