# 素問 刺瘧篇第三十六 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Malaria of the foot lesser yang makes one's body languid; the cold is not severe and the heat not severe; one dislikes seeing people, and on seeing people the heart is apprehensive; there is much heat and profuse sweating. Needle the foot lesser yang.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Malaria of the foot lesser yang makes one's body languid"] The languor of the body is as in the following phrases.

[On "the cold is not severe and the heat not severe"] The yang qi is not yet abundant; therefore it is so.

[On "one dislikes seeing people ... apprehensive"] The gallbladder is paired with the liver; when the liver is depleted there is fear; the evil presses on its qi; therefore one dislikes seeing people, and on seeing people the heart is apprehensive.

[On "there is much heat and profuse sweating"] When the evil is abundant there is much heat; wind strikes; therefore there is sweating.

[On "Needle the foot lesser yang"] Xiaxi governs it. Xiaxi is in the depression in front of the base joint, in the cleft between the fourth and fifth toes; it is the spring point of the lesser yang. One may needle three body-*fen* and retain three breaths; for moxibustion, three cones.

## Notes

The symptoms and needling of malaria of the foot lesser yang.
- 解㑊 is understood as a state of languor and weakness.
- 惕然然 in Wang Bing's note is presumably an error for 惕惕然.
