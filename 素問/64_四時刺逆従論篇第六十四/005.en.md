# 素問 四時刺逆従論篇第六十四 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the greater yang is in surplus, there is bone impediment with a heavy body; when insufficient, kidney impediment. If slippery, there is kidney wind *shan*; if rough, accumulation, with frequent illness of the vertex at times.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the greater yang is in surplus ... kidney impediment"] The greater yang and lesser yin are exterior and interior; hence in both surplus and insufficiency the illness returns to the kidneys.

[On "If slippery, there is kidney wind *shan* ... illness of the vertex at times"] The greater yang vessel crosses on the vertex, enters to network the brain, and descends along the paraspinal muscles to network the kidneys; hence kidney wind and illness of the vertex.

## Notes

Illnesses of the greater yang from surplus, insufficiency, and slippery and rough pulses.
