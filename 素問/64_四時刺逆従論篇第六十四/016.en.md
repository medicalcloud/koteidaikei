# 素問 四時刺逆従論篇第六十四 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good." In needling the five depots: if the heart is hit, death follows in one day, and its sign is belching. If the liver is hit, death follows in five days, and its sign is talking. If the lungs are hit, death follows in three days, and its sign is coughing. If the kidneys are hit, death follows in six days, and its sign is sneezing and yawning. If the spleen is hit, death follows in ten days, and its sign is swallowing. Needling that harms a person's five depots is surely fatal; its sign depends on the change of that depot, and by observing it one knows the death.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In needling the five depots: if the heart is hit, death follows in one day, and its sign is belching"] The *Zhenyao jingzhong lun* says "if the heart is hit, death follows in a cycle"; the *Cijin lun* says "death in one day; its sign is belching".

[On "If the liver is hit, death follows in five days, and its sign is talking"] The *Zhenyao jingzhong lun* lacks it and does not discuss it. The *Cijin lun* says "if the liver is hit, death in five days; its sign is talking".
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 欠 for 語.

[On "If the lungs are hit, death follows in three days, and its sign is coughing"] The *Zhenyao jingzhong lun* says "if the lungs are hit, death in five days"; the *Cijin lun* says "if the lungs are hit, death in three days; its sign is coughing".

[On "If the kidneys are hit, death follows in six days"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes "death in three days".

[On "If the kidneys are hit ... sneezing and yawning"] The *Zhenyao jingzhong lun* says "if the kidneys are hit, death in seven days"; the *Cijin lun* says "if the kidneys are hit, death in six days; its sign is sneezing".
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* lacks the character 欠.

[On "If the spleen is hit, death follows in ten days"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes "fifteen days".

[On "If the spleen is hit ... its sign is swallowing"] The *Zhenyao jingzhong lun* says "if the spleen is hit, death in five days"; the *Cijin lun* says "if the spleen is hit, death in ten days; its sign is swallowing". These three treatises are all Qibo's words, yet the days of death and the signs differ; this is an error of transmission.

[On "Needling that harms a person's five depots ... one knows the death"] 変 means the movement and change of qi. From "if the heart is hit" to here is all duplicate text concerning contravention and compliance.

## Notes

The chapter ends with the times of death and signs when the five depots are wrongly needled.
- There is no speaker marker after 帝曰善. Here the Emperor's words are taken to end at 善, and the rest is punctuated as narrative text. Wang Bing treats this part as duplicating the *Cijin lun* and others.
- The Song editors' 甲乙経作三日死 on 中腎六日死 presumably means that 六日 is written 三日.
