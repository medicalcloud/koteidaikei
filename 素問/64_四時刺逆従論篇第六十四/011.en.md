# 素問 四時刺逆従論篇第六十四 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "If in spring one needles the network vessels, blood and qi overflow outward, causing shortness of qi. If in spring one needles the flesh, blood and qi circle back and counterflow, causing rising qi. If in spring one needles the sinews and bones, blood and qi adhere inside, causing abdominal distension.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If in spring one needles the network vessels ... causing shortness of qi"] When blood and qi overflow outward, the center is insufficient; hence shortness of qi.
Song editors: From "if in spring one needles the network vessels" to "causing unclear vision", the sense is the same as in the 𧦽 [*Zhen*]*yao jingzhong lun* but the wording differs; the note there is much more detailed than here. That chapter divides by four seasons, this one by five. But although this one has the division of needling the flesh in late summer, at every season the needling of the autumn division is missing. We suspect that the flesh division here is the autumn skin division there.

[On "If in spring one needles the flesh ... causing rising qi"] Blood counterflows and qi rises; hence rising qi.
Song editors: The classic lacks needling the autumn division in spring.

[On "If in spring one needles the sinews and bones ... causing abdominal distension"] It adheres inside and does not disperse; hence distension.

## Notes

The harm of needling, in spring, a level that does not suit the season. Qibo's speech continues to unit 015.
- 𧦽要経終論 in the Song editors' note means the *Zhenyao jingzhong lun* (診要経終論).
