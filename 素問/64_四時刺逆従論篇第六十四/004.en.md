# 素問 四時刺逆従論篇第六十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the *yangming* is in surplus, there is vessel impediment with the body at times hot; when insufficient, heart impediment. If slippery, there is heart wind *shan*; if rough, accumulation, with frequent fright at times.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the *yangming* is in surplus ... heart impediment"] When the stomach is in surplus, it returns upward to the heart; when insufficient, there is impediment below the heart; hence this.

[On "If slippery, there is heart wind *shan* ... frequent fright at times"] The heart governor vessel starts in the chest, emerges to connect to the pericardium, descends through the diaphragm and networks the triple burner in turn; hence heart *shan* and frequent fright at times.

## Notes

Illnesses of the *yangming* from surplus, insufficiency, and slippery and rough pulses.
