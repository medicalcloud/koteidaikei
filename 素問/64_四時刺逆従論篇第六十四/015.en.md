# 素問 四時刺逆従論篇第六十四 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All these seasonal needlings [done wrongly] are illnesses of great contravention, and [the seasons] must be followed. Going against them produces disordered qi, which spreads and causes illness. So if in needling one does not know the channels of the four seasons and where illness arises, taking compliance for contravention, the upright qi becomes disordered within and presses against the essence. One must carefully examine the nine indicators; then the upright qi is not disordered and the essential qi does not reverse."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "All these seasonal needlings [done wrongly] are illnesses of great contravention"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition writes 六経之病.

[On "and [the seasons] must be followed. Going against them ... causes illness"] 淫 means out of order. Moving out of order is like soaking and staining, and produces illness.

[On "One must carefully examine the nine indicators ... the essential qi does not reverse"] 不転 means not reversing.

## Notes

A summary of the importance of needling in accordance with the four seasons. Qibo's speech ends here.
