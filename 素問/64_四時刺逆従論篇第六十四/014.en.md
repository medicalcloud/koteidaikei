# 素問 四時刺逆従論篇第六十四 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If in winter one needles the channels, blood and qi are all lost, causing unclear vision. If in winter one needles the network vessels, the inner qi leaks outward and lingers, becoming great impediment. If in winter one needles the flesh, yang qi is exhausted and cut off, causing forgetfulness.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If in winter one needles the channels ... causing unclear vision"] Because blood and qi have nothing to nourish.

[On "If in winter one needles the flesh ... causing forgetfulness"] Yang qi is not strong and becomes exhausted by spring; hence forgetfulness.
Song editors: The classic lacks needling the autumn division in winter.

## Notes

The harm of needling, in winter, a level that does not suit the season.
