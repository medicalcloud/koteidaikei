# 素問 四時刺逆従論篇第六十四 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In spring, the qi of heaven begins to open and the qi of earth begins to leak out; frost thaws and ice melts, water flows and the channels open; hence human qi is in the vessels. In summer, the channels are full and qi overflows, entering the minute network vessels to receive blood, and the skin is full and firm. In late summer, the channels and network vessels are all abundant and overflow inward into the flesh. In autumn, the qi of heaven begins to gather, the interstices close up, and the skin is drawn tight. In winter there is covering and storing; blood and qi are inside, adhering inwardly to the bone marrow and communicating with the five depots. Therefore pathogenic qi always enters and lodges following the qi and blood of the four seasons. Its transformations cannot be measured, but one must follow the channel qi to expel the pathogen; when the pathogen is removed, disordered qi does not arise."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In autumn, the qi of heaven begins to gather ... the skin is drawn tight"] 引 means drawn and shrunk tight.

[On "but one must follow the channel qi ... disordered qi does not arise"] The qi is obtained and regulated, so it is not disordered.

## Notes

Why the location of qi differs by season, and how pathogens enter accordingly.
