# 素問 四時刺逆従論篇第六十四 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If in autumn one needles the channels, blood and qi counterflow upward, causing forgetfulness. If in autumn one needles the network vessels, qi does not move outward, causing one to lie down without wishing to move. If in autumn one needles the sinews and bones, blood and qi scatter inside, causing shivering with cold.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If in autumn one needles the channels ... causing forgetfulness"] Blood and qi counterflow upward and fill the lungs; hence forgetfulness.

[On "If in autumn one needles the network vessels, qi does not move outward"] Song editors: Another edition writes 血気不行, and Quan Yuanqi's edition 気不衛外, as does the *Taisu*.

[On "causing one to lie down without wishing to move"] Because the depletion is severe.
Song editors: The classic lacks needling the late-summer division in autumn.

[On "If in autumn one needles the sinews and bones ... causing shivering with cold"] When blood and qi scatter inside, the central qi is depleted; hence shivering with cold.

## Notes

The harm of needling, in autumn, a level that does not suit the season.
