# 素問 四時刺逆従論篇第六十四 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore, the qi of spring is in the channels, the qi of summer in the minute network vessels, the qi of late summer in the flesh, the qi of autumn in the skin, and the qi of winter in the bone marrow.

## Notes

Where the qi is in each of the four (five) seasons. There is no note by Wang Bing.
