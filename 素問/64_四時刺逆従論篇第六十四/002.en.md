# 素問 四時刺逆従論篇第六十四 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the lesser yin is in surplus, there is skin impediment and hidden rashes; when insufficient, lung impediment. If slippery, there is lung wind *shan*; if rough, accumulation and blood in the urine.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the lesser yin is in surplus ... lung impediment"] Because kidney water counterflows and connects to its mother, the lungs. The foot lesser yin vessel goes up from the kidneys through the liver and diaphragm into the lungs; hence surplus brings skin impediment and hidden rashes, and insufficiency lung impediment.

[On "If slippery, there is lung wind *shan*; if rough, accumulation and blood in the urine"] Its main channel enters the lungs, pierces the kidneys and networks the bladder; hence lung *shan*, accumulation and blood in the urine.

## Notes

Illnesses of the lesser yin from surplus, insufficiency, and slippery and rough pulses.
