# 素問 四時刺逆従論篇第六十四 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If in summer one needles the channels, blood and qi are exhausted, causing lassitude. If in summer one needles the flesh, blood and qi retreat inward, causing proneness to fear. If in summer one needles the sinews and bones, blood and qi counterflow upward, causing proneness to anger.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If in summer one needles the channels ... causing lassitude"] Blood and qi are exhausted and scant, hence lassitude, which cannot be named. 解㑊 means cold yet not cold, hot yet not hot, strong yet not strong, weak yet not weak; hence it cannot be named.

[On "If in summer one needles the flesh ... causing proneness to fear"] 却 means closing. When blood and qi close inside, yang qi does not pass; hence proneness to fear.

[On "If in summer one needles the sinews and bones ... causing proneness to anger"] When blood and qi counterflow upward, angry qi responds; hence proneness to anger.
Song editors: The classic lacks needling the autumn division in summer.

## Notes

The harm of needling, in summer, a level that does not suit the season.
