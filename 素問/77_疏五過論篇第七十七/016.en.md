# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

[With] the *Upper Classic* and *Lower Classic*, *Kuiduo* and *Yinyang*, the *Extraordinary and Constant* and the *Five Centers*, decide by the Bright Hall and examine end and beginning, and you can go freely anywhere."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "[With] the *Upper Classic* and *Lower Classic* ... you can go freely anywhere"] The so-called *Upper Classic* speaks of qi communicating with heaven; the *Lower Classic* speaks of the changes of illness. It means these two classics gauge the qi of yin and yang, and the extraordinary-constant and five centers are all decided by the regions of the Bright Hall. *Kuiduo* means gauging the depth of illness; the extraordinary and constant means unusual illnesses; the five centers means the complexions of the five viscera. The Bright Hall is where one looks at all things, distinguishes white and black, and examines long and short; hence "decide by the Bright Hall". Examining end and beginning means carefully examining the confinement and flourishing of the five colors ending and beginning again. When the Way circulates thus, its applications are inexhaustible; one no longer sees the whole ox, and succeeds ten thousand times in ten thousand. Being thus lofty and far-reaching, one can go freely throughout the world.

## Notes

The Emperor concludes that with these classics and diagnosis by the Bright Hall one can practice anywhere. His speech ends here.
