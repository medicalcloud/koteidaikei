# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All these five come from receiving the art without mastering it and not understanding human affairs.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "All these five come from receiving the art without mastering it and not understanding human affairs"] It says these five are merely called followers who have received the art, not yet fit to understand the subtle principles, and still in the dark about human affairs.

## Notes

A summary of the five errors.
