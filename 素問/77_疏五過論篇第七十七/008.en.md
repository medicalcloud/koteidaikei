# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Whoever diagnoses must know the end and beginning, and also the remaining threads; feel the pulse and ask the name, and match it to male or female.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Whoever diagnoses must know the end and beginning ... match it to male or female"] End and beginning mean the qi and complexion. The *Maiyao jingwei lun* says, "One who knows the outside ends and begins it"; that is, clearly knowing that the images of the five qi and colors end and begin again. Remaining threads means the remaining threads of how the illness started. 切 means pressing the pulse with the fingers. Asking the name means asking the name of the pattern of illness. Men have more yang qi, and a larger left pulse is compliant; women have more yin qi, and a larger right pulse is compliant. Hence one should first match them by the constant signs.

## Notes

The Emperor states what a diagnostician must know.
