# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence it is said: when sages treat illness, they must know the yin and yang of heaven and earth, the regular network of the four seasons, the five viscera and six bowels, female and male, exterior and interior, needling, moxibustion and stone needles, and what toxic medicines govern; they examine human affairs unhurriedly to clarify the way of the classics. Noble and humble, poor and rich each differ in kind and principle. Ask about age, youth or seniority, and the principles of courage and timidity; examine the regions, know the root and beginning of the illness and the eight proper [winds] and nine indicators; then diagnosis will surely fit.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "know the root and beginning of the illness and the eight proper [winds] and nine indicators; then diagnosis will surely fit"] Such is the completeness of the sages' knowledge; practitioners should strive for it.

## Notes

The Emperor lists what sages must know in treating illness.
