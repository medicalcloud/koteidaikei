# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Not knowing the principles of the transport points, the five viscera become pent up and heated, and abscesses break out in the six bowels. Not diagnosing illness carefully is called losing the constant. Carefully keep to this treatment, and it will illuminate the classic and be illuminated by it.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Not knowing the principles of the transport points ... abscesses break out in the six bowels"] 菀 means accumulate; 熟 means heat. Heat accumulates in the five viscera, the six bowels receive it, yang and heat press on each other, and where the heat passes abscesses form.

[On "Not diagnosing illness carefully is called losing the constant"] It means losing the way of the proper use of the constant classical art.

[On "Carefully keep to this treatment, and it will illuminate the classic"] It refers to the earlier "seeking within the qi" and the principle of the meeting of the transport points.

## Notes

The harm of not knowing the principles of the points and the importance of keeping to the treatment.
