# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Diagnosis has three constants: you must ask about rank, about enfeoffed lords who have fallen, and about those aspiring to be lords and kings. One formerly noble who loses power, even without being struck by a pathogen, is injured within in essence and spirit, and his body will surely fail and perish.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "you must ask about rank, about enfeoffed lords who have fallen, and about those aspiring to be lords and kings"] The noble are at ease in body and in will; the humble suffer in body and in will. Suffering and ease run differently, hence one asks first. Enfeoffed lords who have fallen means those demoted from a lord's position to be enfeoffed as ministers. Aspiring to be lords and kings means longing for honor and acting recklessly without stopping.
Song editors: The *Taisu* has 公 for 欲.

[On "even without being struck by a pathogen, is injured within in essence and spirit, and his body will surely fail and perish"] It is caused by worry and fear searing and pressing, and by pent-up resentment.

## Notes

As the three constants of diagnosis, he says to ask about changes in rank and ambitions.
