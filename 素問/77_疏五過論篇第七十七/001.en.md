# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Ah, how far-reaching! How profound, like gazing into a deep abyss, like facing floating clouds. Gazing into a deep abyss, one can still measure it; facing floating clouds, one cannot know their limits. The art of the sages is a model for the myriad people; in discussing and judging intentions there must be rules; following the classics and keeping to the numbers, examining and following medical matters, it is a help to the myriad people. Hence in [medical] matters there are five errors and four virtues. Do you know them?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Gazing into a deep abyss, one can still measure it; facing floating clouds, one cannot know their limits"] "Ah, how far-reaching!" laments the boundlessness of the utmost Way; "how profound" speaks of the inexhaustibility of its wondrous use. A deep abyss is clear and still, and seeing it is certain, so it can be measured; floating clouds drift and lodge, their limits never constant, so they cannot be known.
Song editors: On examination, this text duplicates that of the *Liuwei zhi lun*.

[On "it is a help to the myriad people. Hence in [medical] matters there are five errors and four virtues. Do you know them"] Being careful of the five errors means reverently complying with the virtuous qi of the four seasons. Virtue is the use of the Way and the master of life; hence one must reverently comply with it. The *Shanggu tianzhen lun* says, "That they all exceeded a hundred years without their movements declining was because their virtue was whole and not endangered." The *Lingshu jing* says, "What heaven has in me is virtue." Thus heaven sends down virtuous qi, and humans rely on it to live; governing qi and embracing spirit, they reach up to heaven. The *Shengqi tongtian lun* says, "From of old, communicating with heaven is the root of life"; this is what it means.
Song editors: On 為万民副, Yang Shangshan says, "副 means help."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter: the Yellow Emperor states that medical matters have five errors and four virtues and asks Leigong whether he knows them.
