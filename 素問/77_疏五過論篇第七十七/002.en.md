# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong left his mat, bowed twice and said, "I am young, ignorant and confused, and have not heard of the five errors and four virtues. In comparing categories, forms and names, I only cite the classic emptily, and my mind has nothing to answer."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In comparing categories, forms and names, I only cite the classic emptily, and my mind has nothing to answer"] He has not yet received the classic from a teacher, his mind does not know by birth, and his achievements are slight; hence he speaks humbly.

## Notes

Leigong replies that he does not know.
