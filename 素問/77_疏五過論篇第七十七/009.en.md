# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Separation, severance, pent-up and knotted [feelings], worry, fear, joy and anger empty the five viscera, and blood and qi leave their guard. If the practitioner cannot know this, what art can he speak of?

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "empty the five viscera, and blood and qi leave their guard. If the practitioner cannot know this, what art can he speak of"] 離 means being separated from loved ones; 絶 means cutting off thoughts of what one cherished; 菀 means accumulating worries; 結 means knotting lingering resentment. Those separated from loved ones have wandering ethereal souls; those cut off from what they cherished lose their intent; those accumulating worries tire their spirit; those knotting resentment suffer in will. The worried are blocked and do not move; the fearful are agitated and lose their guard; the very angry are confused and unregulated; the joyful scatter and do not store. Through these eight, the five viscera are emptied and blood and qi leave their guard. If the practitioner does not think and understand, what more can be said?
Song editors: For 蕩憚而失守 the *Jiayi jing* has 不収.

## Notes

The Emperor says one must know that disordered emotions empty the five viscera.
- 問 in 離問 in Wang Bing's note is read as 間 ("separated").
