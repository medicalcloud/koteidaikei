# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Keep to the numbers, rely on the treatments, and do not lose the principles of the transport points. One who can practice this art will be free of danger all one's life.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Keep to the numbers ... free of danger all one's life"] Keeping to the numbers means the amounts of blood and qi and the depths of needling. Relying on the treatments means using the points according to what they treat. Simply keeping to the numbers and relying on the treatments, one does not lose the principles of the points. 殆 means danger.

## Notes

The Emperor says to keep the numbers and not lose the principles of the points.
