# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

One who was rich at first and later poor, even without injury from a pathogen, has scorched skin and bent sinews, wilting and lameness becoming cramps. If the doctor cannot be stern and cannot move the spirit, but is soft and weak outwardly, so that disorder reaches loss of constancy and the illness cannot be shifted, then medical practice does not work. This is the fourth error in treatment.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "even without injury from a pathogen, has scorched skin and bent sinews, wilting and lameness becoming cramps"] The qi of the five viscera linger and the illness combines, producing this.

[On "and the illness cannot be shifted, then medical practice does not work. This is the fourth error in treatment"] 厳 means admonishing, the means to prohibit wrongdoing; [moving] is the means to make [the patient] obey. Being soft and weak outwardly means yielding and complying. If admonition is not enough to prohibit wrongdoing and moving is not enough to make him obey, and one yields and lets things take their course, disorder loses heaven's constancy and the illness does not shift; where then is medicine?

## Notes

The fourth of the five errors: the doctor fails to guide the patient firmly and, yielding to him, cannot shift the illness.
