# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Whoever would diagnose illness must ask about food and drink and dwelling place. Sudden joy, sudden suffering, joy first and suffering after all harm the essential qi; the essential qi is exhausted and the body is ruined. Sudden anger harms yin; sudden joy harms yang; reversal qi travels upward, filling the vessels and leaving the body. When a foolish doctor treats this, not knowing supplementing and draining, not knowing the nature of the illness, the essential splendor slips away day by day and pathogenic qi combines. This is the second error in treatment.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Whoever would diagnose illness must ask about food and drink and dwelling place"] Food and dwelling differ, hence one asks about them. The *Yifa fangyi lun* says: "The eastern region is where heaven and earth first give birth, a land of fish and salt, by the sea and the water; its people eat fish and like salt, are content where they live and relish their food. The west is a region of metal and jade, of sand and stone, where heaven and earth gather in; its people live on the hills, there is much wind, and water and soil are hard; its people wear no silk but coarse wool and mats, eat rich food and grow fat. The north is the region where heaven and earth close and store; the land is high, they live on the hills, wind is cold and ice severe; its people like to dwell in the open and live on milk. The south is where heaven and earth grow and nourish, where yang flourishes; the land is low, water and soil are weak, and mist and dew gather; its people like sour and eat 胕 [fermented] food. The center is flat and damp, where heaven and earth produce the most things; its people eat varied food and do not toil." Thus in the way of diagnosis these must be asked first; hence "the sages combined the methods so that each obtained what suited it".

[On "Sudden joy, sudden suffering, joy first and suffering after"] Song editors: The *Taisu* has 始苦 ("suffering first").

[On "all harm the essential qi; the essential qi is exhausted and the body is ruined"] Joy slackens qi; sorrow dissolves qi. Those whose inner being is moved by grief are exhausted and lose life; hence the essential qi is exhausted, the body ruined, and the heart-spirit dejected.

[On "Sudden anger harms yin; sudden joy harms yang"] Anger makes qi counterflow, so it harms yin; joy slackens qi, so it harms yang.

[On "reversal qi travels upward, filling the vessels and leaving the body"] 厥 means qi counterflow. When counterflowing qi travels upward and fills the channels and network vessels, the spirit qi is frightened, scatters and departs from the body.

[On "the essential splendor slips away day by day and pathogenic qi combines. This is the second error in treatment"] Not knowing the different feelings of joy, anger, sorrow and pleasure, supplementing and draining them all alike, the essential splendor of the five viscera slips away day by day, and pathogenic qi encroaches and combines with the proper true qi.

## Notes

The second of the five errors: not asking about food, dwelling, suffering, joy and anger, and so misapplying supplementing and draining.
