# 素問 疏五過論篇第七十七 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the way of treating illness, the inner qi is the treasure; follow and seek its principle; if seeking does not find it, the error lies in exterior and interior.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the way of treating illness, the inner qi is the treasure ... the error lies in exterior and interior"] In treating illness the practitioner must seek where the error lies within form and qi; this is the sages' treasure. If seeking does not find it, examine it by the yin and yang, exterior and interior of the qi of the viscera and bowels.
Song editors: The Quan Yuanqi edition and the *Taisu* have 気内為実. Yang Shangshan says, "The qi between heaven and earth is outer qi; the qi within the human body is inner qi. Outer qi shapes the myriad things; this is outer substance. Inner qi is shaped and generated by the nutritive and defensive; hence it is inner substance. Being able to seek the principle of inner qi in treating illness is the key to treatment."

## Notes

The Emperor says to seek within the qi and, failing that, to examine exterior and interior.
