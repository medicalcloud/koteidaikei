# 素問 玉版論要篇第十五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I have heard of *kuiduo* (measuring) and *qiheng* (the extraordinary and the constant), but what they refer to differs. How are they used?"

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on how to use *kuiduo* and *qiheng* (both regarded as names of ancient diagnostic methods). There is no note by Wang Bing.
