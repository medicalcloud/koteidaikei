# 素問 刺要論篇第五十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "Illness may be superficial or deep, and needling may be shallow or deep; each should reach its proper level without going beyond its path. Going beyond harms the interior; falling short produces external congestion, and with congestion evil follows. If depth is not right, it instead becomes a great injury, stirring the five zang within and later producing a great illness.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "needling may be shallow or deep; each should reach its proper level without going beyond its path"] 道 means the path along which the qi moves.

[On "Going beyond harms the interior ... evil follows"] Going beyond harms the interior because it is far too deep. Falling short causes external congestion because it recklessly adds to the qi of other divisions; the qi is increased and the exterior congested; therefore evil qi follows the depletion.

[On "If depth is not right ... later producing a great illness"] 賊 means secret harm; 動 means disturb. Falling short causes external congestion, going beyond harms the interior; external congestion and internal harm are steps toward great illness; hence "later producing a great illness".

## Notes

The depth of needling should match the depth of the illness; both too deep and too shallow are harmful. Qibo's speech continues in the next unit.
