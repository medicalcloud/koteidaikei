# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore when the hundred illnesses first arise, they must begin in the skin and hair. When evil strikes it, the interstices open; when they open, it enters and lodges in the network vessels; if it lingers and does not leave, it is transmitted into the channels; if it lingers and does not leave, it is transmitted into the fu and accumulates in the intestines and stomach. When evil first enters the skin, one shivers, the fine hairs stand up and the interstices open. When it enters the network vessels, they become abundant and change colour. When it enters and lodges in the channels, it is felt as depletion and [the vessels] sink. When it lingers between the sinews and bones, if cold predominates the sinews contract and the bones ache; if heat predominates the sinews slacken and the bones waste, the flesh melts, the muscle bulges break down, and the hair stands straight and withers."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "if it lingers and does not leave ... accumulates in the intestines and stomach"] 廩 means accumulate, gather.

[On "When evil first enters the skin ... the interstices open"] 泝然 means aversion to cold. 起 means the hair stands erect. 腠理 means the pores and lines of the skin.

[On "When it enters the network vessels, they become abundant and change colour"] 盛 means abundantly full; 変 means departing from the normal.

[On "When it enters and lodges in the channels ... [the vessels] sink"] The channels are depleted and evil enters; hence "felt as depletion". The vessels are depleted and the qi scant; therefore they sink.

[On "When it lingers between the sinews and bones ... withers"] 攣 means tense; 弛 means slack; 消 means melt. The *Zhenjing* says, "With cold the sinews are tense; with heat the sinews are slack. When cold prevails there is pain; when heat prevails the qi wastes." 䐃 is the prominent part of the flesh; therefore when the flesh wastes, the muscle bulges break down and the hair stands straight and withers.

## Notes

The path by which evil is transmitted from the skin and hair to the network vessels, channels and fu (intestines and stomach), and the symptoms at each stage. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 002, ends here.
