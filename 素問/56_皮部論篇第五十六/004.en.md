# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The yang of the lesser yang is called Shuchi; above and below follow the same rule. Looking in its region, any floating network vessels are all lesser yang network vessels. When the network vessels are abundant, [evil] enters and lodges in the channel. Thus what is in yang governs [entry] inward, and what is in yin governs going out, seeping inward. All the channels are thus.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The yang of the lesser yang is called Shuchi"] 枢 means pivot, the essential point; 持 means holding.

## Notes

The skin region of the lesser yang (Shuchi).
- 在陽者主内，在陰者主出 is obscure and read in various ways; the translation stays close to the characters.
