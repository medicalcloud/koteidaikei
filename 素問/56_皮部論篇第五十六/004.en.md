# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The yang of the lesser yang is called Shuchi; above and below follow the same rule. Looking in its region, any floating network vessels are all lesser yang network vessels. When the network vessels are abundant, [evil] enters and lodges in the channel. Thus what is in yang governs [entry] inward, and what is in yin governs going out, seeping inward. All the channels are thus.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The yang of the lesser yang is called Shuchi"] 枢 means pivot, the essential point; 持 means holding.

## Notes

The skin region of the lesser yang (Shuchi).
- 在陽者主内，在陰者主出: Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* says pathogens always enter the channels from the network vessels; "in the yang governs inward" means entering inward from the yang division, and "in the yin governs going out, seeping inward" means going out of the channel and seeping into the viscera. Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* says that when the lesser yang network vessels are full they enter the channels, hence governing inward, and when the channels are full they overflow outward, hence governing going out. The bracketed supplements follow the *Leijing*.
