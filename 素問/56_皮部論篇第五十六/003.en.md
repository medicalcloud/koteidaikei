# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The yang of the *yangming* is called Haifei; above and below follow the same rule. Looking in its region, any floating network vessels are all *yangming* network vessels. If their colour is mostly green-blue, there is pain; mostly black, impediment; yellow and red, heat; mostly white, cold; if all five colours appear, cold and heat. When the network vessels are abundant, [evil] enters and lodges in the channel. Yang governs the outside, yin governs the inside.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The yang of the *yangming* is called Haifei"] 蜚 means generation and transformation; 害 means killing qi. When killing qi moves, generation and transformation cease; hence it is called Haifei.

[On "above and below follow the same rule ... all *yangming* network vessels"] Above means the hand *yangming*; below means the foot *yangming*.

[On "If their colour is mostly green-blue ... yin governs the inside"] Yang means the yang network vessels; yin means the yin network vessels. This is a general statement; the channels and network vessels seen in the hands, feet and body are all thus.

## Notes

The skin region of the *yangming* (Haifei) and diagnosis by the colour of the floating network vessels.
- The meanings of the names of the skin regions (Haifei, Shuchi, Guanshu, Shuru, Haijian, Guanzhe) are variously explained; the notes follow Wang Bing.
