# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The yin of the heart master is called Haijian; above and below follow the same rule. Looking in its region, any floating network vessels are all heart-master network vessels. When the network vessels are abundant, [evil] enters and lodges in the channel.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The yin of the heart master is called Haijian"] The heart-master vessel enters below the armpit; when its qi is not harmonious, it hinders the movement of the shoulder and armpit.

## Notes

The skin region of the heart master (*jueyin*) (Haijian).
