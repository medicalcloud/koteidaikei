# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The yin of the heart governor is called Haijian; above and below follow the same rule. Looking in its region, any floating collaterals are all heart governor collaterals. When the collaterals are abundant, [pathogen] enters and lodges in the meridian.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The yin of the heart governor is called Haijian"] The heart governor vessel enters below the armpit; when its qi is not harmonious, it hinders the movement of the shoulder and armpit.

## Notes

The skin region of the heart governor (*Jueyin*) (Haijian).
