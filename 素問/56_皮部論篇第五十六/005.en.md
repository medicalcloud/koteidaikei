# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The yang of the greater yang is called Guanshu; above and below follow the same rule. Looking in its region, any floating network vessels are all greater yang network vessels. When the network vessels are abundant, [evil] enters and lodges in the channel.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The yang of the greater yang is called Guanshu"] The gate governs the outside and its task is to calm movement with stillness; when it turns like a pivot, the qi is harmonious and balanced.

## Notes

The skin region of the greater yang (Guanshu).
