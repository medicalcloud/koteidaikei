# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The yin of the lesser yin is called Shuru; above and below follow the same rule. Looking in its region, any floating network vessels are all lesser yin network vessels. When the network vessels are abundant, [evil] enters and lodges in the channel. When it enters the channel, it pours into the channel from the yang region; when it goes out, it pours inward from yin into the bones.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The yin of the lesser yin is called Shuru"] 儒 means comply: guarding the essential and complying with the function of the opening and closing of yin and yang.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 檽 for 儒.

## Notes

The skin region of the lesser yin (Shuru).
