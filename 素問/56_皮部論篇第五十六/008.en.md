# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The yin of the greater yin is called Guanzhe; above and below follow the same rule. Looking in its region, any floating network vessels are all greater yin network vessels. When the network vessels are abundant, [evil] enters and lodges in the channel. In all, the network vessels of the twelve channels are the regions of the skin.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The yin of the greater yin is called Guanzhe"] It closes the gate on hibernating creatures, making them comply and stay stored.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 執 for 蟄.

[On "above and below follow the same rule ... lodges in the channel"] 部 always means the region of the network vessels of the channel itself. 浮 means floating and breathing.

[On "In all, the network vessels of the twelve channels are the regions of the skin"] The positions of yin and yang are arrayed, and the regions govern the skin; hence "the regions of the skin".

## Notes

The skin region of the greater yin (Guanzhe); the network vessels of the twelve channels are summarized as the skin regions.
