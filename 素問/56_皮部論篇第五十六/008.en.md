# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The yin of the greater yin is called Guanzhe; above and below follow the same rule. Looking in its region, any floating collaterals are all greater yin collaterals. When the collaterals are abundant, [pathogen] enters and lodges in the meridian. In all, the collaterals of the twelve meridians are the regions of the skin.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The yin of the greater yin is called Guanzhe"] It closes the gate on hibernating creatures, making them comply and stay stored.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 執 for 蟄.

[On "above and below follow the same rule ... lodges in the meridian"] 部 always means the region of the collaterals of the meridian itself. 浮 means floating and breathing.

[On "In all, the collaterals of the twelve meridians are the regions of the skin"] The positions of yin and yang are arrayed, and the regions govern the skin; hence "the regions of the skin".

## Notes

The skin region of the greater yin (Guanzhe); the collaterals of the twelve meridians are summarized as the skin regions.
