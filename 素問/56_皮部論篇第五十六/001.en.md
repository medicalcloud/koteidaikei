# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I have heard that the skin has its divisions, the vessels their channel order, the sinews their knots and connections, and the bones their measures, and that the illnesses they produce differ. I wish to distinguish their divisions, left and right, above and below, where yin and yang lie, and the beginning and end of illness. I wish to hear the way."

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, concerning the skin regions (皮部) divided according to the channels. There is no note by Wang Bing.
