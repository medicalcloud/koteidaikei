# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The skin is the region of the vessels. When evil lodges in the skin, the interstices open; when they open, evil enters and lodges in the network vessels; when the network vessels are full, it pours into the channel vessels; when the channel vessels are full, it enters and lodges in the fu and zang. Therefore the skin has divisions; if they are not in accord, great illness arises."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The skin is the region of the vessels"] The qi of the vessels, staying and moving, each has its yin and yang; the qi follows where the channel passes and the region governs it; hence "the region of the vessels".

[On "Therefore the skin has divisions; if they are not in accord, great illness arises"] The vessels run in the skin, each with its region; when the vessels receive evil qi, illness arises accordingly; it is not that the qi of the skin itself can produce it.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 不愈 "if not healed" for 不与; Quan Yuanqi's edition writes 不与. Yuanqi says, "If the qi is not in harmony with the channel vessels, the qi is harmed outside, the evil flows inward, and great illness surely arises."

## Notes

Illness of the skin regions is transmitted from the skin through the network vessels and channels to the fu and zang, becoming great illness.
- 不与而生大病也 is obscure; according to the Song editors the *Jiayi jing* writes 不愈 "if not healed". The translation keeps the characters.
