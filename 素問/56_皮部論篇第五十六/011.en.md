# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The skin is the region of the vessels. When pathogen lodges in the skin, the interstices open; when they open, pathogen enters and lodges in the collaterals; when the collaterals are full, it pours into the meridian vessels; when the meridian vessels are full, it enters and lodges in the fu and zang. Therefore the skin has divisions; if one does not [treat] in advance, great illness arises."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The skin is the region of the vessels"] The qi of the vessels, staying and moving, each has its yin and yang; the qi follows where the meridian passes and the region governs it; hence "the region of the vessels".

[On "Therefore the skin has divisions; if they are not in accord, great illness arises"] The vessels run in the skin, each with its region; when the vessels receive pathogenic qi, illness arises accordingly; it is not that the qi of the skin itself can produce it.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 不愈 "if not healed" for 不与; Quan Yuanqi's edition writes 不与. Yuanqi says, "If the qi is not in harmony with the meridian vessels, the qi is harmed outside, the pathogen flows inward, and great illness surely arises."

## Notes

Illness of the skin regions is transmitted from the skin through the collaterals and meridians to the fu and zang, becoming great illness.
- 不与而生大病也 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: 与 equals 預 "in advance"; if one does not treat in advance, the pathogen deepens daily and turns into a great illness. According to the Song editors, the *Jiayi jing* writes 不愈 "if not cured".
