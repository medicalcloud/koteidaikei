# 素問 皮部論篇第五十六 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "To know the skin regions, take the channel vessels as the guide; all the channels are thus.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "To know the skin regions ... all the channels are thus"] Following where the channel vessels run and stop and what they govern, the skin regions can be known. "All the channels" means the twelve channel vessels; all twelve are the same.

## Notes

The skin regions are divided according to the courses of the channels. Qibo's speech continues to unit 009.
