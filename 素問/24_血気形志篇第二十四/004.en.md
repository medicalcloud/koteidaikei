# 素問 血気形志篇第二十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When needling the *yangming*, let out blood and qi. When needling the greater yang, let out blood and avoid [letting out] qi. When needling the lesser yang, let out qi and avoid [letting out] blood. When needling the greater yin, let out qi and avoid blood. When needling the lesser yin, let out qi and avoid blood. When needling the *jueyin*, let out blood and avoid qi.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When needling the *yangming* ... let out blood and avoid qi"] This clarifies the summary of needling according to the amounts of blood and qi in the three yang and three yin above.
Song editors: The *Taisu* says, "When needling the *yangming*, let out blood and qi; when needling the greater yin, let out blood and qi." Yang Shangshan's note says, "Although the *yangming* and greater yin are exterior and interior, their blood and qi are both abundant; therefore both blood and qi are drained." If so, the greater yin is equal to the *yangming*, both having much blood and much qi. The earlier text gives the greater yin in one place as "much blood, little qi" and in another as "much qi, little blood", which cannot be known for certain. Examining the *Taisu*'s point of draining both blood and qi, neither view is correct; it is simply the same as the *yangming*. Moreover, this passage on needling the *yangming* should follow "drain what is in surplus and supplement what is insufficient" above, and should not be separated from it by the grass-measuring method and the five conditions of body and mind.

## Notes

Whether to let out blood or qi when needling each of the six channels; this ends the chapter. It corresponds to the amounts of blood and qi in unit 001.
- The Song editors hold that, following the *Taisu*, the greater yin, like the *yangming*, has much blood and much qi, and that this unit should follow the end of unit 001.
- Daizhige (重广补注黄帝内经素问) has 巻八 at the end of the chapter; this marks the juan division in that source.
