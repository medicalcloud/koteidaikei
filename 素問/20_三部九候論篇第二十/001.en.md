# 素問 三部九候論篇第二十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I have heard about the nine needles from you, Master; they are many, broad and great, beyond counting. I wish to hear the essential way, to entrust it to my descendants and transmit it to later generations, to engrave it on my bones and marrow and store it in my liver and lungs, to receive it with a blood oath and not dare to divulge it rashly, to make it accord with the Way of heaven and have a beginning and end — corresponding above to the lights of heaven, the stars and the calendrical order, and matching below the four seasons and five phases, with noble and base alternating, winter yin and summer yang. How does a person correspond to these? I wish to hear the method."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "store it in my liver and lungs, to receive it with a blood oath and not dare to divulge it rashly"] 㰱血 means drinking blood [in an oath].

[On "to make it accord with the Way of heaven"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition reads "accord with heaven and earth".

[On "corresponding above to the lights of heaven ... I wish to hear the method"] The lights of heaven are the sun, moon and stars; the calendrical order is the divisions by which the sun and moon travel through the twenty-eight lodges and 365 degrees of heaven. It means that the blood, qi, nutritive and defensive qi of the human body circulate in accord with the shifts of the seasons and in correspondence with the paths of the sun and moon. The Dipper and the pole revolve, and the ecliptic and equator differ: in winter the sun follows the ecliptic near the south, hence much yin; in summer it follows the ecliptic near the north, hence abundant yang. Of the qi of the four seasons and five phases, the dominant is noble and the assisting is base.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: Huangdi asks for the essential way of the nine needles, as a method of making the person correspond to the movements of the sun, moon and stars and to the four seasons and five phases.
- 㰱血: Wang Bing glosses it as drinking blood, i.e. the ritual of an oath. The other sources write 歃.
