# 素問 三部九候論篇第二十 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What about winter yin and summer yang?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] It concerns the time of death.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about "winter yin and summer yang" from unit 001 in relation to the time of death.
