# 素問 三部九候論篇第二十 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When a meridian is ill, treat the meridian; when the minute collaterals are ill, treat the blood of the minute collaterals; when the blood is ill and the body has pain, treat the meridians and collaterals. When the illness lies in a strange pathogen, the pulse of strange pathogen is needled contralaterally. When it lingers with wasting and does not move, needle it with measure. When there is excess above and deficiency below, feel and follow it, seek out the knotted collaterals, needle and let the blood out so as to open them. When the pupils are turned up, the greater yang is insufficient; when the eyes are fixed upward, the greater yang is already cut off. This is the key to deciding death and life and must be examined. On the fingers and the hand, five fingers above the outer ankle, retain the needle."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a meridian is ill, treat the meridian"] One seeks out the one at fault.

[On "when the minute collaterals are ill, treat the blood of the minute collaterals"] When blood lingers and stops, needle to remove it.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* reads "when the collaterals are ill, treat the blood of the collaterals", without the two characters 孫 "minute".

[On "when the blood is ill and the body has pain, treat the meridians and collaterals"] The *Lingshu jing* says: "The meridians are the interior; their branches running crosswise are the collaterals; the branches of the collaterals are the minute collaterals." By this, the minute collaterals are branches of the meridians running crosswise.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* lacks the two characters 血病 "blood is ill".

[On "When the illness lies in a strange pathogen ... needled contralaterally"] 奇 means a qi that is strange, crossed and unpaired, lying in a place crosswise to the meridians; therefore it is needled contralaterally. Contralateral needling means needling the collaterals, taking the right for the left and the left for the right.

[On "When it lingers with wasting and does not move, needle it with measure"] When the qi of illness lingers long, the body wastes and the signs do not change, one needles while nourishing by stages. This again clarifies the earlier text, "regardless of the illness, take balance as the goal".

[On "When there is excess above and deficiency below ... so as to open them"] 結 means blood knotted in the collaterals. When the blood is removed, the meridian passages open. The earlier text says, "One must first remove [the stagnation in] the blood vessels and then regulate it," making clear that the knotted collaterals are removed first.
Song editors: For 以見通之 in the classic, the *Jiayi jing* reads 以通其気 "so as to open its qi".

[On "When the pupils are turned up ... must be examined"] This again clarifies the signs of the greater yang qi about to be cut off and already cut off, mentioned before.

[On "On the fingers and the hand, five fingers above the outer ankle, retain the needle"] This is a displaced text.

## Notes

Treatment: for meridian illness treat the meridian; for illness of the minute collaterals, their blood; for blood illness with pain, the meridians and collaterals; strange pathogens are needled contralaterally; lingering illness is needled with measure; knotted collaterals are needled to let blood. Upturned pupils and fixed upward eyes show insufficiency or exhaustion of the greater yang — the key to deciding death and life. The chapter ends here.
- The last sentence, "On the fingers ... retain the needle", is regarded by Wang Bing as displaced text.
- According to the Song editors, the *Jiayi jing* lacks 孫 and 血病 and reads 以通其気 for 以見通之.
