# 素問 三部九候論篇第二十 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When a channel is ill, treat the channel; when the minute network vessels are ill, treat the blood of the minute network vessels; when the blood is ill and the body has pain, treat the channels and network vessels. When the illness lies in a strange evil, the pulse of strange evil is needled contralaterally. When it lingers with wasting and does not move, needle it with measure. When there is repletion above and depletion below, feel and follow it, seek out the knotted network vessels, needle and let the blood out so as to open them. When the pupils are turned up, the greater yang is insufficient; when the eyes are fixed upward, the greater yang is already cut off. This is the key to deciding death and life and must be examined. On the fingers and the hand, five fingers above the outer ankle, retain the needle."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a channel is ill, treat the channel"] One seeks out the one at fault.

[On "when the minute network vessels are ill, treat the blood of the minute network vessels"] When blood lingers and stops, needle to remove it.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* reads "when the network vessels are ill, treat the blood of the network vessels", without the two characters 孫 "minute".

[On "when the blood is ill and the body has pain, treat the channels and network vessels"] The *Lingshu jing* says: "The channels are the interior; their branches running crosswise are the network vessels; the branches of the network vessels are the minute network vessels." By this, the minute network vessels are branches of the channels running crosswise.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* lacks the two characters 血病 "blood is ill".

[On "When the illness lies in a strange evil ... needled contralaterally"] 奇 means a qi that is strange, crossed and unpaired, lying in a place crosswise to the channels; therefore it is needled contralaterally. Contralateral needling means needling the network vessels, taking the right for the left and the left for the right.

[On "When it lingers with wasting and does not move, needle it with measure"] When the qi of illness lingers long, the body wastes and the signs do not change, one needles while nourishing by stages. This again clarifies the earlier text, "regardless of the illness, take balance as the goal".

[On "When there is repletion above and depletion below ... so as to open them"] 結 means blood knotted in the network vessels. When the blood is removed, the channel passages open. The earlier text says, "One must first remove [the stagnation in] the blood vessels and then regulate it," making clear that the knotted network vessels are removed first.
Song editors: For 以見通之 in the classic, the *Jiayi jing* reads 以通其気 "so as to open its qi".

[On "When the pupils are turned up ... must be examined"] This again clarifies the signs of the greater yang qi about to be cut off and already cut off, mentioned before.

[On "On the fingers and the hand, five fingers above the outer ankle, retain the needle"] This is a displaced text.

## Notes

Treatment: for channel illness treat the channel; for illness of the minute network vessels, their blood; for blood illness with pain, the channels and network vessels; strange evils are needled contralaterally; lingering illness is needled with measure; knotted network vessels are needled to let blood. Upturned pupils and fixed upward eyes show insufficiency or exhaustion of the greater yang — the key to deciding death and life. The chapter ends here.
- The last sentence, "On the fingers ... retain the needle", is regarded by Wang Bing as displaced text.
- According to the Song editors, the *Jiayi jing* lacks 孫 and 血病 and reads 以通其気 for 以見通之.
