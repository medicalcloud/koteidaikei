# 素問 三部九候論篇第二十 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It too has heaven, earth and man. Heaven observes the qi of the corners of the head, earth observes the qi of the mouth and teeth, and man observes the qi of the ears and eyes. Each of the three regions has heaven, earth and man. Three completes heaven, three completes earth, three completes man; three times three combined makes nine; nine divides into the nine fields, and the nine fields become the nine storehouses. Therefore there are five storehouses of spirit and four of form, together nine storehouses. When the five zang are already ruined, the complexion must be dull, and dullness means certain death."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Heaven observes the qi of the corners of the head"] Its position is in the region of the corners of the head; therefore it observes their qi.

[On "earth observes the qi of the mouth and teeth"] Its position is near the mouth and teeth; therefore it observes them.

[On "man observes the qi of the ears and eyes"] Its position is in front of the ears and its vessel reaches the outer canthus; therefore it observes them.

[On "Each of the three regions has heaven, earth and man. Three completes heaven"] Song editors: From "three completes heaven" to "together nine storehouses" overlaps with the text of the *Liujie zangxiang lun*; the explanation is given in that chapter.

[On "three times three combined makes nine ... the nine fields become the nine storehouses"] By this it corresponds to the utmost number of heaven and earth.

[On "Therefore there are five storehouses of spirit and four of form, together nine storehouses"] The storehouses of spirit are: the liver stores the *hun*, the heart the spirit, the spleen intention, the lung the *po*, and the kidney the will. Because spirit qi dwells in them all, there are said to be five storehouses of spirit. The storehouses of form are like vessels, stretched outside, empty but not collapsing, containing things; hence storehouses of form. The four storehouses of form are: first, the corners of the head; second, the ears and eyes; third, the mouth and teeth; fourth, the chest.
Song editors: The explanation of the storehouses of spirit in the note is the text of the *Xuanming wuqi*, and it overlaps with the notes to the *Shengqi tongtian lun* and *Liujie zangxiang lun*.

[On "When the five zang are already ruined ... dullness means certain death"] 夭 means the abnormal signs of the colour of death. Colour is the banner of the spirit and the zang are the dwellings of the spirit; therefore when the spirit departs the zang are ruined, and when the zang are ruined abnormal signs appear in the colour and one dies.

## Notes

The sites observed by the three indicators of the upper region (corners of the head, mouth and teeth, ears and eyes); the three regions and nine indicators correspond to the numbers of heaven and earth and the nine storehouses (five of spirit, four of form). When the five zang are ruined, the complexion is dull and death follows.
- According to the Song editors, "three completes heaven" to "together nine storehouses" overlaps with the *Liujie zangxiang lun* (chapter 9).
