# 素問 三部九候論篇第二十 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The utmost number of heaven and earth begins with one and ends with nine. One is heaven, two is earth, three is man; multiplying by three, three threes are nine, corresponding to the nine fields. Therefore a person has three regions, and each region has three indicators, by which one decides death and life, deals with all illnesses, regulates depletion and repletion, and removes evil illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The utmost number ... ends with nine"] Nine is [the highest] odd number; therefore the numbers of heaven and earth culminate in it.

[On "multiplying by three ... corresponding to the nine fields"] The *Erya* says: "Outside the city is the suburb, outside the suburb the *dian*, outside the *dian* the pasture, outside the pasture the forest, outside the forest the *jiong*, outside the *jiong* the field." It means distance.
Song editors: Mr Wang's citation of the *Erya* as proof differs in places from the present *Erya*; this has been noted in the note to the *Liujie zangxiang lun* above.

[On "Therefore a person has three regions ... removes evil illness"] The so-called three regions are the upper, middle and lower parts of the body, not the *cun*, *guan* and *chi*. The channel passages run through the three regions; therefore examining whether [qi] is present or absent is all done through them. By supplementing and draining with the needle, evil illness can be removed.

## Notes

The numbers of heaven and earth run from one to nine; a person has three regions (upper, middle, lower), each with three indicators, nine in all, by which life and death are decided. The chapter title "Three Regions and Nine Indicators" comes from this.
- Wang Bing stresses that the three regions are the upper, middle and lower parts of the body, not the *cun*, *guan* and *chi* positions.
