# 素問 三部九候論篇第二十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "What a subtle question! This is the utmost number of heaven and earth."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] The Way penetrates the subtle; hence "a subtle question". "The utmost number" means the number of the ultimate.

## Notes

Qibo praises the question and says it concerns the utmost number of heaven and earth.
