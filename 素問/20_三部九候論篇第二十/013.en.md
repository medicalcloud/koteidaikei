# 素問 三部九候論篇第二十 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How does one decide death and life?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] In gauging whether the body is fat or thin and regulating fullness and emptiness of qi, one does not ask the patient but takes balance as the standard; [he asks how] one decides by the signs of death and life.

## Notes

Huangdi asks how to decide between death and life.
- 盈 in Wang Bing's note was supplied where the transcription gave only a component description (see the 【補】 note).
