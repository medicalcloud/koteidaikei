# 素問 三部九候論篇第二十 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "There is a lower region, a middle region and an upper region, and each region has three indicators. The three indicators are heaven, earth and man; one must be shown and guided [by a teacher] before taking them as true. The heaven of the upper region is the pulsing vessels on both sides of the forehead; the earth of the upper region, the pulsing vessels on both cheeks; the man of the upper region, the pulsing vessel in front of the ear. The heaven of the middle region is the hand greater yin; the earth of the middle region, the hand *yangming*; the man of the middle region, the hand lesser yin. The heaven of the lower region is the foot *jueyin*; the earth of the lower region, the foot lesser yin; the man of the lower region, the foot greater yin. Therefore the heaven of the lower region observes the liver, the earth observes the kidney, and the man observes the qi of the spleen and stomach."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The three indicators are heaven, earth and man; one must be shown and guided before taking them as true"] It says one must consult and receive from a teacher. The *Zheng sishi lun* (chapter 78) says, "Those who do not complete their training under a teacher, recklessly practise various arts, call false words the Way, change the names to claim the credit, use stone needles recklessly, and later bring blame on themselves" — this is the warning. The *Li* says: "Do not *zhi* doubtful matters." 質 means to settle.

[On "The heaven of the upper region is the pulsing vessels on both sides of the forehead"] They are on both sides of the forehead and beat against the hand; it is where the vessel qi of the foot lesser yang runs.

[On "the earth of the upper region, the pulsing vessels on both cheeks"] They are on both sides below the nostrils near Juliao and beat against the hand; it is where the vessel qi of the hand and foot *yangming* runs.

[On "the man of the upper region, the pulsing vessel in front of the ear"] It is in the hollow in front of the ear and beats against the hand; it is where the vessel qi of the hand [and foot] lesser yang runs.

[On "The heaven of the middle region is the hand greater yin"] This means the lung vessel, at the *cunkou* behind the palm; it is called Jingqu and beats against the hand.

[On "the earth of the middle region, the hand *yangming*"] This means the large-intestine vessel, between the bones of the thumb and index finger, at Hegu, beating against the hand.

[On "the man of the middle region, the hand lesser yin"] This means the heart vessel, at the tip of the sharp bone behind the palm, at Shenmen, beating against the hand. In the *Lingshu jing*, "Discussion on Holding the Needle", it is asked, "The lesser yin has no transport point; does the heart not fall ill?" and answered, "Its outer channel falls ill but the zang does not; therefore one takes only its channel at the tip of the sharp bone behind the palm" — this is precisely it.

[On "The heaven of the lower region is the foot *jueyin*"] This means the liver vessel, outside the hairline, one and a half *cun* below Yangshi, in the hollow at Wuli; it is taken lying down and beats against the hand. In women it is taken at Taichong, in the hollow two *cun* behind the base joint of the big toe.

[On "the earth of the lower region, the foot lesser yin"] This means the kidney vessel, in the hollow behind the inner ankle above the heel bone, at Taixi, beating against the hand.

[On "the man of the lower region, the foot greater yin"] This means the spleen vessel, above the "fish belly", between the sinews, directly below Wuli, at Jimen. Loosening the leg of the single garment and pressing deep one finds it beating against the hand. To observe the stomach qi, one should take it on top of the instep, in the point at Chongyang, where the pulse beats against the hand.
Song editors: The passage from "the heaven of the upper region" to here was originally at the end of the chapter, where its meaning did not connect. It properly discusses the three regions and nine indicators and should be placed here; we have now moved it from the end of the chapter, following the arrangement of Huangfu Mi's *Jiayi jing*.

[On "Therefore the heaven of the lower region observes the liver"] The foot *jueyin* vessel runs there.

[On "the earth observes the kidney"] The foot lesser yin vessel runs there.

[On "and the man observes the qi of the spleen and stomach"] The foot greater yin vessel runs there. The spleen and stomach are connected by a membrane; therefore one observes the spleen and the stomach together.

## Notes

The concrete sites of the three regions and nine indicators: the upper region (head) at the pulsing vessels of the forehead, cheeks and in front of the ears; the middle region (hand) at the hand greater yin, *yangming* and lesser yin; the lower region (foot) at the foot *jueyin*, lesser yin and greater yin, with the organs each observes.
- According to the Song editors, the passage on the sites from "the heaven of the upper region" to the note on "the man of the lower region" was originally at the end of the chapter and was moved here following the *Jiayi jing*.
- 穴 in Wang Bing's note was supplied where the transcription gave only a component description (see the 【補】 note).
- Wang Bing's 手手少陽 is as in the base text (probably a one-character error); the translation reads "hand [and foot]".
