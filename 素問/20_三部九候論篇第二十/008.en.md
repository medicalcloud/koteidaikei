# 素問 三部九候論篇第二十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It too has heaven, earth and man. Heaven observes the lung, earth observes the qi of the chest, and man observes the heart."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Heaven observes the lung"] The hand greater yin vessel corresponds there.

[On "earth observes the qi of the chest"] The hand *yangming* vessel corresponds there. The classic says, "The intestines and stomach are observed together"; therefore it observes the chest.

[On "and man observes the heart"] The hand lesser yin vessel corresponds there.

## Notes

The organs observed by the three indicators of the middle region (lung, chest, heart).
