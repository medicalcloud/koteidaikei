# 素問 三部九候論篇第二十 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "One must first gauge whether the body is fat or thin, so as to regulate the depletion or repletion of its qi: drain what is replete and supplement what is depleted. One must first remove [the stagnation in] the blood vessels and then regulate it; regardless of the illness, take balance as the goal."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "One must first gauge ... supplement what is depleted"] 度 means to measure. Draining the replete and supplementing the depleted is what is called following the Way of heaven. The *Laozi* says: "The Way of heaven reduces what is in surplus and supplements what is insufficient."

[On "One must first remove the blood vessels ... take balance as the goal"] Full, hard blood vessels mean evil is lingering; therefore one first needles to remove the blood and then regulates. One should not ask the patient about fullness and emptiness but take the balancing of the pulse qi as the standard.

## Notes

First gauge the body's build and regulate depletion and repletion — draining the replete, supplementing the depleted — remove stagnant blood and then regulate, aiming at a balanced pulse.
- 盈 in Wang Bing's note was supplied where the transcription gave only a component description (see the 【補】 note).
