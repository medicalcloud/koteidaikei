# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I have now heard about the degrees of heaven; I would like to hear how the numbers of qi match them."

## Notes

Following the degrees of heaven, Huangdi asks how the numbers of qi match them.
- There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 巳 in 余巳聞 is the base text's form, translated as 已 "already".
