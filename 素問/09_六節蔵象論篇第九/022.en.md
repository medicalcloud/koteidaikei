# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When change arrives, there is illness. If it is what one overcomes, it is mild; if it is what one cannot overcome, it is severe; if on top of this one is again affected by evil, one dies. Therefore, not at its time it is mild; at its time it is severe."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole passage] Even the qi spread by the blue heaven do not go beyond the five phases; how can humans, living within qi, not correspond to the way of heaven? When a person's qi is disordered and does not follow the constancy of heaven, there are signs of illness and death. The *Zuozhuan* says, "To go against heaven is inauspicious" — this is of that kind. Suppose in a year in which wood is in charge, fire qi arrives: illness after two years; earth qi arrives: illness after three years; metal qi arrives: illness after four years; water qi arrives: illness after five years. When the true qi is insufficient and one is again affected by evil, the true qi is weak within; therefore being again affected by evil, one dies. Suppose that, in a year one does not govern, qi interfere with one another: there will only be mild illness, not necessarily harming the spirit storehouses within; therefore "not at its time it is mild" and can be sustained. If it is the year one is in charge of, one is easily struck by evil qi; therefore at the time one is in charge the illness is severe. All the qi, at the time of their flourishing, must receive evil; hence "not at its time it is mild; at its time it is severe". The *Tongping xushi lun* (chapter 28) says, "Not at its time one lives; at its time one dies." "At" means the year that is exactly in charge.

## Notes

When change arrives there is illness, mild if it is the qi one overcomes and severe if it is the qi one cannot overcome; being again affected by evil, one dies. Whether it falls at one's own time decides the severity.
- Wang Bing gives examples of how many years later illness arises when other qi arrive in a year of wood.
