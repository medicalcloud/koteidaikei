# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The five qi take their stand in turn, and each has what it overcomes; the changes of abundance and depletion are their norm."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] It says that the appearance of changes of abundance and depletion is the constant way of heaven.

## Notes

The five qi take turns in governing, and changes of abundance and depletion are normal.
