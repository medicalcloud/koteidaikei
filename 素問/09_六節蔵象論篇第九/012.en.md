# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It is that which is without fault."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is that which is without fault"] If the usual signs are not violated, there is no fault.

## Notes

Balanced qi is that which does not depart from the usual signs.
