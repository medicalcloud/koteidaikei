# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. I have heard that when qi combine there is form, and that names are rectified according to changes. As for the circulation of heaven and earth and the transformations of yin and yang, which are few and which many in the myriad things? May I hear about this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] Song editors: The passage from "Qibo replied: 'How illuminating your question is!'" above to here is absent from both Quan Yuanqi's annotated edition and the *Taisu*; we suspect it was supplied by Mr Wang.

## Notes

Huangdi asks how the circulation of heaven and earth and the transformations of yin and yang are distributed, few or many, among all things.
- The Song editors suspect that the passage from "Qibo replied: 'How illuminating your question is!'" in unit 002 to this unit was supplied by Wang Bing, since it is absent from Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu*. Like the "seven great treatises", it contains the doctrine of circulating qi.
