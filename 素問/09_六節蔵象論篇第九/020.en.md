# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The qi of the blue heaven cannot be without norms. When the qi do not succeed, this is called abnormal, and the abnormal means change."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the qi do not succeed ... the abnormal means change"] "Change" means altering the constancy of heaven.

## Notes

The qi of heaven have norms; failure of the qi to succeed one another is abnormal and amounts to change (anomaly).
