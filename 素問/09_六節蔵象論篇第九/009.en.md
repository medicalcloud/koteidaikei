# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "The beginning of the five circulations is like a ring without end; what about their excess and insufficiency?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks about excess and insufficiency within the ring-like cycle of the five circulations.
- There is no note by Wang Bing.
- Daizhige (the plain text) has an extra 如 after 何如.
