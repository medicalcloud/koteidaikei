# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Are there cases of non-succession?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] It asks whether there are cases in which the qi of the five phases do not succeed one another.

## Notes

Huangdi asks whether the qi of the five phases ever fail to succeed one another in order.
