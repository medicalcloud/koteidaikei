# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What are the images of the zang?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] "Image" means what appears outwardly and can be examined.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about the images of the zang (the outward manifestations of the organs). The "Zangxiang" of the chapter title comes from this.
