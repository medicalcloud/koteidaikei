# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "This is what the Lord on High kept secret and the former masters transmitted."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] The Lord on High means the sovereign of high antiquity. The former master is Qibo's ancestral teacher, Jiudaiji, who in high antiquity regulated complexion and pulse. The *Yijing bianqi lun* (chapter 13) says: "In high antiquity Jiudaiji was made to regulate complexion and pulse and communicate with the spirit illumination." The preface to the *Basu jing* says: "The Heavenly Master answered Huangdi: 'I have regulated complexion and pulse under Jiudaiji for three generations already.'" From this it can be known.
Song editors: 素 is written 索 in one version, or 八 is taken as 太. The present *Taisu* does not have this text.

## Notes

Qibo says that the theory of qi he is about to explain was kept secret by the Lord on High and transmitted by the former masters.
- Wang Bing identifies the former master as Jiudaiji, citing the *Yijing bianqi lun* (chapter 13) and the preface to the *Basu jing*.
- 巳 in Wang Bing's 巳三世 is the base text's form, translated as 已.
