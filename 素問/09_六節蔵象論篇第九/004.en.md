# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Heaven takes six times six as its nodes, and earth regulates its meetings by nine times nine. Heaven has ten days; the days complete six rounds to make the cycle of *jia*, and the *jia* returns six times to end the year: this is the rule of 360 days. From of old, communication with heaven has been the basis of life, and [this basis] rests on yin and yang; its qi — the nine provinces and the nine orifices — all communicate with the qi of heaven. Therefore what it generates is five and its qi is three. Three completes heaven, three completes earth, three completes humans; three times three combined makes nine; nine divides into the nine fields, and the nine fields become the nine storehouses. Therefore there are four storehouses of form and five storehouses of spirit, together nine storehouses that correspond to them."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Heaven takes six times six as its nodes, and earth regulates its meetings by nine times nine"] Song editors: The beginning of the chapter says "humans regulate their meetings by nine times nine".

[On "Heaven has ten days ... this is the rule of 360 days"] The ten days are the days *jia*, *yi*, *bing*, *ding*, *wu*, *ji*, *geng*, *xin*, *ren* and *gui*. Ten is the utmost number of heaven and earth; the "Xici" of the *Yi* says "heaven nine, earth ten", which is the meaning. In sixty days the number of *jiazi* completes a cycle; when *jiazi* has gone round six times and begins again, the days of one year are ended. This is the rule of a 360-day year, not the number of the degrees of heaven. This is presumably for twelve months of thirty days each; if the short months are subtracted, the days differ again.

[On "From of old, communication with heaven ... all communicate with the qi of heaven"] Communication with heaven means the original qi, that is, the heavenly truth. The body is born by means of earth, but life is endowed only by heaven; therefore the qi that serves life communicates with and is tied to heaven, and takes its endowment from yin and yang as its root. The *Baoming quanxing lun* (chapter 25) says, "Humans are born of earth and their life hangs on heaven; the union of the qi of heaven and earth is called human." The *Siqi tiaoshen dalun* (chapter 2) says, "Yin and yang and the four seasons are the end and beginning of the myriad things and the root of death and life," and also, "To go against the root is to cut down the foundation and ruin the truth." This is the meaning. The nine provinces are Ji, Yan, Qing, Xu, Yang, Jing, Yu, Liang and Yong. Earth has the nine provinces laid out and humans have the nine orifices; the essence and spirit go and return, and the qi are joined and alike; hence "the nine provinces and the nine orifices". The *Lingshu jing* says, "Earth has nine provinces; humans have nine orifices" — this is the meaning. Mentioning "its qi" first means that the qi of heavenly truth is always tied to the centre. The qi of heaven is unbroken, the true spirit belongs within, and in moving and storing, movement and stillness, all communicate with heaven; hence "all communicate with the qi of heaven".

[On "Therefore what it generates is five and its qi is three"] What the body consists of operates by means of the five phases; tracing its origin, it is generated and completed through the three qi. Hence "what it generates is five and its qi is three". The three of the qi also match the three origins. Hence the text below says:
Song editors: From "From of old, communication with heaven" to here is the same as in the *Shengqi tongtian lun* (chapter 3), but the notes differ considerably; both should be consulted.

[On "Three completes heaven, three completes earth, three completes humans"] It is not only humans who are generated by the three qi; the way of heaven and earth is likewise. Therefore the trigrams *Qian*, *Kun* and the others of the *Yi* all necessarily have three [lines].

[On "three times three combined makes nine ... the nine fields become the nine storehouses"] The nine fields take their meaning from corresponding to the nine storehouses. The *Erya* says: "Outside the city is the suburb, outside the suburb is the *dian*, outside the *dian* is the pasture, outside the pasture is the forest, outside the forest is the *jiong*, outside the *jiong* is the field." This is what is meant.
Song editors: The present *Erya* says: "Outside the city is called the suburb, outside the suburb the pasture, outside the pasture the field, outside the field the forest, outside the forest the *jiong*," which differs from what Mr Wang cites.

[On "Therefore there are four storehouses of form and five storehouses of spirit ... that correspond to them"] The four storehouses of form are: first, the head and horns; second, the ears and eyes; third, the mouth and teeth; fourth, the chest. The form is divided into storehouses, hence the name. The five storehouses of spirit are: first the liver, second the heart, third the spleen, fourth the lung, fifth the kidney. The spirit is stored within, hence the name. The so-called storehouses of spirit are: the liver stores the *hun*, the heart the spirit, the spleen intention, the lung the *po*, and the kidney the will. Therefore these two are distinguished.
Song editors: This is the text of the *Xuanming wuqi* (chapter 23); it overlaps with the note to the *Shengqi tongtian lun* and also with the note to the *Sanbu jiuhou lun* (chapter 20). The explanation of the names "storehouses of spirit" and "storehouses of form" is given in the note to the *Sanbu jiuhou lun*.

## Notes

Heaven's nodes of six times six (the ten stems going round six times to make 360 days) and earth's regulation by nine times nine are explained. Human life communicates with heaven; three completes heaven, earth and humans, and three times three makes nine, corresponding to the nine fields and nine storehouses — four storehouses of form and five of spirit.
- According to the Song editors, unit 001 says "humans regulate their meetings by nine times nine" while here it is "earth"; the two disagree.
- "From of old, communication with heaven ... its qi is three" is almost the same as the opening of the *Shengqi tongtian lun* (chapter 3).
- "Four storehouses of form": Wang Bing lists the head and horns, ears and eyes, mouth and teeth, and chest.
- 異, 楊 and 予 in Wang Bing's list of the nine provinces are the base text's forms (for 冀, 揚 and 豫).
