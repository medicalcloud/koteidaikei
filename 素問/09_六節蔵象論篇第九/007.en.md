# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Please let me hear it in full."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Please let me hear it in full"] 遂 means fully.

## Notes

Huangdi asks Qibo for a full explanation.
