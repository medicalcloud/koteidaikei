# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I have heard that heaven completes a year by its nodes of six times six, and that humans regulate their meetings by nine times nine; counting, humans also have three hundred and sixty-five joints, and this has long been taken as [corresponding to] heaven and earth. I do not know what this means."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "humans regulate their meetings by nine times nine"] Song editors: The text below says, "Earth regulates its meetings by nine times nine."

[On "counting, humans also have three hundred and sixty-five joints ... I do not know what this means"] "Nodes of six times six" means completing the nodal limit of one year by six times completing the six *jia* days. "Regulating the meetings by nine times nine" means regulating the meeting and communication of the human form by nine times going round the number of the nine fields. The correspondence of the human three hundred and sixty-five joints to heaven's nodes of six times six has long been held. If one also takes nine times nine as the measure, a full cycle comes only after two years and a large half; [Huangdi] does not know what the true origin of this method is.
Song editors: Wang Bing's note says "a full cycle comes only after two years and a large half", but if the meetings are regulated by nine times nine, it should say "a full cycle comes only after two and a quarter years".

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: the relation between heaven's nodes of six times six (six cycles of sixty days making 360 days), the human regulation of meetings by nine times nine, and the 365 joints of the body.
- According to the Song editors, the text below (unit 004) reads "earth regulates its meetings by nine times nine" rather than "humans".
- Wang Bing and the Song editors calculate the cycle obtained by taking nine times nine (81) as the measure, with different results.
