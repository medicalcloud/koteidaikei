# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Spring overcomes long summer, long summer overcomes winter, winter overcomes summer, summer overcomes autumn, and autumn overcomes spring. This is what is called gaining the victory of the seasons of the five phases. Each names its zang by its qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] Spring corresponds to wood, and wood overcomes earth; long summer corresponds to earth, and earth overcomes water; winter corresponds to water, and water overcomes fire; summer corresponds to fire, and fire overcomes metal; autumn corresponds to metal, and metal overcomes wood. It is always so. Long summer is added to the four seasons; hence "gaining the victory of the seasons of the five phases". The so-called long summer is the sixth month: earth is born from fire and grows in the midst of summer; having grown, it flourishes; hence it is called long summer. "Naming the zang by the qi" means that the wood of spring corresponds within to the liver, the earth of long summer to the spleen, the water of winter to the kidney, the fire of summer to the heart, and the metal of autumn to the lung; hence "each names its zang by its qi". 命 means to name.

## Notes

The controlling relations of the five seasons and five phases; the five zang are named by the qi of the seasons.
- Wang Bing takes long summer as the sixth month.
- Almost the same text appears in the *Jingui zhenyan lun* (chapter 4).
