# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It is in the classic."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is in the classic"] It says that the *Yuji zhenzang lun* has already set out the meaning of balance, excess and insufficiency of the five qi.
Song editors: Wang Bing's note says it is already set out in the *Yuji zhenzang lun*, but that chapter speaks of excess and insufficiency of the pulse and does not discuss excess and insufficiency of the circulating qi and balanced qi. He should have said that it is already set out in the *Qijiaobian dalun* and *Wuchang zhengda lun*.

## Notes

Qibo says that excess and insufficiency are described in the classic (another chapter).
- As to which chapter is meant, Wang Bing says the *Yuji zhenzang lun* (chapter 19), the Song editors the *Qijiaobian dalun* (chapter 69) and *Wuchang zhengda lun* (chapter 70).
