# 素問 六節蔵象論篇第九 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I have already heard about the meetings of six times six and nine times nine. You, Master, spoke of accumulating qi to fill out the intercalary month; I would like to hear what is meant by qi. I ask you, Master, to dispel my ignorance and resolve my doubts."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I would like to hear what is meant by qi ... resolve my doubts"] [He asks Qibo] to proclaim the essential points, to open up what has not been heard, to resolve the doubts in the hearts of those who are perplexed and open the ears of the ignorant, so that all may understand thoroughly and clearly.

## Notes

Taking up "the accumulated surplus of qi fills out the intercalary month" in unit 002, Huangdi asks what "qi" means.
- Daizhige (*Chongguang buzhu Huangdi neijing suwen*) writes 何為 for 何謂.
- In the base, 1 instance(s) of 巳 meaning 已 "already/stop" to 已 are emended (graphic confusion; the base often does not distinguish 巳, 已 and 己, while Wikisource's plain text and other sources mostly have the correct character).
