# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The east generates wind, and wind generates wood; its virtue is spreading harmony, its transformation generating and flourishing, its government unfolding and opening, its decree wind, its change shaking and issuing, its disaster scattering and falling. The south generates heat, and heat generates fire; its virtue is manifest display, its transformation flourishing luxuriance, its government bright shining, its decree heat, its change smelting and melting, its disaster burning. The center generates damp, and damp generates earth; its virtue is sultry steaming, its transformation abundance and completeness, its government calm stillness, its decree damp, its change sudden downpour, its disaster incessant rain and collapse. The west generates dryness, and dryness generates metal; its virtue is clarity and purity, its transformation tightening and contraction, its government sharp and cutting, its decree dryness, its change austere killing, its disaster falling while green. The north generates cold, and cold generates water; its virtue is chill and cold, its transformation clear and tranquil, its government congealing solemnity, its decree cold, its change biting cold, its disaster ice, snow, frost and hail. Thus, examining their movements, there are virtue, transformation, government, decree, change and disaster; things depend on them and humans respond to them."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The east generates wind ... its disaster scattering and falling"] 敷 means spreading; 和 means harmonious qi. 栄 means nourishing flourishing; 舒 means unfolding; 啓 means opening. 振 means raging; 発 means issuing. 散 means things blown about and scattering down.
Song editors: The *Wuyunxing dalun* says "its virtue is harmony, its transformation flourishing, its government dispersal, its decree spreading out, its change smashing and snapping, its calamity falling"; the sense agrees with this.

[On "The south generates heat ... its disaster burning"] Song editors: On examination, the *Wuyunxing dalun* says "its virtue is manifestness, its transformation luxuriance, its government brightness, its decree sultry steaming, its change blazing and melting, its calamity burning".

[On "The center generates damp ... its disaster incessant rain and collapse"] 溽 means damp; 蒸 means heat. 驟注 means sudden rain; 霖 means prolonged rain; 潰 means rotten mud.
Song editors: The *Wuyunxing dalun* says "its virtue is moistening, its transformation fullness, its government tranquility, its decree clouds and rain, its change agitation and downpour, its calamity soaking rain and collapse".

[On "The west generates dryness ... its disaster falling while green"] 緊 means shrinking; 斂 means gathering in; 勁 means sharp; 切 means urgent; 燥 means dry. Austere killing means wind moving grasses and trees with a dry sound. When killing qi is very extreme, trees dry while green and fall.
Song editors: The *Wuyunxing dalun* says "its virtue is clarity, its transformation contraction, its government sharpness, its decree fog and dew, its change austere killing, its calamity falling while green".

[On "The north generates cold ... its disaster ice, snow, frost and hail"] 淒滄 means light cold. 謐 means still. 粛中列 [presumably congealing solemnity] means severe and orderly. 凓冽 means extreme cold. Ice, snow, frost and hail are formed by the congealing of cold qi; when water retaliates against fire, they occur out of season.
Song editors: The *Wuyunxing dalun* says "its virtue is cold, its transformation solemnity, its government stillness, its change congealing frost, its calamity ice and hail".

[On "Thus, examining their movements ... humans respond to them"] Virtue, transformation, government and decree are harmonious qi; their movement and stillness, prevalence and retaliation act on the myriad things, all of which are generated and completed. Change and disaster are killing qi; they come out violently and swiftly, move suddenly and urgently, and act to damage and harm. Although all are the working of movement and stillness done by heaven and earth themselves, some things cannot withstand the movement and are damaged, fall ill or die.

## Notes

The virtue, transformation, government, decree, change and disaster of the qi of the five directions. The wording differs from that of the *Wuyunxing dalun*.
