# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo bowed his head to the ground twice and replied: "How illuminating the question! This is a clear way. It is what the supreme emperors valued and what my former teachers transmitted. Though I am not clever, I have heard its meaning in the past."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is what the supreme emperors valued ... I have heard its meaning in the past"] It means that this is not what 已 [his own] mind knew by nature, but that he fully heard the meaning handed down and received by the ancients of old.

## Notes

Qibo says he heard this way from his former teachers.
- 已 in Wang Bing's 已心 is read as 己 "own".
