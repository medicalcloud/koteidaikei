# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Your account, Master, of the changes of the five qi and the responses of the four seasons may be called thorough. But the disturbances of qi arise from chance encounters; their outbreaks have no fixed meeting, and disasters suddenly combine. How can they be anticipated?"

## Notes

A question on how to anticipate sudden disturbances of qi. There is no note by Wang Bing.
