# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "You, Master, have said that the indications of the years, in deficiency and excess, correspond above to the five planets. Now virtue, transformation, government and decree, disaster, calamity, change and alteration occur irregularly and move suddenly. Do [the planets] also change because of them?"

## Notes

A question on whether the five planets respond also to sudden disturbances. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- Two 殆知閣 texts have 其 in 歳候其不及其太過 (see the variants). The translation reads it as "the deficiency and excess of the indications of the years".
