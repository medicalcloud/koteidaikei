# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "How thorough the question! When wood is deficient, if in spring there is the transformation of branches singing and pitch-pipes flowing freely, then in autumn there will be the government of fog, dew, clarity and coolness; if in spring there is the prevalence of bleak cold and ravaging, then in summer there will be the retaliation of scorching heat and burning. Its calamity is in the east, its depot is the liver, its illness resides within in the flanks and without in the joints.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "if in spring there is the transformation ... Its calamity is in the east"] Transformation is harmonious qi; prevalence is metal qi; retaliation is fire qi. Fire's retaliation against metal is all because of wood; hence the outbreak of calamity is always in the east. The other calamities likewise.
Song editors: For deficient wood and fire, the transformations of spring and summer and the government of autumn and winter are mentioned first: first the government and transformation of wood and fire, then the changes of prevalence and retaliation.

[On "its depot is the liver, its illness resides within in the flanks and without in the joints"] The governor of the function of the east.

## Notes

For a year of deficient wood, the seasonal transformation, government, prevalence and retaliation, and the direction of calamity, the depot and the site of illness. Qibo's speech continues to unit 026.
