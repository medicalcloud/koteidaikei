# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "How thorough the question! When the year's wood is deficient, dryness prevails greatly; the generating qi fails to respond and plants flourish late. When austere killing is extreme, hard trees are split and stuck, the soft wither and dry while green; above, it corresponds to the Great White Star. The people suffer from coldness in the center, pain in the flanks, lower abdominal pain, rumbling intestines and loose stools; cool rain comes at times; above, it corresponds to the Great White Star; its grain is the green. When *yangming* presides above, the generating qi loses its government, plants flourish a second time, the transforming qi is hasty; above, it corresponds to the Great White and Quelling Stars; what it governs, the green, [withers] early. In retaliation, scorching heat spreads fire, the damp nature becomes dry, soft and fragile plants are scorched and withered, the lower parts grow again, and flowers and fruit transform together. The illnesses are cold and heat, sores and ulcers, prickly heat rashes, carbuncles and boils; above, it corresponds to the Sparkling Deluder and the Great White Star; its grain is the white and hard. White dew falls early, gathering and killing qi moves, cold rain harms things, insects eat the sweet and yellow, spleen earth receives the pathogen, red qi transforms late, heart qi governs late and overcomes lung metal above, white qi yields, its grain does not ripen, and there are coughing and nasal congestion; above, it corresponds to the Sparkling Deluder and the Great White Star.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How thorough the question! When the year's wood is deficient, dryness prevails greatly"] When clear chill comes at times with added light cold, it is called dry qi; dryness is the qi of metal.

[On "the generating qi fails to respond and plants flourish late"] Being behind time is called failing to respond.

[On "When austere killing is extreme ... corresponds to the Great White Star"] When heaven and earth are chill, the sun looks dim, it seems to rain but does not, seems clear but is not, people feel dismal and the atmosphere is congealed and contracted, this is extreme austere killing. 剛 means hard and stiff. 辟著 means split and stuck to the branches and stems, drying without falling. 柔 means soft; 蒼 means green. The leaves of soft trees dry and curl without changing their green color. Wood qi is deficient and metal qi rides over it; the brightness of the Great White Star shines its rays across the sky.

[On "rumbling intestines and loose stools; cool rain comes at times; above, it corresponds to the Great White Star"] Song editors: In the illnesses of the people among the five transformations of deficiency, the corresponding stars always speak of the star of the movement losing its color and the feared star presiding over the associated lodges to bring disaster. Only here is the feared star mentioned without the star of the movement; the classic's text is defective. It should say "corresponds to the Great White Star and the Year Star".

[On "cool rain comes at times; above, it corresponds to the Great White Star; its grain is the green"] Metal qi riding over wood is a liver illness. Those affected by this qi whose intestines rumble of themselves with loose stools have no pain in the flanks or lower abdomen. Slight cases improve and severe cases stop; meeting the qi of summer, they stop of themselves; meeting the qi of autumn, they recur. "Cool rain comes at times" means it comes in its season. Metal and earth transform together, so coolness and rain proceed together. When fire qi comes to retaliate, summer rain is scant. When metal qi overcomes wood and the Great White Star presides over the associated regions, all suffer disaster. If metal prevails for the whole year and fire qi does not retaliate, green-colored grains do not ripen.
Song editors: On examination, coldness in the center and pain in the flanks and lower abdomen are the signs of liver illness from metal riding over wood; rumbling intestines and loose stools are signs of spleen illness. Presumably, since wood is scant, spleen earth has nothing to fear and is insulted, and on the contrary receives the pathogen.

[On "When *yangming* presides above ... what it governs, the green, [withers] early"] All *ding* years. In *dingmao* and *dingyou* years *yangming* presides above; these are called years of heaven's punishment. Metal qi receives heaven and overcomes wood below; hence the generating qi loses its government and plants flourish a second time. Since the generating qi loses its government, the flowers of trees open late; since metal qi suppresses wood, they first flourish in autumn and summer and bear and ripen fruit. Because the transforming qi is hasty, they bear late and are completed. Metal qi overcomes wood, and heaven responds likewise; hence the Great White Star appears with bright, abundant rays. Wood qi being scant, earth qi is unrestrained, so the growth of the transforming qi is hasty. Wood scant and metal prevailing, heaven's qi responds; hence the Quelling Star and Great White Star are lustrous and bright. Green things also wither and fall early, because wood is scant and metal rides over it.
Song editors: Among the five transformations of deficiency, only wood with *yangming* above, earth with *jueyin* above and water with greater yin above are recorded, not wood with *jueyin* above, earth with greater yin above or metal with *yangming* above. The intention of the classic is to record the severe cases. So among the excessive movements only fire presiding over fire and water over water are mentioned, and among these deficient movements only "wood presiding over metal, earth over wood, water over earth"; hence *jueyin* over wood, greater yin over earth and *yangming* over metal are not mentioned.

[On "In retaliation, scorching heat spreads fire ... its grain is the white and hard"] Fire qi retaliates against metal and great heat arises in summer; so the damp nature of things at times turns dry. Flowing fire scorches things, so soft and fragile plants and creepers all die above and grow again below; acrid, hot plants die and do not grow again. With small heat few die; with great heat many die. When the fire's great retaliation is over, earth qi comes in between and cool rain falls; then sour, bitter, sweet and salty things of cold nature sprout again, and the newly opened and the earlier set fruit together receive transformation and ripen. When fire retaliates against metal, the Great White Star dims and the Sparkling Deluder responds above with increased rays; when it presides over the associated lodges, all suffer disaster. Because fire retaliates in turn, it says "the white and hard grain flowers but does not fruit".

[On "White dew falls early ... corresponds to the Sparkling Deluder and the Great White Star"] *Yangming* presides above and metal acts on its own; hence white dew falls early, cold and coolness come greatly, and gathering and killing qi moves. Since greater yang resides in the position of earth-damp, cold and damp combine; hence cold rain harms things and few ripen. Metal moves to attack wood, borrowing its path through earth; a child residing within its mother is the image of insects; hence sweet and yellow things are eaten by insects and grubs. Clear qi prevails first and hot qi retaliates later; after retaliation it prevails again; hence the red qi of fire generates and transforms later. "Red transforms late" means that plants with red flowers and red fruit all flourish again behind time. Among the five depots, heart qi flourishes late and overcomes the lungs; when the heart overcomes the lungs, the white qi of metal yields and retreats. The grain of metal is rice. 鼽 means water coming out of the nose. Metal is overcome by fire and the heavenly images respond likewise; hence the Great White Star's rays diminish and the Sparkling Deluder grows brighter.

## Notes

The weather, illnesses and astral correspondences of a year of deficient wood, the case of *yangming* presiding above, the retaliation of fire and the renewed prevalence of metal. Qibo's speech continues to unit 018.
- For 悉萎, two 殆知閣 texts have 柔萎 (see the variants), and Wang Bing's note explains 柔 as "soft". The translation reads it as 柔.
- For 失気生政, all the other sources have the form 生気失政 (see the variants), and Wang Bing's note also says 生気失政. The translation follows this.
- The Song editors' 木臨金土臨木水臨土 presumably means "metal over wood, wood over earth, earth over water" from the preceding context, but as written the order is reversed; not checked.
