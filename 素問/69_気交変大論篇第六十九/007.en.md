# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What of excess in the transformations of the five movements?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of excess in the transformations of the five movements?"] Excess means that the year qi is in surplus.
Song editors: On examination, the five transformations of excess are given in full in the *Wuchang zheng dalun*.

## Notes

A question on excess of the five movements.
