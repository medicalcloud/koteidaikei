# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "There are joy and anger, worry and mourning, luster and dryness. These are the constant aspects of the images; one must examine them carefully."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "There are joy and anger ... one must examine them carefully"] The five planets are seen deep in the night. When people see them and rejoice, it is the star's joy; when they see them and fear, it is the star's anger. When the light and color shine faintly, now bright, now dark, it is the star's worry. When the light and color are remote, neither manifest nor clear, unlike the others, it is the star's mourning. When the light and color are round and bright, neither swelling nor shrinking, serene and clear, it is the star's joy. When the light and color blaze toward people, the brilliance of its rays overflowing and its image awe-inspiring, it is the star's anger. 沢 means richly moist; 燥 means dry and withered.

## Notes

The six aspects of the images of the five planets.
