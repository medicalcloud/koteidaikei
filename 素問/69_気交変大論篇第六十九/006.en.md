# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It is based on the positions of qi. What is positioned in heaven is the pattern of heaven; what is positioned in earth is the pattern of earth; what penetrates the changes and transformations of human qi is human affairs. So the excessive precede heaven and the deficient follow heaven; this is what is meant by governing transformation to which humans respond."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is based on the positions of qi ... to which humans respond"] The three yin and three yang control heaven and earth and set forth the principles of the generation and transformation of yin and yang; this is called being positioned in heaven and in earth. The five movements reside in the middle and control the changes and transformations of human qi; hence "penetrates human qi". Preceding and following heaven refer to the times governed by the changes of the qi of generation and transformation: in excessive years the transformation arrives ahead of time; in deficient years, behind time.

## Notes

The patterns of heaven, earth and human affairs explained by the positions of qi.
