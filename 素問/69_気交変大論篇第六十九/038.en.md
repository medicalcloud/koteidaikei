# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Each likewise follows its transformation. So in their seasonal arrival there are abundance and decline; in their overriding there are contrary and compliant; in their lingering and guarding there are more and fewer [days]; in their visible form there are good and bad; in their associated lodges there are victory and defeat; and in their signs and responses there are good and bad fortune."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Each likewise follows its transformation ... good and bad fortune"] When the five planets arrive, being in their supporting or flourishing phase is the abundance of their season; in their imprisoned or dead phase, decline. Moving east to override is compliant and the disaster light; moving west to override is contrary and the disaster heavy. Many days of lingering and guarding make the disaster deep; few days, shallow. When the star is joyful and lustrous, it appears good; when the star is angry, 操 [dry], worried or mourning, it appears bad. Associated lodges means the twenty-eight lodges to which one's birth month belongs and the positions assigned among the twelve branches. When one's fate overcomes the star, there is neither disaster nor harm; when it does not overcome the star, the disaster is small or heavy; when one's fate and the star suit each other, though there is disaster, there is no harm. Disaster means lawsuits and illness. Even when the five planets override, if the star meets its imprisoned or dead phase, though there is disaster in that season, it does not materialize. When fire offends, lingers, guards and retrogrades to preside, there is worry of slander and lawsuits; when metal offends, worry of punishment, killing and pent-up qi; when wood offends, worry of tremors, alarms and drumming wind; when earth offends, worry of fullness in the center, diarrhea and swollen insteps; when water offends, worry of cold qi and rushing accumulation. Hence "in their signs and responses there are good and bad fortune".

## Notes

The various aspects of the disaster correspondences of the five planets.
- 操 in Wang Bing's 星怒操 is translated as an error for 燥 "dry", but this has not been checked. 為災小重 is hard to construe.
