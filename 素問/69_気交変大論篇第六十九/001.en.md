# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "The five movements govern in turn, corresponding above to the periods of heaven; yin and yang go and return, cold and heat meet and follow; the true and the pathogenic press on each other, inner and outer separate, the six channels surge, the five qi tilt and shift, with excess and deficiency, exclusive prevalence and combined encroachment. I wish you to speak of their beginnings and give them constant names. May I hear?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The five movements govern in turn ... May I hear?"] The period is three hundred sixty-five and a quarter days. Exclusive prevalence means that the movement governing the year is excessive; combined encroachment means that the movement governing the year is deficient. Constant names means the forms and diagnosis of illness as they spread and transform in the great void and as the human body participates and responds.
Song editors: The *Tianyuan ji dalun* says "the five movements succeed one another and each governs; when the days of the year are completed, they circle round and begin again" and "the five qi move in turn, each completing the days of a year"; the text of the *Taishi tianyuan ce* says "the myriad things rely on it to begin; the five movements complete heaven's [cycle]". These are the meaning of "the five movements govern in turn, corresponding above to the periods of heaven".

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on the excess and deficiency of the five movements and the beginnings and names of the illnesses they cause.
