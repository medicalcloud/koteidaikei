# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the year's fire is deficient, cold prevails greatly, the government of growth is not exercised, and things flourish but stay low. When congealing and bleakness are extreme, yang qi does not transform and the flourishing beauty is broken; above, it corresponds to the Chronograph Star. The people suffer from pain in the chest, propping fullness of the flanks, pain in both flanks, pain in the breast, back, between the shoulder blades and on the inner side of both arms, oppression and dimness, heart pain, sudden loss of voice, enlarged chest and abdomen, and pain drawing between the lower flanks and the lumbus and back; in severe cases one bends and cannot stretch, and the hips and thighs feel as if separated; above, it corresponds to the Sparkling Deluder and the Chronograph Star; its grain is the red. In retaliation, dust gathers and heavy rain is about to come, black qi is humiliated, and the illnesses are duck-like loose stools, abdominal fullness, inability to get food and drink down, cold in the center, rumbling intestines, watery diarrhea, abdominal pain, sudden cramps, flaccidity and impediment, and feet unable to bear the body; above, it corresponds to the Quelling Star and the Chronograph Star; the black grain does not ripen.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When congealing and bleakness are extreme ... corresponds to the Chronograph Star"] Fire is scant and water prevails; hence cold prevails greatly. When the government of growth is not exercised, things stay low. Fire qi being scant and water qi vastly abundant, the heavenly image appears and the Chronograph Star grows brighter.

[On "pain in the flanks, pain in the breast, back, between the shoulder blades and on the inner side of both arms"] Song editors: On examination, these signs are the same as the illness that arises on the contrary in severe excessive fire; see also the *Zangqi fashi lun*.

[On "enlarged chest and abdomen, and pain drawing between the lower flanks and the lumbus and back"] Song editors: The *Zangqi fashi lun* says "when the heart is depleted, the chest and abdomen are enlarged, and there is pain drawing between the lower flanks and the lumbus and back".

[On "in severe cases one bends and cannot stretch ... its grain is the red"] All *gui* years. One suffers because the vessel travels here. Fire qi does not move and cold qi binds and fixes; so the hips and thighs feel as if separated and one bends and cannot stretch. Water moves and rides over fire; so the Sparkling Deluder's rays diminish and the red grain does not ripen. When the Chronograph Star presides over the associated regions, all suffer disaster.

[On "In retaliation, dust gathers ... the black grain does not ripen"] Gathering dust, clouds and rain are the function of earth. The qi that retaliates against cold must do so through damp. When damp qi pervades within, abdominal illness and heaviness of the body arise; hence this. Black qi is water qi; 辱 means humiliation. 鶩 means duck. Since 上 [earth] retaliates against water, the Quelling Star is bright and lustrous; if it presides over and offends the associated lodges, the people suffer the disaster of illness.

## Notes

The weather, illnesses and astral correspondences of a year of deficient fire, and the retaliation of earth.
- 上 in Wang Bing's 上復於水 is presumably an error for 土. 鶩溏 means watery stools like a duck's.
