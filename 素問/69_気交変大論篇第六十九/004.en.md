# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Let me speak of it fully. The *Upper Classic* says: 'The way is to know the patterns of heaven above, the patterns of earth below, and human affairs in the middle; thereby one can endure long.' This is what is meant."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The *Upper Classic* says ... This is what is meant"] The way is so great that it encompasses everything and so fine that it enters everything; hence it penetrates the patterns of heaven, the patterns of earth and human affairs.
Song editors: On examination, the passage 夫道者 duplicates the text of the *Zhu zhijiao lun*.

## Notes

The way penetrates the patterns of heaven, earth and human affairs.
