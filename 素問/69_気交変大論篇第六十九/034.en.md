# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Each follows the transformation of its qi."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Each follows the transformation of its qi"] To the transformation of the Year Star wind responds; to that of the Sparkling Deluder, heat; to that of the Quelling Star, damp; to that of the Great White Star, dryness; to that of the Chronograph Star, cold. When qi changes, they respond; hence "each follows the transformation of its qi". The text above says that prevalence and retaliation all correspond above; now the classic says "they respond to the constant, not to the sudden", meaning that without great change there is no response. But in their prevalence and retaliation there should be differences of withered dryness or moist luster in color, without differences in size appearing in response.

## Notes

Each planet responds according to the transformation of its qi.
