# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the year's fire is excessive, scorching heat prevails and lung metal receives the pathogen. The people suffer from malaria, shortness of qi, coughing and panting, blood overflowing upward, bloody discharge below, watery diarrhea, dry throat, deafness, heat in the center, and heat in the shoulders and back; above, it corresponds to the Sparkling Deluder (Mars). In severe cases there are pain in the chest, propping fullness of the flanks, flank pain, pain in the breast, back and between the shoulder blades, pain on the inner side of both arms, and body heat and bone pain becoming spreading sores. The gathering qi does not act and the growing qi alone is bright; there are rain and ice, frost and cold; above, it corresponds to the Chronograph Star (Mercury). When lesser yin or lesser yang presides above, fire burns, springs dry up, things are scorched and withered; the illness on the contrary becomes delirium and mania, coughing and panting with wheezing breath, and below, severe bleeding and diarrhea without end. Those whose Taiyuan [pulse] is cut off die and cannot be treated; above, it corresponds to the Sparkling Deluder.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the year's fire is excessive, scorching heat prevails and lung metal receives the pathogen"] When fire does not act by virtue, the pathogen harms metal; if it acts by virtue, its government is peaceful.

[On "The people suffer from malaria ... corresponds to the Sparkling Deluder"] Shortness of qi means qi too scant for breathing. 血泄 means bloody dysentery and blood in the stool. 血溢 means blood rising and coming out of the seven orifices. 注下 means watery diarrhea. Heat in the center means within the chest and heart. The back is the residence of the chest, and the shoulders adjoin it; hence heat in the chest, heart, shoulders and back. When fire qi is very abundant, the rays of the Sparkling Deluder retrograde and preside, and the regions of the lodges concerned all suffer disaster.
Song editors: On examination, fire is abundant and conquers metal, cold and heat contend, hence malaria. The *Zangqi fashi lun* says "when the lungs are ill there are coughing and panting; when the lungs are depleted there is shortness of qi so that breath cannot be sustained, deafness and dry throat".

[On "In severe cases there are pain in the chest ... pain on the inner side of both arms"] Song editors: The *Zangqi fashi lun* says "when the heart is ill, there are pain in the chest, propping fullness of the flanks, pain below the flanks, pain in the breast, back and between the shoulder blades, and pain on the inner side of both arms".

[On "and body heat and bone pain becoming spreading sores"] When fire has no virtuous decree and gives free rein to heat to harm metal, water avenges it; hence fire falls ill itself.
Song editors: The *Yuji zhenzang lun* says "when the heart pulse is excessive, it makes the body hot and the skin painful, becoming spreading sores". Saying "bone pain" here is an error.

[On "The gathering qi does not act ... there are rain and ice, frost and cold"] Now on examination, the character 水 should be 氷.

[On "rain and ice, frost and cold; above, it corresponds to the Chronograph Star"] When metal qi retreats and fire qi alone acts, water qi breaks it; hence rain, ice and hail fall and frost and cold descend everywhere and kill things. When water retaliates against fire, the heavenly images respond: when the Chronograph Star retrogrades and overrides, cold brings disaster to things. Divining by the Chronograph Star, it is always within thirty degrees before or after the sun. When its disaster breaks out, it should reach the south. In humans, it first harms the lungs within and later in turn harms the heart.
Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* writes 雨氷霜雹 for 雨水霜寒.

[On "When lesser yin or lesser yang presides above, fire burns, springs dry up, things are scorched and withered"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of *hexi*, with upper *zhi*, the gathering qi is late", and the *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "*wuzi* and *wuwu*, great *zhi* with lesser yin presiding above; *wuyin* and *wushen*, great *zhi* with lesser yang presiding above; whether excessive or deficient, those presided over are all called heavenly tallies".

[On "Those whose Taiyuan [pulse] is cut off ... corresponds to the Sparkling Deluder"] All *wu* years. In *wuwu* and *wuzi* years lesser yin presides above; in *wuyin* and *wushen* years lesser yang presides above; these are called heavenly-tally years. Taiyuan is the lung pulse. Fire prevails and metal is cut off, hence death. Fire being already excessive, and fiery heat presiding above as well, the two fires combine; hence such signs appear. If the Sparkling Deluder retrogrades and offends the associated lodges, all are in danger.
Song editors: On examination, in the *wuchen* and *wuxu* years greater yang appears above; this is called heaven punishing the movement. So it should be abundant but cannot be, and the transformation of fire is halved; it is neither excessive nor deficient.

## Notes

The weather, illnesses and astral correspondences of a year of excessive fire.
- 水 in 雨水霜寒 is read as 氷 following the note and the *Wuchang zheng dalun*; 氷 in 氷泉涸 is read as 水 following the other sources (see the variants).
