# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When fire is deficient, if in summer there is the transformation of brilliance and radiance, then in winter there will be the government of severity, frost and cold; if in summer there is the prevalence of bleak cold and freezing, then out of season there will be the retaliation of dust, darkness and heavy rain. Its calamity is in the south, its depot is the heart, its illness resides within in the breast and flanks and without in the channels and network vessels.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "if in summer there is the transformation ... Its calamity is in the south"] Transformation is the virtue of fire; prevalence is the cruelty of water; retaliation is the change of earth. The south is fire.

[On "its depot is the heart ... without in the channels and network vessels"] The heart, governor of the south.

## Notes

For a year of deficient fire, the seasonal transformation, government, prevalence and retaliation, and the direction of calamity, the depot and the site of illness.
