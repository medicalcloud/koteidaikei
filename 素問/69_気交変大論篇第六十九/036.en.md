# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When [a planet] lingers long on its path and retrogrades to guard, appearing small, this is called examining those below. When it departs along its path, comes back quickly after departing, and passes by in a curve, this is called examining neglected faults. When it lingers long and circles, now leaving, now approaching, this is called deliberating on disaster and virtue. When the response is near, it is small; when the response is far, it is great. When it has rays and is twice its normal size, its transformation is intense; when three times its normal size, its calamity [breaks out] at once. When it is smaller than normal by one, its transformation diminishes; when smaller by two, this is called overseeing, examining the faults and virtues of those below. Those with virtue it blesses; those with faults it punishes. Thus in the appearance of the images, high and far, it looks small; low and near, it looks large. So when large, joy and anger are near; when small, fortune and misfortune are far. When the year's movement is excessive, the movement's star goes north; when the movement and qi suit each other, each moves along its path. So when the year's movement is excessive, the feared star loses its color and takes on that of its mother; when deficient, its color takes on that of what it does not overcome. Those who imitate it look about anxiously, not knowing its subtlety; in their worry, who is right? Acting rashly without proof, they show fear to lords and kings."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When [a planet] lingers long on its path ... examining those below"] On its path means moving directly. Lingering long means exceeding the number of days it should linger in response. Examining those below means scrutinizing which rulers under heaven have virtue and which have faults.

[On "When it departs along its path ... examining neglected faults"] Having moved directly and departed, it then quickly retrogrades and passes by in a winding course. This is called examining at once the faults that were left. Whether it moves fast or slow, goes far or little, presumably reflects whether the offenses are great or small, judged according to what was left.

[On "When it lingers long and circles ... deliberating on disaster and virtue"] 環 means circling round and round like a ring without leaving. Fire deliberates on offenses, metal deliberates on killing, and earth, wood and water deliberate on virtue.

[On "When the response is near, it is small; when the response is far, it is great"] Near means the offending star is constantly present; far means the offending star has long departed. Great and small refer to matters of rejoicing and of punishment.

[On "When it has rays and is twice its normal size ... its calamity [breaks out] at once"] 甚 means government and decrees prevail greatly. 発 means arising; 即 means arriving. This happens with metal and fire.

[On "When it is smaller than normal by one ... examining the faults and virtues of those below"] 省 means scrutinizing which people, officials, lords and kings of the myriad states have virtue and which have faults. So how can lords, kings, people and officials not think deeply and be truly careful?

[On "Those with virtue it blesses; those with faults it punishes"] To those with virtue heaven sends down blessings in response; to those with faults heaven sends down misfortune to overwhelm them. Thus one knows that fortune and misfortune have no gate; they are only what people summon.

[On "Thus in the appearance of the images, high and far, it looks small; low and near, it looks large"] The principle of seeing things.

[On "So when large, joy and anger are near; when small, fortune and misfortune are far"] When the image appears high and small, neither misfortune nor fortune is imminent; when it appears low and large, fortune is not far and misfortune not distant either. One should cultivate virtue, reflect on faults and await the outcome. If one cannot guard against misfortune yet strives to seek blessings, how could it be so?

[On "When the year's movement is excessive, the movement's star goes north"] Fire movement with the fire planet, wood movement with the wood planet, and so on. Going north means moving northward.

[On "when the movement and qi suit each other, each moves along its path"] There is no threat of conquest, so they keep their norm and each moves on the middle path.

[On "So when the year's movement is excessive, the feared star loses its color and takes on that of its mother"] When wood loses its color it takes on [black]; when fire loses its color it takes on green; when earth loses its color it takes on red; when metal loses its color it takes on yellow; when water loses its color it takes on white. This is called taking on its mother.

[On "when deficient, its color takes on that of what it does not overcome"] Wood takes on white, fire takes on black, earth takes on green, metal takes on red, water takes on yellow. This is called taking on what it does not overcome.

[On "Those who imitate it look about anxiously ... who is right?"] Song editors: On examination, from 肖者 to 為良 duplicates the *Linglan midian lun*, where there is a note.

[On "Acting rashly without proof, they show fear to lords and kings"] Not knowing heaven's intention, they privately guess at it and rashly speak of disasters, which in the end have no proof; this only shows signs of fear to lords and kings and misleads the common people.

## Notes

The slowness, speed, retrogradation and direct motion of the five planets, and heaven's examination of human virtue and faults through their size and color.
- For 其眚即也, two 殆知閣 texts have 其眚即発也 with 発 (see the variants), and Wang Bing explains 発 as "arising" and 即 as "arriving". The translation follows this.
- 蘭霊秘典論 is an error for 霊蘭秘典論.
- Wang Bing's 木失色而兼火失色而兼蒼 is read, following the *Leijing* which explains it as 木失色而兼玄, as 木失色而兼〔玄〕，火失色而兼蒼, with 玄 missing.
