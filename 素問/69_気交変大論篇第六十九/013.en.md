# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. What of deficiency?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of deficiency?"] It means that government and transformation are scant.
Song editors: On examination, the five transformations of deficiency are given in full in the *Wuchang zheng dalun*.

## Notes

A question on deficiency of the five movements.
