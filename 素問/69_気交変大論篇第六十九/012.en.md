# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the year's water is excessive, cold qi prevails and the pathogen harms heart fire. The people suffer from body heat, vexation of the heart, agitation and palpitations, yin reversal, cold above, below and in the center, delirium and heart pain; cold qi arrives early; above, it corresponds to the Chronograph Star. In severe cases the abdomen is large and the shins swollen, with panting and coughing, night sweats, and aversion to wind; heavy rain comes, and dust and fog darken the air; above, it corresponds to the Quelling Star. When greater yang presides above, rain, ice, snow and frost fall out of season and damp qi alters things; the illness on the contrary is abdominal fullness, rumbling intestines, loose stools, undigested food, thirst and confusion. Those whose Shenmen [pulse] is cut off die and cannot be treated; above, it corresponds to the Sparkling Deluder and the Chronograph Star."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the year's water is excessive, cold qi prevails and the pathogen harms heart fire"] It is so when water does not attend to virtue and is violent.

[On "The people suffer from body heat ... corresponds to the Chronograph Star"] 悸 means the heart leaping; 譫 means confused speech; 妄 means seeing and hearing things falsely. When the qi of heaven has abundant water, the Chronograph Star is bright and clear; if it presides over the associated lodges, disaster arrives.
Song editors: Yin reversal comes later; it is annotated at the retaliation in deficient metal.

[On "In severe cases the abdomen is large ... aversion to wind"] Song editors: The *Zangqi fashi lun* says "when the kidneys are ill, the abdomen is large, the shins swollen, with panting and coughing, heaviness of the body, night sweats and aversion to wind". Further, on examination, among the five transformations of excess, for wood it says "the transforming qi does not govern and the generating qi alone rules"; for fire, "the gathering qi does not act and the growing qi alone is bright"; for earth, "the storing qi hides and the growing [transforming] qi alone rules"; for metal, "the gathering qi is harsh and the generating qi sinks". For water it should say "the storing qi is abundant and the growing qi loses its government"; that it alone lacks this is missing text.

[On "heavy rain comes, and dust and fog darken the air; above, it corresponds to the Quelling Star"] When water is abundant without stopping, earth rides over it; hence these signs appear. Dust and fog darkening the air are earth qi. The kidney vessel ascends from the sole of the foot into the abdomen, goes up from the kidneys through the liver and diaphragm into the lungs, and follows the throat; hence this illness. The kidneys are yin, so in sleep sweat comes out and there is aversion to wind; sweating while lying asleep is this illness. Earth qi prevails and breaks the strength of water; hence the Quelling Star is bright and abundant, showing its reflection.

[On "When greater yang presides above ... damp qi alters things"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of *liuyan*, with upper *yu*, the growing qi does not transform"; and the *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "*bingchen* and *bingxu*, great *yu* with greater yang presiding above; whether excessive or deficient, those presided over are all called heavenly tallies".

[On "the illness on the contrary is abdominal fullness, rumbling intestines, loose stools, undigested food"] Song editors: The *Zangqi fashi lun* says "when the spleen is depleted, there are abdominal fullness, rumbling intestines and diarrhea with undigested food".

[On "thirst and confusion. Those whose Shenmen [pulse] is cut off ... the Chronograph Star"] All *bing* years. In *bingchen* and *bingxu* years greater yang presides above; these are called heavenly-tally years. Cold qi is very extreme, so rain turns into ice and snow; rain becoming ice is hail. Frost falling out of season shows the cold. When earth retaliates against water, there is heavy, incessant rain and damp qi is deep within; hence things all become damp and change. 神明 [Shenmen] is the heart pulse; water prevails and fire is cut off, hence death. When water is extremely abundant, the Sparkling Deluder dims and the Chronograph Star is bright and clear; if it retrogrades to guard the associated lodges, there is danger of death.
Song editors: On examination, among the five [transformations] of excess, only the presiding of fire and water is recorded, because fire presiding over fire and water over water are heavenly tallies. Fire presiding over water is contravention, water over wood compliance, fire over earth compliance, water over earth the movement overcoming heaven, fire over metal heaven punishing the movement, water over metal contravention; these are not set out further. Also, only here does it say "corresponds to the Sparkling Deluder and the Chronograph Star"; one example is given, and the rest can be known accordingly.

## Notes

The weather, illnesses and astral correspondences of a year of excessive water. The discussion of the five excessive movements, and Qibo's speech, end here.
- 神明 in Wang Bing's note presumably corresponds to 神門 in the main text. The Song editors' 太過五独記 is read as 太過五化独記.
