# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Virtue and transformation are the auspicious signs of qi; government and decree are the rules of qi; change and alteration are the principles of retaliation; disaster and calamity are the beginnings of harm. When [a person's] qi can withstand them, there is harmony; when it cannot, there is illness; when one is affected by a pathogen a second time, it is severe."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Virtue and transformation are the auspicious signs of qi ... it is severe"] 祥 means a good response; 章 means a rule or pattern. Principle of retaliation means the guiding principle of repayment. Being affected a second time means that the year's qi is already deficient and in addition heaven's qi shows a conquering, killing qi; this is being affected again. 重 means doubled and accumulated.

## Notes

The meanings of virtue and transformation, government and decree, change and alteration, disaster and calamity, and how illness arises.
- 相勝 in 気相勝者和，不相勝者病 is read as a person's qi withstanding the seasonal qi.
