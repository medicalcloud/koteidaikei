# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the year's earth is deficient, wind prevails greatly, the transforming qi does not issue its decrees, and plants grow luxuriantly. When gusting is extreme, they flower but do not fruit; above, it corresponds to the Year Star. The people suffer from diarrhea of undigested food, cholera, heaviness of the body, abdominal pain, swaying and recovering of the sinews and bones, twitching and aching of the flesh, and frequent anger; the storing qi takes action, hibernating creatures cling early, all suffer cold in the center; above, it corresponds to the Year Star and the Quelling Star; its grain is the yellow. In retaliation, the government of gathering is severe and harsh, famous trees wither while green, there is sudden pain of the chest and flanks drawing down to the lower abdomen, frequent sighing; insects eat the sweet and yellow, qi lodges in the spleen, the yellow grain decreases, people eat little and lose their sense of taste, and the green grain is damaged; above, it corresponds to the Great White Star and the Year Star. When *jueyin* presides above, flowing water does not freeze, hibernating creatures come out, the storing qi is not exercised, white [metal] does not retaliate; above, it corresponds to the Year Star, and the people are healthy.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the year's earth is deficient ... above, it corresponds to the Year Star"] 水 [Wood] lacks virtue. Wood qi acts alone, so the transforming qi does not issue its decrees and the generating qi has its own way; hence plants grow luxuriantly and gust about to excess. This is wood not acting by virtue. Earth qi is thin and scant, so the fruit of things does not ripen; "not fruit" means blighted. Earth is deficient and wood rides over it; hence the Year Star appears lustrous and bright.

[On "The people suffer from diarrhea ... its grain is the yellow"] All 已 [*ji*] years. Wind lodges in the stomach; hence this illness. Earth qi is deficient and water transforms together with it; hence the storing qi takes action, hibernating creatures cling early to warm places, and people all suffer from cold in the center. 繇 means swaying. When the sinews and bones sway and then return to normal, it stops; this is "swaying and recovering". Earth is suppressed and cannot extend. If the Year Star presides over the associated lodges, all suffer disaster.
Song editors: On examination, although Wang annotates 筋骨繇復 in this text, its meaning cannot be explained. The *Zhizhen yao dalun* has 筋骨繇併, so this 復 is suspected to be an error for 併.

[On "In retaliation, the government of gathering is severe ... the green grain is damaged"] Metal qi retaliates against wood; hence famous trees wither while green. Metal entering earth is the mother carrying her child; hence insects eat inside sweet and yellow things. Metal enters earth; hence qi lodges in the spleen. Metal qi comes greatly and retaliates against earth like an 𬽦 [enemy]; hence the yellow grain loses its fruit and does not ripen.

[On "above, it corresponds to the Great White Star and the Year Star"] The Great White Star's rays are abundant and the Year [Star] loses brightness. One classic lacks these six characters; the text is missing.

[On "When *jueyin* presides above ... the people are healthy"] In *jihai* and *jisi* years *jueyin* presides above, and in those years lesser yang is in the spring [in earth], with fire controlling earth; hence hibernating creatures come out and flowing water does not freeze. Metal cannot retaliate, so the image of the Year Star is normal and the people are healthy and free of illness.
Song editors: On examination, for deficient wood with *yangming* above and deficient water with greater yin above, retaliation is mentioned afterward in both. Here retaliation is mentioned first and the signs of presiding above afterward, presumably because it says "white does not retaliate", to avoid suggesting there is retaliation in this year.

## Notes

The weather, illnesses and astral correspondences of a year of deficient earth, the retaliation of metal, and the case of *jueyin* presiding above.
- 水 in Wang Bing's 水旡徳 is presumably an error for 木, and 已 in 諸已歳 for 己.
