# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the year's metal is deficient, blazing fire moves, the generating qi is exercised, the growing qi prevails exclusively, all things flourish, and scorching dryness proceeds; above, it corresponds to the Sparkling Deluder. The people suffer from oppression and heaviness of the shoulders and back, nasal congestion and sneezing, blood in the stool and watery diarrhea; the gathering qi comes late; above, it corresponds to the Great White Star; its grain is the hard and awned. In retaliation, cold rain comes suddenly, ice, hail, frost and snow fall and kill things, yin reverses and blocks, yang on the contrary goes upward, the head hurts at Naohu, extending to the top of the head, with fever; above, it corresponds to the Chronograph Star; the red grain does not ripen; the people suffer from mouth sores, and in severe cases heart pain.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the year's metal is deficient ... corresponds to the Sparkling Deluder"] Fire does not attend to virtue and attacks metal in its danger. When blazing fire spreads, great heat arises in summer. The generating qi is exercised, so all things flourish. When scorching dry qi arrives, things cannot withstand it; when scorching prevails, stones melt and metals flow, springs dry up and grasses scorch, mountains and marshes burn, and rain does not fall. When blazing fire is very abundant, the heavenly images respond and the Sparkling Deluder appears very bright.

[On "The people suffer from oppression ... its grain is the hard and awned"] All *yi* years. 瞀 means oppression. Receiving the heat pathogen, this illness arises. 収 is metal qi. Fire prevails first, so the gathering qi comes late. Fire qi overcomes metal and metal cannot be abundant. If the Sparkling 感 [Deluder] retrogrades to guard the associated regions, all receive illness.
Song editors: On examination, its grain is hard and awned, and its white color can be seen; hence it does not say "its grain is the white". The classic's "corresponds to the Great White Star", compared with the examples before and after, has lost the two characters 熒惑. And seeing that Wang's note speaks of the Sparkling Deluder retrograding to guard, the defect in the classic is all the more evident.

[On "the head hurts at Naohu ... corresponds to the Chronograph Star"] Song editors: On examination, in a deficient movement, what conquers it acts, and the child of what is overcome comes to retaliate. After retaliation, the prevailing star dims and the retaliating star is bright and large. Here only "corresponds to the Chronograph Star" is said, without the Sparkling Deluder; the text is defective. It should say "corresponds to the Chronograph Star and the Sparkling Deluder".

[On "the red grain does not ripen ... heart pain"] When cold qi breaks fire, ice, hail, frost and snow appear; ice and hail harm first and frost and snow damage later; both are the norm of cold qi. Its disaster harms the red transformation. Whenever a deficient [movement] is attacked by what prevails and the child qi retaliates, it all returns to its own direction. Yin reversal means cold counterflow. 格 means reaching, and also resisting. Water moves to break fire and rescue the distressed metal; the heavenly images respond, the Chronograph Star is bright and clear, and the red grain is damaged by frost and hail.

## Notes

The weather, illnesses and astral correspondences of a year of deficient metal, and the retaliation of water.

- 熒感 in Wang Bing's note is an error for 熒惑.
