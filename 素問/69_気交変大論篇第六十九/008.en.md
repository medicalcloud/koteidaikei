# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the year's wood is excessive, wind qi prevails and spleen earth receives the pathogen. The people suffer from diarrhea of undigested food, reduced eating, heaviness of the body, vexation and oppression, rumbling intestines, and propping fullness of the abdomen; above, it corresponds to the Year Star (Jupiter). In severe cases there is vague, frequent anger, dizziness and illness of the vertex. The transforming qi does not govern and the generating qi alone rules; clouds and things fly about, plants are not at rest, and in severe cases they sway and fall; on the contrary, there are flank pain and severe vomiting. Those whose Chongyang [pulse] is cut off die and cannot be treated; above, it corresponds to the Great White Star (Venus).

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the year's wood is excessive, wind qi prevails and spleen earth receives the pathogen"] Because wood is in surplus, the qi of earth is humbled.

[On "The people suffer from diarrhea ... corresponds to the Year Star"] 飱泄 means food passing out undigested. Because the spleen is depleted, there are reduced eating, heaviness of the body, vexation and oppression, rumbling intestines and propping fullness of the abdomen. When the year's wood qi is very abundant, the Year Star shines brightly, and the regions of the stars it retrogrades to and guards all suffer disaster.
Song editors: The *Zangqi fashi lun* says "when the spleen is depleted, there are abdominal fullness, rumbling intestines and diarrhea with undigested food".

[On "In severe cases there is vague, frequent anger, dizziness and illness of the vertex"] When its overriding is too extreme, it meets metal; hence it falls ill itself.
Song editors: The *Yuji zhenzang lun* says "when the liver pulse is excessive, it makes people prone to anger, vague, dizzy, with illness of the vertex", because the liver is replete. So this illness is not only wood in excess meeting metal and falling ill itself; liver repletion also makes it ill.

[On "The transforming qi does not govern ... corresponds to the Great White Star"] All *ren* years. Wood is in surplus and earth suppressed, so [the transforming qi] cannot spread its government over the myriad things. The generating qi is wood qi; being excessive, it alone rules and generates and transforms. When wind does not attend to virtue and moves out of turn, clouds and things fly about in the great void and plants are not at rest. If it moves without stopping, metal overcomes it; hence in severe cases plants sway and fall. Flank pain on the contrary is wood riding over earth. Chongyang is the stomach pulse; when wood qi prevails and earth qi is cut off, there is death. When metal retaliates and the Great White Star retrogrades to guard the associated stars, there is danger. When the disaster breaks out, it harms the east; in the human body, it first harms the spleen and later injures the liver. The *Documents* says "fullness invites loss"; this is of that kind.
Song editors: On examination, in these five transformations of excess there are three patterns of mentioning stars. For the wood and earth movements, the Year Star or Quelling Star is mentioned first and then the star that overcomes 已 [oneself]. For the 大 [fire] and metal movements, the Sparkling Deluder or Great White Star is mentioned first, then the star that overcomes 已 [oneself], and then again the Sparkling Deluder or Great White Star. For the water movement, the Chronograph Star is mentioned first, then the Quelling Star, then again the Chronograph Star, together with the star that 已 [oneself] overcomes.

## Notes

The weather, illnesses and astral correspondences of a year of excessive wood. Qibo's speech continues below.
- 大 in the Song editors' 大与金運 is presumably an error for 火, and 已 in 勝已 and 已勝 for 己.
