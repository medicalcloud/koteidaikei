# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The government of the five movements is like a balance: what is high is pressed down, what is low is raised, what transforms is responded to, what changes is retaliated against. This is the principle of generation, growth, transformation, completion, gathering and storing, the constancy of qi. If constancy is lost, heaven and earth are blocked on all four sides.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The government of the five movements is like a balance ... blocked on all four sides"] If the principle of constancy is lost, the qi of heaven, earth and the four seasons are closed and blocked with nowhere to move. So movement must have stillness and prevalence must have retaliation; this is the way of the yin and yang of heaven and earth.

## Notes

The government of the five movements keeps balance like a scale.
