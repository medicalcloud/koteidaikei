# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Whether the images appear high or low, their response is one; hence humans too respond to them."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Whether the images appear high or low ... humans too respond to them"] Observing the images and seeing the colors, the responses of inner and outer, of humans and heaven, are all one.

## Notes

The response is the same regardless of the height of the image.
- Only this unit writes 岐伯 in the base text.
