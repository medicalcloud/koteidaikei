# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When water is deficient, if at the four corners there is the transformation of torrents, moisture, dust and clouds, then out of season there will be the response of mild wind and sprouting; if at the four corners there break out the changes of dust, darkness and sudden downpours, then out of season there will be the retaliation of gusting, sweeping, shaking and snapping. Its calamity is in the north, its depot is the kidneys, its illness resides within in the lumbus, spine and bone marrow, and without in the gorges and valleys of the flesh, the calves and knees.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "if at the four corners there break out the changes ... Its calamity is in the north"] Gusting, sweeping, shaking and snapping are caused by great wind.
Song editors: On examination, for deficient metal and water, the transforming decrees of fire and earth and their responses are mentioned first; hence they are mentioned without referring to autumn and winter. What follows is the change of prevalence and retaliation of fire and earth. That this differs from the pattern for wood, fire and earth is complementary phrasing.

[On "its depot is the kidneys ... the calves and knees"] Large meetings of the flesh are valleys; small meetings of the flesh are gorges. In the spaces of the divisions of the flesh, at the meetings of gorges and valleys, the nutritive and defensive circulate and the great qi meets.

## Notes

For a year of deficient water, the transformation, response, change and retaliation at the four corners, and the direction of calamity, the depot and the site of illness.
