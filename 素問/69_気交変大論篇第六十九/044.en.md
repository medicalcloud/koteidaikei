# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Virtue, transformation, government, decree, disaster and change cannot add to one another; prevalence and retaliation, abundance and decline, cannot exceed one another in amount; going and coming, small and great, cannot surpass one another; the rising and descending of their working cannot eliminate one another. Each simply retaliates according to its movement."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Virtue, transformation, government, decree, disaster and change cannot add to one another"] In the movement and stillness of heaven and earth and the going and coming of yin and yang, virtue repays virtue and transformation repays transformation; government, decree, disaster, calamity, movement and retaliation are likewise. Hence "they cannot add to one another".

[On "prevalence and retaliation, abundance and decline, cannot exceed one another in amount"] When prevalence is abundant, retaliation is abundant; when prevalence is slight, retaliation is slight. Abundance does not repay slightness, nor transformation repay change. Hence "they cannot exceed one another".

[On "going and coming, small and great, cannot surpass one another"] The numbers of days of prevalence and retaliation are the same; hence "they cannot surpass one another".

[On "the rising and descending of their working cannot eliminate one another"] When wood overcomes metal, fire must repay it; earth, metal and water are all so. There has never been prevalence without repayment; hence qi cannot make one another disappear.

[On "Each simply retaliates according to its movement"] Movement always has retaliation; one speaks of retaliation by examining movement. The *Changes* says "good fortune, misfortune, regret and humiliation arise from movement"; is this what is meant? Heaven, though high, cannot be measured, and earth, though broad, cannot be gauged; but speaking of them through the movement and retaliation of qi is like looking at one's palm.

## Notes

Virtue, transformation, government and decree balance disaster and change, and prevalence balances retaliation.
