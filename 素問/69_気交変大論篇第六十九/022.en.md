# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When earth is deficient, if at the four corners there is the transformation of dust, clouds, moisture and lushness, then in spring there will be the government of branches singing and drumming open; if at the four corners there break out the changes of shaking, snapping, gusting and soaring, then in autumn there will be the retaliation of austere killing and incessant rain. Its calamity is at the four corners, its depot is the spleen, its illness resides within in the heart and abdomen and without in the flesh and four limbs.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "if at the four corners there is the transformation ... Its calamity is at the four corners"] The southeast, northeast, southwest and northwest. 維 means corner; it refers to the months when the sun is at the four corners.
Song editors: On examination, for deficient earth too, government and transformation are mentioned first, then prevalence and retaliation.

[On "its depot is the spleen ... without in the flesh and four limbs"] The four corners and the center are governed by the spleen.

## Notes

For a year of deficient earth, the transformation, government, change and retaliation at the four corners, and the direction of calamity, the depot and the site of illness.
