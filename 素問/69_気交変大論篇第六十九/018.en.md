# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the year's water is deficient, damp prevails greatly, the growing qi on the contrary is exercised, its transformation is quick, and summer heat and rain come often; above, it corresponds to the Quelling Star. The people suffer from abdominal fullness, heaviness of the body, soggy diarrhea, cold sores oozing fluid, attacks of pain in the lumbus and thighs, impaired movement of the popliteal fossae, calves, thighs and knees, vexation and oppression, flaccid feet with cold reversal, and pain of the soles; in severe cases the instep swells. The storing qi does not govern and kidney qi is not balanced; above, it corresponds to the Chronograph Star; its grain is black millet. When greater yin presides above, great cold arises often, hibernating creatures hide early, solid ice accumulates on the ground, and the sun's brightness does not govern; the people suffer from cold illness below, and in severe cases abdominal fullness and edema; above, it corresponds to the Quelling Star; what it governs is the yellow grain. In retaliation, great wind breaks out suddenly, grasses are flattened and trees shed, growth is not fresh, the complexion changes at times, sinews and bones are contracted, the flesh twitches and spasms, the eyes see dimly, things split and crack, rashes break out in the flesh, qi gathers in the diaphragm with pain in the heart and abdomen, yellow qi is damaged, its grain does not ripen; above, it corresponds to the Year Star."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the year's water is deficient ... corresponds to the Quelling Star"] "Damp prevails greatly" means frequent rain; "transformation is quick" means things ripen early. Fire and damp transform together; hence summer heat and rain come often. Taking advantage of water's deficiency, earth overcomes it; so the image of the Quelling Star increases in brightness, and if it retrogrades, overrides, lingers and offends, it is worse still.

[On "The storing qi does not govern and kidney qi is not balanced ... its grain is black millet"] The storing qi cannot extend its decrees; hence kidney qi cannot achieve harmony and balance within. 衡 means balance. The response of the Chronograph Star should be diminished brightness; or if the Quelling Star is met presiding over the associated lodges, there is disaster.
Song editors: On examination, the classic says "corresponds to the Chronograph Star" and the note speaks of the Quelling Star. Compared with the examples before and after, this classic lacks the two characters 鎮星.

[On "When greater yin presides above ... corresponds to the Quelling Star"] Song editors: On examination, for deficient wood with *yangming* above, it says "corresponds to the Great White and Quelling Stars". That only the Quelling Star is mentioned here and not the Sparkling Deluder is missing text. Presumably when water is deficient and greater yin also presides above, the Quelling Star is bright and abundant in response to the exclusive abundance of earth qi; water being weaker still, the Sparkling Deluder has nothing to fear and is bright and large.

[On "corresponds to the Quelling Star; what it governs is the yellow grain"] All *xin* years. In *xinchou* and *xinwei* years greater yin presides above and greater yang is in the spring; hence great cold arises often. Earth qi is exclusively abundant, so the Quelling Star grows brighter and the yellow grain, corresponding to heaven, ripens in the year.

[On "In retaliation, great wind breaks out suddenly ... corresponds to the Year Star"] Wood retaliates against earth, so the yellow qi is on the contrary damaged and the yellow grain does not ripen—meaning its fruit does not form and cannot be offered in the ritual vessels. When wood qi retaliates violently and the Year Star descends to preside over the associated regions, there is disaster.
Song editors: On examination, it should say here "corresponds to the Year Star and the Quelling Star".

## Notes

The weather, illnesses and astral correspondences of a year of deficient water, the case of greater yin presiding above, and the retaliation of wood. The discussion of the five deficient movements, and Qibo's speech, end here.
- 𥆨𥆨 is read as "dimly".
