# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence the saying: 'In the movement and stillness of heaven and earth, spirit brightness is their guiding principle; in the coming and going of yin and yang, cold and heat make manifest their signs.' This is what is meant."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Hence the saying ... This is what is meant"] Song editors: From 故曰 on agrees with the *Wuyunxing dalun*; the first two phrases also duplicate the *Yinyang yingxiang dalun*, which says "in the rising and falling of yin and yang, cold and heat make manifest their signs".

## Notes

An old saying is cited to conclude. Qibo's speech ends here. There is no note by Wang Bing.
