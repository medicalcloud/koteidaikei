# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I have heard that to find the right person and not teach him is called losing the way, and to transmit it to the wrong person is called carelessly leaking heaven's treasure. I am truly of slight virtue and not worthy to receive the supreme way. Yet the multitudes grieve that it may not last; I wish you, Master, to preserve it without end and let it flow without limit. If I take charge of the matter, how should I take it as a rule and practice it?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If I take charge of the matter, how should I take it as a rule and practice it?"] The supreme way is not hard to transmit nor hard to know, but hard to practice. The sage pitied the common people and wished them all to dwell in long life, so he humbled himself and lowered his ambition to request instruction from the Heavenly Teacher. The Most High values virtue, so he puts 已 [himself] last and others first. If one is not the right person, the way is not given in vain. Huangdi wished to extend benevolence far, spread universal love, honor the way and lower himself to save the common people; hence he says "If I take charge of the matter, take it as a rule and practice it."

## Notes

The Emperor wishes to receive the supreme way and practice it for the people.
