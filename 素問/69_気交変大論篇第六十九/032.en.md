# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "[The planets] move receiving heaven; so they do not move rashly and there is nothing they do not respond to. What moves suddenly is a crossing change of qi, to which they do not respond. Hence the saying 'They respond to the constant, not to the sudden' means this."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Hence the saying 'They respond to the constant, not to the sudden' means this"] Virtue, transformation, government and decree are the constancy of qi; disaster, calamity, change and alteration are sudden meetings of qi with prevalence and defeat. Constant means that the qi of the year and the four seasons do not deviate even by a *ke*. What is not constant does not last.

## Notes

The five planets respond to constant qi, not to sudden changes.
