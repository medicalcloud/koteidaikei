# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When metal is deficient, if in summer there is the decree of radiance and sultry steaming, then in winter there will be the response of severe freezing and orderly austerity; if in summer there are the changes of blazing, scorching and burning, then in autumn there will be the retaliation of ice, hail, frost and snow. Its calamity is in the west, its depot is the lungs, its illness resides within in the breast, flanks, shoulders and back, and without in the skin and hair.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "its depot is the lungs ... without in the skin and hair"] The lungs, governor of the west.

## Notes

For a year of deficient metal, the seasonal decree, response, change and retaliation, and the direction of calamity, the depot and the site of illness.
