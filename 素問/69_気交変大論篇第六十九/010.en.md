# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the year's earth is excessive, rain and damp prevail and kidney water receives the pathogen. The people suffer from abdominal pain, cold reversal, joylessness, heaviness of the body, and vexation and oppression; above, it corresponds to the Quelling Star (Saturn). In severe cases the flesh wastes, the feet are limp and cannot be drawn up, walking brings frequent spasms, the soles hurt, fluid retention causes fullness in the center, eating is reduced, and the four limbs cannot be raised. When change arises and [earth] obtains its position, the storing qi hides and the transforming qi alone rules; springs gush and rivers overflow, fish appear in dried marshes, wind and rain come greatly, the earth collapses, and scaled creatures appear on land. The illness is abdominal fullness, loose stools and rumbling intestines, and on the contrary severe downward discharge. Those whose Taixi [pulse] is cut off die and cannot be treated; above, it corresponds to the Year Star.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the year's earth is excessive, rain and damp prevail and kidney water receives the pathogen"] It is so when earth lacks virtue.

[On "The people suffer from abdominal pain ... corresponds to the Quelling Star"] Abdominal pain means pain of the greater and lesser abdomen. Cold reversal means counterflow cold of the feet. Joylessness is as if there were a hidden worry. When earth comes to punish water, the images respond, and if the Quelling Star retrogrades and offends the associated lodges, there is disaster.
Song editors: The *Zangqi fashi lun* says "when the kidneys are ill, the body is heavy; when the kidneys are depleted, the greater and lesser abdomen hurt, with cold reversal and joylessness".

[On "In severe cases the flesh wastes ... the four limbs cannot be raised"] The spleen governs the flesh and corresponds outwardly to the four limbs; also its vessel starts at the tip of the middle toe, follows the inner side of the kernel bone and emerges obliquely to network the instep. Hence this illness.
Song editors: The *Zangqi fashi lun* says "when the spleen is ill, the body is heavy, one is often hungry, the flesh wastes, the feet cannot be drawn up, walking brings frequent spasms, and the soles hurt"; and the *Yuji zhenzang lun* says "when the spleen is excessive, it makes the four limbs unable to be raised".

[On "When change arises and [earth] obtains its position"] Song editors: On examination, only here among the five transformations of excess does it say "change arises and obtains its position", because by citing one the four other qi can be known; also because the months when earth flourishes are hard to know, it is stated in detail here.

[On "the storing qi hides ... corresponds to the Year Star"] All *jia* years. Obtaining its position means the last months of the seasons. 蔵 is water qi; 化 is earth qi. Transformation is excessive, so the water depot hides and the transforming qi alone rules. Earth prevails and wood retaliates; hence wind and rain come greatly, springs gush, rivers and canals overflow, and fish appear in dry marshes. The damp being already extreme and wind drumming it, the earth collapses. "The earth collapses" means walls crumble, banks 什 [topple], mountains fall and the ground sinks. Rivers overflow, springs gush, withered marshes are filled with water and scaled creatures abound; hence they appear on land. Taixi is the kidney pulse; earth prevails and water is cut off, hence death. When wood comes to break earth and the heavenly images retrograde and preside over the associated lodges, there is indeed cause for worry.
Song editors: The *Zangqi fashi lun* says "when the spleen is depleted, there are abdominal fullness, rumbling intestines and diarrhea with undigested food".

## Notes

The weather, illnesses and astral correspondences of a year of excessive earth.
- 什 in Wang Bing's 岸什 is translated as an error for 仆 "topple", but this has not been checked.
