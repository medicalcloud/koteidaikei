# 素問 気交変大論篇第六十九 §47 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. This is what is called a discourse of essential light, the work of a great sage, proclaiming and clarifying the great way, reaching the inexhaustible and searching the limitless. I have heard: one who speaks well of heaven must find its response in humans; one who speaks well of antiquity must verify it in the present; one who speaks well of qi must make it manifest in things; one who speaks well of responses shares the transformation of heaven and earth; one who speaks well of transformation and change penetrates the principle of spirit brightness. Who but you, Master, could speak of the supreme way?" Then he chose an auspicious day, stored it in the Numinous Chamber, read it every morning, and named it *Qijiaobian* (Intersecting Changes of Qi); without fasting and purification he did not dare to open it, and he transmitted it with care.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "one who speaks well of heaven ... Who but you, Master, could speak of the supreme way?"] The yearly transformations of excess and deficiency are inexhaustible, and the shifting changes of the intersection of qi flow without limit. Yet heaven hangs down images and the sage takes them as a rule to know good and bad fortune. How? In an excessive year the star is large, or bright and clear; in a deficient year the star is small, or loses its color; hence good and bad fortune can be pointed to and seen. What does good and bad fortune mean? Things receive the qi of the five constants to be generated and completed, and none fails to participate above and respond to it; there is obstruction and there is fitness; hence "good and bad fortune arrive here". Hence "one who speaks well of heaven must find its response in humans". When one speaks of the way of antiquity, the present must respond to it; hence "one who speaks well of antiquity must verify it in the present". The qi of transformation generates and completes, and all things receive it; so one who speaks of the response of qi clarifies it through things; hence "one who speaks well of responses must make it manifest in things"; 彰 means clear. The response of the transformation of qi is like the four seasons proceeding and all things being complete; hence one who speaks well of responses must share the creative transformation of heaven and earth. The birth of things is called transformation, the extreme of things change. It means that the beginning and end of the transformation and change of all things must accord with the working of spirit brightness; hence one who speaks of transformation and change penetrates the principle of spirit brightness. The wisdom of the sage encompasses all things and penetrates everything; hence when he speaks, there is always effect and nothing fails to respond.

[On "Then he chose an auspicious day ... transmitted it with care"] The Numinous Chamber means the Linglan chamber, Huangdi's library.
Song editors: On examination, this text is the same as the end of the *Liuyuan zhengji dalun*.

## Notes

The close of the chapter: the Emperor stores this discourse in the Numinous Chamber and transmits it with care.
- 斎 in the main text is written 斉 in the base text and was corrected from the other sources (see the emendation).
- Wang Bing's note partly interchanges the phrases on "speaking well of qi" and "speaking well of responses" in its explanation. The translation follows the characters.
