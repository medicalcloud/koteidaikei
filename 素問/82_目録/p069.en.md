# 素問 目録 §69 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

*Yinyang lei lun* (79); *Fang shengshuai lun* (80)

## Notes

Titles and numbers of the chapters in a fascicle. There are no notes.
- The base text has 方盛衰論八十一, but it is chapter 80, so it is read as 八十.
- The characters of the titles are given as in the chapter headings of the main text.
