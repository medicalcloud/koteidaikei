# 素問 脈解篇第四十九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the greater yang, what is called swelling with pain in the lumbus and buttocks: the first month is the greater yang [establishing] *yin*; *yin* is the greater yang. In the first month the yang qi emerges and is above, but the yin qi is abundant and the yang has not yet attained its own order; therefore there are swelling and pain in the lumbus and buttocks. Illness with one-sided depletion and lameness: in the first month the yang qi thaws the ice and the earth qi comes out. What is called one-sided depletion is a certain insufficiency [left] from the cold of winter; therefore there are one-sided depletion and lameness. What is called stiffness above drawing to the back: the yang qi rises greatly and contends; therefore there is stiffness above. What is called tinnitus: through the yang qi all things rise abundantly and leap; therefore the ears ring. What is called mania and vertex illness when severe: the yang is all above and the yin qi follows below; below is depleted and above replete; therefore there are mania and vertex illness. What is called deafness when floating: all lies in the qi. What is called loss of voice when it enters the interior: the abundance of yang has already declined; therefore there is loss of voice. When the interior is robbed and there is reversal, there are loss of voice and paralysis; this is kidney depletion. When the lesser yin does not arrive, it is reversal.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the greater yang, what is called swelling with pain in the lumbus and buttocks ... *yin* is the greater yang"] 脽 means the flesh of the buttocks. In the first month the three yang arise and it governs the establishment of *yin*; the three yang is called the greater yang; hence "*yin* is the greater yang".

[On "In the first month the yang qi emerges ... the yang has not yet attained its own order"] Although the three yang arise in the first month, the heavenly qi is still cold; because it is still cold, it says "the yin qi is abundant and the yang has not yet attained its own order". 次 means the order of rising to dominance.

[On "therefore there are swelling and pain in the lumbus and buttocks"] Because its vessel reaches the lumbus, enters and passes through the buttocks and passes the hip joint.

[On "Illness with one-sided depletion and lameness ... one-sided depletion and lameness"] Because its vessel follows the posterior edge of the inner thigh, joins in the popliteal fossa, descends along the calf, passes behind the outer ankle, follows Jinggu and reaches the outer side of the little toe.
Song editors: On examination, Mr Wang's "its vessel follows the inner thigh" is quite wrong. According to the *Jiayi jing*, the course of the greater yang does not reach the inner thigh. 股内 is an error for 髀外; it should say "the posterior edge of the outer thigh".

[On "What is called stiffness above drawing to the back ... stiffness above"] 強上 means the neck and nape are clenched and stiff; when severe it draws to the back. This is because its vessel emerges from the brain and branches down the nape and back.

[On "What is called tinnitus ... the ears ring"] Because a branch of its vessel goes from the vertex to the upper corner of the ear.

[On "What is called mania and vertex illness when severe ... mania and vertex illness"] Its vessel rises over the forehead, crosses at the vertex, enters to connect with the brain and comes out again; a branch goes from the vertex to the upper corner of the ear; therefore there are mania and vertex illness. Above the nape is called 巔 "vertex".

[On "What is called deafness when floating: all lies in the qi"] Also because its vessel reaches the ear.

[On "What is called loss of voice when it enters the interior ... loss of voice"] When the yang qi is abundant and enters the interior to press on the uterus and kidney, the qi of the uterine and kidney network vessels does not pass; therefore there is loss of voice. The uterine vessel is tied to the kidney, and the kidney vessel runs alongside the root of the tongue; therefore one loses the voice and cannot speak.

[On "When the interior is robbed and there is reversal ... kidney depletion"] 俳 means paralysis. The kidney vessel emerges with the thoroughfare vessel at Qijie, follows the inner edge of the inner thigh, enters obliquely into the popliteal fossa, follows the inner edge of the shin bone, reaches behind the inner ankle and enters the sole. Therefore when the kidney qi is robbed within and does not comply, the tongue loses voice and the feet are paralysed; hence "this is kidney depletion".
Song editors: On examination, Wang's note says "the kidney vessel emerges with the thoroughfare vessel", but according to the *Jiayi jing* this is the kidney network vessel, not the kidney vessel. Moreover, Wang's notes to the *Wei lun*, *Qibing lun* and *Daqi lun* all say "the kidney network vessel"; so 脈 here should be 絡.

[On "When the lesser yin does not arrive, it is reversal"] The lesser yin is the kidney vessel. If the kidney qi escapes within, the lesser yin pulse does not arrive; when it does not arrive, the qi of the greater yin counterflows upward.

## Notes

This chapter explains the symptoms of the illnesses of the six channels by the months (earthly branches) and the rise and fall of yin and yang, in the form "what is called ...". It contains no dialogue. This unit concerns the greater yang.
- Wang Bing (note in unit 006) says the chapter does not connect with the classic's text before and after and is close to the channel courses of the *Lingshu* (*Jingmai* chapter); the Song editors say it largely explains the "stirring" and "produced" illnesses of the *Jiayi jing*.
- 俳: Wang Bing takes it as "paralysis".
