# 素問 脈解篇第四十九 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *jueyin*, what is called *tui* hernia and swelling of the lower abdomen in women: the *jueyin* is *chen*; the third month is yin within yang, and evil is within; hence it is called *tui* hernia and swelling of the lower abdomen. What is called lumbar and spinal pain preventing bending forward and back: in the third month there is a burst of flourishing and blossoming, and all things bend forward once and do not look up. What is called *tui* retention hernia and skin distension: it means yin too is abundant, and the vessels are distended and blocked; hence it is called *tui* retention hernia. What is called dry throat and heat in the centre when severe: yin and yang press on each other and there is heat; therefore the throat is dry.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the *jueyin*, what is called *tui* hernia ... swelling of the lower abdomen"] Because its vessel follows the inner thigh into the pubic hair, encircles the genitals and reaches the lower abdomen.

[On "What is called dry throat and heat in the centre when severe ... the throat is dry"] This whole chapter does not connect at all with the classic's text before and after. It separately explains the origins of illnesses of the channel vessels; it is roughly the same as the channel courses in the *Lingshu jing*, but what it points to is quite different.
Song editors: On examination, what this chapter explains is largely the "stirring" and "produced" illnesses of the *Jiayi jing*; though there are slight differences, in general they are not different.

## Notes

The symptoms of *jueyin* illness explained by the state of yin and yang in the third month (*chen*); this ends the chapter.
- *Tui* hernia (㿗疝) is understood as hernia with swelling of the scrotum and lower abdomen.
- 曰 in 曰陰亦盛 may be an erroneous character from the context; the translation renders it "it means".
- Daizhige (重广补注黄帝内经素问) has 巻十四 at the end of the chapter; this marks the juan division in that source.
