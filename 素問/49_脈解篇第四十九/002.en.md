# 素問 脈解篇第四十九 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the lesser yang, what is called pain in the heart and flanks means the lesser yang is abundant; its abundance is what the heart manifests. In the ninth month the yang qi is exhausted and yin qi abundant; therefore the heart and flanks ache. What is called inability to turn over: yin qi stores things; when things are stored they do not move; therefore one cannot turn over. What is called leaping when severe: in the ninth month all things decline and plants and trees shed everything; then the qi leaves yang and goes to yin; [yin] qi is abundant and yang goes down and grows; hence it is called leaping.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the lesser yang, what is called pain in the heart and flanks ... what the heart manifests"] When heart qi counterflows, the lesser yang is abundant. Heart qi suits wood and melts lung-metal outwardly; hence "its abundance is what the heart manifests".

[On "In the ninth month ... the heart and flanks ache"] The foot lesser yang vessel follows the inside of the flanks and emerges at Qijie; the heart-master vessel follows the chest and emerges at the flanks; hence this. Fire is entombed at *xu*; therefore in the ninth month the yang qi is exhausted and the yin qi abundant.

[On "What is called leaping when severe"] 躍 means jumping.

[On "in the ninth month all things decline ... hence it is called leaping"] Again because its vessel follows the outer thigh, emerges at the outer edge of the knee, descends in front of the outer auxiliary bone, goes straight down to the end of Juegu, emerges in front of the outer ankle and follows the instep; therefore when the qi is abundant it makes one jump.

## Notes

The symptoms of lesser yang illness explained by the state of yin and yang in the ninth month.
