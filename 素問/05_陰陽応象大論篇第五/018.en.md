# 素問 陰陽応象大論篇第五 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore it is said: yin is within, the guardian of yang; yang is without, the envoy of yin."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the whole sentence] Yin is still, so it is the garrison and guardian of yang; yang moves, so it is the servant and envoy of yin.

## Notes

A well-known phrase on the inner and outer relation of yin and yang: yin guards yang within, and yang works for yin without. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 012, ends here.
