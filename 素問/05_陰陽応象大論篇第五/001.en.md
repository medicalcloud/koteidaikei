# 素問 陰陽応象大論篇第五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi said: "Yin and yang are the Way of heaven and earth, the warp and weft of the myriad things, the father and mother of change, the root and beginning of generation and killing, and the palace of the spirit illumination. In treating illness one must seek the root.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Yin and yang are the Way of heaven and earth"] This means the way of change and generation. The *Laozi* says, "The myriad things carry yin on their backs and embrace yang, and through the blending qi attain harmony," and the "Xici" of the *Yi* says, "One yin and one yang: this is called the Way." This is what is meant.

[On "the warp and weft of the myriad things"] This is the function of nourishing and generating. Yang gives them correct qi so that they are born; yin acts as their master so that they are sustained and established. Therefore they are the warp and weft of the myriad things. The *Yinyang lihe lun* (chapter 6) says, "Yang gives them what is correct; yin acts as their master" — this is what it means.

[On "the father and mother of change"] This is the function of [turning into] different kinds. How so? The hawk turns into a dove, the field mouse into a quail, rotten grass into fireflies, the sparrow enters the sea and becomes a clam, the pheasant enters the sea and becomes a giant clam. All these are different kinds coming into being through change.

[On "the root and beginning of generation and killing"] This is the function of cold and heat. The myriad things are born by borrowing the warmth of yang qi and die through the cold of yin qi; therefore one knows that the root and beginning of generation and killing is what yin and yang perform.

[On "the palace of the spirit illumination"] 府 means palace. Why are generation, killing and change so manifold? Because the spirit illumination resides within. The text below says, "In the movement and stillness of heaven and earth, the spirit illumination is the warp and weft." Therefore when the "Xici" of the *Yi* says, "What is unfathomable in yin and yang is called spirit," it too means that it resides within.
Song editors: From "yin and yang" to "the palace of the spirit illumination" is the same as in the *Tianyuan jidalun* (chapter 66), but the notes differ considerably.

[On "In treating illness one must seek the root"] Yin and yang and the generation, killing and change of the myriad kinds are likewise joined together in the human body; therefore the way of treating illness must first seek them.

## Notes

The opening of the chapter: yin and yang are the Way of heaven and earth, the warp and weft of all things, the father and mother of change, the root of generation and killing, and the palace of the spirit illumination; in treating illness one must seek the root (yin and yang).
- "In treating illness one must seek the root" is a well-known phrase; Wang Bing takes "the root" as yin and yang.
- According to the Song editors, the opening sentence also appears in the *Tianyuan jidalun* (chapter 66).
- In this chapter Huangdi speaks through unit 010.
