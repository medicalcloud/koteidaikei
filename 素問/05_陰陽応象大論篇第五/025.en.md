# 素問 陰陽応象大論篇第五 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore heaven has essence and earth has form; heaven has the eight markers and earth the five districts; therefore they can be the father and mother of the myriad things. Clear yang rises to heaven and turbid yin returns to earth; therefore in the movement and stillness of heaven and earth, the spirit illumination is the warp and weft, and so they can generate, grow, gather and store, ending and beginning again. Only the worthy match heaven above to nourish the head, resemble earth below to nourish the feet, and follow human affairs in between to nourish the five zang. The qi of heaven communicates with the lung; the qi of earth communicates with the throat; the qi of wind communicates with the liver; the qi of thunder communicates with the heart; the qi of the valley communicates with the spleen; the qi of rain communicates with the kidney. The six channels are rivers, the intestines and stomach are the sea, and the nine orifices are the qi where water flows. Taking heaven and earth as their yin and yang, the sweat of yang is named after the rain of heaven and earth, and the qi of yang is named after the swift wind of heaven and earth. Violent qi resembles thunder; counterflowing qi resembles yang. Therefore if treatment does not follow the markers of heaven or use the patterns of earth, calamity will come.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "heaven has essence and earth has form; heaven has the eight markers and earth the five districts"] Yang is heaven, which sends down essential qi to bestow and transform; yin is earth, which spreads harmonious qi to complete form. The five phases are the districts of generation and nurture, and the eight winds the warp and weft of change. The eight markers are the markers of the eight nodal terms; the five districts are the districts of the transformation and nurture of the five phases.

[On "therefore they can be the father and mother of the myriad things"] The yang of heaven transforms qi and the yin of earth completes form; the five districts revolve, the eight winds blow and break, and gathering, storing, generating and growing never fail to suit the time. Being like this, they can be the father and mother of the changes of the myriad things.

[On "Clear yang rises to heaven and turbid yin returns to earth"] Why can they be the father and mother of the myriad things? Because there is this rising and descending.

[On "therefore in the movement and stillness of heaven and earth, the spirit illumination is the warp and weft"] Clear yang rises to heaven and turbid yin returns to earth; but who governs their movement and stillness? It is surely the warp and weft of the spirit illumination. The text above says "the palace of the spirit illumination" — this is what is meant.

[On "and so they can generate, grow, gather and store, ending and beginning again"] Only through the workings of the spirit illumination can they be like this.

[On "Only the worthy match heaven above ... to nourish the five zang"] The head is round and so matches heaven; the feet are square and so resemble earth. Human affairs change and the five zang shift in turn; therefore one follows them to nourish.

[On "The qi of heaven communicates with the lung"] Because it dwells high.

[On "the qi of earth communicates with the throat"] Because it is next below.

[On "the qi of wind communicates with the liver"] Because wind generates wood.

[On "the qi of thunder communicates with the heart"] Because thunder resembles fire having a sound.

[On "the qi of the valley communicates with the spleen"] Because the valley is empty and the spleen receives and takes in.

[On "the qi of rain communicates with the kidney"] Because the kidney governs water.
Song editors: The *Qianjin fang* says: "The qi of wind corresponds to the liver, the qi of thunder stirs the heart, the qi of grain affects the spleen, and the qi of rain moistens the kidney."

[On "The six channels are rivers"] Because they flow without ceasing.

[On "the intestines and stomach are the sea"] Because they all receive. The *Lingshu jing* says: "The stomach is the sea of water and grain."

[On "the nine orifices are the qi where water flows"] What is clear and bright resembles the inner clarity of water; what flows resembles the flowing of water.

[On "Taking heaven and earth as their yin and yang"] When human affairs are matched to images, heaven and earth are pointed to nearby as yin and yang.

[On "the sweat of yang is named after the rain of heaven and earth"] The sweat that leaks through a person's skin is the discharge of the yang qi. Taking an analogy between heaven and earth, it resembles clouds rising and rain falling; hence "the sweat of yang is named after the rain of heaven and earth".

[On "the qi of yang is named after the swift wind of heaven and earth"] The dispersal of the yang qi is like the swift wind flying up; therefore it is matched to it. The old text lacked the two characters 名之 ("is named after"); following the preceding pattern, I have added them.

[On "Violent qi resembles thunder"] Because violent qi strikes and roars, and rolls with sound.

[On "counterflowing qi resembles yang"] Counterflowing qi rises above; the yang qi does likewise.

[On "Therefore if treatment does not follow ... calamity will come"] If one turns one's back on the markers of heaven and violates the districts of earth, the six channels act in reverse, the five qi harm in turn, and once the true qi is harmed, it is clear that calamity will come.
Song editors: The text above says "heaven has the eight markers and earth the five districts". The character 理 in this text and in the note should be 里.

## Notes

The essence and form, the eight markers and five districts of heaven and earth make them the father and mother of all things, with the spirit illumination as their warp and weft. The worthy model themselves on heaven and earth to nourish the head, feet and five zang. The passage relates the qi of heaven and earth to the organs, likens the channels, intestines and stomach, and orifices to rivers, sea and water, and warns that treatment that does not follow heaven and earth brings calamity.
- "Eight markers" and "five districts": Wang Bing takes them as the markers of the eight nodal terms and the districts of the transformations of the five phases.
- Wang Bing notes that the two characters 名之 in "the qi of yang is named after the swift wind" were missing in the old text and that he added them following the preceding pattern.
- "Use the patterns of earth": the Song editors say 理 should be 里, matching "earth has the five districts" above.
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 五理 for 五里.
