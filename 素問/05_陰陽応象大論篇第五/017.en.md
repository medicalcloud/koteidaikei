# 素問 陰陽応象大論篇第五 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore it is said: heaven and earth are the above and below of the myriad things; yin and yang are the male and female of blood and qi; left and right are the roads of yin and yang; water and fire are the signs of yin and yang; yin and yang are the capable beginning of the myriad things.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "heaven and earth are the above and below of the myriad things"] Observing how they cover and carry, one can see the above and below of the myriad things.

[On "yin and yang are the male and female of blood and qi"] Yin governs blood and yang governs qi; yin generates the female and yang the male.

[On "left and right are the roads of yin and yang"] The intervening qi of yin and yang circulate to the left and right; therefore left and right are the roads of yin and yang.
Song editors: The theory of the intervening qi is set out in the *Liuwei zhida lun* (chapter 68). Yang Shangshan says: "Yin qi moves to the right and yang qi to the left."

[On "water and fire are the signs of yin and yang"] Observing the qi of water and fire, the signs of yin and yang can be made clear.

[On "yin and yang are the capable beginning of the myriad things"] This means the origin that is able to bring about change and generation.
Song editors: From "heaven and earth" to "the capable beginning of the myriad things" is the same as in the *Tianyuan jidalun* (chapter 66), but the notes differ considerably. That text lacks the phrase "yin and yang are the male and female of blood and qi", and has "metal and wood are the end and beginning of generation and completion" in place of "yin and yang are the capable beginning of the myriad things".

## Notes

A summary of the relations of heaven and earth, yin and yang, left and right, and water and fire.
- According to the Song editors, almost the same text appears in the *Tianyuan jidalun* (chapter 66), with differences in wording.
- "Capable beginning": Wang Bing explains it as "the origin that is able to bring about change and generation".
