# 素問 陰陽応象大論篇第五 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore those good at using the needle draw yang from yin and yin from yang, treat the left by the right and the right by the left, know the other by the self and the interior by the exterior, and thereby observe the principle of excess and insufficiency. Seeing the subtle, they find the fault; using it, they are not endangered.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "and thereby observe the principle ... they are not endangered"] Because their understanding is deep and clear.

## Notes

The principles of using the needle: using the correspondences of yin and yang, left and right, other and self, exterior and interior, observing excess and insufficiency, and finding illness from its slightest signs, one is not endangered.
- "Draw yang from yin and yin from yang, treat the left by the right and the right by the left" is cited as the basis of later needling methods such as contralateral needling.
