# 素問 陰陽応象大論篇第五 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When yang prevails, the body is hot, the interstices are closed, the panting is coarse so that one bends forward and back, there is heat without sweating, the teeth are dry and one is vexed and oppressed; if the abdomen is full, one dies. One can bear winter but cannot bear summer. When yin prevails, the body is cold, there is sweating, the body is always cold, one shivers frequently and feels cold; with cold there is reversal, and with reversal the abdomen is full and one dies. One can bear summer but cannot bear winter. These are the changes when yin and yang prevail in turn, and the forms of illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When yang prevails ... One can bear winter but cannot bear summer"] Because yang prevails, one can bear winter; because the heat is severe, one cannot bear summer.

[On "with cold there is reversal, and with reversal the abdomen is full and one dies"] "Reversal" means counterflow of qi.

[On "One can bear summer but cannot bear winter"] Because yin prevails, one can bear summer; because the cold is severe, one cannot bear winter.

## Notes

The signs of illnesses in which yang prevails and in which yin prevails are contrasted, with the season each can or cannot bear.
- 能 in "can bear winter" is taken in the sense of "endure" (as Wang Bing also reads it).
- Daizhige writes 俯 for 俛, and Daizhige (the plain text) writes 盛 for 勝 and 長 for 常, among other differences.
