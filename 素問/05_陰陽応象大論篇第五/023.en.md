# 素問 陰陽応象大論篇第五 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why is it so?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks the reason for the different strength of the left and right ears, eyes, hands and feet mentioned in the previous unit.
