# 素問 陰陽応象大論篇第五 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I have heard that the sages of high antiquity discussed and set in order the human form, arranged and distinguished the zang and fu, traced and connected the channels, joined and linked the six unions, each following its own channel; the places where the qi points issue each have a location and a name; the ravines, valleys and attached bones all have their points of origin; divisions and regions, counterflow and compliance each have their pattern; the yin and yang of the four seasons all have their order; the correspondences of outside and inside all have exterior and interior. Is this truly so?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] The six unions are the unions of the twelve channels. The *Lingshu jing* says: "Greater yin and *yangming* form one union, lesser yin and greater yang one union, *jueyin* and lesser yang one union." Since there are three of each for the hands and feet, there are six unions. The hand *jueyin* is the vessel of the pericardium network. The *Qixue lun* (chapter 58) says: "The large meeting places of the flesh are valleys, the small meeting places of the flesh are ravines. The meeting places of ravines and valleys between the divisions of the flesh circulate the nutritive and defensive qi and bring together the great qi." "Attached bones" are the places where bones join and connect. As for "exterior and interior", all the yang channels are exterior and all the yin channels interior.
Song editors: In Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu*, the passage from "The Emperor said" to "Is this truly so?" comes before "In the teaching of the sages of high antiquity".

## Notes

Huangdi asks whether it is true that the sages of antiquity systematized the whole body — its form, organs, channels, qi points, ravines and valleys. From the next unit Qibo answers by setting out the correspondences of the five directions.
- According to the Song editors, Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu* place this question elsewhere (before "In the teaching of the sages of high antiquity").
- Wang Bing takes the six unions as the paired exterior-interior combinations of the twelve channels.
- 胳 in 心包胳 in Wang Bing's note is the base text's form, translated as 絡.
