# 素問 陰陽応象大論篇第五 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "The east generates wind; wind generates wood; wood generates sourness; sourness generates the liver; the liver generates the sinews; the sinews generate the heart. The liver governs the eyes. In heaven it is the dark-mysterious; in humans it is the Way; on earth it is transformation. Transformation generates the five flavours; the Way generates wisdom; the dark-mysterious generates spirit. The spirit in heaven is wind; on earth, wood; in the body, the sinews; among the zang, the liver; among colours, green-blue; among notes, *jue*; among voices, shouting; among changes, gripping; among orifices, the eyes; among flavours, sour; among emotions, anger. Anger harms the liver; grief overcomes anger. Wind harms the sinews; dryness overcomes wind. Sourness harms the sinews; acridity overcomes sourness.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The east generates wind"] The yang qi rises and disperses to become wind. Wind is the command of heaven and the beginning of its teaching; therefore it arises from the east.

[On "wind generates wood"] Wind blows and wood flourishes; this is wind generating wood.

[On "wood generates sourness"] All things sour in flavour are produced by the qi of wood. The "Hongfan" of the *Shangshu* says: "The bending and straight makes sourness."

[On "sourness generates the liver"] "Generate" means to produce and grow. All that is sour first produces and grows in the liver.

[On "the liver generates the sinews"] The essential qi of the liver produces and nourishes the sinews.

[On "the sinews generate the heart"] The book of yin and yang says, "Wood generates fire." When the wood qi of the liver has nourished the sinews within, it then generates the heart.

[On "The liver governs the eyes"] That the eyes see the sun and are bright is because they are of the same kind.

[On "In heaven it is the dark-mysterious"] 玄 means the dark and mysterious; it says that the colour of heaven is high and distant and not yet fully bright.

[On "in humans it is the Way"] "The Way" means transforming by the Way; if people are transformed by the Way, they return and follow.

[On "on earth it is transformation"] "Transformation" means creation; that all kinds are nurtured in season is all the work of creation.

[On "Transformation generates the five flavours"] When the myriad things are born and the five flavours are complete, it is in every case change acting as mother that brings them to completion.

[On "the Way generates wisdom"] Wisdom exists by following correct transformation; hence "the Way generates wisdom".

[On "the dark-mysterious generates spirit"] Within the dark and mysterious, the spirit dwells; hence "the dark-mysterious generates spirit".

[On "The spirit in heaven is wind"] Flying up, blowing and splitting are the functions of wind; that it issues, reaches far and penetrates everywhere is truly possible only by spirit transformation.

[On "on earth, wood"] Soft, pliant, bending and straight — this is the nature of wood.
Song editors: From "in heaven" to "wood" is the same as in the *Tianyuan jidalun* (chapter 66), but the notes differ considerably.

[On "in the body, the sinews"] They bind, connect and join, and so produce strength.

[On "among the zang, the liver"] Its spirit is the *hun*. The *Daojing yi* says: "The *hun* dwells in the liver; when the *hun* is still, the utmost Way is not disordered."

[On "among colours, green-blue"] 蒼 is a pale green-blue, resembling the colour of wood.

[On "among notes, *jue*"] *Jue* is the note of wood, attuned and straight. The "Yueji" says: "When *jue* is disordered there is sorrow, and the people are resentful."

[On "among voices, shouting"] 呼 means crying out; it is also called whistling.

[On "among changes, gripping"] Gripping is that by which one pulls things near.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says: "Gripping, worry, retching, coughing and shivering — these five have names because the emotions change; therefore they are called changes."

[On "among orifices, the eyes"] The eyes govern seeing forms and colours.

[On "among flavours, sour"] Sour can be used to astringe.

[On "among emotions, anger"] Anger is that by which wrong is restrained.

[On "Anger harms the liver"] Though its emotion is anger, in excess it harms itself.

[On "grief overcomes anger"] In grief, the metal of the lung combines with the wood of the liver; therefore it overcomes anger. The *Xuanming wuqi* (chapter 23) says: "When the essential qi combines in the lung, there is grief."
Song editors: The five emotions are anger, joy, thought, worry and fear, so "grief" should be "worry". The reason "worry" is changed to "grief" here is probably that anger and worry that are not resolved harm the intention, while grief and sorrow that stir the centre harm the *hun*; therefore it does not say "worry".

[On "Wind harms the sinews"] When wind prevails, the sinews and network vessels cramp.
Song editors: The *Wuyunxing dalun* (chapter 67) says: "Wind harms the liver."

[On "dryness overcomes wind"] Dryness is the qi of metal; therefore it overcomes the wind of wood.

[On "Sourness harms the sinews"] [This is when] it exceeds measure.

[On "acridity overcomes sourness"] Acrid is the flavour of metal; therefore it overcomes the sourness of wood.

## Notes

Qibo begins setting out the correspondences of the five directions with the east: east, wind, wood, sour, liver, sinews; the dark-mysterious in heaven, the Way in humans, transformation on earth; the colour green-blue, the note *jue*, the voice of shouting, the change of gripping, the orifice of the eyes, the emotion of anger; and harm and victory by the controlling cycle of the five phases (anger harms the liver and grief overcomes anger, etc.).
- Wang Bing adds a short note to each item; on "among the zang, the liver" he takes the spirit dwelling in the liver to be the *hun*, citing the *Daojing yi*.
- "Grief overcomes anger": the Song editors discuss why "grief" is used here where the scheme of five emotions would require "worry".
- According to the Song editors, "In heaven it is the dark-mysterious ... on earth, wood" matches the *Tianyuan jidalun* (chapter 66), and the text from this unit through unit 016 matches the *Wuyunxing dalun* (chapter 67), with different notes.
- Qibo's speech continues to unit 018.
