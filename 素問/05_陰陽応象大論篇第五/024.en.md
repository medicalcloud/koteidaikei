# 素問 陰陽応象大論篇第五 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The east is yang; the essence of yang combines above. When it combines above, the upper part is clear and the lower part depleted; therefore the ears and eyes are keen but the hands and feet are not nimble. The west is yin; the essence of yin combines below. When it combines below, the lower part is abundant and the upper part depleted; therefore the ears and eyes are not keen but the hands and feet are nimble. Thus when both are affected by evil, in the upper part the right is more severe, and in the lower part the left is more severe. This is where the yin and yang of heaven and earth cannot be complete; therefore evil lodges there.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "This is where the yin and yang of heaven and earth cannot be complete; therefore evil lodges there"] The correspondence of yin and yang to heaven and earth is like water in a vessel: if the vessel is round, the water is round; if the vessel is bent, the water is bent. The blood and qi of a person are likewise; therefore evil qi lodges wherever there is insufficiency.

## Notes

The difference in strength between left and right is explained by the essence of yang (east, left) combining above and that of yin (west, right) combining below; when affected by evil, the right is worse above and the left worse below.
- Wang Bing compares the way a person's blood and qi follow the yin and yang of heaven and earth to water taking the shape of its vessel.
- Qibo's speech continues to the end of the chapter (unit 029).
