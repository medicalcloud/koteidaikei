# 素問 陰陽応象大論篇第五 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How are these two to be regulated?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On the question] "Regulate" means to follow the nature of the *tiangui* and govern the blood, qi and essential qi of the body.

## Notes

Huangdi asks how to regulate the two, yin and yang. Wang Bing takes this as governing the body in accordance with the *tiangui* (reproductive qi), linking it to the "seven losses and eight gains" of the next unit.
