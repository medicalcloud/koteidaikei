# 素問 徴四失論篇第七十八 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Not knowing the principles of compliance and counterflow of yin and yang in diagnosis is the first failure in treatment.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Not knowing the principles of compliance and counterflow of yin and yang in diagnosis is the first failure in treatment"] The *Maiyao jingwei lun* says, "Forty-five days after the winter solstice, yang qi rises slightly and yin qi descends slightly; forty-five days after the summer solstice, yin qi rises slightly and yang qi descends slightly. Yin and yang have [冇] their times, which are matched by the pulse." It also says, "The subtlety lies in the pulse, which must be examined; examining it has a guideline, starting from yin and yang." Hence not knowing the principles of compliance and counterflow of yin and yang in diagnosis is the first failure.

## Notes

The first of the four failures: not knowing the principles of compliance and counterflow of yin and yang.
