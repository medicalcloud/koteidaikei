# 素問 徴四失論篇第七十八 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Ah! Dark and obscure — 熟 [who] knows its way? The greatness of the Way compares with heaven and earth and matches the four seas. If you do not know the instruction of the Way, you receive what is bright and make it dark."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Ah! Dark and obscure — who knows its way"] Now on examination, 熟 should be 孰 ("who").

[On "The greatness of the Way compares with heaven and earth ... you receive what is bright and make it dark"] 嗚呼 is a sigh. 窈窈冥冥 means profound and remote. The utmost Way is profound and remote; who can know it? 孰 means who. "Compares with heaven and earth" means its height cannot be measured; "matches the four seas" means its depth and breadth cannot be gauged. If one cannot be enlightened about the Way, even when given the bright Way it becomes dark. 晦 means dark.

## Notes

Concluding the chapter with a lament on the greatness of the Way. The Emperor's speech ends here.
- 熟 in the main text is read as 孰 following the note. The note 今詳熟当作孰 resembles the Song editors' phrasing, but in the base text's transcription it stands within Wang Bing's note.
