# 素問 徴四失論篇第七十八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Are you young and your wisdom not yet sufficient? Or do you mean to speak by mixing [others' views]? The twelve channels and the three hundred sixty-five network vessels are what everyone clearly knows and practitioners follow and use. The reason for lack of complete success is that the essence and spirit are not focused, the intent is not ordered, and outer and inner are not matched; hence at times there is doubt and danger.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Are you young and your wisdom not yet sufficient? Or do you mean to speak by mixing [others' views]"] It says: is it that being young and not yet wise enough one fails to achieve complete success, or that one mixes in words what many people use? The Emperor suspects he already knows and asks him in return.

[On "are what everyone clearly knows and practitioners follow and use"] It means following one's learning and using it.

[On "the essence and spirit are not focused ... hence at times there is doubt and danger"] Outer means complexion; inner means pulse. The essence and spirit are not focused on following and using, the intent does not follow order; this is what is called crude, gauging that loses the constant. Hence complexion and pulse are not matched, and at times one doubts oneself and is in danger.

## Notes

The Emperor answers that the cause is unfocused spirit and failure to match complexion and pulse. His speech continues through unit 11.
