# 素問 徴四失論篇第七十八 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus what people say spreads beyond a thousand *li*, yet they are not clear on the theory of cubit and inch, and their diagnosis lacks human affairs. The way of governing the numbers is the balance of unhurried [comparison].

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Thus what people say spreads beyond a thousand *li*, yet they are not clear on the theory of cubit and inch, and their diagnosis lacks human affairs"] It says a practitioner's successes, failures, praise and blame lie in what people say and can all reach beyond a thousand *li*; but if he is not clear on the diagnostic theory of cubit and inch, by what could he be known and recognized?

[On "The way of governing the numbers is the balance of unhurried [comparison]"] 治 means governing; 葆 means balance. Diagnosing the numbers means taking the qi that should flourish, by the height of the qi, as the basis for comparing categories. Hence the text below says [what follows].

## Notes

Though reputations spread, people do not know diagnosis by cubit and inch or human affairs; unhurried comparison is the key.
- 治数之道，従容之葆 in the main text is translated following Wang Bing's note, but its meaning is hard to construe.
