# 素問 徴四失論篇第七十八 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong replied, "Those who follow the classics and receive the teaching all claim complete success, yet at times there are errors. I ask to hear the explanation of this."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "yet at times there are errors. I ask to hear the explanation of this"] It says those who have studied the classics and received the teaching from their masters all claim complete success with people, but when it comes to applying the proper art and practicing the utmost Way, they sometimes succeed and sometimes fail in the world; hence he asks for an explanation.

## Notes

Leigong asks why those said to achieve complete success still make errors.
