# 素問 徴四失論篇第七十八 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The writings you have mastered and the matters you have been taught are many. Try to tell me the meaning of success and failure: why one succeeds and why one fails."

## Notes

The Yellow Emperor asks Leigong the reasons for success and failure in treatment. There is no note by Wang Bing.
