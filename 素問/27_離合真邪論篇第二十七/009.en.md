# 素問 離合真邪論篇第二十七 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. But when the true and the evil have already merged and the waves no longer swell up, how is it observed?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks how to observe when the true qi and evil qi have merged and no change appears in the pulse. There is no note by Wang Bing.
