# 素問 離合真邪論篇第二十七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I have heard the nine chapters on the nine needles, and you, Master, multiplied them by nine, making nine times nine, eighty-one chapters; I have fully grasped their meaning. The classic speaks of the abundance and decline of qi, of its tilting and shifting left and right, of regulating the lower by the upper and the right by the left, and of supplementing and draining surplus and insufficiency at the spring and stream points; I know this. All of these concern the tilting and shifting of the nutritive and defensive qi and what gives rise to depletion and repletion, not evil qi entering the channels from outside. I wish to hear, when evil qi is in the channels, how does it make a person ill, and how is it to be taken?"

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter. What Huangdi has heard so far concerned the regulation of internal depletion and repletion; now he asks about the pathology and treatment when evil qi from outside is in the channels. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 栄 in 栄輸 means 滎 (Daizhige writes 滎).
