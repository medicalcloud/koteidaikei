# 素問 離合真邪論篇第二十七 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When evil leaves the network vessels and enters the channels, it lodges in the blood vessels. Its cold and warmth have not yet come into accord [with the true qi]; it is like surging waves rising, sometimes coming and sometimes going; therefore it is not always present. Hence it is said: just when it comes, one must press to stop it, and having stopped it, take it; do not meet its surge and drain it. The true qi is the channel qi; [draining then] greatly depletes the channel qi. Therefore 'its coming must not be met' means this. Hence it is said: if one observes the evil without care and drains after the great qi has already passed, the true qi escapes; once it escapes it does not recover, the evil qi arrives again and the illness accumulates further. Therefore 'its going must not be pursued' means this. 'Not [even] a hair can be hung' means waiting for the moment the evil arrives and releasing the needle to drain; if too early or too late, the blood and qi are exhausted and the illness cannot be brought down. Therefore it is said, 'Knowing when it can be taken is like releasing a trigger; not knowing how to take it is like striking with a mallet.' Therefore 'one who knows the way of the trigger cannot hang [even] a hair; one who does not know the trigger strikes it and it does not release' means this."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When evil leaves the network vessels and enters the channels, it lodges in the blood vessels"] The *Miuci lun* (chapter 63) says, "When evil lodges in the body, it first lodges in the skin and hair; if it lingers without leaving, it enters and lodges in the minute vessels; if it lingers without leaving, it enters and lodges in the network vessels; if it lingers without leaving, it enters and lodges in the channel vessels." Hence "leaves the network vessels and enters the channels".

[On "Its cold and warmth ... therefore it is not always present"] It roams around the 16 *zhang* 2 *chi* of the channel vessels; therefore it is not always at the place observed.

[On "Hence it is said: just when it comes ... do not meet its surge and drain it"] 衝 means the normal qi that corresponds to the marks of the water clock. The *Lingshu jing* says, "When the water falls one mark, the person's qi is in the greater yang; two marks, in the lesser yang; three marks, in the *yangming*; four marks, in the yin division." When the qi is in the greater yang, the greater yang alone is abundant; when the qi is in the lesser yang, the lesser yang alone is abundant. If, seeing it alone abundant, one takes it as the arrival of evil and drains it with the needle, one instead harms the true qi. Hence the text below says:

[On "The true qi is the channel qi ... means this"] Taking the channel qi that corresponds to the marks as evil and draining it is a grave error on the practitioner's part. Hence "its coming must not be met".

[On "Hence it is said: if one observes the evil without care ... the illness accumulates further"] Not recognizing the evil and instead punishing the innocent, the true qi leaks and escapes, the evil qi invades again, and the channel qi is greatly depleted; hence the illness accumulates all the more.

[On "Therefore 'its going must not be pursued' means this"] It has already gone with the flow of the channel vessels and cannot be pursued and called back.

[On "'Not [even] a hair can be hung' means waiting for the moment the evil arrives and releasing the needle to drain"] It says that even what is light and subtle is still known; how much more something like surging waves — how could one not know its arrival?

[On "if too early or too late ... the illness cannot be brought down"] It means taking when it should not be taken and missing the time.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition reads "the blood and qi are already depleted"; 尽 should be 虚, a scribal error.

[On "Therefore it is said, 'Knowing when it can be taken ...' means this"] The trigger is the subtlety of movement; it says that knowing the subtle is what is valued.

## Notes

Evil comes and goes in the channels like waves, so one must judge the moment of its arrival to take it; mistiming harms the true qi.
- "Do not meet its surge and drain it": Wang Bing takes 衝 as the normal channel qi that becomes abundant according to the hour, and says it must not be mistaken for evil and drained.
- The phrases introduced by 故曰 and set in quotation marks in the translation are presumably quotations from an older needling classic (similar passages occur in the *Jiuzhen shi'er yuan* and *Xiaozhen jie* chapters of the *Lingshu*).
- 椎 in 扣椎 means a mallet; in contrast with the trigger of a crossbow, it is understood as a figure for sluggish response.
