# 素問 離合真邪論篇第二十七 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "This is attacking evil: quickly let out and remove the abundant blood, and restore the true qi. This evil has newly lodged and is flowing, without a fixed place; push it and it goes forward, draw it and it stops. Meeting it head-on and needling it is [letting out] the warm blood. Needle and let out its blood, and the illness ends immediately."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "This is attacking evil ... restore the true qi"] Look for where there is blood and take it there.

[On "This evil has newly lodged ... [letting out] the warm blood"] It says that newly lodged evil has no fixed residence. If one pushes the needle to supplement it, it advances forward with the supplementing; if one draws the needle to bring it, it stays with the drawing. ## Notes

Newly entered evil should be needled promptly to let out the abundant blood.
- 温血也 is obscure and read in various ways; the translation takes it as letting out the warm blood containing the new evil. Wang Bing comments that warming it without letting out the blood increases the harm.
- 溶溶 describes flowing water and is understood to express that the evil is not settled.
