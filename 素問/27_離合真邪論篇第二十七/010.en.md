# 素問 離合真邪論篇第二十七 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Carefully feel along the three regions and nine indicators for abundance and depletion and regulate them; examine where left and right, upper and lower have lost their balance or diminished; determine carefully which zang is ill and time [the needling] accordingly. One who does not know the three regions does not distinguish yin and yang, and does not separate heaven and earth. Use earth to observe earth, heaven to observe heaven, and human to observe human; regulate them through the central storehouse, so as to fix the three regions. Therefore it is said: if one needles without knowing where the diseased pulse is among the three regions and nine indicators, even when a great fault is about to arrive, the practitioner cannot stop it. Punishing what has no fault is called great confusion: it instead disorders the great channels, and the true [qi] cannot be restored; taking repletion as depletion and evil as true, using the needle without principle, one instead becomes a thief of qi, robbing the person of upright qi, taking compliance as opposition; the nutritive and defensive are scattered and disordered, the true qi is lost, the evil alone lodges within, cutting off the person's long life and bringing calamity from heaven upon him. Not knowing the three regions and nine indicators, one cannot [keep the patient] long. Not knowing how to accord with the four seasons and five phases, through what is added and mutual overcoming, one releases the evil and attacks the upright, cutting off the person's long life. When evil newly lodges and comes, it has no fixed place; push it and it goes forward, draw it and it stops; meet it and drain it, and the illness ends immediately."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Carefully feel along the three regions ... regulate them"] Drain the abundant, supplement the depleted, and when neither abundant nor depleted, take it by the channel; this is the method.

[On "examine where left and right ... time [the needling] accordingly"] When the qi is in yin, wait until it is in the yin division and needle; when the qi is in yang, wait until it is in the yang division and needle. This is called meeting the time. The *Lingshu jing* says, "When the water falls one mark, the person's qi is in the greater yang; when it falls four marks, the person's qi is in the yin division." As the marks accumulate without end, the qi moves accordingly, circling and beginning again. Therefore determine carefully which zang is ill, time it to the qi, and needle.

[On "Therefore it is said: if one needles without knowing ... the practitioner cannot stop it"] 禁 means stop. If one cannot even know where to observe the evil, how could one stop the evil qi?

[On "Punishing what has no fault ... one cannot [keep the patient] long"] With knowledge not refined and learning not comprehensive, one disorders the great channels and becomes a thief of qi, doing harm whenever one acts; how could [the patient] remain well for long?

[On "Not knowing how to accord with the four seasons ... cutting off the person's long life"] Not only is ignorance of the three regions and nine indicators harmful; not knowing the order of the qi of the four seasons and five phases is also enough to destroy life.

[On "When evil newly lodges and comes ... the illness ends immediately"] It is said again because the method is certain.

## Notes

One should needle after knowing the diseased zang through the diagnosis of the three regions and nine indicators; needling without this knowledge robs the upright qi and cuts off life. This ends the chapter.
- For 天殃 "calamity from heaven", Wikisource and Daizhige write 夭殃 "calamity of early death".
- The *Lingshu jing* passage quoted by Wang Bing corresponds to the *Weiqi xing* chapter of the *Lingshu*.
