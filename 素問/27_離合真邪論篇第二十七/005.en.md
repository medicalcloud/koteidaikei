# 素問 離合真邪論篇第二十七 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How does one observe the qi?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How does one observe the qi?"] It means observing the qi that can be taken.

## Notes

Huangdi asks how to judge the moment to take the evil qi.
