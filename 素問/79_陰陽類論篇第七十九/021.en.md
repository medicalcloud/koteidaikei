# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "In illnesses of the three winter months, when the illness combines with yang, even if the pulse shows signs of death in the first month of spring, all [die] after spring has passed.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "even if the pulse shows signs of death in the first month of spring, all [die] after spring has passed"] The illness combining with yang means the earlier yin combines with yang to form the illness. Though in the first month the pulse shows signs of death, yang has already arisen and reaches its flourishing, so there is no death; hence it passes the three months of spring and reaches early summer.

## Notes

The beginning of the account of times of death for seasonal illnesses: illness of the three winter months. The Yellow Emperor's speech continues through unit 25.
