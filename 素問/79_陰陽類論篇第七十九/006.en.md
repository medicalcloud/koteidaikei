# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong said, "I have received the teaching but cannot yet understand it clearly."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I have received the teaching but cannot yet understand it clearly"] It means he has not yet understood how the signs of qi appear in response.

## Notes

Leigong says he does not yet understand.
