# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the two yin and two yang both arrive intermingled, the illness is in the kidneys: cursing, wild behavior, and disease of the vertex becoming madness.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the two yin and two yang both arrive intermingled ... disease of the vertex becoming madness"] The two yin are the kidneys, the viscus of water; the two yang are the stomach, the bowel of earth. Earth qi punishes water, so they arrive intermingled and the illness is in the kidneys. The water of the kidneys does not prevail, so the stomach is abundant, causing disease of the vertex and madness.

## Notes

Illness when the two yin and two yang arrive intermingled.
