# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "What are called the three yang is greater yang as the warp. When the three yang pulse arrives at hand greater yin [the inch opening] string-like and floating, not sunken, decide by the measures, examine with the mind, and match it with the theory of yin and yang. What are called the two yang is *yangming*. When it arrives at hand greater yin string-like, sunken and tense without drumming, and heat arrives with illness, all die. The one yang is lesser yang. When it arrives at hand greater yin, connecting above with *renying*, string-like, tense, suspended and not cut off, this is illness of lesser yang; if there is only yin, death.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What are called the three yang is greater yang as the warp"] Yang qi is abundant and great, hence greater yang.

[On "When the three yang pulse arrives at hand greater yin string-like and floating ... match it with the theory of yin and yang"] Greater yin is the inch opening. The inch opening is hand greater yin, where the pulse qi moves; hence all pulses arrive at the inch opening. The greater yang pulse is surging, large and long. Now if it is string-like and floating and not sunken, judge it by the measures of height of the four seasons, examine and combine it with the signs of the similarities and differences of the five viscera, respond to the theory of yin and yang, and know whether it is good or bad.

[On "What are called the two yang is *yangming*"] The *Lingshu jing* says, "*Chen* is *yangming* of the left foot, 已 [*si*] *yangming* of the right foot; the two yang combine their brightness, hence the two yang are *yangming*."

[On "When it arrives at hand greater yin string-like, sunken and tense without drumming, and heat arrives with illness, all die"] 鼓 means drumming; 炅 means heat. The *yangming* pulse is floating, large and short. Now string-like, sunken and tense without drumming means yin qi overcomes yang and wood comes to ride on earth. When yin overcomes yang and wood rides on earth, yet heat illness arrives instead, it is the decline and failure of yang qi, like a lamp flaring brighter as it is about to go out; hence all die.

[On "The one yang is lesser yang"] Yang qi is not yet great, hence lesser yang.

[On "When it arrives at hand greater yin, connecting above with *renying* ... this is illness of lesser yang"] *Renying* is one and a half inches on either side of the laryngeal prominence, where the pulse moves against the hand. String-like is the lesser yang pulse; now tense, suspended and not cut off means insufficiency of channel qi; hence illness of lesser yang. Suspended means moving and swaying like a hanging object.

[On "if there is only yin, death"] 専 means alone: having yin qi alone without yang qi means death.

## Notes

The Emperor describes how the three, two and one yang pulses appear at the inch opening and their prognoses. His speech continues into the next unit.
