# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In illnesses of the three autumn months, when the three yang rise together, they end of themselves without treatment. When yin and yang intermingle and combine, standing one cannot sit and sitting one cannot rise. When the three yang arrive alone, the term is at stone water; when the two yin arrive alone, the term is at abundant water."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In illnesses of the three autumn months, when the three yang rise together, they end of themselves without treatment"] In autumn yang qi declines and yin qi gradually emerges; yang does not overcome yin, so it ends of itself.

[On "When yin and yang intermingle and combine, standing one cannot sit and sitting one cannot rise"] Because the qi do not follow their proper function.

[On "When the three yang arrive alone, the term is at stone water"] Having yang without yin, it is said to arrive alone. The *Zhu zhijiao lun* says, "When the three yang arrive alone, it is the three yang arriving together"; thus there is only yang and no yin. Stone water means the winter months when water freezes like stone; hence stone water. Fire is entombed at *xu*, and in winter yang qi is faint, hence death at stone water.
Song editors: On examination, this explanation of stone water is originally Quan Yuanqi's, adopted by Wang.

[On "when the two yin arrive alone, the term is at abundant water"] This too is what is called arriving together without yang. Abundant water means the time when rain and snow all melt into water, that is, the central solar period of the first month.
Song editors: The Quan Yuanqi edition has 三陰 for 二陰.

## Notes

Illness of the three autumn months. The chapter, and the Yellow Emperor's speech, end here.
