# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the two yang and three yin [combine], the extreme yin is also present; yin cannot pass beyond yang, yang qi cannot stop yin, yin and yang are both cut off; if floating, there are blood conglomerations; if sunken, pus and rot. When yin and yang are both vigorous, it descends to the yin-yang [genitals]. Matching the bright above and the dark below, diagnosis determines the time of death or life, and matches it to the start of the year."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "yin and yang are both cut off; if floating, there are blood conglomerations; if sunken, pus and rot"] The two yang are *yangming*, the three yin hand greater yin, the extreme yin the spleen; hence "the extreme yin is also present". Yin qi cannot go beyond yang, and yang qi cannot restrain 心 [yin]. Now yin and yang press on each other, so the pulses are both cut off and not continuous. A floating pulse is yang qi pressing on yin, hence blood conglomerations; a sunken pulse is yin qi pressing on yang, hence pus gathering and rotting.

[On "When yin and yang are both vigorous, it descends to the yin-yang"] If yin and yang are both vigorous and press on each other without ceasing, it gradually descends into the yin-yang and becomes a grave illness. Yin-yang means the male yang organ and the female yin organ, because they can contain and receive [the base text has a blank here].

[On "Matching the bright above and the dark below"] The bright means above *yangming*; the dark means within the extreme yin, a dim place.

[On "diagnosis determines the time of death or life, and matches it to the start of the year"] This refers to the meaning of the short terms below.

## Notes

Illness of the two yang and three yin, and determining the time of death or life. The Emperor's speech ends here.
- 心 in 陽気不能制心 in Wang Bing's note is read as 陰 from 陽気不能止陰 in the main text.
