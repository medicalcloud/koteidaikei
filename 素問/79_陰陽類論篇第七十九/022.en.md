# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In illnesses of the three winter months that are in the 理 [interior] and have thereby run their course, all die when grass and willow leaves [appear]. If in spring yin and yang [pulses] are both cut off, the term is the first month of spring.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In illnesses of the three winter months that are in the interior and have thereby run their course, all die when grass and willow leaves [appear]"] The interior means the two yin, the qi of the kidneys. Those whose kidneys are ill and whose pulse shows signs of death in the first month all die when the withered grass has all turned green and willow leaves come out. 理 means 裏 ("interior"); 已 means 以; in ancient usage they were the same.

[On "If in spring yin and yang are both cut off, the term is the first month of spring"] Those whose yin and yang pulses are both suspended and cut off after the Beginning of Spring will die within the first month.
Song editors: The *Taisu* lacks 春.

## Notes

Illness of the three winter months exhausted in the interior (kidneys).
