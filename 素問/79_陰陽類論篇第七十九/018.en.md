# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong said, "May I ask about short terms [of death]?" The Yellow Emperor did not respond. Leigong asked again.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Leigong said, 'May I ask about short terms?' The Yellow Emperor did not respond"] He wanted him to ask again so that he would treasure it.

## Notes

Leigong asks about the time of death; the Yellow Emperor does not answer at once.
