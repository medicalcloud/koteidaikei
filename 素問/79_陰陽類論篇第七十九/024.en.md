# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In illnesses of the three summer months, if in the extreme yin [spleen], it does not pass ten days; if yin and yang intermingle, the term is at the still water.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In illnesses of the three summer months, if in the extreme yin, it does not pass ten days"] This means heat illness. Heat illness of the spleen endangers the five viscera; the completion number of earth is ten, hence not beyond ten days.

[On "if yin and yang intermingle, the term is at the still water"] The *Ping rebing lun* says, "When in warm illness sweat comes out but the heat soon returns, the pulse is agitated and rapid and not reduced by sweating, with wild speech and inability to eat, the illness is called yin-yang intermingling." Suffering summer heat in the sixth month, yin and yang intermingle again and the two qi hold out against each other; hence death at the Beginning of Autumn.
Song editors: The Quan Yuanqi edition says, "Still water is the seventh month; it is established at *shen*, and water is born at *shen*; yin and yang are counter." Yang Shangshan says, "溓 is pronounced *lian*; it means still water. The seventh month is when water is born."

## Notes

Illness of the three summer months.
