# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Illness of the three spring months is called yang killing. If yin and yang [pulses] are both cut off, the term is when the grass dries.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Illness of the three spring months is called yang killing"] Yang illness here does not mean cold damage or warm-heat illness, but heat illness out of season with a surging, abundant, rapid pulse. In the three months of spring yang qi is still slight and should not yet be fully abundant; yet one suffers heat and the pulse corresponds to summer qi. The classic says, "The pulse is not seen again": the summer pulse should be surging and rapid, but there is no yang to respond outside, so one must die at the summer solstice. Because death comes at the summer solstice, when yang qi kills things, it is called yang killing.

[On "If yin and yang are both cut off, the term is when the grass dries"] If it is not yang illness, but simply the yin and yang pulses are both suspended and cut off, death comes when frost falls and the grass dries.

## Notes

Illness of the three spring months.
