# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong replied, "Spring is *jia* and *yi*, blue-green; within it governs the liver and rules for seventy-two days. This is the pulse governing a season. I consider that viscus most noble."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "rules for seventy-two days. This is the pulse governing a season. I consider that viscus most noble"] The eastern *jia* and *yi* are governed by the qi of spring, and the blue-green color naturally connects within to the liver. The *Jingui zhenyan lun* says, "The east is blue-green, which enters and connects with the liver"; hence "blue-green, within it governs the liver". The qi of each of the five phases flourishes for seventy-two days; five of these multiplied complete the year's number of three hundred sixty days; hence "rules for seventy-two days". The qi of the four seasons begins with spring, and among the correspondences of the five viscera the liver matches it; hence he considered that viscus most 責 [noble]. Some read 道 for 蔵; that is wrong.

## Notes

Leigong replies that the liver, governing spring, is most noble.
