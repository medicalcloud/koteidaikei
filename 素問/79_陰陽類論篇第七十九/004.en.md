# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong fasted for seven days, and at dawn again sat in attendance.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Leigong fasted for seven days, and at dawn again sat in attendance"] Realizing his error, he fasted to cleanse his mind; wishing to benefit, he sat and asked again.

## Notes

Leigong fasts and again asks to be taught.
