# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the two yin and one yang [combine], the illness comes from the kidneys; yin qi lodges and wanders in the heart; the empty orifices below the stomach duct are blocked as by an embankment and do not pass; the four limbs are as if detached.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "yin qi lodges and wanders in the heart ... the four limbs are as if detached"] The one yang is hand lesser yang, the triple burner, the bowel of the fire of the heart master. Water rises to attack fire, so the fire illness comes from the kidneys and yin qi lodges and wanders in the heart. Why? The kidney vessel rises from the kidneys through the liver and diaphragm into the lungs, and its branch emerges from the lungs to network the heart and pour into the chest; hence this. When yin lodges in the empty orifices and wanders upward, the stomach cannot control it; that the stomach cannot control it means earth qi has 哀 [weakened]; hence the empty orifices below the stomach duct do not pass. "Embankment" means like a dike that allows no leakage. The stomach vessel follows the feet and the heart vessel networks the hands, so the four limbs are as if detached and useless.
Song editors: Wang says "the stomach vessel follows the feet"; but on examination, since this illness of the two yin and one yang comes from the kidneys, 胃 ("stomach") should be 腎 ("kidneys").

## Notes

Illness of the two yin and one yang.
