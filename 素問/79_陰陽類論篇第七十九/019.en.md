# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "It is in the classic and its discussions."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The Yellow Emperor said, 'It is in the classic and its discussions'"] In the ancient classic.
Song editors: The Quan Yuanqi edition makes the text from "Leigong" onward a separate chapter named *Sishi bing lei*.

## Notes

The Emperor says it is in the classic.
