# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the two yang and one yin [combine], *yangming* governs the illness; it cannot overcome the one yin; [the pulse] is soft and moving, and the nine orifices are all sluggish.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*yangming* governs the illness; it cannot overcome the one yin ... the nine orifices are all sluggish"] The one yin is *jueyin*, the qi of liver wood; the two yang are *yangming*, the qi of stomach earth. Wood and earth hold out against each other, so *yangming* governs the illness. Wood attacks its earth and earth cannot overcome wood; hence "cannot overcome the one yin". The pulse soft and moving: softness is stomach qi, movement is the form of wood. When earth and wood hold out against each other, stomach qi does not turn, so the nine orifices are sluggish and blocked.

## Notes

Illness of the two yang and one yin.
