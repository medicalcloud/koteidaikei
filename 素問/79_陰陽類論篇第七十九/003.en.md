# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Reflecting on the upper and lower classics, on yin and yang and unhurried [comparison], what you call noble is the very lowest."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Reflecting on the upper and lower classics ... what you call noble is the very lowest"] 従容 means calm, unhurried comparison of categories. Reflecting on the yin and yang, comparison of categories, and form and qi in the upper and lower chapters of the *Classic of Pulses*, the Emperor does not hold the liver to be noble; hence he says what Leigong calls noble is the very lowest.

## Notes

The Emperor rejects Leigong's answer.
