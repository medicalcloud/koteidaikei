# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the three yang and one yin [combine], the greater yang pulse prevails and the one yin cannot stop it; within it disorders the five viscera, outwardly it causes fright.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the greater yang pulse prevails and the one yin cannot stop it ... outwardly it causes fright"] The three yang are the qi of foot greater yang; hence "greater yang prevails". Wood generates fire; now abundant yang burns the wood, and the wood in turn receives it; yang qi is vastly abundant and becomes wild heat within, so it disorders the five viscera within. The liver governs fright, so outwardly it shows the form of fright.

## Notes

Illness of the three yang and one yin.
