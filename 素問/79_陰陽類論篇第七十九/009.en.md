# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong said, "I have devoted my whole mind to receiving the transmission on the channels, recited and obtained the way of *Congrong*, and matched it with *Congrong*, but I do not know yin and yang, and I do not know female and male."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "and matched it with *Congrong*, but I do not know yin and yang, and I do not know female and male"] 頌 is today's 誦 ("recite"). Leigong says: though I have recited and now matched the wondrous way of *Congrong* with the ancient *Congrong* and compared categories of forms and names, I still do not know the order of rank of yin and yang or the meaning of the different terms female and male; please state the meaning to clarify the utmost teaching, [showing] how yin and yang, female and male, convey and respond to each other.

## Notes

Leigong says he does not know the meaning of yin and yang, female and male, and asks.
