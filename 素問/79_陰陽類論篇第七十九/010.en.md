# 素問 陰陽類論篇第七十九 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "The three yang are the father, the two yang the guard, the one yang the regulator; the three yin are the mother, the two yin the female, the one yin the sole envoy.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The three yang are the father"] The father supervises and aids the lesser ones; it speaks of height and honor.

[On "the two yang the guard"] The guard repels and wards off pathogens; it speaks of supporting life.

[On "the one yang the regulator"] The regulator organizes form and qi; it speaks of its balance.

[On "the three yin are the mother"] The mother rears and nourishes the children; it speaks of nourishing life.

[On "the two yin the female"] Female is a name for yin.

[On "the one yin the sole envoy"] The viscus of the one yin is matched outwardly with the triple burner; the triple burner governs announcing and guiding the qi and is called envoy; hence "sole envoy".

## Notes

The Emperor likens the three yin and three yang to father, mother and so on, and then describes illnesses where two channels combine. His speech continues through unit 17.
