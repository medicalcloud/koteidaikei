# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Does it rest on something?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Does it rest on something?"] It asks: since the great void has no obstruction, on what does the body of earth rest so as to stay in place?

## Notes

A question on what supports earth.
