# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The *Pulse Method* says: 'Changes of heaven and earth cannot be diagnosed by the pulse.' This is what is meant."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The *Pulse Method* says ... This is what is meant"] Heaven and earth act through qi, not through positions; hence they should not be known by the pulse.

## Notes

A quotation from the *Pulse Method* supplementing the previous unit.
