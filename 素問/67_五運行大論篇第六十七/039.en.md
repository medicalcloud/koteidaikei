# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the qi is in surplus, it controls what it overcomes and insults what it does not overcome. When it is deficient, what it does not overcome insults and exploits it, and what it overcomes slights and insults it. The insulter in turn receives the pathogen; insulting and receiving the pathogen comes from having little awe."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When it is deficient, what it does not overcome insults and exploits it, and what it overcomes slights and insults it"] When wood is in surplus, it controls earth and slights metal; since metal qi does not contend, wood relies on its surplus to deceive and insult. Also when wood is scant, metal prevails and earth in turn insults wood; since wood is deficient, earth recklessly overrides it. The four other qi are all the same. 侮 means overriding and slighting.

[On "The insulter in turn receives the pathogen"] Either because oneself is strong and abundant, or because one meets the other's weakness, one acts recklessly without gauging one's own lowliness and weakness, overriding and slighting. Though one insults in seeking to win, in the end one surely receives the pathogen.

[On "insulting and receiving the pathogen comes from having little awe"] Receiving the pathogen means in each case receiving the pathogen of what 已 [oneself] does not overcome. Leaving 已 [one's own] palace to go to another land, one is strong outside and dry within, the pathogen abundant and the true weak, with little reverence or awe; thereby the pathogen is admitted. Hence "having little awe".
Song editors: The *Liujie zangxiang lun* says: "Arriving before its time is called excess; then it presses on what it does not overcome and exploits what it overcomes; this is called qi excess. Not arriving when it should is called deficiency; then what it overcomes acts recklessly, what it generates falls ill, and what it does not overcome presses on it; this is called qi oppression." This is the same meaning.

## Notes

Exploitation and insult according to surplus or deficiency of the qi governing the year.
- 已 in the main text and notes is read as 己 "oneself".
