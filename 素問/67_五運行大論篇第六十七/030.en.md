# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The east generates wind, wind generates wood, wood generates sourness, sourness generates the liver, the liver generates the sinews, and the sinews generate the heart. In heaven it is the mysterious, in humans the way, on earth transformation. Transformation generates the five flavors, the way generates wisdom, the mysterious generates spirit, and transformation generates qi. Spirit in heaven is wind, on earth wood, in the body the sinews, in qi softness, among the depots the liver. Its nature is warmth, its virtue harmony, its function movement, its color green, its transformation flourishing, its creatures the furred, its government dispersal, its decree spreading out, its change smashing and snapping, its calamity falling, its flavor sour, its emotion anger. Anger harms the liver; sorrow overcomes anger. Wind harms the liver; dryness overcomes wind. Sourness harms the sinews; acridity overcomes sourness.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The east generates wind"] The east is where the sun begins; wind is the beginning of teaching and the messenger of heaven. Since it issues commands and enacts decrees, it arises from the east. When the scene is clear yet the mountains are dim, green dust meets the horizon and cliffs and valleys look as one, this is the wind of crags and peaks. When yellow-white dim dust stands like a wall in the evening sky and only the sky hanging down can be seen, this is the wind of rivers and marshes. When in addition yellow, black and white dust is received below, this is the fierce wind of mountains and marshes.

[On "wind generates wood"] Yang rises and wind drums, and plants spread and flourish; hence "wind generates wood". This is the generation and transformation of harmonious qi. If wind qi acts, things are blown about and snapped; at its extreme of change, trees are uprooted and grasses swept away. When the movement falls on the years *dingmao*, *dingchou*, *dinghai*, *dingyou*, *dingwei* and *dingsi*, the transformation of wind is deficient; on the years *renshen*, *renwu*, *renchen*, *renyin*, *renzi* and *renxu*, the transformation of wind is in surplus for the myriad things.
Song editors: On examination, Wang's note divides surplus and deficiency of the movement by *ding* and *ren*. Some hold that the five years *dingmao*, *dinghai*, *dingsi*, *renshen* and *renyin* are heavenly tally, same heavenly tally, or proper year-meeting, neither surplus nor deficient but balanced wood movement, and consider Wang's note wrong. This is not knowing the general principle. If one insists on fine distinctions, even excluding these five years would still not be exhaustive. The fire, earth, metal and water movements in the text below are all the same.

[On "wood generates sourness"] All things with sour flavor originate from the generation and transformation of wood qi.

[On "sourness generates the liver"] Sour flavor enters the stomach and generates and nourishes the liver depot.

[On "the liver generates the sinews"] Sour flavor enters the liver and, spreading and transforming from the liver depot, generates the sinews and membranes.

[On "the sinews generate the heart"] When the sour qi has finished nourishing the sinews and membranes, it flows and transforms from the sinews into the heart.

[On "In heaven it is the mysterious"] 玄 means dark obscurity. At the end of *chou* the east whitens; at the beginning of *yin* the color of the sky turns black again and the great void is all dark. The image of being the mysterious in heaven can be seen.
Song editors: On examination, the seven phrases from "in heaven it is the mysterious" to "transformation generates qi" speak generally of the great law of generation and transformation of the six qi and five phases and are not peculiar to the east. But Wang's note, saying "the mysterious is the black of the sky at the end of *chou* and the beginning of *yin*", speaks only of the east and does not include the other directions. This note does not work.

[On "in humans the way"] The way of right principle, the governing transformation of generation and nourishment.

[On "on earth transformation"] 化 means generation and transformation. Only after generation and transformation are there the myriad things; none of the myriad things is generated and completed except by the qi of transformation.

[On "Transformation generates the five flavors"] Metals, jades, soils, stones, grasses, trees, vegetables and fruits—their roots, stems, branches, leaves, flowers, husks, fruits and kernels, things without awareness—are all generated by the transformation of earth.

[On "the way generates wisdom"] Wisdom is right knowing and far-reaching thought. When knowledge is right, one does not doubt in affairs; when thought is far-reaching, one does not step into danger. Dealing with things by the way, the principle accords with wisdom. The *Lingshu jing* says: "Dealing with things through deliberation is called wisdom."

[On "the mysterious generates spirit"] The working of spirit has no fixed direction, is deep and subtle beyond measure, its traces seen but its form hidden, and few things can anticipate it. Thus spirit brightness dwells within dark obscurity, hidden and unseen; the mysterious generates spirit brightness.

[On "and transformation generates qi"] The changes of flying, running and crawling creatures, the five classes—scaled, shelled, furred, naked and feathered—belong inwardly to the spirit mechanism. Though encompassed by the five flavors, what they receive at birth differs; hence it also says "transformation generates qi". The seven phrases above speak generally of the great law of generation and transformation of the six qi and five phases and are not peculiar to the east.
Song editors: The *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* and *Tianyuan ji dalun* lack the phrase 化生気.

[On "Spirit in heaven is wind"] Sounding, stirring, opening and splitting are the transformation of wind; shaking, snapping, smashing and uprooting are the function of wind. When the year belongs to *jueyin* above, wind transforms in heaven; when *jueyin* is below, wind moves on earth.

[On "on earth wood"] Long and short, crooked and straight are the body of wood; trunks rising and mechanisms springing are the function of wood.

[On "in the body the sinews"] Tying, binding, bundling and networking are the body of the sinews; contracting, relaxing, curling and stretching are the function of the sinews.

[On "in qi softness"] When the transformation of wood spreads out and the transformation of wind proceeds, the bodies of things are soft and 䎡 [pliant].

[On "among the depots the liver"] The liver has two spreading lobes and one small lobe, like the image of a wood shell splitting. Each has branching vessels traveling within to spread the qi of yang harmony. It is the palace of the ethereal soul, the office of the general, from which plans and deliberation come. When the year is *ding*, the liver depot and its channels and network vessels are first to receive the pathogen and fall ill. The gallbladder bowel likewise.

[On "Its nature is warmth"] 暄 means warm; this is the nature of liver wood.

[On "its virtue harmony"] Spreading harmonious qi to the myriad things is the virtue of wood.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its virtue is spreading harmony".

[On "its function movement"] Wind shakes and things move; without wind all kinds are still.
Song editors: The function of wood is movement, and the government of excessive fire is also movement. Presumably because fire and wood govern violence and speed, both are movement.

[On "its color green"] Among things with form, those that ride on the transformation of wood all show a pale green outer color. Now the plants of the eastern lands are all green. In a *ding* year, green things are mixed with white and yellow and are not pure.

[On "its transformation flourishing"] 栄 means beautiful color. In the four seasons, things that show blossoms and flourishing with fresh, lovely color are all produced by the transformation of wood.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its transformation is generating and flourishing".

[On "its creatures the furred"] The myriad things sprout like hair on skin.

[On "its government dispersal"] It disperses the qi of generation to the myriad things.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its government is unfolding and opening". On examination, the government of wood is dispersal; the government of balanced wood is dispersing outward; the government of excessive wood is dispersal; the qi of deficient earth is dispersal; the function of metal is scattering down; the disaster of wood is scattering down. Thus there are six different uses of 散, but only two meanings: one is the 散 of dispersing outward, the qi of wood; the other is the 散 of scattering down, done by the qi of metal.

[On "its decree spreading out"] The qi of yang harmony unfolds and disperses.

[On "its change smashing and snapping"] It smashes and uproots what is grown.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its change is shaking and issuing".

[On "its calamity falling"] 隕 means falling. A great wind suddenly arises, grasses are destroyed and trees fall.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its disaster is scattering down".

[On "its flavor sour"] All things that transform and change and have a sour flavor are made and unmade by wood qi. Now what grows in the eastern wilds is mostly sour.

[On "its emotion anger"] Anger is a straight voice; by anger one intimidates things.

[On "Anger harms the liver"] Whenever the function of a thing reaches its extreme, it harms itself. Anger arises from the liver and in turn harms the liver depot.

[On "sorrow overcomes anger"] When sorrow arises, anger stops; this is proof of overcoming.
Song editors: On examination, among the five emotions 悲 should be 憂. Presumably, since worry harms intent and sorrow harms the ethereal soul, it says "sorrow overcomes anger".

[On "Wind harms the liver"] This too is like wind snapping trees: wind arises from wood and in turn snaps it; its function reaches the extreme and unfolds.
Song editors: The *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* says "wind harms the sinews".

[On "dryness overcomes wind"] Wind arises from wood; dryness is the transformation of metal. When wind is in surplus, it is controlled by dryness; when the liver is abundant, it is treated with coolness; where coolness and clarity proceed, it is the qi of metal.

[On "Sourness harms the sinews"] Sourness drains liver qi; excessive draining harms its qi. The *Lingshu jing* says "sourness goes to the sinews; in sinew illness do not eat much sour"; this is why. "Goes to the sinews" means it spreads its qi swiftly. Qi, blood, flesh and bone are likewise.
Song editors: On examination, what the note calls the *Lingshu jing* is the text of the *Xuanming wuqi pian* of the *Suwen*. The *Jiayi jing* takes it as *Suwen*. Wang's calling it the *Lingshu jing* is an error.

[On "acridity overcomes sourness"] Acrid is the flavor of metal; hence it overcomes the sourness of wood. When sourness is in surplus, it is overcome by acridity.

## Notes

The east–wind–wood–liver series of generation and transformation, with its nature, virtue, function, color, transformation, creatures, government, decree, change, calamity, flavor and emotion, and the overcoming relations. Qibo's speech continues to unit 035.
