# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The arising of prevalence and retaliation in the qi of heaven and earth does not show in diagnosis.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The arising of prevalence and retaliation ... does not show in diagnosis"] It means that balanced qi and prevalence and retaliation are all observed through outward signs, not known by [pulse] diagnosis.

## Notes

Prevalence and retaliation of the qi of heaven and earth do not appear in pulse diagnosis. Qibo's speech continues into unit 024.
