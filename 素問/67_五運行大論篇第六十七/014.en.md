# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I have heard Guiyuqu say: 'What corresponds to earth is still.' Now you, Master, say that what is below moves left. I do not understand what this means. I wish to hear how it comes about."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I do not understand what this means. I wish to hear how it comes about"] He questions the discrepancy.
Song editors: Guiyuqu's words "what corresponds to earth is still" are found in the *Tianyuan ji dalun*.

## Notes

A question on the discrepancy between Guiyuqu's account and Qibo's.
