# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the qi suit each other, [illness] is slight; when they do not, it is severe."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the qi suit each other, [illness] is slight; when they do not, it is severe"] Wood residing in the position of fire, fire in that of earth, earth in that of metal, metal in that of water, water in that of wood, and wood in the sovereign position are suiting each other. Wood residing in the position of water, water in that of metal, metal in that of earth, earth in that of fire, and fire in that of wood, though suiting each other, are in the end the child usurping the position of the parent, the lower overriding the upper, and are still a minor contravention. Wood residing in the positions of metal or earth, fire in those of metal or water, earth in those of water or wood, metal in those of fire or wood, and water in those of fire or earth are not suiting each other; hence the illness is severe. In all cases, first establish the movement qi and the qi of the controller of heaven; then whether the qi in their places suit each other can be known.

## Notes

The severity of illness depends on whether the guest and host qi suit each other.
