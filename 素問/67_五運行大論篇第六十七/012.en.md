# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What of movement and stillness?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of movement and stillness?"] It refers to the leftward and rightward motion of heaven and earth.

## Notes

A question on the direction of motion of heaven and earth.
