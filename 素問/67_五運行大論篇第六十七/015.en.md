# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "As for the movement and stillness of heaven and earth and the shifting and returning of the five phases, even Guiyuqu only observed what is above and could not yet clarify it fully. In the working of change and transformation, heaven hangs down images and earth forms shapes; the seven luminaries are strung across the void and the five phases adhere to earth. Earth is what carries the forms and kinds that are generated and completed; the void is what arrays the essential qi that correspond to heaven. The movement of form and essence is like root and trunk to branches and leaves. Looking up at the images, one can know them though far away."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "even Guiyuqu only observed what is above and could not yet clarify it fully"] Not being able to clarify it fully is because completeness was not sought.

[On "The movement of form and essence ... one can know them though far away"] Observing the eastward turning of the five planets, the principle of the earth's body moving left can be clearly known. 麗 means adhering. There has never been a thing with form that could be complete without relying on something.

## Notes

The images of heaven and the shapes of earth are as root to branches; by observing the heavens one can know the motion of earth.
