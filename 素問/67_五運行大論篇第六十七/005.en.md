# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "How illuminating the question! I have read the text of the *Taishi tianyuan ce*: the qi of the red heaven passes through Niu and Nü and the division of *wu*; the qi of the yellow heaven passes through Xin and Wei and the division of *ji*; the qi of the green heaven passes through Wei and Shi and Liu and Gui; the qi of the white heaven passes through Kang and Di and Mao and Bi; the qi of the black heaven passes through Zhang and Yi and Lou and Wei. The so-called divisions of *wu* and *ji* are Kui and Bi and Jiao and Zhen, the gateways of heaven and earth. This is where the indications begin and where the way arises; one must understand it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The so-called divisions of *wu* and *ji* ... the gateways of heaven and earth"] *Wu* earth belongs to Qian; 巳 [*ji*] earth belongs to Xun. The *Dunjia jing* says: "The six *wu* are the gate of heaven; the six 已 [*ji*] are the door of earth." Divining rain morning and evening by the northwest and southeast takes its meaning from this. Rain is the function of earth, produced by damp qi; hence the divination here.

## Notes

The origin of the stems' transformation into movements, explained by the qi of five colored heavens passing through the twenty-eight lunar lodges.
- 已分 and 戊巳分 in the main text and 巳土 and 六已 in the note are all read as 己 (*ji*).
