# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The five qi take their turns in succession, each having its time of precedence. Out of its position it is pathogenic; in its position it is upright."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The five qi take their turns in succession, each having its time of precedence"] When it matches the season of the year, that qi takes precedence.

[On "Out of its position it is pathogenic; in its position it is upright"] First establish the movement; then one knows what is out of position and what is in position.

## Notes

The succession of the five qi, and upright and pathogenic according to whether they are in their positions. Qibo's speech ends here.
