# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When *jueyin* is above, lesser yang is below, with *yangming* on the left and greater yin on the right. When lesser yin is above, *yangming* is below, with greater yang on the left and lesser yang on the right. When greater yin is above, greater yang is below, with *jueyin* on the left and *yangming* on the right. When lesser yang is above, *jueyin* is below, with lesser yin on the left and greater yang on the right. When *yangming* is above, lesser yin is below, with greater yin on the left and *jueyin* on the right. When greater yang is above, greater yin is below, with lesser yang on the left and lesser yin on the right. This is what is called naming the positions facing south and stating what appears. Above and below meet, cold and heat face each other; when the qi suit each other there is harmony, when they do not, there is illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "This is what is called naming the positions facing south and stating what appears"] The one governing the year has its position in the south, so its left and right are stated facing north; the one below has its position in the north, so its left and right are stated facing south. Above is the position of heaven, below that of earth. Facing south, left is east and right is west. Above and below differ, so left and right differ.

[On "Above and below meet ... when they do not, there is illness"] Wood and fire facing each other, metal and water, water and wood, fire and earth, earth and metal are suiting each other. Earth and wood, earth and water, water and fire, fire and metal, metal and wood facing each other are not suiting each other. Above presiding over below is compliance; below presiding over above is contravention. Contravention too is constrained and suppressed, and illness arises—such as earth presiding over ministerial or sovereign fire.

## Notes

The spring in earth and its left and right intervening qi, and whether the qi above and below suit each other.
