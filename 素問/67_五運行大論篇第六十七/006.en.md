# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. The treatise says: 'Heaven and earth are the above and below of the myriad things; left and right are the roads of yin and yang.' I do not know what this means."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The treatise says ... I do not know what this means"] "The treatise" means the *Tianyuan ji [dalun]* and the *Yinyang yingxiang lun*.

## Notes

A question on the meaning of above and below, left and right.
