# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "According to where the qi is, look for it on the left and right."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "According to where the qi is, look for it on the left and right"] One receives it at the four positions of the left and right *chi* and *cun*, and thereby knows whether it responds or not, whether it is excessive or not.

## Notes

The intervening qi are observed at the left and right positions of the pulse.
