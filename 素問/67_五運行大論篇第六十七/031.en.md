# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The south generates heat, heat generates fire, fire generates bitterness, bitterness generates the heart, the heart generates blood, and blood generates the spleen. In heaven it is heat, on earth fire, in the body the vessels, in qi growth, among the depots the heart. Its nature is summer heat, its virtue manifestness, its function agitation, its color red, its transformation luxuriance, its creatures the feathered, its government brightness, its decree sultry steaming, its change blazing and melting, its calamity burning, its flavor bitter, its emotion joy. Joy harms the heart; fear overcomes joy. Heat harms qi; cold overcomes heat. Bitterness harms qi; saltiness overcomes bitterness.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The south generates heat"] It arises from abundant yang and is the government of the ministerial and sovereign fire. When the great void is dim and hazy like light dust and all mountains and rivers are so, this is the qi of heat. When the great brightness is not manifest and its color is like cinnabar, this is the qi of pent-up heat. When moving clouds suddenly rise, piled up like heaped leaves, now swelling, now shrinking, this is the heat of cliffs and valleys.

[On "heat generates fire"] It is the qi of extreme heat, the fire movement abundant and bright; hence "heat generates fire". Fire is the generation and transformation of abundant yang. When heat qi acts, there is blazing, sultry heat; at its extreme of change, burning, scorching, smelting and melting. When the movement falls on the years *guiyou*, *guiwei*, *guisi*, *guimao*, *guichou* and *guihai*, the transformation of heat is deficient; on *wuchen*, *wuyin*, *wuzi*, *wuxu*, *wushen* and *wuwu*, it is in surplus. Fire includes sovereign fire and ministerial fire; hence it says "heat generates fire" and also "fire".

[On "fire generates bitterness"] All things of bitter flavor originate from the generation and transformation of fire. Even sweet things become bitter when scorched by fire; that bitterness follows the transformation of fire can be verified thus.

[On "bitterness generates the heart"] Bitter things enter the stomach and transform into the heart. So in *gui* years the transformation of bitterness is little, in *wu* years much.

[On "the heart generates blood"] When the bitter flavor has transformed from the heart, it spreads and transforms to generate the blood vessels.

[On "blood generates the spleen"] When the bitter flavor has finished nourishing the blood, it flows and transforms from the blood to generate and nourish the spleen.

[On "In heaven it is heat"] This too is the qi transformed by spirit. Warmth, summer heat and sultry steaming are the transformation of heat; blazing and boiling are its function. When the year belongs to lesser yin or lesser yang above, heat transforms in heaven; below, heat moves on earth.

[On "on earth fire"] Shining, manifest and bright is the body of fire; burning, kindling and scorching is its function.

[On "in the body the vessels"] Making blood and qi flow is the body of the vessels; blocking, draining, depleting and filling are their function. The network vessels likewise.

[On "in qi growth"] 息 means growing.

[On "among the depots the heart"] The heart is shaped like an unopened lotus flower, with nine hollows inside that guide the qi of heavenly truth. It is the abode of spirit, the office of the sovereign, from which spirit brightness issues. In *gui* years the heart and its channels and network vessels receive the pathogen and fall ill; the small intestine bowel likewise.

[On "Its nature is summer heat"] 暑 means heat; this is the nature of heart qi.

[On "its virtue manifestness"] Clear and manifest, its image visible, fixed and graspable: this is the virtue of fire.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its virtue is manifest display".

[On "its function agitation"] The nature of fire is agitated movement, never settled.

[On "its color red"] Things that are generated and transformed riding on the transformation of fire all show an ocher or cinnabar color on the surface. Now plants in the southern lands all have some red. In *gui* years red things are mixed with black and white.

[On "its transformation luxuriance"] 茂 means flourishing abundance.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its transformation is flourishing luxuriance".

[On "its creatures the feathered"] Uneven, long and short, they image the form of fire.

[On "its government brightness"] Bright, shining, manifest and visible, with nothing hidden: this is the government of fire.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its government is bright shining". Also, the government of fire is brightness, and the qi of water is also brightness. Water and fire differ yet brightness is the same, because the brightness of fire illuminates the outside and that of water the inside; the brightness is the same but its reality differs.

[On "its decree sultry steaming"] 鬱 means abundant; 蒸 means heat; it means abundant hot qi like steam.
Song editors: On examination, the note's taking 鬱 as abundant is not sound. Wang Bing's note to the *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "鬱 means sultry and not freely flowing"; it should be explained thus.

[On "its change blazing and melting"] Extreme heat, blazing so that stones melt and metals flow: this is the extreme change of fire.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its change is smelting and melting".

[On "its calamity burning"] Burning mountains and rivers and spreading to houses is the disaster of fire.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its disaster is burning".

[On "its flavor bitter"] Things that transform and change and have bitter flavor are combined and dispersed by fire qi. Now things growing in the southern wilds are mostly bitter.

[On "its emotion joy"] 喜 means delight and pleasure; delight harmonizes the will.

[On "Joy harms the heart"] It speaks of excess. Joy arises from the heart and in turn harms the heart, just as wind snaps wood. Excess exhausts qi; hence harm.

[On "fear overcomes joy"] When fear arrives, joy and pleasure vanish; the principle of overcoming joy is evident at a glance. Fear is the qi of water.

[On "Heat harms qi"] When heaven is hot, qi hides and is unseen; when a person is hot, qi is hurried and panting. The principle that heat harms qi can also be verified. This all refers to great heat; the qi of small heat still generates the various qi. The *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* says "vigorous fire disperses qi; small fire generates qi"; this is the meaning.

[On "cold overcomes heat"] When cold prevails, heat recedes; when yin is abundant, yang declines. Controlling heat with cold is seeking to overcome.

[On "Bitterness harms qi"] Broadly it is so. Bitterness harms qi because it is drying. When heat is added to bitterness, the harm is greater still. How is this shown? Drinking wine hurries the breath, and much of it causes panting; this is the proof. Taking bitter-cold things one-sidedly for years increases fire all the more and also harms qi. If used briefly as a prescription for treatment, it is like small fire, which on the contrary generates qi.
Song editors: On examination, there are three senses of harm in this treatise. The east's "wind harms the liver, sourness harms the sinews", the center's "damp harms the flesh, sweetness harms the spleen", and the west's "acridity harms the skin and hair" are self-harm. The south's "heat harms qi, bitterness harms qi" and the north's "cold harms the blood, saltiness harms the blood" harm what 已 [oneself] overcomes. The west's "heat harms the skin and hair" is being overcome and harmed oneself. Thus there are three kinds of harm among the five directions. The *Taisu* calls them all self-harm.

[On "saltiness overcomes bitterness"] Wine is dissolved by saltiness; the principle is plain. The overcoming of fire's bitterness is controlled by water's saltiness.

## Notes

The south–heat–fire–heart series.
- 已 in the Song editors' 傷已所勝 and 被勝傷已 is read as 己 "oneself".
