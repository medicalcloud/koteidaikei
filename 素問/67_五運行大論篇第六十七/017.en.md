# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Earth is below humans, and it is in the midst of the great void."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Earth is below humans, and it is in the midst of the great void"] It means that as the place where humans dwell it may be called below, but verifying the supreme principle, it is merely one thing in the midst of the great void. The *Changes* says: "Kun is thick and carries things; its virtue accords with the boundless." This is what is meant.

## Notes

Earth is one thing in the midst of the great void.
