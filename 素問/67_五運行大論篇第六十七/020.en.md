# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus wind and cold are below, dryness and heat are above, damp qi is in the middle, and fire roams between them. Cold, heat and the others—six in all—enter, so that [the earth], though empty, generates and transforms.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Thus wind and cold are below ... generates and transforms"] Within the body of earth there are six that enter: first dryness, second summer heat, third wind, fourth damp, fifth cold, sixth fire. Receiving dryness, the nature of drying arises; receiving summer heat, the nature of steaming; receiving wind, the nature of moving; receiving damp, the nature of moistening; receiving cold, the nature of hardening; receiving fire, the nature of warming. These are called the six qi of heaven.

## Notes

The six qi enter earth and bring about generation and transformation.
