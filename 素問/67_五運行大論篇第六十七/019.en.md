# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The great qi holds it up. Dryness dries it, summer heat steams it, wind moves it, damp moistens it, cold hardens it, and fire warms it.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The great qi holds it up"] The great qi is the qi of creation that bears and holds up the great void. That the 大 [great] void does not collapse, and that earth endures and heaven lasts, is presumably because the qi of creation bears and holds them. If qi transformed and changed and did not bear and hold them, even the vessel of the great void would fail and 瓌 [collapse]. Falling leaves flying through the air do not drop quickly because they ride on qi, so their momentum cannot be swift. All things with form upon the earth are borne and held by the qi of generation and transformation. Vessels differ in size, and their 瓌 [collapse] is slower or quicker; but when the qi no longer bears and holds them, the collapse of large and small is the same.

## Notes

Earth is upheld by the great qi, on which the six qi act. Qibo's speech continues to unit 021.
- 瓌 in Wang Bing's note is read in the sense of 壊 "collapse".
