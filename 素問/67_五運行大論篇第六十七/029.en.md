# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How do cold, summer heat, dryness, damp, wind and fire correspond in humans? And in the myriad things, how do they generate and transform?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How do cold, summer heat ... how do they generate and transform?"] 合 means inner and outer corresponding; 生 means arising by receiving transformation; 化 means establishing the many images.

## Notes

A question on the correspondence of the six qi with humans and the myriad things.
