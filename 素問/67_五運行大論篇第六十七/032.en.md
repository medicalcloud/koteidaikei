# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The center generates damp, damp generates earth, earth generates sweetness, sweetness generates the spleen, the spleen generates the flesh, and the flesh generates the lungs. In heaven it is damp, on earth earth, in the body the flesh, in qi fullness, among the depots the spleen. Its nature is stillness and inclusion, its virtue moistening, its function transformation, its color yellow, its transformation fullness, its creatures the naked, its government tranquility, its decree clouds and rain, its change agitation and downpour, its calamity soaking rain and collapse, its flavor sweet, its emotion thought. Thought harms the spleen; anger overcomes thought. Damp harms the flesh; wind overcomes damp. Sweetness harms the spleen; sourness overcomes sweetness.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The center generates damp"] The center is earth. High mountain soil is damp and springs come out of the ground; at the source of waters in the mountain bends, clouds arise from rocky valleys; these are its images. Its nature is contained within; when it moves and functions, rain falls and clouds rise. That the center generates damp is readily credible. So the calendar's record of "the earth moist and sultry heat" in the sixth month refers to this.

[On "damp generates earth"] When damp qi is contained within, the body of earth is complete. When damp, earth lives; when dry, earth dies. When it dies, all kinds wither and perish; when it lives, the myriad things are nourished and flourish. This is the transformation of damp qi. When damp qi acts, earth settles, clouds rise and rain falls; at its extreme of change, there are sudden downpours and the earth collapses. When the movement falls on the years *jisi*, *jimao*, *jichou*, *jihai*, *jiyou* and *jiwei*, the transformation of damp is deficient; on *jiazi*, *jiaxu*, *jiashen*, *jiawu*, *jiachen* and *jiayin*, it is in surplus.

[On "earth generates sweetness"] All things of sweet flavor originate from the generation and transformation of earth.

[On "sweetness generates the spleen"] Sweet things enter the stomach and first enter the spleen. So in 巳 [*ji*] years the transformation of sweetness is little, in *jia* years much.

[On "the spleen generates the flesh"] Sweet flavor enters the spleen and, spreading and transforming from the spleen depot, grows and generates fat and flesh.

[On "the flesh generates the lungs"] When the sweet qi has 巳 [finished] nourishing the flesh, it flows and transforms from the flesh to generate and nourish the lung depot.

[On "In heaven it is damp"] It speaks of the transformation of spirit. Soft, moist, heavy and lustrous is the transformation of damp; gathering dust, clouds and rain is its function. When the year belongs to greater yin above, damp transforms in heaven; when greater yin is below, damp transforms on earth.

[On "on earth earth"] Solid, still, settled and calm, gathering and dispersing and taking form again, all kinds being generated by it: this is the body of earth. Containing dirt and hiding filth, still and lowly toward the people, the mother of change and transformation: this is the virtue of earth.
Song editors: On examination, the note says "still and lowly toward the people is the virtue of earth"; the sense of 下民 is presumably due to a scribal error.

[On "in the body the flesh"] Covering and wrapping the sinews and bones, with qi issuing between them, is the function of the flesh; untimely looseness or density and blockage between inner and outer are the movement of the flesh.

[On "in qi fullness"] When earth qi acts, all phenomena are full.

[On "among the depots the spleen"] Its shape resembles a horse's hoof and wraps the stomach duct inside, imaging the form of earth. The qi of the channels and network vessels intersect and return to the center, nourishing and circulating the qi of true spirit. It is the dwelling of intent, the office of the granaries, from which the transformation of things issues. In 巳 [*ji*] years the spleen and its channels and network vessels receive the pathogen and fall ill.
Song editors: On examination, the notes on the four depots liver, heart, lungs and kidneys each say "the bowel likewise", but only this note does not say "the stomach bowel likewise"; text is missing.

[On "Its nature is stillness and inclusion"] Inclusion means including the qi of cold, heat, warmth and coolness. The *Baihu tong* says "the spleen means combining", meaning that the four qi are combined in it.

[On "its virtue moistening"] Moist, damp, lustrous: the virtue of earth.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its virtue is sultry steaming".

[On "its function transformation"] Transformation means including the four other transformations and combining them with 已 [itself] to make five transformations—the so-called transformations of wind, heat, dryness and cold—circulating through the myriad things to make generation, growth, transformation, completion, gathering and storing.

[On "its color yellow"] When things ride on the transformation of earth, a yellow color shows on the surface. Now the 土 [tops] of plants in the central lands all have some yellow. In 已 [*ji*] years yellow things are mixed with green and black.

[On "its transformation fullness"] 盈 means full. Wherever the transformation of earth reaches, the myriad things are full.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its transformation is abundance and completeness".

[On "its creatures the naked"] Those with exposed skin and hide, without fur or shell.

[On "its government tranquility"] 謐 means still; the nature of earth is calm and still.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its government is calm and still". On examination, the government of earth is tranquility, and the government of excessive water is also tranquility, presumably because when water is excessive, earth receives it from below, so its government is also tranquil.

[On "its decree clouds and rain"] What the spreading transformation of damp qi brings about.

[On "its change agitation and downpour"] 動 is the opposite of stillness; when earth moves, earth loses its nature and wind shakes it without rest. 注 means rain falling for a long time; if long, walls and banks turn back into earth.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its change is sudden downpour".

[On "its calamity soaking rain and collapse"] 淫 means prolonged rain; 潰 means earth collapsing.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its disaster is incessant rain and collapse".

[On "its flavor sweet"] Things that transform and change and have sweet flavor are all the end and beginning of earth's transformation. Now things of the central plain are mostly sweet and bland.

[On "its emotion thought"] By thought one accomplishes tasks.
Song editors: The *Lingshu jing* says "keeping change in mind through will is called thought".

[On "Thought harms the spleen"] Thought labors the intellect; in excess it harms the spleen.

[On "anger overcomes thought"] When angry, one does not think; in anger one forgets misfortune; so the overcoming is clear. When thought is excessive and does not resolve, control it with anger; this is the way of regulating temperament.

[On "Damp harms the flesh"] Excessive damp becomes water; when water fills, there is swelling; when the water has drained away, the body and flesh are already wasted. The proof that it harms the flesh is near at hand.

[On "wind overcomes damp"] Wind is the qi of wood; hence it overcomes the damp of earth. When damp is excessive, control it with wind.

[On "Sweetness harms the spleen"] Exceeding measure.
Song editors: The *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* says "sweetness harms the flesh".

[On "sourness overcomes sweetness"] When sweetness is in surplus, control it with sourness, to rescue the spleen qi.

## Notes

The center–damp–earth–spleen series.
- Most instances of 巳 and 已 in Wang Bing's notes are read as 己 (the stem *ji*) or 已. 土 in 草木之土 is presumably an error for 上.
