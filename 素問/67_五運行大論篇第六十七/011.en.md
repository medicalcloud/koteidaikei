# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It is because what is below presides over what is above, not in its proper position."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is because what is below presides over what is above, not in its proper position"] When the six positions preside over each other, for example earth over fire, fire over wood, wood over water, water over metal, metal over earth, these are all below presiding over above, not in the proper position. In the relation of father and son, the son is below and the father above; is it not contravention for the son to preside over the father?

## Notes

Even in a generating relation, illness arises when the child presides over the mother.
