# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

So when dryness prevails, the earth is dry; when summer heat prevails, the earth is hot; when wind prevails, the earth moves; when damp prevails, the earth is muddy; when cold prevails, the earth cracks; when fire prevails, the earth becomes firm."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "So when dryness prevails ... the earth becomes firm"] The workings of the six qi.

## Notes

The changes of earth when each of the six qi prevails. Qibo's speech ends here.
