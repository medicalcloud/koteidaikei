# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The west generates dryness, dryness generates metal, metal generates acridity, acridity generates the lungs, the lungs generate the skin and hair, and the skin and hair generate the kidneys. In heaven it is dryness, on earth metal, in the body the skin and hair, in qi completion, among the depots the lungs. Its nature is coolness, its virtue clarity, its function firmness, its color white, its transformation contraction, its creatures the shelled, its government sharpness, its decree fog and dew, its change austere killing, its calamity falling while green, its flavor acrid, its emotion worry. Worry harms the lungs; joy overcomes worry. Heat harms the skin and hair; cold overcomes heat. Acridity harms the skin and hair; bitterness overcomes acridity.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The west generates dryness"] Yang qi has already descended and yin qi rises again; the air is crisp and the wind strong; hence dryness arises. When crags and valleys have green dust, river sources are deep green, mist floats over the plants and from afar all looks hazy, this is the transformation of dryness produced by metal qi. When a white haze rises at night, light as a faint mist, far and near one color, with stars and moon bright, this is the myriad things completed by yin, also the qi of white dew produced by metal qi. When the great void is dim with dust, the qi pent up yellow-black, the distance invisible, moving of itself without wind, passing from yin to yang, like clouds and like fog, this is killing qi, also the qi of frost produced by metal qi. When mountains, valleys, rivers and marshes are murky and dim like fog, the qi pent up and billowing, dreary and dismal so that one cannot see a foot ahead, this is killing qi about to act, also the qi of the movement produced by metal qi. When heaven rains in great downpours, a harmonious qi arises from the west, the clouds roll away, the sun shines and the great void is cleared: here the meaning of dryness arising in the west can be verified. If a west wind rises strongly, trees bend and clouds rise, this is dryness contending with damp without prevailing; so it will rain again. A west wind clearing rain is the constant qi of heaven. Even if an east wind stops the rain, a west wind will surely bring rain again, and through the rain it then clears of itself. Observing this behavior, qi has coming and going, movement has dryness and damp, and the images of change differ in function. From this, the qi of heaven and earth take harmony as superior; what bursts out violently and rushes suddenly is qi that does not prevail, and mostly becomes retaliation.

[On "dryness generates metal"] The qi is strong and the wind cutting, and metal rings with a far-carrying sound; the proof that dryness generates it can be seen and heard. This is the transformation of dryness making the myriad things firm and settled. Such is the action of dryness on things; at its extreme of change, heaven and earth are bleak, austere killing qi moves, people all fear it, and plants wither and fall. When the movement falls on *yichou*, *yimao*, *yisi*, *yiwei*, *yiyou* and *yihai* years, the transformation of dryness is deficient; on *gengzi*, *gengyin*, *gengchen*, *gengwu*, *gengshen* and *gengxu* years, it is in surplus. Year qi differ, so generation and transformation differ.

[On "metal generates acridity"] All things with acrid flavor originate from what the transformation of metal completes.

[On "acridity generates the lungs"] Acrid things enter the stomach and first enter the lungs. So in *yi* years the transformation of acridity is little, in *geng* years much.

[On "the lungs generate the skin and hair"] Acrid flavor enters the lungs and, spreading and transforming from the lung depot, generates and nourishes the skin and hair.

[On "the skin and hair generate the kidneys"] The acrid qi enters the skin and hair of itself, then flows and transforms to generate qi that enters the kidney depot.

[On "In heaven it is dryness"] The transformation of spirit. Clear, strong fog and dew are the transformation of dryness; austere killing and withering are its function. When the year belongs to *yangming* above, dryness transforms in heaven; when *yangming* is below, dryness moves on earth.

[On "on earth metal"] Following and changing, firm and hard, is the body of metal; blades, swords, spades and bindings are its function.
Song editors: Another edition writes 括 for 銛.

[On "in the body the skin and hair"] Soft, tough and enveloping is the body of skin and hair; letting fluids seep and drain is their function.

[On "in qi completion"] When things ride on the transformation of metal, they become firm and complete.

[On "among the depots the lungs"] The lungs are shaped like a person's shoulders, with two spreading lobes and several small lobes, and within them twenty-four hollows in rows to distribute the clear and turbid qi of the depots. They govern storing the corporeal soul, are the office of the minister-mentor, from which regulation issues. In *yi* years the lungs and their channels and network vessels receive the pathogen and fall ill; the large intestine bowel likewise.

[On "Its nature is coolness"] 涼 means clear-cool; this is the nature of the lungs.

[On "its virtue clarity"] Metal takes clarity and coolness as its virtue and transformation.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its virtue is clarity and purity".

[On "its function firmness"] 固 means firm and settled.

[On "its color white"] When things ride on the transformation of metal, their coverings show a plain white color. Now the plants of the western wilds all have some white. In *yi* years white things are mixed with red and green.

[On "its transformation contraction"] 斂 means gathering in. When the transformation of metal flows, the bodies of things become firm and contracted.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its transformation is tightening and contraction". On examination, the transformation of metal is contraction, and the qi of deficient wood is also contraction, presumably because when wood is deficient, metal overcomes it, hence contraction.

[On "its creatures the shelled"] 介 means shell. Covered outside with shells, they image the firmness of metal.

[On "its government sharpness"] 勁 means sharp in front.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its government is sharp and cutting".

[On "its decree fog and dew"] Generated by the transformation of cool qi.

[On "its change austere killing"] When heaven and earth are bleak and people take no pleasure, that is its qi.

[On "its calamity falling while green"] Drying while still green and withering and falling.

[On "its flavor acrid"] Things that transform and change and have acrid flavor are where metal qi separates and combines. Now the plants of the western wilds are mostly acrid.

[On "its emotion worry"] 憂 means deliberation and thought.
Song editors: On examination, 主 [Wang]'s note taking worry as thought harms the sense. In this treatise thought is the emotion of the spleen and worry that of the lungs; clearly worry is not thought. Also the *Lingshu jing* says "with anxious worry, [qi] is blocked and does not move" and "anxious worry that does not resolve harms intent". Thus worry is anxiety, not thought.

[On "Worry harms the lungs"] With anxious worry, qi is blocked and does not move; the lungs store qi; hence worry harms the lungs.

[On "joy overcomes worry"] When the spirit is pleased there is joy; hence joy overcomes worry.

[On "Heat harms the skin and hair"] Fire has two kinds; hence the outward signs of harm by heat are cited again here. When fire qi presses and scorches, things are scorched and dry; hence when heat qi is abundant, skin and hair are harmed.

[On "cold overcomes heat"] Yang is dispelled by yin; hence cold overcomes heat.
Song editors: The *Taisu* writes "dryness harms the skin and hair; heat overcomes dryness".

[On "Acridity harms the skin and hair"] Exceeding measure; acrid and hot things are worse still.

[On "bitterness overcomes acridity"] Bitter is the flavor of fire; hence it overcomes the acridity of metal.

## Notes

The west–dryness–metal–lungs series.
- 主注 in the Song editors' note is presumably an error for 王注. 欎 is a variant of 鬱.
