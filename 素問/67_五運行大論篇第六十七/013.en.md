# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "What is above moves right; what is below moves left. Left and right circle heaven, with a surplus, and meet again."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What is above moves right ... and meet again"] Above is heaven, below is earth. "Circle heaven" means heaven circling the positions of earth's five phases. Heaven lets down the six qi, earth spreads the five phases. Heaven, following earth, revolves to the left; earth, receiving heaven, turns east. After the wood movement, heaven's qi always has a surplus; the surplus qi is not added to the sovereign fire but steps back one step to preside over the ministerial fire. So every five years it retreats one position and shifts right; hence "left and right circle heaven, with a surplus, and meet again". 会 means meeting and combining. It means that in the way of heaven and earth, whenever five years are completed, the surplus qi shifts and is added, meeting and combining again with the seats of the five phases to make the rule of the years. "Circle heaven" means heaven circling the positions of earth, not circling the six qi of heaven.

## Notes

The controller of heaven moves right and the spring in earth moves left, meeting again through the mismatch of the five- and six-year cycles.
