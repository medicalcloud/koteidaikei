# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Is earth below or not?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Is earth below or not?"] It asks whether, since it turns and does not stay, it is below or not.

## Notes

A question on whether earth is below.
