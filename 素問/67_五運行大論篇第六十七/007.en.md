# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Above and below are where the yin and yang that appear above and below in a year are located. As for left and right: whenever *jueyin* appears above, the left is lesser yin and the right greater yang; when lesser yin appears, the left is greater yin and the right *jueyin*; when greater yin appears, the left is lesser yang and the right lesser yin; when lesser yang appears, the left is *yangming* and the right greater yin; when *yangming* appears, the left is greater yang and the right lesser yang; when greater yang appears, the left is *jueyin* and the right *yangming*. This is what is called naming the positions facing north and stating what appears."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "This is what is called naming the positions facing north and stating what appears"] It is said facing north: above is south, below is north, left is west, right is east.

## Notes

The left and right intervening qi of the controller of heaven.
- 太 in 右太陰 is written 大 in the base text and was corrected from the other sources (see the emendation).
