# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The north generates cold, cold generates water, water generates saltiness, saltiness generates the kidneys, the kidneys generate the bone marrow, and the marrow generates the liver. In heaven it is cold, on earth water, in the body the bones, in qi hardness, among the depots the kidneys. Its nature is chill, its virtue cold, its function [missing], its color black, its transformation solemnity, its creatures the scaled, its government stillness, its decree [missing], its change congealing frost, its calamity ice and hail, its flavor salty, its emotion fear. Fear harms the kidneys; thought overcomes fear. Cold harms the blood; dryness overcomes cold. Saltiness harms the blood; sweetness overcomes saltiness.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The north generates cold"] Yang qi hides and yin qi rises; its government spreads and prevails; hence cold arises. When the great void is clear and pure, black qi floats in the sky and the sky is dark, this is the cold qi of the high air. If the qi is like scattered hemp, black from root to tip, faintly visible, this is the cold qi of rivers and marshes. When the great void is clear and white, the sky as if reflecting snow, far and near one color, this is the cold qi of mountains and valleys. When the great void is white and dim, the brightness of fire unclouded, with a qi like misty rain, far and near austere, the north dark in color and congealed mist falling at night, this is the transformation of cold produced by water qi. When the great void is congealed in yin, white dust dims and clouds it, heaven and earth one color, the distance indistinguishable, this is cold and damp congealing and snow about to arrive. When the ground cracks and water freezes, rivers and canals dry up, salt floats on withered marshes, trees contract and soil is hard, this is earth overcoming water so that water cannot clear itself, the function of cold produced by water.

[On "cold generates water"] Cold assists the transformation of yin, from which water arises; this is the generation and transformation of cold qi. When cold qi acts, water freezes into snow and mist; at its extreme of change, water dries up and ice is hard. When the movement falls on *bingyin*, *bingzi*, *bingxu*, *bingshen*, *bingwu* and *bingchen* years, the transformation of cold prevails greatly; on *xinwei*, *xinsi*, *xinmao*, *xinchou*, *xinhai* and *xinyou* years, it is little.

[On "water generates saltiness"] All things of salty flavor originate from what the transformation of water forms and binds. When waters and marshes dry up, brine and salt abound; the ocean tastes salty; salt follows the transformation of water and so is salty; that it is produced from water is evident; boiled water tastes salty, as can be seen near at hand.

[On "saltiness generates the kidneys"] Salty things enter the stomach and first return to the kidneys. So in *bing* years salty things transform much, in *xin* years little.

[On "the kidneys generate the bone marrow"] Salty flavor enters the kidneys and, spreading and transforming from the kidney depot, generates and nourishes the bone marrow.

[On "the marrow generates the liver"] The salty qi generates the bone marrow of itself, then flows and transforms to generate qi that enters the liver depot.

[On "In heaven it is cold"] The transformation of spirit. Congealing, dismal ice and snow are the transformation of cold; biting frost and hail are its function. When the year belongs to greater yang above, cold transforms in heaven; when greater yang is below, cold moves on earth.

[On "on earth water"] When yin qi spreads and transforms and flows into the ground, it becomes springs. Clear and flowing abundantly is the body of water; sweeping away and drowning is its function.

[On "in the body the bones"] A strong, firm trunk is the body of bone; enclosing the marrow and brain is its function.

[On "in qi hardness"] Soft, pliant things become hard on meeting cold; this is the transformation of cold.

[On "among the depots the kidneys"] The kidney depot is two, shaped like cowpeas side by side, curving and attached to the paraspinal sinews. Outside is a wrapping of fat, white inside and black outside. They govern storing essence, are the office of forceful action, from which skill issues. In *xin* years the kidney depot and its channels and network vessels receive the pathogen and fall ill; the bladder bowel likewise.

[On "Its nature is chill"] 凜 means cold; this is the nature of the kidneys.

[On "its virtue cold"] Water takes cold as its virtue and transformation.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its virtue is chill and cold".

[On "its function [missing]"] Originally missing.

[On "its color black"] When things receive water and are formed, they are covered with a dark black color on the surface. Now the plants of the 比 [northern] wilds all have some black. In *xin* years black things are mixed with yellow and red.

[On "its transformation solemnity"] 粛 means still.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its transformation is clear and tranquil". On examination, why is the transformation of water solemnity, and the government of excessive metal also solemnity, the government of balanced metal sharp solemnity, and the change of metal austere killing? Presumably the 粛 of water's transformation is solemn stillness, while the 粛 of metal's government is austere killing; the words are the same but the matters differ.

[On "its creatures the scaled"] Scaled means the kinds of fish and snakes.

[On "its government stillness"] The nature of water is clear, pure and still.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its government is congealing solemnity". On examination, the government of water is stillness, and the government of balanced earth is calm stillness, that of excessive earth also stillness, that of deficient earth also settled stillness. Water and earth differ and yet stillness is the same; but they are not the same: the stillness of water is clear purity, that of earth calm quiet.

[On "its decree [missing]"] Originally missing.

[On "its change congealing frost"] Because the cold is extreme.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its change is biting cold".

[On "its calamity ice and hail"] Occurring out of season, or excessively violent.
Song editors: The *Qijiaobian dalun* says "its disaster is ice, snow, frost and hail".

[On "its flavor salty"] Things that transform and change and have salty flavor are where the transformation of water congeals and disperses. Now the rivers and marshes of the north are mostly salt flats.

[On "its emotion fear"] By fear one keeps misfortune at a distance.

[On "Fear harms the kidneys"] When fear is extreme and stirs the center, it harms the kidneys. The *Lingshu jing* says "fear and dread that do not resolve harm the essence". The kidneys store essence; so the essence is harmed and the harm reaches the kidneys.

[On "thought overcomes fear"] With thought one sees the springs of misfortune, so there is no worry or fear. Writing 憂 for 思, as one version does, is wrong.

[On "Cold harms the blood"] It makes clear that [water] overcomes the heart. When cold is extreme, blood congeals; hence it harms the blood.

[On "dryness overcomes cold"] When cold transforms, water accumulates; when dryness acts, things become hard. Dryness and cold combine, so they overcome each other. This is the transformation of heaven and earth and the constant principle of things.

[On "Saltiness harms the blood"] When the flavor is too salty, the throat is dry and one craves drink; the sense of harming the blood can be known for certain.

[On "sweetness overcomes saltiness"] Thirsty, one drinks from a sweet spring and the dry throat stops of itself. Sweet is the flavor of earth; hence it overcomes the saltiness of water.
Song editors: On examination, from "Qibo said" above to here agrees with the *Yinyang yingxiang dalun*, with small additions and omissions, but the notes differ considerably.

## Notes

The north–cold–water–kidneys series. In the base text no character follows 其用為 or 其令, and Wang Bing's note also says "originally missing".
- Other sources read 其用為蔵 and 其令霰雪, or note 闕一字 and 闕二字 (see the variants). The translation marks them [missing].
- 比方 in Wang Bing's note is presumably an error for 北方.
