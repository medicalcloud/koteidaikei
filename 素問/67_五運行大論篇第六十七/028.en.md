# 素問 五運行大論篇第六十七 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Following its qi brings harmony; going against its qi brings illness. When it is not in its position, there is illness; when the positions are exchanged, there is illness; when it loses and abandons its position, there is danger; when *chi* and *cun* are reversed, there is death; when yin and yang cross, there is death. First establish the year to know its qi and what appears in response on left and right; only then can one speak of the contrary and compliant of life and death."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Following its qi brings harmony; going against its qi brings illness"] This means cases such as should be sunken but is not, should be floating but is not, should be rough but is not, should be hooked but is not, should be stringlike but is not, should be large but is not.
Song editors: The *Zhizhen yao dalun* says: "When *jueyin* arrives, the pulse is stringlike; when lesser yin arrives, hooked; when greater yin arrives, sunken; when lesser yang arrives, large and floating; when *yangming* arrives, short and rough; when greater yang arrives, 太 [large] and long. Arriving in harmony is normal; arriving excessively is illness; arriving contrary is illness; arriving but not coming is illness; coming before it should is illness; when yin and yang are exchanged, there is danger."

[On "When it is not in its position, there is illness"] It appears in another position.

[On "when the positions are exchanged, there is illness"] It means the right pulse appearing on the left and the left on the right; the qi are mismatched.

[On "when it loses and abandons its position, there is danger"] It has already appeared in another place, and in its own palace the qi of a destroyer appears; hence illness and danger.

[On "when *chi* and *cun* are reversed, there is death"] This occurs in the four years *zi*, *wu*, *mao* and *you*. Reversal means that when the year is yin and [the response] should be in the *cun* pulse, it appears instead at the *chi*; when the year is yang and should be at the *chi*, the pulse appears instead at the *cun*. Only when *chi* and *cun* are both so is it called reversal. If only the *chi* or only the *cun* is so, it is failure to respond to the qi, not reversal.

[On "when yin and yang cross, there is death"] This occurs in the eight years *yin*, *shen*, *si*, *hai*, *chou*, *wei*, *chen* and *xu*. Crossing means that when the year is yin and should be on the right, the pulse appears on the left; when the year is yang and should be on the left, the pulse appears on the right. Appearing crossed between left and right is called crossing. If only the left or only the right is so, it is failure to respond to the qi, not crossing.

[On "First establish the year ... the contrary and compliant of life and death"] The year qi spoken of in the classic is complete here.
Song editors: On examination, this is given in full in the *Liuyuan zhengji dalun*.

## Notes

Judging life and death by how the pulse on left and right, *chi* and *cun*, responds to the year qi.
