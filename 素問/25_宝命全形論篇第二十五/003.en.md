# 素問 宝命全形論篇第二十五 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I think of their pain, and my heart is confused and perplexed by it. If [treatment] instead aggravates the illness, it cannot be undone. If the people hear of it, they will regard it as cruel and injurious. What should be done?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If the people hear of it, they will regard it as cruel and injurious. What should be done?"] 残 means to harm; 賊 means to injure and rob. It says he fears straying into inhumanity and causing resentment among the common people.

## Notes

Huangdi worries that treatment that cuts the skin and harms the flesh will seem cruel to the people.
- 不可更代 is taken as "it cannot be undone"; other readings are possible, such as "one cannot take [the patient's] place".
