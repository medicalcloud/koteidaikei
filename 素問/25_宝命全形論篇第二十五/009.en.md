# 素問 宝命全形論篇第二十五 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How is it deficient? How is it excess?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How is it deficient? How is it excess?"] It says: if the blood and qi lie low like a crossbow laid flat and rise like a released trigger, how can their deficiency and excess be fixed by a standard of retaining [the needle] for a number of breaths? How is the form of deficiency and excess to be summarized?

## Notes

Huangdi asks how to discern deficiency and excess.
- 㽞 in Wang Bing's note is a variant of 留, and 凖 of 準.
