# 素問 宝命全形論篇第二十五 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Humans are born with form and are not apart from yin and yang. Heaven and earth combine their qi, separate into the nine fields and divide into the four seasons; months are short or long, days short or long; the myriad things arrive together and cannot be fully measured. As to depletion and repletion, yawning and sighing, I venture to ask the method."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The Emperor said ... I venture to ask the method"] He asks to be told the meaning of using the needle.

## Notes

Amid the immeasurable changes of heaven and earth, the four seasons and the myriad things, Huangdi asks how to distinguish depletion and repletion and use the needle.
