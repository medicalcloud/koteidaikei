# 素問 宝命全形論篇第二十五 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Humans are born on earth and their fate hangs on heaven; heaven and earth combine their qi, and this is called a human. One who can respond to the four seasons has heaven and earth as father and mother; one who knows the myriad things is called a son of heaven. Heaven has yin and yang; humans have the twelve nodes. Heaven has cold and heat; humans have depletion and repletion. One who can constantly [follow] the transformations of yin and yang of heaven and earth does not miss the four seasons; one who knows the principle of the twelve nodes cannot be deceived even by the sage and wise. One who can keep in mind the changes of the eight movements and the alternate rise of the five overcomings, and can penetrate the numbers of depletion and repletion, goes out alone and comes in alone; [he perceives even] the subtlest yawn or sigh, and an autumn hair is [as if] before his eyes."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Humans are born on earth ... this is called a human"] The body is formed by borrowing things, so it is born on earth; fate is endowed only by heaven, so it hangs on heaven. Virtue and qi converge together; hence it is called a human. The *Lingshu jing* says, "What heaven has placed in me is virtue; what earth has placed in me is qi. Life arises when virtue flows and qi presses." Virtue is the function of the Way; qi is the mother of life.

[On "One who can respond to the four seasons has heaven and earth as father and mother"] When a person can respond to the harmonious qi of the four seasons and nourish life, heaven and earth always rear and nourish him; hence they are father and mother. The *Siqi tiaoshen dalun* (chapter 2) says, "The yin and yang of the four seasons are the root of the myriad things. Thus the sage nourishes yang in spring and summer and yin in autumn and winter, following its root; therefore he sinks and floats with the myriad things at the gate of generation and growth."

[On "one who knows the myriad things is called a son of heaven"] One who knows the root of the myriad things is always reared and nourished by heaven and earth; hence he is called a son of heaven.

[On "Heaven has yin and yang; humans have the twelve nodes"] "Nodes" means the solar terms; outwardly they correspond to the twelve months, inwardly they govern the twelve channel vessels.

[On "Heaven has cold and heat; humans have depletion and repletion"] Cold and heat have their cycle of abundance and decline; depletion and repletion show differences of more and less. Hence humans respond to heaven's cold and heat with depletion and repletion.

[On "One who can constantly [follow] ... cannot be deceived even by the sage and wise"] 経 means constant. It says that one who constantly responds to and follows the way of yin and yang of heaven and earth in cultivating and nourishing [life] accords with what suits the generation and growth of the four seasons, and one who can know where the qi of the twelve nodes shifts and arrives is not deceived or slighted even by the sage and wise, who [instead] uphold and practise it.

[On "One who can keep in mind ... an autumn hair is [as if] before his eyes"] 存 means keep in mind; 達 means understand clearly. 呿 means yawning; 吟 means sighing. "An autumn hair before his eyes" means that even the finest things are surely perceived. "The eight movements" means the changes of the winds of the eight nodal days; "the five overcomings" means the mutual overcoming of the qi of the five phases; "rise" means the time when each dominates; "changes" means the alteration when the qi arrives. Knowing these three, the responses and effects are manifest and as swift as shadow and echo. All this is the spirit going out alone and coming in alone, not something ghosts or spirits can summon or dispatch.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says, "呿 means baring the teeth and letting out breath."

## Notes

Humans are born from the combined qi of heaven and earth, and one who knows how to respond to the four seasons and yin and yang can perceive illness.
