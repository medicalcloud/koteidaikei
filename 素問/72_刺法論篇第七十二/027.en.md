# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suppose that in a *gengchen* [year] the hard and soft lose their hold: when the upper position loses its hold, the lower position has nothing to match. *Yi* and *geng* make the metal movement, but therefore they do not summon each other; [the previous controller] spreads in heaven and has not withdrawn, and the middle movement comes to prevail; upper and lower are at odds. This is called losing hold. In Guxian and Linzhong [pitches], the *shang* note does not respond. In such a case the transformation of heaven's movement is altered, and in three years it changes into a great epidemic. Examining heaven's numbers, the deviation is indeed slight or severe: if slight, it arrives slightly after three years; if severe, severely after three years. First supplement the liver transport point, then after three days needle where the lung moves. When the needling is finished, keep the spirit quiet for seven days and beware of great anger; anger will make the true qi withdraw and scatter. Again, when in the lower position, the earth's *jiazi* [cycle], *yiwei* loses its hold, the soft stem *yi* [fails] and the upper *geng* governs alone; this too is called losing hold. The movement of heaven governs alone, and in three years it changes into pestilence, called metal pestilence; its arrival waits for its time. Examine the gradations of earth's numbers and infer slightness or severity, and the speed can be known. Whenever *yi* and *geng* lose their hold in any position, the needling method is the same. To keep the liver calm, do not be angry.

## Notes

The epidemic, needling and self-cultivation when the hard and soft lose their positions in a *gengchen* year (metal movement). The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
