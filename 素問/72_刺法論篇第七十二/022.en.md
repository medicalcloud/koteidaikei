# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the qi passes its time in surplus and spreads again in the proper [position], this is called not withdrawing from position. Earth's qi cannot transform afterward and the new controller of heaven cannot yet shift to its proper position, so the transforming decrees are spread again as before. In *si* and *hai* years, when heaven's numbers are in surplus, *jueyin* does not withdraw from position: wind moves above and wood's transformation spreads in heaven; one should needle where foot *jueyin* enters. In *zi* and *wu* years, when heaven's numbers are in surplus, lesser yin does not withdraw from position: heat moves above and the residual transformation of fire spreads in heaven; one should needle where hand *jueyin* enters. In *chou* and *wei* years, when heaven's numbers are in surplus, greater yin does not withdraw from position: damp moves above and the transformation of rain spreads in heaven; one should needle where foot greater yin enters. In *yin* and *shen* years, when heaven's numbers are in surplus, lesser yang does not withdraw from position: heat moves above and fire's transformation spreads in heaven; one should needle where hand lesser yang enters. In *mao* and *you* years, when heaven's numbers are in surplus, *yangming* does not withdraw from position: metal moves above and the transformation of dryness spreads in heaven; one should needle where hand greater yin enters. In *chen* and *xu* years, when heaven's numbers are in surplus, greater yang does not withdraw from position: cold moves above and the transformation of chilling water spreads in heaven; one should needle where foot lesser yin enters. Thus when the qi of heaven and earth go counter and transform into people's illnesses, needle them by these methods and the sickness can be calmed in advance."

## Notes

The answer describing the six cases in which the previous controller of heaven does not withdraw, and the uniting (entering) points to needle in each. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
