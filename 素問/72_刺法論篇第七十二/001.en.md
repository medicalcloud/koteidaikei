# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked, "That when ascent and descent do not advance and there are changes in the meeting of qi, violent constraint results, I already know. How can living beings be rescued in advance, so that [the harm] may be averted?"

## Notes

A question about how to rescue people in advance from the constraint caused by ascent and descent failing to advance. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
