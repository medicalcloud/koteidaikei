# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "The arrival of the five movements may be early or late, and with ascent and descent, going and coming, there is something that receives and represses them. May I hear the methods of needling for this?"

## Notes

A question about needling for early or late arrival of the five movements and related matters. There are no notes.
