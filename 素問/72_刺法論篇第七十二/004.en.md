# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When ascent does not advance, there is severe misfortune.

## Notes

The beginning of the account of needling when ascent does not advance. Qibo's speech continues through unit 9. There are no notes.
- The base (Wikisource plain text) 期凶 is emended to 甚凶 following the Daizhige texts (see the emendation note).
