# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo bowed his head to the ground twice and said, "A clear question! When the spirit moves and loses its hold, as long as it remains in the body there is no death; but if pathogens invade, it causes premature death. For example, when *jueyin* loses its hold, heaven is depleted; when a person's qi has liver depletion and responds to heaven, there is double depletion, and the ethereal soul wanders upward. When pathogens invade and the great qi reverses, if the body is still warm one can needle: 制 [needle] where foot lesser yang passes, then needle the liver transport point. When a person suffers heart depletion and also meets the loss of hold of the 群 [sovereign] and ministerial fires controlling heaven, responding to it there is triple depletion; when it meets insufficiency of fire, the black corpse-ghost violates him and causes sudden death. Needle where hand lesser yang passes, and then the heart transport point. When a person has spleen illness and also meets the loss of hold of greater yin controlling heaven, responding to it there is triple depletion; and when it meets insufficiency of earth, the blue-green corpse-ghost pathogen violates him and causes sudden death. Needle where foot *yangming* passes, and then the spleen transport point. When a person has lung illness and meets the loss of hold of *yangming* controlling heaven, responding to it there is triple depletion; and when it meets insufficiency of metal, the red corpse-ghost violates him and causes sudden death. Needle where hand *yangming* passes, and then the lung transport point. When a person has kidney illness and also meets the loss of hold of greater yang controlling heaven, responding to it there is triple depletion; and when it meets a year of insufficiency of the water movement, the yellow corpse-ghost invades the person's proper qi, sucks out his spirit and ethereal soul, and causes sudden death. Needle where foot greater yang passes, and then the kidney transport point."

## Notes

The answer describing five cases in which corpse-ghosts violate a person in "triple depletion" (depletion of a viscus, loss of hold of the controller of heaven, insufficiency of the movement), and needling the source (passing) points of the paired yang channels and the back transport points. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 制 in 制其足少陽之所過 in the main text is read as 刺 ("needle"), and 群 in 群相二火 as 君 ("sovereign").
