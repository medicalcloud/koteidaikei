# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "On failure to shift to the proper position I have grasped the essentials. I wish to hear about failure to withdraw: I wish to break its surplus so that there is no error. Can you make it clear?"

## Notes

A question about the previous controller of heaven failing to withdraw. There are no notes.
