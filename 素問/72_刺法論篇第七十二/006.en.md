# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When fire is about to ascend and Tianpeng blocks and represses it, fire seeks to break out of its constraint but must still wait for its time. For both sovereign fire and ministerial fire, needle the brook point of the [heart] enclosing network.

## Notes

The case of fire unable to ascend. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
