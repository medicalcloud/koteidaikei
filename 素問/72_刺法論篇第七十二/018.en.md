# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "One should take from its source of transformation. Hence the excessive is taken from, the insufficient is aided. For the excessive, take from it, then repress its constraint, take from the source of transformation of its movement, and so break the constrained qi. For the insufficient, support and aid it, so as to support the qi of the movement and avoid depletion pathogens. The methods of aiding and taking are set out in the *Secret Words*."

## Notes

The answer giving the principle: take from the excessive, aid the insufficient. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 密語 is a book title, presumably referring to the *Xuanzhu miyu*, as 注玄珠密語云 appears in the text of chapter 73 and in the notes of the Daizhige *Huangdi neijing suwen yipian*.
