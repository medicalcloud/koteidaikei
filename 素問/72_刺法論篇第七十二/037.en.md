# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The spleen is the office of the remonstrating counselor; comprehensive knowledge comes from it. One may needle the source point of the spleen. The stomach is the office of the granaries; the five flavors come from it. One may needle the source point of the stomach. The large intestine is the office of conveyance; transformation comes from it. One may needle the source point of the large intestine. The small intestine is the office of reception; the transformation of things comes from it. One may needle the source point of the small intestine. The kidneys are the office of vigor; skill and ingenuity come from them. Needle the source point of the kidneys. The triple burner is the office of the drainage channels; the waterways come from it. Needle the source point of the triple burner. The bladder is the office of the regional capital; fluids are stored in it, and when qi transforms they can come out. Needle the source point of the bladder.

## Notes

The continuation of the twelve offices. Unlike chapter 8 ("the spleen and stomach are the office of the granaries"), the spleen and stomach are listed separately. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
