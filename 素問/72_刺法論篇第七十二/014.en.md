# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When earth is about to descend and Dicang blocks and represses it, it descends but does not go down; repressed, the constraint breaks out, and when it disperses it can enter. Break what overcomes it, and the constraint can be dispersed: needle where foot *jueyin* emerges and where foot lesser yang enters.

## Notes

The case of earth unable to descend. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
