# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked, "When the two stems, hard and soft, lose hold of their positions, do they make the qi of heaven and of the movement both depleted? They make people ill; can this be calmed?"

## Notes

A question about the hard and soft stems losing their positions. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
