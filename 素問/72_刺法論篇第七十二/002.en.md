# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo bowed his head to the ground twice and replied, "A clear question! I have heard my Master say, 'Having understood the heavenly origin, one must master the methods of needling, by which one can break constraint and support the movement, supplement the weak and preserve the true, drain the abundant and remove the surplus, so as to rid [people] of this suffering.'"

## Notes

The answer that the methods of needling can remove constraint. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
