# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suppose that in a *renwu* [year] the hard and soft lose their hold: the upper *ren* has not come near its proper position, and the lower *ding* alone is so. Then even in a yang year it is deficient and does not reach [excess], unlike [the usual]. Upper and lower lose their hold, and their summoning each other has its time; the slightness or severity of the deviation each has its number. The two *jue* notes of the pitch-pipes are lost and out of harmony; there are days with the same note; when slightness or severity becomes visible, in three years there is a great epidemic. Needle the spleen transport point, then after three days needle where the liver emerges. When the needling is finished, keep the spirit quiet for seven days; do not get very drunk or sing and make merry, [or] the qi will scatter again. Also do not overeat or eat raw things. To make the spleen replete, let the qi not stagnate with fullness; do not sit long; let food not be too sour; eat nothing raw at all; sweet and bland foods are suitable. Again, when in the earth's lower *jiazi* [cycle] *dingyou* loses its position and has not obtained control of the middle, the qi does not match its position and the lower does not join with *ren*; this too is called losing hold, and is not called uniting virtue. Since the soft does not attach to the hard, earth and movement do not match, and in three years it changes into pestilence. Its needling method is the same as that for wood epidemics.

## Notes

The epidemic, needling and self-cultivation when the hard and soft lose their positions in a *renwu* year (wood movement). The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
