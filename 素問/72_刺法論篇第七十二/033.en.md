# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked, "When a person is depleted, the spirit wanders and loses hold of its position, letting ghosts and spirits invade from outside and causing early death. How can the true be preserved? I wish to hear the needling methods."

## Notes

A question about needling when the spirit loses its position and ghosts invade. There are no notes.
