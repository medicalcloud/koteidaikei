# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

These twelve offices must not lose one another. Therefore the needling methods have the purpose of preserving the spirit and nourishing the true, and there is also a way of cultivating the true; they are not merely for treating disease. Hence the key is to cultivate and harmonize the spirit. The Way values constant preservation. If the spirit is supplemented and the root made firm, the essential qi does not scatter, and the spirit guards without dividing, then since the spirit guards and does not depart, the true can be preserved. If a person's spirit does not guard, the utmost truth is not reached. The key to the utmost truth lies in the heavenly mystery: the spirit guards, and the heavenly breath returns and enters the original source. This is called returning to the ancestor."

## Notes

The conclusion of the chapter: needling is not only for treating disease but also a way of preserving the spirit and nourishing the true. Qibo's speech ends here. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
