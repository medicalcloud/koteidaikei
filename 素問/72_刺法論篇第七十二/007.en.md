# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When earth is about to ascend and Tianchong blocks and represses it, earth seeks to break out of its constraint but must still wait for its time. One should needle the stream point of foot greater yin.

## Notes

The case of earth unable to ascend. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
