# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When metal is about to ascend and Tianying blocks and represses it, metal seeks to break out of its constraint but must still wait for its time. One should needle the river point of hand greater yin.

## Notes

The case of metal unable to ascend. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
