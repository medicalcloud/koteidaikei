# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When metal is about to descend and Ditong blocks and represses [it], it descends but does not go down; repressed, the constraint breaks out, and when it disperses it can enter. Break what overcomes it, and the constraint can be dispersed: needle where the heart enclosing network emerges, and 制 [needle] where hand lesser yang enters.

## Notes

The case of metal unable to descend. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 制 in 制手少陽所入 in the main text is read as 刺 ("needle") from the parallel phrases.
