# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When water is about to descend and Difu blocks and represses it, it descends but does not go down; repressed, the constraint breaks out, and when it disperses it can enter. Break its earth, and the constraint can be dispersed: needle where foot greater yin emerges and where foot *yangming* enters."

## Notes

The case of water unable to descend. Qibo's speech ends here. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
