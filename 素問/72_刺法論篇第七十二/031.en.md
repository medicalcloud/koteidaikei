# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I have heard that when the five epidemics arrive, they all 梁 [infect] and pass between people, and young and old alike have similar symptoms. Without applying treatment, how can one avoid their passing from one to another?"

## Notes

A question about how to avoid contagion. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 梁 in 相梁易 in the main text is read as 染 ("infect"), from 不相染者 in the next unit.
