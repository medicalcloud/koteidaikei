# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I have heard that when the five epidemics arrive, they all infect and pass between people, and young and old alike have similar symptoms. Without applying treatment, how can one avoid their passing from one to another?"

## Notes

A question about how to avoid contagion. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- The base (Wikisource plain text) 相梁易 is emended to 相染易 following the Daizhige texts (see the emendation note).
