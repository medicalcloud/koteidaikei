# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo bowed his head to the ground twice and said, "What a thorough question! It is the ultimate principle and the true essence of the Way; who but a sage emperor could explore this source? This is called qi and spirit uniting with the Way and matching heaven above. The heart is the office of the sovereign; spirit illumination comes from it. One may needle the source point of hand lesser yin. The lungs are the office of the minister and mentor; regulation comes from them. One may needle the source point of hand greater yin. The liver is the office of the general; planning comes from it. One may needle the source point of foot *Jueyin*. The gallbladder is the office of the upright and correct; decisions come from it. One may needle the source point of foot lesser yang. The 羶 [*dan*] center is the office of the envoy; joy and pleasure come from it. One may needle where the heart enclosing network flows.

## Notes

The answer: needle the source point for each of the twelve offices (the same text as chapter 8, *Linglan midian lun*). Qibo's speech continues through unit 38. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- In the base (Wikisource plain text), 謀虚 is emended to 謀慮 and 不官 to 之官, following the Daizhige texts and chapter 8 (see the emendation notes). 羶中 is read as 膻中.
