# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus I have set up five years of the earth to make clear the loss of hold and to explore the needling methods fully. Epidemic and pestilence are names for the hard and soft above and below; examined to the end, they return to one body. There are only five methods of needling epidemics; they sum up the loss of hold in all positions, and so simply return to the five phases and are unified by them."

## Notes

Epidemic (loss by the upper hard) and pestilence (loss by the lower soft) are one, and the needling methods come down to five according to the five phases. Qibo's speech ends here. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
