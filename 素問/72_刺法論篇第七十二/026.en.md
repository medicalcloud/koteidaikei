# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suppose that in a *bingyin* [year] the hard and soft lose their hold: the upper hard stem loses its hold, the lower soft cannot govern alone, the water movement in the middle is not excessive, and one cannot fix it by holding to the [usual] rule. [The previous controller] spreads in heaven in surplus and the upper proper [controller] loses its hold; heaven and earth do not match, and the pitches of the pitch-pipes differ. In such a case the movement of heaven loses its order, and in the following three years it changes into an epidemic. Examining its slightness or severity, the deviation may be great or small: the gradual arrives late in the three years, the severe at the beginning of the three years. First supplement the heart transport point, then after five days needle where the kidney enters. Again, when in the lower position, the earth's *jiazi* [cycle], *xinsi*, the soft does not attach to the hard, this too is called losing hold: earth and movement are both deficient, and in the following three years it changes into water pestilence. The needling methods are all like this. When the needling is finished, beware of great joy and desire stirring within; if these are not avoided, the qi will scatter again. Keep the patient quiet for seven days; to make the heart replete, have him think little.

## Notes

The epidemic, needling and self-cultivation when the hard and soft lose their positions in a *bingyin* year (water movement). The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 其刺如畢 is read as "when the needling is finished".
- In the base (Wikisource plain text), 辛已 is emended to 辛巳, 其刺如華 is emended to 其刺如畢, following the Daizhige texts (see the emendation notes).
