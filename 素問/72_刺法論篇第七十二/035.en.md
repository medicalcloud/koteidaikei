# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked, "Among the twelve viscera in their mutual service, when the spirit loses its position and the luster of the spirit is not complete, I fear that pathogens will invade. Can it be treated by needling? I wish to hear the essentials."

## Notes

A question about needling when the spirits of the twelve viscera lose their positions. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
