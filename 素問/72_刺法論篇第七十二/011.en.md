# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Having understood ascent, one must also master descent. The ways of ascent and descent can all be treated beforehand.

## Notes

The answer that descent too can be treated beforehand; the five qi are then described. Qibo's speech continues through unit 16. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
