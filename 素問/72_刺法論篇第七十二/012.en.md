# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When wood is about to descend and Dijing blocks and represses it, it descends but does not enter; repressed, the constraint breaks out, and when it disperses [wood] can obtain its position. When it descends and the constraint breaks out, it is violent, like the intervening qi of heaven waiting for its time. If it descends but does not go down, the constraint becomes rapid. To let it descend, break what overcomes it: needle where hand greater yin emerges and where hand *yangming* enters.

## Notes

The case of wood unable to descend. Dijing, Dixuan, Dicang, Ditong and Difu correspond to metal, water, wood, fire and earth respectively; in each case the one that overcomes the descending qi represses it. The well (emerging) and uniting (entering) points of the channels of the overcoming phase (here metal) are needled. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
