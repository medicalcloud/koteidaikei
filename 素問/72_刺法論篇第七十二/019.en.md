# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked, "Of the needling for ascent and descent I have learned the essentials. I wish to hear about the controller of heaven not yet shifting to its proper position, so that what controls transformation loses its constant governance and the myriad transformations may all go astray. Can what thus makes people ill be removed beforehand? I wish to save the multitudes; let me hear your explanation."

## Notes

A question about needling when the controller of heaven does not shift to its proper position. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
