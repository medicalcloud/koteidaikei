# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo bowed his head to the ground twice and said, "What a thorough question! It speaks of the ultimate principle; your sagely mind, in compassion and pity, wishes to save the multitudes. I shall therefore set out this way in full and expound its depths and subtleties. When greater yang is spread again, *jueyin* does not shift to its proper position; not shifting, the qi is blocked at the 止 [top]; one should drain where foot *jueyin* flows. When *jueyin* is spread again, lesser yin does not shift to its proper position; not shifting, the qi is blocked at the top; one should needle where the vessel of the heart enclosing network flows. When lesser yin is spread again, greater yin does not shift to its proper position; not shifting, the qi lingers at the top; one should needle where foot greater yin flows. When greater yin is spread again, lesser yang does not shift to its proper position; not shifting, the qi is blocked and does not yet get through; one should needle where hand lesser yang flows. When lesser yang is spread again, *yangming* does not shift to its proper position; not shifting, the qi does not yet get through above; one should needle where hand greater yin flows. When *yangming* is spread again, greater yang shifts [does not shift] to its proper position; not shifting, its qi is blocked again; one should needle where foot lesser yin flows."

## Notes

The answer describing six cases in which the previous year's controller of heaven does not withdraw but spreads again, so that the current one cannot shift to its proper position, and the brook (flowing) points to needle in each. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 止 in 気塞於止 in the main text is read as 上 ("top") from the parallel phrases.
- 太陽遷正 in the main text presumably lacks 不 (太陽不遷正), judging from the parallel phrases; the translation reads it so.
