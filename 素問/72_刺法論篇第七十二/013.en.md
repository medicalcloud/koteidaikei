# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When fire is about to descend and Dixuan blocks and represses it, it descends but does not enter; repressed, the constraint breaks out, and when it disperses all is well. Break what overcomes it, and the constraint can be dispersed: needle where foot lesser yin emerges and where foot greater yang enters.

## Notes

The case of fire unable to descend. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
