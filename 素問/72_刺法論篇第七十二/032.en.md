# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Those who are not infected [are so because] when the proper qi is preserved within, pathogenic qi 可幹 [cannot] invade. Avoid the toxic qi. [It] comes in through the heavenly female [nose]; but if it again finds its way out and the qi emerges from the brain, pathogens do not invade. For the qi to emerge from the brain, before [entering] the 室 [room], first imagine the heart as the sun. When about to enter a room of epidemic illness, first imagine blue-green qi emerging from the liver, moving left to the east, and transforming into forest trees; next imagine white qi emerging from the lungs, moving right to the west, and transforming into halberds and armor; next imagine red qi emerging from the heart, moving south and upward, and transforming into blazing light; next imagine black qi emerging from the kidneys, moving north and downward, and transforming into water; next imagine yellow qi emerging from the spleen, remaining in the center, and transforming into earth. When the five qi have finished protecting the body, imagine above the head something brilliant like the Northern Dipper; then one may enter the room of epidemic illness. Another method: on the day of the spring equinox, before sunrise, induce vomiting. Another method: after the Rain Water day, bathe three times and induce sweating with medicine. Another method, the Minor Golden Elixir formula: cinnabar two *liang*, realgar ground with water one *liang*, leaf orpiment one *liang*, purple gold half a *liang*; put them together in a lidded vessel and seal the outside; [dig] one foot into the ground and tamp the earth firm; use no furnace and no medicinal preparation, but forge with twenty *jin* of fire[wood]. After seven days, wait seven days for it to cool and take it out; the next day remove the vessel and bury the medicine in the ground for seven days; take it out and grind it for three days following the sun; make pills the size of paulownia seeds with refined white honey. Each day face east, inhale one mouthful of the sun's radiant qi, take one pill with ice water, and swallow it together with the qi. After taking ten pills, epidemics will not invade."

## Notes

The answer giving visualization to preserve the proper qi, emesis, bathing, and the Minor Golden Elixir formula, so as not to be infected. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 邪気可幹 in the main text is read from the context in the sense of 邪気不可干 ("pathogenic qi cannot invade"), but whether there is a miswritten or missing character has not been verified.
- The reading of 室 in 即室先想心如日 is uncertain; the translation supplies "before entering the room".
