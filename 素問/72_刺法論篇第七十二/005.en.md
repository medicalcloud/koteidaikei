# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When wood is about to ascend and Tianzhu blocks and represses it, wood seeks to break out of its constraint but must still wait for its time. One should needle the well point of foot *jueyin*.

## Notes

The case of wood unable to ascend. Tianzhu, Tianpeng, Tianchong, Tianying and Tianrui are star names of the nine palaces, corresponding to metal, water, wood, fire and earth respectively; in each case the one that overcomes the ascending qi represses it. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
