# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "For ascent failing to advance one can prepare in advance. I wish to hear about descent: can it be guarded against beforehand?"

## Notes

A question about preventing failure of descent. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
