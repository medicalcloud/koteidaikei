# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suppose that in a *wushen* [year] the hard and soft lose their hold: though *wu* and *gui* make the fire movement, even in a yang year it is not excessive. The upper loses its hard, the soft earth governs alone, its qi is not proper, so pathogens invade and the positions shift in alternation. The deviation may be shallow or deep; when [the epidemic] is about to arrive and merge, the musical pitches first become the same. In such a case the movement of heaven loses its time, and within three years a fire epidemic arrives. One should needle the lung transport point. When the needling is finished, keep the spirit quiet for seven days and do not grieve greatly; grief stirs the lungs and the qi scatters again. For one who wishes to make the lungs replete, the key lies in resting the breath. Again, when in the earth's lower *jiazi* [cycle] *guihai* loses its hold, the soft loses its position, and the upper loses its hard; this too is called *wu* and *gui* not uniting in virtue. Movement and earth are depleted, and in the following three years it changes into pestilence, called fire pestilence.

## Notes

The epidemic, needling and self-cultivation when the hard and soft lose their positions in a *wushen* year (fire movement). Unlike the other years, no second point to needle is given. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
