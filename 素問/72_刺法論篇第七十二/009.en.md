# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When water is about to ascend and Tianrui blocks and represses it, water seeks to break out of its constraint but must still wait for its time. One should needle the uniting point of foot lesser yin."

## Notes

The case of water unable to ascend. Qibo's speech ends here. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
