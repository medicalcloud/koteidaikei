# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Suppose that in a *jiazi* [year] the hard and soft lose their hold: the hard has not yet taken its proper position, the soft is isolated and deficient, the seasonal order is not proper and the musical pitches do not comply. In such a case, in three years it changes into a great epidemic. Examine its slightness or severity and observe its shallowness or depth; when it is about to arrive, one can needle. In needling, first supplement the kidney transport point, then after three days needle where foot greater yin pours. Again, when the lower position, 已 [*ji*] *mao*, does not arrive and *jiazi* stands alone, in the next three years earth pestilence arises; the method of supplementing and draining is entirely the same as for *jiazi*. When the needling is finished, the patient must not go out at night or travel far, and must be kept pure, clean, quiet and abstinent for seven days. Those who have had long-standing kidney pain may, at the *yin* hour, face south, purify the spirit without disordered thoughts, hold the breath without breathing seven times, stretch the neck and swallow the qi down as if swallowing something very hard; after doing this seven times, swallow the saliva from under the tongue countless times.

## Notes

The epidemic, needling and self-cultivation when the hard and soft lose their positions in a *jiazi* year (earth movement). The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
- 已卯 in the main text is read as 己卯.
