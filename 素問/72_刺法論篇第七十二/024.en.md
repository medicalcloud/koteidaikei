# 素問 刺法論篇第七十二 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What a profound question! Let me make clear its deep meaning. When [the stems of] heaven and earth shift in alternation, in three years they transform into epidemics. This is called the root being visible, and there is surely a gate of escape.

## Notes

The beginning of the account: when the hard and soft lose their positions, epidemics arise three years later, and there is a way to avoid them. Qibo's speech continues through unit 30. The notes of the lost chapters are not translated (original text only).
