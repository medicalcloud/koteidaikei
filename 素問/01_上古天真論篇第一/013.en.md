# 素問 上古天真論篇第一 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In such a case, the life span received from heaven exceeds the norm, the qi and vessels are always open, and the kidney qi is in surplus. Although such a person has children, [the children] do not go beyond the full eight times eight if male, or the full seven times seven if female, and then the essential qi of heaven and earth is all exhausted."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the life span received from heaven exceeds the norm ... the kidney qi is in surplus"] The qi of heavenly truth they received was originally in surplus.

[On "do not go beyond the full eight times eight ... is all exhausted"] Even if one has children in old age, the children's life span also cannot exceed the numbers of the *tiangui*.

## Notes

Those who have children in old age are people who received a long life span by nature and have surplus kidney qi.
- "Do not go beyond the full eight times eight ... seven times seven": Wang Bing takes this to refer to the children born of such parents, whose span (or reproductive capacity) does not exceed sixty-four or forty-nine. Another reading takes it as the limit of the parents' own fertility. The translation brackets "[the children]" following Wang Bing.
- Daizhige (the plain text) has 子 "child" after "has children" and after "eight times eight", making explicit that the subject is the children.
