# 素問 上古天真論篇第一 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There are those who are already old in years and yet have children. Why is this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "There are those who are already old ... Why is this?"] This says that it seems not to fit the numbers of the *tiangui*.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about exceptions to the stages of age that Qibo has set out.
- Wang Bing explains the point of the question: having children in old age seems not to fit the numbers of the *tiangui* (seven times seven for women, eight times eight for men).
