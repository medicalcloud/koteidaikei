# 素問 上古天真論篇第一 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "When people grow old and have no children, is it that their material strength is exhausted? Or is it that the numbers of heaven make it so?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "their material strength is exhausted"] "Material" means the capacity by which one can establish oneself.

## Notes

The topic turns to reproduction and age. Huangdi asks whether the inability to have children in old age is due to exhausted strength or to numbers fixed by heaven.
- 邪 here is an interrogative particle (= 耶).
- The "numbers of heaven" correspond to the stages of age that Qibo sets out in the next units (multiples of seven for women, eight for men).
