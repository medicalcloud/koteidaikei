# 素問 上古天真論篇第一 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Next there were worthies. They took heaven and earth as their model and the sun and moon as their image; they distinguished and ordered the stars and constellations, went against or followed yin and yang, and divided the four seasons. Seeking to follow high antiquity, they accorded with the Way. They too could increase their life spans, but there was a time of ending."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Next there were worthies. They took heaven and earth as their model and the sun and moon as their image"] Those next to the sages are called worthies. They strengthen themselves without ceasing, are thoroughly versed in a hundred matters, understand without thinking, and hit the mark whenever they make a plan; their will is the same as heaven and earth, and their heart illuminates the deep and hidden. Therefore it says they took heaven and earth as their model and the sun and moon as their image.

[On "they distinguished and ordered the stars and constellations, went against or followed yin and yang, and divided the four seasons"] *Xing* means the many stars; *chen* means the Pole Star. "Distinguished and ordered" means fixing the positions of the inner and outer star officials according to the near and far divisions of the 365 degrees of heaven. "Went against or followed yin and yang" means using methods such as the six *jia* to count backward or forward and so calculate the signs of good and ill fortune. A book of yin and yang says: "The *jiazi* of the human sphere begins from *jiazi*, takes *yichou* as next, and counts forward; the *jiazi* of the earth below begins from *jiaxu*, takes *guiyou* as next, and counts backward." This is what is meant by going against or following. "Divided the four seasons" means dividing the sequence of their qi: spring is warm, summer is hot, autumn is cool, winter is icy cold. This is the sequence of the qi of the four seasons.

[On "Seeking to follow high antiquity ... but there was a time of ending"] "Seeking to follow high antiquity, they accorded with the Way" means [being] like the people of high antiquity who knew the Way, who modelled themselves on yin and yang, harmonized with the arts and numbers, had measure in eating and drinking and regularity in rising and resting, and did not toil recklessly. The people of high antiquity who knew the Way departed after passing a hundred years; therefore it says they could increase their life spans, but there was a time of ending.

## Notes

The fourth grade, the worthy: modelling themselves on heaven and earth, sun and moon, the stars, yin and yang and the four seasons, and according with the Way by following the people of antiquity. They can increase their life spans, but unlike the true persons there is a limit. The chapter ends here.
- Wang Bing links "seeking to follow high antiquity, they accorded with the Way" to the self-cultivation of the people of antiquity who knew the Way, described at the beginning of the chapter (unit 002).
- In the base text there is a one-character blank between 弁列 and 星辰 (it does not affect the translation).
- Daizhige (*Huangdi neijing suwen*) writes 協議 for 合同.
- Huangdi's speech, begun in unit 016, ends here.
