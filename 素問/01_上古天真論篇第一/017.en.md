# 素問 上古天真論篇第一 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In middle antiquity there were perfected persons. They made their virtue pure and their Way whole; they were in harmony with yin and yang and attuned to the four seasons; they left the world and departed from common ways; they accumulated essence and kept the spirit whole; they roamed between heaven and earth, and their sight and hearing reached beyond the eight directions. These, it seems, were people who increased their life spans and made themselves strong; they too returned to [the state of] the true persons.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In middle antiquity there were perfected persons. They made their virtue pure and their Way whole"] Because they make their utmost Way whole, they are called perfected persons. With this virtue of pure simplicity, the perfected person makes whole that Way of wondrous function.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says: "Because they accumulate essence and keep the spirit whole, they can reach virtue; therefore they are called perfected persons."

[On "in harmony with yin and yang and attuned to the four seasons"] "Harmony" means harmonizing together; "attuned" means adjusting and fitting. It says that the perfected person, in movement and in rest, always fits the decrees of the four seasons — generation, growth, gathering and storage — and shares in what is fitting in yin and yang, cold and heat, rising and falling.

[On "they left the world and departed from common ways; they accumulated essence and kept the spirit whole"] Their hearts are far from the turmoil of the world and their bodies are removed from the taint of common ways; therefore they can accumulate essence and also keep the spirit whole.

[On "they roamed between heaven and earth ... beyond the eight directions"] This is because their spirit is whole. Gengsang Chu says: "The person whose spirit is whole understands without thinking and hits the mark without planning; their subtle illumination has no outside, their will is concentrated on the universe, like heaven and earth." He also says: "The body is joined with the heart, the heart with qi, qi with spirit, and spirit with non-being. If there is the slightest existence or the faintest sound, whether far beyond the eight wildernesses or near within my eyebrows and lashes, whatever comes to me I will surely know it all." Being like this is because the spirit is whole; that is how they are able to do it.

[On "These, it seems ... they too returned to the true persons"] They return alike to the Way.

## Notes

The second grade, the perfected person: making virtue and the Way whole, in harmony with yin and yang and the four seasons, leaving the world and keeping essence and spirit whole. They increased their life spans and made themselves strong, and come back to the state of the true person.
- 葢 is the base text's form (a variant of 蓋); the other sources write 蓋.
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 八遠 for 八達.
