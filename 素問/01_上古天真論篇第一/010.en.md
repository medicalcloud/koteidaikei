# 素問 上古天真論篇第一 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In a girl, at seven years the kidney qi is exuberant, the teeth are replaced and the hair grows long. At twice seven the *tiangui* arrives, the *ren* vessel opens, the great *chong* vessel is exuberant, and the menses descend at their times; therefore she can have children. At three times seven the kidney qi is evened out; therefore the wisdom teeth come in and growth reaches its peak. At four times seven the sinews and bones are firm, the hair has reached its full length, and the body is flourishing and strong. At five times seven the *yangming* vessel declines, the face begins to wither and the hair begins to fall. At six times seven the three yang vessels decline in the upper body, the face withers entirely and the hair begins to turn white. At seven times seven the *ren* vessel is depleted, the great *chong* vessel declines and diminishes, the *tiangui* is exhausted and the earth's path no longer flows; therefore the body deteriorates and she has no more children.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "at seven years the kidney qi is exuberant, the teeth are replaced and the hair grows long"] The number of old yang culminates in nine; the number of young yang comes next at seven. A girl is of young yin qi, so the number of young yang is paired with her. This shows that only when yin and yang qi are in harmony can the body be generated. Therefore at seven years the kidney qi is exuberant, the teeth are replaced and the hair grows long.

[On "At twice seven the *tiangui* arrives ... therefore she can have children"] *Gui* refers to *ren-gui*, the name of the stems of water in the north. The *ren* and *chong* vessels are both vessels of the extraordinary channels. When the kidney qi is fully exuberant and the *chong* and *ren* flow freely, the menstrual blood gradually fills and descends in response to the time. Because the qi of heavenly truth descends and works together with it, it is called *tiangui* (heavenly *gui*). The *chong* is the sea of blood and the *ren* governs the womb; because the two support each other, she can have children. It is called the "monthly matter" because the balanced qi regularly appears once every three decades of days; therefore when the period is missed, there is said to be illness.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's annotated edition, the *Taisu* and the *Jiayi jing* all read "hidden *chong*" [for "great *chong*"]; likewise below.

[On "At three times seven ... growth reaches its peak"] The "true teeth" are the teeth that come in last. That the true teeth come in when the kidney qi is evened out shows that the teeth are the surplus of the bones.

[On "At four times seven ... the body is flourishing and strong"] A woman's number for *tiangui* ends at seven times seven; the age of four times seven is half of her capacity. Therefore the body is flourishing and strong, and growth peaks at this point.

[On "At five times seven ... the hair begins to fall"] The qi of the *yangming* vessels circulates in the face; therefore when it declines, the hair falls and the face withers. The *Lingshu jing* says: "The foot *yangming* vessel starts at the nose, meets at the root of the nose, descends along the outside of the nose, enters the upper gums, comes out again to skirt the mouth and circle the lips, meets below at Chengjiang, then runs back along the lower edge behind the chin, emerges at Daying, follows Jiache, rises in front of the ear, passes Kezhuren, and follows the hairline to the forehead. The hand *yangming* vessel rises to the neck, pierces the cheek, enters the lower gums, and comes out again to skirt the mouth." Therefore the face withers and the hair falls.

[On "At six times seven ... the hair begins to turn white"] The three yang vessels all rise to the head; therefore when the three yang decline, the face withers entirely and the hair begins to turn white. The reason for the decline is that a woman's life has a surplus of qi and an insufficiency of blood, because of the repeated loss through menstruation.

[On "At seven times seven ... she has no more children"] The cessation of menstrual water is what is meant by "the earth's path no longer flows". Because the *chong* and *ren* have declined and become faint, it says that the body deteriorates and she has no children.

## Notes

The life of a woman described in seven-year stages: at fourteen the *tiangui* arrives and menstruation begins; at twenty-eight she reaches her prime; at forty-nine the *tiangui* is exhausted, menstruation stops and she can no longer have children.
- *Tiangui*: Wang Bing explains it as the descent of the qi of heavenly truth associated with water of the north (the kidney).
- "Great *chong* vessel": according to the Song editors, Quan Yuanqi's edition, the *Taisu* and the *Jiayi jing* read "hidden *chong*".
- "The earth's path no longer flows": Wang Bing takes this as the cessation of menstruation.
- On the number seven, Wang Bing explains it as the number of young yang (a yang number paired with the yin of the female).
- Qibo's speech continues in the next unit (on men).
