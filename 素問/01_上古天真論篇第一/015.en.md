# 素問 上古天真論篇第一 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Those who have the Way can keep old age at bay and preserve the body whole. Though they are long-lived in years, they can still beget children."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Those who have the Way can keep old age at bay ... they can still beget children"] These are what are called people who have attained the Way. The signs that the Way has been accomplished are as described in the following section.

## Notes

Those who cultivate the Way can keep old age at bay and, even when long-lived, can beget children.
- Wang Bing treats this as the link to the account of the true person, the perfected person, the sage and the worthy that follows.
- Daizhige (*Huangdi neijing suwen* and the plain text) writes 角 for 形.
