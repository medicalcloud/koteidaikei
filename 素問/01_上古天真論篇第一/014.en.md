# 素問 上古天真論篇第一 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Those who have the Way all reach a hundred years. Can they have children?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks whether those who cultivate the Way, living to a hundred, can also have children.
- There is no note by Wang Bing.
- Daizhige (the plain text) writes 百歳 "a hundred years of age" for 百数.
