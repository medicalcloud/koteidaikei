# 素問 上古天真論篇第一 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

That they could all pass a hundred years of age without decline in their movements was because their virtue was whole and they were not imperilled."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "because their virtue was whole and they were not imperilled"] Because they did not venture into danger, their virtue was whole. The *Zhuangzi* says: "One who holds to the Way has whole virtue; one whose virtue is whole has a whole body; one whose body is whole — this is the way of the sage." It also says: "There has never been one who practised non-action and yet whose nature and life were not whole."

## Notes

The conclusion of Qibo's answer (begun in unit 002): the people of antiquity passed a hundred years without decline because their virtue was whole and they were not imperilled.
- This answers directly Huangdi's question in unit 001. Qibo's speech ends here.
