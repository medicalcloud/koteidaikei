# 素問 上古天真論篇第一 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi said: "I have heard that in high antiquity there were true persons. They lifted up heaven and earth and grasped yin and yang; they breathed in the essential qi, stood alone and guarded the spirit, and their flesh was as one. Therefore their life spans exhausted heaven and earth, and they had no time of ending. This is because they lived by the Way.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "in high antiquity there were true persons. They lifted up heaven and earth and grasped yin and yang"] The true person is one who has accomplished the Way. The body of a true person hides and appears unfathomably: becoming small, it enters where there is no gap; becoming large, it pervades the realm of emptiness; in its transformations it passes in and out of heaven and earth, and no trace of it is seen. It follows the utmost truth and displays the signs of the accomplished Way. Because they are like this, they can lift up heaven and earth and grasp yin and yang.

[On "they breathed in the essential qi, stood alone and guarded the spirit, and their flesh was as one"] In the true person, the heart is joined with qi, qi is joined with spirit, and spirit is joined with non-being. Therefore they breathe in the essential qi, stand alone and guard the spirit; their skin is like ice and snow, and they are graceful as a maiden.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's annotated edition reads "their bodily flesh was rooted in the One"; the *Taisu* is the same. Yang Shangshan says: "The flesh and body of the true person are of the same substance as the Supreme Ultimate; therefore it says 'rooted in the One'."

[On "Therefore their life spans exhausted heaven and earth, and they had no time of ending"] Their bodies are the same as the Way and their life spans are the same as the Way; therefore they have no time of ending, and their life spans run to the end of heaven and earth. *Bi* means to exhaust.

[On "This is because they lived by the Way"] Only those who live by the utmost Way can be like this.

## Notes

The latter part of the chapter: Huangdi describes four grades of people who have attained the Way (the true person, the perfected person, the sage and the worthy). The first, the true person, is at one with heaven and earth and with yin and yang, and their life span has no end.
- The speaker is Huangdi, who now speaks for himself after Qibo's answers. His speech continues to unit 019.
- "Their flesh was as one": according to the Song editors, Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu* read "their bodily flesh was rooted in the One", which Yang Shangshan explains as the true person's body being of the same substance as the Supreme Ultimate.
- "Their life spans exhausted heaven and earth": Wang Bing glosses *bi* as "to exhaust", meaning that their life spans last until the end of heaven and earth.
- Wang Bing's phrase "skin like ice and snow, graceful as a maiden" comes from the description of the divine person in the *Zhuangzi* ("Xiaoyao you").
