# 素問 痿論篇第四十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "Why do the five zang cause wilting in people?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why do the five zang cause wilting in people?"] 痿 means being withered and weak, without strength to move.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: the cause of wilting (an illness in which the limbs become weak and powerless).
