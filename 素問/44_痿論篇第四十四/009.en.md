# 素問 痿論篇第四十四 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What you say is right. But the discourse says that to treat wilting one takes the *Yangming* alone. Why?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks the reason for "to treat wilting take the *Yangming* alone", a frequently quoted phrase. There is no note by Wang Bing.
