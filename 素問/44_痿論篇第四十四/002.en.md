# 素問 痿論篇第四十四 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "The lung governs the skin and hair of the body; the heart governs the blood vessels; the liver governs the sinews and membranes; the spleen governs the flesh; the kidney governs the bones and marrow. Thus when the lung is hot and its lobes scorched, the skin and hair become depleted, weak, tight and thin; when [the heat] lodges, it produces wilting and lameness. When the heart qi is hot, the lower vessels reverse and rise; when they rise, the lower vessels are depleted; when depleted, vessel wilting arises: the pivots are as if broken and cannot lift, and the shins are slack and cannot bear weight on the ground. When the liver qi is hot, the gallbladder leaks and the mouth is bitter, and the sinews and membranes dry; when they dry, the sinews tense and contract, producing sinew wilting. When the spleen qi is hot, the stomach is dry and there is thirst, the flesh is numb, producing flesh wilting. When the kidney qi is hot, the lumbus and spine cannot be raised, the bones wither and the marrow diminishes, producing bone wilting."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the heart governs the blood vessels; the liver governs the sinews and membranes"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition says, "Membranes are the sinew membranes beneath the skin and above the flesh."

[On "the spleen governs the flesh; the kidney governs the bones and marrow"] What they govern differs; wilting arises in each according to what it governs.

[On "Thus when the lung is hot ... wilting and lameness"] 躄 means contracted and lame, the feet unable to stretch and walk. When the lung is hot, the kidney receives the hot qi; hence this.

[On "When the heart qi is hot ... cannot bear weight on the ground"] When heart heat is abundant, fire alone shines; when fire alone shines, it blazes upward within. The kidney vessel always runs downward; now fire is abundant and blazes upward in command; therefore the kidney vessel too, following the fire, is scorched and counterflows upward. Yin qi reverses and counterflows, fire burns within again, yin is cut off above and yang does not keep its place below. Heart qi communicates with the vessels; hence vessel wilting arises. Kidney qi governs the feet; therefore the pivots of knee and ankle are as if broken off and cannot lift, and the shin sinews are slack and cannot bear weight.

[On "When the liver qi is hot ... sinew wilting"] The gallbladder is bound to the liver lobes, and its juice is extremely bitter. Therefore when the liver is hot, the gallbladder fluid seeps out; when the gallbladder is ill the mouth is bitter; now the gallbladder fluid seeps out; therefore the mouth is bitter. The liver governs the sinews and membranes; therefore with heat they dry and contract, producing sinew wilting. The *Bashiyi nanjing* says, "The gallbladder lies below between the short lobes of the liver."

[On "When the spleen qi is hot ... flesh wilting"] The spleen and stomach are joined by a membrane; when spleen qi is hot, the stomach fluid seeps out; therefore it is dry and there is thirst. The spleen governs the flesh; now heat presses within; therefore the flesh is numb, producing flesh wilting.

[On "When the kidney qi is hot ... bone wilting"] The lumbus is the house of the kidney; also, the kidney vessel rises along the inner thigh, passes through the spine and belongs to the kidney; therefore when kidney qi is hot the lumbus and spine cannot be raised. The kidney governs bones and marrow; therefore with heat the bones wither and the marrow diminishes, producing bone wilting.

## Notes

According to the tissues governed by the five zang, heat of the five zang produces five kinds of wilting (wilting-lameness [skin and hair], vessel, sinew, flesh and bone wilting).
- 枢折挈 is taken as "the pivots are as if broken and cannot lift"; Wang Bing likewise says "as if broken off and cannot lift".
