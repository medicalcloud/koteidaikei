# 素問 痿論篇第四十四 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When one is gradually soaked by damp, makes water one's business, or lingers somewhere with a damp dwelling, the flesh becomes soaked, there are impediment and numbness, producing flesh wilting. Hence the *Xiajing* says, 'Flesh wilting is contracted from damp ground.'

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When one is gradually soaked by damp ... producing flesh wilting"] If one's occupation is close to damp, or one dwells low in marshes, all this is making water one's business. Even the healthy become sluggish over time; those affected by it all the more. The flesh belongs to the spleen; spleen qi dislikes damp; when damp lodges within, the defensive qi does not nourish; therefore the flesh wilts.

[On "Hence the *Xiajing* says ... from damp ground"] The *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* says, "When the damp qi of earth affects one, it harms the skin, flesh, sinews and vessels." This is what is called harming the flesh.

## Notes

Flesh wilting is attributed to damp.
