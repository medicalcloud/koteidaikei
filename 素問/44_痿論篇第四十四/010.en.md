# 素問 痿論篇第四十四 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The *yangming* is the sea of the five zang and six fu and governs the moistening of the ancestral sinew; the ancestral sinew governs binding the bones and freeing the joints. The thoroughfare vessel is the sea of the channel vessels; it governs seeping into and irrigating the valleys and ravines, and joins the *yangming* at the ancestral sinew. Yin and yang gather at the meeting of the ancestral sinew and meet at Qijie, with the *yangming* as their chief; all belong to the belt vessel and connect with the *du* vessel. Therefore when the *yangming* is depleted, the ancestral sinew slackens and the belt vessel does not draw; therefore the feet wilt and cannot be used."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The *yangming* is the sea of the five zang and six fu"] The *yangming* is the stomach vessel; the stomach is the sea of water and grain.

[On "governs the moistening of the ancestral sinew ... freeing the joints"] The ancestral sinew means the vertical sinews above and below the pubic bone in the pubic hair. Above it connects with the chest and abdomen, below it passes through the hips and buttocks, and it also runs through the back and abdomen up to the head and nape; hence "the ancestral sinew governs binding the bones and freeing the joints". The lumbus is the great joint of the body that governs bending and stretching; hence "joints" (機関).

[On "The thoroughfare vessel is the sea of the channel vessels"] The *Lingshu jing* says, "The thoroughfare vessel is the sea of the twelve channels."

[On "it governs seeping into and irrigating the valleys and ravines, and joins the *yangming* at the ancestral sinew"] Accordingly, the vertical sinews above and below the pubic bone on both sides of the navel are precisely the ancestral sinew. The thoroughfare vessel rises along the abdomen five body-*fen* to each side of the navel, and the *yangming* vessel rises one and a half body-*cun* to each side of the navel; the ancestral sinew pulses between them; hence "joins the *yangming* at the ancestral sinew". Being the sea of the twelve channels, it governs seeping into and irrigating the valleys and ravines. The great meetings of the flesh are valleys; the small meetings are ravines.
Song editors: On examination, 宗筋脈於中 is written 宗筋縦於中 in one version.

[On "Yin and yang gather at the meeting of the ancestral sinew ... connect with the *du* vessel"] The ancestral sinews gather and meet within the pubic bone, coming from above downward; hence "yin and yang gather at the meeting of the ancestral sinew". The ancestral sinew runs down alongside the navel and joins at the pubic bone; the *yangming* assists on its outside and the thoroughfare vessel lies within; hence "meet at Qijie, with the *yangming* as their chief". Qijie is where the pulse beats on both sides of the pubic hair. The belt vessel arises at the lower ribs, circles the body once and connects with the *du* vessel. The *du* vessel arises at Guanyuan and runs up and down along the abdomen; hence "all belong to the belt vessel and connect with the *du* vessel". The *du*, *ren* and thoroughfare vessels arise together but run differently; therefore the classic sometimes cites them variously.

[On "Therefore when the *yangming* is depleted ... cannot be used"] The *yangming* vessel descends from the supraclavicular fossa along the inner edge of the breast, down alongside the navel to Qijie; a branch arises at the lower mouth of the stomach, runs inside the abdomen down to join at Qijie, then descends to the thigh, reaches Futu, enters the kneecap, descends along the outer edge of the shin to the instep and enters the inner side of the middle toe; another branch separates three *cun* below the knee and enters the outer side of the middle toe. Therefore when the *yangming* is depleted, the ancestral sinew slackens, the belt vessel does not draw, and the feet wilt and cannot be used. 引 means draw.

## Notes

The *yangming* (stomach), as the sea of the five zang and six fu, moistens the ancestral sinew and is linked with the thoroughfare, belt and *du* vessels; therefore the *yangming* is taken in treating wilting.
- 閏 in 主閏宗筋 means 潤 "moisten" (other sources write 潤).
