# 素問 痿論篇第四十四 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "With lung heat the complexion is white and the hair ruined; with heart heat the complexion is red and the network vessels overflow; with liver heat the complexion is green-blue and the nails wither; with spleen heat the complexion is yellow and the flesh twitches; with kidney heat the complexion is black and the teeth wither."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "With lung heat ... the teeth wither"] If one names them by seeking the colour of each zang and what it governs and nourishes, these are their correspondences.

## Notes

How to distinguish wilting due to heat of the five zang by colour and changes in the tissues they govern.
