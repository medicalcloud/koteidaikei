# 素問 痿論篇第四十四 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good"] "The month in which it receives" means the time and month of receiving qi, as the liver dominates on *jia* and *yi*, the heart on *bing* and *ding*, the spleen on *wu* and *ji*, the lung on *geng* and *xin*, and the kidney on *ren* and *gui*; all are the rule of the dominant qi. "The month in which it receives" means precisely the month in which each of the five constants receives qi.
