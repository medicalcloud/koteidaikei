# 素問 痿論篇第四十四 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When one travels far, is tired out, meets great heat and becomes thirsty, the thirst makes the yang qi attack within; when it attacks within, heat lodges in the kidney. The kidney is the water zang; now if water does not overcome fire, the bones wither and the marrow is depleted; therefore the feet cannot bear the body, producing bone wilting. Hence the *Xiajing* says, 'Bone wilting arises from great heat.'"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When one travels far ... producing bone wilting"] "The yang qi attacks within" means it attacks the yin qi in the abdomen. "Water does not overcome fire" is because heat lodges in the kidney.

[On "Hence the *Xiajing* says, 'Bone wilting arises from great heat'"] The kidney by nature dislikes dryness; heat instead resides within, presses and dries the bones; therefore the bones wilt and are powerless.

## Notes

Bone wilting is attributed to exhaustion and great heat. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 004, ends here.
