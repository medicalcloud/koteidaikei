# 素問 痿論篇第四十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The lung is the chief of the zang and the canopy of the heart. When there is loss and what one seeks is not obtained, the lung makes a sound; when it sounds, the lung is hot and its lobes scorched. Hence it is said, 'The five zang produce wilting and lameness because the lung is hot and its lobes scorched' — this is what is meant. When sorrow and grief are excessive, the uterine network vessel is severed; when it is severed, the yang qi stirs within, and when it breaks out there is collapse below the heart and frequent blood in the urine. Hence the *Benbing* says, 'When the great channels are empty, flesh impediment arises, which is transmitted as vessel wilting.' When thoughts are endless, what one wishes is not obtained, the mind wanders outward, and one enters the bedchamber excessively, the ancestral sinew slackens, producing sinew wilting and white overflow. Hence the *Xiajing* says, 'Sinew wilting arises from the liver through use of the inner.'

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The lung is the chief of the zang and the canopy of the heart"] Its position is high and it spreads its lobes in the chest; hence it is the chief of the zang and the canopy of the heart.

[On "When there is loss ... its lobes scorched"] Because the will suffers and is not free, and the qi stagnates. The lung stores qi; when the qi stagnates and is not free, there is panting with sound, and the lung is hot and its lobes scorched.

[On "Hence it is said ... this is what is meant"] The lung moves the nutritive and defensive and governs yin and yang; hence the quotation, "The five zang produce wilting and lameness because of lung heat."

[On "When sorrow and grief are excessive ... frequent blood in the urine"] With sorrow the heart connector becomes tense, the lung spreads and its lobes lift, the upper burner does not communicate, the nutritive and defensive do not disperse, and hot qi stays within; therefore the uterine network vessel is severed and the yang qi drums and stirs within; when it breaks out there is collapse below the heart and frequent blood in the urine. "Collapse below the heart" means collapse within the pericardium with bleeding downward. 溲 means urine.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says, "The 胞絡 is the vessel of the envelope network above the heart." On examination, 胞 in the classic and the note should both be 包 "envelope". Quan's edition also writes 肌 for 胞.

[On "Hence the *Benbing* says ... transmitted as vessel wilting"] *Benbing* is the title of a chapter of an ancient classic. The great channels means the great channel vessels. Because of the collapse of the heart and blood in the urine, the great channels are empty; when the vessels are empty, heat presses within, the defensive qi is abundant and the nutritive qi faint; hence flesh impediment arises. First flesh impediment appears, then gradually vessel wilting; hence "transmitted as vessel wilting".

[On "When thoughts are endless ... white overflow"] Thoughts and wishes become cravings and desires, and spending and draining cause exhaustion and damage; therefore there are sinew wilting and white overflow. White overflow means a white substance overflowing like semen; in men it comes down with the urine, in women it trickles continually from the genitals.

[On "Hence the *Xiajing* says ... through use of the inner"] *Xiajing* is the name of a classic of high antiquity. "Use of the inner" means exhausting the yin strength and consuming the essential qi.

## Notes

The causes of lung wilting (wilting-lameness), vessel wilting and sinew wilting, related to emotions and sexual activity. Qibo's speech continues to unit 006.
