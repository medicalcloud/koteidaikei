# 素問 痿論篇第四十四 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Supplement each one's spring point and open its stream point, regulate its depletion and repletion, harmonize its counterflow and compliance; for sinews, vessels, bones and flesh, each according to the month in which it receives [qi], and the illness will end."

## Notes

The treatment of wilting: supplementing the spring points, opening the stream points, and following the seasons. Wang Bing's note on this is given in the next unit.
