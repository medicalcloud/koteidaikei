# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In all these [years] when greater yin controls heaven, the transformations of qi move behind heaven. Yin monopolizes the government, yang qi withdraws, great winds rise at times, heaven's qi descends and earth's qi ascends, the plains are dark and misty, white dust rises on all sides, clouds rush to the south pole, cold rain comes often, and things mature in the late summer. The people suffer cold and damp, abdominal fullness, distension of the body, puffy swelling, obstruction and counterflow, cold reversal and tension. Damp and cold unite their virtue, yellow-black dust darkens and flows in the intersection of qi, and above they correspond to the Quelling Star and the Chronograph Star. Its government is austerity, its decree stillness, its grains yellow and black. So yin congeals above and cold accumulates below; when cold water overcomes fire, there are ice and hail, the sun's light does not govern, and killing qi moves. So in surplus, high [ground] is suitable; in deficiency, low; in surplus, late; in deficiency, early. This is the benefit of earth and the transformation of qi, and the people's qi follows it too. The intervening grains are named by the great [movement]. In the first qi, earth's qi shifts, cold departs, spring qi is upright, wind comes, generation spreads and the myriad things flourish, the people's qi is unfolded and at ease; wind and damp press on each other and rain is late; the people suffer bleeding, stiffness of sinews and network vessels, joints not moving freely, heaviness of the body and flaccid sinews. In the second qi, great fire is upright, things receive transformation, the people are harmonious; the illness is warm epidemics prevailing greatly, near and far alike; damp and steam press on each other and rain falls in season. In the third qi, heaven's government spreads, damp qi descends, earth's qi ascends, rain falls in season and cold follows it; affected by cold and damp, the people suffer heaviness of the body, puffy swelling and fullness of chest and abdomen. In the fourth qi, the dreaded fire presides, sultry steaming transforms, earth's qi ascends, heaven's qi is obstructed, cold winds come morning and evening, steaming heat presses on them, mist congeals on plants; when the transformation of damp does not flow, white dew spreads in the shade to complete the decree of autumn; the people suffer heat in the interstices, sudden overflowing of blood, malaria, fullness and heat of the heart and abdomen, distension of the belly, and in severe cases puffy swelling. In the fifth qi, the bleak decree is already in effect, cold dew falls, frost falls early, plants yellow and drop, cold qi reaches the body, the noble man keeps close and secure, and the people suffer [illness of] the skin and interstices. In the final qi, cold rises greatly, damp transforms greatly, frost accumulates, yin congeals, water freezes hard, the sun's light does not govern; when affected by cold, people's joints are locked and the lumbus and buttocks hurt; cold and damp push into the intersection of qi and cause illness. One must break the pent-up qi and take the source of transformation, increase the year's qi, not let the pathogen prevail, eat the grains of the year to keep the true intact, and eat the intervening grains to preserve the essence. So for the year bitter is suitable, to dry and warm; in severe cases release and drain. Without releasing and draining, damp qi overflows outward, the flesh rots, the skin splits, and water and blood flow together. One must assist yang fire and make it resist severe cold; following the sameness or difference of the qi, set the amounts more or less: for those the same as cold, transform with heat; for those the same as damp, transform with dryness; use less for the different and more for the same. In using coolness, keep away from coolness; in using cold, keep away from cold; in using warmth, keep away from warmth; in using heat, keep away from heat; food follows the same rule. Exceptions go against this. This is the way. Going against this brings illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In all these [years] when greater yin controls heaven, the transformations of qi move behind heaven"] The generation, growth, transformation and completion of the myriad things all arise and complete behind heaven's seasons.

[On "Yin monopolizes the government, yang qi withdraws, great winds rise at times"] Song editors: On examination, in this government of greater yin it says only "great winds rise at times", presumably because *jueyin* is the first qi, residing in the position of wood; spring qi is upright and wind comes; hence "great winds rise at times".

[On "clouds rush to the south pole, cold rain comes often, and things mature in the late summer"] The south pole is the residence of rain. 差夏 means ten days after the Beginning of Autumn.

[On "yellow-black dust darkens and flows in the intersection of qi, and above they correspond to the Quelling Star and the Chronograph Star"] They appear large and bright.

[On "Its government is austerity, its decree stillness, its grains yellow and black"] What the proper qi generates and completes.

[On "when cold water overcomes fire ... killing qi moves"] Yellow-black dark dust is called killing qi; it flows from the north and west to the east and south.

[On "and the people's qi follows it too. The intervening grains are named by the great [movement]"] The grains are named by the great one among the intervening qi.

[On "In the second qi ... rain falls in season"] Rain that responds to heaven's norm and does not miss its time is called timely rain.
Song editors: On examination, it says "great fire is upright" because lesser yin resides in the position of the sovereign fire.

[On "In the fourth qi ... to complete the decree of autumn"] The myriad things obtain it and are completed.

[On "One must break the pent-up qi and take the source of transformation"] Meet and take the source of transformation in the ninth month, so as to supplement and increase.

[On "One must assist yang fire and make it resist severe cold"] The division of winter; its use is in five steps, measuring the qi to use it.

[On "following the sameness or difference of the qi, set the amounts more or less"] It speaks generally of the sameness or difference of the year's movement.

[On "for those the same as cold, transform with heat; for those the same as damp, transform with dryness"] These are the lesser *shang* and lesser *yu* years. In years of the same office as the controller of heaven and also the same as damp, damp is excessive, so dryness is suitable; cold is excessive, so heat is suitable. Lesser *jue* and lesser *zhi* years are handled with balance.
