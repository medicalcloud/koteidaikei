# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The period of *chou* and *wei*. [Controller of heaven] greater yin, [central movement] lesser *jue*, [spring in earth] greater yang; prevalence and retaliation by clarity and heat alike; the same as proper *gong*. The years *dingchou* and *dingwei*. Its movement is wind, clarity and heat. [Steps:] lesser *jue*, great *zhi*, lesser *gong*, great *shang*, lesser *yu*. Greater yin, lesser *zhi*, greater yang; prevalence and retaliation by cold and rain alike. The years *guichou* and *guiwei*. Its movement is heat, cold and rain. [Steps:] lesser *zhi*, great *gong*, lesser *shang*, great *yu*, great *jue*. Greater yin, lesser *gong*, greater yang; prevalence and retaliation by wind and clarity alike; the same as proper *gong*. The years *jichou* [supreme-one heavenly tally] and *jiwei* [supreme-one heavenly tally]. Its movement is rain, wind and clarity. [Steps:] lesser *gong*, great *shang*, lesser *yu*, lesser *jue*, great *zhi*. Greater yin, lesser *shang*, greater yang; prevalence and retaliation by heat and cold alike. The years *yichou* and *yiwei*. Its movement is coolness, heat and cold. [Steps:] lesser *shang*, great *yu*, great *jue*, lesser *zhi*, great *gong*. Greater yin, lesser *yu*, greater yang; prevalence and retaliation by rain and wind alike; the same as proper *gong*. The years *xinchou* and *xinwei*. Its movement is cold, rain and wind. [Steps:] lesser *yu*, lesser *jue*, great *zhi*, lesser *gong*, great *shang*.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "greater yin, lesser *jue*, greater yang; prevalence and retaliation by clarity and heat alike; the same as proper *gong*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of subdued harmony, upper *gong* is the same as proper *gong*".

[On "Its movement is wind, clarity and heat"] First; proper.

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *zhi*, great *gong*, lesser *shang*, great *yu*"] Last.

[On "Greater yin, lesser *gong*, greater yang; prevalence and retaliation by wind and clarity alike; the same as proper *gong*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of humble supervision, upper *gong* is the same as proper *gong*".

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First.

[On "lesser *shang*, great *yu*"] Last.

[On "great *jue*"] First.

[On "Greater yin, lesser *yu*, greater yang; prevalence and retaliation by rain and wind alike; the same as proper *gong*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of dried-up flow, upper *gong* is the same as proper *gong*". Some take these two years as the same as year-meeting and as balanced water movement, and wish to remove the three characters 同正宮; this is wrong. These years have two meanings, and to remove one of them hastily is quite improper.

[On "*xinchou*"] Same as year-meeting.

[On "*xinwei*"] Same as year-meeting.

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First.

## Notes

For the ten years (*chou* and *wei*) when greater yin controls heaven and greater yang is in the spring, the prevalence and retaliation, the movement and its five steps for each central movement, set out as a table. Qibo's speech continues into unit 016.
- Several of the Song editors' notes are cut off in the base text's input, with the continuation appearing as fragments labeled as Wang Bing's notes (委和之紀太 + 宮与正宮同; 卑監之紀上 + 宮与正宮同; 涸流之紀上 + 宮与正宮同或以此二歳……). The translation renders each as a single Song editors' note.
- The first "first; proper" note appears attached after "its movement is wind, clarity and heat" rather than after "lesser *jue*".
