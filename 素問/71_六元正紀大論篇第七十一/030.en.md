# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The use of qi has more and less, and the pervasion of transformation has abundance and decline; decline and abundance, more and less, share the same transformation."

## Notes

There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 同其化也 leads into the explanation of "same transformation" in the next unit.
