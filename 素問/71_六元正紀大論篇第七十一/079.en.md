# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §79 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All these records fixing the periods, prevalence and retaliation and proper transformation, have constant numbers and must be examined. Hence the saying 'One who knows the essentials ends in a single word; one who does not know the essentials scatters without end' means this."

## Notes

A conclusion to the sixty-year list. Qibo's speech ends here. There is no note by Wang Bing.
