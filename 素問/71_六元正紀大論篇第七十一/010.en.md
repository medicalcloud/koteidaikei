# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In all these [years] when *yangming* controls heaven, the transformations of qi move behind heaven. Heaven's qi is urgent and earth's qi bright; yang monopolizes its decree, scorching heat prevails greatly, things are dry and hard, a pure wind governs, wind and dryness move across and flow into the intersection of qi; yang is much and yin little, clouds hasten to the residence of rain, the transformation of damp spreads, and at the extreme of dryness there is moisture. Its grains are white and red; the intervening grains are named by the great [movement]. What it depletes are white shelled creatures and the many kinds of feathered ones. Metal and fire unite their virtue, and above they correspond to the Great White Star and the Sparkling Deluder. Its government is urgency, its decree violence; hibernating creatures appear and flowing water does not freeze. The people suffer cough, blocked throat, attacks of cold and heat, sudden shivering, urinary blockage and constipation. When clarity comes first and strong, furred creatures die; when heat comes after and violent, shelled creatures suffer calamity. Its outbreak is agitated; the arising of prevalence and retaliation is disturbed and greatly disordered; the qi of clarity and heat hold in the intersection of qi. In the first qi, earth's qi shifts, yin begins to congeal, the qi begins to be austere, water freezes, cold rain transforms; the illnesses are heat and distension in the center, puffy swelling of the face and eyes, somnolence, nasal congestion and nosebleed, sneezing and yawning, vomiting, yellow-red urine, and in severe cases painful urination. In the second qi, yang spreads, the people are at ease, things grow and flourish, epidemics come greatly, and people often die suddenly. In the third qi, heaven's government spreads, coolness moves, dryness and heat meet, at the extreme of dryness there is moisture, and the people suffer cold and heat. In the fourth qi, cold rain falls; the illnesses are sudden collapse, shivering, delirium, shortness of qi, dry throat with craving for drink, and also heart pain, carbuncles and swellings, sores and ulcers, malarial cold, bone flaccidity and blood in the stool. In the fifth qi, the decree of spring acts contrarily, grasses grow and flourish, and the people's qi is harmonious. In the final qi, yang qi spreads, the weather is contrarily warm, hibernating creatures come out, flowing water does not freeze, the people are healthy and at peace, and the illness is warmth. So eat the grains of the year to settle its qi and the intervening grains to remove its pathogens. For the year salty, bitter and acrid are suitable, to 汙 [sweat], clear and disperse; settle the qi of the movement so as not to receive pathogens, break the pent-up qi, support the source of transformation, and regulate the amount according to the weight of cold and heat. For those the same as heat, use more of heaven's transformation; for those the same as clarity, use more of earth's transformation. In using coolness, keep away from coolness; in using heat, keep away from heat; in using cold, keep away from cold; in using warmth, keep away from warmth; food follows the same rule. When there is an exception, go against it. This is the way. Going against this disorders the warp of heaven and earth and disturbs the principles of yin and yang."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In all these [years] when *yangming* controls heaven, the transformations of qi move behind heaven"] The generation, growth, transformation and completion of the qi of the six steps, and the movement and stillness of all affairs, all respond behind heaven's seasons. The same holds for the other years of lesser [movements].

[On "yang is much and yin little, clouds hasten to the residence of rain, the transformation of damp spreads"] The residence of rain is where greater yin is.

[On "and at the extreme of dryness there is moisture"] When dry qi is about to end, it transforms into rain and moisture; this is called the division of the third qi.

[On "Its grains are white and red"] What the proper qi of heaven and earth transform and generate.

[On "the intervening grains are named by the great [movement]"] "Named by the great" means those transformed by the qi of great *jue*, [great] *shang* and the like mentioned above. They are generated by the intervening qi; hence "intervening grains".
Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says: "What are the grains of the year and the intervening grains? The spring in earth gives the grains of the year, and the intervening [qi] to the left and right of the spring also give grains of the year. Those transformed between the controller of heaven and the movement are called intervening grains. There is also another kind called intervening grains: when transformation is deficient and, on the contrary, what overcomes it generates them, they are called intervening grains—that is, transformations of pathogenic qi, also called grains of joint transformation, also called intervening grains." This differs considerably from Wang's note.

[On "What it depletes are white shelled creatures and the many kinds of feathered ones"] White shelled creatures and the many kinds of feathered creatures with wings consume the sacrificial grain; insects, birds and armed troubles make disaster in the year, exhausting the kinds of things.

[On "Metal and fire unite their virtue, and above they correspond to the Great White Star and the Sparkling Deluder"] They appear large and bright.

[On "Its outbreak is agitated; the arising of prevalence and retaliation is disturbed and greatly disordered"] Metal prevails first and wood has already suffered harm, so furred creatures die; fire prevails later and metal does not prevail, so shelled creatures in turn suffer calamity. The feathered that prevailed and killed have already perished, and the strong that came later in retaliation also die. What is this if not greatly disordered qi?

[On "the illnesses are heat and distension in the center ... in severe cases painful urination"] The transformation of greater yin.
Song editors: On examination, austere qi and freezing water are not the transformation of greater yin.

[On "In the second qi ... people often die suddenly"] Because the minister is in the sovereign's position.

[On "In the third qi ... the people suffer cold and heat"] Cold and heat means malaria.

[On "In the fourth qi ... bone flaccidity and blood in the stool"] Bone flaccidity means lack of strength.

[On "In the final qi ... the illness is warmth"] The transformation of the sovereign.

[On "so as not to receive pathogens, break the pent-up qi, support the source of transformation"] The source of transformation means meeting and taking it in the sixth month.
Song editors: Metal flourishes in the seventh month, so the qi of metal is drained in advance in the sixth month.

[On "For those the same as heat, use more of heaven's transformation; for those the same as clarity, use more of earth's transformation"] Lesser *jue* and lesser *zhi* years are the same as heat; prescriptions mostly treat them with heaven's transformation of clarity. Lesser *gong*, lesser *shang* and lesser *yu* years are the same as clarity; prescriptions mostly treat them with earth's transformation of heat. Fire is in earth, so for those the same as clarity, use more of earth's transformation; metal is in heaven, so for those the same as heat, use more of heaven's transformation.
