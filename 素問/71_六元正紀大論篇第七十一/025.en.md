# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "The qi of prevalence and retaliation are always present, and disasters arrive at times. How are they to be observed?"

## Notes

A question on how to observe prevalence, retaliation and disasters. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 𠋫 is a variant form of 候; all the other sources write 候 (see the variants).
