# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I wish you, Master, to work it out and set it in order, following its categories and sequence, dividing its sections and governors, distinguishing its sources and controllers, clarifying its numbers of qi, and making plain its proper transformations. May I hear it?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I wish you, Master, to work it out ... May I hear it?"] Sections and governors means dividing what the six qi govern by section. Sources and controllers means assigning the positions of the movement of the five qi. Numbers of qi means the proper numbers by which the qi of heaven, earth and the five movements are used in turn. Proper transformations means the flavors and qualities suited to the year—sour, bitter, sweet, acrid, salty, cold, warm, cool and hot.

## Notes

A request to set out in order the sections, governors, sources, controllers, numbers of qi and proper transformations of the six qi and five movements.
