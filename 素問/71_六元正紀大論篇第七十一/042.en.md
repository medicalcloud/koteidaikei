# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In [a time of] heat, do not offend with heat; in [a time of] cold, do not offend with cold. Compliance brings harmony; contravention brings illness. One must revere, fear and keep away from them. This is what is meant by the rise of the seasons and the six positions."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "One must revere, fear and keep away from them ... the six positions"] In the months when the qi of the four seasons flourish, medicines, food and clothing whose cold, heat, warmth or coolness are the same [as the season] should all be avoided. If one offends the same in the four seasons, it is adding water to water and fire to fire; illness will surely arise.

## Notes

Do not use medicines or food of the same nature as the season's qi.
