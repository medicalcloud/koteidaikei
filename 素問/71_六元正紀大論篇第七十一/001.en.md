# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "The six transformations and six changes, prevalence and retaliation, excess and governance, and the order of sweet, bitter, acrid, salty, sour and bland—these I know. But the transformations of the five movements sometimes follow the five [heavenly] qi, sometimes go against heaven's qi, sometimes follow heaven's qi and go against earth's qi, sometimes follow earth's qi and go against heaven's qi; sometimes they suit each other and sometimes not. I cannot yet understand these matters. I wish to penetrate the principles of heaven, follow the pattern of earth, harmonize the movements, regulate their transformations, so that above and below unite in virtue without disrupting each other's order, the rising and descending of heaven and earth do not lose their fitness, and the five movements proceed without deviating from their government. How does one regulate them by the compliant and contrary use of the proper flavors?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "But the transformations of the five movements sometimes follow the five qi"] Song editors: On examination, 五気 is suspected to be 天気 ("heaven's qi"); then it accords with the following text.

[On "without deviating from their government. How does one regulate them by the compliant and contrary use of the proper flavors?"] Sameness of qi is called compliance; difference of qi is called contrariness. Overcoming and restraining is not suiting each other; generating each other is suiting each other. The qi controlling heaven and earth take turns in excess, and prevalence and retaliation each have their rules of treatment. One wishes to balance the nature of the qi so as not to go against the qi of heaven and earth, and thereby bring about clarity, stillness, harmony and balance.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on the compliance, contrariness and suiting of the five movements and the qi of heaven and earth, and how to regulate them with the proper flavors. Wang Bing explains compliance, contrariness and suiting.
- 五 in 或従五気 is read as 天, following the Song editors and the Wikisource plain text (see the variants).
