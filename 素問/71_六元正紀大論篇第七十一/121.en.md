# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §121 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where *jueyin* arrives there is bending and moving, and meeting and following; where lesser yin arrives, high bright flames, and a reddish-yellow glow; where greater yin arrives, deep yin, white dust, and darkness and gloom; where lesser yang arrives, flashing light, red clouds, and a reddish-yellow glow; where *yangming* arrives, smoke and dust, frost, sharpness and severity, and mournful sounds; where greater yang arrives, rigidity and firmness, hard spikes, and standing [congealment]. This is the constancy of the implementation of decrees.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Where *jueyin* arrives there is bending and moving, and meeting and following"] The nature of wind.

[On "where lesser yin arrives, high bright flames, and a reddish-yellow glow"] 焔 is yang flame; 曛 is a reddish-yellow color.

[On "where greater yin arrives, deep yin, white dust, and darkness and gloom"] Dark and obscured, not bright.

[On "where lesser yang arrives, flashing light, red clouds, and a reddish-yellow glow"] Flashing light is lightning, streaming light, brightness. 彤 is red. Its qi is the same as lesser yin's.

[On "where *yangming* arrives, smoke and dust, frost, sharpness and severity, and mournful sounds"] Killing qi.

[On "where greater yang arrives, rigidity and firmness, hard spikes, and standing"] The transformation of cold.

[On "This is the constancy of the implementation of decrees"] When decrees are implemented, the multitude of things do not deviate.

## Notes

The eighth of the twelve changes: the constancy of the implementation of decrees.
