# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §137 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. The treatise says, 'In heat do not offend with heat; in cold do not offend with cold.' If I wish not to keep away from cold or from heat, what then?"

## Notes

A question about when one may offend against the cold or heat of the season. There is no note by Wang Bing.
