# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §69 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *jiashen* and *jiayin* years, above is lesser yang ministerial fire, in the middle the great *gong* earth movement, below *jueyin* wood. The transformation of fire is two, that of rain five, that of wind eight; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, salty and cold; in the middle, salty and harmonizing; below, acrid and cool. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*jiayin* years, above is lesser yang ministerial fire, in the middle the great *gong* earth movement"] Song editors: On examination, the *jiayin* year differs slightly from *jiashen*, because the wood of *yin* can punish the balance of earth qi.

[On "in the middle the great *gong* earth movement, below *jueyin* wood. The transformation of fire is two"] Song editors: On examination, in *jiashen* the transformation of fire is seven, in *jiayin* two.

[On "The transformation of fire is two, that of rain five, that of wind eight"] Song editors: On examination, in *jiashen* the transformation of wind is three, in *jiayin* eight.

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *jiashen* and *jiayin* years. There is no note by Wang Bing.
