# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §133 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "What of the fullness and emptiness of the qi of the six positions?"

## Notes

A question about fullness and emptiness of the qi of the six positions. There is no note by Wang Bing.
