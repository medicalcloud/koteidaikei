# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In all these [years] when lesser yin controls heaven, the transformations of qi move ahead of heaven. Earth's qi is austere and heaven's qi bright; cold meets summer heat and heat is added to dryness; clouds race to the residence of rain, the transformation of damp moves, timely rain falls, metal and fire unite their virtue, and above they correspond to the Sparkling Deluder and the Great White Star. Its government is brightness, its decree urgency, its grains red and white. Water and fire, cold and heat hold in the intersection of qi and are the beginning of illness. Heat illnesses arise above, clear-cold illnesses below, and cold and heat override each other and contend in the middle; the people suffer cough and panting, bleeding upward and downward, nasal congestion and sneezing, red eyes and sores at the canthi, cold reversal entering the stomach, heart pain, lumbar pain, enlarged abdomen, dry throat and swelling above. In the first qi, earth's qi shifts, the dryness [summer heat] is about to depart, cold begins, hibernating creatures hide again, water freezes, frost falls again, wind [bitter cold] arrives, yang qi is pent up, the people on the contrary keep close and secure, the joints are locked, the lumbus and buttocks hurt, and as scorching heat is about to arise, there are sores inside and out. In the second qi, yang qi spreads, wind moves, spring qi is thereby upright, the myriad things respond and flourish, cold qi comes at times, the people are harmonious; the illnesses are painful urination, blurred and red eyes, and qi pent up above with heat. In the third qi, heaven's government spreads, great fire moves, all kinds flourish brightly, cold qi comes at times; the people suffer qi reversal and heart pain, alternating cold and heat, cough and panting and red eyes. In the fourth qi, sultry heat arrives, heavy rain falls at times, cold and heat come alternately; the people suffer cold and heat, dry throat, jaundice, nasal congestion and nosebleed, and attacks of fluid retention. In the fifth qi, the dreaded fire presides, summer heat returns contrarily, yang transforms, the myriad things grow and flourish, the people are healthy, and the illness is warmth. In the final qi, the decree of dryness proceeds, the remaining fire is blocked within, there is swelling above, cough and panting, and in severe cases bleeding; when cold qi rises often, mist and fog shade [the sky]; illness arises in the skin and interstices, lodges within in the flanks and extends below to the lower abdomen, producing cold in the center. Earth is about to change. One must suppress the qi of the movement, support what the year overcomes, break what is pent up and bursts out, and first take the source of transformation, so that sudden excess does not produce illness. Eat the grains of the year to keep the true qi intact and the intervening grains to ward off depleting pathogens. For the year salty is suitable, to soften and regulate the upper; in severe cases release with bitter and gather with sour to settle the lower; in severe cases drain with bitter. Adjust the amount to the sameness or difference of the qi: for those the same as heaven's qi, transform with cold and clarity; for those the same as earth's qi, transform with warmth and heat. In using heat, keep away from heat; in using coolness, keep away from coolness; in using warmth, keep away from warmth; in using cold, keep away from cold; food follows the same rule. With exceptions, go against it. This is the way. Going against this brings on illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Earth's qi is austere and heaven's qi bright; cold meets summer heat and heat is added to dryness"] Song editors: On examination, "cold meets summer heat" means that the final qi of the previous year is lesser yang and the first qi of this year greater yang; the cold of greater yang meets the summer heat of the previous year's lesser yang. "Heat is added to dryness" means lesser yin is above and *yangming* below.

[On "metal and fire unite their virtue, and above they correspond to the Sparkling Deluder and the Great White Star"] They appear bright and large.

[On "swelling above. In the first qi, earth's qi shifts, the dryness is about to depart"] Song editors: The year before *yangming* is in the spring is lesser yang, and lesser yang is summer heat. Summer heat departs, *yangming* is in earth, and greater yang is the first qi; hence the text above, "cold meets summer heat", means summer heat departs and cold begins. This character 燥 is thus an error for 暑.

[On "hibernating creatures hide again, water freezes, frost falls again, wind arrives"] Song editors: Wang's note to the *Liuwei zhi dalun* says "when greater yang resides in the position of wood, cold wind is cutting and 列 [bitter]". This 風廼至 should be 風廼列 [冽].

[On "producing cold in the center. Earth is about to change"] When the qi ends, it shifts; how could it last long?

[On "break what is pent up and bursts out, and first take the source of transformation"] Meet and take it first in the twelfth month of the previous year.

[On "for those the same as heaven's qi, transform with cold and clarity; for those the same as earth's qi, transform with warmth and heat"] Great *jue* and great *zhi* years are the same as heaven's qi and should be treated with cold and clarity; great *gong*, great *shang* and great *yu* years are the same as earth's qi and should be treated with warmth and heat. 化 means treat.
