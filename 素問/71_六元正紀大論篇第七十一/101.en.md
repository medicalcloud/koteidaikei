# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §101 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the movement is excessive, it arrives early; when the movement is insufficient, it arrives late. This is the constancy of the signs."

## Notes

The answer that excess arrives early and insufficiency late. There is no note by Wang Bing.
