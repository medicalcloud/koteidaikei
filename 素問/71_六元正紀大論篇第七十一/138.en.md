# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §138 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What a thorough question! To release the exterior, do not keep away from heat; to attack the interior, do not keep away from cold."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "To release the exterior, do not keep away from heat; to attack the interior, do not keep away from cold"] Since it drains through sweat, heat is used without keeping away from heat; since it purges downward, cold is used without keeping away from cold. In both cases [the medicine] does not stay in the center. Thus heat may be used in summer and cold in winter. Not to fear or avoid [them] without releasing or draining is called recklessness, and is forbidden by the rule of keeping away. Both refer to using them when there is no alternative. Autumn and winter are the same.
Song editors: According to the *Zhizhen yao dalun*, "In releasing do not keep away from heat; do not offend warmth and coolness."

## Notes

The answer that in sweating and purging one need not keep away from the season's cold or heat.
