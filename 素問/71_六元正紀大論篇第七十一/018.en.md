# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The period of *zi* and *wu*. [Controller of heaven] lesser yin, [central movement] great *jue*, [spring in earth] *yangming*; the years *renzi* and *renwu*. Its movement is drumming wind; its transformation sounding, stirring, opening and splitting; its change shaking, snapping, smashing and uprooting; its illness propping fullness. [Steps:] great *jue*, lesser *zhi*, great *gong*, lesser *shang*, great *yu*. Lesser yin, great *zhi*, *yangming*; the years *wuzi* [heavenly tally] and *wuwu* [supreme-one heavenly tally]. Its movement is scorching heat; its transformation warm brilliance and sultry heat; its change fierce blaze and boiling; its illness heat above and bleeding. [Steps:] great *zhi*, lesser *gong*, great *shang*, lesser *yu*, lesser *jue*. Lesser yin, great *gong*, *yangming*; the years *jiazi* and *jiawu*. Its movement is yin rain; its transformation softness, moisture and timely rain; its change quaking, alarm, gusts and downpours; its illness fullness in the center and heaviness of the body. [Steps:] great *gong*, lesser *shang*, great *yu*, great *jue*, lesser *zhi*. Lesser yin, great *shang*, *yangming*; the years *gengzi* and *gengwu*, the same as proper *shang*. Its movement is coolness and sharpness; its transformation fog, dew and bleak wind; its change austere killing and withering; its illness clear cold below. [Steps:] great *shang*, lesser *yu*, lesser *jue*, great *zhi*, lesser *gong*. Lesser yin, great *yu*, *yangming*; the years *bingzi* [year-meeting] and *bingwu*. Its movement is cold; its transformation congealing bleakness and biting cold; its change ice, snow, frost and hail; its illness cold and [diarrhea] below. [Steps:] great *yu*, great *jue*, lesser *zhi*, great *gong*, lesser *shang*.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The period of *zi* and *wu*. Lesser yin, great *jue*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "when *zhi* is above, its qi counterflows".

[On "Its movement is drumming wind; its transformation sounding, stirring, opening and splitting"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "its virtue is sounding, bending, opening and splitting".

[On "its illness propping fullness. great *jue*"] First; proper.

[On "great *yu*"] Last.

[On "Lesser yin, great *zhi*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "when *zhi* is above, the gathering qi is late".

[On "*wuwu* supreme-one heavenly tally. Its movement is scorching heat"] Song editors: On examination, for the great *zhi* movement, with greater yang controlling heaven it says "heat", with lesser yang "summer heat", and with lesser yin "scorching heat"; the movement is stated together with the qi of the controller of heaven.

[On "its transformation warm brilliance and sultry heat"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* writes "warmth, summer heat and sultry heat". Here 暑 is changed to 曜 because lesser yin presides above.

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First.

[On "Its movement is yin rain; its transformation softness, moisture and timely rain"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "softness, moisture, heaviness and wetness". Also for the three great *gong* movements [the transformation] is written "softness, moisture, heaviness and luster". The two characters 時雨 here are suspected to be an error.

[On "great *yu*"] Last.

[On "great *jue*"] First.

[On "*gengzi*"] Same as heavenly tally.

[On "*gengwu*"] Same as heavenly tally.

[On "the same as proper *shang*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of firm completion, when *zhi* is above, it is the same as proper *shang*".

[On "Its movement is coolness and sharpness"] Song editors: On examination, the movement matches the spring in earth; hence "coolness and sharpness".

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First.

[On "its transformation congealing bleakness and biting cold"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* writes "congealing bleakness and cold mist".

[On "great *yu*"] Last.

[On "great *jue*"] First.

## Notes

For the ten years (*zi* and *wu*) when lesser yin controls heaven and *yangming* is in the spring, the movement, transformation, change and illness for each central movement, and the five steps of the movement, set out as a table. Qibo's speech continues into unit 019.
- In the base text the first half of this unit (to ……太羽少角) and the second half (from 少陰太宮) were separated by a page transposition; they have been restored to the correct order (see the emendation note in unit 016).
- Some of the Song editors' notes are cut off in the base text's input, with the continuation appearing as fragments labeled as Wang Bing's notes (……少陰司天曰炎暑兼司天 + 之気而言運也; 按五常政大論 + 云堅成之紀上徴与正商同). The translation renders each as a single Song editors' note.
- The base text writes 岐伯 in this unit.
