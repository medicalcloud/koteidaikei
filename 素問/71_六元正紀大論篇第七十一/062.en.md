# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §62 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *dingchou* and *dingwei* years, above is greater yin earth, in the middle the lesser *jue* wood movement, below greater yang water. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of clarity and the transformation of heat are alike; these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the third palace. The transformation of rain is five, that of wind three, that of cold one; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and warm; in the middle, acrid and warm; below, sweet and hot. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the *dingchou* and *dingwei* years, above is greater yin earth"] Song editors: On examination, the wood movement here is balanced qi and punishes the upper, so heaven's decree is halved.

[On "*dingwei* years, above is greater yin earth, in the middle the lesser *jue* wood movement"] Song editors: On examination, in *ding* years the first month is *renyin*, a 千 [stem] virtue tally, making proper *jue*.

[On "The transformation of rain is five, that of wind three, that of cold one"] Song editors: On examination, in *dingchou* the transformation of cold is six, in *dingwei* one.

[On "Their treatment: above, bitter and warm ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "above, sour and balanced; below, sweet and warm". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when damp runs rampant and prevails, calm it with bitter and hot; when cold runs rampant within, treat it with sweet and hot".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *dingchou* and *dingwei* years.
- 千 in the Song editors' 千徳符 is presumably an error for 干.
