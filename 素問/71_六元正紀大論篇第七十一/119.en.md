# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §119 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where *jueyin* arrives there is the transformation of birth; where lesser yin arrives, of flourishing; where greater yin arrives, of moistening; where lesser yang arrives, of luxuriance; where *yangming* arrives, of hardening; where greater yang arrives, of storing. This is the constancy of spreading governance.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Where *jueyin* arrives there is the transformation of birth"] The transformation of warmth.

[On "where lesser yin arrives, of flourishing"] The transformation of mild warmth.

[On "where greater yin arrives, of moistening"] The transformation of damp.

[On "where lesser yang arrives, of luxuriance"] The transformation of heat.

[On "where *yangming* arrives, of hardening"] The transformation of coolness.

[On "where greater yang arrives, of storing"] The transformation of cold.

## Notes

The sixth of the twelve changes: the constancy of spreading governance.
