# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §47 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence it is said: do not fail heaven's faithfulness, do not go against what suits the qi, do not assist its prevalence, do not help its retaliation. This is called supreme governance."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Hence it is said: do not fail heaven's faithfulness ... supreme governance"] Heaven's faithfulness means that when the time comes it is surely fixed. 翼 and 賛 both mean assisting. Carefully keeping to heaven's faithfulness is called the supreme true wondrous principle.
