# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. What is the government of *Jueyin*?"

## Notes

A question on the government in years when *Jueyin* controls heaven. There is no note by Wang Bing.
