# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Wind and warmth are the same as the transformation of spring; heat, haze, dimness and fire the same as that of summer; prevalence and retaliation likewise; dryness, clarity, mist and dew the same as that of autumn; clouds, rain, dimness, darkness and dust the same as that of late summer; cold qi, frost, snow and ice the same as that of winter. This is the norm by which the transformations of heaven, earth, the five movements and six qi are used in turn, abounding and declining."

## Notes

The transformations of the six qi and five movements are the same as those of the four seasons. There is no note by Wang Bing.
