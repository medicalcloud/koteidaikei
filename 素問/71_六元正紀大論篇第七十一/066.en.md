# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §66 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *xinsi* and *xinhai* years, above is *jueyin* wood, in the middle the lesser *yu* water movement, below lesser yang ministerial fire. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of rain and the transformation of wind are alike; these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the first palace. The transformation of wind is three, that of cold one, that of fire seven; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, acrid and cool; in the middle, bitter and harmonizing; below, salty and cold. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*xinhai* years, above is *jueyin* wood, in the middle the lesser *yu* water movement"] Song editors: On examination, in the *xinsi* year wood retaliates and earth's [prevalence] ends; water returns to proper *yu* only in the seventh month, *bingshen*. In the *xinhai* year water is balanced qi, assisted by the water of *hai*, making proper *yu*; it differs slightly from the *xinsi* year.

[On "these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the first palace. The transformation of wind is three"] Song editors: On examination, in *xinsi* the transformation of wind is eight, in *xinhai* three.

[On "The transformation of wind is three, that of cold one, that of fire seven"] Song editors: On examination, in *xinsi* the transformation of heat is seven, in *xinhai* two.

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *xinsi* and *xinhai* years. There is no note by Wang Bing.
