# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §72 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *dinghai* and *dingsi* years, above is *jueyin* wood, in the middle the lesser *jue* wood movement, below lesser yang ministerial fire. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of clarity and the transformation of heat are alike; these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the third palace. The transformation of wind is three, that of fire seven; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, acrid and cool; in the middle, acrid and harmonizing; below, salty and cold. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*dinghai*"] Heavenly tally.

[On "In the *dinghai* and *dingsi* years"] Heavenly tally.

[On "*dingsi* years, above is *jueyin* wood, in the middle the lesser *jue* wood movement"] Song editors: On examination, in *ding* years the first month is *renyin*; below it obtains the union with *ren*, a 于 [stem] virtue tally, becoming proper *jue*, balanced qi.

[On "these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the third palace. The transformation of wind is three"] Song editors: On examination, since the movement and the controller of heaven are both wood, it says only "wind three"; wind three is the transformation of the lesser *jue* movement. If it were the transformation of *jueyin* controlling heaven, *dinghai* would have wind three and *dingsi* wind eight.

[On "Disaster is in the third palace. The transformation of wind is three, that of fire seven"] Song editors: On examination, in *dinghai* the transformation of heat is two, in *dingsi* seven.

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *dinghai* and *dingsi* years.
- 于 in the Song editors' 于徳符 is presumably an error for 干.
