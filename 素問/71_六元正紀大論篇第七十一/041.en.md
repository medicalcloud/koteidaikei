# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "You, Master, say 'in using cold, keep away from cold; in using heat, keep away from heat'. I do not yet understand why. I wish to hear what is meant by keeping away."
