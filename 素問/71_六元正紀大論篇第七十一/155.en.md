# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §155 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "How perfect is the way of the sages! The great transformations of heaven and earth, the junctures of the movements, the records of presiding and governing, the governance of yin and yang, the 今 [decrees] of cold and heat: who but you, Master, could comprehend them! Let them be stored in the Chamber of the Spirit Orchid, titled *Six Primordial Correct Records*, not shown without fasting and abstinence, and transmitted with care."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Let them be stored in the Chamber of the Spirit Orchid ... and transmitted with care"] Song editors: On examination, this is the same as the closing text of the *Qijiao bian dalun*.

## Notes

The close of the chapter.
- 今 in 寒暑之今 in the main text is read as 令.
