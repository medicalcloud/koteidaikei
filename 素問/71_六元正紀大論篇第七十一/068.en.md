# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §68 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *guiwei* and *guichou* years, above is greater yin earth, in the middle the lesser *zhi* fire movement, below greater yang water. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of cold and the transformation of rain are alike; these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the ninth palace. The transformation of rain is five, that of fire two, that of cold one; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and warm; in the middle, salty and warm; below, sweet and hot. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*guichou* years, above is greater yin earth, in the middle the lesser *zhi* fire movement"] Song editors: On examination, in *guiwei* and *guichou* the two fires on left and right serve as intervening [qi] and assist each other. Also the fifth month, *wuwu*, is a stem virtue tally; *gui* sees *wu* and the qi is complete; water has not yet prevailed, and it becomes proper *zhi*.

[On "The transformation of rain is five, that of fire two, that of cold one"] Song editors: On examination, in *guiwei* the transformation of cold is one, in *guichou* six.

[On "Their treatment: above, bitter and warm ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "above, sour and harmonizing; below, sweet and warm". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when damp runs rampant and prevails, calm it with bitter and hot; when cold runs rampant within, treat it with sweet and hot".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *guiwei* and *guichou* years. There is no note by Wang Bing.
