# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §52 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *dingmao* and *dingyou* years, above is *yangming* metal, in the middle the lesser *jue* wood movement, below lesser yin fire. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of clarity and the transformation of heat are alike; these are the so-called days of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the third palace. The transformation of dryness is nine, that of wind three, that of heat seven; these are the so-called days of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and slightly warm; in the middle, acrid and harmonizing; below, salty and cold. These are the so-called suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*dingmao*"] Year-meeting.

[On "In the *dingmao* and *dingyou* years"] Song editors: On examination, in *ding* years the first month is *renyin*, a 午 [stem] virtue tally, so the qi is balanced and prevalence and retaliation do not arrive. The movement is the same as proper *jue*; metal does not overcome wood, and wood does not harm earth. Also in the *dingmao* year the wood of *mao* assists it, so *yangming* above cannot harm it.

[On "Disaster is in the third palace"] Song editors: On examination, the third palace is the eastern chamber, the position of Zhen, governed by Tianchong.

[On "Disaster is in the third palace. The transformation of dryness is nine"] Song editors: On examination, in *dingmao* the transformation of dryness is nine, in *dingyou* four.

[On "The transformation of dryness is nine, that of wind three, that of heat seven"] Song editors: On examination, in *dingmao* the transformation of heat is two, in *dingyou* seven.

[On "Their treatment: above, bitter and slightly warm ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when dryness runs rampant and prevails, calm it with bitter and warm; when heat runs rampant within, treat it with salty and cold". Also the *Xuanzhu* says "above, bitter and hot".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *dingmao* and *dingyou* years.
- 午 in the Song editors' 午徳符 is presumably an error for 干 (the note in unit 062 writes 千徳符).
