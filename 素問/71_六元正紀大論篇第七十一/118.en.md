# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §118 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where *jueyin* arrives, hairy creatures are transformed; where lesser yin arrives, winged creatures; where greater yin arrives, naked creatures; where lesser yang arrives, winged creatures; where *yangming* arrives, shelled creatures; where greater yang arrives, scaled creatures. This is the constancy of the transformations of virtue.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Where *jueyin* arrives, hairy creatures are transformed"] Creatures whose forms have hair.

[On "where lesser yin arrives, winged creatures"] The kind with wings that fly.

[On "where greater yin arrives, naked creatures"] The kind without hair, feathers, scales or shells.

[On "where lesser yang arrives, winged creatures"] Thin, transparent wings, the kind of bees and cicadas, not the wings of feathered birds.

[On "where *yangming* arrives, shelled creatures"] The kind with shells.

[On "where greater yang arrives, scaled creatures"] Bodies with scales.

## Notes

The fifth of the twelve changes: the constancy of the transformations of virtue (the transformation of creatures).
