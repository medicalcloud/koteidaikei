# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §81 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When constraint reaches its extreme it breaks out; it waits for its time and arises."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When constraint reaches its extreme it breaks out; it waits for its time and arises"] "Waiting" refers to the five 及 [qi] being displaced from their positions. Great warmth breaks out in *chen* and *si*, great heat in *shen* and *wei*, great coolness in *xu* and *hai*, great cold in *chou* and *yin*. When what [each] is overcome by presides over it, it again waits for the intervening qi to break out. Hence "waits for its time".
Song editors: On examination, 及 in the note is perhaps to be read 気.

## Notes

The answer that constraint, at its extreme, breaks out at its time.
