# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §114 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Let me tell it all. Where the qi arrive: where *jueyin* arrives there is harmony and calm; where lesser yin arrives, mild warmth; where greater yin arrives, dust and dampness; where lesser yang arrives, blazing heat; where *yangming* arrives, clear sharpness; where greater yang arrives, cold vapor. This is the constancy of the seasonal transformations.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Let me tell it all"] 遂 means fully.

[On "Where the qi arrive: where *jueyin* arrives there is harmony and calm"] The first qi, the transformation of wood.

[On "where lesser yin arrives, mild warmth"] The second qi, sovereign fire.

[On "where greater yin arrives, dust and dampness"] The fourth qi, the transformation of earth.

[On "where lesser yang arrives, blazing heat"] The third qi, ministerial fire.

[On "where *yangming* arrives, clear sharpness"] The fifth qi, the transformation of metal.

[On "where greater yang arrives, cold vapor"] The final qi, the transformation of water.

[On "This is the constancy of the seasonal transformations"] The constant signs of the proper transformation of the qi of the four seasons.

## Notes

The manifestations of the six qi on arrival are described under twelve headings (the so-called twelve changes). First, seasonal transformation. Qibo's speech continues through unit 126.
