# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §132 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Name its position, and the direction and month can be known."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Name its position, and the direction and month can be known"] Determine the direction according to where the qi is, and divine by dividing into six; then there is no error in the days or in the divisions of the land.

## Notes

The answer that the direction and month are known from the position.
