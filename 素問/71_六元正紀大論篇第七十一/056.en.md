# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §56 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *xinwei* and *xinchou* years, above is greater yin earth, in the middle the lesser *yu* water movement, below greater yang water. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of rain and the transformation of wind are alike; these are the so-called days of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the first palace. The transformation of rain is five, that of cold one; these are the so-called days of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and hot; in the middle, bitter and harmonizing; below, bitter and hot. These are the so-called suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*xinwei*"] Same as year-meeting.

[On "In the *xinwei* and *xinchou* years"] Same as year-meeting.

[On "*xinchou* years, above is greater yin earth, in the middle the lesser *yu* water movement"] Song editors: On examination, water returns to proper *yu* only in the seventh month, *bingshen*.

[On "these are the so-called days of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the first palace"] Song editors: On examination, the first palace is the northern chamber, the position of Kan, governed by Tianxuan.

[On "Disaster is in the first palace. The transformation of rain is five, that of cold one"] Song editors: On examination, since the movement and the spring in earth are both water, it says only "cold one"; cold one is the transforming qi of lesser *yu*. If it were the transformation of greater yang in the spring, *xinwei* would have cold one and 辛五 [*xinchou*] cold six.

[On "Their treatment: above, bitter and hot ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "above, sour and harmonizing; below, sweet and warm". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when damp runs rampant and prevails, calm it with bitter and hot; when cold runs rampant within, treat it with sweet and hot".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *xinwei* and *xinchou* years.
- 辛五 in the Song editors' note is an error for 辛丑. 天玄 in the Song editors' note is taken as corresponding to Tianpeng, since in the nine stars of Dunjia the first palace (Kan) is governed by Tianpeng (the Dunjia section of the *Gujin tushu jicheng* has 天蓬星……常主一坎水休門).
