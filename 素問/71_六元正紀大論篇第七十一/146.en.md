# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §146 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Always comply with the season; those who have offended are treated with what overcomes."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Always comply with the season; those who have offended are treated with what overcomes"] Spring suits coolness, summer cold, autumn warmth, winter heat; these are the suitabilities of the seasons and must be complied with. But if heat has been offended with, treat with cold; if cold, treat with heat; if [the warmth of] spring, coolness should be used; if [the coolness of] autumn, warmth should be used. This is [treating] with what overcomes. Treating offense with heat by salty and cold, offense with cold by sweet and hot, offense with coolness by bitter and warm, offense with warmth by acrid and cool, is also the way of overcoming.

## Notes

The answer: comply with the season, and treat offenses with what overcomes.
