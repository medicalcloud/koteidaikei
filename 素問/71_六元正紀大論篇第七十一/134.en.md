# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §134 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Great and lesser differ. The arrival of the great is gradual and constant; the lesser is violent and vanishes."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The arrival of the great is gradual and constant; the lesser is violent and vanishes"] Though its force is strong when it acts, it cannot last long; hence it is violent and vanishes. 亡 means 無 ("none").

## Notes

The answer that great and lesser qi arrive differently.
