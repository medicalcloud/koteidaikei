# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §58 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *guiyou* and *guimao* years, above is *yangming* metal, in the middle the lesser *zhi* fire movement, below lesser yin fire. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of cold and the transformation of rain are alike; these are the so-called days of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the ninth palace. The transformation of dryness is nine, that of heat two; these are the so-called days of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and slightly warm; in the middle, salty and warm; below, salty and cold. These are the so-called suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*guiyou*"] Same as year-meeting.

[On "In the *guiyou* and *guimao* years"] Same as year-meeting.

[On "*guimao* years, above is *yangming* metal, in the middle the lesser *zhi* fire movement"] Song editors: On examination, fire returns to proper *zhi* on meeting the fifth month, *wuwu*.

[On "these are the so-called days of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the ninth palace"] Song editors: On examination, the ninth palace is the position of Li, the southern chamber, governed by Tianying.

[On "Disaster is in the ninth palace. The transformation of dryness is nine"] Song editors: On examination, in *guiyou* the transformation of dryness is four, in *guimao* nine.

[On "The transformation of dryness is nine, that of heat two"] Song editors: On examination, since the movement and the spring in earth are both fire, it says only "heat two"; heat two is the transformation of the lesser *zhi* movement. If it were the transformation of lesser yin in the spring, *guiyou* would have heat seven and *guimao* heat two.

[On "Their treatment: above, bitter and slightly warm ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "above, bitter and hot".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *guiyou* and *guimao* years.
