# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §78 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

[In the years] *guisi* and *guihai*, above is *jueyin* wood, in the middle the lesser *zhi* fire movement, below lesser yang ministerial fire. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of cold and the transformation of rain are alike; these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the ninth palace. The transformation of wind is eight, that of fire two; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, acrid and cool; in the middle, salty and harmonizing; below, salty and cold. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*guisi*"] Same as year-meeting.

[On "*guisi* and *guihai*"] Same as year-meeting.

[On "*guihai*, above is *jueyin* wood, in the middle the lesser *zhi* fire movement"] Song editors: On examination, *guisi* is proper *zhi*, with balanced fire qi: first, because *si* is fire and it is also called a year-meeting; second, because water has not yet obtained transformation; third, because in the fifth month, *wuwu*, *gui* obtains union with *wu*. Hence it obtains balanced qi. In the *guihai* year, *hai* is water; water gains the year's strength and comes to prevail; when the fifth month, *wuwu*, arrives, fire returns to proper *zhi* and its qi first becomes balanced.

[On "these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the ninth palace. The transformation of wind is eight"] Song editors: On examination, in *guisi* the transformation of wind is eight, in *guihai* three.

[On "Disaster is in the ninth palace. The transformation of wind is eight, that of fire two"] Song editors: On examination, since the movement and the spring in earth are both fire, it says only "fire two"; fire two is the transformation of the lesser *zhi* fire movement. If it were the transformation of lesser yang in the spring, *guisi* would have heat seven and *guihai* heat two.

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *guisi* and *guihai* years. The sixty-year list ends here.
- Unlike the other years, the main text lacks 歳 here. 已 in the Song editors' 已為火 is read as 巳.
