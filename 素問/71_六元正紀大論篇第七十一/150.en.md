# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §150 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Great accumulations and great gatherings may be offended against; reduce them by more than half and stop. Going beyond that is fatal."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Great accumulations and great gatherings may be offended against; reduce them by more than half and stop. Going beyond that is fatal"] Reducing them by more than half is not enough to harm life; hence stop the medicine when they are reduced by more than half. If one goes past the prohibition and waits until [the accumulation] is gone, toxic qi remains within with no illness left to attack, and [the body] receives the toxic medicine. If the toxin attacks without stopping, it ruins the central harmony; hence going beyond is fatal.
Song editors: On examination, this passage on the pregnant woman does not connect with the sense of the text before and after; perhaps a slip from another fascicle has been misplaced here.

## Notes

The answer that drastic medicine may be used for great accumulations but stopped once more than half is gone.
