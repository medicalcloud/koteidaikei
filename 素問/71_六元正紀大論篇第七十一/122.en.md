# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §122 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where *jueyin* arrives there is internal tension; where lesser yin arrives, sores, rashes and body heat; where greater yin arrives, accumulated fluid with blockage and separation; where lesser yang arrives, sneezing and retching, and sores; where *yangming* arrives, puffy emptiness; where greater yang arrives, difficulty bending and stretching. This is the constancy of illness.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Where *jueyin* arrives there is internal tension"] The sinews slacken and contract, hence tension.

[On "where lesser yin arrives, sores, rashes and body heat"] Fire qi arises.

[On "where greater yin arrives, accumulated fluid with blockage and separation"] Earth congeals.

[On "where lesser yang arrives, sneezing and retching, and sores"] Fire qi arises.

[On "where *yangming* arrives, puffy emptiness"] Puffy emptiness is a slight swelling that springs back when pressed.

## Notes

The ninth of the twelve changes: the constancy of illness.
