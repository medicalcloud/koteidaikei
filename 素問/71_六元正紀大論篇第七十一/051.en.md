# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §51 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *bingyin* and *bingshen* years, above is lesser yang ministerial fire, in the middle the great *yu* water movement, below *jueyin* wood. The transformation of fire is two, that of cold six, that of wind three; these are the so-called days of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, salty and cold; in the middle, salty and warm; below, acrid and warm. These are the so-called suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the *bingyin* and *bingshen* years"] Song editors: On examination, in the *bingshen* year the metal of *shen* generates water, the decree of water's transformation grows stronger, and illness from the ministerial fire controlling heaven is halved.

[On "in the middle the great *yu* water movement, below *jueyin* wood. The transformation of fire is two"] Song editors: On examination, in *bingyin* the transformation of fire is two, in *bingshen* seven.

[On "The transformation of fire is two, that of cold six, that of wind three"] Song editors: On examination, in *bingyin* the transformation of wind is eight, in *bingshen* three.

[On "Their treatment: above, salty and cold ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "below, acrid and cool". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when fire runs rampant and prevails, calm it with salty and cold; when wind runs rampant within, treat it with acrid and cool".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *bingyin* and *bingshen* years.
