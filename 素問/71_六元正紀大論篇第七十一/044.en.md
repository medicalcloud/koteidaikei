# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the controlling qi is heat, do not offend by using heat; when cold, do not offend by using cold; when coolness, do not offend by using coolness; when warmth, do not offend by using warmth. When the intervening qi is the same as its governor, do not offend; when different from its governor, offend slightly. These are called the four fears; examine them carefully."

## Notes

Do not use medicines of the same nature as the controlling qi (the four fears). There is no note by Wang Bing.
