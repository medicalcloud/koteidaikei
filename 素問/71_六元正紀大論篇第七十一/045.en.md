# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §45 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. What of offending them?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good. What of offending them?"] Cases where one must offend.

## Notes

A question on when offending is permissible.
