# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §67 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *renwu* and *renzi* years, above is lesser yin fire, in the middle the great *jue* wood movement, below *yangming* metal. The transformation of heat is two, that of wind eight, that of clarity four; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, salty and cold; in the middle, sour and cool; below, sour and warm. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "in the middle the great *jue* wood movement, below *yangming* metal. The transformation of heat is two"] Song editors: On examination, in *renwu* the transformation of heat is two, in *renzi* seven.

[On "The transformation of heat is two, that of wind eight, that of clarity four"] Song editors: On examination, in *renwu* the transformation of dryness is four, in *renzi* nine.

[On "Their treatment: above, salty and cold ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "below, bitter and hot". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when dryness runs rampant within, treat it with bitter and hot".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *renwu* and *renzi* years. There is no note by Wang Bing.
