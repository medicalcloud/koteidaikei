# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §64 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *jimao* and *jiyou* years, above is *yangming* metal, in the middle the lesser *gong* earth movement, below lesser yin fire. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of wind and the transformation of clarity are alike; these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the fifth palace. The transformation of clarity is nine, that of rain five, that of heat seven; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and slightly warm; in the middle, sweet and harmonizing; below, salty and cold. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*jimao*"] Song editors: On examination, the metal of *jimao* suits the earth of the movement; the child presiding over the father's position is contrary.

[On "*jiyou* years, above is *yangming* metal, in the middle the lesser *gong* earth movement"] Song editors: On examination, even after retaliation ends, earth qi is not yet proper; later, in the ninth month, *jiaxu*, earth returns to proper *gong*. In the *jiyou* year, wood's prevalence and fire are slight.

[On "these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the fifth palace. The transformation of clarity is nine"] Song editors: On examination, in *jimao* the transformation of dryness is nine, in *jiyou* four.

[On "The transformation of clarity is nine, that of rain five, that of heat seven"] Song editors: On examination, in *jimao* the transformation of heat is two, in *jiyou* seven.

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *jimao* and *jiyou* years.
- 巳卯，巳酉 in the main text are read as 己卯，己酉.
