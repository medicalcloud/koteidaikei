# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §75 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *gengyin* and *gengshen* years, above is lesser yang ministerial fire, in the middle the great *shang* metal movement, below *jueyin* wood. The transformation of fire is seven, that of clarity nine, that of wind three; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, salty and cold; in the middle, acrid and warm; below, acrid and cool. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*gengshen* years, above is lesser yang ministerial fire, in the middle the great *shang* metal movement"] Song editors: On examination, the *gengyin* year becomes proper *shang* and obtains balanced qi, because lesser yang ministerial fire appears above and conquers the metal below, so the movement cannot be excessive. In the *gengshen* year the metal of *shen* assists it, so it is great *shang*.

[On "in the middle the great *shang* metal movement, below *jueyin* wood. The transformation of fire is seven"] Song editors: On examination, in *gengyin* the transformation of heat is two, in *gengshen* seven.

[On "The transformation of fire is seven, that of clarity nine, that of wind three"] Song editors: On examination, in *gengyin* the transformation of wind is eight, in *gengshen* three.

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *gengyin* and *gengshen* years. There is no note by Wang Bing.
