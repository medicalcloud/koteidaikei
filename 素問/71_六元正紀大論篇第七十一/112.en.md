# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §112 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied, "The proper records of the six qi include transformation and change, prevalence and retaliation, function and illness, and their signs are not the same. Which does Your Majesty wish to know?"

## Notes

Qibo lists the aspects of the records of the six qi and asks what the Emperor wants. There is no note by Wang Bing.
