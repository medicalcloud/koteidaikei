# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §110 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The Emperor said, 'Good'"] The yin and yang of heaven and earth can be seen by looking. Why ponder the obscure, elaborate methods and search, exhausting wisdom and wearying the heart, only to gain nothing?

## Notes

The Emperor accepts the answer.
