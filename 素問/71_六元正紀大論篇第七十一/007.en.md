# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In all these [years] when greater [yang] controls heaven, the transformations of qi move ahead of heaven. Heaven's qi is austere and earth's qi still; cold presides over the great void, yang qi issues no decrees, water and earth unite their virtue, and above they correspond to the Chronograph Star and the Quelling Star. Its grains are black and yellow; its government austerity; its decree slowness. When the government of cold is greatly raised and the marshes lack yang blaze, fire breaks out in due time. When lesser yang governs in the middle, timely rain reaches its limit; when it stops at the extreme, rain disperses and returns to greater yin, clouds gather toward the north pole, the transformation of damp spreads and moistens the myriad things; cold spreads above and thunder moves below, and the qi of cold and damp hold in the intersection of qi. The people suffer from cold and damp, wasting of the flesh, limp feet unable to be drawn up, watery diarrhea and bleeding. In the first qi, earth's qi shifts, the qi becomes very warm, grasses flourish early, the people suffer epidemics, warm illnesses arise, with fever, headache, vomiting and sores in the interstices of the flesh. In the second qi, great coolness comes contrarily, the people are dismal, grasses meet cold, fire qi is suppressed, the people suffer pent-up qi and fullness in the center, and cold begins. In the third qi, heaven's government spreads, cold qi moves, rain falls, the people suffer cold, with heat in the center contrarily, carbuncles and abscesses, pouring diarrhea, heat in the heart and oppression; those untreated die. In the fourth qi, wind and damp contend, wind transforms into rain, and there is growth, transformation and completion; the people suffer great heat, shortness of qi, wasting of the flesh, limp feet, and red and white diarrhea. In the fifth qi, yang transforms again, grasses grow, transform and complete, and the people are at ease. In the final qi, earth's qi is upright, the decree of damp proceeds, yin congeals in the great void, dust darkens the countryside, the people are dismal and sad, and cold wind arrives; in contrary cases, pregnancies end in death. So for the year bitter is suitable to dry and warm; one must break the pent-up qi, first support the source of transformation, suppress the qi of the movement, assist what is not overcoming, and not let sudden excess produce illness; eat the grains of the year to keep the true intact, and avoid depleting pathogens to keep the upright at peace. Adjust to the sameness or difference of the qi and regulate the amount: for those the same as cold and damp, transform with dryness and heat; for those different from cold and damp, transform with dryness and damp; thus use more for the same and less for the different. In using cold, keep away from cold; in using coolness, keep away from coolness; in using warmth, keep away from warmth; in using heat, keep away from heat; food follows the same rule. When there is an exception, go against the norm; going against this brings illness. This is what is called [observing] the season."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In all these [years] when greater [yang] controls heaven, the transformations of qi move ahead of heaven"] The generation, growth, transformation, completion, gathering and storing of the qi of the six steps all arrive in response ahead of heaven's seasons. The same holds for the other years that are ahead of heaven.

[On "water and earth unite their virtue, and above they correspond to the Chronograph Star and the Quelling Star"] They are bright and large.

[On "Its grains are black and yellow"] What the proper qi of heaven and earth generate, grow, transform and complete. 黅 means yellow.

[On "When the government of cold is greatly raised ... fire breaks out in due time"] When cold is extreme, fire is pent up; waiting for the fourth qi, it breaks out violently as blazing heat.

[On "returns to greater yin, clouds gather toward the north pole, the transformation of damp spreads"] The north pole is the residence of rain.

[On "thunder moves below, and the qi of cold and damp hold in the intersection of qi"] The general character of the year's qi.

[On "The people suffer from cold and damp ... watery diarrhea and bleeding"] Song editors: On examination, bleeding is an illness produced by fire breaking out in due time.

[On "In the first qi, earth's qi shifts, the qi becomes very warm"] The dreaded fire brings this about.

[On "warm illnesses arise ... sores in the interstices of the flesh"] Red spots; these are sores in the skin's interstices, within the skin.

[On "In the second qi ... pent-up qi and fullness in the center, and cold begins"] From coolness it goes on to cold qi; hence cold qi first comes close to people.

[On "In the third qi ... those untreated die"] Being hot when it should be cold goes against heaven's norm. When heat arises in the heart, the spirit is in urgent danger; without urgent help it will surely perish. Hence those treated live and those untreated die.

[On "In the fifth qi, yang transforms again ... the people are at ease"] Great fire presides, so the myriad things unfold and flourish.

[On "in contrary cases, pregnancies end in death. So for the year bitter is suitable to dry and warm"] Song editors: On examination, the nine characters 故歳宜若〔苦〕以燥之温之 should come after 避虚邪以安其正; they are misplaced here.

[On "one must break the pent-up qi, first support the source of transformation"] The source of transformation means meeting and taking it in the ninth month to supplement heart fire.
Song editors: On examination, water is about to prevail. Meeting and taking its source of transformation first in the ninth month is first draining the source of the kidneys. Since water flourishes in the tenth month, it is met and taken first in the ninth month; draining water is the way to supplement fire.

[On "suppress the qi of the movement, assist what is not overcoming"] In great *jue* years the spleen does not overcome; in great *zhi* years the lungs; in great *gong* years the kidneys; in great *shang* years the liver; in great *yu* years the heart. Such is what suits each year. But the qi of the five years when greater yang controls heaven generally call for first helping the heart and then assisting the kidney qi.

[On "eat the grains of the year to keep the true intact, and avoid depleting pathogens to keep the upright at peace"] When wood is excessive, spleen illness arises; when fire is excessive, lung illness; when earth is excessive, kidney illness; when metal is excessive, liver illness; when water is excessive, heart illness. Excess of the qi of heaven and earth is likewise. The grains of the year means the yellow and black ones. Depleting pathogens means winds coming from the opposite direction behind.

[On "for those the same as cold and damp ... transform with dryness and damp"] Great *gong*, great *shang* and great *yu* years are the same as cold and damp and should be treated by transforming with dryness and heat; great *jue* and great *zhi* years differ from cold and damp and should be treated by transforming with dryness and damp.

[On "thus use more for the same and less for the different"] More means dryness and heat; less means dryness and damp. The amount used follows the year.

[On "In using cold, keep away from cold ... This is what is called [observing] the season"] Season means spring, summer, autumn and winter and where the intervening qi is. When they are the same, keep away from them. Even in that season, if the six qi preside and one borrows cold, heat, warmth or coolness to remove illness, one need not keep away. For example, when greater yang controls heaven and cold causes illness, borrow heat to treat it; then using heat need not avoid summer. The other qi follow the same example. Hence "when there is an exception, go against the norm". Food follows the same rule as medicine. Without the rule of borrowing and going against, it becomes the medium of illness and is not the way of prescription and nurturing life.
Song editors: Matters such as "in using cold, keep away from cold" and "when there is an exception, go against the norm" are given in full in the text below.

## Notes

The transformations of qi in years when greater yang controls heaven, the weather and illnesses of the six steps (first to final qi), treatment, and suitable food. Qibo's speech ends here.
- For 凡此太司天之政, all the other sources have 陽 (太陽) (see the variants); the translation supplies [yang].
- The Song editors consider 故歳宜苦以燥之温之 misplaced; their quotation writes 若 for 苦.
