# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §148 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "If there is a cause, there is no loss, and also no loss."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If there is a cause, there is no loss, and also no loss"] A cause means there is a large, hard concretion or conglomeration with unbearable pain; then it is treated with medicines that break accumulations and cure concretions. If she is not rescued, both will surely die. Rescuing her preserves the greater [the mother]. Even taking toxic [medicine], she will not die. The first "no loss" says the mother will surely be preserved; "also no loss" says the child also will not die.

## Notes

The answer that when there is illness, drastic medicine harms neither mother nor child.
