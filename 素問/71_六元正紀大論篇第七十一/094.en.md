# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §94 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "When water breaks out there is hail and snow; when earth breaks out, whirlwinds and squalls; when wood breaks out, destruction and breaking; when metal breaks out, clarity and brightness; when fire breaks out, haze and gloom. What qi makes it so?"

## Notes

A question about why the outbreaks of the five constraints differ in appearance. There is no note by Wang Bing.
