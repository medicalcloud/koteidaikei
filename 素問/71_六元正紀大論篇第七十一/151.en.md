# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §151 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. How are severe constraints treated?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How are severe constraints treated"] [This refers to] the five phases of heaven and earth, in accord with the movements, being severely repressed and unable to extend.

## Notes

A question about treating severe constraint.
