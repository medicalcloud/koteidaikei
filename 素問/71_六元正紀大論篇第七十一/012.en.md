# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The period of *yin* and *shen*. [Controller of heaven] lesser yang, [central movement] great *jue*, [spring in earth] *jueyin*; the years *renyin* and *renshen*. Its movement is drumming wind; its transformation sounding, stirring, opening and splitting; its change shaking, snapping, smashing and uprooting; its illness tremor, dizziness, propping of the flanks, fright and alarm. [Steps:] great *jue*, lesser *zhi*, great *gong*, lesser *shang*, great *yu*. Lesser yang, great *zhi*, *jueyin*; the years *wuyin* [heavenly tally] and *wushen* [heavenly tally]. Its movement is summer heat; its transformation warm clamor and sultry heat; its change fierce blaze and boiling; its illness pent-up heat above, bleeding upward and downward, and heart pain. [Steps:] great *zhi*, lesser *gong*, great *shang*, lesser *yu*, lesser *jue*. Lesser yang, great *gong*, *jueyin*; the years *jiayin* and *jiashen*. Its movement is yin rain; its transformation softness, moisture, heaviness and luster; its change quaking, alarm, gusts and downpours; its illness heaviness of the body, puffy swelling, obstruction and fluid retention. [Steps:] great *gong*, lesser *shang*, great *yu*, great *jue*, lesser *zhi*. Lesser yang, great *shang*, *jueyin*; the years *gengyin* and *gengshen*, the same as proper *shang*. Its movement is coolness; its transformation fog, dew, clarity and keenness; its change austere killing and withering; its illness in the shoulders, back and chest. [Steps:] great *shang*, lesser *yu*, lesser *jue*, great *zhi*, lesser *gong*. Lesser yang, great *yu*, *jueyin*; the years *bingyin* and *bingshen*. Its movement is cold and austerity; its transformation congealing bleakness and biting cold; its change ice, snow, frost and hail; its illness cold and edema. [Steps:] great *yu*, great *jue*, lesser *zhi*, great *gong*, lesser *shang*.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The period of *yin* and *shen*. Lesser yang, great *jue*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "when *zhi* is above, its qi counterflows".

[On "*renyin*"] Same as heavenly tally.

[On "*renshen*"] Same as heavenly tally.

[On "Its movement is drumming wind"] Song editors: On examination, wind and fire join forces; hence "its movement is drumming wind". The great *jue* movement with lesser yin controlling heaven is the same.

[On "its transformation sounding, stirring, opening and splitting"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "its virtue is sounding, bending, opening and splitting".

[On "great *jue*"] First; proper.

[On "great *yu*"] Last.

[On "Lesser yang, great *zhi*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "when *zhi* is above, the gathering qi is late".

[On "Its movement is summer heat; its transformation warm clamor and sultry heat"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* writes 暄暑鬯 [鬱] 燠. Here 暑 is changed to 囂 because lesser yang presides above.

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First.

[On "great *yu*"] Last.

[On "great *jue*"] First.

[On "*gengyin* and *gengshen*, the same as proper *shang*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of firm completion, when *zhi* is above, it is the same as proper *shang*".

[On "Its movement is coolness; its transformation fog, dew, clarity and keenness"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "fog, dew and bleak wind". Also, of the three 大 [great] *shang* movements, two say "bleak wind" and only this one "clarity and keenness". On examination, this too, like the [movement with] *jueyin* [controlling heaven], should be "bleak wind" [?].

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First.

[On "*bingyin* and *bingshen*. Its movement is cold and austerity"] Song editors: On examination, this movement should not be called "cold and austere"; [the explanation] is given in the note on the great *yu* movement with greater yang controlling heaven.

[On "its transformation congealing bleakness and biting cold"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* writes "congealing bleakness and cold mist".

[On "great *yu*"] Last.

[On "great *jue*"] First.

## Notes

For the ten years (*yin* and *shen*) when lesser yang controls heaven and *jueyin* is in the spring, the movement, transformation, change and illness for each central movement, and the five steps of the movement, set out as a table. Qibo's speech continues into unit 013.
- Several of the Song editors' notes are cut off in the base text's input, with the continuation appearing as fragments labeled as Wang Bing's notes (按五常政大論 + 云其徳鳴靡啓坼; 堅成之紀上徴与正商 + 同; ……独此言清切詳 + 此下如厥陰当此蕭飋; 詳此運不当言寒粛以注 + 太陽司天太羽運中). The translation renders each as a single Song editors' note.
- 此下如厥陰当此蕭飋 is hard to construe and is marked [?]. 鬯 in the Song editors' 鬯燠 is presumably an error for 鬱.
