# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §100 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Why does qi arrive early or late?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why does qi arrive early or late"] This refers to such cases as arriving too early when it should not yet arrive, or arriving too late when it should arrive; precisely, the arrival of qi before or after its due time.

## Notes

A question about early and late arrival of qi.
