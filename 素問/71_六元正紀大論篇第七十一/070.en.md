# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §70 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *yiyou* and *yimao* years, above is *yangming* metal, in the middle the lesser *shang* metal movement, below lesser yin fire. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of heat and the transformation of cold are alike; these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the seventh palace. The transformation of dryness is four, that of clarity four, that of heat two; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and slightly warm; in the middle, bitter and harmonizing; below, salty and cold. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*yiyou*"] Supreme-one heavenly tally.

[On "In the *yiyou* and *yimao* years"] Heavenly tally.

[On "*yimao* years, above is *yangming* metal, in the middle the lesser *shang* metal movement"] Song editors: *Yiyou* is proper *shang*; assisted by the metal of *you*, it obtains balanced qi. In the *yimao* year, in the division of the sovereign fire of the second qi, fire comes to prevail; water has not yet retaliated, and the qi is balanced thereby. In the third month, *gengchen*, *yi* obtains *geng* and unites into the metal movement, becoming proper *shang*, and its qi is balanced.

[On "these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the seventh palace. The transformation of dryness is four"] Song editors: On examination, in *yiyou* the transformation of dryness is four, in *yimao* nine.

[On "The transformation of dryness is four, that of clarity four, that of heat two"] Song editors: On examination, in *yiyou* the transformation of heat is seven, in *yimao* two.

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *yiyou* and *yimao* years.
