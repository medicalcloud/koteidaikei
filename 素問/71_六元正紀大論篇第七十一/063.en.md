# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §63 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *wuyin* and *wushen* years, above is lesser yang ministerial fire, in the middle the great *zhi* fire movement, below *jueyin* wood. The transformation of fire is seven, that of wind three; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, salty and cold; in the middle, sweet and harmonizing; below, acrid and cool. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the *wuyin* and *wushen* years"] Heavenly tally.
Song editors: On examination, the *wushen* year differs slightly from the *wuyin* year: *shen* is metal and assists the lungs; though the lungs are punished by fire, their qi is somewhat replete, and the people's illness is halved.

[On "in the middle the great *zhi* fire movement, below *jueyin* wood. The transformation of fire is seven"] Song editors: On examination, in a heavenly tally the controller of heaven matches the movement, so it says only "fire seven"; fire seven is the qi of the great *zhi* movement. If it were the qi of lesser yang controlling heaven, *wuyin* would have fire two and *wushen* fire seven.

[On "below *jueyin* wood. The transformation of fire is seven, that of wind three"] Song editors: On examination, in *wuyin* the transformation of wind is eight, in *wushen* three.

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *wuyin* and *wushen* years.
