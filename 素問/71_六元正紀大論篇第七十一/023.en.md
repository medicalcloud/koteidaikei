# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. Your words, Master, may be called thorough. But how are their responses made clear?"

## Notes

A question on how the responses of the six qi are made clear. There is no note by Wang Bing.
