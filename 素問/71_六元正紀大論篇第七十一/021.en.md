# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The period of *si* and *hai*. [Controller of heaven] *jueyin*, [central movement] lesser *jue*, [spring in earth] lesser yang; prevalence and retaliation by clarity and heat alike; the same as proper *jue*. The years *dingsi* [heavenly tally] and *dinghai* [heavenly tally]. Its movement is wind, clarity and heat. [Steps:] lesser *jue*, great *zhi*, lesser *gong*, great *shang*, lesser *yu*. *Jueyin*, lesser *zhi*, lesser yang; prevalence and retaliation by cold and rain alike. The years *guisi* and *guihai*. Its movement is heat, cold and rain. [Steps:] lesser *zhi*, great *gong*, lesser *shang*, great *yu*, great *jue*. *Jueyin*, lesser *gong*, lesser yang; prevalence and retaliation by wind and clarity alike; the same as proper *jue*. The years *jisi* and *jihai*. Its movement is rain, wind and clarity. [Steps:] lesser *gong*, great *shang*, lesser *yu*, lesser *jue*, great *zhi*. *Jueyin*, lesser *shang*, lesser yang; prevalence and retaliation by heat and cold alike; the same as proper *jue*. The years *yisi* and *yihai*. Its movement is coolness, heat and cold. [Steps:] lesser *shang*, great *yu*, great *jue*, lesser *zhi*, great *gong*. *Jueyin*, lesser *yu*, lesser yang; prevalence and retaliation by rain and wind alike. The years *xinsi* and *xinhai*. Its movement is cold, rain and wind. [Steps:] lesser *yu*, lesser *jue*, great *zhi*, lesser *gong*, great *shang*.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*jueyin*, lesser *jue*, lesser yang; prevalence and retaliation by clarity and heat alike; the same as proper *jue*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of subdued harmony, upper *jue* is the same as proper *jue*".

[On "*dinghai* heavenly tally. Its movement is wind, clarity and heat. lesser *jue*"] First; proper.

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "*guisi*"] Same as year-meeting.

[On "*guihai*"] Same as year-meeting.

[On "lesser *zhi*, great *gong*, lesser *shang*, great *yu*"] Last.

[On "great *jue*"] First.

[On "*Jueyin*, lesser *gong*, lesser yang; prevalence and retaliation by wind and clarity alike; the same as proper *jue*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of humble supervision, upper *jue* is the same as proper *jue*".

[On "lesser *gong*, great *shang*, lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First.

[On "*Jueyin*, lesser *shang*, lesser yang; prevalence and retaliation by heat and cold alike; the same as proper *jue*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of compliant change, upper *jue* is the same as proper *jue*".

[On "lesser *shang*, great *yu*"] Last.

[On "great *jue*"] First.

[On "*xinsi* and *xinhai*. Its movement is cold, rain and wind. lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First.

## Notes

For the ten years (*si* and *hai*) when *jueyin* controls heaven and lesser yang is in the spring, the prevalence and retaliation, the movement and its five steps for each central movement, set out as a table. Qibo's speech continues into unit 022.
- Several of the Song editors' notes are cut off in the base text's input, with the continuation appearing as fragments labeled as Wang Bing's notes (角与正角同). The translation renders each as a single Song editors' note.
- For the prevalence and retaliation of *yisi* and *yihai*, Wikisource has an extra 寒 before 熱寒 (see the variants).
