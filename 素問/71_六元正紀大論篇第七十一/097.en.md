# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §97 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "It is called the deviation."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is called the deviation"] It means deviating from the positions of the proper months of the four seasons.
Song editors: According to the *Zhizhen yao dalun*: "[The Emperor asked,] 'When prevalence and retaliation arise, they move without matching their positions, or arrive later than their time; why is this?' Qibo said, 'The generation and transformation of qi differ from their decline and abundance. The workings of the abundance and decline of cold, heat, warmth and coolness lie at the four corners. Hence the movement of yang begins in warmth and flourishes in heat; the movement of yin begins in coolness and flourishes in cold. Spring, summer, autumn and winter each deviate from their divisions. Hence the *Great Essentials* says: "The warmth of spring becomes the heat of summer; the resentment of autumn becomes the rage of winter. Carefully examine the four corners and observe them, and all return to their end; their beginning can be seen and known."'" That treatise discusses prevalence and retaliation not matching their positions; this one discusses the outbreaks of the five qi not matching their positions. The matters discussed, prevalence and retaliation versus the five outbreaks, differ, but the meaning of "it is called the deviation" is the same.

## Notes

The answer that the deviation is called 差.
