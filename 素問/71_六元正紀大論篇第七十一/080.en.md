# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §80 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. Does the qi of the five movements also retaliate within the year?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Does the qi of the five movements also retaliate within the year"] 復 means requiting. If there is first prevailing and restraining, there must afterwards be retaliation.

## Notes

The question whether the qi of the five movements also retaliates.
