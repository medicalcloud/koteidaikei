# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §126 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All these twelve changes requite virtue with virtue, transformation with transformation, governance with governance, decree with decree. When the qi is high, [illness] is high; when the qi is low, low; when the qi is behind, behind; when the qi is in front, in front; when the qi is in the middle, in the middle; when the qi is outside, outside. This is the constancy of positions. Hence when wind prevails there is movement; when heat prevails, swelling; when dryness prevails, dryness; when cold prevails, puffiness; when damp prevails, soggy diarrhea, and in severe cases water blockage with puffy swelling. One simply speaks of their changes according to where the qi is."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "when the qi is in front, in front ... This is the constancy of positions"] The qi requiting virtue and transformation means the qi of heaven and earth. High and low, front and back, middle and outside refer to where illness arises. The yin and yang [channels] of the hand have high qi; those of the foot have low qi. The qi of foot greater yang is at the back of the body, that of foot *yangming* at the front, those of foot greater yin, lesser yin and *jueyin* in the middle, that of foot lesser yang at the side. Each speaks, according to its location, of the images of illness produced by changes of qi.

[On "Hence when wind prevails there is movement"] Movement means unrest.
Song editors: On examination, the five phrases from "when wind prevails there is movement" to "when damp prevails, soggy diarrhea" duplicate the text of the *Yinyang yingxiang dalun*, but the two notes differ.

[On "when heat prevails, swelling"] When heat prevails over qi, there is erysipelas; over blood, abscess and pus; over bone and flesh, puffy swelling that does not spring back when pressed.

[On "when dryness prevails, dryness"] Dryness outside makes the skin chapped and cracked; dryness inside makes essence and blood wither; dryness of qi and fluids makes the flesh dry and the skin cling to the bones.

[On "when cold prevails, puffiness"] Puffiness means swelling up, with the pressed spot remaining visible.

[On "when damp prevails, soggy diarrhea, and in severe cases water blockage with puffy swelling"] Soggy diarrhea is watery diarrhea. Puffy swelling is flesh like mud that sinks when pressed and does not rise. When water is blocked, it escapes into the skin.

## Notes

A summary of the twelve changes: the location of the qi determines where illness occurs. Qibo's speech ends here.
