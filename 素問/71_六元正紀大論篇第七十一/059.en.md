# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §59 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *jiaxu* and *jiachen* years, above is greater yang water, in the middle the great *gong* earth movement, below greater yin earth. The transformation of cold is six, that of damp five; these are days of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and hot; in the middle, bitter and warm; below, bitter and warm. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*jiaxu*"] Year-meeting; same as heavenly tally.

[On "In the *jiaxu* and *jiachen* years"] Year-meeting; same as heavenly tally.

[On "in the middle the great *gong* earth movement, below greater yin earth. The transformation of cold is six"] Song editors: On examination, in *jiaxu* the transformation of cold is one, in *jiachen* six.

[On "The transformation of cold is six, that of damp five"] Song editors: On examination, since the movement and the spring in earth are both earth, it says only "damp five".

[On "Their treatment: above, bitter and hot ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "above, sweet and warm; below, sour and balanced". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when cold runs rampant and prevails, calm it with acrid and hot; when damp runs rampant within, treat it with bitter and hot".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *jiaxu* and *jiachen* years.
