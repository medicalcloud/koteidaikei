# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §60 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *yihai* and *yisi* years, above is *jueyin* wood, in the middle the lesser *shang* metal movement, below lesser yang ministerial fire. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of heat and the transformation of cold are alike; these are days of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the seventh palace. The transformation of wind is eight, that of clarity four, that of fire two; these are degrees [days] of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, acrid and cool; in the middle, sour and harmonizing; below, salty and cold. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*yisi* years, above is *jueyin* wood, in the middle the lesser *shang* metal movement"] Song editors: On examination, in the *yihai* year, in the third month it obtains the *gengchen* month and early meets the virtue tally, and the qi returns to proper *shang*. Fire is balanced before it flourishes; since fire does not prevail, water does not retaliate. Also *hai* is water and this is a year in which [water] gains strength, so fire does not prevail. In the *yisi* year fire comes and prevails slightly, fire assisting the prevalence: in the second month, on the days of the sovereign fire's transformation at the central solar period, fire comes to prevail, and without waiting for water's retaliation, on meeting the third month, *gengchen*, *yi* sees *geng*, the qi becomes complete of itself, and metal returns to proper *shang*.

[On "these are days of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the seventh palace. The transformation of wind is eight"] Song editors: On examination, in *yihai* the transformation of wind is three, in *yisi* eight.

[On "The transformation of wind is eight, that of clarity four, that of fire two"] Song editors: On examination, in *yihai* the transformation of heat is two, in *yisi* seven.

[On "these are degrees of proper transformation"] 度 means days.

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *yihai* and *yisi* years. From here on, 度 ("degree") is used instead of 日 ("day") in "days of proper/pathogenic transformation".
