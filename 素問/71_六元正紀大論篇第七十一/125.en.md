# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §125 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where *jueyin* arrives there is rib-side pain, vomiting and diarrhea; where lesser yin arrives, talking and laughing; where greater yin arrives, heaviness and puffy swelling; where lesser yang arrives, sudden pouring diarrhea, twitching and spasms, and sudden death; where *yangming* arrives, stuffy nose and sneezing; where greater yang arrives, flowing discharge or retention. This is the constancy of illness.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Where *jueyin* arrives there is rib-side pain, vomiting and diarrhea"] 泄 means diarrhea.

[On "where greater yin arrives, heaviness and puffy swelling"] Puffy swelling means the flesh is like mud and does not spring back when pressed.

## Notes

The twelfth of the twelve changes: the constancy of illness.
- 𥬇 is read as a variant of 笑.
