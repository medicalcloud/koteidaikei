# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §105 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The excessive matches its time; the insufficient reverts to what overcomes it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The excessive matches its time; the insufficient reverts to what overcomes it"] Rain in winter, coolness in spring, heat in autumn, cold in winter [summer], and the like, are all reverting to what overcomes oneself.

## Notes

The answer that in insufficient years the transformation of what overcomes appears.
- 已 in 已勝 in the main text and Wang Bing's note is read as 己.
- 冬寒 in Wang Bing's note is read as 夏寒 ("cold in summer"), since Wang Bing's note as quoted in the medical section of the *Gujin tushu jicheng* has 冬雨，春涼，秋熱，夏寒之類.
