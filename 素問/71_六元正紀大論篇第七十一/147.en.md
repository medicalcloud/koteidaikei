# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §147 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked, "What if a pregnant woman is given toxic [medicine]?"

## Notes

A question about giving drastic medicines to a pregnant woman. There is no note by Wang Bing. In unit 150 the Song editors say this passage (units 147–150) does not connect with the text before and after and may be a misplaced slip from another fascicle.
