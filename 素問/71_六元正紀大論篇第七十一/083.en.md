# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §83 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The qi of the five constants break out differently according to excess and insufficiency."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The qi of the five constants break out differently according to excess and insufficiency"] When the year is excessive, the outbreak is early; when the year is insufficient, it is late.

## Notes

The answer that outbreaks differ by excess and insufficiency.
