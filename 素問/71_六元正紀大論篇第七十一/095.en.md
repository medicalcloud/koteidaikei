# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §95 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Qi may be more or less, and outbreaks slight or severe. The slight matches its own qi; the severe includes what is below it. Take the qi below it as the sign, and what appears can be known."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the severe includes what is below it. Take the qi below it as the sign, and what appears can be known"] Below each of the six qi there is a qi that receives it: below the position of fire, water qi receives it; below the position of water, earth qi; below the position of earth, wood qi; below the position of wood, metal qi; below the position of metal, fire qi; below the position of the sovereign, yin clarity receives it. When each takes what is below it as the sign, the image can be seen. Hence when an outbreak includes what is below it, it differs from its own qi.

## Notes

The answer that severe outbreaks include the receiving qi below them and so look different.
