# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §48 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. In the record of the five movements governing the years, are there constant numbers?"

## Notes

A question on the numbers of movement and qi for each year of the sixty-year cycle. There is no note by Wang Bing.
