# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §111 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked, "How do the responses of the five movements and six qi appear, and what are the norms of the six transformations and the records of the six changes?"

## Notes

The beginning of a new dialogue: a question about the appearance of the five movements and six qi, the norms of the six transformations and the records of the six changes. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- The base text has 之運見, but 運 is presumably an error for 応 (some editions are said to read 応見, but this has not been verified). The translation reads 応.
