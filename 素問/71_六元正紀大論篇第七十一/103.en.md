# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §103 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When it is neither excessive nor insufficient, it arrives at the proper time. What is not so is calamity."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When it is neither excessive nor insufficient ... What is not so is calamity"] "The proper time" means the appointed day and mark. When [qi] arrives early or late though it should not, all is calamity. 眚 means disaster.

## Notes

The answer that balanced qi arrives on time, and other cases are calamity.
