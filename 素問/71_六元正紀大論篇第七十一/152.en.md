# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §152 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "For wood constraint, make it reach through; for fire constraint, release it; for earth constraint, take it away; for metal constraint, drain it; for water constraint, break it. Then regulate the qi. What is excessive, break it with what it fears; this is what is called draining."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "For wood constraint, make it reach through ... Then regulate the qi"] Reaching through means inducing vomiting to make it flow freely. Releasing means inducing sweating to disperse it. Taking away means purging so that there is no obstruction. Draining means percolating drainage, resolving the exterior and promoting urination. Breaking means suppressing it to control its surging counterflow. By these five methods the qi can be balanced and regulated; afterwards observe its deficiency and abundance and regulate it.

[On "What is excessive, break it with what it fears; this is what is called draining"] Excessive means overly abundant. The overly abundant is drained by its flavor: salty drains the kidneys, sour the liver, acrid the lungs, sweet the spleen, bitter the heart. The excessive fears draining; hence draining is called "what it fears".

## Notes

The answer giving the five treatments for the five constraints.
