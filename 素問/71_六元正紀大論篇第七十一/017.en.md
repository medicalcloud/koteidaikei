# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. What is the government of lesser yin?"

## Notes

A question on the government in years when lesser yin controls heaven. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- In the base text this unit was displaced later by a page transposition; it has been restored to the correct order (see the emendation note in unit 016).
