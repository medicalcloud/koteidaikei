# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The period of *mao* and *you*. [Controller of heaven] *yangming*, [central movement] lesser *jue*, [spring in earth] lesser yin; prevalence and retaliation by clarity and heat alike; the same as proper *shang*. The years *dingmao* [year-meeting] and *dingyou*. Its movement is wind, clarity and heat. [Steps:] lesser *jue*, great *zhi*, lesser *gong*, great *shang*, lesser *yu*. *Yangming*, lesser *zhi*, lesser yin; prevalence and retaliation by cold and rain alike; the same as proper *shang*. The years *guimao* and *guiyou*. Its movement is heat, cold and rain. [Steps:] lesser *zhi*, great *gong*, lesser *shang*, great *yu*, great *jue*. *Yangming*, lesser *gong*, lesser yin; prevalence and retaliation by wind and coolness alike. The years *jimao* and *jiyou*. Its movement is rain, wind and coolness. [Steps:] lesser *gong*, great *shang*, lesser *yu*, lesser *jue*, great *zhi*. *Yangming*, lesser *shang*, lesser yin; prevalence and retaliation by heat and cold alike; the same as proper *shang*. The years *yimao* [heavenly tally] and *yiyou* [year-meeting, supreme-one heavenly tally]. Its movement is coolness, heat and cold. [Steps:] lesser *shang*, great *yu*, great *jue*, lesser *zhi*, great *gong*. *Yangming*, lesser *yu*, lesser yin; prevalence and retaliation by rain and wind alike; *xinmao* is the same as lesser *gong*. The years *xinyou* and *xinmao*. Its movement is cold, rain and wind. [Steps:] lesser *yu*, lesser *jue*, great *zhi*, great [lesser] *gong*, great *shang*.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*yangming*, lesser *jue*, lesser yin; prevalence and retaliation by clarity and heat alike; the same as proper *shang*"] Clarity overcomes lesser *jue*, and heat retaliates against the qi of clarity; hence "prevalence and retaliation by clarity and heat alike". The other lesser movements are all the same. "The same as proper *shang*" means *yangming* appears above and upper *shang* is the same as proper *shang*, the year's wood being deficient. The others follow this.
Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of subdued harmony, upper *shang* is the same as proper *shang*".

[On "The years *dingmao* year-meeting and *dingyou*. Its movement is wind, clarity and heat"] A deficient movement is always stated together with the qi of prevalence and retaliation: wind is the qi of the movement, clarity the prevailing qi, heat the retaliating qi. The other lesser movements are all the same.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First; proper.

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "*Yangming*, lesser *zhi*, lesser yin; prevalence and retaliation by cold and rain alike; the same as proper *shang*"] Song editors: [The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says] "in the period of hidden brightness, upper *shang* is the same as proper *shang*".

[On "The years *guimao* and *guiyou*"] Same as year-meeting.
Song editors: The text below in this treatise says "deficiency that is added is the same as year-meeting". This movement is lesser *zhi*, deficient, and lesser yin is added below; hence "same as year-meeting".

[On "great *yu*"] Last.

[On "great *jue*"] First.

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First.

[On "*Yangming*, lesser *shang*, lesser yin; prevalence and retaliation by heat and cold alike; the same as proper *shang*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of compliant change, upper *shang* is the same as proper *shang*".

[On "*yimao* heavenly tally, *yiyou* year-meeting, supreme-one heavenly tally"] Song editors: The *Tianyuan ji dalun* says "the three combining is governance", and the *Liuwei zhi dalun* says "a heavenly tally that is also a year-meeting is called supreme-one heavenly tally". Wang Bing says "this is called the three combining: first the meeting with heaven, second the meeting with the year, third the meeting with the movement". Some say "since this year is the three combining and called supreme-one heavenly tally, it should not also be called year-meeting"; this is quite wrong. *Yiyou* is originally a year-meeting and is also a supreme-one heavenly tally, so the name year-meeting cannot be removed. Some ask "why are *jichou*, *jiwei* and *wuwu* not also called year-meetings but only supreme-one heavenly tallies?" The answer: when one corner is raised, one should return the other three; raising one, the three can be known. If it were removed, even a supreme-one heavenly tally would not be a year-meeting; hence it cannot be removed.

[On "lesser *shang*, great *yu*"] Last.

[On "great *jue*"] First.

[On "*Yangming*, lesser *yu*, lesser yin; prevalence and retaliation by rain and wind alike; *xinmao* is the same as lesser *gong*"] Song editors: According to the *Wuchang zheng dalun*, among the five deficient movements, apart from those the same as proper *jue*, proper *shang* and proper *gong*, *guichou* and *guiwei* have lesser *zhi* the same as lesser *yu*; 巳 [*ji*]*mao* and 乙 [*ji*]*you* have lesser *gong* the same as lesser *jue*; *yichou* and *yiwei* have lesser *shang* the same as lesser *zhi*; *xinmao*, *xinyou*, *xinsi* and *xinhai* have lesser *yu* the same as lesser *gong*—ten years in all. Now this treatise says "the same as lesser *gong*" only here, presumably because for *guichou* and *guiwei*, *chou* and *wei* are earth, so they are not further made the same as lesser *yu*; *jimao* and 巳 [*ji*]*you* are [*yangming*] metal [below], so they are not further made the same as lesser *jue*; *xinsi* and *xinhai* are great *zhi*, so they are not further made the same as lesser *gong*; *yichou* and *yiwei* have greater yang, water, appearing below, so they are not further made the same as lesser *zhi*. Apart from these eight years, only the two years *xinmao* and *xinyou* have lesser *yu* the same as lesser *gong*.

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First.

## Notes

For the ten years (*mao* and *you*) when *yangming* controls heaven and lesser yin is in the spring, the prevalence and retaliation, the movement and its five steps for each central movement, set out as a table. Qibo's speech continues into unit 010.
- 巳卯 and 巳酉 in the main text are read as 己卯 and 己酉.
- In the last row of steps, 殆知閣 (重广补注) has 少宮 for 太宮 (see the variants); the translation shows [lesser] alongside.
- Several of the Song editors' notes are cut off in the base text's input, with the continuation appearing as fragments labeled as Wang Bing's notes (従革之紀上商与 + 正商同; 按天元紀大論……六微旨大論云 + 天符歳会曰太一天符……; 按五常政大論 + 云五運不及……, etc.). Wang Bing's own notes 清熱勝復同也余少運皆 + 同也同正商者…… and 同歳 + 会 are also continuous. The translation renders each as one note.
- 巳卯 and 乙酉 in the Song editors' note are read as 己卯 and 己酉 from the context.
