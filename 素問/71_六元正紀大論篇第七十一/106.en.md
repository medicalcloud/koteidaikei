# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §106 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "The qi of the four seasons arrive early or late, high or low, left or right. What are their signs?"

## Notes

A question about the different ways the seasonal qi arrive. There is no note by Wang Bing.
