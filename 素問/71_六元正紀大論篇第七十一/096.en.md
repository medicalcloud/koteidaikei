# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §96 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Good. Why do the outbreaks of the five qi not match their positions?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why do the outbreaks of the five qi not match their positions"] It means not matching their proper months.

## Notes

A question about why the outbreaks of the five qi deviate from their proper times.
