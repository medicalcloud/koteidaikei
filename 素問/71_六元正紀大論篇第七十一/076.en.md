# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §76 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *xinmao* and *xinyou* years, above is *yangming* metal, in the middle the lesser *yu* water movement, below lesser yin fire. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of rain and the transformation of wind are alike; these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the first palace. The transformation of clarity is nine, that of cold one, that of heat seven; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and slightly warm; in the middle, bitter and harmonizing; below, salty and cold. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*xinyou* years, above is *yangming* metal, in the middle the lesser *yu* water movement"] Song editors: On examination, in this year water returns to proper *yu* in the seventh month, *bingshen*.

[On "these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the first palace. The transformation of clarity is nine"] Song editors: On examination, in *xinmao* the transformation of dryness is nine, in *xinyou* four.

[On "The transformation of clarity is nine, that of cold one, that of heat seven"] Song editors: On examination, in *xinmao* the transformation of heat is two, in *xinyou* seven.

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *xinmao* and *xinyou* years. There is no note by Wang Bing.
