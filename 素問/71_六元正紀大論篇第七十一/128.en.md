# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §128 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The functions of the six qi each go to what cannot overcome [them] and become transformation. Hence the rain transformation of greater yin is bestowed on greater yang, the cold transformation of greater yang on lesser yin, the heat transformation of lesser yin on *yangming*, the dry transformation of *yangming* on *jueyin*, the wind transformation of *jueyin* on greater yin. In each case name where it is and take that as the sign."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The functions of the six qi each go to what cannot overcome [them] and become transformation"] Function means bestowing their transforming qi.

[On "Hence the rain transformation of greater yin is bestowed on greater yang, the cold transformation of greater yang on lesser yin"] Song editors: On examination, this should say "lesser yin and lesser yang".

## Notes

The answer that each of the six qi bestows its transformation where the qi it overcomes is.
- The Song editors seem to mean that since cold's transformation is bestowed on fire, not only lesser yin (sovereign fire) but also lesser yang (ministerial fire) should be named; but this has not been verified.
