# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §71 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *bingxu* and *bingchen* years, above is greater yang water, in the middle the great *yu* water movement, below greater yin earth. The transformation of cold is six, that of rain five; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and hot; in the middle, salty and warm; below, sweet and hot. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*bingxu*"] Heavenly tally.

[On "In the *bingxu* and *bingchen* years"] Heavenly tally.

[On "in the middle the great *yu* water movement, below greater yin earth. The transformation of cold is six"] Song editors: On examination, since the movement and the controller of heaven are both water, it says only "cold six"; cold six is the transformation of the great *yu* movement. If it were the transformation of 大 [greater] yang controlling heaven, *bingxu* would have cold one and *bingchen* cold six.

[On "Their treatment: above, bitter and hot ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "above, sweet and warm; below, sour and balanced". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when cold runs rampant and prevails, calm it with acrid and hot; when damp runs rampant within, treat it with bitter and hot".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *bingxu* and *bingchen* years.
