# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §54 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *jisi* and *jihai* years, above is *jueyin* wood, in the middle the lesser *gong* earth movement, below lesser yang ministerial fire. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of wind and the transformation of clarity are alike; these are the so-called days of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the fifth palace. The transformation of wind is three, that of damp five, that of fire seven; these are the so-called days of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, acrid and cool; in the middle, sweet and harmonizing; below, salty and cold. These are the so-called suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*jihai* years, above is *jueyin* wood, in the middle the lesser *gong* earth movement"] Song editors: On examination, only when the ninth month, *jiaxu*, is reached does 己 [*ji*] obtain *jiaxu* and return to proper *gong*.

[On "these are the so-called days of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the fifth palace"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "its calamity is at the four corners". Also the *Tianyuan yuce* says "the central chamber, governed by Tianqin". It is not a corner palace, the same as proper *gong*, lodged in the second palace, the position of Kun.

[On "Disaster is in the fifth palace. The transformation of wind is three"] Song editors: On examination, in 巳 [*ji*]*si* the transformation of wind is eight, in 巳 [*ji*]*hai* three.

[On "The transformation of wind is three, that of damp five, that of fire seven"] Song editors: On examination, in 巳 [*ji*]*si* the transformation of heat is seven, in 巳 [*ji*]*hai* two.

[On "Their treatment: above, acrid and cool ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when wind runs rampant and prevails, calm it with acrid and cool; when fire runs rampant within, treat it with salty and cold".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *jisi* and *jihai* years.
- 巳巳，巳亥 in the main text are read as 己巳，己亥, as the other sources write (see the variants). 巳巳, 巳亥 and 巳得 in the Song editors' notes are also read with 己.
- The Song editors' note on the fifth palace is partly hard to construe.
