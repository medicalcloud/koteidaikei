# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §55 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *gengwu* and *gengzi* years, above is lesser yin fire, in the middle the great *shang* metal movement, below *yangming* metal. The transformation of heat is seven, that of clarity nine, that of dryness nine; these are the so-called days of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, salty and cold; in the middle, acrid and warm; below, sour and warm. These are the so-called suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*gengwu*"] Same as heavenly tally.

[On "In the *gengwu* and *gengzi* years"] Same as heavenly tally.

[On "*gengzi* years, above is lesser yin fire, in the middle the great *shang* metal movement"] Song editors: On examination, in the *gengwu* year the decree of metal is halved, because lesser yin sovereign fire appears above and the year's *wu* is also fire. In the *gengzi* year, *zi* is water and the qi of metal suit it, so it differs from the *gengwu* year.

[On "in the middle the great *shang* metal movement, below *yangming* metal. The transformation of heat is seven"] Song editors: On examination, in the *gengwu* year heat is two and dryness four; in the *gengzi* year heat is seven and dryness nine.

[On "Their treatment: above, salty and cold ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "below, bitter and hot". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when dryness runs rampant 干 [within], treat it with bitter and hot".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *gengwu* and *gengzi* years.
- 干 in the Song editors' 燥淫干内 is an error for 于.
