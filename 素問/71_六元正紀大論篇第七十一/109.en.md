# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §109 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Spring qi moves west, summer qi moves north, autumn qi moves east, winter qi moves south. Hence spring qi begins below, autumn qi begins above, summer qi begins in the middle, winter qi begins at the tip; spring qi begins on the left, autumn qi on the right, winter qi at the back, summer qi at the front. This is the constancy of the proper transformation of the four seasons. Hence on the highest ground winter qi is always present; on the lowest ground spring qi is always present. You must examine this carefully."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Spring qi moves west, summer qi moves north, autumn qi moves east, winter qi moves south"] Observing the birth, growth, gathering and storing of the myriad things, it is as these words say.

[On "winter qi at the back, summer qi at the front. This is the constancy of the proper transformation of the four seasons"] It can be known by examining things to clarify it.

[On "Hence on the highest ground winter qi is always present; on the lowest ground spring qi is always present"] On the summits of high mountains there is ice and snow even in midsummer; in low-lying rivers and marshes plants grow even in deep winter. The meaning of "always present" is fully clear.
Song editors: According to the *Wuchang zheng dalun*, "the earth has high and low, qi has warm and cool; where high, the qi is cold; where low, the qi is hot".

## Notes

The answer about the directions and starting points of the seasonal qi and the difference by height of terrain.
- 六 in 新校正六按 at the head of the Song editors' note is presumably an error for 云.
