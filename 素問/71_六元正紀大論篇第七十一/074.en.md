# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §74 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *jichou* and *jiwei* years, above is greater yin earth, in the middle the lesser *gong* earth movement, below greater yang water. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of wind and the transformation of clarity are alike; these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the fifth palace. The transformation of rain is five, that of cold one; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and hot; in the middle, sweet and harmonizing; below, sweet and hot. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*jichou*"] Supreme-one heavenly tally.

[On "In the *jichou* and *jiwei* years"] Supreme-one heavenly tally.

[On "*jiwei* years, above is greater yin earth, in the middle the lesser *gong* earth movement"] Song editors: On examination, in this year wood obtains the first qi and comes to prevail, and the spleen falls ill for long; when earth reaches danger, metal comes to retaliate. When the ninth month, *jiaxu*, arrives, 已 [*ji*] obtains union with *jia*, and earth returns to proper *gong*.

[On "these are degrees of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the fifth palace. The transformation of rain is five"] Song editors: On examination, since the movement and the controller of heaven are both earth, it says only "rain five".

[On "Disaster is in the fifth palace. The transformation of rain is five, that of cold one"] Song editors: On examination, in *jichou* the transformation of cold is six, in *jiwei* one.

[On "Their treatment: above, bitter and hot ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "above, sour and balanced". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when damp runs rampant and prevails, calm it with bitter and hot".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *jichou* and *jiwei* years.
