# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §73 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *wuzi* and *wuwu* years, above is lesser yin fire, in the middle the great *zhi* fire movement, below *yangming* metal. The transformation of heat is seven, that of clarity nine; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, salty and cold; in the middle, sweet and cold; below, sour and warm. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*wuzi*"] Heavenly tally.

[On "In the *wuzi* and *wuwu* years"] Supreme-one heavenly tally.

[On "in the middle the great *zhi* fire movement, below *yangming* metal. The transformation of heat is seven"] Song editors: On examination, since the movement and the controller of heaven are both fire, it says only "heat seven"; heat seven is the transformation of the great *zhi* movement. If it were the transformation of lesser yin controlling heaven, *wuzi* would have heat seven and *wuwu* heat two.

[On "The transformation of heat is seven, that of clarity nine"] Song editors: On examination, in *wuzi* the transformation of clarity is nine, in *wuwu* four.

[On "Their treatment: above, salty and cold ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "below, bitter and hot". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when dryness runs rampant within, treat it with bitter and warm".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *wuzi* and *wuwu* years.
