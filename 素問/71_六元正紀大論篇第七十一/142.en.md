# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §142 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In those without it, it produces illness; in those with it, it makes it severe."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In those without it, it produces illness; in those with it, it makes it severe"] Even those without illness may produce illness by breaking the prohibition; how much more difficult for those with illness that has not yet lightened!

## Notes

The answer that it produces illness in those without it.
