# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §50 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *yichou* and *yiwei* years, above is greater yin earth, in the middle the lesser *shang* metal movement, below greater yang water. Prevalence and retaliation of the transformation of heat and the transformation of cold are alike; these are the so-called days of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the seventh palace. The transformation of damp is five, that of clarity four, that of cold six; these are the so-called days of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and hot; in the middle, sour and harmonizing; below, sweet and hot. These are the so-called suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "these are the so-called days of pathogenic transformation. Disaster is in the seventh palace"] Song editors: On examination, the seventh palace is the western chamber, the position of Dui, governed by Tianzhu [Tianzhu]. The direction of disaster is stated by the direction of the movement.

[On "Disaster is in the seventh palace. The transformation of damp is five"] Song editors: On examination, greater yin properly controls in *wei* and oppositely in *chou*, but its transformation is five in both, by the generation number. It does not use the completion number because earth flourishes in the four seasonal months and has no proper direction, and because heaven has nine palaces and cannot reach ten.

[On "The transformation of damp is five, that of clarity four"] Song editors: The text below in this treatise says "the deficient take the generation number". *Yi* years are the lesser *shang* metal movement, deficient; hence "the transformation of clarity is four"; four is the generation number of metal.

[On "that of clarity four, that of cold six"] Song editors: On examination, in *yichou* the transformation of cold is six, in *yiwei* one.

[On "Their treatment: above, bitter and hot ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "above, sour and balanced; below, sweet and warm". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when damp runs rampant and prevails, calm it with bitter and hot; when cold runs rampant within, treat it with sweet and hot".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *yichou* and *yiwei* years.
- 天住 in the Song editors' note is presumably an error for 天柱, the star name of the palace.
