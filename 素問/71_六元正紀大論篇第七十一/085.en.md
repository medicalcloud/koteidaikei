# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §85 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The excessive is violent, the insufficient gradual. The violent makes severe illness; the gradual makes lingering illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The violent makes severe illness; the gradual makes lingering illness"] 持 ("lingering") means holding on against each other.

## Notes

The difference between outbreaks in excess and insufficiency, and the illnesses they cause.
