# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "*Jiachen* and *jiaxu* are great *gong*, with greater yin added below; *renyin* and *renshen* are great *jue*, with *jueyin* added below; *gengzi* and *gengwu* are great *shang*, with *yangming* added below: such are three. *Guisi* and *guihai* are lesser *zhi*, with lesser yang added below; *xinchou* and *xinwei* are lesser *yu*, with greater yang added below; *guimao* and *guiyou* are lesser *zhi*, with lesser yin added below: such are three. *Wuzi* and *wuwu* are great *zhi*, with lesser yin presiding above; *wuyin* and *wushen* are great *zhi*, with lesser yang presiding above; *bingchen* and *bingxu* are great *yu*, with greater yang presiding above: such are three. *Dingsi* and *dinghai* are lesser *jue*, with *jueyin* presiding above; *yimao* and *yiyou* are lesser *shang*, with *yangming* presiding above; *jichou* and *jiwei* are lesser *gong*, with greater yin presiding above: such are three. Apart from these twenty-four years, there is neither adding nor presiding."

## Notes

The twenty-four years in which the spring in earth matches the movement (adding) or the controller of heaven matches the movement (presiding). There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 巳未 is read as 己未.
