# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Three excessive [movements] are the same as heaven's transformation, and three deficient ones likewise; three excessive ones are the same as earth's transformation, and three deficient ones likewise. These make twenty-four years in all."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Three excessive ... twenty-four years in all"] In sixty years, those the same as the transformation of heaven and earth are twenty-four years in all. All the rest follow 已 [their own] amount.

## Notes

There are twenty-four years the same as heaven's or earth's transformation; each set of three consists of pairs of years (next unit).
- 已 in Wang Bing's 随已多少 is hard to construe.
