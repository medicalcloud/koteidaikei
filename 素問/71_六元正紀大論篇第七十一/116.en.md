# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §116 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where *jueyin* arrives there is birth, and swaying in the wind; where lesser yin arrives, flourishing, and appearance of forms; where greater yin arrives, transformation, and clouds and rain; where lesser yang arrives, growth, and luxuriance and freshness; where *yangming* arrives, gathering, and mist and dew; where greater yang arrives, storing, and thorough sealing. This is the constancy of the transformation of qi.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Where *jueyin* arrives there is birth, and swaying in the wind"] The transformation of wood.

[On "where lesser yin arrives, flourishing, and appearance of forms"] The transformation of fire.

[On "where greater yin arrives, transformation, and clouds and rain"] The transformation of earth.

[On "where lesser yang arrives, growth, and luxuriance and freshness"] The transformation of fire.

[On "where *yangming* arrives, gathering, and mist and dew"] The transformation of metal.

[On "where greater yang arrives, storing, and thorough sealing"] The transformation of water.

## Notes

The third of the twelve changes: the constancy of the transformation of qi.
