# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §99 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In every case it lags by thirty-odd degrees [days]."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In every case it lags by thirty-odd degrees"] "Lags" means after the four seasons. The deviation is thirty days plus eighty-seven and a half marks, and the qi still comes and goes in great abundance. 度 means days. Lagging behind the four seasons is constant even now.
Song editors: On examination, "eighty-seven and a half marks" in the note should read "forty-three marks and thirty fortieths of a mark".

## Notes

The answer that the deviation is always a lag of thirty-odd days.
