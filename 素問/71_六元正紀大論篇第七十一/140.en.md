# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §140 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Cold and heat harm the interior, and the illness becomes ever more severe."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Cold and heat harm the interior, and the illness becomes ever more severe"] It is adding water to water and fire to fire, quite enough to produce further illness; how could it be only that the original illness grows worse?

## Notes

The answer that the illness worsens.
