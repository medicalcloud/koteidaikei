# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Excess with adding is the same as heavenly tally; deficiency with adding is the same as year-meeting."

## Notes

Definitions of "same as heavenly tally" and "same as year-meeting". There is no note by Wang Bing.
