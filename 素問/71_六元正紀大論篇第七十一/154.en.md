# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §154 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the qi is borrowed, there is no prohibition. This is the case called 'host qi insufficient, guest qi prevailing'."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the qi is borrowed, there is no prohibition"] When the proper qi is insufficient and the presiding qi overcomes it, one borrows cold, heat, warmth or coolness to aid the qi of the four proper [seasons]. Then one may offend heat with heat, cold with cold, warmth with warmth, coolness with coolness.

[On "This is the case called 'host qi insufficient, guest qi prevailing'"] Guest qi means the qi of the six qi presiding in turn. Host qi means the five viscera flourishing properly in accord with the four seasons, spring, summer, autumn and winter.

## Notes

The answer that when host qi is insufficient and guest qi prevails, one may offend against the season's cold and heat.
