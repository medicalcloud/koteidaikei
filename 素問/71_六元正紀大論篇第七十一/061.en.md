# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §61 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *bingzi* and *bingwu* years, above is lesser yin fire, in the middle the great *yu* water movement, below *yangming* metal. The transformation of heat is two, that of cold six, that of clarity four; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, salty and cold; in the middle, salty and hot; below, sour and warm. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*bingzi*"] Year-meeting.

[On "in the middle the great *yu* water movement, below *yangming* metal. The transformation of heat is two"] Song editors: On examination, in the *bingzi* year heat is seven, and the disaster to metal is halved, because the movement's water is excessive and overcomes heaven's decree, so heaven's decree is halved. In *bingwu* heat is two: *wu* is fire and lesser yin sovereign fire controls heaven; although the movement is water, one water cannot overcome two fires; hence it differs from the *bingzi* year.

[On "The transformation of heat is two, that of cold six, that of clarity four"] Song editors: On examination, in *bingzi* the transformation of dryness is nine, in *bingwu* four.

[On "Their treatment: above, salty and cold ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "below, bitter and hot". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when dryness runs rampant within, treat it with sour and warm".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *bingzi* and *bingwu* years.
