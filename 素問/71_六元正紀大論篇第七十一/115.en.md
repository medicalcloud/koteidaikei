# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §115 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where *jueyin* arrives there is a store of wind, and cracking and opening; where lesser yin arrives, a store of fire, and unfolding and flourishing; where greater yin arrives, a store of rain, and roundness and fullness; where lesser yang arrives, a store of heat, and going out; where *yangming* arrives, a store controlling killing, and changing of the green; where greater yang arrives, a store of cold, and returning to storage. This is the constancy of controlling transformation.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Where *jueyin* arrives there is a store of wind, and cracking and opening"] 璺 is a slight crack; 啟 is opening and splitting.

[On "where greater yin arrives, a store of rain, and roundness and fullness"] Things receive earth's transformation and their substance becomes round and full. Also rain makes boundaries on the ground, and green patterns appear like rings. That it is the transformation of roundness is clear.

[On "where lesser yang arrives, a store of heat, and going out"] What stored heat goes out.

[On "where *yangming* arrives, a store controlling killing, and changing of the green"] 庚 means 更 ("change"); 更 means replacing, altering.

[On "where greater yang arrives, a store of cold, and returning to storage"] Things are cold, so they return to storage.

## Notes

The second of the twelve changes: the constancy of controlling transformation.
