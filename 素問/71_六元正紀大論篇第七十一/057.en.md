# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §57 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *renshen* and *renyin* years, above is lesser yang ministerial fire, in the middle the great *jue* wood movement, below *jueyin* wood. The transformation of fire is two, that of wind eight; these are the so-called days of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, salty and cold; in the middle, sour and harmonizing; below, acrid and cool. These are the so-called suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "*renshen*"] Same as heavenly tally.

[On "In the *renshen* and *renyin* years"] Same as heavenly tally.

[On "in the middle the great *jue* wood movement, below *jueyin* wood. The transformation of fire is two"] Song editors: On examination, in *renshen* the transformation of heat is seven, in *renyin* two.

[On "The transformation of fire is two, that of wind eight"] Song editors: On examination, since the movement and the spring in earth are both wood, it says only "wind eight"; wind eight is the transformation of the great *jue* movement. If it were the transformation of *jueyin* in the spring, *renshen* would have wind three and *renyin* wind eight.

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *renshen* and *renyin* years.
