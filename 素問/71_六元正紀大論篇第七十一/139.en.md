# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §139 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "What if one offends with cold or heat without releasing or attacking?"

## Notes

A question about offending with cold or heat without sweating or purging. There is no note by Wang Bing.
