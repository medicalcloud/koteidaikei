# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In all these [years] when lesser yang controls heaven, the transformations of qi move ahead of heaven. Heaven's qi is upright and earth's qi disturbed; wind rises suddenly, trees are flattened and sand flies, blazing fire flows, yin moves and yang transforms, rain responds in season, fire and wood share their virtue, and above they correspond to the Sparkling Deluder and the Year Star. Its grains are red and green; its government severity; its decree disturbance. So wind and heat spread together, clouds and things boil up, greater yin flows across, cold comes at times, and coolness and rain arise together; the people suffer cold in the center, sores and ulcers breaking out externally, and diarrhea and fullness internally. So the sage, meeting it, harmonizes and does not contend. Through the arising of going and returning, the people suffer cold and heat, malaria, diarrhea, deafness and blurred vision, vomiting, constraint above, swelling and change of complexion. In the first qi, earth's qi shifts, wind prevails and shakes, cold departs, the weather is very warm, plants flourish early, cold comes but does not kill, and warm illnesses arise; the illnesses are qi constrained above, bleeding, red eyes, cough and counterflow, headache, uterine bleeding, fullness of the flanks, and sores in the interstices of the skin. In the second qi, fire is on the contrary pent up, white dust rises on all sides, clouds hasten to the residence of rain, wind does not overcome damp, rain falls, and the people are healthy; the illnesses are heat pent up above, cough and counterflow, vomiting, sores breaking out in the center, obstruction of chest and throat, headache, fever, confusion and pus sores. In the third qi, heaven's government spreads, scorching heat arrives, lesser yang presides above, rain reaches its limit; the people suffer heat in the center, deafness and blurred vision, bleeding, pus sores, cough and vomiting, nasal congestion and nosebleed, thirst, sneezing and yawning, throat obstruction and red eyes, and often die suddenly. In the fourth qi, coolness arrives, scorching heat transforms intermittently, white dew falls, the people's qi is harmonious and calm, and the illness is fullness and heaviness of the body. In the fifth qi, yang departs, cold comes, rain falls, the qi gates close, hard trees wither early, the people avoid cold pathogens, and the noble man keeps close and secure. In the final qi, earth's qi is upright, wind arrives, the myriad things on the contrary grow, mist and fog move; the illnesses are the gates failing to close [and open] properly, heart pain, and cough from yang qi not being stored. Suppress the qi of the movement, assist what is not overcoming, be sure to break the pent-up qi and first take the source of transformation; then sudden excess will not arise and severe illnesses will not occur. So for the year salty and acrid are suitable, and sour is suitable, to seep, drain, soak and release; observe the cold and 用 [warmth] of the qi to regulate its excess. For those the same as wind and heat, use more transformation of cold; for those different from wind and heat, use less transformation of cold. In using heat, keep away from heat; in using warmth, keep away from warmth; in using cold, keep away from cold; in using coolness, keep away from coolness; food follows the same rule. This is the way. When there is an exception, go against it. Going against this is the stairway to illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In all these [years] when lesser yang controls heaven ... Heaven's qi is upright"] Song editors: On examination, lesser yang controlling heaven and greater yin controlling earth exactly obtain the uprightness of heaven and earth. When *jueyin* or lesser yang controls earth, each is said to "obtain its uprightness" because earth governs generation and flourishing. Some editions write 天気止 ("heaven's qi stops"), but since the nature and function of lesser yang fire is movement and agitation, "stops" makes no sense.

[On "fire and wood share their virtue, and above they correspond to the Sparkling Deluder and the Year Star"] They appear bright and large.
Song editors: On examination, among the six qi, only when lesser yang and *jueyin* control heaven and earth are above and below in harmony without overcoming each other; hence "fire and wood share their virtue". The other qi all have overcoming; hence "unite their virtue".

[On "cough and counterflow, headache, 𡹌"] Now on examination, the character 𡹌 should be 崩 (uterine bleeding).

[On "uterine bleeding, fullness of the flanks, and sores in the interstices of the skin"] The transformation of lesser yin.

[On "In the second qi, fire is on the contrary pent up"] Because it is the division of greater yin.

[On "In the fifth qi ... rain falls, the qi gates close"] Song editors: Wang's note to the *Shengqi tongtian lun* says "the qi gates are the dark mansions [pores], through which the qi of the channels and of the nutritive and defensive are discharged; hence they are called qi gates".

[On "be sure to break the pent-up qi and first take the source of transformation"] The source of transformation is met and taken in the twelfth month of the previous year.
Song editors: On examination, Wang's notes on supporting and taking the source of transformation all give months, with four kinds of meaning. When greater yang controls heaven it is taken in the ninth month, when *yangming* controls heaven in the sixth month; these two first take the qi in heaven. When lesser yang controls heaven it is taken in the twelfth month of the previous year, when greater yin controls heaven in the ninth month; these two take the qi in earth ahead of time. That for lesser yin controlling heaven it is taken in the twelfth month of the previous year and for *jueyin* in the fourth month cannot be explained. According to the *Xuanzhu* it is otherwise: the months for greater yang and *yangming* agree with Wang's notes, but for lesser yang and lesser yin both take the third month, greater yin the fifth month, and *jueyin* the twelfth month of the previous year. The meaning of the *Xuanzhu* can be explained; we suspect errors in the months in Wang's notes.

[On "then sudden excess will not arise and severe illnesses will not occur"] 苛 means severe.
Song editors: On examination, eating the grains of the year and intervening grains is not mentioned here, presumably because in this year the qi of heaven and earth are upright and above and below in harmony.

[On "For those the same as wind and heat, use more transformation of cold ... less transformation of cold"] Great *jue* and great *zhi* years are the same as wind and heat; use more transformation of cold. Great *gong*, great *shang* and great *yu* years differ from wind and heat; regulate their excess with coolness.

## Notes

The transformations of qi in years when lesser yang controls heaven, the weather and illnesses of the six steps, and treatment. Qibo's speech ends here.
- 𡹌 in 血𡹌 had been lost in the base text's input and was supplied from the Daozang edition (see the note in the variants); it is read as 崩 following Wang Bing's note.
- 瞑 in 聾瞑 had been lost in the base text's input and was supplied from the Wikisource plain text (see the note in the variants).
- For 開 in 開閉, the other sources have 関, and for 用 in 寒用, 温 (see the variants); the translation follows them.
