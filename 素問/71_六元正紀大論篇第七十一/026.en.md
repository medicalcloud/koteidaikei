# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "What is not the transformation of qi is called disaster."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What is not the transformation of qi is called disaster"] The twelve changes are complete.

## Notes

The definition of disaster. Wang Bing's "twelve changes" presumably refers to the "twelve changes" in the later text (around unit 126).
