# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §49 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Let me set them in order. In the *jiazi* and *jiawu* years, above is lesser yin fire, in the middle the great *gong* earth movement, below *yangming* metal. The transformation of heat is two, that of rain five, that of dryness four; these are the so-called days of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, salty and cold; in the middle, bitter and hot; below, sour and hot. These are the so-called suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "below *yangming* metal. The transformation of heat is two"] Song editors: On examination, opposite transformation follows the branch and takes the completion number; proper transformation follows the root and takes the generation number. In the *jiazi* year the transformation of heat is seven and that of dryness nine; in the *jiawu* year heat is two and dryness four.

[On "The transformation of heat is two, that of rain five"] Song editors: The main text of this treatise says "how do the numbers of excess and deficiency begin? The excessive take the completion number, the deficient the generation number, and earth always takes the generation number". *Jia* years are the great *gong* earth movement, excessive; hence "the transformation of rain is five"; five is the number of earth.

[On "that of dryness four; these are the so-called days of proper transformation"] The transformation of proper qi.

[On "Their treatment: above, salty and cold ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "below, bitter and hot". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when heat runs rampant and prevails, calm it with salty and cold; when dryness runs rampant within, treat it with bitter and warm". Here "below, sour and hot" is suspected to be an error.

## Notes

For each year of the sixty-year cycle, the controller of heaven (above), the central movement (middle) and the spring in earth (below), the numbers of their transformations, and the flavors suited to treatment. The same format continues to unit 078; Qibo's speech continues to unit 079.
- The numbers such as "heat two" are the generation and completion numbers of the five phases; the Song editors hold that the number varies depending on whether it is proper transformation (the qi in its own position) or opposite transformation.
