# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "First establish the year to clarify its qi—the numbers of the movement of metal, wood, water, fire and earth, and the transformations of cold, summer heat, dryness, damp, wind and fire presiding over them; then the way of heaven can be seen, the people's qi can be regulated, and the furling and unfurling of yin and yang will be near at hand without confusion. Let me speak fully of the numbers that can be counted."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Let me speak fully of the numbers that can be counted"] 遂 means fully.

## Notes

Establishing the year to clarify movement and qi reveals the way of heaven and the people's qi.
