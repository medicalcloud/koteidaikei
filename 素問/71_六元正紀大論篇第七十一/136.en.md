# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §136 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When heaven's qi is insufficient, earth's qi follows it; when earth's qi is insufficient, heaven's qi follows it. The movement dwells between them and always goes first. It detests what it cannot overcome and returns to what is alike and harmonious; following the movement, [the qi] return and comply and produce illness. Hence when the upper prevails, heaven's qi descends downward; when the lower prevails, earth's qi shifts upward; according to more or less, their divisions deviate. The slight deviates slightly, the severe greatly; in severe cases positions are exchanged and the qi intersect, and when they are exchanged great changes arise and illness occurs. The *Great Essentials* says, 'For severe records five parts, for slight records seven parts; their deviation can be seen.' This is what it means."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When heaven's qi is insufficient ... The movement dwells between them and always goes first"] The movement means wood, fire, earth, metal and water each governing a year. When earth's qi prevails, the year's movement rises; when heaven's qi prevails, the year's qi descends. In rising and descending, the movement's qi always shifts and descends first.

[On "It detests what it cannot overcome ... and produce illness"] When not in its position, change arises; when change arises, illness occurs.

[On "Hence when the upper prevails, heaven's qi descends downward; when the lower prevails, earth's qi shifts upward"] 勝 ("prevail") means more. When the upper is more, it descends of itself; when the lower is more, it shifts [upward] of itself. That more and less move into each other is the constancy of qi.
Song editors: According to the *Liuwei zhi dalun*: "When rising is finished, there is descent; descent refers to heaven. When descent is finished, there is rising; rising refers to earth. When heaven's qi descends, qi flows to earth; when earth's qi rises, qi soars to heaven. Hence high and low summon each other, rising and descending depend on each other, and changes arise." This too is the meaning of rising and descending.

[On "according to more or less, their divisions deviate"] When more, the shifting and descending is more; when less, it is less. The responses of more and less differ in being slight or severe.

[On "The *Great Essentials* says ... This is what it means"] By the five parts and seven parts one knows the excess and deviation of yin and yang in heaven and earth.

## Notes

The answer on fullness and emptiness and the rising and descending of the qi of heaven and earth, the position of the movement, and the degree of deviation.
