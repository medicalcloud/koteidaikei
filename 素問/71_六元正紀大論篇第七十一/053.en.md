# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §53 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *wuchen* and *wuxu* years, above is greater yang water, in the middle the great *zhi* fire movement, below greater yin earth. The transformation of cold is six, that of heat seven, that of damp five; these are the so-called days of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and warm; in the middle, sweet and harmonizing; below, sweet and warm. These are the so-called suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In the *wuchen* and *wuxu* years, above is greater yang water, in the middle the great *zhi* fire movement"] Song editors: On examination, greater yang appears above, so the transformation of fire is halved.

[On "in the middle the great *zhi* fire movement, below greater yin earth. The transformation of cold is six"] Song editors: On examination, in *wuchen* the transformation of cold is six, in *wuxu* one.

[On "Their treatment: above, bitter and warm ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when cold runs rampant and prevails, calm it with acrid and hot; when damp runs rampant within, treat it with bitter and hot". Also the *Xuanzhu* says "above, sweet and warm; below, sour and balanced".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *wuchen* and *wuxu* years. There is no note by Wang Bing.
