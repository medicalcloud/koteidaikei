# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What is the government of greater yang?"

## Notes

A question on the government in years when greater yang controls heaven. The following units ask about each of the six qi as controller of heaven. There is no note by Wang Bing.
