# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §43 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What of warmth and coolness?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What of warmth and coolness?"] Warmth and coolness are milder than cold and heat; may they be offended lightly?

## Notes

A question on how to treat warmth and coolness.
