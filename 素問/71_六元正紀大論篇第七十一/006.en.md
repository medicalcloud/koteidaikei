# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The period of *chen* and *xu*. [Controller of heaven] greater yang, [central movement] great *jue*, [spring in earth] greater yin; the years *renchen* and *renxu*. Its movement is wind; its transformation sounding, stirring, opening and splitting; its change shaking, snapping, smashing and uprooting; its illness dizziness, tremor and closed eyes. [The steps of the movement:] great *jue*, lesser *zhi*, great *gong*, lesser *shang*, great *yu*. Greater yang, great *zhi*, greater yin; the years *wuchen* and *wuxu*, the same as proper *zhi*. Its movement is heat; its transformation warmth, summer heat and sultry steaming; its change fierce blaze and boiling; its illness pent-up heat. [Steps:] great *zhi*, lesser *gong*, great *shang*, lesser *yu*, lesser *jue*. Greater yang, great *gong*, greater yin; the years *jiachen* [year-meeting] and *jiaxu* [year-meeting]. Its movement is yin dust; its transformation softness, moisture, heaviness and luster; its change quaking, alarm, gusts and downpours; its illness damp and heaviness below. [Steps:] great *gong*, lesser *shang*, great *yu*, great *jue*, lesser *zhi*. Greater yang, great *shang*, greater yin; the years *gengchen* and *gengxu*. Its movement is coolness; its transformation fog, dew and bleak wind; its change austere killing and withering; its illness dryness, oppression of the back and fullness of the chest. [Steps:] great *shang*, lesser *yu*, lesser *jue*, great *zhi*, lesser *gong*. Greater yang, great *yu*, greater yin; the years *bingchen* [heavenly tally] and *bingxu* [heavenly tally]. Its movement is cold; its transformation congealing bleakness and biting cold; its change ice, snow, frost and hail; its illness great cold lodging in the gorges and valleys [of the flesh]. [Steps:] great *yu*, great *jue*, lesser *zhi*, great *gong*, lesser *shang*.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Its movement is wind; its transformation sounding, stirring, opening and splitting"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "its virtue is sounding, bending, opening and splitting".

[On "its change shaking, snapping, smashing and uprooting"] Song editors: On examination, these "its movement", "its transformation" and "its change" arise from the movements such as great *jue*.

[On "its illness dizziness, tremor and closed eyes"] Song editors: On examination, these signs are stated according to the movement being the same as heaven and earth.

[On "great *jue*"] First; proper.

[On "great *yu*"] Last.

[On "*wuxu*, the same as proper *zhi*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "in the period of blazing brilliance, when *yu* is above, it is the same as proper *zhi*".

[On "Its movement is heat; its transformation warmth, summer heat and sultry steaming"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* writes 蒸 for 燠.

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First.

[On "*jiachen* year-meeting"] Same as 大 [heavenly] tally.

[On "*jiaxu* year-meeting"] Same as heavenly tally.
Song editors: The *Tianyuan ji dalun* says "receiving the year is called year-direct", and the *Liuwei zhi dalun* says "wood movement meeting *mao*, fire movement meeting *wu*, earth movement meeting the four seasonal branches, metal movement meeting *you*, water movement meeting *zi* are called year-meetings; the qi is balanced". Wang Bing says "year-direct is also called year-meeting". This *jia* is great *gong*, and *chen* and *xu* are seasonal branches; hence it is called a year-meeting. It is also called "same as heavenly tally" because the text below in this treatise says "excess that is added is the same as heavenly tally"; so this year is both a year-meeting and the same as heavenly tally.

[On "Its movement is yin dust"] Song editors: On examination, the three great *gong* movements all say "yin rain", but only here is it "yin dust"; 埃 is suspected to be 雨.

[On "its transformation softness, moisture, heaviness and luster"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* writes 淖 for 沢.

[On "great *yu*"] Last.

[On "great *jue*"] First.

[On "lesser *yu*"] Last.

[On "lesser *jue*"] First.

[On "Greater yang, great *yu*"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* says "when *yu* is above, the growing qi does not transform".

[On "*bingchen* heavenly tally, *bingxu* heavenly tally"] Song editors: The *Tianyuan ji dalun* says "corresponding to heaven is called heavenly tally", and the *Liuwei zhi dalun* says "in a year of earth movement greater yin appears above ... in a year of water movement greater yang appears above ... heaven meeting it"; hence it is called heavenly tally. Also the text below in this treatise says "the five movements that move the same as heaven's transformation are called heavenly tally", and "whether excessive or deficient, those presided over are all called heavenly tallies".

[On "Its movement is cold"] Song editors: On examination, among the three great *yu* movements, this one is with *yu* above, and those with lesser yang or lesser yin controlling heaven are great *zhi* [?]. For the movement with lesser yang controlling heaven it says "cold and austere", while here and for lesser yin controlling heaven it says "its movement is cold". We suspect that this movement with greater yang controlling heaven, matching great *yu*, should say "its movement is cold and austere", and the movements with lesser yang or lesser yin controlling heaven should say "its movement is cold".

[On "its transformation congealing bleakness and biting cold"] Song editors: The *Wuchang zheng dalun* writes "congealing bleakness and cold mist".

[On "great *yu*"] Last.

[On "great *jue*"] First.

## Notes

For the ten years (*chen* and *xu*) when greater yang controls heaven and greater yin is in the spring, the movement, transformation, change and illness for each central movement, and the five steps of the movement (from first to last), set out as a table. Qibo's speech continues into unit 007.
- The original is a table; the translation supplies [controller of heaven], [central movement], [spring in earth] and marks the order of steps with [Steps:]. The notes "first" and "last" mark the first and last of the five steps and appear as Wang Bing's notes.
- Several of the Song editors' notes are cut off in the base text's input, with the continuation appearing as fragments labeled as Wang Bing's notes (赫曦之紀上 + 羽与正徴同; 按天 + 元紀大論云……; 按五 + 常政大論沢作淖; 按天元紀大論……水 + 運之歳……; 新校正云 + 詳太羽三運……). The translation renders each as a single Song editors' note.
- The Song editors' note on "its movement is cold" is partly hard to construe (marked [?]); the translation follows the characters.
- 大 in 同大符 is an error for 天. Two 殆知閣 texts have 同天符 in the main text (see the variants).
