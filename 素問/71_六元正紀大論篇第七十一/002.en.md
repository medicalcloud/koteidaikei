# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo bowed his head to the ground twice and replied: "How illuminating the question! This is the guiding principle of heaven and earth and the source of change and transformation. Who but a sage emperor could fathom its supreme principle? Though I am not clever, let me set out its way, so that it will never perish and will long remain unchanged."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Though I am not clever, let me set out its way ... long remain unchanged"] The governing qi circulate like heaven and earth, and excess and deficiency are the constant order of qi. If the fixed rule were not stated permanently, it would change over time; how could those far removed from the sages understand it?

## Notes

Qibo undertakes to set out the way.
