# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §98 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Does the deviation have a number?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Does the deviation have a number"] It means the number of days.

## Notes

A question about the number of days of the deviation.
