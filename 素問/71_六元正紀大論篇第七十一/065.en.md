# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §65 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the *gengchen* and *gengxu* years, above is greater yang water, in the middle the great *shang* metal movement, below greater yin earth. The transformation of cold is one, that of clarity nine, that of rain five; these are degrees of proper transformation. Their treatment: above, bitter and hot; in the middle, acrid and warm; below, sweet and hot. These are the suitable medicines and foods.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "in the middle the great *shang* metal movement, below greater yin earth. The transformation of cold is one"] Song editors: On examination, in *gengchen* the transformation of cold is six, in *gengxu* one.

[On "Their treatment: above, bitter and hot ... suitable medicines and foods"] Song editors: The *Xuanzhu* says "above, sweet and warm; below, sour and balanced". Also the *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "when cold runs rampant and prevails, calm it with acrid and hot; when damp runs rampant within, treat it with bitter and hot".

## Notes

The movement, qi and treatment of the *gengchen* and *gengxu* years. There is no note by Wang Bing.
