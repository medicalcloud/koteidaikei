# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §107 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Movement may be contrary or compliant, arrival slow or fast. Hence for the excessive, transformation precedes heaven [the season]; for the insufficient, transformation follows heaven."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Hence for the excessive, transformation precedes heaven; for the insufficient, transformation follows heaven"] With surplus qi, transformation comes first; with deficient qi, transformation comes later.

## Notes

The answer that in excess transformation precedes the season and in insufficiency follows it.
