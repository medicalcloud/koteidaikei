# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In all these [years] when *jueyin* controls heaven, the transformations of qi move behind heaven; in all those the same as proper years, the transformations of qi move with heaven. Heaven's qi is disturbed and earth's qi upright; wind arises high and far, and blazing heat follows it; clouds hasten to the residence of rain, the transformation of damp moves, wind and fire share their virtue, and above they correspond to the Year Star and the Sparkling Deluder. Its government is agitation, its decree swiftness, its grains green and red; the intervening grains are those of the great [movement]. What it depletes are patterned horned creatures and the many kinds of feathered ones. Prevalence and retaliation of wind and dryness, fire and heat arise in turn; hibernating creatures come out, flowing water does not freeze; heat illnesses move below, wind illnesses above, and the prevalence and retaliation of wind and dryness take form in the middle. In the first qi, cold begins to be austere, killing qi is just arriving, and the people suffer cold on the lower right. In the second qi, cold does not depart, snow flowers and water freezes, killing qi acts, frost falls, the tops of fine grasses are scorched, cold rain comes often, yang transforms again, and the people suffer heat in the center. In the third qi, heaven's government spreads, wind rises at times, and the people suffer tearing, tinnitus, tremor and dizziness. In the fourth qi, sultry heat and damp heat press on each other and contend at the upper left; the people suffer jaundice and puffy swelling. In the fifth qi, dryness and damp prevail in turn, deep overcast spreads, cold qi reaches the body, and wind and rain move. In the final qi, the dreaded fire controls the decree, yang transforms greatly, hibernating creatures come out, flowing water does not freeze, earth's qi bursts forth, grasses grow, people are at ease, and the illness is warm epidemics. One must break the pent-up qi, support the source of transformation, assist the qi of the movement and not let the pathogen prevail. For the year acrid is suitable to regulate the upper and salty to regulate the lower; do not rashly offend the qi of the dreaded fire. In using warmth, keep away from warmth; in using heat, keep away from heat; in using coolness, keep away from coolness; in using cold, keep away from cold; food follows the same rule. With exceptions, go against the norm. This is the way. Going against this brings illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "in all those the same as proper years, the transformations of qi move with heaven"] In excessive years the transforming qi of the movement moves ahead of heaven's seasons; in deficient years transformation and completion lag behind heaven's seasons. In those the same as proper years, transformation and completion keep pace with the twenty-four solar periods of heaven, neither ahead nor behind.
Song editors: On examination, this note's "those the same as proper years are the same as the twenty-four solar periods" is suspected to be wrong; probably it means the same as the exchange on the day of Great Cold, the climate being the same.

[On "the illness is warm epidemics. One must break the pent-up qi, support the source of transformation"] The source of transformation is the fourth month; meet and take it.

[On "salty to regulate the lower; do not rashly offend the qi of the dreaded fire"] Song editors: On examination, why does this movement not mention "adjusting to the sameness or difference of the qi and regulating the amount"? Presumably because the government of *jueyin* is the same as that of lesser yang: among the six qi dividing government, only the governments of *jueyin* and lesser yang have no difference of conquest and punishment between above and below, and their treatment is single. Hence "for those the same as wind and heat, use more transformation of cold; for those different, less" is not repeated.

## Notes

The transformations of qi in years when *jueyin* controls heaven, the weather and illnesses of the six steps, and treatment. The discussion of the governments of the six qi controlling heaven ends here, as does Qibo's speech.
