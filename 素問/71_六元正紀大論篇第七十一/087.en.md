# 素問 六元正紀大論篇第七十一 §87 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "For the excessive the number is the completion number; for the insufficient the number is the generation number; earth always uses the generation number."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "For the excessive the number is the completion number ... earth always uses the generation number"] The numbers are those by which the transformations of the five constants proceed. The number of water is one, of fire two, of wood three, of metal four, of earth five. The completion numbers are water six, fire seven, wood eight, metal nine, earth five; hence "earth always uses the generation number". "The number is the generation number" means taking the size of each generation number for divination. Thus the days on which government, decree, virtue and transformation, prevalence and retaliation rest or act, and also feet, inches, tenths and hairs, are all reckoned by them. This clarifies all their uses.

## Notes

The answer: excess uses the completion numbers, insufficiency the generation numbers, earth always the generation number. The numbers in the sixty-year list of this chapter ("cold six", "clarity four", etc.) follow this.
