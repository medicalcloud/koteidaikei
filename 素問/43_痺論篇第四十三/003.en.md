# 素問 痺論篇第四十三 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why are there five kinds?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why are there five kinds?"] It says: if the differences of wind, cold and damp produce three impediments, which qi prevails to produce five?

## Notes

Huangdi asks how five kinds of impediment arise.
