# 素問 痺論篇第四十三 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. Impediment may be painful or painless, numb, cold, hot, dry or damp. Why?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks the reasons for the different symptoms of impediment. There is no note by Wang Bing.
