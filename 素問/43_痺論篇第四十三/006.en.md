# 素問 痺論篇第四十三 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The five zang all have their counterparts. When illness lasts long without leaving, it lodges within in its counterpart. Thus if bone impediment does not end and one is again affected by evil, it lodges within in the kidney; if sinew impediment does not end and one is again affected by evil, it lodges within in the liver; if vessel impediment does not end and one is again affected by evil, it lodges within in the heart; if flesh impediment does not end and one is again affected by evil, it lodges within in the spleen; if skin impediment does not end and one is again affected by evil, it lodges within in the lung. What is called [zang] impediment is being affected again by wind, cold and damp, each in its season.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The five zang all have their counterparts ... lodges within in its counterpart"] The liver corresponds to the sinews, the heart to the vessels, the spleen to the flesh, the lung to the skin, the kidney to the bones. When illness lasts long without leaving, it enters these.

[On "What is called [zang] impediment ... each in its season"] "Season" means the month in which the qi dominates: the liver dominates in spring, the heart in summer, the lung in autumn, the kidney in winter, and the spleen in the last months of the four seasons. 感 means to be affected.

## Notes

When superficial impediment lasts long and one is again affected by evil in its season, it enters the corresponding zang and becomes impediment of the five zang. Qibo's speech continues to unit 008.
