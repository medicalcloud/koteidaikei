# 素問 痺論篇第四十三 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When impediment is in the bones there is heaviness; in the vessels, the blood congeals and does not flow; in the sinews, they bend and do not stretch; in the flesh, there is numbness; in the skin, there is cold. Therefore those with these five are painless. In all kinds of impediment, on meeting cold there is a crawling sensation; on meeting heat there is slackness."

## Notes

The five kinds of symptoms of painless impediment. Wang Bing's note on this is given in the next unit.
- 虫 in 逢寒則虫: according to Wang Bing (unit 021) it means a sensation as of insects crawling in the skin; according to the Song editors the *Jiayi jing* writes 急 "tense".
