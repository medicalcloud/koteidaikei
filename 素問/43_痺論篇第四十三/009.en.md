# 素問 痺論篇第四十三 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "With impediment, some die, some suffer long pain, and some recover easily. Why?"

## Notes

Huangdi asks the reason for the different prognoses of impediment. There is no note by Wang Bing.
