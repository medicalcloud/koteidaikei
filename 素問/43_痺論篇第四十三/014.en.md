# 素問 痺論篇第四十三 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The five zang have their stream points and the six fu their uniting points. Following the divisions of the vessels, each has where it issues; follow each where it passes, and the illness is cured."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Following the divisions of the vessels, each has where it issues; follow each where it passes"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 治 "treat" for 随 "follow".

[On "each has where it issues; follow each where it passes, and the illness is cured"] The stream point of the liver is Taichong, of the heart Taling, of the spleen Taibai, of the lung Taiyuan, of the kidney Taixi; all are where the channel vessels pour. Taichong is between the toes, in the depression two *cun* behind the base joint of the big toe. [Song editors: The note to *Ci yaotong* says, "Taichong is on the inner side behind the base joint of the big toe, in the depression two *cun* back, where the artery is felt."] One may needle three body-*fen* and retain ten breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Taling is in the depression between the two tendons behind the bone of the palm. One may needle six body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Taibai is in the depression below the round bone on the inner side of the foot. One may needle three body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Taiyuan is in the depression behind the palm. One may needle two body-*fen* and retain two breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Taixi is in the depression at the artery behind the inner ankle above the heel bone. One may needle three body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. The uniting point of the stomach enters at Sanli, of the gallbladder at Yanglingquan, of the large intestine at Quchi, of the small intestine at Xiaohai, of the triple burner at Weiyang, and of the bladder at Weizhong. Sanli is three *cun* below the knee between the two sinews on the outer edge of the shin; one may needle one body-*cun* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Yanglingquan is one *cun* below the knee in the depression on the outer edge of the shin; one may needle six body-*fen* and retain ten breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Xiaohai is outside the large bone on the inner side of the elbow, in the depression five *fen* from the tip of the elbow, found with the elbow bent; one may needle two body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, five cones. Quchi is at the outer auxiliary bone of the elbow, in the crease of the bone when the elbow is bent; one may needle five body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. Weiyang is between the two sinews on the outer edge of the popliteal fossa; one may needle seven body-*fen* and retain five breaths; for moxibustion, three cones; take it bending and stretching. Weizhong is at the artery in the transverse crease at the centre of the popliteal fossa; one may needle five body-*fen* and retain seven breaths; for moxibustion, three cones. [Song editors: The note to *Cire* puts Weizhong at the bend behind the knee; the rest is the same.] Hence the classic says, "Following the divisions of the vessels, each has where it issues; follow each where it passes, and the illness is cured." 過 means where the vessel passes.
Song editors: On examination, Mr Wang takes Weiyang as the uniting point of the triple burner. The *Jiayi jing* says, "Weiyang is the lower auxiliary transport point of the triple burner, a separate network vessel of the foot greater yang." The uniting point of the triple burner is the Tianjing point of the hand lesser yang channel, where the lesser yang vessel enters, which is its uniting point. On examination, for all the six fu here the points where their own channels enter are cited, except for the triple burner; this is Mr Wang's error. Mr Wang merely saw that the *Jiayi jing* says "the triple burner unites at Weiyang", but that view is different: it also unites the large intestine at the upper Juxu and the small intestine at the lower Juxu, whereas here Quchi and Xiaohai replace them. Therefore Tianjing should be taken as the uniting point.

## Notes

For needling impediment, the stream points are used for the five zang and the uniting points for the six fu. Wang Bing's note gives the location and needling of each point; Song editors' remarks inserted within the note are given in square brackets.
- The Song editors reject Wang Bing's taking Weiyang as the uniting point of the triple burner and hold that it should be Tianjing.
- Taling (太陵) in Wang Bing's note is the Daling (大陵) point.
