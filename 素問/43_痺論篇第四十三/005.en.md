# 素問 痺論篇第四十三 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What qi makes it lodge within the five zang and six fu?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What qi makes it lodge within the five zang and six fu?"] It says: the impediments of skin, flesh, sinew and vessel are met outside in the five seasons; by what do they come to reside in the zang and fu within?

## Notes

Huangdi asks why impediment enters the zang and fu.
