# 素問 痺論篇第四十三 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good"] 虫 means as if insects were crawling in the skin; 縦 means slack and loose, not holding together.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 急 for 虫.

## Notes

This ends the chapter. Wang Bing's note is on 虫 and 縦 in the previous unit.
