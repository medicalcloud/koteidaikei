# 素問 痺論篇第四十三 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Those who meet them in winter have bone impediment; in spring, sinew impediment; in summer, vessel impediment; in the utmost yin, flesh impediment; in autumn, skin impediment."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Those who meet them in winter ... skin impediment"] Winter governs the bones, spring the sinews, summer the vessels, autumn the skin, and the utmost yin the flesh; therefore each forms its impediment. The utmost yin means the months of *wu* and *ji* and the months in which earth dominates by lodging.

## Notes

Impediment divides into five kinds (bone, sinew, vessel, flesh, skin) by season.
- 戊巳 in Wang Bing's note means 戊己.
