# 素問 痺論篇第四十三 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Those in which it enters the zang die; those in which it lingers between the sinews and bones have long pain; those in which it lingers in the skin recover easily."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Those in which it enters the zang die ... recover easily"] Those in which it enters the zang die because the spirit departs; those in the sinews and bones have long pain because it is fixed; those in the skin recover easily because it is superficial and shallow. Because of this depth or shallowness there are these differences.

## Notes

The prognosis of impediment depends on the depth of the evil.
