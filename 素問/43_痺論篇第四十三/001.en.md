# 素問 痺論篇第四十三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "How does impediment arise?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How does impediment arise?"] 安 means how: it asks by what it arises.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: the cause of impediment (an illness with numbness, pain, stiffness, etc.).
