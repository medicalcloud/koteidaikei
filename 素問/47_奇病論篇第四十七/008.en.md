# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

hence it is said that a chronic illness is produced."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "hence it is said that a chronic illness is produced"] The fetus constricts the uterine network vessel and the kidney qi does not pass; if one drains it, the kidney essence comes out with it, the essential fluid is exhausted within, and the fetus is not whole; the fetus dies in the abdomen, clings and does not leave; thus [the illness] alone dominates; therefore a chronic illness is produced.
