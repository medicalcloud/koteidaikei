# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The *Cifa* says, 'Do not diminish the insufficient or add to the surplus, so as to produce a chronic illness; afterwards regulate it.' 'Do not diminish the insufficient' means that when the body is emaciated, the arrowhead needle and stones must not be used. 'Do not add to the surplus' means draining when there is a form in the abdomen; if it is drained, the essence comes out and the illness alone dominates the interior;

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The *Cifa* says, 'Do not diminish the insufficient or add to the surplus, so as to produce a chronic illness'"] 疹 means chronic illness. If one treats against the rule, the fetus dies and does not come out, and it becomes a chronic, fixed illness.

[On "afterwards regulate it"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu* lack these four characters. Quan Yuanqi's note says, "'Not treating' means that at nine months the body has lost its voice and is heavy, and cannot be treated; one must wait until after the birth at the full tenth month, when it returns to normal." Thus the four characters 然後調之 were originally Quan Yuanqi's note mistakenly written here and should be deleted.

[On "'Do not diminish the insufficient' means ... must not be used"] In the ninth month of pregnancy the sinews and bones are thin and tired, strength is scant, the body heavy, and grain is refused; therefore the body is emaciated and must not be harmed with the arrowhead needle and stones.

## Notes

Citing the *Cifa*, it warns against wrong needling of pregnant women.
- The Song editors hold that the four characters 然後調之 are an intrusion from Quan Yuanqi's note and should be deleted.
- *Cifa* is a lost chapter (see Wang Bing's note in chapter 33).
