# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There is an illness with a bitter taste in the mouth, for which Yanglingquan is taken. What is the illness of bitter taste called, and how is it contracted?"

## Notes

There is no note by Wang Bing.
- According to the Song editors (unit 021), Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu* lack the six characters 口苦取陽陵泉, which are suspected to be an error.
