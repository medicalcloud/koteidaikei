# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good"] Quan's note says, "A person is first generated in the brain; because there is a brain, there are bones and marrow; the teeth are the root of the bones."

## Notes

Concludes the preceding dialogue. Wang Bing's note quotes Quan Yuanqi's note.
