# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The illness is called gallbladder heat. The liver is the general of the interior; it takes decisions from the gallbladder, and the throat is its envoy. Such a person often deliberates without deciding; therefore the gallbladder is depleted, its qi overflows upward, and the mouth becomes bitter. Treat it with the alarm and transport points of the gallbladder. The treatment is in *Yinyang shi'er guan xiangshi*."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The illness is called gallbladder heat"] This too means heat. Bile is bitter; therefore the mouth is bitter.
Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition and the *Taisu* lack the six characters 口苦取陽陵泉; considering the flow of the text before and after, they are suspected to be an error.

[On "The liver is the general of the interior ... the throat is its envoy"] The *Linglan midian lun* says, "The liver is the office of the general, from which deliberation comes; the gallbladder is the office of the upright judge, from which decisions come." The liver and gallbladder are paired, their qi and nature communicate; therefore all deliberations take decisions from the gallbladder. The throat and gallbladder correspond; therefore the throat is the envoy.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says, "The gallbladder is the fu of central essence; the five zang take decisions from the gallbladder, and the throat is its envoy." This text is suspected to be in error.

[On "therefore the gallbladder is depleted ... the alarm and transport points of the gallbladder"] Those on the chest and abdomen are called alarm points; those on the back are called transport points. The alarm point of the gallbladder is outside two ribs below the nipple, five body-*fen* below Qimen; its transport point is below the tenth vertebra, one and a half body-*cun* to each side.

[On "The treatment is in *Yinyang shi'er guan xiangshi*"] It says the method of treatment is given in that chapter, which is now lost from the classic.

## Notes

Gallbladder heat (bitter mouth), arising when the gallbladder is depleted through indecisive deliberation, and its treatment.
- According to Wang Bing, *Yinyang shi'er guan xiangshi* is a lost chapter.
