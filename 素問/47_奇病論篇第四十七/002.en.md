# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "The network vessel of the uterus is cut off."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The network vessel of the uterus is cut off"] 絶 means the vessel is cut off and does not flow, so that one cannot speak; it is not that the heavenly true qi is cut off.

## Notes

It is due to the network vessel of the uterus being compressed by the fetus and blocked.
