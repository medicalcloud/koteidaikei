# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The illness is called hidden beam; its root is wind. Its qi overflows into the large intestine and clings to the *huang*; the source of the *huang* is below the navel; therefore there is pain around the navel. It must not be moved; moving it produces an illness of rough urination."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The illness is called hidden beam"] Because it is an illness of the thoroughfare vessel, it is called hidden beam. The thoroughfare vessel, with the network vessel of the foot lesser yin, arises below the kidney, emerges at Qijie, follows the inner edge of the inner thigh, enters obliquely into the popliteal fossa, follows the inner edge of the shin bone, runs down alongside the foot lesser yin channel behind the inner ankle and enters the sole. Its ascending part emerges three body-*cun* below the navel in the region of Guanyuan, runs straight up alongside the navel, follows the abdomen and meets at the throat. Therefore the body and hips are all swollen with pain around the navel; it is called hidden beam. 環 means circling round like a ring.

[On "Its qi overflows into the large intestine ... pain around the navel"] The large intestine here is the broad intestine; what the classic calls the large intestine should be called the winding intestine. Why? The *Lingshu jing* says, "The winding intestine circles to the right at the navel and goes down in layered folds; the broad intestine is attached to the spine, receives the winding intestine, circles to the left, and in layered folds widens above and below." Accordingly this is the winding intestine and should not be called the large intestine. But both the large intestine and the winding intestine are paired with the lung and named by this pairing; hence they are generally called the large intestine.

[On "It must not be moved ... rough urination"] The thoroughfare vessel arises below the kidney and emerges at Qijie; its ascending part arises in the uterus and rises to emerge in the region of Guanyuan below the navel; therefore moving it produces water and rough urination. "Moving" means preparing toxic drugs to strike and move it, causing a great purge. The meaning of this question and answer is the same as in the *Fuzhong lun*; because it is an unusual illness, it is repeated here.
