# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "One must have been exposed to great cold, which reached the bones and marrow within. The marrow has the brain as its master; the brain counterflows; therefore there are headache and also toothache. The illness is called reversal counterflow."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The marrow has the brain as its master ... also toothache"] The brain is the master of the marrow, and the teeth are the surplus of the bones. The brain counterflows and becomes cold, and cold also enters the bones; therefore there are headache and toothache.

## Notes

Headache and toothache from great cold entering the bones, marrow and brain are called reversal counterflow.
