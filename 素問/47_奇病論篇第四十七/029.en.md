# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The illness arises in the kidney and is called kidney wind. In kidney wind with inability to eat and proneness to fright, those whose heart qi wilts after the fright die."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The illness arises in the kidney and is called kidney wind"] The pulse is like a bowstring, large and tight. Taxation qi accumulates within, and cold contends within as well; taxation qi is pressed by cold and transforms into wind; the wind prevails over the kidney; hence it is called kidney wind.

[On "In kidney wind with inability to eat ... die"] Kidney-water receives wind and heart-fire wilts and weakens; both fire and water are distressed; therefore one surely dies.

## Notes

This illness is called kidney wind; those whose heart qi wilts die.
- The base text writes 己 for the 已 of 驚已; the translation takes it as 已.
