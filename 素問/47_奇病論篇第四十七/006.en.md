# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "There is no treatment; it should recover in the tenth month.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "There is no treatment; it should recover in the tenth month"] In the tenth month the fetus leaves, the uterine network vessel opens again, and the kidney vessel rises to nourish; therefore speech returns as before.

## Notes

It recovers naturally after childbirth, so it is not treated. Qibo's speech continues to unit 008.
