# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "Some women in the ninth month of pregnancy lose their voice. Why is this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Some women in the ninth month of pregnancy lose their voice. Why is this?"] 重身 means a body within the body, that is, pregnancy. 瘖 means inability to speak. In the ninth month of pregnancy the foot lesser yin vessel nourishes the fetus; when [the fetus] constricts and the qi is cut off, there is loss of voice and inability to speak.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, which discusses unusual illnesses in dialogue. This unit asks about loss of voice in the ninth month of pregnancy.
