# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There are people born with vertex illness. What is the illness called, and where is it contracted?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "There are people born with vertex illness ... where is it contracted?"] All illnesses arise from wind and rain, cold and heat, yin and yang, joy and anger. But one who is just born with a form and has not yet encountered evil qi already has vertex illness; how could evil qi have harmed him beforehand? Hence the question. 巔 means the top, that is, the head.

## Notes

Huangdi asks the cause of congenital vertex illness (epilepsy).
- Wang Bing takes 巔 as (illness of) the head; Daizhige writes 癲 "epilepsy".
