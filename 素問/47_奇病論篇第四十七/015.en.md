# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There are people with headache that has not ended for several years. Where is it contracted, and what is the illness called?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "There are people with headache ... what is the illness called?"] Headache should not last beyond a month, yet this does not heal for years; therefore he wonders and asks.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about headache that has not healed for years.
