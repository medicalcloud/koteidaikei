# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There is an illness with puffiness as if there were water, a large and tight pulse on palpation, no pain in the body, a form not emaciated, inability to eat, and little intake. What is it called?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "There is an illness with puffiness ... What is it called?"] 痝然 means the face and eyes are puffy with mixed colour. "Large and tight" means like a bowstring; large indicates qi, tight indicates cold. Cold qi presses within, yet there is no pain; this differs from the usual and is abnormal; hence the question.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about an oedema-like illness with a large, tight pulse and no pain.
