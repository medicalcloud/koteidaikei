# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The network vessel of the uterus is tied to the kidney; the lesser yin vessel passes through the kidney and is tied to the root of the tongue; therefore one cannot speak."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the lesser yin vessel passes through the kidney ... cannot speak"] The lesser yin is the kidney vessel. The qi does not nourish; therefore the tongue cannot speak.

## Notes

Loss of voice explained by the connection of the uterine network vessel, the kidney and the root of the tongue.
