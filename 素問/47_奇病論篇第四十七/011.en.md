# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There are people whose body, hips, thighs and shins are all swollen, with pain around the navel. What illness is this?"

## Notes

The same question as unit 017 of the *Fuzhong lun* (chapter 40). There is no note by Wang Bing.
