# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The illness is called fetal illness. It is contracted while in the mother's womb: the mother suffered a great fright, her qi rose and did not descend, and the essential qi stayed together; therefore the child develops vertex illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "and the essential qi stayed together; therefore the child develops vertex illness"] Essential qi means the essential qi of yang.

## Notes

Congenital vertex illness is called fetal illness and attributed to a great fright of the mother during pregnancy.
