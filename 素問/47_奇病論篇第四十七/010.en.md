# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The illness is called breath accumulation. It does not hinder eating. Do not apply moxibustion or needling; persist with *daoyin* and take drugs; drugs alone cannot cure it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The illness is called breath accumulation ... drugs alone cannot cure it"] There is no form in the abdomen, yet below the flanks there is counterflow and fullness that does not heal for years; the breath shows in the form, the qi counterflows and breathing is difficult; hence it is called breath accumulation. The qi is not in the stomach; therefore it does not hinder eating. Moxibustion would melt within with the heat of fire, transforming the qi into wind; needling would surely drain the channel, turning it into depletion and ruin; therefore neither moxibustion nor needling may be applied. One should persist with *daoyin* to make the qi flow, and over time attack within with drugs to dissolve the stagnant accumulation. If one relies only on drugs without persisting with *daoyin*, the drugs alone cannot cure it.

## Notes

For breath accumulation, moxibustion and needling are avoided and *daoyin* is combined with drugs.
