# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There are people whose *chi* pulse is very rapid and whose sinews appear tense. What illness is this?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "There are people whose *chi* pulse is very rapid ... What illness is this?"] "Sinews tense" means the two sinews in the *chi* region behind the palm are tense. The *Maiyao jingwei lun* says, "The outside of the *chi* observes the kidney; the inside of the *chi* observes the abdomen." Now the *chi* pulse is rapid and tense; a rapid pulse indicates heat, and with heat the sinews should be slack; instead the sinews in the *chi* appear tense, so the sinews in the abdomen must be tense; hence he asks whether it is an illness. The *Lingshu jing* says, "With heat the sinews are slack; with cold the sinews are tense."

## Notes

Huangdi asks about an illness with a rapid *chi* pulse and tense sinews in the *chi* region.
