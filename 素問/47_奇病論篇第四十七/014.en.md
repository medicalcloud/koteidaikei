# 素問 奇病論篇第四十七 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "This is what is called sinew illness. Such a person's abdomen must be tense. If white or black colour appears, the illness is severe."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "This is what is called sinew illness ... the illness is severe"] "The abdomen tense" means the vertical sinews alongside the navel are all tense. Since the inside of the *chi* observes the abdomen, when the sinews in the *chi* are seen to be tense, the abdomen is surely cramped. "Colour appears" means it appears on the face. In reading the five colours, white indicates cold and black indicates cold; therefore when these two colours appear, the illness is all the more severe.

## Notes

This illness is called sinew illness, and its prognosis by facial colour is given.
