# 素問 厥論篇第四十五 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Reversal sometimes causes abdominal fullness, sometimes sudden loss of consciousness, recovering only after half a day or even a whole day. Why?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Reversal sometimes causes abdominal fullness ... Why?"] 暴 means sudden: suddenly stupefied and oppressed, not waking. "Not knowing people" means the oppression is so severe that one does not recognize people. Some call it corpse-like reversal.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about abdominal fullness and loss of consciousness due to reversal.
