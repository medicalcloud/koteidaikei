# 素問 厥論篇第四十五 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The front yin is where the ancestral sinew gathers and where the greater yin and *yangming* join. In spring and summer yang qi is plentiful and yin qi scant; in autumn and winter yin qi is abundant and yang qi declines. Such a person, though of strong constitution, is robbed [of essence] in autumn and winter through use; the lower qi contends with the upper and cannot recover, the essential qi overflows downward, and the evil qi follows it upward. With the qi lodged in the centre, the yang qi declines and cannot seep into and nourish the channels and network vessels; the yang qi diminishes daily and the yin qi alone remains; therefore the hands and feet become cold."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The front yin is where the ancestral sinew gathers and where the greater yin and *yangming* join"] The ancestral sinew runs down alongside the navel and joins at the genitals; hence "the front yin is where the ancestral sinew gathers". The greater yin is the spleen vessel and the *yangming* the stomach vessel; both lie close beside the ancestral sinew; hence "where the greater yin and *yangming* join".
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* reads 厥陰者衆筋之所聚 "the *jueyin* is where the many sinews gather" for 前陰者宗筋之所聚. Quan Yuanqi says, "The front yin is the *jueyin*," which differs from Wang's note; this too is a view in its own right.

[On "In spring and summer ... yang qi declines"] This is the natural way of heaven.

[On "Such a person ... the evil qi follows it upward"] 質 means physical constitution. "Robbed through use" means losing essential qi through excessive desire.

[On "With the qi lodged in the centre"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 所中 for 気因於中.

## Notes

Cold reversal arises when essential qi is lost in autumn and winter through sexual activity and the like, and yang qi declines.
