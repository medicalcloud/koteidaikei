# 素問 厥論篇第四十五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "Why does reversal have cold and heat?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why does reversal have cold and heat?"] 厥 means qi counterflowing upward. It has been mistakenly transmitted as leg *qi* (beriberi), and prescription treatises have elaborated it widely.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: cold reversal and hot reversal (counterflow of qi).
- Wang Bing criticizes the contemporary mistaken view that identifies reversal with leg *qi* (beriberi).
