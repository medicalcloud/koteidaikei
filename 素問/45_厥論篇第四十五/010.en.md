# 素問 厥論篇第四十五 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When alcohol enters the stomach, the network vessels become full and the channel vessels depleted. The spleen governs moving the fluids for the stomach. When yin qi is depleted, yang qi enters; when yang qi enters, the stomach is not in harmony; when the stomach is not in harmony, the essential qi is exhausted; when the essential qi is exhausted, it does not nourish the four limbs. Such a person surely often enters the bedchamber drunk or full; the qi gathers in the spleen and cannot disperse, the qi of alcohol and the qi of grain press on each other, and heat is abundant within; therefore heat pervades the body, there is internal heat and the urine is red. The qi of alcohol is abundant and fierce; the kidney qi declines and yang qi alone prevails; therefore the hands and feet become hot."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When alcohol enters the stomach ... it does not nourish the four limbs"] The front yin is where the greater yin and *yangming* join; therefore when the stomach is not in harmony, the essential qi is exhausted. The essence within is insufficient; therefore the four limbs have no qi to nourish them.

[On "Such a person surely often enters the bedchamber drunk or full ... become hot"] Entering the bedchamber drunk and full, one loses essential qi within; the centre is depleted and heat enters. Thus the kidney declines, yang is abundant and yin depleted; therefore heat arises in the hands and feet.

## Notes

Hot reversal arises when sexual activity after drinking or overeating weakens the kidney and makes yang abundant.
