# 素問 厥論篇第四十五 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When yin qi is abundant above, the lower part is deficient; when the lower part is deficient, the abdomen is distended and full. When yang qi is abundant above, the lower qi doubles upward and the pathogenic qi counterflows; when it counterflows, the yang qi is disordered; when the yang qi is disordered, one does not know people."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When yin qi is abundant above ... one does not know people"] Yin means the qi of the foot greater yin.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 腹満 "abdominal fullness" for the five characters 陽気盛於上; the *Jiayi jing* should be followed. Why? Elsewhere the *Jiayi jing* says, "When the yang vessels fall below and the yin vessels contend above, sudden syncope breaks out." How could it say yin qi is abundant above and also say yang qi is abundant above? Moreover, Zhang Zhongjing says, "When the lesser yin pulse does not arrive, the kidney qi is feeble and scant, the essence and blood rush, the qi is urgent and rises into the chest and diaphragm, the ancestral qi instead gathers, blood knots below the heart, yang qi retreats downward, heat returns to the inner thighs and stirs with yin, making the body numb; this is sudden syncope." Zhongjing's "yang qi retreats downward" means yang qi cannot be abundant above; therefore one knows the *Jiayi jing* should be followed. Moreover, Wang's note that "yin means the foot greater yin" is also incomplete. The *Miuci lun* says, "Pathogen lodges in the collaterals of the hand and foot lesser yin and greater yin and the foot *Yangming*; these five collaterals all meet in the ear and rise to connect at the left corner [of the head]; when all five are exhausted, all the vessels of the body pulsate but the body is without awareness, looking like a corpse; some call it sudden syncope." How can yin be explained solely as the greater yin?

## Notes

Explains abdominal fullness and loss of consciousness (sudden syncope) due to reversal.
- The Song editors hold that 陽気盛於上 should be 腹満 following the *Jiayi jing*, and consider Wang Bing's "yin means the foot greater yin" incomplete.
- The passage of Zhang Zhongjing quoted by the Song editors corresponds to 少陰脈不至，腎氣微，少精血，奔氣促迫，上入胸膈……此為尸厥 in the "Pingmai fa" of the *Shanghan lun*.
