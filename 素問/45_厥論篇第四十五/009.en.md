# 素問 厥論篇第四十五 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How does heat reversal of the feet come about?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How does heat reversal of the feet come about?"] He traces its origin.

## Notes

Huangdi asks the cause of heat reversal of the feet.
