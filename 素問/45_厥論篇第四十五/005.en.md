# 素問 厥論篇第四十五 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why does the cold of cold reversal of the limbs always rise from the five toes to the knees?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why does the cold of cold reversal of the limbs ...?"] Yin governs the inside, yet the reversal is outside; therefore he asks.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why cold reversal of the limbs rises from the toes to the knees.
