# 素問 厥論篇第四十五 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why does the heat of hot reversal always begin under the feet?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why does the heat of hot reversal always begin under the feet?"] Yang governs the outside, yet the reversal is inside; therefore he asks.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why hot reversal begins at the soles.
