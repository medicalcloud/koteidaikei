# 素問 厥論篇第四十五 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "When yang qi declines below, there is cold reversal; when yin qi declines below, there is hot reversal."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When yang qi declines below ... hot reversal"] Yang means the three yang vessels of the foot; yin means the three yin vessels of the foot; below means the feet.

## Notes

Decline of yang qi in the feet gives cold reversal; decline of yin qi gives hot reversal.
