# 素問 厥論篇第四十五 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Yang qi arises on the surface of the five toes; the yin vessels gather under the feet and converge at the centre of the sole. Therefore when yang qi prevails, the soles are hot."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Yang qi arises on the surface of the five toes ... the soles are hot"] Broadly speaking, the foot greater yang vessel emerges at the outer side of the tip of the little toe, the foot lesser yang vessel at the tip of the fourth toe, and the foot *yangming* vessel at the tips of the middle and big toes; all rise along the yang side of the foot. The vessels of the liver, spleen and kidney gather under the feet and converge at the centre of the sole. Yin is weak; therefore the soles are hot.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 走於足 "runs to the feet" for 起於足; 起 should be 走.

## Notes

Hot reversal explained by the distribution of the yang and yin channels of the foot.
