# 素問 厥論篇第四十五 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Yin qi arises on the inside of the five toes, gathers below the knees and converges above the knees. Therefore when yin qi prevails, it is cold from the five toes to above the knees. This cold does not come from outside; it all comes from within."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Yin qi arises on the inside of the five toes ... from within"] This too is a broad statement. The foot greater yin vessel arises at the inner side of the tip of the big toe, the foot *jueyin* vessel at the tip of the big toe in the three hairs, and the foot lesser yin vessel below the little toe, running obliquely to the sole. All rise along the yin side of the foot, follow the inner thigh and enter the abdomen; hence "gathers below the knees and converges above the knees".

## Notes

Cold reversal explained by the distribution of the yin channels of the foot.
