# 素問 厥論篇第四十五 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. I wish to hear the forms and manifestations of reversal in the six channel vessels."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good. I wish to hear the forms and manifestations of reversal in the six channel vessels"] The previous question having been resolved, he asks to hear fully about the reversal of all the channels.

## Notes

Huangdi asks the symptoms of reversal of the six channels.
