# 素問 解精微論篇第八十一 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong said, "Great indeed! May I ask why, when a person wails and weeps, tears do not come out, or come out with little snivel that does not follow them?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "or come out with little snivel that does not follow them? Why"] He wonders that what belongs to the same thing comes out differently.

## Notes

Leigong asks again about cases where tears and snivel do not accompany each other.
