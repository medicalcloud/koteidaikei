# 素問 解精微論篇第八十一 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The will is the master of the bones; hence water flowing and snivel following it are of the same kind in their movement. Snivel and tears are like brothers: in urgency they die together, in life they live together. When the will grieves early, snivel and tears come out together and flow across. That a person's snivel and tears come out together and follow each other is because they belong to the same kind."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "hence water flowing and snivel following it are of the same kind in their movement"] 類 means the same kind.

[On "like brothers: in urgency they die together, in life they live together"] Sharing a source, they live and die together.
Song editors: The *Taisu* has 出則倶亡 for 生則倶生.

[On "When the will grieves early, snivel and tears come out together and flow across"] 行 should probably be 流 ("flow").

[On "That a person's snivel and tears come out together and follow each other is because they belong to the same kind"] What they belong to is the brain. Why? The text above says "snivel and tears are the brain".

## Notes

The Emperor says tears and snivel come out together because they are of the same kind. His speech ends here.
