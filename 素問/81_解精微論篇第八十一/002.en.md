# 素問 解精微論篇第八十一 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Great indeed!"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The Emperor said, 'Great indeed!'"] It is what people take as most essential.

## Notes

The Emperor accepts the question.
