# 素問 解精微論篇第八十一 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "It is in the classic."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The Emperor said, 'It is in the classic'"] The *Lingshu jing* has the meaning of sorrow, grief, snivel and tears.

## Notes

The Emperor says it is in the classic.
