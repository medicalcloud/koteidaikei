# 素問 解精微論篇第八十一 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "When tears do not come out, the wailing is not sorrowful. When one does not weep, the spirit is not tender. When the spirit is not tender the will does not grieve; yin and yang hold each other, so how could tears come alone?

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the spirit is not tender the will does not grieve; yin and yang hold each other, so how could tears come alone"] Tears not coming out refers to tears; not weeping — here 泣 means wailing. The essence of water is the will, the essence of fire the spirit; water is yin and fire yang. Hence "yin and yang hold each other, so how could they come alone?"

## Notes

The Emperor answers that tears do not come out when one is not truly sorrowful. His speech continues through unit 13.
