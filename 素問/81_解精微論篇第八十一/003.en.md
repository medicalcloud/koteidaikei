# 素問 解精微論篇第八十一 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong asked, "When one wails and weeps but no tears come out, or they come out with little snivel, what is the reason?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When one wails and weeps but no tears come out ... what is the reason"] It asks which viscus does this.

## Notes

Leigong asks why tears sometimes do not come out when weeping.
