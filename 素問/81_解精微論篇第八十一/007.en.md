# 素問 解精微論篇第八十一 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence with sorrow and grief, tears fall; when tears fall, it is where water arises. The ancestor of water is accumulated water; accumulated water is the extreme yin; the extreme yin is the essence of the kidneys. That the water of the ancestral essence does not come out is because the essence holds it, supports it and wraps it, so the water does not move. The essence of water is the will; the essence of fire is the spirit. When water and fire affect each other and spirit and will both grieve, water arises in the eyes. Hence the proverb says, 'Sorrow of the heart is called sorrow of the will'; the will and the essence of the heart converge together in the eyes.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "when tears fall, it is where water arises. The ancestor of water is accumulated water"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* has 衆精 for 水宗.

[On "When water and fire affect each other and spirit and will both grieve, water arises in the eyes"] The eyes are the path of the upper fluids; hence when water and fire affect each other and spirit and will both grieve, the water fluid rises and only then arises in the eyes.

[On "Hence the proverb says ... the will and the essence of the heart converge together in the eyes"] Water and fire affect each other, hence "sorrow of the heart is called sorrow of the will". Spirit and will rise together, so the will and the heart-spirit rush together to the eyes.

## Notes

The Emperor says tears arise in the eyes when the essence of the kidneys (will) and of the heart (spirit) affect each other.
