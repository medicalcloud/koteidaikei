# 素問 解精微論篇第八十一 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hence when both grieve, the spirit qi is transmitted to the essence of the heart and not upward to the will, and the will grieves alone; hence tears come out. Snivel comes from the brain; the brain is yin; marrow is what fills the bones; hence the brain seeps out as snivel.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Snivel comes from the brain; the brain is yin"] The *Wuzang bie lun* holds that the brain is produced by earth's qi and, like the others, stores in yin and resembles earth; hence the brain is called yin. Yang rises and melts it; melting, it dissolves.
Song editors: The Quan Yuanqi edition, the *Jiayi jing* and the *Taisu* have 陽 for 陰.

[On "the brain is yin; marrow is what fills the bones"] 充 means full; marrow fills the bones and makes them full.

[On "marrow is what fills the bones; hence the brain seeps out as snivel"] The nasal orifices connect with the brain, so the brain seeps out as snivel that flows into the nose.

## Notes

The Emperor says snivel seeps out from the brain.
- The phrase 故言脳者陰陽上鑠也 in Wang Bing's note is hard to construe; the translation is tentative (not verified).
