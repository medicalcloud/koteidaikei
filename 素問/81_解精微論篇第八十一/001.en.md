# 素問 解精微論篇第八十一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor was in the Bright Hall. Leigong requested, saying, "I have received the teaching and in transmitting it I teach by means of the classic and its discussions, *Congrong* and the *Xingfa*, yin and yang, needling and moxibustion, and what decoctions and medicines nourish; but those who practice treatment are worthy or unworthy, and cannot necessarily achieve complete success. What you said earlier about sorrow and grief, joy and anger, dryness and damp, cold and heat, yin and yang, and women, and my asking why these are so; humble and noble, poor and rich, what human bodies follow, communicating with and employing subordinates, and applying the art of the Way to the matter at hand: these commands I have respectfully heard. Now I have some crafty, foolish, abrupt and leaky questions not found in the classic; I wish to hear about them."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "and what decoctions and medicines nourish; but those who practice treatment are worthy or unworthy, and cannot necessarily achieve complete success"] It says that what he himself received and uses can achieve complete success, but what is transmitted, taught and practiced by others cannot necessarily do so. Worthy means clear-minded and far-seeing; unworthy means clumsy and not following the rules.

[On "communicating with and employing subordinates, and applying the art of the Way to the matter at hand: these commands I have respectfully heard"] All this he had heard as the sage's instruction before, but had not yet fathomed the ends of its meaning.

[On "Now I have some crafty, foolish, abrupt and leaky questions not found in the classic; I wish to hear about them"] It means that lacking wisdom and seeing craftily, he asks many abrupt questions. 漏 means omissions; it refers to what the classic has not explained. 毚 means crafty; 愚 means an unwise view; 仆 means abrupt, that is, not gradual.
Song editors: The Quan Yuanqi edition has 朴 for 仆.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter: Leigong says he has questions not found in the classic and asks to be taught.
