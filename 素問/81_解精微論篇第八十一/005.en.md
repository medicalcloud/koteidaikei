# 素問 解精微論篇第八十一 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

[Leigong] asked again, "I do not know where water [tears] arises from, or where snivel comes from."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "[Leigong] asked again, 'I do not know where water arises from, or where snivel comes from'"] Asking again means asking a second time; he wants to know the origin of water and snivel.

## Notes

Leigong asks again where tears and snivel come from.
