# 素問 解精微論篇第八十一 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Asking about this is of no use for treatment, yet it is what the practitioner should know and what the Way produces. The heart concentrates the essence of the five viscera; the eyes are its orifices; the radiant complexion is its flourishing. Hence when a person has virtue, the qi is harmonious in the eyes; when there is loss, worry is known in the complexion.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Asking about this is of no use for treatment, yet it is what the practitioner should know and what the Way produces"] It says snivel and water are all produced by the qi of the Way; why ask about them?

[On "The heart concentrates the essence of the five viscera"] 専 means entrust: the essential qi of the five viscera are entrusted to the heart's direction, making it the house of the spirit illumination; hence it can do so.

[On "the eyes are its orifices"] The spirit guards within and the illumination reflects without; hence the eyes are its orifices.

[On "the radiant complexion is its flourishing"] The radiant complexion is the outer adornment of its spirit illumination.

[On "Hence when a person has virtue, the qi is harmonious in the eyes; when there is loss, worry is known in the complexion"] Virtue is the use of the Way and the life of humans. Laozi says, "The Way gives birth to them, virtue nurtures them." Qi is the master of life and the dwelling of the spirit. Heaven spreads virtue and earth transforms qi, and so humans live by them. When qi is harmonious the spirit is at peace; when the spirit is at peace its outward reflection is clear. When qi is not harmonious the spirit does not guard; when the spirit does not guard, the outward flourishing diminishes. Hence "when a person has virtue, the qi is harmonious in the eyes; when there is loss, worry is known in the complexion".
Song editors: The *Taisu* has 得 for 徳.

## Notes

The Emperor begins to explain the origin of tears from the relation of heart and eyes. His speech continues through unit 9.
