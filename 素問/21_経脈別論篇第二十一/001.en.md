# 素問 経脈別論篇第二十一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "Does the pulse also change with a person's dwelling, movement and rest, courage and timidity?"

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: does the pulse change with a person's way of life and temperament? There is no note by Wang Bing.
