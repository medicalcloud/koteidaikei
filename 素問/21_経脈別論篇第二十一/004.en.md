# 素問 経脈別論篇第二十一 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the greater yang zang [pulse] arrives alone, with reversal, panting, depletion and counterflow of qi, yin is insufficient and yang in surplus; both exterior and interior should be drained, taking the lower transport points. When the *yangming* zang arrives alone, the yang qi is doubly combined; one should drain yang and supplement yin, taking the lower transport points. When the lesser yang zang arrives alone, it is reversal qi; in front of the *qiao* it suddenly enlarges; take the lower transport points. The lesser yang arriving alone is excess of the one yang. When the greater yin zang beats, use the mind to examine whether it is the true [zang pulse]. When the qi of the five pulses is scant and the stomach qi not balanced, it is the three yin; one should treat its lower transport points, supplementing yang and draining yin. When the one yang alone whistles, it is reversal of the lesser yang: yang combines above, the four pulses contend and distend, and the qi returns to the kidney; one should treat its channels and network vessels, draining yang and supplementing yin. When the one yin arrives, it is governed by the *jueyin*: the true [qi] is depleted, the heart aches, reversal qi lingers and presses, and it breaks out as white sweat; regulate the food and harmonize the drugs, and treatment lies in the lower transport points."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the greater yang zang arrives alone ... yin is insufficient and yang in surplus"] Yin means the kidney and yang the bladder. Hence the text below says:

[On "both exterior and interior should be drained, taking the lower transport points"] "Yang arriving alone" means the yang qi arrives abundantly. When yang arrives alone, yang is in surplus and yin insufficient, and yang evil enters; therefore drain both exterior and interior, taking the six transport points of the foot. The lower transport points are the transport points of the foot.
Song editors: 六 "six" should be 穴 "point", a scribal error. The fu have six transport points and the zang only five; now zang and fu are both drained, so one should not say "six transport points", which cannot include the zang; saying "point transport points" covers both zang and fu.

[On "When the *yangming* zang arrives alone ... taking the lower transport points"] The yang qi is doubly combined; therefore drain yang and supplement yin.

[On "When the lesser yang zang arrives alone ... take the lower transport points"] *Qiao* means the yang *qiao* vessel, below the outer ankle. The foot lesser yang vessel runs to the end of the Juegu bone, descends to emerge in front of the outer ankle and follows the instep. When it suddenly enlarges in front of the *qiao*, the lesser yang qi is abundant; therefore take the lesser yang transport point of the foot.

[On "The lesser yang arriving alone is excess of the one yang"] The one yang is the lesser yang; 過 means excess. Because of its excess, it suddenly enlarges in front of the *qiao*.

[On "When the greater yin zang beats, use the mind to examine whether it is the true [zang pulse]"] When one sees the greater yin pulse beating hidden and drumming, one should examine it carefully; if it is the true-zang pulse, it should not be treated.

[On "When the qi of the five pulses is scant and the stomach qi not balanced, it is the three yin"] The three yin is the greater yin spleen pulse. When the pulses of the five zang are scant and the stomach qi not regulated, it too is the fault of the greater yin.

[On "one should treat its lower transport points, supplementing yang and draining yin"] Because the yin qi is excessive.

[On "When the one yang alone whistles, it is reversal of the lesser yang"] "Whistling" means a sound in the ears like whistling. The vessels of the gallbladder and triple burner both enter the ear; therefore when the qi counterflows upward, the ears ring.
Song editors: Above, the three yang were explained; here the three yin are discussed. That it speaks again of the lesser yang and not of the lesser yin suggests that this "one yang" is an error for "two yin". Moreover, Quan Yuanqi's edition has "reversal of the lesser yin", which clearly shows it is the two yin.

[On "yang combines above, the four pulses contend and distend, and the qi returns to the kidney"] When the four pulses of the heart, spleen, liver and lung contend and distend and yang combines above, the kidney qi is insufficient; therefore the qi returns to the kidney.

[On "one should treat its channels ... draining yang and supplementing yin"] When the yin qi is sufficient, the yang qi will no longer combine above.

[On "When the one yin arrives ... treatment lies in the lower transport points"] "One" is sometimes written "two", which is wrong. The *jueyin* is the one yin. Above it says that when the two yin arrive the lesser yin should govern; below it speaks of governance by the *jueyin*, so it should be the one yin arriving. The classics of the Three Sovereigns have long been neglected, few people study them, and many characters contain copying errors.

## Notes

The pathology when the pulse of each of the six channels arrives alone, and treatment by the lower transport points (points of the foot). Qibo's speech, begun in unit 002, ends here.
- The Song editors take "six transport points" in Wang Bing's note as an error for "point transport points", and suggest that "one yang" in "the one yang alone whistles, reversal of the lesser yang" is an error for "two yin" (lesser yin), as Quan Yuanqi's edition reads "reversal of the lesser yin".
- The passage is difficult and read in various ways; the translation follows Wang Bing.
- Wikisource (the plain text) writes 騨 for 㾓.
