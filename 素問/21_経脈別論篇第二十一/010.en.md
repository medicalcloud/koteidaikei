# 素問 経脈別論篇第二十一 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It resembles largeness and floating. The greater yin zang beating means hidden and drumming. The two yin beating and arriving means the kidney is sunken and not floating."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It resembles largeness and floating"] Song editors: The *Taisu* and Quan Yuanqi's edition read "it resembles the great floating of the heart".

[On "The greater yin zang beating ... not floating"] This clarifies the forms of the pulses arriving alone mentioned before.
Song editors: The two yin are missing earlier and the one yin is missing here; it is clear there are lacunae.

## Notes

The images of the pulses of *yangming*, greater yin and the two yin (lesser yin); this ends the chapter.
- According to the Song editors, the *Taisu* and Quan Yuanqi's edition read "it resembles the great floating of the heart", and descriptions of the two yin and one yin are missing in places.
