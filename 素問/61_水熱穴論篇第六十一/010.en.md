# 素問 水熱穴論篇第六十一 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In spring wood begins to rule and liver qi begins to arise. Liver qi is urgent and its wind is swift. The channels are always deep, and [in spring] the qi is scant and cannot enter deeply. Therefore one takes the network vessels and the spaces between the divisions of the flesh."

## Notes

Why the network vessels and divisions of the flesh are taken shallowly in spring. There is no note by Wang Bing.
