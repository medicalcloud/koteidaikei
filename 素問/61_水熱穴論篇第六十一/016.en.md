# 素問 水熱穴論篇第六十一 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In winter water begins to rule; the kidneys are just closing, yang qi is declining and scant, yin qi is firm and abundant, the greater yang lies hidden and sunken, and the yang vessels depart. Therefore one takes the well points to bring down the yin counterflow, and the *ying* points to replenish yang qi. Hence the saying 'Take the well and spring points in winter, and there will be no nasal congestion or nosebleed in spring' means this."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "the greater yang lies hidden and sunken, and the yang vessels depart"] 去 means going down and away.

[On "Therefore one takes the well points to bring down the yin counterflow, and the *ying* points to replenish yang qi"] Song editors: Quan Yuanqi's edition writes 遣 for 実, and the *Jiayi jing* and *Qianjin fang* write 通.

[On "Hence the saying 'Take the well and spring points in winter, and there will be no nasal congestion or nosebleed in spring'"] Song editors: Huangfu Shi'an says "this is the variation of treatment at the end of winter".

[On "means this"] Song editors: This differs considerably in sense from the *Sishi ci nicong lun* and the *Zhenyao jingzhong lun*, and agrees with the sense of the *Nine Scrolls*.

## Notes

Why the well and spring points are taken in winter. The Song editors point out differences from other chapters on seasonal needling.
