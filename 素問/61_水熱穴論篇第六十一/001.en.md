# 素問 水熱穴論篇第六十一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "Why does the lesser yin govern the kidneys? Why do the kidneys govern water?"

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter, on the relation of the lesser yin, the kidneys and water. There is no note by Wang Bing.
