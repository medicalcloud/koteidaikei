# 素問 水熱穴論篇第六十一 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "You, Master, have spoken of the fifty-nine points for treating heat illness. I have considered their meaning but cannot yet distinguish their locations. I wish to hear their locations, and through them their meaning."

## Notes

A question on the locations and significance of the fifty-nine points for heat illness. There is no note by Wang Bing.
