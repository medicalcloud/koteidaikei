# 素問 水熱穴論篇第六十一 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When cold is abundant, it produces heat."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold is abundant, it produces heat"] Cold qi congeals outside and yang qi is constrained inside; the interstices become firm and dense, and the 元府 (dark mansions) close and seal. When dense, qi cannot spread and pass; when sealed, damp qi binds inside. Inside and outside press on each other; cold is abundant and heat is produced. Hence when a person is injured by cold, it turns into heat. Since sweating cures it, the principle of congealing outside and constraint inside can be understood. This is a new illness of a few days.

## Notes

The chapter ends by stating that abundant cold produces heat.
- 元府 in Wang Bing's note means 玄府, presumably rewritten to avoid a Song taboo character.
- 殆知閣 (重广补注黄帝内经素问) places a 巻十七 break in this unit; this is that source's division into juan.
