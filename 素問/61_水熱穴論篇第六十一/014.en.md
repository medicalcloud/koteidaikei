# 素問 水熱穴論篇第六十一 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In autumn metal begins to rule; the lungs are about to gather and kill, and metal is about to overcome fire. Yang qi is at the *he* points; yin qi first prevails, and damp qi reaches the body. Yin qi is not yet abundant and cannot yet enter deeply. Therefore one takes the *shu* points to drain the yin pathogen and the *he* points to deplete the yang pathogen. Because yang qi begins to decline, one takes the *he* points."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In autumn metal begins to rule; the lungs are about to gather and kill"] The three yin have already risen, so [the season] gradually moves to gathering and killing.

[On "metal is about to overcome fire. Yang qi is at the *he* points"] Metal flourishes and fire declines; hence "metal is about to overcome fire".

[On "yin qi first prevails, and damp qi reaches the body"] Because one is gradually soaked by rain, damp, fog and dew, it says "damp qi reaches the body".

[On "the *he* points to deplete the yang pathogen ... one takes the *he* points"] Song editors: Huangfu Shi'an says "this is the variation of treatment in early autumn".

## Notes

Why the *shu* and *he* points are taken in autumn. The question names "channel and *shu*", while the answer speaks of *shu* and *he*.
