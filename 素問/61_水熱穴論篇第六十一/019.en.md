# 素問 水熱穴論篇第六十一 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why is it that when a person is injured by cold, it is transmitted into heat?"

## Notes

A question on why cold turns into heat. There is no note by Wang Bing.
