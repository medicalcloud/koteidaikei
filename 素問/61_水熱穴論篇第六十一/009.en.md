# 素問 水熱穴論篇第六十一 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why are the network vessels and the divisions of the flesh taken in spring?"

## Notes

The first of the questions on needling in the four seasons. There is no note by Wang Bing.
