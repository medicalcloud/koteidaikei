# 素問 水熱穴論篇第六十一 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why are the *jing* (well) and *ying* (spring) points taken in winter?"

## Notes

The question on needling in winter. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 栄 is written 滎 in the other sources (see the variants); it is translated as the *ying* (spring) points.
