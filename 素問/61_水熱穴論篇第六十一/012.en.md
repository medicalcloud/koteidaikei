# 素問 水熱穴論篇第六十一 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In summer fire begins to rule and heart qi begins to grow. The vessels are thin and the qi weak; yang qi lingers and overflows, heat fumigates the divisions and interstices and reaches inward to the channels. Therefore one takes the abundant channels and the divisions and interstices. That the illness departs merely by cutting the skin is because the pathogen resides shallowly. The so-called abundant channels are the yang vessels."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The vessels are thin and the qi weak; yang qi lingers and overflows"] Song editors: In another edition 留 is in one version written 流.

[On "That the illness departs merely by cutting the skin is because the pathogen resides shallowly"] 絶 means cutting through so that the illness can come out.

## Notes

Why the abundant channels and divisions and interstices are taken in summer.
