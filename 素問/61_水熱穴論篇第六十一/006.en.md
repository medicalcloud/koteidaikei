# 素問 水熱穴論篇第六十一 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The kidneys are the female depot. What rises from earth qi belongs to the kidneys and produces water and fluids; hence they are called the utmost yin. When one exerts oneself bravely and labors excessively, kidney sweat comes out. If kidney sweat meets wind on coming out, inside it cannot enter the depots and bowels, outside it cannot pass beyond the skin; it lodges in the dark mansions, travels within the skin, and is transmitted into puffy swelling. Its root is in the kidneys, and it is called wind water. The so-called dark mansions are the sweat hollows."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The kidneys are the female depot"] 牝 means yin. They also govern the yin position; hence "the female depot".

[On "When one exerts oneself bravely ... it is called wind water"] Exerting oneself bravely and laboring excessively means forceful sexual activity. When one labors and sweats, the dark mansions open; when sweat meets wind, they close again. Once they are closed, the remaining sweat has not come out; it lies hidden within the skin and is transformed into water. Because water arises from wind, it is called wind water.

[On "The so-called dark mansions are the sweat hollows"] Sweat is dark in color and comes out through the hollows. Because sweat gathers inside, they are called dark mansions; 府 means gathering.

## Notes

The kidneys as the female depot, and wind water arising when wind strikes sweat produced by exertion.
