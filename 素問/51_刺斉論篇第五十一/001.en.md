# 素問 刺斉論篇第五十一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi asked: "I wish to hear the limits of depth in needling."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I wish to hear the limits of depth in needling"] It means the positions of skin, flesh, sinew, vessel and bone.

## Notes

The opening question of the chapter: the limits of needling depth.
