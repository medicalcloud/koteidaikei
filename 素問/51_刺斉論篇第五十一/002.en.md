# 素問 刺斉論篇第五十一 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "In needling the bones, do not injure the sinews; in needling the sinews, do not injure the flesh; in needling the flesh, do not injure the vessels; in needling the vessels, do not injure the skin. In needling the skin, do not injure the flesh; in needling the flesh, do not injure the sinews; in needling the sinews, do not injure the bones."

## Notes

The principles of needling depth from both sides, too shallow and too deep; explained in the next unit. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- In this chapter the base text writes 岐伯 rather than 歧伯; the original keeps the base text.
