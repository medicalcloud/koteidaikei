# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When cold qi lodges in the vessels of the back transport points, the vessels become rough; when the vessels are rough the blood is depleted; when the blood is depleted there is pain. Its transport point pours into the heart; therefore [the heart and back] draw on each other and ache. Pressing brings hot qi; when hot qi arrives, the pain stops.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold qi lodges in the vessels of the back transport points ... the pain stops"] The back transport point means the heart transport point; its vessel is also the foot greater yang vessel. All transport points communicate inwardly with the zang; hence "its transport point pours into the heart, and they draw on each other and ache". Pressing lets warm qi enter; when warm qi enters, the heart qi issues outward; therefore the pain stops.

## Notes

Explains pain drawing between the heart and back.
