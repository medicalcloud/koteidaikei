# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When cold qi lodges in the thoroughfare vessel — the thoroughfare vessel arises at Guanyuan and rises straight up along the abdomen — when cold qi lodges, the vessel is blocked; when the vessel is blocked, the qi follows it; therefore it throbs against the hand.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold qi lodges in the thoroughfare vessel ... it throbs against the hand"] The thoroughfare vessel is one of the extraordinary vessels. Guanyuan is the name of a point, three *cun* below the navel. It says the vessel starts from this point and rises along the abdomen; it does not originate here. Its original source arises below the kidney. "Rises straight up" means it rises to meet at the throat. "The qi follows it" means that when the thoroughfare vessel is blocked, the qi of the foot lesser yin follows it and rises to fill it. The thoroughfare vessel runs alongside the lesser yin; therefore it throbs against the hand.

## Notes

Explains pain that throbs against the hand.
- 喘動応手 is understood as abdominal pulsation felt by the hand.
