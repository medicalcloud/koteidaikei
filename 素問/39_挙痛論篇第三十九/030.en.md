# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

With pensiveness the heart has something it holds on to, the spirit has somewhere it returns, and the upright qi stays and does not move; therefore the qi knots."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "With pensiveness the heart has something it holds on to ... the qi knots"] It is tied to the heart and does not disperse; therefore the qi also stops and stays.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 止 for the two characters 帰正.

## Notes

Explains pensiveness; this ends the chapter. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 022, ends here.
- According to the Song editors, the *Jiayi jing* reads 神有所止，気留而不行.
