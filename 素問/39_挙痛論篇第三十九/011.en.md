# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When reversal qi lodges in the inner thigh and cold qi rises to reach the lower abdomen, the blood is rough and pulls from below; therefore the abdominal pain draws to the inner thigh.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When reversal qi lodges in the inner thigh ... draws to the inner thigh"] This too is the qi of the *jueyin* liver vessel. Its vessel follows the inner thigh, enters the pubic hair, encircles the genitals and rises to reach the lower abdomen; hence "reversal qi lodges in the inner thigh and cold qi rises to reach the lower abdomen".

## Notes

Explains abdominal pain drawing to the inner thigh.
