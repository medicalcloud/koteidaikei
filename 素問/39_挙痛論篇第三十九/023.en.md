# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

With joy the qi is harmonious and the will unimpeded, and the nutritive and defensive flow freely; therefore the qi slackens.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "With joy the qi is harmonious ... the qi slackens"] The qi and vessels are harmonious and regulated; therefore the will is unimpeded and expansive. The nutritive and defensive flow freely; therefore the qi is slow and slack.

## Notes

Explains joy.
