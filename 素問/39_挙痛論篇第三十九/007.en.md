# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When cold qi lodges in the vessels alongside the spine, even deep pressing cannot reach it; therefore pressing is of no help.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold qi lodges in the vessels alongside the spine ... pressing is of no help"] The vessels alongside the spine are the *du* vessel in the middle and the foot greater yang vessels on both sides. The *du* vessel runs inside the spine, and the greater yang passes through the spinal muscles; therefore even deep pressing cannot reach them. Pressing the middle bends the spinal joints; pressing both sides gathers the spinal muscles together. Both bending and gathering prevent the defensive qi from passing, and the cold qi gathers more and is stored within; therefore pressing is of no help.

## Notes

Explains pain not helped by pressing.
