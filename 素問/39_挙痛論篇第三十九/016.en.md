# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When hot qi stays in the small intestine, there is pain in the intestines, burning heat and parching thirst, and [the stool] is hard and dry and cannot come out; therefore there are pain and blockage without passage."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When hot qi stays in the small intestine ... blockage without passage"] Heat makes the fluids seep away; therefore the stool is hard.

## Notes

Explains pain with blockage (constipation). Of the fourteen kinds of pain, this is the only one not due to cold. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 004, ends here.
