# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When cold qi lodges outside the vessels, the vessels are cold; when the vessels are cold they contract and curl; when they contract and curl, the vessels are bent and tense, and pull on the small network vessels outside; therefore there is sudden pain. With heat, the pain stops at once. If one is struck by cold again, the pain lasts long.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold qi lodges outside the vessels ... With heat, the pain stops at once"] The vessels circulate left and right; therefore with cold they contract, curl, bend and tense. When they contract, curl, bend and tense, the defensive qi cannot flow; therefore they pull on the small network vessels outside. The defensive qi does not enter, the cold presses within, and the vessels are tense and not relaxed; therefore pain arises. With heat the defensive qi moves again and the cold qi retreats; therefore the pain stops. 炅 means heat; 止 means end.

[On "If one is struck by cold again, the pain lasts long"] Doubled cold is hard to dissolve; therefore the pain lasts long without dissipating.

## Notes

From here to unit 016 the fourteen kinds of pain in the question are explained one by one; Qibo's speech continues to unit 016. This unit explains pain that stops suddenly and pain that lasts long.
