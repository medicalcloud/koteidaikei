# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What about graspable by touching?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What about graspable by touching?"] 捫 means feel: to follow and feel with the hand.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about palpation.
