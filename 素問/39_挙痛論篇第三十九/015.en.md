# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When cold qi lodges in the small intestine, the small intestine cannot gather [its contents]; therefore there are diarrhoea and abdominal pain.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold qi lodges in the small intestine ... diarrhoea and abdominal pain"] The small intestine is the fu of reception. When it is full inside, the cold evil does not stay; therefore it cannot knot and gather but is transmitted down into the ileum. The ileum is the broad intestine, the fu of conveyance; things cannot stop and stay there; therefore there are diarrhoea and pain.

## Notes

Explains abdominal pain followed by diarrhoea.
