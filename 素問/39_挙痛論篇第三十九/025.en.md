# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

With fear the essence retreats; when it retreats the upper burner closes; when it closes the qi turns back; when it turns back the lower burner distends; therefore the qi does not move.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "With fear the essence retreats ... the qi does not move"] With fear the yang essence retreats upward and does not flow down; hence "when it retreats the upper burner closes". Once the upper burner is closed, the qi does not flow, and the yin qi of the lower burner also turns back without dispersing and gathers into distension. Thus the upper burner is firmly blocked and the qi of the lower burner turns back, each keeping to one place; therefore the qi does not move.
Song editors: On examination, 気不行 should be 気下行 "the qi moves downward".

## Notes

Explains fear.
- The Song editors hold that 気不行 should be 気下行, to match "with fear the qi descends" in the question.
