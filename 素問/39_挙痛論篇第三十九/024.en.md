# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

With sorrow the heart connector becomes tense, the lung spreads and its lobes lift, the upper burner does not communicate, the nutritive and defensive do not disperse, and hot qi stays within; therefore the qi dissipates.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "With sorrow the heart connector becomes tense ... the qi dissipates"] 布葉 means the large lobes that spread and cover.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu* write 両焦不通 "the two burners do not communicate" for 而上焦不通. Moreover, Wang's note taking 肺布葉挙 as the large spreading, covering lobes is suspected to be wrong. Quan Yuanqi says, "Sorrow damages the heart; when the heart connector is tense it moves the lung; the lung qi, tied to the various channels, counterflows; therefore the lung spreads and its lobes lift." How can 肺布 be taken as the large spreading, covering lobes of the lung?

## Notes

Explains sorrow.
- 肺布葉挙: Wang Bing takes it as the large lobes of the lung; the Song editors, following Quan Yuanqi, take it as "the lung spreads and its lobes lift". The translation is closer to the latter.
