# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The five zang and six fu all have their regions [on the face]. Look at the five colours: yellow and red indicate heat, white indicates cold, green-blue and black indicate pain. This is what is called visible by looking."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The five zang and six fu all have their regions"] It means the regions on the face.

[On "Look at the five colours: yellow and red indicate heat"] When the interior is hot, the colour is yellow and red.

[On "white indicates cold"] Yang qi is scant and the blood does not rise to colour the face; therefore it is white.

[On "green-blue and black indicate pain"] When the blood congeals it changes for the worse; therefore when the colour is green-blue and black there is pain.

## Notes

Diagnosis by facial colour.
