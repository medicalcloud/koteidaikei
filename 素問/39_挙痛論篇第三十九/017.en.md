# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "That is what is called knowable by speaking. What about visible by looking?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "That is what is called knowable by speaking. What about visible by looking?"] It refers to observing the complexion.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about "visible by looking" from the opening question.
