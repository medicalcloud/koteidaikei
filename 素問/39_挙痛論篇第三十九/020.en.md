# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Look at the vessel that governs the illness; where it is hard and bloody, or sunken, all can be grasped by touching."

## Notes

Diagnosis by palpation. There is no note by Wang Bing.
