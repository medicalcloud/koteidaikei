# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

With heat the interstices open, the nutritive and defensive flow, and sweat pours out; therefore the qi leaks.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "With heat the interstices open ... the qi leaks"] People are relaxed in yang and gloomy in yin; therefore with heat the interstices of the skin open, the nutritive and defensive flow greatly, fluids seep outward and sweat pours out.

## Notes

Explains heat.
