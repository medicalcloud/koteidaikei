# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Among such pains, some stop suddenly; some are severe and unceasing; some are severe and cannot be pressed; some stop when pressed; some are not helped by pressing; some throb against the hand; some draw between the heart and back; some draw between the flanks and lower abdomen; some draw from the abdomen to the inner thigh; some persist and form accumulations; some are so sudden that one falls as if dead and does not know people, then revives after a while; some come with vomiting; some are abdominal pain followed by diarrhoea; some come with blockage and no passage. All these pains differ in form; how are they distinguished?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "All these pains differ in form; how are they distinguished?"] He wishes to clarify the origins of the different signs.

## Notes

Huangdi asks how fourteen kinds of pain are distinguished.
