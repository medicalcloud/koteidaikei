# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When cold qi lodges inside the channel vessels and presses against hot qi, the vessels become full; when full, there is pain that cannot be pressed. When cold qi lingers and hot qi follows it from above, the vessels are filled and enlarged and the blood and qi disordered; therefore the pain is severe and cannot be pressed.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold qi lodges inside the channel vessels ... pain that cannot be pressed"] Why pressing makes the pain severe is explained in the text below.

[On "When cold qi lingers ... cannot be pressed"] The vessels are already full and enlarged and the blood and qi disordered; pressing them makes the evil qi attack the interior; therefore they cannot be pressed.

## Notes

Explains pain that cannot be pressed.
