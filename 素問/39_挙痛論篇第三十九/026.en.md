# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

With cold the interstices close and the qi does not move; therefore the qi contracts.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "With cold the interstices close ... the qi contracts"] 腠 means where fluids seep out; 理 means where the lines of the skin meet. 閉 means close tightly; 気 means the wei-defensive qi; 行 means flow; 収 means contract. When the body is cold, the wei-defensive qi sinks; therefore the lines of the skin and the places of seepage all close tightly, the qi does not flow, and the wei-defensive qi contracts within and does not disperse.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 営衛不行 "the ying-nutrient and wei-defense do not move" for 気不行.

## Notes

Explains cold.
- 逄 in Wang Bing's note is a variant of 逢.
