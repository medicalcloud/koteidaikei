# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

With cold the interstices close and the qi does not move; therefore the qi contracts.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "With cold the interstices close ... the qi contracts"] 腠 means where fluids seep out; 理 means where the lines of the skin meet. 閉 means close tightly; 気 means the defensive qi; 行 means flow; 収 means contract. When the body is cold, the defensive qi sinks; therefore the lines of the skin and the places of seepage all close tightly, the qi does not flow, and the defensive qi contracts within and does not disperse.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 営衛不行 "the nutritive and defensive do not move" for 気不行.

## Notes

Explains cold.
- 逄 in Wang Bing's note is a variant of 逢.
