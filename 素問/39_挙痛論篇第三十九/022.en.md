# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "With anger the qi counterflows; when severe, there are vomiting of blood and diarrhoea with undigested food; therefore the qi rises.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "With anger the qi counterflows; when severe, there are vomiting of blood and diarrhoea with undigested food"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu* write 食而気逆 "eating with counterflow of qi" for 飱泄.

[On "therefore the qi rises"] With anger the yang qi counterflows upward and the liver qi encroaches on the spleen; therefore when severe there are vomiting of blood and diarrhoea with undigested food. How is this made clear? With anger the face is red; when severe, the colour is green-blue. The *Lingshu jing* says, "Violent anger that does not stop harms the will." This shows that with anger the qi counterflows upward and does not descend.

## Notes

From here to unit 030 each of the nine qi is explained; Qibo's speech continues to unit 030. This unit explains anger.
