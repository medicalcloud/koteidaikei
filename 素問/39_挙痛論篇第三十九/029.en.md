# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

With toil there are panting and sweating, and both outside and inside go beyond limits; therefore the qi is consumed.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "With toil there are panting and sweating ... the qi is consumed"] When exhausted by labour, the qi rushes fast; therefore there is panting. When the qi rushes fast, yang issues outward; therefore there is sweating. Panting and sweating together, inside and outside all exceed their normal order; therefore the qi is consumed and damaged.

## Notes

Explains toil.
