# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When cold qi lodges in the *jueyin* vessel — the *jueyin* vessel connects with the genitals and is tied to the liver — when cold qi lodges in the vessel, the blood is rough and the vessel tense; therefore the flanks and lower abdomen draw on each other and ache.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold qi lodges in the *jueyin* vessel ... draw on each other and ache"] The *jueyin* is the liver vessel; it enters the pubic hair, encircles the genitals, reaches the lower abdomen, rises through the liver and diaphragm and spreads over the flanks; hence "connects with the genitals and is tied to the liver". The vessel is tense and pulls the flanks and lower abdomen, causing pain.

## Notes

Explains pain drawing between the flanks and lower abdomen.
