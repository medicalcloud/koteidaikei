# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When cold qi lodges in the five zang, there is reversal and counterflow leaking upward; the yin qi is exhausted and the yang qi has not yet entered; therefore one suddenly falls with pain as if dead and does not know people. When the qi returns, one lives.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold qi lodges in the five zang ... When the qi returns, one lives"] It says the qi of the zang is blocked by cold and does not move; when the qi can pass again, it ends.
Song editors: On examination, 擁胃 in the note is suspected to be 擁冒 "covered and blocked".

## Notes

Explains sudden pain with loss of consciousness and recovery after a while.
