# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When cold qi lodges between the intestines and stomach, below the membrane source, the blood cannot disperse and the small collaterals become tense and pull; therefore there is pain. Pressing disperses the blood and qi; therefore pressing stops the pain.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold qi lodges between the intestines and stomach ... pressing stops the pain"] 膜 means the membrane between the diaphragm; 原 means the source of the diaphragm and *huang*. "The blood cannot disperse" means the blood inside the small collaterals within the diaphragm membrane. When the collaterals are full they are tense; therefore they pull and pain arises. Pressing with the hand disperses the cold qi and relaxes the small collaterals; therefore the pain stops.

## Notes

Explains pain that stops on pressing.
