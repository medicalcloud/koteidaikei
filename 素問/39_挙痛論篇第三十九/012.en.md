# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When cold qi lodges between the small intestine and the membrane source, in the blood of the network vessels, the blood becomes rough and cannot pour into the great channels; the blood and qi linger and cannot move; therefore over time it forms accumulations.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold qi lodges between the small intestine ... forms accumulations"] It says the blood is congealed and knotted by cold qi and forms accumulations.

## Notes

Explains pain that persists and forms accumulations.
