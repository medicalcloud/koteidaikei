# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I wish to hear what qi causes sudden pain in a person's five zang." Qibo replied: "The channel vessels flow without stopping and circulate without rest. When cold qi enters the channels, they are delayed and rough and do not move. When it lodges outside the vessels the blood becomes scant; when it lodges inside the vessels the qi does not pass. Therefore there is sudden pain."

## Notes

The basic cause of sudden pain is cold qi entering the channels and stagnating the flow of blood and qi. There is no note by Wang Bing.
