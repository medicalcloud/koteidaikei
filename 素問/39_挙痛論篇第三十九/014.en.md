# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When cold qi lodges in the intestines and stomach, there is reversal and counterflow going upward; therefore there are pain and vomiting.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When cold qi lodges in the intestines and stomach ... pain and vomiting"] When cold lodges and stays in the intestines and stomach, the yang qi cannot flow downward and instead moves upward. If the cold does not leave, pain arises; when yang moves upward, there is vomiting. Therefore there are pain and vomiting.

## Notes

Explains pain with vomiting.
- 𠸺 in Wang Bing's 嘔𠸺 denotes a vomiting symptom close to retching, as in 嘔吐不已則傳𠸺，𠸺之不已即作噦 in the *Shengji zonglu*; it is translated as retching.
