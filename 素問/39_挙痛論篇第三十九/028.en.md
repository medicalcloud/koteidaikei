# 素問 挙痛論篇第三十九 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

With fright the heart has nothing to lean on, the spirit nowhere to return, and thought nothing to settle on; therefore the qi is disordered.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "With fright the heart has nothing to lean on ... the qi is disordered"] The qi rushes and overflows; therefore it is not regulated.
Song editors: The *Taisu* writes 憂 "worry" for 驚 "fright".

## Notes

Explains fright.
