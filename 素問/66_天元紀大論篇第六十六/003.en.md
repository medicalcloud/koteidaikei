# 素問 天元紀大論篇第六十六 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "I wish to hear how the five movements govern the seasons."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I wish to hear how the five movements govern the seasons"] The seasons are the four seasons.

## Notes

A question on the relation between the five movements and the four seasons.
