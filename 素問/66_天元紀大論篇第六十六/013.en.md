# 素問 天元紀大論篇第六十六 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Your words, Master, exhaust the qi of heaven above and complete the cycle of earth below; they may be called thorough. I wish to hear and store them, to govern the people above and to govern myself below, to make the hundred clans enlightened, those above and below harmonious and close, virtue and benefit flowing down, descendants free of worry, transmitting it to later ages without end. May I hear it?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "transmitting it to later ages without end. May I hear it?"] Not forgetting danger in safety and not forgetting ruin in survival is the supreme teaching of the great sage. Seeking out the ills of the people and pitying their 𨼆 [hidden suffering] is the deep benevolence of the great sage.

## Notes

The Emperor wishes to transmit this way to later ages.
