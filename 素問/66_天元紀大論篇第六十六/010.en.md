# 素問 天元紀大論篇第六十六 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guiyuqu said: "Cold, summer heat, dryness, damp, wind and fire are the yin and yang of heaven, and the three yin and three yang serve them above. Wood, fire, earth, metal, water and fire are the yin and yang of earth, and generation, growth, transformation, gathering and storing respond to them below. Heaven generates by yang and grows by yin; earth kills by yang and stores by yin. Heaven has yin and yang, and earth also has yin and yang. Wood, fire, earth, metal, water and fire are the yin and yang of earth: generation, growth, transformation, gathering and storing. Thus within yang there is yin, and within yin there is yang. To know the yin and yang of heaven and earth: the qi corresponding to heaven moves without rest, so in five years it shifts to the right; the qi corresponding to earth is still and keeps its position, so in six years it comes round to meet. Movement and stillness summon each other, above and below face each other, yin and yang intermingle, and change arises from this."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Cold, summer heat ... serve them above"] The greater yang is cold, the lesser yang summer heat, the *yangming* dryness, the greater yin damp, the *jueyin* wind, the lesser yin fire. Their origin is all in heaven; hence "the yin and yang of heaven".

[On "Wood, fire, earth, metal, water and fire ... respond to them below"] Wood is the first qi, fire the second, ministerial fire the third, earth the fourth, metal the fifth, water the final qi. Being on earth and responding to heaven, they are said to "respond below". Since the qi are on earth, they are called "the yin and yang of earth".
Song editors: The *Liuwei zhi dalun* says "How does the pattern of earth correspond to the positions of the six-stage qi?" and Qibo says "To the right of the manifest brightness is the position of the sovereign fire; one step back, ministerial fire governs; another step, earth qi governs; another step, metal qi governs; another step, water qi governs; another step, wood qi governs." This is the meaning of "wood, fire, earth, metal, water and fire are the yin and yang of earth".

[On "Heaven generates by yang and grows by yin; earth kills by yang and stores by yin"] Generation and growth are the way of heaven; storing and killing the way of earth. Heaven, as yang, governs generation, so it generates by yang and grows by yin; earth, as yin, governs killing, so it kills by yang and stores by yin. Though heaven and earth differ in height, each has the working of yin and yang.
Song editors: On examination, this text duplicates the *Yinyang yingxiang dalun*, but the notes differ considerably.

[On "Heaven has yin and yang, and earth also has yin and yang"] Heaven has yin, so it can descend; earth has yang, so it can rise. Hence each has yin and yang. When yin and yang meet in harmony, transformation and change are completed through them.

[On "Wood, fire ... Thus within yang there is yin, and within yin there is yang"] When the qi of yin and yang reach the extreme, they become excessive; hence each includes the other. The *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* says "extreme cold produces heat; extreme heat produces cold" and also "doubled yin must become yang; doubled yang must become yin", meaning that qi changes at its extreme. So yang includes yin and yin includes yang. In the hexagrams of the *Changes*, Li is empty in the middle and Kan full in the middle; this is its image.

[On "To know the yin and yang of heaven and earth ... in six years it comes round to meet"] Heaven has six qi, earth five positions. Heaven presides over earth with six qi; earth receives heaven with five positions—presumably because heaven's qi is not added to the sovereign fire. Adding six to five, in five years one qi is left over, so it shifts one position. Receiving six with five, it always takes six years to complete the qi of the heavenly origin; hence in six years it comes round to meet—the so-called circling round to begin again. Earth qi moves left and goes without returning. Heaven's qi turns east; counting five years from the fire movement, the next qi falls precisely above the qi of the sovereign fire. Since by rule it is not added there, it shifts right past the sovereign fire to preside above the ministerial fire; hence "in five years it shifts to the right". Through this, movement and stillness, above and below face each other, and the dispositions of heaven, earth and the myriad things and the mechanism of change can be seen.

[On "Movement and stillness summon each other ... change arises from this"] The way of heaven and earth and the subtlety of change come from this. Confucius said: "Heaven and earth set their positions, and change proceeds between them." This is what is meant.
Song editors: The *Wuyunxing dalun* says "above and below meet, cold and heat face each other; when the qi suit each other there is harmony, when not, illness", and also "the upper moves right, the lower moves left; left and right circle heaven with a surplus and meet again".

## Notes

The relation between the six qi of heaven and the five phases (six positions) of earth, and change arising from the mismatch of the five- and six-year cycles.
- 木火土金水火，地之陰陽也，生長化収蔵 occurs twice; the second may be a repetition, but the notes do not mention it. The translation follows the characters.
- The blank in Wang Bing's 五歳而　余一気 is as in the base text.
