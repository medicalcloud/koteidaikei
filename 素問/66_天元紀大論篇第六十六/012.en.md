# 素問 天元紀大論篇第六十六 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guiyuqu said: "Heaven takes six as its measure; earth takes five as its regulation. Circling heaven's qi, six years make one complete set; completing earth's cycle, five years make one round. The sovereign fire acts by brightness; the ministerial fire acts by position. Five and six combine, and seven hundred and twenty qi make one period, thirty years in all; one thousand four hundred and forty qi, sixty years in all, make one full cycle, and deficiency and excess are all seen in it."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Heaven takes six as its measure ... five years make one round"] The six measures are the divisions of the six qi; the five regulations are the divisions of the five positions. A position corresponds to one year and a qi governs one year; hence five years make one round and six years one complete set. 備 means passing through all of heaven's qi; 周 means circling earth's positions. Earth's positions are six but are called five because heaven's qi does not preside over the sovereign fire.

[On "The sovereign fire acts by brightness; the ministerial fire acts by position"] The sovereign fire is to the right of the ministerial fire; it merely establishes its name in the sovereign position and does not establish a year qi. So heaven's six qi do not pair with its qi. What carries out the government of the sovereign fire keeps its position, receives heaven's command and proclaims the decrees of fire. Because it serves heaven by name, it is said "the sovereign fire acts by name (名)"; because it keeps its position and receives the command, it is said "the ministerial fire acts by position".

[On "Five and six combine ... deficiency and excess are all seen in it"] In the calendar one qi is fifteen days. Multiplying accordingly, seven hundred and twenty qi amount to thirty years, and one thousand four hundred and forty qi to sixty years. The classic says "when surplus goes, insufficiency follows it; when insufficiency goes, surplus follows it". So in sixty years deficiency and excess are all seen.
Song editors: The *Liujie zangxiang lun* says: "Five days are called a period, three periods a qi, six qi a season, four seasons a year, each governed according to its ruler. The five movements succeed one another and each governs; when the days of the year are completed, they circle round and begin again. The seasons are established and the qi spread like a ring without end; the periods follow the same rule. So if one does not know what is added to the year, the abundance and decline of qi, and where depletion and repletion arise, one cannot become [a practitioner]."

## Notes

The cycles of the six qi and five movements, and the periods of seven hundred and twenty qi (thirty years) and one thousand four hundred and forty qi (sixty years).
- Wang Bing reads 君火以明 as 君火以名, explaining that the sovereign fire serves heaven by name. The translation follows the characters of the main text.
