# 素問 天元紀大論篇第六十六 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guiyuqu said: "The five qi move in turn, each completing the days of a year; they do not merely govern a season."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The five qi move in turn ... do not merely govern a season"] The days of one movement change only after three hundred and sixty-five and a quarter degrees are completed. It does not govern one season and become extinct according to flourishing, supporting, imprisonment and death; within the intersection of qi it exists far apart and distinct.

## Notes

Each of the five movements governs a whole year.
