# 素問 天元紀大論篇第六十六 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guiyuqu said: "I have repeatedly studied the text of the *Taishi tianyuan ce*, which says: 'The great void is vast and empty; it founds the origin of transformation. The myriad things rely on it to begin; the five movements complete heaven's [cycle], spread qi to the true spirits, and govern the origin of earth. The nine stars hang bright; the seven luminaries circle round. It is called yin and yang, called soft and hard. The hidden and the manifest take their places; cold and heat slacken and tighten; generating and generating, transforming and transforming, all kinds of things become manifest.' My family has transmitted this for ten generations; this is what it means."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "I have repeatedly studied the text of the *Taishi tianyuan ce*, which says"] The *Tianyuan ce* records the principles of the movement of the true original qi of heaven. From the age of Shennong, Guiyuqu's ancestor ten generations back first recited and practiced it. It is a numinous text of divination from high antiquity; by the time of Fuxi it had already been engraved on 王 [jade] tablets and named the 冊文 (tablet text). Being a numinous text of high antiquity, it is named the *Taishi tianyuan ce*.
Song editors: On examination, there is today a *Tianyuan yuce*, which some consider to be this *Taishi tianyuan ce*; this is not so.

[On "The great void is vast and empty; it founds the origin of transformation"] The great void means the realm of empty mystery, where true qi fills, the palace of spirit brightness. True qi is subtle and reaches everywhere, however far; hence it can be the root and beginning of generation and transformation and the true origin of the movements and qi. 肇 means beginning; 基 means root.

[On "The myriad things rely on it to begin; the five movements complete heaven's [cycle]"] The five movements are the movements of wood, fire, earth, metal and water. "Complete heaven" means one year of three hundred and sixty-five and a quarter degrees. End and beginning alternate and circle round to begin again. It means that the five movements govern the great void in turn, the four seasons shift and return by their divisions, the six qi reside separately with different governance, and the myriad things are generated and transformed through them. If not by nature, who could begin them? Hence "the myriad things rely on it to begin". The *Changes* says: "Great is the originating power of Qian! The myriad things rely on it to begin; it governs heaven. Clouds move and rain falls, and all kinds of things take form." Confucius said: "What does heaven say? The four seasons proceed and the hundred things are born." This is the meaning.

[On "spread qi to the true spirits, and govern the origin of earth"] The true qi of the great void reaches everywhere. Since qi equally produces and gives being, what receives qi and contains spirit embraces true qi and is born. "Govern the origin of earth" means that the original qi of heaven always controls the way of generation and transformation of earth qi. The *Changes* says: "Perfect is the originating power of Kun! The myriad things rely on it to be born; it complies and receives heaven."

[On "The nine stars hang bright; the seven luminaries circle round"] The nine stars belong to the time of high antiquity. In high antiquity the world was simple and people pure; they returned to truth and simplicity, the nine stars hung bright, and the five movements were equally displayed. In middle antiquity, as virtue somewhat declined, the marker stars hid their light, so the visible stars counted seven. The nine stars are Tianpeng, Tiannei, Tianchong, Tianfu, Tianqin, Tianxin, Tianren, Tianzhu and Tianying; this presumably begins from the marker, and the *dunjia* divination method still uses it. The seven luminaries are the sun, moon and five planets; foreign lands today all use this calendar as the sign of good and bad fortune for their actions. 周 means the degrees of the circuit of heaven; 旋 means moving leftward following the degrees of heaven. The courses of the five planets each have advance and retreat, high and low, small and large.

[On "It is called yin and yang, called soft and hard"] Yin and yang are the way of heaven; soft and hard are the way of earth. Heaven generates by yang and grows by yin; earth transforms by softness and completes by hardness. The *Changes* says: "Establishing the way of heaven, it is called yin and yang; establishing the way of earth, it is called soft and hard." This is what is meant.

[On "The hidden and the manifest take their places; cold and heat slacken and tighten"] "The hidden and manifest take their places" means that humans and spirits each obtain their order. "Cold and heat slacken and tighten" means that yin and yang do not lose their fitness. Humans and spirits each keep to their places without trespassing on each other; yin and yang do not lose their order; things obtain their fitness. If even the way of heaven and earth is so, the principle of humans and spirits is likewise.
Song editors: The *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "What are the hidden and the bright?" and Qibo says "When two yin meet and are exhausted, it is called hidden; when two yang unite in brightness, it is called bright. The pairing of hidden and bright is the difference of cold and heat."

[On "generating and generating, transforming and transforming, all kinds of things become manifest"] The first 生 means generating beings with feeling and awareness; the second 生 means generating beings without feeling or awareness. The first 化 means those whose forms are manifest; the second 化 means those whose forms are concealed. Those with feeling and awareness and manifest forms are governed by heaven's qi; those without feeling or awareness and with concealed form and substance are governed by earth's qi. All are transformed and nurtured by receiving the original numinous qi. The *Changes* says "Heaven and earth intermingle, and the myriad things are transformed and refined"; is this what is meant?

[On "My family has transmitted this for ten generations; this is what it means"] This text has been transmitted and studied for ten generations down to Guiyuqu without daring to let it be lost.

## Notes

Citing the *Taishi tianyuan ce*, Guiyuqu sets out the outline of the movements of heaven.
- 廖 in 廖廓 is written 寥 in the other sources (see the variants); the translation reads it as 寥.
- In Wang Bing's note 王　版 presumably stands for 玉版, and 天内 among the nine stars for 天芮, but the latter has not been checked.
