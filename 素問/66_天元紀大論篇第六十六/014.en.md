# 素問 天元紀大論篇第六十六 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guiyuqu said: "The mechanism of the ultimate numbers is close and subtle; its coming can be seen and its going can be followed. Those who revere it prosper; those who slight it perish. Acting selfishly against the way surely brings heaven's calamity. Respectfully serving the way of heaven, let me speak of the true essentials."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Those who revere it prosper ... heaven's calamity"] It means transmitting it to the wrong people, giving it to intimates or to those who come seeking fame and profit.

[On "Respectfully serving the way of heaven, let me speak of the true essentials"] He declares an oath of caution to the sovereign and then plainly speaks the supreme true essentials of the way of heaven.

## Notes

The way of the ultimate numbers must be transmitted with care.
