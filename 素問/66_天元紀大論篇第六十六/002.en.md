# 素問 天元紀大論篇第六十六 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guiyuqu bowed his head to the ground twice and replied: "How illuminating the question is! The five movements and yin and yang are the way of heaven and earth, the guiding principles of the myriad things, the father and mother of change and transformation, the root and beginning of generation and killing, and the residence of spirit brightness. Can one fail to understand them? Thus the birth of things is called transformation, the extreme of things is called change, the unfathomable in yin and yang is called spirit, and the use of spirit without fixed direction is called sagehood. The working of change and transformation: in heaven it is the mysterious, in humans it is the way, on earth it is transformation. Transformation produces the five flavors, the way produces wisdom, the mysterious produces spirit. Spirit in heaven is wind, on earth wood; in heaven heat, on earth fire; in heaven damp, on earth earth; in heaven dryness, on earth metal; in heaven cold, on earth water. Thus in heaven it is qi, on earth it forms shape, and shape and qi interact to transform the myriad things. Heaven and earth are the above and below of the myriad things; left and right are the roads of yin and yang; water and fire are the signs and omens of yin and yang; metal and wood are the end and beginning of generation and completion. Qi has more and less, shape has abundance and decline; above and below summon each other, and loss and gain become manifest."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The five movements and yin and yang ... Can one fail to understand them?"] 道 means the way of transformation and generation. 綱紀 means the guiding principles of generation, growth, transformation, completion, gathering and storing. 父母 means what precedes the forms of the myriad things. 本始 means that generation and killing both arise from it. There has never been anything that has form and receives qi yet is not governed by the five movements and yin and yang. Why can creation be inexhaustible and be the origin of the generation and transformation of the myriad things? Because it is the residence of spirit brightness. That combination and dispersal are unfathomable and generation and transformation endless cannot be unless spirit brightness moves them.
Song editors: On examination, from "yin and yang" to "the residence of spirit brightness" agrees with the *Yinyang yingxiang dalun*, but the notes of the two treatises differ considerably.

[On "Thus the birth of things is called transformation ... is called sagehood"] This is the so-called way of transformation, change, sagehood and spirit. Transformation is bestowing and transforming; change is dispersing and altering; spirit is having no set time; sagehood is having no deliberation. Because qi bestows and transforms, it is called birth; because qi disperses and alters, it is called extreme; because it receives no set time or indication, it is called spirit; because it involves no deliberation or measurement, it is called sagehood. Through transformation and change, the myriad things cannot escape the five movements and yin and yang; through sagehood and spirit, the many wonders cannot go beyond the principle of the profound. How deep! Its wondrous working cannot be described.
Song editors: The *Liuwei zhi dalun* says: "The birth of things follows transformation; the extreme of things comes from change; the pressing together of change and transformation is the source of success and failure." Also the *Wuchang zheng dalun* says: "When qi begins, there is generation and transformation; when qi disperses, there is form; when qi spreads, there is flourishing; when qi ends, the image changes. What they bring about is one."

[On "The working of change and transformation"] The working that responds to the myriad transformations.

[On "in heaven it is the mysterious"] 玄 means far. The way of heaven is far and profound, and its changes and transformations are endless. A commentary says: "The way of heaven is far; the way of man is near."

[On "in humans it is the way"] 道 means the way of wondrous working. Classical learning and government cannot succeed without the way.

[On "on earth it is transformation"] 化 means generation and transformation. What generates the myriad things is earth; without the gestation of earth qi, form and substance would not come about.

[On "Transformation produces the five flavors"] The sour, bitter, sweet, bland, acrid and salty of metals, stones, and the roots, leaves, flowers and fruits of plants are all produced by the qi of transformation and appear according to the season.

[On "the way produces wisdom"] Wisdom penetrates wondrous working and is produced only by the way.

[On "the mysterious produces spirit"] Being far, hidden and deep, it produces spirit. The working of spirit penetrates mysteriously whatever it touches, accords with things to transform and complete them, and responds to all.

[On "Spirit in heaven is wind"] Wind is the beginning of teaching, the messenger of heaven, the command of heaven.

[On "on earth wood"] The transformation of the east.

[On "in heaven heat"] It works in correspondence with fire.

[On "on earth fire"] The transformation of the south.

[On "in heaven damp"] It works in correspondence with earth.

[On "on earth earth"] The transformation of the center.

[On "in heaven dryness"] It works in correspondence with metal.

[On "on earth metal"] The transformation of the west.

[On "in heaven cold"] It works in correspondence with water.

[On "on earth water"] The transformation of the north. The working of spirit is as in the five transformations above. Wood is generated by wind, fire blazes from heat, metal issues from dryness, water is supported by cold, earth is completed by damp. These are what they first rely on to be established. Although at first they rely on these to transform and be completed, in the end they rely on them to fail and disperse. Is this so only for the five phases? Whatever is established through what it relies on also scatters and falls through what it relies on.
Song editors: On examination, from "in heaven it is the mysterious" to here, the text duplicates the *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* and *Wuyunxing dalun*, but the notes differ considerably.

[On "Thus in heaven it is qi, on earth it forms shape"] Qi means wind, heat, damp, dryness and cold; shape means wood, fire, earth, metal and water.

[On "and shape and qi interact to transform the myriad things"] This is the great principle of creation and generation.

[On "Heaven and earth are the above and below of the myriad things"] Heaven covers and earth carries; above and below face each other, and the myriad things are generated and transformed without omission. So the myriad things of themselves arise, grow, transform, complete, fill, empty, return and change. What is change? It is when the qi of generation reaches the extreme of its root and begins to transform anew. Confucius said: "He shapes the myriad things in detail without omission."

[On "left and right are the roads of yin and yang"] Heaven has six qi presiding over what is below; earth has five phases serving what is above. The one that matches the year is above and governs as the controller of heaven; the one that receives the year is below and governs as the controller of earth. Of those that do not match the year, two qi are on the right and turn moving north, two are on the left and turn moving south. When the movements of metal, wood, water and fire are set facing north, left always becomes right and right left; so the left moves south and the right north, in reverse.
Song editors: On examination, the meaning of the theory of above, below, left and right is given in full in the *Wuyunxing dalun*.

[On "water and fire are the signs and omens of yin and yang"] 徴 means proof and verification; 兆 means precursor. By the cold and heat of water and fire, the precursors of yin and yang are made manifest and confirmed.

[On "metal and wood are the end and beginning of generation and completion"] Wood governs emergence and corresponds to spring, and spring is the beginning of generation and transformation; metal governs gathering in and corresponds to autumn, and autumn is the end of completion and fruition. End and beginning never cease and their transformation always proceeds; so the generation, growth, transformation, completion, gathering and storing of the myriad things last of themselves.
Song editors: The *Yinyang yingxiang dalun* says: "Heaven and earth are the above and below of the myriad things; yin and yang are the male and female of blood and qi; left and right are the roads of yin and yang; water and fire are the signs and omens of yin and yang; yin and yang are the power and beginning of the myriad things." This partly overlaps with the present treatise.

[On "Qi has more and less ... loss and gain become manifest"] "Qi has more and less" means that the three grades of yin and yang in heaven differ in amount and rank. "Shape has abundance and decline" means that the qi of the five movements has excess and deficiency. Thus, through more and less, abundance and decline, heaven and earth summon each other, and the loss and gain of yin and yang become plainly visible.
Song editors: On examination, the meaning of the three grades of yin and yang is given in the notes to the text below.

## Notes

The five movements and yin and yang as the way of heaven and earth; definitions of transformation, change, spirit and sagehood; the correspondence of the five qi of heaven and the five phases of earth; and the meaning of above and below, left and right, water and fire, metal and wood.
- For 形気相感而化万物矣, all the other sources have 化生万物 with 生 (see the variants). The translation follows the base text.
