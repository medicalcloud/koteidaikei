# 素問 天元紀大論篇第六十六 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Guiyuqu said: "The qi of yin and yang each have more and less; hence they are called the three yin and three yang. 'Shape has abundance and decline' means that the governance of the five phases each has excess and deficiency. So at the beginning, when surplus goes, insufficiency follows it; when insufficiency goes, surplus follows it. Knowing how to meet and how to follow, one can anticipate the qi. Corresponding to heaven is called heavenly tally; receiving the year is called year-direct; the three combining is called governance."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The qi of yin and yang each have more and less; hence they are called the three yin and three yang"] Since qi has more and less, it is divided into three according to its rising and falling.
Song editors: The *Zhizhen yao dalun* says "What are the three of yin and yang?" and Qibo says "The qi have more and less and different functions." Wang Bing says: "The 大 [greater] yin is the true yin, the greater yang the true yang; the next less is the lesser yin, the next less the lesser yang, next again the *yangming*, and next again the *jueyin*."

[On "'Shape has abundance and decline' means ... excess and deficiency"] Excess is surplus; deficiency is insufficiency. When qi arrives insufficient, excess meets it; when qi arrives excessive, insufficiency follows it. The waxing and waning of the qi of heaven and earth is thus; hence "shape has abundance and decline".

[On "So at the beginning ... one can anticipate the qi"] It means waxing and waning are irregular and they win and lose in turn. "Beginning" means the *jiazi* year. The *Liuwei zhi dalun* says: "The qi of heaven begins at *jia*, the qi of earth at *zi*; the union of *zi* and *jia* is called the establishment of the year." This is what is meant. So the qi received in the three hundred and sixty-five days of the first *jiazi* year should be insufficient. Reckoning in order, the six *jia* are completed. Thus when surplus ends there is insufficiency, and when insufficiency ends there is surplus. There are also years whose movement is neither surplus nor insufficient, presumably because they are the same as the transformation of heaven and earth. If surplus ends and is again surplus, or scarcity ends and is again scarcity, the way of heaven and earth departs from its norm, disasters arise and severe illnesses occur.
Song editors: The *Liuwei zhi dalun* says: "Wood movement meeting *mao*, fire movement meeting *wu*, earth movement meeting the four seasonal branches, metal movement meeting *you*, and water movement meeting *zi* are called year-meetings; the qi is balanced." Also the *Wuchang zheng dalun* says: "In the period of *weihe*, upper *jue* is the same as proper *jue*, upper *shang* as proper *shang*, upper *gong* as proper *gong*. In the period of *fuming*, upper *shang* is the same as proper *shang*. In the period of *beijian*, upper *gong* is the same as proper *gong*, upper *jue* as proper *jue*. In the period of *congge*, upper *shang* is the same as proper *shang*, upper *jue* as proper *jue*. In the period of *heliu*, upper *gong* is the same as proper *gong*. In the period of *hexi*, upper *yu* is the same as proper *zhi*. In the period of *jiancheng*, upper *zhi* is the same as proper *shang*." Also the *Liuyuan zhengji dalun* says "deficient and added to become the same, and year-meetings"; all the years above are proper years with balanced qi. Wang's note taking "the same as the transformation of heaven" as neither surplus nor insufficient is wrong.

[On "Corresponding to heaven is called heavenly tally ... the three combining is called governance"] "Corresponding to heaven" means that in a year of wood movement *jueyin* appears above; in a year of fire movement lesser yang or lesser yin appears above; in a year of earth movement greater yin appears above; in a year of metal movement *yangming* appears above; in a year of water movement greater yang appears above. In these five, heaven's qi descends as if matching the movement like a tally; hence "corresponding to heaven is called heavenly tally". "Receiving the year" means that in a year of wood movement the year falls on *mao*; of fire movement, on *wu*; of earth movement, on *chen*, *xu*, *chou* or *wei*; of metal movement, on *you*; of water movement, on *zi*. These five are what the year falls on; hence "receiving the year is called year-direct". "The three combining" means that in a year of fire movement lesser yin appears above and the year branch meets *wu*; in a year of earth movement greater yin appears above and the year branch meets *chou* or *wei*; in a year of metal movement *yangming* appears above and the year branch meets *you*. In these three, the qi of heaven, the qi of the movement and the year branch all meet; hence "the three combining is called governance". Year-direct is also called year-position, and the three combining is also called heavenly tally. The *Liuwei zhi dalun* says "a heavenly tally that is also a year-meeting is called supreme-one heavenly tally", meaning that heaven, the movement and the year all meet.
Song editors: The details of heavenly tally and year-meeting are given in the *Liuwei zhi dalun*. Further, on examination, fire movement with lesser yin above and the year branch meeting *wu* is the *wuwu* year; earth movement with greater yin above and the year branch meeting *chou* or *wei* is the *jichou* or *jiwei* year; metal movement with *yangming* above and the year branch meeting *you* is the *yiyou* year.

## Notes

The more and less of the three yin and three yang, the excess and deficiency of the five movements, and heavenly tally, year-direct and the three combining (supreme-one heavenly tally).
- Technical terms of the movements-and-qi doctrine (heavenly tally, year-meeting, year-direct, supreme-one heavenly tally, etc.) are translated literally.
