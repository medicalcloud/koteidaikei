# 素問 天元紀大論篇第六十六 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How brilliant the way! How clear the discourse! Let it be inscribed on jade tablets, stored in a golden casket, and titled *Tianyuan ji* (Record of the Heavenly Origin)."

## Notes

The close of the chapter. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 殆知閣 (黄帝内经素问) places a 巻第十九 break at the end of this unit; this is that source's division into juan.
