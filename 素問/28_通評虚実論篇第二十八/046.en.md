# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Measures of form, of bone, of vessels and of sinews — how does one know these measures?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Measures of form ... how does one know these measures?"] The measures of form are given in the *Sanbei jing*; the measures of sinews, vessels and bones are all given in the *Lingshu jing*. This question too belongs at the head of a chapter in that classic; it is a displacement. One classic places this question at the head of the *Nicong lun*, which is wrong.

## Notes

A question without an answer; Wang Bing regards it as a displacement.
- According to the Song editors (unit 023), 脈浮而濇，濇而身有熱者死 originally stood below this question.
- The *Sanbei jing* cited by Wang Bing has not been checked.
