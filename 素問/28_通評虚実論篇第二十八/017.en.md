# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the qi of the pulse is depleted above and the *chi* is depleted, this is called double depletion."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the qi of the pulse is depleted above ... double depletion"] It means the pulses at both *chi* and *cun* are depleted.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* reads "pulse depleted, qi depleted, *chi* depleted: this is called double depletion". This [text] lacks one 虚 and has an extra 上. Mr Wang's note says "the pulses at both *chi* and *cun* are depleted", which does not include depletion of qi. On examination, earlier the hot qi and full pulse in febrile illness were double repletion; here pulse depletion, qi depletion and *chi* depletion are double depletion. That is, pulse and qi both replete is double repletion and both depleted is double depletion; it is not merely depletion of both *chi* and *cun*.

## Notes

The definition of double depletion. The translation follows the text as it stands.
- The Song editors hold that it should read "pulse depleted, qi depleted, *chi* depleted" following the *Jiayi jing*, and reject Wang Bing's interpretation of depletion at both *chi* and *cun*.
