# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the qi of the pulse is deficient above and the *chi* is deficient, this is called double deficiency."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the qi of the pulse is deficient above ... double deficiency"] It means the pulses at both *chi* and *cun* are deficient.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* reads "pulse deficient, qi deficient, *chi* deficient: this is called double deficiency". This [text] lacks one 虚 and has an extra 上. Mr Wang's note says "the pulses at both *chi* and *cun* are deficient", which does not include deficiency of qi. On examination, earlier the hot qi and full pulse in febrile illness were double excess; here pulse deficiency, qi deficiency and *chi* deficiency are double deficiency. That is, pulse and qi both excess is double excess and both deficient is double deficiency; it is not merely deficiency of both *chi* and *cun*.

## Notes

The definition of double deficiency. The translation follows the text as it stands.
- The Song editors hold that it should read "pulse deficient, qi deficient, *chi* deficient" following the *Jiayi jing*, and reject Wang Bing's interpretation of deficiency at both *chi* and *cun*.
