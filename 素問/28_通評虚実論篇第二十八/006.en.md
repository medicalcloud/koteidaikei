# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "What is called double excess refers to a great febrile illness in which the qi is hot and the pulse full. This is called double excess."

## Notes

Double excess is when both the qi and the pulse are in excess. There is no note by Wang Bing.
