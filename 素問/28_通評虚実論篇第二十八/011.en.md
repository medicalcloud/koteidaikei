# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the qi of the network vessels is insufficient and the qi of the channels in surplus, the pulse opening is hot and the *chi* is cold. In autumn and winter this is adverse; in spring and summer, compliant. Treat what governs the illness."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the qi of the network vessels is insufficient ... Treat what governs the illness"] In spring and summer the yang qi is high; therefore a hot pulse opening and cold *chi* are compliant. The twelve channels and fifteen network vessels each have excess or insufficiency on the left or right; the practitioner should seek where it arrives and responds and apply needles or moxa. Hence "treat what governs the illness".

## Notes

The findings when the qi of the channels is in surplus and that of the network vessels insufficient, and their compliance or adversity according to the season.
