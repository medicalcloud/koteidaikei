# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "If the hands and feet are warm, one lives; if cold, one dies."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If the hands and feet are warm, one lives; if cold, one dies"] Song editors: The *Taisu* lacks 手 "hands". Yang Shangshan says, "When the feet are warm, the qi descends; therefore one lives. When the feet are cold and the qi does not descend, it is adverse and leads to death."

## Notes

The prognosis depends on the warmth or coldness of the hands and feet.
- 旡 in the Song editors' note is a variant of 無.
