# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "If the pulse beats large and slippery, it ends of itself in time; if the pulse is small, hard and tense, one dies and it cannot be treated."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If the pulse beats large and slippery ... cannot be treated"] A small, hard and tense pulse means that in a yang illness a yin pulse appears; therefore one dies and it cannot be treated.
Song editors: Chao Yuanfang says, "If the pulse is sunken, small, tense and replete, one dies and it cannot be treated; small, firm and tense also cannot be treated."

## Notes

The prognosis of epilepsy by the pulse.
- The base text writes 己 for 已; the translation takes it as 已 "end".
- 陰陽病 in Wang Bing's note is translated as "yang illness" from the context (陰 may be an interpolation).
