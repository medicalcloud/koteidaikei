# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "What is called compliant is warm hands and feet; what is called adverse is cold hands and feet."

## Notes

Compliance and adversity are indicated by the warmth or coldness of the hands and feet. There is no note by Wang Bing.
