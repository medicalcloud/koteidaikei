# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the meridians are deficient and the collaterals full, the *chi* is hot and full and the pulse opening cold and hesitant. In spring and summer this means death; in autumn and winter, life."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the meridians are deficient ... in autumn and winter, life"] In autumn and winter the yang qi descends; therefore a hot *chi* and cold pulse opening are compliant.

## Notes

The findings and prognosis in the case opposite to the previous unit.
