# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What are depletion and repletion like?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What are depletion and repletion like?"] It refers to the general features of depletion and repletion of the five zang.

## Notes

Huangdi asks for an outline of depletion and repletion of the five zang.
