# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "If the body is hot, one dies; if cold, one lives."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If the body is hot, one dies; if cold, one lives"] Heat means the blood is ruined; therefore one dies. Cold means the nutritive qi remains; therefore one lives.

## Notes

The prognosis of intestinal flux with bloody stools.
