# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "If replete and slippery, one lives; if replete and adverse, one dies."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If replete and slippery, one lives; if replete and adverse, one dies"] "Adverse" means rough.
Song editors: Mr Wang's taking 逆 as 濇 is quite wrong. Ancient texts are concise, with much complementary phrasing: above it says "slippery" and below "adverse". Mentioning "slippery" implies "compliant"; saying "adverse" shows "rough". It does not mean that "adverse" is "rough".

## Notes

Wang Bing takes "adverse" as meaning "rough"; the Song editors take it as complementary phrasing (slippery/compliant and adverse/rough supplementing each other).
