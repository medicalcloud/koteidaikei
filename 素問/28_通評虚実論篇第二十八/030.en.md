# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "When a nursing [mother or infant] is struck by wind-heat, with wheezing and shoulder breathing, what is the pulse like?"

## Notes

There is no note by Wang Bing.
