# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §49 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Huangdi said: "Jaundice, sudden pain, epilepsy and reversal-mania are produced by long-standing counterflow. Imbalance of the five zang is produced by blockage of the six fu. Headache, tinnitus and impairment of the nine orifices are produced by the intestines and stomach."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Jaundice ... produced by the intestines and stomach"] The three yang of the foot run from the head to the feet. When there is long reversal and counterflow and [the qi] does not descend, the qi stagnates and accumulates in the upper burner; hence jaundice, sudden pain, epilepsy and mania, and counterflow of qi. When food and drink are improper and vomiting and diarrhoea exceed measure, the six fu are blocked and the qi of the five zang becomes unharmonious and unbalanced. When the intestines and stomach are obstructed, the qi does not follow its order; when the qi does not follow its order, upper and lower, inner and outer, overcome one another; hence headache, tinnitus and impairment of the nine orifices.

## Notes

The causes of illness are summarized in three lines (long-standing counterflow, blockage of the six fu, the intestines and stomach); this ends the chapter.
