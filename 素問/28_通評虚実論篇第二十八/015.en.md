# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the collaterals are full and the meridians deficient, apply moxibustion to yin and needle yang; when the meridians are full and the collaterals deficient, needle yin and apply moxibustion to yang."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the collaterals are full ... apply moxibustion to yang"] Because the yin division governs the collaterals and the yang division governs the meridians.

## Notes

How to use needling and moxibustion according to the deficiency or excess of meridians and collaterals.
