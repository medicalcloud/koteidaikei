# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the network vessels are full and the channels depleted, apply moxibustion to yin and needle yang; when the channels are full and the network vessels depleted, needle yin and apply moxibustion to yang."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the network vessels are full ... apply moxibustion to yang"] Because the yin division governs the network vessels and the yang division governs the channels.

## Notes

How to use needling and moxibustion according to the depletion or repletion of channels and network vessels.
