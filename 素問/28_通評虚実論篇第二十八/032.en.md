# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What about intestinal flux with bloody stools?"

## Notes

腸澼 is understood as an intestinal illness with diarrhoea (later called dysentery). There is no note by Wang Bing.
