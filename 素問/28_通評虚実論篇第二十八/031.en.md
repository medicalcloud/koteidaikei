# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "In wheezing and shoulder breathing, the pulse is replete and large. If it is relaxed, one lives; if tense, one dies."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In wheezing and shoulder breathing ... if tense, one dies"] "Relaxed" means slack, like something loosened; "tense" means taut, like a drawn string; it is not the slowness or rapidity of the coming and going. The *Zhengli shanghan lun* says, "Relaxed means wind strike." Therefore when a nursing [mother or infant] is struck by wind and the pulse is relaxed, one lives; if tense, one dies.

## Notes

The pulse and prognosis of a nursing [mother or infant] with wheezing and shoulder breathing.
- The *Zhengli shanghan lun* passage quoted by Wang Bing has not been checked.
