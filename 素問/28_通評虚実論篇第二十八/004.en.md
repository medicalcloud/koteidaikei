# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Depletion of qi is depletion of the lung; counterflow of qi is cold feet. If it is not its time, one lives; if it is its time, one dies. The other zang are all like this."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Depletion of qi ... if it is its time, one dies"] "Not its time" means the years before and after the year that corresponds [to the zang]; "its time" means precisely the corresponding year.

[On "The other zang are all like this"] The five zang are the same.

## Notes

Using the lung as an example, the prognosis according to the depletion of a zang and the time.
- "Time": Wang Bing takes it as the year (corresponding to the zang). Others take it as the season (for the lung, autumn, or summer when fire controls metal).
