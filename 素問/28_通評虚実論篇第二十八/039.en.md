# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Slippery and large means life; suspended and rough means death. Time it by the zang."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Slippery and large means life ... Time it by the zang"] The liver dies on *geng* and *xin* days; the heart on *ren* and *gui*; the lung on *bing* and *ding*; the kidney on *wu* and *ji*; the spleen on *jia* and *yi*. This is called timing it by the zang.

## Notes

The date of death is predicted by the days of the phase that controls the diseased zang.
- 戊巳 in Wang Bing's note means 戊己; the translation gives 戊己.
