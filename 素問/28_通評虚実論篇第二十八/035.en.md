# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "If the pulse is sunken, one lives; if the pulse is floating, one dies."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If the pulse is sunken, one lives; if the pulse is floating, one dies"] It is a yin illness, yet a yang pulse appears, contrary to the pattern; therefore one dies.

## Notes

The prognosis of intestinal flux discharging white foam.
