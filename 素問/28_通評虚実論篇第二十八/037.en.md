# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "If the pulse is suspended and about to break off, one dies; if slippery and large, one lives."

## Notes

The prognosis of intestinal flux discharging pus and blood. There is no note by Wang Bing.
