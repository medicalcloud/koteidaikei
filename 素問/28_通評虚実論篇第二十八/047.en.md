# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §47 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "In spring urgently treat the channels and network vessels; in summer urgently treat the channel transport points; in autumn urgently treat the six fu; in winter there is closure. In closure, use drugs and few needles and stones. 'Few needles and stones' does not refer to abscesses: abscesses must not be allowed to turn even for a moment. When an abscess's location is unknown, pressing does not meet the hand, and it comes and goes suddenly, needle beside the hand greater yin three times, and the *ying* vessels two times each. For an armpit abscess with great heat, needle the foot lesser yang five times; if after needling the heat does not stop, needle the hand heart master three times, and the hand greater yin channel and network vessel at the meeting of the great bones three times each. For a sudden abscess with contracted sinews, pain along the divisions [of the flesh], unending sweat, and insufficiency of the qi of the bladder, treatment lies in the channel transport points. For sudden abdominal fullness that does not go down when pressed, take the hand greater yang channel and network vessel, which is the alarm point of the stomach, and [needle] five times beside the lesser yin transport point three *cun* from the spine, using the round-sharp needle. For cholera, needle five times beside the transport point, and the foot *yangming* and three [times] above beside it. To needle convulsions with fright, [needle] the vessel five times, needle the hand greater yin five times each, needle the channel greater yang five times, needle beside the hand lesser yin channel and network vessel once, the foot *yangming* once, and five *cun* above the ankle with three needles.

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In spring urgently treat ... use drugs and few needles and stones"] 亟 means urgently. "Closure" means the gates of qi are closed.

[On "'Few needles and stones' does not refer to abscesses"] Although the gates of qi are closed in the winter months, abscesses have fierce qi and form great pus within; if not drained urgently, they rot the sinews and bones. Therefore even in the winter months needles and stones should be used to open and remove them.

[On "abscesses must not be allowed to turn even for a moment"] Why can needles and stones still be used for abscesses in the winter months? Because in the moment this illness turns, if it passes without being drained, it rots the sinews and bones within and perforates the zang and fu.

[On "When an abscess's location is unknown ... the *ying* vessels two times each"] One only senses signs that seem to be of an abscess without knowing exactly where it has arisen; therefore pressing does not meet the hand. "Comes and goes suddenly" means the pain is not fixed in one place. The foot *yangming* vessel beside the hand greater yin refers to the region of the six points such as Qihu in the stomach section. The *ying* vessels are also the foot *yangming* vessel; being the vessels near the *ying*, they are called *ying* vessels. 纓 means the cap strings. Because there are left and right, it says "two each".

[On "For an armpit abscess with great heat ... three times each"] The meeting of the great bones is the shoulder, namely the Jianzhen point, in the depression in the bone cleft behind Jianyu.

[On "For a sudden abscess with contracted sinews ... treatment lies in the channel transport points"] When an abscess breaks out suddenly, the sinews along the course of the vessel are angry and contracted, there is pain in the divisions of the flesh, and sweat seeps out as if without end; where there is also insufficiency of the qi of the bladder, all can be supplemented or drained at the points of the channel concerned.
Song editors: These two items were formerly scattered in the chapter; now they have been moved together.

[On "For sudden abdominal fullness ... the alarm point of the stomach"] Greater yang means the hand greater yang. It is where the hand greater yang and greater yang channels and network vessels arise; therefore take the Zhongwan point, the alarm point of the stomach. The *Zhonggao* says, "Zhongwan is the alarm point of the stomach, between the xiphoid process and the navel, where the hand greater yang and lesser yang and foot *yangming* vessels arise." Hence "the channel and network vessel ... is the alarm point of the stomach".
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says "take the blood of the greater yang channels and network vessels", lacking the words "the alarm point of the stomach". Moreover, Yang Shangshan's note says "foot greater yang". The views all differ; it is not known which is right.

[On "and [needle] five times beside the lesser yin transport point ... using the round-sharp needle"] It means taking the points on both sides outside the foot lesser yin transport point, three *cun* from the spine, five times each. The lesser yin transport point means the kidney transport point on both sides below the fourteenth vertebra.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* says, "Use the round-sharp needle; after needling, wait about the time of a meal, and it ends at once. One must look for where the channel passes over to yang and needle it repeatedly."

[On "For cholera, needle five times beside the transport point"] For cholera, take the Zhishi point beside the lesser yin transport point.
Song editors: Yang Shangshan says, "To needle what governs cholera, take five beside the transport point."

[On "and the foot *yangming* and three [times] above beside it"] The foot *yangming* means the stomach transport point. Take the stomach transport point, and also the third point upward on both sides outside the lesser yin transport point, namely the Weicang point.

[On "To needle convulsions with fright, [needle] the vessel five times"] This refers to Yanglingquan, in the depression on the outer side above the knee.

[On "needle the hand greater yin five times each ... with three needles"] "Channel greater yang" means the foot greater yang. "The hand greater yin five" means the Yuji point, on the scattered vessels on the inner side behind the base joint of the thumb. "The channel greater yang five" means the Chengshan point, in the depression between the divisions of the flesh below the calf. "Beside the hand lesser yin channel and network vessel" means the Zhizheng point, five body-*cun* behind the wrist, between the divisions of the flesh on the upper edge of the bone; it is the network vessel of the hand greater yang that branches off to the lesser yin. "The foot *yangming* once" means the Jiexi point, in the depression above the ankle. "Five *cun* above the ankle" means Guangming, the network point of the foot lesser yang. According to the *Neijing mingtang* and *Zhonggao tujing*, all of these govern cholera, each with explicit statements.
Song editors: Another edition's note says "none of these govern cholera"; what it means is unclear. Moreover, according to the *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu*, from "to needle convulsions with fright, the vessel five times" to here concerns needling fright convulsions; Wang's note taking it as needling cholera is wrong.

## Notes

The emphasis of treatment in the four seasons, the use of needles and stones for abscesses even in winter, and needling methods for abscesses, armpit abscesses, sudden abscesses, abdominal fullness, cholera and convulsions with fright.
- The unit begins with "The Emperor said", though the content explains treatments; the translation keeps it as Huangdi's speech, read as continuing into unit 048.
- Wang Bing takes the passage from 刺癇驚脈五 on as also concerning cholera, but the Song editors, following the *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu*, take it as needling for fright convulsions. The translation follows the division of the text and renders it "to needle convulsions with fright".
- 痏 is a unit counting needle insertions (needle marks); the translation supplies "times".
- 巳 in the Song editors' 巳無 means 已.
- The punctuation of this unit is difficult and read in various ways; the translation draws on Wang Bing.
