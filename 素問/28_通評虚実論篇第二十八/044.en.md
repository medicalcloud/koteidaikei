# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What about depletion and repletion in wasting-thirst?"

## Notes

消癉 is understood to correspond to the later 消渇 (an illness with excessive drinking and urination and emaciation). There is no note by Wang Bing.
