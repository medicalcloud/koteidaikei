# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What about cold qi rising suddenly, with the pulse full and replete?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What about cold qi rising suddenly, with the pulse full and replete?"] It means: hot qi and a full pulse have already been called double repletion, slippery being compliant and rough adverse; can cold qi with a full pulse also be called double repletion? How do slippery and rough, life and death, adversity and compliance apply?

## Notes

Huangdi asks the prognosis of a replete pulse due to cold qi.
