# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "When evil qi is abundant, there is repletion; when essential qi is taken away, there is depletion."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When evil qi is abundant ... there is depletion"] "Taken away" means the essential qi is diminished, as if it had been taken away.

## Notes

The definition of depletion and repletion: abundance of evil qi is repletion; loss of essential qi is depletion.
