# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied: "When pathogenic qi is abundant, there is excess; when essential qi is taken away, there is deficiency."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When pathogenic qi is abundant ... there is deficiency"] "Taken away" means the essential qi is diminished, as if it had been taken away.

## Notes

The definition of deficiency and excess: abundance of pathogenic qi is excess; loss of essential qi is deficiency.
