# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What about a nursing [mother or infant] with a febrile illness whose pulse is suspended and small?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What about a nursing ... suspended and small?"] "Suspended" means like the movement of a hanging object.

## Notes

乳子 is taken by some as a nursing mother and by others as a suckling infant; Wang Bing does not comment on this.
