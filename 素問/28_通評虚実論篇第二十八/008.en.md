# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When both channels and network vessels are replete, the *cun* pulse is tense and the *chi* is slack; both should be treated.

## Notes

When both channels and network vessels are replete, the pulse at the *cun* is tense and the *chi* skin is slack. Qibo's speech continues in the next unit. There is no note by Wang Bing.
- 尺 is often understood as the *chi* skin (the skin of the forearm).
