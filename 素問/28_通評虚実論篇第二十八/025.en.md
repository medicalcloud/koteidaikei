# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When the form is entirely full, the pulse is tense, large and hard, and the *chi* is rough and does not correspond. In such cases, if compliant one lives; if adverse, one dies."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When the form is entirely full ... does not correspond"] "The form entirely full" means the four form-storing zang are all full.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu* write 満 "full" for 濇 "rough".

## Notes

The findings of pulse and *chi* and the prognosis when the form is full (swelling, etc.).
- "The four form-storing zang" in Wang Bing's note are the 形蔵四 of the *Liujie zangxiang lun* (chapter 9), which Wang Bing there identifies as the head and horns, ears and eyes, mouth and teeth, and chest.
