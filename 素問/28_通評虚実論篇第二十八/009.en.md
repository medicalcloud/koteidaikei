# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Therefore it is said: slippery is compliant, rough is adverse. Depletion and repletion all begin from the kinds of things; therefore when the five zang, bones and flesh are slippery and free, one can live long."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Therefore it is said: slippery is compliant, rough is adverse"] "Pulse tense" refers to the pulse opening.

[On "Depletion and repletion all begin ... one can live long"] When things are alive they are slippery and free; when things die they are withered and rough. Therefore rough is adverse and slippery compliant. 従 means compliant.

## Notes

When the pulse and body are slippery it is compliant; when rough, adverse. Qibo's speech, begun in unit 008, ends here.
- The first note by Wang Bing ("pulse tense refers to the pulse opening") appears to be a note on "the *cun* pulse is tense" in the previous unit.
