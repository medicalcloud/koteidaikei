# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What is called double deficiency?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What is called double deficiency?"] This asks the converse of the double excess above.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about double deficiency in contrast to double excess (units 005–006).
