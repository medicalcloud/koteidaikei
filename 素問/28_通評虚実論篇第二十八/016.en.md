# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "What is called double depletion?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "What is called double depletion?"] This asks the converse of the double repletion above.

## Notes

Huangdi asks about double depletion in contrast to double repletion (units 005–006).
