# 素問 通評虚実論篇第二十八 §43 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "If depleted, it can be treated; if replete, one dies."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "If depleted, it can be treated; if replete, one dies"] Because it is contrary to the pattern.

## Notes

The prognosis of epilepsy by the depletion or repletion of the pulse.
