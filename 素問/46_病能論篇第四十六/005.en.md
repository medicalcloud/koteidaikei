# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Why can some people not lie flat on their backs?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Why can some people not lie flat on their backs?"] It means being unable to lie face up.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why one cannot lie on one's back.
