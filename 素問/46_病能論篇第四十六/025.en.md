# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What is called "deep and fine" is that it strikes the hand like a needle; rubbing and pressing it, what is gathered is hard and what is broad is large. The *Shangjing* speaks of how qi communicates with heaven; the *Xiajing* speaks of the transformations of illness; the *Jingui* decides death and life; the *Kuiduo* means measuring by pressing; the *Qiheng* speaks of extraordinary illnesses.

## Notes

A fragment explaining terms describing the pulse and the names of ancient classics. Wang Bing regards it as a displacement unconnected with the surrounding text (see his note in unit 026). There is no note by Wang Bing.
