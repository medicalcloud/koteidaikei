# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "There are those ill with angry mania. Where does this illness arise?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "There are those ill with angry mania"] Song editors: The *Taisu* writes 善怒 "prone to anger" for 怒狂.

## Notes

Huangdi asks the cause of angry mania. There is no note by Wang Bing.
