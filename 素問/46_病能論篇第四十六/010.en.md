# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The lesser yin vessel passes through the kidney and connects with the lung. Now a lung pulse is obtained because the kidney is ill; therefore the kidney has the illness of lumbar pain."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The lesser yin vessel passes through the kidney ... lumbar pain"] The floating, slow left pulse is not the lung appearing; because the left kidney is insufficient and the pulse cannot sink, a lung pulse is obtained and the kidney is ill.

## Notes

Explained by the lesser yin vessel linking the kidney and lung.
