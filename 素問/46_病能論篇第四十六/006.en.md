# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The lung is the canopy of the zang. When lung qi is abundant, the pulse is large; when the pulse is large, one cannot lie flat on the back. This is discussed in the *Qiheng yinyang*."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The lung is the canopy of the zang"] It lies high and spreads its lobes, with the other four zang below; hence "the lung is the canopy of the zang".

[On "When lung qi is abundant ... cannot lie flat on the back"] When lung qi is abundant and full, lying on the back makes the qi short and the panting urgent; therefore one cannot lie on the back.

[On "This is discussed in the *Qiheng yinyang*"] *Qiheng yinyang* is the title of a chapter of an ancient classic, missing from current editions.

## Notes

Inability to lie on the back is due to abundant lung qi.
