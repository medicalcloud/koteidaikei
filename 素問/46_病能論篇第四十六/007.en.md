# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "In a patient with reversal, the right pulse is sunken and tight and the left pulse floating and slow. Since [the left] is not so, where does the illness lie?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "In a patient with reversal ... where does the illness lie?"] 不然 means not sunken.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 不知 "I do not know" for 不然.

## Notes

Huangdi asks the location of a reversal illness in which the left and right pulses differ.
