# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "It is because the yang qi is suddenly thwarted and cannot be resolved; therefore one is prone to anger. The illness is called yang reversal."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "It is because the yang qi is suddenly thwarted ... yang reversal"] It says the yang qi is thwarted, stagnates and does not disperse. Such a person is often angry also because of having once been suddenly thwarted, so that the heart is not free and open. All such cases arise from yang counterflow and extreme agitation; therefore the illness is called yang reversal.

## Notes

Angry mania arises when yang qi is thwarted and stagnates; it is called yang reversal.
