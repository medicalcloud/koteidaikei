# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "When a zang is injured, if the essence has somewhere to go and rest, one is at ease; for one cannot suspend one's illness [in the air]."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "When a zang is injured ... suspend one's illness"] When the five zang are injured, if the essential qi of water and grain has somewhere to go and rest to support them from below, one lies at ease. Since the injury reaches the zang, one cannot suspend the illness in empty space.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* reads "if the emotions have something to lean on, one lies uneasily" for 精有所之寄則安, and the *Taisu* reads "if the essence has something to lean on, one is uneasy".

## Notes

The reason for unease when lying down. The text is obscure and differs among editions; the translation follows Wang Bing.
- According to the Song editors, the *Jiayi jing* and *Taisu* read "... then [one lies] uneasily", reversing the meaning.
