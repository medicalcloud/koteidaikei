# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Diagnosed in winter, the right pulse should indeed be sunken and tight; this accords with the season. The left pulse floating and slow goes against the season. Being on the left, it should indicate illness in the kidney, somewhat involving the lung, and there should be lumbar pain."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Diagnosed in winter ... there should be lumbar pain"] In winter the left pulse is floating and slow; floating is the lung pulse; hence "somewhat involving the lung". The lumbus is the house of the kidney; therefore when the kidney receives illness there is pain in the lumbus.

## Notes

A floating, slow left pulse in winter indicates kidney illness involving the lung, with lumbar pain.
