# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Take ten parts each of *zexie* and *zhu* and five parts of *mixian*, combine them, and take a three-finger pinch before meals."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Take ten parts each of *zexie* and *zhu* ... before meals"] *Zhu* is bitter, warm and neutral, and governs great wind and stops sweating. *Mixian* is bitter, cold and neutral, and governs wind-damp and sinew wilting. *Zexie* is sweet, cold and neutral, and governs wind-damp and benefits qi. Because of these functions they form the prescription; therefore it is taken first. Food after and medicine first is called 後飯.

## Notes

The treatment of alcohol wind with a powder of *zexie* (water plantain), *zhu* (atractylodes) and *mixian*; one of the few drug prescriptions in the *Suwen*.
- 朮 is an atractylodes-type drug.
- 後飯: Wang Bing takes it as medicine first and food afterward (taking before meals), as in his note to chapter 40.
