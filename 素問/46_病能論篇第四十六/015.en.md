# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "How does yang make people manic?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "How does yang make people manic?"] Being angry without considering the consequences is called mania.

## Notes

Huangdi asks how yang causes mania.
