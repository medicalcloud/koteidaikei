# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The illness is called alcohol wind."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The illness is called alcohol wind"] It is being struck by wind after drinking alcohol. The *Feng lun* says, "When one is struck by wind after drinking alcohol, there is leaking wind"; this too is called leaking wind. Those who drink to excess have abundant yang qi, loose interstices and open sweat pores. When yang is abundant the sinews wilt and weaken; therefore the body is languid. When the interstices are loose, wind attacks within; when the sweat pores open, qi leaks out; therefore sweat pours as if bathed. Wind qi presses outside and the interstices open again; with much sweating the interior is depleted and burning heat fumigates the lung; therefore there are aversion to wind and shortness of breath. Because it is caused by alcohol, it is called alcohol wind.

## Notes

The illness is called alcohol wind; Wang Bing identifies it with leaking wind in the *Feng lun* (chapter 42).
