# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said: "Good. For neck abscesses, some treat with stones and some with needles and moxibustion, and all recover. Where is the true [method]?"

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Good. For neck abscesses ... Where is the true [method]?"] It says that the means of attack differ but the cure is the same; he wishes to hear where the true method lies.

## Notes

Huangdi asks why different treatments work for the same illness.
