# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "The *yangming* [pulse] always beats; the greater yang and lesser yang do not beat. When those that do not beat beat greatly and rapidly, this is its sign."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "The *yangming* [pulse] always beats ... this is its sign"] It says the pulses of the neck and nape all beat without stopping. "The *yangming* always beats" refers to the beat beside the laryngeal prominence, the position of Renying and Qishe. The beat of the lesser yang is below the angle of the jaw, the position of Tianchuang and Tianyou. The beat of the greater yang is in the depressions in front of the great sinews on both sides of the nape, the position of Tianzhu and Tianrong. When those that should not normally beat instead beat strongly, the beating indicates illness.
Song editors: On examination, Wang's note takes Tianchuang as a lesser yang position and Tianrong as a greater yang position. According to the *Jiayi jing*, Tianchuang is where the vessel qi of the greater yang issues and Tianrong where that of the lesser yang issues; the two are interchanged. The *Jiayi jing* should be taken as correct.

## Notes

As a diagnostic sign of yang reversal, the greater yang and lesser yang pulses, which normally do not beat, beat strongly.
- 天䆫 and 天牎 in the notes are Tianchuang (天窓).
