# 素問 病能論篇第四十六 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said: "Take away his food and it will end. Food enters yin and grows qi in yang; therefore taking away his food ends it. Have him take raw iron flakes as a drink; raw iron flakes bring down qi quickly."

## Translation of Wang Bing's commentary and the Song editors' notes

[On "Food enters yin ... taking away his food ends it"] With little food the qi declines; therefore restricting the food makes the illness stop of itself.
Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 衰 "reduce" for 奪; the *Taisu* is the same.

[On "Have him take raw iron flakes as a drink"] Song editors: The *Jiayi jing* writes 鉄落 for 鉄洛 and 為後飯 "before meals" for 為飲.

[On "raw iron flakes bring down qi quickly"] 之 is sometimes written 人, a copying error. Iron flakes are acrid, slightly warm and neutral, and govern bringing down qi. Some popularly call them "iron syrup", which is wrong; they are not the fluid of raw iron.

## Notes

The treatment of yang reversal: reducing food and drinking raw iron flakes; one of the few drug prescriptions in the *Suwen*.
