# 霊枢 五音五味第六十五 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Excellent! The sage's understanding of the myriad things is like the light and shadow of the sun and moon, or the drum's echo of a sound: hearing the sound, he knows the form. If not you, Master, who could illuminate the essence of the myriad things?

## Notes

The Yellow Emperor's speech continues into unit 12. The *Leijing* note: the sun and moon have light whose shadow can be seen and known; sounds have echoes that can be heard and known; only the sage can illuminate the essence of the principles of things, and so knows the one from the other, the inside from the outside.
