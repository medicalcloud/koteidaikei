# 霊枢 五音五味第六十五 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "This is a deficiency from heaven [at birth]. The *Ren* and *Chong* meridians are not abundant, the ancestral sinew does not develop, there is qi but no blood, and the lips and mouth are not nourished, so the beard does not grow."
