# 霊枢 五音五味第六十五 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Eunuchs have had their ancestral sinew removed, injuring the *Chong* meridian; the blood is drained and not restored, the inside of the skin is knotted, and the lips and mouth are not nourished, so beards do not grow."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: in the man whose yin qi is injured, the ancestral sinew is not destroyed; the eunuch has had it removed, injuring the *Chong* meridian; once the blood is drained it cannot be restored, the inside of the skin is knotted and the meridian pathway does not flow, so the *Chong* meridian does not nourish the mouth and the beard does not grow.
