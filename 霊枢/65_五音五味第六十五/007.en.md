# 霊枢 五音五味第六十五 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When a man injures his genitals so that the yin qi is cut off, he cannot achieve erection and the genitals are of no use, yet his beard does not disappear—why? Why does it disappear only in eunuchs? I wish to hear the reason."
