# 霊枢 五音五味第六十五 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The great *gong* and upper *jue* are both at the upper right foot *yangming*. The left *jue* and great *jue*, both at the upper left foot *yangming*. The lesser *yu* and great *yu*, both at the lower right foot greater yang. The left *shang* and right *shang*, both at the upper left hand *yangming*. The added *gong* and great *gong*, both at the upper left foot lesser yang. The *zhi* half and great *gong*, both at the lower left hand greater yang. The half *jue* and great *jue*, both at the lower left foot lesser yang. The great *yu* and great *jue*, both at the upper right foot greater yang. The great *jue* and great *gong*, both at the upper right foot lesser yang.

## Notes

Pairs belonging to the same part. As in unit 1, many disagree with the previous chapter; the *Leijing* note marks 左角与大角 and 加宮与大宮 as 義似不合. Translated literally.
- 大陽 is the same as 太陽.
