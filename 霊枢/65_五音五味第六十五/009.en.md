# 霊枢 五音五味第六十五 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "There are 'heaven's eunuchs', who have never been injured nor lost blood, yet grow no beard. Why is that?"

## Notes

天宦, per the *Leijing* note: a man who has no beard all his life, as if born a eunuch, hence "heaven's eunuch".
