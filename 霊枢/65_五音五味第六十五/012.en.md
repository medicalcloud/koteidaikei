# 霊枢 五音五味第六十五 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus the sage looks at the complexion: yellow and red mean much heat qi; azure and white, little heat qi; black, much blood and little qi. Fine eyebrows mean much blood in the greater yang; continuous whiskers reaching the beard mean much blood in the lesser yang; a fine beard means much blood in the *Yangming*. These are so according to the time. The constant numbers of the human being are: the greater yang always has much blood and little qi; the lesser yang much qi and little blood; the *Yangming* much blood and much qi; the reverting yin much qi and little blood; the lesser yin much blood and little qi; the greater yin much blood and little qi. These are the constant numbers of heaven."

## Notes

The Yellow Emperor's words; the end of the chapter.
- 通髯 is the hair on the cheeks, and 鬚 the hair below the chin (Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu*).
- 太陰常多血少気 differs among sources. The *Taisu* reads 太陰常多血気, and Xiao Yanping's note says the *Suwen* reads 太陰常多気少血, the *Lingshu* 多血少気, and the *Jiayi jing* the same. The *Lingshu jizhu* also points out that this text gives reverting yin much qi and little blood and greater yin much blood and little qi, while the *Suwen*'s *Xueqi xingzhi* chapter and this text's *Jiuzhen lun* (ch. 78) give reverting yin much blood and little qi and greater yin much qi and little blood. Since which is correct cannot be determined, the base is translated as is.
