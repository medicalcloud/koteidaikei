# 霊枢 五音五味第六十五 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The upper *zhi* and right *zhi* are the same: grain, wheat; animal, sheep; fruit, apricot; the hand lesser yin; zang organ, heart; color, red; flavor, bitter; season, summer. The upper *yu* and great *yu* are the same: grain, soybean; animal, pig; fruit, chestnut; the foot lesser yin; zang organ, kidney; color, black; flavor, salty; season, winter. The upper *gong* and great *gong* are the same: grain, millet; animal, ox; fruit, jujube; the foot greater yin; zang organ, spleen; color, yellow; flavor, sweet; season, late summer. The upper *shang* and right *shang* are the same: grain, glutinous millet; animal, chicken; fruit, peach; the hand greater yin; zang organ, lung; color, white; flavor, acrid; season, autumn. The upper *jue* and great *jue* are the same: grain, hemp; animal, dog; fruit, plum; the foot *Jueyin*; zang organ, liver; color, azure; flavor, sour; season, spring.

## Notes

The correspondences of grains, animals, fruits, etc. for nourishing the persons of the five notes. The *Lingshu jizhu* says the five flavors of grains, animals and fruits regulate and nourish both the persons of the five notes and the twenty-five variants; "the upper *zhi* and right *zhi* are the same" names one to stand for all four.
- The base's 手少陽 is emended to 手少陰. The *Leijing*, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text read 手少陰. The meridian of the heart is the hand lesser yin, and the other four entries (foot lesser yin, foot greater yin, hand greater yin, foot *Jueyin*) all name the yin meridian of the zang organ, so 陰 is adopted.
