# 霊枢 五音五味第六十五 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The thoroughfare vessel and the controlling vessel both start in the womb, rise along the inside of the back and form the sea of the channels and network vessels. Their floating, outer branches follow the right side of the abdomen upward, meet at the throat, and branch off to connect with the lips and mouth. When blood and qi are abundant they fill the skin and warm the flesh; when blood alone is abundant it seeps into the skin and produces fine hairs. Now women by nature have a surplus of qi and an insufficiency of blood, because they frequently lose blood; the thoroughfare and controlling vessels do not nourish the mouth and lips, so beards do not grow."

## Notes

On 循腹右上行, the *Leijing* note says the thoroughfare and controlling vessels are yin vessels, hence they rise along the right of the abdomen. 数脱血 refers to menstruation (the *Jiayi jing* reads 以其月水下数脱血).
- The base's 澹㵕 is emended to 澹滲. The *Leijing*, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text read 澹滲, the *Lingshu jizhu* 淡滲 (the *Jiayi jing* 滲灌). Since the base's 㵕 is found in no other source, 滲 is adopted.
