# 霊枢 脈度第十七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I wish to hear the measurements of the vessels."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, describing the lengths of the meridians, the five zang organs and the seven openings, block and rejection, and the springing vessels.
