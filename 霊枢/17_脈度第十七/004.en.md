# 霊枢 脈度第十七 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Where do the springing vessels start and where do they end? What qi nourishes them [like] water?"

## Notes

何気栄水 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "of which channel's qi are the springing vessels, and do they circulate like the channel waters?"
