# 霊枢 脈度第十七 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The six yang of the hand run from the hand to the head, five *chi* long; five times six is three *zhang*. The six yin of the hand run from the hand to the chest, three *chi* five *cun*; three times six is one *zhang* eight *chi*, five [*cun*] times six is three *chi*, together two *zhang* one *chi*. The six yang of the foot run from the foot up to the head, eight *chi*; six times eight is four *zhang* eight *chi*. The six yin of the foot run from the foot to the chest, six *chi* five *cun*; six times six is three *zhang* six *chi*, five [*cun*] times six is three *chi*, together three *zhang* nine *chi*. The springing vessels run from the foot to the eye, seven *chi* five *cun*; two times seven is one *zhang* four *chi*, two times five [*cun*] is one *chi*, together one *zhang* five *chi*. The governing and conception vessels are each four *chi* five *cun*; two times four is eight *chi*, two times five [*cun*] is one *chi*, together nine *chi*. Altogether sixteen *zhang* two *chi*; these are the great meridian tunnels of the qi. The meridians are the inside; their branches running crosswise are the collaterals; the separate branches of the collaterals are the grandchild vessels. Where they are abundant with blood, quickly remove it; where abundant, drain; where deficient, give medicine to drink to supplement.
