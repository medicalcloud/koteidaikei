# 霊枢 脈度第十七 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "Qi cannot but move, like the flowing of water, like the ceaseless movement of the sun and moon. Thus the yin vessels nourish the zang organs and the yang vessels nourish the bowels, like a ring without end; no one knows its starting point; it ends and begins again. Its flowing and overflowing qi irrigates the zang-fu organs within and moistens the interstices without."
