# 霊枢 脈度第十七 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The *Qiao* meridian is a branch of the lesser yin. It starts behind the Rangu bone, rises above the inner ankle, rises straight along the inner thigh, enters the genitals, rises along the inside of the chest, enters the empty basin, rises to come out in front of Renying, enters the cheekbone, belongs to the inner canthus, and unites with the greater yang and the *Yangqiao* meridian to rise. When the qi joins and circulates back, it moistens the eyes; when the qi does not nourish, the eyes cannot close."
