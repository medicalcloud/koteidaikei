# 霊枢 脈度第十七 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The five viscera constantly inspect the seven openings above from within. Thus lung qi communicates with the nose; when the lungs are harmonious, the nose can tell smells and fragrances. Heart qi communicates with the tongue; when the heart is harmonious, the tongue can tell the five flavors. Liver qi communicates with the eyes; when the liver is harmonious, the eyes can distinguish the five colors. Spleen qi communicates with the mouth; when the spleen is harmonious, the mouth can tell the five grains. Kidney qi communicates with the ears; when the kidneys are harmonious, the ears can hear the five notes. When the five viscera are not harmonious, the seven openings are blocked; when the six bowels are not harmonious, [qi] lodges and becomes abscesses. Hence when a pathogen is in the bowels, the yang vessels are not harmonious; when the yang vessels are not harmonious, qi lodges in them; when qi lodges in them, the yang qi becomes abundant. When the yang qi is too abundant, the yin is not free-flowing; when the yin vessels are not free-flowing, blood lodges in them; when blood lodges in them, the yin qi becomes abundant. When the yin qi is too abundant, the yang qi cannot flourish; hence it is called block. When the yang qi is too abundant, the yin qi cannot flourish; hence it is called rejection. When yin and yang are both abundant and cannot nourish each other, it is called block and rejection. One with block and rejection dies before completing the allotted span."

## Notes

The five viscera and the seven openings; block and rejection. Qibo's speech ends here.
