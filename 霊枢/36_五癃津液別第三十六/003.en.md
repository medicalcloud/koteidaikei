# 霊枢 五癃津液別第三十六 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The fluids of the five grains that combine to form ointment-like matter seep inward into the hollows of the bones, replenish the brain and marrow, and flow down to the inner thighs. When yin and yang are not harmonious, the fluid overflows and flows down to the genitals, the marrow fluid all diminishes and goes down; when the going down is excessive there is depletion, and with depletion the lower back and back ache and the shins are sore. When the paths of yin and yang qi are blocked, the four seas closed, the triple burner not draining and the fluids not transformed, water and grain move together in the intestines and stomach, separate at the winding intestine and lodge in the lower burner; unable to seep into the bladder, the lower burner distends, and when the water overflows there is water distension. These are the counterflow and compliance of the five divisions of the fluids."

## Notes

Marrow and water distension. Qibo's speech ends here, concluding the chapter.
