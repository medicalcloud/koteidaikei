# 霊枢 五癃津液別第三十六 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Water and grain all enter the mouth; their flavors are five, each pouring into its sea, and the fluids each run along their paths. Thus what the triple burner sends out as qi to warm the flesh and fill the skin becomes the thin fluid; what flows but does not move becomes the thick fluid. When the weather is hot and clothing thick, the interstices open, so sweat comes out. When cold lodges between the partings of the flesh and froth gathers, there is pain. When the weather is cold, the interstices close, the qi and damp do not move, and water goes down and lodges in the bladder, becoming urine and qi. Of the five viscera and six bowels, the heart is the master, the ears hear for it, the eyes watch for it, the lungs are its minister, the liver its general, the spleen its guard, and the kidneys govern the outside. So the fluids of the five viscera and six bowels all seep up to the eyes. When the heart grieves and the qi gathers, the heart connection tenses; when the heart connection tenses, the lungs lift; when the lungs lift, the fluids overflow upward. The heart connection and lungs cannot stay lifted constantly; they rise and fall by turns, so one coughs and tears come out. When there is heat in the center, grain is consumed in the stomach; when grain is consumed, the worms move up and down, the intestines and stomach fill and expand, so the stomach slackens; when the stomach slackens, the qi counterflows, so spittle comes out.
