# 霊枢 五癃津液別第三十六 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "When water and grain enter the mouth and are transported to the intestines and stomach, their fluids divide into five: when the weather is cold and clothing thin, they become urine and qi; when the weather is hot and clothing thick, they become sweat; when grief makes the qi gather, they become tears; when there is heat in the center and the stomach is slack, they become spittle; when pathogenic qi counterflows within, the qi is blocked and does not move, and when it does not move there is water distension. I know that this is so, but I do not know how it comes about. I wish to hear the way."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking why the fluids divide into five (urine, sweat, tears, spittle and marrow), and about water distension.
