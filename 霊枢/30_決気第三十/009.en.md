# 霊枢 決気第三十 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When essence is lost, there is deafness. When qi is lost, the eyes are not clear. When thin fluid is lost, the interstices open and there is profuse sweating. When thick fluid is lost, the joints do not bend and stretch freely, the complexion is dull, the brain and marrow waste away, the shins ache, and the ears often ring. When blood is lost, the complexion is white, dull and lusterless, and the vessels are empty. These are the signs."
