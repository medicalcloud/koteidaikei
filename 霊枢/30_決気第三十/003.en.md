# 霊枢 決気第三十 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What the upper jiao opens and sends out, spreading the flavors of the five grains, warming the skin, filling the body and moistening the hair, like the irrigation of mist and dew, is called qi." [The Yellow Emperor said,] "What is called thin fluid?"
