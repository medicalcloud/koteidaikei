# 霊枢 決気第三十 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When grain enters and the qi is full, what pours richly into the bones, lets the joints bend and stretch, seeps out to replenish the brain and marrow, and moistens the skin, is called thick fluid." [The Yellow Emperor said,] "What is called blood?"
