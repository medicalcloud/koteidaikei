# 霊枢 決気第三十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What arises when the two spirits [male and female] struggle together and unite to form a body, always arising before the body, is called essence." [The Yellow Emperor said,] "What is called qi?"

## Notes

From this unit through unit 6, the question "what is called ...?" follows Qibo's answer in the base without 黄帝曰. "[The Yellow Emperor said,]" is supplied.
