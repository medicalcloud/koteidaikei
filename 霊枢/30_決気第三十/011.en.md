# 霊枢 決気第三十 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Each of the six qi has the region it governs; their importance and their goodness or badness have their constant governor. But the five grains and the stomach are their great sea."

## Notes

The end of the chapter. The final 溱 is the headword of a sound gloss; the interlinear note (pronunciation) is not translated (original text only).
