# 霊枢 決気第三十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "How does one know the surplus and insufficiency of the six qi, the amount of qi, the depletion and repletion of the brain and marrow, and the clarity and turbidity of the blood and vessels?"
