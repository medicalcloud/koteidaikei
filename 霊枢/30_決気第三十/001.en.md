# 霊枢 決気第三十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I have heard that people have essence, qi, thin fluids, thick fluids, blood and vessels. In my view they are just one qi, yet now they are distinguished by six names. I do not know why."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about the distinction among the six qi: essence, qi, thin fluids, thick fluids, blood and vessels.
