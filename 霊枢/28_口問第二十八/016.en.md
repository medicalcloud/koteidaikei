# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Yang qi is harmonious and free-flowing, fills the heart and comes out through the nose; hence sneezing. Supplement the brook [point] of the foot greater yang at the root of the eyebrow [Zanzhu]. Another view says above the eyebrow."

## Notes

栄眉本 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "supplement the brook of the foot greater yang at the root of the eyebrow, called Zanzhu; 'above the eyebrow' is the same point". Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* takes the greater yang brook as Tonggu on the foot, separate from Zanzhu.
