# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Yang qi is harmonious and free-flowing, fills the heart and comes out through the nose; hence sneezing. Supplement the brook point of the foot greater yang and the root of the eyebrow. Another view says above the eyebrow."

## Notes

栄 in 栄眉本 is provisionally read as 滎 "brook point". Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
