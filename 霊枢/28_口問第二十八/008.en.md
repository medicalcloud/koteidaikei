# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When grain enters the stomach, the stomach qi rises to pour into the lungs. If there is old cold qi along with new grain qi, both return into the stomach; the new and the old disturb each other, the true and the pathogenic attack each other, the qi together counterflows and comes out of the stomach again; hence hiccup. Supplement the hand greater yin and drain the foot lesser yin."
