# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Cold qi lodges in the stomach, counterflows from below and disperses upward, and comes out of the stomach again; hence belching. Supplement the foot greater yin and *yangming*. Another view says supplement the root of the eyebrow."
