# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The heart is the master of the five zang organs and six fu organs; the eyes are where the ancestral vessels gather and the path of the upper fluids; the mouth and nose are the gates of the qi. So when there is grief, sorrow and worry, the heart stirs; when the heart stirs, the five zang organs and six fu organs all shake; when they shake, the ancestral vessels are affected; when the ancestral vessels are affected, the paths of the fluids open; and because the paths of the fluids open, tears and snivel come out. The fluids are what irrigate the essence and moisten the openings. So when the path of the upper fluids opens, there is weeping; when weeping does not stop, the fluids are exhausted; when the fluids are exhausted, the essence is not irrigated; when the essence is not irrigated, the eyes cannot see. Hence it is called robbing of essence. Supplement the Tianzhu meridian where it flanks the neck."
