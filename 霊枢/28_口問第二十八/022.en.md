# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Worry and thought make the heart connection tense; when the heart connection is tense, the paths of the qi are constricted; when constricted, they are not free; hence one sighs deeply to stretch them out. Supplement the hand lesser yin and heart governor, and retain [the needle] at the foot lesser yang."
