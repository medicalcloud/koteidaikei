# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Here yin qi is abundant and yang qi depleted, yin qi quick and yang qi slow, yin qi abundant and yang qi cut off; hence sobbing. Supplement the foot greater yang and drain the foot lesser yin."
