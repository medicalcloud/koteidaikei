# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The ears are where the ancestral vessels gather. So when the stomach is empty, the ancestral vessels are depleted; when depleted, [the qi] flows downward and the vessels are exhausted somewhere; hence ringing in the ears. Supplement Kezhuren and the place on the thumb where the nail meets the flesh."
