# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All these twelve pathogens are irregular pathogens running to the openings. So wherever the pathogen is, there is insufficiency. Thus when the upper qi is insufficient, the brain is not full, the ears suffer from ringing, the head suffers from tilting, and the eyes are dizzy. When the middle qi is insufficient, urine and stool change and the intestines suffer from rumbling. When the lower qi is insufficient, there is wilting with reversal and oppression of the heart. Supplement below the outer ankle and retain [the needle]."

## Notes

A summary of the twelve pathogens. Qibo's speech ends here.
