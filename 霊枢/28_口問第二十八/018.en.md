# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the stomach is not replete, the vessels are depleted; when the vessels are depleted, the sinews and vessels are limp; when the sinews and vessels are limp and one engages in the yin [intercourse] and exerts oneself, the qi cannot recover; hence limpness. According to where it is, supplement between the partings of the flesh."
