# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The kidneys govern yawning; take the foot lesser yin. The lungs govern hiccup; take the hand greater yin and foot lesser yin. In sobbing, yin and yang are cut off from each other, so supplement the foot greater yang and drain the foot lesser yin. For shivering with cold, supplement the yang meridians. For belching, supplement the foot greater yin and *Yangming*. For sneezing, supplement the foot greater yang and the root of the eyebrow. For limpness, according to where it is, supplement between the partings of the flesh. For tearing, supplement the Tianzhu meridian where it flanks the neck; flanking the neck means the middle division of the head. For sighing, supplement the hand lesser yin, heart governor and foot lesser yang, and retain [the needle]. For drooling, supplement the foot lesser yin. For ringing in the ears, supplement Kezhuren and the place on the thumb where the nail meets the flesh. For biting one's tongue, observe which governs the illness and supplement it. For dizziness and tilting of the head, supplement below the outer ankle and retain. For wilting with reversal and oppression of the heart, needle two *cun* above the space between the big toe and the second toe and retain; another view says retain below the outer ankle."

## Notes

A summary of the treatments of the twelve pathogens; the end of the chapter.
- No explanation of 侠頸者，頭中分也 was found in the *Leijing*, *Taisu* or *Lingshu jizhu*; its meaning is unknown, and the translation is provisional.
