# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "All food and drink enter the stomach. When there is heat in the stomach, the worms move; when the worms move, the stomach slackens; when the stomach slackens, Lianquan opens; hence drooling. Supplement the foot lesser yin."
