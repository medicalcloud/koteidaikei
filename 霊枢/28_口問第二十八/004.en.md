# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "When the hundred illnesses first arise, they all arise from wind and rain, cold and heat, yin and yang, joy and anger, food and drink, dwelling, great fright and sudden fear. Then the blood and qi separate, yin and yang are broken, the meridians and collaterals are reversed and cut off, the paths of the vessels are blocked, yin and yang counter each other, the wei-defensive qi stagnates, the meridians are empty, the blood and qi lose their order, and the norm is lost. Let me tell you the methods for what is not discussed in the classics."
