# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "What qi makes people bite their own tongues?" [Qibo said,] "This is reversal running upward, with the vessel qi arriving in turn. When lesser yin qi arrives, one bites the tongue; when lesser yang qi arrives, one bites the cheek; when *Yangming* qi arrives, one bites the lips. Observe which governs the illness and supplement it.

## Notes

The base has no 岐伯曰 before the answer (the Daizhige edition has it; see collation). "[Qibo said,]" is supplied. Qibo's speech continues through unit 28.
