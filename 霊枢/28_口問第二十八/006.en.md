# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The defensive qi moves in the yang during the day and in the yin at midnight. Yin governs the night, and the night is for lying down. Yang governs the upper, yin the lower. So when yin qi accumulates below while yang qi is not yet exhausted, yang pulls upward and yin pulls downward; yin and yang pull against each other, so one yawns repeatedly. When yang qi is exhausted and yin qi abundant, the eyes close; when yin qi is exhausted and yang qi abundant, one wakes. Drain the foot lesser yin and supplement the foot greater yang."
