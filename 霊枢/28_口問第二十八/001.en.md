# 霊枢 口問第二十八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor, at leisure, dismissed his attendants and asked Qibo, "I have already heard the classic of the nine needles, and the discussion of yin and yang, counterflow and compliance, and the six meridians is complete. I wish to receive the oral questions."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about the pathology and treatment of twelve pathogenic conditions, such as yawning, hiccup and sobbing, transmitted orally rather than in writing.
