# 霊枢 五変第四十六 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu answered, "When trees are damaged, it is always their branches; if the branches are tough and hard, no damage results. When people are constantly ill, it is likewise because the parts of their bones, joints, skin and interstices that are not firm become lodgings for pathogens; hence they are constantly ill."
