# 霊枢 五変第四十六 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Shaoyu, "I have heard that when the hundred illnesses begin, they always arise from wind and rain, cold and heat; following the fine hairs they enter the interstices; some return, some stay; some become wind swelling with sweating, some wasting thirst, some cold and heat, some lingering impediment, some accumulations; the irregular pathogens overflow in countless ways. I wish to hear the reason. When people fall ill at the same time, some get this illness and some that. Does heaven produce a [different] wind for each person? Why are they different?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. The interlocutor is Shaoyu. It asks why illnesses differ by constitution even when people meet the same pathogen.
