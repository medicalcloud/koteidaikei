# 霊枢 五変第四十六 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu answered, "When the skin is thin and lusterless and the flesh is not firm but soggy, the intestines and stomach are poor; when they are poor, pathogenic qi stays and accumulations cause harm; between the spleen and stomach, cold and warmth are out of order, pathogenic qi gradually arrives, builds up and stays, and great accumulations arise."
