# 霊枢 五変第四十六 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu answered, "Such a person has thin skin, eyes that are firm and deep-set, and long eyebrows raised straight. Their heart is hard; being hard they are often angry; anger makes the qi counterflow upward and accumulate in the chest; blood and qi counterflow and stagnate, widening the skin and filling the muscles; the blood vessels do not move, turning into heat; heat consumes the flesh and skin, hence wasting thirst. This speaks of a person who is violently hard but weak in flesh."

## Notes

長衝直揚 is provisionally read as long eyebrows raised straight, and 臗皮充肌 as widening the skin [of the chest] and filling the muscles. Both are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
