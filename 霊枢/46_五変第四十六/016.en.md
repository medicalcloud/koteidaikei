# 霊枢 五変第四十六 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu answered, "Such a person has thin skin, eyes that are firm and deep-set, with a gaze that thrusts far and rises straight. Their heart is hard; being hard they are often angry; anger makes the qi counterflow upward and accumulate in the chest; blood and qi counterflow and stagnate, filling the skin of the hips and the muscles; the blood vessels do not move, turning into heat; heat consumes the flesh and skin, hence wasting thirst. This speaks of a person who is violently hard but weak in flesh."

## Notes

長衝直揚 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* ("衝 means the light of the eyes thrusting out"): a gaze that thrusts far and rises straight (the *Jiayi jing* writes 衡 for 衝).
- For 臗 in 臗皮充肌, the *Taisu* has 髋 (same meaning) and the *Jiayi jing* reads 腹皮充脹; the *Lingshu jizhu* explains it as [blood and qi] filling and blocking the flesh. It is translated as filling the skin of the hips and the muscles.
