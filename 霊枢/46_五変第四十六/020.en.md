# 霊枢 五変第四十六 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu answered, "The cheekbones are the root of the bones. Large cheekbones mean large bones; small cheekbones mean small bones. When the skin is thin and the flesh has no bulges, the arms are feeble, and the color of the earth [lower face] is dull, not matching the color of the heaven [upper face], dirty and alone different—these are the signs. Moreover, those with thin arms do not have full marrow, so they are prone to cold and heat."

## Notes

地 and 天 are taken as the lower and upper face, following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* on 在地為厥 in chapter 49 ("the earth is the lower part of the face"). His note on this unit says the earth qi is turbid and the heaven qi clear: the substance-color is in surplus but the spirit-color insufficient.
