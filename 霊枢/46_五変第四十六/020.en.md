# 霊枢 五変第四十六 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu answered, "The cheekbones are the root of the bones. Large cheekbones mean large bones; small cheekbones mean small bones. When the skin is thin and the flesh has no bulges, the arms are feeble, and the color of the earth [chin] is dull, not matching the color of the heaven [forehead], dirty and alone different—these are the signs. Moreover, those with thin arms do not have full marrow, so they are prone to cold and heat."

## Notes

地色 and 天 are provisionally translated as the chin and forehead. Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
