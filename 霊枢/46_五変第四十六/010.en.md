# 霊枢 五変第四十六 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu answered, "When the flesh of the back of the knee is not firm and has no clear divisions, or only coarse ones, and with coarse divisions the skin is not dense, the interstices are loose. This speaks of it in general terms."

## Notes

One view holds that 膕肉 is an error for 䐃肉 (bulging muscles); unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points). The base is followed.
