# 霊枢 五変第四十六 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu answered, "When the flesh of the back of the knee is not firm and has no clear divisions, or only coarse ones, and with coarse divisions the skin is not dense, the interstices are loose. This speaks of it in general terms."

## Notes

For 膕 in 膕肉, the *Jiayi jing* has a different character (㬷), but the Lingshu editions and *Leijing* agree with the base, and Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* and the *Lingshu jizhu* explain it as the flesh at the back of the knee (around Weizhong). Following them, the base is kept.
