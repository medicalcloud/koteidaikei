# 霊枢 五変第四十六 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu said, "Heaven produces wind without partiality toward the people; its working is fair and upright. Those who offend against it get it; those who avoid it are not endangered. It does not seek people out; people offend against it themselves."
