# 霊枢 五変第四十六 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu answered, "First establish the year to know the times. When the time is high, [the patient] recovers; when the time is low, there is danger. Even if it does not sink low, if in that year there is a clash, the illness will surely arise. This is called illness arising according to form; it is the guideline of the five variations."

## Notes

The end of the chapter.
- 時高則起，時下則殆 and 当年有衝通 follow Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: establishing the year shows which of the five movements and six qi govern; meeting a generating, flourishing time, the time is high and one recovers; meeting decline and conquest, it is low and dangerous; even otherwise, if the year has a clash, the qi passes through and illness arises.
