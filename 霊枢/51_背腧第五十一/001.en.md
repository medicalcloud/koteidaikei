# 霊枢 背腧第五十一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I wish to hear about the transport points of the five zang organs that emerge on the back."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, describing the locations of the back transport points of the five zang organs and supplementing and draining with moxibustion.
