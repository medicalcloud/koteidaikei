# 霊枢 背腧第五十一 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The great transport point of the chest is at the end of the pillar bone [Dazhu]; Feishu is between the third vertebrae, Xinshu between the fifth, Geshu between the seventh, Ganshu between the ninth, Pishu between the eleventh, and Shenshu between the fourteenth; all flank the spine, about three *cun* apart [left and right]. To find and verify them, press the place: where there is a response and an aching, relaxing sensation within, that is the transport point. Moxibustion is permissible, but needling is not. When the qi is abundant, drain; when deficient, supplement. To supplement with fire, do not blow on the fire but let it go out by itself; to drain with fire, blow on the fire quickly, change the moxa [one after another], and wait for the fire to go out."

## Notes

The locations of the back transport points and supplementing and draining with moxibustion; the end of the chapter.
- 焦 is translated as vertebra, following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "焦 means 椎, the spaces between the vertebrae; in ancient times written 焦 or 顀, later 椎". The points on each side are one and a half *cun* from the midline, about three *cun* apart (*Leijing*).
- 応在中而痛解 follows the *Leijing* ("pressing the point is surely painful and 解, i.e. aching and relaxing"); 伝其艾 follows the *Leijing* ("to drain, blow the fire quickly and change the moxa").
