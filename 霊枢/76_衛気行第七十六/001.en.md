# 霊枢 衛気行第七十六 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I wish to hear about the movement of the defensive qi and where it goes out and comes in. How is it?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. It describes the course and measures of the circulation of the defensive qi in correspondence with the twenty-eight lunar lodges and day and night, and the method of needling according to where the defensive qi is.
