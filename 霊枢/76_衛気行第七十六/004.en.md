# 霊枢 衛気行第七十六 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When it first enters the yin, it always pours from the foot lesser yin into the kidneys; the kidneys pour into the heart, the heart into the lungs, the lungs into the liver, the liver into the spleen, and the spleen again into the kidneys, making a circuit. Thus at night, for each lodge, the human qi circulates through the yin viscera once and eight tenths of the viscera, and, as in the yang, twenty-five times, then rejoins at the eyes. Yin and yang together, in a day and a night, leave a remainder of four tenths of the body and two tenths of the viscera. Thus the times at which people go to bed and rise are early or late because the remainder is not used up."

## Notes

The *Leijing* notes: at night the defensive qi begins with the foot lesser yin kidney channel and circles the five viscera in the order of mutual restraint—kidney, heart, lung, liver, spleen—making one circuit and pouring again into the kidneys. In a day and night, as the sun moves twenty-eight lodges and the human qi circulates fifty times, the remainder is four tenths in the body and two tenths in the viscera; the remainder means the qi exceeds its measure without being used up, hence the variation in when people sleep and rise.
