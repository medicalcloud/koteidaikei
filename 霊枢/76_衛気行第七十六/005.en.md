# 霊枢 衛気行第七十六 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The wei-defensive qi in the body moves up and down, to and fro, not at fixed times. How does one wait for the qi and needle it?"

## Notes

From here the dialogue is with Bogao. 不以期 means that it is now above, now below, now in the yin, now in the yang, at no fixed time (*Leijing*).
