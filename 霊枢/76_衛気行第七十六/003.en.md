# 霊枢 衛気行第七十六 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus when the sun moves one lodge, the human qi circulates one time and eight tenths of the body; when the sun moves two lodges, three times and six tenths; three lodges, five times and four tenths; four lodges, seven times and two tenths; five lodges, nine times; six lodges, ten times and eight tenths; seven lodges, twelve times and six tenths; when the sun moves fourteen lodges, the human qi circulates twenty-five times through the body with a remainder of two tenths of the body. The yang is exhausted into the yin, and the yin receives the qi.

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: 舎 means lodge. The defensive qi circulates fifty times in a day and night; dividing fifty by twenty-eight gives, per lodge, one circuit and 0.785… of the body as the exact number; saying eight tenths is like heaven exceeding the sun by a degree, leaving a remainder. The *Leijing* computes the exact number and remainder for each lodge.
- The base's 二周 is emended to 三周. The *Leijing*, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text read 三周. Since each lodge adds one circuit and eight tenths, two lodges give three circuits and six tenths, so 三 fits the arithmetic.
- The base's 十分身之四 is emended to 十分身之二. The *Leijing*, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text read 十分身之二, and the *Leijing* note says the remainders of fourteen lodges add up to two tenths of the body, the remainder for one daytime. This also agrees with 一日一夜合有奇分十分身之四 (the total for day and night) in the next unit, so 二 is adopted.
