# 霊枢 癰疽第八十一 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the nutritive and defensive are retained within the channels, the blood becomes rough and does not move; when it does not move, the defensive qi follows it and is blocked, obstructed and unable to move, hence heat. When great heat does not stop and heat prevails, the flesh rots; when the flesh rots, it becomes pus. But it cannot sink [deep]: the bone marrow is not scorched or withered and the five viscera are not injured. Therefore it is called *yong*."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: from here the severity of *yong* and *ju* is distinguished. The *yong* toxin floats shallow at the surface and cannot sink to the bones, so the marrow does not wither and the five viscera are not injured; those with *yong* need not worry.
