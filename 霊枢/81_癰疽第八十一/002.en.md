# 霊枢 癰疽第八十一 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The meridians flow without stopping, sharing their measure with heaven and their order with earth. So when the lodges of heaven lose their measure, the sun and moon are eclipsed; when the rivers of earth lose their order, the waterways overflow, grasses do not grow, the five grains do not flourish, roads are impassable and people do not travel; even those gathered in lanes and living in towns are separated and scattered. Blood and qi are just the same; let me explain why. The blood vessels and the ying-nutrient and wei-defense circulate without rest, corresponding above to the stars and below to the number of the rivers. When a cold pathogen lodges in the meridians and collaterals, the blood becomes rough; when it is rough it does not flow; when it does not flow, the wei-defensive qi returns there and cannot go back, hence abscess and swelling. The cold qi transforms into heat; when heat prevails, the flesh rots; when the flesh rots, it becomes pus; if the pus is not drained, it erodes the sinews; when the sinews are eroded, the bones are injured; when the bones are injured, the marrow wastes away; if [the abscess] is not over a bone opening, it cannot drain; the blood dries and becomes empty, and then sinews, bones and flesh no longer nourish one another, the meridians break down and leak, fumes reach the five zang organs, the zang organs are injured, and so death follows."

## Notes

The *Leijing* notes: 衛気帰之不得復反 means it stays gathered without dispersing; 萓 is the day lily. The *Taisu* writes 萓 as a different character, and Yang Shangshan explains it as the name of a plant, also meaning withered stalks.
- 蔵傷故死: the abscess toxin progresses from shallow to deep, and when it injures the zang organs there is death (*Leijing*).
