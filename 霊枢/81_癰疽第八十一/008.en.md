# 霊枢 癰疽第八十一 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When heat qi is intense and abundant, it sinks down into the flesh and skin, the sinews and marrow wither, it connects inward with the five viscera, the blood and qi are exhausted, and beneath the abscess none of the sinews, bones or healthy flesh remains. Therefore it is called *ju*. In *ju*, the skin over it is dull and hard, like the skin of an ox's neck; in *yong*, the skin over it is thin and glossy. These are their signs."

## Notes

The end of the chapter and of the *Lingshu*. The *Leijing* notes: *yong* is shallow and *ju* deep, the toxin differing in severity; that which connects inward with the five viscera and outwardly ruins the sinews, bones and healthy flesh is called *ju*, and is to be feared. 夭 refers to color—dark and lusterless; the depth can be judged by the appearance of the skin.
