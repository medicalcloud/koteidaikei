# 霊枢 癰疽第八十一 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "An abscess arising in the throat is called the fierce deep abscess. If the fierce deep abscess is not treated it turns to pus; if the pus is not drained it blocks the throat and death comes within half a day. If it has turned to pus, after draining give lard mixed in, eaten cold, and it heals in three days. One arising on the neck is called the premature-death deep abscess; it is large, red and black; if not treated urgently, the heat qi descends into Yuanye, injures the controlling vessel in front, and fumes the liver and lungs inside; once it fumes the liver and lungs, death comes in ten-odd days. When yang lingers and erupts greatly, consuming the brain and lingering at the nape, it is called brain melting; the complexion is cheerless, the nape hurts as if pricked by needles; if there is vexation of the heart, death follows and it cannot be treated. One arising on the shoulder and upper arm is called the flaw abscess; it is red and black; treat it urgently. It makes the patient sweat down to the feet but does not harm the five viscera; if the abscess has been present four or five days, moxa it quickly. One arising below the armpit, red and hard, is called the rice deep abscess; treat it with stone needles, which should be thin and long, applied sparsely, and coat it with lard; it heals in six days; do not wrap it. If the abscess is hard and does not break, it is a saber [scrofula] beside the neck; treat it urgently. One arising on the chest is called the well deep abscess; it is like a soybean, rising in three or four days; if not treated early it descends into the abdomen, and untreated, death comes in seven days. One arising on the breast is called the sweet deep abscess; it is azure, shaped like grains or a snake-gourd, with constant suffering from cold and heat; treat it urgently to remove the cold and heat; death comes in ten years, and pus comes out after death. One arising on the flank is called the ruinous flaw; the ruinous flaw is a disease of women; moxa it. If the illness has become a large abscess with pus, when treating it there will be raw flesh inside as large as an adzuki bean. Chop one *sheng* each of the roots of water caltrop and forsythia, boil them in one *dou* six *sheng* of water until reduced to three *sheng*, make the patient drink it forcibly, dress warmly and sit over a cauldron so that sweat comes down to the feet, and it heals. One arising on the thigh and shin is called the thigh-shin deep abscess; its appearance changes little, but the pus presses against the bone; if not treated urgently, death comes in thirty days. One arising on the buttocks is called the sharp deep abscess; it is red, hard and large; treat it urgently; untreated, death comes in thirty days. One arising on the inner thigh is called the red spread; if not treated urgently, death comes in sixty days; if it is on the inside of both thighs and untreated, death will come in ten days. One arising on the knee is called the flaw abscess; it is a large abscess of unchanged color, with cold and heat, hard as stone; do not lance it—if lanced, death follows; wait until it softens and then lance it, and the patient lives. Any abscesses arising at the joints that correspond to each other cannot be treated; those arising in the yang lead to death in a hundred days, those in the yin in thirty days. One arising on the shin is called the rabbit bite; it is red and reaches the bone; treat it urgently; untreated, it harms the patient. One arising on the inner ankle is called the slow runner; it is an abscess of unchanged color; repeatedly lance its site to stop the cold and heat, and the patient will not die. One arising on the top or sole of the foot is called the four excesses; it is a large abscess; treat it urgently; death comes in a hundred days. One arising on the side of the foot is called the malign abscess; it is not large, starting like the little finger; treat it urgently, removing the black part; if it does not subside it soon enlarges; untreated, death comes in a hundred days. One arising on a toe is called the shedding abscess; if it is red and black, it is fatal and cannot be treated; if not red and black, the patient will not die. If it does not subside, cut [the toe] off at once; otherwise death follows."

## Notes

Abscesses by region, translated following the *Leijing* notes.
- Fierce deep abscess: named for the urgency of its harm; lard is refined pork fat.
- Premature-death deep abscess: the neck here is the front of the neck; Yuanye is a point of the foot lesser yang; arising on the neck it connects with the lung system, and descending into the foot lesser yang it reaches the liver, hence death.
- Brain melting: great eruption of yang means extreme pathogenic heat; a cheerless complexion means the spirit is injured; vexation of the heart means the pathogen has invaded the viscera.
- Flaw abscess (shoulder and upper arm): not a vital place, so it does not reach the five viscera; 逞 means quick and 焫 moxa cones—moxa promptly to remove it.
- Rice deep abscess: the stones are thin so as not to injure the flesh and long for deep use, so sparse application is better than dense.
- Saber beside the neck is scrofula; 挟纓 is written 俠癭 in the *Jingmai* chapter (ch. 10).
- Sweet deep abscess: the breast is the raised flesh beside the chest; "grains" describes the abscesses clustering like grains; 𦸈蓏 is snake-gourd, soft, unbroken and holding something like seeds; it lingers and is hard to cure, probably a kind of breast abscess. The sound gloss at the end of the chapter says 𦸈蓏 is "an old way of writing 括楼".
- Remedy for the ruinous flaw: 䔖 is water caltrop and 翹 forsythia; the roots of both detoxify.
- Thigh-shin deep abscess: the outward form is not evident and the pus adheres to the bone—what people now call the bone-adhering abscess.
- Red spread: the inner thigh is where yin qi gathers, so untreated it is fatal; if both thighs are affected, injury to the yin is extreme and death even quicker.
- Flaw abscess (knee): lancing a knee abscess before it has formed injures the house of the sinews and leads to death; once soft, pus has formed and lancing does no harm.
- 発於節而相応者: those that arise above and correspond below, or arise on the left and correspond on the right. Those arising in the three yang divisions have shallow toxin in the bowels and death is somewhat slower; those in the three yin have deep toxin in the viscera and do not last a month (*Leijing*).
- 数石其輸 for the slow runner means lancing the swollen place (*Leijing*).
- Four excesses: the yang receives qi at the four extremities, and a large abscess spreading there is the extreme of yang toxin; malign abscess: 不消輒益 means that though it starts as small as a little finger, untreated it grows daily; shedding abscess: the toes are where the six well points arise; if red and black the toxin is especially severe, and if it does not subside the toe must be cut off at once (*Leijing*).
- The base's 天疽 is emended to 夭疽. The *Taisu*, the *Lingshu jizhu*, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text read 夭疽 (the *Leijing* 天疽). As the name of a grave, fatal abscess, 夭 ("premature death") fits the context, so the graphically similar 天 is taken as an error for 夭.
- 翹 is supplied in the blank of the base's 剉䔖　草根. The base has a one-character blank after 䔖. The *Jiayi jing* reads 䔖翹草根, the *Taisu* and the *Lingshu jizhu* 剉翹草根, and the *Leijing* has after 剉䔖 a character that cannot be displayed in the digital text, which its note says is the same as 翹, i.e. forsythia. 翹 is supplied in the blank.
