# 霊枢 癰疽第八十一 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I wish to hear fully about the forms of abscesses and deep abscesses, their fatal times and their names."

## Notes

For 忌曰名 the *Taisu* reads 忌日名, and Yang Shangshan's note says there are three questions: the forms of the abscesses, their [times of] death or survival, and [their] names (the *Leijing*, the *Jiayi jing* and the *Lingshu jizhu* agree with the base). The translation follows the *Taisu* note.
