# 霊枢 衛気失常第五十九 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "The regions of the skin transport to the four extremities. The pillars of the flesh are between the partings of the flesh of all the yang on the arms and shins, and between the partings of the foot lesser yin. The transport points of the blood and qi transport into the network vessels; when qi and blood linger, they become abundant and rise. The regions of the sinews have neither yin nor yang, neither left nor right; observe where the illness is. The attachments of the bones are where the bone hollows receive and replenish [fluids], replenishing the brain and marrow."

## Notes

皮之部，輸于四末 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: illness in the skin is in the yang aspect; yang receives qi from the four extremities; the skin is shallow and its qi floating, so its regions transport to the extremities.
