# 霊枢 衛気失常第五十九 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "The ointment-like have much qi; with much qi there is heat, and with heat one endures cold. The fleshy have much blood, which fills the form; a filled form is balanced. The fat have clear blood and smooth, scant qi, so they cannot become large. This is how they differ from ordinary people."
