# 霊枢 衛気失常第五十九 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "The changes of illness, floating or sinking, deep or shallow, are inexhaustible, but each is in its place. When the illness is mild, needle shallow; when severe, deep; when mild, use few [needles]; when severe, many; follow the changes and regulate the qi. This is called the superior practitioner."
