# 霊枢 衛気失常第五十九 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao replied, "When it accumulates above, drain Renying, Tiantu and the inside of the throat. When it accumulates below, drain Sanli and Qijie. When both above and below are full, take it above and below and one *cun* below the free ribs [Zhangmen]. In severe cases, take it with chicken-foot [needling]. On examining the pulse, if it is large, stringlike and tense, or cut off and not arriving, or if the abdominal skin is very tight, it must not be needled."

## Notes

The interlinear note is not translated (original text only).
- 季脅之下一寸 is probably Zhangmen of the foot *Jueyin*, and 鶏足取之 means needling in a cluster, the union-valley needling of chapter 7 (Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*).
