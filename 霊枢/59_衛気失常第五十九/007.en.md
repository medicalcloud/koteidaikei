# 霊枢 衛気失常第五十九 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "When color arises between the eyebrows, thin and lustrous, the illness is in the skin. When the lips are green, yellow, red, white or black, the illness is in the flesh. When the nutritive qi is moist, the illness is in the blood and qi. When the eyes are green, yellow, red, white or black, the illness is in the sinews. When the ears are scorched and withered as if covered with dust and grime, the illness is in the bones."

## Notes

営気濡然 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "濡 is moist: sweating of the skin, swelling of the flesh and loose stools are all 'moist'."
