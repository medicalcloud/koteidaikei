# 霊枢 衛気失常第五十九 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "People may be fat, ointment-like, or fleshy."

## Notes

The base's 「有膏有内」 is emended to 「有膏有肉」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige and the *Leijing* read 肉, and the following text speaks of 肉者, so 肉 is adopted. 肥 is "what is called fat (脂) below" (Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*).
