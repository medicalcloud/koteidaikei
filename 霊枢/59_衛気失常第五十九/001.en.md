# 霊枢 衛気失常第五十九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When the wei-defensive qi lingers in the abdomen, accumulating without moving, pent up and unable to find its usual place, so that the flanks and the stomach are full, with dyspnea, shouting and counterflow breathing, how does one remove it?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, describing how to treat the wei-defensive qi when it loses its norm, how to recognize illnesses of skin, flesh, qi, blood, sinews and bones, and human body types (ointment, flesh, fat).
- Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: the wei-defensive qi normally runs through the skin and the partings of the flesh; losing its norm, it sinks inward with the pathogen, lingers in the abdomen and becomes pent up; 苑 is the same as 鬱.
