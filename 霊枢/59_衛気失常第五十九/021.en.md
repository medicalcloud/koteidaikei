# 霊枢 衛気失常第五十九 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "In the ointment-like, the flesh is soft; those with coarse texture have a cold body, those with fine texture a hot body. In the fat, the flesh is firm; fine texture means heat, coarse texture cold."
