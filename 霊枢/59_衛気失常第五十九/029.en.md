# 霊枢 衛気失常第五十九 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "One must first distinguish the three forms, the amount of blood and the clarity or turbidity of the qi, and then regulate them; in treatment do not depart from the normal channels. Thus the ointment-like person has a sagging belly and hanging fat, the fleshy person is large above and below, and the fat person, though fat, cannot become large."

The end of the chapter.
