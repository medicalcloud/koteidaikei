# 霊枢 衛気失常第五十九 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "In ordinary people, skin, flesh, fat and ointment do not add to one another, and blood and qi do not exceed one another, so their form is neither small nor large, each in proportion to the body. They are called ordinary people."
