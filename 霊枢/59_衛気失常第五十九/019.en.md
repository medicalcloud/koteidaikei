# 霊枢 衛気失常第五十九 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "Those whose bulging muscles are firm and skin full are fat; those whose bulging muscles are not firm and skin slack are ointment-like; those whose skin and flesh do not separate are fleshy."

## Notes

The base's 膕内堅, 膕内不堅 and 皮内不相離 are emended to 䐃肉堅, 䐃肉不堅 and 皮肉不相離. The *Leijing* has this reading, and the base's interlinear note says "one edition has 䐃内"; Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*) and Daizhige have 膕肉 and 皮肉. Zhang Jiebin notes that 䐃肉 is where the flesh gathers, which suits a description of body types. The interlinear note is not translated (original text only).
