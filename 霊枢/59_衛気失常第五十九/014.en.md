# 霊枢 衛気失常第五十九 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Bogao, "People differ in fatness and thinness, size, cold and warmth, and are old, mature, young or small. How does one distinguish them?"

## Notes

The base's 「問于歧伯曰」 is emended to 「問于伯高曰」. Wikisource (both), Daizhige, the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* all name Bogao, who also answers, so 伯高 is adopted.
- 寒温 refers to the yin and yang of one's endowment (Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*).
