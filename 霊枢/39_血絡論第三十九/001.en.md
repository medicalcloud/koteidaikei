# 霊枢 血絡論第三十九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I wish to hear about the irregular pathogens that are not in the meridians."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking the reasons for the various reactions that occur when the blood collaterals are needled.
