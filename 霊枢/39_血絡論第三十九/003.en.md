# 霊枢 血絡論第三十九 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Why does one faint when the blood network vessels are needled? Why does the blood spurt out? Why is the blood scanty, dark and turbid? Why is the blood that comes out clear and half of it watery? Why is there swelling when the needle is withdrawn? Why does the face turn pale whether much or little blood comes out? Why is there vexation and oppression without change of complexion when the needle is withdrawn? Why is there no disturbance even when much blood is let? I wish to hear the reasons."
