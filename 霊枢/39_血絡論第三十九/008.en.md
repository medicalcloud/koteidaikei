# 霊枢 血絡論第三十九 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When hot qi attaches to the needle, the needle becomes hot; when hot, the flesh adheres to the needle, so it becomes firm."

## Notes

The end of the chapter.
