# 霊枢 血絡論第三十九 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The blood vessels are abundant, firm, transverse and red, with no fixed location above or below; the small ones like needles, the large ones like chopsticks. Drain them at once and it is entirely safe. So do not lose the rules; losing the rules and going against them, each [harm] follows its measure."

## Notes

則 in 則而瀉之 is read as 即 "at once".
- 失数而反，各如其度 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: going against the method produces fainting, collapse, deficiency, swelling and so on, each corresponding to the manner of needling.
