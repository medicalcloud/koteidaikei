# 霊枢 百病始生第六十六 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The qi of the three parts differ: some arise in the yin, some in the yang. Let me explain the way. When joy and anger are immoderate they injure the zang organs, and when the zang organs are injured the illness arises in the yin. When chill and damp assail a deficiency, the illness arises below; when wind and rain assail a deficiency, the illness arises above. These are called the three parts. As for their spreading, it cannot be counted."
