# 霊枢 百病始生第六十六 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus when a depletion pathogen strikes a person, it begins in the skin. When the skin is slack the interstices open; when they open the pathogen enters by the hair; having entered, it reaches deeper; when it is deep the hair stands up; when the hair stands up there are shivers, hence the skin hurts. If it stays and does not leave, it passes on to lodge in the network vessels; while it is in the network vessels, the flesh hurts. When the pain at times ceases, the great channels take over. If it stays, it passes on to lodge in the channels; while in the channels, there are shivers and a tendency to fright. If it stays, it passes on to lodge in the transport points; while in the transport points, the six channels are blocked, the joints of the four limbs hurt, and the lower back and spine are stiff. If it stays, it passes on to lodge in the hidden thoroughfare vessel; while there, the body is heavy and painful. If it stays, it passes on to lodge in the intestines and stomach; while there, there are rushing sounds and abdominal distension; with much cold, borborygmus and diarrhea with undigested food; with much heat, loose, mushy stools. If it stays, it passes on to lodge outside the intestines and stomach, between the membranes, adhering to the vessels; if it lingers and does not leave, it settles and forms an accumulation. It may adhere to the minute vessels, the network vessels, the channels, the transport vessels, the hidden thoroughfare vessel, the paraspinal sinews, or the membranes of the intestines and stomach, connecting above to the slack sinews. The spreading of the pathogenic qi cannot be fully discussed."

## Notes

The path by which a depletion pathogen passes in stages from the skin deeper until it forms an accumulation.
- 其痛之時息，大経乃代 follows the *Leijing* note: when the pain in the flesh gradually ceases, the pathogen is about to leave the network vessels and the deeper great channels take it over.
- The hidden thoroughfare vessel is the part of the thoroughfare vessel at the spine, called "hidden" because it is deepest (*Leijing*).
- 在輸之時，六経不通: the transport points are where channel qi gathers, located between joints and hollows; when the pathogen passes from the channels to the transport points, the six channels are blocked and the joints and lower back hurt and stiffen (*Leijing*).
- 賁嚮 is 奔向, "rushing" (*Leijing*). 溏出麋: with heat, turbid filth flows down as loose, mushy stools; 麋 is filth like mud (*Leijing*).
- 腸胃之外，募原之間 means inside the skin and outside the membranes—hidden, winding places where qi and blood do not flow easily; if the pathogen lodges there it settles into an accumulation (*Leijing*).
