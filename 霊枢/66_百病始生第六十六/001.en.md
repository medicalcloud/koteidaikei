# 霊枢 百病始生第六十六 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "All the hundred illnesses arise at first from wind and rain, cold and summer heat, chill and damp, joy and anger. When joy and anger are immoderate they injure the zang organs; wind and rain injure the upper part; chill and damp injure the lower part. The qi of the three parts injures in different ways. I wish to hear the essentials."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. It explains why illness arises in the three parts—upper, lower and internal—the path by which deficiency pathogens pass from the skin deeper and form accumulations, how accumulations arise, illnesses from internal injury, and treatment.
