# 霊枢 百病始生第六十六 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Worry and pondering injure the heart; double cold injures the lungs; rage and anger injure the liver; entering the bedchamber drunk, or being exposed to wind while sweating, injures the spleen; excessive exertion, or bathing after sweating in the bedchamber, injures the kidneys. These are where illnesses of the three parts, inner and outer, arise."
