# 霊枢 百病始生第六十六 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "Examine where it hurts to know what it corresponds to; [judging] surplus and insufficiency, supplement where one should supplement and drain where one should drain, without going against the season of heaven. This is called perfect treatment."

## Notes

The end of the chapter. The final 泆 is the headword of a sound gloss (interlinear note 音亦).
