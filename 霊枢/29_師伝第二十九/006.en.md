# 霊枢 師伝第二十九 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Those with heat in the center and wasting and thirst are suited by cold; those of the cold-center type are suited by heat. When the stomach is hot, grain is consumed, the heart feels suspended, there is frequent hunger, and the skin above the navel is hot. When the intestines are hot, yellow matter like gruel comes out, and the skin below the navel is cold. When the stomach is cold, the abdomen is distended; when the intestines are cold, there is rumbling and swill diarrhea. When the stomach is cold and the intestines hot, there is distension and also diarrhea; when the stomach is hot and the intestines cold, there is quick hunger and pain and distension of the lower abdomen."

## Notes

- The base's 「令人県心養饑」 is emended to 「令人懸心善饑」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 令人懸心善饑; 懸心善 is adopted.
