# 霊枢 師伝第二十九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I have heard that the former masters kept some things in their hearts and did not write them in the formularies. I wish to hear and keep them, follow and practice them, to govern the people above and the self below, so that the people have no illness, those above and below are harmonious and close, virtue flows down, descendants have no worries, and it is transmitted to later generations without end. May I hear them?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about following what suits (便) the patient and how to examine the viscera and bowels from the outside of the body.
