# 霊枢 師伝第二十九 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "*The Root Viscera* uses the body's form, limbs and joints, and bulging muscles to examine the size of the five viscera and six bowels. But when kings, dukes and great men, lords who have ascended the throne and preside over the court, ask about it, who could answer only after stroking and tracing them?"

## Notes

Following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* ("本蔵 is the title of a chapter of this classic"), 本蔵 is taken as chapter 47 (*Benzang*), which indeed explains how to judge the size of the viscera and bowels from the body's form and limbs.
