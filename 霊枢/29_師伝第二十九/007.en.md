# 霊枢 師伝第二十九 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When the stomach wants cold drinks and the intestines want hot drinks, the two conflicting, how does one suit them? Moreover, kings, dukes and great men, lords who eat meat, are arrogant and self-indulgent, slight others, and no one can restrain them; restraining them goes against their will, following them adds to their illness. How does one suit them? What is to be treated first?"

## Notes

- The base's 「胃欲寒饑」 is emended to 「胃欲寒飲」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 胃欲寒飲, paired with 腸欲熱飲; 飲 is adopted.
