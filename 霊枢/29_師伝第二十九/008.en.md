# 霊枢 師伝第二十九 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "It is human nature to hate death and delight in life. Tell them what will ruin them, speak to them of what is good, guide them by what suits them, and open up to them what pains them; then even unprincipled people—how could any not listen?"
