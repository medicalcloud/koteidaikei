# 霊枢 師伝第二十九 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Of the five zang organs and six fu organs, the lungs are the cover. Whether the shoulders are large or the throat deep, the signs are seen on the outside."

## Notes

巨肩陥咽，候見其外 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "large shoulders or a deep throat are the outer signs from which the size, height, firmness and alignment of the lungs can be known".
