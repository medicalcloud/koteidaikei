# 霊枢 師伝第二十九 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Of the five viscera and six bowels, the lungs are the cover. Whether the shoulders are large or the throat sunken, the signs are seen on the outside."

## Notes

The reading of 巨肩陥咽 is unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); translated provisionally.
