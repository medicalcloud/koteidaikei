# 霊枢 師伝第二十九 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Of the six fu organs, the stomach is the sea. A broad frame, a large neck and an expanded chest mean the five grains can be contained. The nasal passages being long are used to examine the large intestine. Thick lips and a long philtrum are used to examine the small intestine. When the lower eyelids are large, the gallbladder is strong. When the nostrils turn outward, the bladder leaks. When the middle of the bridge of the nose is raised, the sanjiao is firm. This is how the six fu organs are examined. When the upper, middle and lower three are equal, the zang organs are at peace and good."

## Notes

The signs of the six fu organs; the end of the chapter. The final 便 is the headword of a sound gloss; the interlinear note (pronunciation) is not translated (original text only).
- 上下三等 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: when the upper, middle and lower thirds of the body and face are equal, the zang-fu organs are at peace.
