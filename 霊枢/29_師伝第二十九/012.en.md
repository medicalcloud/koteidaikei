# 霊枢 師伝第二十九 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "To suit this, food, drink and clothing should also be adapted to cold and warmth: in the cold, no shivering; in the heat, no sweating. As for food and drink, when hot, not scalding; when cold, not icy. When cold and warmth are moderate, the qi is maintained and deviant pathogens are not brought on."
