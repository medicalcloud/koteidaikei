# 霊枢 師伝第二十九 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Of the five viscera and six bowels, the heart is the master and the empty basin its path. The surplus of the 骷 bone is used to examine the xiphoid."

## Notes

The meaning of 骷骨有余，以候𩩲骬 is unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); translated provisionally.
