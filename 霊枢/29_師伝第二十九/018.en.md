# 霊枢 師伝第二十九 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Of the five zang organs and six fu organs, the heart is the master and the empty basin its path. The surplus of the 骷 bone is used to examine the xiphoid."

## Notes

𩩲骬 is "the bone covering the heart, also called Jiuwei" (Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*). For 骷骨, the *Leijing* cites the *Guangya* as naming a knee bone, which does not fit this context about the heart; what it refers to is unknown, and the translation is provisional.
