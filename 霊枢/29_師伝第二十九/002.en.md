# 霊枢 師伝第二十九 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What a far-reaching question! In governing the people and governing oneself, governing that and governing this, governing the small and governing the great, governing the state and governing the household, no one has ever governed well by going against [them]; there is only following. Following is not only the counterflow and compliance of yin and yang, the vessels and the qi; all the people wish to have their will followed."
