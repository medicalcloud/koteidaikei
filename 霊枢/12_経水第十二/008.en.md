# 霊枢 経水第十二 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "What can be measured is taken from those of medium build, whose flesh is not greatly wasted and whose blood and qi have not declined. If the person to be measured is emaciated with wasted flesh, how can one needle by measurement? Carefully feel, trace, stroke and press, observe the cold and warmth, abundance and decline, and regulate accordingly. This is called the truth of acting according to what is suitable."
