# 霊枢 経水第十二 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "An excellent question! 'Heaven is so high it cannot be measured, earth so wide it cannot be gauged' refers to this. People are born between heaven and earth, within the six directions; the height of heaven and the width of earth cannot be measured and reached by human strength. But for a man of eight *chi*, the skin and flesh are right here; from outside they can be measured and found by feeling and tracing, and after death he can be dissected and examined. The firmness or fragility of the viscera, the size of the bowels, the amount of grain, the length of the vessels, the clarity or turbidity of the blood, the amount of qi, and which of the twelve channels have much blood and little qi, little blood and much qi, much blood and qi, or little blood and qi, all have their general numbers. In treating with needle and moxa, one regulates the qi of each channel; surely their norms have correspondences."
