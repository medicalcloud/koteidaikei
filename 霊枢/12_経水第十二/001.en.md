# 霊枢 経水第十二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "The twelve channels correspond outwardly to the twelve channel waters and belong inwardly to the five viscera and six bowels. The twelve channel waters differ in size, depth, width and length; the five viscera and six bowels also differ in height, size and the amount of grain they receive. How do they correspond? The channel waters receive water and make it flow; the five viscera combine spirit qi and the ethereal and corporeal souls and store them; the six bowels receive grain and make it move, receive qi and spread it; the channels receive blood and circulate it. How does one treat by combining them? May I hear about the depth of needling and the number of moxa cones?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter: comparing the twelve channels to twelve rivers (channel waters), it asks about the depth of needling and the number of moxa cones.
- The base's 「近各不固」 is emended to 「近各不同」. All other sources (Wikisource, Siku, Daizhige, *Taisu*, *Leijing*, *Lingshu jizhu*) read 各不同; 同 is adopted.
