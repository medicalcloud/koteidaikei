# 霊枢 経水第十二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "The twelve meridians correspond outwardly to the twelve meridian waters and belong inwardly to the five zang organs and six fu organs. The twelve meridian waters differ in size, depth, width and length; the five zang organs and six fu organs also differ in height, size and the amount of grain they receive. How do they correspond? The meridian waters receive water and make it flow; the five zang organs combine spirit qi and the ethereal and corporeal souls and store them; the six fu organs receive grain and make it move, receive qi and spread it; the meridians receive blood and circulate it. How does one treat by combining them? May I hear about the depth of needling and the number of moxa cones?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter: comparing the twelve meridians to twelve rivers (meridian waters), it asks about the depth of needling and the number of moxa cones.
- The base's 「近各不固」 is emended to 「近各不同」. All other sources (Wikisource, Siku, Daizhige, *Taisu*, *Leijing*, *Lingshu jizhu*) read 各不同; 同 is adopted.
