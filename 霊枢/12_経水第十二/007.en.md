# 霊枢 経水第十二 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Can the size of the channels, the amount of blood, the thickness of the skin, the firmness of the flesh and the size of the bulging muscles be measured?"

## Notes

The base is followed.
- The base's 「及膕之大小」 is emended to 「及䐃之大小」. The Lingshu editions and *Leijing* agree with the base (膕), but Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* glosses the character as "lumps of flesh". Measured alongside channels, blood, skin and flesh, 䐃 (bulging muscle) fits better than 膕 (back of the knee), so 䐃 is adopted.
