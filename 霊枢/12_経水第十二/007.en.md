# 霊枢 経水第十二 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Can the size of the channels, the amount of blood, the thickness of the skin, the firmness of the flesh and the size of the back of the knee be measured?"

## Notes

One view holds that 膕 is an error for 䐃 (bulging muscles); unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points). The base is followed.
