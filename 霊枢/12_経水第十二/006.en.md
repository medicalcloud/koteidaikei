# 霊枢 経水第十二 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The foot *Yangming* is the sea of the five zang organs and six fu organs; its vessel is large with much blood, its qi abundant and its heat strong. In needling it, without depth [the pathogen] will not disperse, without retention it will not drain. For the foot *Yangming*, needle six *fen* deep and retain for ten exhalations. For the foot greater yang, five *fen* deep, retain for seven exhalations. For the foot lesser yang, four *fen* deep, retain for five exhalations. For the foot greater yin, three *fen* deep, retain for four exhalations. For the foot lesser yin, two *fen* deep, retain for three exhalations. For the foot *Jueyin*, one *fen* deep, retain for two exhalations. The yin and yang of the hand have short paths for receiving qi and their qi comes quickly, so needling should never exceed two *fen* in depth or one exhalation in retention. For youth or age, size, fatness or thinness, gauge with the mind. This is called following the constancy of heaven. Moxibustion is likewise. If moxibustion exceeds this, one gets malign fire, and the bones wither and the vessels become rough; if needling exceeds this, the qi collapses."

## Notes

The needling depth and the number of breaths of retention for each meridian.
