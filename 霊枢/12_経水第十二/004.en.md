# 霊枢 経水第十二 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "This is how people participate in heaven and earth and correspond to yin and yang; it must be examined. The foot greater yang corresponds outwardly to the Qing River and belongs inwardly to the bladder, opening the waterways. The foot lesser yang corresponds outwardly to the Wei River and belongs inwardly to the gallbladder. The foot *Yangming* corresponds outwardly to the sea and belongs inwardly to the stomach. The foot greater yin corresponds outwardly to the lakes and belongs inwardly to the spleen. The foot lesser yin corresponds outwardly to the Ru River and belongs inwardly to the kidneys. The foot *Jueyin* corresponds outwardly to the Mian River and belongs inwardly to the liver. The hand greater yang corresponds outwardly to the Huai River and belongs inwardly to the small intestine, from which the waterways come out. The hand lesser yang corresponds outwardly to the Ta River and belongs inwardly to the sanjiao. The hand *Yangming* corresponds outwardly to the [Yangzi] River and belongs inwardly to the large intestine. The hand greater yin corresponds outwardly to the [Yellow] River and belongs inwardly to the lungs. The hand lesser yin corresponds outwardly to the Ji River and belongs inwardly to the heart. The hand heart governor corresponds outwardly to the Zhang River and belongs inwardly to the pericardium. All these five zang organs, six fu organs and twelve meridian waters have sources outside and something they receive inside; they all pass through one another inside and out, like a ring without end, and so do human meridians. Hence heaven is yang and earth yin; above the waist is heaven, below the waist is earth. Hence north of the sea is yin; north of the lakes is yin within yin; south of the Zhang is yang; north of the [Yellow] River up to the Zhang is yin within yang; south of the Ta up to the [Yangzi] River is greater yang within yang. These are the yin and yang of one corner [of the land], by which people participate with heaven and earth."

## Notes

The correspondence of the twelve meridians to twelve rivers, and their yin and yang.
