# 霊枢 腸胃第三十一 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

From where [the grain] enters the intestines and stomach to where it leaves is six *zhang* four *cun* four *fen* long, with thirty-two winding bends."

## Notes

The total length of the digestive tract. Bogao's speech ends here, concluding the chapter.
