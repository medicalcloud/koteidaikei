# 霊枢 腸胃第三十一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Bogao, "I wish to hear about the six fu organs that transmit grain: the size and length of the intestines and stomach, and how much grain they receive."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about the measurements and capacities of each part of the digestive tract.
