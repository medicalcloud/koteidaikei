# 霊枢 腸胃第三十一 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The small intestine is attached at the back to the spine, circles to the left and folds over in layers; where it pours into the winding intestine, it is attached on the outside above the navel; it winds around in sixteen bends, is two and a half *cun* in circumference, eight and one-third *fen* in diameter, and three *zhang* two *chi* long. The winding intestine lies at the navel, circles to the left, winds around in leaf-like layers downward, winds back in sixteen bends, is four *cun* in circumference, one and one-third *cun* in diameter, and two *zhang* one *chi* long. The broad intestine is attached to the spine and receives [the contents of] the winding intestine; it circles to the left in layers along the spine, opens above and below, is eight *cun* in circumference, two and two-thirds *cun* in diameter, and two *chi* eight *cun* long.

## Notes

The measurements of the small intestine, winding intestine and broad intestine. 少半 is taken as one-third and 大半 as two-thirds.
- 広腸伝脊 and 左環葉脊 follow Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: the broad intestine is the lower part of the large intestine, also called the rectum; it runs down along the spine (伝 = spread along), and 葉脊 means layered along the spine down to the coccyx.
