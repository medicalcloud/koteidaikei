# 霊枢 腸胃第三十一 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The stomach is curved; stretched out, it is two *chi* six *cun* long, one *chi* five *cun* in circumference and five *cun* in diameter, with a capacity of three *dou* five *sheng*.

## Notes

䊸 in 䊸曲 of the base is read as 紆 "winding", as other sources have it (see collation).
