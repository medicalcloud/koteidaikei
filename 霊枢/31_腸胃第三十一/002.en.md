# 霊枢 腸胃第三十一 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "Let me tell you fully the measurements of depth, distance and length of where the grain goes in and out. From the lips to the teeth is nine *fen* long, and the mouth is two and a half *cun* wide. From behind the teeth to the epiglottis is three and a half *cun* deep, with a capacity of five *ge*. The tongue weighs ten *liang*, is seven *cun* long and two and a half *cun* wide. The pharynx weighs ten *liang*, is one and a half *cun* wide, and is one *chi* six *cun* long to the stomach.
