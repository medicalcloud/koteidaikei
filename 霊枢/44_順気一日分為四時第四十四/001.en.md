# 霊枢 順気一日分為四時第四十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The hundred illnesses always arise from dryness and damp, cold and heat, wind and rain, yin and yang, joy and anger, food and drink, and dwelling; the qi combines to take form and, reaching the zang organs, acquires a name. I know that this is so. Why are the hundred illnesses mostly clear in the morning, calm at midday, worse in the evening and severe at night?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, which explains the waxing and waning of illness by dividing a day into four seasons and describes the correspondence of the five variations and the five transport points.
