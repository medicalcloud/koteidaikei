# 霊枢 順気一日分為四時第四十四 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the illness is in the viscera, take the well point. When the illness changes the complexion, take the brook point. When the illness is sometimes mild and sometimes severe, take the stream point. When the illness changes the voice, take the river point. When the channel is full with blood, the illness is in the stomach; this, and illness contracted from immoderate eating and drinking, are taken at the uniting point. Hence it is said that flavor governs the uniting point. These are called the five variations."
