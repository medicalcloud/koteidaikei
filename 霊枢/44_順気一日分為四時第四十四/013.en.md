# 霊枢 順気一日分為四時第四十四 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "How do they govern the five transport points?" [Qibo said,] "The zang organs govern winter; in winter needle the well points. Color governs spring; in spring needle the brook points. Season governs summer; in summer needle the stream points. Note governs late summer; in late summer needle the river points. Flavor governs autumn; in autumn needle the uniting points. This is called the five variations governing the five transport points."

## Notes

The base has no 岐伯曰 before the answer; "[Qibo said,]" is supplied. 栄 is 滎 (brook point).
