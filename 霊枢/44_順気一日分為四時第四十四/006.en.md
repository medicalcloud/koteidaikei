# 霊枢 順気一日分為四時第四十四 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "These do not correspond to the qi of the four seasons; the viscus alone governs the illness. They are always severe at the times that the qi of the viscus cannot overcome, and recover at the times that it overcomes."
