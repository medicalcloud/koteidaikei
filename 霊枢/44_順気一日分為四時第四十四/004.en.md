# 霊枢 順気一日分為四時第四十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Spring generates, summer grows, autumn gathers and winter stores; this is the norm of qi, and people correspond to it. Dividing one day into four seasons, morning is spring, midday summer, sunset autumn and midnight winter. In the morning the human qi begins to arise and the qi of the illness declines, so one is clear in the morning. At midday the human qi grows, and growing it overcomes the pathogen, so one is calm. In the evening the human qi begins to decline and the pathogenic qi begins to arise, so [the illness] worsens. At midnight the human qi enters the zang organs and the pathogenic qi alone occupies the body, so it is severe."
