# 霊枢 順気一日分為四時第四十四 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The liver is a male [yang] zang organ; its color is green, its season spring, its note *jue*, its flavor sour, its days *jia* and *yi*. The heart is a male zang organ; its color is red, its season summer, its days *bing* and *ding*, its note *zhi*, its flavor bitter. The spleen is a female [yin] zang organ; its color is yellow, its season late summer, its days *wu* and *ji*, its note *gong*, its flavor sweet. The lungs are a female zang organ; their color is white, their note *shang*, their season autumn, their days *geng* and *xin*, their flavor acrid. The kidneys are a female zang organ; their color is black, their season winter, their days *ren* and *gui*, their note *yu*, their flavor salty. These are the five variations."

## Notes

- The base's 「其日戊巳」 is emended to 「其日戊己」. This passage names the days of the ten stems, so 巳 is taken as a graphic error for 己.
