# 霊枢 順気一日分為四時第四十四 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "What is meant by the viscera governing winter, season governing summer, note governing late summer, flavor governing autumn and color governing spring? I wish to hear the reason."
