# 霊枢 禁服第四十八 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong bowed twice, rose and said, "I accept your command." Then he fasted and stayed overnight for three days, and requested, "May I say that today at noon this humble one wishes to receive the covenant?" The Yellow Emperor then entered the purification chamber with him, and they cut their arms and smeared their lips with blood.
