# 霊枢 禁服第四十八 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The inch opening governs the inside and Renying the outside. The two correspond, coming and going together, equal in size like a pulled rope. In spring and summer Renying is slightly larger; in autumn and winter the inch opening is slightly larger. Such a person is called healthy.

## Notes

The Yellow Emperor's speech continues through unit 16. The comparison of the pulses at Renying and the inch opening has the same content as chapter 9 (*Zhongshi*).
