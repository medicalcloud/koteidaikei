# 霊枢 禁服第四十八 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When Renying is once as large as the inch opening, the illness is in the foot lesser yang; once as large and agitated, in the hand lesser yang. When Renying is twice as large, the illness is in the foot greater yang; twice as large and agitated, in the hand greater yang. When Renying is three times as large, the illness is in the foot *yangming*; three times as large and agitated, in the hand *yangming*. Abundance means heat, depletion cold, tightness painful impediment, and an intermittent pulse that it is now severe, now mild. Drain when abundant, supplement when depleted; when tight and painful, take it at the partings of the flesh; when intermittent, take the blood network vessels and also give medicine to drink; when sunken, apply moxibustion; when neither abundant nor depleted, take it at the channel; this is called channel needling. When Renying is four times as large, both large and rapid, it is called overflowing yang; overflowing yang is outer rejection, death without cure. One must carefully press the root and branch and examine cold and heat to verify the illness of the viscera and bowels.
