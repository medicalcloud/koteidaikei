# 霊枢 禁服第四十八 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The principle of all needling begins with the meridians: circulate their courses, know their measures, needle the five zang organs within and the six fu organs without. Carefully examine the wei-defensive qi, for it is the mother of the hundred illnesses. Regulate deficiency and excess, and deficiency and excess will cease. Drain the blood collaterals, and when the blood is exhausted there is no danger."

## Notes

This is nearly the same as the passage Leigong quotes as "the words of *Prohibited Vessels*" at the beginning of chapter 10 ("the principle of all needling begins with the meridians; circulate their courses, fix their measures, order the five zang organs within and distinguish the six fu organs without").
