# 霊枢 禁服第四十八 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The principle of all needling begins with the channels: circulate their courses, know their measures, needle the five viscera within and the six bowels without. Carefully examine the defensive qi, for it is the mother of the hundred illnesses. Regulate depletion and repletion, and depletion and repletion will cease. Drain the blood network vessels, and when the blood is exhausted there is no danger."

## Notes

This is nearly the same as the passage Leigong quotes as "the words of *Prohibited Vessels*" at the beginning of chapter 10 ("the principle of all needling begins with the channels; circulate their courses, fix their measures, order the five viscera within and distinguish the six bowels without").
