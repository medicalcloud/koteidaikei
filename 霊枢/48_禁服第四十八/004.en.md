# 霊枢 禁服第四十八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor himself pronounced the invocation: "Today at noon, with lips smeared with blood, the method is transmitted. Whoever dares to betray these words shall in turn suffer calamity."
