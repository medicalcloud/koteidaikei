# 霊枢 禁服第四十八 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "A good question! This is what the former masters prohibited; transmitting it privately is a crime; it is [passed on with] a covenant of cutting the arm and smearing the lips with blood. If you wish to obtain it, why not fast and purify yourself?"

## Notes

㰱血 of the base is read as 歃血 "smearing the lips with blood", as other sources have it (see collation).
