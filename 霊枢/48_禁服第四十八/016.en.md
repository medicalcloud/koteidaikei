# 霊枢 禁服第四十八 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Only when one has mastered the nutritive [vessels] and transport points can the great numbers be transmitted. The great numbers say: when abundant, simply drain; when depleted, simply supplement; when tight, apply moxibustion and needling and also give medicine; when sunken, simply apply moxibustion; when neither abundant nor depleted, take it at the channel. What is called treating by the channel means giving medicine, and is also said to be moxibustion and needling. When the pulse is tense, use guiding exercises; when the pulse is large and weak, keep [the patient] calm and still, and even when exerting, do not let them tire."

## Notes

The Yellow Emperor's speech ends here, concluding the chapter. The final 歃 is the headword of a sound gloss; the interlinear note (pronunciation) is not translated (original text only).
- 大数 could be a book title, but here it is taken as "the great essentials". Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
