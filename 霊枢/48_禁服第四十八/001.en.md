# 霊枢 禁服第四十八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong asked the Yellow Emperor, "This humble one has received the teachings and studied the sixty chapters on the nine needles, working at them morning and evening, so that the newer ones have broken bindings and the older ones have soiled bamboo strips; yet though I keep reciting them without pause, I have not fully grasped their meaning. *Inferring from the Outside* speaks of binding all into one, but I do not know what it means. What is so large that it has no outside and so small that it has no inside, with no limit to its size and no measure to its height—how can it be bound? Scholars differ in talent; with narrow and shallow understanding they cannot be broad and profound, but strive at learning as this humble one does. I fear it will scatter in later generations and be lost to descendants. May I ask how to make it concise?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. Leigong asks and the Yellow Emperor answers. As a method of making the way of the needle concise, he transmits diagnosis and treatment by the pulses at Renying and the inch opening.
- 外揣 is the same as the title of chapter 45 and is taken as referring to it.
