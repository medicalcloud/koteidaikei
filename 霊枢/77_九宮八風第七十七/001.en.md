# 霊枢 九宮八風第七十七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

[The nine palaces:] Beginning of Autumn, two; Autumn Equinox, seven; Beginning of Winter, six; Summer Solstice, nine; Zhaoyao, the center; Winter Solstice, one; Beginning of Summer, four; Spring Equinox, three; Beginning of Spring, eight. Taiyi always, from the day of the winter solstice, resides in the palace of Xiezhe for forty-six days; the next day it resides in Tianliu for forty-six days; the next day in Cangmen for forty-six days; the next day in Yinluo for forty-five days; the next day in Tiangong for forty-six days; the next day in Xuanwei for forty-six days; the next day in Cangguo for forty-six days; the next day in Xinluo for forty-five days; the next day it again resides in the palace of Xiezhe, and this is called the winter solstice. In Taiyi's daily wandering, starting from residence in Xiezhe on the day of the winter solstice, count the days of its location: from the first place to the ninth day, it returns to the first. It is always so without end, finishing and beginning again.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, not in dialogue form. It describes Taiyi's yearly circuit through the nine palaces and the good or ill fortune and illnesses brought by winds from the eight directions.
- The opening 立秋二……立春八 renders the diagram of the nine palaces in text; the interlinear notes give each palace's name and direction: Beginning of Autumn two is Xuanwei (southwest), Autumn Equinox seven Cangguo (west), Beginning of Winter six Xinluo (northwest), Summer Solstice nine Shangtian (south), Winter Solstice one Xiezhe (north), Beginning of Summer four Yinluo (southeast), Spring Equinox three Cangmen (east), Beginning of Spring eight Tianliu (northeast); Zhaoyao is the center.
- Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: Taiyi is the North Star; the pole stays unmoving in the center while the Dipper revolves outside; the Dipper's handle points in turn to the twelve branches to set the seasons, under the pole's command. Xiezhe is the *kan* palace, Tianliu *gen*, Cangmen *zhen*, Yinluo *xun*, Tiangong *li*, Xuanwei *kun*, Cangguo *dui*, Xinluo *qian* (*Leijing*).
- The *Leijing* note: dividing the days of the year among the eight palaces gives forty-six days each, except *qian* and *xun*, which have forty-five, for a total of 366 days.
- 従一処至九日，復反於一: Taiyi begins at *kan* and ends at *qian*—the days of the eight palaces; when eight are exhausted and it comes to nine, it returns to one and cycles endlessly. The *Hetu* has nine palaces but only eight are resided in here, because the central palace is Taiyi's own and oversees the eight (*Leijing*).
