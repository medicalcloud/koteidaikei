# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "When the body is struck by wind, the zang organs are not necessarily disturbed. So when a pathogen enters a yin meridian and the qi of that zang organ is in excess, the pathogenic qi enters but cannot lodge, and returns to the bowels. Hence striking the yang, it flows to the meridians; striking the yin, it flows to the bowels."
