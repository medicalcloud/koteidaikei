# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "When the body is struck by wind, the viscera are not necessarily disturbed. So when a pathogen enters a yin channel and the qi of that viscus is replete, the pathogenic qi enters but cannot lodge, and returns to the bowels. Hence striking the yang, it flows to the channels; striking the yin, it flows to the bowels."
