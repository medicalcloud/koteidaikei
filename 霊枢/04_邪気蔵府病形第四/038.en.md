# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "All tense [pulses] mean much cold; slack ones much heat; large ones much qi and little blood; small ones little blood and qi; slippery ones abundant yang qi with slight heat; rough ones much blood and little qi with slight cold. Therefore, needling the tense, insert deep and retain long. Needling the slack, insert shallow and withdraw the needle quickly to remove the heat. Needling the large, drain the qi slightly and do not let out blood. Needling the slippery, withdraw the needle quickly after inserting shallow, to drain the yang qi and remove the heat. Needling the rough, be sure to hit the vessel, retain long following its counterflow or compliance, always first pressing and tracing it; after withdrawing the needle, quickly press the needle hole and do not let blood out, so as to harmonize the vessel. All the small have both yin and yang, form and qi insufficient; do not take them with the needle, but regulate them with sweet medicines."
