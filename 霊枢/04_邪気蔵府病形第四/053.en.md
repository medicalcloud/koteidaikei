# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §53 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In illness of the small intestine, the lower abdomen is painful, the lower back and spine pull at the testicles with pain, at times there is urgency to defecate, and there is heat in front of the ears, or severe cold, or severe heat only on the shoulders, and heat between the little finger and the ring finger, or the vessels are sunken. These are its signs. It is an illness of hand greater yang; take Juxu Xialian.
