# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §50 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "Heat in the face is an illness of foot *Yangming*; blood in the collaterals of the fish [thenar eminence] is an illness of hand *Yangming*; when the vessels above both insteps stand up [hard] or are deep, it is an illness of foot *Yangming*; this is the stomach vessel.

## Notes

Qibo's speech continues through unit 56, describing the illnesses of each of the six fu organs and the points to take.
