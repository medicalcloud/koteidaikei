# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §50 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "Heat in the face is an illness of foot *yangming*; blood in the network vessels of the fish [thenar eminence] is an illness of hand *yangming*; when the vessels above both insteps stand up [hard] or are sunken, it is an illness of foot *yangming*; this is the stomach vessel.

## Notes

Qibo's speech continues through unit 56, describing the illnesses of each of the six bowels and the points to take.
