# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "How does pathogenic qi strike people?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter: a question about where pathogenic qi strikes and how it reaches the zang organs, bowels and meridians.
