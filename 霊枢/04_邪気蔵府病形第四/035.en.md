# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §35 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the spleen pulse is very tense, there is tugging and slackening; when slightly tense, diaphragm center [obstruction]: food and drink enter and come back out, afterwards with frothy spittle. When very slack, there is wilting with reversal; when slightly slack, wind wilting: the four limbs are useless, yet the mind is clear as if there were no illness. When very large, one falls as if struck; when slightly large, *shan* qi with much pus and blood inside the abdomen, outside the intestines and stomach. When very small, there is cold and heat; when slightly small, wasting and thirst. When very slippery, there is *tui* with urinary block; when slightly slippery, worm poison, roundworms, and heat in the abdomen. When very rough, there is intestinal *tui*; when slightly rough, internal *tui* with much discharge of pus and blood.

## Notes

Illnesses of the spleen pulse.
- Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* says 㿉 is the same as 𤻊 and means *shan* (swelling of the scrotum). 㿉癃 is taken as *tui shan* with urinary block, and 腸㿉, 内㿉 as *tui* affecting the intestines and the interior.
- The base's 「無病太甚為撃」 is emended to 「無病大甚為撃」. Wikisource (both) and Daizhige read 大甚; 大 is adopted as in the heart section.
