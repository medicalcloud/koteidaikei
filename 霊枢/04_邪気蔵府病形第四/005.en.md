# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Yin and yang have different names but are of the same kind; they meet above and below, and the channels and network vessels run through one another like a ring without end. When pathogens strike people, they strike the yin or the yang, above or below, left or right, with no constancy. Why is this?"
