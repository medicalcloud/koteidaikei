# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "When the pulse is tense, the skin of the chi is also tense; when the pulse is slack, the skin of the chi is also slack; when the pulse is small, the skin of the chi is also reduced and short of qi; when the pulse is large, the skin of the chi is also swollen and raised; when the pulse is slippery, the skin of the chi is also slippery; when the pulse is hesitant, the skin of the chi is also hesitant. All these changes may be slight or severe. Hence one who examines the chi well need not wait for the cun [pulse], and one who examines the pulse well need not wait for the complexion. One who can combine them in practice may be a superior practitioner; the superior practitioner cures nine in ten. One who practices two is a middling practitioner; the middling practitioner cures seven in ten. One who practices one is an inferior practitioner; the inferior practitioner cures six in ten."
