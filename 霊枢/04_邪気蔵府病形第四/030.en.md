# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "When the pulse is tense, the skin of the cubit is also tense; when the pulse is slack, the skin of the cubit is also slack; when the pulse is small, the skin of the cubit is also reduced and short of qi; when the pulse is large, the skin of the cubit is also swollen and raised; when the pulse is slippery, the skin of the cubit is also slippery; when the pulse is rough, the skin of the cubit is also rough. All these changes may be slight or severe. Hence one who examines the cubit well need not wait for the inch [pulse], and one who examines the pulse well need not wait for the complexion. One who can combine them in practice may be a superior practitioner; the superior practitioner cures nine in ten. One who practices two is a middling practitioner; the middling practitioner cures seven in ten. One who practices one is an inferior practitioner; the inferior practitioner cures six in ten."
