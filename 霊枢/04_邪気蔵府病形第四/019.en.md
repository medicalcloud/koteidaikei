# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When a deficiency pathogen strikes the body, there are shivers that shake the form. When a proper pathogen strikes people, it is subtle: it first appears in the complexion and is not felt in the body; as if present, as if absent, as if perishing, as if surviving, with form and without form, no one knows its nature."
