# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "Striking the yin always begins at the forearms and shins. The forearms and shins have thin skin on their yin [inner] side and soft, moist flesh; so although both receive the wind, only their yin is harmed."
