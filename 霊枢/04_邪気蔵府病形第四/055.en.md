# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §55 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In illness of the bladder, the lower abdomen is swollen on one side and painful; when pressed with the hand, one immediately wishes to urinate but cannot; there is heat on the shoulders, or the vessels are sunken, and heat along the outer edge of the little toe and behind the shin and ankle, or the vessels are sunken. Take the center of Wei [Weizhong].
