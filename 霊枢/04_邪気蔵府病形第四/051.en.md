# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §51 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In illness of the large intestine, there is cutting pain in the intestines with gurgling; on winter days, if one is again affected by cold, there is immediate diarrhea, pain at the navel, and one cannot stand for long. It shares its signs with the stomach; take Juxu Shanglian.
