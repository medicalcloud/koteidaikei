# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The complexion, the pulse and the cubit [skin] correspond to one another as drumstick and drum, shadow and echo; they cannot fail one another. This too is the manifestation of root and branch, root and leaf; so when the root dies, the leaves wither. The complexion, the pulse and the form and flesh cannot fail one another. Hence knowing one makes one skilled, knowing two makes one spirit-like, and knowing three makes one both spirit-like and clear."
