# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §54 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In illness of the triple burner, there is qi fullness in the abdomen, the lower abdomen is especially hard, one cannot urinate and feels urgency; when it overflows, water is retained and there is distension. Its signs are in the great network vessel outside foot greater yang; the great network vessel lies between the greater yang and lesser yang, and [the illness] also shows in the vessel. Take Weiyang.
