# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §56 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In illness of the gallbladder, there is frequent sighing, a bitter taste in the mouth, vomiting of old juices, palpitations below the heart as if fearing that someone is about to seize one, a feeling of obstruction in the throat, and frequent spitting. [Look] at the root and branch of foot lesser yang, and also observe where its vessels are deep and apply moxibustion there. For cold and heat, take Yanglingquan."

## Notes

Illness of the gallbladder; Qibo's speech ends here.
