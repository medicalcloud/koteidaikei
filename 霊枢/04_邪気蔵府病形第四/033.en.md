# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the lung pulse is very tense, there is epilepsy; when slightly tense, cold and heat of the lungs, lassitude, coughing and spitting blood pulling into the lower back, back and chest, or nasal polyps blocking the nose. When very slack, there is much sweating; when slightly slack, wilting and fistulas, hemilateral wind, and sweating from the head downward that cannot be stopped. When very large, the shins are swollen; when slightly large, lung impediment pulling into the chest and back, and on rising an aversion to sunlight. When very small, there is diarrhea; when slightly small, wasting and thirst. When very slippery, there is rushing breath with rising qi; when slightly slippery, bleeding above and below. When very rough, there is vomiting of blood; when slightly rough, mouse fistulas between the neck and armpit, the lower part cannot bear the upper, and correspondingly there is frequent aching.

## Notes

Illnesses of the lung pulse.
- The base's 「可止太甚為脛」 is emended to 「可止大甚為脛」. Wikisource (both) and Daizhige read 大甚; 大 is adopted as in the heart section.
