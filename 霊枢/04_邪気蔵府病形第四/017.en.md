# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The blood and qi of the twelve channels and the three hundred sixty-five network vessels all rise to the face and run to the openings. Their essential yang qi rises to the eyes and becomes the pupils; their separate qi goes to the ears and becomes hearing; their ancestral qi rises out through the nose and becomes smell; their turbid qi comes out of the stomach and goes to the lips and tongue and becomes taste. The fluids of the qi all rise to steam the face; moreover its skin is thick and its flesh firm. So even when the weather is very cold, it cannot overcome it."

## Notes

For 気 in 天気甚寒, some sources have 熱 (see collation); the base is followed.
