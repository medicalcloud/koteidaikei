# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "All the yang [channels] meet in the face. [Pathogens] strike people just when they take advantage of a time of depletion, or right after exertion, or when one has eaten and drunk and is sweating with the interstices open; then one is struck by the pathogen. Struck in the face, it descends the *yangming*; struck at the nape, it descends the greater yang; struck at the cheek, it descends the lesser yang. When struck at the chest, back or flanks, it likewise strikes the respective channel."
