# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Only when yin and yang are both affected can the pathogen go there."

## Notes

That is, the pathogen enters the zang organs only when the inside (the zang organs) is already harmed and a pathogen is also contracted from outside.
