# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I have heard that for the qi of the five zang organs and six fu organs, [after] the brook and stream points, where it enters is the uniting point. By which path does it enter, and where does it connect and pass after entering? I wish to hear the reason."
