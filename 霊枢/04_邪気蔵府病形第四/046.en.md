# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The stomach unites at Sanli; the large intestine unites and enters at Juxu Shanglian; the small intestine unites and enters at Juxu Xialian; the sanjiao unites and enters at Weiyang; the bladder unites and enters at the center of Wei [Weizhong]; the gallbladder unites and enters at Yanglingquan."

## Notes

The lower uniting points of the six fu organs. 委中央 is Weizhong.
