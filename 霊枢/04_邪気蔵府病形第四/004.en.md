# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Above the middle of the body, pathogens strike it; below the middle of the body, dampness strikes it. Hence it is said: pathogens strike people without any constancy; striking the yin, they flow to the bowels; striking the yang, they flow to the meridians."

## Notes

Pathogens (such as wind) strike the upper half of the body, dampness the lower half.
