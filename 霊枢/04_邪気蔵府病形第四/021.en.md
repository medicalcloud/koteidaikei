# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I have heard that knowing the illness by looking at the complexion is called clarity; knowing the illness by pressing the pulse is called spirit; knowing its place by asking about the illness is called skill. I wish to hear how to know by looking, obtain by pressing, and get to the bottom by asking. How is it done?"
