# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §34 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the liver pulse is very tense, there is foul speech; when slightly tense, fat qi below the flanks like an overturned cup. When very slack, there is frequent vomiting; when slightly slack, water conglomeration impediment. When very large, there is internal abscess with frequent vomiting and nosebleeds; when slightly large, liver impediment with retracted genitals and coughing pulling into the lower abdomen. When very small, there is much drinking; when slightly small, wasting and thirst. When very slippery, there is *tui shan* [swelling of the scrotum]; when slightly slippery, enuresis. When very rough, there is subcutaneous fluid retention; when slightly rough, spasms and sinew impediment.

## Notes

Illnesses of the liver pulse.
- The base's 「痺也太甚為内」 is emended to 「痺也大甚為内」. Wikisource (both) and Daizhige read 大甚; 大 is adopted as in the heart section.
