# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §52 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In illness of the stomach, the abdomen is distended, the stomach duct below the heart is painful, [the pain] props up both flanks, the diaphragm and throat are blocked, and food and drink do not go down. Take Sanli.
