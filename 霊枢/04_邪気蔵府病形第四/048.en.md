# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §48 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "To take Sanli, lower the instep. To take Juxu, raise the foot. To take Weiyang, bend and stretch and search for it. Weizhong is taken by bending. Yanglingquan is taken squatting upright with both knees level, and going down to the yang [outer] side of Weiyang. To take the outer meridians, draw them out and make them clear before following them."

## Notes

Postures for taking the lower uniting points.
- 正竪膝予之斉 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* ("squat with the body upright and the knees level"); 揄申 follows the *Leijing* ("揄 is to draw, 申 is to make clear"). The *Lingshu jizhu* glosses 揄 as "draw, draw out". The *Leijing* also takes 委陽之陽 as an error for 委中之陽.
