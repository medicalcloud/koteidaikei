# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §48 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "To take Sanli, lower the instep. To take Juxu, raise the foot. To take Weiyang, bend and stretch and search for it. Weizhong is taken by bending. Yanglingquan is taken by holding the knees straight upright and level, and going down to the yang [outer] side of Weiyang. To take the outer channels, stretch and follow them."

## Notes

Postures for taking the lower uniting points.
- The readings of 予之斉 and 揄申 are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points). 斉 is provisionally taken as leveling the knees, and 揄申 as stretching [the limbs].
