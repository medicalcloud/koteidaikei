# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "The head and face and the body are attached to the same bones, connected by the same sinews, share the same blood and are joined in the same qi. When the weather is cold, the ground cracks and ice forms; in sudden cold the hands and feet may become sluggish. Yet the face needs no clothing. Why?"
