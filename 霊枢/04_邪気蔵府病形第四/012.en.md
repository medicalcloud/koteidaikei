# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Sorrow, worry, fear and dread harm the heart. A chilled body and cold drinks harm the lungs: the two colds affect each other, the inside and outside are both harmed, and so the qi counterflows upward. When one has fallen, and malign blood stays inside, or when one has been greatly angered and the qi rises without descending and accumulates below the flanks, the liver is harmed. When one has been struck or has fallen down, or has entered the bedchamber drunk, or sweated and been exposed to wind, the spleen is harmed. When one has exerted oneself lifting heavy things, or entered the bedchamber to excess, or sweated and bathed in water, the kidneys are harmed."

## Notes

- The base's 「気道而上行」 is emended to 「気逆而上行」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige and the *Lingshu jizhu* read 逆 ("the qi counterflows upward"), which fits the sense; 逆 is adopted.
