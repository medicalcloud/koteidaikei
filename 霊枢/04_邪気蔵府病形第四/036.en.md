# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §36 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the kidney pulse is very tense, there is bone epilepsy; when slightly tense, sinking reversal and running piglet, the feet cannot be drawn in, and there is no passage front or back. When very slack, the spine feels broken; when slightly slack, there is *dong* [throughflux]: food is not transformed and comes back out after going down the throat. When very large, there is impotence; when slightly large, stony edema, which from below the navel to the lower abdomen hangs heavily and, when it rises to the stomach duct, means death without cure. When very small, there is throughflux diarrhea; when slightly small, wasting and thirst. When very slippery, there is urinary block with *tui*; when slightly slippery, bone wilting: when seated one cannot rise, and on rising one cannot see. When very rough, there is a great abscess; when slightly rough, absence of menses and deep hemorrhoids."

## Notes

Illnesses of the kidney pulse; Qibo's speech ends here.
- 腄腄然 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* ("heavy and sagging").
- The base's 「上至胃腕」 is emended to 「上至胃脘」. Daizhige, the *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 胃脘 (the *Leijing* has both forms); the stomach duct is meant, so 脘 is adopted.
- The base's 「還出太甚為陰」 is emended to 「還出大甚為陰」. Wikisource (both) and Daizhige read 大甚; 大 is adopted as in the heart section.
