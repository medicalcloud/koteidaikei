# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Let your servant speak of the illness changes of the five viscera. When the heart pulse is very tense, there is tugging and slackening; when slightly tense, heart pain pulling into the back and food does not go down. When very slack, there is mad laughter; when slightly slack, there is a hidden beam below the heart, moving up and down, with occasional spitting of blood. When very large, there is obstruction in the throat; when slightly large, heart impediment pulling into the back, with frequent tearing. When very small, there is frequent hiccup; when slightly small, wasting thirst. When very slippery, there is frequent thirst; when slightly slippery, heart *shan* pulling into the navel and rumbling in the lower abdomen. When very rough, there is loss of voice; when slightly rough, overflowing of blood, *wei* reversal, ringing in the ears and illness of the vertex.

## Notes

Qibo's speech continues through unit 36. For each of the five viscera he lists the illnesses of very and slightly tense, slack, large, small, slippery and rough pulses.
- 太 in 太甚 of the base appears as 大 in other sources (see collation); from context it is read as 大甚 "very large". The same applies to the following units.
- 喉吤 is provisionally translated as obstruction in the throat. This and the meaning of 維厥 (reversal of the four limbs, among other views) are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
