# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Let your servant speak of the illness changes of the five zang organs. When the heart pulse is very tense, there is tugging and slackening; when slightly tense, heart pain pulling into the back and food does not go down. When very slack, there is mad laughter; when slightly slack, there is a hidden beam below the heart, moving up and down, with occasional spitting of blood. When very large, there is a rasping sound in the throat; when slightly large, heart impediment pulling into the back, with frequent tearing. When very small, there is frequent hiccup; when slightly small, wasting-thirst. When very slippery, there is frequent thirst; when slightly slippery, heart *shan* pulling into the navel and rumbling in the lower abdomen. When very rough, there is loss of voice; when slightly rough, overflowing of blood, *wei* reversal [cold four limbs], ringing in the ears and illness of the vertex.

## Notes

Qibo's speech continues through unit 36. For each of the five zang organs he lists the illnesses of very and slightly tense, slack, large, small, slippery and hesitant pulses.
- 喉吤 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* ("a rasping sound in the throat") and Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* ("the qi rises and strikes the throat, which rasps"). 維厥 follows the *Leijing* ("cold four limbs"); Yang Shangshan takes it as reversal of the yang linking vessel.
- The base's 「唾血太甚為喉」 is emended to 「唾血大甚為喉」. Wikisource (both), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 大甚 "very large", one of the six qualities tense, slack, large, small, slippery, hesitant; 大 is adopted here and in the following four zang organs.
