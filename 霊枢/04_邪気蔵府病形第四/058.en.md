# 霊枢 邪気蔵府病形第四 §58 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "In needling these, one must hit the qi points and not hit the flesh joints. Hitting the qi points, the needle roams in the lane; hitting the flesh joints, the skin is painful; reversing supplementing and draining makes the illness worse; hitting a sinew, the sinew becomes slack, the pathogenic qi does not come out but struggles with the true [qi], becoming disordered without leaving, and instead returns inward and lodges. Using the needle without care turns compliance into counterflow."

## Notes

The end of the chapter. The interlinear note is not translated (original text only).
- The base's 「鍼染于巷」 is emended to 「鍼遊于巷」. Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 遊/游, and the base's own interlinear note says "one edition has 遊"; only the *Leijing* agrees with the base (染). 遊 is adopted.
