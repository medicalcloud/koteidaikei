# 霊枢 五味第五十六 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I wish to hear how the five flavors of the grain qi are distinguished as they enter the five zang organs."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, describing how the five flavors enter their zang organs, the flavors of the five grains, fruits, domestic animals and vegetables, and the foods suitable and forbidden for each illness.
