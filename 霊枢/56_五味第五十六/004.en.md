# 霊枢 五味第五十六 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "When grain first enters the stomach, its essential subtleties first go out from the stomach to the two burners [upper and lower] to irrigate the five viscera, and separately go out along two courses, the paths of the nutritive and the defensive. The great qi that gathers without moving accumulates in the chest and is called the sea of qi; it comes out at the lungs and follows the throat, so it goes out with exhalation and comes in with inhalation. Of the essential qi of heaven and earth, in general three parts always go out for one that comes in; hence if no grain enters, in half a day the qi declines, and in one day the qi is scant."

## Notes

The nutritive and defensive and the sea of qi.
- 両焦 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: the essential qi of grain first comes out of the stomach (the middle burner) and then reaches the upper and lower burners to irrigate the five viscera. 両行: the clear enters the nutritive and moves inside the vessels, the turbid enters the defensive and moves outside (*Leijing*).
- 出三入一 follows Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu*: with exhalation three parts of the grain's essential qi go out, with inhalation one part returns, so food must replenish it.
