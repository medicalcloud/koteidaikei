# 霊枢 五味第五十六 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "Let me tell you all. The five grains: non-glutinous rice is sweet, hemp sour, soybeans salty, wheat bitter, yellow millet acrid. The five fruits: jujube sweet, plum sour, chestnut salty, apricot bitter, peach acrid. The five domestic animals: beef sweet, dog sour, pork salty, mutton bitter, chicken acrid. The five vegetables: mallow sweet, chives sour, bean leaves salty, shallot bitter, scallion acrid. The five colors: yellow suits the sweet, green the sour, black the salty, red the bitter, white the acrid. Each of these five has what suits it.

## Notes

The flavors of the five grains, fruits, domestic animals and vegetables, and the five colors. Bogao's speech continues through unit 7.
