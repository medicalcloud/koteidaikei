# 霊枢 五味第五十六 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "The stomach is the sea of the five viscera and six bowels; water and grain all enter the stomach, and the five viscera and six bowels all receive qi from the stomach. Each of the five flavors goes to what it favors: sour grain goes first to the liver, bitter to the heart, sweet to the spleen, acrid to the lungs, salty to the kidneys. When the grain qi and fluids have moved and the nutritive and defensive flow freely, [the rest] is transformed into dregs and passed down in order."
