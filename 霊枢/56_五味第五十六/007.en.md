# 霊枢 五味第五十六 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The five suitabilities: as for the five colors, in spleen illness it is suitable to eat non-glutinous rice, beef, jujube and mallow; in heart illness, wheat, mutton, apricot and shallot; in kidney illness, soybean sprouts, pork, chestnut and bean leaves; in liver illness, hemp, dog meat, plum and chives; in lung illness, yellow millet, chicken, peach and scallion. The five prohibitions: liver illness forbids the acrid, heart illness the salty, spleen illness the sour, kidney illness the sweet, lung illness the bitter. The color of the liver is green; it is suitable to eat the sweet: rice, beef, jujube and mallow are all sweet. The color of the heart is red; it is suitable to eat the sour: dog meat, hemp, plum and chives are all sour. The color of the spleen is yellow; it is suitable to eat the salty: soybeans, pork, chestnut and bean leaves are all salty. The color of the lungs is white; it is suitable to eat the bitter: wheat, mutton, apricot and shallot are all bitter. The color of the kidneys is black; it is suitable to eat the acrid: yellow millet, chicken, peach and scallion are all acrid."

## Notes

The five suitabilities and prohibitions, and the flavors suited to the colors of the five viscera. Bogao's speech ends here, concluding the chapter.
- 大豆黄巻 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "soybean sprouts".
