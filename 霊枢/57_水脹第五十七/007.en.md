# 霊枢 水脹第五十七 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Cold qi lodges outside the intestines and struggles with the defensive qi; the qi cannot nourish, so something becomes attached, forms a lump that adheres inside, malign qi arises and polyps grow. When it first arises it is the size of a hen's egg; it gradually grows until, when formed, it looks like pregnancy; long-standing ones last for years. Pressed, it is firm; pushed, it moves; menstruation comes at the proper time. These are its signs."

## Notes

The signs of intestinal mushroom.
- 離歳 follows Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu*: "離 means to pass through; long-standing ones last through the years".
