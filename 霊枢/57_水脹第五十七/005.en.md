# 霊枢 水脹第五十七 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The abdomen is distended and the whole body large, as large as in skin distension, but the complexion is green-yellow and the abdominal sinews [vessels] stand out. These are its signs."

## Notes

The signs of drum distension. For 腹脹, the Siku edition has 鼓脹, but the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* agree with the base, which is kept.
