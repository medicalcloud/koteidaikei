# 霊枢 水脹第五十七 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "When water first arises, there is slight swelling above the eye sockets, as if one had just got up from sleep; the neck vessels pulse, there is occasional cough, coldness between the inner thighs, swelling of the feet and shins, and the abdomen enlarges; then the water has formed. When the abdomen is pressed with the hand, it rises back following the hand, like a bag of water. These are its signs."
