# 霊枢 水脹第五十七 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In skin distension, cold qi lodges in the skin; [tapped,] it sounds hollow like a drum and is not firm; the abdomen is large, the whole body swollen, the skin thick; when the abdomen is pressed, it stays deeply indented without rising, and the color of the abdomen does not change. These are its signs." [The Yellow Emperor said,] "What about drum distension?"

## Notes

The signs of skin distension.
- 𪔣𪔣然 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "the sound of a drum; cold qi lodges in the skin, the yang qi does not move and the illness is in the qi aspect, hence a drumlike sound" (Daizhige has 鼕鼕然). 窅 means "deep" (*Leijing*).
- In the base there is no 黄帝曰 before the question 鼓脹何如; "[The Yellow Emperor said,]" is supplied. The same applies to units 6 and 8.
