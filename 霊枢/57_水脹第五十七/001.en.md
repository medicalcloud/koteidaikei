# 霊枢 水脹第五十七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "How are water [swelling], skin distension, drum distension, intestinal mushroom, stone conglomeration and stony edema distinguished?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, describing how to distinguish several illnesses resembling water swelling.
