# 霊枢 水脹第五十七 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Stone conglomeration arises in the uterus. Cold qi lodges at the uterine gate, the gate is blocked, the qi cannot pass, the malign blood that should be discharged is not, and the clotted blood stays and grows daily, looking like pregnancy; menstruation does not come at the proper time. It arises only in women and can be guided down and discharged."

## Notes

The signs of stone conglomeration.
- 衃 is "congealed, spoiled blood" (Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*). The *Leijing* also notes that the question about stony edema at the beginning of the chapter has no answer, so text appears to be missing.
