# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the upper part of the hand lesser yang, when blood and qi are abundant the eyebrows are fine and long and the ears of good color; when both are scarce the ears are scorched and of poor color. In the lower part of the hand lesser yang, when blood and qi are abundant the fist is fleshy and warm; when both are scarce it is cold and thin; with little qi and much blood it is thin with many [visible] vessels.
