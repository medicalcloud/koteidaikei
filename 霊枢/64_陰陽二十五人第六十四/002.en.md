# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, 'Within heaven and earth and the six directions, nothing departs from five, and the human being corresponds to this.' Hence five times five makes the division into twenty-five persons, and the yin and yang persons are not included in it; that there are five whose dispositions do not accord with the multitude, I already know. I wish to hear about the forms of the twenty-five persons and where their blood and qi arise, so as to distinguish them and examine them, knowing the inside from the outside. How is it?"

## Notes

Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: this passage quotes Shaoshi's answer in the earlier *Tongtian* chapter, yet says Bogao; perhaps Shaoshi was another name for Bogao—there is no way to verify. Accordingly 伯高曰 is translated as the Yellow Emperor quoting.
- 二十五人之政……其態又不合于衆者五 follows the same note: within each of the five phases there are again five, divided into left and right, upper and lower; five times five gives twenty-five persons. This is a detailed division by the five phases, unlike the five dispositions of the *Tongtian* chapter, hence the yin and yang persons are not included.
