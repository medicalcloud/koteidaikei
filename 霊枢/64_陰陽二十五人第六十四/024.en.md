# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Fine eyebrows mean much qi and blood in the foot greater yang vessel; poor eyebrows mean little. Those who are plump and glossy have a surplus of blood and qi; plump but not glossy, a surplus of qi and insufficiency of blood; thin and without gloss, insufficiency of both. Examine carefully the surplus and insufficiency of the form and qi and regulate them, and one can know what is counterflow and what is compliant."
