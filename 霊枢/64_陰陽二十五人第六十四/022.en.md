# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the upper part of the hand greater yang, when blood and qi are abundant there is much beard and the face is fleshy and smooth; when both are scarce the face is thin and of poor color. In the lower part of the hand greater yang, when blood and qi are abundant the flesh of the palm is full; when both are scarce the palm is thin and cold."
