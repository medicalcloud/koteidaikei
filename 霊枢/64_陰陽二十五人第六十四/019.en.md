# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the upper part of the foot greater yang, when blood and qi are abundant the eyebrows are fine, with long hairs in them; with much blood and little qi the eyebrows are poor and the face has many fine lines; with little blood and much qi the face is fleshy; when blood and qi are harmonious the complexion is fine. In the lower part of the foot greater yang, when blood and qi are abundant the flesh of the heel is full and the heel firm; with little qi and much blood the heel is thin and hollow; when both are scarce there tend to be cramps and pain under the heel.

## Notes

少理 is translated as fine lines, following the *Lingshu jizhu*'s remark that 少理 should be 小理.
- The base's 足太陰之下 is emended to 足太陽之下. The *Leijing*, the *Lingshu jizhu*, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text read 足太陽之下 (the *Jiayi jing* 足太陰). The preceding phrase is 足太陽之上, and the heel lies on the course of the foot greater yang vessel (*Leijing* note), so 太陽 is adopted.
