# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Be careful, be careful! Let me tell you. The wood-form person belongs to the upper *jue* [note] and resembles the Azure Emperor. In makeup he is azure in color, with a small head, long face, large shoulders and back, straight body, and small hands and feet; he likes to have talent, labors his mind, has little strength, worries much and toils at his affairs. He endures spring and summer but not autumn and winter; when affected [then] illness arises. [He belongs to] the foot reverting yin, soft and heavy [佗佗然]. The great-*jue* person belongs to the left foot lesser yang, the upper part of the lesser yang, yielding and retiring [遺遺然]. The left-*jue* person belongs to the right foot lesser yang, the lower part, compliant [随随然]. The *dai*-*jue* person belongs to the right foot lesser yang, the upper part, pressing forward [推推然]. The half-*jue* person belongs to the left foot lesser yang, the lower part, square and upright [栝栝然].

## Notes

The wood-form person and its four partial types. Qibo's speech continues through unit 11.
- 比 is taken as "belong to" and 能 as "endure", following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*. The meanings given in brackets for the reduplicated descriptive words all follow the *Leijing* notes (likewise in the following units).
- The *Leijing* note takes the upper *jue* (foot reverting yin) as the complete wood qi, and the great, left, *dai* and half *jue* as partial types assigned to the upper and lower parts of left and right. The upper and lower parts correspond to the descriptions from unit 16 on (足少陽之上, 足少陽之下, etc.).
- 釱 also means "great" (*Leijing*); 判 means "half" (*Leijing*).
- The interlinear notes say the left *jue* is "also called the lesser *jue*" and the *dai jue* "also called the right *jue*".
