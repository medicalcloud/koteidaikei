# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I have heard what the yin and yang persons [the five dispositions: greater yin, lesser yin, greater yang, lesser yang, and balanced] are like.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, describing the twenty-five persons by the forms of the five phases—their build, temperament and illnesses—the outward signs of abundance and scarcity of blood and qi, and the general rule of needling. The Yellow Emperor's speech continues into unit 2.
- 余聞陰陽之人何如 is taken as referring to what he heard about the five yin–yang dispositions in the *Tongtian* chapter (ch. 72), following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* that the next words, introduced by 伯高曰, quote Shaoshi's answer in that chapter.
