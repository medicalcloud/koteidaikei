# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "First establish the five forms—metal, wood, water, fire, earth—distinguish their five colors, and differentiate the persons of the five forms; then the twenty-five persons are complete."
