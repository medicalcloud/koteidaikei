# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the upper part of the hand *Yangming*, when blood and qi are abundant the mustache is fine; with little blood and much qi it is poor; when both are scarce there is none. In the lower part of the hand *Yangming*, when blood and qi are abundant the armpit hair is fine and the flesh of the thenar eminence is warm; when both are scarce the hands are thin and cold.

## Notes

*Leijing* notes: hair above the mouth is 髭, below it 鬚; the fish flesh of the hand is the thick flesh behind the base joint of the thumb.
