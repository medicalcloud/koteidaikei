# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the upper part of the foot lesser yang, when qi and blood are abundant the continuous whiskers [cheek and chin] are fine and long; with much blood and little qi they are fine but short; with little blood and much qi the whiskers are sparse; when both are scarce there is no beard, and when affected by cold and damp there tends to be impediment, bone pain and withered nails. In the lower part of the foot lesser yang, when blood and qi are abundant the shin hair is fine and long and the outer ankle plump; with much blood and little qi the shin hair is fine but short and the skin of the outer ankle firm and thick; with little blood and much qi the shin hair is sparse and the skin of the outer ankle thin and soft; when both are scarce there is no hair and the outer ankle is thin and fleshless.

## Notes

Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: the foot lesser yang vessel descends to the jaw, so its condition shows in the beard and whiskers; on the chin it is called 鬚, on the cheeks 髯. 通髯 is taken as whiskers continuous from cheek to chin (the translator's interpretation).
