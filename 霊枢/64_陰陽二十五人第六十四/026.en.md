# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Press the inch opening and Renying to regulate yin and yang, and probe and examine where the channels and network vessels are congealed and rough, knotted and blocked. In the body these all become painful impediment; when severe, [blood and qi] do not move, hence the congealing and roughness. For congealing and roughness, bring the qi to warm it and stop when the blood is harmonized. For knotted network vessels, where the vessel is knotted and the blood does not move, open them and it will move. Hence it is said: when qi is in surplus above, lead it down; when qi is insufficient above, push [the needle] and let it rest [retaining it to await the qi]; when it lingers and does not arrive, go out to meet it. Only with a clear understanding of the channel pathways can one take hold of it. When cold and heat contend, lead them and make them move; where there is stagnant old [blood] but the blood is not knotted, treat it according to its tendency. One must first clearly know the twenty-five persons; then where the blood and qi lie, left and right, above and below—the rule of needling is complete."

## Notes

The general rule of needling; the end of the chapter.
- Follows the *Leijing* notes: 切 is "deeply", 循 "examine"; 推而休之 means needling the upper point, pushing the needle and letting it rest—retaining it to await the qi; 経隧 are the pathways of the channels, and only with a clear understanding of them can one take hold; in 則而予之, 則 means "follow the measure"—treat according to its tendency; 予 equals 与.
- The base's 血不和 is emended to 血不行. The *Leijing*, the *Jiayi jing*, the *Lingshu jizhu* and the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource read 血不行, which corresponds to 決之乃行 (the base's 和 is taken as influenced by the preceding 血和乃止).
- The base's 能待之 is emended to 能持之. The *Leijing*, the *Jiayi jing*, the *Lingshu jizhu*, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text all read 持, and the *Leijing* note glosses it as "take hold". The graphically similar 待 is taken as an error for 持.
