# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The earth-form person belongs to the upper *gong* and resembles the Yellow Emperor of high antiquity. In makeup he is yellow in color, with a round face, large head, fine shoulders and back, large belly, fine thighs and shins, small hands and feet and much flesh, upper and lower in proportion; he walks steadily on the ground, lifts his feet lightly, has a calm mind, likes to benefit others, does not delight in power, and readily attaches himself to others. He endures autumn and winter but not spring and summer; affected in spring and summer, illness arises. [He belongs to] the foot greater yin, solid and heavy [敦敦然]. The great-*gong* person belongs to the left foot *yangming*, the upper part, gently compliant [婉婉然]. The added-*gong* person belongs to the left foot *yangming*, the lower part, deep and firm [坎坎然]. The lesser-*gong* person belongs to the right foot *yangming*, the upper part, round and turning [枢枢然]. The left-*gong* person belongs to the right foot *yangming*, the lower part, standing alone unmoving [兀兀然].

## Notes

The earth-form person and its four types.
- The interlinear notes say the added *gong* is "also called *zhong zhi ren*", and the left *gong* "also called *zhong zhi ren*, also said to be the upper *yangming*".
- On the left *gong*, the *Leijing* note says that on examination it should be the right *gong*, hence it belongs to the lower right foot *yangming*. The base reading is kept.
