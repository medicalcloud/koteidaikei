# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The metal-form person belongs to the upper *shang* and resembles the White Emperor. In makeup he has a square face, white color, small head, small shoulders and back, small belly and small hands and feet, as if bone grew out at the outside of the heel; his bones are light, his person pure and incorrupt, his mind impatient, quiet but fierce [when roused]; he makes a good official. He endures autumn and winter but not spring and summer; affected in spring and summer, illness arises. [He belongs to] the hand greater yin, solid [敦敦然]. The *dai*-*shang* person belongs to the left hand *yangming*, the upper part, angular [廉廉然]. The right-*shang* person belongs to the left hand *yangming*, the lower part, free and easy [脱脱然]. The great-*shang* person belongs to the right hand *yangming*, the upper part, observant [監監然]. The lesser-*shang* person belongs to the right hand *yangming*, the lower part, grave [厳厳然].

## Notes

The metal-form person and its four types.
- 如骨発踵外 follows the *Leijing* note: the outside of the heel is firm, as if bone grew out there. 静悍 follows the *Leijing*: metal is quiet by nature but fierce when moved.
- On the positions of the right and great *shang*, the *Leijing* note says the right *shang* should be the right hand *yangming* and the great *shang* the left hand *yangming*. The texts also differ (the Siku edition on Wikisource reads 左商 for the second and 右商 for the third; the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text read 左商 for the third). Since the original form cannot be determined, the base reading is translated.
