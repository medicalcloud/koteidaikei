# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In the upper part of the foot *yangming*, when blood and qi are abundant the side whiskers are fine and long; with little blood and much qi they are short; with little qi and much blood they are sparse; when both are scarce there are none, and there are many lines at the corners of the mouth. In the lower part of the foot *yangming*, when blood and qi are abundant the lower hair is fine and long, reaching the chest; with much blood and little qi it is fine but short, reaching the navel; one tends to lift the feet high in walking, the toes have little flesh and the feet are often cold. With little blood and much qi the flesh tends to swell like chilblains; when both are scarce there is no hair, or if any, it is sparse and withered, and there is a tendency to wilting with reversal and impediment of the feet.

## Notes

The signs of abundance and scarcity of blood and qi in the upper and lower parts of the three yang of hand and foot. Qibo's speech continues through unit 22.
- Follows the *Leijing* notes: the foot *yangming* vessel circles the lips above, so its condition shows in the whiskers by the mouth; 吻 is the corner of the mouth and 画 lines; lifting the feet high is due to much blood, and little flesh on the toes and cold feet to little qi; 瘃 is cold swelling—with little blood and much qi it floats outward, so the flesh of the lower body swells.
