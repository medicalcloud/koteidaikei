# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In general, the year taboos of the upper and lower persons: the great taboo always begins at age seven and is added [every nine years]: sixteen, twenty-five, thirty-four, forty-three, fifty-two and sixty-one are all great taboos for people, and they must keep themselves at peace. If affected, illness follows; if careless, there is cause for worry. At these times do nothing improper. These are called the year taboos."

## Notes

Follows the *Leijing* note: the upper and lower persons are those of the five forms above; the year taboos begin at seven and are added every nine years, since seven is lesser yang and nine greater yang; yang numbers culminate at nine, and culmination brings change, hence every ninth year is a taboo year.
