# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor left his seat, drew back respectfully and said, "I have heard that to find the right person and not teach him is called a grave loss; to obtain it and let it leak out is something heaven will abhor. I wish to obtain it and make it clear, store it in a golden casket, and not dare to spread it."
