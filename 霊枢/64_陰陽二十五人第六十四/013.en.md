# 霊枢 陰陽二十五人第六十四 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the form overcomes the color or the color overcomes the form, then on reaching the season and year of that overcoming, if one is affected, illness follows; if one is careless, there is cause for worry. Those whose form and color match are wealthy, noble and very happy."

## Notes

Follows the *Leijing* note: form overcoming color is like a wood-form person showing yellow; color overcoming form is like a wood-form person showing white. At the time and year of overcoming—e.g. when wood is flourishing and earth declining, and the year is a *ding*/*ren* wood movement—the flourishing qi is added, and if one is affected one falls ill; if one is careless after falling ill, there is cause for worry.
