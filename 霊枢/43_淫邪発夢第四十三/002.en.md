# 霊枢 淫邪発夢第四十三 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When proper pathogens assail the inside from without and have not yet found a fixed lodging, they instead overflow into the zang organs; without a fixed place, they move together with the ying-nutrient and wei-defense and fly about with the ethereal and corporeal souls, so that one cannot rest when lying down and dreams often. When the qi overflows into the bowels, there is surplus outside and insufficiency inside; when the qi overflows into the zang organs, there is surplus inside and insufficiency outside."
