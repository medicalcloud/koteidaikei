# 霊枢 淫邪発夢第四十三 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When yin qi is abundant, one dreams of crossing great waters in fear. When yang qi is abundant, one dreams of great fires burning. When yin and yang are both abundant, one dreams of killing each other. When the upper is abundant, one dreams of flying; when the lower is abundant, one dreams of falling. When very hungry, one dreams of taking; when very full, one dreams of giving. When liver qi is abundant, one dreams of anger. When lung qi is abundant, one dreams of fear, weeping and flying. When heart qi is abundant, one dreams of much laughter and fear. When spleen qi is abundant, one dreams of singing and music, and of the body being heavy and unable to rise. When kidney qi is abundant, one dreams of the lower back and spine coming apart in two. For all these twelve abundances, drain when [the dream] arrives, and it ends at once.

## Notes

Twelve dreams due to abundance of qi. Qibo's speech continues through unit 5.
