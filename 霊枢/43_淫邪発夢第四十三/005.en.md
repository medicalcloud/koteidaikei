# 霊枢 淫邪発夢第四十三 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When reversal qi lodges in the heart, one dreams of hills, mountains, smoke and fire. Lodging in the lungs, one dreams of flying and seeing strange objects of gold and iron. Lodging in the liver, one dreams of mountain forests and trees. Lodging in the spleen, one dreams of hills, great marshes, ruined houses, wind and rain. Lodging in the kidneys, one dreams of standing at an abyss or being submerged in water. Lodging in the bladder, one dreams of wandering. Lodging in the stomach, one dreams of eating and drinking. Lodging in the large intestine, one dreams of fields. Lodging in the small intestine, one dreams of crowded towns and thoroughfares. Lodging in the gallbladder, one dreams of fighting, lawsuits and cutting oneself open. Lodging in the genitals, one dreams of intercourse. Lodging in the nape, one dreams of being beheaded. Lodging in the shins, one dreams of walking without being able to advance, and of being in deep underground cellars or enclosures. Lodging in the thighs and upper arms, one dreams of performing rites, bowing and rising. Lodging in the bladder and rectum, one dreams of urinating and defecating. For all these fifteen insufficiencies, supplement when [the dream] arrives, and it ends at once."

## Notes

Fifteen dreams due to lodging of reversal qi. Qibo's speech ends here, concluding the chapter.
- 胞䐈 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: 胞 is the bladder and 䐈 the large intestine; in front one dreams of urinating, behind of defecating.
