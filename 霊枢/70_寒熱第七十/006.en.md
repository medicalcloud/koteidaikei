# 霊枢 寒熱第七十 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Draw out the tip from the root, and it can be made to weaken and leave, and the cold and heat cut off. Carefully press along its path and apply [the needle], moving slowly in and slowly out to remove it. Those as small as a grain of wheat respond to one needling and are cured by three."

## Notes

Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu*: the root is the zang organ, the tip where [the scrofula] appears, and the path the course of the points where the vessels of the zang-fu organs run and emerge; moving slowly in and out is a method of manipulating the needle. The *Leijing* note: those as small as wheat are just beginning, so one needling shows effect and three cure them; treatment should be early, and one must not neglect them because they are small.
