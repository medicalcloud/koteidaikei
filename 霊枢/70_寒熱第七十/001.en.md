# 霊枢 寒熱第七十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "Cold and heat with scrofula in the neck and armpits—what qi causes them?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, describing how scrofula (mouse fistula) arises, how to needle it, and how to judge life and death.
