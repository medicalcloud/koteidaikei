# 霊枢 寒熱第七十 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The root of mouse fistula is always in the zang organs, and its tip comes up between the neck and armpits. When it floats in the vessels and has not yet attached inwardly to the flesh, and forms pus and blood outside, it is easy to remove."

## Notes

Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu*: being in the vessels and not in the flesh means it is shallow; when it forms pus and blood, much qi escapes outward, so it is easy to remove.
