# 霊枢 寒熱第七十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "These are all the toxic qi of cold and heat of mouse fistula, lodged in the vessels and not leaving."

## Notes

Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: scrofula is so called because the lumps form strings that run up and down, appearing in the neck and armpits; since they are like mouse holes—stop one and another opens—they are also called mouse fistula. The toxin of cold and heat lodges in the meridians, so they continue in chains.
