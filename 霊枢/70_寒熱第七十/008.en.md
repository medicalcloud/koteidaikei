# 霊枢 寒熱第七十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Turn back the eyelid and look: if there is a red vessel in it running from top to bottom through the pupil—if one vessel is seen, death in one year; one and a half, death in a year and a half; two, death in two years; two and a half, death in two and a half years; three, death in three years. If a red vessel is seen but does not run down through the pupil, it can be cured."

## Notes

The end of the chapter. The *Leijing* note: the eye is where the ancestral vessels gather and the pupil is the essence of the bones; a red vessel running down through the pupil shows the blaze of the pathogenic toxin deeply damaging the yin, a sign of death. When two or three vessels are seen the qi is scattered and slower; when concentrated into one the toxin is sharp and focused—hence the difference in the time of death.
