# 霊枢 官能第七十三 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong asked the Yellow Emperor, "The *Discussion of Needling* says, 'Transmit it to the right person; do not speak of it to the wrong one.' How does one know who may receive it?"

## Notes

From here on the dialogue is with Leigong. The *Discussion of Needling* refers to the preceding section (units 1–5) (*Leijing*).
