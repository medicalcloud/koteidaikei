# 霊枢 官能第七十三 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Those with clear eyes can be set to observe complexions; those with sharp ears, to listen to sounds; those quick and fluent of speech, to transmit the teachings; those who speak slowly and are calm and quiet, deft of hand and careful of mind, to practice needling and moxibustion, to order the blood and qi and regulate counterflow and compliance, to examine yin and yang and combine the various methods; those with supple joints and soft sinews and a harmonious mind, to practice guiding and stretching to circulate the qi; those with venomous tongues who belittle others, to spit on abscesses and curse illnesses; those with bitter nails and poisonous hands who tend to injure in whatever they do, to press accumulations and suppress impediments. Only when each is in accord with his ability can the methods be practiced and his name become known. Without the right person the work is not accomplished and the teacher has no renown. Hence the saying 'Speak of it to the right person; do not transmit it to the wrong one.' This is what it means. As for those with poisonous hands, have them try pressing a tortoise: put the tortoise under a vessel and have them press on top; in fifty days it will die. With someone whose hands are sweet, it revives as before."

## Notes

The end of the chapter. Translated following the *Leijing* notes.
- 捷疾辞語者，可使伝論: guidance, admonition, resolving doubts and correcting errors are all "transmitting the teachings" (*Leijing*).
- 導引 aims only to circulate the blood and qi without harm, so only those with supple joints, soft sinews and a harmonious mind can do it (*Leijing*).
- 唾癰呪病: a foul, venomous tongue is also inborn; useless for anything else, it serves only for spitting and cursing illnesses (*Leijing*).
- 按積抑痺 is of the same kind as guiding and stretching, but hard accumulations and fixed impediments can be broken only by those with bitter nails and poisonous hands (*Leijing*).
- 手甘者 refers not to taste but to the absence of poison (*Leijing*).
- The final 出入之合, 把而行之 and 窈冥 are headwords of textual notes on variants of words in this chapter; the interlinear notes say "another text has 会", "another text has 犯而行之" and "another text has 冥冥" respectively. They are not translated.
