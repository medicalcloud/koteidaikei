# 霊枢 官能第七十三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I have heard from you, Master, so much about the nine needles that it cannot be counted. I have extended and discussed it and compiled it into one whole. I will recite it; please listen to its principles, tell me where it is wrong, and correct its way, so that it may be handed down long to later generations without harm—transmitted to the right person and not spoken of to the wrong one."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. In the first part the Yellow Emperor recites his compilation of the teaching on the nine needles (units 3–5); the second part, a dialogue with Leigong, describes assigning tasks according to people's abilities (官能).
- 一紀 means "a compilation" (Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*). The Siku edition on Wikisource and the Daizhige text read 請正其道 (as does the *Leijing*).
