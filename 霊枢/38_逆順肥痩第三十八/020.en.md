# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The three yin of the hand run from the viscera to the hands; the three yang of the hand run from the hands to the head; the three yang of the foot run from the head to the feet; the three yin of the foot run from the feet to the abdomen."
