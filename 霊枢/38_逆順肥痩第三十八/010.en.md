# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Look at whether they are fair or dark and regulate each accordingly. Those who are upright and sincere have harmonious blood and qi; in needling them, do not depart from the usual numbers."
