# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "How exacting! The way practiced by the sages is clearer than the sun and moon and finer than a hair. Who but you, Master, could explain it?"

## Notes

The end of the chapter.
