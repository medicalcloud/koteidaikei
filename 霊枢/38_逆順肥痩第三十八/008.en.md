# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Thin people have thin skin and little color, lean and angular flesh, thin lips and light speech; their blood is clear and their qi smooth; they easily lose qi and easily suffer harm to the blood. In needling them, needle shallow and quick."
