# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Infants have fragile flesh, little blood and weak qi. In needling them, use the filiform needle, needle shallowly and withdraw quickly; twice a day is permissible."

## Notes

豪刺 of the base is read as 毫鍼 "filiform needle", as one source has it (see collation).
