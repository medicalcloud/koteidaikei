# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Breaching water at a deep place, the water can be drained without effort; following the dug meridian and breaching the key point, the meridian can be opened. This speaks of the smoothness or roughness of the qi, the clarity or turbidity of the blood, and the counterflow or compliance of their movement."
