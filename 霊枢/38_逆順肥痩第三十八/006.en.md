# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When one in the prime of life with a large frame, full blood and qi and firm skin is invaded by a pathogen, needle deep and retain; this is the fat person. One with broad shoulders, armpits and nape, thin flesh, thick skin and a dark complexion, with drooping lips, dark and turbid blood, rough and slow qi, and a person greedy in taking and giving: needle deep and retain, and increase the number of needlings."

## Notes

臨臨然 is provisionally translated as "drooping". Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
