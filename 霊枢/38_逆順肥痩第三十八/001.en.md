# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I have heard the way of the needle from you, Master, and have learned a great deal of it in full. Your way responds [to illness] so that it seems to vanish, and relying on it has never failed. Did you master it by asking and learning, or did it arise in your mind by carefully observing things?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about needling according to constitution (fat and thin, ordinary people, strong men, infants) and about the counterflow and compliance of the courses of the vessels.
