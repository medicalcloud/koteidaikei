# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Guide [the patient] with words and verify by feeling; [confirming] that it does not necessarily beat, one can then make clear the course of counterflow and compliance."

## Notes

其非必動 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: if pulses that should always beat (Chongyang, Taixi, Taichong, etc.) do not beat now, those not beating are counterflow and those beating are compliance. Yang Shangshan explains it as two methods, questioning and palpation, to distinguish the thoroughfare vessel from the lesser yin.
