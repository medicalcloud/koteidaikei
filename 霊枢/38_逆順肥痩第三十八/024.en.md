# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Guide [the patient] with words and verify by feeling; [confirming] that it does not necessarily beat, one can then make clear the course of counterflow and compliance."

## Notes

The reading of 其非必動 is unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); translated provisionally.
