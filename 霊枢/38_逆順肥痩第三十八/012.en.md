# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In needling strong men with true bones, [they have] firm flesh and loose joints and are imposing. If such a person is heavy, the qi is rough and the blood turbid; needle deep and retain, and increase the number. If agile, the qi is smooth and the blood clear; needle shallow and quick."

## Notes

監監然 is provisionally translated as "imposing". Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
