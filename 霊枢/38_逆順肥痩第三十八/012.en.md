# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In needling strong men with true bones, [they have] firm flesh and loose joints and appear watchful. If such a person is heavy, the qi is rough and the blood turbid; needle deep and retain, and increase the number. If agile, the qi is smooth and the blood clear; needle shallow and quick."

## Notes

監監然 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* on the same word in chapter 64 (*Yinyang ershiwu ren*): "watchful". The *Jiayi jing* has 騐騐 with a note "one edition has 監監". Whether it fits this context is uncertain.
