# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the sages practice the way, they accord with heaven above, earth below and human affairs in between; there must be clear laws by which to establish measures and numbers, check them against models and standards, and only then can it be transmitted. Thus a carpenter cannot discard the rule and guess lengths, or abandon the plumb line and establish a level; a craftsman cannot put aside the compass and make a circle, or abandon the square and make a square. Knowing how to use these is the natural way of things, an easily used teaching, the norm of counterflow and compliance."
