# 霊枢 逆順肥痩第三十八 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "It is not so. The thoroughfare vessel is the sea of the five zang organs and six fu organs, from which they all draw. Its upper part comes out at the nasopharynx, seeps into the yang meridians and irrigates the essences. Its lower part pours into the great collaterals of the lesser yin, comes out at Qijie, follows the inner edge of the inner thigh, enters the back of the knee, runs hidden inside the shinbone, descends to the attachment behind the inner ankle and separates. Its lower branch runs alongside the lesser yin meridian and seeps into the three yin. Its front branch runs hidden, comes out at the instep attachment, descends along the instep, enters between the big toes, and seeps into the collaterals to warm the flesh. So when the separate collaterals is knotted, [the pulse] on the instep does not beat; when it does not beat, there is reversal; with reversal, there is cold."

## Notes

It explains that what runs downward is not the lesser yin but the thoroughfare vessel.
- The base's 「入大指問」 is emended to 「入大指間」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), the *Taisu*, *Leijing* and *Jiayi jing* read 大指間; 間 is adopted.
