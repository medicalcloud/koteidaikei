# 霊枢 平人絶穀第三十二 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The small intestine is two and a half *cun* in circumference, eight and one-third *fen* in diameter and three *zhang* two *chi* long; it receives two *dou* four *sheng* of grain and six *sheng* three and two-thirds *ge* of water. The winding intestine is four *cun* in circumference, one and one-third *cun* in diameter and two *zhang* one *chi* long; it receives one *dou* of grain and seven and a half *sheng* of water. The broad intestine is eight *cun* in circumference, two and two-thirds *cun* in diameter and two *chi* eight *cun* long; it receives nine *sheng* three and one-eighth *ge* of grain.
