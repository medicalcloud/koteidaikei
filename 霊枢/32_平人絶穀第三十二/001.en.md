# 霊枢 平人絶穀第三十二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I wish to hear why a person who does not eat dies in seven days."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, explaining from the capacities of the intestines and stomach why one dies in seven days without food.
