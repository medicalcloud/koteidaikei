# 霊枢 平人絶穀第三十二 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "Let your servant tell you the reason. The stomach is one *chi* five *cun* in circumference, five *cun* in diameter and two *chi* six *cun* long; lying transversely and curved, it receives three *dou* five *sheng* of water and grain. Of these, two *dou* of grain and one *dou* five *sheng* of water constantly remain in it, and it is full. The upper burner discharges qi, putting out its essential subtleties, which are swift, fierce, smooth and quick; the lower burner irrigates the intestines below.
