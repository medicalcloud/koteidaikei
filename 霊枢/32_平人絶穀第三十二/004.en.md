# 霊枢 平人絶穀第三十二 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The total length of the intestines and stomach is five *zhang* eight *chi* four *cun*, and they receive nine *dou* two *sheng* one and two-thirds *ge* of water and grain. These are the amounts of water and grain the intestines and stomach receive. In a healthy person it is not like this: when the stomach is full, the intestines are empty; when the intestines are full, the stomach is empty; they alternately empty and fill. So the qi can go up and down, the five zang organs are settled, the blood vessels are harmonious and free-flowing, and the essence and spirit dwell there. Thus the spirit is the essential qi of water and grain. So in the intestines and stomach there should remain two *dou* of grain and one *dou* five *sheng* of water. A healthy person defecates twice a day, two and a half *sheng* each time, five *sheng* in one day; in seven days, five times seven is three *dou* five *sheng*, and the remaining water and grain are exhausted. Hence a healthy person who neither eats nor drinks dies in seven days because the essential qi of water and grain and the fluids are all exhausted."
