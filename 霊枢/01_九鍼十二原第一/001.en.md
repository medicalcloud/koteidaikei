# 霊枢 九鍼十二原第一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I treat the myriad people as my children and nurture the hundred clans, and I collect their taxes. I grieve that they do not have enough and are constantly afflicted with illness. I wish not to subject them to toxic medicines or use stone needles, but by means of fine needles to open their channels, regulate their blood and qi, and manage the meeting points of their counterflow and compliance, their exits and entries. To make it transmissible to later generations, I must make its rules clear, so that it will not perish at the end nor be cut off over long time, easy to use and hard to forget, and establish its guidelines. I will distinguish its sections, separate its exterior and interior, and set out its end and beginning. I will give each a definite form and first establish a classic of needling. I wish to hear its details."

## Notes

The beginning of the *Lingshu*. The Yellow Emperor states his wish to establish, and hand down to later generations, a method of treatment with fine needles rather than medicines or stone needles, and asks Qibo.
