# 霊枢 九鍼十二原第一 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The five viscera have five transport points each, five times five making twenty-five; the six bowels have six transport points each, six times six making thirty-six. There are twelve channels and fifteen network vessels, twenty-seven qi in all, moving up and down. Where it emerges is the well, where it flows is the brook, where it pours is the stream, where it moves is the river, where it 以 [enters] is the uniting point; where the twenty-seven qi move is all at the five transport points. The meeting places of the joints number three hundred sixty-five; one who knows the essentials ends in a single word; one who does not scatters without end. What are called joints are where the spirit qi wanders, exits and enters; they are not skin, flesh, sinews or bones. Look at the complexion and examine the eyes to know whether [the qi] is scattered or restored; unify one's form, listen to its movement and stillness, to know whether it is pathogenic or proper. The right [hand] governs pushing, the left holds and controls; when the qi arrives, withdraw. Whenever one is about to use needles, one must first examine the pulse and see whether the qi is severe or mild; only then can one treat. When the qi of the five viscera is already cut off within, and the needler instead fills the outside, this is called double exhaustion; double exhaustion means certain death, and the death is quiet; it is because the treater acts against the qi, taking the armpit and chest. When the qi of the five viscera is already cut off outside, and the needler instead fills the inside, this is called counterflow reversal; counterflow reversal means certain death, and the death is agitated; it is because the treater instead takes the four extremities. As for the harms of needling: if one hits [the illness] but does not withdraw, the essence leaks; if one [does not] hit it but withdraws, qi is brought. When essence leaks, the illness grows worse with debility; when qi is brought, abscesses and sores arise.

## Notes

The five transport points (well, brook, stream, river, uniting) of the five viscera and six bowels and the twenty-seven qi, examining the pulse before needling, and the harms of double exhaustion and counterflow reversal. Qibo's speech continues through unit 10.
- 害中而去則致気 is often understood as contrasting "hitting and withdrawing" with "not hitting and withdrawing" (cf. chapter 3, *Xiaozhen jie*). The translation supplies "[does not] hit", but this has not been verified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
- The base's 「所以為合」 is emended to 「所入為合」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* all read 所入為合, which parallels 所出為井, 所溜為滎, etc.; 入 is adopted.
