# 霊枢 九鍼十二原第一 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When qi is in the vessels, pathogenic qi is above, turbid qi in the middle, and clear qi below. Hence when the needle [enters] the deep vessels, pathogenic qi comes out; when the needle [enters] the middle vessels, turbid qi comes out; when the needle goes too deep, the pathogenic qi sinks instead and the illness increases. Hence it is said: skin, flesh, sinews and vessels each have their place; illnesses each have what suits them; [needles] each differ in form, and each is assigned to what suits it. Do not fill the excess or deplete the deficient. Damaging the insufficient and adding to the excessive is called aggravating the illness, and the illness grows worse. Taking the five vessels brings death; taking the three vessels brings debility. Robbing yin brings death; robbing yang brings madness. These are all the harms of the needle. If needling does not bring the qi, do not count [the needlings]; if needling brings the qi, then withdraw and do not needle again. Needles each have what suits them, each differ in form, and each is assigned to its task. The essential of needling is that the qi arrives and there is effect; the certainty of the effect is like wind blowing away clouds, clear as seeing the blue sky. That completes the way of needling."

## Notes

The depth of the qi and of needling, the harms of wrong needling, and the arrival of qi as the essential of needling. Qibo's speech ends here.
- 陥脈 and 中脈 are explained in chapter 3 (*Xiaozhen jie*): "when the needle enters the deep vessels ... take the upper [points]; when the needle enters the middle vessels ... take the uniting point of *Yangming*".
