# 霊枢 九鍼十二原第一 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied, "Let me reason it out and set it in order, giving it guidelines, beginning with one and ending with nine. Let me speak of its way. The essentials of the small needle are easy to state but hard to enter into. The crude guard the form; the superior guard the spirit. Spirit, oh spirit! The guest is at the gate. Without seeing the illness, how can one know its origin? The subtlety of needling lies in speed and slowness. The crude guard the joints; the superior guard the mechanism. The movement of the mechanism does not leave its hollow. The mechanism within the hollow is clear, still and subtle. When it comes, it must not be met; when it goes, it must not be pursued. One who knows the way of the mechanism does not miss by a hair; one who does not know the way of the mechanism strikes and it does not go off. Know its going and coming, and the key is to time oneself with it. How benighted are the crude! How wondrous that only the skilled possess it! What goes is counterflow; what comes is compliance. Clearly knowing counterflow and compliance, act rightly without asking. Going against it to seize it, how can there not be deficiency? Pursuing it to aid it, how can there not be excess? Meet it, follow it, and harmonize it with intent; then the way of the needle is complete.

## Notes

The essentials of the small needle: guard not the form but the spirit and the mechanism (the movement of qi), and supplement or drain in time with the coming and going of qi. The phrases of this passage are explained in chapter 3 (*Xiaozhen jie*). Qibo's speech continues through unit 6.
- For 逆 in 逆而奪之, some other sources have 迎 (see the collation notes).
