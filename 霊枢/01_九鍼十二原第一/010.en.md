# 霊枢 九鍼十二原第一 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Now the illnesses of the five zang organs are like a thorn, like dirt, like a knot, like a blockage. A thorn, though long embedded, can still be pulled out; dirt, though long ingrained, can still be washed off; a knot, though long tied, can still be untied; a blockage, though long closed, can still be opened. Some say long-standing illnesses cannot be taken; that is not correct. One who is good at using needles takes the illness as one pulls out a thorn, washes off dirt, unties a knot, opens a blockage. Though the illness be long-standing, it can still be ended. Those who say it cannot be treated have not yet obtained the art. Needle all heat as if testing hot water with the hand; needle cold and chill as if one were reluctant to leave. When there is a yang illness in the yin, take Sanli at Xialing, going straight without hesitation; stop when the qi descends, and if it does not, begin again. When the illness is high and internal, take the Yin Mound Spring; when the illness is high and external, take the Yang Mound Spring."

## Notes

Using similes, the chapter says long-standing illnesses can be cured with needles, describes needling heat and cold, and the use of Sanli, Yinlingquan and Yanglingquan, concluding the chapter. Qibo's speech ends here.
