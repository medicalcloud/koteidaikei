# 霊枢 九鍼十二原第一 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The shear needle has a large head and a sharp tip and removes and drains yang qi. The round needle is egg-shaped; it rubs between the divisions of the flesh without harming the muscles, to drain the qi of the divisions. The arrowhead needle has a point as sharp as a grain of millet; it is for pressing the vessel without sinking in, to bring the qi. The sharp needle has a three-edged blade, to dispel chronic illness. The sword needle has a tip like the point of a sword, to take out large amounts of pus. The round-sharp needle is as thick as a yak hair, both round and sharp, slightly larger in the middle of the body, to take sudden qi. The filiform needle has a tip like the beak of a mosquito or gadfly; it goes in quietly and slowly, is retained gently and long to nourish, to take painful impediment. The long needle has a sharp point and a thin body, and can take deep impediment. The large needle has a tip like a staff with a slightly rounded point, to drain water from the joints. That completes the nine needles.
