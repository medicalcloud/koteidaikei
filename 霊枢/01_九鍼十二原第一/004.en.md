# 霊枢 九鍼十二原第一 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The nine needles each have a different form. The first is the arrowhead needle, one *cun* six *fen* long; the second the round needle, one *cun* six *fen* long; the third the spoon needle, three and a half *cun* long; the fourth the sharp needle, one *cun* six *fen* long; the fifth the sword needle, four *cun* long and two and a half *fen* wide; the sixth the round-sharp needle, one *cun* six *fen* long; the seventh the filiform needle, three *cun* six *fen* long; the eighth the long needle, seven *cun* long; the ninth the large needle, four *cun* long.

## Notes

The names and dimensions of the nine needles.
