# 霊枢 九鍼十二原第一 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I wish to hear where [the qi of] the five viscera and six bowels emerge."

## Notes

A question about where the qi of the five viscera and six bowels emerges (the well, brook, stream, river and uniting points).
