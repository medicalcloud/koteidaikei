# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For warm malaria without sweating, [needle] the fifty-nine needle points.

## Notes

The "fifty-nine needle points" are taken as the fifty-nine points for treating heat illness (*Suwen*, *Shuire xue lun*; *Lingshu*, *Rebing*).
