# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the abdomen constantly rumbles, the qi rises to strike the chest, and one pants and cannot stand long, the pathogen is in the large intestine. Needle the source of the *huang*, Juxu Shanglian and Sanli.

## Notes

盲 of the base is read as 肓, as all the other sources have it (see collation). The same applies to the next unit.
