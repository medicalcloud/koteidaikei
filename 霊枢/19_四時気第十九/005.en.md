# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For swill diarrhea, supplement above the three yin [Sanyinjiao] and supplement Yinlingquan, retaining long in both, and stop when heat moves.

## Notes

三陰之上 is taken as Sanyinjiao, following the *Jiayi jing*'s reading 三陰交. Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* explains it as supplementing the three foot yin meridians, taking Guanyuan etc. above and Yinlingquan below.
