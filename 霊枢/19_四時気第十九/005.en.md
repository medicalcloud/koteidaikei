# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For swill diarrhea, supplement above the three yin [Sanyinjiao] and supplement Yinlingquan, retaining long in both, and stop when heat moves.

## Notes

三陰之上 is provisionally taken as referring to Sanyinjiao, following one view. Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
