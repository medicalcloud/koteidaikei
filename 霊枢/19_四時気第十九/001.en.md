# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "The qi of the four seasons each have a different form, and the hundred illnesses all arise from somewhere. In the way of moxibustion and needling, what is the fixed principle?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about needling according to the four seasons and the treatment of various illnesses. The interlinear note is not translated (original text only).
