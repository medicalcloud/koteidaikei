# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

'Look at the complexion, examine the [eyes] to know whether it is scattered or restored' means looking at the color of the eyes to know whether the illness persists or is gone. 'Unify the form and listen to its movement and stillness' means holding the qi opening and Renying to observe the pulse: if it is firm, abundant and slippery, the illness advances daily; if the pulse is soft, the illness will soon recede; if all the channels are replete, the illness ends in three days. The qi opening indicates the yin, Renying the yang."

## Notes

An explanation of phrases from chapter 1 ("look at the complexion, examine the eyes, know whether it is scattered or restored, unify the form, listen to its movement and stillness"). Qibo's speech ends here, concluding the chapter.
- The base reads 察其以知 without 目; "[eyes]" is supplied following chapter 1.
