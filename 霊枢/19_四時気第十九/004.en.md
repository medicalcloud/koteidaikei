# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For wind edema and skin distension, [needle] the fifty-seven needle points; where the blood [vessels] of the skin are to be taken, take them all.

## Notes

㽷 is taken in the sense of water (edema). The "fifty-seven needle points" are taken as the fifty-seven points for treating water (*Suwen*, *Shuire xue lun*).
