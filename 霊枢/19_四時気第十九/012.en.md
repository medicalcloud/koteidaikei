# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the lower abdomen pulls at the testicles, pulls the lower back and spine and rises to strike the heart, the pathogen is in the small intestine. [The small intestine] connects with the testicular connection, belongs to the spine, passes through the liver and lungs and connects with the heart connection. When the qi is abundant, there is reversal counterflow, rising to strike the intestines and stomach, fuming the liver, dispersing in the *huang* and knotting at the navel. Therefore take the source of the *huang* to disperse it, needle the greater yin to give to it, take the *jueyin* to bring it down, take Juxu Xialian to remove it, and press the channels through which it passes to regulate it.

## Notes

不 in 巨虚不廉 of the base is read as 下, as other sources have it (see collation).
