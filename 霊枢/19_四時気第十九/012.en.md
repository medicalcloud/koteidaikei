# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the lower abdomen pulls at the testicles, pulls the lower back and spine and rises to strike the heart, the pathogen is in the small intestine. [The small intestine] connects with the testicular connection, belongs to the spine, passes through the liver and lungs and connects with the heart connection. When the qi is abundant, there is cold limbs, rising to strike the intestines and stomach, fuming the liver, dispersing in the *huang* and knotting at the navel. Therefore take the source of the *huang* to disperse it, needle the greater yin to give to it, take the *Jueyin* to bring it down, take Juxu Xialian to remove it, and press the meridians through which it passes to regulate it.

## Notes

- The base's 「散于盲」 is emended to 「散于肓」. The Siku edition and Daizhige read 肓; 肓 is adopted as in 肓原 of the same unit.
- The base's 「取之盲原」 is emended to 「取之肓原」. Wikisource (both), Daizhige, the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 肓原; 肓 is adopted.
- The base's 「巨虚不廉」 is emended to 「巨虚下廉」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 巨虚下廉 (the lower uniting point of the small intestine); 下 is adopted.
