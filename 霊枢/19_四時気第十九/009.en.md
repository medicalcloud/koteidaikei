# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The bones are the trunk. When the intestines are not free-flowing, take Sanli; drain when abundant, supplement when depleted.

## Notes

骨為幹 does not connect with the surrounding text; one view treats it as a misplaced passage. Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
