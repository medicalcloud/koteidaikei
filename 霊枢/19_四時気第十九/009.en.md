# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The bones are the trunk. When the intestines are not free-flowing, take Sanli; drain when abundant, supplement when depleted.

## Notes

骨為幹 does not connect with its context. Neither the *Leijing* nor the *Lingshu jizhu* comments on it here, and Xiao Yanping's notes on the *Taisu* give no clue. It may be a stray phrase from the beginning of chapter 10 ("the bones are the trunk, the vessels the circulation…"), but this is unknown.
