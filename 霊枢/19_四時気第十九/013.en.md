# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When one frequently vomits, the vomit is bitter, one sighs deeply, and the heart pounds as if fearing someone is about to seize one, the pathogen is in the gallbladder and the counterflow is in the stomach. When gallbladder juice leaks, the mouth is bitter; when the stomach qi counterflows, one vomits bitterness; hence it is called gallbladder vomiting. Take Sanli to bring down the counterflow of stomach qi, needle the blood collaterals of the lesser yang to shut off the gallbladder counterflow, then regulate deficiency and excess to remove the pathogen.
