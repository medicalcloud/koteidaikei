# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the lower abdomen is painful and swollen and one cannot urinate, the pathogen is in the binding of the sanjiao [the bladder]. Take the great collaterals of the greater yang, looking at its collaterals and the small collaterals of the *Jueyin* for those that are knotted with blood. If the swelling rises to the stomach duct, take Sanli.

## Notes

三焦約 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: the lower transport point of the sanjiao joins the foot greater yang divergent, enters to connect with the bladder and binds the lower jiao; hence the bladder. Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* reads it as the pathogen in the sanjiao, bound and blocked.
