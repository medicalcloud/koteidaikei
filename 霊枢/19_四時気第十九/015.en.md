# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the lower abdomen is painful and swollen and one cannot urinate, the pathogen is in the binding of the triple burner [the bladder]. Take the great network vessel of the greater yang, looking at its network vessels and the small network vessels of the *jueyin* for those that are knotted with blood. If the swelling rises to the stomach duct, take Sanli.

## Notes

三焦約 is provisionally taken as the bladder, following one view. Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
