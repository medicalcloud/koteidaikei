# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The qi of the four seasons each have their location. In the way of moxibustion and needling, obtaining the qi points is the fixed principle. Thus in spring take the channels, the blood vessels and the spaces between the partings of the flesh: needle deep for severe cases and shallow for mild ones. In summer take the abundant channels and grandchild network vessels, taking the spaces between the partings and cutting through the skin. In autumn take the river and stream points; when the pathogen is in the bowels, take the uniting points. In winter take the well and brook points, always deep and retaining.

## Notes

Point selection according to the four seasons. Qibo's speech continues through unit 16, listing treatments for various illnesses item by item.
- 別 in 灸別 and 并 in 并滎 of the base are read as 刺 and 井, as other sources have them (see collation).
