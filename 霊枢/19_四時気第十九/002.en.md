# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The qi of the four seasons each have their location. In the way of moxibustion and needling, obtaining the qi points is the fixed principle. Thus in spring take the meridians, the blood vessels and the spaces between the partings of the flesh: needle deep for severe cases and shallow for mild ones. In summer take the abundant meridians and grandchild collaterals, taking the spaces between the partings and cutting through the skin. In autumn take the river and stream points; when the pathogen is in the bowels, take the uniting points. In winter take the well and brook points, always deep and retaining.

## Notes

Point selection according to the four seasons. Qibo's speech continues through unit 16, listing treatments for various illnesses item by item.
- The base's 「灸別之道」 is emended to 「灸刺之道」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 灸刺之道, answering the question's 灸刺之道; 刺 is adopted.
- The base's 「冬取并滎」 is emended to 「冬取井滎」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 井滎 (well and brook points); 井 is adopted.
