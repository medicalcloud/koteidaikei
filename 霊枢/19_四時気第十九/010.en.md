# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For pestilential wind, regularly needle over the swelling; after needling, needle the place with a sharp needle, press out the foul qi, and stop when the swelling is gone. Always eat the prescribed food and nothing else.

## Notes

素 in 素刺 is read as "regularly", following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*.
