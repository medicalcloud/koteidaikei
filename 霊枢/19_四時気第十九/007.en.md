# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For simple water [swelling], first take three *cun* below Huangu [the navel, i.e. Guanyuan] and needle with the sword needle; after needling, insert a tube, inserting it again and again to drain all the water; [the body] must be bound firmly; if [the binding] is loose, there is vexation and oppression; if it is tight, there is calm. Needle once every other day, and stop when the water is exhausted. Give a medicine that opens blockage to drink, giving it only at the time of needling; when drinking it, do not eat; when eating, do not drink; and eat nothing else for one hundred thirty-five days.

## Notes

Needling for water swelling (draining water through a tube) and aftercare.
- 環谷 follows Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* ("Huangu is the navel; three *cun* below it is Guanyuan"); Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* says its meaning cannot be traced and suggests Huantiao. 必堅，来緩則煩悗，来急則安静 follows the *Taisu* reading 必堅束之，緩則煩悗，束急則安静 and Yang Shangshan's explanation ("as the water leaves the person is depleted, so bind the body firmly"); 来 appears to be an error for 束, but the base is kept. 閉薬 follows the *Leijing*: "water illness usually blocks urination, so give a medicine that opens blockage".
