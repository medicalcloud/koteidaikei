# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For simple water [swelling], first take three *cun* below Huangu and needle with the sword needle; after needling, insert a tube, inserting it again and again to drain all the water; it must be firm. If [the water] comes slowly, there is vexation and oppression; if it comes quickly, there is calm. Needle once every other day, and stop when the water is exhausted. Give a blocking medicine to drink, giving it only at the time of needling; when drinking it, do not eat; when eating, do not drink; and eat nothing else for one hundred thirty-five days.

## Notes

Needling for water swelling (draining water through a tube) and aftercare.
- The location of 環谷, the reading of 必堅，来緩則煩悗，来急則安静 and the meaning of 閉薬 are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); translated provisionally.
