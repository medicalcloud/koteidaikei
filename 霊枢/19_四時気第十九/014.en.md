# 霊枢 四時気第十九 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When food and drink do not go down and the diaphragm is blocked, the pathogen is in the stomach duct. If it is in the upper duct, needle to suppress and bring it down; if in the lower duct, disperse and remove it.
