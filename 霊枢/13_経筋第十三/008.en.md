# 霊枢 経筋第十三 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The sinew of the hand lesser yang starts at the tip of the ring finger, knots at the wrist, rises along the forearm, knots at the elbow, rises around the outer edge of the upper arm, rises to the shoulder, runs to the neck and unites with the hand greater yang. Its branch, at the curve of the cheek, enters to attach to the root of the tongue. Its branch rises to the curved tooth, follows in front of the ear, belongs to the outer canthus, rises over the chin and knots at the corner [of the head]. Its illness: propping and cramping wherever it passes, and curling of the tongue. The treatment is quick needling with a heated needle, taking the effect as the number and the painful spot as the point. It is called late-summer impediment.

## Notes

The sinew of the hand lesser yang.
