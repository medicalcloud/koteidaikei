# 霊枢 経筋第十三 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The sinew of the hand greater yang starts on the little finger, knots at the wrist, rises along the inner edge of the forearm, knots behind the sharp bone on the inside of the elbow (flicking it is felt on the little finger), and enters to knot below the armpit. Its branch runs back to the rear edge of the armpit, rises around the shoulder blade, follows the neck and comes out to run in front of the greater yang, and knots at the mastoid bone behind the ear. Its branch enters the ear. Its straight part comes out above the ear, descends to knot at the chin and rises to belong to the outer canthus. Its illness: propping of the little finger, pain at the rear edge behind the sharp bone on the inside of the elbow, [pain] following the yin side of the forearm into the armpit, pain below the armpit, pain at the rear edge of the armpit, pain winding around the shoulder blade and pulling the neck, correspondingly ringing and pain in the ears, pulling of the chin, and eyes that can see only after being closed for a long while; when the neck sinews are tense, there are sinew fistulas and neck swelling. For cold and heat in the neck, the treatment is quick needling with a heated needle, taking the effect as the number and the painful spot as the point; where it has become swollen, needle it again with a sharp [needle]. The 本 [its] branch rises to the curved tooth [the angle of the jaw], follows in front of the ear, belongs to the outer canthus, rises to the chin and knots at the corner [of the head]. Its pain, wherever it passes, is propping and cramping. The treatment is quick needling with a heated needle, taking the effect as the number and the painful spot as the point. It is called mid-summer impediment.

## Notes

The sinew of the hand greater yang.
- The meanings of 復而鋭之 and 本支者 are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points). 本支者 is provisionally read as 其支者.
