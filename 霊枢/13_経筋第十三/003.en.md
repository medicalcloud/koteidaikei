# 霊枢 経筋第十三 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The sinew of the foot *Yangming* starts at the middle three toes, knots on the instep, rises obliquely outward over the auxiliary bone, rises to knot at the outer edge of the knee, rises straight to knot at the hip pivot, rises along the flank and belongs to the spine. Its straight part rises along the shin and knots at the knee. Its branch knots at the outer auxiliary bone and unites with the lesser yang. Its straight part rises along Futu, rises to knot at the thigh, gathers at the genitals, rises over the abdomen and spreads, reaches the empty basin and knots, rises over the neck, rises to flank the mouth, unites at the cheekbone, descends to knot at the nose, and rises to unite with the greater yang. The greater yang forms the upper net of the eye, the *Yangming* the lower net. Its branch goes from the cheek to knot in front of the ear. Its illness: propping of the middle toe, cramping of the shin, jumping and hardening of the leg, cramping at Futu, swelling in front of the thigh, *tui shan*, tension of the abdominal sinews pulling the empty basin and the cheek, and sudden deviation of the mouth. On the tense side the eye cannot close; with heat the sinews slacken and the eye cannot open. When the cheek sinews have cold, they tense and pull the cheek, shifting the mouth; with heat, the sinews slacken and, being slack, cannot contract, hence the deviation. Treat it with horse fat, applying it to the tense side; mix cinnamon with white wine and smear it on the slack side; hook it with a mulberry hook; then put fresh mulberry charcoal in a recess [made in the wall], at a height level with the seated [patient], and apply the fat as a compress to the tense cheek; also have [the patient] drink good wine and eat good roast meat; even one who does not drink should make the effort. Stroking it three times ends it. The treatment is quick needling with a heated needle, taking the effect as the number and the painful spot as the point. It is called late-spring impediment.

## Notes

The sinew of the foot *Yangming* and the treatment of deviation of the mouth.
- The base's 「結于　其支者」 is emended to 「結于膝其支者」. The base has a blank here; Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Leijing* and *Jiayi jing* read 結于膝, so 膝 is supplied.
- The base's 「筋縦自不開」 is emended to 「筋縦目不開」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 目不開, contrasting with 目不合; 目 is adopted.
- The base's 「以生桑灰置之」 is emended to 「以生桑炭置之」. Daizhige, the *Taisu* and *Leijing* read 生桑炭 (charcoal), as in chapter 6, used to heat the compress; 炭 is adopted.
