# 霊枢 経筋第十三 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The sinew of the foot lesser yin starts below the little toe, runs alongside the sinew of the foot greater yin obliquely to below the inner ankle, knots at the heel, unites with the sinew of the greater yang, rises to knot below the inner auxiliary [bone], runs alongside the sinew of the greater yin up the inner thigh, knots at the genitals, follows the inside of the spine flanking the paravertebral muscles, rises to the nape, knots at the occipital bone and unites with the sinew of the foot greater yang. Its illness: cramping of the sole, with pain and cramping wherever it passes and knots. Illness here governs epilepsy, tugging and convulsions. When it is on the outside, one cannot bend forward; when on the inside, one cannot bend back. Hence in yang illness, the lower back arches and one cannot bend forward; in yin illness, one cannot bend back. The treatment is quick needling with a heated needle, taking the effect as the number and the painful spot as the point. When it is on the inside, use compresses, guiding exercises and medicine to drink. When this sinew breaks and twists, and the twisting breaks out often and severely, there is death without cure. It is called mid-autumn impediment.

## Notes

The sinew of the foot lesser yin.
