# 霊枢 経筋第十三 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The sinew of the foot greater yin starts at the inner side of the tip of the big toe and rises to knot at the inner ankle. Its straight part connects with the inner auxiliary bone of the knee, rises along the inner thigh, knots at the thigh, gathers at the genitals, rises over the abdomen to knot at the navel, follows the inside of the abdomen to knot at the ribs, and disperses in the chest. Its inner part adheres to the spine. Its illness: propping of the big toe, pain at the inner ankle, cramping pain, pain at the inner auxiliary bone of the knee, pain of the inner thigh pulling the thigh, twisting pain of the genitals, pulling down to the navel, pain in both flanks, and pain pulling the middle of the chest and the inside of the spine. The treatment is quick needling with a heated needle, taking the effect as the number and the painful spot as the point. It is called early-autumn impediment.

## Notes

The sinew of the foot greater yin.
