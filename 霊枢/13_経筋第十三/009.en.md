# 霊枢 経筋第十三 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The sinew of the hand *Yangming* starts at the tip of the index finger, knots at the wrist, rises along the forearm, rises to knot at the outside of the elbow, rises over the upper arm and knots at the shoulder bone. Its branch winds around the shoulder blade and flanks the spine. Its straight part rises from Jianyu to the neck. Its branch rises to the cheek and knots at the cheekbone. Its straight part rises to come out in front of the hand greater yang, rises to the left corner [of the head], connects with the head and descends to the right chin. Its illness: propping pain and cramping wherever it passes, inability to raise the shoulder, and inability to turn the neck to look left or right. The treatment is quick needling with a heated needle, taking the effect as the number and the painful spot as the point. It is called early-summer impediment.

## Notes

The sinew of the hand *Yangming*.
