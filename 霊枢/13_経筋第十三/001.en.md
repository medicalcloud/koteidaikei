# 霊枢 経筋第十三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The sinew of the foot greater yang starts at the little toe, rises to knot at the ankle, and rises obliquely to knot at the knee. Its lower part follows the outer side of the foot to knot at the heel, rises along the heel and knots at the back of the knee. Its separate part knots at the outside of the calf, rises to the inner edge of the back of the knee, and together with [the sinew in] the back of the knee rises to knot at the buttocks, then rises flanking the spine to the nape. Its branch separates to enter and knot at the root of the tongue. Its straight part knots at the occipital bone, rises over the head, descends over the face and knots at the nose. Its branch forms the upper net of the eye and descends to knot at the cheekbone. Its branch goes from the outer edge behind the armpit to knot at Jianyu. Its branch enters below the armpit, rises to come out at the empty basin and rises to knot at the mastoid bone. Its branch comes out at the empty basin and rises obliquely to come out at the cheekbone. Its illness: propping of the little toe, swelling and pain of the heel, cramping of the back of the knee, arching of the spine, tension of the nape sinews, inability to raise the shoulder, propping in the armpit, twisting pain in the empty basin, and inability to turn left or right. The treatment is quick needling with a heated needle, taking the effect as the number [of needlings] and the painful spot as the point. It is called mid-spring impediment.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, which describes, without a dialogue, the courses and illnesses of the twelve sinew channels and their treatment. At the end of each section, the impediment of that sinew is named after one of the twelve months.
- 以知為数，以痛為輸 is translated as "taking the effect as the number [of needlings] and the painful spot as the point"; the same applies to each section below.
