# 霊枢 経筋第十三 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The sinew of the hand lesser yin starts at the inner side of the little finger, knots at the sharp bone, rises to knot at the inner edge of the elbow, rises into the armpit, crosses the greater yin, flanks the inside of the breast, knots in the chest, follows the arm [diaphragm], and descends to attach to the navel. Its illness: inner tension, the heart bearing a hidden beam, and below it becomes the net of the elbow. Its illness: propping and cramping wherever it passes, and sinew pain. The treatment is quick needling with a heated needle, taking the effect as the number and the painful spot as the point. When it becomes a hidden beam with spitting of blood and pus, there is death without cure. In illnesses of the sinew channels, with cold there is arching and tension of the sinews; with heat the sinews slacken and do not contract, and there is impotence. When the yang [back] is tense, there is arching; when the yin [front] is tense, one bends forward and cannot straighten. Quenching needling is for needling cold tension; with heat the sinews slacken and do not contract, so the heated needle must not be used. It is called late-winter impediment. When the sinews of the foot *yangming* and hand greater yang are tense, the mouth and eyes deviate, and the canthi are tense so that one cannot see at once; all are treated by the methods above.

## Notes

The sinew of the hand lesser yin and a general account of illnesses of the sinew channels; the end of the chapter. The final 頄 is the headword of a sound gloss; the interlinear note (pronunciation) is not translated (original text only).
- For 臂 in 循臂, the base is likewise kept and [diaphragm] supplied, for the same reason as in unit 11. Whether Zhang Jiebin comments on this passage could not be confirmed.
- 心承伏梁，下為肘網 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* ("承 is bearing from below; the hidden beam is a hard concealed accumulation; 網 is tightening like a net") and Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* ("the accumulation of the heart is called the hidden beam, from above the navel to below the heart; the sinew runs below the diaphragm, hence 'bearing'").
- The base's 「口目為噼」 is emended to 「口目為僻」. Daizhige and the *Jiayi jing* read 僻, the *Taisu* 辟 (same meaning); the *Leijing* agrees with the base (噼). 僻 (deviate) is adopted.
