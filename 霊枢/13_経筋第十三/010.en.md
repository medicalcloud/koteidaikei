# 霊枢 経筋第十三 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The sinew of the hand greater yin starts on the thumb, follows the thumb upward, knots behind the fish, runs on the outer side of the inch opening, rises along the forearm, knots in the elbow, rises along the inner edge of the upper arm, enters below the armpit, comes out at the empty basin, knots at the shoulder bone in front of the shoulder, rises to knot at the empty basin, descends to knot inside the chest, disperses through the diaphragm, unites below the diaphragm and reaches the free ribs. Its illness: propping, cramping and pain wherever it passes; in severe cases it becomes rushing breath, with tension of the flanks and vomiting of blood. The treatment is quick needling with a heated needle, taking the effect as the number and the painful spot as the point. It is called mid-winter impediment.

## Notes

The sinew of the hand greater yin. 賁 is provisionally translated as the diaphragm.
