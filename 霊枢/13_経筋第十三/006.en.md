# 霊枢 経筋第十三 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The sinew of the foot *Jueyin* starts on the big toe, rises to knot in front of the inner ankle, rises along the shin, rises to knot below the inner auxiliary [bone], rises along the inner thigh, knots at the genitals and connects with all the sinews. Its illness: propping of the big toe, pain in front of the inner ankle, pain at the inner auxiliary [bone], pain and cramping of the inner thigh, and uselessness of the genitals: harmed by [excess of] the bedchamber, there is no erection; harmed by cold, the genitals retract; harmed by heat, they slacken and protrude without retracting. The treatment lies in moving water and clearing the yin qi. When the illness is cramping, the treatment is quick needling with a heated needle, taking the effect as the number and the painful spot as the point. It is called late-autumn impediment.

## Notes

The sinew of the foot *Jueyin*.
