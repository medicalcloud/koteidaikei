# 霊枢 経筋第十三 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The sinew of the hand heart governor starts at the middle finger, runs alongside the sinew of the greater yin, knots at the inner edge of the elbow, rises along the yin side of the arm, knots below the armpit, and descends to disperse front and back flanking the ribs. Its branch enters the armpit, disperses in the chest and knots at the arm [diaphragm]. Its illness: propping and cramping wherever it passes, pain in the front and chest, and rushing breath. The treatment is quick needling with a heated needle, taking the effect as the number and the painful spot as the point. It is called early-winter impediment.

## Notes

The sinew of the hand heart governor.
- 臂 in 結于臂 does not fit after dispersing in the chest; one view holds it is an error for 賁 (diaphragm). Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
