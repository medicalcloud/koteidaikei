# 霊枢 陰陽繋日月第四十一 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "This is the yin and yang of heaven and earth, not the sequential order of the four seasons and five phases. Moreover, yin and yang have names but no form; so they can be counted as ten, divided into a hundred, scattered into a thousand, extended to ten thousand. This is what it means."

## Notes

The end of the chapter.
