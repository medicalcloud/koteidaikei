# 霊枢 陰陽繋日月第四十一 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "*Yin* is the first month, where yang is born; it governs the left foot lesser yang. *Wei*, the sixth month, governs the right foot lesser yang. *Mao*, the second month, governs the left foot greater yang. *Wu*, the fifth month, governs the right foot greater yang. *Chen*, the third month, governs the left foot *yangming*. *Si*, the fourth month, governs the right foot *yangming*; here the two yang unite in front, hence it is called *yangming* (yang brightness). *Shen* is the seventh month, where yin is born; it governs the right foot lesser yin. *Chou*, the twelfth month, governs the left foot lesser yin. *You*, the eighth month, governs the right foot greater yin. *Zi*, the eleventh month, governs the left foot greater yin. *Xu*, the ninth month, governs the right foot *jueyin*. *Hai*, the tenth month, governs the left foot *jueyin*; here the two yin meet and are exhausted, hence it is called *jueyin* (reverting yin).

## Notes

Assignment of the twelve branches (twelve months) to the twelve channels of the feet. Qibo's speech continues through unit 6.
