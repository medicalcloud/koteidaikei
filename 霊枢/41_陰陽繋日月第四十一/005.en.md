# 霊枢 陰陽繋日月第四十一 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

*Jia* governs the left hand lesser yang, *ji* the right hand lesser yang, *yi* the left hand greater yang, *wu* the right hand greater yang, *bing* the left hand *Yangming*, *ding* the right hand *Yangming*; here the two fires combine, hence it is *Yangming*. *Geng* governs the right hand lesser yin, *gui* the left hand lesser yin, *xin* the right hand greater yin, *ren* the left hand greater yin.

## Notes

Assignment of the ten stems (ten days) to the ten meridians of the hands.
- The base's 「巳主右手」 is emended to 「己主右手」. This passage assigns the ten stems (*jia, yi, bing, ding, wu, geng, xin, ren, gui*) to the hand meridians, so 巳 is taken as a graphic error for 己.
