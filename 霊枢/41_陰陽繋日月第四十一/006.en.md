# 霊枢 陰陽繋日月第四十一 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus the yang of the feet is the lesser yang within yin; the yin of the feet is the greater yin within yin; the yang of the hands is the greater yang within yang; the yin of the hands is the lesser yin within yang. Above the waist is yang, below the waist is yin. As for the five viscera, the heart is the greater yang within yang, the lungs the lesser yin within yang, the liver the lesser yang within yin, the spleen the utmost yin within yin, and the kidneys the greater yin within yin."

## Notes

Qibo's speech ends here.
- The base's 「肺為陰中之少陰」 is emended to 「肺為陽中之少陰」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*) and the *Taisu* read 肺為陽中之少陰, and the *Lingshu jizhu* (note on chapter 1) also calls the lungs "lesser yin within yang". The lungs lie above the diaphragm, so 陽 is adopted.
