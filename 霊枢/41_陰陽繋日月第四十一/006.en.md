# 霊枢 陰陽繋日月第四十一 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus the yang of the feet is the lesser yang within yin; the yin of the feet is the greater yin within yin; the yang of the hands is the greater yang within yang; the yin of the hands is the lesser yin within yang. Above the waist is yang, below the waist is yin. As for the five viscera, the heart is the greater yang within yang, the lungs the lesser yin within yin, the liver the lesser yang within yin, the spleen the utmost yin within yin, and the kidneys the greater yin within yin."

## Notes

Qibo's speech ends here.
- For 肺為陰中之少陰, one source reads "lesser yin within yang" (see collation); the base is followed.
