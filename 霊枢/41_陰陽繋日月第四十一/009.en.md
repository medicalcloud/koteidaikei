# 霊枢 陰陽繋日月第四十一 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "In the five phases, the east is *jia* and *yi*, wood, which flourishes in spring; spring is green and governs the liver, and the liver is the foot *jueyin*. Now *jia* is assigned to the left hand lesser yang, which does not agree with the numbers. Why?"
