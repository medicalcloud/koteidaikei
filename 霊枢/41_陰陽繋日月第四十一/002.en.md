# 霊枢 陰陽繋日月第四十一 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Above the waist is heaven, below the waist is earth; hence heaven is yang and earth yin. Thus the twelve meridians of the feet correspond to the twelve months; the moon arises from water, so what is below is yin. The ten fingers of the hands correspond to the ten days [stems]; the sun governs fire, so what is above is yang."
