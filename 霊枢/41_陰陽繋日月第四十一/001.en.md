# 霊枢 陰陽繋日月第四十一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I have heard that heaven is yang and earth yin, the sun yang and the moon yin. How do they match the human body?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, assigning the twelve branches and ten stems to the meridians of the hands and feet.
