# 霊枢 陰陽繋日月第四十一 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In the first, second and third months, the human qi is on the left; do not needle the yang of the left foot. In the fourth, fifth and sixth months, the human qi is on the right; do not needle the yang of the right foot. In the seventh, eighth and ninth months, the human qi is on the right; do not needle the yin of the right foot. In the tenth, eleventh and twelfth months, the human qi is on the left; do not needle the yin of the left foot."
