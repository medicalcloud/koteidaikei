# 霊枢 五色第四十九 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The bright hall is the nose; the gate is between the eyebrows; the courtyard is the forehead; the hedge is the side of the cheeks; the screen is the ear gate. The spaces between them should be square and large, all visible from ten paces away. Such a person will surely live to a hundred years."
