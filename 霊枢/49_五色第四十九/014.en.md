# 霊枢 五色第四十九 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Always examine the middle of the gate. Thin and lustrous means wind; deep and turbid means impediment; [color] in the earth [chin] means reversal. This is the norm; speak of each illness by its color."

## Notes

沖 in 沖濁 is provisionally read as "deep". Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
