# 霊枢 五色第四十九 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Each has its regions; since there are regions, use yin to harmonize yang and use yang to harmonize yin. When the regions are clearly understood, ten thousand attempts hit ten thousand times. Being able to distinguish left and right is called the great way; men and women differ in position, hence yin and yang. One who carefully examines luster and dullness is called a good practitioner. Deep and turbid means inside; floating and lustrous means outside. Yellow and red mean wind; green and black mean pain; white means cold. Yellow and greasy-moist means pus; intense red means blood; severe pain means cramps; severe cold means numbness of the skin. The five colors each appear in their regions: examine whether they float or sink to know depth; examine luster or dullness to see success or failure; examine whether scattered or concentrated to know distance; look at whether the color is above or below to know where the illness is; concentrate the spirit in the mind to know past and present. Thus if one does not observe the qi minutely, one cannot know right from wrong; keep the attention fixed without wandering, and one will know what is new and what is old. When the color is bright and not coarse [it is mild]; when deep and dull, it is severe. When neither bright nor lustrous, the illness is not severe. When the color is scattered, unsettled like a colt and not yet gathered, the illness is dispersed with qi pain, and the accumulation has not yet formed. When the kidneys ride over the heart, the heart falls ill first and the kidneys respond; the colors are all like this. In men, color at the king of the face means lower abdominal pain; below it, testicular pain; if it hangs round [below the king of the face], penile pain; high means the root, low means the tip; these are of the fox *shan* and *tui* genital kind. In women, at the king of the face it means illness of the bladder and uterus; scattered means pain, concentrated means accumulation; square or round, left or right, each follows the form of the color. If it follows down to the sacrum, it means discharge; if moist like grease, it is from overeating or unclean food. Left means left, right means right. When the color is deviant, gathering and scattering irregularly, [the illness is] where the facial color points. The colors—green, black, red, white and yellow—are all upright and full, and some appear in other places. When the color in another place is red, and the red is as large as an elm seed, at the king of the face it is untimely. When the top of the color is pointed, the head is empty and it points upward; when the bottom is pointed, it points downward; on the left and right the rule is the same. The five colors name the zang organs: green is the liver, red the heart, white the lungs, yellow the spleen, black the kidneys. The liver unites with the sinews, the heart with the vessels, the lungs with the skin, the spleen with the flesh, and the kidneys with the bones."

## Notes

General principles of color diagnosis. The Yellow Emperor's speech ends here, concluding the chapter.
- The difficult expressions in this unit follow Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: 駒駒然 "unsettled like a colt, scattered and not gathered"; 其圜直 "the color hangs and circles below the king of the face"; 高為本，下為首 "the root and tip of the penis according to whether the color is high or low"; 至胝為淫 "胝 should be 骶 (the sacrum); when the color reaches it there is seeping discharge"; 別郷 "a place other than the proper direction"; 不日 "not in its proper place or time, losing the norm"; 上鋭首空上向 "the pointed end shows the direction in which the pathogen advances, exploiting deficiency".
- The base's 「不粗沈大為甚」 is emended to 「不粗沈夭為甚」. Wikisource (both) read 沈夭; 夭 is adopted as in unit 12.
- The base's 「膏潤為膿亦甚者」 is emended to 「膏潤為膿赤甚者」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 赤甚者為血; 赤 is adopted.
- The base's 「其色亦大如楡」 is emended to 「其色赤大如楡」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*) and Daizhige read 其色赤大如楡莢, taking up 別郷赤者; 赤 is adopted.
- The base's 「肺合脾脾合肉」 is emended to 「肺合皮脾合肉」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 肺合皮, alongside liver–sinews, heart–vessels, spleen–flesh, kidneys–bones; 皮 is adopted.
