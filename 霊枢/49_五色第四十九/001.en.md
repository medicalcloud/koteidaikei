# 霊枢 五色第四十九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong asked the Yellow Emperor, "Are the five colors determined only at the bright hall? This young one does not know what is meant."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. Leigong asks and the Yellow Emperor answers. It describes diagnosis of illness by the regions of the face and the five colors.
