# 霊枢 五色第四十九 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Both outside and inside show it. Feeling the pulse opening, if it is slippery, small, tight and deep, the illness is worsening and is in the center. If the qi at Renying is large, tight and floating, the illness is worsening and is outside. If the pulse opening is floating and slippery, the illness advances daily; if Renying is deep and slippery, the illness diminishes daily. If the pulse opening is slippery and deep, the illness advances daily and is inside; if the Renying pulse is slippery, abundant and floating, the illness advances daily and is outside. When the floating and sinking of the pulses and the size of the qi at Renying and the inch opening are equal, the illness is hard to end. When the illness is in the zang organs, a deep and large pulse means it ends easily, a small one is counterflow. When the illness is in the bowels, a floating and large pulse means it ends easily. When Renying is abundant and firm, there is harm from cold; when the qi opening is abundant and firm, there is harm from food."
