# 霊枢 五色第四十九 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When the five colors appear, each appears in its own color region. Where the bone of a region is sunken, illness surely cannot be avoided. Where [another color] rides over and invades a color region, even if the illness is severe, there is no death."

## Notes

乗襲 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: when changes appear in a color region but only mutually generating or flourishing colors ride over each other, without conquering colors, the patient does not die even if the illness is severe.
