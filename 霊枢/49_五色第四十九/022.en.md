# 霊枢 五色第四十九 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The courtyard is the head and face; above the gate is the throat; the middle of the gate is the lungs; the lower extreme [between the eyes] is the heart; directly below is the liver; left of the liver is the gallbladder; below [the liver] is the spleen; the square top [above the nasal wings] is the stomach; the center [of the cheek] is the large intestine; flanking the large intestine are the kidneys; facing the kidneys is the navel; above the king of the face [tip of the nose] is the small intestine; below the king of the face are the bladder and uterus. The cheekbones are the shoulders; behind the cheekbones are the arms; below the arms are the hands; above the inner canthus are the chest and breasts; rising along the cord [line in front of the ear] is the back; following the jaw downward are the thighs; the center is the knees; below the knees are the shins; below the shins are the feet; the great division [the large folds beside the mouth] is the inner thigh; the great bend [below the cheek] is the kneecap. These are the regions of the five viscera, six bowels, limbs and joints.

## Notes

The correspondence of the regions of the face with the five viscera, six bowels, limbs and joints. The Yellow Emperor's speech continues through unit 23.
- The locations of the regions (lower extreme, square top, center, king of the face, cord, great division, great bend, etc.) are provisionally supplied in brackets following common commentarial views, but are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
