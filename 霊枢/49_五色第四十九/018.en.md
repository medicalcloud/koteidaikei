# 霊枢 五色第四十九 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When a red color the size of a thumb appears on both cheekbones, even though the illness improves somewhat, there will surely be sudden death. When a black color the size of a thumb appears on the courtyard, there will surely be sudden death without illness."
