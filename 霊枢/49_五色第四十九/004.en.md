# 霊枢 五色第四十九 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When the bone of the bright hall is high and raised, level and straight, the five viscera are arranged in order in the center, the six bowels flank both sides, the head and face are above the gate and courtyard, the royal palace [heart] is at the lower extreme, and the five viscera are at peace in the chest, then the true color appears, the color of illness does not show, and the bright hall is moist and clear. How could the five officials not be distinguished?"

## Notes

王宮 in 王宮在于下極 is taken as referring to the heart. Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
