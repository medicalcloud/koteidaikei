# 霊枢 五色第四十九 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When the color is distinct and bright [the illness is mild]; when it is deep and dull, it is severe. When the color moves upward, the illness is worsening; when the color moves downward and disperses like clearing clouds, the illness is about to end. Each of the five colors has its visceral region, its outer region and its inner region. When the color runs from the outer region to the inner, the illness runs from outside to inside; when the color runs from inside to outside, the illness runs from inside to outside. When the illness arises inside, first treat the yin and then the yang; doing the opposite makes it worse. When the illness arises in the yang, first treat the outside and then the inside; doing the opposite makes it worse. When the pulse is slippery, large, intermittent and long, the illness has come from outside; the eyes see things and the mind has aversions; this is a gathering of yang qi, and it ends by transforming it."

## Notes

- The base's 「以明沈大者」 is emended to 「以明沈夭者」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), the *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 沈夭 (the Siku edition has 天): deep and dull color; 夭 is adopted.
- The base's 「者病方以五色」 is emended to 「者病方已五色」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 病方已 "the illness is about to end"; 已 is adopted.
