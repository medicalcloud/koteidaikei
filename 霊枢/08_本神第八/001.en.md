# 霊枢 本神第八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "All methods of needling must first be rooted in the spirit. Blood, vessels, nutritive qi, essence and spirit are what the five viscera store. When they become excessive, overflow and leave the viscera, the essence is lost, the ethereal and corporeal souls fly off, the will and intention are confused, and wisdom and deliberation leave the body. What causes this? Is it the fault of heaven, or the error of man? What is meant by virtue and qi producing essence, spirit, ethereal soul, corporeal soul, heart, intention, will, thought, wisdom and deliberation? May I ask the reason?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter: a question about the meaning of spirit, essence, ethereal and corporeal souls and so on, and the illnesses when they are harmed.
- 夭 in 夭之罪 of the base is read as 天, as other sources have it (see collation).
