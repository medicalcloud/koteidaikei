# 霊枢 本神第八 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "What heaven puts in us is virtue; what earth puts in us is qi. Virtue flows and qi presses, and life arises. Thus what life comes from is called essence; the two essences struggling together are called spirit; what comes and goes following the spirit is called the ethereal soul; what goes out and in alongside the essence is called the corporeal soul; that which takes charge of things is called the heart; what the heart recalls is called intention; what the intention keeps is called will; considering changes on the basis of the will is called thought; longing for the distant on the basis of thought is called deliberation; dealing with things on the basis of deliberation is called wisdom.

## Notes

Qibo's speech continues through unit 5, defining in turn essence, spirit, ethereal soul, corporeal soul, heart, intention, will, thought, deliberation and wisdom.
