# 霊枢 本神第八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the heart has fear and anxious thought, the spirit is harmed; when the spirit is harmed, one is fearful and loses oneself, the bulging muscles break down and the flesh wastes, the hair is lusterless and the complexion dull, and death comes in winter. When the spleen has worry that does not resolve, the intention is harmed; when the intention is harmed, there is oppression and confusion, the four limbs cannot be raised, the hair is lusterless and the complexion dull, and death comes in spring. When the liver has grief stirring the center, the ethereal soul is harmed; when the ethereal soul is harmed, there is madness and forgetfulness without clarity; without clarity, [conduct] is not proper; the genitals retract and the sinews cramp, the ribs of both flanks cannot be raised, the hair is lusterless and the complexion dull, and death comes in autumn. When the lungs have boundless joy, the corporeal soul is harmed; when the corporeal soul is harmed, there is madness; the mad have no regard for others in their mind, the skin is scorched, the hair is lusterless and the complexion dull, and death comes in summer. When the kidneys have great anger that does not stop, the will is harmed; when the will is harmed, one often forgets what one has just said, the lower back and spine cannot bend forward or back, flex or extend, the hair is lusterless and the complexion dull, and death comes in late summer. When fear and dread do not resolve, the essence is harmed; when the essence is harmed, the bones ache and there is wilting with reversal, and the essence sometimes descends by itself. Therefore the five viscera govern the storing of essence; they must not be harmed; if harmed, they lose their guard and the yin is depleted; when the yin is depleted, there is no qi; without qi, there is death. Therefore one who uses the needle observes the patient's condition to know whether the essence, spirit, ethereal and corporeal souls are present or lost. If the five are already harmed, the needle cannot treat them.

## Notes

The harm of the emotions to each of the five viscera and the times of death.
- 不正当人 is provisionally translated as "[conduct] is not proper"; its punctuation and meaning are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
