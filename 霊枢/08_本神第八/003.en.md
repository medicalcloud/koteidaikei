# 霊枢 本神第八 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Thus the wise, in nourishing life, always follow the four seasons and adapt to cold and heat, harmonize joy and anger and live in peace, regulate yin and yang and balance the hard and soft. In this way, deviant pathogens do not arrive, and one lives long and sees long. Therefore, fear and anxious thought harm the spirit; when the spirit is harmed, there is fear and dread, and [the essence] flows out without stopping. Grief and sorrow stirring the center exhaust and cut off [the qi] and life is lost. Joy and pleasure make the spirit scatter and not be stored. Worry and anxiety make the qi blocked and not move. Great anger makes one confused and disordered. Fear and dread make the spirit agitated and not gathered.
