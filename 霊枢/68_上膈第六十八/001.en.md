# 霊枢 上膈第六十八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When qi causes upper diaphragm blockage, food and drink come back out as soon as they enter—this I already know. When worms cause lower diaphragm blockage, the food comes out only after a full day; I have not grasped the meaning. I wish to hear it all."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, explaining how lower diaphragm blockage due to worms arises and how to needle it.
- Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: diaphragm disorders are divided into upper and lower, and differ in being caused by qi or by worms. Those due to qi are above, and food comes out as soon as it enters; those due to worms are below, and food comes out after 晬時, a full day and night.
