# 霊枢 上膈第六十八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Press the abscess lightly and observe where the qi goes; first needle shallowly beside it, insert gradually deeper, then return and needle it, not more than three times. Examine whether it is sunken or floating to decide the depth. After needling, always apply a hot compress so that heat enters inside; day by day let the heat go in, the pathogenic qi will weaken further, and even a large abscess will burst. Weigh [diet and daily life] and keep the prohibitions to prevent [further] internal injury; be tranquil and free of striving, and the qi can circulate. Afterwards, digest the grain with salty and bitter [flavors], and [the food] will go down."

## Notes

The end of the chapter. The final 潰 is the headword of a sound gloss (interlinear note 音会).
- 還而刺之，毋過三行 follows the *Leijing* note: first needle shallowly beside it and gradually deeper to release the flowing pathogen, then return to needle the main point of the illness to uproot the accumulation; stop after two or three times, never more. Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* glosses 還 as "again".
- 伍以参禁 follows the *Leijing* note: three combined is 参, five aligned is 伍; in eating, resting and daily life one must weigh what is suitable and keep the prohibitions, so as to prevent further internal injury.
- The *Jiayi jing* reads 後服酸苦化穀 for 後以鹹苦化穀.
