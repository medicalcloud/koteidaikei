# 霊枢 上膈第六十八 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When joy and anger are not appropriate, eating and drinking are immoderate, and cold and warmth are untimely, cold juices flow into the intestines; when they flow into the intestines the worms become cold; when the worms are cold they gather and hold to the lower duct, the intestines and stomach become distended, the wei-defensive qi does not circulate, and pathogenic qi settles there. When a person eats, the worms rise to feed; when they rise to feed the lower duct is deficient; when it is deficient the pathogenic qi overcomes it, accumulates and lingers; lingering, it forms an abscess; when the abscess forms, the lower duct is constricted. When the abscess is inside the duct, it is sunken and the pain deep; when it is outside, the abscess is external, the pain superficial, and the skin over it hot."

## Notes

管 is the same as 脘 (*Lingshu jizhu*). Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* gives three causes: joy and anger injuring the spirit, immoderate eating and drinking, and receiving cold and warmth out of season.
- The base's 即而痛深 is emended to 則沈而痛深. The *Taisu* and the *Jiayi jing* read 則沈而痛深, and Xiao Yanping's note says the *Lingshu* writes 即 for the two characters 則沈 (the *Leijing* and the *Lingshu jizhu* agree with the base). The following 則癰外而痛浮 forms a pair (sunken/floating, deep/superficial), so the form of the *Taisu* and the *Jiayi jing* is adopted.
