# 霊枢 五味論第六十三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Shaoyu, "When the five flavors enter the mouth, each goes somewhere and each causes illness. Sour goes to the sinews; eating much of it causes urinary block. Salty goes to the blood; eating much of it causes thirst. Acrid goes to the qi; eating much of it causes a hollow heart [the heart qi drains away]. Bitter goes to the bones; eating much of it causes vomiting. Sweet goes to the flesh; eating much of it causes oppression of the heart. I know that this is so, but not why. I wish to hear the reason."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. The interlocutor is Shaoyu. It explains where each of the five flavors goes and why eating much of it causes illness.
- 洞心 follows Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu*: "the heart qi flows out and leaks rapidly".
