# 霊枢 五味論第六十三 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu said, "When bitter enters the stomach, none of the qi of the five grains can overcome it; bitter enters the lower duct, the paths of the sanjiao all close and are blocked, hence vomiting. The teeth are where the bones end, so bitter enters and goes to the bones; that it enters and comes back out shows that it goes to the bones."

## Notes

入而復出 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: excessive bitter suppresses the stomach yang so it cannot transform; the paths of the sanjiao close, so it enters and comes back out as vomiting; and since the teeth are the end of the bones, the bitter that reaches the bones cannot be received and comes back out through the mouth and teeth.
