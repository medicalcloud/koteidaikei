# 霊枢 五味論第六十三 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu answered, "When sour enters the stomach, its qi is astringent and contracting; the two upper jiao cannot move it out or in. Not going out, it stays in the stomach; when the stomach is mild and warm, it flows down to the bladder. The bladder's sac is thin and soft; meeting sour it shrinks and curls, constricts and closes, the waterways do not flow, hence urinary block. The genitals are where the sinews gather and end, so sour enters and goes to the sinews."

## Notes

陰者，積筋之所終也 follows Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu*: "the genitals are where all the sinews of the body end and gather, so sour enters and goes to them".
