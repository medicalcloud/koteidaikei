# 霊枢 五味論第六十三 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu said, "When acrid enters the stomach, its qi goes to the upper jiao. The upper jiao receives qi and circulates it through the yang. The qi of ginger and chives fumigates it; the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi receive it at all times, and it lingers below the heart, hence the hollow heart. Acrid moves together with the qi, so it enters and comes out together with sweat."
