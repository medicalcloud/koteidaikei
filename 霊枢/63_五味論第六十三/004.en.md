# 霊枢 五味論第六十三 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu said, "When salty enters the stomach, its qi rises to the middle burner and pours into the vessels; the blood and qi run there, and when blood meets salt it congeals; when it congeals, the juices of the stomach pour into it; when they pour, the stomach is drained; when drained, the throat is parched, so the root of the tongue is dry and there is frequent thirst. The blood vessels are the path of the middle burner, so salty enters and goes to the blood."
