# 霊枢 五味論第六十三 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu said, "When sweet enters the stomach, its qi is weak and small and cannot rise to the upper burner; staying in the stomach with the grain, it makes one soft and moist. When the stomach is soft it is slack; when slack, the worms move; when the worms move, they cause oppression of the heart. Its qi reaches the flesh outside, so sweet goes to the flesh."

The end of the chapter.
