# 霊枢 経別第十一 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The divergent of the hand greater yang points to the earth, separates at the shoulder joint, enters the armpit, runs to the heart and attaches to the small intestine. The divergent of the hand lesser yin separates to enter between the two sinews at Yuanye, belongs to the heart, runs up to the throat, comes out at the face and unites at the inner canthus. This is the fourth union.

## Notes

The fourth union (hand greater yang and hand lesser yin).
