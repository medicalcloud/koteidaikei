# 霊枢 経別第十一 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The divergent of the hand *yangming* goes from the hand along the chest and breast, separates at Jianyu, enters the pillar bone, runs down to the large intestine, belongs to the lungs, rises along the throat, comes out at the empty basin and unites with the *yangming*. The divergent of the hand greater yin separates to enter Yuanye in front of the lesser yin, enters and runs to the lungs, disperses to the large intestine, rises to come out at the empty basin, follows the throat and again unites with the *yangming*. This is the sixth union."

## Notes

The sixth union (hand *yangming* and hand greater yin). Qibo's speech ends here, concluding the chapter.
- For 散之太陽 of the base, one source reads 散之大腸 (see collation); from the meaning, the translation follows it.
