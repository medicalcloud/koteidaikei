# 霊枢 経別第十一 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The divergent of the foot greater yang separates to enter the back of the knee; one path goes five *cun* below the buttocks, separates to enter the anus, belongs to the bladder, disperses to the kidneys, follows the paravertebral muscles and at the level of the heart enters and disperses; the straight part goes from the paravertebral muscles up to come out at the nape and again belongs to the greater yang. This is one channel. The divergent of the foot lesser yin reaches the back of the knee, separates and runs to the greater yang and unites with it, rises to the kidneys, at the fourteenth vertebra comes out to belong to the girdle vessel; the straight part attaches to the root of the tongue, again comes out at the nape and unites with the greater yang. This is the first union. Sometimes the separations of all the yin are all regarded as divergents.

## Notes

The first union (foot greater yang and foot lesser yin).
- The base's 「当十四顀」 is emended to 「当十四椎」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 椎 (the *Leijing* agrees with the base); 椎 (vertebra) is adopted.
- The base's 「成以諸陰之別」 is emended to 「或以諸陰之別」. The Lingshu editions, *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* agree with the base (成), but the *Taisu* reads 或以諸陰之別, and a note in the *Jiayi jing* quotes the *Jiuxu* as 或以諸陰之別皆為正也. "Sometimes the divergences of the yin channels are all regarded as divergent channels" makes sense, so 或 is adopted.
