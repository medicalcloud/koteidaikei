# 霊枢 経別第十一 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The divergent of the foot greater yang separates to enter the back of the knee; one path goes five *cun* below the buttocks, separates to enter the anus, belongs to the bladder, disperses to the kidneys, follows the paravertebral muscles and at the level of the heart enters and disperses; the straight part goes from the paravertebral muscles up to come out at the nape and again belongs to the greater yang. This is one channel. The divergent of the foot lesser yin reaches the back of the knee, separates and runs to the greater yang and unites with it, rises to the kidneys, at the fourteenth vertebra comes out to belong to the girdle vessel; the straight part attaches to the root of the tongue, again comes out at the nape and unites with the greater yang. This is the first union. The separations of all the yin, completing [their union], all become divergents.

## Notes

The first union (foot greater yang and foot lesser yin).
- The punctuation and meaning of 成以諸陰之別，皆為正也 are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); translated provisionally.
- The base's 「当十四顀」 is emended to 「当十四椎」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 椎 (the *Leijing* agrees with the base); 椎 (vertebra) is adopted.
