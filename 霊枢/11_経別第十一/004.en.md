# 霊枢 経別第十一 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The divergent of the foot lesser yang winds around the thigh, enters the pubic hairline and unites with the *jueyin*; its separate part enters between the free ribs, follows the inside of the chest, belongs to the gallbladder, disperses up to the liver, passes through the heart, then rises to flank the throat, comes out at the chin and jaw, disperses over the face, attaches to the eye connection, and unites with the lesser yang at the outer canthus. The divergent of the foot *jueyin* separates on the instep, rises to the pubic hairline, unites with the lesser yang, and travels together with its separate part. This is the second union.

## Notes

The second union (foot lesser yang and foot *jueyin*).
- 一 in 此為一合 of the base is translated as 二 "second", as other sources have it (see collation).
