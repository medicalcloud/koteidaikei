# 霊枢 経別第十一 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The divergent of the foot *Yangming* rises to the thigh, enters the inside of the abdomen, belongs to the stomach, disperses to the spleen, rises to communicate with the heart, rises along the throat, comes out at the mouth, rises to the bridge of the nose and below the eye, returns to attach to the eye connection, and unites with the *Yangming*. The divergent of the foot greater yin rises to the thigh, unites with the *Yangming*, travels together with its separate part, rises to knot at the throat and passes through the tongue. This is the third union.

## Notes

The third union (foot *Yangming* and foot greater yin).
