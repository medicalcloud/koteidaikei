# 霊枢 経別第十一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I have heard how people accord with the way of heaven. Within are the five zang organs, corresponding to the five notes, five colors, five seasons, five flavors and five positions; without are the six fu organs, corresponding to the six pitch pipes. The six pitch pipes establish the yin and yang meridians and match them with the twelve months, twelve branches, twelve nodal periods, twelve meridian waters, twelve double hours and twelve meridians. This is how the five zang organs and six fu organs correspond to the way of heaven. The twelve meridians are that by which people live, by which illness forms, by which people are treated, by which illness arises; where learning begins and where the practitioner's skill reaches its limit; what the crude find easy and the superior find difficult. May I ask how they separate and unite, go out and enter?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter: a question about how the "divergent meridians" (called 正 in the text), which branch from the meridians, separate, unite, go out and enter.
