# 霊枢 経別第十一 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo bowed his head to the ground twice and said, "What an enlightened question! This is what the crude pass over and what the superior dwell upon. Let me tell you all of it.

## Notes

Qibo's speech continues through unit 8, describing the divergent meridians of paired meridians as six unions.
