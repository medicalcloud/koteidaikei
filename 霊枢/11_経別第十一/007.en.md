# 霊枢 経別第十一 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The divergent of the hand lesser yang points to heaven, separates at the vertex, enters the empty basin, runs down to the sanjiao and disperses in the chest. The divergent of the hand heart governor separates and descends three *cun* below Yuanye, enters the chest, separately belongs to the sanjiao, comes out to follow the throat, comes out behind the ear, and unites with the lesser yang below the mastoid bone. This is the fifth union.

## Notes

The fifth union (hand lesser yang and hand heart governor).
