# 霊枢 天年第五十四 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the paths of the messengers [the seven openings used by the five viscera] are deep and long, the base and walls [the bony frame and outline of the face] are high and square, the nutritive and defensive flow harmoniously, the three regions and three districts [of the face] are raised, the bones high and the flesh full, then one may reach a hundred years before the end."

## Notes

使道 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: the seven openings, the paths used by the five viscera (lung qi to the nose, liver qi to the eyes, etc.); 隧 means deep; 基牆 refers to the face (bones as base, cheeks and ears as walls). Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* takes 使道 as the nostrils. The *Leijing* gives no specific explanation of 三部三里; it is provisionally translated as the three regions of the face, but its exact meaning is unknown.
