# 霊枢 天年第五十四 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the five zang organs are firm, the blood vessels harmonious, the flesh supple, the skin dense, the movement of the ying-nutrient and wei-defense does not depart from its norm, breathing is light and slow, the qi moves according to its measure, the six fu organs transform grain, and the fluids spread, each according to its norm, then one can live long."
