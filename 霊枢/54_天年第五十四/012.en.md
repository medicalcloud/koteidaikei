# 霊枢 天年第五十四 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the five zang organs are all weak, the paths of the messengers are short, the openings gape outward, breathing is rapid, the base and walls are low, the vessels are thin with little blood, the flesh is not firm, one is often struck by wind and cold, the blood and qi are deficient, the vessels are blocked, and the true and pathogenic attack each other, disordered and pulling at each other, then life ends at middle age."

## Notes

The end of the chapter.
- 空外以張 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* ("the nine openings gape and are exposed"), and 石 in 其肉不石 follows its note "石 means firm".
