# 霊枢 天年第五十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I wish to hear: when a person first comes into being, what qi is built up as the foundation, and what is set up as the framework? What, when lost, brings death, and what, when gained, brings life?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, describing human birth, life span, and the rise and decline of each decade.
- 楯 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "基 is the foundation, 楯 the building material".
