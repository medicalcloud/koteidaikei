# 霊枢 天年第五十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the blood and qi are harmonized, the nutritive and defensive flow, the five viscera are formed, the spirit qi dwells in the heart, and the ethereal and corporeal souls are complete, then a human being is formed."
