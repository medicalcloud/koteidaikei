# 霊枢 天年第五十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the blood and qi are harmonized, the ying-nutrient and wei-defense flow, the five zang organs are formed, the spirit qi dwells in the heart, and the ethereal and corporeal souls are complete, then a human being is formed."
