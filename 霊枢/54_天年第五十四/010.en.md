# 霊枢 天年第五十四 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "At ten years of age, the five zang organs begin to settle, the blood and qi already flow, and the qi is below, so one likes to run. At twenty, the blood and qi begin to flourish and the flesh is growing, so one likes to hurry. At thirty, the five zang organs are well settled, the flesh firm, and the blood vessels abundant and full, so one likes to walk. At forty, the five zang organs, six fu organs and twelve meridians are all greatly abundant and level; the interstices begin to loosen, the bloom of the complexion fades, the hair grows quite gray, and [the qi], level and abundant, no longer stirs, so one likes to sit. At fifty, the liver qi begins to decline, the lobes of the liver thin, the bile begins to diminish, and the eyes begin to dim. At sixty, the heart qi begins to decline, one suffers from worry and sorrow, the blood and qi are sluggish, so one likes to lie down. At seventy, the spleen qi is deficient and the skin withers. At eighty, the lung qi declines and the corporeal soul departs, so one often misspeaks. At ninety, the kidney qi is scorched and the meridians of the four [other] zang organs are empty. At a hundred, the five zang organs are all deficient, the spirit qi has all gone, the body alone remains, and life ends."

## Notes

The rise and decline of qi in each decade.
- The base's 「胆汁始滅」 is emended to 「胆汁始減」. Daizhige, the *Taisu* and *Leijing* read 減 (diminish); 滅 is taken as a graphic error.
- For 苦 in 苦憂悲, the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* agree with the base, while the *Taisu* has 喜 and Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*) 善. The base makes sense and is kept. 悮 is a variant of 誤.
