# 霊枢 本輸第二 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The gallbladder emerges at Qiaoyin. Qiaoyin is at the tip of the toe next to the little toe; it is the well, metal. It flows to Xiaxi; Xiaxi is between the little toe and the next; it is the brook. It pours into Linqi; Linqi is in the depression one and a half *cun* further up; it is the stream. It passes Qiuxu; Qiuxu is in the depression in front of and below the outer ankle; it is the source. It moves to Yangfu; Yangfu is above the outer ankle, in front of the auxiliary bone, at the end of the severed bone; it is the river. It enters the Yang Mound Spring; the Yang Mound Spring is in the depression on the outside of the knee; it is the uniting point, found by stretching. This is foot lesser yang.

## Notes

The five transport points and the source point of the gallbladder (foot lesser yang).
