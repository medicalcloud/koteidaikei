# 霊枢 本輸第二 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Foot *Yangming* is the pulsing vessel flanking the throat; its transport points are in the chest. Hand *Yangming* comes next, outside its transport points, one *cun* short of the curve of the cheek. Hand greater yang is at the curve of the cheek. Foot lesser yang is below the ear, behind the curve of the cheek. Hand lesser yang emerges behind the ear and goes up over the mastoid. Foot greater yang is at the hairline within the large sinews flanking the nape. The yin chi pulsing vessel is at Wuli; it is forbidden among the five transport points.

## Notes

The positions of the vessels of the neck; Wuli is a forbidden point.
- Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* says "since yang is the cun, yin is the chi": the yin [inner] side of the chi region (forearm). Wuli, three *cun* above the elbow, is a transport point of the great vessel qi of the five zang organs and is forbidden to needle (Yang Shangshan, citing the *Mingtang*).
