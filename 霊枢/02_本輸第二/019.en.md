# 霊枢 本輸第二 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In spring take the collaterals, the brook points, and between the great meridians and the divisions of the flesh; take deeply for severe cases and shallowly for mild ones. In summer take the stream points, the minute collaterals, and above the muscles and skin. In autumn take the uniting points; otherwise as in spring. In winter take the well and stream points, aiming deep and retaining [the needle]. This is the order of the four seasons, where the qi dwells, where illness lodges, and what suits the zang organs. For cramps, take with the patient standing, and they can be ended; for wilting and reversal, needle with the limbs stretched out, and immediate relief can be had."

## Notes

The differences in point selection by season and the needling of cramps and wilting-reversal, concluding the chapter. Qibo's speech ends here.
