# 霊枢 本輸第二 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The large intestine joins above with hand *Yangming* and emerges at Shangyang. Shangyang is at the tip of the finger next to the thumb; it is the well, metal. It flows to Erjian in front of the base joint; it is the brook. It pours into Sanjian behind the base joint; it is the stream. It passes Hegu; Hegu is between the forked bones of the thumb; it is the source. It moves to Yangxi; Yangxi is in the depression between the two sinews; it is the river. It enters Quchi, in the depression of the auxiliary bone outside the elbow, found by bending the arm; it is the uniting point. This is hand *Yangming*.

## Notes

The five transport points and source point of the large intestine (hand *Yangming*).
