# 霊枢 本輸第二 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When needling Shangguan, [the mouth is] open and cannot close; when needling Xiaguan, [the mouth is] closed and cannot open. When needling Dubi, [the knee is] bent and cannot be stretched; when needling the Two Gates, [the limb is] stretched and cannot be bent.

## Notes

The postures for needling Shangguan, Xiaguan, Dubi and the Two Gates.
- The meanings of 呿 and 欠 (opening or closing the mouth) and what 両関 refers to are disputed. The translation is provisional and has not been verified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
