# 霊枢 本輸第二 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When needling Shangguan, [the mouth is] open and cannot close; when needling Xiaguan, [the mouth is] closed and cannot open. When needling Dubi, [the knee is] bent and cannot be stretched; when needling the Two Gates [Neiguan and Waiguan], [the limb is] stretched and cannot be bent.

## Notes

The postures for needling Shangguan, Xiaguan, Dubi and the Two Gates.
- Translated following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: 呿 is opening the mouth, 欠 opening and then closing it. Shangguan (Kezhuren) has an opening when the mouth is open, so it is taken with the mouth open; Xiaguan has one when the mouth is closed. The Two Gates are Neiguan and Waiguan, both taken with the arm stretched.
