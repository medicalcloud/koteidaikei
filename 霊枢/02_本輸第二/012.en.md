# 霊枢 本輸第二 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Hand greater yang, the small intestine, joins above with greater yang and emerges at Shaoze. Shaoze is at the tip of the little finger; it is the well, metal. It flows to Qiangu; Qiangu is in the depression in front of the base joint on the outer edge of the hand; it is the brook. It pours into Houxi; Houxi is behind the base joint on the outer side of the hand; it is the stream. It passes Wangu; Wangu is in front of the wrist bone on the outer side of the hand; it is the source. It moves to Yanggu; Yanggu is in the depression below the sharp bone; it is the river. It enters Xiaohai; Xiaohai is in the depression outside the large bone on the inside of the elbow, half a *cun* from the tip, found with the arm stretched; it is the uniting point. This is the hand greater yang channel.

## Notes

The five transport points and source point of the small intestine (hand greater yang).
