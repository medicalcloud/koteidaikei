# 霊枢 本輸第二 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The kidneys emerge at Yongquan. Yongquan is the heart of the sole; it is the well, wood. They flow to Rangu; Rangu is below the Ran bone; it is the brook. They pour into Daxi; Daxi is in the depression behind the inner ankle and above the heel bone; it is the stream. They move to Fuliu; Fuliu is two *cun* above the inner ankle, moving without rest; it is the river. They enter Yingu; Yingu is behind the auxiliary bone, below the large sinew and above the small sinew; pressed, it responds to the hand; it is found by bending the knee; it is the uniting point. This is the foot lesser yin meridian.

## Notes

The five transport points of the kidneys (foot lesser yin).
