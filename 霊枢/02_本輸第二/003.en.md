# 霊枢 本輸第二 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The lungs emerge at Shaoshang. Shaoshang is at the inner side of the tip of the thumb; it is the well, [of] wood. They flow to Yuji; Yuji is the fish of the hand [thenar eminence]; it is the brook. They pour into Dayuan; Dayuan is in the depression one *cun* behind the fish; it is the stream. They move to Jingqu; Jingqu is within the inch opening, moving without rest; it is the river. They enter Chize; Chize is the pulsing vessel in the elbow; it is the uniting point. This is the hand greater yin channel.

## Notes

The five transport points of the lungs (hand greater yin).
