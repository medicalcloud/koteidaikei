# 霊枢 本輸第二 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The bladder emerges at Zhiyin. Zhiyin is at the tip of the little toe; it is the well, metal. It flows to Tonggu; Tonggu is on the outer side in front of the base joint; it is the brook. It pours into 朿 [Shu]gu; Shugu is in the depression behind the base joint; it is the stream. It passes Jinggu; Jinggu is below the large bone on the outer side of the foot; it is the source. It moves to Kunlun; Kunlun is behind the outer ankle, above the heel bone; it is the river. It enters Weizhong; Weizhong is the center of the back of the knee; it is the uniting point, taken with the knee bent. This is foot greater yang.

## Notes

The five transport points and the source point of the bladder (foot greater yang). For the six fu organs the well is metal, and the source point is added to make six transport points.
- 朿骨 is read as 束骨.
