# 霊枢 本輸第二 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Let me speak of them in order.

## Notes

Qibo begins his answer, going through the five transport points (well, brook, stream, river, uniting; for the bowels also the source) of the five zang organs and six fu organs. Qibo's speech continues through unit 19.
