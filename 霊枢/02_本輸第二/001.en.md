# 霊枢 本輸第二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "The way of needling requires mastery of where the twelve channels end and begin, where the network vessels branch off, where the five transport points lodge, what the six bowels are joined with, where [the qi] goes out and in through the four seasons, where the five viscera flow and dwell, the measures of width, the conditions of shallow and deep, and how high and low they reach. I wish to hear the explanation."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter: a question about the knowledge of channels, transport points and so on needed for needling.
