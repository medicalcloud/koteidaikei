# 霊枢 本輸第二 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The triple burner joins above with hand lesser yang and emerges at Guanchong. Guanchong is at the tip of the finger next to the little finger; it is the well, metal. It flows to Yemen; Yemen is between the little finger and the next; it is the brook. It pours into Zhongzhu; Zhongzhu is in the depression behind the base joint; it is the stream. It passes Yangchi; Yangchi is in the depression above the wrist; it is the source. It moves to Zhigou; Zhigou is in the depression between the two bones three *cun* above the wrist; it is the river. It enters Tianjing; Tianjing is in the depression above the large bone on the outside of the elbow; it is the uniting point, found by bending the elbow. The lower transport point of the triple burner lies in front of the big toe [greater yang] and behind lesser yang, emerging at the outer edge of the back of the knee, and is called Weiyang; it is the network vessel of greater yang. This is the hand lesser yang channel. The triple burner is led by foot lesser yang and greater yin [yang] and is a branch of greater yang; five *cun* above the ankle it branches in, threads the calf, emerges at Weiyang, runs alongside the main greater yang vessel, enters to network the bladder, and binds the lower burner. When replete there is urinary blockage; when depleted, incontinence. For incontinence supplement; for blockage drain.

## Notes

The five transport points and source point of the triple burner (hand lesser yang), its lower transport point (Weiyang), and the treatment of its illnesses. The interlinear notes are not translated (original text only).
- 足大指之前 does not fit as a location, and some take it as an error for 足太陽之前; for 太陰 the interlinear note says another edition has 陽. Both are translated provisionally and have not been verified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
