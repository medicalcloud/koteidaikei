# 霊枢 本輸第二 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The lungs are joined with the large intestine; the large intestine is the bowel of conveyance. The heart is joined with the small intestine; the small intestine is the bowel of reception. The liver is joined with the gallbladder; the gallbladder is the bowel of central essence. The spleen is joined with the stomach; the stomach is the bowel of the five grains. The kidneys are joined with the bladder; the bladder is the bowel of fluids. Lesser yang belongs to the kidneys, and the kidneys connect above with the lungs; hence it leads two zang organs. The sanjiao is the bowel of central drainage; the waterways come from it; it belongs to the bladder; it is the solitary bowel. These are what the six fu organs are joined with.

## Notes

The pairing of zang-fu organs and the functions of the bowels.
- For 少陽 in 少陽属腎, the *Taisu* and *Jiayi jing* have 少陰, but the Lingshu editions, *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* agree with the base. Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* explains 少陽 as the sanjiao, which belongs to the kidneys because its lower transport point belongs to the bladder, the kidneys' paired bowel; this also connects with the following 三焦者，中瀆之府也, so the base is kept.
