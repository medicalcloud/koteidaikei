# 霊枢 本輸第二 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

These are called the transport points of the five viscera and six bowels: five times five, twenty-five; six times six, thirty-six. The six bowels all emerge from the three yang of the foot and join above with the hand.

## Notes

A summary of the number of transport points of the viscera and bowels.
