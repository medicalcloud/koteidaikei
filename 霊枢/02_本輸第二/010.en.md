# 霊枢 本輸第二 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The stomach emerges at Lidui. Lidui is at the tip of the toe next to the big toe on its inner side; it is the well, metal. It flows to Neiting; Neiting is in the outer space of the next toe; it is the brook. It pours into Xiangu; Xiangu is in the depression two *cun* up from the inner space of the middle toe; it is the stream. It passes Chongyang; Chongyang is in the depression five *cun* up on the instep; it is the source, found by shaking the foot. It moves to Jiexi; Jiexi is in the depression one and a half *cun* above Chongyang; it is the river. It enters Xialing; Xialing is three *cun* below the knee, outside the shinbone, [called] Sanli; it is the uniting point. Three *cun* further below Sanli is Juxu Shanglian; three *cun* further below Shanglian is Juxu Xialian. The large intestine belongs to the upper, the small intestine to the lower; it is the foot *Yangming* stomach vessel. The large and small intestines both belong to the stomach; this is foot *Yangming*.

## Notes

The five transport points and the source point of the stomach (foot *Yangming*), and the lower uniting points of the large and small intestines (Juxu Shanglian and Xialian).
