# 霊枢 本輸第二 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the middle of the supraclavicular fossa is the *ren* vessel, called Tiantu. Next after it, the pulsing vessel beside the *ren* vessel is foot *yangming*, called Renying. The second vessel is hand *yangming*, called Futu. The third vessel is hand greater yang, called Tianchuang. The fourth vessel is foot lesser yang, called Tianrong. The fifth vessel is hand lesser yang, called Tianyou. The sixth vessel is foot greater yang, called Tianzhu. The seventh vessel, the vessel in the middle of the neck, is the *du* vessel, called Fengfu. The pulsing vessel inside the armpit is hand greater yin, called Tianfu. Three *cun* below the armpit is the hand heart master, called Tianchi.

## Notes

The order of the points of the vessels of the neck, with Tianfu and Tianchi.
- Assigning Tianrong to foot lesser yang differs from the later assignment (hand greater yang); this is not discussed.
