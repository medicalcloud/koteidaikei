# 霊枢 本輸第二 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The spleen emerges at Yinbai. Yinbai is at the inner side of the tip of the big toe; it is the well, wood. It flows to Dadu; Dadu is in the depression below and behind the base joint; it is the brook. It pours into Taibai; Taibai is below the 腕骨 [the protruding bone]; it is the stream. It moves to Shangqiu; Shangqiu is in the depression below the inner ankle; it is the river. It enters the Yin Mound Spring; the Yin Mound Spring is in the depression below the auxiliary bone, found by stretching; it is the uniting point. This is foot greater yin.

## Notes

The five transport points of the spleen (foot greater yin).
- 腕骨 in "Taibai is below the 腕骨" is often understood as the head of the first metatarsal, but this has not been verified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
