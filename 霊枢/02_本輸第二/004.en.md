# 霊枢 本輸第二 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The heart emerges at Zhongchong. Zhongchong is at the tip of the middle finger; it is the well, wood. It flows to Laogong; Laogong is in the palm, on the inner side of the base joint of the middle finger; it is the brook. It pours into Daling; Daling is behind the palm, right below between the two bones; it is the stream. It moves to Jianshi; the course of Jianshi is between the two sinews, within three *cun*; when there is a fault [illness] it arrives, when there is none it stops; it is the river. It enters Quze; Quze is in the depression below the inner edge of the elbow, found by bending; it is the uniting point. This is hand lesser yin.

## Notes

The five transport points of the heart. The points listed are those of the later hand *Jueyin* pericardium meridian, but the text calls it hand lesser yin.
