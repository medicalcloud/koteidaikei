# 霊枢 本輸第二 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The liver emerges at Dadun. Dadun is at the tip of the big toe, in the midst of the three hairs; it is the well, wood. It flows to Xingjian; Xingjian is between the big toes; it is the brook. It pours into Dachong; Dachong is in the depression two *cun* above Xingjian; it is the stream. It moves to Zhongfeng; Zhongfeng is in the depression one and a half *cun* in front of the inner ankle; if made to go counter it becomes stagnant, if harmonized it flows; it is found by shaking the foot; it is the river. It enters Ququan; Ququan is below the auxiliary bone, above the large sinew, found by bending the knee; it is the uniting point. This is foot *jueyin*.

## Notes

The five transport points of the liver (foot *jueyin*).
