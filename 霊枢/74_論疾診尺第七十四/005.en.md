# 霊枢 論疾診尺第七十四 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a person is ill and the pulses at the inch opening and Renying are equal in size and equal in floating and sinking, the illness is hard to cure. When a woman's hand lesser yin pulse beats strongly, she is pregnant. When an infant is ill and the hair of its head all stands on end, it will surely die. When blue vessels appear around the ears, there is pulling pain. With red shreds in the stool and diarrhea with undigested food, a small pulse and cold hands and feet, it is hard to cure; with diarrhea and a small pulse but warm hands and feet, the diarrhea is easily cured.

## Notes

Translated following the *Leijing* notes.
- On the inch opening and Renying: the qi opening reflects yin and Renying yang; in spring and summer Renying is slightly larger, in autumn and winter the inch opening; when the two are equal in size and depth, there is a one-sided leaning to yin or yang, so the illness is hard to cure (*Leijing*).
- 手少陰脈: Wang Bing (Qixuanzi) takes it as the hollow behind the palm on the little-finger side where the pulse is felt, i.e. Shenmen (*Leijing*).
- 頭毛皆逆上: hair is the flourishing of kidney water; when water is insufficient the hair is dry and scorched, standing stiff like withered grass (*Leijing*).
- 耳間青脈起者，掣痛: the ear belongs to the lesser-yang gallbladder channel and blue is the color of the reverting-yin liver; the liver governs the sinews, hence pulling pain (*Leijing*).
- 赤弁: filthy blood forming streaks or shreds; 弁 should be 瓣 (*Leijing*).
- 者手足寒難已飱泄脈小 is supplied into the base's 飱泄脈小手足温. The *Taisu*, the *Leijing*, the *Lingshu jizhu*, the Siku edition and the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource, and the Daizhige text all read 飧泄脈小者手足寒難已，飧泄脈小手足温泄易已, contrasting cold and warm hands and feet. The base is taken to have dropped the phrase by eye-skip between the two occurrences of 飱泄脈小.
- The base's 泄易巳 is emended to 泄易已. The *Leijing* (main text) and the *Lingshu jizhu* read 泄易已; 巳 makes no sense, so it is taken as an error for the graphically similar 已.
