# 霊枢 論疾診尺第七十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I wish, without looking at the complexion or taking the pulse, to examine only the forearm, and so state the illness, knowing the inside from the outside. How is it done?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. It describes diagnosis by the skin of the forearm, diagnosis by the eyes, teeth, blood vessels and in infants, and the seasonal transformations of illness.
