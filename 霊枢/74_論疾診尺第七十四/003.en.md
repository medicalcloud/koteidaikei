# 霊枢 論疾診尺第七十四 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

If the eyes are red, the illness is in the heart; white, in the lungs; azure, in the liver; yellow, in the spleen; black, in the kidneys; a yellow that cannot be named, in the chest. In diagnosing eye pain: red vessels running from above downward indicate greater-yang illness; from below upward, *Yangming* illness; from outside inward, lesser-yang illness. In diagnosing cold and heat: if red vessels run from top to bottom to the pupil—one vessel, death in one year; one and a half, in a year and a half; two, in two years; two and a half, in two and a half years; three, in three years. In diagnosing toothache from caries, press the arriving *yang* [*Yangming*] pulse: where it is excessive, [the meridian] alone is hot—on the left, the left is hot; on the right, the right; above, the upper; below, the lower.

## Notes

Translated following the *Leijing* notes.
- On red vessels in eye pain: the foot greater yang is the upper net of the eye, so vessels from above down indicate greater-yang illness; the foot *Yangming* is the lower net, so from below up indicate *Yangming* illness; the foot lesser yang runs behind the outer canthus, so from outside in indicate lesser-yang illness (*Leijing*).
- The red vessels in cold and heat are as in chapter 70 (*Hanre*).
- On caries: the foot *Yangming* enters the upper teeth and the hand *Yangming* the lower; pressing the arriving yang pulse, where it is excessive that meridian alone is hot, and left and right, upper and lower can be told by location (*Leijing*).
