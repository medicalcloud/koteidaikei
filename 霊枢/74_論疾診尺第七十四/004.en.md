# 霊枢 論疾診尺第七十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In diagnosing the blood vessels: much red means much heat; much blue, much pain; much black, chronic impediment; when red, black and blue all appear in abundance, there is cold and heat. Body pain with a slightly yellow complexion, yellow tartar on the teeth and yellow nails is jaundice. One who likes to lie quietly, with yellow-red urine and a small, rough pulse, has no appetite.

## Notes

The *Leijing* notes: the blood vessels are the network vessels of each region; when red, black and blue all appear, these are colors of yin and yang alternately prevailing, so there is cold or heat. A small, rough pulse indicates yin jaundice, with weak spleen earth, hence no appetite.
