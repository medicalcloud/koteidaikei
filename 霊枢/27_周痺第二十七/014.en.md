# 霊枢 周痺第二十七 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Excellent. I have grasped its meaning and also its practice. The classic contains the principle of the nine [needles], [covering] the yin and yang illnesses of the twelve meridians."

## Notes

The end of the chapter. The final 慉 is the headword of a sound gloss; the interlinear note (pronunciation) is not translated (original text only).
- 九者，経巽之理 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "九 is the needles, 巽 means 'contains'; the meaning and method lie in the nine needles, the classic contains the principle, and all yin and yang illnesses of the twelve meridians are covered".
