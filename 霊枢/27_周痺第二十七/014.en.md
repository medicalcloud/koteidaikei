# 霊枢 周痺第二十七 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Excellent. I have grasped its meaning and also its practice. The nine [needles] are the principle of following the channels, [for] the yin and yang illnesses of the twelve channels."

## Notes

The end of the chapter. The final 慉 is the headword of a sound gloss; the interlinear note (pronunciation) is not translated (original text only).
- The meaning of 九者，経巽之理 is unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points). 九 is provisionally read as the nine needles and 巽 as 順 "follow".
