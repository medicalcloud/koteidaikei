# 霊枢 周痺第二十七 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied, "When the pain moves from above downward, first needle below to stop it, then needle above to remove it. When the pain moves from below upward, first needle above to stop it, then needle below to remove it."

## Notes

The interlinear note is not translated (original text only). For 過 the interlinear note says "one edition has 遏"; it is translated in the sense of 遏 "stop".
