# 霊枢 周痺第二十七 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied, "Each is in its own place, alternately breaking out and stopping, alternately staying and arising; the right responds to the left and the left to the right. They cannot circle; they alternately break out and rest."
