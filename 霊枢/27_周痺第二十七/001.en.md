# 霊枢 周痺第二十七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "When circling impediment is in the body, it moves up and down following the vessels, above and below, left and right corresponding to one another, with no gap between. I wish to hear whether this pain is in the blood vessels or between the partings of the flesh. What causes it? When the pain moves, there is no time to insert a needle; when the pain moves, there is no time to settle on a treatment before the pain has already stopped. What makes it so? I wish to hear the reason."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about the difference between circling impediment and multiple impediment and how to needle them.
- 慉痛 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "pain that moves".
