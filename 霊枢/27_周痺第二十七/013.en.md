# 霊枢 周痺第二十七 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Excellent. I have grasped its meaning. It is not in the viscera within, nor has it broken out in the skin without; it stays only between the partings of the flesh, and the true qi cannot circulate; hence it is called circling impediment. Therefore, in needling impediment, one must first feel and trace the six channels below it, observe their depletion and repletion, the blood of the great network vessels knotted and not flowing, and the depleted vessels that are sunken and empty, and regulate them, apply compresses to open them, and for those that are cramped and hard, turn and stretch them to make them move."

## Notes

転引 is provisionally translated as guiding and stretching exercises.
