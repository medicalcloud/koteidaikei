# 霊枢 周痺第二十七 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied, "Wind, cold and damp qi lodge between the outer partings of the flesh, press on [the fluids] and turn them into froth; when the froth meets cold it gathers; when it gathers it pushes apart the partings of the flesh and splits them; the splitting causes pain; when there is pain the spirit goes there; when the spirit goes there, there is heat; heat relieves the pain; when the pain is relieved there is reversal; reversal makes impediment break out elsewhere; and when it breaks out, it is like this."
