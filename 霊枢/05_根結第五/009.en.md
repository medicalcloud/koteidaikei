# 霊枢 根結第五 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "How can the taste of fine meat and grain be the same as that of beans and bean leaves? When the qi is smooth, withdraw quickly; when the qi is rough, withdraw slowly. When the qi is fierce, the needle is small and enters shallow; when the qi is rough, the needle is large and enters deep. Deep means one wishes to retain; shallow means one wishes to be quick. Seen in this light, needling commoners, go deep and retain; needling great men, go lightly and slowly. This is all because of the swift, fierce, smooth and fluent qi."
