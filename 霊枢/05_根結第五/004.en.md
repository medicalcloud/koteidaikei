# 霊枢 根結第五 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The greater yin is rooted at Yinbai and knots at the Great Granary. The lesser yin is rooted at Yongquan and knots at Lianquan. The *jueyin* is rooted at Dadun, knots at Yuying, and connects with Danzhong.

## Notes

The roots and knots of the three yin of the foot.
