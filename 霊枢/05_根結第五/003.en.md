# 霊枢 根結第五 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the closing breaks, the qi has nowhere to stop and rest and wilting illnesses arise; hence wilting illnesses are taken at the *Yangming*, observing surplus and insufficiency. Having nowhere to stop and rest means the genuine qi stagnates and the pathogenic qi dwells there. When the pivot breaks, the bones shake and one is unstable on the ground; hence bone shaking is taken at the lesser yang, observing surplus and insufficiency. Bone shaking means the joints are slack and not drawn together. What is called bone shaking is so called because of the shaking. One should get to the root of it.
