# 霊枢 根結第五 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The greater yang is rooted at Zhiyin and knots at the Gate of Life; the Gate of Life is the eyes. The *yangming* is rooted at Lidui and knots at Sangda; Sangda is what clamps the ears [Daying]. The lesser yang is rooted at Qiaoyin and knots at the Window Cage; the Window Cage is inside the ears. The greater yang is the opening, the *yangming* the closing, the lesser yang the pivot. So when the opening breaks, the flesh and joints decay and sudden illnesses arise; hence sudden illnesses are taken at the greater yang, observing surplus and insufficiency. Decay means the skin and flesh are stagnant, parched and weak.

## Notes

The roots and knots of the three yang of the foot, and the opening, closing and pivot.
- Following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: Sangda is Daying, below the cheek beside the ears, hence "clamping the ears". The *Jiayi jing* reads 結於頏顙，頏顙者鉗大，鉗大者耳也 (according to Xiao Yanping's note on the *Taisu*).
