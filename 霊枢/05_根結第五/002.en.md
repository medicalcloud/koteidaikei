# 霊枢 根結第五 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The greater yang is rooted at Zhiyin and knots at the Gate of Life; the Gate of Life is the eyes. The *yangming* is rooted at Lidui and knots at Sangda; Sangda is what clamps the ears. The lesser yang is rooted at Qiaoyin and knots at the Window Cage; the Window Cage is inside the ears. The greater yang is the opening, the *yangming* the closing, the lesser yang the pivot. So when the opening breaks, the flesh and joints decay and sudden illnesses arise; hence sudden illnesses are taken at the greater yang, observing surplus and insufficiency. Decay means the skin and flesh are stagnant, parched and weak.

## Notes

The roots and knots of the three yang of the foot, and the opening, closing and pivot.
- The location of 鉗耳 in 顙大者鉗耳也 (Touwei, among other views) is unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points). It is translated by the characters as "what clamps the ears".
