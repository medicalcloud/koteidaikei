# 霊枢 根結第五 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "*Counterflow and Compliance of the Five Bodies* speaks of the size of people's bones and joints, the firmness or fragility of their flesh, the thickness of their skin, the clarity or turbidity of their blood, the smoothness or roughness of their qi, the length of their vessels, the amount of their blood, and the number of channels and network vessels; I already know this. These all concern commoners in cloth. But kings, dukes and great men, lords who eat meat, have soft and fragile bodies and weak flesh, and their blood and qi are swift, fierce, smooth and fluent. Can the speed, depth and amount of needling be the same for them?"
