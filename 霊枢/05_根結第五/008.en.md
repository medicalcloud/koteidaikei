# 霊枢 根結第五 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The counterflow and compliance of the five body types refers to the size of people's bones and joints, the firmness or fragility of their flesh, the thickness of their skin, the clarity or turbidity of their blood, the smoothness or roughness of their qi, the length of their vessels, the amount of their blood, and the number of meridians and collaterals; I already know this. These all concern commoners in cloth. But kings, dukes and great men, lords who eat meat, have soft and fragile bodies and weak flesh, and their blood and qi are swift, fierce, smooth and fluent. Can the speed, depth and amount of needling be the same for them?"

## Notes

逆順五体 is not a book title; following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* ("the five bodies are the people of the five forms"), it is translated as the counterflow and compliance [in needling] of the five body types.
