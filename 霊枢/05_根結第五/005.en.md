# 霊枢 根結第五 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The greater yin is the opening, the *jueyin* the closing, the lesser yin the pivot. So when the opening breaks, the granaries have nowhere to transport to and there is diaphragm throughflux; diaphragm throughflux is taken at the greater yin, observing surplus and insufficiency. Thus the breaking of the opening means illness arising from insufficient qi. When the closing breaks, the qi is cut off and there is a tendency to sorrow; sorrow is taken at the *jueyin*, observing surplus and insufficiency. When the pivot breaks, the vessels have knots and do not flow; non-flow is taken at the lesser yin, observing surplus and insufficiency. Wherever there are knots, all are taken as insufficiency.

## Notes

The opening, closing and pivot of the three yin.
- The base's 「少陽為枢故開折則倉」 is emended to 「少陰為枢故開折則倉」. Wikisource (both editions), the *Taisu* and the *Lingshu jizhu* read 少陰為枢; this is the passage on the three yin and the text continues "take it at the lesser yin", so 陰 is adopted.
