# 霊枢 根結第五 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The foot greater yang is rooted at Zhiyin, flows to Jinggu, pours into Kunlun, enters at Tianzhu, [and connects at] Feiyang. The foot lesser yang is rooted at Qiaoyin, flows to Qiuxu, pours into Yangfu, enters at Tianrong, [and connects at] Guangming. The foot *Yangming* is rooted at Lidui, flows to Chongyang, pours into Xialing, enters at Renying, [and connects at] Fenglong. The hand greater yang is rooted at Shaoze, flows to Yanggu, pours into Shaohai, enters at Tianchuang, [and connects at] Zhizheng. The hand lesser yang is rooted at Guanchong, flows to Yangchi, pours into Zhigou, enters at Tianyou, [and connects at] Waiguan. The hand *Yangming* is rooted at Shangyang, flows to Hegu, pours into Yangxi, enters at Futu, [and connects at] Pianli. These are the so-called twelve meridians; when the collaterals are abundant, they should all be taken.

## Notes

The roots, flowing, pouring and entering points of the six yang meridians of hand and foot, and their network points. Feiyang, Guangming, Fenglong, Zhizheng, Waiguan and Pianli at the end of each clause are the network points of each meridian; "[and connects at]" is supplied.
- Following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* ("counting left and right of hands and feet"), the twelve meridians are the six yang meridians counted on both sides. For Tianrong on the foot lesser yang, the *Leijing* says Tianrong is a hand greater yang point and this should be Tianchong, while Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* places Tianrong below the ear behind the curve of the cheek as part of the foot lesser yang. The base is kept.
