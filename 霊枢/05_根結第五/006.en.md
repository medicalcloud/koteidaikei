# 霊枢 根結第五 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The foot greater yang is rooted at Zhiyin, flows to Jinggu, pours into Kunlun, enters at Tianzhu, [and connects at] Feiyang. The foot lesser yang is rooted at Qiaoyin, flows to Qiuxu, pours into Yangfu, enters at Tianrong, [and connects at] Guangming. The foot *yangming* is rooted at Lidui, flows to Chongyang, pours into Xialing, enters at Renying, [and connects at] Fenglong. The hand greater yang is rooted at Shaoze, flows to Yanggu, pours into Shaohai, enters at Tianchuang, [and connects at] Zhizheng. The hand lesser yang is rooted at Guanchong, flows to Yangchi, pours into Zhigou, enters at Tianyou, [and connects at] Waiguan. The hand *yangming* is rooted at Shangyang, flows to Hegu, pours into Yangxi, enters at Futu, [and connects at] Pianli. These are the so-called twelve channels; when the network vessels are abundant, they should all be taken.

## Notes

The roots, flowing, pouring and entering points of the six yang channels of hand and foot, and their network points. Feiyang, Guangming, Fenglong, Zhizheng, Waiguan and Pianli at the end of each clause are the network points of each channel; "[and connects at]" is supplied.
- One view holds that the "twelve channels" are the six yang channels counted on both sides; another holds that Tianrong does not fit as a point of the foot lesser yang. Both are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
