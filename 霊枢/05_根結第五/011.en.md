# 霊枢 根結第五 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the qi of the form is insufficient and the qi of the illness in surplus, the pathogen prevails; drain it at once. When the qi of the form is in surplus and the qi of the illness insufficient, supplement it at once. When the qi of the form is insufficient and the qi of the illness insufficient, the qi of yin and yang are both insufficient; do not needle. Needling it doubles the insufficiency; doubled insufficiency exhausts both yin and yang, the blood and qi are all used up, the five viscera are empty, the sinews, bones and marrow wither, the old perish and the strong do not recover. When the qi of the form is in surplus and the qi of the illness in surplus, this is called yin and yang both in surplus; drain the pathogen at once and regulate depletion and repletion. Hence it is said: drain what is in surplus, supplement what is insufficient. This is what it means. Hence it is said: if one needles without knowing counterflow and compliance, the true and the pathogenic struggle with each other. Supplementing what is full makes yin and yang overflow in all directions, the intestines and stomach fill and expand, the liver and lungs swell inside, and yin and yang are confused with each other. Draining what is depleted empties the channels and vessels, exhausts and dries up the blood and qi, makes the intestines and stomach 㒤辟, the skin thin and clinging, the hair and interstices withered and scorched, and gives a term of death. Hence it is said: the key to using the needle lies in knowing how to regulate yin and yang; when yin and yang are regulated, the essential qi shines, form and qi are united, and the spirit is stored within. Hence it is said: the superior practitioner balances the qi, the middling practitioner disorders the vessels, the inferior practitioner cuts off the qi and endangers life. Hence it is said: the inferior practitioner must be cautious. One must carefully examine the changing illnesses of the five viscera, the correspondences of the five pulses, the repletion and depletion of the channels and network vessels, and the softness or coarseness of the skin, and only then take them."

## Notes

Supplementing and draining according to the surplus or insufficiency of the qi of the form and the qi of the illness; the end of the chapter.
- The meaning of 㒤 in 腸胃㒤辟 is unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); it is translated by the characters.
- The base's 「夭膲子之死期」 is emended to 「夭膲予之死期」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*) and Daizhige read 予, the same phrase as 予之短期 in unit 7; 予 is adopted.
