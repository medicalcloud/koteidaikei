# 霊枢 根結第五 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In one day and one night there are fifty circulations, to nourish the essence of the five viscera; what does not accord with this number is called reckless life. The so-called fifty circulations mean that the five viscera all receive qi; one holds the pulse opening and counts its arrivals. If there are fifty beats without a single pause, the five viscera all receive qi. If there is a pause in forty beats, one viscus lacks qi; a pause in thirty beats, two viscera lack qi; a pause in twenty beats, three viscera lack qi; a pause in ten beats, four viscera lack qi; a pause in fewer than ten beats, the five viscera lack qi. The key to giving a short term [before death] lies in *End and Beginning*. What is called fifty beats without a single pause is taken as the norm, by which one knows the terms of the five viscera. Those given a short term have a pulse now rapid, now sparse."

## Notes

The fifty beats of the pulse and the qi of the five viscera; Qibo's speech ends here.
- The base's 「無気子之短期」 is emended to 「無気予之短期」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 予 (the *Jiayi jing* has 与, same meaning): "give a short term". 予 is adopted.
- The base's 「之期子之短期」 is emended to 「之期予之短期」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 予 (the *Jiayi jing* has 与); 予 is adopted as in the preceding phrase.
