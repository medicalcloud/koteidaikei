# 霊枢 根結第五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Heaven and earth affect each other, cold and warmth replace each other. In the way of yin and yang, which is less and which more? The way of yin is even, the way of yang is odd. For [illnesses] arising in spring and summer, yin qi is less and yang qi more; when yin and yang are out of balance, what does one supplement and what drain? For [illnesses] arising in autumn and winter, yang qi is less and yin qi more; yin qi is abundant and yang qi declines, so the stems and leaves wither and the damp rain returns downward. When yin and yang replace each other, what does one drain and what supplement? The irregular pathogens that leave the channels are countless. Without knowing the roots and knots, [in] the five viscera and six bowels the bar is broken and the pivot ruined, opening and closing run off, yin and yang are greatly lost, and they cannot be taken again. The mystery of the nine needles lies essentially in the end and beginning. So one who can know the end and beginning can sum it up in one word; without knowing the end and beginning, the way of the needle is entirely cut off.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, which opens with Qibo's speech without a question from the Yellow Emperor. Qibo's speech continues through unit 7. It explains the "roots" (where the channels start) and the "knots" (where they bind).
