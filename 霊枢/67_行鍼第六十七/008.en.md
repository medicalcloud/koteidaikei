# 霊枢 行鍼第六十七 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Those with much yang are often joyful; those with much yin are often angry. One who is often angry but easily appeased is said to have some yin. The separating and joining of his yin and yang are difficult, so his spirit cannot go ahead."

## Notes

Follows the *Leijing* note: frequent anger shows some yin; being easily appeased is rooted in yang. With yin within yang, the yang is burdened by the yin, so their separating and joining are difficult and the spirit cannot go ahead.
