# 霊枢 行鍼第六十七 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Their yin qi is abundant and yang qi scant; the yin qi sinks and even the floating yang qi is stored within. So after the needle has been withdrawn, the qi follows behind; hence it moves on its own."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: yin by nature is slow and its qi stored within, so in those with more yin than yang the qi follows on its own after the needle is withdrawn. The phrasing of 陰気沈而陽気浮者内蔵 is uncertain; it is translated close to Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu*, "the yin qi is deep and stored within".
