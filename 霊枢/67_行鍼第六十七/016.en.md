# 霊枢 行鍼第六十七 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Counterflow of qi and worsening of illness with repeated needling are not due to the qi of yin and yang or the tendency to float or sink. All of these are failures of the crude practitioner and errors of the physician; the patient's form and qi are not at fault."

## Notes

The end of the chapter. The *Leijing* note: if the order of counterflow and compliance is not lost, the qi does not counterflow; if supplementing and draining are appropriate, the illness does not worsen. Such cases are failures and errors of the physician, not faults of yin and yang, exterior and interior, or form and qi.
- The base's 上之所失 is emended to 工之所失. The *Taisu*, the *Jiayi jing*, the *Lingshu jizhu*, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text all read 工之所失, which pairs with 粗之所敗 (failures of the crude practitioner). The graphically similar 上 is taken as an error for 工.
