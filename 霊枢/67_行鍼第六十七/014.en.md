# 霊枢 行鍼第六十七 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "This person has much yin and little yang; his qi is sunk and goes with difficulty, so it is felt only after repeated needling."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: this too is yin stagnation, making it hard for the qi to go and arrive—more severe than in the preceding case.
