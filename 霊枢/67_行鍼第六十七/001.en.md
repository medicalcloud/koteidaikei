# 霊枢 行鍼第六十七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I have heard about the nine needles from you, Master, and applied them to the people. The blood and qi of the people differ in form: in some the spirit moves and the qi goes ahead of the needle; in some the qi and the needle meet; in some the qi moves on its own after the needle has been withdrawn; in some it is felt only after repeated needling; in some the qi counterflows when the needle is applied; in some the illness worsens with repeated needling. These six differ in form. I wish to hear the reason."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, explaining why the response of the qi to needling differs among people in six ways.
- The base's 鍼巳出 is emended to 鍼已出. Units 11 and 12 read 鍼已出, and Xiao Yanping's note on the *Taisu* also says the *Lingshu* reads 已出 (the Siku edition on Wikisource and the *Taisu* read 以, interchangeable with 已). The earthly branch 巳 makes no sense here, so it is taken as an error for the graphically similar 已.
