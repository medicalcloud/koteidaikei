# 霊枢 行鍼第六十七 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Persons of double yang are bright and vigorous and unbending; they speak quickly and lift their feet high; the qi of the heart and lung zang organs is in surplus, and the yang qi is smooth, abundant and rising; hence the spirit moves and the qi goes ahead."

## Notes

Follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: persons of double yang are those in whom yang prevails; 熇熇 is bright and vigorous, 高高 unbending; the heart and lungs are the two yang zang organs, and with their yang qi smooth, abundant and rising, the spirit moves easily and the qi goes ahead of the needle.
