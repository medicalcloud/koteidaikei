# 霊枢 癲狂第二十二 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When madness first arises, there is first self-pity, forgetfulness, bitter anger and frequent fear; it is contracted from worry and hunger. To treat it, take the hand greater yin and *Yangming*, stop when the blood changes, and also take the foot greater yin and *Yangming*. When madness first breaks out, there is little sleep and no hunger; one considers oneself superior and worthy, argues one's own wisdom, considers oneself noble, and curses and scolds day and night without stopping. To treat it, take the hand *Yangming*, greater yang and greater yin and the lesser yin below the tongue; look at them and take all that are abundant, and leave those that are not. Mad talk, fright, frequent laughter, fondness for singing and music, and wandering about without rest are contracted from great fear. To treat it, take the hand *Yangming*, greater yang and greater yin. Madness in which the eyes see wildly, the ears hear wildly and one frequently shouts arises from shortness of qi. To treat it, take the hand greater yang, greater yin and *Yangming*, the foot greater yin, and both cheeks of the head. Madness in which one eats much, often sees ghosts and spirits, and often laughs without making it heard outside is contracted from great joy. To treat it, take the foot greater yin, greater yang and *Yangming*, and afterwards the hand greater yin, greater yang and *Yangming*. When madness has newly broken out and does not yet respond in this way, first take the pulsing vessels on left and right at Ququan and the abundant ones until blood appears, and it will end shortly. If it does not, take it according to the method and apply twenty cones of moxa to the coccyx.
