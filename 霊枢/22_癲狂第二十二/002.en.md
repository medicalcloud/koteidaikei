# 霊枢 癲狂第二十二 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

One who treats epilepsy should always stay with [the patient] and examine where it should be taken. When the illness arrives, look for where the fault is and drain it, and place the blood in a gourd vessel; when the next attack comes, the blood will move by itself. If it does not move, apply twenty cones of moxa to the end bone; the end bone is the coccyx.
