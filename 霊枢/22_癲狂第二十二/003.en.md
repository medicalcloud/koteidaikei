# 霊枢 癲狂第二十二 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In bone epilepsy, the transport points and partings of the flesh around the cheeks and teeth are all full, the bones alone remain, there is sweating and vexed oppression; when there is much vomiting of froth and the qi leaks downward, it cannot be cured. In sinew epilepsy, the body is weary and cramped and [the pulse] large; needle the Dazhu vessel of the great channel of the nape; when there is much vomiting of froth and the qi leaks downward, it cannot be cured. In vessel epilepsy, one falls suddenly and the vessels of the four limbs are all distended and slack. If the vessels are full, needle them all and let blood; if not full, apply moxa to the greater yang flanking the nape, apply moxa to the girdle vessel at the lower back three *cun* apart [from the spine], and to the root transport points of all the partings of the flesh. When there is much vomiting of froth and the qi leaks downward, it cannot be cured. In epilepsy, when the attacks are like madness, there is death without cure.

## Notes

Bone, sinew and vessel epilepsy.
- The readings of 骨居 and 身倦攣急大 are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); translated provisionally.
- 抒 in 大抒 of the base is read as 杼, as other sources have it (see collation).
