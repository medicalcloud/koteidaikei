# 霊枢 癲狂第二十二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The corner of the eye that splits outward toward the face is the sharp canthus; the one inside near the nose is the inner canthus. The upper [lid] is also called the outer canthus, the lower the inner canthus. When epilepsy first arises, there is first unhappiness, heaviness and pain of the head, red eyes when looking up, and in severe attacks it reaches an extreme, after which the heart is vexed. Examine it at the forehead, take the hand greater yang, *yangming* and greater yin, and stop when the blood changes [color]. When epilepsy first strikes with pulling of the mouth, crying out, panting and palpitations, examine it at the hand *yangming* and greater yang; if the left side is stiff, attack the right; if the right side is stiff, attack the left; stop when the blood changes. When epilepsy first strikes with arching and stiffening, and then pain in the spine, examine it at the foot greater yang, *yangming* and greater yin and the hand greater yang, and stop when the blood changes.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, which describes, without a dialogue, epilepsy, madness, wind counterflow, reversal counterflow and other illnesses, and their treatment.
- 上為外眥，下為内眥 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: in terms of inside and outside the corners are the sharp and inner canthi, but in terms of above and below the upper net of the eye is also called the outer canthus and the lower net the inner canthus.
