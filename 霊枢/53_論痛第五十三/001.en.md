# 霊枢 論痛第五十三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Shaoyu, "The strength of the sinews and bones, the firmness of the flesh, the thickness of the skin and the density of the interstices all differ. How do people respond to the pain of needles, stones and fire? The thickness and firmness of the intestines and stomach also differ. How do they respond to toxic medicines? I wish to hear all of it."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. The interlocutor is Shaoyu. It asks why people differ in enduring the pain of needling and moxibustion and the toxicity of medicines.
