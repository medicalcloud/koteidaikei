# 霊枢 論痛第五十三 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu said, "Those with a thick stomach, a dark complexion, large bones and fatness can all withstand toxins; hence those who are thin with a thin stomach cannot withstand toxins."

## Notes

The end of the chapter.
- The base's 「故骨痩」 is emended to 「故其痩」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 故其痩而薄胃者, contrasting "those who are thin" with the preceding; 其 is adopted.
