# 霊枢 論痛第五十三 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu answered, "Those who in addition have a dark complexion and fine [strong] bones endure fire."

## Notes

美骨 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "fine bones means strong bones".
