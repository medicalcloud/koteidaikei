# 霊枢 経脈第十 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the qi of foot lesser yin is cut off, the bones wither. The lesser yin is the winter vessel, which moves hidden and moistens the bones and marrow. So when the bones are not moistened, the flesh cannot adhere; when bone and flesh do not cling to each other, the flesh softens and recedes; because the flesh softens and recedes, the teeth lengthen and are coated and the hair has no luster; when the hair has no luster, the bones die first; it worsens on a *wu* day and death comes on a *ji* day, because earth overcomes water.

## Notes

- The base's 「戊篤已死」 is emended to 「戊篤己死」. The *Lingshu jizhu* reads 己 (the *Leijing* and *Jiayi jing* have 已 like the base). The day following *wu* is *ji*, so 已 is taken as a graphic error for 己.
