# 霊枢 経脈第十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When a person first comes into being, essence forms first; when the essence has formed, the brain and marrow arise; the bones become the trunk, the vessels the circulation, the sinews the strength, the flesh the wall; the skin becomes firm and the hair grows. When grain enters the stomach, the paths of the vessels open and the blood and qi move."
