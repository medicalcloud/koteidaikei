# 霊枢 経脈第十 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The triple burner hand lesser yang vessel starts at the tip of the ring finger, rises to come out between the two fingers, follows the back of the hand and the wrist, comes out between the two bones on the outer forearm, rises through the elbow, follows the outer upper arm up to the shoulder, crosses to come out behind the foot lesser yang, enters the empty basin, spreads in Danzhong, disperses to connect with the pericardium, descends through the diaphragm and belongs in turn to the triple burner. Its branch rises from Danzhong to come out at the empty basin, rises to the nape, attaches behind the ear, rises straight to come out at the upper corner of the ear, then bends down to the cheek and reaches below the eye. Its branch enters the ear from behind it, comes out and runs in front of the ear, passes in front of Kezhuren, crosses at the cheek and reaches the outer canthus. When it is stirred, the illness is deafness with a muffled, indistinct [hearing], swollen throat and throat impediment. The illnesses produced by the qi, which it governs, are: sweating, pain at the outer canthus, cheek pain, pain behind the ear and along the outside of the shoulder, upper arm, elbow and forearm, and uselessness of the ring finger. For these illnesses, drain when abundant, supplement when depleted, be quick when hot, retain when cold, apply moxibustion when sunken, and when neither abundant nor depleted, take it at the channel. When abundant, Renying is once as large as the inch opening; when depleted, Renying is instead smaller than the inch opening.

## Notes
