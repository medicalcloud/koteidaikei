# 霊枢 経脈第十 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The bladder foot greater yang vessel starts at the inner canthus, rises over the forehead and crosses at the vertex. Its branch goes from the vertex to the upper corner of the ear. Its straight part enters from the vertex to connect with the brain, returns to come out and separately descends the nape, follows the inside of the shoulder blade, flanks the spine, reaches the middle of the lower back, enters to follow the paravertebral muscles, connects with the kidneys and belongs to the bladder. Its branch descends from the middle of the lower back flanking the spine, passes through the buttocks and enters the back of the knee. Its branch separates left and right from the inside of the shoulder blade, descends through the scapula, flanks the inside of the spine, passes the hip pivot, follows the outside of the thigh along its back edge, descends to unite in the back of the knee, then descends through the calf, comes out behind the outer ankle, follows Jinggu and reaches the outer side of the little toe. When it is stirred, the illness is a surging headache, the eyes feeling as if they would fall out, the nape as if pulled out, pain in the spine, the lower back as if broken, the hip unable to bend, the back of the knee as if tied, the calf as if torn; this is called ankle reversal. The illnesses produced by the sinews, which it governs, are: hemorrhoids, malaria, madness, epilepsy, pain of the fontanel and nape, yellow eyes, tearing, runny nose and nosebleed, pain in the nape, back, lower back, sacrum, back of the knee, calf and leg, and uselessness of the little toe. For these illnesses, drain when abundant, supplement when depleted, be quick when hot, retain when cold, apply moxibustion when sunken, and when neither abundant nor depleted, take it at the channel. When abundant, Renying is twice as large as the inch opening; when depleted, Renying is instead smaller than the inch opening.

## Notes

The course and illnesses of the bladder channel.
