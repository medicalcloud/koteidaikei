# 霊枢 経脈第十 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The heart hand lesser yin vessel starts in the heart, comes out to belong to the heart connection, descends through the diaphragm and connects with the small intestine. Its branch rises from the heart connection to flank the throat and attaches to the eye connection. Its straight part again goes from the heart connection back up to the lungs, descends to come out below the armpit, descends along the back of the inner upper arm, running behind the greater yin and heart governor, descends to the inside of the elbow, follows the back of the inner forearm, reaches the tip of the sharp bone behind the palm, enters the back of the inner palm, follows the inner side of the little finger and comes out at its tip. When it is stirred, the illness is a dry throat, heart pain, and thirst with desire to drink; this is called arm reversal. The illnesses produced by the heart, which it governs, are: yellow eyes, pain in the flanks, pain and reversal along the back of the inner upper arm and forearm, and heat and pain in the palms. For these illnesses, drain when abundant, supplement when depleted, be quick when hot, retain when cold, apply moxibustion when sunken, and when neither abundant nor depleted, take it at the channel. When abundant, the inch opening is twice as large as Renying; when depleted, the inch opening is instead smaller than Renying.

## Notes

The course and illnesses of the heart channel.
- 脱 in 掌後脱骨 of the base is read as 鋭, as other sources have it (see collation).
