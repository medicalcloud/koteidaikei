# 霊枢 経脈第十 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the qi of the five yin are all cut off, the eye connection turns; when it turns, the eyes roll; when the eyes roll, the will has died first; when the will has died first, death comes within a day and a half at the most. When the qi of the six yang is cut off, yin and yang separate; when they separate, the interstices open and leak and expiry sweat comes out. So if divined in the morning, death comes in the evening; if divined in the evening, death comes in the morning.
