# 霊枢 経脈第十 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the qi of foot *Jueyin* is cut off, the sinews are cut off. The *Jueyin* is the liver vessel, and the liver is what the sinews are joined to. The sinews gather at the genitals, and the vessel connects with the root of the tongue. So when the vessel does not nourish, the sinews tense; when the sinews tense, they pull the tongue and testicles. So when the lips are green, the tongue curls and the testicles retract, the sinews die first; it worsens on a *geng* day and death comes on a *xin* day, because metal overcomes wood.

## Notes

聚于陰気 follows the *Lingshu jizhu* ("the qi of the sinews meets at the ancestral sinew; the sinews gather at the genitals").
