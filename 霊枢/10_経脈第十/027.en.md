# 霊枢 経脈第十 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The collaterals cannot pass between the large joints; they must take separate paths to go out and in, and reunite in the skin, where their meeting places are all visible on the outside. Hence in needling the collaterals, one must needle above their knots; where there is much blood, even without knots, take it at once to drain the pathogen and let out the blood; if left, it will break out as impediment. In examining the collaterals, a green color means cold and pain, red means heat. When the stomach is cold, the collaterals of the fish of the hand are mostly green; when the stomach has heat, the collaterals of the fish border are red; when they suddenly turn black, it is a long-lingering impediment; when there is red, black and green, it is qi of cold and heat; when they are green and short, it is shortness of qi. Needling cold and heat, there are always many blood collaterals; one must take them once every other day, stopping when the blood is exhausted, and then regulate deficiency and excess. When they are small and short, the qi is scant; in severe cases, draining causes oppression; with severe oppression, one falls down and cannot speak; when oppressed, sit [the patient] up at once."

## Notes

The appearance of the collaterals and how to needle them.
