# 霊枢 経脈第十 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The separate [collaterals] of hand greater yang is called Zhizheng. Five *cun* above the wrist it pours inward into the lesser yin. Its separate branch runs up to the elbow and connects with Jianyu. When excess, the joints slacken and the elbow is useless; when deficient, warts grow, the small ones like scabs on the fingers. Take it where it separates. The separate [collaterals] of hand *Yangming* is called Pianli. Three *cun* from the wrist it separates and enters the greater yin. Its separate branch rises along the forearm, rides over Jianyu, rises to the curve of the cheek and slants to the teeth. Its separate branch enters the ear and unites with the ancestral vessels. When excess, tooth decay and deafness; when deficient, cold teeth and inner impediment and blockage. Take it where it separates. The separate [collaterals] of hand lesser yang is called Waiguan. Two *cun* from the wrist it winds around the outside of the forearm, pours into the chest and unites with the heart governor. Its illness: when excess, cramping of the elbow; when deficient, [the elbow] cannot be drawn in. Take it where it separates.

## Notes

The collaterals of the three yang of the hand.
- Following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: the large ones are warts, the small ones are like scabs between the fingers; 痺隔 is inner impediment and blockage, with cold teeth.
