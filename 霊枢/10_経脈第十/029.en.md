# 霊枢 経脈第十 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The separate [network vessel] of hand greater yang is called Zhizheng. Five *cun* above the wrist it pours inward into the lesser yin. Its separate branch runs up to the elbow and connects with Jianyu. When replete, the joints slacken and the elbow is useless; when depleted, warts grow, the small ones like scabs on the fingers. Take it where it separates. The separate [network vessel] of hand *yangming* is called Pianli. Three *cun* from the wrist it separates and enters the greater yin. Its separate branch rises along the forearm, rides over Jianyu, rises to the curve of the cheek and slants to the teeth. Its separate branch enters the ear and unites with the ancestral vessels. When replete, tooth decay and deafness; when depleted, cold teeth and inner impediment and blockage. Take it where it separates. The separate [network vessel] of hand lesser yang is called Waiguan. Two *cun* from the wrist it winds around the outside of the forearm, pours into the chest and unites with the heart governor. Its illness: when replete, cramping of the elbow; when depleted, [the elbow] cannot be drawn in. Take it where it separates.

## Notes

The network vessels of the three yang of the hand.
- Following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: the large ones are warts, the small ones are like scabs between the fingers; 痺隔 is inner impediment and blockage, with cold teeth.
