# 霊枢 経脈第十 §28 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The separate [network vessel] of hand greater yin is called Lieque. It starts in the partings above the wrist, runs alongside the greater yin channel, enters straight into the palm and disperses into the fish border. Its illness: when replete, heat in the [area of the] sharp bone of the hand and the palm; when depleted, yawning, and frequent and incontinent urination. Take it half a *cun* from the wrist; it separates and runs to the *yangming*. The separate [network vessel] of hand lesser yin is called Tongli. One and a half *cun* from the wrist it separates and runs upward, follows the channel into the heart, attaches to the root of the tongue and belongs to the eye connection. When replete, propping in the diaphragm; when depleted, inability to speak. Take it one *cun* behind the palm; it separates and runs to the greater yang. The separate [network vessel] of the hand heart governor is called Neiguan. Two *cun* from the wrist it comes out between the two tendons, follows the channel upward and attaches to the pericardium. When the heart connection is replete, heart pain; when depleted, stiffness of the head. Take it between the two tendons.

## Notes

The first three of the fifteen network vessels (the three yin of the hand). 列欠 is Lieque (列缺).
- The meanings of 手鋭 and 欠㰦 are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); they are translated provisionally.
- For 去腕半寸 the Daizhige edition differs by one character (see collation).
