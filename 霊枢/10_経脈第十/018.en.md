# 霊枢 経脈第十 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the qi of hand lesser yin is cut off, the vessels do not flow; when the vessels do not flow, the blood does not move; when the blood does not move, the hair loses its luster. So when the face is black like lacquered firewood, the blood dies first; it worsens on a *ren* day and death comes on a *gui* day, because water overcomes fire.

## Notes

髦 is read as 髪 "hair", as some sources have it (see collation).
