# 霊枢 経脈第十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Leigong asked the Yellow Emperor, "The words of *Prohibited Vessels* say that the principle of all needling begins with the channels: making their courses circulate, fixing their measures, ordering the five viscera within and distinguishing the six bowels without. I wish to hear all of this way."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. Leigong asks and the Yellow Emperor answers. It describes the courses and illnesses of the twelve channels, the signs when the qi of the channels is cut off, and the fifteen network vessels.
- The quoted words are nearly the same as chapter 48 (*Jinfu*), unit 7, so 禁脈 appears to refer to *Jinfu*.
