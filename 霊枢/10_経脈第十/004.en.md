# 霊枢 経脈第十 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The channels are that by which one can decide life and death, deal with the hundred illnesses and regulate depletion and repletion; one must master them.

## Notes

The Yellow Emperor's speech continues through unit 23, describing the twelve channels one by one.
