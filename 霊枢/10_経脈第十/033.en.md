# 霊枢 経脈第十 §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All these fifteen network vessels are surely visible when replete and surely sunken when depleted. If they cannot be seen, look for them above and below; for people's channels are not the same, and the network vessels differ in where they separate.

## Notes

A summary of the fifteen network vessels; the end of the chapter.
