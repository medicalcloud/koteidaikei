# 霊枢 経脈第十 §33 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

All these fifteen collaterals are surely visible when excess and surely deep when deficient. If they cannot be seen, look for them above and below; for people's meridians are not the same, and the collaterals differ in where they separate.

## Notes

A summary of the fifteen collaterals; the end of the chapter.
