# 霊枢 経脈第十 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The separate [network vessel] of foot greater yang is called Feiyang. Seven *cun* from the ankle it separates and runs to the lesser yin. When replete, a blocked nose and pain in the head and back; when depleted, runny nose and nosebleed. Take it where it separates. The separate [network vessel] of foot lesser yang is called Guangming. Five *cun* from the ankle it separates and runs to the *jueyin*, and descends to connect with the instep. When replete, reversal; when depleted, wilting and lameness, inability to rise when seated. Take it where it separates. The separate [network vessel] of foot *yangming* is called Fenglong. Eight *cun* from the ankle it separates and runs to the greater yin. Its separate branch follows the outer edge of the shinbone, rises to connect with the head and nape, unites with the qi of all the channels, and descends to connect with the throat. Its illness: when the qi counterflows, throat impediment and sudden loss of voice; when replete, madness and epilepsy; when depleted, the feet cannot be drawn in and the shins wither. Take it where it separates.

## Notes

The network vessels of the three yang of the foot.
- 瘁 in 瘁瘖 is read as 卒 "sudden", following the *Taisu*'s reading 喉痺卒瘖.
- The base's 「実則狂巔」 is emended to 「実則狂癲」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), the *Taisu* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 癲 (the *Leijing* agrees with the base); the illness name 癲 is adopted.
