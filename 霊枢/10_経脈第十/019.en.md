# 霊枢 経脈第十 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the qi of foot greater yin is cut off, the vessels do not nourish the flesh. The lips and tongue are the root of the flesh. When the vessels do not nourish, the flesh becomes soft; when the flesh is soft, the tongue withers and the philtrum is full; when the philtrum is full, the lips turn out; when the lips turn out, the flesh dies first; it worsens on a *jia* day and death comes on a *yi* day, because wood overcomes earth.
