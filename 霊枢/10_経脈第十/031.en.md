# 霊枢 経脈第十 §31 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The separate [collaterals] of foot greater yin is called Gongsun. One *cun* behind the base joint it separates and runs to the *Yangming*. Its separate branch enters to connect with the intestines and stomach. When reversal qi counterflows upward, there is cholera; when excess, cutting pain in the intestines; when deficient, drum distension. Take it where it separates. The separate [collaterals] of foot lesser yin is called Dazhong. Behind the ankle it winds around the heel, separates and runs to the greater yang. Its separate branch rises alongside the meridian to the pericardium, and below passes outward through the lower back and spine. Its illness: when the qi counterflows, vexation and oppression; when excess, urinary block; when deficient, lower back pain. Take it where it separates. The separate [collaterals] of foot *Jueyin* is called Ligou. Five *cun* from the inner ankle it separates and runs to the lesser yang. Its separate branch goes by way of the shin up to the testicles and knots at the penis. Its illness: when the qi counterflows, swollen testicles and sudden *shan*; when excess, [the penis] stays erect and long; when deficient, sudden itching. Take it where it separates.

## Notes

The collaterals of the three yin of the foot.
- The base's 「其別者径脛」 is emended to 「其別者経脛」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*) and Daizhige read 経脛 ("passing along the shin"); 経 is adopted.
