# 霊枢 経脈第十 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the qi of hand greater yin is cut off, the skin and hair are scorched. The greater yin moves qi to warm the skin and hair; so when the qi does not flourish, the skin and hair are scorched; when the skin and hair are scorched, the fluids leave the skin and joints; when the fluids leave the skin and joints, the nails wither and the hair breaks; when the hair breaks, the hair dies first; it worsens on a *bing* day and death comes on a *ding* day, because fire overcomes metal.

## Notes

From here, the signs when the qi of each channel is cut off and the days of death are described.
