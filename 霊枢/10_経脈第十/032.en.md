# 霊枢 経脈第十 §32 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The separate [network vessel] of the conception vessel is called Weiyi. It descends from Jiuwei and disperses in the abdomen. When replete, the skin of the abdomen hurts; when depleted, itching and scratching. Take it where it separates. The separate [network vessel] of the governing vessel is called Changqiang. It flanks the paravertebral muscles up to the nape, disperses over the head, descends to the left and right of the shoulder blades, separates and runs to the greater yang, and enters to pass through the paravertebral muscles. When replete, the spine is stiff; when depleted, the head is heavy and sways high [unable to hold itself]. Where there is fault along the spine, take it where it separates. The great network vessel of the spleen is called Dabao. It comes out three *cun* below Yuanye and spreads over the chest and flanks. When replete, the whole body aches; when depleted, all the joints are slack. Where this vessel has blood in its net-like network vessels, take it all at the great network vessel of the spleen.

## Notes

The network vessels of the conception and governing vessels, and the great network vessel of the spleen.
- 高揺之 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: the head is heavy and sways because it is too weak to hold itself and trembles.
