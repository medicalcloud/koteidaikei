# 霊枢 経脈第十 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The twelve channels run hidden between the partings of the flesh, deep and invisible. The one that is always visible is where the foot greater yin passes above the outer ankle, because there is nothing to hide it. All vessels that float and are always visible are network vessels. Of the network vessels of the six channels, the great network vessels of hand *yangming* and lesser yang start between the five fingers and rise to unite in the elbow. When one drinks wine, the defensive qi first moves in the skin and first fills the network vessels, so the network vessels become abundant first; thus when the defensive qi has leveled off, the nutritive qi becomes full and the channels become greatly abundant. When a vessel suddenly pulses, it is always because pathogenic qi dwells in it and lodges at the root or branch; if it does not pulse, there is heat; if it is not firm, it is sunken and empty; being unlike the others, it tells one which vessel is pulsing."

The difference in visibility between channels and network vessels. The Yellow Emperor's speech ends here.
