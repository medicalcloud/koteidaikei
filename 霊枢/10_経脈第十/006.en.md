# 霊枢 経脈第十 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The large intestine hand *Yangming* vessel starts at the tip of the index finger, follows the upper edge of the finger, comes out between the two bones at Hegu, rises into the space between the two tendons, follows the upper edge of the forearm, enters the outer edge of the elbow, rises along the front of the outer upper arm, rises to the shoulder, comes out at the front edge of the shoulder bone, rises to come out above the meeting of the pillar bone, descends into the empty basin, connects with the lungs, descends through the diaphragm and belongs to the large intestine. Its branch rises from the empty basin to the neck, passes through the cheek, enters the lower teeth, returns to come out around the mouth, crosses at the philtrum, left going to the right and right to the left, and rises to flank the nostrils. When it is stirred, the illness is toothache and swelling of the neck. The illnesses produced by the fluids, which it governs, are: yellow eyes, dry mouth, runny nose and nosebleed, throat impediment, pain in the front of the shoulder and upper arm, and pain and uselessness of the index finger. When the qi is in surplus, there is heat and swelling along the course of the vessel; when deficient, chills and shivering without recovery of warmth. For these illnesses, drain when abundant, supplement when deficient, be quick when hot, retain when cold, apply moxibustion when sunken, and when neither abundant nor deficient, take it at the meridian. When abundant, Renying is three times as large as the inch opening; when deficient, Renying is instead smaller than the inch opening.

## Notes

The course and illnesses of the large intestine meridian.
