# 霊枢 経脈第十 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The lung hand greater yin vessel starts in the middle jiao, descends to connect with the large intestine, returns along the mouth of the stomach, rises through the diaphragm and belongs to the lungs; from the lung connection it goes out sideways below the armpit, descends along the inner side of the upper arm, running in front of the lesser yin and heart governor, descends into the elbow, follows the lower edge of the upper bone on the inside of the forearm, enters the inch opening, rises to the fish, follows the fish border and comes out at the tip of the thumb. Its branch goes straight from behind the wrist to the inner edge of the index finger and comes out at its tip. When it is stirred, the illness is lung distension and fullness, puffing, dyspnea and coughing, pain in the empty basin, and in severe cases crossing the hands with blurred vision; this is called arm reversal. The illnesses produced by the lungs, which it governs, are: cough, rising qi, dyspnea and thirst, vexation of the heart, fullness of the chest, pain and reversal along the front of the inner upper arm and forearm, and heat in the palms. When the qi is abundant and in surplus, there is pain in the shoulders and back, wind and cold, sweating and wind attack, and frequent but scanty urination. When the qi is deficient, there is pain and cold in the shoulders and back, shortness of qi insufficient for breathing, and discolored urine. For these illnesses, drain when abundant, supplement when deficient, be quick when hot, retain when cold, apply moxibustion when deep, and when neither abundant nor deficient, take it at the meridian. When abundant, the inch opening is three times as large as Renying; when deficient, the inch opening is instead smaller than Renying.

## Notes

The course and illnesses of the lung meridian. Each of the following meridians is described in the same pattern, listing "when it is stirred, the illness is..." and "the illnesses produced by ..., which it governs", followed by the treatment and the pulses at Renying and the inch opening.
- 欠 in 小便数而欠 is translated as "scanty".
