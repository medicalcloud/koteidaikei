# 霊枢 経脈第十 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The stomach foot *yangming* vessel starts at the bridge of the nose where [the two sides] meet, takes in the greater yang vessel at its side, descends along the outside of the nose, enters the upper teeth, returns to come out around the mouth and encircle the lips, descends to cross at Chengjiang, then follows the lower edge behind the chin, comes out at Daying, follows Jiache, rises in front of the ear, passes Kezhuren, follows the hairline and reaches the forehead. Its branch descends from in front of Daying to Renying, follows the throat, enters the empty basin, descends through the diaphragm, belongs to the stomach and connects with the spleen. Its straight part descends from the empty basin along the inner edge of the breast, descends to flank the navel, and enters Qijie. Its branch starts at the mouth of the stomach, descends along the inside of the abdomen, reaches Qijie and unites there; from there it descends to Biguan, reaches Futu, descends into the kneecap, descends along the outer edge of the shin, descends to the instep, and enters the inner side of the middle toe. Its branch separates three *cun* below [the knee] and descends into the outer side of the middle toe. Its branch separates on the instep, enters between the big toes, and comes out at the tip. When it is stirred, the illness is shivering with chills, frequent groaning, frequent yawning, a dark face; when the illness arrives, aversion to people and fire, fright at the sound of wood, a heart that wants to stir, staying alone with the door closed and the windows blocked; in severe cases wanting to climb high places and sing, to throw off one's clothes and run, rumbling and abdominal distension; this is called shin reversal. The illnesses produced by the blood, which it governs, are: madness, malaria, warm excess, sweating, runny nose and nosebleed, a crooked mouth, sores on the lips, swollen neck, throat impediment, water swelling of the greater abdomen, swelling and pain of the kneecap, pain all along the chest and breast, Qijie, thigh, Futu, the outer edge of the shin and the top of the instep, and uselessness of the middle toe. When the qi is abundant, the whole front of the body is hot; when it is in surplus in the stomach, grain is consumed with frequent hunger and the urine is yellow. When the qi is insufficient, the whole front of the body is cold and shivering; when the stomach is cold, there is distension and fullness. For these illnesses, drain when abundant, supplement when depleted, be quick when hot, retain when cold, apply moxibustion when sunken, and when neither abundant nor depleted, take it at the channel. When abundant, Renying is three times as large as the inch opening; when depleted, Renying is instead smaller than the inch opening.

## Notes

The course and illnesses of the stomach channel. The interlinear note is not translated (original text only).
- For 納 in 旁納太陽之脈 the interlinear note says "one edition has 約"; it is provisionally translated as "takes in [meets]". Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
- 下廉三寸而別 is provisionally translated as three *cun* below the knee.
