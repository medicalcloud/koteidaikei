# 霊枢 経脈第十 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The gallbladder foot lesser yang vessel starts at the outer canthus, rises to the corner of the head, descends behind the ear, follows the neck running in front of the hand lesser yang, reaches the top of the shoulder, turns back to cross and come out behind the hand lesser yang, and enters the empty basin. Its branch enters the ear from behind it, comes out and runs in front of the ear to behind the outer canthus. Its branch separates at the outer canthus, descends to Daying, unites with the hand lesser yang, reaches below the eye, descends over Jiache, descends the neck to unite at the empty basin, then descends into the chest, passes through the diaphragm, connects with the liver, belongs to the gallbladder, follows the inside of the flanks, comes out at Qijie, winds around the pubic hairline and enters the hip socket sideways. Its straight part descends from the empty basin to the armpit, follows the chest, passes the free ribs, descends to unite in the hip socket, then descends along the yang side of the thigh, comes out at the outer edge of the knee, descends in front of the outer auxiliary bone, descends straight to the end of Juegu, descends to come out in front of the outer ankle, follows the top of the instep, and enters between the little toe and the fourth toe. Its branch separates on the instep, enters between the big toe and the second toe, follows the inside of the forked bones of the big toe and comes out at its tip, then returns through the toenail and comes out at the three hairs. When it is stirred, the illness is a bitter taste in the mouth, frequent sighing, pain in the heart and flanks with inability to turn over, and in severe cases a face as if lightly coated with dust, a body without luster, and heat instead on the outside of the feet; this is called yang reversal. The illnesses produced by the bones, which it governs, are: headache, pain of the chin, pain at the outer canthus, swelling and pain in the empty basin, swelling below the armpit, saber and pearl-string lumps, sweating with shivering and chills, malaria, pain along the outside of the chest, flanks, ribs, thigh and knee down to the shin, Juegu and the front of the outer ankle, and in all the joints, and uselessness of the fourth toe. For these illnesses, drain when abundant, supplement when deficient, be quick when hot, retain when cold, apply moxibustion when sunken, and when neither abundant nor deficient, take it at the meridian. When abundant, Renying is once as large as the inch opening; when deficient, Renying is instead smaller than the inch opening.

## Notes

The course and illnesses of the gallbladder meridian.
- The base's 「別銃眥」 is emended to 「別鋭眥」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*) and Daizhige read 鋭眥, as elsewhere in the same unit; 鋭 is adopted.
- The base's 「馬刁侠癭」 is emended to 「馬刀侠癭」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 馬刀侠癭 (scrofula); 刀 is adopted.
- The base's 「外髁前」 is emended to 「外踝前」. The Siku edition, Daizhige and the *Lingshu jizhu* read 外踝 (ankle); 踝 is adopted.
