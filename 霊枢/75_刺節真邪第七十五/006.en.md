# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Shaking off dust is [for] when the yang qi counterflows greatly, rises and fills the chest, with distension and shoulder breathing; the great qi counterflows upward, with dyspnea and wheezing, sitting bent forward; the patient hates dust and smoke and chokes so that he cannot breathe. It is called shaking off dust because [its effect] is even quicker than shaking off dust."

## Notes

The *Leijing* notes: the yang pathogen is above, so it fills the chest, causing distension, shoulder breathing, counterflow, dyspnea and choking as if from dust and smoke; treating what is above is quicker than shaking off dust, i.e. as fast as brushing off dirt. 䭇 is an old form of 噎, "food not going down". According to Xiao Yanping's note on the *Taisu*, the *Jiayi jing* reads 病咽噎不得息.
