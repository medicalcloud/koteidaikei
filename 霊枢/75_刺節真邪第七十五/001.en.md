# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I have heard that needling has five sections. What are they?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. It describes the five sections of needling (shaking off dust, clearing the clouded, removing the nails, taking off clothes, resolving confusion), needling the five pathogens, the discussion of untying knots, and true, upright and pathogenic qi with the illnesses caused by deficiency pathogens.
