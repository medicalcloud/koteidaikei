# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The sections of needling speak of removing the nails, and you say it is needling the joints and branch collaterals. I wish to hear it all."

## Notes

The base's 刺節善去爪 is emended to 刺節言去爪. The *Taisu*, the *Leijing*, the *Lingshu jizhu*, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text all read 刺節言去爪, and the other four sections have the same form (刺節言振埃, 刺節言発矇, etc.), so 言 is adopted.
