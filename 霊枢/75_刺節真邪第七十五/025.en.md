# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "This is when the yang qi is in surplus and the yin qi insufficient. With insufficient yin qi there is internal heat; with surplus yang qi there is external heat. The heats contend, hotter than embracing charcoal; outside, one dreads the touch of cotton or silk, cannot bear anyone near, nor even the mat. When the interstices are closed, sweat does not come out; the tongue is scorched, the lips withered, the flesh dried out, the throat parched, and one cannot tell good food from bad."

## Notes

The *Leijing* notes: 熱於懐炭 is intense heat; 外畏綿帛近 is not wanting clothes; 不可近身 is dreading people's breath; 不可近席 is aversion to cold; 腊乾 is dry flesh; 飲食不譲美悪 is inability to distinguish flavors. 内熱相搏 is taken as internal and external heat contending.
