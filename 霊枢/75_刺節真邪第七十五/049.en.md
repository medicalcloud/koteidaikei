# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §49 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Genuine qi is what is received from heaven, combined with grain qi to fill the body. Healthy qi is the healthy qi wind, coming from one direction, neither an repletion wind nor a deficiency wind. Pathogenic qi is the deficiency wind that harms and injures people; when it strikes it goes deep and cannot leave of itself. The healthy qi wind strikes shallowly, combines [with the genuine qi] and leaves of itself; its qi comes soft and weak and cannot overcome the genuine qi, so it leaves of itself.

## Notes

Qibo's speech continues through unit 51. Translated following the *Leijing* notes.
- Genuine qi is the original qi; the qi of heaven is received through the nose, that of water and grain through the mouth; in the yang it is yang qi, in the yin yin qi, at the exterior wei-defensive qi, in the interior ying-nutrient qi—all merely other names (*Leijing*).
- 正気者，正風也，従一方来: coming from one direction means coming from the direction where Taiyi resides; wind in accord with the season is upright wind. Upright and repletion winds come from the same direction, but the healthy qi wind comes gently and mildly, the repletion wind violently (*Leijing*).
- Pathogenic qi: wind from the opposite direction is deficiency wind; it strikes severely, enters deep and cannot leave of itself. 合而自去: the pathogen combines with the healthy qi and the healthy qi prevails, so it leaves of itself (*Leijing*).
- The base's 𡵨伯 is emended to 歧伯. The *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource reads 歧伯, the Daizhige text and the *Leijing* 岐伯. 𡵨 is taken as a corrupted form of 歧 and changed to 歧 as elsewhere in the base.
