# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I do not understand the meaning of 'clearing the clouded' in the sections of needling. Clearing the clouded concerns ears that hear nothing and eyes that see nothing; yet you say it is needling the transport points of the bowels to remove illness of the bowels. Which transport point does this? I wish to hear the reason."
