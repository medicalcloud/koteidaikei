# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §27 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Take it at Tianfu and Dazhu with three needlings, then needle Zhonglü to remove the heat; supplement the foot and hand greater yin to remove the sweat. The heat leaves and the sweat subsides faster than taking off clothes."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: Tianfu is a point of the hand greater yin; Dazhu and Zhonglüshu are points of the foot greater yang, and needling them removes heat; supplementing the foot greater yin (spleen) and hand greater yin (lung) channels to bring out sweat, the heat leaves, the sweat stops and the illness is removed as quickly as taking off clothes. This is probably a kind of cold damage with pathogenic heat.
- For 以去其汗 the *Leijing* and the *Lingshu jizhu* read 以出其汗. The base is translated.
- The base's 或之 is emended to 取之. The *Leijing*, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text read 取之 (the *Lingshu jizhu* reads 或 like the base, but quotes 取之於其天府 in its commentary). Since it answers the question 取之奈何, the graphically similar 或 is taken as an error for 取.
