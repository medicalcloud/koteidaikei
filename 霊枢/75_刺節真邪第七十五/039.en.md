# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §39 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In needling large pathogens, make them smaller day by day, draining and seizing the surplus until it is depleted. Strike their passage with the stone, needle the pathogen, examine the flesh closely, and do not mistake its true nature. Needle between the flesh divisions of the yang.

## Notes

The *Leijing* notes: large pathogens are repletion pathogens, too great to remove at once, so they are reduced day by day; 剽 is stone needling, 通 the path of the illness; pathogen and upright, pulse and color must be examined closely—taking small for large would mistake the truth; large repletion pathogens are mostly in the three yang, hence needling the yang flesh divisions.
