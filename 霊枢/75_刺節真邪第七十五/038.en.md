# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §38 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In needling abscess pathogens, do not confront the peak; [proceed gently] as if changing customs and altering temperament. If no pus forms, soften the path and try another way; drive it from where it is heading and give it no place to settle, and it will disperse. Where abscesses pass along the yin and yang channels, take their transport points and drain them.

## Notes

Translated following the *Leijing* notes: 隴 means "peak"; abscess pathogens come sharply and should be avoided; 易俗移性 means harmonizing gradually without haste; 脆 means softening to break down the hardness—if the abscess toxin does not transform, no pus forms, so one supports from within, warms from without, needles or moxas, striving to transform the toxin: this is 脆道更行; 郷 means the direction it heads; 安 means lodging and gathering.
