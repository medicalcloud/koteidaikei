# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When needling the pathogen, press both nostrils firmly with the hand and quickly have the patient lie back; the sound will surely respond to the needle."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: this is the method of testing the sound—needle the point, press the nostrils firmly with the hand, and quickly have the patient lie back; the sound then responds to the needle.
