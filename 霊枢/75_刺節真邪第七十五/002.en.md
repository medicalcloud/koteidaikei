# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "There are indeed five sections: first, shaking off dust; second, clearing the clouded; third, removing the nails; fourth, taking off clothes; fifth, resolving confusion."
