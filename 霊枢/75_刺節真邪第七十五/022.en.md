# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The lower back and spine are the great joints of the body; the limbs and shins are the keys by which a person runs and soars; the penis and testicles are the mechanism within the body, the gauge of yin essence and the path of the fluids. Thus when eating and drinking are immoderate and joy and anger untimely, the fluids overflow inside and flow down to lodge in the testicles; the blood path is blocked, the swelling grows daily without stopping, bending and stretching are difficult, and running is impossible. In this illness water seems to have collected, going neither up nor down. It is taken with the sword needle and stone; its form cannot be hidden and can never be covered. Hence it is called removing the nails."

## Notes

The *Leijing* notes: the lower back and spine hold the body upright, hence the great joints; the limbs and shins enable running, hence the keys; the penis and testicles are the anterior yin and ancestral sinew, where the rise and fall of the original qi of the gate of life show, hence the mechanism within the body; since essence is released from here, they are the gauge of yin essence and the path of the fluids. The fluids overflowing inside and lodging in the testicles, growing daily and impossible to hide, is probably a kind of scrotal hernia; it is named "removing the nails" as if removing a wart.
- For 栄然, the *Taisu* and the *Leijing* read 滎然. It is translated as describing collected water.
