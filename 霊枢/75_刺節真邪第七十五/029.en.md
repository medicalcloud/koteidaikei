# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §29 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The sections of needling speak of resolving confusion, and you say it is knowing fully how to regulate yin and yang, supplementing and draining surplus and insufficiency so that they shift toward each other. How is the confusion resolved?"
