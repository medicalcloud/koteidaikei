# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "To needle this, one must do it at midday, needling Tinggong; [the sensation] reaches the pupil and a sound is heard in the ear. This is its transport point."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: midday is when yang is strong and the qi moves; Tinggong is a bowel transport point of the hand greater yang, whose vessel communicates with the eye, so [the sensation] can reach the pupil; the spot where, on needling, the sound responds in the ear is the point.
