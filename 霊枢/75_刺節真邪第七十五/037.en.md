# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §37 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The methods for needling the five pathogens are no more than five articles: heat of impediment is extinguished; swellings and gatherings are dispersed; cold impediment is warmed; the small is given more yang; the large must be removed. Let me explain the methods.

## Notes

Qibo's speech continues through unit 42. 五章 means five articles, detailed below (*Leijing*).
- For 痺熱, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text read 癉熱. The *Leijing* agrees with the base, so the base is translated.
