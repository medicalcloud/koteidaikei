# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §40 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In needling small pathogens, make [the upright qi] larger day by day, supplementing the insufficiency, and there will be no harm. Observe where it is and meet it at its boundary; [the true qi] from near and far all arrives, [the pathogen] cannot invade from outside and move on, and it dissipates of itself. Needle between the flesh divisions.

## Notes

The *Leijing* notes: small pathogens are depletion pathogens; supplementing them makes the upright qi grow daily so the pathogen withdraws of itself. 迎之界 means meeting the qi where it moves; first supplement the insufficient channel, then drain the surplus one; when the pathogen leaves and the upright recovers, the true qi from near and far all arrives, the pathogen cannot invade from outside and must disperse.
