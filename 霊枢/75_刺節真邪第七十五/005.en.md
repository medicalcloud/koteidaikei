# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The sections of needling speak of shaking off dust, and you say it is needling the outer channels to remove yang illness. I do not understand what you mean. I wish to hear it all."
