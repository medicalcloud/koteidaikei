# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Shaking off dust means needling the outer channels to remove yang illness. Clearing the clouded means needling the transport points of the bowels to remove illness of the bowels. Removing the nails means needling the joints and branch network vessels. Taking off clothes means needling all the extraordinary transport points of the yang. Resolving confusion means knowing fully how to regulate yin and yang, supplementing and draining surplus and insufficiency so that they shift toward each other."

## Notes

Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: shaking off dust is like shaking off dust—taking the outer channels removes yang illness; clearing the clouded is like opening up blindness and deafness—needling the bowel transport points treats bowel illness; removing the nails is like stripping off surplus nails—taking the joints and branch network vessels treats illness where the blood path is blocked; taking off clothes is like removing clothing—needling all the extraordinary yang transport points; resolving confusion is like dispelling bewilderment—knowing yin and yang fully, one regulates depletion and repletion and shifts the illness.
