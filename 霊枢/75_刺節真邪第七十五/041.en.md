# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §41 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In needling heat pathogens, disperse them swiftly; if they go out and do not return, there is no illness. Open and clear the passage, open the gates, let the pathogen get out, and the illness ends.

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: 越 is "dispersing upward", 蒼 "sudden, swift", 出遊 "going out and scattering", 帰 "returning"; in needling heat pathogens swift dispersal is valued, and if they scatter and do not return there is no illness.
