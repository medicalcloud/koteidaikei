# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §44 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "For abscesses use the sword needle; for large [pathogens], the sharp-edged needle; for small, the round-sharp needle; for heat, the arrowhead needle; for cold, the filiform needle.

Qibo's speech continues through unit 45.
