# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §42 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In needling cold pathogens, warm day by day, going and coming slowly, to bring forth the spirit [yang qi]. When the gates are closed, the qi does not scatter, depletion and repletion are regulated, and the [true] qi is preserved."

## Notes

The *Leijing* notes: warming means warming the upright qi; going and coming slowly means being gentle and gradual; bringing forth the spirit means that when the yang qi is brought forth the cold pathogen is removed of itself; supplementing the depletion closes the gates so the qi does not leak, depletion and repletion are regulated, and the true qi is preserved.
