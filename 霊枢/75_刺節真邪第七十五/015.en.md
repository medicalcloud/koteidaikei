# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What a subtle question! This is the great essential of needling, the summit of the needle, of the order of the divine; neither spoken explanation nor written scrolls can reach it. It is called clearing the clouded because [its effect] is even quicker than clearing the clouded."

## Notes

疾於発矇 refers to the speed of the effect (*Leijing*).
