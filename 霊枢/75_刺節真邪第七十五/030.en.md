# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §30 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When great wind is in the body, the blood vessels are depleted on one side; the depleted side is insufficient and the replete side in surplus, the balance of light and heavy is lost; the patient leans and topples, cannot tell east from west or south from north, now up, now down, now turning over, now falling back, tumbling without constancy—worse than bewilderment."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: when wind pathogens are in the body, the blood vessels must be depleted; the upright cannot overcome the pathogen, so there are disorders of balance and leaning; since the tumbling is inconstant, it is worse than bewilderment. This is a kind of wind stroke.
