# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §46 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "One vessel may produce dozens of illnesses—pain, abscess, heat, cold, itching, impediment, numbness—changing without end. Why is this?"

## Notes

From here, true, upright and pathogenic qi and the illnesses caused by depletion pathogens. The *Leijing* note: one vessel means one channel; the pathogenic qi is the depletion wind below, which moves readily and changes often, so the illnesses it causes change without end.
