# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When there is cough with rising qi, the body bent over and chest pain, how does one needle it?"

## Notes

詘 is 屈, "unable to stretch" (*Leijing*).
