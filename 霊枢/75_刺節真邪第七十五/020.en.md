# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Good. This is what is called doing it without seeing, without looking with the eyes, yet taking it as if seen—spirit and illumination in accord."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: the illness has no visible form, and it can be taken without being seen—truly the wonder of spirit and illumination in accord.
