# 霊枢 刺節真邪第七十五 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When needling Tianrong, do not exceed [the time it takes to walk] one *li*; when needling Lianquan, stop when the blood changes [color]."

## Notes

*Leijing* note: 無過一里 means about the time it takes a person to walk one *li*; 血変 means the blood changes color.
