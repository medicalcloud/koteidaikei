# 霊枢 五乱第三十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The twelve channels are divided according to the five phases and apportioned among the four seasons. What is lost so that they become disordered, and what is gained so that they are in order?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about the five cases of disordered qi (the five disorders) and how to needle them.
