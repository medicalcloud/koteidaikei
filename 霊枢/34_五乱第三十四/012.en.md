# 霊枢 五乱第三十四 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Inserting slowly and withdrawing slowly is called guiding the qi. Supplementing and draining without form is called uniting the essence. This is not a matter of surplus or insufficiency, but of disordered qi going counter to each other."
