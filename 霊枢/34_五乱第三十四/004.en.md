# 霊枢 五乱第三十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The twelve channels correspond to the twelve months. The twelve months are divided into the four seasons. The four seasons are spring, autumn, winter and summer, each with its own qi. When the nutritive and defensive follow each other, yin and yang are harmonized, and the clear and turbid do not interfere with each other, then there is accord and order."

## Notes

于 in 不相于 of the base is read as 干 "interfere", as other sources have it (see collation). The same applies to unit 6.
