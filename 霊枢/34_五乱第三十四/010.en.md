# 霊枢 五乱第三十四 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the qi is in the heart, take the stream points of the hand lesser yin and heart governor. When the qi is in the lungs, take the brook point of the hand greater yin and the stream point of the foot lesser yin. When the qi is in the intestines and stomach, take the foot greater yin and *yangming*; if it does not descend, take Sanli. When the qi is in the head, take Tianzhu and Dazhu; if there is no effect, take the brook and stream points of the foot greater yang. When the qi is in the arms and feet, first remove the blood vessels, then take the brook and stream points of the *yangming* and lesser yang."

## Notes

Point selection for the five disorders. 栄輸 is 滎輸.
