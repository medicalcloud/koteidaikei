# 霊枢 五乱第三十四 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the clear qi is in the yin and the turbid qi in the yang, the nutritive qi follows the vessels but the defensive qi moves counter, and the clear and turbid interfere with each other and become disordered in the chest, it is called great oppression. So when the qi is disordered in the heart, there is vexation of the heart, silence, a bowed head and lying still. When disordered in the lungs, one bends forward and back, panting and wheezing, clasping the hands to breathe. When disordered in the intestines and stomach, there is cholera. When disordered in the arms and shins, there is reversal of the four limbs. When disordered in the head, there is reversal counterflow, heaviness of the head, dizziness and falling."

## Notes

The illnesses of the five disorders.
- The base's 「清濁相于」 is emended to 「清濁相干」. Wikisource (both), the *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 干; 干 is adopted as in the preceding unit.
