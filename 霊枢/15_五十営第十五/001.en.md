# 霊枢 五十営第十五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I wish to hear about the fifty circulations. What are they?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, explaining the fifty circuits of qi through the body in a day and night in terms of the movement of heaven and the water clock.
