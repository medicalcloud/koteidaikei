# 霊枢 五十営第十五 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The water clock drips down one hundred marks to divide day and night. Thus with one exhalation the pulse beats twice and the qi moves three *cun*; with one inhalation the pulse also beats twice and the qi moves three *cun*; with a complete breath the qi moves six *cun*. In ten breaths the qi moves six *chi*, and the sun moves two divisions. In two hundred seventy breaths the qi moves sixteen *zhang* two *chi*, the qi passes through the center and makes one circuit of the body, the water drips down two marks, and the sun moves twenty-five divisions. In five hundred forty breaths the qi circles the body twice, the water drips down four marks, and the sun moves forty divisions. In two thousand seven hundred breaths the qi circles the body ten times, the water drips down twenty marks, and the sun moves five lodges and twenty divisions. In thirteen thousand five hundred breaths the qi makes fifty circulations of the body, the water drips down one hundred marks, the sun passes through the twenty-eight lodges, the water of the clock is all used up, and the [daily round of the] vessels is complete.

## Notes

The correspondence between the number of breaths, the distance moved by the qi, the water clock and the movement of the sun.
- Some figures, such as "twenty-five divisions" and "forty divisions", are not proportional to one another, and one view holds they are errors. Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); the base is followed.
