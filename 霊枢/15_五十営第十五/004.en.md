# 霊枢 五十営第十五 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What is called passing through means moving [through the twenty-eight vessels] as a single count. Hence when the fifty circulations are complete, one can live out the full span given by heaven and earth. In all, [the qi] travels eight hundred ten *zhang*."

## Notes

Qibo's speech ends here, concluding the chapter.
