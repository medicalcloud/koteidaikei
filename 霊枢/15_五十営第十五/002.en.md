# 霊枢 五十営第十五 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The circuit of heaven has twenty-eight lunar lodges, each lodge thirty-six divisions; one circuit of human qi [corresponds to] one thousand and eight divisions. The sun passes through the twenty-eight lodges; the human meridians, above and below, left and right, front and back, are twenty-eight vessels, totaling sixteen *zhang* two *chi* around the body, corresponding to the twenty-eight lodges.

## Notes

Qibo's speech continues through unit 4. In this chapter the base writes 岐伯.
- 人気行一周，千八分 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: 28 lodges × 36 divisions = 1008 divisions, matching one circuit of the vessel qi in a day and night. Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* says the actual figure is 1000.
