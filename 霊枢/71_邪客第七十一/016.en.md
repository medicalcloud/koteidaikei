# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Its outer meridian falls ill, but the zang organ does not. Therefore one takes only its meridian at the tip of the sharp bone behind the palm. As for the rest, the emerging, entering and winding of the vessel and the speed of its flow are all like those of the hand lesser yin and heart governor vessels. Hence its own transport points are all needled according to the deficiency or excess, speed or slowness of its qi. This is called draining according to the surge and supplementing according to the decline. In this way the pathogenic qi can be removed and the genuine qi made firm. This is called following the order of heaven."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: the zang organ is within and the meridian runs outside; the heart zang organ is firm and does not admit pathogens, but the meridian cannot be free of illness, so when the lesser yin meridian is ill one takes the tip of the sharp bone behind the palm, i.e. the stream point Shenmen. "The rest" means that the transport points of the lesser yin and heart governor run similarly. "Its own transport points" are those of the lesser yin meridian itself; the *Jiayi jing* lists them (Shaochong, Shaofu, Shenmen, Lingdao, Shaohai).
- 衝 in 因衝 means "abundance" (Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu*).
