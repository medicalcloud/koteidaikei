# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Its outer channel falls ill, but the viscus does not. Therefore one takes only its channel at the tip of the sharp bone behind the palm. As for the rest, the emerging, entering and winding of the vessel and the speed of its flow are all like those of the hand lesser yin and heart governor vessels. Hence its own transport points are all needled according to the depletion or repletion, speed or slowness of its qi. This is called draining according to the surge and supplementing according to the decline. In this way the pathogenic qi can be removed and the true qi made firm. This is called following the order of heaven."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: the viscus is within and the channel runs outside; the heart viscus is firm and does not admit pathogens, but the channel cannot be free of illness, so when the lesser yin channel is ill one takes the tip of the sharp bone behind the palm, i.e. the stream point Shenmen. "The rest" means that the transport points of the lesser yin and heart governor run similarly. "Its own transport points" are those of the lesser yin channel itself; the *Jiayi jing* lists them (Shaochong, Shaofu, Shenmen, Lingdao, Shaohai).
- 衝 in 因衝 means "abundance" (Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu*).
