# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao answered, "Heaven is round and earth square; the human head is round and the feet square in correspondence. Heaven has the sun and moon, and the human being two eyes. Earth has nine provinces, and the human being nine orifices. Heaven has wind and rain, and the human being joy and anger. Heaven has thunder and lightning, and the human being a voice. Heaven has four seasons, and the human being four limbs. Heaven has five notes, and the human being five zang organs. Heaven has six pitch pipes, and the human being six fu organs. Heaven has winter and summer, and the human being cold and heat. Heaven has ten days [stems], and the human being ten fingers. The branches are twelve, and the human being has ten toes, with the penis and testicles to make up the number; women lack these two joints, and so can embrace a human form [conceive]. Heaven has yin and yang, and the human being husband and wife. The year has 365 days, and the human being 360 joints. Earth has high mountains, and the human being shoulders and knees. Earth has deep valleys, and the human being armpits and the backs of the knees. Earth has twelve meridian rivers, and the human being twelve meridian vessels. Earth has springs, and the human being wei-defensive qi. Earth has grasses, and the human being fine hairs. Heaven has day and night, and the human being sleeping and waking. Heaven has rows of stars, and the human being teeth. Earth has small hills, and the human being small joints. Earth has mountain rocks, and the human being prominent bones. Earth has forests, and the human being sinew gatherings. Earth has settlements, and the human being bulging muscles. The year has twelve months, and the human being twelve joints. Earth has places where no grass grows in any season, and some human beings have no children. These are the correspondences between the human being and heaven and earth."

## Notes

Follows the *Leijing* notes: the twelve branches are the earthly branches, corresponding to the toes; there are only ten toes, and the penis (the ancestral sinew) and testicles make twelve; women lack these two joints and so can embrace a human form, 抱 meaning to conceive; 募 is where sinews and vessels gather; the four limbs have three joints each, making twelve; earth has barren land and people include those who bear no children.
- The base's 㬷肉 is emended to 䐃肉. The *Leijing* reads 䐃肉 and notes that it is where fatty flesh gathers (the Daizhige text reads 膕肉, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource another character). 䐃肉, where flesh gathers, fits the parallel with 地有聚邑, so the base's 㬷 is taken as an error for the graphically similar 䐃.
- 草蓂 is taken as "grasses" (the meaning of 蓂 could not be verified).
