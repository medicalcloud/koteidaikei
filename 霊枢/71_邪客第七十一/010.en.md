# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What Your Majesty asks covers the whole of the way of needling."

## Notes

The base's 鍼道乖矣 is emended to 鍼道畢矣. Xiao Yanping's note on the *Taisu* (the *Lingshu* reads 針道畢矣), the *Leijing*, the *Lingshu jizhu*, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text all read 畢. Since Qibo goes on to explain the way of needling in detail, 乖 ("goes against") does not fit, and 畢 ("is complete") is adopted.
