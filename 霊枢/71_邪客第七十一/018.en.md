# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "One must first clearly know the roots and branches of the twelve meridian vessels, the cold and heat of the skin, and the abundance, decline, smoothness and hesitancy of the vessels. If the pulse is smooth and abundant, the illness advances daily; if deficient and fine, it will persist long; if large and hesitant, it is painful impediment; if yin and yang are alike, the illness is hard to cure. If the roots and branches are still hot, the illness is still present; if the heat has already declined, the illness too has gone. Hold the forearm and examine whether its flesh is firm or brittle, small or large, smooth or rough, cold or warm, dry or damp. Then look at the five colors of the eyes to know the five zang organs and determine life and death. Look at the blood vessels and examine their color to know cold, heat, pain and impediment."

## Notes

On 久以持, the *Leijing* note says that where the pathogenic qi is abundant or the original qi deficient, quick results are hard to obtain; one should treat slowly and remove it gradually.
