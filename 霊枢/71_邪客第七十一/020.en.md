# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The way of holding the needle requires uprightness and correctness, calm and stillness. First know depletion and repletion, then apply quick or slow [technique]. Hold the bone with the left hand and follow along with the right, not letting the needle be wrapped in the flesh. In draining, be upright and correct; in supplementing, always close the skin, assist the needle and guide the qi, so that the pathogen disperses and the true qi can settle."

## Notes

Follows the *Leijing* note: hold [the point] with the left hand and follow with the right, striking the point and not the flesh; 果 is 裹, "wrap"; ... only after the pathogenic qi has dispersed and the true qi has resettled is the needle withdrawn—this is the way of holding, loosening and retaining the needle. The *Lingshu jizhu* says 無与肉果 means needling the vessel without injuring the flesh.
