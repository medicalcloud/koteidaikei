# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "For the decoction, take eight *sheng* of water that has flowed more than a thousand *li*, stir it ten thousand times, take five *sheng* of the clear part and boil it over a fire of reed stalks; when it boils, add one *sheng* of glutinous millet and five *ge* of prepared *banxia (hange)*, simmer slowly until reduced to one and a half *sheng*, remove the dregs, and drink one small cup of the liquid three times a day, increasing gradually until it takes effect. If the illness is new, one sleeps as soon as the cup is set down, and it is cured when sweat comes out; if it is long-standing, it is cured after three doses."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: measures differ between ancient and modern times; the ancient eight *sheng* is roughly two *sheng* five *ge* six *shao* of today, and five *sheng* about one *sheng* six *ge* (conversion into Ming measures). Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu*: sleeping as soon as the cup is set down and being cured when sweat comes out mean the illness heals quickly.
