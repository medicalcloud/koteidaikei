# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Bogao, "When pathogenic qi lodges in a person and keeps him from closing his eyes and sleeping, what qi makes it so?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. It treats sleeplessness and the *banxia* decoction and the correspondence of the human body to heaven and earth (dialogues with Bogao), and then the windings of the hand greater yin and heart governor vessels, why the lesser yin has no transport points, the method of holding the needle, and the eight depletions (dialogues with Qibo).
- 不臥出 adds 出 to 不臥 and is hard to construe. It is translated as "not sleeping", and 出 is left untranslated.
