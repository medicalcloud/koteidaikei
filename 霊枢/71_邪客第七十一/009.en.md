# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I wish to hear about the method of holding the needle, the principle of inserting it, the meaning of loosening and retaining, and stretching the skin to open the interstices—how is it done? The windings of the vessels, the places where they emerge and enter: where they arrive and come out, where they arrive and stop, where they arrive and slow, where they arrive and speed, where they arrive and enter, and the transport points of the six bowels in the body—I wish to hear them all. And in order, the places of separation: where they separate and enter the yin, branch and enter the yang—by what paths do they travel? I wish to hear the whole method."

## Notes

From here on the dialogue is with Qibo.
- The *Leijing* note: emerging, stopping, slowing, speeding and entering refer to the five transport points; the places of separation are where the channels and network vessels branch, part and rejoin. The *Leijing* reads 少叙 for 少序. It is taken as "in some order", but this is uncertain.
