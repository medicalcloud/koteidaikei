# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Good. This is what is called breaking through the blocked meridians so that the meridians and collaterals communicate freely and yin and yang attain harmony. I wish to hear the prescription."
