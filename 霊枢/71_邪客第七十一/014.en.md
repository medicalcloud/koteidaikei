# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The lesser yin is the vessel of the heart. The heart is the great ruler of the five viscera and six bowels, the dwelling of the essence and spirit. That viscus is firm and solid, and pathogens cannot be accommodated in it. If it accommodated them, the heart would be injured; when the heart is injured the spirit leaves; when the spirit leaves, there is death. Therefore all pathogens in the heart are in the heart's enclosing network. The enclosing network is the heart governor vessel. Hence [the lesser yin] alone has no transport points."
