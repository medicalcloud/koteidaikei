# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "When the five grains enter the stomach, their dregs, fluids and ancestral qi divide into three paths. The ancestral qi accumulates in the chest, comes out at the windpipe, penetrates the heart vessel and drives respiration. The nutritive qi secretes the fluids, pours them into the vessels, transforms them into blood to nourish the four extremities, and inside pours into the five viscera and six bowels, corresponding to the count of the water-clock marks. The defensive qi is the fierce, swift part of the qi, which goes first to the four extremities and between the flesh and skin without rest. By day it moves in the yang, by night in the yin, always passing from the division of the foot lesser yin through the five viscera and six bowels. Now when reversal qi lodges in the five viscera and six bowels, the defensive qi guards only the outside, moving in the yang and unable to enter the yin. Moving in the yang, the yang qi becomes abundant; when the yang qi is abundant, the yang springing vessel sinks [is damaged], and it cannot enter the yin; the yin is depleted, so the eyes do not close."

## Notes

以応刻数: the nutritive qi circulates through the whole body, and its cycles correspond to the marks of the water clock (Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*).
- 陽蹻陥 follows the *Leijing* note that 陥 means "suffer damage". According to Xiao Yanping's note on the *Taisu*, the *Jiayi jing* reads 陽蹻満.
