# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Following the divisions of the flesh, separate the skin with the left hand, insert slightly and advance slowly and straight; then the spirit is not scattered and the pathogenic qi can be removed."

## Notes

Follows the *Leijing* note: in needling, always follow the grain of the flesh divisions; separate the skin with the left hand and insert slightly with the right, advancing slowly and straight; then one naturally reaches the right spot with ease, the spirit is not scattered, the pathogenic qi leaves, and the skin is not injured.
