# 霊枢 邪客第七十一 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "Supplement the insufficiency, drain the surplus, regulate depletion and repletion to open the path and remove the pathogen. Give one dose of *banxia* decoction to drink; once yin and yang communicate, sleep comes at once."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: after needling, one should further treat with medicine; the *banxia* decoction is a method designed specifically to remove the pathogen.
