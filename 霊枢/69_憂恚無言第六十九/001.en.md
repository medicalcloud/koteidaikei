# 霊枢 憂恚無言第六十九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Shaoshi, "When a person suddenly becomes worried or resentful and speaks without voice, which path is blocked? Which qi goes out and makes the voice unclear? I wish to hear the reason."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. The interlocutor is Shaoshi. It explains the functions of the organs that produce the voice, why the voice is suddenly lost, and how to needle it.
