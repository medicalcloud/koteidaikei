# 霊枢 憂恚無言第六十九 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The foot lesser yin rises to connect with the tongue, networks with the transverse bone, and ends at the epiglottis. Drain its blood vessels on both sides, and the turbid qi will open. The vessel of the epiglottis rises to network with the *Ren* meridian; take it at Tiantu, and the epiglottis will rise again."

## Notes

The end of the chapter. The interlocutor had been Shaoshi, but here Qibo answers (so also in the *Leijing*).
- *Leijing* notes: 両瀉 means needling both feet; the blood vessel of the foot lesser yin is probably its point Taixi; 辟 means "open"; Tiantu is the meeting of the *Yinwei* meridian and the *Ren* meridian, and needling it treats sudden loss of voice.
