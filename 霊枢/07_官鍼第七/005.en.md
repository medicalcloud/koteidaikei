# 霊枢 官鍼第七 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Where the vessels lie deep and cannot be seen, needle them by inserting the needle slightly and retaining it long, to bring the qi of the vessel to the hole. Where the vessels are shallow, do not needle [at once]; press to cut off the vessel and then needle, so that the essence is not let out and only the pathogenic qi comes out. What is called "needling three times brings out the grain qi" means: first needle shallow through the skin to let out the yang pathogen; needling a second time lets out the yin pathogen, going a little deeper, through the skin into the muscle but not yet entering between the partings of the flesh; once between the partings of the flesh, the grain qi comes out. Hence the *Needling Method* says: "At first needle shallow to expel the pathogenic qi and bring the blood and qi; next needle deep to bring the pathogen of yin qi; finally needle very deep to bring down the grain qi." This is what it means. Hence one who uses the needle without knowing what the year adds, the abundance and decline of the qi, and where deficiency and excess arise cannot be a practitioner.

## Notes

Needling according to the depth of the vessels, and "triple needling".
- 刺法曰 is taken as a quotation from an old book.
