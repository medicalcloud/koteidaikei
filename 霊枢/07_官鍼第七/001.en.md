# 霊枢 官鍼第七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The key to all needling is that the official needles are most subtle. Each of the nine needles has its proper use: long and short, large and small, each has its application; without the right use, the illness cannot be moved. If the illness is shallow and the needle deep, the good flesh inside is harmed and abscesses form in the skin. If the illness is deep and the needle shallow, the qi of the illness is not drained and instead becomes a large pus. If the illness is small and the needle large, the qi is drained too much and the illness will surely do harm. If the illness is large and the needle small, the qi is not drained, and again there is failure. Losing the proper use of needles, the large drain [too much] and the small do not move [the illness]. Having spoken of the faults, let me speak of their applications.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, which describes the use of the needles without a dialogue.
- The base's 「支為大膿」 is emended to 「反為大膿」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), the *Taisu* and *Jiayi jing* read 反為大膿; the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* agree with the base (支). Reading "instead becomes a large pus", parallel to 皮膚為癰, is more natural and is supported by the older texts; 反 is adopted.
