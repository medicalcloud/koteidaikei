# 霊枢 官鍼第七 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the illness is in the skin with no fixed location, take it with the arrowhead needle at the diseased place; if the skin is white, do not take it. When the illness is between the partings of the flesh, take it with the round needle at the diseased place. When the illness is in the channels and network vessels as a chronic impediment, take it with the sharp needle. When the illness is in the vessels and the qi is scant and should be supplemented, take it with the spoon needle at the divisions of the well and brook points. When the illness forms a large pus, take it with the sword needle. When an impediment illness flares suddenly, take it with the round-sharp needle. When an impediment illness has qi pain that does not go away, take it with the filiform needle. When the illness is in the center, take it with the long needle. When the illness is water swelling that cannot pass the joints, take it with the large needle. When the illness is firmly lodged in the five viscera, take it with the sharp needle, draining at the divisions of the well and brook points, taking them according to the four seasons.

## Notes

The uses of each of the nine needles.
