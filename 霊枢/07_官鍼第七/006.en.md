# 霊枢 官鍼第七 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

There are five kinds of needling, corresponding to the five viscera. The first is called half needling: insert shallow and withdraw quickly, without the needle harming the flesh, like plucking a hair, to take the qi of the skin; this corresponds to the lungs. The second is called leopard-spot needling: needle left, right, front and back, aiming to hit the vessels, to take the blood of the channels and network vessels; this corresponds to the heart. The third is called joint needling: needle straight onto the ends of the sinews on left and right to take sinew impediment, taking care not to let blood; this corresponds to the liver; it is also called deep-pool needling, or *qi* needling. The fourth is called union-valley needling: needle left and right like a chicken's foot between the partings of the flesh to take muscle impediment; this corresponds to the spleen. The fifth is called transport needling: insert straight and withdraw straight, inserting deep to the bone to take bone impediment; this corresponds to the kidneys.

## Notes

Five kinds of needling corresponding to the five viscera; the end of the chapter.
