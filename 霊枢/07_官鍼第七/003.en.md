# 霊枢 官鍼第七 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

There are nine kinds of needling, corresponding to nine variations. The first is called transport needling: needling the brook and stream points of the channels and the transport points of the viscera. The second is called distant needling: when the illness is above, take it below, needling the transport points of the bowels. The third is called channel needling: needling the knotted network vessels of the great channels and the channel divisions. The fourth is called network needling: needling the blood vessels of the small network vessels. The fifth is called partition needling: needling between the partings of the flesh. The sixth is called great drainage needling: needling large pus with the sword needle. The seventh is called hair needling: needling superficial impediment in the skin. The eighth is called great needling: taking the right for the left and the left for the right. The ninth is called quenching needling: needling with a heated needle to take impediment.

## Notes

Nine kinds of needling corresponding to nine variations.
- The base's 「刺有九日応」 is emended to 「刺有九以応」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 以応九変, the same form as 以応十二経 below; 以 is adopted.
