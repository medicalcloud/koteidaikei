# 霊枢 陰陽清濁第四十 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The hand greater yang alone receives the turbidity of the yang, and the hand greater yin alone receives the clarity of the yin. The clear runs up to the openings, the turbid runs down through the meridians. All the yin are clear, but the foot greater yin alone receives turbidity."
