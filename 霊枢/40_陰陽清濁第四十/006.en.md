# 霊枢 陰陽清濁第四十 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What receives grain is turbid; what receives qi is clear. The clear pours into the yin, the turbid into the yang. The clear part of the turbid rises and comes out at the throat; the turbid part of the clear goes downward. When the clear and turbid interfere with each other, it is called disordered qi."
