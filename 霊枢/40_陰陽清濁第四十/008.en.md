# 霊枢 陰陽清濁第四十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The great division of the qi is that the clear pours up into the lungs and the turbid runs down into the stomach. The clear qi of the stomach rises and comes out at the mouth; the turbid qi of the lungs descends to pour into the channels and accumulates within in the sea."
