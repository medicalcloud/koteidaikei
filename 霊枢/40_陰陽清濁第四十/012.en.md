# 霊枢 陰陽清濁第四十 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The clear has smooth qi, the turbid has rough qi; this is the norm of qi. Hence needling the yin, go deep and retain; needling the yang, go shallow and quick; where clear and turbid interfere, regulate by the numbers."

## Notes

The end of the chapter. 于 in 相于 of the base is read as 干 "interfere", as other sources have it (see collation).
