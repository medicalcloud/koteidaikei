# 霊枢 陰陽清濁第四十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I have heard that the twelve meridians correspond to the twelve meridian waters, whose five colors differ and whose clarity and turbidity are not the same. People's blood and qi seem to be one; how do they correspond?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about the clarity and turbidity of human qi and how to needle accordingly.
