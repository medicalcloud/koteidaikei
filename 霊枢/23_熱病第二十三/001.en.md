# 霊枢 熱病第二十三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In hemilateral withering, one side of the body is useless and painful, but speech is unchanged and the mind is not disordered; the illness is between the partings and interstices. Take it with the great needle, adding to what is insufficient and reducing what is in surplus, and recovery is possible. As for the illness of *fei* [paralysis], the body has no pain, the four limbs cannot be drawn in, and the mind is not greatly disordered; if the speech is feeble but intelligible, it can be treated; if it is so severe that one cannot speak, it cannot be treated. When the illness first arises in the yang and later enters the yin, first take the yang and later the yin, taking it shallowly.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, which describes, without a dialogue, hemilateral withering, *fei* paralysis, heat illness and other illnesses and their treatment.
- 巨鍼 is taken as the large needle.
