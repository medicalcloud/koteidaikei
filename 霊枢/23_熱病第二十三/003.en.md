# 霊枢 熱病第二十三 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For qi filling the chest with panting, take the foot greater yin at the tip of the big toe, the width of a shallot leaf from the nail; retain if cold, be quick if hot, and stop when the qi descends. For heart *shan* with sudden pain, take the foot greater yin and *jueyin*, needling all and removing the blood network vessels. For throat impediment with a curled tongue, a dry mouth, vexation of the heart, heart pain, and pain along the inner edge of the arm so that the hand cannot reach the head, take the ring finger below the nail, the width of a chive leaf from the tip. For redness and pain in the eye beginning at the inner canthus, take the yin springing vessel. For wind convulsions with the body arched back, first take the foot greater yang, the back of the knee and the blood network vessels, letting blood; if there is cold within, take Sanli. For urinary block, take the yin springing vessel, above the three hairs and the blood network vessels, letting blood. When men are as if with *gu* [swollen as by worm poison] and women as if pregnant, the body, lower back and spine feel as if coming apart, and there is no desire to eat or drink, first take Yongquan until blood appears, then look for abundant ones on the instep and let blood from them all.

## Notes

Points for various illnesses; the end of the chapter.
- 男子如蠱，女子如怚 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: men are distended and oppressed as if by worm poison; 怚 should be 胎 (pregnancy); the form resembles these though the illness is not. The *Taisu* reads 女子如妲, and Yang Shangshan explains it as illnesses of infatuation between men and women.
