# 霊枢 五邪第二十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen is in the lungs, the illness is pain of the skin, cold and heat, rising qi with dyspnea, sweating, and coughing that shakes the shoulders and back. Take it at the outer transport points in the chest and beside the third and fifth vertebrae of the back; press quickly with the hand, and where it feels relieved, needle there. Take it in the empty basin to dispel it.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, which describes, without a dialogue, the illnesses when pathogens are in the five zang organs and the points to take. The interlinear note is not translated (original text only).
- The base's 「背三節五蔵之傍」 is emended to 「背三節五節之傍」. The *Leijing* reads 背三節五節之傍, and Zhang Jiebin's note says beside the third vertebra is Feishu and beside the fifth Xinshu; the base's interlinear note also says "one edition has 五顴, or 五節" (the *Lingshu jizhu* agrees with the base). The third and fifth vertebrae are meant, so 節 is adopted.
