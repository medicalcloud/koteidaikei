# 霊枢 五邪第二十 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen is in the kidneys, the illness is pain of the bones and yin impediment. Yin impediment is pain that cannot be located by pressing, with abdominal distension, lower back pain, difficult defecation, pain in the shoulders, back, neck and nape, and occasional dizziness. Take it at Yongquan and Kunlun, and wherever blood is seen, take it all.
