# 霊枢 五邪第二十 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen is in the heart, the illness is heart pain, a tendency to sorrow, and occasional dizziness and falling. Observe surplus and insufficiency, and regulate at its transport points.

## Notes

The end of the chapter. The final 顀 is the headword of a sound gloss; the interlinear note (pronunciation) is not translated (original text only).
