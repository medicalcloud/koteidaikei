# 霊枢 五邪第二十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen is in the liver, there is pain in both flanks, cold in the center, malign blood inside, frequent jerking of the joints when walking, and occasional swelling of the legs. Take Xingjian to draw [the qi] below the flanks, supplement Sanli to warm the stomach, take the blood vessels to disperse the malign blood, and take the green vessels around the ears to remove the jerking.
