# 霊枢 五邪第二十 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When a pathogen is in the spleen and stomach, the illness is pain of the flesh. When yang qi is in surplus and yin qi insufficient, there is heat in the center with frequent hunger; when yang qi is insufficient and yin qi in surplus, there is cold in the center with rumbling intestines and abdominal pain; when yin and yang are both in surplus or both insufficient, there is both cold and heat. In all cases regulate at Sanli.

## Notes

- The base's 「邪左脾胃」 is emended to 「邪在脾胃」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 邪在脾胃, like 邪在肺 and 邪在肝; 在 is adopted.
