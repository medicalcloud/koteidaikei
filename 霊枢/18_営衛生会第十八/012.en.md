# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The middle jiao also lies alongside the stomach and comes out behind the upper jiao. What it receives as qi, it separates from the dregs, steams the fluids, transforms their essential subtleties, and pours them up into the lung vessel, where they are transformed into blood to support the living body. Nothing is more precious than this, so it alone can move in the meridian tunnels; it is called the ying-nutrient qi."
