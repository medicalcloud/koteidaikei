# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The lower jiao separates at the winding intestine, pours into the bladder and seeps into it. Thus water and grain always stay together in the stomach, become dregs, go down together into the large intestine and form the lower jiao; seeping, they go down together, the juices are filtered and separated, and they follow the lower jiao and seep into the bladder."

## Notes

- The base's 「済泌別注」 is emended to 「済泌別汁」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu* and *Leijing* read 済泌別汁; 汁 is adopted.
