# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The lower burner separates at the winding intestine, pours into the bladder and seeps into it. Thus water and grain always stay together in the stomach, become dregs, go down together into the large intestine and form the lower burner; seeping, they go down together, the juices are filtered and separated, and they follow the lower burner and seep into the bladder."

## Notes

注 in 済泌別注 of the base is read as 汁 "juice", as other sources have it (see collation).
