# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The nutritive comes out from the middle burner, the defensive from the lower burner."

## Notes

One view holds that 衛出于下焦 is an error for the upper burner; unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points). The base is followed.
