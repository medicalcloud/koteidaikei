# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The ying-nutrient comes out from the middle jiao, the wei-defense from the upper jiao."

## Notes

The base is followed.
- The base's 「衛出于下焦」 is emended to 「衛出于上焦」. The *Taisu* and *Jiayi jing* read 衛出于上焦; the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* agree with the base (下焦). Unit 8 of this chapter says the upper jiao moves together with the ying-nutrient, and chapter 30 calls what the upper jiao sends out "qi", so 上 is adopted following the *Taisu* and *Jiayi jing*.
