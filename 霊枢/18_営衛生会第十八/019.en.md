# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Excellent. I have heard that the upper jiao is like mist, the middle jiao like soaking, and the lower jiao like a drainage ditch. This is what it means."

## Notes

The end of the chapter.
