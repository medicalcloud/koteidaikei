# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "This is because one is harmed by wind outside and the interstices open inside; the hair is steamed and the pores leak, and the defensive qi rushes there, so it surely cannot follow its path. This qi is swift, fierce, smooth and quick; seeing an opening, it goes out, so it cannot follow its path. Hence it is called leaking discharge."
