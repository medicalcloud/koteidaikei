# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "People receive qi from grain. Grain enters the stomach and is passed on to the lungs, and the five viscera and six bowels all receive qi from it. The clear part becomes the nutritive, the turbid part the defensive. The nutritive is inside the vessels, the defensive outside the vessels. The nutritive circulates without rest and after fifty [circuits] meets again in a great meeting; yin and yang run through each other like a ring without end. The defensive qi moves twenty-five times through the yin and twenty-five times through the yang, divided into day and night. Thus when the qi reaches the yang one rises, and when it reaches the yin one rests. Hence it is said: at midday the yang peaks, which is double yang; at midnight the yin peaks, which is double yin. Thus the greater yin governs the inside and the greater yang governs the outside, each moving twenty-five times, divided into day and night. Midnight is the peak of yin; after midnight the yin declines; at dawn the yin is exhausted and the yang receives the qi. At midday the yang peaks; as the sun goes west the yang declines; at sunset the yang is exhausted and the yin receives the qi. At midnight there is the great meeting, when all people lie asleep; this is called uniting yin. At dawn the yin is exhausted and the yang receives the qi. So it goes without end, sharing the order of heaven and earth."
