# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The nutritive and defensive are essential qi; the blood is spirit qi. Hence blood and qi are of different names but the same kind. Therefore one who has lost blood must not be made to sweat, and one who has lost sweat must not be bled. Thus human life has a double death but no double life."

## Notes

The meaning of 両死 and 両生 is unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); translated provisionally.
