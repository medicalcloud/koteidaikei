# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The ying-nutrient and wei-defense are essential qi; the blood is spirit qi. Hence blood and qi are of different names but the same kind. Therefore one who has lost blood must not be made to sweat, and one who has lost sweat must not be bled. Thus human life has a double death [collapse of yin or of yang] but no double life [life by yin or yang alone]."

## Notes

両死 and 両生 follow Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: blood and sweat are interior and exterior; robbing both leads to collapse of yin or of yang, and either is fatal, hence a double death; and no life is possible with yang alone or yin alone, hence no double life.
