# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The upper jiao comes out from the upper mouth of the stomach, rises alongside the throat, passes through the diaphragm and spreads in the chest, runs to the armpit, moves along the divisions of the greater yin, returns to the *Yangming*, rises to the tongue, descends to the foot *Yangming*, and constantly moves together with the ying-nutrient, twenty-five times through the yang and twenty-five times through the yin, [making] one circuit. Thus after fifty times it meets again in a great meeting at the hand greater yin."
