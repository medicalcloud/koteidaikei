# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When a person takes hot food and drink and it goes down to the stomach, sweat comes out before its qi has settled, sometimes on the face, sometimes on the back, sometimes on half the body. Why does it come out without following the path of the defensive qi?"
