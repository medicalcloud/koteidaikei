# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "From where do people receive qi? Where do yin and yang meet? Which qi is ying-nutrient and which is wei-defense? From where does the ying-nutrient arise, and where does the wei-defense meet? The old and the strong differ in qi, and yin and yang are in different places. I wish to hear about their meeting."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking where the ying-nutrient and wei-defense qi arise and meet, and about the sanjiao.
