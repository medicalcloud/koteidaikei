# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "From where do people receive qi? Where do yin and yang meet? Which qi is nutritive and which is defensive? From where does the nutritive arise, and where does the defensive meet? The old and the strong differ in qi, and yin and yang are in different places. I wish to hear about their meeting."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking where the nutritive and defensive qi arise and meet, and about the triple burner.
