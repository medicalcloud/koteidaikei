# 霊枢 営衛生会第十八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "In the strong, the qi and blood are abundant, the flesh is smooth, the paths of the qi are open, and the ying-nutrient and wei-defense move without losing their norm; so they are alert in the day and sleep at night. In the old, the qi and blood decline, the flesh withers, the paths of the qi are rough, the qi of the five zang organs struggle with one another, the ying-nutrient qi declines and diminishes, and the wei-defensive qi is assailed within; so they are not alert in the day and cannot sleep at night."
