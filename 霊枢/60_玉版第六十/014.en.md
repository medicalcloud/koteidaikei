# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Abdominal distension, a hot body and a large pulse are the first counterflow. Rumbling and fullness of the abdomen, cold limbs, diarrhea and a large pulse are the second. Unceasing nosebleed with a large pulse is the third. Cough with blood in the urine, wasting of the form and a small, forceful pulse is the fourth. Cough, wasting of the form, a hot body and a small, rapid pulse are called the fifth. Such cases die within fifteen days. Great abdominal distension, cold extremities, wasting of the form and severe diarrhea are the first counterflow. Abdominal distension with bloody stools and a large pulse that is at times cut off is the second. Cough, blood in the urine, wasting of the flesh and a beating pulse are the third. Vomiting blood, fullness of the chest pulling into the back and a small, rapid pulse are the fourth. Cough and vomiting, abdominal distension with swill diarrhea and a cut-off pulse are the fifth. Such cases die within a day. When a practitioner needles without examining this, it is called counterflow treatment."

## Notes

The counterflows of various illnesses.
- The base's 「形内脱」 is emended to 「形肉脱」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige and the *Lingshu jizhu* read 形肉脱 (wasting of the flesh); 内 is taken as a graphic error for 肉.
