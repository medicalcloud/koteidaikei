# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the pus has formed, nine in ten die and one lives. Therefore the sage does not let it form; he clearly made good methods, wrote them on bamboo and silk, and had capable people follow and transmit them to later generations without end, so that [people] would be prepared even when not meeting [a physician]."
