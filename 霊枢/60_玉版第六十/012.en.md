# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In those harmed [by abscesses], whites of the eyes turned green and small dark pupils are the first counterflow; vomiting after taking medicine is the second; abdominal pain with severe thirst is the third; impaired movement of the shoulders and nape is the fourth; a hoarse voice and loss of color are the fifth. Apart from these five, it is compliance."

## Notes

The five counterflows of abscesses. Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* links them with "abscesses in the five regions mean death" in chapter 21 (*Hanre bing*).
