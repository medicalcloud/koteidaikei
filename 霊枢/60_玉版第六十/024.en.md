# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §24 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Needling it by peeping at the gate [shallowly], one dies at home; needling it by entering the gate [deeply], one dies in the hall."

## Notes

Following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: the gate is the qi gate; peeping is shallow, and the harm is slow, so one dies at home; entering is deep, and the harm is fast, so one dies in the hall.
