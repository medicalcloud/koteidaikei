# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Your words on the needle, Master, are very grand: it is matched with heaven and earth, counting the patterns of heaven above and measuring the order of the earth below, distinguishing the five viscera within and ordering the six bowels without; the twenty-eight meetings of the channels all have their circuits. [The needle] can kill the living but cannot raise the dead. Can you reverse this?"
