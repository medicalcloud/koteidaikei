# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Treating the small with the small gives small results; treating the large with the large does much harm. Hence what has already formed pus and blood can only be taken with the stone, the sword needle and the sharp needle."
