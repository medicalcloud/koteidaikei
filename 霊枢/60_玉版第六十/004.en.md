# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Even the sage cannot transform [what has formed]; therefore the pathogen must not be allowed to linger. When two armies face each other, banners in sight of each other and bare blades arrayed in the field, this is not the plan of one day. To be able to command the people so that orders are carried out and prohibitions obeyed, and the soldiers suffer no harm from blades, is not the teaching of one day, nor gained in a moment. To let the body suffer abscesses and the gathering of pus and blood—is this not far from the way? The arising of abscesses and the forming of pus and blood neither descend from heaven nor come out of the earth; they arise from the accumulation of the minute. Hence the sage treats himself before anything has taken form, while the foolish meet it when it is already formed."

## Notes

聖人不能使化者，為之邪不可留也 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "a pathogen in the realm is disorder, in the body illness; once formed, even a sage cannot transform it, so the pathogen must not be allowed to linger". For 須臾之得也 the *Taisu* reads 須久之方得也 (Xiao Yanping's note).
