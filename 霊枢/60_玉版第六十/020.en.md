# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What people receive qi from is grain; where grain pours is the stomach; the stomach is the sea of water and grain, qi and blood. Where the sea sends its clouds is all under heaven; where the stomach sends out qi and blood is the meridian tunnels. The meridian tunnels are the great collaterals of the five zang organs and six fu organs; meet them and rob them, and that is all [it takes to kill]."
