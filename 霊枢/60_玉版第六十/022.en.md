# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "If one meets it at Wuli, [the qi] stops halfway; after five arrivals it ceases; after five goings the qi of the zang organ is exhausted; thus after five times five, twenty-five, its transport points are exhausted. This is what is called robbing the heavenly qi; it is not that [the needle] itself can cut off life and overturn the life span."

## Notes

The prohibition on needling Wuli (as in chapter 2, "the yin chi pulsing vessel is at Wuli; forbidden among the five transport points"). The *Leijing* also quotes this passage, but no explanation of 非能絶其命而傾其寿者也 was found; the translation is provisional.
