# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "What is greater than heaven? What is greater than the needle is only the five weapons. The five weapons are equipment for death, not instruments of life. Moreover, man is the anchor of heaven and earth; how can he not participate with them? To govern the people, there is only the needle. Between the needle and the five weapons, which is smaller?"
