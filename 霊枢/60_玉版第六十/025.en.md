# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §25 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "How good the method, how clear the way! Let it be inscribed on jade tablets as a precious treasure, transmitted to later generations as a prohibition on needling, so that the people dare not violate it."

## Notes

The end of the chapter.
