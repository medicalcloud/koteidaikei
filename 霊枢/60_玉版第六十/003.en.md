# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When illness arises from unpredictable joy and anger and immoderate eating and drinking, with insufficient yin qi, surplus yang qi and the ying-nutrient qi not moving, it breaks out as abscesses. When yin and yang do not communicate and the two heats struggle, they turn into pus. Can the small needle take it?"
