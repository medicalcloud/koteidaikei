# 霊枢 玉版第六十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I regarded the small needle as a trifling thing, yet you, Master, say that it accords with heaven above, earth below and man in between. I think this overstates the meaning of the needle. I wish to hear the reason."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, describing the prevention and treatment of abscesses, the counterflow and compliance of illnesses, and the prohibition on needling Wuli.
