# 霊枢 営気第十六 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "In the way of the ying-nutrient qi, taking in grain is the treasure. When grain enters the stomach, it is passed on to the lungs, flows and overflows within and spreads and disperses without. Its essential and pure part moves in the meridian tunnels, circulating constantly without stopping, beginning again when it ends; this is called the order of heaven and earth. Thus the qi comes out from the greater yin, pours into the hand *Yangming*, rises to pour into the foot *Yangming*, descends to the top of the instep, pours between the big toes and unites with the greater yin, rises to reach the thigh, pours from the spleen into the heart, follows the hand lesser yin, comes out at the armpit and descends the arm, pours into the little finger and unites with the hand greater yang, rises over the armpit, comes out on the inside below the eye, pours into the inner canthus, rises to the vertex, descends the nape and unites with the foot greater yang, follows the spine down to the sacrum, descends to pour into the tip of the little toe, follows the center of the sole and pours into the foot lesser yin, rises to pour into the kidneys, pours from the kidneys into the heart, disperses outward in the chest, follows the heart governor vessel, comes out at the armpit and descends the arm, comes out between the two tendons, enters the palm, comes out at the tip of the middle finger, returns to pour into the tip of the ring finger and unites with the hand lesser yang, rises to pour into Danzhong, disperses in the sanjiao, pours from the sanjiao into the gallbladder, comes out at the flanks and pours into the foot lesser yang, descends to the top of the instep, again pours from the instep between the big toes and unites with the foot *Jueyin*, rises to the liver, pours from the liver up into the lungs, rises along the throat, enters the opening of the nasopharynx and ends at the storehouse gate [the opening from the roof of the throat into the nose]. Its separate branch rises over the forehead, follows the vertex, descends the middle of the nape, follows the spine and enters the coccyx; this is the governing vessel. It connects with the genitals, rises through the pubic hair, enters the navel, rises along the inside of the abdomen, enters the empty basin, descends to pour into the lungs, and comes out again at the greater yin. This is the course of the ying-nutrient qi, the norm of counterflow and compliance."

## Notes

The chapter consists of a single unit of the Yellow Emperor's speech, describing the order in which the ying-nutrient qi circulates through the twelve meridians and the governing and conception vessels.
- 畜門 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "the storehouse gate is the opening above the roof of the throat leading to the nose".
