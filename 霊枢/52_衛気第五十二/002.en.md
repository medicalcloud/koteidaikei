# 霊枢 衛気第五十二 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "How broad is the sage emperor's discussion! Let your servant exhaust his understanding and tell all. The root of the foot greater yang is five *cun* above the heel; its branch is at the Gate of Life on both [sides]; the Gate of Life is the eyes. The root of the foot lesser yang is at Qiaoyin; its branch is in front of the Window Cage; the Window Cage is the ear. The root of the foot lesser yin is three *cun* above [the point] below the inner ankle; its branch is at the back transport point and the two vessels below the tongue. The root of the foot *jueyin* is about five *cun* above Xingjian; its branch is at the back transport point. The root of the foot *yangming* is at Lidui; its branch is at Renying, where the cheeks flank the nasopharynx. The root of the foot greater yin is four *cun* in front of and above Zhongfeng; its branch is at the back transport point and the root of the tongue. The root of the hand greater yang is behind the outer [wrist] protuberance; its branch is one *cun* above the Gate of Life. The root of the hand lesser yang is two *cun* above [the space] between the little finger and the ring finger; its branch is at the upper corner behind the ear and at the outer canthus below. The root of the hand *yangming* is in the elbow bone, rising to Bieyang; its branch is below the forehead, joining above the clamp. The root of the hand greater yin is in the inch opening; its branch is at the pulse inside the armpit. The root of the hand lesser yin is at the tip of the sharp bone; its branch is at the back transport point. The root of the hand heart governor is two *cun* between the two tendons behind the palm; its branch is three *cun* below the armpit. In examining these, depletion below means reversal, abundance below means heat; depletion above means dizziness, abundance above means heat and pain. Hence what is replete is cut off and stopped; what is depleted is drawn up and raised.

## Notes

The root and branch of the twelve channels. Qibo's speech continues through unit 3.
- 両絡命門: Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* identifies it as Jingming, one on each side, hence "both"; he suggests the root of the foot greater yang is Fuyang.
- For 別陽 the *Leijing* says its meaning is unclear; Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* calls it the network vessel of the hand *yangming*. For 鉗上 the *Leijing* refers to 鉗耳 in chapter 5 (flanking the ears), and Yang Shangshan places it one *cun* below the cheek, behind Renying, above Futu. The translation keeps the characters.
- 石 in 石者 is read as 実 following the *Leijing*.
