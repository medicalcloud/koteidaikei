# 霊枢 衛気第五十二 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Let me speak of the qi streets: the qi of the chest has a street, the qi of the abdomen has a street, the qi of the head has a street, the qi of the shins has a street. Hence when the qi is in the head, stop it at the brain; when in the chest, stop it at the chest and the back transport points; when in the abdomen, stop it at the back transport points and the pulsing vessels of the *Chong* meridian left and right of the navel; when in the shins, stop it at Qijie, Chengshan, and above and below the ankle. To take these, use the filiform needle; always first press and hold for a long time until there is a response under the hand, then needle and give. What it treats: headache, dizziness and falling, abdominal pain, fullness in the center and sudden distension, and new accumulations. Pain that moves is easily ended; accumulations without pain are hard to end."

## Notes

The four qi streets. Qibo's speech ends here, concluding the chapter.
- The base's 「衝脈干臍」 is emended to 「衝脈于臍」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*) and Daizhige read 於, the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* 于; 干 is taken as a graphic error for 于. Zhang Jiebin's note identifies the pulsing vessels beside the navel as Huangshu, Tianshu, etc.
