# 霊枢 五禁第六十一 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the flesh of the form is already wasted, that is the first robbery; after great loss of blood, the second; after great sweating, the third; after great diarrhea, the fourth; just after childbirth or after great bleeding, the fifth. None of these may be drained."
