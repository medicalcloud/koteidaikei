# 霊枢 五禁第六十一 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In heat illness, a calm pulse, or a pulse abundant and agitated after sweating has already come out, is the first counterflow. Diarrhea with a surging, large pulse is the second. Fixed impediment that does not move, broken-down bulging muscles, a hot body and a pulse cut off on one side are the third. Dissipation with wasting of the form, a hot body, a dull white complexion, and passing clotted blood from behind, with heavy clots, is called the fourth. Cold and heat with wasting of the form and a firm, beating pulse is called the fifth."

## Notes

The five counterflows; the end of the chapter. Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* says all five are illnesses of yin deficiency that must not be needled.
