# 霊枢 五禁第六十一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I have heard that needling has five prohibitions. What are the five prohibitions?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, describing the five prohibitions, five robberies, five excesses, five counterflows and nine suitabilities of needling.
