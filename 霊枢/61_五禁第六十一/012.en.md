# 霊枢 五禁第六十一 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "On *jia* and *yi* days, when the day itself governs, do not needle the head or perform 'clearing the clouded' inside the ears. On *bing* and *ding* days, do not perform 'shaking off dust' at the shoulders, throat and Lianquan. On *wu* and *ji* days, and in the four [earth] seasons, do not needle the abdomen, perform 'removing the nails', or drain water. On *geng* and *xin* days, do not needle the joints of the thighs and knees. On *ren* and *gui* days, do not needle the feet and shins. These are the five prohibitions."

## Notes

The five prohibitions according to the ten-stem days and body regions.
- 日自乗 means "when the day itself governs" (Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*): *jia* and *yi* correspond to the head, *bing* and *ding* to the shoulders and throat, *wu*, *ji* and the four seasons to the abdomen and limbs, *geng* and *xin* to the joints, thighs and knees, *ren* and *gui* to the feet and shins. 発矇, 振埃 and 去爪 are needling methods explained in chapter 75 (*Leijing*).
- The base's 「戊巳」 is emended to 「戊己」, a graphic error in naming the stem days (the *Leijing* also writes 戊巳 but reads 戊己 in its note).
