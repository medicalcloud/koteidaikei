# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The heart corresponds to the vessels. Those with thick skin have thick vessels, and those with thick vessels have a thick small intestine. Those with thin skin have thin vessels, and those with thin vessels have a thin small intestine. Those with slack skin have slack vessels, and those with slack vessels have a large and long small intestine. Those with thin skin and empty, small vessels have a small and short small intestine. Those whose yang channels are all very winding have a knotted small intestine.

## Notes

沖 in 脈沖小 is read as "empty", following Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*.
