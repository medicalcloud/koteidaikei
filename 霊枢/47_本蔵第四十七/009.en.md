# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Those with a red complexion and fine skin texture have a small heart; those with coarse texture have a large heart. Those without a [visible] xiphoid have a high heart; those with a small, short, raised xiphoid have a low heart. Those with a long xiphoid have a [low and] firm heart; those with a weak, small and thin xiphoid have a fragile heart. Those whose xiphoid hangs straight down without rising have an upright heart; those whose xiphoid leans to one side have a tilted heart.
