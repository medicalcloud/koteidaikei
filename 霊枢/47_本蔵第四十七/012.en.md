# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Those with a yellow complexion and fine texture have a small spleen; those with coarse texture have a large spleen. Those with upturned lips have a high spleen; those with slack, drooping lips have a low spleen. Those with firm lips have a firm spleen; those with large lips that are not firm have a fragile spleen. Those with good upper and lower lips have an upright spleen; those with lips raised on one side have a tilted spleen.

## Notes

The outer signs of the spleen.
