# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Those with a green complexion and fine texture have a small liver; those with coarse texture have a large liver. Those with a broad chest and outward-turned rib ends have a high liver; those with close flanks and rib ends [low] like a hare have a low liver. Those with good chest and flanks have a firm liver; those with weak rib bones have a fragile liver. Those whose chest and abdomen are well matched have an upright liver; those whose rib bones are raised on one side have a tilted liver.

## Notes

The outer signs of the liver.
- The meanings of 反骹 and 兎骹 are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); translated provisionally.
