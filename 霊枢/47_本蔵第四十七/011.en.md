# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Those with a green complexion and fine texture have a small liver; those with coarse texture have a large liver. Those with a broad chest and rib bones raised and spread have a high liver; those with close flanks and rib ends [low] like a hare have a low liver. Those with good chest and flanks have a firm liver; those with weak rib bones have a fragile liver. Those whose chest and abdomen are well matched have an upright liver; those whose rib bones are raised on one side have a tilted liver.

## Notes

The outer signs of the liver.
- 反骹 and 兎骹 follow Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: 骹 normally means the narrow part of the shin near the foot, but since the liver is being examined it seems to mean the bones below the flanks; 反骹 is ribs raised and spread, 兎骹 ribs low and close like a hare's.
