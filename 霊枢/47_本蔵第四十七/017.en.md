# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The lungs pair with the large intestine, and the skin corresponds to the large intestine. The heart pairs with the small intestine, and the vessels correspond to the small intestine. The liver pairs with the gallbladder, and the sinews correspond to the gallbladder. The spleen pairs with the stomach, and the flesh corresponds to the stomach. The kidneys pair with the triple burner and bladder, and the interstices and fine hairs correspond to the triple burner and bladder."
