# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The lungs correspond to the skin. Those with thick skin have a thick large intestine; those with thin skin have a thin large intestine. Those with slack skin and a large abdomen have a large and long large intestine; those with tight skin have a tight and short large intestine. Those with smooth skin have a straight large intestine; those whose skin and flesh do not separate have a knotted large intestine.
