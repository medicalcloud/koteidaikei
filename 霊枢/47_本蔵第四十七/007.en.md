# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the kidneys are small, the zang organ is calm and hard to harm. When the kidneys are large, one is prone to lower back pain, cannot bend forward or back, and is easily harmed by pathogens. When the kidneys are high, one suffers from pain of the back and paravertebral muscles and cannot bend forward or back. When the kidneys are low, the lower back and sacrum ache, one cannot bend forward or back, and there is fox *shan*. When the kidneys are firm, there is no lower back or back pain. When the kidneys are fragile, one is prone to wasting and thirst and easily harmed. When the kidneys are upright, they are harmonious and hard to harm. When the kidneys are tilted, one suffers from pain of the lower back and sacrum. These twenty-five variations are what people constantly suffer from."

## Notes

The twenty-five variations of the five zang organs. Qibo's speech ends here.
