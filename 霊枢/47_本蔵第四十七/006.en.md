# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the spleen is small, the zang organ is calm and hard to harm by pathogens. When the spleen is large, one suffers from crowding and pain in the soft flanks and cannot walk fast. When the spleen is high, the soft flanks pull the free ribs with pain. When the spleen is low, it presses down on the large intestine, and then the zang organ suffers from receiving pathogens. When the spleen is firm, the zang organ is calm and hard to harm. When the spleen is fragile, one is prone to wasting-thirst and easily harmed. When the spleen is upright, it is harmonious and hard to harm. When the spleen is tilted, there is frequent fullness and distension.
