# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied, "What an exacting question! The five viscera participate in heaven and earth, accompany yin and yang, connect with the four seasons and transform with the five seasonal divisions. The five viscera certainly differ in size, height, firmness and alignment; the six bowels likewise differ in size, length, thickness, knottedness and straightness, slackness and tension. These twenty-five all differ: some good, some bad, some auspicious, some ominous. Let me speak of the method.
