# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The five viscera and six bowels are the lodgings of pathogens. Let me tell you the reason. Those whose five viscera are all small rarely fall ill but suffer from a burning heart and great worry. Those whose five viscera are all large are easygoing in affairs and hard to make worry. Those whose five viscera are all high like lofty conduct. Those whose five viscera are all low like to put themselves below others. Those whose five viscera are all firm have no illness. Those whose five viscera are all fragile are never free of illness. Those whose five viscera are all upright are harmonious and win people's hearts. Those whose five viscera are all tilted have a crooked mind and are prone to stealing; they cannot be fair to others and go back on their words."
