# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Emperor said, "Excellent. But that is not what I asked. I wish to hear why some people cannot fall ill, living out their heavenly span: even deep worry, great fear and anxiety cannot diminish them, and severe cold and great heat cannot harm them; while others, who never leave the shelter of their rooms and have no fear or anxiety, still cannot escape illness. I wish to hear the reason."
