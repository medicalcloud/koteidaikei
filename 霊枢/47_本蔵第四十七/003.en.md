# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the heart is small, it is calm and pathogens cannot harm it, but it is easily harmed by worry. When the heart is large, worry cannot harm it, but it is easily harmed by pathogens. When the heart is high, it fills the lungs, there is oppression and forgetfulness, and it is hard to open up with words. When the heart is low, the zang organ is outward, easily harmed by cold and easily frightened by words. When the heart is firm, the zang organ is calm and its guard secure. When the heart is fragile, one is prone to wasting and thirst and heat in the center. When the heart is upright, it is harmonious and hard to harm. When the heart is tilted, one's resolve is inconstant and has nothing to hold to.

## Notes

- The base's 「中悦而善忘」 is emended to 「中悗而善忘」. Daizhige and the *Leijing* read 悗 (Wikisource *Huangdi neijing* has 俛): the heart filling the lungs causes oppression; 悗 is adopted.
