# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The spleen corresponds to the flesh. Those whose bulging muscles are firm and large have a thick stomach; those whose bulging muscles are slender have a thin stomach. Those whose bulging muscles are small and slender have a stomach that is not firm. Those whose bulging muscles are out of proportion to the body have a low stomach, and with a low stomach the lower duct is constricted and not free. Those whose bulging muscles are not firm have a slack stomach. Those whose bulging muscles have no small nodules have a tense stomach. Those whose bulging muscles have many small nodules have a knotted stomach, and with a knotted stomach the upper duct is constricted and not free.

## Notes

麽 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* ("thin and slender"). For 裏累, the *Leijing* says its meaning is unclear and cites Gao Zhizhai: small knotted lumps felt in the bulging muscles; the translation follows this provisionally, but the exact meaning is unknown.
