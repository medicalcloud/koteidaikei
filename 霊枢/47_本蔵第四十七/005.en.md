# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the liver is small, the zang organ is calm and there is no illness below the flanks. When the liver is large, it presses on the stomach and the throat; pressing on the throat causes diaphragm obstruction and pain below the flanks. When the liver is high, it props up and cuts into the diaphragm, and the flanks are oppressed, causing rushing breath. When the liver is low, it presses on the stomach and the space below the flanks is empty; when that space is empty, pathogens are easily received. When the liver is firm, the zang organ is calm and hard to harm. When the liver is fragile, one is prone to wasting and thirst and easily harmed. When the liver is upright, it is harmonious and hard to harm. When the liver is tilted, there is pain below the flanks.
