# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The kidneys correspond to the bones. Those with dense texture and thick skin have a thick triple burner and bladder; those with coarse texture and thin skin have a thin triple burner and bladder. Those with loose interstices have a slack triple burner and bladder; those with tight skin and no fine hairs have a tense triple burner and bladder. Those with beautiful, coarse fine hairs have a straight triple burner and bladder; those with sparse fine hairs have a knotted triple burner and bladder."

Qibo's speech ends here.
