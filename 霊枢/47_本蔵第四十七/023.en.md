# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §23 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The kidneys correspond to the bones. Those with dense texture and thick skin have a thick sanjiao and bladder; those with coarse texture and thin skin have a thin sanjiao and bladder. Those with loose interstices have a slack sanjiao and bladder; those with tight skin and no fine hairs have a tense sanjiao and bladder. Those with beautiful, coarse fine hairs have a straight sanjiao and bladder; those with sparse fine hairs have a knotted sanjiao and bladder."

Qibo's speech ends here.
