# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Those with a white complexion and fine texture have small lungs; those with coarse texture have large lungs. Those with large shoulders, an outward-curving chest and a sunken throat have high lungs; those with close armpits and spread flanks have low lungs. Those with good shoulders and a thick back have firm lungs; those with thin shoulders and back have fragile lungs. Those with a thick back and chest have upright lungs; those whose ribs are sparse on one side have tilted lungs.

## Notes

The outer signs of the lungs.
