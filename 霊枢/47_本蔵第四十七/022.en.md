# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The liver corresponds to the nails. Those with thick, yellow nails have a thick gallbladder; those with thin, pink nails have a thin gallbladder. Those with hard, green nails have a tense gallbladder; those with soft, red nails have a slack gallbladder. Those with straight, white nails without ridges have a straight gallbladder; those with poor, black nails with many lines have a knotted gallbladder.
