# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the lungs are small, one drinks little and does not suffer dyspnea. When the lungs are large, one drinks much and is prone to chest bi-impediment, throat impediment and counterflow qi. When the lungs are high, there is rising qi, breathing with the shoulders, and cough. When the lungs are low, they rest on the diaphragm, which presses on them, and there is frequent pain below the flanks. When the lungs are firm, there is no cough or rising qi. When the lungs are fragile, one suffers from wasting-thirst and is easily harmed. When the lungs are upright, they are harmonious and hard to harm. When the lungs are tilted, there is pain on one side of the chest.
