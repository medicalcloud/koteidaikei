# 霊枢 本蔵第四十七 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Those with a black complexion and fine texture have small kidneys; those with coarse texture have large kidneys. Those with high ears have high kidneys; those with a hollow behind the ears have low kidneys. Those with firm ears have firm kidneys; those with thin ears that are not firm have fragile kidneys. Those with good ears positioned in front at the jaw angle have upright kidneys; those with one ear higher have tilted kidneys. In all these variations, maintaining them brings calm, diminishing them brings illness."

## Notes

The outer signs of the kidneys. Qibo's speech ends here.
- 持則安，減則病 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* ("if one maintains oneself according to these variations one is at peace; any loss brings illness"); Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* is similar.
