# 霊枢 病伝第四十二 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Silent without a sound, vague without a form, it breaks the hair and opens the interstices; the proper qi tilts aside, the excessive pathogens spread and overflow, the blood vessels transmit and pool, the great qi enters the zang organs, the abdomen aches and the lower parts are flooded; it can bring death but cannot bring life."
