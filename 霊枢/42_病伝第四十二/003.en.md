# 霊枢 病伝第四十二 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "This is what is called 'hold to the one without losing it, and all things are accomplished.' Now I have heard the essentials of yin and yang, the principles of deficiency and excess, the faults of tilting and shifting, and the kinds that can be treated. I wish to hear about the changes of illness, those that spread, are transmitted, are cut off and ruined, and cannot be treated. May I hear it?"
