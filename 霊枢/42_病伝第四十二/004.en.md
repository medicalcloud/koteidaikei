# 霊枢 病伝第四十二 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "An essential question about the way! Its clarity is like being awake in daylight; its difficulty is like closing the eyes at night. If one can take it on and follow it, the spirit is accomplished together with it; following it to the end, the spirit obtains it naturally. The principle of generating spirit can be written on bamboo and silk, but cannot be passed on to descendants."

## Notes

The meaning of 可著于竹帛，不可伝于子孫 is unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); translated by the characters.
