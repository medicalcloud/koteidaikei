# 霊枢 病伝第四十二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I have received the nine needles from you, Master, and have privately looked through the various methods: guiding exercises and moving the qi, massage, moxibustion, compresses, needling, cautery, and drinking medicines. May one hold to just one, or should one practice them all?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about the order in which illness is transmitted from viscus to viscus and the time of death.
- 喬摩 is taken as massage.
