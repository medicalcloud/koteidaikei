# 霊枢 寒熱病第二十一 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The body of the five viscera has five regions: Futu is the first; the calf is the second (the calf is the gastrocnemius); the back is the third; the transport points of the five viscera are the fourth; the nape is the fifth. Abscesses in these five regions mean death. When the illness begins in the hands and arms, first take the hand *yangming* and greater yin to induce sweating; when it begins in the head, first take the greater yang of the nape to induce sweating; when it begins in the feet and shins, first take the foot *yangming* to induce sweating. The arm greater yin can induce sweating, and the foot *yangming* can induce sweating. So if taking the yin causes excessive sweating, stop it at the yang; if taking the yang causes excessive sweating, stop it at the yin. The harm of needling is this: if one hits [the qi] but does not withdraw, the essence leaks; if one withdraws without hitting it, the qi is brought [and stagnates]. When the essence leaks, the illness becomes severe and debilitating; when the qi is brought, abscesses arise.

## Notes

The five regions, inducing sweat, and the harms of needling; the end of the chapter. The final 槁腊齲頄悗腓 are headwords of sound glosses; the interlinear notes (pronunciations and meanings) are not translated (original text only).
- The base's 「眥三」 is emended to 「背三」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige and the *Leijing* read 背三 (the back is the third region); 背 is adopted.
