# 霊枢 寒熱病第二十一 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The branch of the arm *yangming* that enters the cheekbone and spreads through the teeth is called Daying; take it for decay of the lower teeth. If the arm is averse to cold, supplement; if not averse to cold, drain. The branch of the foot greater yang that enters the cheekbone and spreads through the teeth is called Jiaosun; take it for decay of the upper teeth. It lies in front of the nose and cheekbone. At the time of the illness, its pulse is abundant; drain when abundant, supplement when depleted. Another view says take it where it comes out beside the nose. The branch of the foot *yangming* that flanks the nose and enters the face is called Xuanlu; it belongs to the mouth and enters opposite to attach to the root of the eye. Observing where there is fault, take it, reducing what is in surplus and adding to what is insufficient; doing the opposite makes it worse. The branch of the foot greater yang that passes through the nape and enters the brain belongs directly to the root of the eye and is called the eye connection. For painful head and eyes, take it in the nape between the two sinews. Having entered the brain, it divides into the yin springing and yang springing vessels; yin and yang cross each other, yang entering yin and yin coming out to yang, crossing at the outer canthus. When yang qi is abundant, the eyes stare open; when yin qi is abundant, the eyes close.

## Notes

The vessels and points related to the teeth and eyes.
- 反者益其 is provisionally read as 益甚 "makes it worse". Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
- 足太陽…名曰角孫 does not fit the usual assignment of Jiaosun to the hand lesser yang. Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
