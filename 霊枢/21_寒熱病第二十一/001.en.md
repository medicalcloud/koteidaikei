# 霊枢 寒熱病第二十一 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In cold and heat of the skin, one cannot touch the mat, the hair is scorched, the nose is dry and parched, and there is no sweating. Take the network vessels of the three yang and supplement the hand greater yin. In cold and heat of the muscles, the muscles ache, the hair is scorched and the lips dry and parched, and there is no sweating. Take the three yang below to remove the blood, and supplement the foot greater yin to bring out the sweat. In cold and heat of the bones, there is no rest from the illness and sweat pours without stopping. If the teeth have not yet withered, take the lesser yin at the network vessels of the inner thigh; if the teeth have already withered, there is death without cure. Bone reversal is likewise.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, which describes, without a dialogue, cold and heat of the skin, muscles and bones and other illnesses, and the points to take.
