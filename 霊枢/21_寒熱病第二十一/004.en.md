# 霊枢 寒熱病第二十一 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For heat reversal of the feet, take the foot greater yin and lesser yang, retaining in both. For cold reversal of the limbs, take the foot *Yangming* and lesser yin at the feet, retaining in both. For a slack tongue with drooling and vexed oppression, take the foot lesser yin. For shivering with chills, chattering jaw, no sweating, abdominal distension and vexed oppression, take the hand greater yin. Needling deficiency, needle as [the qi] goes; needling excess, needle as it comes. In spring take the collaterals, in summer the partings and interstices, in autumn the qi opening, in winter the river and stream points. In all four seasons, take each season as the standard. The collaterals treat the skin, the partings and interstices treat the flesh, the qi opening treats the sinews and vessels, and the river and stream points treat the bones and marrow.
