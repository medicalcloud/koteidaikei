# 霊枢 寒熱病第二十一 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In bone impediment, all the joints are useless and painful, sweat pours, and the heart is vexed. Take the channels of the three yin and supplement them. When the body has been injured with much bleeding, and also struck by wind and cold, or one has fallen, and the four limbs are limp and cannot be drawn in, it is called body sloth. Take the triple-knot crossing below the navel in the lower abdomen. The triple-knot crossing is [where] the *yangming* and greater yin [cross the conception vessel], three *cun* below the navel, Guanyuan. In reversal impediment, reversal qi rises to the abdomen. Take the network vessels of yin and yang, observing which governs the illness; drain the yang and supplement the yin channel. The pulsing vessel at the side of the neck is Renying; Renying is the foot *yangming*, in front of the neck sinew. Behind the neck sinew is the hand *yangming*, called Futu. The next vessel is the hand lesser yang vessel, called Tianyou. The next vessel is the foot greater yang, called Tianzhu. The pulsing vessel below the armpit is the arm greater yin, called Tianfu. For yang counterflow with headache, fullness of the chest and inability to breathe, take Renying. For sudden loss of voice with stiffened qi, take Futu and the root of the tongue and let blood. For sudden deafness with clouded qi and unclear ears and eyes, take Tianyou. For sudden cramps, epilepsy and dizziness, with legs that cannot support the body, take Tianzhu. For sudden heat with internal counterflow, the liver and lungs struggling with each other and blood overflowing from the nose and mouth, take Tianfu. These are the five regions of Tianyou.

## Notes

Bone impediment, body sloth and reversal impediment, and the five points of the neck vessels (the five regions of Tianyou). The interlinear note is not translated (original text only).
- The base's 「次脈足少陽脈也名曰天牖」 is emended to 「次脈手少陽脈也名曰天牖」. The Lingshu editions agree with the base (足少陽), but the *Taisu* and *Lingshu jizhu* read "the next vessel is the hand lesser yang, called Tianyou". Tianyou is a point of the hand lesser yang (as in chapters 2 and 5), so 手 is adopted.
