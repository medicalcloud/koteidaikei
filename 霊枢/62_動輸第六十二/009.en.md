# 霊枢 動輸第六十二 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The thoroughfare vessel is the sea of the twelve channels. Together with the great network vessel of the lesser yin it starts below the kidneys, comes out at Qijie, follows the inner edge of the thigh, enters the back of the knee obliquely, follows the inner edge of the shinbone alongside the lesser yin channel, descends behind the inner ankle and enters the sole. Its branch enters the ankle obliquely, comes out to attach on the instep, enters between the big toes and pours into the network vessels to warm the feet and shins. This is why this vessel always beats."
