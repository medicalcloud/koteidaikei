# 霊枢 動輸第六十二 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The *Chong* meridian is the sea of the twelve meridians. Together with the great collateral of the lesser yin it starts below the kidneys, comes out at Qijie, follows the inner edge of the thigh, enters the back of the knee obliquely, follows the inner edge of the shinbone alongside the lesser yin meridian, descends behind the inner ankle and enters the sole. Its branch enters the ankle obliquely, comes out to attach on the instep, enters between the big toe and the second toe and pours into the collaterals to warm the feet and shins. This is why this vessel always beats."
