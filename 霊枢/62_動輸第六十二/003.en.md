# 霊枢 動輸第六十二 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The stomach is the sea of the five zang organs and six fu organs; its clear qi rises and pours into the lungs, and the lung qi moves it along the greater yin. It moves back and forth with the breath, so with one exhalation the pulse beats twice and with one inhalation it beats twice again; breathing never stops, so the pulse beats without stopping."
