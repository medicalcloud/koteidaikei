# 霊枢 動輸第六十二 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the qi leaves the zang organs, it is sudden, like the release of a crossbow, like water rushing down a bank; rising to the fish, it declines instead, and its remaining qi, declining and scattering, counterflows upward; hence its movement is feeble."

## Notes

Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: even a strong crossbow loses force at the end and a rapid stream slows; the pulse, abundant at the inch opening, declines on reaching the fish border, and its remaining qi counterflows upward in a scattering way, hence its movement is feeble.
