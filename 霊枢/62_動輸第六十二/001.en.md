# 霊枢 動輸第六十二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "There are twelve channels, yet only the hand greater yin, the foot lesser yin and the *yangming* pulse without rest. Why?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, explaining why the pulses of the hand greater yin (inch opening), foot *yangming* (Renying) and foot lesser yin (Taixi) always beat.
