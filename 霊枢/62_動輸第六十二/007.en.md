# 霊枢 動輸第六十二 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The stomach qi rises and pours into the lungs. Its fierce qi that rises to strike the head follows the throat, rises to the openings, follows the eye connection, enters to connect with the brain, comes out at the cheek, descends to Kezhuren, follows the jaw, unites with the *yangming*, and descends together to Renying. This is the stomach qi branching off and running to the *yangming*. Hence yin and yang [the inch opening and Renying], above and below, beat as one. Hence in yang illness a small yang pulse is counterflow, and in yin illness a large yin pulse is counterflow. Yin and yang are both still and both moving, [balanced] like a pulled rope; when they tilt, there is illness."

## Notes

Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* takes yin and yang here as the hand greater yin (inch opening) and the *yangming* (Renying); since the stomach qi rising to the lungs comes from the same source, their beats are as one.
