# 霊枢 動輸第六十二 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The meeting places of yin and yang at the four extremities are the great collaterals of the qi; the four streets are the shortcuts of the qi. So when the collaterals are cut off, the shortcuts open; when the four extremities are released, the qi follows and unites, transporting to each other like a ring."

## Notes

四街 is taken as the four qi streets (chest, abdomen, head, shins) described in chapter 52 (*Weiqi*).
