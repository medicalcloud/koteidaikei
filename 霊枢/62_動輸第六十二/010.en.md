# 霊枢 動輸第六十二 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The ying-nutrient and wei-defense move through each other above and below like a ring without end. Now, if one suddenly meets pathogenic qi or great cold, so that the hands and feet are limp, and the movement at the meeting places where the yin and yang paths of the vessels transport to each other is lost, by what route does the qi return?"
