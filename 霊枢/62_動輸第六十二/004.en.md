# 霊枢 動輸第六十二 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When the qi passes the inch opening, rising to ten [abundance], where does it grow from? Sinking to eight [decline], where does it hide? By what path does it return? I do not know its limit."

## Notes

上十焉息，下八焉伏 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: up and down are the force of advance and retreat; ten and eight are images of abundance and decline; 焉 is "where"; 息 is to grow. As the pulse advances its qi is abundant—where does it come from? As it retreats its qi declines—where does it go to hide? The *Jiayi jing* reads 上出焉息，下出焉伏 (Xiao Yanping's note on the *Taisu*).
