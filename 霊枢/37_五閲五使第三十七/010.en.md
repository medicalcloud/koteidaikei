# 霊枢 五閲五使第三十七 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The five zang organs are examined. So when the lungs are ill, there is dyspnea and flaring of the nostrils; when the liver is ill, the canthi are green; when the spleen is ill, the lips are yellow; when the heart is ill, the tongue is curled and short and the cheekbones red; when the kidneys are ill, the cheekbones and forehead are black."

## Notes

- The base's 「喘息鼻脹」 is emended to 「喘息鼻張」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 鼻張 (flaring nostrils); 張 is adopted.
