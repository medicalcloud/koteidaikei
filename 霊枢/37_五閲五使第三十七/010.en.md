# 霊枢 五閲五使第三十七 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The five viscera are examined. So when the lungs are ill, there is panting and flaring of the nostrils; when the liver is ill, the canthi are green; when the spleen is ill, the lips are yellow; when the heart is ill, the tongue is curled and short and the cheekbones red; when the kidneys are ill, the cheekbones and forehead are black."

## Notes

脹 in 鼻脹 is read as 張 "flaring", as other sources have it (see collation).
