# 霊枢 五閲五使第三十七 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I have heard that in needling there are the five officials and five inspections, by which one observes the five qi. The five qi are the messengers of the five zang organs and the companions of the five seasons. I wish to hear where these five messengers appear."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking how to examine the five zang organs through the five officials (nose, eyes, lips, tongue and ears) and the complexion.
