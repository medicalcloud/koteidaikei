# 霊枢 五閲五使第三十七 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The pulse appears at the qi opening and the color at the bright hall [nose]; the five colors appear in turn to correspond to the five seasons, each according to its norm. When the channel qi enters the viscera, one must treat the interior."
