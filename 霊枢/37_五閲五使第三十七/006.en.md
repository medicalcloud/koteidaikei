# 霊枢 五閲五使第三十七 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the five officials are already distinct and the gate [between the brows] and the courtyard [forehead] are surely broad, then the bright hall is established. When the bright hall is broad and large, the screens [cheeks and front of the ears] are visible outside, the square walls have high bases, and [the earlobes] hang down outside, the five colors are in order, level, broad and large, and one's life span reaches a hundred years. When this is seen, needling will surely end [the illness]. Such people have surplus blood and qi and firm, dense flesh, so they can bear the needle."

## Notes

己 in 己弁 of the base is read as 已 "already". 已 in 苦已鍼 is read as 以, as other sources have it (see collation).
- The regions referred to by 蕃蔽 and 方壁高基，引垂居外 are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); translated provisionally.
