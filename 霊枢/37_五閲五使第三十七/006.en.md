# 霊枢 五閲五使第三十七 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the five officials are already distinct and the gate [between the brows] and the courtyard [forehead] are surely broad, then the bright hall is established. When the bright hall is broad and large, the screens [cheeks and front of the ears] are visible outside, the square walls have high bases [bone frame], and [the features] hang open on the outside [clearly spread], the five colors are in order, level, broad and large, and one's life span reaches a hundred years. When this is seen, needling will surely end [the illness]. Such people have surplus blood and qi and firm, dense flesh, so they can bear the needle."

## Notes

- 蕃蔽 and 方壁高基，引垂居外 follow Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: 蕃 is the side of the cheek, 蔽 the ear gate, 壁 the walls, 基 the bone frame; 引垂居外 means clearly open and spread, referring to the height and thickness of these regions apart from the colors.
- The base's 「五官己弁」 is emended to 「五官已弁」. The sense is 五官已弁 "the five officials are already distinct"; 己 is taken as a graphic error for 已.
- The base's 「故可苦已鍼」 is emended to 「故可苦以鍼」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing* (quoting it) and *Lingshu jizhu* read 故可苦以鍼 "can be pained with the needle"; 以 is adopted.
