# 霊枢 五閲五使第三十七 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When the five pulses appear calmly and the five colors are seen calmly, what are those whose normal color is nonetheless in danger

## Notes

The unit boundary in the base falls between 如 and 何, so the translation continues into unit 12.
- 安 in 五脈安出，五色安見 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: the pulse and color appear calm and untroubled.
