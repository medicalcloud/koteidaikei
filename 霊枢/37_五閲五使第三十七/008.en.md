# 霊枢 五閲五使第三十七 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The nose is the official of the lungs; the eyes are the official of the liver; the mouth and lips are the official of the spleen; the tongue is the official of the heart; the ears are the official of the kidneys."
