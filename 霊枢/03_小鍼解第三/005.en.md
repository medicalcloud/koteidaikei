# 霊枢 小鍼解第三 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

"Look at the complexion and examine the eyes to know whether it is scattered or restored; unify one's form, listen to its movement and stillness" means that the superior practitioner knows how to read the five colors in the eyes, and knows how to examine the size, slackness or tension, slipperiness or hesitancy at the chi and cun, so as to tell what is ill. "Knowing whether it is pathogenic or proper" means knowing how to discuss the winds of deficiency pathogens and proper pathogens. "The right [hand] governs pushing, the left holds and controls" means holding the needle and moving it in and out. "When the qi arrives, withdraw" means withdrawing once the qi is regulated by supplementing or draining. "Unify" in "regulating the qi lies in end and beginning" means holding the mind [single]. "The meeting places of the joints number three hundred sixty-five" refers to the collaterals seeping into and irrigating the joints.
