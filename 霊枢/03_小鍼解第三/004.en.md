# 霊枢 小鍼解第三 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

"When qi is in the vessels, pathogenic qi is above" means pathogenic qi strikes people high up, hence pathogenic qi is above. "Turbid qi is in the middle" means water and grain all enter the stomach, their essential qi rises to pour into the lungs, and the turbid remains in the intestines and stomach; when cold and warmth are unsuitable and eating and drinking immoderate, illness arises in the intestines and stomach; hence it is called turbid qi in the middle. "Clear qi is below" means the clear and damp earth qi always strikes people starting from the feet; hence clear qi is below. "When the needle [enters] the sunken vessels, pathogenic qi comes out" means taking the upper [points]. "When the needle [enters] the middle vessels, turbid qi comes out" means taking the uniting point of *Yangming*. "When the needle goes too deep, the pathogenic qi sinks instead" means one should not needle shallow, floating illnesses deeply; if deep, the pathogenic qi follows it in, hence "sinks instead". "Skin, flesh, sinews and vessels each have their place" means the meridians and collaterals each have what they govern. "Taking the five vessels brings death" means that while the illness is in the center and the qi insufficient, one uses the needle simply to drain all the yin vessels greatly. "Taking the three yang vessels" means simply draining all the qi of the three yang, leaving the patient debilitated without recovery. "Robbing yin brings death" means taking Wuli of the chi, going five times. "Robbing yang brings madness" is to be taken literally.

## Notes

Explanation of the phrases of chapter 1, unit 6. "Deep vessels" means taking the upper [points of the head], "middle vessels" the uniting point of *Yangming*.
- Chapter 1's 取三脈者恇 is explained here as 取三陽之脈者.
