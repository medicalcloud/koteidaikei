# 霊枢 小鍼解第三 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What is called "the qi of the five viscera is already cut off outside" means that the qi at the pulse opening is cut off outside and does not arrive, yet one instead takes the transport points of the four extremities, retaining the needles to bring yin qi. When yin qi arrives, yang qi enters instead; entering, there is counterflow; counterflow means death; and the death is agitated because yin qi is in surplus. The reason for examining the eyes is that the five viscera make the five colors show clearly in sequence; when they show clearly, the voice becomes distinct; a distinct voice means the voice differs from usual.

## Notes

Explanation of "counterflow reversal" in chapter 1, unit 8 and of why the eyes are examined, concluding the chapter.
