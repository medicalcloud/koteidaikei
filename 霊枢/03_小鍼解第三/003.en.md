# 霊枢 小鍼解第三 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What is called "fill what is deficient" means that when the qi opening is deficient one should supplement. "Drain what is full" means that when the qi opening is abundant one should drain. "Remove what is stagnant and old" means removing [stagnant blood from] the blood vessels. "Deplete what pathogens prevail in" means that whenever any meridian has abundance, its pathogen is drained. "Slow then quick makes excess" means inserting slowly and withdrawing quickly. "Quick then slow makes deficiency" means inserting quickly and withdrawing slowly. "Speaking of excess and deficiency, it is as if present, as if absent" means excess has qi and deficiency has none. "Examining after and before, as if perishing, as if surviving" refers to the deficiency and excess of the qi and the order of supplementing and draining, examining whether the qi has already descended or is still present. "Producing deficiency and excess, as if gaining, as if losing" means that supplementing feels full as if something were gained, and draining feels empty as if something were lost.
