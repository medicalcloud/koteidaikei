# 霊枢 小鍼解第三 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

"The subtlety of needling lies in quickness and slowness" means the idea of slow and quick. "The crude guard the joints" means they guard the four limbs without knowing the coming and going of blood, qi, proper and pathogenic. "The superior guard the mechanism" means knowing how to guard the qi. "The movement of the mechanism does not leave its hollow" means knowing the deficiency and excess of the qi and using slow or quick needling. "The mechanism within the hollow is clear, still and subtle" means that once the qi is obtained with the needle, one guards it closely with attention and does not lose it. "When it comes, it must not be met" means that when the qi is abundant one must not supplement. "When it goes, it must not be pursued" means that when the qi is deficient one must not drain. "Not missing by a hair" means the qi is easily lost. "Strikes and it does not go off" means not knowing the idea of supplementing and draining: the blood and qi are already exhausted, yet the qi does not descend. "Know its going and coming" means knowing the counterflow and compliance, abundance and deficiency of the qi. "The key is to time oneself with it" means knowing the moment when the qi can be taken. "How benighted are the crude" means they are in the dark and do not know the subtlety of the qi. "How wondrous that only the skilled possess it" means they fully know the meaning of the needle. "What goes is counterflow" means the qi is deficient and small; the small is counterflow. "What comes is compliance" means form and qi are balanced; the balanced is compliance. "Clearly knowing counterflow and compliance, act rightly without 間 [asking]" means knowing where to take. "Meeting it to seize it" is draining; "pursuing it to aid it" is supplementing.

## Notes

Explanation of the phrases in the latter part of chapter 1, unit 2.
- Here 速遅 of chapter 1 appears as 数遅, 叩之 as 扣之, 無問 as 無間, and 逆而奪之 as 迎而奪之.
- The base's 「扣之不発言者不知」 is emended to 「扣之不発者言不知」. The *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 扣之不発者，言不知…; every other phrase in the passage has the form "…者，言…也", so the base's 言者 is taken as a transposition.
