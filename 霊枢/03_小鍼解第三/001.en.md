# 霊枢 小鍼解第三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What is called "easy to state" means easy to say. "Hard to enter into" means hard to make clear to people. "The crude guard the form" means they keep to the rules of needling. "The superior guard the spirit" means they watch the surplus and insufficiency of a person's blood and qi and can supplement and drain. "Spirit and guest" means the proper and the pathogenic meet together: the spirit is the proper qi, the guest is the pathogenic qi. "At the gate" means the pathogen follows where the proper qi goes out and in. "Without seeing the illness" means first knowing in which meridian the illness of the pathogenic and proper is. "How can one know its origin" means first knowing which meridian is ill and where to take it.
