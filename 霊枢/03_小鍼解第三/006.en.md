# 霊枢 小鍼解第三 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

What is called "the qi of the five viscera is already cut off within" means that the qi at the pulse opening is cut off within and does not arrive, yet one instead takes the diseased places outside and the uniting points of the yang channels, retaining the needles to bring yang qi. When yang qi arrives, the inside is doubly exhausted; double exhaustion means death; and the death is quiet because there is no qi to move.
