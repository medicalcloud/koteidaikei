# 霊枢 脹論第三十五 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "The zang-fu organs inside the chest, flanks and abdomen are like precious vessels stored in boxes and chests; each has its order and place; with different names they share the same space, and within one region their qi differ. I wish to hear the reason."
