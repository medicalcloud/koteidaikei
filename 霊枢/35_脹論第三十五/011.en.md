# 霊枢 脹論第三十五 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

let me ask again." Qibo said, "The chest and abdomen are the outer walls of the viscera and bowels. Danzhong is the palace of the heart governor. The stomach is the great granary. The throat and small intestine are for transmission. The five openings of the stomach [the pharynx, cardia, pylorus, ileocecal gate and anus] are the gates and doors of the village. Lianquan and Yuying are the paths of the fluids. So each of the five viscera and six bowels has its boundaries, and each of their illnesses has its form. The nutritive qi follows the vessels; when the defensive qi goes counter, there is vessel distension. When the defensive qi runs alongside the vessels along the partings, there is skin distension. Drain at Sanli: once for a recent [illness], three times for a long-standing one. Regardless of depletion or repletion, the skill lies in draining quickly."

## Notes

- 胃之五竅 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: not five openings in the stomach itself, but the pharynx, cardia, pylorus, ileocecal gate and anus, since everything from the stomach duct to the intestines belongs to the stomach (Yang Shangshan lists the throat, stomach, large intestine, small intestine and bladder). 近者一下，遠者三下 follows the *Leijing*: draining once or three times, since the pathogen may be near or far.
- The base's 「廉泉玊英」 is emended to 「廉泉玉英」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 玉英, a point name (Yutang); 玉 is adopted.
