# 霊枢 脹論第三十五 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied, "This refers to [the needle] sinking into the *huang* of the flesh and hitting the qi point. If it does not hit the qi point, the qi is shut inside; if the needle does not sink into the *huang*, the qi does not move; if it overshoots and hits the flesh, the wei-defensive qi is disordered and yin and yang chase each other. In distension, when one should drain but does not, the qi therefore does not go down. If it does not go down after three times, one must change the way; stop when the qi goes down, and if it does not, begin again. Then complete success is possible; how could there be danger? In distension, one must carefully examine the pulse, drain when draining is called for and supplement when supplementing is called for, like a drum answering the drumstick; how could it not go down?"

## Notes

The end of the chapter.
- The base's 「陥于肉盲」 is emended to 「陥于肉肓」. The Siku edition, Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 肉肓 (the membrane below the skin); 肓 is adopted.
- The base's 「而中気宂」 is emended to 「而中気穴」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 気穴; 穴 is adopted.
- The base's 「不中気宂」 is emended to 「不中気穴」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 気穴; 穴 is adopted.
- The base's 「鍼不陥盲」 is emended to 「鍼不陥肓」. The Siku edition, Daizhige, the *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 肓; 肓 is adopted.
