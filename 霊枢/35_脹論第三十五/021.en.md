# 霊枢 脹論第三十五 §21 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo replied, "This refers to [the needle] sinking into the *huang* of the flesh and hitting the qi point. If it does not hit the qi point, the qi is shut inside; if the needle does not sink into the *huang*, the qi does not move; if it overshoots and hits the flesh, the defensive qi is disordered and yin and yang chase each other. In distension, when one should drain but does not, the qi therefore does not go down. If it does not go down after three times, one must change the way; stop when the qi goes down, and if it does not, begin again. Then complete success is possible; how could there be danger? In distension, one must carefully examine the pulse, drain when draining is called for and supplement when supplementing is called for, like a drum answering the drumstick; how could it not go down?"
