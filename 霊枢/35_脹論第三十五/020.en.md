# 霊枢 脹論第三十五 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "The *Discussion of Distension* says that regardless of depletion or repletion, the skill lies in draining quickly: once for a recent [illness], three times for a long-standing one. Now there are cases where even after three times it does not go down. Where is the fault?"

## Notes

間 in 無間 of the base is read as 問, as other sources have it (see collation).
