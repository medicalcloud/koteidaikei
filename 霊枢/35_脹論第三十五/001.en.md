# 霊枢 脹論第三十五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "How does the pulse at the inch opening respond when there is distension?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about the pulse in distension illness, where it lodges, the forms of distension of each zang organ and bowel, and how to needle them.
