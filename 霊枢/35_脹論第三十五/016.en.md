# 霊枢 脹論第三十五 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The defensive qi in the body always runs alongside the vessels and follows the partings of the flesh; its movement has counterflow and compliance; yin and yang follow each other, attaining the harmony of heaven; the five viscera begin in turn, the four seasons follow their order, and the five grains are transformed. Then, when reversal qi is below, the nutritive and defensive stagnate, cold qi counterflows upward, the true and pathogenic attack each other, and the two qi struggle, they combine to form distension."
