# 霊枢 脹論第三十五 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The wei-defensive qi in the body always runs alongside the vessels and follows the partings of the flesh; its movement has counterflow and compliance; yin and yang follow each other, attaining the harmony of heaven; the five zang organs begin in turn, the four seasons follow their order, and the five grains are transformed. Then, when reversal qi is below, the ying-nutrient and wei-defense stagnate, cold qi counterflows upward, the true and pathogenic attack each other, and the two qi struggle, they combine to form distension."
