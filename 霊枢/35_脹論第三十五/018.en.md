# 霊枢 脹論第三十五 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Match it with the true; combining the three, one obtains it."

## Notes

三合 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: distension arises from the defensive qi but may unite with the blood vessels, the viscera or the bowels; understanding these three unions, one obtains the truth. Yang Shangshan explains it as matching the true qi once for near and three times for far.
