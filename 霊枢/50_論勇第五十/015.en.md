# 霊枢 論勇第五十 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu said, "The timid man has large eyes that are not firm, his yin and yang have lost each other, the texture of his [triple] burner runs lengthwise, his xiphoid is short and small, his liver connection is slack, his gallbladder is not full but slack, his intestines and stomach are straight, and the space below his flanks is empty. Even when greatly angered, his qi cannot fill his chest; though his liver and lungs rise, the qi weakens and falls again, so he cannot stay angry long. This is what makes a timid man."
