# 霊枢 論勇第五十 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Shaoyu, "Here are people who walk together and stand together, equal in age and equally clothed. When they suddenly meet fierce wind and violent rain, some fall ill and some do not; sometimes all fall ill, sometimes none do. Why?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. The interlocutor is Shaoyu. It describes illness from the winds of the four seasons according to constitution, and the difference between the brave and the timid.
