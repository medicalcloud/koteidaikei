# 霊枢 論勇第五十 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu said, "Those with a yellow complexion, thin skin and weak flesh cannot withstand the deficiency wind of spring; those with a white complexion, thin skin and weak flesh cannot withstand the deficiency wind of summer; those with a green complexion, thin skin and weak flesh cannot withstand the deficiency wind of autumn; those with a red complexion, thin skin and weak flesh cannot withstand the deficiency wind of winter."
