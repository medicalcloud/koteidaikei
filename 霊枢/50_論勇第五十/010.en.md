# 霊枢 論勇第五十 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Whether people can endure pain or not is not a matter of bravery or timidity. A brave man who cannot endure pain advances when he sees difficulty but stops when he sees pain. A timid man who can endure pain is frightened when he hears of difficulty but is unmoved when he meets pain. A brave man who can endure pain is not frightened by difficulty and is unmoved by pain. A timid man who cannot endure pain, faced with difficulty and pain, rolls his eyes and turns away his face, is too frightened to speak, loses his breath in alarm, his complexion changes, and he seems now dead, now alive. I have seen that it is so, but do not know why. I wish to hear the reason."
