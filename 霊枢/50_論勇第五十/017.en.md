# 霊枢 論勇第五十 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu said, "Wine is the essence of water and grain, the liquid of ripened grain. Its qi is swift and fierce; when it enters the stomach, the stomach distends, the qi counterflows upward and fills the chest, the liver floats and the gallbladder becomes strong. At that time he is certainly comparable to a brave man, but when the qi wanes he regrets it. Being of the same kind as the brave and not knowing to avoid them is called wine-induced recklessness."
