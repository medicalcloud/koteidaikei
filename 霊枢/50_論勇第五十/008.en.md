# 霊枢 論勇第五十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu said, "Those with a black complexion, thick skin and firm flesh are certainly not harmed by the winds of the four seasons. Those whose skin is thin, flesh not firm and complexion uneven fall ill when late summer arrives with a deficiency wind. Those with thick skin and firm flesh do not fall ill when late summer arrives with a deficiency wind. Those with thick skin and firm flesh fall ill only when they are doubly affected by cold, both outside and inside."
