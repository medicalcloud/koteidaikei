# 霊枢 論勇第五十 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu said, "The brave man has deep-set, steady eyes, long eyebrows raised straight, the texture of his triple burner running crosswise, an upright heart, a large and firm liver, and a full and expansive gallbladder. When angry, his qi is abundant and his chest expands, his liver rises and his gallbladder is strong, his eyes open wide as if the canthi would split, his hair stands on end and his face turns green. This is what makes a brave man."

## Notes

For 衡 in 長衡 of the base, other sources have 衝 (see collation). As with 長衝直揚 in chapter 46, it is provisionally read as long eyebrows raised straight.
