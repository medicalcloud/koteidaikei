# 霊枢 論勇第五十 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoyu said, "The brave man has deep-set, steady eyes, a gaze that thrusts far and rises straight, the texture of his triple burner running crosswise, an upright heart, a large and firm liver, and a full and expansive gallbladder. When angry, his qi is abundant and his chest expands, his liver rises and his gallbladder is strong, his eyes open wide as if the canthi would split, his hair stands on end and his face turns green. This is what makes a brave man."

## Notes

For 衡 in 長衡, Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige and the *Lingshu jizhu* have 衝, while the *Leijing* and *Jiayi jing* agree with the base. The base is kept; the meaning follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* on 長衝直揚 in chapter 46 ("衝 means the light of the eyes thrusting out", noting that this chapter writes 衡): a gaze that thrusts far and rises straight.
