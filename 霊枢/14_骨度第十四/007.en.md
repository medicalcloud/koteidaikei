# 霊枢 骨度第十四 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Behind the ears, at the mastoid bones, the width is nine *cun*. In front of the ears, at the ear gates, the width is one *chi* three *cun*. The two cheekbones are seven *cun* apart. Between the two breasts the width is nine and a half *cun*. Between the two thighs the width is six and a half *cun*. The foot is one *chi* two *cun* long and four and a half *cun* wide.

## Notes

The bone measurements of widths.
