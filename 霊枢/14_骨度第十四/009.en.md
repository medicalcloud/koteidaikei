# 霊枢 骨度第十四 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

These are the bone measurements of ordinary people, by which the lengths of the meridians are established. Therefore, observing the meridians in the body: those that appear floating and firm, clear and large, have much blood; those that are thin and deep have much qi."

## Notes

Bogao's speech ends here, concluding the chapter.
