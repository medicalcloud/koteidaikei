# 霊枢 骨度第十四 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

From the corner [of the head] down to the pillar bone is one *chi* long. The part running through the armpit that cannot be seen is four *cun* long. From the armpit down to the free ribs is one *chi* two *cun*. From the free ribs down to the hip pivot is six *cun*. From the hip pivot down to the middle of the knee is one *chi* nine *cun*. From the knee down to the outer ankle is one *chi* six *cun*. From the outer ankle down to Jinggu is three *cun*. From Jinggu down to the ground is one *cun*.

## Notes

The bone measurements of the side of the body.
