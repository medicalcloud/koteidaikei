# 霊枢 骨度第十四 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

From the shoulder to the elbow is one *chi* seven *cun* long. From the elbow to the wrist is one *chi* two and a half *cun*. From the wrist to the base joint of the middle finger is four *cun*. From the base joint to the tip is four and a half *cun*. From the hairline of the nape down to the backbone is two and a half *cun*. From the paravertebral bone down to the coccyx, twenty-one segments, is three *chi* long; each upper segment is one *cun* four and one-tenth *fen*, the remainder being in the lower ones; hence the upper seven segments, reaching the paravertebral bone, are nine *cun* eight and seven-tenths *fen*.

## Notes

The bone measurements of the upper limbs and spine.
- The arithmetic of 分之一 and 分之七 and the location referred to by 膂骨 are unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points); translated provisionally.
