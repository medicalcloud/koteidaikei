# 霊枢 骨度第十四 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I wish to hear the measurements of ordinary people. For a person seven *chi* five *cun* tall, what are the size and length of each of the bones and joints?"

## Notes

問 in 願問 of the base is translated as 聞 "hear", as other sources have it (see collation).
