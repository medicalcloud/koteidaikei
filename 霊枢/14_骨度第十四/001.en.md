# 霊枢 骨度第十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Bogao, "*Measurements of the Vessels* speaks of the lengths of the channels. On what basis are they established?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about the measurements of the bones. 脈度 is the same as the title of chapter 17, which gives the lengths of the twenty-eight vessels (16 *zhang* 2 *chi* in total), so it is taken as referring to that chapter. The *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* do not comment on this, so it is a judgment based on the content.
