# 霊枢 骨度第十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Bogao, "*Measurements of the Vessels* speaks of the lengths of the channels. On what basis are they established?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about the measurements of the bones. 脈度 is the same as the title of chapter 17 and is translated as referring to it, but this is unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
