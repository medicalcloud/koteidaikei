# 霊枢 骨度第十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "The circumference of the large bone of the head is two *chi* six *cun*, of the chest four *chi* five *cun*, of the waist four *chi* two *cun*.

## Notes

Bogao's speech continues through unit 9.
- The base's 「腰囲四尺三寸」 is emended to 「腰囲四尺二寸」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu*, *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing* and *Lingshu jizhu* all read 四尺二寸; 二 is adopted.
