# 霊枢 骨度第十四 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "The circumference of the large bone of the head is two *chi* six *cun*, of the chest four *chi* five *cun*, of the waist four *chi* three *cun*.

## Notes

Bogao's speech continues through unit 9.
- For 三 in 腰囲四尺三寸, some sources have 二 (see collation).
