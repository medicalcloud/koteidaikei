# 霊枢 病本第二十五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When illness comes first and counterflow later, treat the root. When counterflow comes first and illness later, treat the root. When cold comes first and illness arises later, treat the root. When illness comes first and cold arises later, treat the root. When heat comes first and illness arises later, treat the root. When diarrhea comes first and other illnesses arise later, treat the root; one must first regulate it and then treat the other illnesses. When illness comes first and fullness in the center later, treat the branch. When illness comes first and diarrhea later, treat the root. When fullness in the center comes first and vexation of the heart later, treat the root. There is guest qi and there is shared qi. When urination and defecation are obstructed, treat the branch; when they are free, treat the root. When an illness breaks out with surplus, go from root to branch: first treat the root, then the branch. When an illness breaks out with insufficiency, go from branch to root: first treat the branch, then the root. Carefully examine whether it is mild or severe and regulate it with judgment: in mild cases treat both together; in severe cases treat one alone. When urination and defecation become obstructed first and other illnesses arise later, treat the root.

## Notes

The chapter consists of a single unit, describing without a dialogue the order of treating the branch and root of an illness. It is nearly identical to *Suwen*, *Biaoben bingchuan lun*.
- 有客気，有同気 does not connect with its context; its meaning is unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
