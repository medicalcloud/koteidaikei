# 霊枢 雑病第二十六 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For a full and enlarged lower abdomen that runs up to the stomach and reaches the heart, with shivering and occasional cold and heat in the body and obstructed urination, take the foot *jueyin*. For abdominal fullness with obstructed defecation, an enlarged abdomen that also runs up to the chest and throat, and wheezing breath, take the foot lesser yin. For abdominal fullness with undigested food, a rumbling abdomen and inability to defecate, take the foot greater yin. For heart pain pulling into the lower back and spine with a desire to vomit, take the foot lesser yin. For heart pain with a tight, distended abdomen and obstructed defecation, take the foot greater yin.
