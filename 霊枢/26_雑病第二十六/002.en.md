# 霊枢 雑病第二十六 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For a dry throat and heat in the mouth like glue, take the foot lesser yin. For pain in the knee, take Dubi with the round-sharp needle, needling and needling again [if once is not enough]; the needle is as thick as a yak hair; needle the knee without hesitation. For throat impediment with inability to speak, take the foot *Yangming*; if one can speak, take the hand *Yangming*. For malaria without thirst, occurring every other day, take the foot *Yangming*; with thirst, occurring daily, take the hand *Yangming*. For toothache without aversion to cold drinks, take the foot *Yangming*; with aversion to cold drinks, take the hand *Yangming*. For deafness without pain, take the foot lesser yang; for deafness with pain, take the hand *Yangming*. For nosebleed that does not stop, with dark clotted blood flowing, take the foot greater yang; for clotted blood [alone], take the hand greater yang; if it does not stop, needle below Wangu; if it still does not stop, needle the back of the knee and let blood. For lower back pain, if it is cold above the painful area, take the foot greater yang and *Yangming*; if hot above the painful area, take the foot *Jueyin*; if one cannot bend forward or back, take the foot lesser yang. For heat in the center with dyspnea, take the foot lesser yin and the blood collaterals in the back of the knee. For frequent anger without desire to eat and ever quieter speech, needle the foot greater yin; for anger with much talking, needle the foot lesser yang. For cheek pain, needle the hand *Yangming* and the abundant vessels of the cheek and let blood. For pain of the nape with inability to bend forward or back, needle the foot greater yang; with inability to turn the head, needle the hand greater yang.

## Notes

宛骨 is taken as Wangu.
- 発而間之 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "needle and needle again, since once will not suffice".
