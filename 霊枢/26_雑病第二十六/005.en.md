# 霊枢 雑病第二十六 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For cheek pain, needle the pulsing vessel at Quzhou on the foot *yangming*; when blood appears, it stops at once. If it does not stop, press Renying along the channel, and it stops at once. For rising counterflow of qi, needle the depression in the chest and the pulsing vessel below the chest. For abdominal pain, needle the pulsing vessels left and right of the navel and press after needling; it stops at once. If it does not stop, needle Qijie and press after needling; it stops at once. For wilting with reversal, bind the four extremities until there is oppression, then quickly release them; do this twice a day; in those with numbness, sensation returns in ten days; continue without rest and stop when the illness has ended. For hiccup, prick the nose with a grass stalk to cause sneezing, and the sneezing stops it; or hold the breath and quickly meet and draw it [the qi] in, and it stops at once; giving a great fright can also stop it.
