# 霊枢 雑病第二十六 §5 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For cheek pain, needle the pulsing vessel at Quzhou on the foot *Yangming*; when blood appears, it stops at once. If it does not stop, press Renying along the meridian, and it stops at once. For rising counterflow of qi, needle the depression in the chest and the pulsing vessel below the chest. For abdominal pain, needle the pulsing vessels left and right of the navel and press after needling; it stops at once. If it does not stop, needle Qijie and press after needling; it stops at once. For wilting with reversal, bind the four extremities until there is oppression, then quickly release them; do this twice a day; in those with numbness, sensation returns in ten days; continue without rest and stop when the illness has ended. For hiccup, prick the nose with a grass stalk to cause sneezing, and the sneezing stops it; or stop the breath through mouth and nose and quickly meet the qi and draw it away, and it stops at once; giving a great fright can also stop it.

## Notes

The end of the chapter.
- 曲周 is placed by Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* below the brain, in front of the temple hair and above Kezhuren; Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* says the pulsing vessel at Quzhou belongs to the foot *Yangming*. 無息而疾迎引之 follows the *Leijing*: close the breath of mouth and nose, then meet the qi and draw it away so that it does not rise.
- The base's 「病已止歳以草」 is emended to 「病已止噦以草」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), the *Taisu* and *Leijing* read 噦 (hiccup) (the Siku quanshu edition on Wikisource, the Daizhige *Huangdi neijing lingshu* and the *Lingshu jizhu* have 歳 as in the base); 噦 is adopted.
