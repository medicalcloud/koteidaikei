# 霊枢 雑病第二十六 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In reversal with pain flanking the spine up to the vertex, a heavy, sinking head, blurred vision and stiffness of the lower back and spine, take the blood collaterals in the back of the knee on the foot greater yang. In reversal with fullness of the chest, swelling of the face, soaked lips, sudden difficulty in speaking, and in severe cases inability to speak, take the foot *Yangming*. When reversal qi runs to the throat so that one cannot speak, with cold hands and feet and obstructed defecation, take the foot lesser yin. In reversal with a rumbling abdomen, much cold qi, gurgling in the abdomen and difficult urination and defecation, take the foot greater yin.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, which lists, without a dialogue, various illnesses and the meridians to take, item by item.
