# 霊枢 雑病第二十六 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For heart pain pulling into the back with inability to breathe, needle the foot lesser yin; if it does not stop, take the hand lesser yang. For heart pain pulling into the lower abdomen with fullness, moving up and down with no fixed location, and difficult urination and defecation, needle the foot *jueyin*. For heart pain with only shortness of qi insufficient for breathing, needle the hand greater yin. For heart pain, needle at the level of the ninth vertebra and press; pressing after needling stops it at once; if it does not stop, search above and below, and when found it stops at once.
