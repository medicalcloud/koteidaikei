# 霊枢 厥病第二十四 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In reversal heart pain, when [the pain] pulls with the back, there are frequent spasms as if the heart were being prodded from behind, and one is bent over, it is kidney heart pain. First take Jinggu and Kunlun; if it does not end after needling, take Rangu. In reversal heart pain, when the abdomen is distended, the chest full and the heart especially painful, it is stomach heart pain; take it at Dadu and Taibai. In reversal heart pain, when the pain is like an awl or needle piercing the heart and the heart pain is severe, it is spleen heart pain; take it at Rangu and Taixi. In reversal heart pain, when the complexion is ashen as if dead and one cannot take a deep breath all day, it is liver heart pain; take it at Xingjian and Taichong. In reversal heart pain, when lying down or staying still eases the heart pain, movement makes it worse, and the complexion does not change, it is lung heart pain; take it at Yuji and Taiyuan. In true heart pain, the hands and feet are cold up to the joints and the heart pain is severe; if it starts in the morning there is death in the evening, if it starts in the evening there is death in the morning. Heart pains that cannot be needled are those with abundant accumulations inside; they cannot be taken at the transport points. When there are worm conglomerations and roundworms in the intestines, they cannot be taken with small needles. When the heart and intestines ache, with oppressive pain, swollen lumps coming and going up and down, pain that comes and goes, heat in the abdomen, frequent thirst and drooling, it is roundworms. Gather and press them firmly with the hand so that they cannot move, needle them with the large needle and hold it long; when the worms no longer move, withdraw the needle. A full abdomen with oppressive pain, whose form rises from the middle upward, [is also treated as worms].

## Notes

Types of reversal heart pain and their treatment, and heart and abdominal pain due to worms.
- The final 㤣腹憹痛，形中上者 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: "㤣 means full; this restates the symptoms: when the form gradually rises from the middle upward, treat it as worms."
- The base's 「発狂不已」 is emended to 「発鍼不已」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), the *Taisu*, *Leijing* and *Lingshu jizhu* read 発鍼不已 (the Siku edition has 針): "if needling does not stop it"; 鍼 is adopted.
