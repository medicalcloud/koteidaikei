# 霊枢 厥病第二十四 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

For deafness with no hearing, take inside the ear. For tinnitus, take the pulsing vessel in front of the ear. Ear pains that cannot be needled are those with pus inside the ear or dry earwax causing loss of hearing. For deafness, take the ring finger where the nail meets the flesh, first the hand and then the foot. For tinnitus, take above the nail of the middle finger, the right for the left and the left for the right, first the hand and then the foot. When the thigh cannot be raised, take it with [the patient] lying on the side, in the middle of the pivot joint, using the round-sharp needle; the large needle must not be used. For illness with pouring down of blood, take Ququan. When wind impediment with limp weakness cannot be cured, the feet feel as if treading on ice, at times as if in hot water, the thighs and shins are limp and weak, the heart is vexed and the head aches, there is vomiting at times and oppression at times, sweating after dizziness, and in time blurred vision, sorrow and frequent fear, shortness of qi and unhappiness; death comes within three years.

## Notes

Ear illnesses, illness of the thigh, and wind impediment with limp weakness; the end of the chapter.
- 淫濼 is provisionally translated as "limp weakness". Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
