# 霊枢 厥病第二十四 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In reversal headache, when the face seems swollen and the heart is vexed, take it at the foot *yangming* and greater yin. In reversal headache, when the vessels of the head ache, the heart is sorrowful and there is frequent weeping, look for the pulsing vessels of the head that are abnormally abundant, needle them and remove all the blood, and afterwards regulate the foot *jueyin*. In reversal headache, when the head is heavy and throbbing with pain, drain the five lines on top of the head, five [points] per line; first take the hand lesser yin, then the foot lesser yin. In reversal headache, when the mind is forgetful and [the pain] cannot be found by pressing, take the pulsing vessels on left and right of the head and face, then the foot greater yin. In reversal headache, when the nape aches first and the lower back and spine respond, first take Tianzhu, then the foot greater yang. In reversal headache, when the headache is severe and the vessels in front of and behind the ears surge with heat, drain them to let out the blood, then take the foot lesser yang. In true headache, the headache is severe, the whole brain aches, and the hands and feet are cold up to the joints; there is death without cure. Headaches that cannot be taken at the transport points are those from a blow or fall with malign blood inside, or from injured flesh whose pain has not yet ended; these may be needled [locally] but not taken at a distance. Headaches that cannot be needled are those caused by great impediment; when they occur daily, they can be eased somewhat but not ended. For cold pain in half the head, first take the hand lesser yang and *yangming*, then the foot lesser yang and *yangming*.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, which describes, without a dialogue, reversal headache, reversal heart pain, ear illnesses and others, and their treatment. The interlinear note is not translated (original text only).
- 貞貞 is provisionally translated as "throbbing". Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
