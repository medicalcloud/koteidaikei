# 霊枢 終始第九 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In the way of all needling, stop when the qi is regulated. Supplementing yin and draining yang makes the voice and qi ever clearer and the ears and eyes sharp and bright; going against this, the blood and qi do not move. What is called "the qi arrives and there is an effect" means: draining makes it more deficient; deficiency means the pulse is as large as before but no longer firm; if it is as firm as before, then although [the patient] says all is back to normal, the illness has not yet gone. Supplementing makes it more excess; excess means the pulse is as large as before but firmer; if it is as before and not firm, then although [the patient] says it feels better, the illness has not yet gone. Hence supplementing produces excess and draining deficiency; although the pain does not [immediately] follow the needle, the illness will surely decline and go. One must first master the illnesses produced by the twelve meridians, and only then can *End and Beginning* be transmitted. Hence when yin and yang do not shift, and deficiency and excess do not tilt, take it at its meridian. In all needling, three needlings bring the grain qi. When deviant pathogens wrongly combine, yin and yang exchange places, counterflow and compliance are reversed, sinking and floating are in the wrong places, the four seasons are not attained, and [the qi] stagnates and overflows, it waits for the needle to be removed. Hence the first needling lets out the yang pathogen, the second lets out the yin pathogen, the third brings the grain qi; when the grain qi arrives, stop. What is called the arrival of the grain qi means that after supplementing there is excess and after draining deficiency; by this one knows the grain qi has arrived. When the pathogenic qi alone has gone, although yin and yang are not yet regulated, one knows the illness will be cured. Hence it is said: supplementing produces excess and draining deficiency; although the pain does not follow the needle, the illness will surely decline and go.
