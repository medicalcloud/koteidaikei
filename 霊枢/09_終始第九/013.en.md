# 霊枢 終始第九 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Painful illness is yin; pain that cannot be located when pressed with the hand is yin; needle it deep. Illness above is yang; illness below is yin. Itching is yang; needle it shallow. When the illness first arises in the yin, first treat the yin and then the yang; when the illness first arises in the yang, first treat the yang and then the yin.
