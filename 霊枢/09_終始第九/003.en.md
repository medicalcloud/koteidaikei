# 霊枢 終始第九 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When Renying is once as abundant [as the pulse opening], the illness is in the foot lesser yang; once as abundant and agitated, in the hand lesser yang. When Renying is twice as abundant, the illness is in the foot greater yang; twice as abundant and agitated, in the hand greater yang. When Renying is three times as abundant, the illness is in the foot *yangming*; three times as abundant and agitated, in the hand *yangming*. When Renying is four times as abundant, both large and rapid, it is called overflowing yang; overflowing yang is outer rejection.

## Notes

The degree of abundance at Renying and the affected yang channels.
