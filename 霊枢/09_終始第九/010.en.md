# 霊枢 終始第九 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When yin is abundant and yang depleted, first supplement the yang, then drain the yin to harmonize them. When yin is depleted and yang abundant, first supplement the yin, then drain the yang to harmonize them. Three vessels pulse between the big toes; one must carefully examine their repletion and depletion. Draining what is depleted is called double depletion; with double depletion the illness becomes worse. In needling these, press with the finger: where the pulse beats replete and quick, drain it quickly; where depleted and slow, supplement it. Going against this makes the illness worse. As for their beats, the *yangming* is on top, the *jueyin* in the middle, and the lesser yin below. The transport points of the chest are hit in the chest, the transport points of the back in the back; when the shoulders and upper arms are depleted, take it above. For double tongue, needle the tongue pillar with the sword needle. When the hand bends but cannot straighten, the illness is in the sinews; when it straightens but cannot bend, the illness is in the bones. If in the bones, guard the bones; if in the sinews, guard the sinews. In 補須, where one side is replete, take it deep, press the needle hole rarely, and let out all the pathogenic qi; where one side is depleted, needle shallow to nourish the vessel and quickly press the needle hole so that no pathogenic qi can enter. The pathogenic qi comes tight and quick; the grain qi comes slow and gentle. When the pulse is replete, needle deep to discharge the qi; when the pulse is depleted, needle shallow so that the essential qi cannot escape, nourishing the vessel and letting out only the pathogenic qi.

## Notes

The order of supplementing and draining according to the depletion and repletion of yin and yang, and needling by location and symptom.
- The reading of 補須 in 補須一方実 is unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
- For the second 邪気来也 of the base, other sources have 穀 instead of 邪 (see collation); from the meaning it is translated accordingly.
