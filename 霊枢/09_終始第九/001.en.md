# 霊枢 終始第九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The way of all needling is complete in *End and Beginning*. If one clearly knows the end and beginning, with the five zang organs as the guideline, yin and yang are determined. Yin governs the zang organs, yang governs the bowels; yang receives qi from the four extremities, yin receives qi from the five zang organs. Hence draining meets it and supplementing follows it; knowing how to meet and how to follow, one can make the qi harmonious. The method of harmonizing the qi requires mastery of yin and yang. The five zang organs are yin, the six fu organs yang. In transmitting this to later generations, make a covenant with blood: those who respect it will prosper, those who neglect it will perish; acting selfishly against the way will surely bring calamity from heaven. Respectfully upholding the way of heaven, let me speak of the end and beginning.

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, explaining "end and beginning" (diagnosis of yin and yang by the pulse opening and Renying, and needling methods) without a dialogue.
