# 霊枢 終始第九 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In spring the qi is in the hair, in summer in the skin, in autumn in the partings of the flesh, in winter in the sinews and bones. In needling these illnesses, take each season as the standard. Hence needle fat people by the standard of autumn and winter, and thin people by the standard of spring and summer.
