# 霊枢 終始第九 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse opening is once as abundant [as Renying], the illness is in the foot *jueyin*; when *jueyin* is once as abundant and agitated, it is in the hand heart governor. When the pulse opening is twice as abundant, the illness is in the foot lesser yin; twice as abundant and agitated, in the hand lesser yin. When the pulse opening is three times as abundant, the illness is in the foot greater yin; three times as abundant and agitated, in the hand greater yin. When the pulse opening is four times as abundant, both large and rapid, it is called overflowing yin; overflowing yin is inner block; inner block without passage means death without cure.

## Notes

The degree of abundance at the pulse opening and the affected yin channels.
