# 霊枢 終始第九 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The prohibitions of needling: one who has just had intercourse must not be needled, and one just needled must not have intercourse; one who is drunk must not be needled, and one needled must not get drunk; one who has just been angry must not be needled, and one needled must not get angry; one who is just fatigued must not be needled, and one needled must not get fatigued; one who has eaten to the full must not be needled, and one needled must not eat to the full; one who is hungry must not be needled, and one needled must not go hungry; one who is thirsty must not be needled, and one needled must not go thirsty. For great fright and great fear, one must settle the qi before needling. One who has come by carriage should lie down and rest for about the time of a meal before being needled; one who has come on foot should sit and rest for about the time of walking ten *li* before being needled. In all these twelve prohibitions, the pulse is disordered, the qi scattered, the ying-nutrient and wei-defense go counter, and the meridian qi is out of order; if one needles then, yang illness enters the yin, yin illness comes out as yang, and the pathogenic qi arises again. The crude practitioner does not examine this; this is called assaulting the body. The form and body become dissipated, the brain and marrow waste away, the fluids are not transformed, and the five flavors are lost; this is called losing the qi.

## Notes

The twelve prohibitions on needling. The full-width spaces in the base are replaced with punctuation.
