# 霊枢 終始第九 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

*End and Beginning* takes the channels and vessels as its guideline: holding the pulse opening and Renying, one knows the surplus and insufficiency of yin and yang, whether they are balanced or not; and the way of heaven is complete. The so-called healthy person does not fall ill. Not falling ill means the pulse opening and Renying correspond to the four seasons, upper and lower correspond to each other and come and go together, the pulses of the six channels are not knotted or agitated, the cold and warmth of root and branch guard each other, and the form, flesh, blood and qi are in balance; this is called a healthy person. In one with scant qi, both the pulse opening and Renying are weak and do not match the cubit and inch. Such a person has both yin and yang insufficient: supplementing yang exhausts yin, draining yin makes yang collapse. Such a person may be nourished with sweet medicines but must not be given drastic preparations to drink; such a person is not given moxibustion. If one drains because [the illness] does not end, the qi of the five viscera will be ruined.

## Notes

The pulse of the healthy person and the treatment of those with both yin and yang insufficient.
- 不已者因而瀉之 is read as "if one drains because [the illness] does not end".
- The base's 「此者弗炙」 is emended to 「此者弗灸」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*), Daizhige, the *Taisu* and *Jiayi jing* read 灸 (moxibustion); 灸 is adopted.
