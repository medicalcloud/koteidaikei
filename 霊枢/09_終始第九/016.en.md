# 霊枢 終始第九 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In all methods of needling, one must examine the form and qi. When the flesh of the form has not yet wasted, but the qi is scant and the pulse agitated, with agitated reversal, one must use cross needling; then scattered qi can be gathered and gathered qi can be spread. [The needler] stays deep in a quiet place, divines the comings and goings of the spirit, closes the door and blocks the window so that the ethereal and corporeal souls do not scatter, concentrates the intention and unifies the spirit, separates the essential qi, hears no human voice, gathers the essence, unifies the spirit and fixes the will on the needle. Keep it shallow and retain it, keep it light and floating, to move the spirit; rest when the qi arrives. Men avoid the inside [intercourse], women the outside; firmly resist and do not let it out, carefully guard and do not let it in; this is called obtaining the qi.

## Notes

The mental attitude of the needler and obtaining the qi.
- 精気之分 follows the *Lingshu jizhu* (the kidneys store essence and open into the ears; the essence is divided by being distracted by hearing, hence one hears no human voice). 男内女外 follows Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* ("after needling, men avoid the inside [intercourse], women the outside"). Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu* takes them as inner and outer qi, and the *Lingshu jizhu* as drawing yang inward and yin outward to harmonize them.
