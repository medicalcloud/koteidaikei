# 霊枢 終始第九 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In needling heat reversal, retain the needle to turn it into cold; in needling cold reversal, retain the needle to turn it into heat. Needling heat reversal, use two yin and one yang; needling cold reversal, two yang and one yin. What is called two yin means needling the yin twice; one yang means needling the yang once.
