# 霊枢 終始第九 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The reason for taking it twice a day is that the *Yangming* governs the stomach and is greatly rich in grain qi, so it may be taken twice a day. When Renying and the pulse opening are both more than three times as abundant, it is called overflowing of both yin and yang. In such a case, if [the block] is not opened, the blood vessels are blocked, the qi has nowhere to go, it floods the center, and the five zang organs are harmed inside. If moxibustion is applied in such a case, it changes into another illness.

## Notes

Why it is taken twice a day, and the overflowing of both yin and yang.
- The base's 「太陽主胃」 is emended to 「陽明主胃」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*) and the *Lingshu jizhu* read 陽明主胃; the *Taisu* reads 太陰主胃 (its editor notes that the Lingshu has 陽明). The *Yangming* governs the stomach and is what is taken twice a day in the preceding units, so 陽明 is adopted.
