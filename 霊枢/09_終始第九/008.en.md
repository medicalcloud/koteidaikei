# 霊枢 終始第九 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The reason for taking it twice a day is that the greater yang [*yangming*] governs the stomach and is greatly rich in grain qi, so it may be taken twice a day. When Renying and the pulse opening are both more than three times as abundant, it is called overflowing of both yin and yang. In such a case, if [the block] is not opened, the blood vessels are blocked, the qi has nowhere to go, it floods the center, and the five viscera are harmed inside. If moxibustion is applied in such a case, it changes into another illness.

## Notes

Why it is taken twice a day, and the overflowing of both yin and yang.
- 太陽主胃 of the base does not fit the context (in units 6–7 the *yangming* and greater yin are taken twice a day), and one source has an added 明 (see collation). It is provisionally translated as *yangming*. Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
