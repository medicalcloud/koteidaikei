# 霊枢 終始第九 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the greater yang vessel comes to its end, the eyes turn up, the body arches back, there is tugging and slackening, the complexion is white, the skin is cut off and there is expiry sweating; with expiry sweating, it is the end. When the lesser yang comes to its end, there is deafness, all the joints slacken, and the eye connection is cut off; a day and a half after the eye connection is cut off, there is death; at death the complexion turns green-white. When the *Yangming* comes to its end, the mouth and eyes twitch, there is a tendency to fright, wild speech, a yellow complexion, and when its upper and lower meridians are abundant but do not move, it is the end. When the lesser yin comes to its end, the face is black, the teeth lengthen and are coated, the abdomen is distended and blocked, above and below do not communicate, and it is the end. When the *Jueyin* comes to its end, there is heat in the center, a dry throat, frequent urination, vexation of the heart, and in severe cases the tongue curls and the testicles retract upward, and it is the end. When the greater yin comes to its end, the abdomen is distended and blocked, one cannot breathe, there is belching and frequent vomiting; vomiting brings counterflow, counterflow makes the face red; without counterflow, above and below do not communicate; when above and below do not communicate, the face is black and the skin and hair are scorched, and it is the end.

## Notes

The signs when the qi of the six meridians comes to an end; the end of the chapter. It closely resembles the description of the end of the six meridians in *Suwen*, *Zhenyao jingzhong lun*.
