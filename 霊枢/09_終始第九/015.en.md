# 霊枢 終始第九 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

In long-standing illness, the pathogenic qi has entered deep. In needling such illness, insert deep and retain long, and needle again every other day. Always first regulate left and right and remove the blood vessels; then the way of needling is complete.
