# 霊枢 終始第九 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When needling pains, the pulses are all excess. Hence it is said: above the waist, hand greater yin and *Yangming* govern; below the waist, foot greater yin and *Yangming* govern. When the illness is above, take it below; when the illness is below, take it high; when the illness is in the head, take it at the feet; when the illness is in the lower back, take it at the back of the knee. When the illness arises in the head, the head is heavy; in the hands, the arms are heavy; in the feet, the feet are heavy. In treating illness, first needle where the illness arose.

## Notes

- The base's 「病在足者取之膕」 is emended to 「病在腰者取之膕」. All other sources (Wikisource, Siku, Daizhige, *Taisu*, *Leijing*, *Jiayi jing*, *Lingshu jizhu*) read 病在腰者取之膕; 腰 is adopted.
