# 霊枢 終始第九 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When Renying is once as abundant, drain the foot lesser yang and supplement the foot *Jueyin*, two drainings to one supplementing, taking it once a day; always feel and verify [the pulse]; if agitated, take it above; stop when the qi is harmonized. When Renying is twice as abundant, drain the foot greater yang and supplement the foot lesser yin, two drainings to one supplementing, taking it once every two days; always feel and verify; if agitated, take it above; stop when the qi is harmonized. When Renying is three times as abundant, drain the foot *Yangming* and supplement the foot greater yin, two drainings to one supplementing, taking it twice a day; always feel and verify; if agitated, take it above; stop when the qi is harmonized.

## Notes

Needling when Renying is abundant.
- 疏 in 疏取之上 is read as 躁 "agitated", following the *Lingshu jizhu* ("疏 should be 躁, referring to 'once as abundant and agitated'").
