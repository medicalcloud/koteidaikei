# 霊枢 終始第九 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

When the pulse opening is once as abundant, drain the foot *Jueyin* and supplement the foot lesser yang, two supplementings to one draining, taking it once a day; always feel and verify; if agitated, take it above; stop when the qi is harmonized. When the pulse opening is twice as abundant, drain the foot lesser yin and supplement the foot greater yang, two supplementings to one draining, taking it once every two days; always feel and verify; if agitated, take it above; stop when the qi is harmonized. When the pulse opening is three times as abundant, drain the foot greater yin and supplement the foot *Yangming*, two supplementings to one draining, taking it twice a day; always feel and verify; if agitated, take it above; stop when the qi is harmonized.

## Notes

Needling when the pulse opening is abundant. 疏 is read as in unit 6.
