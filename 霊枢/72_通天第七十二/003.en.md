# 霊枢 通天第七十二 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I wish to hear the outline. Are there worthies and sages whose minds can embody [both] and act on them?"

## Notes

Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: 心能備而行之乎 asks whether, since the minds of worthies and sages differ from those of ordinary people, there are some who embody both yin and yang.
