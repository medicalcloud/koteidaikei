# 霊枢 通天第七十二 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoshi answered, "The multitude is unlike the five dispositions. Hence five times five makes twenty-five persons, and the five dispositions are not included among them. The five dispositions are especially unlike the multitude."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: the multitude means the twenty-five persons of the *Yinyang ershiwu ren* chapter (ch. 64), which differ from the five dispositions, hence "unlike the multitude". The *Leijing* and the Daizhige text read 不知 for 不如, but the *Leijing*'s explanation is closer to 不如 ("not like"), so the base is translated.
