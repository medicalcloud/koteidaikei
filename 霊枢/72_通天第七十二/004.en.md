# 霊枢 通天第七十二 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoshi said, "There are, it seems, greater-yin persons, lesser-yin persons, greater-yang persons, lesser-yang persons, and persons of balanced yin and yang. These five differ in disposition, and their sinews, bones, qi and blood are not equal."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: greater yin, lesser yin, greater yang and lesser yang here differ from the three yin and three yang of the channels. Those whose endowment is pure yin are called greater yin; more yin than yang, lesser yin; pure yang, greater yang; more yang than yin, lesser yang; together with the balanced persons, they make five dispositions.
