# 霊枢 通天第七十二 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Shaoshi, "I once heard that people have yin and yang. What is meant by a yin person, and what by a yang person?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. The interlocutor is Shaoshi. It describes the temperament, treatment and outward appearance of the five dispositions (greater yin, lesser yin, greater yang, lesser yang, and balanced yin–yang) determined by the innate proportions of yin and yang.
