# 霊枢 通天第七十二 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoshi said, "Within heaven and earth and the six directions, nothing departs from five, and the human being corresponds to this; it is not merely one yin and one yang. I can only speak in outline; words cannot make it all clear."
