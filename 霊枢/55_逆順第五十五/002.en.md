# 霊枢 逆順第五十五 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "The counterflow and compliance of qi correspond to heaven and earth, yin and yang, the four seasons and the five phases. The abundance and decline of the pulse are how one examines the depletion and repletion, surplus and insufficiency of blood and qi. The great rule of needling is to know clearly which illnesses may be needled, which may not yet be needled, and which may no longer be needled."
