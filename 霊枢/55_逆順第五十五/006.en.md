# 霊枢 逆順第五十五 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "The superior practitioner needles before [the illness] arises; the next needles before it is abundant; the next needles when it has already declined. The inferior practitioner needles when it is just attacking, when the form is abundant, or when the illness and the pulse contradict each other. Hence it is said: when it is at its height, do not dare to damage it; needle it when it has declined, and the undertaking will surely prosper greatly. Hence it is said: the superior practitioner treats what is not yet ill, not what is already ill. This is what it means."

## Notes

Bogao's speech ends here, concluding the chapter.
