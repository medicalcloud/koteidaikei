# 霊枢 逆順第五十五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Bogao, "I have heard that qi has counterflow and compliance, the pulse has abundance and decline, and needling has its great rules. May I hear them?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, describing when needling is and is not permissible.
