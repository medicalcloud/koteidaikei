# 霊枢 逆順第五十五 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao said, "The *Art of War* says, 'Do not meet a qi that surges on; do not attack an imposing formation.' The *Needling Method* says, 'Do not needle a blazing fever; do not needle dripping sweat; do not needle a turbid, confused pulse; do not needle when the illness and the pulse contradict each other.'

## Notes

Bogao's speech continues through unit 6. 兵法 and 刺法 are taken as quotations from old books.
