# 霊枢 寿夭剛柔第六 §22 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao answered, "When the nutritive produces illness, there are cold and heat, shortness of qi, and blood moving up and down. When the defensive produces illness, there is qi pain that comes and goes, with oppression, distension and rumbling, and wind and cold lodge in the intestines and stomach. The illness of cold impediment stays without leaving, with occasional pain and numbness of the skin."
