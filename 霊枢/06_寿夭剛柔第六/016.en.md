# 霊枢 寿夭剛柔第六 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao answered, "In a healthy person, when the qi prevails over the form, there is longevity. In illness with wasting of the flesh, when the qi prevails over the form, there is death; when the form prevails over the qi, there is danger."
