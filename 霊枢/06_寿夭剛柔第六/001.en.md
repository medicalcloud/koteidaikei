# 霊枢 寿夭剛柔第六 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Shaoshi, "I have heard that among people's constitutions there are the hard and the soft, the weak and the strong, the short and the tall, the yin and the yang. I wish to hear the method [of needling them]."

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. The interlocutor is Shaoshi; from unit 3 it is Bogao.
