# 霊枢 寿夭剛柔第六 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao answered, "An illness of nine days ends after three needlings; an illness of one month ends after ten needlings. For more or fewer, longer or shorter, apportion them accordingly. When a long-standing impediment does not leave the body, look at the blood collaterals and let out all their blood."
