# 霊枢 寿夭剛柔第六 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao answered, "When form and qi match each other, there is longevity; when they do not, early death. When skin and flesh wrap each other, there is longevity; when they do not, early death. When the blood, qi, channels and network vessels prevail over the form, there is longevity; when they do not, early death."
