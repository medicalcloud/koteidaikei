# 霊枢 寿夭剛柔第六 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao answered, "When the form is full and the skin slack, there is longevity; when the form is full and the skin tight, early death. When the form is full and the pulse firm and large, it is compliance; when the form is full and the pulse small and weak, the qi is declining, and decline is dangerous. If the form is full but the cheekbones do not stand out, the bones are small, and small bones mean early death. When the form is full and the large muscles are firm with clear divisions, the flesh is firm, and firm flesh means longevity; when the form is full but the large muscles have no divisions and are not firm, the flesh is fragile, and fragile flesh means early death. This is the life given by heaven, the means by which one establishes the form, determines the qi and sees longevity or early death. One must be clear about establishing the form and determining the qi, and only then approach the patient and decide life or death."
