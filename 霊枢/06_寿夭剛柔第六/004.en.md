# 霊枢 寿夭剛柔第六 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao answered, "Wind and cold harm the form; worry, fear, resentment and anger harm the qi. When the qi harms the viscera, the viscera fall ill; when cold harms the form, the form responds; when wind harms the sinews and vessels, the sinews and vessels respond. This is the mutual correspondence of form and qi, outside and inside."
