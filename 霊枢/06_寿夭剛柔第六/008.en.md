# 霊枢 寿夭剛柔第六 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao answered, "When the form falls ill first and [the illness] has not yet entered the viscera, needle for half the number of days; when the viscera fall ill first and the form responds afterwards, needle for twice the number of days. This is the correspondence of difficulty and ease for outside and inside."

## Notes

- The base's 「此月内難易」 is emended to 「此外内難易」. Wikisource (*Huangdi neijing*) and the *Jiayi jing* read 此外内難易之応也, answering the question about outer and inner illnesses; 外 is adopted.
