# 霊枢 寿夭剛柔第六 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Bogao, "I have heard that the form may be slack or tight, the qi abundant or declining, the bones large or small, the flesh firm or fragile, the skin thick or thin. How does one determine longevity or early death by them?"
