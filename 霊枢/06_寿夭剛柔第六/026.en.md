# 霊枢 寿夭剛柔第六 §26 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao answered, "Use twenty *sheng* of pure wine, one *sheng* of Sichuan pepper, one *jin* of dried ginger and one *jin* of cinnamon heart, four ingredients in all; chop them all fine and steep them in the wine. Use one *jin* of silk floss and four *zhang* of fine white cloth, and put them together into the wine. Place the wine in smoldering horse dung, cover and seal it with mud so that nothing escapes. After five days and five nights, take out the cloth and floss and dry them in the sun; when dry, steep them again, until the juice is used up. Each time they are steeped, they must be left a full day before being taken out to dry. When dry, use the dregs together with the floss, and make lined kerchiefs of doubled cloth, six or seven *chi* long, six or seven kerchiefs. Then roast the kerchiefs over fresh mulberry charcoal and apply them to the needled place of the cold impediment, letting the heat enter as far as the diseased place; when they cool, roast them again and apply them, stopping after thirty times. When sweat comes out, wipe the body with the kerchief, also stopping after thirty times. [Have the patient] get up and walk about indoors, not exposed to wind. Apply the compress after every needling; in this way the illness will end. This is what is called putting heat in."

## Notes

How to make and use the medicinal compress for cold impediment.
