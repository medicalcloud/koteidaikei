# 霊枢 寿夭剛柔第六 §14 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Bogao answered, "When the wall base is low and its height does not reach the ground, one dies before thirty; if an illness is added to this, one dies before twenty."

## Notes

牆基 and 地 are provisionally translated as referring to the bony frame and flesh of the face. Unverified (recorded in the list of deferred points).
