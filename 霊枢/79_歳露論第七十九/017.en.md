# 霊枢 歳露論第七十九 §17 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoshi said, "Riding the decline of the year, meeting the emptiness of the moon, losing the harmony of the season, and so being injured by a thief wind—this is called the three depletions. Thus a practitioner who discusses [illness] without knowing the three depletions becomes crude instead."
