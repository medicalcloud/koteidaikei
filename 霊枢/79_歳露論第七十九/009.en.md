# 霊枢 歳露論第七十九 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoshi answered, "Not so. Thief winds and pathogenic qi strike people not according to season. But if they strike when [the interstices] are open, they enter deep, the illness inside becomes extreme, and it comes on suddenly and violently; if they strike when the interstices are closed, they enter shallowly and linger, and the illness is slow and gradual."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: perverse, irregular qi of the four seasons is thief wind and pathogenic qi; unlike the deficiency pathogens of the eight regulars where Taiyi resides, which have constant signs, these have no fixed time or place, hence "not according to season". But they penetrate deep only when the skin's interstices are open, and when deep the internal illness is extreme, hence sudden and violent; when the interstices are closed, they strike shallowly, but if they do not leave they linger and cause illness, only slowly.
