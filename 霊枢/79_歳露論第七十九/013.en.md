# 霊枢 歳露論第七十九 §13 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoshi said, "The human being forms a triad with heaven and earth and corresponds to the sun and moon. Thus when the moon is full, the sea swells in the west; in people the blood and qi accumulate, the flesh is full, the skin dense, the hair firm, the interstices closed, and the grime adheres. At such a time, even if one meets a thief wind, it enters only shallowly. When the moon's disk is empty, the sea swells in the east; people's qi and blood are deficient, the wei-defensive qi departs and the body is left alone; the flesh diminishes, the skin slackens, the interstices open, the hair is damaged, the skin texture thins, and the grime falls off. At such a time, if one meets a thief wind, it enters deep, and the illness comes on suddenly and violently."

## Notes

For 㕁 in 腠理㕁, the *Leijing* and the *Jiayi jing* read 郄; the sound gloss 理郄 at the end of the chapter (interlinear note: reading *qi-ni*) appears to refer to this character. It is translated as "closed, dense".
- Yang Shangshan's note in the *Taisu*: the human body forms a triad with the shapes of heaven and earth, and its waxing and waning correspond to the sun and moon.
