# 霊枢 歳露論第七十九 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "Each time the defensive qi reaches Fengfu the interstices open, and when they open the pathogen enters. But if the defensive qi descends one vertebra a day, it does not coincide with Fengfu. How is this?"
