# 霊枢 歳露論第七十九 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "When a pathogen lodges at Fengfu, the illness descends along the paraspinal muscles. The wei-defensive qi always has a great meeting at Fengfu once each day and night; from the next day it descends one vertebra a day, so the attacks come later each day. This is because it first lodged in the spine and back. Each time [the wei-defensive qi] reaches Fengfu, the interstices open; when they open, the pathogenic qi enters; when it enters, the illness attacks. That is why the attacks come later each day. The wei-defensive qi goes from Fengfu down one vertebra a day; in twenty-one days it reaches the coccyx; on the twenty-second day it enters inside the spine and pours into the hidden *Chong* meridian; traveling for nine days, it emerges in the supraclavicular fossa. Its qi then ascends, so the attacks gradually come earlier. When [the pathogen] strikes inward at the five zang organs and extends sideways to the membranes, its path is long, its qi deep and its movement slow, so it cannot attack daily, and it accumulates and attacks every other day."

## Notes

The *Suwen*'s *Nüe lun* chapter has nearly the same text.
- 次日乃稸積而作 is taken as attacks every other day, since Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing* refers to "attacks on alternate days" in the earlier *Nüe lun*.
- 其病稍益 is taken as the attacks gradually coming earlier, by comparison with the *Taisu* and the *Suwen*'s *Nüe lun* (the translator's interpretation).
