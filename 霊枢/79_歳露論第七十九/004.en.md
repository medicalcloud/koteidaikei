# 霊枢 歳露論第七十九 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The 'wind palace' has no fixed place. Wherever the wei-defensive qi responds, the interstices must open; the vertebra where the qi lodges is the palace."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: for 衛気之所応 the earlier *Nüe lun* reads 所発; 所舎節 means the vertebra where [the pathogen] lodges.
