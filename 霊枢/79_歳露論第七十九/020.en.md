# 霊枢 歳露論第七十九 §20 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "How good this discussion! How clear this way! Let it be stored in the golden casket and named 'the three repletions'. But this is a discussion of one individual."

## Notes

命曰三実 stands here in the *Taisu* and the *Leijing* as in the base, but the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the *Lingshu jizhu* place it at the end of Shaoshi's preceding speech (after 不能危之也). The base is translated.
- 一夫之論 refers to illness of one individual; the year's common illnesses are discussed below (*Leijing*).
