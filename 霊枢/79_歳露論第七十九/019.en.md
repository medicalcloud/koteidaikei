# 霊枢 歳露論第七十九 §19 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoshi said, "Meeting the abundance of the year, encountering the fullness of the moon, and obtaining the harmony of the season—then even if there are thief winds and pathogenic qi, they cannot endanger one."
