# 霊枢 歳露論第七十九 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Shaoshi, "I have heard that when the eight winds of the four seasons strike people, there is of course cold and heat: in cold the skin tightens and the interstices close; in heat the skin slackens and the interstices open. Can thief winds and pathogenic qi enter thereby? Or must there be a depletion pathogen of the eight regulars before people can be injured?"

## Notes

From here the dialogue is with Shaoshi.
