# 霊枢 歳露論第七十九 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "The classic says that one injured by summer heat in summer falls ill with malaria in autumn. Why do malarial attacks come at set times?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. The first part (dialogue with Qibo) explains why malaria attacks at set times; the second (dialogue with Shaoshi) explains how thief winds and deficiency pathogens injure people, the three depletions and three repletions, and how to read the year's fortune from the "year's exposure".
