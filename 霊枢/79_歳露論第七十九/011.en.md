# 霊枢 歳露論第七十九 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Shaoshi answered, "Does Your Majesty not know how pathogens enter? Even in ordinary life, the opening and closing, slackening and tightening of the interstices always have their reasons in time."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: even in uneventful times, those who fall suddenly ill through the opening, closing, slackening and tightening of the interstices each have a reason, namely the qi of the season.
