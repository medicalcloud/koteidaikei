# 霊枢 外揣第四十五 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I have heard the nine chapters on the nine needles and personally received their tuning, and have grasped much of their meaning. The nine needles begin with one and end with nine, yet I have not grasped their essential way. The nine needles are so small that they have no inside and so large that they have no outside; so deep that nothing can be below them, so high that nothing can cover them; elusive without end, flowing and overflowing without limit. I know that they accord with the way of heaven, human affairs and the changes of the four seasons. But I wish to gather even the finest hairs and bind them all into one. Is that possible?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, explaining the key to diagnosis: inferring the inside from the outside.
