# 霊枢 外揣第四十五 §7 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "How exacting! A bright light cannot be obscured; it cannot be obscured because it does not lose yin and yang. Combining and examining, feeling and verifying, seeing and grasping—it is like clear water and a bright mirror never missing the form. When the five notes are not distinct and the five colors not bright, the five zang organs are in turmoil. In such a case inside and outside affect each other like the drum answering the drumstick, the echo answering the sound, the shadow resembling the form. Hence from afar, one attends to the outside to infer the inside; from close by, one attends to the inside to infer the outside. This is called the ultimate of yin and yang, the canopy of heaven and earth. Let it be stored in the Chamber of the Spirit Orchid and never be let out."
