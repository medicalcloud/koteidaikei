# 霊枢 外揣第四十五 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The sun and moon, water and mirror, drum and echo. The light of the sun and moon never misses the shadow; the reflection of water and mirror never misses the form; the response of drum and echo never lags behind the sound. When there is movement, there is a corresponding response, and the whole situation is grasped."
