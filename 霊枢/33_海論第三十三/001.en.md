# 霊枢 海論第三十三 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor asked Qibo, "I have heard the methods of needling from you, Master, and what you say never departs from the ying-nutrient and wei-defense, blood and qi. The twelve meridians belong inwardly to the bowels and zang organs and connect outwardly with the limbs and joints. Do you, Master, match them with the four seas?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking about the four seas of the body (the sea of marrow, the sea of blood, the sea of qi and the sea of water and grain).
