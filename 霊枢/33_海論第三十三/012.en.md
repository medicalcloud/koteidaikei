# 霊枢 海論第三十三 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the sea of qi is in surplus, qi fills the chest, there is oppressed breathing and a red face; when the sea of qi is insufficient, the qi is scant and insufficient for speaking. When the sea of blood is in surplus, one constantly imagines one's body to be large and feels vexed without knowing where the illness is; when the sea of blood is insufficient, one likewise constantly imagines one's body to be small and feels cramped without knowing where the illness is. When the sea of water and grain is in surplus, the abdomen is full; when it is insufficient, one is hungry but cannot take in food. When the sea of marrow is in surplus, one is light, vigorous and strong, exceeding one's own limits; when the sea of marrow is insufficient, the brain spins, the ears ring, the shins ache, one is dizzy and clouded, the eyes see nothing, and one is listless and wants to lie down."
