# 霊枢 海論第三十三 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The stomach is the sea of water and grain; its transport points are Qijie above and Sanli below. The *Chong* meridian is the sea of the twelve meridians; its transport points are Dazhu above and the upper and lower Juxu below. Danzhong is the sea of qi; its transport points are above and below the pillar bone and Renying in front. The brain is the sea of marrow; its transport points are its cover above and Fengfu below."
