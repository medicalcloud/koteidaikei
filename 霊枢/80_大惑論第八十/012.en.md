# 霊枢 大惑論第八十 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The defensive qi lingers in the yin and cannot move into the yang. Lingering in the yin, the yin qi becomes abundant; when the yin qi is abundant, the yin springing vessel is full; unable to enter the yang, the yang qi is depleted, so the eyes close."
