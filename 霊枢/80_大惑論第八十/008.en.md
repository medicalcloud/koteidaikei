# 霊枢 大惑論第八十 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The essence qi combines in the spleen and heat qi lingers in the stomach; when the stomach is hot it digests the grain, and when the grain is digested one is often hungry. When the stomach qi counterflows upward, the stomach duct is cold, so one has no appetite."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: the stomach qi counterflowing upward and unable to move is the "cold"; the spleen and stomach are hot while the stomach duct is cold, so though hungry one does not want to eat.
