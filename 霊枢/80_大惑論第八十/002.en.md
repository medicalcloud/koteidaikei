# 霊枢 大惑論第八十 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo answered, "The essence qi of the five zang organs and six fu organs all rises and pours into the eyes to become their essence [their clear sight]. The nest of the essence is the eye. The essence of the bones becomes the pupil; the essence of the sinews, the dark of the eye; the essence of the blood, the collaterals; the essence of the qi of the nest, the white of the eye; the essence of the flesh, the eyelids, which wrap and gather the essence of sinews, bones, blood and qi and together with the vessels form the eye connection, attaching above to the brain and emerging behind at the nape. So when a pathogen strikes the nape and meets a deficiency in the body, it enters deep, follows the eye connection into the brain; entering the brain, the brain spins; when the brain spins, it pulls the eye connection tight; when the eye connection is tight, the eyes are dizzy and turn. The eyeballs slant, and what they focus on does not align; then the essence scatters; when it scatters, vision splits; when vision splits, one sees double. The eyes are the essence of the five zang organs and six fu organs, where the ying-nutrient and wei-defense, the ethereal and corporeal souls constantly operate, and where the spirit qi arises. So when the spirit is overworked, the souls scatter and the will and intention are disordered. Thus the pupil and the dark of the eye model the yin, the white of the eye and the red vessels the yang; yin and yang combine and transmit, and there is clear sight. The eyes are the messengers of the heart; the heart is the dwelling of the spirit. So when spirit and essence are disordered and do not turn [smoothly], and one suddenly sees an extraordinary place, the essence, spirit and souls scatter and do not hold together—hence it is called confusion."

## Notes

Translated following the *Leijing* notes.
- From 骨之精為瞳子: the essence of the bones is governed by the kidneys, which belong to water and are dark in color, so the pupil is pure black; that of the sinews by the liver, whose color is azure, so the dark of the eye is lighter than the pupil; that of the blood by the heart, so the vessels at the canthi are red; that of the nest's qi by the lungs, metal, hence the white of the eye. The eyelids, which open and close, are the essence of the flesh governed by the spleen; the spleen, earth, stores things, so it wraps and gathers the essence of the four other zang organs to form the eye connection; 擷 is gathering things in the lap of a garment (*Leijing*).
- 邪其精，其精所中不相比也: the *Leijing* reads 邪其睛，其睛所中不相比 and notes that the first 邪 means pathogenic qi and the second equals 斜 "slanting": when the eye connection tightens, the eyes are dizzy and the eyeballs slant, the left and right vessels being alternately slack and tense, so that what the two eyeballs focus on is not aligned and one thing is seen as two. This is followed. The *Taisu* reads 邪中其精，所中不相比也 and the *Jiayi jing* 邪中之精，則其精所中者不相比, meaning "the pathogen strikes the essence".
- The base's 裏擷 is emended to 裹擷. The *Taisu*, the *Leijing* and the *Lingshu jizhu* read 裹擷 (the *Jiayi jing* 裹契, noting "also written 擷"), and the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text also read 裹. The *Leijing* explains that the spleen, belonging to earth and storing things, wraps and gathers the essence of sinews, bones, blood and qi into the eye connection. The graphically similar 裏 is taken as an error for 裹.
