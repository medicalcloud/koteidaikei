# 霊枢 大惑論第八十 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I doubt that this is so. Every time I go to the Eastern Park, I never fail to be confused, and when I leave, I recover. Is it only for the Eastern Park that I overwork my spirit? How strange!"

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: being confused every time he goes to the Eastern Park means confusion occurs even without climbing high, hence his doubt.
