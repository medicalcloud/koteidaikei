# 霊枢 大惑論第八十 §16 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Pathogenic qi lingers in the upper jiao, and the upper jiao is closed and blocked; then, after eating or drinking hot liquids, the wei-defensive qi lingers long in the yin and does not move, so one suddenly sleeps a great deal."

## Notes

The *Leijing* notes: when pathogenic qi resides in the upper jiao and food and drink are added, the wei-defensive qi is retained within and cannot reach out to the yang division, hence sudden excessive sleep; 膲 equals 焦.
- The base's 已食者飲湯 is emended to 已食若飲湯. The *Taisu*, the *Leijing*, the *Jiayi jing*, the *Lingshu jizhu*, the *Huangdi neijing* on Wikisource and the Daizhige text all read 已食若飲湯, "after eating or drinking hot liquids", which fits the context, so the graphically similar 者 is taken as an error for 若.
