# 霊枢 大惑論第八十 §10 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The wei-defensive qi cannot enter the yin and always lingers in the yang. Lingering in the yang, the yang qi becomes full; when the yang qi is full, the *Yangqiao* meridian is abundant; unable to enter the yin, the yin qi is deficient, so the eyes do not close."
