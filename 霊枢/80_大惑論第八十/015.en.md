# 霊枢 大惑論第八十 §15 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "When it is not one's usual pattern and one suddenly sleeps a great deal, what qi makes it so?"

## Notes

非常経 means a change from the usual, explained here as caused by pathogenic qi (*Leijing*).
