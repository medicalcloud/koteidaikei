# 霊枢 大惑論第八十 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Not so. When the heart has something it delights in and the spirit something it dislikes, and they suddenly confound each other, the essence qi is disordered and vision errs, hence confusion; when the spirit shifts, one recovers. So the mild form is bewilderment and the severe form is confusion."

## Notes

The *Leijing* notes: going on an outing is what the heart delights in; suddenly meeting something strange is what the spirit dislikes; delight and dislike suddenly affecting each other disorder the essence qi; on leaving, the spirit shifts and one recovers. 間者 means the mild cases. The *Leijing* reads 相感 for 相惑.
