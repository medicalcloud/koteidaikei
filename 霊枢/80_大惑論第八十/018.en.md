# 霊枢 大惑論第八十 §18 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "First [determine] the viscera and bowels, eliminate the minor faults, and then regulate the qi, draining the abundant and supplementing the depleted. One must first clearly know the hardships and pleasures of the body and mind; only when [the diagnosis] is settled does one needle."

## Notes

The end of the chapter. The *Leijing* note: "these various pathogens" sums up the eight conditions of this discussion; "first the viscera and bowels" means distinguishing the depth in yin and yang; "eliminate the minor faults" means that although these are not grave illnesses they must still be removed. Each person comes to this for a reason, so the hardships and pleasures of body and mind must be examined most closely: hardship—worry and toil—mostly injures the yang of heart and lungs; pleasure—indulgence—mostly injures the yin of spleen and kidneys. Only with a settled judgment can one treat.
