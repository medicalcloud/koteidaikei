# 霊枢 大惑論第八十 §6 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The upper qi is insufficient and the lower qi in surplus; the intestines and stomach are replete and the heart and lungs deficient. With deficiency, the ying-nutrient and wei-defense linger below and for a long time do not rise at the proper times; hence forgetfulness."

## Notes

The *Leijing* note: surplus of the lower qi is said relative to insufficiency of the upper, not a true excess below; when the heart and lungs are deficient above and the ying-nutrient and wei-defense linger below, the spirit qi cannot circulate, hence forgetfulness—a sign of yang declining above.
