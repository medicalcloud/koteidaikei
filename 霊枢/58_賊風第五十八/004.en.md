# 霊枢 賊風第五十八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "Here too an old pathogen has stayed without breaking out; then the mind has aversions or longings, the blood and qi are disordered within, and the two qi struggle. Their origin is so subtle that looking one cannot see it and listening one cannot hear it; hence it seems like ghosts and spirits."
