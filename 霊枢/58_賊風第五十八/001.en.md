# 霊枢 賊風第五十八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "You say, Master, that bandit winds and pathogenic qi harm people and make them ill. Yet there are people who never leave the shelter of screens or go out of their rooms, and still suddenly fall ill, although they have not been exposed to bandit winds and pathogenic qi. Why?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter, asking why people fall ill without meeting bandit winds, and about illnesses cured by incantation.
