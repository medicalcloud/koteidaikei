# 霊枢 賊風第五十八 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "In all these cases the person was once harmed by damp qi, which is stored in the blood vessels and between the partings of the flesh and has stayed long without leaving; or the person once fell, and malign blood remains inside. Then, when joy and anger are suddenly immoderate, eating and drinking unsuitable, and cold and warmth untimely, the interstices close and are blocked; when they open and meet wind and cold, the blood and qi congeal and combine with the old pathogen to form cold impediment. If there is heat, there is sweating, and with sweating one receives wind. Even without meeting bandit winds and pathogenic qi, something is always added that makes it break out."
