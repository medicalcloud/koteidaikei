# 霊枢 賊風第五十八 §3 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "What you have described, Master, are all things the patient knows himself. But some, without meeting pathogenic qi and without fearful thoughts, suddenly fall ill. Why? Is it only due to ghosts and spirits?"
