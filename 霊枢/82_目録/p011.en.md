# 霊枢 目録 §11 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

*Benshen* (8) (modelled on the winds)

## Notes

Chapter title and number. The interlinear note 法風 says that this chapter is modelled on the winds; chapters 1–9 are matched to the series in chapter 78 (*Jiuzhen lun*): heaven, earth, humankind, seasons, notes, pitch-pipes, stars, winds, fields.
