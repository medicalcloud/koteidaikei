# 霊枢 目録 §95 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Originally twenty-four fascicles, now combined into twelve; eighty-one chapters in all.

## Notes

A note at the end of the table of contents. Shi Song's preface says he arranged the work in twenty-four fascicles with the sound glosses added (see the preface); this note says that in this edition they are combined into twelve. Who combined them, and when, is not stated.
