# 霊枢 目録 §8 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

*Genjie* (5) (modelled on the musical notes)

## Notes

Chapter title and number. The interlinear note 音法 says that this chapter is modelled on the musical notes; chapters 1–9 are matched to the series in chapter 78 (*Jiuzhen lun*): heaven, earth, humankind, seasons, notes, pitch-pipes, stars, winds, fields. The others put 法 first (法天, 法地 …), so 音法 is probably an inversion of 法音; it is left as in the base.
