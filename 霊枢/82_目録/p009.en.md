# 霊枢 目録 §9 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

*Shouyao gangrou* (6) (modeled on the pitch-pipes)

## Notes

Chapter title and number. The interlinear note 法律 says that this chapter is modeled on the pitch-pipes; chapters 1–9 are matched to the series in chapter 78 (*Jiuzhen lun*): heaven, earth, humankind, seasons, notes, pitch-pipes, stars, winds, fields.
