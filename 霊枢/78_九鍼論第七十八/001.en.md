# 霊枢 九鍼論第七十八 §1 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The Yellow Emperor said, "I have heard from you, Master, about the nine needles; it is vast and extensive, and I still cannot grasp it. May I ask where the nine needles came from, and why they have their names?"

## Notes

The beginning of the chapter. It describes how the nine needles correspond to the numbers of heaven and earth, their shapes and lengths, the correspondence of the body to the nine fields, illnesses of the five forms and minds, the correspondences of the qi of the five viscera, the five flavors, the five combinations, etc., and the amounts of qi and blood of the six channels.
