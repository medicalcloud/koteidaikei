# 霊枢 九鍼論第七十八 §12 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

The *Yangming* has much blood and much qi; the greater yang much blood and little qi; the lesser yang much qi and little blood; the greater yin much blood and little qi; the *Jueyin* much blood and little qi; the lesser yin much qi and little blood. Hence it is said: needling the *Yangming*, let out blood and qi; needling the greater yang, let out blood but avoid [letting out] qi; needling the lesser yang, let out qi but avoid blood; needling the greater yin, let out blood but avoid qi; needling the *Jueyin*, let out blood but avoid qi; needling the lesser yin, let out qi but avoid blood. The foot *Yangming* and greater yin are exterior and interior; the lesser yang and *Jueyin*; the greater yang and lesser yin—these are called the yin and yang of the foot. The hand *Yangming* and greater yin are exterior and interior; the lesser yang and heart governor; the greater yang and lesser yin—these are called the yin and yang of the hand."

## Notes

The end of the chapter.
- The amounts of qi and blood of the three yin here differ from chapter 65 (*Wuyin wuwei*: *Jueyin* much qi and little blood, lesser yin much blood and little qi, greater yin much blood and little qi), as the *Lingshu jizhu* also notes (see the note on chapter 65). The base is translated.
