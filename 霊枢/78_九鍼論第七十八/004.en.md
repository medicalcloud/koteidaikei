# 霊枢 九鍼論第七十八 §4 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "When the sages devised the numbers of heaven and earth, they multiplied one by nine and so established the nine fields; nine times nine, eighty-one, gave rise to the number of the Yellow Bell. The needles correspond to these numbers.

## Notes

Qibo's speech continues through unit 5. Zhang Jiebin's note in the *Leijing*: from one to nine, and nine times nine is eighty-one, from which the number of the Yellow Bell arises; the Yellow Bell is the root of all things, so the number of needles corresponds to it, and their uses vary without end.
