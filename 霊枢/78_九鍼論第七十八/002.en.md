# 霊枢 九鍼論第七十八 §2 (English translation)

## Translation of the text

Qibo said, "The nine needles are the great numbers of heaven and earth, beginning with one and ending with nine. Hence it is said: the first models heaven, the second earth, the third humans, the fourth the seasons, the fifth the notes, the sixth the pitch pipes, the seventh the stars, the eighth the winds, the ninth the fields."
